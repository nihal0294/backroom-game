*"The Backrooms Robotic's only goal is to help wanderers."*

#### Profile: Head of Backrooms Robotics STATUS: ACTIVE

## NAME: True name unknown, goes by Yellow.

##### Possible Location(s): [Level 522](level-522.md)

**Backrooms Robotics Title: CEO**

# Description:

The Head of Backrooms Robotics, who refuses to reveal her true name and instead goes by Yellow, is a very important figure. She controls [Level Achromatomaly](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-achromatomaly), [Level Achromatopsia](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-achromatopsia), and [Level 522](level-522.md). She is also considered an ally to [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) because of her many inventions such as [Object 38](object-38.md), [Entity 82](entity-82.md), [Object 97](object-97.md), and [Object 8](object-8.md) many being exceedingly helpful to The M.E.G.

Her height is somewhere around 6'2" and she's often described as having very sharp features. She often wears a suit and has never been spotted without one on. She has black hair, purple eyes, and a very sharp expression along with glasses that always rest low on her nose.

Her exact age isn't know but she's estimated to be 20-25 physically and is said to be of Finnish descent. Not much about her past is known, although many speculate that she had some sort of training before entering The Backrooms because her engineering, fighting, and leadership skills are all far above adequate.

# Behaviors:

Many of Yellow's employees have said she, (although strict at times), is a very good leader. Although she usually displays anti-social qualities, . She naturally has great intuition and always has a plan. She can be often seen planning and designing experiments or projects that could be created years in the future. Being highly goal-oriented, accomplished, and excelling at what she does is what has made her so successful.
