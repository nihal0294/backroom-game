# Featured

##### Monthly Features

The monthly features are a collection of four pages posted within the last month that were chosen to be the best representations of our site, showcasing the talent of our authors and our content goals for the wiki. All pages posted within a monthly period are voted on by the Greenlighter Team, and the four pages that are regarded as the highest quality pieces of literature are then recognized as such.
