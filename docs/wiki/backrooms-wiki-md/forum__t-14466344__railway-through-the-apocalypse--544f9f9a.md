Railway Through The Apocalypse

25 Jan 2022 17:15

I'm going for an easy level to survive that was inspired by riding on a tram.

This level is a tram ride through an entity infested city. Staying on the tram will keep you safe, but if you stay on the tram when it departs "Final Destination", it is unknown what will happen. Despite the countless entities in the city, only one entity will board the tram. Said entity will be friendly and have the appearance of a human male. No one remembers specific features of this entity besides age and clothing. This level is considered SAFE since surviving the level is easy as long as you remain on the tram. However, if you were to exit the tram at any station other than Final Destination, you will face certain death.

One thing I'm not sure of is whether to call this level secure or unsecure, due to the hostile environment outside of the tram. I could use some input on this.

Here's the draft: <http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/jack-grimm>
