> If the only guarantee you have as a human being is that you are going to die, the Backrooms certainly isn't the place to look for exceptions. Even for those who manage to obtain pseudo-immortality, the Backrooms will still find its workarounds. With its randomness and instability being its most lethal aspects, wanderers may find themselves wondering how much agency they truly have in their survival.

Most end up accepting what they have—learning to live in the moment. Cherish what little peace they find in the chaos. After all, if man cannot conquer Mother Nature, what chance would they have against the Backrooms?

*It'd take a special kind of crazy—perhaps stupidity—to even try.*
