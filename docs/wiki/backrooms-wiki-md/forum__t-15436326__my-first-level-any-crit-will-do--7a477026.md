Hello.  
Well, i'm not a green lighter and i'm probably as green as you.  
Here's my critic:

Visual : OK

Writing: if you're looking for classical experience, you have to rewrite some part in clinical tone.  
Example :  
Level 969, the Black Swamp, is a swamp stretching for gods knows how long. The dead trees stretch up to 48 feet1 with branches stretching to 6 feet2.  
Become  
=>  
Level 969 named "Black swamp" is a swamp stretching on an unknown area. No borders or limits have been spotted during exploration. Many dead trees can be found in the swamp with a maximum height of 48 feet for the main body and 6 feet for the branches.

Concept: Well, it can be summarized to "swamp with black liquid make entity more aggressive yet less prone to hearing".  
The concept has potential, but need to be further exploited.  
Does the entities spawn with black liquid on them or do they get corrupted over time?  
Does the black liquid also corrupt humans?  
What effect the heat generation ability of the black liquid has on the level?  
…  
..  
.

If you're looking for originality, always ask yourself what make the level different from others.  
Don't hesitate to reverse concepts for fun. It can be inspiring.

Population:  
Huntermen: Well, they're mercenaries.  
Why do they live in this place? ressources? heritage? ideology?  
How do they get their resources (they're in a swamp)?  
Why do they keep hunting the entities covered with black liquid?  
Are they aware of a specific danger associated to the liquid?  
They are 2 leaders. What is their relationship? Conflict? peace? difference of ideologies?

Glad to help.

Understood. I'll give it a once-over and write down what I see as I'm reading. Let's begin. I will italicize different grammar rules I feel are important to know in this situation. Or I'll assume you know it.

- **Level 969 named "Black Swamp" is a swamp stretching on an unknown area.** Add a comma after "969" and after "Swamp". *Parenthetical information is information that isn't needed, but you can still have it. How to know if it's parenthetical: If you take it out of the sentence, does the sentence still make sense?*
- **Many dead trees can be found in the swamp with a maximum height of 48 feet for the main body and 6 feet for the branches.** Put a comma after "swamp".
- **The black liquid in the swamps smells slightly of sulfur.** Take the s off the end of "swamps".
- **A small amount of heat radiates from it from an unknown origin, so check your boat frequently to see if it has burned.** If it's radiating from the liquid, then the heat isn't radiating from an unknown source.
- **The liquid in this level covers 75% of it, making it a little safe to travel.** Does the liquid cover 75% of the Level? I believe that should be specified. Also, you should specify that it is only safe to travel the liquid through boats.
- **The liquid feels a little bit of slime but is not sticky like it.** I get what you're trying to say here, but the sentence could use a bit of a rewrite. Consider using: "The liquid has a similar consistency to slime, though it does not stick to most surfaces."
- **The swamp versions of Entities are covered in the black liquid found in the swamp and twigs are all over them.** Instead of "and twigs are all over them", use "as well as twigs". The first one is restating that they are covered in something, instead of just the other thing they're covered in. It's a bit redundant.
- **The swamp version of Clumps moves around in the liquid, so be careful to watch where you're going.** "version" should be changed to "versions" and "moves" should be "move".
- **The only difference that happened was that they fell apart and died within 10 minutes of being the swamp Entity.** Do you mean "he" instead of "they"? You don't need "that happened". It takes away from the flow.

Aside from that, no problems from me! Happy writing!
