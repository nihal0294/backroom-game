The concept is liminal enough (kind of like level 0), the picture isn't bad, but the description definitely lacked of something. Yes, it has enough characters to be considered as a level, and the picture is good, but it lacked of interesting details in the description, in fact, the whole level is like a maze and just a big entity chasing a person, and a few small details :/  
The entries and the exits were good and there's not much to say about that. However, the discovery was really interesting to read, it had details and it was perfectly-written, even though that I don't really like reading about the levels' discoveries usually. To change fonts you can press ctrl+b, ctrl+i, or ctrl+u but I think you've known it before, I think you can see more about the font on [https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/faq](faq.md) . In overall, the level is a 6.5/10 for me, it deserves to be a level even if it could have been better if not the description. I've written mostly about the aspects of the level you could've improved, but I really appreciate the level, it's good, keep up the good work!
