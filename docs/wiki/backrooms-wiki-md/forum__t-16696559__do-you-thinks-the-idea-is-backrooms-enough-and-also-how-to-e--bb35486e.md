Description: when a wanderer dip a smiler's tooth in almond water and use it as a necklace it vibrate like a level key when the smiler entity is near it and when close enough (less than two meters or six feets away) it will brake and flash the room with an intense light for between seven to ten seconds which can blind or scare the nearby entities enough to make them run away.  
Frequency:It will happen at a 99,9% rate at any levels exept the negatives ones for an unknown reason.  
Relevant Parties: The M.E.G theorised that the phenomenon is caused by a chemical reaction between almond water and a non toxic gaz emitted by decomposing smiler. Perhaps we still don't know why it light up when extremly close to smilers.  
Discovery: The first one to discover this is a twelve years old boy named Jake in november 2019 who found a smiler's tooth on the ground of level! and decided to clean it up with almond water to make a "cool necklace for my girlfriend!". He unfortunatly died in 2021 of a bonestealer attack in order to save the 14 years old Emily who was wandering whith him.
