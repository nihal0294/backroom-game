since you've waited so patiently and no one has responded yet, I'll drop you some feedback

As I was reading this, my reaction went from "hmm kinda boring there doesn't really seem to OH MY FUCKING GOD HOLY SHIT." If that's the vibe you're going for, mission accomplished, damn.

My first impressions after reading are as follows:

-Very good concept. Reminiscent of the SCP "When day breaks," but not to the degree that I'd say is a negative.

-Decent SPaG as far as I could tell. I'm not usually someone that crits this though, so I'd recommend seeking some crit for that specifically.

-Despite the writing being pretty good, the page still suffers from a lack of formatting and eye-catchiness. I think adding a little CSS formatting stuff to spice it up would go a long way, as well as an image or two. A theme is also definitely something to consider.

My main suggestion for what to do here would be simply to expand on what you already have. What else can you touch on with this level? You've got the skeleton of a good page, but you still need to actually build the structure around it. A lot of the transitions between sections feel a bit choppy, so those could be some places to look at first. How do you connect the page together within itself?

Something in second person might help you out here— maybe like someone exploring the level as you're explaining it. Level 8 is a very good example of how to do this. If you want a more simple example, the recently written level 412 is also a good showcase of what I'm talking about.

You don't have to do that idea specifically, it's just to give you a bit of "direction" on how to properly finish off the page.

Definitely looking forward to seeing this continue.
