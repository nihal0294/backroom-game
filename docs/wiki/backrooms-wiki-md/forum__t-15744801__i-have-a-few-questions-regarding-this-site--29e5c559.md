1. What Aaron said  
2. Ditto  
3. You would recieve a message from the staff saying that you aren't allowed to be on site for a specified period of time. You would be kicked. You would not be allowed to contact users regarding work on site, as that would be considered ban evasion. You would not be able to reapply for membership until the ban ends; any applications sent during the ban to rejoin would be denied. You would be able to contact staff to appeal the ban be undone and discuss your situation.  
4. You would at least need critique from a greenlighter. 5. Number of critique needed ranges depending on the article and person. You only need one greenlight to post.
