## Backrooms Thai Wiki

---

Through thick and thin, our community has stood. After a long period without our own official wiki, having to gather around private server and private server. The Thai Backrooms Wiki was finally established in July the 16th 2023, with the help of . Originally only hosting translations, we have expanded to include our own array of original pages, notably entities.

Note that our site are moderated independently and will have different rules and regulations than this site, so if you join it you're under our staff members' care. Don't assume you're eligible to join it just because you're a member of this site!
