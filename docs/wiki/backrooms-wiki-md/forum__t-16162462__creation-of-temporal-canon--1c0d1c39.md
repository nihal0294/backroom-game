Considering you're doing multiverse stuff, you already do a lot more than just time related stuff that many people don't even consider in their stories. I'm not sure if your canon would be limited to just time stuff. It sounds more like a sort of Temporal Multiverse Canon. Some could say the psychic stuff as well is unique enough to be specific to a canon, but then again, in an endless multiverse, there are bound to be many interesting and new things, even universes where the rules as we understand them are fundamentally different. I'm not entirely sure if the other stuff matters so much, since there are other canons/stories that use the multiverse and psychic ideas, although maybe not always in the same ways. Actually, given that there are so many different models of time and so many different ways it can glitch/mess up, wouldn't it be accurate to say that no model of time rules the multiverse? Unless you're going with some idea of one true flow of time existing around all these universes with their glitchy time, allowing universes to move in sync with one another, in a sort of way.

Although, to add to this, the temporal side-effects of no-clipping (like the ghost of level 11), the aging phenomena where some people age slower or faster than others, some even to the point of basically never aging (Ratif is making a page on this), and more would definitely contribute to the temporal part of things. The bottom area of The Sh3dy Gr4y also has some time stuff going on.
