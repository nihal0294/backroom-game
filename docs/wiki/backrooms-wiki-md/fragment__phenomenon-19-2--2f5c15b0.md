# Phenomenon 19 - "R.W.P."

The second incident was more local and occurred at a small outpost. Over a private radio channel a man's voice was was heard saying this:

> "Yesterday…\*static\*… a date which will live in infamy the…Backrooms…was suddenly and deliberately attacked…by…\*static\*"

After getting replacements to stand for them, guards at the outpost found that the Backrooms had not been attacked the previous day and that the man's statement on the radio had been false. The words were identified to be an altered version of Franklin Roosevelt's "Day of Infamy" speech to the U.S. Congress.
