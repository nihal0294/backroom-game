Sure, although I can't greenlight it becuase I'm not a greenlighter. I'll look at it tomorrow.  
I'm going to point out the confusing parts of your article rather than SPaG errors or concept critique.

> Hiders that live in Level 0 act as warnings for other Hiders and Skin-Stealers. Level 0 Hiders are not aggressive to anyone and will only warn Wanderers about other Hiders and Skin-Stealers

Why are these ones different? I also want to note that this wiki's interpretation of Level 0 is different from other wiki's. It is devoid of entities, and completely safe. I would suggest moving this variation to level 1 instead of level 0, since it at least has some danger. They could serve as a warning for when the lights go out.

> it is possible to have a Hider without it attacking you if you feed it Almond water every now and then.  
> Do:  
> Give one Almond water every now and then  
> Don't:  
> try to tame one

These statements are contradicting each other. Why would you give one almond water if it not taming it? Why would you want to keep a deadly monster around? Wouldn't you just be wasting supplies? Please explain.

> Make sure everyone you are traveling with can actually understand you

How would you make sure of this? Couldn't the entity pretend to understand?
