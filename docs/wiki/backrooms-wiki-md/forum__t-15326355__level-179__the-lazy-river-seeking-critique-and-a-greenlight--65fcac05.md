Hi, so if you remember I reviewed this level about a month ago, and after looking through I can say that this is a really good page and definitely could have a place on the wiki. There are just a few issues that shouldn't be too hard to fix:

> If you're in the water, you will be pushed in different directions, you may find floaties in the water as the water takes you in random directions.

Replace the second comma with a period and capitalize the Y on the second "you".

> W.P. (Wanderers Paradise)

I think there is too little information on the group. Add a few more bullet points, describing the group's goals and origins. Doesn't have to be too complicated, just a sentence or so.

Lastly, there is a stray string of text under the Author's section and Level select. (Scroll down to the very bottom and you should see it, the text is "inhabited by people.]".) You will probably need to delete that text or move it somewhere else.

That's really all I have. Hope you get greenlighted soon!
