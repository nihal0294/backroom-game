**Level -3** is a strange but welcome change of pace to the rest of the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

Level -3 is entirely made up of a system of grey, circular, concrete tunnels that seemingly are located above ground. The walls are barren and have nothing on them. The tunnels are very dimly lit by an unknown source, but Periodically you may find a small crack in the wall emitting a bright light. Looking through any of the cracks shows you what looks to be a grassy plane with a forest in the distance.

The tunnel itself is strangely raining on the inside. There is rain coming from the ceiling constantly, providing a good source of clean water. Another strange property of this Level is that while you are walking, you begin to feel very calm and rested, as if you just woke up from a good night's rest. This Level provides a safe rest to those who enter the level.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There are no know Bases, Outposts or Communities on this level.

**Entrances And Exits:**

### Entrances

The only known Entrance is by finding and large metal door in Level 3. An easy way to find one is by listening for rain.

Exits  
The only know way of exiting is by closing your eyes and taking ten or more steps backwards, doing so will leave you in level 10
