This rewrite doesn't necessarily feel like it expands on anything in Level 10, besides just the removal of the things that didn't work in the original. I like that fact, as it definitely gives Level 10 an actual feeling of uniformity instead of being the Stuff HappensTM level

There are some good additions, too, although mostly I like the fact some elements of the level were removed. The worms are… Okay? like, they exist, and maybe for some it's creepy that worms exist underneath all of Level 10, but for me it feels kinda pointless. Like it only exists to pad out an extra two paragraphs and add in some kind of weird, vague threat/hazard. It's an eh change, I don't mind it's there but I wouldn't exactly winge about it not being there if it was removed (or about any entities, for that matter)

The SD banner I don't like at all, not because it's badly designed, but because it just feels like abunch of div boxes. it's a lot of stuff happening in a small area, a hell of a lot of dead space, and the fact it's nothing but square div boxes make it feel less like a banner and moreso just like a random assortment of, well, div boxes. The blur effect on the other boxes are okay, although personally they look weirdly darkened? idk, maybe just a me thing.

Overall, yeah, it's okay. I'm not going to upvote or downvote it, since it's… Just okay. It's an improvement to the old Level 10, but not in any way that intrigued me more than "oh, a new Level 10" and "thank god the weird groups and shit were removed". Can't deny there wasn't time and effort put into this, too, although I'm not going to upvote because of that fact.

(Also, I would personally use the third image as the first image, goes for the liminal and lonely vibes a lot more than the first one actually does due to the amount of large trees getting in the way of the composition)
