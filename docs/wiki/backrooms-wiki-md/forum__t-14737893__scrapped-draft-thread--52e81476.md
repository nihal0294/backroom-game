Alright, let's do this, I have a bunch to pick from. Kick it off with a bang, y'know?

<http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/snomwriting2>

This sandbox has four drafts i'm submitting. Kaiser's POI page, Saberzoa, The Pianist and the Dream Theater. Here's a few short descriptions.

**Frederick Kaiser**  
The man in charge of Level 817's scrapyard. An ace mechanic with a penchant for heavy metal, explosions, and straight up batshit insane ideas.

**The Pianist**  
A scrapped Terror Hotel entity, a pianist that uses corpses as marionettes for his fleshy orchestra, and you better stay to see the whole show…

**Saberzoa**  
Small floating jellyfish entities that go around aimlessly- though they can be trained to deliver messages across Levels, since they can float seamlessly through the Blue Channel.

**Dream Theater**  
A movie theater room that plays only your biography in movie form, but with one key change. Maybe you went to a different college, maybe you never met that one friend, maybe you confessed to that girl, who knows?

Additionally, here's a few other ideas I'd like to submit that have no associated drafts.

**Reality Acid**  
The acid produced by Reality Bugs to digest their food. While hard to obtain and store, it can erase entire levels from existence if you have enough of it.

**The Star**  
A level comprised of the interior of a star. Nothing can survive there, as you get immediatly bathed in nuclear fire. And yet, there's something coming from deep within the burning hydrogen… a mysterious radio signal. What could it be?

**Giant Axolotls**  
Like, axolotls, but they're 30 feet and you can ride them around. Very docile boyos.

**Home Sweet Home**  
Ever watched Encanto? This idea was inspired by the living house in that movie, Casita. It's a non-hostile entity found in level 11, and it's basically a living apartment that feeds on dust. Different instances of this entity would have different personalities- some can be shy, afraid to show they're more than a home, while others can be little rascals…

Feel free to contact me if you have any questions! You can find me through my socials in my author page, on the official Discord, or through Wikidot PMs.

## Church of the Veiled rewrite

<http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/collab:cotvpoc>

This was an article originally written by Papasnowy. I was super stoked to see a pure evil GOI finally appear on the site — and then promptly let down by numerous problems with the original article. I reached out to him requesting permission to collab on a rewrite, which he accepted! I worked quite hard on this for a while… then dropped it, because life got busy and I had many IRL things to attend to.

Notably, one concept I've developed but isn't very clear in the article presently is their philosophy. That was one roadblock I hit as I was writing. There's a lot of waffle about their practices, but not much of a clear philosophy in mind. I don't want to go the Sarkite-Mekanite route of order vs chaos, because that's been done before. I want this group to be organized and orderly. But I didn't initially have any ideas on how to flesh this out properly, so the article is really vague about it.

Eventually, I've settled on this: **The COTV's way of life is to gain status by vicious, brute force. They live to conquer and to enslave, and to subject their victims to utmost suffering for their own sick pleasure. They attempt to take power by force from their superiors, and subdue those below them by enforcing their own authority, ruling with fear.** Think kind of like the Sith philosophy from Star Wars. Okay, a LOT like the Sith from Star Wars. But I don't mind — their vibes and cultural practices are different enough that I don't see an issue. I obviously haven't had the chance to incorporate this into the article yet, though.

Sadly, I don't believe Snowy is still on the site. Permission was previously given by him to look for another author to finish what we'd worked on… so I believe it's alright to put this up for scrapcon.

Some requests I'd really really appreciate, if you choose to use this:

- Keep this group PURE EVIL. There is nothing, NOTHING, good in their intentions and practices. They are a punching-bag bad guy for other groups to have conflicts with.

- While you may draw on motifs from existing real-world religious systems, be sure to portray the COTV as practicing a distorted and corrupted version of religion. It would horrify me greatly to directly disrespect a particular real-world religion by portraying its way of life as actually evil.

- Try to keep the parts I've written intact, or rewrite with the same general idea.

- I'd prefer an author with relative prowess in SPaG, and who can match the tone and style I have used, which is intentionally somewhat archaic (or even pick out the inconsistencies in my writing and fix them!)

- Reach out to me for questions and ideas for lore!

Of course, as per the rules, I can't obligate you to adhere to this, so do as you wish!

The Model Exhibit is a secret level of the Backrooms, containing a large, model diorama of every single backrooms level in the first cluster and negative cluster. It's date of discovery and true existence is not known, and it's only proof of existence is an audio recording found on a phone in [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/the-hub The Hub].

+ **Description:**
When You arrive in The Model Exhibit, you will always end up in the hallway behind a door. The hallway is long and seemingly ever expansive, there are lights, but they're extremely dim and dull. The walls are yellow brick colour, with a grey-blue skirting and white roof. The floor itself being a light grey. There are doors either side that are a white-yellow colour, and each are labelled with a number from 0 all the way 999. 

Inside every door reveals the same, usual architecture as the hallway. Just with a much taller roof and the area inside being more akin to an extremely large auditorium, the lights in these room differ depending on what's inside. 

The things that make each room different is what models are found inside, inside each room is an extremely small(yet far stretching) model of a Backrooms level, corresponding to the number seen on the door. (Door 0 goes to [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-0 Level 0], door 1 goes to [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-1 Level 1], etc)

When you enter a room, you will be immediately greeted by the model layout ahead of you, as well as a remote control on a stand. The controller in question usually being used to operate model trains, though that's not what it does here. When walking up to the model layout, you'll realise that you can simply walk right through it. You can't break or destroy the diorama, or any diorama in this Level in fact. The one known explorer to end up here even chucked a water bottle at a layout for [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-4 Level 4], only for the bottle to go through the layout get lost underneath it on the floor. 

While walking through the massive miniature recreation, you may occasionally find a small figure of a human. They look to be made of one singular plastic mould, and simply glide along the model floor. going around the rooms while locked in one random pose. Though most of the poses will be walking ones, there are exceptions seen. Examples being stationary poses, one where someone looked to be climbing something and a seated pose.  However, as well as human figures that roam around, one can also find figures of Entities. there seem to be ones for every single recorded entity, they look to made similarly to the human figures. Inarticulate and made as one plastic mould. Stuck in a variety of different poses. They seem to mimic the movement patterns of their real life counterparts, hound models for example tend to move around extremely fast. 

Should you have taken the remote control with you, sometimes a random button or the screen will light up, prompting you to press it. Doing so will cause a variety of different outcomes, for example, when the only explorer pressed it while he was following around a little figure, a model of a hound seemingly spawned out of nowhere and lunged at the figure of the human. The human figure ceasing to exist right after.
