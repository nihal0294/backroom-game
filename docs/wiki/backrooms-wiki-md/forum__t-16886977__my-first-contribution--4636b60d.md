I'd say my issue is the way the visual effects are described. Right now, the structure is like this:

1. 1 sentence introducing the effect and describing the negative light
2. 1 sentence mentioning that colours are nullified
3. 1 sentence mentioning achromatopsia and describing the specific colours of the level
4. 1 sentence mentioning…
5. and so on

The amount of points included make the structure of "one sentence, one point" feel really slow, which may not serve the hostile nature of the level well. Instead, try condensing multiple points into one or two shorter sentences each, for example:

1. 1 sentence mentioning negativity and grayscale
2. 1 sentence mentioning what survivors see and highlighting that they may take time to adapt
3. 1 or 2 sentences mentioning the permanent effects on wanderers and their equipment
