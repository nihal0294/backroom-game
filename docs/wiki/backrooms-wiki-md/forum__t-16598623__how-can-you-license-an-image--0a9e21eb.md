Re: How can you license an image?

02 Jan 2024 15:17

To license an image, it must be something you created, like art work or a photograph. Then, you'd attach the file to the page, and in the license box[1](javascript:;), you'd put your username, the title of the image, and a license that is compatible with CC BY SA 3.0. The easiest compatible license is CC BY SA 3.0, but our licensing guide has some other licenses that are compatible.

You do not have to use an external page. We prefer you didn't, and just download the image to your device before uploading it to the page via the "Files" button.

Yes, everything should be in the Creative Commons (CC).

Images *must* be licensed and with a license that's compatible with CC BY SA 3.0
