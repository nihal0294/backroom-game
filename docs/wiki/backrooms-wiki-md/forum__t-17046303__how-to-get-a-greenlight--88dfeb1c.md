How to get a greenlight?

22 Feb 2025 18:38

I'm new to the site and I've read the Greenlight policy but I still got a few questions left.

1. If I'm done with my project, should I contact a greenlighter in personal messages or post the work on the forum?  
2 . Is obtaining 3 pieces of critique about a level/sublevel/entity/room/phenomena/tale necessary?  
3. If it is, can I get the critique on other sites?  
4. If no, would it be allowed to repost one project multiple times on the forum?  
5. Why are there no Greenflame Trialees at the moment?

Re: How to get a greenlight?

22 Feb 2025 19:24

1. Post your work in the forum. That way you can get more eyes on it. However, I do recommend joining the Discord if possible. People are more active there, and you'll have the ability to ping critics.

2. Yes. Each piece of critique also needs to be 100 words. You could *try* to DM a greenlighter to see if your page is ready, but having glanced at your draft and because I am a greenlighter, I'm going to say you need to get the 3 critiques. There aren't any SPAG issues, but it is a bit barebones and falls into the trap of writing about just a "place that is weird."

3. Oh I suppose so. I don't recommend it unless they're also Backrooms-related because they might not know what our standards are. If they do need some idea of what we're looking for, I recommend looking at pages posted in 2024. We have changed a lot from just writing about "places that are weird."

4. That clutters up the forums and breaks apart your critique. If your forum isn't getting any traction, you can reply to your message to "bump" it. If you've updated your draft, you can also bump the forum so people know you've done some revisions.

5. No one's applied, or if they have applied, our Greenlighters and staff didn't think they were qualified. If you want to become a Greenflame Trialee, you can apply here: <http://glmp.wikidot.com/apply>
