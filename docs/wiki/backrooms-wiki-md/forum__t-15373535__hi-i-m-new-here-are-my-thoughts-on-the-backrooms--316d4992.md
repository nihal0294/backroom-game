hi, i'm new, here are my thoughts on the backrooms

09 Nov 2022 20:21

i've been really interested in the concept of a place where nothing is certain. it really blows my mind the creativity of some people here. i've been lurking for a while, and just made an account in order to spread my own thoughts on it.

to me, the backrooms feels like zoning out. you know those times where you're paying attention and focusing, and then you realize thirty seconds later that you were zoning out? the backrooms reminds me of just that. you've been daydreaming for so long, you've been zoning out for so long, that your mind has left your body. left your soul. left your reality, into a new world.

however, what i dislike about the idea of the backrooms is the structure. why should there be a predetermined set of levels with predetermined exits and entrances? i think that the backrooms is an extension of reality. a gate to a new, imaginary place. an outside ring of our world.

the backrooms is like complex numbers. the square root of -2 does not exist. what did people do? they made i, an imaginary number. i is everywhere in math, but hidden in plain sight. the backrooms should be whatever your mind wanders to. it shouldn't be a set in stone place. it could be heaven, or hell. it can be sweet, or sour. it should be a place where reality is bent in multiple ways.

i think the reason why you are able to escape the backrooms is because you stopped zoning out. in most cases of escaping, you don't remember your experience in the backrooms. you fell out of this zoning out, you fell out of the bends.

in outer space, it is common knowledge that wormholes exist. it is a four dimensional gateway to a completely different place in our three dimensional universe. if you bend a piece of paper, the edge of the paper will touch the other edge, despite (in a two dimensional world) being far apart. if you were to cross this bend, it would appear that you have magically teleported. this is how you are able to noclip. you are a three dimensional being in who knows how many dimensional space.

as said above, this is how you are able to zone out into the backrooms. your mind wanders into the most mysterious places. your mind becomes flexible, able to go through and travel anywhere. it just so happens that outside of our three dimensional world, you find the four dimensional world known as the backrooms (the back of the dimensional world) which is behind the frontrooms.

because of the backrooms' unique ability to not be perceived as it is by three dimensional beings, this opens up a can of worms.

as established previously, entering the backrooms is possible by losing your grip on reality for an extended period of time, allowing your mind, a flexible substance, to escape out of the three dimensional insides into the four dimensional ring around it. why is this important to know?

we see light through waves. different wavelengths correspond to different colors. we see materials differently due to the way light bounces off them. how can we see the fourth dimension, if we are simply three dimensional beings? the simple answer is, we can't.

it's established in most levels that some places are harder to no-clip in than others. what if no-clipping is simply a way to travel using the fourth dimension? we already discussed that materials look different because of the way light bounces off them. when no-clipping, it is difficult to no-clip in some places because the materials that are being no-clipped through have different densities.

because these materials are in the third dimension, the only way to travel through them is no-clipping, a four-dimensional travel system. when no-clipping, the goal is to touch the least amount of molecules possible in order to pass through it. there is a tiny chance that when you slap your hand on a table, your hand will avoid the molecules on the table. no-clipping is four-dimensional, so it's easier to avoid the molecules when no-clipping. with the backrooms' endless materials and possibilities, some materials might as well be infinitely dense, which is why you can't no-clip through some materials.  
this does not answer the question about how the backrooms is four dimensional. there is no way to know how the backrooms uses the fourth dimension, however it's a given that the fourth dimension gives the backrooms its mysterious properties.

i'd like to hear opinions, thanks

If you were to use a wormhole to travel somewhere else, material density would not matter the slightest. If noclipping is like using an additional dimension/wormhole, then how could material density matter?

Secondly, you appear to claim the Backrooms is four dimensional, yet have no evidence. I think of noclipping as a way to travel through an additional dimension. That does not mean that your destination must be four-dimensional (or three-dimensional either). If you were to use the third dimension to travel on a two dimension world, you needn't touch any molecules except before you land and after you arrive.

Lastly, how come the Backrooms has to be the product of your imagination alone, and not of everyone's imagination? What if it isn't just your imagination producing them, but an actual place?
