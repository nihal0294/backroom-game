Level -54: The Ferrymen Woods

09 Jun 2020 22:42

**Page Type:** Negative Level

**Genre (Optional) :** Other (psychological thriller)

**Elevator Pitch:** This level takes the form of a seemingly-abandoned pier, jutting out of an infinite deserted island covered with trees and flora. The sides of the pier are painted snow-white and have a strange blue hue that irradiates from the entire structure. and mysterious voices without any origin emit from the planks of wood that form the floor of the pier.

These voices give off a nostalgic, familiar feel, and sound like the conversations of fishermen and sailors; these voices will often talk about catching and eating fish, even though the ocean isn’t populated by any known sea life, and talk about sailing throughout the ocean on fishing boats and ferries, even though there are no boats in sight. The origin of these voices is unknown, though many wanderers have speculated that these voices are invisible entities that haven't been discovered or fully understood yet.

The pier is attached to a deserted island, with no wildlife or fauna, but plenty of flora and trees that are the size of skyscrapers. The deserted island is seemingly infinite, large in size, and extending endlessly across a boundless ocean. A settlement of wanderers live on this deserted island, simply referred to as “the castaways.” They offer almond water, edible food, and supplies to any new wanderer who finds the pier and island.  
The ocean surrounding the pier and the deserted island is highly corrosive, killing and dissolving any object or entity thrown into the body of water. Despite this, neither the pier or the island are affected by the corrosive ocean in any way, shape, or form. No living being or structure exists under the ocean’s surface, as the ocean dissolves and kills anything and anyone that enters it, and is completely devoid of life.

The flora and trees are edible, though eating them will cause a severe decrease in sanity because of unknown factors. Those who eat from the flora and trees will begin to have visual hallucinations, hallucinating figures of sailors and fishermen on the pier, and boats and other structures floating on the ocean, despite any structure or object dissolving upon impact on the body of water, excluding the island and pier.

These visual hallucinations will goad the wanderer to take off their clothes and go on a “swim,” diving into the ocean, despite any physical contact with the ocean being lethal. This will kill the wanderer instantly. Because of this, it is recommended that any wanderer or individual who finds Level -54 to carry their own food, supplies, and Almond Water with them to Level -54, and not eat anything on the deserted island. Many have managed to survive on Level -54, and find a method of escape, though others have not been so fortunate, accidentally eating the trees and flora, hallucinating, and inadvertently killing themselves by diving into the ocean.

**Central Narrative:** A series of abandoned documents are found on the infinite deserted island. These documents are written by an unnamed male wanderer who found himself accidentally on Level -54 by sleeping in the lukewarm water that fills the hotel rooms in Level -33 and waking up on the glowing blue pier. Having found himself in this unusual place with no food or water, he ate from the flora and trees around the island. This causes him to hallucinate figures on the pier that resemble fishermen and sailors, who goads the wanderer into “taking a swim” in the highly corrosive ocean.

In the last document, the male wanderer writes that he desires to join the “fishermen and sailors” in the ocean, and the document cuts off there. “The Castaways” explain that they found the remains of the male wanderer on the side of the island, and attempts to identify the male wanderer have proven inconclusive.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** What is novel/unique about my premise are the dangerous and parasitic entities that are the only inhabitants in Level -54 other than wayward wanderers trapped on the island: the trees and flora on the island, collectively referred to by the Castaways as “the Ferrymen Woods.” While these flora and trees seem harmless and inanimate on the outside, removing the exteriors of these trees and flora reveals teeth, eyes, and living organs, as these trees and flora are parasitic entities that are nourished by the corrosive ocean that surrounds the island and pier.  
Should any unfortunate wanderer eat from the Ferrymen Woods, these parasitic entities will enter their bodies and minds and take over their bodily and mental functions, and taking the form of visual hallucinations of sailors and fishermen, hence the term “Ferrymen Woods.”

These hallucinations are what the Ferrymen Woods use in order to lure fishermen and sailors into the sea, and compel them to dive in and become dissolved by the ocean. Once these wanderers are dissolved by the ocean, the Ferrymen Woods will absorb the remains of these wanderers along with the corrosive ocean, like a pitcher plant using its acidic saliva to dissolve and devour its prey. Even if you resist the attempts by the hallucinations to lure you into the surrounding ocean, you will be forced against your will by the parasitic entities infecting your body into the ocean anyway, as by the time you see these hallucinations, the flora and trees of the Ferrymen Woods will have completely taken over your body and mind. One you eat from any of the plants and trees on the deserted island, you have already doomed yourself to a swift and sudden death.

**Additional Notes:** Level -54 has a survival difficulty of 4, due to the high concentration of parasitic entities on the island. To enter Level -54, you must sleep in the lukewarm water that partially fills the hotel rooms in Level -33. In order to exit Level -54, bury yourself in the sand that covers the deserted island, and fall unconscious. Once you awaken, you will find yourself in Level 0.

It is not recommended that you stay in Level -54 for too long, as once you run out of food and supplies, you will not have anything to eat except the parasitic trees and plants of the Ferrymen Woods around the island, and you will have nowhere to go because of the corrosive ocean that dissolves and murders anybody and anything outside of the pier or the island.
