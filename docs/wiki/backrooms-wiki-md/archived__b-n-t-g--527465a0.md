# **Who are The B.N.T.G.?:**

The B.N.T.G. (a.k.a The "Backrooms Nonaligned Trade Group") are a Faction made by a previous team of [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md), until it became dependent on its own. The Faction has since made a huge city in [Level 1](level-1.md)'s halls called "Traders Keep". This city has everything one would need: a police department, fire department, food department, and more. The Faction's most important part of the city is called "[Traders Vault](traders-vault.md)", a heavily guarded place where all of the equipment, tools, weapons, and food is stored for further use for the people of the Backrooms. Everything is stored in multiple vaults found in a portion of [Level 1](level-1.md), which resembles a long hallway of storage rooms, with garage doors. The leaders of the Faction are a part of "[Traders Vault](traders-vault.md)".
