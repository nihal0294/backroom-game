Howdy, Unity-xk, this is the first entity I'm reviewing so not all of my critique may be applicable to this page, but I still think you'd benefit from what I suggested a lot. The critique is shown below:

[+ Critique](javascript:;)

[- Critique](javascript:;)

The first thing I notice about this page is the grammar, it's not riddled with grammar mistakes and isn't written very professionally. I suggest putting it through a grammar checker such as grammarly. Sentence structure seems to be a big issue for you and some sentences don't sound very natural when read out loud. Partnering with someone who's skilled with grammar would be a good way to solve this issue.This site has a page with several websites that have writing tips, I've linked some that I believe would be beneficial to you down below:  
<http://theoatmeal.com/comics/apostrophe>  
<http://grammar.about.com/od/punctuationandmechanics/a/punctrules.htm>  
<https://www.quickanddirtytips.com/education/grammar/how-to-use-commas-a-summary>

As for the idea itself, it doesn't really stick with me. A dude with magical powers who offers help isn't something that appeals to me personally, though I can't speak for everyone. I feel like the best direction this page could go is by focusing on the entity's personality, maybe having interview logs or something of the sort. He's described as "very chatty", so it'd make sense for interviews with him to be provided. Bulking up the other sections would also be nice, you currently have a couple of small pieces of text and then one huge clump of information, spreading it out would make it a lot better to read. You could also benefit from changing the format of your page, if you don't have much to say about his biology you can just put what you would've put there in the description.

Overall, I feel like the grammar is pulling down this page a lot and prevents me from really enjoying it. As it is now, 2/10.
