There are a couple of ways of making an image block. The one that I use is as follows:

```
[[include component:image-block
    |name= **image url goes here**
    |caption= **Caption goes here**
]]
```

This should allow you to caption the image. Just copy and paste this to your page. Hope this helps!!

Regarding adding images,

> [[include component:image-block  
> |name=Insert image link  
> |caption=Insert Caption  
> |align=whatever way you want it to be on the screen  
> ]]

This is the code for the image.  
As for how to get a image that will show up to everyone:  
"With that image, take a screenshot of it, and then go to "files", press "Upload File From Your Computer", press "Choose File", and then choose the screenshot. Then press "Upload". Once it is uploaded, press "Info", and copy the link. Close out of it, press edit, and go to the image thing. Copy the link in place of the one you currently have. Then the image should work for everyone."  
To clarify, put the link you copied in front of the "name" part.  
If you need further help or clarification, feel free to pm me.
