Level 828 - The Missing Flight

Level 828 is the 829th level of The Backrooms

Class 0  
Safe, Secure, Devoid of Entities

Level 828 is a Montego Airlines plane that consists of around 200 people on board, the people here are harmless and think they are taking a regular flight from Montego Bay to New York City. The plane (AKA Flight 828) clipped out of normal reality through unknown means, somehow not ending up in Level 0, Level 1, or Level 2, the cause of this is as of writing, being investigated by the M.E.G. to decipher why this is.

Discovery  
Operatives from Base Alpha no clipped here from Level 36 after going through no clipping through a sign labeled 828. They began to interview the passengers and crew to decipher who they were.

Bases, Outposts, and Communities

Flight 828

This community consists of the Crew and Passengers of Missing Flight 828 however they are oblivious to the fact that they are in the backrooms, multiple tries at proving this to them have proved unsuccessful and M. E. G. has refrained from trying to prove this to them

Confirmed Entrances  
No clipping through a sign that says 828 in Level 36 will bring you here  
No clipping through a plane in Level 36 will also bring you here

Unconfirmed Entrances

A wanderer claimed that they went through a hole in Level 2 that brought them here, M. E. G. is currently investigating this claim  
A group known as the Church of 828 claimed that they thought of a passenger on Flight 828 that brought them here.

Confirmed Exits

Going through the emergency door will lead you to Level 7's main area  
No clipping through the wall of the plane will lead you to back to Level 36

Unconfirmed Exits

Several Wanderers have claimed that no clipping through a passenger will bring you to Level 11  
A group known as Anti-828 has said that killing a passenger will send you to The Void, while not confirmed it is not recommended to try this.
