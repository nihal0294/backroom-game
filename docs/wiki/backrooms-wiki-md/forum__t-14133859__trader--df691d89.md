Trader.

30 Jun 2021 12:31

**Entity Number:** 124

**Habitat** - Pipe dreams, Lights out, Welcome to hell, Zenith.

**Warning**  
These entities are not confirmed to exist. The only people that have described this entity have low sanity and therefore this description may be inaccurate.

### Description

"Traders" are unconfirmed Entites in the backrooms. The people that we have asked about this entity describe it like docile skin stealers. The reports say that they never get hungry. They are only dangerous if you aggravate them. When aggravated their eyes will turn from green to black as a sign to start running then it will chase you down and if it touches you, you will be transported to level 999.

### Behaviors

Their behaviors are apparently docile. They walk around and if you give one an item, they will materialize paper with some writing on it and it will give you a pen. The paper will say an item and have a yes or no check box. It will not get aggravated if you say no. If you say yes the item that you gave it will dissolve into it and then a the item that you were promised will materialize into your hand. Do not freak out if this happens.

### Biology

Traders are the same height as an average adult and will have grey skin. Its eyes are green when docile. They are black when aggravated.  
It has 4 legs and 2 arms. No other description has been provided.

### Discovery

Only a small people have reported this entity and therefor can't be registered as fact. This is why we will not include them in the levels official documents unless more proof is provided.

---

# **Do's and Dont's:**

### **Do:**

- Trade with this entity.
- Be friendly towards this entity.
- Aggravate it if you wish to go to level 999.

### **Don't:**

- Try to hurt it.
- Doodle on its paper.
