Entity XXX - The Vlamirus AND Level XXX - Hell's Path

08 Dec 2022 20:15

**THE REASON I HAVE TWO CONCEPTS IN HERE IS BECAUSE THE LEVEL AND THE ENTITY ARE CONNECTED AND IT IS HARD TO DESCRIBE ONE WITHOUT THE OTHER!**

**Page Type:** Entity

**Core Concepts:**

- The entity is a species that has been described an a fire-adapted amphibian/mammal with dark skin to allow either heat absorption
- The entity is large in size, reaching a whopping 2.5+ meters in height while sitting, and around 2 meters while standing. While standing on their hind legs, they can reach things that are over 4 meters above the ground
- Despite the entity's physical appearance and size, it is not an aggressive creature- Rather the opposite. It temporarily 'bonds' to wanderers (Becoming almost a companion, similar to how dogs bond to people) and use that bond to track where the wanderer as they travel to new levels, allowing it to follow
- They need heat to survive, so if their body temperature drops below 27 C, they will either try to find an entrance back to Level XXX or pass away if their internal temperature drops too low. If a wanderer wants one to follow them long term, they need to stay aware of the temperatures of the levels they are in and give the entity time to heat up if they find a hot enough heat source (If a wanderer enters a cold Level, that could end up putting the entity in danger and they might not make it out if they stay there too long/there's no entrances to Level XXX. If their body reaches 10 C, that's deadly to it, and they will begin to have bodily functions shut down
- Their diet consists of most materials that are flammable since their bodies can convert the flames created by the items being ingested by a high temperature body into energy. Despite this, they can also eat other entities since their bodies are still able to handle semi-organic materials of other entities and use it as energy. These are rare occasions, and typically are a result of another entity attacking the bonded wanderer or the Vlamirus itself. They have been observed eating skin-stealers, hounds, and stranglers
- They can be found only in Level XXX, and they are typically not seen by many wanderers. They can occasionally be seen in the forest right near the pathway of Level XXX, but they also can be rarely seen crossing the path wanderers travel across. They are more commonly found in the forest around 0.3 km off the pathway
- Aggression can be taken as a threat, and depending on the Vlamirus encountered, the entity may either run away or attack if it feels the threat could mean its life is in danger. Staying calm and talking in a soft voice can help bring one towards the wanderer, allowing it to bond with them

**Additional Notes:** Not a concern, but more of an explanation behind the name. For those curious, Vlamirus is the mixture of the word 'Vlam' (Flame in Dutch) and virus (Leading to the nickname 'Flame Virus'). The face shape is similar to the shape of a biohazard symbol, which plays into the 'Virus' part.

---

**Page Type:** Level

**Core Concepts:**

- The level is a Deadzone
- Hell's Path is a level that is entirely made up of a continuously burning forest. Despite the flames always being present, the charred trees never disappear or become further damaged, and nothing has been shown to put the flames out. A center pathway made up of dried dirt is in the center of the forest, spanning 2 km. The exit is found at the end of this path
- The temperature of the level is constantly at 45 C, which makes staying in the level for long periods of time cause the risk of heat-related issues such as heat cramps, heat exhaustion, or possibly heat stroke
- There is only one known entity that lives in the level, and that is the Vlamirus. They typically stay away from the path that wanderers travel through, so some who enter this level and leave may never encounter the entity
- The flames of the forest get more intense as a person goes further out into the forest. By the edge of the path, the temperature remains at 45 C and the flames are typically only in small clusters that can be avoided. As the wanderer reaches around 0.5 km from the path, the flames will be almost unable to be seen through and the air will reach around 70-80 C
- Once the wanderer exits the level, they will enter a randomized level. They are able to return the Level XXX if they manage to find another entrance
