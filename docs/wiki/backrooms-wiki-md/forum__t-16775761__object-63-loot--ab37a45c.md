Even though the concept for this object ties into your level, which I have not read, **this draft completely lacks the amount of substance needed to be a functional object page**. You cannot just take a snippet of information intended for your level, place it into the object format, and expect it to tell a cohesive story. You've listed the following things:

- A diamond that makes the user invisible.
- A sword that cuts through anything.
- An armband that constructs portals.
- A Level Key that also functions as a level.

These, on their own, aren't enough to fill the conceptual requirements for an object page; you don't even go in-depth enough to make such a considerable possibility. If you really wanted this to be a page able to be posted onto the main site, I would cut *most* of the ties to the level of which these objects come from, expand the depth that the objects present and either tie these object into other concepts or add more objects. **If you want this to do well, don't just cover things on a surface level.** You can't be lazy just because this isn't a level draft.
