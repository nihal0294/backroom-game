With how the Wiki has progressed, formatting usually correlates to CSS coding and making everything look nice. Having a old-school format like your articles is not bad at all; people still do it to this day, but there are some things that people want like

---

horizontal lines (five hyphens) and

> quote blocks

put an arrow (>) at the beginning of a new line.

This is what people usually refer to as formatting. Horizontal lines are for changing to different subjects, and quote blocks are for, well, quotes.
