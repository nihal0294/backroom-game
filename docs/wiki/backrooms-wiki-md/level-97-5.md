{$caption}

The Lighthouse, for 97 years you remained lit, long after the sea had gone with him.

Your light has no more water to shine onto, Lighthouse. Despite that, you shine — across empty plains of salt, revolving perpetually without ships left for you to guide.

There is not a time your light has failed since.

None has entered you, Lighthouse, and none has left you but one. Inside, the book beside your lantern receives a new entry every dawn, in the handwriting of your keeper, who drowned 97 years ago, when your light failed to guide him home.
