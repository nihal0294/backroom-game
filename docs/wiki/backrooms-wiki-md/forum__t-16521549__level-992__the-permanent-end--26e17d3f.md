Level 992: The Permanent End

14 Dec 2023 15:21

Looking for critique  
SURVIVAL DIFFICULTY:  
Class 5

Level 992 is the 993rd Level of the Backrooms.  
In appearance, this level has many similarities to our baseline reality. The stark differences can only be perceived by experiencing the level. Some basic differences being blue land, green water and other colors representing different liquids or solids.

Description  
Here, the oceans and the waves are rock solid while the land consists of multicolored liquid with varying types depths but little to no movement. In addition, the habitats of the animals (excluding humanoid creatures and humans) from this level are the opposite of reality. (Ex. Birds fly underwater and fish swim in mid air.) This is not only a complete reversal from what is experienced on Earth, but also completely goes against the laws of science. It’s the backrooms, right? Nothing needs to make sense here.

Bases, Outposts and Communities  
"Intangible settlements are the only outposts in this level and are mostly abandoned. This is explained by the fact that there are very few documented cases of humans entering this level. No one has ever been documented returning to the rest of the backrooms after this level. An unknown group sent a drone with a camera into this level, helping to collect the information available at this time. Drone footage captured video of the entities, the outposts and the few humans that have made it here. It also captured one human explorer running into a door and entering reality. Unfortunately, the drone, after taking a picture of this incident, was destroyed by an entity, not having exited, upon relaying information to its crew on another level. No other projects of this type have been carried out due to difficulty finding the resources needed.

Intangible Settlements  
Buildings and tents existing in these settlements are visible and usable, but intangible.  
Human explorers using these outposts must take care not to be seen or perceived by entities when entering these shelters. Entities can also move through intangible settlements and attack explorers inside.  
In addition, do not enter a settlement that appears to glitch in and out, as this can leave anyone exposed at an unpredictable time making one vulnerable to being perceived and attacked by entities.  
Other aspects to watch out for!  
- Black liquid: could be liquid silence, mud, ash or another substance.  
- Red liquid: Avoid this, as it commonly turns out to be liquid pain.  
Other colored liquids could be varying substances.  
- Before entering this level, stock up on resources. The only ones here are provided in nature. It is unknown whether all food substances here are safe to eat. There is no almond water provided here.

Entities  
The entities found in this level are atypical and not found in other levels of the backrooms  
- Zombified facelings (hostile)  
- Zombified wanderers (hostile/unconfirmed presence)  
- Zombified skin stealers (hostile)  
- Flying fish (passive)  
- Flying sharks (hostile)  
- Varying types of aquatic insects including spiders and death moths (hostile)  
- Birds that live underwater only (neutral)

Entrances  
There is only one entrance to this level. The exit from level 991 will bring someone to this level.

Exits  
At an unknown point in this level, there is a guaranteed door entrance to the Frontrooms (reality). It is impossible to return or no-clip to any other level in the backrooms after entering level 992 or after entering reality from this level.
