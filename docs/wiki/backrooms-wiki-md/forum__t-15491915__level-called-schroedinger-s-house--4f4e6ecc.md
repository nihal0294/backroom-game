Hello, Dr James Quin,  
being a long time.

I'm taking a look at your entry and the following ideas came to my mind.

**Level 000 is a mysterious level capable of existing in several states at once, in a similar fashion to Quantum Superposition9 , but once observed, will be forced into one seemingly random state. The properties, layout, entities, and environment seem to be "mixed and matched" to create entirely new states**

**While the topography does change, it generally sticks with being a small, one story house, with 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, a living room, a hallway, and a small kitchen. The different States of Level 000 typically only change the houses, paint, room placement, level of degradation, and the stage of the construction process.**

Why don't change more the topography while being coherent (you can retain the same space not the same layout) ? Level of construction? Does this mean that wanderers can see the void ?  
I imagine you meant an apparent level of construction that still hide the void yet the other trail can be inspiring too.

I think a probability study could be interesting and relevant as Quantum mechanics use it a lot.

**It appears to be possible to be in different states of Level 000 at once. This is achieved by having having one person enter the level and close their eyes, while another individual enters afterwards.**

**Those who cannot see or simply stop observing Level 000 while in it10, will apparently randomly switch between States of Level 000.**

Therefore, observation only depends on sight. Using other senses could also add risk of destroying the superposition ? Does a discussion break the multiples states ?  
What is the interest for the group to being in a superposed state ?  
What if each time you observe the level and break superposition, you're sent to a different stratum and states. Therefore being the only solution for the group to stay together is to limit observation at the cost of the superposed state.

Also, imagine that the level can blink each time you blink.

**Objects in separate states of Level 000 can interact in certain ways with each other, but cannot make any physical contact.**  
well, does they perceive each other outside of interactions ?  
Well, there is a difference between states and stratum of your level.  
States like being wet, being hot, destroyed vs being on another plane of the level.

Here it is for now.

I have been thinking for a while, and I have decided that it's gonna have a rewrite(sort of). It's main points will be the same but there will be significant changes. That said, this will help me in figuring that out.

1. The stage of construction is meant to be the actual construction of the house itself, not the level.

2. A probability study is a good idea. I'll figure that one out somehow, but that seems like something I'll need a bit more help and/or research for.

3. In the rewrite, I intend to make it so that as long as you are not observing a particular spot, that spot by itself will have many different states, and this includes the states you mentioned, such as simply becoming wet. Anything can and will change. The overall properties of the level itself can change too, as long you are not observing anything in anyway that would make it obvious the property just manifested. For example, if it has a property wher water has a weird smell, if I'm smiling it, it can't change that property, but if I stop smelling it, it can now change. As long as I don't observe the change in rel time, it can change. Notably, object currently used to observe something, like a live video feed, cannot undergo any kind of change untill it is no longer used. Recordings are, because of this, untrustworthy unless watched live as its recording the entire time.

4. While I do like the idea of different stratum, they aren't necessarily in existence all at once the untill observed, the way I see it, and therefore that doesn't quite fit my main idea the way I want it to. So I don't think that's something I'm gonna do.

5. While I do think the original being in different states of the level idea was good, again that makes stratum, which isn't what I intended so im probably cutting that out.

I'm not entirely sure on all this, because this is based on a rather complex subject, so I'll need far more research than I thought to get all the kinks worked out. So if it's possible, help would be much appreciated. On a side note, your the best critiquer that I've interacted with so far, thank you.
