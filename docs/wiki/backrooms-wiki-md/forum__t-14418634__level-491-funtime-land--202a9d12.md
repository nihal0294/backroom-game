Listen, I like FNaF, but I don't think it would make a good level idea. People would know "Anwir" is just a Foxy clone, making people an animatronic is the whole "William Afton" thing (although it is a little bit different in the games, he kills kids in the game and the animatronics get possessed by the kids' souls, here, it's just Human —-> Animatronic), and overall, it's clear this is, at least inspired, at most a straight-up copy of Five Nights at Freddy's.

I can't greenlight, but if I could, I wouldn't.
