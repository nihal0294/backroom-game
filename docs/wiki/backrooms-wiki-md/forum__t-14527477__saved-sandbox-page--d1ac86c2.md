Hey there!

For me, the simplest way to find your page in the sandbox is to scroll all the way down the main sandbox page, click +Site Tools, then click orphaned pages (the collapsible should release a longgggg listnof pages). It will list almost if not all sandbox pages that was created. If you remember the title in which you named your page, and you’re using a computer, simply hold ctrl + f, then type in the title of your page (if the whole title doesn’t work, try using only one or two words). To edit your page, scroll all the way down your sandbox and click “Edit”. Hope this helped!
