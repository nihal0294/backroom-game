Initial thoughts and my wishes for future entries

10 Jan 2026 21:28

I'll be excited to read the new entries as they come in. Even outside any legal considerations, I think it's fun that people can showcase their creativity.

In the first batch, there were a couple surprisingly poetic ones:

> I am a wanderer in a kingdom of walls  
> And in this kingdom, I have lived to see many of its duchies  
> But despite my wandering, I have found no lord or king  
> So I wander ever more -from nobody going to nowhere

> In the morning I once awoke with a sense of dread  
> And while I got out of my bed  
> There was a torrent of snow.
>
> I placed the blame on the oncoming wind  
> This corrosive thought held within, though  
> Told me I was wrong.
>
> Then I went with my umbrella and hat,  
> brimmed out wide until the edges were flat  
> My boots hit the ground, oh-so-hard.
>
> For in my last sight down came asunder  
> My boots fell harder than that of thunder,  
> And I found myself in a place, not snow,  
> but damp.

Hmm, I like these, and I appreciate the boldness of the authors, but I think something less traditionally poetic might work better. There's also the consideration of screen space with the second one. Also regarding it, it doesn't really describe the Backrooms at all other than that they are "damp". It's possible the author intentionally made such a choice, maybe to highlight the unknown nature of the Backrooms or how the speaker misses the normal world, but it sort of falls flat and feels a bit too snowy. It's just hard for me to imagine something with this vibe at the top of the front page. I enjoy the way the first poem describes the Backrooms, though. I'd hope future entries focus more on the Backrooms and their characteristics.

> By falling through the wrong places in reality, you'll end up in what's called The Backrooms. A place outside of reality looking like a labyrinth of nearly six hundred million square miles of dank carpets, vintage-styled yellow wallpaper, and the hum buzz of fluorescent lighting.
>
> Pray if you hear something nearby, because it might have already heard you.

Not gonna lie, this feels a bit like "the original post but just legally distinct enough", especially with the ending and keeping the number there. Definitely makes me appreciate the boldness of the poem ones. Of course, if people just want more of the old, they can choose this one. Now that this niche is covered, though, I wish future entries would show a bit more divergence from the original post.

> What happens when someone breaks reality? The punishment is being sent to that damned prison to be trapped in forever, The Backrooms. Where all mysteries of reality culminate into a place of despair, with only the places you wander through to keep you sane.

Short and sweet, I like it. Though it feels like it could use its potential to a fuller extent with a few more details. The idea of being sent to the Backrooms as a punishment for breaking reality by noclipping is also rarely used in modern lore. I wish future entries would try to emphasize those parts of the original post that are most relevant to the lore today.

> Earth is home and keeps us in a warm embrace. Resources, companions, and warmth. But is as fragile as glass. One wrong step and it'll break right under you. When you fall, reality won't be their to catch you. When you land, you'll be in a somewhere new. The Backrooms, your new home, a home not for you. One that only gives you the cold shoulder, and as rigidity as wood. No embrace to keep you warm, a fight for resources, and scarce companions.
>
> Watch your back, something may always be behind it.

I like this a lot, especially the glass metaphor and the writing. Not much to say about this one, though it is sort of sparse on descriptions of the physical structure of the Backrooms, which was one thing I thought made the original post so memorable. Of course, I don't want us mimicking the original post too closely. We need more like this one.

> You are inside the Backrooms, a large supercluster of perverted replications of your memories accessible only by noclipping out of reality. Inside are other people like you, displaced here, as well as other… things native to this place, things that are alive, inanimate and/or incorporeal. You'll be spending an eternity here.

This feels a bit scifi-y with some technobabble-y terminology. It reads like it's trying to impress the reader over with fancy words, with the expressiveness suffering in my opinion due to the abstractness. Of course, the original post also uses some curious words: "hum-buzz", "monoyellow" - yet they clearly paint a mental image. I wish future entries would focus on expressiveness and concrete mental images in comparison to this one. BTW, the lore implications of the beginning are a bit opinionated for a motto/header.

Things That I Think

13 Jan 2026 15:49

As I have written many blurbs for authors, publishers, and various media, what comes to mind while crafting my entry for Blurbcon?

Our Backrooms Wiki is constantly evolving. While Level 0 is nostalgic, the varieties of levels, entities, objects, phenomena, groups, canons, and tales have pushed the envelope of our creativity to new heights. The Backrooms are so much more today.

Our website blurb could be something that embraces the miscellany of features that future readers might discover as they learn about the futility of wanderers trapped in such a weird and wonderful place. More than anything, I hope it is an invitation for visitors to contemplate and contribute.
