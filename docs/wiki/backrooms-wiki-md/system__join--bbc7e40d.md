# Join this site

**Before applying to the site, you must read the following guides:**

- [Site Rules](site-rules.md)
- [Greenlight Policy](greenlight-policy.md)

Your first step is to create a Wikidot account. To do this, you can find a sign-up/login button on the top-right of the screen. If you can't find this button, click [here](index.md).

If you already have a Wikidot account, you can login as normal.

## Due to a bug within Wikidot, when you first sign up for a Wikidot account there can be a delay or fail in the sending of confirmation emails. Please, when creating your Wikidot account, choose an email with *no numbers*, and *do not use Hotmail.* If it still does not work, try again with something other than a Gmail. You should be fine with a Gmail though. You can try it.

---

Once you have an account, to become a member of this site, you must join the [Discord](discord.md) and put these four things in English in the verification:

- Your age
- A short message about why you want to join
- The Discord password, which can be found hidden in the Discord's rules

Once you've joined the server, please send your wikidot username in the *#wikidot-usernames* channel. Give staff a few days to get to you. You will receive an invitation to join the wiki sent by one of our [admins.](meet-the-staff.md)

*Confused? You can create a ticket on the server and staff will be there to help.*
