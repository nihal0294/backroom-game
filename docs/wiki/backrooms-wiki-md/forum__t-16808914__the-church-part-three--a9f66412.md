Sooo does anybody remember this tale series?

09 May 2024 22:33

Hello!!!

This is part three of a tale series that I started well over a year ago called The Church. For those who don't know, it's the story of three people, each related to the Eyes Of Argos in some way, all searching for one another in a bid for revenge. You can read the first entry in the series [here](the-church.md).

For those wondering why this took so long to come out: I wanted to release it at the same time that Elias's great commissioned artwork was finished, but that took a little longer to be completed than either of us expected. In the end I'm glad they took their time with it, though, I like their art a lot :)

Looking back, I'm not really a huge fan of the series as a whole nor this entry in particular. My style has improved a lot over the past year and I think it shows with how uneven the prose is in this one. Could I have spent extra time sprucing up the series to my current standards? Probably, but I have more important stuff to write these days. Regardless I figured I'd release it anyways, just to tie up the loose end and so that Elias's amazing art wouldn't go to waste!

Anyways, hope y'all enjoy it regardless of the wait. Peace out.
