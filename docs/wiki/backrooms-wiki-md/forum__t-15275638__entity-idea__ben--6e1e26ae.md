[Concept Critique](javascript:;)

[Hide Crit](javascript:;)

First things first, it's too short for the idea it has.  
Ideas I thought of:  
More description/Biology section (Is it the whole body that's pink, or just a part? What do you mean by it *slightly* resembles a humanoid [besides on the no arms and floating hands]?)  
Mystery about the entity (I suggest having a short section at the end where a wanderer stumbles upon the entity and witnesses it glowing a bright light before disappearing. This is just my idea, however.)  
Add a section about the scream and add unique effects to it.  
As for the entity idea itself, it just needs expanded upon. The things I said above could help with that.  
Different things:  
Reads the minds of wanderers, becomes hostile to criminals and becomes extremely helpful towards good people? {IE screams at criminals even through they haven't really angered the entity, and gives a bottle of almond water or healing supplies to a good person.)  
Add an interview section where someone with a tape recorder talks to the entity. You never mentioned if it motioned wanderers to follow them or if it tells them where to go.

You could add the things mentioned above, or think of your own and put them in.
