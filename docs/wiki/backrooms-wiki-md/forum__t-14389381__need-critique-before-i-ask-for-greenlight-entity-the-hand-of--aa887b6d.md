# Description:

**The Hand of God** is a dark hand with long sharp fingers. It can be sighted on the corner where two walls intersect. The hand reaches around the corner and seems to stay there until the area is left. It has been sighted on [Level 1](level-1.md), [Level 2](level-2.md), and [Level 3](level-3.md). It is unknown if there are multiple "**Hands of Gods**" or if there is only one.

# Behaviors:

**The Hand of God** will only appear if it is out of sight of any wanderer and at least 5 meters away from any living thing. It will then make scratching noises until a wanderer looks at it. Once sight has been achieved, the hand will whisper things that only the target wanderer can hear. The whispers are usually about the wanderer's deepest desires, no matter how gruesome or horrid they may be, in hopes of driving the wanderer closer to the hand. If the target wanderer breaks line of sight by leaving the area, the hand will disappear. Just blinking or turning around is not enough to get rid of the hand.  
It is not hard to oppose the whispers of **The Hand of God**, but sometimes if a person is desperate enough, they will be persuaded by anything. If one gets too close to **The Hand of God**, they will essentially go insane. One person that had been influenced by the hand was rescued by [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) and interviewed.

# Biology

It is currently unknown if **The Hand of God** has a body (other than the hand) or if it is just a floating hand. The fingers of the hand are abnormally long and skinny and a few strands of hair/fur are able to be seen on the back of the palm, but it is hard to get a good look without getting too close. The hand is black, but it's too dark to be any normal human's skin.  
The biggest question around **The Hand of God** is how it whispers. It seems to telepathically communicate with its target because no one hears the exact same thing, even if their desires are the same.

# Discovery

The earliest record of **The Hand of God** was filed in 2013 by a [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) agent who was on their break. The next sightings came from wanderers who reported them to other [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) agents. Then, in 2019, [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) finally had their first case of someone who got too close to **The Hand of God**. Sadly, they killed themselves before [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) could do anything with them. The next case of **The Hand of God**'s influence was Subject A, which they were able to successfully interview, but they were not able to get much information.

# Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- Get out of the area without getting close
- Keep eye contact
- Ignore what it says

### Don't:

- Get close
- Make contact
- Listen to it
