07 Oct 2023 02:06

Hello, I wanted to ask whether or not the character who is speaking in first person was supposed to have formal or informal dialogue.  
If the tone you were going for was "guy who is fuckin over The Beast of Level 5 's shit", you may want to add a quite few more contractions, as the tone sounds too clinical to seem loose and free-flow. On the topic, try to make sure the conversation happening between Ryan and The Beast of Level 5 is a plausible discussion with a manipulator and someone who is over their bull.

Also, the flow of the dialogue is quite choppy. A good chunk of the transcript's "conversational replies", if you will, were either too short or too long. If you throw everything at your reader in one go, they aren't gonna know what hit em'.

My advice is to run a fine-toothed comb through your article, perhaps based on the guidelines above. Improvement in SPaG errors should come naturally whilst following the revising process. Go with the flow, man.

Have a good one =]
