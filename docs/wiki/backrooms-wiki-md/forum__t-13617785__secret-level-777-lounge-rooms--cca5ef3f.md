Secret level 777 (Lounge Rooms)

04 Aug 2020 17:35

**Page Type:** Secret Level 777 (The Lounge Rooms)

**Genre (Optional):** Surrealism

**Page Layout (Optional):** It will be structured like most other level entries. If the lore is liked enough, an accompanying tale can be written. A new item page would also probably need to be created.

**Elevator Pitch:** Occasionally on reddit, there is talk of an alternate reality called the “Frontrooms”, even though this isn’t accepted as canon. We know that the Frontrooms are just the observable universe in which wanderers originated. It’s usually described as a safe place in the theme of a lounge or study. However, this place does exist, but only as a singular level. Even though it is a Class 1 level, it is sometimes dangerous to get to.

**Central Narrative:** An anonymous user found a pair of dice in Level 11 in a casino. It is presumed that dice are rare. They put them in their pocket and didn’t give it a second thought. This same user found themselves at Level 0 later in their travels. While there, they spotted an Entity 81. Upon entering the elevator, they dropped a bottle of almond water. While picking up the water, the dice fell out of their pocket. The elevator began to make slot machine noises and a button with the letters LR appeared on the floor. The user pressed it and was transported to a small room with obsidian walls, marble floors, a pair of red double doors, and a front desk. A faceling wearing a tuxedo offered to take their coat and bags for storage. The level appeared to be roughly the same size and layout as a post-war Manhattan high rise, with 77 floors but no windows. All the rooms were small and intimate, but the halls were spacious. Cats, possibly a new entity, can occasionally be seen wandering the level, with some being friendly and others indifferent. Hand written manuscripts can be found in every room. They were written by someone called The Author on the subjects of art history, philosophy, Russian literature, and quantum physics. Stairs linked each floor. Everything was decorated like a lounge or study. A variety of jazz music played through speakers in the ceiling. Simply thinking of the faceling at the front desk, summons it. It can conjure any food item. It, however, can only give almond water for a beverage. Other than cats and the faceling butler, no entities spawn.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** There is a door at the top of the floor 77 staircase that presumably leads to another level, but is locked. Also, a wanderer named The Author is rumored to live on secret Level 777, though this is unconfirmed. It is advised that when attempting reach this level to first throw the dice into the elevator while not standing in it. Rolling snake eyes may result in being sent to a dangerous level.

**Additional Notes:** Wanderers can leave coats and bags at the front desk indefinitely. Please let me know what you think. I’d love to hear feedback and improve.
