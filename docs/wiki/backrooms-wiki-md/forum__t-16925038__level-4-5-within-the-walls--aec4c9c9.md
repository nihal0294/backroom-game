You upload a file by going to the bottom, clicking on Files, and then Upload a File From you Computer. Then, the name will just be the name of the file.

Overall, this page is rather well-written. It's organized well, no glaring SPaG errors, and has a pretty solid concept. The issue I do have is that it's still "a place that is weird." If you read some of our newer pages, you'll see we don't stick to the format as much anymore (not that you're obligated to do a format screw if you don't want to). We've moved beyond just documenting places that are weird. Now, we're attempting to answer bigger questions like, "What is this level's place in the Backrooms as a whole?", "What are the greater mysteries held within this place?" and "What does this level represent?"

The idea you have is intriguing, specifically this line

> Level 4.5 is a sub-level believed to exist within the walls

What does it mean for something to exist within the walls of something? What does this imply about the geometry of the backrooms? What does this imply for the people who wander the backrooms?

These are the questions I'd like for you to answer in your page. Either expand more on the human element or make the level a character and not just a setting for where spooky things happen.

As a final word, our Discord server is much more active than our forums. If you want more critique, I highly recommend joining the server and critiquing other people's pages in exchange for them looking at yours.
