Hi

I've briefly read through your Description section. I like the way you used footnotes to describe the more scientific/methodical parts of your piece. Which is great for a first read through. It allows the text to flow quickly without any prolonged interruption. Although, I would suggest bleeding a bit of Footnote 5's description into the text. Leaving just "group of objects will perform" felt a bit too vague.

I did not note any grammar errors (although please note I am not a native English speaker, so something may have slept by). However, there are a few places where sentences could be split. For instance:

> While music is playing, each group of objects will perform in response to one or more instruments, melodies, percussion, et cetera heard in the song.

Could read

> Objects will perform in response to music. Such as: but not limited to, instruments, melodies or percussion.

You could even make a footnote list of tested sounds to fit in with the rest of your piece.

I tripped upon some repetition scattered throughout the page. Such as:

> There are various different objects and structures floating in the air or mounted on poles and other structures. These structures are also frequently surrounded by pits that appear to go on indefinitely.

I'd suggest lightening the text. It'll make it feel less opaque and flow better.

> Floating in the air, mounted on poles or upon each other are various structures. The latter are frequently surrounded by pits that appear to go on indefinitely.

I hope this helped with the points you requested aid on!
