03 May 2023 20:31

Hey, the image you’re using has to be under some sort of Creative Commons license, indicated by it being under something like CC-BY-SA 3.0 etc. This can also be done with images you have taken yourself.  
Unless it is an image under Creative Commons, you cannot use it.

In the case that this image IS a Creative Commons image, you will credit the poster, site and the license it is under.

I hope this clears things up a bit.
