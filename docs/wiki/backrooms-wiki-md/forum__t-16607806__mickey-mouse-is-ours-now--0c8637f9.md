Re: Mickey mouse is ours now

07 Feb 2024 04:07

Everything on this site is Creative Commons license CC BY-SA 3.0 or a compatible license (in more specific cases), and public domain is allowed. You can do whatever you want with Steamboat Willie now on this site. You could, if you wanted to, make a Mickey Mouse entity. Just be careful, since Disney is still trying to strike down usage of Steamboat Willie. You'd win a court case, because legally they cannot do that, but is it worth going to court to fight? It's abuse of their influence, for sure, but they've been doing it anyway, at least on Youtube. It could just be an automatic thing I suppose. Best to do your research first.

So this means one simple thing, this is up to staff discretion. Yes, legally and according to site policy, you can do what you want, but whether staff decides to allow it, or alters the policy for it, is another matter.
