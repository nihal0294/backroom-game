The Endless Performance (Seeking Critique)

24 Feb 2024 15:00

I was thinking of a level where you're in a school hall and you have to perform. There will be an entity called ''The Director''. He judges your every act and speech, and he will decide if you are worthy get out or not. If not, then you'll have to repeat the performance again and again. You can enter if you receive a mysterious invite or by playing a specific music piece. Breaking the loop is hard but not impossible, all you have to do is find a few wanderers who are also acting and stage and confront ''The Director'' with them.

There are six different hazards in the level.

The Infinite Performance: Once you step onto the stage, the loop begins. You find yourself in the midst of a performance, playing a lead role in a play or musical. The scenes and scenarios change with each loop, but the underlying theme is always your deepest fears and insecurities. You may find yourself forgetting lines, stumbling over choreography, or facing critical audiences.

Audience Interactions: The audience in the level is not merely passive observers. They can directly interact with you, whispering discouraging comments, hurling insults, or even throwing objects onto the stage. Their reactions are unpredictable and can range from supportive applause to merciless ridicule.

Endless Rehearsals: The only way to break free from the loop is to perfect your performance and reach the end of the play flawlessly. However, each loop brings new challenges, forcing you to adapt and improve. The difficulty increases with each repetition, pushing you to the limits of your abilities.

Stage Hazards: The stage itself poses various dangers. Trapdoors may suddenly open beneath your feet, sending you tumbling into hidden chambers or underground passages. The set pieces and props may come alive, attacking or obstructing your progress. The lighting fixtures could malfunction, plunging you into darkness.

Entity: The Director: The Director is a mysterious entity that oversees the performances in Level 707. It takes the form of a tall, imposing figure dressed in a black suit, with a stern expression and piercing eyes. The Director's role is to ensure that you remain trapped in the loop, constantly pushing you to improve and face your fears.

The Enigmatic Prompter: In the level, there is a figure known as the Enigmatic Prompter. This entity appears as a cloaked figure lurking in the shadows of the stage. The Prompter's purpose is to guide and assist those trapped in the loop, providing cryptic hints and clues to help navigate the challenges. However, their motives and intentions remain unclear, and trusting them completely can be risky.
