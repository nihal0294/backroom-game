I imagine that's okay. The backrooms is meant to be an ever-growing story, at least in the newer versions of it. People can be as creative as they want with new levels, items, entities, and so on.

Some of the entities are original characters themselves, and in many M.E.G logs for discoveries, interviews, etc., there are many named characters. Outposts, communities and bases often have major people leading them too, so there are many ways to implement a new character into the Backrooms. You can even just use tales/some kind of story to introduce someone who isn't necessarily important to the overall lore of it.

So using an original character in the backrooms is fine. It adds to the expansive story, and it's meant to be a creative outlet for people who want to make their own connected stories. With the seemingly endless possibilities in the backrooms, don't be afraid to add your own personal flair to it.
