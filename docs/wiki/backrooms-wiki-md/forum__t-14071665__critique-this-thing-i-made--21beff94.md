Critique this thing I made.

23 Apr 2021 05:28

Counter entities are a rare entity in the backrooms, as the name suggests, they will attack entities in the backrooms most notably the partygoers.

**Description**

Counter entities mimic the appearance of real crime agencies across the world, however, the uniforms of different crime agencies vary, for example a counter entity might be wearing FBI uniform while another will be wearing a GIGN uniform.

The most common outfit that counter entities wear is the FBI uniform other uniforms include, GIGN, GSG 9, RCMP, SAS, and other uniforms related to crime agencies.

Counter entities also sport the weapons of the organization to have the uniform of.

**Behavior**

Counter Entities almost always kill entities that don't pose a threat to humans.

If a counter entity is attacked by a human the counter entity will proceed to attack the entity, almost always via gun.

a M.E.G. outpost was once raided by counter entities because there were captive counter entities.

Counter Entities sometimes will trade items such as: Knifes, Water, 9mm Ammunition, and Night Visions goggles. only 5 recorded trades have been where counter entities would trade a pistol, such as the Glock or M1911.

This idea is rather interesting!

Human-like entities are pretty common, so here are some questions I'd like to ask to help stretch out the concept:

- Besides what they wear, what do their faces look like? Are they faceless?
- There are some entities that have certain communication abilities and disadvantages. Are they able to talk freely? Do they seem to be sentient?
- Are they aware that they are an entity in the Backrooms? Are they aware they're in there in the first place?
- It seems what the Counter entities do most is kill. Though I'd like to think there is a goal. What is their goal? To serve justice? To rid the world of evil?
- What about biology? Do they have human-like properties inside their body? What makes them different from humans?

Because the amount of info for the idea is rather small, there isn't much more to add as critiquing would be far more efficient if all of this was turned into a fully fleshed out draft. As such, you're welcome to create a draft in the Sandbox! I'll be happy to critique it after. I anticipate your work!
