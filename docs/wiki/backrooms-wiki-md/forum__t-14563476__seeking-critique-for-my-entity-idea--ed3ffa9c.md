Seeking Critique for my Entity Idea.

15 Mar 2022 06:25

Page Type: Entity

Core Concept: The entitys are very tall (aprx 2.5 meters tall), and lanky with humanoid characteristics. their arms are aprx 2.8 meters long and drag on the floor behind them when they walk, unless attacking, which they will lift their arms up to attack. Their fingers are long, and extremely rigid at the end, with sharp points, which it uses to stab, slice, and bludgeon victims. They have unsettlingly human eyes, contrary to their clearly non-human appearance. Their bodies are pale and have no traces of hair. They are extremely hostile, and violent. The ability they possess to lure in prey, is that after they have killed a victim, they will be able to repeat exactly, any word that the victim had said 3-4 days before being killed. They will hide themselves behind doors/corners and trick a wanderer into believing that another wanderer is nearby by calling for help, etc. Once they've lured in prey they will kill, and eat the victim, often very loudly. They have been reported to be able to open closed doors and are able to sprint long distances to catch escaping prey, likely so they cannot tell others not to trust the hidden voices.
