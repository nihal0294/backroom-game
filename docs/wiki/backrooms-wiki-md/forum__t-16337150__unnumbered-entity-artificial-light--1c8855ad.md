An emergency notice should be concise and to the point, yet this article uses:

**Double negatives:**

- Instead of "DO NOT LOOK IN THE DIRECTION OF ARTIFICIAL LIGHT UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES," it's clearer to say, "AVOID LOOKING AT ARTIFICIAL LIGHT ALWAYS."

**Unnecessarily complicated expressions:**

- "An instruction manual detailing proper termination procedure of an infected creature is provided below for reference:" could be simplified to "Below are methods for eliminating infected creatures."

- "Depriving them of power is ineffective and does NOT stop emission of contaminated light." should be "Cutting off power does NOT work."

- "Quarantine attempts unsuccessful and NOT recommended." could be better stated as "Do not attempt quarantine."

**Unnecessary paragraphs:**

- "THESE INSTRUCTIONS WILL BE STATED AS CONCISELY AS POSSIBLE—DO NOT WASTE TIME." Since it don't want to waste time, just skip this sentence and get straight to the instructions!

And, overusing capital letters for emphasis can be annoying, similar to highlighting nearly 70% of sentences in a textbook. It's more effective to use capital letters sparingly for true emphasis.

Emergency notices should be straightforward, giving clear instructions on what to do and what to avoid without delving into too many details.
