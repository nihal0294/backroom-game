Well, here is my review. I must clarify that in the lower section where the other levels are, you must put Level 300, Level 301 and Level 302.

[At Level 301, you hear a melody...](javascript:;)

[And it tells you the errors you have, in your draft...](javascript:;)

> Due to the extremely hostile and otherworldly nature and environment of **Level 301**

Delete the first "and" and add a comma, change "otherworldly nature" to "unnatural".

> an accurate and tangible report is very difficult to extract. Radio communications break

Before "radio" add an "the", and after "communications" put an "are".

> video recording systems are extremely distorted, and above all,

Delete "video".

> An ex-B.N.T.G. member

Maybe you meant: "An Ex-Member of the B.N.T.G".

> What sets "The Singing Caverns"

Delete "The Singing Caverns" and add "Level 301".

> It's as if the very structure of reality is in flux within "The Singing Caverns."

Delete "The Singing Caverns" and add "Level 301" (again).

> that haunt the caverns, and ultimately, they collapse,

Delete the comma after "and ultimately".

> He began to show signs of Alzheimer's Disease

Delete "Disease", everyone knows that Alzheimer's is a disease.

> Level 301 past the 30-minute mark when the hypnosis effect takes place.

Add a comma after "mark".

I recommend that you change the inputs and outputs, since if you only enter **Level 8** and the output directs you to **Level 8**, that would be a Sublevel of **Level 8**. Unless you want to change to a sublevel, but if you want to stay At **Level 301** you should say, for example, that the entrances are through levels related to caves: **Level 8**, **Level 27**, **Level 197**, etc…

I like your idea of ​​a level, some strange caves that give off a melody that little by little destroys your sanity. It would be good if you added more things, for example: The date it was discovered, interviews and other things. But I really liked your level idea, I hope you continue to be creative with the writing.
