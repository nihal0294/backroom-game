level Idea

21 Sep 2022 05:22

This level would be a abandoned prison complex built in the 21st century. It would be non-Euclidian. There would be no entities. To enter, you have to find a prison building on the infinite field of Level 10. And to exit this level, you would have to find a way to enter the locked cells (One of the methods would be to find a Warden's Key)

I wanted this level to be liminal but I don't really feel like it should be anymore. Still, let me know what you think of the level in it's current state.
