being a lifelong fan of guns I would like to think they would exist in the backrooms but of course the nature of the backrooms is definitely not a DOOM/CALL of DUTY shootem up place. Nor would one last long doing such things. Evasion and tactics seem to be dominant here. Of course given the spawning supply crates on L1 with supplys randomly assorted inside Fire arms are definitely already a reality of the backrooms but I would assume they are only reliably obtained at certain places like the b.n.t.g or other outposts And not too common throughout the levels but seldom found and hoarded to certain areas. Definitely many people carry guns in the back rooms but rarely are anyone strapped like a commando.Guns definitely do more damage than a knife or spear and can penetrate armor but are unreliable in comparison and need ammo which would have to be manufactured also.
