I'll be 100% honest, here. The concept is great. However, how the article was elaborated was good, but not the best.  
In particular:

**Concept: 10/10**

---

I like the idea of a mysterious parking lot of sorts. Love the picture as well.

---

**SPaG: 10/10**

---

There are no issues with it.

---

**Execution: 7/10**

---

Somehow poorly explained yet very detailed. I'm not sure if I like it. It is overall…too simple, I don't know. You should have explained better the abyss as well.

---

**Tone: 7.75/10**

---

Gotta hate how the description starts. The abyss surrounding the lot is dangerous, I get it, but it doesn't explain *why*. I know that I already said this, but this should have been elaborated after explaining the main properties of the lot[1](javascript:;). It also starts off too *fast paced*. I don't know how to explain this… Maybe, it's how I read it.

  
Additional: Also, it's a shame that Level 14 is now totally different and makes the corrisponding exit to it in Level 35, therefore, a bit off-theme.

I'd upvote, but I'm just not sure about it.
