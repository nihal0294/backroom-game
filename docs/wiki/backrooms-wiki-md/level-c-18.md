**Level C-18** is the 18th level of Cluster C of the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

Level C-18 is an endless space constructed from countless wooden shelves, on which are displayed a dazzling array of exquisite wine bottles.

The liquid contents of each bottle vary, and this seems to depend on the personal preferences of those who enter Level C-18. All types of liquids are clearly labeled on the packaging outside the bottle. Currently documented beverages include: Tsingtao Beer, Snowflake Beer, Wuliangye, Vodka, Tequila, Hennessy, Kvass, Coca-Cola, Latte Coffee, Nutri-Express, Jiannanchun, and so on.[1](javascript:;)

However, all beverages in Level C-18 possess the intoxicating properties of alcoholic drinks, and will cause individuals to become heavily intoxicated after consuming large quantities[2](javascript:;).

The bottles cannot be broken. The only way to open a bottle is to find a circular hall located somewhere within this level. A bottle opener will be placed on the wooden table in the center of the hall[3](javascript:;). In all cases, a note with identical text is found beneath the bottle opener:

> *If there is wine today, then today is the day to get drunk*

# **Bases, Communities, and Outposts**

There are no known bases, outposts, or communities on this level.

# **Entrances and Exits:**

## **Entrances[4](javascript:;):**

- Entrances to Level C-18 manifests as a dark red sandalwood door of indefinite shape, which may randomly appear in commonly understood "openings" (such as windows, cabinet doors, etc.), or may directly replace an otherwise unremarkable door.

## **Exits:**

- Go back the same way.[5](javascript:;)
- Drunk to the point of unconsciousness. You will awaken in the last place where you slept peacefully.
