A level with good potential and creativity. I'm normally not the SPaG guy but I see no problems with it. What I will mention is don't repeat every point.

> Level 890 is the 891st Level of the Backrooms, and appears to be an infinite drywall maze.
>
> # **Description**
>
> Level 890 appears to be a maze of drywall walls, and concrete flooring.

With these sentences you'll notice you repeated the exact same description. I would personally remove the section highlighted in red.

> Level 890 is the 891st Level of the Backrooms, and appears to be an infinite drywall maze.
>
> # **Description**
>
> Level 890 appears to be a maze of drywall walls, and concrete flooring.

---

Yet again the same point is repeated. You only need to mention that the hallucinations are rare once. Same with the fact that the windows are passive.

> passive windows can be found. These windows appear empty on the outside, other than a basic white or blue sky. These windows are passive, and will not attack like normal.

---

Yet again another repeated point is that the levels dangerous.

> as this level can be quite dangerous at times.

I didn't take the full extract because a paragraph explaining it is time wasting to read.

---

Onto the next part

> With that, many people who enter simply either freeze or overheat when entering, as one can enter this level on any floor.

With the section highlighted in red I would move that closer to the start as it seems that you've just changed your idea.

---

Also the links to levels are dead. To fix this lay it out like this:

[[[http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-11|level 11]]]

When written on the page it will appear as a link to level 11 and it will be usable like this one:[level 11](level-11.md)

---

Add links to entities and tell us what entities are in your level as you describe there to be alot of entities but the only one you mention is windows. For example is the level just full of Samantha's or is there a Duller around every corner.

---

Also where you put your author name in the section at the bottom, if you want it to be a link to your author profile, lay it out this:

[[\*user LocalBlockHead20]]

Obviously put your username in instead of mine.  
It will then appear like this:
