Level 81: War Changes

25 Jul 2020 11:33

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre (Optional):** Horror

**Elevator Pitch:** The basic idea is a regular american war museum, however, the farther the wanderer explores, the more the exhibits change, instead telling about the wanderer as if they were a war hero. Replacing dates and items with more familiar ones.

There's also an Entity that 'lives' in this level, a soldier that will chase wanderer's throughout the level as if he knows how to traverse it, and attempt to kill them with a bayonet equipped rifle. This soldier's physical appearance looks the same, though his uniform changes from wanderer to wanderer.

**Central Narrative:** there's not really a story, though i think some interesting stories could be made out of it!

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** I think it's special how this one changes depending on the wanderer, and how it presents some unique challenges, both physically and mentally.

**Additional Notes:** I think these ideas could be refined a small bit, and a bit more added, if you could, please focus on how i could make the ideas i already have better. Oh and the info here is just a brief summary, more will be included in the article to the same standard! thanks!
