Sup Dragon King, here we go again:

"insatiable desire and eat whatever comes": "and" should be "to"

"found in dimly light forested or urbanized environments": "light" should be "lit"

"seek a large, flat non-reflective surface" : flat(,) non-reflective surface

"muzzle of the entity is flexible lips": "is" should be "has"

"Theranodons, lack eyelids": No comma needed

"inflexible ears, atop its head": No comma is needed

"allow Theranodon to move at speeds": "Theranodon" should be plural, based on other instances you have used "Theranadon**s**"

"dispatching of large prey, these shed away": Correct me if I'm wrong, but I believe the comma should be a semicolon.

"Do's and Don'ts  
Dos": Correct "Do's" to "Dos", but I may be wrong.

"Utilizing bear spray, mace, and smoke bombs": "Utilizing" should be "Utilize"

"Attack an adult head-on; unless you have a death wish": The semicolon should be a comma.

"Leave out food in a highly frequented area.": "Leave food out" is more understandable, but both are correct.

Now, the big stuff! I quite frankly love this entity. I can tell you went into a very deep description on this, mentioning reproductive habits and joeys. I especially love your detailed drawings, assuming they are yours based on other entries. I was into the article until the end, and I like the idea of an ugly, scrappy, but hostile entity like this. One thing you could do, but you really do not have to, is add (I'm forgetting what they are called), tables at the top of an entity page showing its danger, what kind it is, here is a reference on [Entity 206](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-206). Other than that, you have a strong article, and I will be waiting for more updates! As always, Pm me with questions. -Iamalemon

Edit: I am also blown away by your audio recording! This is the second article I have seen with one, the other being Level 5.1, and it gave me a better feel of what the entity really is. I had invisioned it as something similar to a naked Plague Goblin, but hearing the noise, I made sure to double-check the facts.
