Hardest boss you have faced

04 Apr 2023 18:55

Note: this is kinda a rant cause I'm still mad I wasted all of my (premium currency) to this boss and still lost (also im a bit of a nerd in this game so watch out)

Let me introduce you to Raging bahumat from the game: The Battle Cats, the only game where you can contain a zombie outbreak in france with kung fu and weed whacking then go to the big bang to kill god then loose to a black duck after getting a unit called gas mask cat to counter it (true story).

Before i get into the boss I need to explain some mechanics:  
it is a tower defense game attack enemy base while protecting yours.  
There are 2 main type of bosses slow and fast: slow bosses have alot of peons (supporting enemies that tend to shield the boss) and the boss comes out of the base immediately and tends to have slow movement speed. Fast bosses have less peons and the boss comes out when you attack the base and has fast movement speed and attack. (Bahumat is a fast boss)  
You have 10 slots for 10 units of your choice for fast bosses your units look like this: ~4 meat shields, ~4 midrange attackers/supporters, and ~3 long range attackers/supporters.  
When beating a stage there is a chance for a treasure to drop (there are 3 stages of treasures each being better) having all treasures in a area can activate special abilities like extra energy, but anti alien treasures are important for this story

Ok story time, my first serious attempt to kill the boss was a train wreck and wasted an hour of my life, so ill visualize what it was like: opening the level the enemy base spits out alot of aliens units (you want to stall as long as possible to save up money) fast forward to when i attack the base raging bahumat comes rushing out of the base ( he has 1.2 million health and 44 k damage and attacks like 3 times a sec, but bc i didnt have all anti alien crystals he had ig like 5 mil health damage doesnt matter cause he basically one shots everything) he pretty much killed all my mid attackers immediately and soon killed my back liners, when he destroyed my base he was at 85% hp you can reset where you are at full money for 30 cat food (premium currency) and i had 700 at the time so [montage of widling down his health] he finally died, but wait… There is this alien camel that showed up sometime in the process it outranged all of my units and had a somewhat quick attack so i was unable to damage it when all. When all hope seemed lost i opened up cat gods shop ( you can get boosts in exchage for cat food) one of the options was to summon a lighting strike 3x more powerful than the cat cannon, so i proceeded to loose all my money and the camel still wasnt dead so yeah that was sad that i lost.[treasure hunting montage] i faced him again, he wasn't so bad, and i beat him before the camel came. So yeah at least i now have talents (yay)

Seeing That you made it this far, enjoy these ludicrous sounding sentences about talents:  
Weed wacker can get aku targeting so now you can mow down both the undead and cultists  
Ramen (an angels worst nightmare) can be boosted in health and damage and can obliterate anything holy in his path  
The kitty of liberty can knock back aliens through talents (not much else to add)
