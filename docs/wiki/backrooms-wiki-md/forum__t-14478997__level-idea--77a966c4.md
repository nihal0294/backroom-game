Level Idea

10 Feb 2022 17:25

Page Type: Level

Core Concept: The Level is a large old cave, that when walked in, the entrance is closed. You have to dodge traps, entities, and such others to escape the level. However, there is a hidden entity here in this cave, just waiting for the wrong wanderer for a wrong move. Basically, at the end, I’ll write a short little tale.

Additional Notes: So, my first thought was a Canon, then realized I wasn’t good enough for a Canon, so I decided to make it a level. Other than that, I don’t know how to *describe* the entity. I’m not so good at writing entities.
