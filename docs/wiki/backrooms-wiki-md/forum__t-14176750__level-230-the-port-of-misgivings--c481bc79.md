Level 230- "The Port of Misgivings"

25 Aug 2021 15:28

**Page Type:** New Level Idea

**Genre:** Horror

**Page Layout:** In this Level's page, it will use the customary normal level template, but add a "Areas" Section.

**Elevator Pitch:** This Level transports the reader to an unsettling port, with various entities and a safe place for wanderers to trade. Includes a few well known entities such as Smilers and Facelings. Also includes well known groups such as B.N.T.G and the M.E.G.

**Central Narrative:** When you enter this ominous level, you are aware something is different. A port forever locked in nighttime. Passing through its dark streets is a perilous thing. Until you stumble upon a settlement. A safe haven for wanderers to rest and trade for unique items. Be aware for wolves in sheep's clothing.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** A port shrouded in darkness. Unseen Perils Lurking just around that corner. A sleepy settlement vigilant in the night. Sleep peaceful tonight for other levels are not as merciful.

**Additional Notes:** For full level article, please visit the Level Page at this link: <http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/unforeseendrop>
