Level Idea: Lost and Found

27 Feb 2023 18:10

My idea is a level referred to as the Lost and Found.

It's just a completely black room with no walls or anything, only a floor. The room would be filled with piles and piles of various objects, many of said piles being several feet tall. I'm debating if I want to create an entity to go along with it.

The lore behind yhe room is that anything that people have lost in the past and could never find again essentially noclipped and ended up in this room.

If wanderers choose to, they can search through the piles for something they may have lost in the past. Upon finding and identifying an object that once belonged to them, the wanderer will disappear in a bright flash of light.

I wouldn't specify or even hint to this within the actual article, but it's meant to actually be an exit to the Frontrooms! That's the main reason I'm asking, because even if I don't say it's an exit to the Frontrooms, I'm not sure if it actually BEING an exit to the Frontrooms is against the rules or not.

Thanks in advance!!
