# Morte

rating: +55[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

  

![morteref](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/art:morte/morteref)  
  
Hello! I'm doc and I like to play and draw. This is my Deathmoth guy Morte, or Morty, and they're my entry for Mascotcon 2024! As for design notes I suppose I tried my best to stick with a "backroomsy" color pallette without making them look too boring, their outfit is mostly pilot-inspired, but they did begin to look more like some kind of mechanic with the caution patterns. And I don't care if you interpret them as masc or fem, since I did make them look androgynous on purpose (also applies to pronouns, use whatever). Even if they don't win, I already love this guy dearly and I'll gladly keep them around to use for other endeavors of mine. Regardless, I hope you like them :3
