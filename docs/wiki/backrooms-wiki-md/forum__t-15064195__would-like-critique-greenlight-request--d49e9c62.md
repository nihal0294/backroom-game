Would like critique/greenlight request

01 Aug 2022 06:03

Level 228, also known as Level “Wake Up,” warps itself into the most twisted and horrifying nightmare you’ve ever slept through. This level was found on January 23rd, 2005, by the wanderer known as [wouldn’t you like to know =)] and his level is highly dangerous for wanderers due to many entities being spotted in the different regions. Level 228 is capable of containing more than one wanderer; however, each wanderer sees this level as an entirely different nightmare than the others. Entities such as Smilers, Rattlers, Skin Stealers, Screamers and Scream Eaters have been spotted lurking in the darkness of Level 228 and there have also been recordings from previous wanderers discussing the presence of Partygoers where there seems to be party-like settings.

The entity known as “the Mare” seems to have taken control and has power over all of the other entities in level 228. The Mare is an entity that warps itself into one of three things: The wanderer’s worst fear, another entity, or pure darkness. When the Mare shapeshifts into another entity or pure darkness, it is studying the wanderer’s actions, so wanderers must be mindful when exploring this level. You might never be alone.

Wanderers have explained that in this level, they felt extreme exhaustion and fatigue, but they were physically unable to go to sleep until they found the exit. No matter how hard they would try, they would not be able to physically sleep at all. These wanderers have said it “felt like pure mind torture to not be able to rest at all.” Objects such as almond water, Lucky O’ Milk and candy have been found in vending machines and have been known to help with exhaustion and fatigue however, it is recommended you start looking for the exit as fast as you can. Liquid silence can also be harvested here, and it must be found if you want to escape.

Outposts:

While there is a way to exit this level, you cannot do so without a specific liquid. A group of wanderers who found each other became known as “the Silencers,” built an outpost in order to harvest, experiment with and use liquid silence to find their escape. According to logs that have been received from this group, all of them found beds randomly throughout the level that were identical to their actual beds in the Frontrooms. They observed that the entity “the Mare” seems to have a weakness to liquid silence and turns around at the sight of it.

Another log was found from the group, discussing their plans for an experiment regarding the liquid silence and the mysterious beds. They discussed taking the liquid silence and pouring a circle of it around the beds that are identical to their actual beds and attempt to fall asleep to see what would happen. It seems the experiment worked, and they were taken to level 0. However, the group’s outpost, since becoming abandoned, has been nicknamed “the Silenced” by other wanderers.

Entrances:

The only way to enter this level is through the rare chance that a person no-clips while they are sleeping.

Exits:

While the Silencers experimented with liquid silence, they also experimented with the beds and found that the beds had a strange connection to the liquid. They experimented by pouring liquid silence on the ground in a few spots and discovered that beds would generate in those areas when someone else arrived to the level. The beds were attracted to silence. Before exiting this level, one of the Silencers decided to scatter open vials of the liquid silence all over the level so that other wanderers’ beds would appear next to the open vials. The only way to escape is for the wanderer to find their identical bed, use the open vial and pour a circle of liquid silence around your bed and lay down. Once you lay down, you will immediately fall asleep and fall into level 0.
