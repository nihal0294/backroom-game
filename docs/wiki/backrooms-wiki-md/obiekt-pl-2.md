# Description

  
**Object PL-2** is an anomalous mailbox utilized across the Polish Backrooms. It is used to move letters to the enigmatic level ["Post Office"](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/unnumbered-level-post-office), where they are delivered to a mailbox near the recipient. Its appearance varies according to its origin and is not consistent. Although they may seem to be from the 20th or 21st century, older ones can occasionally be found.

Interdimensional Mailboxes that were not created by wanderers are instead assembled by [Facelings](entity-9.md) at the ["Post Office"](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/unnumbered-level-post-office). They attach them to the walls of levels that have or resemble cityskapes.

**Objects PL-2** appear diverse, and the only feature that distinguishes them from regular boxes is that they are completely black inside. There are also mysterious whispers coming from them that seem to say the words "send in!" and "open!" These words are always spoken when someone nearby desires to send something or when the recipient has received a message.

# How to send messages and items?

To send a letter or parcel to someone, it must be properly addressed. Please carefully sign the shipment with the name and surname or nickname of both the addressee and the recipient. Failing to do both of these will result in the delivery being burned inside the mailbox after insertion. After addressing the message, place it in the mailbox. After a brief period, the parcel will disappear from the box and will reach the ["Post Office"](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/unnumbered-level-post-office) in milliseconds.

# What cannot be sent using the interdimensional mailbox?

Some objects are not accepted by **Object PL-2**. For example, all types of explosives placed in a mailbox will be ejected without damaging the mailbox, and living creatures placed in the mailbox will be teleported approximately 100 meters away from it.

# How can you receive a message from an interdimensional mailbox?

  
Packages can be picked up at any Interdimensional Mailbox across the Polish backrooms. The message always seems to move to the box next to the designated recipient.[1](javascript:;) This mechanism has not yet been explained. Mailboxes themselves most often appear on flat structures resembling those built in Frontrooms by humans.

# How to create your own Interdimensional Mailbox?

  
To create your own **Object PL-2**, you must bring your mailbox to the enigmatic level ["Post Office"](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/unnumbered-level-post-office). After giving it to one of the [Facelings](entity-9.md) working there, ask him to turn it into an Interdimensional Mailbox; the entity will take it and then go to the back of the post office. Any box, regardless of age or condition, will be accepted. The creation process takes about 5 minutes and is shrouded in mystery. Once it's finished, the [Faceling](entity-9.md) will give you a ready-made interdimensional mailbox.  
You have now obtained your own **Object PL-2**.
