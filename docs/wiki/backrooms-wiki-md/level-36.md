Welcome, traveler, to the Timeless Airport: the biggest safe hub where every level is only one flight away!  
Within this labyrinthine complex you'll find all sorts of typical airport facilities, and more notably, 26 terminals labeled A to Z, each with infinitely numbered gates. These gates have a direct connection to possibly every Level, making this a crucial nexus point for traveling through the Backrooms!

Here are the steps to follow for a smooth journey to your next destination:

1. **Orientation:** figure out where you want to go and how.
2. **Navigation:** traverse the level to reach your gate.[1](javascript:;)
3. **Anticipation:** wait for your flight to be called for boarding[2](javascript:;) and then hop on board.

Come see us at booth 0 in the check-in area for more in-depth information!

# Section 1: Orientation

---

## Baggage claim

[![Baggage carousels near a Lost and Found office](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-36/luggage/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-36/luggage)

The Lost And Found Office is mostly empty, save for the occasional discarded or forgotten luggage.

This is where you first arrive! Yes, it is normal that you're brought in by conveyor belt; they import every being and object that no-clips into Level 36. Endless rows of baggage carousels stretch out in both directions, attached to a wall that is the only known bounded edge of the level.

Each active conveyor belt corresponds to a specific entrance point from a particular level. If you and your companion used the same no-clip point, you'll appear on the same conveyor belt. The numbers denoting each carousel are not correlated with the entrance level number. For example, Level 11 has multiple entrances to the Timeless Airport, which are linked to carousels #9, #28, #147, etc.[3](javascript:;)

Infinite baggage carousels imply infinite possible entrances, but only a finite number of them are actually active. Despite the constant machine hum and creaking of metal filling the air, most conveyor belts are inactive and immobile. They can become active if a new entrance point is formed; similarly, active carousels can cease to work if the particular entrance they represent is no longer viable.[4](javascript:;)

Head towards the check-in area and don't forget your belongings! Anything that remains on the carousel for 10 rounds will get rerouted and deposited in the Lost and Found office. It is common courtesy to not touch other people's luggage.

## Check-in

[![Rows of check-in desks with information screens over each booth](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-36/checkin/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-36/checkin)

The check-in desks go on endlessly.

Past the baggage claim area you'll encounter the check-in zone, an infinite row of deserted service counters. The screens directly above these, as well as plenty of similar screens scattered throughout the level, all display the same crucial information: a list of outbound flights, each denoted by a countdown, unique flight number, assigned gate, and status.

Here's how to make sense of it all! When a flight's countdown reaches 1000, it will begin "boarding," in which the indicated gate acts as a threshold to the intermediary [Sublevel 36.1](level-36-1.md), the Airplane. Once the countdown hits 0, the threshold closes and the flight disappears from the list. Now, all passengers who had entered that instance of Sublevel 36.1 get shuttled directly to a specific level.[5](javascript:;)

There are always dozens of flights scheduled and infinite gates, which means connections to virtually anywhere in the Backrooms. But before you go jumping on any random flight you see, be aware: no one knows exactly which level each gate will lead to. The vast majority of flights have yet to be documented, meaning that blindly boarding one might bring you to desolate or life-threatening levels.[6](javascript:;)

#### Flying through the Backrooms with us is easy!

If you need assistance, this is where we come in! Through first-hand traveling and exchanging data with all sorts of wanderers who pass through the Timeless Airport, the resident BTA team has been accumulating information on flights and where their passengers end up.

We've confirmed many destinations so far, including several pleasant and habitable levels, so we can recommend the best course of action based on your situation. *What danger can you handle? Are you looking for a specific type of environment?* And the most important question: *how long are you willing to wait?*

But of course, we can talk more in-depth in person, and you can browse our updated catalogue of confirmed flights there!

# Section 2: Navigation

---

[![Nondescript hallway with a blank blue sign](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-36/hall/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-36/hall)

If the signs are ever void or malfunctioning, go back.

After passing through the check-in lines and defunct security checks, you'll now step into the more topologically inconsistent area of the airport. While the previous zones were orderly and easily crossable, due to being infinite only horizontally, the rest of the level is a non-Euclidian maze.

Although seemingly unnoticeable, the transition can be likened to swimming out from an enclosed pool into the open sea—if the sea contained invisible overlapping currents that pulled you in every possible direction, making it impossible to traverse in an ordinary manner.

This manifests as concourses that connect to themselves from multiple different points, signs that are contradictory depending on where you came from, distances and rotations not adding up linearly, loss of cardinality,[9](javascript:;) etc. Despite how overwhelming it may appear, this is all manageable by paying close attention to all signs and trusting the directions.

#### Unsure? Here's some quick tips!

Many travelers might tell you that this seems quite overwhelming, but we assure you that it is actually very manageable by just paying attention to all signs and trusting the directions, no matter how absurd!

- If a sign directly contradicts a previous one (example: you were walking forwards, and now the new sign tells you to go backwards), just keep following the new sign.
- Don't trust any "exit" signs. There is no real exit and you don't know where it may lead you.
- Always pay attention to the PA announcements and/or the information screens for potential updates.
- Empty sign = no good!

*Excerpt from the BTA navigation flyer*

  
The only real issue is empty or broken signage. This happens in out-of-bounds areas, functionally dead-end zones that loop on themselves and lead to nowhere. In this case it is imperative to trace your steps back until there are coherent signs again.

Finally, be aware of the temperature of your surroundings. Different areas of the level have drastically different temperatures, ranging from a chilling cold to stuffy warmth. While navigating through the various hallways, you could easily pass through cold and warm areas in quick succession or stand in overlapping concourses which feel hot and cold at the same time. Due to time dilation, a cold (or other infection) will not pass as quickly as it usually would.

[![Hallway floor covered in reflections, lights, and shadows.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-36/light/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-36/light)

Light distortion in a hall.

Light behaves strangely the more anomalous a space is, occasionally appearing fractured, distorted, and repetitive. This can happen in corridors that spatially overlap with other hallways or have recursive entrance points.

The result can look like reflections that don't line up, unusually dark or bright areas, or flickering effects. If you see signs or screens flickering, it doesn't necessarily indicate a malfunction, but it could very well be this optical distortion.

Rest assured though, as these are innocuous mirage-like illusions observed mainly in reflective surfaces and glass. Afterimages or hazy shadows of wanderers who recently walked by may also be visible hanging in the air in the distance.

[![Shopping concourse with various shops on both sides.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-36/shoppingnew/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-36/shoppingnew)

A carpeted shopping hall.

These are large intertwining spaces and concourses designed to walk you through a myriad of shops and businesses. You can find virtually every type of Frontrooms brand and business in these areas; unfortunately, most products are actually worthless.[10](javascript:;) The only useful articles tend to be the clothes, storage equipment, and food products (largely snacks, sweets, and packaged goods).

Rarely, you may find Backrooms-native objects inside relevant stores, such as: [Worn Sacks](object-87.md) in backpack shops, [Almond Water](object-1.md), [Lucky O' Milk](object-28.md) and [ChocoBytes](object-67.md) in food aisles, [Babel Balm](object-25.md) in makeup stores. You're welcome to take any of these, but don't spend too much time scavenging or you might end up straying farther away from your initial position than you expect.

[![Group of travelers on a travelator in an airport terminal](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-36/group/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-36/group)

A BTA Travel Group on a travelator!

Travelators are useful for rapidly and smoothly crossing large distances. They often directly connect concourses and terminals, for example, bringing you from the check-in to concourse W in a few minutes.

One thing to keep in mind is that they are often one-way, and even if it took you one travelator to go in one direction, it might take several different travelators to get back to the same place.

It is recommended to avoid malfunctioning travelators that stutter or aren't moving at all.

[![Meeting area with rows of seats and unusual wallpaper](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-36/meeting/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-36/meeting)

The enigmatic Meeting Place.

The Meeting Place is an anomalous location that can be found in at least 47 distinct positions throughout the Timeless Airport, but they are all actually one singular place. It is impossible to map and seems to disappear and reappear arbitrarily, on account of contradictory reports of its locations.

For those lost or in need of assistance, it is a good idea to seek The Meeting Place for a higher chance of meeting other wanderers. BTA staff routinely pass through here, ready to give a helping hand.

# Section 3: Anticipation

---

## Terminals

Breaking free of the labyrinth of concourses, you'll finally reach the terminals!

There are 26, labeled from A to Z, containing infinitely numbered gates. Each gate consists of waiting areas with accessible bathrooms, water fountains, and rows upon rows of seats and tables, some of which contain power outlets and USB ports, which come in handy for powering devices. There is Wi-Fi everywhere, but it is prone to disconnecting every hour.[11](javascript:;)

[![Wide view of a deserted airport gate](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-36/terminal/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-36/terminal)

A deserted gate.

Lounges can be regularly found among the terminals. They are highly sought out for their luxurious lounging areas and facilities, such as:

- Entertainment in the form of books, magazines, video games;
- Comfortable, clean bathrooms and showers;
- High-quality catering such as buffets and fully stocked fridges;
- Rarely, gyms and treadmills.

Seeing that you might have to be waiting here for a while, feel free to make yourself comfortable!

## Time Dilation

Compared to the rest of the Backrooms, time runs extremely fast here, yet its perception feels dreadfully slow. Any timekeeping devices brought into the level instantly freeze and will not work until they are brought back out. Combined with all the clocks showing random times (or missing the hands altogether) and no discernible day/night cycle, the illusion of time shatters and all temporal notions lose meaning.

It can be estimated that spending one week here is equivalent to one second passing in Level 11. For yet unknown reasons, your biological body won't age,[12](javascript:;) but your mind will perceive an excruciatingly slow passage of time.

An unfortunate side effect of this is that afflictions of the physical body (colds, infections, headaches, bruises, injuries, etc.) will also appear to heal extremely slowly. This applies to both complications that you suffered before entering Level 36 and those aquired within this level. There is no increased risk, as this is purely a mental perception, but you may find that they linger on for an uncomfortably long time.

Disclaimer: mental aging is a real phenomenon, and long-term stay in the level can cause a jarring disparity between your physical and mental age. This may produce the following undesirable effects, which might continue to affect you even after exiting: disorientation, restlessness, anxiety, derealization. Despite how useful it might appear to be able to "save time" on this level, the psychological pressure and confusing relationship with deters most groups from forming permanent bases here.

#### Bored? What to do while you wait!

You might think to take a nap and sleep while you wait, but the bad news is that you won't be able to. It is physically impossible to sleep throughout the entire airport, no matter how tired you may be.[13](javascript:;) Here are our trusty tips to help you kill time!

- **Plan your next steps.** There is often little time to sit and think in this reality, but now that you're not preoccupied with surviving, escaping, or seeking food and shelter, you can meditate on your future plans in all serenity.
- **Explore and find people.** It is essential to keep up some physical activity and human connection. If there are others at your gate, get to know them!
- **Remember why you're here.** You're waiting for a flight that will directly bring you to a level of your choosing, quickly and safely. This is a rare luxury in the chaos of the Backrooms, so don't squander this opportunity!
- **Remember to eat.** Just because time *feels* frozen doesn't mean it is. Maintaining your energy levels is crucial.[14](javascript:;)

If you reach the point where you truly need to leave the airport as soon as possible, we recommend coming to see us again for advice instead of hastily jumping onto the first flight that's boarding.

## Bases, Outposts, and Communities

### Backrooms Travel Agency

We have set up our main booth in the check-in area, which you may have noticed from our bright purple signs and [BTA](travel-rooms-agency.md) branding! A team of our dedicated Travel Planners and Travel Guides helps direct incoming wanderers so that they successfully locate and catch their flights.[15](javascript:;)

We are also constantly reaching out to past travelers for information on the flights they took, in order to keep adding useful data to our catalogue of flights. You can contact our other bases or send a trusted messenger for this purpose as well.

With the common goal to help people safely reach their ideal levels, the BTA is a friendly collaborator to other groups residing here. Below is a summary of their outposts, but we're also leaving space for them to officially introduce themselves if they wish to do so.

[+ Major Explorer Group](javascript:;)

[- Major Explorer Group](javascript:;)

A sub-team of Track Mappers from the [MEG](the-m-e-g.md) has a keen interest in Level 36's temporal characteristics and has transformed one of the luxury passenger lounges in Concourse M into a temporary outpost. They are intent on exploring the anomalous layout of the airport with us, such as linking entrance levels with each conveyor belt. They're also attempting to accurately determine the factor of time dilation, without significant progress due to the difficulty of coordinating experiments in such radically different time flows.

[+ Backrooms Nonaligned Trade Group](javascript:;)

[- Backrooms Nonaligned Trade Group](javascript:;)

Members of the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) related to delivery services often frequent Level 36 due to its utility in providing fast and direct access to specific levels. We cooperate for coordination of safe travel routes and occasional exchange of navigation data. A series of undisclosed first-class lounges (the exact locations of which are known only to BNTG employees) acts as an intermediary stockpile for goods and supplies that need to be delivered elsewhere.

[+ Gate C32 Support Group](javascript:;)

[- Gate C32 Support Group](javascript:;)

To an ear so accustomed to buzzing silence and resounding echoes, the sounds of amicable chitter-chatter are a harmonious relief, and by following the noise you will find its source to be the largest known community in the Timeless Airport: the Gate 32 Support Group.

[![Booth at the end of a gate with a sign that says "C32"](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-36/gate/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-36/gate)

The famous Gate C32.

Spread all around gate C32 is a large gathering of wanderers, huddled on the seats and lying in circles on the carpeted floor, using their bags as pillows or footrests. They are waiting for flight 1784XBA8JLP, directed to [Level 186](level-186.md), the Infinite Valley. Their flight details occupy the very last row on the display screens, with a countdown that shows 94612753.

They have been waiting for a long time, and will be waiting for even longer.

The Support Group acts as a solidary lighthouse of life in a barren ocean of timeless artificiality. They establish their own day/night schedule, organize times for meals, and plan group activities and events to pass time, such as visiting luxurious lounges and shops, passing by The Meeting Place, or accompanying other wanderers to their gates. Approximately 150 people of the group are actual passengers intent on boarding their plane, but other meandering wanderers come to hang out with them as well.

The group is composed of wanderers with wildly different backgrounds and goals, yet they are all united in the simple act of waiting for a flight that will change their lives. An easy ride to a safe level is a tempting offer, even if the wait is interminable.

Many wanderers have gotten discouraged and left, while new ones take their place with renewed perseverance. Some see it as a solemn test of patience, or a hopeless last resort.

*"At one point the screen showed the status "delayed" for so long, it felt like hours. I swear I almost lost my mind, praying for that absurd number to return. But it did. And I keep on waiting."*

*"Leave? and then what, go back to roughing it out in some dingy level? This is, like, the most surefire way to the valley (laughs) I've been waiting for, what, five months? I'm not gonna give up now."*

*"They're a bit delusional—they think it's an easy ticket to paradise. Personally, I couldn't stand to just wait around helplessly, so I left."*

*"I have 3 children with me. I cannot simply…hike through the Backrooms with them. We got separated from my husband Matthew back in [those halls](level-356-1.md), and our end goal has always been to settle down in a beautiful valley. For our family to be whole again…I would wait as long as it takes."*

For many, the wait is too long. But for others, it's the only thing worth waiting for.
