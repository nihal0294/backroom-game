**Level 253** is the 254th Level of the Backrooms.

# **Description**

**Level 253** is characterized by a long hallway with dark, black walls that extend as far as the eye can see. The space is devoid of any entities, making it a relatively safe environment for individuals who may find themselves trapped within the backrooms. The ceiling is void of any fixtures, but towards the end of the hallway, there are lights attached to the roof, which provide a faint, almost eerie glow.

At the end of the hallway, two doors can be observed on one of the walls. The doors are identical in appearance, and it is unclear what lies beyond them. Despite the presence of these doors, there is no indication of any means of entry or exit other than the initial point of entry into the backrooms.  
[[include component:image-block  
name=Doors.jpg|  
caption=The doors at the end of the hallway on the left.  
]]

While this level may initially appear to be benign, individuals are advised to exercise caution when traversing the long, black hallway. The lack of any visible landmarks or points of reference may lead to disorientation or confusion. Additionally, the presence of the two doors may cause individuals to become fixated on them, potentially leading to dangerous behavior.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There are no known bases, outposts or communities on this level

# **Entrances And Exits**

### Entrances

On levels [1](level-1.md), [4](level-4.md), [11](level-11.md) and [20](level-20.md), you may find a black door with no handles or doorknob. This door can be pushed, and when walked through, you will be greeted by a staircase, which upon walking up, you will be in **Level 253**. If you turn back, you will just see a wall.

### Exits

There are no known exits for this level, but it is theorized that the doors at the end of the corridor have something to do with the exit of the level.

Hi!

[You open a bottle, hoping to find Almond Water.](javascript:;)

[Instead, you find a handwritten critique notice.](javascript:;)

First off, you can't have the Level number in the forum title, as someone else may take it. Come up with a catchy name and use that. You can totally use the desired number in the draft, but not the forum title.  
Anyway, without further ado, here you go!

> **Level 253** is characterized by a long hallway with dark, black walls that extend as far as the eye can see.

I wouldn't say "is characterized by". I would instead say "appears to be". Also, the length seems to come into play here. How long is it?

> The space is devoid of any entities, making it a relatively safe environment for individuals who may find themselves trapped within the backrooms.

Anyone who finds themselves trapped in the Backrooms starts out on [Level 0](level-0.md), so that doesn't work. If the Level is devoid of entities, then you should state it in the Safety Class Description. Just because a Level doesn't have entities doesn't mean it's safe. There are also environmental dangers to take into account.

> The ceiling is void of any fixtures, but towards the end of the hallway, there are lights attached to the roof, which provide a faint, almost eerie glow.

This sentence seems to contradict itself. If the ceiling is devoid (that's the word you're looking for) of any fixtures, then how are lights on the roof? Is the cieling clear or translucent? Please clarify.

> While this level may initially appear to be benign, individuals are advised to exercise caution when traversing the long, black hallway.

Another inconsistency. How can the hallway be black, but you have light in some other areas?

> This door can be easily mistakened for the door of level 998 This door can be pushed, and when walked through, you will be greeted by a staircase, which upon walking up, you will be in Level 253.

Put a period after "998". Also, state thawanderers will go unconscious. Never use "you". It's very informal and personal, and doesn't match the clinical tone the authors strive for. I've made this mistake many times, and the easiest (and most common) way to fix this is to switch it out with Wanderers.

All in all, I think there may be some juicy potential here, but there isn't much meat on the bones. If you add some more to the description, as well as the potential effects of the Level, you could have a really savory piece.

[Instead, you find a handwritten critique notice.](javascript:;)
