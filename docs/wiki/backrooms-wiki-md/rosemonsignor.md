**Haaai Welcome to Rose's Author Page**

---

I'm a 20-something year old amateur author and photographer (I guess). I live on the eastern United States, but I'm a huge fan of different cultures and walks of life. I'm also a fan of various games and genres of music.

# Why and what I write?

I write, for the most part, because I've enjoyed being able to express my thoughts and ideas in a medium which others can appreciate. I'm primarily a fiction writer, but I don't mind whatever setting or genre it is. I'm a huge fan of poetry and romance, though.  
I've had experience writing plays and short stories of my own for the most part, but I also dabble in a bit of roleplay, depending on whatever I feel like doing.

# What I like

I'm a huge fan of all sorts of games. My biggest inspiration for my Backrooms writing came from playing games like Yume Nikki and its various fangames. Silent hill was also another huge inspiration for me, but moreso Yume Nikki.  
Aside from those, I play a lot of Destiny 2, Minecraft, and games in the Fallout series. I'm a general FPS/RPG fan, but I've played other games on and off such as Ace Combat and Valkyria Chronicles (I'm a nerd, I know).

When it comes to music, I have two major playlists and a collection of various CD albums. The genre I mostly listen it would probaby be rock or metal, but I enjoy most music (except modern country, that stuff blows). I listen to a lot of Coheed and Cambria, Deftones, The Mars Volta, Tool, and Breaking Benjamin.

Other hobbies include exploring/hiking, collecting random junk, socializing with people, and cooking all sorts of stuff (I kinda suck at that).
