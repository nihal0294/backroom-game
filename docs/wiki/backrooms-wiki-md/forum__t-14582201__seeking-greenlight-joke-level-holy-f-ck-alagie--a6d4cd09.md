SEEKING GREENLIGHT - Joke Level \*Holy F\*ck Alagie\*

28 Mar 2022 14:31

![ov3cqhw7wav61.png?width=824&format=png&auto=webp&s=b476f292b3620ce2999795c99cfd20d8e6d2cbf1](https://preview.redd.it/ov3cqhw7wav61.png?width=824&format=png&auto=webp&s=b476f292b3620ce2999795c99cfd20d8e6d2cbf1)

Oh god not again, alagies basement. If only I could remember more from this level since all I remember was running and running until I exited, I managed to take two pictures of the level since I had my cell phone with me. From what I recall, this level has 2 phases, second one being almost inhabitable and super unsafe, I am glad I got out.

# **Description:**

This level has entities such as [The Ghost Of Alagie](http://www.backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-the-ghost-of-alagie) and other entities that look like rats and insects identical to the ones that are in the [frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md). Nothing much has been documented in the level other than being quite dangerous and having weird environmental properties some of them being:

- Always having a slight feeling of anxiety and fear
- Ground shaking at a huge rate *(Only at phase 2)*
- Having a super high pressure *(Only at phase 2)*

**Phase One**  
![holy-fuck-alagie.jpg](https://i.ibb.co/C9vN8wV/holy-fuck-alagie.jpg)

Even tho some of the rooms have the lights off, it is still pretty calm and safe until you enter phase 2, and you will have to enter it because there is no way else out of this level only a slight chase there is another way out but none of which are documented just yet.

**Phase Two**  
![holy-fuck-alagie-phase2.jpg](https://i.ibb.co/PMkZz8K/holy-fuck-alagie-phase2.jpg)

*Phase two*, at this phase of this level all the lights are on and the ground starts shaking due to high pressure of the phase, in this phase dangerous entities like [The Ghost Of Alagie](http://www.backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-the-ghost-of-alagie) start to creep out creating high pressure point waves, although the [The Ghost Of Alagie](http://www.backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-the-ghost-of-alagie) don't attack you physically, they are known to be very hostile toward wanderers. The scariest thing about this is that The Ghost Of Alagie does not have a physical form, and are almost invisible to human eyes, they only have a thin outline of reflection representing their form.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There are no documented bases or communities in this level.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

**Entrances**

You can enter this level by falling asleep in [the backroom](joke-level-backroom.md)

**Exits**

At the end of the hall there is a door, in order to open this door you have it insert a iron key that you can find on the floor either next to the door or at your spawnpoint if you are lucky.
