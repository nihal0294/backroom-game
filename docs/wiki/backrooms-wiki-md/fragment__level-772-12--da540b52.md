A New Era

  

Hello everyone! I am Jimmy Karlson, and today in Radio Backrooms, we'll start with this year's most exciting news!

Liberation that has oppressed wanderers in the four corners of Backrooms during this long year officially fell this Thursday morning with the death of its leader, the Patriarch, otherwise known as Father Richard Simmons Delacroix.

At this very moment, the many wanderers of the Backrooms are returning to their former lives: they're free to explore without fear of being caught by aggressors of their own kind; they're rebuilding outposts and bases while reclaiming their homes and burning every symbol of the former tyrannical regime they can find.

Indeed, ladies and gentlemen. Despite the horrors suffered by many, this event has also allowed us to unite in common purpose, despite our differences, in these difficult times.

So we're delighted to announce the start of a new era for mankind!

An era of peace and collaboration between wanderers, communities, and factions, the like of which has never been seen before.

Bound for survival; Bound for a better future, as our motto suggest.

-

And above all, let's not forget,

A minute's silence for our heroes who gave their lives in battle for mankind:

I quote Seras Valentina alongside Evi'Ka or Conor McNamara, and countless brave soldiers who fell in our fight against the enemy…

-  
  
They will always be remembered as humanity's saviors; their names engraved on the Backrooms National Memorial. May their souls rest in peace.

And that concludes today's news!

See you tomorrow, on Radio Backrooms.
