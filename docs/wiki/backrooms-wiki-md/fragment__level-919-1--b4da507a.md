"Hey, over here! I've been waiting for you!"

  
As expected, I find a wanderer seated at the picnic table, waving frantically. They seem very enthusiastic to see me. I'm glad to see them as well; they look like a talkative person, so I should have no problem coaxing out information.

I look down. Like noticing something in a dream that wasn't there before, there is a wicker picnic basket in my hand. It feels heavy, and glass clinks inside it as I walk.

"Thank you, my friend! Hello, I'm THE ONE BEFORE; what's your name?"

Although wary, I answer.

"Well, no reason to waste a nice picnic, huh? Open it up, I can't wait to eat!"

I cautiously open up the basket, only to feel a little foolish to discover a perfectly normal picnic set. The inside compartments are lined with classic red and white gingham. There are two sets of plates, silverware, glasses, and cloth napkins. THE ONE BEFORE takes the gingham blanket and unfolds it over the table.

I'm pleasantly delighted as I take out the food, which, compared to my usual office meals, looks mouthwateringly fresh and appetizing: two sandwiches, two bunches of grapes, strawberries, a box of chocolates, two pieces of cheese, a carton of juice, and a bottle of red wine.

[![picnic.png](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/fragment:level-919-1/picnic.png/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-919-1/picnic.png)

THE ONE BEFORE immediately grabs one of the sandwiches and pours a glass of juice.

"Please, go ahead, dig in! Trust me, you'll need it."

I pick up a sandwich, nonplussed by how casually they're acting. Alas, I'm not one for small talk. After a few bites, curiosity takes over me.

"So, do you know the exit?"

THE ONE BEFORE is already finished wolfing down a sandwich, but pauses to answer before picking up the bottle of wine.

"Yes."

"What is it? Why can't I find it anywhere on the database? Is it dangerous?"

"Hm? No, no, nothing like that. Don't worry, I'll show you."

  
THE ONE BEFORE takes a long swig of wine and then, without a break, starts popping grapes into their mouth.

"Damn, you must be hungry. How long have you been here?"

"Days? Weeks? Hell if I know. That's why I'm telling you, eat up."

  
"If you know the exit, why haven't you updated the database? Actually, why are you still here and not left yet?"  

"I couldn't leave. Well, not until now, thanks to you!"

  
THE ONE BEFORE winks at you.

"I'm being serious here, this is very important information that nobody seems to know or remember. Do you lose your memory after exiting? Or are people purposely hiding it… for whatever reason? It makes no sense."

"Don't ask me, I wouldn't know. I'm still here. Guess I'll find out soon, but then I won't be able to answer your questions."

  
"Alright, so just tell me now."

They take the last handful of chocolates and chew slowly, appraising me.

Our gazes meet, unrelenting, as a tense beat passes.

"The reason I couldn't leave—even though I knew how to do so—is because I was the only one here. Because this level has a minimum capacity of one. Which means: it must contain at least one wanderer at all times and will not allow them to leave."

They lean in, their face growing serious.

"Until you arrived. Now if a second wanderer enters the level, then an exit becomes available for either of them to use—but not both. If a group of four enters, four exits appear, but there's already another person there, so somebody's bound to get left behind. You get my point?"

  
For a moment, I'm stunned into silence.

"What? I've never heard of something like that. How…?"

"Why would I lie? Although you are correct in a sense: I don't 100% know if this is the whole truth. This is simply what the guy who was here before me told me. And what *he* was told by the *previous* wanderer, et cetera et cete-"

  
"Wait. Wait wait, there's no way to… have two people use the same exit? I mean, how does that even work, you can't-"

THE ONE BEFORE shakes their head preemptively.

"Well, what if that one wanderer dies?"

"Can't happen. By whatever rules govern this level, it won't allow that one person in here to cease to exist."

  
"So when you arrived here, there was already someone else," I reason out loud, watching them nod along, "and they told you this exact script, and then left, leaving you here alone-"  

"Actually there was a group of 3 friends in my case, very nice folks, they were actually waiting together 'cuz they didn't want to leave one member behind-"

  
"And now if you leave, *I'll* be trapped here?!"  

"*When* I leave, yes, you'll be trapped here, like I was, until another wanderer shows up. And like me, you will distract them with the food and a bit of small talk, and then you will take your leave—but not before explaining it to them like I am explaining to you now."

  
THE ONE BEFORE raises an eyebrow.  

"Or, you could publish the truth about the exit on the database, tell everyone and their mom about it, and the next wanderer who arrives here will easily find it and no-clip out of here before you can even notice them through all the fog. People will pass by one after the other, while you remain unfairly stuck here, waiting to no avail for the world's loneliest picnic."

  
"What if I write it down on a note here, I have—I have a camcorder, I can literally record you no-clipping."  

"You think you can outsmart this reality? The fog resets everything. This is just how it works. You're welcome to try, but… there's a reason the GDP article is continuously being edited and unedited."

  
THE ONE BEFORE wipes their mouth with the napkin, chuckling.  

"It's an odd conundrum, but really, I think the solution is simple. Either one person gets left here forever, or: everybody takes their turn, waiting their due time until the next person shows up. No line skips. No fighting. No cheats."

  
THE ONE BEFORE stands up and brushes crumbs off their jacket. Their gaze is piercing.  

Watching them gather their things, I panic, still processing the information overload.

"Wait! Are you going? Are you leaving right now!? But I don't know the exit!"

"I'll show you right now."

"Ok, what is it??"

"Watch carefully. And why so stressed? You're about to find out. The next person will also find out because you will tell them. The only people who need to know are the ones who end up in here, and they will find out. Who cares if no one else in the fucking universe knows?"

I grip my camcorder halfway pulled out of my backpack, finger twitching on the record button.

"How long will I have to wait until someone new appears??"

"Long enough to convince you."

██ ████ ██ ███ ████ ███ ███ ██████ ██████ and in the blink of an eye, THE ONE BEFORE vanishes, passing their role down to me.

The haze of the fog grows thicker, more opaque, engulfing my view like a passing sandstorm. I try to emulate their actions, but I can barely see anything…  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
Then before I know it, the mist retreats.  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
The basket, the tablecloth, and every remainder of food have disappeared as well.  
  
  
  
  
  
It's just me, an empty picnic table, and the silent fog.
