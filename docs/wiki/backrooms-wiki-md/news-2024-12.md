Hello folks!

Happy new year! As we go into 2025, we want to recap not only the month of December, but all of the progress we've made in 2024! , , and hope you will follow along as we take you through a time capsule of 2024!

Please let us know if you have any suggestions for next month's newsletter! We're always looking for ways to improve (which you will see more of later). :3

# Secret Santa has ended!

---

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/balloony-tunes/JasovskaJaskyna2.jpg)

What better way to celebrate christmas than following two partygoers in a cave? Actually probably a lot of things but let's not worry about that.

Twenty people have made and recieved a gift for this year's installment of the [Secret Santa!](secret-santa-2024.md). Huge thanks to everyone who participated, and we hope you had fun writing or drawing a gift and enjoyed the one you got.

Out of all the gifts, two have even been uploaded on the site! They are [Balloony Tunes](balloony-tunes.md) and [Silent Knight](silent-knight.md), and more might be uploaded in the future!

A huge thanks again to all participants, and we hope to see you next year!

# Mass Trimming Progression

---

![Missing alt text.](http://reyday.wikidot.com/local--files/2024-news/Capture.PNG)

This is the disclaimer you will see on pages open for rewrite.

The mass trimming of old pages had a lot of progress and changes going on in the past few weeks.

So far, 4 pages have been trimmed, and 15 have been opened for rewrite. In addition, [Windows](entity-2.md), [Deathmoths](entity-4.md), [Jerry](entity-7.md), [Jerry's Room](trimmed__level-274--513663d0.md), [The Hive](level-276.md), [The Plane](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/trimmed:level-36-1) and [The Carrier](trimmed__level-17--e931cc57.md) are all being rewritten right now. You can find all of the information in the [Trimmed Pages Hub](trimmed__hub--ad64229d.md).

In addition, we also made a slight tweak in how pages are moved. Now, pages open for rewrite won't be moved into the trimmed: category, but kept in the main list with a note saying it's open for rewrite and a disclaimer at the top (see image). This should make them more noticeable to the public, leading to more applications. If you're interested by one, feel free to apply!

# Alternative Pages Hub

---

![Missing alt text.](http://scutoidbox.wikidot.com/local--files/level-4/level4basic.png)

This comes from an alternative Level 4 page!

This wiki is using the Creative Commons license, which means people are technically free to create their own takes on existing wiki pages. However, so far, only the [Desolation Canon](desolation-canon-hub.md) really took advantage of this.

To give people a place to upload their own takes on wiki pages, the [Alternative Pages Hub](alternative-pages-hub.md) has been created! There, you can find all pages tagged with the **alternate** tag, which are all articles that are different takes on pages in the main lists. Want to give it a shot? make sure you check the guidelines at the bottom of the page!

# Yearly Community Poll!

---

Like every year, we have our community poll for the year 2024! You can find it [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScOoN7Bxt48KhfQ7k_w-5xE6PlAv18dgPH6vA_DKLNujaMv4w/viewform).

Like every year, this is YOUR chance to give us your feelings and thoughts about what happened this year. 2024 had a lot of changes as a way to give the wiki a purpose and a direction. What do you think of everything? Do you have concerns? Do you have ideas to share? Now is the time to tell us!

The form only takes five to ten minutes to answer, and your answers will allow us to see what the general community, writers or readers, thought about this pivotal year for the wiki.

# We got another Rey stats section before GTA 6

---

Well, now that the year has ended, that begs the question: how much progress have we *actually* made with the site? Well, I have an answer for you stats nerds and Cluster I completionists!

In 2024, we got a whopping 178 new pages! Here’s how it was broken down over the course of the year:

- We got 80 new levels(59 normal levels, 16 sublayers, and 5 unnumbered)  
- We got 16 new entities(14 normal entities and 2 unnumbered)  
- We got 11 new objects  
- We got 9 new phenomena  
- We got 55 new tales  
- We got 3 new groups  
- We got 4 new POI’s  
- We got 5 new canons (though only 3 have hubs)

That is a heck of a lot of progress in Cluster I and lore development! Additionally, 9 pages were rewritten this year, 24 art pages were made, and we had 8 people post their author pages!

I can’t wait to see the progress we make in 2025. :3

Alongside these replacements, , , and have all made incredible progress with adding license boxes to older pages![2](javascript:;)

We have also changed the requirements for a POI/Character tag! In order to get a tag for your character, it must meet one of the following quotas without being too highly associated with an existing tag:  
1. Has 5 pages where the character plays a vital role in the page from 3 different authors(including yourself)  
2. Has 7 pages where the character plays a vital role in the page

With these new requirements, the following tags have been added:  
- [Janus](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/janus)  
- [Diana Evelynn](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/diana-evelynn)  
- [The Actor](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/the-actor)

If you know of a POI or a character that meets these requirements, please make a post in the [Tag Suggestion Thread](forum__t-16298236__tag-suggestion-thread--09c155bc.md) so that we can add the tag!

# Timeline of 2024

### January

- The canon hub was reworked by to look much nicer.
- Users sort themselves into groups as [canonicon](canonicon2024.md) begins.

### February

- [Traveling](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/traveling) and [incorporeal](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/incorporeal) attribute tags added.

### March

- [Unique](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/unique) attribute tag added.
- The Enigmatic category was replaced with unnumbered.
- Canonicon concludes with the aptly named Winning Team being the winning team.
- The Backrooms Wikidot turned 4 years old!

### April

- April Fools day saw the server be taken over by the French, along with a dictatorship. On site, the Backrooms time traveled back to the horror that is 2020.

### May

- After a long and seemingly impossible search, the location of the original Backrooms image was found!

### June

- A new posting policy was introduced to the site that changed the way clears work.
- Users look through levels to inspire them at the beginning of [sublayercon](slcon2024.md).

### July

- Sublayer con comes to an end, with and winning with [Level 422.1](level-422-1.md).
- [Space](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/space) attribution tag added.
- [The Reverence](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/the-reverence) group tag added.

### August

- The original Backrooms image, along with more from the same photoshoot, are [released](https://archive.org/details/backrooms-photos/807OREGON/DSC00158.jpg) under CC0 1.0.
- [A writing blitz](roots2024.md) is held sending us back to the site's roots with the creation of pages based around images from the original photoshoot.
- Art pages are introduced to the sits.
- [Tool](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/tool), [consumable](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/consumable), and [manufactured](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/manufactured) attribution tags added

### September

- Our first news page is posted to the site, written by .
- Users put pen on paper (or drawing tablets) to create the Backrooms Wikidot a [mascot](mascotartcon2024.md).
- Page of the week introduced, which showcases random pages to reignite interest in them.
- [Mechanical](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/mechanical) and [liquid](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/liquid) attribution tags added.

### October

- The spooky season brought with it prompts to inspire users with [Promptcon and Backtober](prompt2024.md).
- [Settlement](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/settlement) attribution tag created.
- Mascotcon was voted on and a winner was decided: [Tesseract](art__tesseract--7a9923e1.md), by and !
- [Church of the Veiled](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/church-of-the-veiled) and [Ariane Circle](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/ariane-circle) group tags created.
- We made an [official Backrooms Bluesky account](https://bsky.app/profile/ts.thebackrooms.wiki).
- The first crit night was hosted on the server, organised by .
- The GLMP server opened up to critics.

### November

- [The Macchina](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/the-macchina) entity tag added.
- The [Components Hub](components.md) is created by , making it much easier to find components created for the site.
- created a [linktree](https://linktr.ee/backroomswiki) for a convenient place to find our social media accounts.
- Trimming begins to rework outdated pages and remove pages that no longer hold up to modern standards.
- Promptcon is won by and with [The Jaws Of Emendation](the-jaws-of-emendation.md).

### December

- Users made gifts for each other in our [Backrooms Secret Santa](secret-santa-2024.md), organised by .
- The [Alternative Pages Hub](alternative-pages-hub.md) is created by , where users can write alternative versions of any page on site.
