Im the guy who said he’ll give you some critique. I might not be the best but here we go.

*“Entity 0 is the first level of the backrooms level 0 it is believed to be the first entity to exist; also it is not known how the entity came to be or if it had a different form. The origin of the entity is unknown but its believed that the entity was asleep and some sort of event wake him up but it is not known what that event was  
The entity talks to wanderers through telekinesis and the wanderers reported hearing echoes in their head while the entity talks to them the. The voice of the entity has been described to be different to every wanderer.”*

- ***“Entity 0 is the first level of the backrooms level 0”*** Sorry if this sounds harsh but this makes no sense. I think you wanted to say ‘Entity 0 is the first entity of the backrooms.’ There is no need for the ‘level 0’ after backrooms.
- After ‘backrooms’, before ‘it is’ there needs to be a full stop.
- From ‘it is’ to ‘event was’ the grammar is poor. You could reword this to ‘It is believed that **Entity 0** was the first entity to exist. How the entity came to be or if it can change form is unknown. The running theory is the entity lay in a deep sleep until an event occurred, waking the entity.’
- Talking to someone through their mind is called telepathy not telekinesis. Telekinesis is where you move things with your mind
- The sentence talking about telekinesis is long and confusing. Put a full stop after ‘telekinesis’ and then go straight to ‘wanderers reported’
- It would also be more grammatically correct to put ‘wanderers report’ rather than ‘reported’.
- The word ‘the’ is just randomly at the end of a sentence which shouldn’t be there.
- You are also looping around the same point. You don’t need to put ‘the wanderers reported hearing echoes in their head while the entity talks to them the.’ The idea of it using telepathy is inferred as this.
- ***“ The voice of the entity has been described to be different to every wanderer.”*** is worded in the most complex way possible. You should just say ‘The voice of the entity is different to every wanderer.

# On to behaviour

*“The entity's behaviour is unknown. The entity seems to have different behaviours depending on the sublevel. There have been many reports of the entity attacking the wanderers, but there have also been reports of the entity helping the wanderers. The entity has also been reported to ask waderers questions. The questions are mostly easy and harmless to, but there are reports of the entity sending wonderers to unsafe levels if they answer in a rude or disrespectful manner. The entity seems the answer any question a wanderer asks them from the reason of the life  
To when the world will end”*

- Instead of just saying everything about the entity is unknown you should state it’s behaviour. Even though it is perfectly fine to say the origin is unknown, we must know something about the behaviour. Otherwise how do we know it exists.
- Also straight after saying it’s behaviour is unknown you talk about how it acts on different sub-levels. Your article needs a smooth flow rather than to contradict itself every line.
- Straight after this you contradict yourself again by saying it attacks wanderers and it helps them. If you are going to give the entity juxtaposing traits you should make it so doing something makes it hostile or you have to interact with it a certain way and then it will help you. It cannot do both of these things at once, the same way it cannot be hot and cold in a room at once.
- For the last half of behaviour, about the questions, it is worded really poorly. ***“ The questions are mostly easy and harmless to, but there are reports of the entity sending wonderers to unsafe levels if they answer in a rude or disrespectful manner.”*** There is no need for you to say ‘harmless to’ as this sentence is already far to long. I would personally break it down to ‘The questions are mostly easy. There are reports of…’
- You spelt wanderers wrong. It should be an ‘a’ instead of an ‘o’
- Between ‘them’ and ‘from’ in the final sentence there should be a comma.

# Biology

*“Entity 0 seems to be an infinite room with yellow halls, buzzing lights, and moist carpet; it is not known if it's his true form. Entity 0 has mind-bending abilities and is believed to have telekinetic abilities as well, but that has not been confirmed. The entity can noclip wonderers into any level of the backrooms. This can happen randomly, and it can be any level of the backrooms, from very safe to very dangerous. The entity also has a physical but it has not been confirmed to be it's true form an just a form he can interact with wanderers entity's body is slender like body faceless,It floats in the air and is see-through.”*

- Instead of copying level 0 as your entities form, come up with something unique. The Backrooms is already currently an entity so maybe have it be some invisible force inside level 0 or a type of spore or something that lives in level 0 rather than actually being level 0.
- Also, I noticed that the entity doesn’t have a name and is referred to as **’entity 0’**. Im not sure is that’s intentional but please clear that up.
- If you are going to bring up true form I would explain what the true form is rather than just saying: ‘It is unknown if it is it’s true form.’
- Yet again wanderers has been spelt wrong. I would recommend finding a software or website to check spellings. At the bottom of this critique I will recommend and link some useful resources.
- ***“ This can happen randomly, and it can be any level of the backrooms, from very safe to very dangerous.’*** Personally I don’t think this sentence is needed at all as it repeats what has already been said in the previous sentence.
- This last sentence: ***“ The entity also has a physical but it has not been confirmed to be it's true form an just a form he can interact with wanderers entity's body is slender like body faceless,It floats in the air and is see-through.”*** Is far to long and confusing. It overlaps with what you described earlier and it brings up the concept of true form again. I do not understand what is being said in this sentence as a lot of random words are in places they don’t need to be and then there’s also words missing. For example it should be ‘Physical body’ not just “Physical”. I’m really not sure how to correct this part and would recommend a complete rewrite of this part.

# Discovery

*“ Entity 0 was discovered on October 20, 2023. It was discovered by a man named Mike Murphy. He found the entity when he heard voices and thought he was just going insane, but the voices talked to him and helped him get out of level 0. The man later reported it to the B.R.I., and they found out that level 0 is alive.”*

- ***“ Entity 0 was discovered on October 20, 2023. It was discovered by a man named Mike Murphy.”*** This can be shortened to ‘Entity 0 was discovered on October 20, 2023 by a man named Mike Murphy.’ There was no need to make this two sentences.
- As I said earlier The Backrooms is already an entity so we knew Level 0 was alive. Instead of just taking the concept of level 0, make Entity 0 its own entity.
- Also who are the B.R.I. I might be a bit behind on backrooms lore but I don’t believe I’ve ever heard of a group called the B.R.I.

# Sub-Level descriptions

### Level redrooms

The behaviors of the entity changes drastically, becoming more hostile, harmful, and rude. The entity has been reported to attack the wanderers. We don't know much about this form of entity because it is impossible to do research on it because it is way too hostile.

### Level 0.3/The icy rooms

The entity's behavior in this level is exactly like the behaviour on level 0

### level 0.2/remodeled mess

The entity acts similar to level 0, but with a little difference: the entity will get frustrated and mad if you don't answer it and will make the walls around the wanderers fall on them. This event is mostly harmless, but there have been reports of wanderers getting severe injuries.

### level Echo Chamber

The description of this entity is the same as the level

### note Level 0.1/zenith station isn't a part of the entity

- Starting with the red rooms and working down the list.
- ***“We don't know much about this form of entity because it is impossible to do research on it because it is way too hostile. “*** I feel this could be worded better as this sentence waffles a bit and doesn’t have good flow, making it hard to read. I would shorten it to: ‘We don't know much about this entity because it is difficult to research due to it being way too hostile.’
- For the second heading here there needs to be a full stop at the end of the sentence.
- For the third heading I would put just ‘frustrated’ instead of “frustrated and mad”. There is no need for both.
- ***“the entity will get frustrated and mad if you don't answer it and will make the walls around the wanderers fall on them. ”*** This sentence is far too long. I think it should be broken down into two by putting a full stop here‘…don't answer it. It will make the…’
- For the echo chamber, include it in the previous one if it is the same as the previous one.
- The note at the bottom does not need to be in x3 size. You should include it with the area you are describing or add it as a footnote.

***“Dr. ██████: You were attacked by an entity. We are here to***

So to explain collapsible text it is text that you can choose to fold up so it can show or hide text. The way this works is there will be a small piece of text; just like the one below:

[Show More](javascript:;)

[Show Less](javascript:;)

Now that you’ve pressed show more you can see this extra text. If you press show less above this text will disappear.

The way this is done is by typing the following code:

```
[[collapsible show="Show More" hide="Show Less"]]

Whatever text you want to be able to show or hide goes here.

[[/collapsible]]
```

When this is typed in it will turn out like this:

[Show More](javascript:;)

[Show Less](javascript:;)

Whatever text you want to be able to show or hide goes here.

You can also change the buttons from ‘show more’ and ‘show less’ by replacing those terms in the code for example you could write:

```
[[collapsible show="Extra Text" hide="I don’t want extra text"]]

Whatever text you want to be able to show or hide goes here.

[[/collapsible]]
```

So just changing what’s in the “speech marks” will change the buttons.

[Extra Text](javascript:;)
