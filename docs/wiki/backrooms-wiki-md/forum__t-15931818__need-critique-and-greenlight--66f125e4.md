My critique and greenlight

17 Jun 2023 14:41

I don't usually do negative scripts, because I can usually find something to work on, but I feel I have to make an exception. I will address my points in order of appearance:

- This is not the "Draft Critique" forum; it's the "Ideas and Advice" forum. Wrong spot to put this.
- You couldn't even put it on the critique forum because it's incomplete. This article is extremely bare bones, and while Zalgo text *looks* cool, I had a hard time reading it, and I love reading and trying to look through that corrupted filter. If I had a hard time reading it, then I can guarantee others won't be able to. Bring the chaos down.
- There is no information here. You need to actually have some story. Don't expect that just a few repetitions of the same phrase gives the level all the story it needs, actually put some meat on the bone.
- All in all, I'm pretty disappointed here. If you brought the chaos level down, it'd be just barely legible, while still keeping it's coolness. I would also add information here. I would wait until it's completely done before I ask for critique again.

I'm sorry if all of that seemed rude and beat down-y, but I'm just trying to help you out.
