Honestly I feel like this is written really well, there's no detail spared on the towns, I can believe the place's existence, it honestly sounds like a lovely place to live in lol. Plus I can tell *a lot* of effort went into crafting and making this believable. Which well done! It paid off!

My issue is that it's just… not Backrooms, I guess. And I know that sounds like something completely stupid, considering that The Backrooms as a whole does pretty much whatever it feels like (not a problem in my eyes) though there are still basic unspoken principles that are followed with a level. In my eyes, at least.

The whole thing just feels like a write-up for a fantasy world without the presence of magic or other races. Which would be great! If this was what the site is, which it's not. I'm not saying you couldn't pull off the idea of having a bunch of towns in one shared Level and have it really down-to-earth feeling, I just feel that this Level detaches itself so far from the usual tropes and conventions that even the most out-there format screws follow.

So is it good? Yes! Whole heartedly so! Genuinely nice work! :3. But is it Backroomy? Not really. I don't wanna downvote as this is good work, but I also don't wanna upvote for the reasons I've said above. Hence my choice to not vote at all

Hope you understand, and nice work on 132!
