**READ THEM.**  
  
"Read what?" You may ask. Well, the Page of the Weeks!  
  
"What are those?" You may also ask. Well Page of the Weeks are randomly chosen pages from anywhere on our site. New or old.

# Deletion Threshold III: Revenge of the authors

---

![Missing alt text.](http://reyday.wikidot.com/local--files/september-news/liberteeee)

The Backrooms Wikidot staff turns green and recycles their jokes.

As a result of numerous debates and polls and arguing and proposals, us staff folks have decided that we need to lower the threshold a bit. Instead of pages needing to have a rating of +15 to stay on the site(with the exception of tales), your page only needs +10 to stay on the site. Tales are remaining at +10 as well. On top of that, a page needs to have 3 downvotes at least in order to be deleted. Happy posting, folks!  
  
the system is, of course, still being monitored, and further changes in either direction are still possible depending on how it goes.

# Author Poll on Passive Collaboration

---

Passive Collaboration is a term showcasing when authors are deliberately using works from other people in their own, either to expand it or to make theirs feel more cohesive in the universe. We asked authors if and why they tend to do this, and it they avoided it, why.  
  
The reasons can actually be quite different from author to author, but the common denominator is an interest in the linked work, whether it is only one page or a wider background.  
  
Some authors like to include other works in order to make their own articles more intertwined within the big rabbit hole that is the wiki, allowing for the universe to feel interacting and cohesive (for example [Level 206 - "Bones of Bones"](level-206.md) by , with the many small mentions making the universe feel alive).  
  
Others want to feature works they appreciate for people to discover and enjoy them as a result, giving new opportunities to readers (for example The Lost Hall Society in [Level 5.3 - "Promethei Bibliotheca"](level-5-3.md) by ).  
  
Finally, authors can include works of other writers to sublime their own writing, by adding elements that either spark creativity (for example [Entity 67 - "Partygoers"](entity-67.md), by revolving around the [Phobic Centipede](entity-134.md)), or by adding more relevancy or potential to the page (for example the many destinations showcased in [Phenomenon 18 - "The Infinite Wanderer"](phenomenon-18.md) by ).  
  
However, some authors also stated why they might not passively collaborate as much as others. Since usually, mentioning or using other pages is tied to interest gathered after reading such articles, it can be harder for newer authors or people sticking to specific themes to mention other works. The other difficulty can be to avoid disrespecting the original author's vision and storyline in their writing, as it is not always possible to communicate with each other. Fortunately, elements such as the search features of the wiki, as well as events such as pages of the wiki, help cover such difficulties.  
  
Ultimately, passive collaboration narrows down to interest, both from the writer, as their appreciation of a topic or article can make them develop it, but also as a reader, by discovering new pages that might spark creativity.
