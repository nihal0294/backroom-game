16 Nov 2022 14:15

Okay, I'm confused about this… Why is the threshold for deletion at -1? To me, that seems a bit harsh(and a little perfectionist, in my opinion), especially since I see a lot of joke articles have a lower threshold than -1. I want to possibly suggest a system for deletion thresholds.

Possibly -10 or below for regular articles, -5 for enigmatic articles, -20 for joke articles, and -15 for Tales/Phenomena. I just don't see the -1 being very fair, especially to new writers.

That is gonna be way, way to much. -20????? The amount of articles that reach -20 is… extremely low.

Why does it increase for tales? That also seems illogical considering tales is the least voted on catagory.

-10 for regular catagories is also just too much. It used to be that articles were deleted at -5. It got changed to -1 with a 48 hour grace period before the deletion period begins.

About it not being fair for new writers…new writers aren't getting stuff published anyways. The gl system ensures new writers already need to spend tons of time to get an article out. Lowering the voting threshold, at least ATM, really only benefits GLE authors (until the gl system is altered, but when that happens lowering the voting threshold will cause an even bigger hit in site quality.

I consider this idea deplorable. Lowering threshold for this website is the opposite of what we need.

-1 and a 48 hour grace period is perfect, because it allows for one to quickly spot bad coldposts and overall bad quality posts. It doesn't take much time, nor can people battle to try and keep a bad article up for funny reasons or whatever. -1 means that if one extra person downvotes, the post's done for when it reaches an admin's eyes.

Those values are oddly specific too. Like, I'm 100% sure a single value for site-wide (sub?)pages to be far superior. Less brain activity.
