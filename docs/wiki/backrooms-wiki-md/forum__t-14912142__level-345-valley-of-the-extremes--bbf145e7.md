Re: Level 345- Valley of the Extremes

23 Jun 2022 05:18

I find this level quite interesting, but I noticed many things that could be improved upon. Don't take this negatively, I think the level has many opportunities.

First of all, the simplest thing is your SPaG+. You had a few "coding" errors (like "+" when you meant for text to be enlarged), and basic SPaG errors. I won't go over all of them, but make sure to reread and correct them.

Your clinical tone. I understand the fact that the majority of this article is written by the level's prominent entity, but when the entity attempts to be factual, a decent clinical tone should be used, and therefore needs work.

The Entity. The role of this entity is well explained: A highly powerful creature that can control the weather and conditions of this level, as well as life and death, particularly on this level. Why can't he just go to other levels and cause havoc if he can control these things and has an exit? What does he look like? Is their pronoun even he? This leaves a lot of the decision up to the reader, which is not a very good thing in this case. I personally pictured it as the pokemon Thundurus. If you could give more character to it, despite the level being written by the entity, that would be key to success.

Desc. I think the description is mediocre, but I can't really visualize the level very well. Use some more of that empty space to color it with detail, so the reader understands.

Wrap-Up: I genuinely think this level has potential for greatness. Whereas it is not near finished, I enjoy the unique idea of the level, the entity, and the mechanics. I also enjoy all stupidly dangerous levels >=), and the Sd class looked nice. Pm me with any questions. GL! -Iamalemon
