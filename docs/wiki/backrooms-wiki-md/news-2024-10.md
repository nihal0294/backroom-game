# The Revival of the GLMP

---

![Missing alt text.](http://reyday.wikidot.com/local--files/october-news/glows_in_the_darklogo.png)

New logo by !

Thanks to the work of , we recently had a complete overhaul of both the Critic Server and the Greenlighter Mentoring Program on Discord. Indeed, while the GLMP worked on training new greenlighters to help them make better critics and develop their literacy skills, the Critic Server has been left in the dust as a consequence, which didn't allow for most people to be able to help each other and learn by seeing other people's advice.  
  
Therefore, the two servers have been merged. While greenlighters still have places where they can vote on features or rewrite perms, as well as training people who want to become greenlighters, the system is now much more public, and critics can also join to help each other and learn how to give more helpful advice to people.  
  
In addition, we also have a new role on the discord server, called "Star Critic". It can be earned by being nominated by a staff member or a greenlighter for someone who demonstrates exceptional activity in both the discord and the site forums. Star Critics are able to discuss and vote for the monthly featured pages, so if that is something that appeals to you, try and leave an impression!  
  
If you want to join the program, you can go to our Discord server in the "meet the critics" channel and create a critic profile there. Once done, a staff member will give you the pingable critic role, which will grant you access to the server.  
  
We hope to see you there!

# Promptcon is happening!

---

![Missing alt text.](http://reyday.wikidot.com/local--files/promptcon/prompts)

The prompts in question:

With the end of an art contest comes the last writing contest of the year! And this time, we're making something a little different. For almost three years we have tried to make contests bring light to new categories (like [phenomecon2023](phenomecon2023.md), or underused categories (like [canonicon2024](canonicon2024.md)) and themes (like [classicon2023](classicon2023.md)). However, Promptcon completely shatters this habit. As a parallel of Backtober, authors have to, this time, write an article using at least one of the 31 prompts of the event, and are encouraged to use more than one in order to challenge themselves.  
  
We are currently in the posting period, and there is a week left before posting! So if you wanna participate, remember to post your entry using the **prompt2024** posting password November 15th 2024!  
  
Whether you participate in the contest or not, we would also love to hear your thoughts about the theme! Should prompts have been more specific, or maybe a minimum should have been present? or should the contest have had a more defined and cohesive theme? Let us know on our [discord](https://discord.gg/yBFwugQ) by creating a community feedback thread!

# Reading Nights

We did these. They're fun, join 'em.  
  
What we do in these Reading Nights is basically, well, reading! We all hop in a VC and read the same page, then discuss them. Currently we're trying to do multiple for both American timezones (PST-EST) and then also other times for people on the other side of the globe (like for Europeans). # Rey’s nerdy stats or whatever

---

If you know me, you know I love numbers. My favorite page is probably [How Many Pages](how-many-pages.md), and with the encouragement of a suggestion from last month's newsletter, Prae and Robot have made the mistake of dedicating an entire block to my findings. How fun!

In the later weeks of the month, I sent out a survey to collect some data about our site's activity demographic and to predict how much progress would be made with Cluster 1[2](javascript:;) in the next year. Here's what I've found:

- 46.4% of people who filled out the survey were authors[3](javascript:;)
- 70.4% of people who filled out the survey plan on posting a level in the next year.
- There are about 78 predicted numbered levels coming out in the next year.
- There are about 24 predicted numbered entities coming out in the next year
- There are about 163 predicted objects coming out in the net year[4](javascript:;)
- There are about 20 predicted phenomena coming out in the next year.

A part of the survey was dedicated to category popularity as well. Users were asked to rank the categories from favorite to least favorite in regards to how much they liked to read them. The average rankings are as follows:

1. Levels - 2.89  
2. Objects - 3.22  
3. Phenomena - 3.74  
4. Entities - 3.89  
5. Groups - 4.52  
6. Tales - 5.37  
7. Joke Pages - 5.85  
8. POI's - 5.93

Concluding this section, while unrelated to the survey, these are the total pages posted this month:

- 5 levels have been posted (including translations and unnumbereds)
- 1 (rewritten) entity was posted
- 1 (translated) object was posted
- 1 group was posted
- 1 tale was posted
- 8 art pages were posted
