Still kind of sad the old level is gone since it made it into that one american horror stories episode but this is definitely much more interesting.  
I love how the level shifts around like a puzzle. It’s a unique way to prevent any long term establishments while still having a “safe level”. I think you absolutely nailed the aesthetic of peaceful-creepy that the backrooms thrives off.  
There wasn’t a ton of descriptive details about the actual rooms of the levels which isn’t that bad since the properties surrounding it are very interesting but I would like to see more.  
The name also doesn’t work too well for me. Since this is a hard to access restaurant-esque level, the name “pit stop” feels disconnected.  
Overall a good read! I can already see some cool sublevels taking advantage of the unique properties of this level.
