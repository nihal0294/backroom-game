Level 209 - Ol Chippy

23 Nov 2022 20:28

Hey all, I've already made a partially complete article on a google doc before joining the wiki, but felt like I should at least get someone else's thoughts on the concept before attempting to get greenlit.

**Page Type: Level**

**Genre: Mystery**

**Core Concept:** A level primarily made up of a long reaching street corner (L-Shaped), lined with English-style Fish & Chip stalls. They are ran by Adult Facelings, which become violent if any form of violence occurs on the level's street. The outskirts of the street are surrounded by a thick black fog. Those who go beyond the fog disappear temporarily before reappearing with vague threatening memories of what happened, with no physical effect on their body.

**Additional Notes:** I'm not entirely sure if the idea is completely original, and I don't know if the English theming of the level would resonate.

So why would the adult facelings become violent just because they see violence on the streets? Aren't they "busy" running the stalls? Also, are they the only entities on the level? What makes it interesting?

I think you could better expand on the concept of the fog and what's in it if you want to make it a mystery genre level. Make it to where there is some kind of entity producing the fog or in it, and by taking in accounts of people who went into the fog of what they can recall, slowly piece together what it might look like, what it's goals are, etc.

Good luck!
