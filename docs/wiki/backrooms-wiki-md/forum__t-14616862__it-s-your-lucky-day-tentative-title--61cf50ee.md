Basically, I drafted a tale about a guy who lands in [Level 5.1](level-5-1.md) not knowing anything about the Backrooms and gambles his life away - literally. The tale is single-part, but may be fairly long to read. I'm going for a tone of dark humor and horror, and need help with making sure that tone is consistent and doesn't get too formal for a snarky New Yorker protagonist. Also, since most of the characters' dialogue is color-coded, I'd like to know if all of it is still readable.

Thank you for your time and any feedback you give.
