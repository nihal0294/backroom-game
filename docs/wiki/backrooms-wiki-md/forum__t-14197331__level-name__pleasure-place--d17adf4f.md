Level name: "Pleasure place"

15 Sep 2021 04:56

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre (Optional):** Horror, comedy

**Page Layout (Optional):** The page will begin with explaining the overall layout and anomalous effect(s) of this level, followed by a letter/message to MEG in regards to a person who went missing for weeks, but returned in an extreme mental state: this person is reported to take pleasure in grotesque and harmful actions, such as cutting oneself and dislocating bones. Then, the "control room" will be explained with an extract from a radio conversation between two MEG operatives.

**Elevator Pitch:** What is the basic core idea? Use a bullet point list if necessary.  
A Complex full of offices, bedrooms and random rooms that include: chiropractor clinics, doctor clinics, rooms full of chairs (decorated with streamers and party equipment), and rooms full of strange 'medieval-like' equipment.

This level includes a great amount of facelings whom experience pleasure from strange actions such as cracking or even breaking their back, being stabbed, being decapitated, ect. These facelings can somehow laugh and emit sounds of joy, despite having a face, after having their head taken off or being harmed.

By staying in this complex for a prolonged period of time, subjects slowly behave similar to the facelings in this level. The level is made of Four sections, given the names: "Pleasure rooms", "Pleasure clinics', "Pleasure party", and "The control room". MEG operatives recently discovered this room which is heavily guarded, and a operative, before dying, reported that he saw a large light blue wall room full of monitors and strange machinery. Some images were able to be captured and sent before the manifestation of several Partygoers and unique facelings

**Central Narrative:** Essentially this level gives rise to the possibility of the backrooms' effects being controlled by operatives or at least, this certain level, being able to be controlled if enough people held off the entities within the "control room"

Just an idea. Share some comments. thansk
