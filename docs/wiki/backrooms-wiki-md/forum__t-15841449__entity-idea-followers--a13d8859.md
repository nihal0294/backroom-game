Entity Idea-Followers

03 May 2023 20:01

**Page Type:** Entity

**Core Concept:** A follower is an entity that takes the resemblance of a regular human being, usually one that it has seen. Followers do exactly as their name suggests, they follow you, at your pace, only a few steps back. Followers **will not** physically attack/harm you **unless** it is triggered by some stimulant, this stimulant varies depending on the level. It could be something as simple as turning on a light, to something more complex like saying a specific word.  
Although Followers will not generally attack you, it can still kill you through your very mind. If a wanderer begins to think about the follower for too long, the wanderer will begin to feel a growng sense of paranoia, eventually leading to the wanderer either dying from shock/cardiac arrest, or the wanderer making an attempt on their own lives.  
It is also important to note tat Followers will not pursue you forever, because as soon as it spots another person, or even just a living being (eg. an animal) the entity will instead pursue said animal.  
As for the Followers themselves, they are incapable of speech and will not show any understandable form of communication, body language or otherwise. They are also impossible to kill. Their skin can be penetrated and bruised, but they do not die, especially since attacking one makes you think of it, or could even be the stimulant, both of which would result in your death before the follower's even if it could die. Followers also have inhuman regeneration properties, taking mere seconds to patch a wound, and short of a minute to regrow a body part. These entities look and walk like humans, but they should never be confused with one, for they will not communicate with a wanderer or even react o anything with any facial expressions. (they wear clothes by the way I felt I had to mention that)

**Additional Notes:** I dont really have a solid nam for them yet. My three ideas were:  
Stalkers  
Followers  
Friendly Followers

Please feel free to give as much critique as your heart desires. I'm pumped to release some article on this site and need all the assistance I can get.

Thank you for reading!

Admittedly speaking, entities that are humanoid are a tad cliche. They're like NPC's from a video game for the most part, just kind of- well as the name suggest, follows you.

The immortality thing is iffy, generally, I would try and do something that makes this entity stand out from the mold. You don't have to do something grandiose but just something to catch the attention of the reader.
