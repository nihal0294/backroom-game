**Level 989** is located in a house with 8 Big rooms, The House have a kitchen, bathroom, 2 bedrooms, living room, attic,  
Guest room and a Library

# **Description**

This level is a very hard one, if a entity sees you **DONT MOVE STAY STILL.** Since entities here is blinded by some wanderers, There's a theory that if you move and get caught, there's a chance you will become a statue in a random level

Every rooms has a kit in it to keep you alive and if it runs out, it will restock itself every 2 days so you need to save up some food.

The entities here will explore the house and if they're in your room don't move stay still. But if they're far from you you can go ahead and collect some supplies and try to find the exit.

•"**HELP!** I've been stuck here for hours, It takes them 30 minutes to get out of the room! Please, **HELP M-**"

•"Okay… I've been in this place for 5 hours. And I think I give up, I will fight them. Fight them till death if you see this.. My Fellow Humans… **Let's Roll.**

•"I'm In Here… but at what cost..? I finally finished this level… For what.. **Nothing.** …"

The level itself also has water in it for you to stay hydrated. But I don't reccomend sleeping in this level.

# **Entities**

### **Skin Stealers**

Level 989 is full of skin stealers and don't worry! These skin stealers can't detect you If you don't move and just stay still. They will go to your room your in for like 3 Hours and 30 minutes it's actually just easy to not get caught by these all you need to do is after they leave the room they were in you just need to go in there but be careful to not be too early and enter a room while they are searching for you.

### **Insanitiy**

Unlike the skin stealers there is only 1 insanity, But dont be so confident about it These Insanities are not that hungry but if it sees you **Run.** Keep running, Don't look back till you find a hiding spot. And it's the closet there's no entities here so you can always hide here if a Insanity found you in this closet… **Give up.**

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There's currently no bases, outpost and communities in this level

# **Entrances And Exits**

### Entrances

•You can get here by noclipping into a building in level 11  
•You can also get here by noclipping into the wall in level 155  
•You can get here by crashing into one of the wall in level 69

### Exits

•You will see a door with a sign exit in the living room but it's locked all you need to do is reach the attic while skin stealers and a Insanity is trying to find you after you reach the attic all you need to do is find a case with a note "Don't touch" and inside it is the key to the exit
