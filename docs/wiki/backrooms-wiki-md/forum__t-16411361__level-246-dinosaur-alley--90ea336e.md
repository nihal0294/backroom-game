PenOrSaber1,

I am truly sorry that you found this article so frustrating. Some of it is written that way by design.

1.) **Everything here suggests artificiality.**

Artificiality is defined as *the quality of being made or produced rather than occurring naturally.* That red sun does not rise or set; night and day turn on and off like a ceiling light fixture. Every fifth morning, a gentle rain falls from a cloudless sky for exactly three hours. Later, we learn that every tree on the middle layer is exactly like every other tree. The place is real, but not natural.

2.) **It is impossible to ascend these plateaus by climbing the perilous outer cliffs because daytime contact with the exposed bedrock has a detrimental effect on the consciousness of the mountaineer.**

The red sun generates an effect upon stone. The size of the stone dictates this effect. Coming into contact with limestone cliffs, etc during the day will render a wanderer unconscious. You can only no-clip through the stone pillars during the night cycle. Physical contact with the stones of “Stonehenge 2.0” under the blood red sun of the day cycle results in electrical shock and temporary unconsciousness. This is not a matter of “mental health,” but of physical consciousness. Yes, you can climb the cliffs at night. Or simply use the extensive network of tunnels created for your convenience.

3.) **"Time travel," in a manner of speaking, is accomplished by traversing this temporal landscape.**

Every inch of terrain here is linked to certain years in the Frontrooms. Footsteps taken in any direction — forward or back, left or right — can move the wanderer to a new chronological position where so-called erratics (items and lifeforms) from that moment in time can appear. The land itself is anchored to moments in time in the Frontrooms.

4.) **The middle plateau targets human existence within mere decades or centuries (past and future) of the present.**

Target can also mean “select as an object of attention.” The middle plateau targets (or reflects or echoes) the Age of Humans.

5.) **The Community Plateau is the only layer on this level where human habitation is feasible.**

Dinosaurs in the basement (bottom layer), cryptids in the attic (top layer)! Feasible means "possible to do easily or conveniently."

6.) **Like the witch in The Wizard of Oz, if a house (or ship or plane) falls on you, you will die.**

Like the highway signs that say WATCH FOR FALLING ROCK, (these signs are used in canyons, valleys, or mountains where rocks are prone to randomly stumble down across your way,) you have to watch the sky to see where a humongous object materializes, if a blue glow appears, once in a while. Erratics are Frontrooms objects that “bleed” into the Backrooms. Some small. Some large.

7.) **The Backrooms never failed to give Alice in Wonderland a run for the money.**

I triple-checked this and “a run for THE money” is absolutely, positively correct. “Its” is also correct. Why are we even discussing this?

8.) **Without warning or fanfare, the door opened, and a goddess emerged, trailing a hint of fragrance in her wake.**

A goddess is defined as “a woman who is adored, especially for her beauty.” Alayna is a goddess, because of her beauty.

9.) **Anne immediately felt her astral self shooting through the celestial tunnel to the cosmic rendezvous beyond time and space.**

Like astral projection, Anne’s “astral self” is her soul (or spiritual essence) leaving her body to attend this meeting with the entity who takes the form of Jimmy Stewart playing the role of George Bailey. The meeting cannot happen on a physical level because the entity is “between incarnations” or “of the spirit.”

10.) **Where are the humans of tomorrow? Why is this whole thing crossed out?**

Ha! Not telling. No! Not telling.
