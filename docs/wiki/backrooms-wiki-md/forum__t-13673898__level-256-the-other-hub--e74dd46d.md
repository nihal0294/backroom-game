Level 256 - The Elevators

25 Aug 2020 21:13

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre (Optional):** Undefined

**Page Layout (Optional):** The exact template used in a typical Level article

**Elevator Pitch:** Level 256 is a series of never-ending claustrophobic corridors similar in appearance to a modern office building. Level 256’s unique characteristics are its intense density of elevators in the corridors in said level. Elevators in Level 256 can transport visitors to multiple levels of the Backrooms. Level 256 is inhabited by Hounds, Skin-Stealers, Insanities and an entity exclusive to Level 256 nicknamed “Climbers”

**Central Narrative:** None

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** The first level to be directly related to elevators.

**Additional Notes:** If there are any mishaps in this concept are found, please notify me.
