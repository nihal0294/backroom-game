First of all, i chose this theme since it's very similar to the base wikidot theme — it's meant to be an example of an acceptable use of component:css-revert, and a base for more simplistic themes (themes that don't want flashy bhl elements) to work off of. This site looking like scp but edited with a few set tweaks has been how it's looked this whole time — actually, it started off by just straight stealing Black Highlightyear theme from scp before it was edited to be backroomsy.

---

second of all, with nuliminal, the scp logos are tiny and i've been removing them pretty steadily. all of them being gone WASN'T a criterion for us releasing the new theme, we'd spend nearly a year on it so we wanted to get it out, but almost all page bottom options that had scp references are replaced with backrooms logos, and the sidebar scp logos are gone, and the search magnifying glass' little scp ridges are so tiny and unnoticeable that i never even noticed them until they were pointed out to me (unless your comment is just that it shouldn't look like a magnifying glass and then— that's literally the symbol for searching across the entire web)

idk what you mean by high-tech looking elements of the theme, but they're also on wanderer's library lmao. and this site is still, even though it isn't **the m.e.g.'s database** anymore and it's certainly not **the scp foundation** database, it is still a database.

edit: or, well, it's generally construed to be a database, a lot of pages refer to it as either the m.e.g. database or the general public database. and and and anyway you are looking at it through the lens of a wiki. almost all visual effects in the theme are just less janky looking versions of effects that were in the old version of the theme anywho
