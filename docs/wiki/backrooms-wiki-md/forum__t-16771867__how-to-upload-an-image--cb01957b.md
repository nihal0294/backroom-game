*Easy!*

Click the "File" button at the bottom of your page, then click upload an image. The first bar is to upload an image or photo from your gallery, the second bar is to change the name of the uploaded file (highly recommended) and the third bar is to add a description to the image (not recommended).

Then copy and paste the link to your already uploaded image file. When you click edit, enter the link in this section:

```
[[include component:image-block
|name= Filename here!
|caption=Level 0 is the first level of the Backrooms
|align=right
]]
```

*I hope it helped you!*
