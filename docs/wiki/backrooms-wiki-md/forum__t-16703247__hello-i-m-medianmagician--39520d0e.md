Hello!

I've been interested in Backrooms content, and especially stuff on the Wikidot, for a couple years now. I finally decided to create an account and join. I have a few ideas that I'd like to contribute, and may end up writing some of them at some point, but that depends on how much free time I have (I'm a uni student and it can get pretty busy at times).

I also tried writing a SCP a few years ago. Unfortunately I had to give up because I just didn't have enough free time for it, but hopefully things go better for me on this wiki. And I'm pretty familiar with the greenlighting process since it's similar to the SCP Wiki.

I had ideas for a level, an entity, and an object, but unfortunately I discovered that my level and object ideas are very similar to articles that already exist, which just leaves the entity. (The level idea may still be salvageable at some point though).

Just one question. Getting greenlit on the SCP Wiki required two greenlighters iirc, but on the Backrooms Wikidot it seems like you only need one greenlighter. Is this correct?

Hello, welcome to the Backrooms wiki. I am (as a nickname) the most active user on the wiki, since the others use *Discord*, but I can't. Anyway, regarding your question, yes, you only need one **Greenlight** to be able to publish an article on this site. If you need anything else you can ask me or the staff members as well.

*Don't worry about your studies, I understand that it is very difficult. But with effort you can keep going.*

*Don't worry if some ideas of levels, entities or objects are repeated. On the wiki there are already some articles about space/universe, hospitals, fields, etc. In fact, a recent article of mine about an entity that is a very bizarre planet has to do with space. I would recommend reading some primary articles (from [level 0](level-0.md) to [level 99](level-99.md) or [level 100](level-100.md)), so that you get an idea. And when you want to create an atmosphere for a level, some that I recommend (which do give an eerie atmosphere) are [Level 18.1](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-18-1) and [Level 854](level-854.md).*
