Greetings, Kailen. I was pretty intrigued about this, more so than I thought I would be. Down below is my critique, you may want to seek out some of this site's guides in order to improve what you wrote, I feel like it'd really benefit you.

[+ Critique](javascript:;)

[- Critique](javascript:;)

The inspiration from the early levels is pretty obvious here, and the idea of it seems like it would fit in pretty well with them. That being said, this looks extremely unfinished and unpolished, certain sections lack any real grammar and seem very out of place when compared to everything else written. I've linked some pages about grammar down below that've been provided by this site:

<http://theoatmeal.com/comics/apostrophe>  
<http://grammar.about.com/od/punctuationandmechanics/a/punctrules.htm>  
<https://www.quickanddirtytips.com/education/grammar/how-to-use-commas-a-summary>

The idea of this level isn't exactly new, it's an infinite version of a place common in real life. The atmosphere set up with the image in the first part of this page was actually pretty cool, but the second part of the page strayed really far from what I think you were trying to achieve. A dark, mysterious and creepy hallway is scary until it's no longer dark and mysterious. Even so, the idea of having to be asleep by the time the lights go out is actually pretty interesting, it adds a slight layer of depth in the safe sections which I really enjoy.

The idea of the books having effects on you was actually really interesting, though I feel like it could be expanded upon a lot more. The random words in the book is pretty cool, but could be shown better through improved formatting. You should really try to add on to them, I'd say having a reason for people to read the book would give a reason for people to not just ignore them. Maybe they could have a chance of healing you, producing almond water, letting you exit the level or something of the sort would be nice, instead of only negative effects.

As it is now, it's a pretty lackluster page, but you have a small piece of something that could be cool. Expand on the interesting parts and try to improve your grammar, and your page would be a lot better. 4/10.
