# Integration Point Symphony 2

# Interlude

---

You find yourself in a strange, blue room. There's no furniture, no decoration, no features to speak of, only four walls, a ceiling and a floor, all in the same shade of crystal blue. Upon further inspection, you can see ripples in the center of the wall in front of you, as if it were fluid. You try to touch it, but your hand phases through. Looking around, the same is true for the other three walls…
