Sub-levels (I'm assuming that is what you mean) don't count as rooms. Sub-levels aren't considered large enough to be their own levels but are too big to be seen as rooms. To continue, whilst sub-levels are normally related to the level in mind in terms of lore or form, rooms are straight up inside the level, and are usually vastly different in concept in comparison to their level. Both of these things, nonetheless, are usually made for areas that are more significant in lore and/or are larger.

Of course, you can choose to keep these rooms in your level article. But it does expand the content you make by separating them into their own articles, plus it makes it easier to read your article by going over the most relevant (or, by what I jus said earlier, less relevant).
