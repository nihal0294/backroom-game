Ok, so, this is basically just copying something that exists in real life and relabelling it to fit on the wiki. I will be the one to say it; **that makes this unoriginal.**

There is also nothing to this level — and I don't mean in terms of the environment. The page lacks any significant lore, substance, and is even mentioned in your own writing to be too similar to Level 7.

With a concept like this, you cannot only go for a physical description of the level. Since it is lacking much in terms of physical environment, you will need to make it feel important somehow. Connecting it to the rest of the Backrooms lore and adding links (I don't mean URLs) to groups or people will be beneficial in this regard.

I have two pages that you should read to try and get a more unique idea than "copy of the Pacific Ocean", and see if you come up with a better concept on your own.  
[First page](phenomenon-24.md).  
[Second Page](blue-channel.md).
