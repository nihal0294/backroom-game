I'm significantly late, but I'm going to give both some critique and suggestions on this.

For the entity recommendation: mangrove trees are known to be much more mobile than other flora. They have been recorded moving towards more suitable conditions in several cases. My idea is that an entity could be the trees themselves, not being openly hostile towards humans but being capable of fighting if they feel threatened. Perhaps they could drag people underwater with their roots.

Now, I can tell that this level is unfinished (judging by some sentences cutting off) but I have some other suggestions nonetheless:  
For different zones, it doesn't necessarily have to be "the first zone is safe, the second zone is dangerous, and the third zone is lethal". It is entirely possible to have different zones be purely defined by variations in terrain, temperature, stability, etc. For the last one, maybe it could relate to things appearing in different places to where they actually are; this effect could intensify in different zones, making navigation more difficult.  
Additionally, there could be attempts made to find this "G" person. Whether they succeed or fail, it doesn't really matter as long as you do it well (although, it will much more likely be enjoyed if they are successful).

Now, a few things that are mandatory to fix:  
You need to decide a number at some point, although that's quite obvious.  
Add licensing information about the picture of the level in the "Licensing / Citation" section.  
"MEG" should be "M.E.G".  
Rather than "was quickly made apparent", "quickly became apparent" works better.  
"near at" implies two different locations at once. Remove either of the two words.  
Since the logs are written by an in-universe person, I'm not going to comment on whatever writing style they choose.  
Semicolon after "interactions are normal", rather than a comma. Additionally, "it may have a random chance" should be something like "it has a random chance". Either it has a random chance, or it doesn't.  
Remove "however" from "exits, however, due" make it two sentences, the first ending at "exits".

This is all for now.
