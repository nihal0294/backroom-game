# **Description**

This level is thought to be a video in its entirety. The creator of **the Video** is unknown; however, it may be found on one of [the End's computer's](level-601.md) folders. When someone opens up **the Video**, they will get teleported to the level. While the wanderer is stuck in it, the computer can be utilized by other explorers in [the End](level-601.md) to interact with the wanderer in the level.

[+ Video options rules](javascript:;)

[- Hide video options](javascript:;)

- If someone enters **the Video** while someone else is in it, the original occupant will vanish. This makes it impossible to make bases in the level.
- If **the Video** is paused, the person in the level along with the entire level will freeze until **the Video** is unpaused.
- If it is slowed down, the wanderer will walk slowly and their body may ache.
- If **the Video** is sped up, everything will get worse, for going back to the minute the wanderer was is impossible.
- If one closes **the Video**, the wanderer that was present in it will disappear.

  
This level consists of a medium-sized city that includes shops, restaurants, hotels, and other structures. This city has anything that can be found in a normal city in [the Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md). Houses are rare in this city and little is known about them; those who have visited them have never returned.

This city also has [facelings](entity-9.md) with the "The Level 11 Effect". Both communication and interaction with these [facelings](entity-9.md) is impossible. Cars are also present throughout the city and follow the street plan normally. However, they will always ignore wanderers who cross their path.

One can also find [almond water](object-1.md) throughout the level, but it is not recommended to ingest, as it is unsafe for consumption. Given the level’s nature as a video, it has a definitive time limit of 5 minutes. **The Video** gets increasingly dangerous with every passing minute; thus, it is imperative that the wanderer finds an exit as soon as possible. The following document explains what happens in **the Video**:

# **Less than 1 minute:**

The level is safe to explore at this time, but an inconspicuous [faceling](entity-9.md) will begin to follow the wanderer. One should not interact with or talk to this [faceling](entity-9.md) and move forward peacefully.

# **1 minute:**

The skin of the [faceling](entity-9.md) following the wanderer will turn white. A warped smile will develop on the wanderer’s face, their arms and legs will become thinner, and [hounds](entity-8.md) with “The Level 11 Effect” will start to appear on the streets.

# **2 minutes:**

The [faceling](entity-9.md) following the wanderer will progressively distort, glass fragments are scattered on the floor, the colors of the traffic signals will change to black, and the city will appear noticeably dirtier.

# **3 minutes:**

The level will get darker with light sources being a rarity. Disturbing noises will occasionally be heard, and normal [facelings](entity-9.md) will hardly be seen. The [faceling](entity-9.md) chasing the wanderer will become increasingly distorted and move faster.

# **4 minutes:**

The [faceling](entity-9.md) pursuing the wanderer becomes even more hostile, and fleeing from it will be a requirement for the remaining time. The environment begins to deteriorate, with fires and collapsed structures being common. Additionally, bloody bodies of [facelings](entity-9.md) and [hounds](entity-8.md) appear throughout the level. Lastly, the cacophony of disturbing noises becomes omnipresent and threatens the mental health of the wanderer.

# **5 minutes:**

As said above, **the Video** stops at 5 minutes and the wanderer in the level will vanish.

# **Entrances and Exits**

### Entrances

Clicking on a video called "test.mp4" on one of the folders in [the End's computer.](level-601.md)

### Exits

Attempting to no-clip through a large, translucent wall on a building has a slight chance of sending the wanderer back to [the End.](level-601.md)
