02 Jan 2022 21:30

##### **NAME:** Kevin

##### **KNOWN AFFILIATIONS:** Pies

##### **LAST KNOWN LOCATION(S):** Nobody fucking knows.

---

Description:  
**Kevin** appears to be around the age of 10, and is often spotted around- well that's the problem. This kid somehow transports from levels that don't even correlate. [The M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md) has tried tracking him but it didn't work because this little dipshit idiotic fucking- he seems to be untrackable. If spotted, he often will take out a pie, and slam it into the victims face. Sometimes even taking multiple pies and throwing them around the room at multiple people, as well as setting up elaborate traps and schemes that all end in a pie to the face. At the moment [The M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md) has over 8000 reports of him slamming pies in peoples faces. Where he gets these pies is unknown, however rumour has it he sold his soul to the devil for an infinite supply of pies.

Behaviors:  
Kevin has a very interesting sense of humor, which he likes to display often. Usually by slamming pies in peoples faces, and then yelling rather, *ungentlmanly* things. Recently, [[[The M.E.G did catch him, for a short ammount of time.

# Radio Transmission, Log ID: 3907

The following is a radio transmission between The M.E.G and 2 agents of The M.E.G.

## LOG START ID: 3907

Jessica Ryans: This is Jessica. I'm here with Alex on [Level 3](level-3.md). Reporting for the mandatory check-in.  
M.E.G.: Copy that. password.  
Alexander Azeil: Shloopum  
M: Copy Alex, status.  
J: We believe we're nearing it. \*Long pause\* All the strange sightings in the area have to lead to something!  
\*30 minutes pass\*  
M: Status?  
A: I see another room. There appears to be something in it.  
J: Perhaps we should check it out?  
A: Okay, but we must proceed with caution.  
\*pause\*  
M: Status?  
J: We're here.  
A: There's some sort of pedestal.  
M: Is there something on it?  
J: We're not sure… We're nervous to approach it.  
A: It's very… bright.  
J: We're approaching it.  
\*pause\*  
A: Is that…?  
M: What do you see?  
J: I think we've stumbled upon some… It appears to be… Are you sure this is where the clues lead?  
A: It definitely is.  
M: So you found it?  
A: We believe so.  
M: Take it, but be cautious.  
\*Long pause… sudden screaming\*  
M: What's happening?  
J: I- I DON'T KNOW, ALEX JUST REACHED OVER AND GRABBED IT THEN THE CEILING OPENED UP A- AND NOW THERE ARE PIES.  
M: pies?  
A: EVERYWHERE. HELP.  
M: We'll send someone.

## LOG END ID: 3907
