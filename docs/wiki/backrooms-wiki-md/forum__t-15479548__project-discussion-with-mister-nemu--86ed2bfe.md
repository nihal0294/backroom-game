Project discussion with Mister Nemu

25 Dec 2022 19:46

As of currently, no changes have been made to basic idea.

**OBJECT NUMBER**: 000

**DESCRIPTION**: Object 000 is a stone tablet of unknown origin, made of what appears to be granite. The object is seemingly indestructible, and all radiation except thermal radiation simply bounces off of it. An inscription is always present on Object 000, determined to be backlog. The inscription changes depending on unknown factors[1](javascript:;). It has been observed to change based on the intent of anyone holding it, but this is not always the case, changing when no one is around to do so. It's believed to have a possible sentience, but due to a of lack testing, it is only a speculation.

---

**DISCOVERY**: The origins of Object 000 are entirely unknown, and no record of how it came to the possession of the M.E.G. exists.

---

This is by no means a draft at this time. Grammar errors may be overlooked as I am not worried about it untill it becomes an actual draft.

Hello.  
Time to get started.  
After many tests, i think i found a cool concept for the object.  
Imagine, upon touching the stone, the wanderer mysteriously vanishes and reappear some times later.  
A trial of the soul happens here.  
As the memories of the trial are wiped out, the wanderer only know whether he failed or not.

The prize is also unique in its own sense.  
A picture will flash in the head of the wanderer, a picture of the last level he will ever visit.  
When he'll be in this level, he'll know.

However, this could held many meanings. Often, only death awaits.  
In some extremely rare cases, the last level will be a level where the wanderer will settle or it's even hypothesized this could be the level where there is an exit to earth.

Another nuance, the wanderer won't die, but this doesn't means he can't be harmed.  
Some of theses people who thought they were invincible were horribly mutilated thanks to their  
lack of precautions. Fated wanderers can still transform into wretches or hounds for example.

Finally, a fate can be broken. The wanderer will feel it immediately as the prediction came untrue.

(The psychological effect on people can be interesting to study)

Here it is for now.
