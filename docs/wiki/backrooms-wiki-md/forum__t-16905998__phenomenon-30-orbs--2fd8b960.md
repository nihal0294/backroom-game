First of all, thank you ever so much for the +1 upvote.

**You, my friend and colleague, did not miss a thing!**

So many questions were left unanswered. True.

*Like paranormal mysteries in real life…*

Welcome to my world.

To be honest, I had no inspirations for writing anything for Roots2024 until I saw the photo with the orbs. They are still debated in many photography and paranormal circles.

I thought, "What if we had a phenomenon common to several universes? Like a multiverse version of quicksand? Except that all life essence would disappear while the body remains? Like those unfortunates in real life who are sucked out of small holes in high altitude aircraft?"

I wanted to keep things open-ended. We'll see where it goes.

*(Edit:) I thought that it would be interesting for readers to try and "capture" orbs in photographs on their cell phones. Not the dangerous kind. Just the "fun" ones!*
