# **fragments**

The truth is, I had (and still have) no clue what happened. I must’ve passed out, because the next thing I know, I’m in this twisted, deranged place. It feels like a prison. For me? Fuck if I know. I've been walking for some time, but death isn't coming quick. It's erasing more parts of me as I continue.

It looks like my parents’ hometown, the place where Aya and I met. I didn’t know anything about that kind of life; I was just visiting on vacation. What can I say, those green eyes did it for me. But fuck. I didn’t expect to fall in love. I wish I hadn’t. Maybe if I hadn’t had the balls to go up to her and start rambling about whatever came to my mind, she would still be here with me.

You know what our greatest sin was? Love. We loved each other, and we didn’t care what anyone thought. She was my worst mistake, and I love her so much it hurts. I wanted her to be happy so badly, but I didn’t know what that would cause. *Your olive hair looks like these trees. I see you in them, when nothing else makes sense.*

Being here destroys me by the second. Homes ruined by decades of arguing over which god gets to own us, and the greenery sprouting out of every crack in the sidewalk, the murmurs I keep hearing, it’s all tied to this place. It’s getting inside my head, no doubt. I’m starting to forget how she sounded, how she looked like. The longer I stay here, the more I’m starting to forget how the love of my life used to call out to me after a long day. I’m starting to lose myself as the days go on. I won't be able to keep going soon.
