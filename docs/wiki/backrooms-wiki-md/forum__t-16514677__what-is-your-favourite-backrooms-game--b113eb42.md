What is your favourite backrooms game?

11 Dec 2023 11:54

Since a lot of us are young whippersnappers, I assume a lot of you play video games like me. That leads me to question, have you played any backroom video games? There are a lot out there, whether on Roblox or Steam, or whether they're good or bad, which ones stood out to you?

I play Roblox often, and I really love a few of them like Da Backrooms (A open world survival game which focuses on crafting, loot, quest), The True Backrooms Renovated (A story puzzle backrooms game which is great with friends) and The Backrooms Infinite (A beautiful single player backrooms game). You should check them out if you're interested!

What are your thoughts?
