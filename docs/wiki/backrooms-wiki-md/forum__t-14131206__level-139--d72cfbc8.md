Nice but I have nitpicks

26 Jun 2021 01:21

1. DECOMMISSIONED HAS 2 ‘M’s!!! Sorry this one really bothered me LOL

2. I found the use of the Database theme interesting, as well as the idea that the MEG has their own sandbox of sorts. But in this day and age, it feels odd to me that the MEG draft site would be so stripped-back and minimalistic, especially given how stylized the ‘published’ site is (particularly with the sd-classes and such).

3. I find the idea of someone having the job-title ‘Chief Team Organizer’ kinda odd. I feel that “Chief of Administrative Staff”, or “Chief Secretary” sounds more appropriate and professional. Additionally, it’s odd to me that the MEG would ask for a volunteer team of Operatives to explore the level, since they have a whole Regiment of trained teams dedicated to front-line exploration already! Why not just send one of these teams?

These are just my personal nitpicks! Overall great work, thematically solid. +1
