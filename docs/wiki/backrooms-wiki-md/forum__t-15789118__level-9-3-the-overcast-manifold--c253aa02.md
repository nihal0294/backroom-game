Good read! If I were to describe it with one word, it would be *entrancing*. I really liked the tone game between clinical and the more tale-esque one, it made the experience quite surprising and smooth, and left a lot of room for thought, very interesting.

In addition, this article has a really immersive setup and overall atmosphere. This was further enhanced by the good image game which adds plenty of liminality, neat. And not only that, it also has interesting gimmicks: my personal favourite are the engine noises that allow for some psychological torment; they are constant and harrassing, but in the end, nothing happens, which leaves the wanderer full of useless expectation. Yep, this is Backrooms right here! And those levels where I can see Backrooms are my favourite kind.

All in all, imo, this level feels like a satisfying philosophical page that stands our from the other "forest" levels. It makes the reader think, something many articles attempt but rather few actually accomplish. A +1 for you.
