I am aware that you have not rated this page. My issue is not for this specific page, but the pattern I have observed. There's something almost insulting with being dismissive of works purely because they do not fit your perception of what a Level should be.

Look, I do not particularly like works written in the clinical tone, but the reason is that their execution of that tone is, more often than not, poor, with a tendency to tell facts and build little to no intrigue rather than show a setting and create a story[1](javascript:;), making a lot of these works rather drab. Does that mean that the entire genre of clinical works are to be dismissed? No, certainly not! I've liked a few of them in the past, would love to see others try their hand at writing a page in that style, and I even tried my hand at it. It's a genuinely difficult style to write in, it's a good challenge… If you know what you're doing.

By contrast, my impression is that you reject an entire genre of works based on your assumption that they do not fit the criteria for a category, and thus shouldn't be on the website, therefore dismissing works where a lot of effort was put in. Some of them are quite good too, so of course I feel frustrated when those works are thrown away for reasons I believe are baffling. You will probably reassert that your message here was a joke, but we both know that there is a level of truth and frustration behind it.
