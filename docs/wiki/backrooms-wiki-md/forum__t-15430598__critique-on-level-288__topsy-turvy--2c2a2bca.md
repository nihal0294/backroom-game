I think this needs work.

1 - **All instances of Level 288 must have the beginning of the word "Level" capitalized.** There is no such thing as a "level 288"; only Level 288.

2 - **There are several SPaG errors throughout the article.** I can't list them all here right now, but I can remember:  
· *Usage of "it's" when you refer to something belonging to another thing*. It's means "it is". Its means "the thing's thing", or the Entity's thing, or the Level's thing, characteristic, whatever.  
· *Mixing up "they're", "their" and "there".*  
· *Awkward breaks and lacks of breaks when needed.* You have a slight tendency to weirdly punctuate your sentences.

On another hand, I see that this article is all about… the weird Entities. Which are weird! Good job on that.

3 - **One of your Entities is… immortal? That tends to be a very cliche thing in horror** - so, please, don't do that.

4 - This might be my own opinion, and maybe I'm being picky - **take this critter with a grain of Firesalt** - but **your Entities aren't all that interesting.**. The spike cat things, for example, don't add much to the story. —(Who the fuck tried to drink their blood? Ew. That's an idea for an Exploration Log!.)- I'd like to know more about them. Give a bit more info about some Entities. Not just filler text - **add some more substance and/or information about them.** Also - I don't know, it sounds cruel to leave them stuck on the floor.  
Similarly, **some characters are… downright poorly imagined.** It sounds rather mean, perhaps, but I can assure you that a car with a human or animal head stuck to it doesn't really scare me, even if it comes driving or honking my way.

5 - I know that your Level is all about the **Entities**, but **the Level in itself needs MORE.** A bit more description, a bit more features, just a bit MORE.

This is my first critique of a *draft*, or of an unfinished level - so I apologize if I gave you any bad advice.
