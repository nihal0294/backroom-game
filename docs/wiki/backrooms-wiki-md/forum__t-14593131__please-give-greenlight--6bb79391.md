please give greenlight

04 Apr 2022 07:48

level Quixotic looks like an abandoned airport this level is split into 4 sections

Section 1  
section 1 looks like a grand open room full of items like suitcases, laptops and over airport related equipment

there is no know entity in this section though wanderers do claim to here screams from below them.

section 2  
section 2 is made up of a long hallway with food stores the walls of this hallway are made of giant windows. outside you can see wrecks of planes and helicopters. the estimated length of this hallway is estimated to be about 10 miles.  
smilers, clumps, and deathmoths can rarely be found here

section 3  
section 2 consists of airplane terminals and boarding areas. it is not recommended to board a plane though as they have high amounts of entity lurking inside them. there is a few supplies in these planes

section 4  
section 4 is outside of the airport. this is extremely dangerous as rubble is all over the hard concrete floor. there are also crawlers in the crevasses.

to enter this level you can:  
find a white door surrounded by windows on level 11

to exit:  
entering a plane in section 3 has a small chance of bringing you to level 998  
breaking a window and jumping through it may lead you to level 756
