My Permanent Leave of the Backrooms

14 Nov 2023 18:06

Hey. Never thought I would come to this so soon.  
I'm permanently leaving. Yes, really. Discord *and* Site, and I will no longer be in the community. I will still be on my discord account though.  
This is mainly due to several things, and I'll list em:  
1. The Changes/Decisions as of lately. Not only of Staff but of Community as well. You know the drama that's been happening, yada yada yada, don't discuss it on this thread nor site and instead discuss it in private, but this isn't only that. There have been what I would call generally bad decisions from both staff and the community, but I won't continue on this more so I can remain respectful. I understand the reasoning that the sides I'll call bad decisions have, but one must also look at how the cons outweigh the pros before making a cast of vote.  
2. The Community. This is drama, but also how the general behaviour and quality of the community and staff has just been going downhill at the same time. I understand, don't worry, but it has driven me away from communicating at times and honestly one of the main reasons I haven't been in the community *at all.* Before, the site and discord felt like a group of friends just having fun and writing. Sure, there were some moments of anger and stuff, but those were generally fixed pretty soon. Now, most of the time, it feels like that gif of that dude coming in with pizza and the room is on fire, people are freaking out, etc.  
3, and the final, main defining factor of my leave: My motivation and mental health. Because of the two past things I have discussed, the site has just been draining my motivation more than it has been giving me motivation.  
So, I'm leaving, permanently, and not coming back, because with how things are going now, it's only going to get worse in my eyes and I rather wouldn't worry myself more. Know that this decision has been difficult.  
I resign from rewriting the Allures as well.  
See ya, and have a good day,  
~🌱
