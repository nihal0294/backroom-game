Full review of the article:

SPaG:  
Kai pointed out the SPaG I noticed immediately. There were a few typos, along with some other wonky punctuation. The SPaG was mostly good though, and that isn't typically something that affects my opinion of the article too much (unless it's out of control). However, I do think a final proofread should've been done to catch these last few things.

Writing:  
The loose tone of the article seemed a little generic. I feel like it didn't seem very specific to this character. If you're going to write the article from first person perspective of a character, you have to write it in a way that makes it connected to that character. I could see this being written from nearly any other character's perspective in the same tone and format and still working due to that. All I got was "Guy who owns a museum and doesn't want you stealing or being nosy". That's like pretty much a generic format for anyone. I do understand there is a connected poi article for this, but if you're going to completely depend on a character for an article, i think you could've done a little more to make this specific to Leo himself. As of writing this, I haven't read the poi article yet, and I currently have no vision of what this character is beyond regular guy who owns a library. I also think that you kind of missed the point of connecting this to the lost here. This article briefly mentions the lost, once. It starts off with the theme giving the reader the impression that this article will tie into the lost, and when reading the article, you just get "oh yeah, the lost come here sometimes too. Anyways…". Given that the goal of the competition is to focus on the group, and the lost is pretty much completely ignored in this article, I was relatively disappointed on that aspect.

Overall in world concept:  
The concept was pretty good. I like the museum level, and having it be like a welcome type deal from the owner. I also like the whole centuries of knowledge thing. While I do think the idea of one person who owns an archive of unlimited knowledge is somewhat similar to Blanche, it was still alright on its own.

Final verdict:  
I am reading every goicon article and giving a binary choice of upvote or downvote. Due to reasons listed above, I have chosen to downvote your article — not because it's bad, I just think you could've done a much better job.
