As a whole, this piece is littered by grammatical mistakes and conceptual inconsistency that I don't believe I can fully help you with just because of English not being your primary language. I would generally suggest that you read some more and do private practice writing to help you in that regard. For this draft specifically, though, an easy thing you could do to this page is [applying this grammar checker](https://quillbot.com/grammar-check) to it. It will help you learn and fix some more middle level issues.

> I thought the word disillusion and delusion were the same thing, when I found out it wasn’t the same thing, it was a bit too late to fix…

It isn't too late to fix. When editing the page, you can change the title of the draft to more accurately match the spelling of the word and you can replace the use of the word throughout your page.

> also called the “the servant delusioner”, “the ambassador’s nightmare”, “the nightmare machine”, “the soul torturer”, or “the personality caller”

There isn't really a point to having the object go by so many different names, right? It's not like you're actively calling back to those[1](javascript:;), so I would suggest lessening the amount of nicknames it goes by. You should aim for a maximum of two if you wish to not confuse the reader.

> the objective to “delusion” and “awaken” instances of entity 187,

This is probably a translation issue, but neither delusion nor awaken make sense here. The first makes it seem as though the object is tricking them into believing a fake version of reality; the second could make sense if used within the correct context. I would suggest you use "enlighten" or "mentally unshackle" while dropping the quotation marks.

> it containing three(3) main

Add a space between three and (3), or remove (3) entirely. It looks sloppy if you don't do the former, and it isn't needed enough to not implement the latter suggestion.

> instances of entity 187, removing them from entity 141’s control,

Whenever you have the word entity/object/level/phenomenon followed by a numerical value, you need to capitalize it. You would say, **"This level, Level 852, is pure hell."** but you wouldn't say **"This Level, level 852, is pure hell."** I'll ignore this issue throughout the rest of the work assuming that you work on it.

> These conditions should not take long to manifest as instances of Entity 141 are known to check on their “servants” every 5 hours with the longest recorded time of Entity 141 not checking on it’s servants is 16 hours so stay calm.  
> When one of the above mentioned conditions are met the machine will automatically activate, first it will fire a strong radio signal of 5 kilohertz of energy in to the brain of the entity 187 instance this will cause the instance of Entity 141 to be pull into the direction of the entity 187 instance in the chair and there brains will merge to one with the entity 187 instance controlling the front of the brain and the 141 instance controlling the back. Next the machine will use the electricity it got during the cease function period and channeling it through one of the power lines connected to the brain affecter this will cause the electricity to circulate through the metal net of the dome, this will create an electromagnetic field that will disrupt any attempts to escape by 141 instance as the instance will be contained by the electromagnetic field prison and susceptible to all tortures that will occur, entity 141 does not have a physical form so it can not damage the machinery, whilst the entity 187 instance is already tied up making no rooms for escape, hints the nickname “the container” or “the prison”.  
> After the above process is completed the machine will now use the breathalyzer to administer the hallucinogen and the pentobarbital, this will cause both 141 and entity 187 to enter a coma as they now share the same brain so they are susceptible to the same chemicals administered.  
> After the two instances had entered a coma, the machine will now begin to use the the projector to produce nightmarish imagery into the entity 187 instance’s head whilst using the radio waves to create a surreal environment by affecting the brain, using liquid pain to amplify the effects of the torture, and using almond water to chip away at the 141 instances whilst preserving the entity 187 instance’s sanity, the entity 187 instances will only be mentally tortured but the 141 instances is receiving actual life-threatening tortures that will always lead to 141 instance’s death.  
> The time experienced by the tortured victim is 1d/30s of the actual time meaning, when you experience 30 minutes, 10 days would have passed for them and this torture will go on until the 141 instance is killed averaging around 50 hours which will be 6000 days or 16,43 years of torture in there perspective, they will also feel like they are going through every single torture as the same time for example they will feel their stomach are cut open and will also feel like they are being trampled 300 elephants at the same time, there is no end to the pain, note the more brutal the torture the torture the quicker the process is.

You may notice how blocky that text looks. You need to go ahead and add a link break, by using the enter key, between each paragraph, like so:

> You need to approach it.  
>   
> You need to back away.

That's the end of what I'll say. You should really just read more content both on and off site so that you can get a better grasp of how to write things out. A lot of the issues within the draft are really nuanced in nature, and it would take me a very long time to address every one of those. This obviously isn't an attack on you, but I believe you reading more pieces of media would help you a lot more than me just holding your hand while you write your draft.
