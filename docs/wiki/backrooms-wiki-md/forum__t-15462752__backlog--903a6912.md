The concept is actually pretty cool, but a question comes to mind.  
How was this language discovered? That isn't detailed in this entry. It's probably more of a phenomenon (phenomena's singular cousin), but see what someone more experienced says.  
Secondly, I noticed a couple spag errors, but they're easy fixes.  
**This language doesn't seems tailored to human in the first…** Correct "seems" to "seem". The subject verb agreement is already there with doesn't.  
**The smallest effects are on small an very light objects…** Typo. Just make " an" "and".  
**…it's highly possible that Backlog, or at least its modern version is a fusion of multiples anomalous languages.** Put a comma after "version" and correct "multiples" into "multiple". Multiple implies that there is a plural, and you only use it's plural in math, as far as I know.  
**The last element that tend to prove this theory…** Correct "tend" into "tends". Again, subject verb agreement.  
That's all I noticed! Love the concept, and I could see this on the site (no, I'm not a GL…). To be honest, I've wondered if the Backrooms had its own language.
