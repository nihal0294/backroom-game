25 Mar 2022 19:28

The concept is really good, although when you describe the lake, you say it has almond water in it. Someone is already making a draft about an almond water lake, so maybe edit that part to avoid sounding unoriginal. Other than that, a few grammatical errors, but overall, well polished and a fresh idea, love to see it! Run it by some greenlighters (list of greenlighters [here](greenlight-policy.md), make sure to look at their info to see what they're comfortable with. They could prefer Discord pings, Discord DMs, or a private message on Wikidot.), and I think you should be good. Just make sure you get a greenlight first.

Good luck!
