# MoonDay

Have you ever heard that Night and Day know each other? As you run through the great prairies, you will see that the Sun accompanies you. Oh, and the Moon? Where is it? Just a few hours away and the sky will fall. Your vision will become cloudy. Stars in the darkness, will illuminate your life.

![moonday.jpeg](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/art:moonday/moonday.jpeg)  

**The MoonDay is coming….**
