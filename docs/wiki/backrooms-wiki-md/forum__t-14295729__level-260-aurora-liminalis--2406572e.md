Level 260 - Aurora Liminalis

14 Nov 2021 00:06

**Page Type:** Level

**Page Layout:** Description, Discovery & History, B.O & C, Entrance and Exit Points

**Elevator Pitch:** Level 260 is a wintry tundra upon a plateau. Level 260’s main feature and namesake is the aurora that appears in the level’s atmosphere which gives the level life.

**Central Narrative:** Level 260 was discovered on 21 November 1973 by a group of four wanderers who had prior been expeditioning in Level 118. By 1976, these wanderers had constructed the first and only current community to exist in Level 260. Mass-knowledge of Level 260 was only started on 20 Jan 1999 after the M.E.G. began Expedition 118-03. Six expeditions have been carried out by the M.E.G..

**Attention-Grabber:** Level 260 is one of the first levels given life by one single entity.

**Additional Notes:** Level 260 has a wildly different survival class layout.
