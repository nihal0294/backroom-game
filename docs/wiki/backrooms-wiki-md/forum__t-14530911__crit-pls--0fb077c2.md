# **Description:**

The Middlerooms is the name given to the void between The Frontrooms and The Backrooms. While this rarely occurs, you will end up in The Middlerooms if you get stuck noclipping to the Backrooms. If this happens, then there's a small chance that you will find yourself trapped in the space between The Frontrooms and The Backrooms, known to us as, "The Middlerooms". Many would rather die or be trapped in The Backrooms then fall through this meaningless void for eternity…

As of right now, the only way we know of the existence of The Middlerooms is through those trapped there. People have tried to send messages for help using any devices they had on them as they noclipped. The M.E.G has tried to do rescue missions, but these attempts have proved futile. They have never succeeded in accessing The Middlerooms on purpose.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

### Entrances

There is no confirmed way to access The Middlerooms, although it takes a lot of bad luck. There's around a 1% chance of accessing The Middlerooms.

### Exits

Unfortunately there is no confirmed way of leaving The Middlerooms at the moment. There are very little accounts of The Middlerooms to begin with, and these are from people trying to reach out for help. Some have claimed to have gone to The Middlerooms and say you noclip back the way you came, although none of these have been proven.
