**Level 134** is the 135th level of The Backrooms. It resembles an endless mansion, with an odd warp effect.

# **Description:**

**Level 134** resembles a mansion with endless halls and rooms. A constant warp and distortion effect is often seen, yet does not really do anything.

# **The Distortion**

The Distortion could be it's own entity, although this may not be possible.  
Everything else known about it is that it can cause an exit, even if it takes an extended period of time.

# **Discovery**

The level was discovered by a wanderer only known as AU Fan.  
When AU Fan was walking around [Level 1](level-1.md), he saw a distorted wall and came to investigate.  
He noclipped through and ended up here.

# **Entities**

One wanderer reported to have seen a [Partygoer](entity-67.md).

# **Entrances And Exits:**

# **Entrances**

Entering is extremely hard, but noclipping through a distorted wall may lead you here.

# **Exits**

The warp may send you to any level of The Backrooms if you wait long enough.

**Level 134** is the 135th level of The Backrooms, resembling a corrupted endless mansion.

# **Description**

**Level 134** is a corrupted endless mansion, with a constant distortion and warp.  
It is recommended to try to keep away from corrupted walls in any level, which could send you here.  
It is shown that the distortion levels are climbing at a steady rate, and may tear the level apart.

# **Entities**

The distortion is considered to be an entity. [The Memory Worm](entity-42.md), [Smilers](entity-3.md) and [Partygoers](entity-67.md) have all been found in the level.

# **Entrances And Exits**

# **Entrances**

In any level, corrupted walls may lead you here.

# **Exits**

At random times you can be warped to any level.
