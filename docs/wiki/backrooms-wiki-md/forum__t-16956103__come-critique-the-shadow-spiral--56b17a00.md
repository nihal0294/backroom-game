02 Oct 2024 17:43

Hello! This is my first submission to the site - it is an unnumbered Level called the\_shadow\_spiral. Its description takes the form of a journal of an explorer who entered the Backrooms in 1908 and gained immortality on one of its Levels; he finds himself in a dark unexplored Level that radiates grief, which seems to be a battle site. I tried to tell a personal story about a singular character but also wanted to touch on some bigger, eerier happenings relating to my headcanons about the Backrooms.

I'm looking for feedback on the content and quality of the story, but this is also my first real time trying to make a Wikidot page so if my code could use some work, definitely let me know. Thank you!

Very nice.

The level was an immediate hook for me. The repetition was fantastically used to create a feeling of dread. I skimmed most of this (because I'm at work and not supposed to be reading lol), but overall, I liked what I saw.

Remind me to come back and give you a bit more of an in-depth view. However, I did have one piece of critique:

> This Level consists of a seemingly infinite series of darkened rooms

Within the page, you say the author uses Tier in place of Level in a footnote. You've already explained that this person has their own way of referring to things, so, rather than him using Level, you should stay consistent to his character and use Tier. It'll feel more natural.

Alrighty then.

> We all know that there are plenty of worlds contained here which must have had some hand in inspiring the horror stories of both ancient and modern Earth…

This passage is a bit wonky to read. Change "which" to "that," see if that clears up the confusion. You may have to break the sentence apart or reorganize the ideas though. Even with "that," it's still quite a sentence to get through.

> Some rooms have windows through which day- and moonlight flow, but the glass of these windows is too foggy and blurred to see anything through.

I just liked this sentence. Wanted to highlight it :)

There is not much in this page that jumps out to me as needing to be fixed or changed or smoothed over. If I had to do a critique, I think it would just be cautionary critique that the monologue may get a bit much for some of the readers. If you can find spots where it's repeating the same idea in the same way (and it's not for literary purposes), I would cut it out.

Overall though, there's not much I would change from this piece. The character voice is strong and compelling, leading me through the whole page. Additionally, he brings up interesting ideas about the nature of the Backrooms. The repetition of the pictures adds to the repetitive and futile nature of it all. Nicely done.

Frankly, I may just clear this page for posting because I'm not sure people can find things to fix in the page. But if you want to do it by the book, the next step would be to offer critique for critique, especially on the forums. Our discord is MUCH more active, but I know not everyone can access it. So, let me know if you think you need more critique or if you think it's ready.
