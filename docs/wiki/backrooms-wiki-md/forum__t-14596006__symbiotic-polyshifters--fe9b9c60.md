"Symbiotic Polyshifters"

06 Apr 2022 07:37

- [*"symbiotic polyshifters"*](javascript:;)

**ENTITY NUMBER**: 53

**DESCRIPRION**: Entity 53 is a species of polymorphic creatures that is rarely seen in level 37. They appear to be symbiotic, defending its owner and are extremely fierce when protecting their owner. They are very friendly, and are tamed by simply interacting with it in a playfull or protective fashion. They will slowly absorb small amounts of nutrition from its symbiotic partner, and in return will perform any actions it is trained to do, but by default they will protect their owner, or if they detect that they are not healthy, will attempt to find whatever they think will help. They have an average intelligence of 150 and can teach each other tricks they have learned very quickly. However what makes them so special is their ability to transform into a functional replica of almost any object they encounter. They have the ability to innately sense the interior of any object and reverse engineer these items, and use that information to become the object in question. They will even be able to use the abilities of said object, for example, a deulcidator. If anymore of it gets damaged as an item, or separated from its main body, it will lose small chunks of its body when transforming back.

---

**BIOLOGY**: entity 53 instances don't have any known organs, simply transforming into whatever tools they need and absorbing nutrients for their hosts. Because of their polymorphic nature, no actual anatomically correct data can be obtained. However it is known that they appear to use fragmentation to reproduce, once every 5 years, splitting into 3 identical polyshifters.

---

**DISCOVERY**: Entity 53 was discovered by a wanderer named James Quin. After entering level 37, he ran into one and they became symbiotic partners ever since. He has name his "poly".  
Poly generally takes the form of a fuzzy ribbon-like object wrapped around James's left arm. Most information on polyshifters has come from poly.

---

**DO'S AND DONT'S**

DO

- tame one if you find one.

- start training yours as soon as possible.

DONT

- aggravate a polyshifter.

- attack someone who owns a polyshifter.
