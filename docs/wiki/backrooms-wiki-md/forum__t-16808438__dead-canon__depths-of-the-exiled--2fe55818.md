Canon : Depths of the Exiled

09 May 2024 14:26

Main Concept:  
It's about a canon dealing with exiled people by the powerful.  
Being exiled in the backrooms erase traces and memories of your existence in the frontrooms.  
So, they may be some elements in the backrooms that could have altered drastically the frontrooms (Alternative History elements).

Navigation and resources gathering are also higlighted due to:  
-No-clip doesn't work in the backrooms.  
-Items doesn't appear, you have to find them or craft them  
-The levels move and are non-euclidian

Finally, death works in particular ways:  
-You gain a younger body, but lose memories in the process  
-Is unavoidable due to aging  
-With each death, you becoming closer to being an entity and a good ending would be  
to become a beneficial entity due to personal path.

More details about the canon are below.  
If you want to know more details and/or want to participate, feel free to leave a message on this thread.

Edit:  
To have more details on how the death mechanic operates to transform people into entity:  
Inner self, actions, circumstances orientates the direction of the evolution, increasing the probability to acquire certain characteristics.

People tend to not become better, nor worse, mostly different.

For example, dying of starvation would direct you have more chances to gain wretches's characteristics, having manic tendencies would direct you to the partygoer road, being more feral or eating raw meat may make you gain hound characteristics.

As such, mixed breed and unique entities are more common in this canon.
