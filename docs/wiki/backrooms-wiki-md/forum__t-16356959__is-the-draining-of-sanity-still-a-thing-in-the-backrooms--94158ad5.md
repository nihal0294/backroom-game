Is the draining of sanity still a thing in the backrooms?

22 Sep 2023 09:28

Maybe it never was a thing and was just something backrooms game developers created to make their games more exciting, but I remember there being sanity drop/draining in many backrooms levels. I remember this being one of the reasons why [Almond water](object-1.md) was so useful because it would stop this and help regain the wanderer's sanity. It feels like this has either gone out of fashion or something that wasn't very popular in the first place I don't know. What are your guys' thoughts and experiences about this?
