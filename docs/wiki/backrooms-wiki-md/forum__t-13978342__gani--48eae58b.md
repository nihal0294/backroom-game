-1, lacks substance

25 Mar 2024 07:17

While this page has the **roots** of a potentially good read, it doesn't do a lot of good on expanding on the actual ideas it presents and fails to let its concepts **blossom**. Let's see what things we do know:

- Gani's appearance and personality
- Gani's vague immortality
- The page on them needed to be rewritten

We, the reader, are told nothing beyond that. It's quite a short list and some things on it aren't even really discussed in detail. I don't know how their immortality works or what caused a rewrite for the page to be needed and everything else is just building up Gani as a character, which is important, yes, but the character needs to be used beyond that. This can be cleared up by saying, "The character will be used in tales about the larger group." but there are no links to the tales in this page, so this doesn't actually lead anywhere. There's a good foundation, and I would love for things to be taken further, but there just isn't enough here for me to give this page anything other than a **down-vote**.
