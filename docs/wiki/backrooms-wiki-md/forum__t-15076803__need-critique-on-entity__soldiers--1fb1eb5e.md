Hey Exterminator,

You have certainly fixed your run-on sentences, but they could still use a little work. A tip of mine is that if you have 2 separate ideas in a sentence, they should either become 2 sentences or be separated by a semicolon. Semicolons can be specifically used to add a statement referring to the previous sentence, EX: "The entities are soldiers; They have guns," or smth.

You do have a few other errors, like incorrectly spelled words, or incorrect pluralization, EX: "Their countries flag" -> "Their country's flag."

I actually like the idea quite a bit. They are human in every way except for their extremely tough skin and devotion to killing entities is what you mentioned. I can see some problems with other people who may not like the idea, so you could add something else. (Maybe they have weapons freaking built into their arm?)

Finally, the B.D.C. logo is described, but not shown. In some cases, this works, but it would be better to have an image. Either draw it yourself, or ask someone else ig. (I happen to be a great artist…)

Happy writing!
