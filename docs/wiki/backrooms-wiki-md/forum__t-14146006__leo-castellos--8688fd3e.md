21 Jul 2021 07:15

This article just doesn't do it for me for two key reasons: first, the amount of SPaG errors in this article, and the inconsistency of the character's narration and tone.

I caught at least twelve spag errors before I was halfway through the article. I feel like this article could have really benefitted from more critique before it was posted. The amount of SPaG here made reading this very choppy, further exacerbated by the inconsistent tone.

To me, it doesn't really feel like you have a solid grasp on who this POI truly is. The reason for that it is because his dialogue switches back and forth from several different styles. It's casual at some points, seeming comfortable in day-to-day modern conversation. Then, it's overly formal and icily stuff. Then, it's calm and composed, but still welcoming. Then, it's outdated and "is this what the kids nowadays are up to?"

It feels like there are like four different separate people writing this article. If you asked me what this guy's personality was, there's too many conflicting tonal cues for me to give you a definite answer. Overall, he feels like he's still in the conceptual phase. I've already read several entries just like him, and so he doesn't really feel unique to me, either.

Another thing of note is that it feels like the Lost reference was very contrived. If you completely removed his affiliation with the Lost, he would not transformitavely change as a character. This is a red flag that the actual Lost backstory part of the article, which is supposed to be strong in order to tie the article to GOICON, is actually not very effective. If I didn't know this was for GOICON, and I looked at this from the perspective a new reader, I would be very confused about the purpose of the Lost reference. I would wonder why it couldn't just be removed.

There are also some logical inconsistencies, such as him knowing what fun facts are but not knowing what chill means. It also feels like you tried to emulate the Blanche formula in that you made him really like scones. The problem is, this article is so short and with so little supplementary substance that there's no foundation for him to build up all these loveable foibles upon.

There's also a plethora of salient dead ends here in terms of storytelling. He replaces his picture with a picture of space, but never goes in depth about why he likes space. Why didn't he put his profile as a picture of scones? Why is he named Leo Castellan, and how did the M.E.G. find him? What's the history you teased at with his previous contributions? These roads lead to nowhere as of now, and overall this article leaves a lot to be desired.
