# **Description**

Entity Å, officially named **Isulinniq**ᐃᓱᓕᓐᓂᖅ[1](javascript:;), is an exclusive entity in Level 110, first discovered by M.E.G. operative Clephens Edmont and Nathaniel Lucia.  
  
  
The entity takes on the appearance of a black hole, bearing a resemblance to [Null-06](entity-52.md); however, recent observations indicate that it may be similar to the quasar mentioned in the document [Ad Astra](ad-astra.md). Unlike [Null-06](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/null-06), **Isulinniq** has been proven to possess "intelligence beyond human comprehension", and can absorb electromagnetic waves and matter particles while releasing Hawking radiation (it may release powerful and extremely dangerous relativistic jets under extremely rare circumstances) in order to receive or transmit information. Therefore, it is classified as a living organism rather than a celestial body in the conventional sense. It is still unknown how this "intelligence" manifests.  
  
  
**Isulinniq** is highly dangerous and, combined with the extreme environment of [Level 110](level-110.md), it is able to kill wanderers immediately. However, as [Level 110](level-110.md) has become extremely difficult to enter since the "Ad Astra" collision, there is no need for common wanderers to worry too much about the threat of this entity. Recently, the M.E.G. detected a radio signal source suspected to be from **Isulinniq** in the Pavonis Constellation of [Level 11](level-11.md), so all personnel must be vigilant to prevent **Isulinniq** from endangering other levels.

# **Behaviors**

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-110/Light2)

The photo of **Isulinniq** releasing a relativistic jet.

**Isulinniq** is usually in a stationary state. As per the classification standard of the Frontrooms, **Isulinniq** is theoretically a Schwarzschild black hole. However, there is a large accretion disk around **Isulinniq** that can rotate around it. **Isulinniq** can continuously consume nearby matter, and when the accumulated consumption mass reaches about ██×10█M⊙[2](javascript:;), it will release high-intensity relativistic jets vertical to the accretion disk, destroying anything in its path. All entrances and exits to Level 110 will temporarily become invalidated until the jets stop.  
  
  
The enormous mass of **Isulinniq** is enough to distort its surrounding space-time. Time in Level 110 will flow extremely slowly due to the distorted space-time.

![Missing alt text.](http://backroomssandboxcn.wikidot.com/local--files/angstrom-hole/IMG_20220610_222518.jpg)

A photo of received signals. "RSSL" represents signal strength, and "t" represents time. The three troughs and three peaks on the graph represent ".-.-.-" (the Morse code for ".")

What's most incredible is that **Isulinniq** can release regular and receivable pulse waves, indicating that it probably possesses the ability to exchange information with the outside world. According to wanderers who have entered [Level 110](level-110.md), this kind of signal can be received by various electronic devices, and some even claim to have directly felt the signal in their brains. Signals can be decoded in Morse code, but signals received by the cerebral cortex can be directly understood by wanderers.

## Summary of Level 110’s 2nd Exploration Mission

#### Background and Plan:

Since the discovery of Level 110, the M.E.G. has classified it as a high-risk level and blocked all known entrances. However, recent observation results have shown a strong radio signal source in the Pavonis Constellation of Level 11, which has been then confirmed to have 97.8% similarity with the signal released by the celestial body at the center of Level 110. The M.E.G. hopes to uncover more information about the radio source and estimate its impact on other levels, thus it was decided to launch a second exploration mission.  
  
  
Considering the painful lesson of the first exploration mission, the second mission will take the form of unmanned exploration. The M.E.G. dispatched 10 trained Facelings to prevent M.E.G. personnel from being directly exposed to the extreme environment of the level. According to the "virtual pair" principle and comprehensive cost estimation, the M.E.G. planned to sacrifice 5 Facelings.

#### Process:

- The 10 Facelings were dispatched to the entrance to Level 110 in Level 78 and all entered the target level.
- After a while, the CT base in Level 11 received video recordings transmitted by their cameras, but the frame number was low for unknown reasons. Some time later, the videos were severely disrupted by signal interference and the transmission eventually failed.
- Only one Faceling successfully returned to Level 78.

#### Result: The second exploration mission ended unsuccessfully. The unmanned exploration plan was proven to be impractical.
