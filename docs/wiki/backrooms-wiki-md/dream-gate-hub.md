*I have dreams where I fall and dreams where I fly.*

*A paradise island buried under the parking lot of a Walmart and an elevator cage larger than a waiting room in size. I feel the elevator drop around me, and my head lolls off my shoulder.*

*I don't die.*

**Introduction**  
*Here lies the premise: this is a setting where [no-clipping](phenomenon-5.md) is nonexistent. Instead, wanderers travel from level to level in gleaming [elevators](entity-81.md), with each button on their control panel matching their corresponding level on the database.*

**Preface**  
*This series is rooted in my dreams. I invite you to embark on this journey through [chevron-patterned hallways](level-0.md), [blinding office spaces](level-4.md), elevator cages, and sunlit hellscapes. As we travel, ask yourself: What would you give for an ivory world?*
