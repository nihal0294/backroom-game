Hi, Kimmingi!

Here is my honest crit of your level so far:

SPaG: Putting it out there, you have many SPaG errors, but I will list as many as I can remember. Make sure to fix these first thing!

> "Level 547 is home to only young clowns, and the looks like a cute boy with a height"

I wasn't quite sure, but you may have meant "Level 547 is home to only young clowns that look like cute boys with heights of…"

> "it will move to Level 10."

"it" should be "you". All of your other exits also referred to "it" moving, so change 'em all to "you".

> " But if you're hungry, or if you're attacked by explorers, you turn very ferocious and bite explorers."

This sentence is referring to the entity found on the level, not the wanderer, so change "you" and "you're" to "it" and "it's".

> "explorers"

On the wiki, we refer to humans exploring the Backrooms as "Wanderers", but you may have been referring to explorers from the MEG, in which case I'm sorry.

> "you can't see the faces of the actors. Obviously, there are features, but for some reason, you can't see the faces of the actors."

You do not need to restate that you cannot see their faces.

> The Halloween Contest 2021 theme??

I see where you were going, but this theme is reserved for articles that were a part of the 2021 Halloween Contest. You could create a custom theme with cobwebs, instead.

> It's impossible to get out of the circus ground, and there's There's a little

The second "There's" should be removed. Even Grammarly is getting mad at me.

Done! Time for getting into a *real* crit…

I might as well start off by saying that this level is *severely* unfinished. I like the basic concept, but you gave us the bare-bones version. A few plotholes I saw were that firstly, you described the level as a large cirus ground. You then said it consists of a tent, and described the tent area. Is the tent the whole circus ground, or was there more you didn't say?

Next, wdym by "you can't see their faces, but they have features"? I had automatically assumed you meant facial features, and if this is not the case, make it more obvious that these are not facial features.

Besides that, you just need to go into much more detail, tell us more about the entity, and just give this a "fresh coat of paint." Go in, give me some details, and PM me when you are done.
