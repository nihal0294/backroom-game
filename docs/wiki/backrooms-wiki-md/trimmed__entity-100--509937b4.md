**Entity Number:** 100

### Habitat(s): N/A

### Description:

Entity 100, or **[REDACTED]** is an entity that is very dangerous. The entity usually will be in a "docile" state. It'll usually lie in [Level -998](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-minus-998), although getting to the entity has been deemed impossible. This entity has been documented in a "awoken" state. Entity 100 will usually start in their mind. Then it'll expand, either taking full control no control of the body or physically come out of their mind and kill them. letting them free.

### Behaviors:

Entity 100 is usually very hostile. kind. The entity will infect their mind, slowly taking it over. Then controlling their thoughts. Then, they'll start taking over the rest of the body. when it's done, they'll use the body for either evil give acts of kindness, act of communication, or both. The entity seems to speak any language very fluently. The entity is extremely intelligent, even smarter than humans. Took you long enough to notice that I'm stronger, smarter, and faster than you.

### Biology:

Due to Entity 100's location, it is hard to really know what it looks like. However, it does appear in its raged state. Its appearance resembles a black figure, with either flashing eyes or pitch white eyes. It uses its eyes to blind others. It also usually has something on its back, however it varies from person to person. Its clothing usually is a black hoodie, red baseball cap, blue jeans, and grey sneakers. Entity 100 also may just be a pitch black creature or missing parts of clothing mentioned. The entity has attempted to speak, but the most he has been able to say in person is "Hello, I am—" The entity seems to only appear to a certain amount of people.

### Discovery:

It is unknown when Entity 100 was discovered.
