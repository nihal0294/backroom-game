Level 226, "The Dream Feast"

11 Jun 2020 00:23

**Page Type:** Normal Level

**Elevator Pitch:** Quite an inviting location, a long wooden table decked out with medieval era royalty dishes, an impossibly long table. The table is possibly endless, as no definite measurement has been taken of the table as nobody has made it across to begin with. The sides of the table are lined with quite comfortable seats, those of designs from the medieval era, that fit for old royalty and the royal courts. Nobody is present inside of this level besides a specific humanoid entity that always forms out of literal thin air with food in place to replace the food that has been eaten, which will ignore individuals in the level as long as said individuals do not interrupt with the server's job. The table is surrounded by a vast white void, not necessarily blinding, just an infinite white dome around the table combined with a large (but not infinite) white floor that spans for exactly 300 miles away from the table, where doors to level 998 can be found inside of the expansive white floor. Falling off of this floor will just lead to either a negative level, most likely level -9 (I'll make a separate post for level -9)

**Central Narrative:** None

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** Food is an issue in the backrooms, and this feast hall would seem infinitely welcoming if it were not the fact that most of the food has an effect that comes from eating the food, ranging from transportation to another level, somewhat determined by the food, also can have effects on the body, whether it be minor sicknesses, pain, deformation, or just death. In rare cases, the food is completely normal, as you can eat it without issue, however it is impossible to determine when an item of food is normal as it can be any type of food. The food in question will never spoil unless has been at least partially consumed.

**Additional Notes:** This is my first entry of the backrooms wiki and most likely not the last, so please try to find potential expansions on the idea as well as the already existing criticisms.
