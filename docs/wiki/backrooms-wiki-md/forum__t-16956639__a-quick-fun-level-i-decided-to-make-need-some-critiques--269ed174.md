There's quite a lot that can be worked on with this one, so I'll try to point you in some directions.

First of all, I'd highly recommend getting someone to give you spag crit, or putting this page through a spell checker. I don't give spag crit, so I won't mention the grammatical errors, but know there are a substantial amount.

Currently, the main issue with your page is the length. There simply isn't enough worldbuilding or story to accomplish the goals that the page sets out to meet.

All this page really is, is a short collection of tropes. "DO NOT COME TO THIS LEVEL!!! VERY DANGEROUS!!! LOOK I FOUND A DEAD PERSONS JOURNAL OOOH SPOOKY!!!" pages are dime a dozen on the site. You've got to do something to set yourself apart.

As I was reading through the page, I kept trying and failing to identify what the significance of the Level actually was. In other words, I struggled to find the reason as to why I was supposed to "care" about the page.

The most important thing in writing a page is making your reader care. The way you do this is by simply showing effort in a way that conveys the point that you, the author, have something worth reading. If you don't care, why should your readers?

What I'm getting at here is this: Spend some more time conceptualizing what you actually want to do with the page. Come up with something you're passionate about, and start over from the beginning. You don't have to scrap the ideas you currently have, but you do need to figure out a "base" for how to apply them.

Hope this gets you on track B)
