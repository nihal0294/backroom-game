The Grey Halls (First brainstorming)

11 Jan 2021 08:24

So, this is my first idea for a contribution.

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre (Optional):** Horror, maybe

**Elevator Pitch:** The basic idea for this level is a monochrome set of hallways and rooms mimiking a silent film from the early 20th century, including:  
-Design and architecture based off art-déco.  
-Everything is silent unless otherwise specified  
-Everything is seen through some sort of "filter" that includes visual noise common in old films.  
-The only sounds come from the crackling of the "film" and a bunch of radios scattered throught the level playing music from the time period. (I´ll explain more in detail in the draft)

**Central Narrative:** None, yet.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** I haven´t seen any levels based off art-déco and/or silent films, I thought it could be an interesting concept.

Overall, I think it's an interesting concept but without an attention grabber- it's a bit hard to critique it in-depth yet. Maybe add a few movie references might spice it up a bit to add to that movie atmosphere. Maybe sometimes a camera can be seen, or you end up with lapses of memory when filming happens.

An idea is that after a few hours you'll eventually pass out and leave the level. When you wake up, you'll end up in a random level with a recording of the "movie" you were in.
