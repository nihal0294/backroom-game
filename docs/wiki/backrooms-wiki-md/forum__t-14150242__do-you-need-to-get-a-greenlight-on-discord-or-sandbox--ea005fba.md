29 Jul 2021 04:50

You can't give a greenlight on the Sandbox because it has no intrinsic messaging system. Greenlights can be given by greenlighters through PMs on Wikidot itself, through Discord DMs, or in our Discord server. The Discord server is by far the most used method of greenlighting. We have a channel in the Discord where you link your sandbox, and another channel dedicated to critiquing sandboxes, which is where greenlights usually go.
