# **Description**

This level seems to be an area in outer space, yet any effects of outer space other than barely any gravity do not seem to be here. It has a frequent entrance, all spawning in a star cluster with a moon in the center. When present, a rose will always be in the corner of your left eye.

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/lost-love/rose.png)

The Rose

# **Entities**

Only one entity is present here, and that is a star-like entity. This entity does not communicate, nor does it attempt to harm wanderers. It can be seen pulling in matter and assumingly consuming it. Any attempts to get close to it or being pulled towards it will result in the wanderer in the level waking up. This star does not look like its counterparts on earth, as it seems like it has another universe inside it.

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/lost-love/star.png)

The Star.

# **Events**

This level can rarely change its typical background to a planet and a spaceship. The rose that typically obstructs your view will be gone. The first report of this event was a planet filled with water and barely any land. The spaceship doesn't change its shape, but it can change color and sometimes have the logo of Backroom's GoIs. Rarely, [Blanche's](entity-140.md) signature can be found on the side of the ship.

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/lost-love/space_ship.png)

The Spaceship

If the person gets close to the spaceship and observes it for a small period, a spacesuit will float out of the ship's back. Observing this suit the wanderer will see the wanderer's closest person, whether it'll be a close friend of a few years or one's love. The person inside seems to be a replica, as it will have its eyes closed and will not respond to anything. In their hand will be the rose that the wanderer in the level would typically see blocking their view. Soon after noticing this rose, any wanderer in the level will wake up.  
The real version of the person seen inside the suit will soon suffer from negative effects. This can range from a sprained knee to a life-threatening disease. The only countermeasure that has worked against this is a sign of friendship between you and the person. This could be accomplished through talking, sharing inside jokes, or the person simply telling the other about their dream.

## Further Elaboration:

This is a real level, despite mainly only being able to be seen in a dream, as wanderers may change locations after waking up, it is possible to be injured(and wake up with those injuries), and the effects that happen to close ones after seeing them in the level.

# Entrances or Exits

## Entrances:

This level can rarely be entered by falling asleep on any level.  
It is rumored that this level has a physical form in the backrooms, and be rarely entered through no-clipping through any bedroom or sleep-related level, but it is still unconfirmed.

## Exits

Getting close to the star will cause you to wake up in the level you started in.  
Observing the suit's flower will cause you to wake up in the level you started in.  
Falling asleep in the level will result in waking up in [Level 114](level-114.md).  
Touching the spaceship's logo(if visible) will result in being sent to a random safe level.  
If Blanche's signature is on the side of the ship, touching it will result in surroundings being morphed into [Level 906](level-906.md), and you will end up in the middle of the library.
