Christmas Levels Contest! (unoficial)

23 Nov 2023 01:17

Hello, I welcome you to the (unofficial) christmas level contest. You can participate whenever you want, but it has to be on time. The contest rates more the levels that have to do with christmas or something christmassy in their description, but it will not rate the enigmatic levels or the sublevels (even if they are christmas). Any item that has to do with sublevel or enigmatic level will not be counted for you, but you have the possibility to change it to a normal level. Below are the instructions to participate:

# **Instructions**

*1-The articles have to do with christmas, if not, they will not be counted or they will be left out of the contest if they do not change it.*

*2-You can use any theme, except those that contain something related to a group or are dark in the background, such as the Danger Zone or Biofell theme.*

*3-They have to be published in this thread on time, otherwise articles that arrived late will not be counted.*

*4-The articles published in this thread will be reviewed, such as grammar or coding. But more importantly the SPaG, if an article has many problems with any of the three things it will earn fewer points.*

*5-The article can be in various ways, such as a set of files, a story and even altered information.*

*6-The schedules given below must be respected.*

# **Schedules**

•November 22 - 30: Time to register: Take advantage of the time to send your application, in order to be registered to participate in the contest.

•December 1 - 15: Writing time: You can start writing earlier, but it has to be posted in this thread before the 15th.

•December 16 - 24: Article review: When the articles are ready, they will be reviewed. The most important is the SPaG, since it lowers points if you don't have it right. From this point no more items will be counted.

•December 25: Winners announced: Those who have obtained the most points will be the winners, the winning item will have to receive the Green Light.

# **Additional Note**

Those who will participate will have to put a new post in this thread. Those who apply to participate will have to explain why. And such posts have to be edited to add the already made article.

Those who are going to participate will be noted by me, so that I can rate each article.

If you want to tag your articles as "Christmas Levels Contest!" No problem, this is not mandatory.

Stories about christmas levels are also allowed, so… What are you waiting for to experience christmas in the best possible way on the wiki! I wish you good writing.

Don't feel sad if you didn't win, this is just a contest for fun, so *Merry Christmas!*
