level -11 - The Illogical Path

04 Oct 2020 04:23

**Page Type:** Negative Level

**Elevator Pitch:** The level appears simple, and is anything but. The level takes the form of an infinite parking lot with a small garage in the center, and lacks any obvious dangers, being completely devoid of entities or normal hazards. It follows geometry that deviates significantly from Euclid's axioms, with its true danger being of becoming lost among innumerable identical lamps in the endless twisting space.

**Central Narrative:** The level's seeming banality is a trap. The lamps lighting the parking lot are in fact the walls of an endless and inescapable maze, warped to our perceptions by the distorted space in which they reside, while the garage is in fact a safe hallway leading to the exit. The level is built on warped geometry that defies normal reasoning and inverts futility and productivity. To escape one must follow seemingly nonsensical instructions, and is repeatedly warned against the apparent logical path, testing one's ability to follow instructions they disagree with. After hours of seeming to walk in circles, the temptation to go beyond the garage grows despite the repeated warnings until one may find themselves going against what they know in their heads but do not truly believe.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** The danger of the level is easily avoidable, but many die as they give in to their own doubts and go against the page's many warnings. The strange and confusing explanations conflict with the seeming simplicity of the level, leaving the reader in a similar state of doubt to the unfortunate wanderers caught in the level who are left to question whether the strange instructions are true or are merely meant to misguide them.

**Additional Notes:** The level is meant to confuse the reader, but the basic premise should be clear.
