Hey, everyone. It's been a hot minute, ain't it? Don't call it a comeback, though. This tale is the result of a sudden burst of inspiration and an idea that was too good to waste, in my eyes. Still, it's good to be able to put something out again.

I don't plan on returning to the site or the discord full time. If I ever feel like writing something again, I likely will. Only time will tell, I suppose.

I'd also like to take this chance to apologize to some people. All the people who were looking forward to seeing drafts I never actually finished, all the people who submitted things to Blanche.net that I never got to, and all the people who I lost touch with during my hiatus.

That said… hope you people like this one. I know some of you won't, but frankly, I don't care much. I had fun writing this, and I'm proud of it.
