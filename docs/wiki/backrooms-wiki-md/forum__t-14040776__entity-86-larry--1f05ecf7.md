Entity 86 - "Larry"

03 Apr 2021 20:10

[[module Rate]]

**Entity number:** 86

**Habitat:** Majority (other than smaller levels like [level-6](level-6.md))

# **Description:**

Entity 86, more commonly known as Larry, is a tall humanoid (roughly 8.8 ft tall). Entity 86 will attack any human in its line of sight, and will go through whole levels to kill that person. Entity 86 will always be found in a room labelled on a blue door 'Larry's play room'. The interior of 'Larry's play room' has bright purple wallpaper with stains resembling vomit and blood. The first time a victim find it, it will be devoid of a sign, and without Entity 86 in. On the second time the victim finds it, the sign will be up, and Entity 86 will be in there.

# **Behaviours:**

Entity 86 is extremely docile, and will sit in its room, until it has a target and/or finds a target.

# **Biology:**

Entity 86 resembled an 8.8 ft tall humanoid with heavy pigmentation in the skin, Entity 86 seemingly has no bones and organs, and is just made of a thick, rubbery skin like material. Entity 86 has a wide jaw that can stretch 5 inches down and is filled with 23 layers of small, sharp teeth.

Entity 86 seems to have the light ability to say certain English word including 'Larry', 'Help', 'Run', 'Die' among many others.

# **Discovery:**

Entity 86 was discovered through a new, young adventurers diary, and was later spotted by █████ and █████████.

---

# **Do's and Dont's:**

### **Do:**

- Don't go in through a blue door.
- If being tracked by it, hide under desks or in cupboards.

### **Don't:**

- Go in Larry's play room.
- Stall Entity 86.
