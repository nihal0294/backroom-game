[Quick Idea Critique Request] - Kyandi : ENTITY

13 Jun 2022 03:37

**ENTITY: Kyandi**

**[Summary]**  
Basically, this entity is a pacifist, yet a dangerous one. According to Tsukiko, this entity was made to be a “truly grey character,” but in very different ways other than moral. Khandī and Kyandi are not the same person, so don’t mix them up.

Would it be a good idea to make a disease based on this character? Or an entirely new species of set entities instead, as the infected technically aren’t human anymore? Would it be a good idea to have them be connected to a specific human, like Tom and Blanche, but not quite? Perhaps it would be a good idea to list their known abilities?

**[Additional Notes]**  
Heads up: Any ideas you give are welcome, any ideas you don't like can be criticized. However, if you know what the design looks like, just know I am not changing Kyandi’s design on any circumstance.
