09 Mar 2023 17:16

When I heard the concept, I was super concerned it was going to be a fandom-esq, nostalgia bait/fake frontrooms type deal. I am so glad the concept is a lot more creative than that.

First, the cons:  
I don’t like the dialogue here, there are like three lines in a row that start with yes which is super unrealistic, breathe some life into these characters. The M.E.G ops aren’t robots so feel free to give them unique personalities or dialogue quirks. [Level 789](level-789.md) is great example imo. Also not huge on the memory gimmicks or the U.S setting but those are more personal preferences.

And the stuff I like:  
I really do like the place you took your initial idea by going absolute ham with the 5e class and coming up with so many unique disasters that can merge. It’s kinda like Monster’s upcoming phenomena article but more balls to the wall. That is what sets it apart from the fandom articles, the creativity that played off the initial concept.

Both sides pull me equally so I’m gonna no vote for now but keep going.
