Level 995 (hopefully): The Upside Down City

12 Oct 2021 22:18

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre:** Mystery

**Elevator Pitch:** A cave system containing a massive upside-down Victorian city, along with a code and a mysterious entity…

**Central Narrative:** Details surrounding Level 997 are scarce. The only reliable source of information the M.E.G has on this level is a mysterious man who claims to have visited the level, and attacked a member of the B.N.T.G and demanded to speak with a M.E.G writer. The Level 997 article draws heavily from his testimony. There are small packs of entities scattered across the level, in addition to an entity the man only referred to as "The Gambler". Who or what that entity is, he refuses to tell.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** The article will have a secret code, and a mystery that will be solved in due time.

**Additional Notes:** This is my first time posting any real page on this site. I promise after this I will move on to levels less "heavy", for lack of a better word.
