# Level 127 - "Headache"

rating: +31[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

  

Info

Warning: includes mentioning of thoughts of suicide.

[Close](index.md)

You wake up to the soft buzzing of artificial lighting, cold tile pressed against your back, and an aching soreness in your head. As you slowly sit up and survey your surroundings, you notice… nothing. All that's in front of you is a typical hallway setting: large, square ceiling lights, chilling vinyl tiles covering every surface, and steel doors that could lead to nothing but more halls: nothing that could stand out. It reminds you of the ‘behind-the-scenes’ walkways of a school theater production, where teenagers rush back and forth between changing rooms and the stage. Except… It's so… Clean. Too clean, even—almost sterile. You take a deep breath in through your nose, and it feels like the inside of your nostrils is burning off in the way only bone-chilling cold and a space completely free of any single speck of life can.

After taking in the plain area surrounding you, you finally stand up completely—only to suddenly stumble and crash into the wall next to you, the throbbing pain in your head disappearing as quickly as it had arrived. You assume it was just motion-related dizziness from getting up too fast. Stabilizing yourself after that surprise, you continue onward, aiming to learn if this unexciting hall system really does go on forever.

You keep trekking on, hallway after hallway, when you finally gather the courage to open up one of the million steel doors decorating the corners. Slowly and carefully, you approach the one closest to you, tentatively reaching a hand towards the metal handle protruding from the middle of the vertical steel slab. Your hand makes contact, and you feel a stinging chill that reminds you of the feeling on your back from earlier—it's an unsettling feeling of familiarity that urges you to leave this place as soon as possible.

That realization fills you with a new sense of determination, and—with haste—you swing open the door in front of you, only to find… more hallway. Your hand slips off the handle, and you feel the panic that’s been itching under your skin start to rise even further. The realization that this labyrinth goes on frightens you.

Acting almost solely on instinct, your legs move you quickly in the opposite direction of what can only be defined as your eternal hell. The back of your head slams into the wall across from the opening—you turn to look at it and—BANG—the door shuts. You jump and flip back around, raising your hand to your heart—it's beating rapidly. Recovering from that outburst, you decide it’s better to continue onward through the door again, seeing as staying back here isn’t getting you anywhere. You walk through the door and flinch when it shuts loudly behind you.

\*\*\*

It’s been a while since you walked through that door—you don’t even know how long; it could’ve been seconds, minutes, hours… days. Nothing has happened; everywhere you look, it’s just the same walls, same floor, same lights, same doors. The repetition is starting to make you dizzy. You feel that same incessant pain from when you first arrived here, though duller—the lights are giving you a headache. You decide to ignore it.

The further you stalk down the winding tunnels of fluorescent light, the worse the pain gets. It didn’t instantly disappear like you thought it would, or as it did before. What was once a dull buzz is now a screaming throb scattered throughout your brain. You can barely walk, barely see, and barely think. You still have enough sense to pull out your [Almond Water](object-1.md); the frigid coating around the glass of the bottle mimics the feeling in the expanse of corridors—blending in perfectly, terrifyingly so.

You take a swig of the water, swinging your head all the way back and letting the almond-flavored liquid drip down your throat. Almost instantly, you feel a brilliant relief from the painful sting that plagued you moments before. Freshly painless, you move forward at a rapid pace—feeling more desperate than ever to get out of the maze of beige.

As you continue forward, the surrounding sounds become more and more noticeable—the clicking of your shoes on the vinyl floor, the pressurizing blare pumping behind your ears, and the faint buzzing of the lights that you know all too well. Except, you can’t help but think you hear something. You’re not sure what it is, but you swear that an almost imperceptible ding tolls every few minutes—or seconds? You’re not sure, but it’s making you feel insane. You know nothing's there. Every time you look back at the sound, nothing is there. But… What if there is? What if you just can't see it? What if it can see you?

The thought of something hiding its presence so well terrifies you, and you are gradually becoming more and more anxious. There are tiny little twinges of electricity running throughout your body; you can feel your heartbeat getting faster, and you’re losing feeling in your limbs.

The pain in your head isn’t getting much better either—the effects of the water have long worn off—so you reach down for your bottle to take another sip. However, instead of feeling the cold glass on your fingertips, you feel nothing but air. You could've sworn you put it there… right? This new information sends you into yet another panic—with no ‘magic remedy’ for your pain, how will you even begin to deal with this elusive thing that you’re sure has evil intentions? Even just the thought of having to meet this creature in your subpar state is making you feel even more sick.

Interrupting your train of thought, you suddenly hear the same ding that’s been terrorizing you this whole time—except… it’s louder, much louder than it was before. It’s closer. It’s gaining on you.

*ding*

*ding*

*ding*

You start a near sprint in the opposite direction of the noise. All the short hairs on your body are sticking upwards, and your skin flexes itself into goosebumps. The constant ache throughout your body is definitely not helping your efforts to get away, but you persist.

In your despairing panic, you completely topple over and skid across the floor—the skin on your elbow peeling off from the friction. You hardly care, though, because out of the corner of your eye, you notice something—a red glow shining through the gap between the floor tiles and the door closest to you. You thought that these doors could only lead to more of the same moldy beige—not this brilliant red that’s mimicking the blood that’s now dripping from your elbow.

What’s behind this door is different. This door is your [salvation](level-11-3.md).

You attempt to get to your feet, only to find that you can't—each time you try, the pain in your legs grows larger. Despite the ache that persists in even the deepest parts of your bones, you continue to pull yourself toward the door—you just have to be high enough to reach the handle, and then you can push yourself through.

The blood that’s slowly drying on the floor gets smudged around from your scooching, but you’re almost there! You can almost taste the freedom that this mysterious door promises - just a little more and you’re home free!

The frigid metal makes contact with the skin of your hand. The chill that it sends through your hand is no longer foreboding or dangerous—it fills you with a sort of twisted sense of comfort, reassured that this is the last time you will feel the chilling bite of stale air and metal.

You’re finally able to make it up to the door, and with all your strength, you manage to yank it open just enough to fit through. Now that you can finally get a good look at what’s behind, you’re more sure than ever that this area is the key. The hall behind the door is, quite literally, the opposite of the outside—it's mostly the same area, except flipped completely upside-down, with blistering red lights instead of the tiring beige ones. You don’t know if you'll ever be able to look at the color beige same again.

*You don't know if you'll ever be the same again at all…*

You pull yourself away from that line of thought—you don’t want to get too ahead of yourself. You continue to hoist yourself forward, propping the door open with your half-extended left leg. Once your head is all the way in the passage, the pain you were so used to dealing with suddenly gets much, much worse. It feels like your brain has been transformed into wood, with thousands upon thousands of tiny, little termites nibbling at it—decaying. Your vision follows.

You can’t go back the way you came; there’s nothing for you there. Moving forward, inch by inch, you finally make it all the way through as the door slams behind you one final time.

It’s only the thought of being saved that consumes you as you [fall](level-11-3.md).

[You'll never be the same again.](javascript:;)

[There's no going back.](javascript:;)

**Level 127** exists as an endless complex of brick hallways enshrouded in headache-inducing beige light.

# **Description**

The hallways that make up **Level 127** are composed of large, white-painted bricks and the occasional thick steel door typical of what you would find in an office or school setting in [The Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md). Electrical outlets are found near the floor on some walls of the level, though a negligible number actually supply power.

The ceiling in **Level 127** is the normal type of drop ceiling you'd find within a commercial building, sporting the occasional square-shaped light fixture every few tiles, along with non-functional sprinklers and cameras. Above every door within the level, a red glowing exit sign can be found hanging from the ceiling, though none of these doors usually lead to a true exit from the level.

The floors of **Level 127** are made of a potently reflective hard vinyl tile, in which the color appears to be random, ranging from white or off-white to dark grey. The features of the walls and ceiling of the level are constantly reflected off the flooring, along with the beige light produced by the lighting fixtures.

At first glance, **Level 127** appears to be completely harmless due to its lack of entities. Though, after some time within the level, wanderers will find themselves beginning to develop extremely painful and incessant headaches stemming from the beige lighting emitted by the square fixtures on the ceiling. These headaches, though non-lethal, cause intense disorientation and confusion, making traversal extremely difficult the longer one resides within the level. Oftentimes, these headaches will also induce violently realistic visual and auditory hallucinations, enabling wanderers to believe entities are actively pursuing them despite the lack thereof.

All known symptoms of the beige light within **Level 127** include:

- Perpetual and agonizing headaches that progressively worsen with extended exposure.
- Extreme confusion and disorientation.
- Horrifying visual and auditory hallucinations.
- Temporary long-term memory loss.
- Immensely painful eye strain.
- Sudden loss of consciousness.
- Intense tinnitus, or ringing of the ears.
- A sense of impending doom.

These symptoms can also give rise to many secondary effects, such as:

- Difficulty traversing through the level.
- The belief that someone or something is following you.
- Possible physical injury due to loss of consciousness.
- Thoughts of suicide.

[Almond Water](object-1.md) can sometimes have a slight nullifying effect on the headaches and hallucinations caused by the beige lighting, though this is usually negligible and should not be relied on in the long term.

There are currently no known *physical* long-term effects that the beige light within **Level 127** has on wanderers—but you'll never be the same again.

[![A blurry picture of a dark brick hallway illuminated by sinister red light, seemingly flipped upside-down with a door present at the end.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-127/Red/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-127/Red)

The only known image of the red hallways present within **Level 127**, the person who took it seemingly falling through the drop ceiling.

The steel doors within **Level 127** typically lead to additional beige-lit hallways. However, the geometry of the level remains Euclidean in nature. Under very rare circumstances, a door within the level may lead to a red-lit, upside-down version of the normal hallways. Entry into the red hallways within **Level 127** should be avoided at all costs, as falling through the drop ceiling on the bottom of the room will always lead to a random building within [The Red Light District](level-11-3.md) of [Level 11](level-11.md). The light present within the red hallways has a similar effect on wanderers as the beige light normally found within **level 127**. However, the symptoms caused by the red light are almost always tremendously more potent than those caused by the beige light. Some doors within **Level 127** may lead to empty storage rooms that contain various useful objects, such as [Almond Water](object-1.md), [Firesalt](object-15.md), [Liquid Pain](object-48.md), [Cashew Water](object-44.md), and, though scarcely, first aid kits. Furthermore, a smaller number of doors can lead to the empty offices of [Level 4](level-4.md).

Attempts have been made by wanderers and [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) to block out the beige lights on **Level 127**, though no efforts have come to fruition, as the light seems to shine through anything used to block the ceiling units. Because of this, long-term dwelling within the level is not advised, and simply covering your eyes or using a blindfold cannot stop you from seeing the light.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

No bases, outposts, or communities have been established in **Level 127**, and any attempts to do so have led to failure due of the harmful effects of the beige light.

# **Entrances And Exits**

#### Entrances

- Although rarely, some doors within [Level 4](level-4.md) can lead to **Level 127**.
- Wanderers may no-clip into **Level 127** through [Level 87](level-87.md).
- Although extremely rare, it is possible to no-clip into **Level 127** through [Level 1.5](level-1-5.md).

#### Exits

- Falling through the drop ceiling on the floors of the red hallways of **Level 127** will always lead to a random building within [The Red Light District](level-11-3.md) of [Level 11](level-11.md).
- A small number of doors within **Level 127** can lead to the empty offices of [Level 4](level-4.md).
