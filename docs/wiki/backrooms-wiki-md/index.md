# The Backrooms Wiki

*They say that it's an afterlife–not for people, but for places. For every chain storefront demolished, every retail outlet bought out, every dead mall razed to the ground. A limbo for every building without purpose, those in-between spaces. Endless structures so unfathomable, yet so clearly man-made. Edges in the curtains of memory, corners that open up at the seams of reality to let the unlucky wanderer in.*  
  
*Be careful out there, for the footsteps that fill these halls may not only be your own.*

##### Welcome to the Backrooms Wiki

We are a **collaborative fiction project** that has been running **since 2020**, chronicling various stories and features of the infinite otherworldly realm known as **the Backrooms**. This project focuses on varying interpretations of the setting and is open for anyone to contribute to.

##### Monthly Features

The monthly features are a collection of four pages posted within the last month that were chosen to be the best representations of our site, showcasing the talent of our authors and our content goals for the wiki. All pages posted within a monthly period are voted on by the Greenlighter Team, and the four pages that are regarded as the highest quality pieces of literature are then recognized as such.
