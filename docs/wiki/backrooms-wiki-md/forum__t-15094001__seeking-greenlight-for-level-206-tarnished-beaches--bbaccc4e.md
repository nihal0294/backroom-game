Level 206 “tarnished beach”

[[include:backrooms-wiki:component:level-class  
|class=deadzone  
]]

This level takes the appearance of a Wheat-Field and a beach.The wheat fields are Dotted with Trailer-homes,which should not be entered unless  
As a last resort,as these are inhabited by Aggressive facelings,which attack on sight.The Wheat fields are also dangerous,as they Have an infestation of entities.It is recommended to enter this level with a class-5 firearm or High-firepower

The beaches are,Effectively,the only areas that should be stayed in for a Break.  
These beaches are,however,geographicly,extremely unstable,if you see a Castle on top of the cliff,Hide as  
**she** will give you a trade,**do not accept it**  
If you see ships on the sea,It is advised to Swim as fast as you can towards them,as they Are  
**usually** devoid of entities

Entrances:

It is possible to enter this level by Suffocating to death in sand on Any beach level.  
Noclipping from the frontrooms has an extremely rare chance to send you here  
Sleep inside a trailer-home or sandbox inside level 9

Exits:

Falling into a sinkhole will send you to level !  
**transporters**send you to a random level(dangerous)  
Survive the entity horde (5 days) to be sent to level 999
