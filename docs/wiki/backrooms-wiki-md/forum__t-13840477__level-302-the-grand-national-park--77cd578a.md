Level 302 - The Grand National Park

30 Oct 2020 17:27

**Page Type:** Level

**Layout:** Same ol’

**Genre:** Adventure

**Elevator Pitch:** Level 302 is a coniferous forest surrounded by mountainous terrain labeled as “The Grand National Park” by the sign at the entrance to Level 302. Level 302 is renowned for it’s large quantity of entities, both hostile and passive, however, as the entities in Level 302 an’t be seen anywhere else in the Backrooms, they are instead referred to as “wildlife”.

**Central Narrative:** In 20██, two corpses were found near Level 302’s entrance. On one, was found with a leather-backed notebook, composed of their experiences in the Backrooms including their discovery of Level 302. (More will be revealed in the article)

**Attention Grabber:** This is the first level that is protected and preserved by a group of the Backrooms. That group being the M.E.G.

**Addenda:** My entry for the SD Class Contest (Class ω)

Re: Level 302 - The Grand National Park

31 Oct 2020 07:20

Okay, saw this in forums, figured I'd be of assistance.

> In 20██, two corpses were found near Level 302’s entrance.

I see no purpose in redacting an exact year.

> My entry for the SD Class Contest (Class ω)

Give the exact class details in idea forum, else we won't know what you mean by this. Based on what I read, its entities which are protected, but theres typically three bullet points for that.

> This is the first level that is protected and preserved by a group of the Backrooms. That group being the M.E.G.

That'll hook ur reader in at the beginning, sure, but how do you keep that *focused*? I'd suggest going more into the M.E.G. themselves, that'd help a lot.

> however, as the entities in Level 302 an’t be seen anywhere else in the Backrooms, they are instead referred to as “wildlife”.

That… doesn't help much. Theres no real reason to protect Entities in the first place, and unless you can find one, the M.E.G. really shouldn't be putting efforts down to protect Entities. If these Entities had a purpose, per say, that'd help a lot. Give the Entities a reason to be protected. Being endangered isn't good enough.

---

I'd *strongly* urge you to remove the hostility from the Entities themselves. Why would the M.E.G. actively protect something that'd harm them. I'd suggest giving these Entities a purpose to be protected. For an example, they can provide something which is beneficial to the M.E.G. However, as it stands, the custom SD won't help you much here.

Beyond that, the SD is what needs work. It's what makes this Level unique, and the Level revolves around it, so make it *work*.
