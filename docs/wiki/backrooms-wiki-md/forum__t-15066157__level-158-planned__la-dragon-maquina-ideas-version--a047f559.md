Level 159 (planned): La Draġon Máquina (Ideas Version)

01 Aug 2022 22:03

Page Type: Level

Core Concept: Level 158 appears to be a quaint medieval village in the wintertime months, however the temperature is as warm as summertime, and the houses are made of plastic, making it seem as if the level is a movie set. There are living mannequins dressed in costumes which only move and talk if nobody is looking, and a massive mechanical dragon that stalks the level. The nearby castle, which is bigger than any Frontrooms structure, is a massive labryinth containing strange vintage rooms and plenty of entities, and one of them may have become sentient…

Additional Notes: The draft for this level is on the draft critique forum, so go see it over there, come back here or just stay at that page to write a review.
