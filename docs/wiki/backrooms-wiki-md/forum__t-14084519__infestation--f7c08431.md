# Entity N - Infestation

---

**Entity Number:** n

**Habitat** - [Majority]

### Description

This small spider-like entity is a parasite that feeds off of other beings, namely humans. They posses a hive-mind before reaching maturity. If one is discovered to have infected a human, the victim should be killed painlessly and their body burned. It's a much better fate than letting them live.

### Behaviors

Infestations will wait in a dark place, on a ceiling or on a wall before jumping onto a being. They will try to remain undetected until the human falls asleep, at which point they will enter the cranium via the nasal cavity. Due to the nature of their feeding, they seem to prefer humans because of the large brain.

### Biology

After entering the human, the Infestation will impregnate themselves and will explode when their young are born. They will proceed to feast on the grey matter of the brain, similar to the human botfly. Because they depend on the blood as well, they do not kill the human. They preserve the brain stem even after they have fed.

It was at this point he was diagnosed with alien hand syndrome, a condition in which the left and right hemispheres of the brain are separated, causing one arm to work independently of the other.

Less than a week later, he went into a comatic state. Reportedly, his eyes had "torn open from behind" and hundreds of small bugs could be seen crawling out of the sockets. This is the last stage of the Infestation larvae, after the entire brain is consumed. After exit, they lose their hive mind and become solitary beings.

Autopsy revealed that his brain cavity was nearly empty. The only thing left was the brain stem, which had left him in a vegetative state. There was no human left. They ate it.

# **Do's and Dont's:**

### **Do:**

- Try and groom yourself and clothes for any bugs. They will not enter the body until one is unconscious.
- Take mercy on someone infested. A quick stab in the neck while asleep or a shot in the head. Destroy the remains, as the bugs may search for a new host.

### **Don't:**

- Try and save them. It won't work. I'm sorry.
