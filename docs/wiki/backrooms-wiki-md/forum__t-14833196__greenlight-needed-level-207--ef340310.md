> Level 207 is the 208th Level of the Backrooms.

The second use of "Level" shouldn't be capital. Generally, you'd want to add more information to the introduction of your level; provide a brief overview of your level's environment and abnormal properties.

> Quick shot of a room in green

Add punctuation to this caption.

> A Multicolored Hallway in Level 207

Don't capitalize "Multicolored" and "Hallway" and add punctuation to this caption.

> The level resembles a living space, kind of like The Whiteout,

I'd reword this as:

\*The level resembles a living space, visually similar to The Whiteout,\*

> Certain area's will have ether a

Remove the apostrophe from "area's" since it's not a possessive term.

> will have ether a lighter or darker shade

"ether" should be "either".

> outside the level to which a device plays the music,

Replace "to which" with "where".

> it isn't confirmed that there is an area outside the level.

Remove "that there is an area outside the level" for brevity.

> Some hallways will have Windows lined up all the way down. But are one of the only things in the

Change the period to a comma.

> change color, but in fact, stay the same color

Change this to what's shown below:

\*change color, they instead remain as a singular color\*

> and the exit symbol above a chroma door, leading to Level 208

Add punctuation here.

> and even sanity if you stay in the level too long.

"sanity/insanity" is no longer a mechanic used on the wiki. Please remove all mentions of it from your page.

> Sunglasses, or any type of head protection

Change "head" to "ocular".
