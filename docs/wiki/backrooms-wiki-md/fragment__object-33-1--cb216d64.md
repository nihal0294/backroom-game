# <https://backroomsrobotics.org/robopets>

> # RECALL NOTICE:
>
> ---
>
> All RoboPets have been recalled by Backrooms Robotics due to their harm to all entities and humans. We believe this is because of a fatal error in the programming, as the RoboPets have been malfunctioning and set to attack all entities, and humans are classified as entities in the [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) database. We sincerely apologize for the destruction RoboPets have caused since their manufacturing and retail. It is our utmost priority to capture the thousands of loose RoboPets throughout all levels of the Backrooms.
>
> They often traverse alone and will target lone wanderers, but you can evade them by moving in unpredictable patterns and hiding out of their line of sight. [Firesalt](object-15.md) and other weapons do not work on them, and we recommend not attempting to fight them by any means. We designed them to the best of our abilities to be indestructible, and they are incredibly difficult to outsmart and beat successfully. Attempting to fight them is useless; they are programmed to track your every move and predict your next, and landing a hit on them is near impossible. They cannot be tamed or beaten, and they will kill you if given the chance. There is an off switch underneath their chin, but we recommend only attempting to shut it off as a last resort.  
>   
> We wish you all safe travels and the best of luck. In the inevitable case that you come across a RoboPet, run as fast as you can away from it and report it directly to the nearest Backrooms Robotics member. Once more, we sincerely apologize for the casualties and harm that this product has caused.  
>   
> May the odds be forever in your favor.  
> - Backrooms Robotics
