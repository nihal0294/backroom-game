Im not much in the grammar department so you will probably need another person or 2.

The only thing that i can see is inconsistent usage of oxford commas; like:

"Your favorite place to eat, rest and live"

Does not contain an oxford comma, while:

"We have removed the wooden boards, bicycles, and more things"

Does contain an oxford comma. The site doesn't force you to use it or to not use it, but it is best to stick with the one you are use to for consistent.
