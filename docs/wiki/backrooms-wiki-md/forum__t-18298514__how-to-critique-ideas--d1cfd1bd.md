The questions were mostly about making the page more detailed and adding lore. But while this is cool, I don't think it tends to be enough on our site, nor is the lack of lore something I think pages suffer from nowadays. The questions are deliberately vague because the crtitic is meant to figure out the specifics themselves. I can't write a details essay that will cover every idea forum's critique because that is not possible. The point of this page is to encourage people to actually do idea critique instead of only having 2 people willing to do it. It's not a comprehensive guide, it's just something to get people started.
