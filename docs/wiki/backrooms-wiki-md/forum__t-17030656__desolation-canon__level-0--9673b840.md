04 Feb 2025 13:18

I don't see a Desolation Level 0 on the site, so you should be fine.

So, this looks very similar to the regular Level 0 that we have on the site. While the levels in this canon do share the same environments as the normal versions, there are some significant variations - for example, Desolation's Level 10 is closer to the Frontrooms, so people there often see glimpses of Earth every so often.

What could be done here to distinctify this level is to explain it more as a dangerous first trial for those who come to the Backrooms, one that must be completed in order to progress. Emphasising a lack of resources (forcing Wanderers to keep going to escape or die trying), having the Desolation Effect (where Wanderers turn into Facelings, losing their soul and being absorbed into the environment) be more intense here, making it impossible to sleep (so that there is no way to restore mental energy), etc may work well. These things still fit within the essence of Level 0, but make it more separate from the original.

If you need SPaG help, I can do that, too.
