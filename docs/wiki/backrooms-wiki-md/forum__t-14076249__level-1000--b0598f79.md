Level 1000

27 Apr 2021 12:58

**Page type:** level

**Hook/Attention grabber** This level would be the first installation in a storyline that I feel like was on the wiki at one point but cannot find any evidence of.

**Elevator Pitch:** A checkpoint for the wanderers who made it this far. Home of a near forgotten colony, It is by far one of the safest levels in the entire backrooms due to the progress that they have done.

**SD Class:** 0

**Description**  
Level 1000 takes place in a giant campground. The level has a day/night cycle lasting 8 hours each rotation. The campground is approximately 10 miles in each direction from the center with new wanderers to the level spawning in the northwest corner of the level. There are hundreds of camp sites strewn all across the level.

**Camp Type 1**  
This campsite is the most common type only housing only an electric lamp with infinite batteries, a hunting knife, an unlit campfire, and a 3 foot by 6 foot tent.

**Camp Type 2**  
This campsite is completely in shambles having firewood thrown through parts of the tent, pieces of shattered glass on the ground from the lantern, and ash littering the ground. It is unknown if these tent types were here when the level was first made.

**Camp type 3**  
This is the rarest type of tent with only 2 of them being reported so far. They house a campfire which changes to your preferred temperature, a worn sack, a machete that cannot dull and a flashlight.

**Colonies and Outposts**

**The New Yellow Tent**  
The yellow tent is a group that was set up in the end however was transported to level 999 by the partygoers through unknown means. They have set up camp in both of the Camp Type 3s and have put yellow tarps on the ground from the entrance of the level to their base. They have also exterminated all of the entities that once roamed the level which is possible due to how hard the level is to access. The last partypoopers also live here and seem to be reproducing with each other and the human population there.

**Entrances and Exits**

**Entrances-**

- The only known way to enter this level and cluster 2 is by repenting in level 999.

**Exits-**

- To exit you must find a Camp Type 2. You must then pick up one of the glass shards and stab it into your calf as hard as you can. The scream you let out will be 130 dB loud and you will pass out after 5 seconds and wake up in level 1001 with all wounds healed weather related to the incident or not. This exit was discovered after the yellow tent watched a wanderer attempt to kill themselves and get a notification from the person 7 minutes later.

I actually really like this! There's a lot of potential in this idea, but I have a few things to nitpick.

- Level 1000 and upward (aka Cluster 2) is (I'm pretty certain) a section that's going to be saved until every Level in Cluster 1 is filled up. So sadly, the Level can't really serve as Level 1000. I still like the idea of it being some kind of checkpoint though, so you could find any other Level number that's most suitable for it.
- I really like the description as well as the camp-type variants, though I feel it's lacking a little bit. I recommend talking about perhaps the terrain, entities, etc to lengthen it a little more.
- Something about the New Yellow Tent colony feels very… off. I like the idea of reviving the Partypooper species, but doing it this way feels incredibly… anti-climatic. It may just be my opinion, but I think it would be better to develop the way they somehow, if ever at all, revive their species.
- With this kind of unique setup, I feel there are tons of ways of creating Entities native to this Level. Camp Counselors, perhaps? Also including a few more other passive Entities would be great too. I understand this does change the Survival Difficulty though, so that would really be up to you.
- The exit in particular is rather unique. I feel you could keep this way of exiting the level and adding a few more exits, but perhaps if you want to use it exclusively you could write a reason for it. Means, give the camp meaning. Is there a slogan for the camp related to this kind of exit? What did it use to be about? What was the campsite's goal? That sort of thing.

I like the idea so I'll give you the Greenlight to go and make a ***draft*** for the [Sandbox](index.md). From there, I'll be happy to lend a hand and give some more critique! I anticipate your future work.
