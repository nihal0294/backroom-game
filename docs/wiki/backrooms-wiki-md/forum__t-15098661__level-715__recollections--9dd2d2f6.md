# **Crit:**

> Level 715

I suggest replacing it with bold text: "**Level 715**".

> Though Level 715 contains no branching paths

This statement is useless. You just pointed out that it's *a tunnel*, not *multiple tunnels*.

> The dates represented on the plaque can range from minutes to years

I get what you tried to say, but this sentence is clinically incorrect.  
**Suggestion:** The dates written on the plaques may display years, months, days, hours and even minutes.

> including such values as

Omit this. Add a period to the previously critted sentence and just say "For example" and then *you list them.*

> freezing, and skipping.

No need for a comma there.

> “Blank Slates,”

Remove the comma.

> Level 200

I suggest omitting it, along with Level 404. They are more connected to Nostalgi Gaius than they are to the wanderer, as far as I can tell. Remove them, just to be sure.

> two conditions must be met

It's not wrong, but it's better to replace it with "two **requirements** must be met".

> the Hub

"The Hub"

# **Notes:**

Not bad. Very cool. Much better than I initially expected. Decent clinical tone, there isn't much to say about the article itself. The only issue is that, canonically, it's infeasible to reenter The Hub.
