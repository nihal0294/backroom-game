I don't like this level, and i'm giving it a downvote, here's why:

[Things I Dislike](javascript:;)

[Hide](javascript:;)

It's generic. Like, there is just *nothing* to it. It's just a desert with a house, woods, and entities. Nothing really cool about it.

Along with this, the entities and properties are just so… well, *out there.* Like why are they there? Can you go into detail? Do they have a reason to exist? I don't get it. This is with the entities too, but it's not as bad. But basically, here is what I got

- If it doesn't serve any purpose other than the level is to generic for a greenlight, make it interesting enough to make people think it has a reason. Or what you should do, **actually give it a reason.**
- **Give detail.** Seriously, give interesting details that intrigue the reader.
- Make it interesting. Don't just say "Oh there is this hill and blah blah" because I was bored throughout the entire thing.

[Things I Like](javascript:;)

[Hide](javascript:;)

I like the properties. Now I know your probably thinking "Oh didn't you just say the properties are bad??", which yes, I did say that. But I like the properties itself, but I don't like how its being used and the environment it's in. All you gotta do here is expand. Maybe even make a log about it? Add something, because that was the only semi interesting thing.

Overall, you could do better. I like the building blocks, just add more to it.
