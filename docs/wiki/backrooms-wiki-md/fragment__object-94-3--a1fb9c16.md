## Operation Details

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/object-94/level-12-model.jfif)

A representation of [Level 12](level-12.md) hallways made with our brand new "**Liminal 3D**" software.

Due to [Level 12](level-12.md) nature and the way it behaves, the level was strongly suspected to be where Back OS™ originated, therefore being the location of the theorized root servers.

An anomalously high concentration of Wi-Fi traffic has been detected at some parts of the level. However, every time we tried to get closer to them, all our electronic devices exploded, causing serious injury to our personnel in all attempts.

We believe this is some kind of self-defense mechanism: for instance, if you don't intend to track the Wi-Fi signals, nothing happens. Therefore, without the assistance of any electronic device, we kept trying to find a potential "secret room" or "sub-section" that might contain the root servers.
