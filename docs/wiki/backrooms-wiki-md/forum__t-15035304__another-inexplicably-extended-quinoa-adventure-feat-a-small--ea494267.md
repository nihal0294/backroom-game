!!WHOLESOME QUINOA ALERT!!  
This is not a drill. Wikidot user CutTheBirch has written a tale where Quinoa from the hit radio show, "Talking With Quinoa" is wholesome.

You may proceed throughout your day as normally but please be on the lookout for:

- Wholesome Quinoa interactions.
- Blanche-Quinoa communication.
- Quinoa in a maid dress.

I repeat, this is not a drill.

---

About 8 months ago, I wrote a version of the "Episode 906" part of this tale, basically just as an excuse to draw quinoa in a maid dress lol. At the time, I had some ideas of maybe extending it to have Blanche visit him in his dreams, but that kind of fizzled out. Then, a couple weeks ago, I was like "Hmm, I feel like making Quinoa wholesome would have a lot of potential", so I wrote this!

[maidoa](javascript:;)

[the joke is that in the early days, blanche used to be depicted wearing a maid dress for some reason, so, since blanche is the only librarian quinoa has even met, that's just what he thinks the librarian uniform is](javascript:;)
