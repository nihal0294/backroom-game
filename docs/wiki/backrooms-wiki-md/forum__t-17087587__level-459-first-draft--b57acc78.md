# Backrooms Level 459

Class 1

- Safe
- Secure
- High Entity Count

Level 459 is the 460th level of the backrooms. It manifests as a large courtyard, surrounded by concrete walls.

# Description-

The level consists of a large square courtyard, around 100 metres on a side, surrounded by infinitely tall walls.

The floor of the courtyard is made of hard, packed dirt, from which grass grows to around 1 metre tall at the walls of the courtyard, gradually reducing in height closer to the centre of the courtyard, terminating after a sharp reduction in height at around 10 metres from the centre of the courtyard, at around 0.3 metres tall. Digging directly downwards, after around 10-15 centimetres of dirt, gravel is found, and following 50 centimetres of gravel, hard stone, which appears to be of the same type that make up the walls. Further digging has proven impossible at this time, as the M.E.G has judged further attempts as a poor use of limited resources.

The walls of the courtyard are made of a hard, grey stone, likely granite, although no testing has been performed at this time, due to concerns around aggravating the inhabitants of the level. 5 metres above the floor of the level, cavities can be seen cut at regular intervals into the wall, cuboid in shape, 2 to 3 metres in depth, and usually 0.5 metres in length along each square side. Around 1 in 3 of these cavity contain an instance of Entity 459-1, detailed further below. All other intrusions thus far explored contain a nacreous gel, deposited at the bottom of the cavity. Study on this substance has thus far found it highly unstable in composition, evaporating rapidly when removed from the level, even if cooled or frozen prior to departure.

The courtyard is lit from above, in a manner similar to sunlight, however, spectrographic analysis has determined that the spectrum of the light is closer to fluorescent lighting, similar to the lighting found on Level 0.

In the centre of the courtyard is a raised square dais, around 1 metre high, and 5 metres on a side, with a staircase on one of its sides. It appears to be made of the same substance as the walls. Carvings can be seen across the entire surface of the dais, elaborate markings along both its sides and top, depicting a variety of scenes (detailed below) as well as what appears to be a language, with looping intricate symbols, yet to be translated by the M.E.G. Atop the dais, is an elaborately forged knife, coated in what appears to be red paint, elaborately carved with similar symbols to those found on the dais. The knife hums slightly when picked up, and grows louder when removed from the dais. The paint cannot be chipped or cleaned off in any way.

The dais is marked with 8 distinct scenes on its edges, with one additional scene on its top. Each of these is accompanied by a few lines of yet to be translated text. They appear in chronological order, starting from the right of the staircase, to the left of the staircase, with the scene on top appearing separately from those on its sides.

1- Several human forms appear in profile, following after a larger and more detailed form. This is depicted above a depiction of what appears to be a settlement, loosely drawn as a variety of squares and triangles, populated by smaller forms. The large figure bears a knife, resembling the one found on the top of the dais.

2- The large, detailed form depicted in the prior carving stands away from the settlement, moving with its followers, toward a sun, depicted as a circle, daubed with red on the left side of the scene.

3- The detailed form appears to be drawn cut in half horizontally by a long line that goes from the right of the scene to the left. Above the line is a carved depiction of a forest, below the line is a carved depiction of a geometric space.

4- This scene is a continuation of the geometric space, with both the detailed individual and the followers drawn travelling through it. The symbols that cover much of the background of this scene appear similar to those that are found on the wallpaper of Floor 0.

5- This scene is split into several scenes, each depicting the detailed figure traversing another space. These include a depiction of an ocean, drawn as a simple waving line, another settlement, drawn as large rectangles, and a scene that depicts the figure carved in a void.

6- A depiction of the figure encountering what appears to be a snake, painted with the same red as the sun from prior. The text’s location in this scene indicates the two are conversing.

7- The figure is consumed by the snake. The followers are once again depicted, observing the scene from afar.

8- The snake dominates the bottom of this scene, now bearing a large cut in its side. Above it is a depiction resembling Entity 459-1. It now stands atop a raised platform, with sunbeams in the same red as the snake radiating from it. The followers appear to bow before it.

Top of Dais- A large depiction of the snake from the later scenes of the dais’s sides. It coils around the square edges of the dais, with its head in the centre, tapering out further until ending on a point. The red utilised in other depictions of the snake paints the entire top of the dais.

# Entities and Objects-

## Entity 459-1

Entity 459-1 (known in shorthand as E459-1) are usually docile, humanoid entities that are native to Level 459, although they can occasionally be found exploring other levels. Their body-plan is similar to that of a human, with individual examples of these entities demonstrating variance in build, height, skin tone or other characteristics typical of normal human genetic variance. They have large eyes and pupils, long, often double jointed fingers, and wide mouths, alongside a throat sac similar to that of a frog, seemingly utilised for both storage and communication between individuals. They are able to dislocate their lower jaw to permit storage of valuables or food. They have several anomalous qualities, able to climb near frictionless surfaces or ceilings and navigate both between and within levels seemingly unerringly between specific locations. They are unusually co-ordinated and co-operative, seemingly able to communicate through a language unique to their species, consisting mostly of guttural throat noises and clicks produced by their sac. Many of them wear clothing, utilise tools or otherwise demonstrate intelligence roughly equivalent to a human, although lacking the more sophisticated knowledge of a modern human. Whilst in their passive state within the level, instances of E459 -1communicate with each other, engage in crafting, often producing tools or what appears to be artworks. Notably, E459-1 instances have never been seen eating, and lack juvenile members as other humanlike entities, such as facelings do. Attempts to communicate directly with instances of E459-1 have been unsuccessful, the entities are either unable to communicate with humans, or unwilling to.

Interaction with E459-1 has been categorised by the M.E.G into several categories, to help guide Wanderer and Civilian interaction with these entities.

### Out of Level Behaviours-

#### Wandering

E459-1 instances can be rarely located outside of their home level, usually in groups of 5 to 10 individuals. They usually appear to be scavenging, gathering objects from other levels and storing them within their throat sacs. Such groups usually avoid going near humans, and on human-dominated levels, such as Level 11, immediately attempt to exit the level. Interaction between these groups and other entities are usually passive, with both parties ignoring the other, unless one group attempts to harm the other. An exception to this rule is Partygoers, who are immediately hostile toward E459-1 instances, and as a consequence draw their immediate ire in return. E459-1 instances terminate individuals of their own species who are harmed by Partygoers, prioritising this over the termination of the Partygoer themselves. This may be of use to a wanderer, as leveraging the hostility between these two entity groups may allow an individual to escape either group if needs be, although use of this strategy is by its nature inconsistent.

#### Hunting

E459-1 instances engaging in hunting behaviours can be identified by their use of weaponry and reduced communication between instances within a group, usually consisting of a party around 10-15 individuals in size. Hunting groups are only formed when a specific individual entity or human has been actively threatening the collective found on Level 459, or when the knife usually found upon the dais has been taken. These hunting groups seek their target across several levels, and can usually be found guarding commonly utilised throughways, if their target is not carrying the knife. If the target is carrying the knife, the hunting groups appear to be unerringly capable of tracking the individual carrying it at any given time. Whoever possesses the knife at the time of its location is usually killed, after which the knife is reclaimed. Following this, normal hunting procedures follow for individuals who had carried the knife prior to its loss, should it have traded hands. The M.E.G does not recommend the theft of the knife as a consequence, despite its potential usefulness as a tool within large scale combat, leveraging the Entities hunting behaviour is likely to cause unnecessary collateral damage to both the individual leveraging it, as well as any others who have held the knife.

### In Level Behaviours

Level 459 interacts anomalously with E459-1, appearing to grant them additional anomalous traits, and consequently additional behaviours. In addition, as the entities appear to regard the level as their personal domain, they tend towards territorial behaviour within the level.

#### Passive

Whilst in their passive state, in which instances of E459-1 spend the majority of their time, the entities engage in what appears to be conversation and leisure activities. Whilst in this state, they do not engage with humans unless directly spoken to. Long-term observation of this state has led the M.E.G to believe that the entities appear to operate as smaller units within their broader society, grouping around similar cavities, exchanging gifts and conversation, and occasionally no-clipping as groups, presumably to engage in wandering activities. The entities have not been observed to sleep. It is safe for wanderers to remain within the level whilst E459-1 instances are in this state, although the M.E.G encourages wanderers to report any unusual activities observed whenever possible.

#### Chime

Once every approximately 20 hour period, the entities end their existing behaviours, and exit their cavities immediately. Each individual approaches the ground, and prostrate themselves before the large dais in the centre of the room. This process lasts around five minutes after the final individual has reached the floor. Headcounts of individuals during this time has turned up around 1000 individuals, varying due to fluctuations in group size, due to Wandering groups and hunters. Wandering groups also complete this action if possible, regardless of circumstance. Hunting groups do not. Humans that attempt to interrupt this process are pushed backwards to the furthest edge of the room. Repeating interruptions eventually draws attacks from the entities, and further interruptions increase the intensity of this violence until the individual is killed. Wanderers may remain during this time, so long as they do not accidentally or intentionally antagonise the entities. The M.E.G encourages wanderers to travel as far as is possible toward the wall, as the entities appear to prize proximity to the raised dais. When occupying this level for an extended period of time, it is a good idea to set alarms for this time, so as to avoid being asleep during the chime.

#### Defence

E459-1 instances grow immediately hostile towards individuals that attempt to enter occupied cavities in the wall. This hostility intensifies with repeating attempts, starting with the individual being pushed from the cavity, and intensifying over time, until the instance may kill the individual, after around 7-10 attempted intrusions. An individual who intentionally harms or kills an instance of E459-1 is permanently barred from engaging in their future trading behaviours, and is immediately subject to hunting behaviour. Should the individual then escape the level, hunting behaviour goes on for 6-7 months if an instance of E459-1 was harmed, and for as long as the individual is alive, if an instance of E459-1 was killed. The M.E.G recommends that individuals avoid antagonising these entities as a result. If you have harmed an instance of E459-1 the M.E.G requests that you avoid occupying well populated levels for the duration of your hunting period, as your presence may place civilians at risk, or would force the M.E.G to antagonise an otherwise docile entity group.

#### Trade

The behaviour of E459-1 is of most interest to the M.E.G, presenting a means of acquiring otherwise in-accessible or dangerous to retrieve materials.

Any object placed upon the dais for an extended period of time will eventually draw the attention of an instance of E459-1, which will inspect the object, before either taking it back into the cavities in the walls, or leaving it upon the dais to be reclaimed. This behaviour will not occur if organisms of any kind are within 10 metres of the dais, or if visibly armed individuals are within 40 metres of the dais. If the object is judged suitable by the instance, it shall be taken back to the cavity it exited from, and another instance of E459-1 will climb down to leave another object in the taken object’s place.

Objects unique to the backrooms, such as reality fresheners, almond water or the bodies of any given entity group will never provide successful trade, as will any more mundane object taken from a backrooms level. It is unknown by what means E459-1 instances are able to determine the providence of such objects. Only objects taken from the Frontrooms can be traded, replaced with higher quality, often anomalous equivalents. Objects gained by trade cannot be returned, presumably due to originating within the backrooms. A list of trades can be found below, to act as a guide for trading with these Entities. Trades have not been repeated, due to the limited, valuable and often sentimental nature of these Frontroom commodities. The M.E.G requests that wanderers report any further trades directly to them, in order to further update this list.

### Experiment Log 1

A civilian by the name of Sheila Marie agreed to engage in fatal testing on behalf of the M.E.G. She was accompanied by M.E.G Researcher Herman Knox, who had suggested the experiment. Marie was a 87 year old female, who arrived in the backrooms around 5 years ago. She occupied level 11 for the majority of her time spent within the backrooms, and after her second year, was found to have contracted cancer, a condition that grew significantly more severe in the months prior to the procedure. Failing the possibility of any proper medical treatment for her disease, Marie was put forward by the M.E.G Medical board to undergo experimental treatment, utilising the anomalous properties of Level 459, based on the theories of Knox.

The following material was taken directly from Herman Knox’s real time log of the experiments progress. All time is taken on assumed military time, referred to as Backrooms Standard Time (BRST).

Experiment Report By: Dr Herman Knox, M.E.G Researcher.

Date Beginning: 18/02/23 Time Beginning: 1430

18/02/23-20/02/23 [Prior to direct reporting of events within the level, I have chosen to leave unreported the process of arriving within Level 459. Travelling from Level 11 to the research site took around two days, without unstudied anomalous intervention.]

0931- Subject, following arrival within the level, expresses exhaustion expected of her age and medical condition. At her urging, the task-force has set up camping equipment, alongside preparing my equipment for the observation of the test. Instances of E459-1 are present within the Level, as standard, and behaving in a manner usual of their Passive State.

1005- Subject, whilst at rest, has began to report audio/visual hallucinations. These have been reported as ‘a strange hissing’ and ‘dark shadows in my peripheral vision’. Subject has shown no prior history of hallucination, indicating that this is an anomalous affect specifically targeting the subject.

1023- Subject has shown increasing distress at the intensity of audio/visual hallucinations, expressing an increase in these anomalous effects. Following psychological investigation, subject has been found psychologically able to continue testing, and has expressed interest in the completion of the experiment, at an increased rate. Instances of E459-1 have been identified by my team members as appearing to take interest in the Subject. It is suspected they are causing these hallucinations.

1027- Subject taken toward dais in centre of courtyard. Whilst this occurred, she expressed increased audio/visual hallucinations, alongside mania or delusions, stating that ‘it’s trying to tell me something’ repeatedly. When asked to elaborate on these delusions, subject gestured wildly towards the walls, before walking on her own toward the dais.

1039- Subject has been in trance for several minutes, muttering to herself. Attempts to terminate experiment without harm to subject have been unsuccessful. Instances of E459-1 have demonstrated restlessness, have not yet indicated actual aggression, although my team has been ordered to guard the dais.

1055- Subject awakened from trance. Following another psychological evaluation, as well as brief physiological testing to determine possible physical effects from trance, subject has been found sound of body and mind, and therefore able to continue experiment. Attempts to terminate experiment have been resisted by subject, who has demonstrated increased enthusiasm for completion of experiment when out of trance.

1101- Subject terminated utilising knife, as agreed.

1106- Subject’s body taken from dais by instance of E459-1. As opposed to usual behaviour of travelling back to crevice from which they arrived, instance of E459-1 travelled directly upwards for a significant quantity of time, eventually out of view of observers, due to intense light glare. Instance has yet to return.

1136- Instance has yet to return.

1202- Instance returned, bearing several articles of clothing, as standard for a trade involving human clothes, alongside a white, egg-like object. Object appears opalescent and warm to the touch. Object taken in for further study. Subject’s body has not been returned. Instances of E459-1 appear to celebrate upon egg being recovered from dais, before returning to initial passive state.

Egg-like object taken into M.E.G custody for further study, designated Entity 459-2 (E459-2).

End Log.

## Entity 459-2

[[collapsible show="Show Entity 459-2 (Outdated File)" hide="Hide Entity File"]]

An egg like object, weighing around 1.5kg, with an opalescent surface, similar to the substance found at the bottom of the crevices within Level 459. Classed as an entity for its ability to move independently, but seemingly incapable of thought or behaviour beyond rolling toward the closest source of almond water and absorbing it, at a rate of 1 litre per hour, through pores that cover its entire surface, only visible when entity is observed. Testing has determined that E459-2 is capable of self preservation, avoiding active sources of harm, and moving towards safer sources of almond water, even when doing so involves moving further than moving towards a threatened one. Entity has been provided source of almond water for further testing.

Entity 459-2 hatched from its prior egg form, after the aforementioned object had consumed 70 litres of almond water. It presents nearly identically in personality to Sheila Marie, with a few key differences, discussed further below. However, it appears as an instance of E459-1, with genetic similarities to Sheila Marie, matching her hair and skin tone, eye colour and similar details. It appears to have health roughly equivalent to a human of 25-30 years old, and shows no sign of developing cancer. Psychologically, it varies from Marie in its inability to comprehend the Frontrooms, apparently believing itself to have been born, and lived its entire life in the backrooms, with all former memories apparently altered so as to have taken place in various backrooms levels. In addition, it appears innately able to understand the language of E459-1, but is able to speak english, although falteringly. It takes part in the Chime behaviour, but otherwise appears disinterested in engaging with other instances of E459-1. E459-2 has expressed interest in becoming a Wanderer, and with direct oversight from Herman Knox, shall be permitted to wander the backrooms, in exchange for semi-regular reports and psychological evaluations, to determine the effectiveness of utilising the anomalous properties of Level 459 for medical purposes in future.

# Bases/Outposts/Persons of Interest

The Level itself is known to be unoccupied, due to high quantities of entity occupation. The B.T.N.G is known to take regular trips to the Level in order to trade located Frontroom goods for valuable commodities. Otherwise, the M.E.G encourages wanderers to avoid settling within this level for extended periods, as doing so may prove detrimental to the study of the Level.

# Entrances and Exits

## Entrances

As part of their Wandering behaviour, Entity 459-1 will occasionally paint snake-like symbols on various walls, utilising a red pigment. Making direct physical contact with one of these symbols will transport an individual to Level 459.

## Exits

Crawling into an unoccupied crevice and falling asleep will return an individual to the level they arrived from.

No-Clipping directly through the floor of the level will result in a momentary sensation of heat, and a strong sensation of being watched, before translocating an individual into the ocean of Level 8.
