I'm not a greenlighter but here's some crit:

[SPaG](javascript:;)

[I am going to put in italics what you originally wrote and my corrections in bold](javascript:;)

*"Level 882 is an ideal resting place for wanderers and perhaps even a potential home for those who wish to end there backrooms journey."*  
**It's easy to confuse there and their but there but in this context you would use "their".**  
*"In 2003 the M.E.G discovered level 882, though the rhombusens have been in Level 882 since 1967."*  
**Capitalize level when you say "Level 882".**  
*"After spending 7 months on level 5 it seemed that he spent a prolonged amount of time viewing a tropical beach painting before eventually being chased by a Growler and fell into it. "*  
**Again capitalize level when talking about a specific level such as Level 5. Also say "chased by a growler and falling into it"**  
*"The rhombus moon worshiped by the "natives" of level 882 has undergone extensive research and theorizing to attempt to better understand it's properties."*  
**Level 882 (capitalize level when referring to a specific level)**
