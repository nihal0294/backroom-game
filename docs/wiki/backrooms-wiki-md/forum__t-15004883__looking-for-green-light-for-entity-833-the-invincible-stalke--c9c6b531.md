> Habitat: Odd numbered Levels

I'd capitalize "numbered" here too since that's a thing that most entity pages do. Also, add a rating module in the page, the CSS for it can be found here: [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/templates](templates.md)

> Entity 833 are a species of entities

This is like saying "this floor is made out of floor"; it's redundant information, remove this from the sentence.

> They also do not have blood and are not fully visible.

"are not fully visible" makes it sound like the entities are invisible, which is not the case. (i think so, at least)

> within 15-70 years old

I'd change "old" to "of age".

> This entity is docile to those that are not between 15-70 years old.

There could be some more explanation on how the entity can detect how old the human they find is; currently, it seems like an odd detail.

> Only when it decides to attack the human will it reveal its true form

I'd remove "will" and add a comma after "human".

> to impress the person. The exact method of

I'd change the period to a semicolon.

> are the victim's eyes, brain and skull. Everything else that is part

I'd replace the period with a semicolon.

> Everything else that is part of the victim is eaten by Entity 833.

How is this known? Add some further explanation there.

> body to "inject" victim's brain.

Add "the" after "'inject'".

> There is no known way to continue reverse the process.

Continue seems like an odd word to use there; maybe you could explain this a bit more? Makes the reader wonder tbh.

> Entity 833 cannot be photographed as it makes pictures of it distorted.

"Entity 833 cannot be photographed" if it only makes pictures of it distorted, this is untrue. Elaborate that is can not be seen clearly in its photos.
