Hey, look at this, Dragon is coming!

> it's not recommended to stay outside for too long as the gloom could take over your mind making you very hostile to other wanderers, so only go outside when necessary

It's too long, and the first letter is not capitalized. There are many questions about this statement. Since the haze will occupy your mind, why do you need to go out? Also, where is the period?

> According to your schedule, you must proceed to your first-period classroom within 10 minutes before class starts.

So, what is schedule and why do I have to attend classes? I have been wondering if I have missed something, but in reality, I haven't. What would happen if I didn't go to the library or attend classes? Will I explode?

> The cafeteria is where you can get your lunch, usually served by ladies in their late 40s, and socialize and trade with other students. you can also get snacks from the vending machine

Who are these people? Are they humans or entities? Also, you forgot the period once again.

> The students are humanoid entities who mostly reside in classrooms but can also be found in the library, cafeteria, or GYM.

Do these students have the same biological characteristics as humans? Do they have any special abilities? Will they shine?

> The Ones With The Gloom are very hostile entities but used to be students and survivors who succumbed to the Gloom, they now roam outside of the campus looking for anyone to attack, they should never be approached, as they will attack anyone they see.

What do they look like, like normal humans? How do they attack humans? Spit fire? Or use weapons?

> The Teachers are seen in classrooms teaching students or entertaining them, they are mostly docile unless you go outside the classroom without their permission.

What is their purpose. To be honest, I don't really like a humanoid entity that appears as an NPC.

> The gloom is the most dangerous part of the level and should be avoided at all costs, if you go outside then you must hold your breathe or wear a mask.

Capitalize the first letter of The Gloom.

> if you breathe into the gloom then the first effect you feel is a sore throat which can be treated with almond water, the second effect you feel is your vision being a little blurry and seeing purple, and once you see purple 𝑻𝒉𝒆𝒏 𝑰𝒕'𝒔 𝑻𝒐𝒐 𝑳𝒂𝒕𝒆 𝑻𝒐 𝑺𝒂𝒗𝒆 𝒀𝒐𝒖𝒓𝒔𝒆𝒍𝒇, 𝑻𝒉𝒆 𝑮𝒍𝒐𝒐𝒎 𝑯𝒂𝒔 𝑻𝒂𝒌𝒆𝒏 𝑶𝒗𝒆𝒓 𝒀𝒐𝒖𝒓 𝑴𝒊𝒏𝒅 𝑨𝒏𝒅 𝒀𝒐𝒖 𝑯𝒂𝒗𝒆 𝑱𝒐𝒊𝒏𝒆𝒅 𝒖𝒔, 𝒀𝒐𝒖 𝑨𝒓𝒆 𝑶𝒏𝒆 𝑾𝒊𝒕𝒉 𝑻𝒉𝒆 𝑮𝒍𝒐𝒐𝒎.

I am curious why you classify The Gloom as a phenomenon. I think it's more like a virus.

> 𝑪𝒐𝒎𝒆 𝑶𝒖𝒕𝒔𝒊𝒅𝒆, 𝑻𝒉𝒆 𝑮𝒍𝒐𝒐𝒎 𝑰𝒔 𝑽𝒆𝒓𝒚 𝑷𝒓𝒆𝒕𝒕𝒚.

Okay, this idea is too outdated. I feel the atmosphere of Partygoers. Please make your article unique.

> Nolan's Journal Log Found In A Classroom

Who is Nolan?

> Abigail can also be found reading a book or doing homework for a class.

Do I still have to do homework at Level 285?! Okay, after reading your previous description, I still don't know that.

> the second effect you feel is your vision being a little blurry and seeing purple, and once you see purple 𝑻𝒉𝒆𝒏 𝑰𝒕'𝒔 𝑻𝒐𝒐 𝑳𝒂𝒕𝒆 𝑻𝒐 𝑺𝒂𝒗𝒆 𝒀𝒐𝒖𝒓𝒔𝒆𝒍𝒇, 𝑻𝒉𝒆 𝑮𝒍𝒐𝒐𝒎 𝑯𝒂𝒔 𝑻𝒂𝒌𝒆𝒏 𝑶𝒗𝒆𝒓 𝒀𝒐𝒖𝒓 𝑴𝒊𝒏𝒅 𝑨𝒏𝒅 𝒀𝒐𝒖 𝑯𝒂𝒗𝒆 𝑱𝒐𝒊𝒏𝒆𝒅 𝒖𝒔, 𝒀𝒐𝒖 𝑨𝒓𝒆 𝑶𝒏𝒆 𝑾𝒊𝒕𝒉 𝑻𝒉𝒆 𝑮𝒍𝒐𝒐𝒎.

What happened to seeing the purple wanderers? Did they mutate? Did they explode? Still dead?

> and with these Chromebooks you can access websites like The Backrooms Database, CoolMathGames, and other unblocked websites to keep yourself sane and prevent boredom in this level, there are also board games to play and books to read to keep yourself sane and entertained.

Ah, this makes me want to keep playing computer games here. So why is the survival difficulty class 3 here?

> Those who fail to make it to class on time must go to the library since the library doors are always open.

Why doesn't The Gloom appear in the library, even though its doors are open?

> Masks are also provided in the classrooms so you don't inhale the gloom when you go outside.

The Gloom becomes harmless in this way. I think surviving at this level is too easy.

> The School’s Salesmen consist of exactly 23 members.

Why did you use Chinese apostrophes here? Not good SPaG.

> You could leave Level 285 by going outside which will take you to Level 147 or Level 9, but it's very dangerous as the Campus security will take you back to your classroom or entities outside the campus will try and kill you, so use this escape route at your own risk

Are there any entities outside the campus? Not mentioned earlier. In addition, there is still no period, the third time.

After reading the entire article, I think this concept is too simple. This is just a school, you need to attend classes and do homework. It's very dangerous outside, with (unclear number of) entities and unknown viruses. So you should enrich this concept. My suggestion is to allow these entities to blend in with the students and make them suspicious of each other, in order to increase the fun of this article.

I really hope to see it released, but I think it needs a little modification. You can stick to your ideas and explain them, and I will be happy to answer any of your questions.
