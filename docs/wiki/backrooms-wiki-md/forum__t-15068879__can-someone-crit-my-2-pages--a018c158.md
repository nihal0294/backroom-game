> disappearance of Level 282.

Level 282 has been deleted by its author; using the wayback machine is a funny work around, though it will not work in this aspect as its generally rude to the author who wanted their work to no longer be mentioned on the actual site. Please remove all mentions of this from your page and substitute it for another level that would make sense for its context.

> ramp that goes downwards into infinity.

I get why this was only stated as "infinity" but I feel like it'd be more fitting to say something like "a cold and inhospitable abyss" or something of the sort to actually convey this emptiness to the reader more effectively.

> Impossible environments, so fake yet real, start

I'd like some more vivid details about what these environments look like. It's just really vague currently and makes it hard to picture what's actually going on here.

> All of them have been confirmed to not be hallucinated

I'd rewrite this bit as:

\*All of them have been confirmed to not be hallucinations caused by the human psyche\*

> Open Survival Guide

Don't have the survival guide be a collapsible; there's no real reason to.

> Oxygen tanks/Gas masks

"Gas" shouldn't be capitalized here.

> Food and water (that's a no-brainer)

Remove the information inside this parentheses, it's very unnecessary here and doesn't really provide the reader any more insight into why it was added here, as the other parentheses try to do.

> found to feed on wanderers' sanities

"sanity" is no longer something that's allowed to be mentioned on the Backrooms site. Remove all references of it from your page.

## Round two baby!

---

> Your Search for "Level 513" matched 2 results!

Don't capitalize "Search" in this instance.

> any artificial intelligence, such as drones.

Drones aren't the best example of artificial intelligence tbh; I'd personally change the wording to just say artificial constructs instead.

> Let's take a look at them.

Just plainly remove this; it doesn't add anything to the section and makes it seem pretty unprofessional.

> to a substance named 'Obsidian' from

"Obsidian" shouldn't be capitalized; why are you treating this real world resource so weirdly? Real word items exist in the Backrooms, it doesn't need to be covered in such an odd manner lmao.

> from the real world.

This is rather unprofessional; I'd rather you just say the Frontrooms instead to be completely honest.

> tunnel also breaks into many different

Add "off" after "breaks".

> all of which wind into a dead end

Replace "wind" with "lead" as that would make more sense for this context.

> The Drone Colony

"colony" is no longer a term used on the wiki; please remove all mentions of it from your page.

> The drone ended up in Level 513.

Add a link to Level 513 so that the reader can get some context.

> Entity 666: Hello there, my dear  
> Trevor: Uhmm… what do you want?  
> Entity 666: I see you've tried to kill me, huh?

Okay, this is where most of my issues with the page boil down to. This isn't remotely how Entity 666 behaves and the entity also isn't capable of talking! The author said "It doesn’t even speak nor does it have a god complex" when I showed this to them; I'd really recommend that you work with the actual author of Entity 666, of whom I can get you in contact with if needed, to really nail down what Entity 666 is like.
