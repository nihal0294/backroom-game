Mostly due to my own doubts about how he should've been classified. I had previously intended and to some extent still do intend for him to be an POI, but at the time of writing I felt that he was less of a wanderer of considerable notoriety than someone who'd been in the Backrooms so long that they'd adapted anomalously to its oddities to such an extent as to be tantamount to an entity.

It's for that reason that I wrote him as an entity, although I have considered moving him in the past, I've just never gotten around to doing it.
