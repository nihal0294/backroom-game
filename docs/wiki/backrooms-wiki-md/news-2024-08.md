# The end of Roots2024

---

![Missing alt text.](https://archive.org/download/backrooms-photos/807OREGON/DSC00164.jpg)

The rooms are so back.

Crazy to think that in the last few weeks, we not only got the origin of the Backrooms picture, but also 109 other very liminal pictures of the original Backrooms location right? Welp, roots2024 certainly will make this event remembered through writing forever. Seeing so many people use those unassuming images differently has been incredible for such a small writing blitz. Some members used the same image in different ways, showing how creative an author can be with the same base material, while others imagined wild ways to use those pictures outside of their context. We heavily encourage you all to check those pages for yourself and see how Hobbytown has been once again an incredible source of creativity.  
  
Thanks to all participants, and we hope the wiki enjoyed this little writing blitz! We look forward to making more of those little events outside the classic official contests, so we hope to see you all there as well!

# Deletion threshold II electric boogaloo

---

![Missing alt text.](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a7/Eug%C3%A8ne_Delacroix_-_La_libert%C3%A9_guidant_le_peuple.jpg/640px-Eug%C3%A8ne_Delacroix_-_La_libert%C3%A9_guidant_le_peuple.jpg)

TS when new system (2024 colorized)

Oh man this change was definitely something.  
  
It's been three months since the introduction of the new posting policy. Three crits to post, and a higher deletion threshold. During that time, we got a big increase in new posted pages, but also an increase in deleted pages (obviously). After some discussions with authors and other community members, it is clear that the system is flawed in some way. After those three months, we have seen that:

- Unlike our expectations, the policy hasn't allowed people to improve better, quite the contrary actually, as downvotes became more of a danger to the page than a way to express criticism and help for improvement.
- the fear factor is also something we didn't account for. Even established authors feel scared about their page's potential deletion.
- Lastly, the lack of readers also make the current total vote number needed feel very difficult, which puts some perfectly good articles near the threat of deletion.

After some discussions with the GLMP, we doubled the grace period to account for the lack of quick voters and make the power of a single downvote less present. During September's period, we will closely look at the deletions and ratings, as well as the author's feelings, in order to find a way to tweak it to allow for this criticism and improvement mind that is necessary for the wiki moving forward while keeping a positive and healthy posting ratio.  
  
If you feel a little lost by staff's direction as of late, give our [site direction](http://backrooms-oversight.wikidot.com/site-direction-summary) a read! It contains a summary of everything staff has been discussing about the site, and what we plan to work on to make this community a growing place.

# A look into the future

---

There's a lot you can expect in the next few weeks and months!

- First, we are actively working with our international branches (as well as with new ones!) to bring you a new improved translation system. Hopefully this allows this wiki to discover other expansions of the lore by different cultures and countries!

- We are also debating on the future of the GLMP, which isn't as needed to post new pages as it used to be. The case of the Critic Mentoring Program is also discussed, with possible ways to improve the way we passively train critics.

- Discussions about a lore guide and a potential cohesive site canon are also ongoing, although they are still in their early stages.

- You can also expect an improved disc system, with more clearly defined sanctions in the future.

- Two components are currently/will be worked on, but for different reasons. While the Concord component is currently being fixed for official integration on the wiki, the classic level class is also getting some changes in the future, although nothing is set in stone right now.

- And more events! Whether they are reoccurring or one-of-a-kind like roots2024, we want to see more activity on this wiki, and are working toward this goal.

Do you think staff should focus on something specific, or even something we have no plans on yet? Let us know!

I hope you liked this little report of the month! If you have any feedback about it, about the elements showcased, the structures or some suggestions about stuff to talk about, make sure to let me know!  
  
And with that said, I hope to see you next month here as well!
