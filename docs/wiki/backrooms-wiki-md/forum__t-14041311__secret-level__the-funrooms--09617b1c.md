Re: Secret Level: The Funrooms

07 Apr 2021 02:19

I'm unsure of what to make of this. The level sounds far too similar to Level 283, which oddly enough, would be able to stand on its own without any mentions of the Partygoers. The idea of the Partydemons completely undermines the concept of Partygoers. Based on the extremely limited description of them, they're supposed to and will be better than Partygoers. The "Tale" is far too limited and sounds more like a Reddit post more than anything.The two wanderers simply dying and not allowing readers to feel any sort of connection to them seems really dull, and not quite attention-grabbing as it can be believed. Not to mention the lack of any team or outpost whatsoever, despite the miraculously helpful objects like Almond Water? The description in your Secret Level page is, again, far too small. Perhaps if the Level or Tale can be less dependent on the concept of Partygoers, or change the Level's description entirely whilst keeping the concept, then it could be considered.
