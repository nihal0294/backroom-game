20 Sep 2021 11:29

Quick first impressions:

- Would be nice if there’s a link to a larger goal. In Level 230 you mention ships arriving at the port. Expand on that, and provide speculation and theories about what they’re trying to accomplish. Ask:

1. What do they want FROM wanderers? Why might they need the resources?

2. Why intentionally attempt to harm wanderers? What could their goals be? (Perhaps link this to the other entities of Level 230 that they seem to coordinate with)

- For most of the article, I was confused at how they impersonated FTB traders *before* the FTB existed, until you explained in Discovery that they only started appearing *after*. **Either way, an entity mimicking such a specific and recently-formed group is interesting, and I suggest you mention how strange it is!** You could explain that with a comment on their intelligence being pretty high - it is theorized that they ‘learnt’ to do this, perhaps? Or even better - there is some unseen force within the level that created them, because it knows that people moved in. Or something like that

- Interview log would give Baggers more character and allow them to express themselves
