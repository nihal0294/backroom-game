-1 but great potential

12 Aug 2026 07:52

I like the idea of the constant horror of having a terrifying fate that happens after the level pressures you enough, and the idea that even someone aware of it could succomb to the temptation, but it's not really that present in the story itself.

The narrative also feels kinda generic. The character isn't all that unique in how they act and feel, and their story of falling there, getting tempted, then joining the survivor feels like a textbook presentation of what happens to wanderers here instead of "what would happen with that specific guy". There's nothing leading me to be interested in what they might do next (maybe explore, maybe figure out something big) because there isn't really a hook that would lead me to think the story is gonna have a unique twist.

I do appreciate the formatting of the end, with the pictures, as well as the parts where the character wonders about the rot below. They're the most creative and interesting parts of the level, and even if they don't do enough to hook me on what's essentially the context page of a yet-to-come larger story, I do believe you can make the following page more catchy for us, since you do seem to have something interesting planned for it.
