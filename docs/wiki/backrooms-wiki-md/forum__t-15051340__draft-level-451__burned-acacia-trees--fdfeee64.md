Hi Fulbion! First of all, welcome to the site! I have the itching feeling that you will contribute greatly to the site, and really want to see your ideas published, as I do for any member.

Next, your level idea. I think this is a pretty cool idea. Of course, I can't judge much as you have a very small idea in a draft, but I will be checking to see if you update it further. Imo, I think you could add a certain "safe area". This could be some large dirt area in the very center or just a section that never lights.

I also think that maybe 10 minutes of flame is a bit short, and it could rage for maybe, idk, 1-1.5 hrs? Also, add the temperature in Farenheight for the 'mericans like me. I just don't get celsius lol.

Here's one tip I could give you: I'm currently awaiting greenlight on [this](forum__t-15040108__level-148__the-living-level--32d1738f.md) level, and I do happen to have a different type of very powerful flame I used, which I called "Infinifire". You could maybe check it out and see if you want to use it in your level.

As for your class 3 question, I think it fits. You should customize the class module to say "environmental hazards" or class 3-E. It doesn't have to have entities for it to kill you.

I am on WAY too often and will critique legit anything u wanna show me, so feel free to PM me at . I can answer questions, give crit, and brainstorm ideas for you.

Best of luck, and happy writing!
