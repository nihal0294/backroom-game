07 Sep 2023 08:09

I felt doing something like that would have been extremely cheap. I didn't want to cover an actual imminent threat someone was facing so much as a threat to their comfort, which I think is a type of worry that WOULD happen in the Backrooms, but isn't touched on often.

This person isn't going to get killed by this other guy anyway, he's obsessed but he wouldn't like hurt her or anything, he'd be easy to escape (or you could just tell the M.E.G. you don't want to go and they wouldn't take you there) — a physical threat would be hard to justify

Edit: that being said I did escalate it slightly. I wanted to imply that this would be a pretty terribly lonely existence for this guy apart from his obsession with this lady, and that she would have a terrible time with just him there. So I included the line near the bottom

> I like that we'd be alone if we were here!

and that's as far as I wanted to take it
