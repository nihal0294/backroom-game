Ye they *technically* are. However, people tend to associate them with the SCP foundation, which breaks the immersion of the read a bit. That's why people on this site don't like them too much, and it's understandable.

I personally would avoid using them unless it is strictly, 100% necessary. If missused it can also lead to the interpretation that the author didn't feel like writing that part of the article; it can be seen as lazy overall. So as penorsaber said, be careful.
