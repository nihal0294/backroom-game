Re: Does an Objectcon post count towards an unrequired greenlight and the 3 articles needed for an Auth

13 Aug 2022 03:06

They do. In a somewhat recent announcement from CutTheBirch over on the Discord, he said the following:

"Additionally, the staff team has decided that we will be making slight changes to the greenlight policy in that contest entries will have a higher upvote threshold to count as one of the three required articles for an author to get greenlight exemption. Staff has not decided on an exact upvote number yet, and we will update you when we come to that decision."

Alongside pushing back the posting period and limiting it to one submission per person, articles posted for the contest will be able to qualify towards greenlight exemption. Although it will be with a higher upvote count, which is yet to be determined.
