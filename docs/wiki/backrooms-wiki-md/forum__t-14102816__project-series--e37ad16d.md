"Project" series

18 May 2021 16:50

**Page Type:** Tale, Entity, Secret/Sub Level

**Genre:** Action, Mass Loretm

**Page Layout (Optional):** I'll be honest, I don't know how to do screw formats, so it's probably going to be pretty standard for a while before I figure stuff out.

**Elevator Pitch:** Young wanderer goes missing, accidentally obtains godly powers, tries to keep people from leaving the Backrooms while they figure out how to get rid of it all.

**Central Narrative:** A wanderer, while exploring the negative levels, goes too far down, and ends up in the SH4DY GR3Y, at which point they escape the final level. Afterward, they end up stuck in a weird "null" space and manage to contact the MEG; explaining their situation. Hilarity ensues, and they end up trying to help out as many people as possible in the time they have left.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** There's finally a good reason for people to not want to get out of there.

**Additional Notes:** Just the usual, this is my first-ever idea for here, I already have a Tale thing on my sandbox ready that lays out the basic idea, if anyone wants to see.
