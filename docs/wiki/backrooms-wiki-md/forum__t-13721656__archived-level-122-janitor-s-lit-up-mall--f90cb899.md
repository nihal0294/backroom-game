Though this is an interesting concept, there's a considerable amount of problems that make me go "huh?"

1 - How do neon rays charge phones? This feels like a "fuck it, why not" thing, and not a well thought-out feature.  
Secondly, this would be quite harmful to batteries - being used while being charged is not helpful at all.

2 - Though SPaG issues are present throughout the entirety of this level, which don't allow for immersion, THERE IS A HUGE AMOUNT of weirdly connected sentences, especially at the beginning of the article. As such, I found myself paying less attention to the actual Level, and spent most of my time fixing these problems in my head. Which led me to barely pay attention to the actual content of the article.

3 - What even is The Server? Why is it invisible and also terrible at trades, but generous and willing to lend you basic supplies? There is so much to be explained here. It took me a while to realize they were an Entity.

4 - Why is cooking a "Special" going to /tp you to whatever Level you thought of???

There is many confusion in my mind x-x much questions
