I'm writing a tale that's a script for the "Baking with Blanche" show mentioned in [the Actor's PoI article](lines-please.md) per popular demand in TS. To do this, I need baking recipes for the Actor to show off, except with each successive baked item getting more fucked up in some manner (replacing ingredients, just doing weird shit to the ingredients, etc).

If you have any recipes for baked items (including any dishes that just require using an oven to bake like casseroles or things that get finished in the oven in a skillet), please drop them here. I'll include you in the author collapsible credits for each recipe.

No need to list the whole recipe exactly! A basic idea of ingredients and steps is generally all I need. I'll probably use what is offered here as a springboard to look for things.

Also, you have to be okay with the recipe getting really messed up. It's gonna be used for sillyfunny weirdness, after all.

I have an idea for a recipe

It's about baking something random

Ingredients:  
1 teaspoon of vanilla extract  
2 cups of flour  
1 cup of sugar  
2 eggs  
1 stick of butter  
1 teaspoon of almond extract  
1 teaspoon of baking soda

Instructions:  
1. Start by putting one stick (250g) of butter into the bowl  
2. Then add 1 cup of sugar  
3. Put two eggs  
4. Beat them all together  
5. Put 2 cups of flour  
6. Put 1 teaspoon of almond extract and 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract.  
7. Beat them all together  
8. Use anything to shape them however you like and bake them for 30 minutes at 320°F (160°C). And then you may add any decorations you like.

I watched some baking tutorials and came up with this idea. So you can mess up with the recipe.
