What would you guys do if you were promoted to Overseer within the M.E.G.?

Imagine you found yourself in The Backrooms in the early years of [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) and quickly got integrated as an operative. Not only were you a veteran within [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md), but you also were the mind behind many missions, and your ideology for the group quickly got you a lot of support. You climbed [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) corporate ladder, gaining respect and support from many members until you were eventually nominated and elected as an Overseer.

What would you do? Which levels would you set missions on? How would you make [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) the most powerful group and conquer the entire Backrooms?
