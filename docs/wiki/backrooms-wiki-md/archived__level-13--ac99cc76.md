# **Description:**

**Level 13** is a seemingly infinite apartment building with an extremely high number of floors.[1](javascript:;) Within each floor, there are many small residences, each with a number on the door. These numbers are in a system where it lists the floor, the section of the floor, and the apartment number.[2](javascript:;) The current design of the building is similar to that of typical 1980's architecture. The walls in the hallway are white, and the carpet is of a brown, geometrical design. This design is consistent throughout all floors.

The main transportation between floors on the level are the stairways that are located on each floor. These are the main means of intra-level transport because all [Elevators](entity-81.md) will take you to other levels, not transporting you through **Level 13**. Another prominent entity on this level are [Windows](entity-2.md). These will usually show a bright blue void on the exterior of them, and they are less hostile on this level. However, these [Windows](entity-2.md) are still very dangerous, so be sure to avoid them.

[Wi-Fi](object-21.md) is present on this floor and available for use. Computers can be found within some apartments, along with many other materials, so this level is very useful for supplies. You are also able to claim housing within **Level 13**, but you should be sure to claim housing away from any [Clump](entity-5.md) infestations. If you want to claim housing, make sure to go down to the lobby first.

The lobby is on Floor 0, and within it, there will be a single [Faceling](entity-9.md) attending the front desk. This [Faceling](entity-9.md) is unresponsive unless you ask for an apartment. If you do, you will receive an apartment key from this Entity. This key can be used to open up its respective apartment and it allows you to have an apartment on **Level 13**.

## **Entities:**

Entities present on this floor are rare, however, these can be rather dangerous. These include [Clumps](entity-5.md), [Windows](entity-2.md), [Elevators](entity-81.md), [Smilers](entity-3.md), and [Deathmoths](archived__entity-4--35e09fc6.md). [Clumps](entity-5.md) are most commonly found within cabinets in apartments and other enclosed places. It is recommended to keep apartments illuminated due to [Smilers](entity-3.md) and [Deathmoths](archived__entity-4--35e09fc6.md). Stay away from all [Windows](entity-2.md) as they are most likely an Entity.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

## **Entrances:**

- Entering an apartment building on [Level 11](level-11.md).
- No-Clipping through the floor on [Level 12](level-12.md).
- No-Clipping into a yellow wall on [Level 289](level-289.md).

## **Exits:**

- No-Clipping through a red wall will lead you to [Level 287](level-287.md).
- No-Clipping through the floor will cause you to fall down every floor. Once you reach the ground floor and clip through it, you will be on [Level 14](level-14.md).
- Touching a rusty pipe will lead you to [Level 3](level-3.md).
- Exiting through the front door in the lobby will lead you into [Level 557](level-557.md).
