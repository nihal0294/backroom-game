Hello B218, to write an article you need to go to the sandbox which is under the wiki option once you are there (preferably) type in your name and press create page. There you can write to your hearts desires.

Though before you do much please read over some of the guides here [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/guide-hub](guide-hub.md)  
Mainly Please check out the criticism policy (when you finish your article), greenlight policy (so you know how to legally post articles on the main page), and syntax guide.

If you have any more questions going forwards, please post in the help: questions help area.

Goodluck going forwards
