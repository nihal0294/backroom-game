# DESCRIPTION

---

  
**Phenomenon 38** is a unique phenomenon that has been observed to exist widely across most of the levels. Its manifestation is similar to objective laws. This phenomenon was officially confirmed on December 3, 2024, after rigorous investigation and recording, and was subsequently included in the official database.

**Phenomenon 38** can be simply expressed as a sentence: The appearance and anomalous effects of all known levels in the Backrooms are influenced by subjective human emotions. Individuals living in these environments can have measurable effects on abnormal phenomena around them through their emotional state and feelings. This finding fundamentally challenged the old consensus, indicating for the first time a direct connection between human consciousness and the construction of the Backrooms, whereas previously it was believed that the operation of the Backrooms was only governed by mechanical or random factors.

In the early stages after the discovery of **Phenomenon 38**, its cause remains largely unclear. Researchers and experts face significant challenges in understanding how human emotions interact with and alter the physical and metaphysical properties of the Backrooms. Because the complexity of this phenomenon and the difficulty in reproducing or predicting its effects, some members of the M.E.G. scientific community are skeptical about it. This has led to intense questioning and debate about the existence of this phenomenon. Many people view it as a theoretical anomaly or experimental illusion, rather than a true, reliable, and reproducible effect.

However, as time passed and research data continued to accumulate, a large number of subsequent cases emerged one after another. Most of them confirmed the existence and credibility of **Phenomenon 38** without exception. Here are a few typical cases:

#### [Level 18](level-18.md)

**Phenomenon 38** is the most typical in [Level 18](level-18.md). According to a survey, wanderers who have a good impression of their childhood experiences are less likely to feel sad or encounter dangerous situations in Level 18. On the contrary, wanderers who have a negative view of their childhood[1](javascript:;) are more likely to think that Level 18 is a tormenting level, and they are more likely to enter [Level 18.1](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-18-1).

  
  

#### [Phenomenon 18](phenomenon-18.md)

Individuals affected by [Phenomenon 18](phenomenon-18.md) do not physically differ from other wanderers, but their chances of surviving danger are far higher than ordinary people. This is believed to be due to the hope and satisfaction that Phenomenon 18 gives them, which triggers **Phenomenon 38**.

  
  

#### [Entity 15](entity-15.md)

It's enough evidence to show that the Wretch cycle happens more slowly in wanderers who believe they'll get better. This was previously thought to be due to them sleeping better, but a small number of them do not meet this condition. This is currently believed to be an effect of **Phenomenon 38**.

  
  

# UTILIZATION

---

  
Due to the great potential of **Phenomenon 38**, it is considered desirable to artificially create conditions that can trigger the phenomenon to utilize it. Currently, the [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) plans to use it to help wanderers escape from dangerous levels. The following are the details of the plan:  
  

Confidentiality level

8

**The main target:** Create hope for those in desperate situations and help them escape with the power of Phenomenon 38.  
**Start date:** 5/12/2024  
**Implementation steps:**

1. Lights shaped like open doors are placed on many dangerous levels to serve as exit signs.
2. In public documentation, inform wanderers that these lights are a product of Phenomenon 38, and that approaching them will increase the chance of escape.
3. As a result, wanderers who see them will be more confident in leaving. This will trigger Phenomenon 38, affecting the stability of the surrounding environment and possibly causing the level to generate an exit immediately.

**Supplementary explanation:** The M.E.G. conducted a small-scale feasibility test of the proposed plan. Specifically, they recorded whether any wanderers who contacted the M.E.G. in a dangerous level, when rescue efforts were impossible, had seen the "Lights Behind the Door," the fictional Phenomenon 38. They then contacted these individuals several days later to confirm their survival. The results showed that those who had seen the Lights Behind the Door and believed they could escape were significantly more likely to survive, thus eliminating the possibility of survivorship bias.

**Status:** Success.

More and more wanderers are escaping from dangerous places and benefiting from my fictional Phenomenon 38, which is also the true one, the power of hope. The Backrooms is brutal, no doubt about it. However, it also leaves us with an opportunity to create hope. Humans will still survive here tenaciously and pass on hope.

- Justin

  
Currently, the plan has been authorized by Overseer Justin and is being supervised by him.
