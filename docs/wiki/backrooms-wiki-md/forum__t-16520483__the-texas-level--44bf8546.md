The Texas Level

14 Dec 2023 01:17

Hey gamers, I've had this idea of creating this level that involves a small Texas town. It would be quite similar to a town like Fredericksburg, Texas. Everything in the town looks run down and quite deteriorated, but oddly enough, many resources (Almond Water and etc) can be found in the level in perfect condition. The only issue with the items is that they are obviously coated in a mysterious slime-like substance that, when interacted with, causes the individual who did so to start rapidly aging and decompose. Of course I will hint at to what might be causing the mysterious slime to appear on the objects, but that is for you to find out if this level concept is received. I know this isn't much of a ***original concept***, but I thought it would an interesting little level to write about. Thank you to anyone who reads this and responds.

Sincerely,  
Lord\_Dumb
