Vehicular Clockwork

07 Nov 2021 17:26

**Page Type:** Room (Level Template)

**Elevator Pitch:** Vehicular Clockwork (named Room 11.5 in article) is a standard roundabout that has great influence over time in multiple levels.

**Central Narrative:** Room 11.5 was discovered soon after the first M.E.G. documentation of Level 11. Utilising Room 11.5’s anomalous immunity to time manipulation, the M.E.G. stationed Base Gamma nearby to Room 11.5 for security. In recent years, Project: Gyroscope to create a gyroscope-esque drone beneath Room 11.5 to prohibit negative effects on time in Room 11.5.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** There is no room to have any effects upon levels beyond itself.

**Additional Notes:** None
