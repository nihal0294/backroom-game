So, please call me out if I'm rude or impatient, but I do have a question/problem with the site. I am on the site, I kid you not, every night. Every night I find an amazing article with at least great potential on the Draft Forum. There are so many awesome levels, that I sit there grinding out critiques on, the good, the bad, the outstanding, and the ones who aren't even on the right forum. But you know what? I never see yall. There are hundreds of entity and level slots waiting to be filled, with INSANE articles, but yall are only ever on the forum bugging us about incorrect image licensing. Now, there may be behind the scenes communications between them and staff, but by the looks of it, nope. I will say that many of these are people who have read 2 articles and just typed up 2 paragraphs and said "NEW LEVEL WAITING FOR GREENLIGHT PLSPLSPLS", but what about genuinely great authors with months of work done, but left for the dark page 2 of the forums? Many of us passionate authors without the capability to use discord are not refused greenlights and more importantly, critique, but are not even given the chance for it. Now of course you all are busy, and of course, you have a life unlike mine, but does it kill yall to leave an honest review on a well-written page? I most likely have no authority to do this, but there is a very good entity you may or may not have seen quite yet, and I want you to see it as a sample of the hidden gems youll find. [Will-O'-Waves](forum__t-14942112__entity-207__will-o-waves--3dfd5c34.md), written by Here are my 2 levels while I'm at it,( [The Escape Rooms](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/as-your-username) and [The Living Level](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/iamalemon)), which I have been working on for months, forced to keep on updating due to boredom and low critique to know what I am actually doing wrong. I understand that most others relying on the Drafts Forum are not as passionate as I am, but seeing one of your friendly faces review a level once in a while definitely helps us get them onto the site. I wanna thank for at least adding me to his queue for greenlight and critiquing my article in the early stages.

Okay, now that I have explained my plea for help, I want to know what you think. Ban me for anger issues? Sure. I'm like the calmest and friendliest guy ever besides this. Strictly run me down on site rules and sharing Dragon King's work? Okay, I deserve it. Tell me to fix my attitude and tell me you are doing as much as you can? Definitely. If this is taken into consideration I will be astounded. I am not doing this as "I want my levels greenlit waah." I'm doing this for the benefit of the site, bcs A LOT of people don't or can't have discord. I will answer to the consequences of my actions, and that will be that. I am most likely overreacting about this whole thing and you guys are on top of things, greenlighting and critiquing like THAT, but from the point of view of a small author criticizing more levels than you think is healthy? (bcs its true lol), I would be overjoyed to see an on-staff member check these artcicle out once in a while. I also would like to join the staff as an emergency critique staff member, whether it be in a year and a half or 3, so that I can spread my love for reading, writing, and creativity. Thx for reading. -Iamalemon

Hi, so there's quite a bit of stuff, so I'll take it piece by piece.

---

Firstly

> Ban me for anger issues?

There's really no need here. So it's okay on that side of the issue.

---

Secondly

> I also would like to join the staff as an emergency critique staff member

There are no "critique staff member". In a more broad way, criticizing articles isn't even one of the [staff's duties.](http://backrooms-oversight.wikidot.com/roles)  
Critics are made by any users, staff, greenlighters or anyone else.

Also, having discord is required in order to be staff, has we're doing all the staff discussions on it.

---

So, in the end, this whole ordeal is about the critique bottleneck. One thing to know is that the situation you're describing is the same on our Discord.  
We have a "sandbox link" channel, links there are deleted 2 months after being posted. We're deleting about 3 to 10 every day.  
Just this week there were 50 new drafts published on there.

Everyone is looking for crits, but there's only one real solution to have them: Reach out to people and directly ask them.

I know that the forum being empty sucks and several staff members tried to push our users to go there but in the end we can't force anyone to do so.
