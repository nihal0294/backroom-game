Hi there! Sorry for the late reply. There are many reasons why a link might be broken. Here’s 3 of them!

1. The article did exist in the past, but it was deleted. This could be because:

- The article was unpopular and got downvoted till it hit deletion range (-5, I think)

- The user left the site or was banned, and requested that their content be removed from the site

2. The article exists in-universe, but hasn’t been written yet! The author is still working on it!

3. There’s an unintentional bug or typo!  
Some links have typos in them that the author didn’t catch before posting the article. Other times, the page url was changed, and it broke links on other pages. If you discover a link that *should* work but *doesn’t*, you can help by flagging out these links to the author in the forum!

4. The article has intentional broken links, sometimes as a joke. See [Level 14](deleted-level-14.md) as an example!

To my knowledge, the wiki is *not* deteriorating or dying, but is actually working to improve in quality every day, through rewrites and higher critique standards. Additionally, people are much more active on discord server (though I’m not in there myself), and that’s why this forum appears somewhat ‘dead’.

Hope this answers your questions!
