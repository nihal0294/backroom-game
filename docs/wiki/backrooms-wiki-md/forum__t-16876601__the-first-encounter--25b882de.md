We all have a choice to make when we read something from a fellow creator.

We can be harsh or we can be kind.

Yes, there were issues with punctuation and word choice. But I stuck it out through the l-o-n-g chase because the character began to matter to me. I kept telling her "Don't do that!" I groaned at her every poor choice in judgement. Has she no "street smarts?" It's only a wallet. No good deed goes unpunished. **There were two long chases going on here.**

The author did their best to create suspense. This is the Backrooms and the reader more than likely knew how this would end. Still… I felt the effort as the author chased after the story.

I chose to be kind.
