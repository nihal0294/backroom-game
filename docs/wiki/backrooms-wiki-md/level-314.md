# Description

Level 314 consists of a vacuumous space of unknown size but is presumed to be infinite. The only robotic expedition to the level revealed no form of matter or energy exists in this space. The level is completely dark, and lights do not reveal anything, as there is nothing for the lights to reflect off. The first probe to enter the level had no readings on its accelerometer or magnetometer, indicating that the level is devoid of gravity and magnetic fields. After the probe ran out of battery, the M.E.G. declared that there will be no more expeditions to the level; as a result, the entrance to the level has been sealed off.

> "We were hesitant about calling it a level, and the only thing that convinced us that it was a level was the interdimensional location sensor detecting that this was indeed Level 314."  
> — Raylene Devereux, robotic operator of the only expedition to the level in 2014.
