Unofficial Backrooms Starter Picks

20 Feb 2026 21:10

THIS IS A MIRROR OF A DISCORD THREAD ON THE OFFICIAL BACKROOMS WIKI DISCORD SERVER. THE THREAD CAN BE FOUND [HERE.](https://discord.com/channels/695664941247627274/1445757739425988652)

Hello, all.

I'm here today because I'd like to announce a new project that I'm working on and which needs a large volume of tiny contributions. As stated in the thread, the basic idea is this: For new readers to the Wiki, whom are often unaware of what the best content is or where to find it, there should be a guide that is maintained by active Backrooms Wiki authors and which highlights what the community believes to be its best work, separate from the Top Rated works. Personally, I would like it to be comprehensive — sortable by page type, genre, and word count — so every suggestion, even a self-promotion, helps to narrow down the content that such a page would hold.

If you are an author on the Wiki, and feel any interest in this, please drop by the Discord and give tale or article suggestions within the relevant thread! You can also drop them here if you prefer, or send them to me directly through Wikidot or Discord

Have a good day :)
