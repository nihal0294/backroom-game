02 Feb 2022 14:51

So one of the ideas that I'm hoping will get greenlit so I can start properly writing about them (which I will link at the bottom if anyone is interested) is going to be called "The Atelier." There's a good bit there, but the main thing is that it's a large mansion that appears to be a modernized Victorian-era design. I was wondering if anyone had any good sources that could lead me to typical floor plans for this sort of house during this time period or anything else along those lines?

[+ My current plans for the level can be found here!](javascript:;)

[- Hide block](javascript:;)

It is decorated like what appears to be a somewhat modernized Victorian-era mansion. The main room that people find themselves in is something that appears to be a ballroom, based off of the wide floor space and the dark brown piano in the middle of one of the walls. In this particular room, there are also many tables placed along the outer edges of the room. Each of them are decorated in various ways, including vases of flowers, candles, silver plates and tea ware. All tea kettles are filled with almond water and any food that is set out has been found to be safe to eat. The piano is functional and does not appear to display any anomalous properties to date.
