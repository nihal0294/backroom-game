Level -9 - "Acceptable Home...?"

28 Jun 2022 10:04

**Page type:** level.  
**Genre:** mystery, wonder, format-screw.  
**Core concept:** I have an idea for my draft and I actually made an incomplete version. **Level -9** is supposed to be its own space-time, where Level 0 might have originated from[1](javascript:;): the chaotic arrangement of rooms, the madness of a monochrome level and a distorted space-time.

The level is composed of four (4) known sections:

- The office skyscraper with 30+1 floors.
- An "outside", a shattered realm.
- The dangerous **Floor 21**.
- The unexplorable **Floor 30**.

"Are you sure that you aren't alone?" is a question that fills the wanderer's mind with fear and terror.

*Also, please, don't open the windows…*

What do you think?
