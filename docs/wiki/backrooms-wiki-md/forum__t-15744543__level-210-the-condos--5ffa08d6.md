16 Mar 2023 18:50

the page just could do with a lot more fleshing out of its ideas. i like the setting, but it needs some *more.* It feels like it's pulling its punches, not going in-depth. the condos are cool but they're all empty - why not write about some more interesting things that could be found inside? the park is pretty, but most of its details feel glossed over. the concept of there being paths that lead to darkness and nothing outside of the area being visible is quite visually cool, but there needs to be some *more* to it.

barely anything is known about this level. things that you explicitly state are unknown are:

- the origin of the smell
- the validity of reports of wanderers finding bottles of almond water
- the properties of black voids
- the language spoken by we, the people
- how the lamps are able to remain lit

but most other things are also not really known about, like why the buildings are empty, or why the people are the way they are, or the cats, or really anything on this level.

the level doesn't have an air of *mystery*, it just feels like it says nothing. so that's why i'm giving it a novote — it wasn't really very *bad* but i didn't really find much of value in it

that being said, it's a massive improvement from the previous version, and i'll be looking forward to how you improve in the future!
