Entity # - Climbers  
Entity number: #  
Habitat: level 0 and level 1

\*Description:  
Climbers are a kind of humanoid being, with the size of an average adult male. They have hair like humans, but of unnatural colors (pink, white, blue…). They are usually found climbing walls or playing with other individuals of their species.

\*Behaviors:  
Climbers are peaceful beings, mostly harmless to humans. They are curious and carefree, which makes explorers eye-catching for them. Explorers often compare them to companion dogs for their docile and loyal nature. They are usually found climbing the walls or ceiling (that's why they are known as "climbers") .

\*Biology:  
The skin of these beings varies between shades of purple or blue (specimens with other colors are not known yet). They have antennae, which are believed to be used as the main pair of ears (although they also have ears similar to those of humans).They have three eyes (two located like human eyes and one in the middle of the forehead). Their bodies are flexible, especially their legs, as they are specialized to be able to walk like a quadrupled animal. On their toes (both feet and hands) are pads that allow them to climb almost every surface.  
They have no gender.

\*Discovery:  
Climbers are known from the earliest times, as they are one of the easiest identities to find (for its abundance and curiosity)

\*Do's and Dont's:  
¬Do's:  
- talk to them in a friendly way.  
- Express trust towards them.  
- Give them food.  
- Make eye contact.  
¬Dont's:  
- Run towards them.  
- Make sudden moves.  
- Shout/Scream.  
- Assault them (they'll get confused and try to attack back).
