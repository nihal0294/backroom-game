# **Begin Log:**

I had just fallen asleep after training with the rest of the team. Somehow I remembered this dream above others, and now I'm glad I did.

I was walking through [Level 625](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-625) in that dream, and it was after the whole incident with [Entity 206](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-206). I was talking to Han before I randomly fell through the floor and onto a massive snake's body. After I oriented myself the scales never seemed to end. They were just one long, solid, breathing foundation that made the ground.

As the serpent slowly breathed and the ground rose and fell, I looked down to see that I had a katana in my hand and I had gauntlets on. Not that it mattered for long, though, as snakes rose from the ground and began to charge me. I'm not sure *how* I did this in my dream, but I was chopping them up for sushi.

It felt like hours had passed, and my exhaustion had gotten the better of me. They were around 12 to 19 feet tall, if I had to guess, so those things weren't thin. One clamped down on my head, and then the dream ended. While I blacked out, I could hear something being whispered in my ears. It was in a different language, and I could almost make it out. Sounded like Latin or something.

God, I don't think I'll ever like snakes again.

*Well… that was certainly a experience.*

*I had a dream today. I don't usually have those, but enough babbling. It started off in [my domain](level-5.md) as always. I was then greeted by [The Bellhopper](entity-135.md), who told me something was wrong. I got up from my seat and went with them through my hotel. *As I realized this minor oddity, the mahogany-red walls transformed into an inside of an interesting long creature. I was clueless as to what was taking place in-front of me, so I tried to run back to [my office.](entity-18.md)*

*This was when I fell through the floor, but instead of falling down completely, a strange gravity effect pulled me back up around the creature's body. The wind felt cold as I stood upon a large serpent that seemed to never end. As soon as I rose to the top, I was met with a horde of large serpents rushing towards me. I looked at my hand and I was holding an [elongated blade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katana), while wearing an armor set of an ancient warrior.*

*I fought for what felt like hours on end until the serpents stopped manifesting. Out of breath, I then turned around as I saw golden grains of sand flying through this despicable void. They… spun around, almost, and flew right past me. Once I turned around completely, I was in-front of a great cathedral. Inside was empty but… warm. Comforting. Different from the cold winds I felt earlier.*

*As I took the time to appreciate the scenery, I realized the entirety of the floor was covered in a pool of blood. With no choice, I walked through it to reach my destination. I don't know why I trekked through such disgusting liquid, but there must be reasons that I am unsure of. Then, a tall obsidian structure adorned with animal faces appeared before me. It spewed this energy from the mouths of the carvings, which created a small purple orb in the mouth of a dragon. I, for reasons beyond myself, placed the weapon inside the orb. It then… absorbed the weapon, and the pool of blood started to move towards the mouths of one of the carved creatures, and before I knew it, the cathedral had not a single drop left.*

*I walked out of the cathedral to find the Bellhopper, to my surprise. I walked towards them to ask what was going on before everything disappeared beneath me and I was falling into the purple void…*

*That's when I had awoken from my slumber, and I fell out of my seat in shock.*

*I got up to continue my business as always. I do not know what this dream meant, if it had any meaning at all, but I must continue my work. Feel free to visit us if you wish.*

*I landed on my feet to realize that I was on the body of a large serpent. I could not describe the size of it to you. One scale was enough to fill one hundred of me, and ten times more crystals. It was enormous.*

*I felt something in my hands, and as I looked down I discovered that I was grasping a [large, ancient greatsword](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Cdachi) that was not my own. As it launched down and bit into my arm I cut the foul beast's head clean off, ripping out the fangs that it had bitten me with*

*The beasts never seemed to stop coming. I had to move, get out of the way of their attacks, and to somewhere safe. The persisting issue is that no matter where I looked I could not find a safe place of residence.*

*That was, of course, until I saw the building in the distance.*

*I ran as fast as my legs could take me, immediately opening the doors of the large cathedral and falling into the blood pool that took up the space in the floor. I waited until I heard nothing more than the crunching of my joints, and when the hissing stopped and the slithering trailed away, only then I rose.*

*I exited the cathedral, dripping and coated in wherever the crimson blood came from, and looked up in the sky. I do not know why I did, but the feeling overcame me. This one was different from every other one I had seen. While the others were opal white, this one was onyx black, like myself. I could see its details of the scales, the length of its body, and how it [bit its own tail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%B6rmungandr).*

*Unfortunately that is where my dream ended. I awoke back in my cavern with my whispers yelling at me to wake up. I do hope this helps you on your investigation, my friend.*

It ended there. Woke up in a cold sweat and I had to write this down.

The Serpent's Void

  
The Astral Motion

SURVIVAL DIFFICULTY:

Class Cyclical

The Serpent's Void is an unnumbered level of the Backrooms, which is still speculated to even exist.

## | Description |

---

The Serpent's Void is a never-ending plane of snake bodies, which are much larger than humans.[1](javascript:;) The snake bodies slowly rise and fall in varying intervals, suggesting that this is either one massive entity, or thousands, maybe even millions of snakes, which compose the ground.

## | Level Formation |

---

The bodies of large snakes frequently move and shift, causing the surrounding area to rumble and shake, which wanderers have dubbed a snake-quake. Wanderers are advised to instantly drop to the ground and wait until the quaking stops, as when the floor shifts there are openings which lead to what is termed the "Purple Void." This empty space is seemingly endless, and items dropped through openings in the ground do not make a noise, indicating no bottom.

[![Purple](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/the-serpents-void/Purple/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/the-serpents-void/Purple)

The sky of the Serpent's Void is a purple gradient, flowing into pink in some areas and black in others.[2](javascript:;) There are black opal orbs that float in the sky, which can size from a golf ball to a miniature planet satellite,[3](javascript:;) which are all based on perspective. Currently, these orbs do not seem to have any impact or importance, yet some have speculated that these orbs are "worlds" or "universes" that have been consumed by [Jörmungandr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J%C3%B6rmungandr)

## | Entering the Level |

---

Upon entering the level, a wanderer will be given a random weapon from the Edo Period of Japan, more commonly known as the Age of the Samurai. Wanderers have reported receiving wakizashi, the most common, tanto, yumi, and in rare instances a katana. All other weaponry disappears from a wanderer's pack or hand and is replaced with this weaponry. With these weapons comes a piece of samurai's armor, which is usually a kabudo, or helmet, but other pieces like tekkō (gauntlets) and menpō (faceplate.)

These weapons are required to be used in the Serpent's Void, as smaller snakes separate from the floor will emerge at random. These snakes are still larger than most humans, yet are somewhat docile. Approaching a snake will cause it to become hostile and hiss, preparing for an attack. Using the weapon given will assist you in killing the snake.

## | Dangers |

---

The Serpent's Void is mainly overrun with large snakes, which range from 12' to 17' tall and 4' to 7' wide. These snakes are always hostile, and will not stop chasing a wanderer unless they enter a Cathedral. Snakes are always opal white, just like the ground, and may use this to their benefit for camouflage.

There are instances of other entities after "sacrificing" one's weapon to the Obsidian Pillar. Once the blood is drained from the floor, a gate will appear at the back of the cathedral with many entities screaming for release. As shown in the Masked Maiden's report, these beings will reach through the bars in a possible attempt to escape.

The only other danger in this level is Jörmungandr, a being of Norse mythology, which is an example of an [ouroboros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouroboros). Jörmungandr is not fully understood in the realm of the Backrooms, and even less within the Serpent's Void, yet the Repository believes that this mythological beast is a danger to all of existence.

## | Theories |

---

Theories are the most difficult part of the Serpent's Void. With its speculated existence yet overwhelming effect on dreams of wanderers and entities alike, many of the Repository and M.E.G. believe that the Serpent's Void does exist. Entrances and exits cannot be determined at this time.

The leading theory about the effect of the Serpent's Void is Jörmungandr and the Obsidian Pillars. These are the most consistent traits among all recorded dreams, and many believe that the pillars absorbing blood represents the blood of snakes killed being cleansed from the level.

Others have speculated that the pillars are not absorbing the blood of snakes for cleansing, yet they are adding more fuel to the fire and causing more dangerous entities to appear. Following Sullivan's story from the Masked Maidens, there were a large amount of entities on the other side of bars in the cathedral after the totem pole was activated. Some have used this as damning evidence that there are greater beings attempting to be released, which may have more malicious intent.

One of the many mysteries of The Serpent's Void is the weaponry and the obsidian pillars, with the faces of many animals carved into it. In the ending event in the Serpent's Void, you will find the obsidian pillars in the middle of the cathedral building. According to all witnesses that had survived the second dream event, claimed that the purple orb was held by a different animal for each individual person, and the same can be said for their weaponry. These two factors are seemingly connected. We at the Repository with the help of everyone involved had created a list of weapons and what animals they seem to relate to, and who was initiated with them. None are in any particular order.

It seems that both the armor and weaponry originate from Japan dating from the 19th century, all the way back to the 4th century. The relation between the weaponry and the animal carvings is currently unknown, and more research must be conducted.

One theory that has gained much traction was brought up by Master Administrator V. Valentin of the Eternal Repository, the Overseers of the Major Explorer Group, the Hand of Athena of the Unbound Explorer's Coalition, Madame Holmes of the Masked Maidens, the Dark Sovereign of Level 197, and Miss Blanche Von Haderach. This theory states that Jörmungandr itself is the main threat of the Serpent's Void

The black orbs floating in the sky, as recorded by the second unnamed wanderer, are theorized by the M.E.G., U.E.C., and Masked Maidens to be other universes that are consumed by Jörmungandr. Miss Blanche questions the Dreamweavers' reports and shows that she is deeply worried by the discovery of the Void. The Dark Sovereign's interest in Jörmungandr in his dream may show a trance-like effect of sorts, yet seeing as his dream ended soon after viewing Jörmungandr and his worry in the report, Jörmungandr may be the real threat.

Miss Blanche's depiction of a "great awakening" within the Serpent Realm is what hooked the Hand of Athena. While Jörmungandr is meant to show stability, the Hand of Athena proposes that these entities shown behind the gates within cathedrals are trying to awaken Jörmungandr from a long slumber to disrupt balance and cause Jörmungandr to consume everything it can. The M.E.G., Masked Maidens, the Repository, Miss Blanche, and possibly the Dark Sovereign agree with this proposal.

Another mysterious factor is the flying sand. Many theories have been proposed, but the most agreed upon was brought up by Miss Blanche. Her theory was that this is a dream being known as The Sandman, or one of it's wisps. The Sandman is known as a benevolent being that protects the Dream Realm. The M.E.G., Masked Maidens, and Miss Blanche agree with this proposal.

#### The Eternal Repository and all factions involved have requested that, if one manages to enter the Serpents Void, to **never** release the entities behind the gate.
