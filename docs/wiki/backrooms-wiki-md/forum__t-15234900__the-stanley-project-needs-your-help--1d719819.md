The Stanley Project needs your help.

26 Sep 2022 13:20

I don't wanna make a good story. I wanna make the *best* MSPaint story. I wanna make some images that make you go: 'that's weird' and make it just stick to your mind.

But sometimes my expectations get lowered. It's hard to even get some big audience invested into this project. But that's why I'm making this post.  
**It's a long way to get a conclusion to this story I have to tell.** I want to tell it in parts, with different people collaborating with me and other people. I've been reading this wiki for a year and a half, and one word sticks out of mind here:  
**Collaboration.**  
So that's why I'm making a roadmap towards the end. This will make doing this a lot easier:  
![tzkCFp3.png](https://i.imgur.com/tzkCFp3.png)  
You might see most of these are Community voted on. I wanna see what people want me to tell, and I want to hear it. Don't wanna hog the whole entire stage here. Here's how that works.  
![obDCwmT.png](https://i.imgur.com/obDCwmT.png)  
Every week or so, I'll post a vote thread for people to go and recommend which level, group, item or entity Stanley will meet. Whatever has the most suggestions by 48 hours gets made into a comic/image. Hopefully this will make the process much easier to get inspiration and actually make stuff.

I do hope that people like what I do, and what I will do. Rustor out.
