# **Description**

**Level 943** is a small interior of a lower middle class suburban household which has been overcome with rot and filth. The entire level is considered a bio-hazard, inside and out, as the amount of parasites and diseases which are cultivated within the hot, moist environment of the building's interior is somewhere within the thousands. Because of this, the level's entrance has become a guarded quarantine zone, and only permitted M.E.G. Researchers are allowed inside. Anyone who seeks to enter will be equipped with a hazmat suit, boots, rubber gloves, a gas mask, a seal-able container for samples, and any simple weaponry that may be available. Upon exit, all of this equipment is to be burned and the ashes buried. If one's suit is torn or broken in any way, and airflow is allowed inside of the suit unfiltered, one will not be allowed out of the level, and **permission to end your life is granted. Do this for the safety of us all, lest we end up like them, flailing like a newborn in a pile of its own vomit.**

Multiple entities have taken up residence in this level. These entities appear to see each other as family, despite not being related in species. All of them resemble unnaturally enlarged and grotesque versions of insects or small creatures, and can speak English like people, with a slight Gaelic accent. In many cases, the creatures talk badly about each other, either to each other or to whoever they happen to be talking to. **They hate each other. They hate being this close to each other. It's like being stuck together with dried sweat after not moving for ages. Like when your lips dry when your mouth is closed and you have to pull them apart, ripping their soft flesh. It's hot, sweaty. Being this close to each other this long drives you fucking crazy. That's why dad's dead, we got sick of him.**

# **Rooms & Entities**

Within **Level 943**, there are a number of entities, each residing within their own rooms. Below, every one listed with a description of the rooms within the household and the entities that reside within. The entities appear to coexist with each other, though each prefers to stay within their respective rooms. It remains to be concluded whether or not the rooms exist because of the creatures, or the other way around. All of these beings are considered highly dangerous, if not just for the amount of disease they carry, then the violent tendencies they all possess. The only exception is 943-F, which has been neutralized.

## **Entity 943-A, Aka, "Gunner"**

The living room, and entrance of this house are the first example of the **disgusting** nature of its interior. The couches are spongy and wet, and the only light source (besides the ceiling lamp, which drips with a brown unknowable fluid) is the television. The television itself plays different shows from the early 21st century on repeat, and it does not appear to matter what channel or company they are from for them to appear on the same channel. There is a depression in the carpet where "Gunner", the entity that resides in the living room, most often sits to watch the television. This appears to be its favorite activity.

![Missing alt text.](https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/49675102022_0b020b689e.jpg)

A photo of the living room of **Level 943**.

"Gunner," as it is referred to by the other creatures, is an unnaturally enlarged tick. Unlike the other members of the household, Gunner is not just a giant bug. Rather he is a tick who has consumed and carried enough blood to be the size of a small child. This is visible, as looking at his body one can almost see particulates floating within his **body. His skin has grown thin over time as he stretched to fill more and more blood within him. His blood sack of a torso pulses sometimes. You can almost see something swimming inside of it. A leech, perhaps? Maybe something worse. Like a fetus, evolving in a swollen womb.** Gunner is almost a meter tall and walks with his long front legs that are barely long enough to reach the ground. Gunner is considered dangerous due to his want to drink the blood of anyone who passes him. This is unfortunate, as he seems to frequent the living room and entrance to the house.

Thankfully, Gunner is unable to climb on the furniture, nor jump on top of it. When entering the household, be quick to climb on top of a couch or chair. Gunner is not fast, so, dependent on his location during entry, there may be time to get out of range. Despite this, he is not to be harmed by anyone who is visiting the level. All the other "family members" or entities within the level see him as a little sibling, son, or grandchild, and bringing harm to him would cease our ability to enter the level peacefully.

## **Entity 943-B and 943-C, Aka "Fester" and "Boil"**

Connected to the hallway attached to the living room is the children's bedroom. The door was apparently white at one point; Pink flowers are drawn on the door with colored chalk. A layer of grime and residue resembling dried wax covers the door and cements it in a half open position. Entering, one can see that this room is meant to hold many children, too many for the size of the household. This is most obvious by the many mattresses which have been pushed up against the wall, with yellow-brown liquid stains **of god knows what** on all of them. The center of this room has been taken up by the two entities which inhabit it, listed together here as they are literally inseparable and never seen without the other.

Fester and Boil are two giant snails whose shells appear to have been fused at the side, **or maybe they were just created like that. Perhaps they were always meant to be together forever, rotting next to each other for all eternity until they end up one large colony of fungus and maggots whose roots and burrows dig through their body like loose needles. Like ingrown hairs.** Despite their situation, the pair of snails seem to enjoy each other's company, and often speak for one another or interrupt to finish the other's sentence. **It's almost like they think with each other. Their brain connected through their shared home. Imagine being connected by the skull to someone else. Brains being interwoven tissue, knotting and twisting around each other to make a pile of pasta-like thought signals. Imagine trying to pull that apart from each other, feeling the bone stretch as far as it can, your brains catching on each other like crochet.**

[Marie approaches one of the pimples on the walls, which is seen to be a bit larger than a human hand in diameter. Cilia is heard running over before her hand grabs Marie's wrist.]

**Cilia:** "What are you doing? Don't touch anything!"

**Marie:** "Oh, a-alright."

[The pair continue in silence, their steps echo in the large chamber, stepping in something squishy and wet repeatedly. The only light in this area is the ones brought by Marie and Cilia, so not a lot can be seen. Marie trails the light on Cilia's back to not get lost, showing her look side to side before picking a direction. Distantly, a noise can be heard echoing through the area from a great distance which sounds like a rumbling, akin to a small earthquake. The noise stops, and then starts again a moment later.]

**Marie:** "What is that?"

**Cilia:** "What is what?"

**Marie:** "The noise, obviously."

**Cilia:** "Oh, that. No idea."

**Marie:** "You're a lot of— Shit!"

[Marie slips on the floor, falling backward. As they hit the ground, their flashlight points up at the ceiling, showing what appears to be a black cord or wire that trails back the way they came and goes forward to an unknown location. The video feed cuts.]

---

**End Log: 12:06, 4th August 2022**

**Begin Log: 12:20(?), 4th August 2022, Frontrooms Time.**  
**Perspective: Marie Reyes**  
  
Exploration of the basement of **Level 943**, recorded by Marie Reyes, led by Entity 943-I.

---

[The camera turns on to a whooping noise from Marie, displaying the moist, fleshy caves that were shown in the previous log. The characters have apparently been walking for a little while, as the environment has grown smaller. A tall but thin hallway is shown that has just enough room for both of them to face forward in a single file line.]

**Marie:** "Boom! Camera is working! I'm so smart. Turns out, the SD card just came loose. I hope we didn't lose any footage."

**Marie:** (after a moment of silence) "Cilia? What's up?"

**Cilia:** "Hm? Oh, nothing, sorry. Just focused."

[A noise begins, a rumbling noise that grows in strength as it approaches. Unlike the previous log, they both appear to be much closer to the source now, as the noise grows increasingly loud. The camera, or the environment itself, begins to shake.]

**Cilia:** "Oh shit— Hold on!"

**Marie:** "To what?!"

**Cilia:** "Anything!"

[The fleshy environment begins to churn and ungulate, as if muscles underneath it are moving. A loud groaning sound can be heard, akin to stomach acid churning. The floor shifts like a wave moving toward Marie. Ahead, Cilia can be seen using her claws to latch onto the floor, digging them into it. This is met with the floor bleeding a red substance, potentially blood. Marie scrambles for a moment to hold onto something, but his gloved hands slip off a bump on the wall. Instead, he falls into the wall, a loud squish can be heard as this happens. A moment later, the rumbling stops, as does the moving environment.]

**Marie:** "Ugh— that sucked—"

**Cilia:** (turning back and approaching) "Don't move!"

**Marie:** "What? Why?"

**Cilia:** "You landed on one of those— the bumps!"

**Marie:** "The gross wall-pimples?"

**Cilia:** "Yes! That!"

**Marie:** "Alright— Uh— What do we do?"

**Cilia:** "Uh— Uhm…"

**Marie:** "And how do you know how to deal with these anyway? I thought it had been a while since you were down here!"

**Cilia:** "It has been! We get these upstairs sometimes too!"

**Marie:** (making a fake barfing sound) "That's so disgusting. Why are these things so dangerous anyway?"

**Cilia:** "The shit inside of them, it burns through anything. You're lucky it hasn't leaked onto your suit yet!"

**Marie:** "Shit… What— how are we going to do this?"

[Cilia thinks for a moment, examining what can be seen of Marie's back.]

**Cilia:** "Alright, I have an idea, but you've got to trust me."

**Marie:** "Uh… Sure, yes. Woah!"

[Cilia can be seen grabbing Marie's wrist before yanking him forward. The pair then begin rushing down the hallway, as many loud, squishy popping sounds are heard. Followed by sizzling. Marie looks back momentarily, seeing that the one popped pimple has caused a chain reaction, and each one in the hallway is now popping and spraying the yellowish white liquid inside all over the hall. The pair continue running to the end of the hallway, which opens up into a dark space that cannot be seen in detail. The pair run for a few more moments as the end of the hall approaches and the popping gets closer. Finally, they both burst into the dark space ahead and fall to the floor with a loud thump. The pair breathes hard for a moment, simply laying on the floor.]

**Marie:** (begins laughing)

**Cilia:** "Pff— What the fuck are you laughing about?"

**Marie:** (still laughing) "No idea!"

**Cilia:** (also begins laughing.)

**Cilia:** "Alright— We should really get going now."

**Marie:** "You're right— let me check the camera."

**Begin Log: 12:35(?), 4th August 2022, Frontrooms Time.**  
**Perspective: Marie Reyes**  
  
Exploration of the basement of **Level 943**, recorded by Marie Reyes, led by Entity 943-I.

---

[The camera turns on to view an all black room. A moment passes until a flashlight clicks on, it flicks around the room, spotting Cilia standing a few feet ahead. Looking at the floors, then the walls, both seem to be covered in numerous thick black wires. A single one trails back to the hallway from the previous log, and all of the wires eventually give way to the back flesh behind them. Up ahead, the wires converge around a beige painted metal door. A rumbling sound begins, shaking the entire room and camera.]

**Cilia:** "There."

**Marie:** "Wait— for real? In there?"

**Cilia:** "I don't see somewhere else to go, do you?"

**Marie:** "…No."

**Marie:** "In we go then?"

[Marie approaches the door followed by Cilia. After a few steps, his hand touches the doorknob, and pushes it down. A click signifies the door unlatches, and Marie pulls it open. The room behind the door is also covered from floor to ceiling in the black wires, as shown by a small light bulb which hangs from the ceiling on a wire. Below it, a metal table and chair are in the center of the room, painted the same color beige as the door to enter. On the table, an old computer, yellowed and shaped like a cube, with a rounded, square screen. Displayed on the screen are diagonal green lines, and underneath the screen, the word "[Primum Cimex Symphonia](hymenoptera.md)" in black text. The back of the computer appears to be the source of the wires, as they all flow out of it and onto the back wall.]

**Marie:** "What the…"

[The room begins to shake as a rumbling noise radiates out from the computer. On the screen, the green horizontal lines begin to shake and shift as if they were fluid, forming into the shape of an anatomically correct human heart, and beating a few times. As the rumbling stops the lines lose their shape and lay horizontal once more.]

**Marie:** "What… what do we do?"

**Marie:** (after no response from Cilia) "Cilia— What do we do now?"

**Cilia:** "I'm sorry— I don't know if I can do this. We should leave."

[Cilia is a few feet behind Marie, staring back and forth between him and the room's exit. There are tears wetting the stained fur around her eyes.]

**Marie:** "Cilia what— what do you mean? We came this far to—"

**Cilia:** "To do what? To destroy my home? My family? What happens when we unplug this!"

**Marie:** (silence)

**Cilia:** "Did you even think about what this would do to them? To me? My little brother and sisters?"

**Marie:** "What about the rest of your family Cilia? Your mother and grandmother would *eat* you given the chance. Your brother *hates* you. It's disgusting!"

**Cilia:** "Its disgusting to *you*! This is how it's been my whole life! This is fine to me!"

**Marie:** (reaching out to Cilia) "Cilia, listen to me—"

**Cilia:** (swats at Marie's hand) "No!"

[A tearing sound is heard as Cilia's claw rips through the rubber suit. She notices a moment after, gasping and covering her mouth.]

**Marie:** "Cilia— Cilia I need something to wrap it with, now!"

**Cilia:** "I don't… I don't…"

**Marie:** "Cilia do you have anything to close this off with?"

**Cilia:** "No— no I don't I-I'm sorry Marie, I didn't mean to!"

**Marie:** "No, It— it's fine, I can handle it. Lets just get out of he—" (cut off by a string of coughs)

[In the coming moments, Marie falls to the ground onto his knees, apparently having breathed in something harsh. He coughs, repeatedly. Each one becomes worse than the last, initially dry, before spit and phlegm start to spray onto the screen of his mask. Then blood. Cilia steps back, pressing herself against the wall and sliding down it until she's on her knees. Marie vomits, filling the inside of the hazmat suit, before collapsing to the ground and beginning to convulse.]

[The camera shuts off.]

**End Log: 12:45(?)**

**Begin Log: 12:45(?), 4 August 2022, Frontrooms Time.**  
**Perspective: Marie Reyes'**  
  
Incident log of the burning of **Level 943** and its inhabitants. Recorded by Marie Reyes via. a chest-mounted camera which was left on. Maria Reyes' was unconscious during recording.

---

**Cilia:** "Come on, come on, don't be dead, *please* don't be dead."

[Cilia is seen crouching above Marie who lays on the floor, face up. She appears to be trying to check for any sign of life, but is unsuccessful. She begins to cry and stands, rubbing tears from her fur.]

**Cilia:** (breathing outward, as if psyching herself up) "Come on, Cilia. You've done worse than this. Just pull them, pull them all out and it'll all be fine."

[Cilia walks over to the computer on the desk, then around the table to where the bundle of wires flow out of the back of the PC. She wraps her arms around them, a little tough considering her arms are about half the size of a human's, and pulls. On her first try, she fails, and exhales in frustration. On the second try, however, a few come loose, sparks flying off of the back of the computer. Then, on her third, the whole bundle comes loose, and she falls onto the ground as sparks begin to spray out of the computer. The screen goes black as this happens, the green lines closing from the outer edges into the center.]

**Cilia:** "Haha! Yes!"

[Cutting her celebration short, Cilia scrambles to a crawl and makes her way to Marie, scooping her arms underneath his body and lifting. With a loud grunt, she is able to do so, and starts walking out of the room as fast as she can while holding Marie. Behind her, the sparks hit the wires on the floor and catch ablaze. It begins as a small flame, which illuminates Cilia from behind, until something more flammable is hit and an explosion blasts the pair forward, onto the ground.]

**Cilia:** "Fuck! Marie!"

[Cilia is seen running back over to the body and picking it up as the fire grows much larger very quickly. She manages it again, and heads to the tight hallway seen in **Video Log 943-2**. It's a squeeze, having barely fit a single person's shoulder width. However, she manages it, and squeezes through the passage while apologizing repeatedly to Marie.]

**Cilia:** "Come on… come on, almost there."

[The fire is seen behind Cilia as she pushes toward the end of the hallway. By appearance, it makes its way to a few feet behind her as she manages to escape the tunnel and sprint, holding Marie, into the open cavern. Cilia sprints ahead, putting distance between herself and Marie and the flames. A moment later, she reaches the webbing of Entity 943-E's den, continuing to rush through. The flames reach the webbing soon after, quickly catching fire to it.]

**Cilia:** "Fuck, fuck, fuck, fuck, fuck— Which way was it?"

[Cilia pauses at a split in the road, looking left and right, before turning right. The floor starts to angle upward and Cilia steps up it unfamiliarly, until being blocked by a door, nearly horizontal, made of metal above her. Held shut by webbing. She looks back, seeing the fire, and appears to decide to try the door. Grunting in exertion, She hits the door with her shoulder, but it doesn't move. She tries again, to little change in result, as the fire approaches. By the third try, she appears tired and is struggling to continue to carry Marie.]

**Cilia:** "Please, please… Help! Help us!"

[Cilia begins to yell out of the crack between the door, before giving up and trying to hit the door open again. This time, when she attempts to knock the door open, she is met instead with sunlight as the doors are pulled open from the outside. M.E.G. Biohazard Watch in standard-issue hazmat suits stare down at the pair.]

Transcription ends: Logged Elsewhere

**Summary:** In the moments after the end of the transcription, Marie Reyes was quarantined and looked after by medical staff. We are happy to say that he has made a full recovery, with some minor brain damage from lack of oxygen after his throat closed. Entity 943-I was also quarantined and cleaned repeatedly over the course of a week. The house that contained **Level 943.** has burnt to the ground, but the actual status of the level is unknown. Cilia has been given residence with Marie in [Level 11](level-11.md), upon agreement that she work with the M.E.G. to contain future, similar incidents.
