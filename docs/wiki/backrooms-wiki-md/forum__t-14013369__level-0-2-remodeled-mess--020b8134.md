Why this Level is one of the best

22 Nov 2022 22:53

This level is likely to be one of my all-time favorites. It's not an insane deadly trip into your own doom, nor an inexplainable abstract journey.  
It's just a mess that a bunch of sloppy construction workers caused while trying to keep their jobs together.  
I think this reminds us that the Backrooms has people in it; not just monsters and feral people.
