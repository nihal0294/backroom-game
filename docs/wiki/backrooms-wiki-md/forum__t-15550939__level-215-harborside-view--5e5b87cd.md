Not bad at all! It was an enjoyable read so far.

- Great sense of liminality. The depiction of the fog and the usage of precisely-themed imagery enhance the experience a good chunk.
- Immersion comes in clutch, even the more with the ending logs that help with visual representation of the real threats of the level.
- Avoids cliché territory with the implementation of unique idas, such as the rusting (one personal favourite of mine) and the pained canvasses, something that not few ammount of class 5 entries struggle to accomplish.

Overall, it's an ephemeral yet nicely detailed page, which doesn't fail to achieve its main objective: to entertain.
