The Partstarters turn people on mass into The Partygoers and that's why The Partycrashers kill The Partystarters. The Partycrashers are designed to be highly adaptable to compensate for their low numbers. Besides that while The Partypoopers are possibly extinct in the same level as The Partygoers normally are, both the biologically altered variant of The Partypoopers (The Partycrashers) and The Partygoers (The Partystarters) are supposed to be located solely in Level 994 "Dr. Pariah's Laboratory" along with a whole assortment of altered variants of many other entities each of which are made stronger and more frightening than their unaltered counterparts. Also The Partycrashers only kill Partystarters if they see one either heading their way or converting masses of people into Partygoers, Otherwise Partycrashers just mind their own business. If "Partypower" were to exist naturally they'd probably have to aim for Partypoopers and Partygoers only. While if they are biologically altered from another entity or as a chimera of both they'd have to tranquilize Partycrashers since they are as easy to permanently kill as SCP-682, kill Partystarters since Dr. Pariah can always make more from the bountiful number of Partygoers, and deal with the horde of Partygoers that come from The Partystarters by either killing them or converting them back into humans. If they were to exist they could act as a barrier of sorts to either the natural party entities or the modified ones to ensure they don't spread outside their native level range. BTW if they are supposed to balance things out I think "B|" would make more sense since it is a neutral expression unless there is another variant of them too to which "B\" could be for one of them while "B/" is for the other. I could imagine if they have either a natural or experimental counterpart it would be called either "The Partykeeper" or "The Partysaver".

Yes, Dr. Pariah gave The Partycrashers regenerative and adaptive abilities similar to SCP-682 to make sure they don't go extinct as a result of their small numbers from the other hostile Experimental Entities, It basically retrieved all the remaining survivors of the war against The Partygoers and turned them into Partycrashers. It doesn't want any backroom entity to truly go extinct so it makes their enhanced counterpart stronger so that their survival is all but guaranteed especially since all of its experiments have biological immortality. Luckily for any wanderers though The Partycrashers maintain their humanitarian nature so they have nothing to fear about them at least. Some of the other Experimental Entities though including The Partystarters are terrifyingly hostile while others have hostile states such as Entity 998.0004 "The Skin Thief" that is docile as long as it is wear a human's boneless flesh.
