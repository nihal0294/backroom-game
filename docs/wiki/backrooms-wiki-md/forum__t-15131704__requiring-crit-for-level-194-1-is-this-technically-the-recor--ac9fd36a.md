Requiring Crit for Level 194.1 (Is this technically the record any% sub-level write speedrun?)

01 Sep 2022 02:25

It has been **1** day since the release of Level 194.1. This is my first level and first sub-level. Sure I'm not 'new' to writing (both in concept and in wikis) but it's always best to ask for crit as soon as you think all of the grammar errors are out of the way (they are probably not, please forgive me). I'm mainly looking for crit on grammar, which I struggle with, and some crit with the consistency of tone, but getting my grammar in order is the top priority.

Anyhow, without further adieu, let me describe the concept—level 194.1 is a stolen schematic for a multi-universal energy transfer device constructed by a group called the Arcadian Dynasty. The B.N.T.G. plan on selling the energy produced at increasingly high prices in order to earn the most profit (as you'd expect from the corporate entity known as the B.N.T.G.).
