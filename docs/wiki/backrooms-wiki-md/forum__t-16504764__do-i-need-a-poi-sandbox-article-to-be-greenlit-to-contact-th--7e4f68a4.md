Correct, your PoI article must be greenlit before you can add the PoI to the list. However, the PoI **does not** have to appear in any other articles (eg. a level page ), it only needs to have a PoI article that has been greenlit.

> "The authors of this entry are CutTheBirch And Sariastuff. Should you need help with adding your POI to the list, shoot one of us a DM."

Shoot, this is actually outdated, as Saria isn't a staff member anymore, and it's possible Birch won't be either soon. We'll fix this soon.

-jan Jejasa, on behalf of the Staff Team
