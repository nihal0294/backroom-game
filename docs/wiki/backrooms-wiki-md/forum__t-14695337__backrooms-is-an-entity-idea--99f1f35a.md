Entity number 999 - the backrooms

Habitats: outside our universe

Description  
The Backrooms are series of endless mazes, levels upon levels. It is inhabited by entities which are its children. (REDACTED) discovered that it is similar in shape and size to our own universe.

Behavior  
The Backrooms have no emotion. It does not feel things such as fear or pain. Sound tests have been shown that it can breath. There is a possibility that it grabs people from our universe instead of them no clipping but nothing has been proven yet.

Biology  
As mentioned before the backrooms are similar in shape and size to our universe. When traveling to the hive (REDACTED) came to the conclusion that the hive is a womb to give birth to the other entities.
