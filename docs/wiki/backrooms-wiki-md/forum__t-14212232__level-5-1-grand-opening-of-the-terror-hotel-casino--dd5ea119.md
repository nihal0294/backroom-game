Yay! My 10th page, + a fully voice acted audio log! This is my terror hotel article and my contribution to the canon ^-^. I took heavy inspiration from Cuphead for this, in fact it was what inspired me to write a casino level with living machines and staff that resemble various aspects of the casino. For credit purposes, [This](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vqge1S8kDLQ&list=RDMM&start_radio=1&rv=ipKCkYrYcnI) song was my mood music for this, haha. For those wondering exactly why I chose terror hotel, I figured that it was a bit weird that people would willingly stay in a hotel with soul-stealing staff, so I figured, why don’t I write a sub level that gives guests an incentive to stay in the hotel, so here we are!

Giant thanks to *ArgonMighty* does not match any existing user name and for critique, for green light, and for voice acting, and BIGGEST thanks to for the radical art and helping me brainstorm stuff to get this down on paper. This wouldn’t have been possible without those folks! Enjoy! This took me a while.

Edit: Entity 18 is now canonically bri’ish hwuhwuhwuhwuhwuh
