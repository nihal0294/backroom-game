**There are 27 different connections between the sections of this article.**

For those of you interested in scavenger hunting, I've listed all of them below, split into 4 classes and written in order of increasing obscurity (based exclusively on my biased opinion lol).

[Show Connections (SPOILERS!)](javascript:;)

[Hide Connections](javascript:;)

### Notation

The bolded text in the square brackets is there to show what recovered files pertain to the discussed connection. For instance, **[MEG1-BOV3/4]** indicates this connection links together **the first M.E.G. file, third Boveta file, and fourth Boveta file.**

### Class I: Obvious

> Most of these are just the plot beats of the article. You should've noticed all of these.

- **[MEG4/5-UEC1/3/6/7-FRO2/4]**: Absolute War I, and the looming threat of it on the horizon, is everywhere in the article.
- **[MEG4/5-UEC4/6-FRO2]**: The U.E.C. caused the Frost Boar population decline.
- **[UEC1/3/4/6/7-FRO4]**: Each of these files mention the idea that raw material is a cost for arcane glyph magic.
- **[MEG2-BOV2]**: The Daily Rooms reports Frost Boars appearing in Level 93; this is followed up with Bovetan engineers researching said boars.
- **[MEG1-BOV3-FRO1]**: Each of these files reference the fact that Frost Boars are the national animal of the Froidetownites.
- **[BOV5-UEC2]**: The reason the Eternal Repository was able to help the U.E.C. is because they had the knowledge from the Boveta section sold to them.
- **[MEG1-BOV3/4-UEC3/4]**: The fact that Frost Boars have an excellent sense of smell, particularly when it comes to unoccupied burrows, is mentioned in each of these files.
- **[MEG1-BOV4]**: The fact that Frost Boars raise their young in their burrows is confirmed by the Bovetan drone transcript.
- **[MEG1-BOV5]**: The fact that Frost Boars have super sensitive skin is explained by Ivo's theory.
- **[MEG4-UEC6]**: The reason why the Carnifices came under so much scrutiny from Froidetown is because they're genuinely involved in all of it: they've taken glyphs from the U.E.C.
- **[MEG5-FRO4]**: The Froidetownite compound from MEG5 had arcane glyph magic slipped into it.
- **[MEG3-UEC4/6]**: The joint operation to drive the U.E.C. out of former Veiled territory is described in MEG3, and then referenced in UEC4 and UEC6.

### Class II: Missable

> Similar to Class I, but you might've missed a few if you were reading quickly, or not all in one sitting.

- **[MEG5-UEC7]**: The Frost Boars were immune to fire in UEC7 because they'd been given the compound from the Frost Boar repopulation project, which provides fire resistance.
- **[MEG5-UEC7]**: The recording devices spotted on the boars by the U.E.C. are the same ones mentioned in MEG5.
- **[MEG4-UEC6]**: Both of these files mention that it is easy for Frost Boar populations to be destabilised.
- **[UEC4-FRO3]**: In the attack on Iceshed, few materials of Frost Boar origin were found because the U.E.C. relegated all collected boar materials to internal use only.
- **[MEG2-BOV3]**: The idea that some entities "spawn in" and some don't is mentioned in both of these articles: once in the form of a "spawner-spreader dichotomy", and once in the form of the Frost Boar distribution "disprov[ing] the theory that they spawned in".

### Class III: Obscure

> Substantially harder to spot, but by no means insurmountable. You may have noticed a few of these.

- **[BOV2/3/4/5-FRO1]**: FRO1 mentions that the similarity of Boveta and bovine is a common joke in the Backrooms. This explains Flo's username from the Boveta files: bovetabovine13.
- **[MEG4-UEC4]**: Andrew Donovan says that the M.E.G.'s public relations insist war is only a "threat". We saw this first-hand in MEG4: the Daily Rooms states that *averting* an all-out war is a top priority, implying war is no guarantee.
- **[FRO3-UEC5/7]**: The sketch of the recovered glyph in the Iceshed base attack images is precisely the second glyph recovered by the U.E.C., just rotated. This explains the effect it had on the boars it was exposed to: giving fire resistance (obvious in the U.E.C. documents) and organisational skills (explains why so many boars were able to unite so quickly).
- **[MEG2-BOV2/3/4/5/6]**: The Ivo from the Boveta chatlogs is the same Ivo as from the Daily Rooms news report.
- **[N/A]**: The loading percentages are always the same: 15, 47, 83

### Class IV: Very Obscure

> Extremely niche, be proud of yourself if you pieced together any of these. Most Class IV connections are really just Easter Eggs.

- **[UEC5/7-FRO2/3]**: The sigils on the Hounds' bellies in the Fuarville attack were almost certainly the square-like, fire resistance-causing, organisation-improving glyphs used by Froidetown later in the file. This is because (a) we see later that the Carnifices definitely have that glyph on them, and (b) it explains why the Hound murder was so much larger than usual: it's the same phenomenon that led to the giant Frost Boar horde the U.E.C. dealt with in UEC7. Incidentally, this means that the decision of the Fuarville Emergency Services Department to not use pyroil may have saved their lives.
- **[MEG2-FRO4]**: Andrew Donovan mentions that his colleagues are "younger and more optimistic" than him. This is almost certainly because Justin and Kat are both dead by 2043, and replaced with younger overseers. MEG2 foreshadows this, by mentioning an "Overseer Michael", who — in Entity 73 — is said to be the successor to Justin Zimals.
- **[MEG4-UEC3]**: The level map from UEC3 explains why Level 129 in particular was unaffected by the Frost Boar population decline. The map shows that a Frost Boar in Level 129 would have to travel through 3 tunnels to get to Level 70, the killzone. Since very few boars could do this, Level 129 was unaffected by the collapse.
- **[MEG4-FRO2]**: Both of these files mention one "Angela Holt", a Froidetown resident.
- **[MEG3-UEC7]**: Whenever an [ISO 8601 time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601#Time_zone_designators) appears, the timezone listed is "+BKR". This probably refers to Backrooms Standard Time/M.E.G. Standard Time.
