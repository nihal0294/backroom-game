To create a page, you must go to the [Sandbox website](index.md) and create your draft there. If you are not in the Discord server (which I highly recommend joining), go to the Draft Critique forums **only when you are done with the draft.** The forums are not as active as the Discord, so getting a greenlight (which is **required** to make a page) will take considerably longer. Keep in mind that to join the Discord you must be 15 years old or above and have an account that is older than 10 days.

Hope this helps! Happy writing! ^^

Hey, hey, Pen here!  
So, regarding adding images,

> [[include component:image-block  
> |name=Insert image link  
> |caption=Insert Caption  
> |align=whatever way you want it to be on the screen  
> ]]

This is the code for the image.  
As for how to get a image that will show up to everyone:  
"With that image, take a screenshot of it, and then go to "files", press "Upload File From Your Computer", press "Choose File", and then choose the screenshot. Then press "Upload". Once it is uploaded, press "Info", and copy the link. Close out of it, press edit, and go to the image thing. Copy the link in place of the one you currently have. Then the image should work for everyone."  
To clarify, put the link you copied in front of the "name" part.  
There is section when you first go to files that says:  
"Rename the uploaded file if you want to:"  
It's underneath the "Select a file from your computer/Upload File From Your Computer" header.  
As for naming the title/link of the page, you should put the name of what you want the page to be in the editable bar(I don't recall the official name of it). If you don't like what you originally called it, you can press the edit button down in the bottom options list, and change the text under the "Title of the Page" section. To rename the link, go to the "+Options" button, then press "Rename". Edit the "NEW PAGE NAME" to what you want the new link to be called(this would only change the part beyond the '/').  
As for the rate module, there are several ways to do it, but the simplest way is this:

> [[>]]  
> [[module rate]]  
> [[/>]]

(By the way the [[>]][[/>]] things are to align it to the right, you don't necessarily have to align it.)  
Or you can do it like this:

> [[module css]]  
> .module-rate {  
> margin-bottom: 0.5em;  
> text-align: right;  
> }  
> [[/module]]

If you need further help or clarification, feel free to pm me.
