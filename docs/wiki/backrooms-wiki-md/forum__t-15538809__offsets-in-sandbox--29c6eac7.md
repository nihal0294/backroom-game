Noting that I'm not a genius when it comes to wiki syntax.  
I could go:  
You've got mail from [[image http://www.wikidot.com/common--theme/base/images/karma/karma\_4.png style="width: 7px; height: 16px;"]][[image http://www.wikidot.org/common--images/avatars/default/a48.png style="width:16px; height:16px;"]] [/dead-link//offset/1 Messenger]  
To view your message from them, click their profile link.  
~whatever text I'll have here in the offset~  
I took this code from with their permission.

Once they read the "message", they could get the option to reply.  
I could do this for when the user/reader replies:  
Do you wish to reply, [[image http://www.wikidot.com/common--theme/base/images/karma/karma\_2.png style="width: 7px; height: 16px;"]][[image http://www.wikidot.org/common--images/avatars/default/a48.png style="width:16px; height:16px;"]] [/dead-link//offset/1 Replier]?
