Make 'WARNING' larger. You can do this by making it a header (yes, you can do that in blockquotes).  
EG:

> +++ Some code

becomes

> ### Some code

It would also look nice if you italicized the warning.  
//this// becomes *this*

> Devoid of entities.

Don't end this with a full stop, and capitalize 'entities'.

> of Recliners, Chairs, Tables, Beds, and Couches

Don't capitalize any of these.

> No available images of

Add 'There are' and remove capitalization on 'No'.

> The furniture is not flammable, and they are the only usable platforms in this level, as there is rock on the side of the river, but the ground immediately combusts when stepped on.

I would rewrite this as:

> The furniture is not flammable, and they are the only usable platforms in this level. There is rock on the side of the river, but the ground immediately combusts when stepped on, so it is best avoided.

> are healed. Or, a much safer

Remove 'Or,' and capitalize 'a'.

> jump in a section of the lava river that is either blue, white, green, which send you to either Level 317, Level 255,or Level 321.

I'd rewrite this as:

> jump in a section of the lava river that is either blue, white, or green. This will send you to either Level 317, Level 255, or Level 321.

Make sure you add tags. For your level, you should put:

level  
sd-class-3  
format-screw

*Overall, I still think the level is pretty short. Even though the level is extremely difficult to explore, that doesn't stop you from extending the page. Maybe some drones got sent to document the outskirts of the level. Try writing about that! Also, if you can't set up a base or outpost due to floating furniture, you could still make a community! In my sandbox, there's a level that crumbles and reforms every once in a while. However, one can still make a group that moves around the level or something like that. That's what I did. Also, if you want to add fancy themes, go to [this link](themes.md) and you can find many different themes you can use! For your page, I think the 'Dying Embers' theme works best. Just copy + paste this line anywhere into your page (make sure it's in its own line):*

[[include :backrooms-wiki:theme:embers]]  
*Also, just keep posting in this thread. I can keep helping you if you need it.*  
*I hope to see your page become even better in the future!*
