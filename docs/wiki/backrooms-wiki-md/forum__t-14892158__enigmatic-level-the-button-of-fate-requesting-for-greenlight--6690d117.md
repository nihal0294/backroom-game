Re: Enigmatic Level - "The Button of Fate" Requesting for Greenlight.

05 Aug 2022 08:28

> The Button of Fate is an Enigmatic Level located within the Backrooms.

"Enigmatic Level" shouldn't be in capitals.

> The Button of Fate is a completely dark and infinitely large space. The exact area of this level is still undetermined.

I'd replace the first use of the period with a semicolon.

> the surroundings; It is the

Don't capitalize "It".

> It is the only known light source on this level.

I feel like this could be stated earlier on in the page when "serves as a light source." was stated.

> essential object on this level; It is indestructible as well

Don't capitalize "It".

> to a random Cluster 1 Level.

Don't capitalize "Level" here.

> specific levels, e.g., Entity 99 - "The Game Master"

Punctuation is needed here.

> it to a random Safe Level or to a

Don't capitalize "Safe" and "Level".

> to a random basecamp of M.E.G.

I'd prefer the term "base" over "basecamp".

> Bases, Outposts, and Communities  
> As of now, there are no known bases or outposts on this level.

If there are no bases or outposts, you don't need an entire section dedicated to it; just mention it in the description.

> « Level -0 | Level 0 | Level 1 »

You've forgotten to change the level footer navigator; since you're making an enigmatic level, you can remove it entirely.

---

I don't think this page can really succeed as a level without heavy modifications. This is literally **the** random chance level; there's an object in the middle of the room that does different things. The way a trope like this can be pulled off is if there is a large multitude of different outcomes; but your page is **very** limited on that front. My biggest tip would just be to add more effects; the quirkier the better.
