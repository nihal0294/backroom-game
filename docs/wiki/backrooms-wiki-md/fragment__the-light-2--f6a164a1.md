The first rule of the Backrooms is to never believe anything is truly real. Not until you can touch it, not until you can feel it. Even then, be on your guard around it, as it could become harmful in an instant.

We failed to follow that rule when it came to the sun.

Once a familiar, safe comfort to all, any sun within the Backrooms is now to be considered an immediate threat to all life. The light they provide is dangerous, their warmth is a tempting falsity. While the severity varies from place to place, there is a list of common signs that a being may be infected with Synthesial Reversal. While the remainder of the symptoms are left unreported, the conclusion can be drawn from this that this - whether it be considered a virus, the control of some external force, or otherwise, the infected host completely loses control of their body and mind, entering a state of complete delirium. As of the creation date of this document, 19465, it is unknown if an infected host is able to be saved.

Furthermore, it is not recommended to attempt doing so unless you are certain of your methods. Should a loved one succumb to Synthesial Reversal, the kindest solution is to end their life. Do not join them. Do not follow them into the light. The you that once was will cease to be, and it will be agony the entire time the change occurs.

#### Transmission

Transmission, as far as we know, can only occur between a being and the sunlight. It cannot be transmitted between other wanderers, and it cannot be transmitted from entities. Therefore, standing close to someone or something affected by Synthesial Reversal is safe for the most part.

WARNING: AS OF 19465 THE FOLLOWING INFORMATION IS INCORRECT, PLEASE READ [CASE 5](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/things-that-go-bump-in-the-light/offset/5) FOR MORE INFORMATION.

#### Prevention

Prevention, for the most part, is easy to do. The best suggestion is to simply stay out of any level with sunlight present. However we do understand that to get from one safe level to another, the trip may be required. If absolutely necessary, it is highly recommended to have the following:

- Complete coverage of the body through clothing. Dark clothes are recommended as they block out the light the easiest. Sunscreen has proven to be ineffective at this current time.
- Other blockout material to shade yourself. Umbrellas are highly recommended and it is suggested you layer them with other black material such as curtains and thick blankets.

Furthermore, when it comes to the sun, simply **do not look at it. Do not approach it. Stay vigilant.** Keep your eyes to the ground and navigate your way to your next exit as soon as possible.

### AFFECTED LEVELS

  
As it currently stands, any level with either a day/night cycle or permanent sunlight is affected by this event, and will emit Synthesial Reversal as described. The following list, however, is a collection of levels where the side effects of the event have been more severe:

| Level Affected | Details |
| --- | --- |
| [Level 7](level-7.md) | While the level itself seems relatively unaffected, it would seem that its main predator has fallen to the sun. For further info, read [Case 9](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/things-that-go-bump-in-the-light/offset/9). |
| [Level 10](level-10.md) | The crops have withered, and worms have become both carnivorous and cannibalistic. The classification of the level has been updated to **Class 3** to reflect. Despite the low classification, residing on this level is not recommended due to the present sun. |
| [Level 23](level-23.md) | The Heart of Water is beginning to dry up. The plant material that makes up this level is deteriorating, mould and rot forming within the cracks and seams in its wake. On top of that, the beasts are becoming more and more erratic in behaviour, and the Buried Sentries are consistently activated. The classification of the level has been updated to **Class 5**, however concerns of its deterioration lead to beliefs that this will change to **N/A** in the future. Base Seedling shall monitor the level in the meantime. |
| [Level 29](level-29.md) | Hyperian as we once knew it is destroyed. Synthesial Reversal drew that aquatic monstrosity out of its depths, and the ensuing destruction took the entire island down with it.
