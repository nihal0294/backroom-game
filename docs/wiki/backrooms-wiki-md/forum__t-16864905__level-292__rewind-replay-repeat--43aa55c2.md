Well, the concept itself is good. I know what you're getting at with all the information and stuff. But I have to ask you some questions about the level respectively:

- Why hasn't the MEG decided to release such information to the public? Each article must have some answer about that, otherwise, it would be somewhat ambiguous.

- Does this level change every time you re-enter it? Does it change over time? These are questions that have arisen in me, since I have not fully understood how the level can change suddenly.

- How have they managed to collect the information from "Document 3", if up to that point, it is known that wanderers can die? This last question left me with doubts. Due to the great instability that flows within the level, how is it possible for them to have that information.

Hey

I love this concept but this page feels too clinical to really explore it. Additionally, the page is not subtle at all. It goes "this level is great" to "this level is evil and bad and dangerous" to "this level is hell". It's all very obvious and stereotypical which is a shame.

For starters, why are the MEG bothering to write whole documents if they aren't releasing them? Also, why aren't the MEG releasing these documents when they are clearly very concerned about the level? These are choices that unjustified and only seem to exist to suit the narrative. I like the idea of a MEG that aren't wholly morally just but this doesn't execute it well enough to be believable.

Focusing on these documents themselves, as I said before they are so obvious. I can tell exactly what you want the reader to think based on the overly emotive language used. The reality is, people don't become scared of something because you say "this thing is really scary".  
Some examples of what I mean

> While in this menacing level  
> To enter this hazardous void  
> Other than following the dangerous "humans"  
> Level 348 is an infinite, white void from hell  
> A deadly phenomenon occurs in Level 348 that endangers all people in it  
> Upon escaping, which will not occur,

Secondly, why is there any narrative involved on this page? It doesn't really do anything other than say the MEG are bad and there's some guy. It ends on a completely unjustified cliffhanger about someone we don't even know. The narrative isn't being used effectively at all.

The conept is great but the execution falls short. Don't give up tho. This is a really solid concept and something I think people will like. Here is my advice:

You have narrative and you have the idea of an article that doesn't reveal all the information needed. These are some great ingrediants for a good page. Instead of explicitly showing us the MEG changing the article and saying "it's really bad now", have the first article be the real article and the rest explored through narrative. Make full use of the capabilities of narrative. What's great about using narrative is that you don't have to approach is from the POV of a group who already know everything. You can even utilise the structure of "it's great" -> "it's bad" -> "it's awful" that you use with the artcles (e.g. main article where things are good, email from someone discouraging the main character from going to the level saying that it's not good anymore, main character going and it's really bad).

Also, avoid language that simply states what should be felt about something. Show not tell kind of thing. Some pages I recommend for inspiration in overcoming this:  
[http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-298](level-298.md)  
[http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-789](level-789.md)  
[http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-204](level-204.md)  
You don't have to read them but they do a good job at creating emotion without forcing it.

Overall, I sound mean but it's because I like the idea of this page. I think you've come up with something really creative that deserves to be on site.  
Feel free to ask any questions or talk about anything. Let me know when you've made changes and you're ready for me to look at the page again.  
Good luck!
