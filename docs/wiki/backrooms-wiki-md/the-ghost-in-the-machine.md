![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/the-ghost-in-the-machine/cbd.png)

a random store in the middle of nowhere in particular

**Level 6c75746579** is the one level beneath your mind and also the ninth circle of hell. Coroners go here sometimes. And also if you're visiting, please tell my cousin [Ringu](insanity-emporium.md) they left the cats outside again thx.

Five months ago, an unfortunate moderator made the terrible mistake of whitelisting paginations from the Hub of Mundanity. Since then, strange forces from beyond human comprehension have been flooding this wiki with vague dialogue, spooky creepypasta, and undue complaints about literally anything + everything in chatrooms during ungodly hours of the eve.

## Description:

> 15 16 17-year-old[1](javascript:;) writer / gamer / illustrator. Struggling with chronic insomnia so expect me to be posting at odd times throughout the day. Mother of three (cats).

**Level 6c75746579** is a godless land resulted from leaving Centurys Lute in the same house as an ignitable stove for more than a dozen seconds. There used to be content and stuff here before the entire place was unfortunately swept up in a bout of hellfire and the sky wept with horror as the ground was salted 57 times over.

It used to be a heart-pumpingly terrifying place before the fiery ash angels burnt down too and now all that's left is a depressing plane of gravel and just like that, the heart of entropy let out its last pathetic beat as the entire universe finally came to a standstill in preparation for a long, boring eternity.

And also as the world awaited its sad, gloppy fate, someone took the time to build up a CBD on the main street. Idk why, but it's just there now.

**[Dream Gate Hub](dream-gate-hub.md)**

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/the-ghost-in-the-machine/twin.png)

In these halls, the sun shall always shine.

A while ago, I had a dream.

I was sleeping in my bedroom during the day, under the light of the noon sun. This was back when I first moved, so all my stuff lay stacked in stiff cardboard boxes pushed to the corners. The bedroom I was assigned happened to be on the second floor, where the ground was this shifting fiber-made carpet you find in every millennial suburban house.

I didn't have a bed at that point, so I slept on a mattress, slumped on the carpet. The sun from the window opposite me was able to fall squarely where I laid, and I could see whirlwinds of dust dancing in the light. The ceiling was low on the second floor; the window reached pretty much up to where the wall ended. To my left was the white door leading to my bathroom, to my right was an empty wall.

Until one moment, it wasn't. A crawlspace extended there, padded with floral wallpaper, barely larger than a vent. The sun cooked it warm and stuffy under the weather, and I didn't know where in the house it led. I was still staring at it when I heard something rustling inside.
