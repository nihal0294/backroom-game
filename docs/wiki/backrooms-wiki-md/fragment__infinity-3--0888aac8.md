## Automoville Attack on Leaders Results in Tragedy

---

*On July 17th, 1986, around 400 citizens of the nation Emstable, previously known as Automoville, led a violent protest against the Leaders and their primary base on [Scene 00.2](trimmed__level-286--3ff092b9.md). This violent outburst lasted for just under 5 hours, resulting in casualties and injuries on both sides.*

*While no one could have predicted the tragedy that would occur on this day, tensions have been rising between the two factions for quite some time, and some would argue that events such as this were a long time coming. In fact, this is not the first time Emstable has attempted to cause harm to the Leaders, though this is by far the most violent and most serious incident.*

*Estimates say that the tension between these two communities has been rising ever since the early 70’s, with citizens of Emstable frequently leaving Scene 02.0 and relocating in one of the many areas under Leader ownership. This resulted in a significant amount of attempts from the oligarchs of Emstable to tug on the reins and grow their population, even forbidding exit from various floors of Scene 02.0 to prevent the decay in population numbers. Indeed, several former citizens of Emstable are eager to report their dissatisfaction with the governing body of Floor 1.*

> *Floor 1 would send out these PSAs every couple of years saying that they were so “proud of the growth in the community” and a bunch of generic bullshit like that. They’d make these promises to people to give out resources and opportunities to people who brought in more new citizens or had kids and would always dig on other factions in the process. I lived on Floor 14, and I didn’t really have much, so I got a few friends to move in on some of the lower floors. We all went through the whole process together. I never saw a dime, and neither did they.*  
> — *Jimmy Moore, a former resident of Emstable for 17 years*

*It would appear that Emstable governors used these PSAs to rally against the Leaders, attempting to breach the Leaders’ ArchwayOS in 1983 and led several nonviolent protests against smaller areas of Leader residence. As evidenced by the lack of public awareness, these attempts were unsuccessful, leading Emstable to take more drastic measures.*

*During the attack on Scene 00.2, several shops and businesses were subject to rampaging, leaving structures in a state of disrepair that could take weeks or even months to completely restore. Numerous shopkeepers and residents attempted to fight back during this chaos, the situation even escalating to the point where firearms were used.*

*The attacks started near the Scene’s entrance and climaxed near City Hall and its surrounding areas, and it is only because of the brave citizens of the Leaders that they were not able to enter the building and cause harm to the Figureheads. After properly alerting City Hall security, the attackers were quickly taken care of and escorted out of the Scene.*

*The exact number of injuries and deaths on both sides are still being determined. We at the Dailyrooms encourage all citizens of the Exteriority to stay home and stay safe, as we are unsure what will come next.*

> Document Date of Origin: July 17th, 1986.  
> Document Scene of Origin: [Scene 07.0](level-3.md)
