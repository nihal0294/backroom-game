> Entity 112, or "skinsects" are medium-to-large…

Capitalize Skinsects in this context, as a proper name should always be capitalized. The same goes for all other uncapitalized usages of **Entity 112**.

> Their colours vary wildly, and may even be various colours at the same time.

What specific colours can these entities be, Are these skin-tone colours, or RGB type colours? I'd recommend changing this sentence to "There is a wide range of Entity colouring and Entity 112 instances can be many colours simultaneously."

> you may offer to bond completely by rubbing the area you want it to latch on with some almond water.

Almond Water should be capitalized, and you may want it to link to the [Object 1](object-1.md) Article.

> Many outposts and even some partypoopers have/used to have little farms…

Partypoopers should be changed to [Entity 68](entity-68.md), and link to the article as well.

> Unconfirmed, but it’s said that a specific instance called the “mumbling armour” exists.

Mumbling Armour should be capitalized, as it's a special form of **Entity 112**.

> The first confirmed instance of 112 were discovered in late 2020, crawling around on level 78 eating the various hydroponics plants, and being very well hidden within the brambles of the plant they were found under.

I recommend changing most of this, as I feel like it lacks the Clinical tone.  
"The first confirmed instances of **Entity 112** was discovered in [Level 78](level-78.md) during late 2020. **Entity 112** was found consuming various hydroponic plants. **Entity 112** was obscured in the brambles of most plants;.
