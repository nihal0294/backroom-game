Glitches in No-clipping?

05 Jan 2023 17:00

**"Glitches in no-clipping have resulted in a disproportionate number being sent to a particular location on Level 234."**

A friend and colleague wondered about my logic. After all, we do not really understand how no-clipping works, so how can there be a glitch?

I can explain. Knowing (or not knowing) the actual mechanisms of no-clipping is not necessary for this statement to be true. An examination of the lore of many levels indicates that there are times when no-clipping is rather consistent (enough to be documented as either an entrance or an exit). If a wanderer does a certain thing in a certain place on a level (Point A), then that wanderer will almost consistently no-clip to a specific place (Point B). The "glitches" occur when the wanderer no-clips to Level 234 (Point C) instead of the traditional destination.

WHY these mysterious "glitches" occur is explained in the article:

**"#2) Malfunctioning teleportation targeting lenses are responsible for hijacking the large number of no-clips. Attempts are being made to isolate and disengage the damaged mechanisms."**
