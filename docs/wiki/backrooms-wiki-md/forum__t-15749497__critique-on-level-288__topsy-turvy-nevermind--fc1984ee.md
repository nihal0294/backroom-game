You should try adding spaces underneath the exits category because at the moment, when you open the exclusive entities part, it will push all the images out of the table. In the spikats information paragraph, why did you add the detail that says the navy blue blood is safe to drink? It just sounds a bit off and unnerving to me so maybe consider removing it or changing to a different property, for example, the blood is not poisonous and contains a high amount of nutrients, which will make the sentence less gory but also have relatively the same meaning. In the Cravums paragraph, the solution to escaping Cravums should be "run to safety" or some other way to escape as to who can outrun a car with a human head on it. Also, the different heads of Cravums should have different properties to affect the Cravum itself because it makes no sense to have cars with heads on them that have no different properties compared to a normal car. In the semi-final sentence of the Azlers paragraph, why does it say the population of Azlers is increasing and then in the next sentence said the Cravums are hunting them down? Makes no sense to me. In the last paragraph of the descriptions for Level 228, it should be decisions, not decision. Plus, a lot of the pictures need better spacing by using the "" in each line to create a gap. And the pictures themselves need to be polished if it is yours and if it isn't, you should find better sources that are more detailed. A lot of grammar problems, you should run it through something to check the grammar and spelling as it is quite common within your article and I won't be mentioning all of them here cuz it will just take too long. Also, I don't know whether the "revisions" are intentional for the final post or not but you should very tidy it up as the article is very hard to read.
