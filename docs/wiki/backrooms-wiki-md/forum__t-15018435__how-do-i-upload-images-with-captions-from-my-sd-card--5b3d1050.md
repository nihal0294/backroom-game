Well first to actually get an image uploaded, you need to be able to find where it is located on your PC. Windows 7, or any other version, shouldn't really affect your ability to upload files. Though it may have different locations for certain files.

Simply go to the bottom of your page, and click on the "files" button. You simply need to click on "Upload a file from your computer" and find the image file(s) you're looking for.

Looking at your page, it seems you've already uploaded files to it. I'm assuming that's not the issue.

If you are struggling to find your pictures once they are uploaded to your PC, the default location for imported pictures in Windows 7 should be in the "My Pictures" folder, which should be in your user folder.

So it should be under: C:\Users\(Your username here)\My Pictures

Though if you can't find a "My Pictures" folder, maybe there's another folder for pictures in your user name folder somewhere. I hope that helps if that's your issue.

If the issue is with putting the picture on the page, you should use the following code block:

[[include component:image-block  
|name= photo name goes here  
|caption= caption text goes here  
|align=right  
]]

Just replace "photo name goes here" with the photo url, and replace "caption text goes here" with whatever you want the caption to be. You can use multiple lines for the caption if you want.

The "align" part is simply where you want the photo to appear, so "|align=right" will put it on the right side of the page, replacing "right" with "middle" will put it in the center of the page, and replacing it with "left" will put it on the left side of your page.

If you can't find the photo url and you uploaded it to the sandbox, just click the file name after clicking files at the bottom of the sandbox page, and copy the url from that new page with just the picture. That is what you use as the photo url.

Seeing as how you already have some photos showing on your page, I imagine this isn't your issue, but I wanted to be thorough. Whatever your exact problem was, I likely covered it here somewhere. Let me know if that's not the case.
