Hey, InSaneDB!

I remember critiquing your level a few weeks back, so here I am again!

First of all, I did not see any SPaG errors, good job.

Now, for the level design…  
I have noticed the changes that you have made according to my suggestions last time, and I *thought* you had covered all of my questions. But I do have another idea >;)

You have 2 specific word choices I want to focus on here: "Base Mute" and "Languageless". These both refer to being unable to speak or having no language. Can you see where I'm headed with this? What if… you physically couldn't communicate through speech or writing? Just an opinion based on your word choice, but you do you.

One more thing: I don't think that a huge section of 10 or so different vehicles you can find is neccessary. As author told me a few days ago, "If you can say something in 3 words instead of 10, say it in 3." Pretty much, I'm telling you it's unnecessary.

Again, PM me with any questions or if u want me to crit after an update! Iamalemon
