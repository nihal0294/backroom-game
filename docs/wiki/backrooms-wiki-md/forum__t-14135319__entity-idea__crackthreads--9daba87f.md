Entity Idea: Crackthreads

02 Jul 2021 19:26

Greetings! Magus Serling Here. I've been reading up on the backrooms for a while and had an idea for an Entity that's been floating in my head for a bit. I hope you enjoy the concept:

**Page Type:** Entity

**Genre:** Horror,

**Page Layout (Optional):** I intend to make this a report written by an explorer making observations of entities in the backrooms akin to a biologist. The document intends to be a fully summarized report similar to other Entity entries, as well as recommendations on dealing with them.

**Elevator Pitch:** The Basic concept is a jellyfish like series of threads that disguises itself as cracks in wallpaper. Occasionally it detaches from the wall to drift and float to another wall. It's initially assumed to feed on moisture until it comes in contact with a person who gets caught in its threads, like a fish snared by a man-o-war.

**Central Narrative:** The crackthread has no discernible 'head' or anatomy. It is a colony of connected black threads so thin, that when pressed to a wall they resemble dried cracks. They react to sudden changes of temperature in the room. this can be from body heat, or subtle adjustments in the air from vents or other things. when it senses the presence of heat, it detaches from the wall and lazily drifts across the room with the hope of colliding with anything that might be passing by, like a sentient spider web. If nothing is caught, it merges with the wall on the other side and goes dormant for a few moments, permitting a brief window to pass by unaffected.  
The observer initially had concluded that it was meant to gain sustenance from the moisture in the air, the wallpaper and the floor, which is why the section of room where the crackthreads reside seems so bone dry. but they only seem to be in either dry locations or where there are suspicious drawings of people on the wall, similar to a child's stick figure drawings.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** Unlike many of the other entities, This one is meant to blend in seamlessly with the environment of the lobby floor, and can be easily missed. The primary hook is its effect on passerby. When a person is caught in the crackthreads feelers, their skin begins to wrinkle and drain of its moisture. If they pull away fast enough, they have the possibility to heal if water is applied to the area within a few hours, otherwise the affected area will feel the skin is extremely taut and thing in that area and will be prone to injury.

However, if the subject cannot escape the crackthread feelers, they will grow disoriented and use the walls to keep balance. the feelers will drape over the person and continue to drain its prey. rapidly dehydrating them until they start to shrivel. It is not actually feeding exclusively on its moisture, but rather, its third dimension. feeding on it to extend its size and provide sustenance for longer periods of floating. By the time a victim is completely drained, they will resemble a vague chalk drawing on the wall they were pressing against. drained of all detail and dimension.

**Additional Notes:** Personally, I'm on the fence about the name. I have a very clear image of what these things would look like and how they work. but the name might need some workshopping. I am unsure if the 'chalk drawing' is a thing that feels consistent with the rest of the crackthread ethology. I wanted there to be a threat of running into these things and I was reminded of my unfortunate brush with a man-o-war when I was younger. I don't want these things to be sentient, just an example of something that's not quite flora, not quite fauna, that would thrive on entities or visitors alike in the room.

So. What do you think?
