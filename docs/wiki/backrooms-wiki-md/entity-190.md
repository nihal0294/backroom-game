### Description (Frontrooms):

Whenever they are encountered in the Frontrooms, Men in Black are never imagined to be anything other than human beings. They are mysterious men, often dressed in black, who silence witnesses to so-called Unidentified Flying Objects and terrorize researchers of UFOs and esoteric subjects. Any genuine strangeness is remembered only after they depart.

No one has ever reported meeting a group larger than three, with pairs being common and single individuals encountered the most often.

Some witnesses describe them as cadaverous and devoid of body hair. Others see them as swarthy. There is always something slightly "off" about their appearance and mannerisms.

Another curious trait is that the clothes that the MiB are wearing look as if they had purchased them in a clothing store ten minutes beforehand, and even the bottom of their shoes seem unwalked-on in appearance, no matter the weather. Everything is always brand new.

The Men in Black received official United States Air Force recognition in a confidential memo sent to all commands that began: *"Information, not verifiable, has reached Hq USAF that persons claiming to represent the Air Force or other Defense establishments have contacted civilians who have sighted unidentified flying objects."* The letter recounted how one imposter demanded and received photos belonging to a private citizen and another bogus officer, in full Air Force uniform, assembled local police and other citizens to warn them not to speak about their recent UFO sightings.[1](javascript:;)

### Behaviors (Frontrooms):

Behaviors are where things get really interesting, really fast.

The MiB often intimidates a witness **before** that person has a chance to tell anyone else about seeing a UFO. In a surprising turn of events, they describe the UFO sighting to the witness in astonishing detail and know when the witness is lying or hiding something. They are aware of any video, photos, or tangible proof that might "upset the balance of the multiverse."

They warn the witness to keep silent and confiscate any physical evidence. Victims speak of being unable to think or react rationally until after the enigmatic visitors depart. To some, this "deer in the headlights" confusion suggests altered states of consciousness or subliminal hypnotic techniques.

Men in Black appear and disappear at will. Their black automobiles often vanish down dead-end roads. These Cadillacs and Jaguars, reported in the United States and Europe, respectively, are usually vintage models that belong in a showroom.

### Earth and Psi:

Psychics from Earth trapped here (how did they not predict that coming?) find their occult abilities growing sharper and stronger day by day. According to Alayna, an evolved Faceling from the distant future now stationed at the Harbinger Heights region of [Level 246](level-246.md), this is because the Earth is under the influence of metaphysical suppression due to the concentration of humans found there. This inherent filter permeates every being in the Frontrooms, native or visitor, and greatly reduces many supernatural abilities.

The suppressive anti-psychic field does not exist in the Backrooms because humans do not belong here. The Backrooms environment permits Men in Black to be genuinely terrifying.

### Description (Backrooms):

We identify life on Earth by its appearance. Men in Black are shapeshifters who can change their appearance more easily than we change our clothes, with access to an infinite closet. MiB can become an entity, your best friend, or even **you**.

Men in Black are able to become invisible or pass through objects like ghosts. Unlike a poltergeist, MiB cannot move or affect solid matter while in this ghostly state. They do not possess telekinetic powers.

[Addendum]

**Estimate of the Situation**

Having chosen this existence to live in the Backrooms has opened a number of doors for me. Beings with access to temporal travel and predestined knowledge have filled in a number of blanks in my research. This is what I have learned:

Human beings on Earth are under strict **quarantine**.

The Men in Black enforce said quarantine.

Certain arcane truths, like the existence of other worlds and beings,[2](javascript:;) temporal manipulation, quantum consciousness, and free energy, are off-limits to humanity on Earth. If mankind learns these secrets, it can apparently "upset the balance of the multiverse." Punishments can include stern warnings, threats of violence, misfortune, or, ultimately, an exile to the Backrooms. That final judgment is basically a death sentence. When the "execution" fails, the MiB arrive to "tie up loose ends."

Extrapolating this, the Major Explorer Group needs to recognize that anyone finding a way to the Frontrooms is in mortal danger from the MiB.

Anne Dunne[3](javascript:;)  
M.E.G. Team "Epiphany"

The "deer in the headlights" trauma that victims suffer during MiB presence is nothing compared to what Entity 190 can do to the human mind in the Backrooms. We call it "**enchantment**." Imagine that the human mind is a massive room of standing dominoes. These dominoes control not only emotions but also bodily functions. Enchantment is a chain reaction of toppling dominoes until death results.

The latest official fatality from exposure to enchantment occurred to M.E.G. Security Technician Alberto Sanchez of Team "Harbinger." (*Wanderers are directed to **The Visitor** section of the article describing* [Level 710](level-710.md) *for more information.*) Sanchez was pronounced Dead On Arrival by medical personnel at Base Alpha.

### Biology:

[Confidential]

The Man in Black was chasing us across [Level 19](level-19.md) when, in an attempt to avenge the death of my friend, Alberto Sanchez, I let the entity have it with two blasts of [firesalt](object-15.md). The MiB did everything a creature aflame should do. The flesh blistered and blackened. It screamed and staggered. Then it laughed. The Man in Black promised to cuddle us in a hellish embrace and drew closer, wrapped in flames.

We ran for our lives until we fell to our knees in exhaustion. The MiB caught up with us, not even breathing hard. In fact, it wasn't even breathing. There was no hint of a skull or skeleton; there were no holes for the eyes, nose, or mouth. The creature was little more than crawling dots and flickering filaments. It was unnerving to watch it reconstitute flesh, face, and clothes from nothing.

It stood up, tipped its fedora, and said, ["A mind is a terrible thing to waste."](annes-escape.md)

That was the last thing that I remembered until Anne snapped me out of the enchantment.

Amy Cochrane  
M.E.G. Team "Epiphany"
