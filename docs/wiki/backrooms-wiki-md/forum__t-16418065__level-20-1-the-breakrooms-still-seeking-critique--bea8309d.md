I’m not the best at critique but I’ll give it a go.

First thing I will say is a lot of the sentences could probably be a bit shorter. For example:

*‘ Every room in Level 20.1 is known to be almost identical in size and appearance though there may be a few notable differences such as; a different number of doors located in the room, the relocation of vending machines or tables, slight decrease in the ceilings height and the sudden appearance of additional items such as a coffee machine or toaster.’*

This is one massive sentence and it doesn’t have the best flow. I feel like putting a full stop after appearance and before though would break it up a bit. Another thing to do with punctuation here is ceilings should have an apostrophe due to the height belonging to the ceiling. The start of the list should also have a colon rather than a semicolon. (Sorry for being so picky with punctuation it just makes it flow smoother and seem more professional)

*‘Currently four unconfirmed reports regard Partygoers roaming specific rooms in Level 20.1, two additional reports mention the possibility of Skin-Stealers hunting in Level 20.1.’*

All I would do here is break this into two sentences so it reads easier. This can be done by replacing the comma after 20.1 with a full stop.

*‘To purchase items from these machines one is required to pay in the United States Dollar around one to three dollars depending on the item being purchased.’*

I’m unsure if it’s just me but this sentence doesn’t read very well with my brain. It think it could be worded a bit better. I would probably reword it something similar to this:

‘To purchase items from these machines one is required to pay in United States Dollars. Between one and three USD is required depending on the item being purchased.’

Sorry if I have just been rambling, mainly about punctuation. I hope the little critique I have given helps. I like the concept that you have here and wish you all the best in getting the green light!

Differences  
More repetitive: I tried to make the Level repeat and never have a true end.  
Varying details: The food in this Level is more common food you'd find in a break room.  
Hostile Entities: I tried to make the Level a mix of safe and dangerous at the same time and used the zeta SD class for that.  
No Bases: I made the time limit in Level 20.1 purposeful to keep a group from establishing a base or outpost in the level.  
Time limit: keeps wanderers from staying with Level 20.1 for more than twenty minutes.  
Entrances/Exits: Level 20.1's exits are linked to levels that would have break rooms in real life.
