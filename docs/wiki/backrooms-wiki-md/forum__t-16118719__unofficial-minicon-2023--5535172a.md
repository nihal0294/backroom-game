Unofficial Minicon 2023!

05 Jul 2023 00:13

Recently, CSS contest 2023 was made but as of writing, people have not appreciated that idea. So i thought, what if we take an old contest that people enjoyed and then remade it? If you don't know what MiniCon is you can check out this link: <http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/mincon2021#pages>  
(it's basically short but enjoyable pages)

This is an unofficial challenge which was made entirely for fun and is inspired by 's unofficial contest. If you want to participate in this contest, reply to this thread saying "I will participate." so that i can keep track of people.

The contest is hosted by if you see somebody else pretending to be the host, don't trust them.

## Ranking

Ranking will be done via total upvotes on each page in order to prevent bad-faith downvoting. Standard deletion threshold rules apply. There are no tangible prizes for this challenge, other than bragging rights.

## Rules

- Take the contest seriously, don't just make a coldpost level article, be creative and express your thoughts.
- If you think your submission won't be posted to the site by Monday the 1st due to lack of GL or critique checks, let me know so we can work something out.
- One submission per person.
- Collabs are allowed but no more than 3 people in one as the articles are quite short.
- Don't be the reason unoficial contests get banned from the site.

## Time Periods

#### Tuesday 4th of July - Monday 11th of July

Sign-up time.

#### Monday 10th of July - Monday 18th of July

Writting time

#### Monday 18th of July - Monday 1st of August

Submission time

#### Monday 1st of August - Monday 8th of August

Voting time
