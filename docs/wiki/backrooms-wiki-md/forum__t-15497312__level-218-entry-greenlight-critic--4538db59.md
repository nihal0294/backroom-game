[Here's what I got...](javascript:;)

Just some light crit, don't mind me…  
Let's begin.

> Lights will usually remain on the majority of time on the level, but it is known for the level to occasionally shut off any lights for approximately 1-3 minutes.

So, you included a redundancy in saying "usually" and "majority of the time". Perhaps you could rephrase it to "The lights are known to stay constantly on, but are known to occasionally shut down for 1-3 minutes at a time."

> Any contact with the lights will result in a third degree burn, and long exposure to them at a three foot distance will result in death.

Just how long is "long exposure"? You should detail that.

> If any tree is cut or split in some way, it will emit leaks of almond water. How it does this is unknown.

"If any trees are cut or pierced, it will leak Almond Water with a consistency similar to tree sap." That's how it does that. Trees hve sap, so why not make the sap Almond Water?

> Below the tree are multiple colorful and wrapped presents all with the label to a relative or loved one known to the receiver.

Put a comma after "presents". This prevents the sentence from being a run-on.

> Presents vary in size depending on the gift.

The M.E.G. will not simply assume they're "gifts". Istead, they may say something like "package". You'll also want to put a comma before "depending".

> The area can be found infested by multiple entities roaming around the level such as skin stealers, smilers, howlers, etc.

This phrase is easily understandable, but there is perhaps a better way to phrase it. Consider "Multiple entities can be found on this level, including Skin Stealers, Smilers, and Howlers." Never use etc. when listing entities that are here. Wanderers want to know what the hell they're up against, and the M.E.G. won't generally withold that information from them. Capitalize the entity names. Make sure to include a link for the entities you list. I'll use an example with Skin Stealers.

> [[[http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-10 | Skin Stealer]]]

That's the code you would use for that. Replace the url and name as needed.

> It is unknown if the structure is actually a stairwell due to the abilities the area has to drop a victim’s sanity at a dangerous rate.

Now my question is, how does it drop their sanity? (not a clinical phrase either) There are many different kinds of insanity, so you may want to do a bit of research on that.

On another note, you need to detail the houses more. Not much is really known about them, and very little information is provided. Please elaborate on it.

> What makes this level so special is that, when you are to spend eight months on the level, you are transported back to reality. though it faces a consequence to take 1 month to 1 year of your life.

Anyone unlucky enough to get stuck in the Backrooms is unable to leave it That's the whole thing behind the Backrooms, so you'll want to delete this.

> The level can be entered by exploring too deep into Level 211, which will have a chance to no clip you into **Level 218**

How deep is too deep? Add a period at the end of your sentence.  
  
  
That's all I have for now! I think this is an interesting Christmas concept and has potential. It just needs some more detail, like with the houses. You spend about two sentences on them and move on. You shouls add more to the vents and the houses to add to the story. Thanks for reading this and happy new year!
