27 Jan 2022 17:30

<http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/poliacci>  
Hello guys. It is my fist draft ever, so I need as much critique and advices as it can possible be given to me. There are a lot of ambitions and ideas in my head but it is better to start with something simple, like carnivorous door for example :D  
But at first I think I should mention several things:  
-For sure there are two main problems: my inexperience and the fact that I'm not a native english speaker, so there might be a lot of poorly chosen words, lack of synonyms and etc. If you find egregious examples of my bad english - I will try to fix it, just show me where it is.  
-This concept is pretty simple, so I'm curious if someone is using this idea already (or if it already exists and i simply didn't found it). If this idea is already taken I'll do something else.  
-In the rules mentioned that gore and other cruel moments are forbidden, so I need an opinion about level of cruelty in my entity. If it is over the top I'll decrease it for sure!

I think it is all for now, hope I didn't forgot something. Thank you in advance for taking the time to me.

I do like the idea, and I’ll probably like it more if you can get the author of [Level 136](level-136.md) to add this entity as one of the entrances. Like, say someone just jump though the door before the door had the chance to bite. Where would it lead?  
Edit: there is a couple of mistakes, such as  
During this phase entity tries it's best to look just like other nearby rooms if it is not hungry

Probably try “During this phase, this entity tries it’s best to look like a seemingly normal door, waiting for a wanderer to have their curiosity turn against them.”  
Phase of attack - In this state, the entity [[410]] (not needed)reveals it's true appearance. [[taking off it's disguise]](not needed). In this phase, all the rooms of the entity will turn into acid, enough to melt the flesh, along with several flexible appendages. The space inside the entity quickly shrinks, with the door turning into curved teeth becoming a proper jaw. If the entity is hungry or taunted, it can go into it’s phase of attack. When a wanderer is trapped, the entity shifts it’s insides from acid to jaw and eats the victim. This is why it is unsafe to come to the entity less than 5 ft. (≈1.5 meters)
