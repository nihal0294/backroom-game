# **Description:**

Due to the nature of the Level, all data was obtained by a single male survivor out of the 14 that discovered the level, and as a result the information about the Level is limited. Due to HonestLier445 being mute and suffering from PTSD, all information may be inaccurate.

All information HonestLier445 gave was in written form due to his condition.

**Level 148** allegedly is a large, but not infinite, labyrinth of Greek origin. The Labyrinth apparently resembles the famous Cretan Labyrinth in both its intact state and its dilapidated counterpart, depending on the person viewing it. The Level apparently has multiple small to medium sized holes in the ceiling, revealing daylight. This appears to be the only source of light in the Level. It is unknown if you can escape the Level this way. It is also unknown if the Level has a day/night cycle. **Level 148** has 3 distinct areas, the entrance, the maze, and the exit. The entrance is a small room with a door leading into the labyrinth. The chosen youth supposedly used twine tied on the door to find his way back through the maze, an event that took place in the legend.

The exit is another large room, but with copious amounts of skeletons scattered on the ground. Within this room, there is a locked door that seemingly cannot be opened until the Minotaur is killed. Every other area within the level is a part of the labyrinth. Multiple galleries and doors are designed to mislead people within the Level. According to HonestLier445, a male Wanderer that claimed he managed to escape after he discovered the Level, the doors in **Level 148** are tunnels and the galleries are either very derelict or absent entirely. HonestLier445 claimed that the chosen youth repeatedly stated the doors and galleries were in pristine condition, although allegedly no one else in the Level could see what the youth was seeing. When questioned how he knew what the youth was saying, HonestLier445 claimed that he was deafened while in the Level after The Minotaur charged through a wall. It is suspected he has a Labyrinthine Concussion.

**Level 148** seems to correspond with the legend of The Minotaur with some major differences. **Level 148** seemingly causes 7 maidens(young women) and 7 youths( young men) to appear in the Level every year, though this is only based on the legend and not confirmed as of now. All 14 individuals appear in different areas within the level, with only the chosen youth appearing at the entrance. All individuals present within the Level will seemingly don a Greek robe, and all items originally on the Wanderers will disappear. The one youth will take on the appearance of Theseus, and obtains the weapons and the ball of twine Theseus received from Ariadne in the legend. The youth apparently gained heroic qualities, even though the youth affected did not possess these qualities. The youth took on a leadership role, and attempted to help the Wanderers escape.

According to HonestLier445, he was the only survivor, which is much different from the legend, as all 14 people in the labyrinth were saved in the legend. The other deviation is that the youth that killed the Minotaur apparently turned into the Minotaur. This process reportedly caused the youth to mutate into a Minotaur that looks identical to how it was described in the legend.

The main threat of **Level 148** presumably is the Minotaur. This entity is apparently extremely aggressive. The entity is slow, but can charge for short periods of time, though the exact speed and amount of time it can charge is unknown. The entity reportedly has enhanced strength and its preferred methods of killing is goring or crushing its victim. According to HonestLier445, the creature was only able be killed by the chosen youth, with the sword they possess. All other means of attack proved futile. HonestLier445 claimed that the Minotaur could not injure the chosen youth, and the youth ended up killing the Minotaur, resulting in the youth turning into the new Minotaur. Utilizing this opportunity as a chance to escape, HonestLier445 exited the Level through the exit door.

This entire Level could be fictitious in nature, but the amount of detail given and HonestLier445 not having anything to gain from lying makes this unclear. Due to this, HonestLier445 is currently under examination.

## Bases, Outposts and Communities

There are no known Bases or Outposts on this Level.

## Entrances and Exits:

### Entrances:

It is unknown exactly how to get into **Level 148**, but HonestLier445 claimed that he appeared in the Level while he was running away from a Clump in Level 1. It is assumed that once a year, 7 random youths and 7 random maidens will appear inside the level, though this is only based on the Legend of the Minotaur, and has no actual proof outside of HonestLier445's account.

### Exits:

According to HonestLier445, there is currently only one known exit within **Level 148** and that is to escape the labyrinth via the exit door, which only opens when the chosen youth kills the Minotaur. Once he escaped, he appeared within [Level 11](level-11.md), but it is currently unknown if you will appear in [Level 11](level-11.md) consistently.
