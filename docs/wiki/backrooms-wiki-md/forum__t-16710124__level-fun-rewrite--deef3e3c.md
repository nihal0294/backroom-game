There is nothing wrong with writing stuff based around Level Fun, or partygoers and party hosts for that matter, though, it might be safest to wait until we get our rewrite out for it if you include someone stumbling upon it randomly. It'll be different, yet familiar. The biggest thing with Level Fun will be that it's very hard to get to. We may add more entrances and exits, but the plan as it is right now will make randomly encountering it incredibly unlikely.

Alternatively, you could just create a separate party room level. While Level Fun is seen as *the* party room level, I don't see why multiple couldn't exist if there are multiple hotels, cities, heck, there are even 3 aircraft carrier levels. That's oddly specific. So having multiple party room levels is more than reasonable.
