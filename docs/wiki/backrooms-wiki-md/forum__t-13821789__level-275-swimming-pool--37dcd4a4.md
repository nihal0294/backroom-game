Level 275 - Swimming Pool

16 Oct 2020 16:48

Entrance: via Level 11, waking 5 miles due west of the M.E.G base will get you to a grove of trees with a double paved road, following it will lead you to an aqua-colored hangar shaped building. Going in to get to Level 275

When you enter you will be faced with an abandoned swimming pool, but the water lights are on and the pool is filled with water. When entering the level, you will feel an extreme urge to swim, regardless of whether or not you know how to swim. There is no pool bottom, it is infinite. The water is roughly 50 degrees Fahrenheit, and your clothes will not get wet. When you get in the water, a noise that resembles a cheering crowd will begin. Once you swim 100 yards (4 laps) the pool will push you out. After roughly ten minutes, you will feel the urge to swim again. After swimming around 2000 yards, there will be an opening back to Level 11. WARNING: There will be a fake entrance, this entrance takes you to Level Fun. You will notice this as if you get close to it, you will hear clown music.  
No entities, unknown if the monster of Level 7 resides here to.  
Exits: By finishing the pool race, true exit takes you back to Level 11  
Taking the fake exit after the race takes you to Level Fun  
Drowning, you pass out and wake up in Level 7.  
Classing: 1  
What do you think about this?
