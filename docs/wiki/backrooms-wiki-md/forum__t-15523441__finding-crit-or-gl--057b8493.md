[You open a bottle, hoping to find Almond Water.](javascript:;)

[Instead, you find a handwritten critique notice.](javascript:;)

- I'd like to start that this could be classified as SD 1e. There are definitely environmental hazards that can kill. Avoidable, yes, but lethal nonetheless.
- In your customized points about the safety of the Level, "Avoidable Environmental Hazards" should be shortened to "Environmental Hazards".

> Level 347 appears to be a huge amusement park. The level is too big that the M.E.G. tends to thinks that this level is endless.

- These sentences do not fit the clinical tone meant to be used in the articles. I would reword these sentences to something… detached. Purely objective. "Level 347 appears to be a large amusement park. It is large enough that the M.E.G. has theorized that it is liminal." That would fit the tone better.

> There are many recreation facilities in the level, appears same as the frontrooms, most of them are red and useable. Examples of the recreation facilities are roller coaster, ferris wheel, swing and so on.

- Make sure that everything listed here is *plural*.

- I'm not done, but I have to sign off for now. I'm leaving with this note. I scanned through your draft and I found several issues with the tone. I would go through it and ask myself: "How cold and scientific does this sound?" If the answer is "Not very." like this, then reword it. Don't re*write*, just re*word*. Have a great day/night.

[You've Got Mail.](javascript:;)

[I lied, just critique](javascript:;)

I will be breaking your level down paragraph by paragraph for SPaG mistakes, and for things you could elaborate more on. I suggest you run it through Grammarly, you missed some errors when proofreading it.

> Level 347 appears to be a large amusement park. It is large enough that the M.E.G. has theorized that it is liminal. There are many recreation facilities in the level, appears same as the frontrooms, most of them are red and useable. Examples of the recreation facilities are roller coasters, ferris wheels, swings and so on. They electricity source that activates the recreation is currently not discovered. Most of the facilities are working continuously. There will be a lot of goods inside the facility, such like Almond Water, Royal Rations, and Fire Salt.

Your first sentence is good.  
Your second sentence could use a change of wording. Perhaps instead of saying that "It is large enough that the" say something like "Due to its large appearance…[rest of sentence]"  
Your third sentence also needs some work. You need to include "they" between "level," and "appears".  
We don't need the detail about them being red; get rid of it.  
Your fourth sentence needs a comma between "and" and "so".  
In your fifth sentence, you accidentally added a "y" onto "The" making it "They" at the beginning of it.  
You don't need the sixth sentence; you already said most of them are usable.  
"There will be" in the seventh sentence sounds like there aren't useful items in the level as of right now. I suggest changing it to "There are". "lots of goods" should be changed to sound more formal. For instance, you could change it to "useful items".

> The time of the level is constant at 3 p.m..

Two periods here. Delete one.

> It should be noticed that, there is a shop near the big rabbit sculpture. Wanderers can find goods in the shop, such as Almond Water, Royal Rations, Squirt Gun and more. Also, there is clear bottled water that can be drinked safely that are sold in the shop. There is an entity that appears to be a dolphin toy on the table of the shop, known as The Cashier. When wanderers take the goods out of the shop, it will ask the wanderers to pay Candy as money. However, if wanderers tried to have goods inside the shop or take the goods out without paying, they will disappear and turn to one Hazardous Waste. Other than checking out, The Cashier will stay on the table without a movement.

The first sentence in this paragraph has an unneeded comma between "that" and "there".  
In the next sentence, I suggest replacing "goods" with "materials".  
In your fourth sentence, add "that is" before "known" and after the comma. It will read better that way.  
In your fifth sentence, it is very hard to figure out what you're trying to say. You need to reword it to be easier to understand.

> There'll be some random environmental hazard in the level. Here's the list that're reported:  
> An electronic leakage, especially in the facilities that're not red. Causing serious injury.  
> A random MegaDrop leading you to the sky, the safety belt disappears. Causing death.  
> A particular roller coaster, remaining grey, was designed to be a "suicide roller coaster". Wanderers will get a brain hypoxia caused by the overspeed of the roller coaster. Causing serious injury or death.

Avoid contractions such as "that're" unless it is to avoid repeating words. Additionally, "Here's the list that are reported" does not sound right. Reword it to something like: "This is a list of said hazards that have been reported:".  
In the first example of hazards in the level, instead of saying "especially" say "usually". Change all of the "causing" to "causes" or "can cause".  
The MegaDrop sentence needs to be improved. A machine can't lead you into the sky. You should change it to make more sense.

> All of the environmental hazard type mentioned above can be avoided by not taking the facilities.

Taking the facilities??? What do you mean here? You can't take a facility.  
"Type" is not needed here.

> Other slient environmental hazards. For example, a rock on the ground, the falling tree branches or get hurt by a book flying. These hazards will occur much more often that those mentioned above, and they cannot be avoided. However, they can only cause a small injury.

This paragraph as a whole needs to be worded better. "Other silent environmental hazards" is a dependent clause and needs to be edited to become an independent clause or have an independent clause added to it to become a complex sentence.  
How is "a rock on the ground" going to hurt you? Explain more(I know how, but add it anyway). For instance, say "tripping on a rock on the ground".  
"the" is not needed in the sentence.  
"get" needs to be changed to "getting" switch "flying" and "book" around in the sentence so it says, "flying book".  
"a" is not needed in the last sentence.

> The level seems to possess a self-protecting machanism. All the attempts to damage the level, such as digging the ground or drawing on the facilities in order to change it's color all ended in failure. Also, there are no discovered entity in this level except The Cashier

Regarding the second sentence, I don't see how you can damage a level. Digging up the ground isn't damaging, and drawing is simply defacing a building, or repairing the worn-out paint. "Also," is not needed in the next sentence. Additionally, you need a period at the end of the sentence.

> When you are enduring near-death experience, there is a minimal probability to get transfered here.

Picking a feather in Level 11 will transfer you here.  
You misspelled transferred. It has two "r"s, not one.

> Taking 10 recreation facilities will transfer you to Level 48.  
> Drinking a brown liquid from the shop will transfer you to Level 146.  
> Jumping into a swimming pool will transfer you to Level 7.  
> No-clipping into a pink wall will transfer you to Level 974.

You can't "take" a recreational facility. Word that first sentence better. Additionally, experience needs to be plural. The rest of your sentences are fine.

> After a short survey held by The M.E.G., The investigators from The M.E.G. discovered that the entity The Cashier will expand after recieving the Candies. The specific reason for this function is still unknown. In addition, an meterologist from The M.E.G. discovered that the high-layer clouds of Level 347 are moving in an extremely fast speed. Thus, he thinks that this level is expanding countinously.

Well, seeing as this message has come from the M.E.G sent this message, they should refer to themselves as "we" instead of "the M.E.G."  
The section about the invalid entrence seems completely unnecessary.  
Oof, now I have to critique a whole new section of the article.

> Also, the wanderers that goes into this level disappeared. The camera that The M.E.G. put in the level in order to check the situation of the level captured a huge burst in the level. The whole level disappeared after the burst. Currently, The M.E.G. didn't open the right to view this page to public. Thus, they will consider that this level can be entered by enduring near-death experience.

In your first sentence, it should be "have gone in this level have" not "goes". In your third sentence, I suggest changing "the burst" to "the event". Again, experience needs to be plural.  
"The M.E.G. didn't open the right to view this page to public. Thus, they will consider that this level can be entered by enduring near-death experience." I don't understand what you mean in this first sentence here. I also don't understand how that caused the M.E.G. to believe that you can still enter the level through a near-death experience.

> The M.E.G. predict that the size of the level depends on the size of The Cashier. Now, The M.E.G. assumed that The Cashier bursted because of having too much Candies that can make it big. This is also the reason of the hugeness of this level.

Instead of saying "predict" in this first sentence, change it to "believe". "assumed" should be "assumes" in the next sentence. Also, delete "can" in the sentence. The third sentence is not needed; delete it.

> There are no remaining bases, outposts and communities in this level.

Seeing as there were no bases in the original level before it disappeared, you can't say "there are no remaining bases, outposts, and communities in this level." as there weren't any in there in the first place. You also need a comma between "outposts" and "and".

> This level has already disappeared thus this module cannot be filled.  
> However the public thinks that this level can be entered by enduring near-death experience.

This first sentence does not need "already"; delete it. Get rid of "However" in the next sentence. Change "thinks" to believe.  
That's all (from me) folks!

  
Cheers, and good luck with your level,  
- Pen.
