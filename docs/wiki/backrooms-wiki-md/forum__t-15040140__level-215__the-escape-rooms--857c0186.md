Level 215: The Escape Rooms

24 Jul 2022 05:09

As aforementioned, when placed in this level you must escape a puzzle-filled room with a 1hr time limit. The catch? You may be forced to sacrifice a limb, or maybe push your physical ability to the max. But don't break the rules, or The Escape Artist won't be so kind… Calling all critters and greenlighters, I would appreciate any and all crit/giving a green light on this level; it's the result of 3 months of brainstorming and writing. Enjoy!

Thanks Robert!

I have updated the picture with a smaller mouth, and have gotten rid of the blood sacrifice, replacing it with almond water. I did however keep the sacrifice of a limb, as to foreshadow how dark and horrifying the Escape Artist really is. I felt that having the Escape Artist literally tear you apart fitted his alternate behaviors, as he is a ruthless savage if his "creations" are not respected.

Also, A fun fact: The Escape Artist's title works in 2 ways. The first being he is a master of escape, attempting to escape the Backrooms, and secondly, he is an artist of escapes, builing escape rooms.

I'm glad you enjoyed!
