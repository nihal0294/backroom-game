**Level 4** is the 5th Level of the Backrooms.

It is the level where [Almond Water](object-1.md) can be found most frequently.

# **Description:**

**Level 4** resembles an empty office building, though it is almost completely devoid of furniture. Some rooms on this level have windows, though most of them have been completely blacked out. Any [windows](entity-2.md) that have not been blacked out are considered traps, and should be avoided at all costs.

**Level 4** is mostly devoid of entities; [Hounds](entity-8.md) and [Dullers](entity-6.md) are the only ones to have been observed. One person claimed they saw a [Smiler](entity-3.md), but no evidence exists to prove this. Because of this lack of entities, there are lots of wanderers on **Level 4**. Water coolers, vending machines, and fountains containing Almond Water can be found scattered across this Level. **Level 4** is very easy to escape from, and also return to.

**Level 4** is the best place to meet other people and find supplies. Before attempting to move on to the next Levels, it is recommended that you stock up on Almond Water; it will be needed, especially in [Level 5](level-5.md) and [Level 6](level-6.md).

# **Bases, Outposts, and Communities**

## [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) (A.k.a The "Major Explorer Group") Base Omega:

- This is the second main base of [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md).
- This is where the other half of the group's teams live.
- They reside close to one of the entrances of [Level 5](level-5.md) and [Level 6](level-6.md).
- This base is heavily guarded.

## Amor Incrementum:

- This group is a religious cult that worships agriculture.
- This group is very small, having only around 10 members.
- The only plants they grew were various species of mold, harvested from walls in [Level 0](level-0.md) and [Level 1](level-1.md).
- They're incredibly hostile and not normally up for trade (unless you have religious objects - such as objects depicting Jesus or other religious entities - in which case they most likely *would* be willing to trade).

## T.B.D. (A.k.a "To Be Determined"):

- The group likes to hoard various objects and items for themselves.
- The group is one of the smallest in the Backrooms, with only 6 members.
- The group will ignore you unless you try to interact with them.
- They only trade for really strange objects that don't really have a function.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

### **Entrances:**

There are many ways to reach this level. Some of the most known ways are to No-Clip into a wall in [The Hub](the-hub.md). You can also get here from [Level 3](level-3.md) using the elevators, or by finding a door on [Level 2](level-2.md) that is unlocked. Falling through The Pit in [Level 283](level-283.md) has the chance of teleporting divers through the ceiling of **Level 4**.

### **Exits:**

**Level 4** has occasional exits that lead to [Level 5](level-5.md) and [Level 6](level-6.md). Most exits take the form of office-style stairways or occasional elevators that may even lead back to [Level 3](level-3.md). Rarely, stairs leading to a basement can be found, and these go to [Level 71](level-71.md). As with almost all exits in the backrooms, once you lose a direct line of sight with them, they may disappear unexpectedly.
