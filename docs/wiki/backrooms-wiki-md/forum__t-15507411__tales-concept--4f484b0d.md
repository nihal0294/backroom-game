**Hello, wanderers.**  
I want to give my concept to you guys, and hopefully recieve some good feedback on it!  
Here's a short excerpt of the series' general premise.

> *A young musician no-clips into the Backrooms. As she traverses through the levels, she is reminded of various songs and musical pieces from the Frontrooms. As she travels through a specific level, you'll see the lyrics pop up for that song in real time with her experiences. Most songs will have a Weirdcore vibe, since I think that the aesthetic fits the Backrooms very well.*
