Stuck Criticism - Object 110

12 Dec 2023 13:53

Hello, I'm sorry if it's late to see many things, the truth is that these days I've been very distracted. But it doesn't matter, I will try to be more active in the forums and reviews. Now going directly to the article, the reviews will be updated here.

[Something teleports you to a new dimension...](javascript:;)

[With a note that says the following...](javascript:;)

*I didn't steal the idea of ​​the foldable from at all.*

Paragraph:

> **Space Clock is an hourglass with the property to cycle through the different slices of the 4th dimension in a 3rd-dimensional perspective.**

  
I thought this would modify it to:

> **Space Clock is an hourglass that can travel through the sectors of the *4th Dimension* in perspective to the *3rd Dimension*.**

---

Paragraph:

> **Space Clocks** resemble a traditional hourglass, with the sand being black round pieces with magnetic properties.

  
I had read this sentence several times and it seemed strange.

I thought this would modify it to:

> **Space Clock** is a common hourglass, which contains small magnetic balls inside.

---

> **Space Clocks** are invincible, indestructible, and everlasting.

  
You should remove "invincible" or "indestructible", since invincible and indestructible mean practically the same thing.

And I also added after "Clock" a '.
