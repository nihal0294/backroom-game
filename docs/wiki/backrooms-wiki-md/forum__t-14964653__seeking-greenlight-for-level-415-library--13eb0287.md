The idea is cool, but what if several book based object were also present on the 9th floor? Like magic tomes. I'm thinking of making an object that is a bookshelf containing multiple books, each one with different abilities. When destroyed, depleted of energy, the due date is reached, or the user tosses it with the intend to send it to the book, it will disapear from its current location and transport the book shelf regardless of where the bookshelf is. And damage will be completely repaired to access a book, a you need an item called a library card, which could be be found on level 415 anywhere from the 5th floor up, becoming more common the higher the level, but still rare overall. Simply put the card against a book and it will vanish, and the book itself will levitate into your hands. For a period of 1 week, the book now belongs to you. Any conditions mentioned before will cause you lose owner ship of the object. While in ownership, the user can teleport it to their hand and use any "spells" or follow other instructions in the book. Any attempts to follow the instructions in a book without being the owner gave met failure in all cases. There is a book about potions, 6 books about spells, and a book about enchantments.
