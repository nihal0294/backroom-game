## **What do we do?**

We provide professional entertainment venues for all wanderers. If you feel empty inside, then join us right away! Just pay Almond Water, and you can have fun here all day long!

## **Can I join?**

Of course! We are happy to see new members join our big family. We offer some of the best work benefits in the Backrooms including yearly vacations. At work, you can freely show your talents alongside our kind staff, write down the wildest ideas you can think of, and become a wealthy, admired designer! On vacation, you can enjoy wonderful trips to the habitable areas of Cluster C here or have an all-night party with entities *(100% SAFE)*!

Q: Does Backrooms Entertainment look like a bunch of lunatics causing chaos? Is that true?  
A: Backrooms Entertainment is all about the abstract and the spontaneous, so don't expect the people inside to be in great mental shape. With that in mind, you can try imagining yourself as part of this group of eccentric inventors, picturing any strange object created by your hands! Like an addictive drug that hooks anyone who sees it or a capsule that turns you into a balloon after you eat it.

Q: There are many strange objects in Backrooms Entertainment. How should I write similar ones?  
A: Objects that are too straightforward as weapons or fit common sense just aren't "entertaining" enough. An object can look perfectly normal on the outside but have a very bizarre function, perfect for pranking someone who's overly cautious. Or it may exhibit astonishing abnormalities in itself—ideally they should be neon-colored / multicolored. This is the main color scheme of Backrooms Entertainment and also an important clue for judging whether something comes from there.

Q: Do the levels in Backrooms Entertainment all have a consistent style?  
A: Yes! They all feature synthwave-style elements, such as sunsets, sports cars, and polygonal grids. But in reality, apart from the main base level, other places have been modified, so you don't have to write every level exactly like Level C-70, just having similar elements is enough~

Q: Is Backrooms Entertainment safe? Are their promises just a joke?  
A: It's not a joke, but whether or not it's safe is questionable, because "safety" in the eyes of lunatics may differ somewhat from the common understanding… But at least they hold no malice toward wanderers—guests are always welcome! This group of seemingly clueless people actually gets little attention from other groups—after all, you can't attack a monster without a health bar.

Q: Is it true that Paul Simmons, the founder of Backrooms Entertainment, used to be part of the Dragon Blood Clan and was involved in the destruction of the M.E.G.?  
A: It's true, but for certain reasons, that setting has now been scrapped. So currently, Backrooms Entertainment is really just a group of inventors brimming with wild ideas creating mischievous stuff together. Of course, as the founder, Paul has managed to keep this bunch of extraordinary people well-organized, so he must possess some unknown abilities of his own.
