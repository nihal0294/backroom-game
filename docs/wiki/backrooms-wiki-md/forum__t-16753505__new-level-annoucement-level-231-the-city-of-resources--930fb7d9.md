18 Mar 2024 06:47

Hey site users! I just posted my final [Canonicon2024](canonicon2024.md) contest page and probably the most important one of mine. I worked hard to make it enjoyable, so let me know what you think!

**Link:** [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-231](level-231.md)

**Genre:** Mystery, urban, wonder

**Description:** It features an expensive and important project of the B.N.T.G. that has the potential to provide infinite wealth and resources, however, one fatal mistake ruins it all. Shortly after the mistake, they find a mysterious building 14 miles south of the city, and they find themselves faced with the immeasurable power of the mysterious Backrooms Robotics corporation.
