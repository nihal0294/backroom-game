The Future of Stanley (and me as well)

13 Jan 2023 02:08

As I see the rest of the wiki, I have come to realize one simple factor that is scary for me to admit:

**I am no longer having fun reading stuff from people.**

This is why I'm taking the tough decision of going on a hiatus for…some years, at least 1. Perhaps I'll come back sooner than expected, but for now, I take my leave.

This community has given me so much experience with what to write and how to do it right. I've read stories that I want to give their OWN THEATRICAL PERFORMANCE. And I met some cool writers along the way of my….spectation to say the least.

Thank you for letting me get onto this site. I expect very good from all of you. Ciao.
