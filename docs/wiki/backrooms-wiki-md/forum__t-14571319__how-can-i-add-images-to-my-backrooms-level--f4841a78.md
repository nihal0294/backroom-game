Re: How can I add images to my Backrooms level?

20 Mar 2022 20:40

Scroll to the bottom of your page and open the "Files" section. Upload the image and name it what you want. After you do that, insert the following code below wherever you want the image to be.

```
[[include :backrooms-wiki:component:image-block
|name=(file name exactly)
|caption=(whatever caption you want)
|align=(left, center, or right of the page)
]]
```

Before uploading your images, make sure they are compliant by our [image use policy](image-use-policy.md). If they do not follow those guidelines specifically, they cannot be used on our website and your images will be removed.

If you have any further questions, reply here.
