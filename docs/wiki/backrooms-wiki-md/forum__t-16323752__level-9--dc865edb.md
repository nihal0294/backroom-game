Level -9

06 Sep 2023 18:33

**Type of page**: Level  
**Genre**:A psychological thriller may soon become a drama.(yeah)  
**The main concept**: Level -9 and its name is "Polluted Landscape". The level is something like a woods with micro lakes that often come across. The level has a psychological effect on wanderers (and even on entities!) And yes, quite popular entities, such as Smilers and Dullers, live on the level (the full list is on the level's page). I also came up with and even visualized an entity that unique to my level, but now this picture is not on the page (if you suddenly want to look on it, dm me). I've supplemented the level with several structures that may appear there and other features.

This is my first article here. I have long been interested in the topic of negative levels of Backrooms, and dreamed of expanding their list with ninth negative level, which, for some reason, was ignored for long time. I made all the images of the level completely by myself. [Here](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/firstkeshorder) is link to the draft, which is already done. And btw, I can't use the discord server of this wiki, since I was banned (violation of rule 2, although I just didn't understand how to register). Your criticism and opinion are very important to me if you paid attention to my article and I hope that I be able to publish it soon.
