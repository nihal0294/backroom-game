# Description

Level 9.9 is a dangerous sub-section of [Level 9](level-9.md) that serves mainly as a transition toward [Level 10](level-10.md), consisting of residential suburban streets adjacent to barren but overgrown fields separating the neighbourhoods. Due to a disastrous M.E.G. operation failure in 2019 (see D-Day document excerpt below for more information), Level 9.9 is now infested with hordes of rogue M.E.G. drones under the control of [Entity 96](entity-96.md) (The Neighbourhood Watch). The drones territorially hunt all lifeforms from this sub-section that are deemed threats to their domain in Level 9.

![Field with wonky electricity poles and drones in the sky](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/samedo07-3/Droneinsky)

M.E.G.'s first contact with the drones in 2019.

Level 9.9 has a distinctive shape, being broad yet extremely short in length. The width is unbounded, granting it non-Euclidean properties. Field surveys taken document that travelling to the furthest extremities will result in a wanderer looping back to their point of entry; this generally occurs after traversing longitudinally for upwards of two days. On the other hand, the sub-level’s length is only 10 kilometres, making it possible to cross within a few hours, after which the landscape will transition into either Level 9 or Level 10, depending on the direction of travel.

The weather is humid and intensely hot, similar to a prolonged summer heatwave, with temperatures fluctuating between 35 and 40°C (95-104°F). Both Level 9.9’s diurnal cycle and weather pattern are determined by distance. Travelling close to Level 9 will cause the sun to set, while traversing nearer to Level 10 results in the skies becoming more overcast. Despite this, the inner 80% portion of Level 9.9 is exposed to searing heat and clear, midday skies.

The homes contain usual interior furnishings, although most exteriors have become severely damaged, with broken windows and missing doors commonplace. Inside, televisions serve as a reliable gauge of the proximity to the Level 10 border; the displays become less distorted, to the extent of being entirely functional upon reaching the Level 10 transition.

***The mind has funny ways of filling in the blanks.***

***Before I resigned, I hardly ever got a break from the relentless barrage of responsibility, but now, as a free man, an empty void engulfs my purpose.***

***It's very apt that I currently find myself wandering in the calm summer haze of Level 9.9; to think that in a month's time, I was supposed to march a sorry cohort here and begin the work on constructing the 'impenetrable stronghold' just across the border and into the shadows.***

***It wasn't my idea anyway; it was an assignment I was given and forced to achieve.***

***That outpost project will be shelved in my absence because nobody who remains there has the gut and drive to jump headfirst into the infested cesspit of Level 9.***

***I will prove to them what a cataclysmic operation it is and always was; they're too delusional to pull the plug on the project, even if all the cannon fodder strung along cry out in anguish that they don't want to die.***

***I was told that revenge is a dish best served cold. If the M.E.G. fails to realise the ever-present prospect of danger, I will give them danger.***

Standard suburban features exist on the roads, such as compact substations and private car garages. Much like the drones, both the substations and garages are under the control of Entity 96; approaching a substation too closely may cause it to explode and catch on fire with no warning. Likewise, the private garages are all fitted with heavy-duty electronic shutters which Entity 96 has been observed using to seal sheltering wanderers inside. Once trapped, escape is extremely difficult without assistance, leaving those unprepared to painfully succumb to dehydration or heatstroke.

The fields in Level 9.9 are fairly standard in size and separate the neighbourhoods roughly every ten streets. They are mostly barren, lacking any structures other than electricity poles that connect to the substations; shrubbery makes up the borders of the fields.

In the darker areas of Level 9.9, close to the border of Level 9, Entity 96 in its physical form can rarely be seen roaming around, specifically the strider category. Due to the blistering speeds and aggression of this entity, encounters have a near 100% mortality rate, so it is strongly encouraged to vacate the vicinity near the Level 9 border as quickly as possible.

# Drones

There are two different categories of drones in Level 9.9: cargo drones in the sky and the rarer but more dangerous exploding ground drones. The drones all use active infrared sensors to locate their targets.

Contrary to popular belief, Entity 96 is capable of piloting the drones with precision, and unlike other electronic devices it interferes with, the drones have not yet been observed to malfunction. In addition, no drone has been observed running out of charge; they are powered by electrical motors or batteries, which Entity 96 is seemingly able to manipulate.

When not actively hunting, the drones scope the entire sub-level linearly. The aerial drone's flight altitude during this state varies but has never been recorded above 40 metres or lower than 10 metres. The drones' vertical field of view is similar to that of the human eye, measuring just over 100 degrees, whereas their horizontal field of view extends to 360 degrees, enabling them to spot targets from all surrounding angles.

Recent research into Entity 96 on Level 9 has provided insight into their hostile behaviour; they are extremely territorial and will go to extraordinary measures to protect their domain. They are far more intelligent than once thought, facilitating impressive spatial awareness, memory and problem-solving skills. The motive behind the hijacking of the drones is thought to be Entity 96 using Level 9.9 as a buffer zone to keep beings out of Level 9 by means of a deterrent.

In 2025, M.E.G. surveys concluded that over 500 drones populate Level 9.9, a number that vastly exceeded initial counts. One widely accepted theory for this phenomenon suggests that Level 9.9 is now manifesting drones into existence as an inherent characteristic, much like how other levels appear capable of generating seemingly infinite resources.

### Aerial Drones

The aerial drones possess an arachnid shape with four unguarded propellers extending outward from the body. The drones each weigh roughly 30 kg (70 lbs), and their material is a mix of carbon fibre and aluminium alloys. Upon detecting a lifeform, they swarm and hunt in a manner akin to gannet birds, plunging from the skies at quick speeds and striking their target in the upper body.

Singular impacts are typically fatal; if not, however, the force can knock wanderers to the ground, leaving them susceptible to frenzied attacks. The drones won't relent on their assault until their target is dead or unreachable. Reports state that attacks on groups are concentrated on the closest individual.

Recently it was revealed through a video tape that the cargo drones have begun carrying objects in their payload and dropping them onto individuals. Such objects range from car doors to microwaves, and more interestingly, a symbiotic relationship with the ground drones has been observed, where the aerial drones can drop their exploding counterparts on wanderers to detonate with devastating results.

### Ground Drones

Ground-based drones have been scarcely reported patrolling the suburbs. These units possess a dumbbell-shaped frame and have been observed reaching speeds of 15 m/s on flat surfaces. Their usual attack method involves accelerating towards detected wanderers and detonating upon contact. Unlike their aerial counterparts, the ground drones are largely silent when patrolling, making their approach difficult to anticipate.

The explosion is triggered through a controlled thermal runaway, in which Entity 96 rapidly overheats the drone's electrical components. The resulting blast resembles a fragmentation grenade as the carbon fibre exterior shatters and disperses at high velocity. This is lethal to any individual in close proximity, with near-guaranteed death. Due to their self-destructing function, these drones are diminishing in numbers, despite established theories suggesting that the level spawns extra units into existence. They are assumed to be the reason why many doors and windows are damaged – facilitating the drones’ access to refuge points.

M.E.G. SURVIVAL GUIDE

##### **Rule #1: Turn back**

If you haven't yet committed to traversing Level 9.9, turn back, but only if you have entered through Level 10; Level 9 is still graded as a more dangerous level.

##### **Rule #2: Look and listen**

The drones are very fast, whether on the ground or descending from the skies, so be hyperaware, listen carefully and check all angles so that you are not caught off guard.

##### **Rule #3: Stay cool**

Every home will have an outside tap, and the water is safe to drink. The climate is extremely humid and hot, so profuse sweating is unavoidable; keep in the shade where possible.

##### **Rule #4: Make progress**

Level 9.9 is not very large, so make steady progress. Sprinting is going to make you more obvious to the drones and can cause heat stroke, so only run in order to save your life. The best course of action is to make headway in the field sections and then use the suburbs to quickly rest; staying in one location for too long increases the chance of being discovered.

##### **Rule #5: Seek shelter**

The houses on Level 9.9 are the safest temporary refuge locations; take rests frequently and stock up on supplies. That being said, avoid homes that have damaged doors and windows, as these can be access points for the drones to ambush you.

##### **Rule #6: Avoid garages**

While the rows of private garages may look like the perfect shelter from the drones, boasting thick concrete walls and no windows, the rolling shutters are electronic, allowing Entity 96 full control to open and close the doors to corner or trap targets.

##### **Rule #7: Keep out of the open**

To escape Level 9.9, you will have to traverse through a few fields in between the suburb sections; while doing so, always follow the tree line that separates the fields, as this is shaded and has cover.

##### **What to do if you get attacked by an aerial drone:**

If you get struck by a drone, you should absolutely attempt to stay on your feet and evade the drone as quickly as possible, although if you do find yourself stuck on the ground being mobbed by multiple drones, follow these steps:

- Curl into a ball to protect vital organs.

- Pretend to be dead (this does not have a guaranteed success rate).

- Once they leave you alone, get into a house, apply pressure to wounds and call for help.

##### **What to do if you get attacked by a ground drone:**

If you encounter the rare ground drone in the suburbs section, then you must act very quickly.

- If possible, get somewhere high (at least 2 metres) and curl into a ball to minimise damage from the blast.

- Run in a zig-zag pattern to force the drone to lose momentum and speed.

- Put something physical between you and the drone, like a fence or door.

##### **Fighting the drones:**

Fighting the aerial drones with your bare fists is like sticking your arm into a meat grinder; it is highly ill-advised. If you happen to have a firearm at hand, they will typically leave you alone but can still ambush quickly from behind. The most reliable way of dealing with attacks if you are out in the open is to use a long, sturdy object like a wooden plank and attempt to block attacks with it; this may buy you enough time to escape.

It is imperative that you do not attempt to fight the ground drones and instead focus on seeking refuge or high ground as fast as possible.

### Rescue Report

**Date Written:** 26/07/19  
**Author:** Laurie Clyde  
**Location:** Level 9.9

*This report included descriptions of mildly graphic scenes.*

On the 20th of July at approximately 17:00, the Quick Match and Entity Removal teams were sent to investigate a cryptic distress call from Level 9.9 as well as to assess the welfare of the Level 9 Outpost founding team. When we arrived in Level 9.9, we were instantaneously swarmed by over ten cargo drones. The behaviour of the drones seemed to be coordinated as they aggressively descended on us from above, carrying various items ranging from chimney pots to scrap metal. Entity Removal was forced to shoot down several of the drones; this proved effective, and the remaining units withdrew. Many cars parked along the kerbside had their doors ripped off and windows smashed inward. Several houses had sustained exterior damage, with collapsed walls and doors torn from their hinges.

After searching the vicinity, an operative located the source of the distress calls inside a bombed-out house. The male individual was found deceased, slumped against a collapsed wall with devastating burns across the body. This victim's injuries were not consistent with other bodies we found later. In an adjacent room, the M.E.G. radio device that transmitted the distress call was recovered; it had been purposefully destroyed by human force. No other distinguishable items were recovered at the scene. Due to concerns over the structural integrity of the building, the body could not be safely recovered or identified.

The border between Level 9.9 and Level 9 contained the aftermath of the Level 9 Outpost staging area. Four bodies, identifiable as M.E.G. operatives, were found on the ground. Unlike the body found in the house, deep surface wounds and bruising were visible. One individual was located significantly farther down the street from the others, lying on his back and holding a handgun pointed upward toward the sky. All four bodies were secured and sent to Base Omega.

The on-site power generator had multiple bullet impact marks along its broadside. Recovered ammunition matched M.E.G. rounds, implying the damage was the result of panicked misfire or sabotage. Sealed crates and plastic totes from the supply convoy had been torn open with what appeared to be inhuman force. The contents of the containers remained mostly intact, with the exception of the units labelled 'UAV'[1](javascript:;) and 'UGV'[2](javascript:;) which were found completely empty. It was at this point that it became apparent the drones observed overhead were M.E.G. equipment. At the time, no explanation could be determined for their autonomous and aggressive behaviour.

Our search extended across the border into Level 9, where two tarpaulins and an unassembled barricade were discovered within close proximity to the Level 9.9 border, presumed to mark the team’s initial attempt to establish the outpost. These findings were recovered for forensic purposes.

Further down the street, an unlit flare was spotted on the ground, along with several M.E.G. equipment bags scattered across the pavement. The contents consisted of typical supplies, including water bottles which appeared to be undrunk. It is assumed that the operatives were attacked by Entity 96 and had to retreat. The Entity Removal unit assisting us reported low ammunition following the earlier drone encounter, and as entities became increasingly aware of our presence, we mutually agreed to terminate the search.

On our return through Level 9.9, the drones stalked our position in a passive manner, indicating they may be under the control of a sentient entity.

Overall, four confirmed M.E.G. bodies were recovered. There are still 23 M.E.G. operatives missing as of July 26th.

# **Entrances and Exits**

### **Entrances**

- Exiting Level 9 via a grass field has a slight chance of transporting wanderers to Level 9.9 instead of the usual destination of Level 10.
- If a wanderer enters Level 10 from Level 9 and quickly turns back, a guaranteed, gradual noclip into Level 9.9 will occur.

### **Exits**

- Traversing the 10-kilometre length of Level 9.9, or immediately turning back upon entry, will lead to Level 9 or Level 10 depending on the direction of travel. This is the only documented exit.
