## What are Unnumbered Entities?

---

Unnumbered entities, as is implied by their namesake, are beings who for a variety of reasons either cannot or just don't have a number. Sometimes, giving an entity a number doesn't make much sense, and other times, it simply might be not be warranted. Prime examples of this are [Icarus Procidens](icarus-procidens.md), a being that isn't on the database yet, or the [Jaws of Emendation](the-jaws-of-emendation.md), which is a narrative. Whether it's just personal choice or some lore-dependent decision, you can have any number of reasons for not using a number; as long as it can be inferred from your page, anything goes, so be creative, and make sure it makes sense!
