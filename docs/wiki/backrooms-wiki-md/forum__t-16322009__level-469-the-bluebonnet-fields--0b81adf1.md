*Hey, sleepwalker,*  
*Don’t you miss the way*  
*Wildflowers paint the western hills?*

My original intent with this page was for it to lean far more heavily into the negative effects of nostalgia, but it moved into something less intense. I might write a tale about my original ideas later.

[+ Do not open until you’ve finished reading the page](javascript:;)

[– I warned you!](javascript:;)

- 469 is one of the area codes serving Ennis, TX, officially designated as the Bluebonnet City of Texas. Incidentally, all the other area codes were taken as level numbers already.
- Bluebonnets (of all kinds) are the state flower of Texas. The state puts a lot of effort into wildflower preservation, and a lot of highways in North Texas have wildflowers planted along the ramps and side of the road. They’re plentiful even in urban areas.
- Driving to see the bluebonnet trails is a pretty popular thing to do in the North Texas area. A few specific places are super popular because of how wide the bluebonnet and wildflower fields spread.
- I grew up in North Texas, and I no longer live there. I would love to go back because the landscape is strongly burned into my memory and my soul, everything from the way the afternoon sun turns the tall grass golden, the way the clouds float in the blue sky, the way the grass goes yellow-dry in summer and green-soft in spring, the way deer and coyotes poke their heads out from the tree line just long enough for a human to see them. I can’t go back right now, maybe never, for reasons far beyond my control, and that separation can’t be eased by photos or decor or souvenirs or memories and sometimes that nostalgia is so strong that it takes over and buries everything underneath it and all I’m left with is how hollow the fabrications of the real thing are
- But, sometimes it’s nice to think about those places. Sometimes it’s not so hollow.
- There’s nothing wrong with the level. The level does nothing in particular to anyone. What the people who enter it feel is entirely their own.
- Look for the blue.
