Alright so the first sentences are pretty good but "Supply closets are scarce throughout Level 2.1 but are completely safe. Inside there is an air vent which blows air that makes the air inside of the closet 75°F or 23.9°C, even if the closet door is open." maybe change Inside there to Inside supply closets.

"If you no-clip in an extremely hot area in Level 2, you will end up here."  
I would change this to "If one no-clips in an extremely hot area within Level 2 one will find themselves within this Level."

"Finding and walking through a set of wide iron bars will take you to Level 3."  
I would change this also "Should one locate and walk through a set of wide iron bars one will be taken to Level 3."

Overall I like the concept and hope to see the finished product.
