Well,  
apparently, the ping seems to don't work.

Sorry to have missed your text:

For the point of improvements:

**The Black Rainbow appears randomly at just about any outdoor level. It can occur at random when few people are watching.**  
=> repetition, can be rewritten in one sentence.

**We classified this as a Phenomena, for it did not fit anything else. It isn't sentient, so we couldn't classify it as an Entity, and it cannot be used as an object, so it couldn't be classified as an Object.**  
=> Well, it's the definition of phenomena, could be a bit redundant  
=> **it cannot be used as an object**  
Does that imply that it can be used as something else?

**Do not touch the substance, for you will go mad and act mad. It will be as if you are no longer in control of your body and you will laugh insanely.**  
=> not in control of your body. Convulsion? epilepsy? erratic movements?  
=> Go mad, act mad + not in control of your body: is it more a kind of possession ?

**When the Black Rainbow appears, it does not appear to fall down onto Wanderers. It falls down in places where Wanderers are not standing. It seems to want the Wanderers alive when it comes.**  
=> Is it sentient? thus, an entity?  
=> Is it some kind of medium? thus a phenomenon made by an entity?

**to make people go mad for their own entertainment**  
=> wouldn't that be more coherent if they were actually controlled by the rainbow, yet resilient enough to not laugh?  
=> they're undefined and need more characterization.
