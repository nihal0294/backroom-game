burger restaurant level idea

03 Feb 2022 00:04

Page Type: Level.

Core Concept: This is an infinite burger restaurant with the mascot being Baxter Burger. You can eat burgers in here but when the lights go off various nightmarish versions of Baxter appear to try and transform you in a Baxter statue, the process is painful and you'll still be alive when you turned into a statue. You can leave to an outside area which is safe and that no nightmarish versions of Baxter can enter and to leave the level you have to go out of boundaries on the outside.
