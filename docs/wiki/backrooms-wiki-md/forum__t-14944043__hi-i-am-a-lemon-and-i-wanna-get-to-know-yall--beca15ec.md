Heyo! My name is Henry, and as is obvious, I am a lemon. In all seriousness, I came here to let my hardcore horror and creative mindset explode. I am an aspiring author and site greenlighter, so I can effectively help others get their articles greenlighted too! I most always critique 1-3 articles on the draft forum a day, because i love seeing yall's (I'm from the south), cool ideas and work. I am not very busy, so this is pretty much what I'm doing with my life right now: Backrooms Obsession.

MY PROFILE PIC: My profile pic looks like some weird "lemonish(?)" thing with eyes, but its actually my favorite of a huuuuge collection of "monsters" I have drawn on pixel art. I may be giving these entity pages in the future, especilly this yellow guy… >;)

More stuff u didnt ask for but I gave you: Things I like are the horror genre, creative writing, fictional reading, video games, food, bike rides, and hanging with the boys. Fun fact: I don't actually like lemons. Lemonade is gud tho. My hobbies are just sleep, eat, game, backrooms, repeat. +School. I am a VERY social, optimistic, and extroverted person, so feel free to PM me just to talk all things backrooms or in general! Hopefully [Level 215: The Escape Rooms](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/as-your-username), and [Level 148: The Living Level](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/iamalemon) will soon be greenlighted, and feel free to check em out as long as ur reading a stranger's bio.

I would love to talk to a site mod, as I have many concerns and questions left unanswered, AS LONG AS that person isnt strictly serious, and will take my jokes and open personality! (me thinking of a cheesy catchphrase…) yeah i got nothin. Have a nice life. lul. -Iamalemon/Henry
