Level 110 - Post Singularity

05 Nov 2020 23:05

**Page Type:** Level

**Page Layout:** Same ol’

**Genre:** Science-Fiction

**Elevator Pitch:** Level 110 is a spatial anomaly similar in appearance to a dumping ground cluttered with debris trapped within the singularity of a blackhole designated as Level 110-Alpha.

**Central Narrative:** Level 110 and Level 110-Alpha was created by several M.E.G. scientists tinkered with a broken particle accelerator in a previous occupier of the designation of Level 110. Once Level 110-Alpha was created, Level 110-Alpha eventually devoured the entirety of the designation’s last occupation and the previous Level 110 was reclassified as this level.

**Attention-Grabber:** The first level that was created by humans.
