Alright, here's my take on the current state of the draft.

So, I'm going to be honest, the current execution of this level and the concept of it donn't match right now. Here are the things that seem out of place:

First off, the level doesn't really match Level 777. With no mention of the wanderer having to place a bet to start the challenges, it seems as though the level instead just sends entities at you and occasionally drops items. This makes it feel like less of a gambling game and more of a wave-based survival simulator.

Next, the risk-reward balance seems heavily lopsided toward the risk. As pressing the Double button once releases a horde (this is usually used when an amount of something is enough to be a threat) of entities, while only offering one item per victory. Unless these entities are incredibly easy to defeat, this doesn't make much sense. Going with your own concept, I believe it would work better to make the starting amount of enemies less, as well as doubling the value of the reward for every victory. This may also help with promoting the gambling theme.

And now, the singula complentia fabulam:

So… Why does this whole thing take place in a psychiatric hospital? The level has the part where you can keep risking yourself for better rewards, and then… It's suddenly a haunted building? If this is how it's supposed to be, there really must be a link explained in the page. As an idea, maybe the level, when someone with a mental condition enters, could double their condition instead of throwing entities at them.

Also, the descriptions of the entities are somewhat lacking. I can picture them somewhat, which is good, but I don't know if they're humanoid, animal-like, or something else — nor do I know their strength, their size, etc. You don't need a whole section dedicated to the entities if they're just a basic thing that the level sends at people who double, but it could do with a little extra.

On the contrary to what I suggest expanding, I think that the sections describing the physical level push the main Double Or Nothing concept to the periphary of the page's contents. It may help to further shift the focus to the concept you actually want, rather than where it takes place. I do think that the descriptions are done quite well, but they should not take center stage here — rather, the gambling and risk part should.

That's all I have to suggest for now.
