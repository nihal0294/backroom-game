Conceptually, these guys seem a little too OP as a food source. I think you would do well to elaborate on their *downsides* a little too! For example:

- Only found on Level 10 (have to get to them first). Maybe only in a specific spot in Level 10?  
- Can’t store a clump for too long in an enclosed space, as they spontaneously generate more worms, and will crowd out the storage area with worms (eww)  
- Live worms can escape! Gotta bring a closed box or jar.  
- Cooked worms spoil like regular meat

---

*or what* **as** *I like to call it*

---

*The M.E.G are just some ragtag group of people, do we really have to have a specific format for this shit? Whatever..*  
This is a little heavy-handed and unnatural, and the grammar is funky. Do note that an ellipsis should have three dots! I suggest:  
The M.E.G are just some ragtag group of people, d. Do we really *have* to have **use** a specific format for this shit? Whatever..**.**

---

I recognize you might prefer to leave the formatting gritty to complement the vibe of the guy submitting it. But neater formatting might be easier for readers to look at too. I suggest:  
- Use slightly larger headers for description/behavior. ++ seems proportionate, rather than +++.

- Put begin log and end log in teletype text, {{like this}}. It’ll look like this which is cooler. Have the names in **bold**. Also, separate with a horizontal rule (5 dashes), like so:

> BEGIN LOG
>
> ---
>
> **Joe Mama:** Log yada yada yada
>
> ---
>
> END LOG

That looks neater, doesn’t it?

- Neaten your recipes into quote blocks. For example:

> ### Recipe 1: Skewered worms
>
> ---
>
> **Step 1:** Take three worms of each colour and stick them on a skewer. Place one green worm in between other colours.  
> **Step 2:** Repeat Step 1 to prepare as many skewers as you like.  
> **Step 3:** Roast the skewers over an open fire for roughly 10-15 minutes.
>
> ---
>
> **Serve**: Eat them hot! 2-3 skewers serve one person.

That’s all I got! Nice work!
