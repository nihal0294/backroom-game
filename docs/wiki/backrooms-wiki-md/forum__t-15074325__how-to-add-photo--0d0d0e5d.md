You can add a photo with this syntax:

[[include component:image-block  
|name=(link to ur image)  
|caption=(What is depicted in this image)  
]]

Note that PNGs do *not* work, and need to be converted into links. You can easily do this by adding your PNG to the page's files at the bottom, and then copying *that* link into the "|name" bit. Hope this helps.
