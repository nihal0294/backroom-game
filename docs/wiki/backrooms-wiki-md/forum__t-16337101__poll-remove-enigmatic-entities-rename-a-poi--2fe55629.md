Hello everyone! We have not one, but two polls for the community to decide the fate of… well, the community.

First, staff acknowledges we made a mistake preemptively announcing the removal of Enigmatic Entities. It's a decision that greatly affects current and future pages, and so we've left it up to the community to decide what to do.

Second, this site has the history of banned authors contributing many ideas that the wiki relies on. This next poll is an attempt to see what the community wants to do about one author's work in particular: Etoisle. This is not going to set a precedent for future POIs.

Now go vote and do your civic Backrooms duty! Results will not be available until Sunday, September 17 at noon MST
