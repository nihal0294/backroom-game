Where to put the image is up to you, and you can put it anywhere. For putting it right next to the text, just paste the image block code right before the paragraph.

As for the image, I don't believe it can be used. Many websites that aren't explicitly giving you permission can't be used, so you may have to find another. (if u want gimme a theme and ill try getting one).

And yeah, put the image licensing either under the "author" collapsible at the bottom, or (after the page is greenlit), put the license in the comment section.
