Okay, so I think this is a very interesting article! The one thing that you have to work on a bit is "clinical tone". In universe, these articles are written by the M.E.G. (group of people in the backrooms), and they tend to write things very formally. It definitely takes some practice to write clinically, so don't worry if you don't get it right away. I'll try to help reword some of your sentences where I can.

[+ Click to Show Crit](javascript:;)

[- Click to Hide Crit](javascript:;)

> Habitat : Anywhere with a computer

You say later in the article that it is only known to be in Level 2, so either change this to Level 2, or change the other mention

> The entity known as Photoshop is a dangerous creature of some nature when up-close at times. It's known to have a blocky appearance and is near a desktop computer on an office desk in Level 2. The best way to avoid Photoshop is by keeping your distance and staying out of its way.

Normally, you want the very first sentence of the description to describe what the entity looks like, and also you have some clinical tone issues here  
**Suggestion:** Entity 104, also known as "Photoshop" has a blocky appearance and can be near a desktop computer on an office desk in Level 2. This entity is very dangerous to anyone who is near it, so it is suggested that you avoid Photoshop by keeping your distance and staying out of its way.

> Photoshop was just a statue that standed still, and stared at the direction it's faced. If you get too close to it, there will be a huge, transparent box with black edges that will surround you.

Some tonal issues  
**Suggestion:** When no one is near it, Photoshop stands completely still, like a statue. If anyone gets gets within [put a number here] meters of the entity, a large transparent box with black edges will surround them.  
Also, you never explicitly say when the entity does when a wanderer is trapped in the box. It's implied later that the walls close in on them or something, and if that's the case, you should say it

> The box has walls that will cut you in pieces if you touch it. It's like the entity is cropping you out of existence.

Some tonal issue, and also, it's a bit on the nose to explicitly say it crops them imo  
**Suggestion:** Any matter that passes through the walls of this box are destroyed.

> The entity doesn't attack if you are holding a camera. If you take a picture of it, it doesn't attack, even if you get up-close.

Why doesn't the entity attack you if you hold a camera? I actually think this is a neat detail, but you should try to talk about it more, elsewhere in the article

> (like almond water)

Putting clarification in parentheses isn't very clinical, also this doesn't really add much, so I would just remove it

> arm will come out of the computer, grab the object, and will be placed in the desktop computer.

Could be reworded to be clearer  
**Suggestion:** arm will come out of the computer screen, and pull the object into the screen. After this, the object will appear on the monitor

> But if Photoshop has enough items, it will use the objects, change the hue with dark red, and use them as legs to move around. The entity will not attack you if give it an item and will find its prey somewhere else.

I feel like thses two details are not really needed. It's your choice, but I would suggest removing them.

> The entity has two photos of eyes (female and male), a picture of a dog's mouth (pitbull)

Like I wrote above, putting clarification in parentheses isn't very clinical, also they don't really add much, so I would just remove both statements in parentheses

> Photoshop is as big as an average school bus and as wide as a hippopotamus.

I would put size stuff in description rather than biology, but more importantly, judging by the image, and it standing next to a computer, it certainly doesn't look like it's as big as a bus.

> wanderer that no-clip in level 2

no-clipped, also "level 2" should be capitalized.

> escaped the backroom

So in universe, you can't escape from the backrooms. It is okay that you did it here though, and I'm not going to tell you to change it, but you should at least know what the deal is. So, basically on the wiki there's more than on "canon" which are like different universe I guess. In the main one where most of the articles take place, you cannot exit the backrooms, but if you want this to just not take place in the "main canon" that's totally fine. (note: my explanation of canons is going to be slightly inaccurate soon because of some changes that are happening, but the basic idea is still the same)

> \*\*Interviewee:[CLASSIFIED]

Small formatting error, Should be \*\*Interviewee:\*\* [CLASSIFIED]

> **Interviewer:** Ok, one last question: What are you going to do with the rest of your life?
>
> **Interviewee:** Well, I'm going to find out more about…the backrooms on the internet. I know how the place fucks you up. But I need to know more. I have to.

Idk about these two lines, I would personally just recommend deleting them since they don't really do much

> (or any other entity. That's not cool)

Same parentheses thing as the others

**Conclusion:** I thought it was a pretty interesting idea, just needs some polishing. Let me know when you make these changes (or at least the ones you agree with), and I can take another look!

> It was just a lifeless statue from affar

Should be "It appears to be just a lifeless statue from afar"

> If Photoshop has enough items, it will use the objects, change the hue with dark red, and use them as legs to move around.

**Suggestion:** "If Photoshop has enough items, it will materialize the objects from the desktop screen, change the hue with dark red, and use them as legs to move around.

> The entity has two photos of eyes, a picture of a pitbull's mouth wide open for its face, and a block that has a photograph of flesh on all sides for the body.

**Suggestion:** The entity has two photos of eyes and a picture of a pitbull's mouth wide open for its face. For its body, it has a block that has a photograph of flesh on all sides.
