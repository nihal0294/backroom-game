Hello folks!

September is here, and that means is here to bring you the summer recap!

As always, please let us know if you have any suggestions for next month's newsletter! We're always looking for ways to improve. =)

![banner](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/pride-2025/banner)

Earlier this summer, we wrapped up the [The Backrooms Wiki Pride 2025 Showcase](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/pride-2025/banner), a month-long celebration of LGBTQ+ representation with featured articles, art submissions, and Crit Nights. A huge thank you to everyone who joined in!

![DuelBanner](http://reyday.wikidot.com/local--files/duelcon2025/DuelBanner)

[DuelCon2025](duelcon2025.md) came to us in the middle of the summer and left as one of the most participated-in contests in site history! This team writing event pitted *Hope* against *Despair* in a contest of creativity, subversion, and storytelling. In the end, Hope triumphed over despair, keeping humanity's flame burning for just a bit longer. Thanks to all who battled it out and made the competition truly unforgettable!

# Star Critics

Each month the [Crit Crew](critic-crew.md) rewards a "Star Critic" that is chosen using a point-based system. These points are given based on the number of crits given and for individual actions that are good examples of what we want to accomplish as a team. At the end of the month, whoever did the best job gets a front-page "Critic Spotlight" featuring an article of their choice and the golden Star Critic role for the following month.
