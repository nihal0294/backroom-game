So, the idea is fine and I'd been fine with it being a level. *But*, there are lots of things that need to be rewritten or fixed. I noticed grammar issues, forgotten capital letters, weirdly worded sentences and unlinked level as well as a constant use of "we" at the start of most sentences (I'm not entirely sure if it's worded like that on purpose of just written wrong). I'd recommend using the free version of Grammarly for basic mistakes or you can buy the premium version for more complex mistakes. Overall, the idea is a 6-7. The draft is more like a 3-4. I myself am not a critiquer nor am I any good at spelling, grammar, etc. It's just my honest review and rating. Hope that it turns out better in the final post.
