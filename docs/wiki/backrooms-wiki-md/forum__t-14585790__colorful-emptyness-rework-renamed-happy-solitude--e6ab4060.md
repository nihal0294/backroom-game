I like the idea of a level with the sole purpose of getting your hopes up. It kinda reminds me of SCP-999, which is basically the SCP version of this level. Not to mention I can practically enter it at almost any time I want? That's great. Although I kinda wonder why being such an upbeat level it's an asylum. Normally that's associated with things like depression, anxiety, fear, violence, or insanity. The contrast here in this particular area makes it seem more strange and unusual compared to other levels that would use that kind of place to make something scary. I love the way it's been laid out that way, given how foreign the idea seems, even for the backrooms. Also I noticed a few grammar errors and I wanted to know if I could fix them cause their driving me a little insane. But that has nothing to do with the level. I definitely would've Given an upvote if it would let me.
