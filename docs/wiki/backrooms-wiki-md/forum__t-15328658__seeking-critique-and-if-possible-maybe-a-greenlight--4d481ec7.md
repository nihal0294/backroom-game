27 Oct 2022 13:29

I'll give you a little critique before ya find a GL.

> being colossal silhouettes at distances.

Change to:

> looking like colossal silhouettes from large distances.

> Though records

Change "Though" to "However,".

> Gifted the nickname "The Plague" to wanderers…

"to" should be "by".

> level, it is an infection

Change the comma to a semicolon and add "also" in front of "it is".

> protracted periods of time spent should not be partaken in as this can easily risk contamination or infection.

This whole part of the sentence is redundant and should be removed.

> It is unknown whether Level 295 can convert infinite or indefinite spaces as there are no records of Level 295 becoming present in these spaces.

- Change "becoming" to "being".
- Change the "spaces" at the end of the sentencd to "locations".
- Move this whole footnote to (the word that should have the footnote is boldened):

> levels or areas **unafflicted**.

> Haphazard encounters of "wooden doors" have been reported to appear in Level 294. Entering these doors will lead to this level.

Unless you're gonna be making Level 294, the latter doesn't exist. I suggest you replace it with another level.

> typically all below Level 394.

Levels are usually not designated by The Backrooms or its entities themselves and are instead designated by humans. Please replace this with something like "nearly any level of the Backrooms".

Other than that, I don't see any problems! Lore isn't breaking bcs of this level, the length is good enough, and the idea is quite great! Once again, I'd take this to a GL, probably someone like , or .
