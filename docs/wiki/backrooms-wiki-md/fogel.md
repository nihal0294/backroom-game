**FULL NAME:** VLADIMIR, Vitya Tal

**CODENAME:** "FOGEL"

**BIRTHPLACE:** Voroshilovsky District, Volgograd (then Stalingrad), RSFSR, USSR

**BIRTHDATE:** 14/02/1934

**NATIONALITY:** Russian

**CITIZENSHIP:** USSR

**SEX:** Male

**MARITAL STATUS:** Married

**HEIGHT:** 198 cm

**WEIGHT:** 80 kg

**EYE COLOR:** Black

**HAIR COLOR:** Dark Blonde

**BUILD:** Athletic

**BLOOD TYPE:** AB-

**DISTINGUISHING FEATURES:** Shrapnel scar on right cheek and upper lip.

**EDUCATION:** No applicable primary and secondary education. Studied in Moscow State Institute of International Relations in 1952 by special recommendation of █████ ████████, with a bachelor's degree on International Relations and Linguistics (sequential). Studied in the Felix Dzerzhinsky Higher School of the KGB in 1953 with specialized training in cryptography, interrogation, and covert operations; graduated in 1957. Graduated in 1956 with a Red Diploma for International Relations. Graduated in 1958 with a Red Diploma for Linguistics. Applied for a master's program for International Relations in 1959 and acquired a master's degree in 1964.

**MILITARY SERVICE:** Assigned to Soviet Armed Forces from 1950 to 1952; specialized training in counterintelligence. Recruited by the KGB in 1953 for intelligence operations. Assigned to Directorate S in 1956 for his first posting. Operated under diplomatic cover in East Germany from 1960 to 1963; returned to Moscow in 1964 and relocated to the United Kingdom in the winter of 1964 to establish networks among sympathizers. Returned to Volgograd in 1967 to assume internal affair duties within the Soviet High Command.

**OPERATIONAL SKILLS:** Fluent in Polish, German, French, and English. Advanced proficiency in Dutch, Spanish, and Italian. Conversational Chinese. Specialized in counterintelligence, covert operations, cryptography, interrogation, propaganda operations, and sabotage.

**ACTIVE SERVICE:** No

**CURRENT ASSIGNMENT:** None

**LOYALTY TO USSR:** Questioned
