Overall very neat and cool idea. Perhaps a little elaboration on a few details though such as; How/Why do Smilers distort time? What is causing the invisible force fields around safe rooms and why do they not affect us? A thing I like to see in levels is when, throughout the description, the author occasionally notes different theories as to why the level acts the way it does. For example; It is assumed by many that the smilers of level 235 do not willingly distort time but rather the ability is forced upon them by the level itself (obviously you would write your own thing there). Also the end video log story esque thing feels a bit out of place so I would recommend scattering a few more story bits in the page, even if just a few words or sentences. Other than just those few suggestions I don't see why this level couldn't receive a greenlight. I sincerely hope it does!

I’m not a greenlighter but I have some crit for you

### SPaG (Which stands for spelling and grammar… I think.)

> The walls are green, yellow, and purple; The walls reek of mildew.

This should be written as “The walls can be green, yellow, or purple and reek of mildew.”

> The leader is heavily armed and unfriendly towards outsiders, approach with caution.

This should be written as “ The leader is heavily armed and unfriendly towards outsiders. It is recommended to approach with caution.

### General

> Some rooms are blocked by glass doors labeled "Party!" Inside these rooms are long plastic tables draped in a green and yellow tablecloth. On the table, there are presents varying in size from tiny to room-sized.

Seems like the partygoers. Maybe it saying something like “Birthday Room!” would fix this.

> The Wi-Fi is very strong; But nearby smilers distort time in this level, making it difficult for wanderers to gain a signal. However, this can be useful to determine if a smiler is near.

Why do the smilers distort time? That is not a normal smiler trait. This needs to be elaborated or it could be a trait of the duplicates.

> Unnamed Organized Community  
> Around 30 members.  
> The community is located in a Safe Area.  
> They are friendly and open to trade.  
> The walls are lined with stalls selling items collected from presents.

Do they not have a name? Are they mysterious? Is the lack of name from little communication? A little elaboration would help with this.

### Final Thoughts

This is a really good level! This has a lot of potential but needs a little more elaboration. I agree with ’s post. Can’t wait to see the finished product! 7/10
