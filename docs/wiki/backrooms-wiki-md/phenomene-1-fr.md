# Description

  
Phenomenon 1-FR, commonly known as **Noclipping**, is the most common phenomenon in the Backrooms. This makes it the first phenomenon that most wanderers encounter. The word "Noclip" comes from [the Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md) pop culture and refers to the act of passing through a solid barrier in a video game. In the Backrooms, **Noclipping** refers to the act of passing through reality itself via an element of the environment to change locations or levels. This phenomenon is present in the entirety of the Backrooms and can be considered as an ally or enemy of wanderers, depending on the context.

  

# Function

  
  
Even though it is possible to "master" noclipping with enough experience, its nature and function remain very unpredictable. Few walls/floors allow passage between levels. Furthermore, those that do often require a certain position or speed to transport an individual. In concrete terms, running into a solid wall (or rarely, a liquid, like on [Level 37](level-37.md)) at a certain speed allows you to traverse reality and end up on another level.  
  
The fact that wanderers can't see the destination before Noclipping causes the phenomenon to be quite risky because it can lead to a dangerous level. Due to this, it's advised to not try to noclip if the level database does not list a safe exit or even no exits at all.  
  
Despite its inherent unpredictability, noclipping is still a rather reliable phenomenon, although it is generally not possible to see the arrival level of a noclip, because many noclip points can lead to the same level. This creates a risk of wanderers accidentally randomly noclipping in other levels. It is therefore advisable to find out about the risks associated with noclipping on levels you want to visit.  
  

### Entity-Noclip Relationship

  
  
Although it is likely that this phenomenon is valid for any object or physical life form, few cases of entity noclipping have been confirmed. The most known case where this occurred involved a gigantic [Wrangler](entity-75.md) which accidentally noclipped onto [Level 8](level-8.md), discovered by a wanderer in December 2019. The creature had unusual dimensions, even for a Wrangler, and it is believed only half of it had noclipped from an unknown level. The corpse was burned with [Pyroil](object-15.md) to create passages in it. Apart from this Wrangler, no others from this species seemed to have been able to noclip, which seems to prove the hypothesis of an accidental noclip.

### Object-Noclip Relationship

  
  
The case of motionless objects is more complex than it seems. Indeed, it is theorized that some of those can noclip because a wanderer has never lost one of their possessions after noclipping, accidentally or not. However, no instances of objects noclipping without external or human influence has been observed (except for [some bags](object-87.md)). This could mean they need an already initiated movement to traverse reality. Nonetheless, some rumors say that unexplained disappearances of objects when they are not looked at[1](javascript:;) is because of a sudden noclip of those objects. Due to the difficulty of verifying such a phenomenon, little to no progress has been made on this.

However, some objects seem to control the noclipping of wanderers, while not being able to noclip themselves. One example is the case of [Warpberries](object-74.md), which teleports their consumers. If you noclipped accidentally after eating one of these fruits, we invite you to check the **advice in case of an accidental noclips** section.

### Dangers associated with noclipping

  
  
The main danger of noclipping is its randomness. Indeed, without any way to know where and when a wanderer will go through reality, it is not possible for them to protect themself from it. The danger is rare but omnipresent. Every step might teleport an individual from a secure level to a nightmarish place. Of course, staying in a small area is not a solution; being so paranoid is useless. Nevertheless, to limit the risks of an accidental noclip, it is advised to inform yourself of noclip possibilities inside the whole level. If, nonetheless, you noclip accidentally, refer to the **CLIPP** directive in the ***Advice*** section of this article.  
  
Another danger of noclipping is linked to the surface used to traverse reality. Indeed, some of them do not allow a complete and safe noclip. A known example of this is [Level 130](trimmed__level-130--6d1a86bc.md) where, if a wanderer tries to noclip, they will be transported into the level's pipes, filled with a thick, black liquid that leads every individual trapped inside to drown and decompose. Even if the reason why noclipping does not lead to another level is unknown, facts are recorded for the concerned levels in the database, which remains the best source of information on this topic.

# Special Cases

  

### Initial noclip

  
  
Initial noclip is one of the greatest mysteries in the Backrooms, as much for its unique function than for its impossibility to be studied. As this name suggests, the Initial Noclip is the fated moment where a human will traverse through [the Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md), sending them to the Backrooms. Initial Noclip transports individuals onto [Level 0](level-0.md), and, in some cases, into [Level 1](level-1.md) or [Level 2](level-2.md).  
  
Some exceptional cases were noted where Initial Noclip transported an individual to a different level[2](javascript:;). The most strange characteristic of Initial Noclip is that, though going from the Frontrooms to the Backrooms is simple[3](javascript:;), the opposite is as of today impossible. Some theories nonetheless exist, such as those linked to [Level 710](level-710.md).  
  
The mission of [the M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) since its creation is to find a way to perform a "Final Noclip" so as to finally quit this dimension. However, the Ariane Circle deems this possibility to be far too small to justify the means.

### Case of a Specific Environment

  
  
It's common to go from one level to another by the use of objects, sceneries, or more generally with an element from the environment. The element in question is usually easily identifiable and leads to a guaranteed noclip, sometimes remaining in the same place, which makes them very useful for traveling between levels and reaching a precise destination.  
These elements include (but are not limited to) doors, windows, trapdoors, and other openings, but also photo frames and posters, or even trivial objects like mirrors or furniture.  
  
It is also important to note that the aforementioned cases refer to clear, instant transitions, as opposed to smooth ones which can be found in corridors, stairways, or elevators (for example [Level 4-FR](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/niveau-4-fr)). The reasons for those very specific and permanent noclips on easily recognizable elements of the environment to humans are unknown, although they emphasize the Pseudo-Matrice Theory. It is usually harmless to use those passages because their destinations are well-known and documented most of the time. They seem to usually have a look that seems linked to one of the "thresholds" that grants access. This could be the general appearance, the color, the smell, or the feeling towards a door, furniture, an object, or even an image or the theme depicted on the photo or poster.

### -FR Enigmatic Level — "The Source"

  
  
The possibility that [The Source](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/la-source) could be reached by noclip is still a theory, seeing that the only explorer who documented the exploration of the level apparently fainted when he entered and exited it, after having felt increasing pressure on his body. The supporters of this highlight the fact that the flask Marco took was simply lying on a seemingly normal part of Level 7-FR . It is by touching this very flask that he noclipped into The Source.

The theory suggesting that supplies (especially liquids) appearing abnormally throughout the Backrooms come from locations such as The Source leads to the belief that supplying of said liquids would be done through noclipping, since they wouldn't come from the level they can be found on. This, however, doesn't explain how liquids like Almond Water can be found in bottles.

### Level 15-FR — "The Trap"

  
  
[Level 15-FR](niveau-15-fr.md) can only be reached by noclip, according to current information. However, it has the particularity of having "transition zones" that are stable in both space and time. Any individual, object, or liquid can entirely or partially noclip into those transition zones. The sensation has been described as being "like if a warm slab was moving through your body, just enough to have the sensation of touch. We feel like we are crossing over the wall, the feeling being throughout the skin and inside it, even if it's not painful".  
  
It is therefore theorized that there is sort of a barrier between [15-FR](niveau-15-fr.md) and the other levels, a theory supported by the fact that the atmosphere and the micro-organism of [Level 15-FR](niveau-15-fr.md) are confined there. This barrier may become more tangible as the density of the body diminishes while noclipping, even if verifying this theory is impossible due the lack of access to the level.

### Level 10.1-FR — "The Cage"

  

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/phenomene-1-fr/Capture.png)

Intricate noclip summary diagram

[Level 10-FR](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-10-fr) as well as [Sub-section 10.1-FR](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-10-1-fr) currently present the only occurrence of "Intricate noclip". This particular case involves the instantaneous noclip of two wanderers, against their will, located respectively on Level 10-FR and Level 10.1-FR.  
The "Intricate noclip" happens when a wanderer stumbles on a bear trap of Level 10-FR; instead of hurting them, this trap will trigger a noclip through the ground of the level which leads to the sub-section. At the same time, the wanderer who is already in the Cage will also noclip into the ground and be brought back to Level 10-FR.

Bear traps are scattered throughout Level 10-FR and have never been studied in depth because a single touch with them triggers a noclip. It should be noted that the "Intricate noclip "does not recur in The Cage; it only takes place if the other survivor is present in the first room of the Sub-section and not in the pipe network, which reduces the first wanderer's noclip phenomenon to a simple noclip.

### Enigmatic Level — "Mind Palace"

  
  
The [Mind Palace](mind-palace.md) is a special level in its relationship with noclipping, since it doesn't exist there. Indeed, you can't enter nor exit the level with a noclip. This is due to the nature of the Mind Palace, which exists only in the unconscious mind of each wanderer, and therefore does not allow the ability to noclip.

### Level 6.1 — "The Snackrooms"

  
  
The entrance of [Level 6.1](level-6-1.md) is unique in that it requires a very specific noclip. Unlike most levels where noclipping is provoked by the wanderer at a precise point, access to this sub-section of [Level 6](level-6.md) can only be achieved by accidentally noclipping. For this to occur, a wanderer must stumble over a cable on **Level 6**. Although this is difficult and, above all, random, it is often worth the effort due to the beneficial nature of the sub-section.  
  
Interestingly, it's possible to see Level 6.1 from the [Blue Channel](blue-channel.md). It will appear as a multitude of bars that seem to noclip together, appearing and disappearing without any reason. However, this is not visible within Level 6.1 itself.

### Level 158 — "Onslaught Station"

  
  
Entities boarding trains of [Level 158](level-158.md) will noclip through the ground once killed. The same applies to the wanderer, who will be brought back to the original station by noclipping if they "die". Although it is not possible to study the phenomenon, it's very reminiscent of "friendly combat" mechanics which could be the real purpose of these trains acting as arenas.

  

# Advice

  
Noclipping is a widely known phenomenon to wanderers and is sometimes used as a means of transport because of its practicality in moving quickly from one level to another. Nevertheless, the phenomenon is not without risk, which is why it is essential to review the instructions and scenarios that can arise when an individual noclips, whether voluntarily or involuntarily.  
  

### Advice to noclip safely

  
  
Noclipping is systematically documented on levels where it is consistently or randomly present. With time and progress in exploration and mapping, it is possible to know where noclippable passages may lead, minimizing the risk of "bad luck".  
  
Nevertheless, as these zones are brusque with rough transitions between different levels (or zones between levels), there are certain guidelines to be followed to minimize risks when crossing them.[4](javascript:;)  

- For all noclip zones presented in the form of a threshold (door, trapdoor, specific object, etc.), keep your arms and legs alongside your body. If the threshold is in a thin object, step aside and cross it as if in "sidestep".

- For physical objects leading up to precise levels, such as photographs, frames or posters, it's best to make a small jump by crouching down to keep your limbs as close to your body as possible when touching the object.

*Instant Noclip must often be considered on a case-by-case basis, depending on the threshold to be crossed.*

### Advice in case of an accidental noclip

  
Despite today's extensive knowledge of the places and passages where an individual can noclip, there's always a risk of being accidentally transported, potentially into unknown levels or portions of known but unexplored levels, so vigilance should always be prioritized.  
  
If you accidentally noclipped to another level, don't panic and remember the **CLIPP** protocol, which acronym contains, in order, the five most important steps for staying safe, even when disoriented:  
  

##### C - Check your environment

The biggest danger occurring when you noclip into an unknown place is to be stunned by a random hazard that lurks near your location. Whether it's an environmental or entity threat, it's vital to check the surrounding area to make sure there are no hazards present. It is only after this verification that you can continue the **CLIPP** protocol. Even so, never forget to continue to surveil your surroundings for the rest of the operations in order to make sure no new danger gets the better of you. In the case where analysis of the environment reveals an entity, hide without noise as fast as possible and imperatively consult the database to find information about it if it is unknown to you.

##### L - Locate an exit

Sometimes, noclip areas can be taken from both sides, although this is not frequent. Try to perform the same action you did when entering, attempting to return to your starting level using a nearby wall or floor, depending on the surface which you noclipped into. If this method proves unsuccessful, if you don't know how you noclipped or if no attempt is possible for some reason, try to locate a possible exit door. Although they can take many forms, observing your immediate surroundings in search of a way out can make the difference between a harmless story and a terrible accident. However, not all doors lead to a safe or beneficial place. Levels, such as [7](level-7.md), should not have their doors crossed before double-checking. For this reason, if you see a door, proceed to the next step before rushing in headlong.

##### I - Inspect the database

While [Wi-Fi](object-21.md) isn't available at all levels, it is vital to try to reach the M.E.G. database (or any database at your disposal). Indeed, decades of exploration allowed many organizations to map levels alongside their characteristics, dangers and exits. If you succeed in connecting to Wi-Fi, check if the level you are on is in the database. If the answer is yes, carefully read the information that has been collected. It is thanks to these types of databases that you will avoid certain dangers, such as opening a door that could further aggravate your situation.

##### P- Prepare your equipment

Once you have obtained (or attempted to) the information, you can prepare yourself to face this new place. The Backrooms is a hostile location with permanent dangers, therefore, it is essential to be wholly prepared for travel across levels. The list of the most important objects to prepare/verify can be found below:

- An efficient yet silent weapon so as to defend yourself against hostile entities or groups
- A tool for making your way in difficult-to-navigate zones, such as forests.
- A headlamp or flashlight, for levels that are extremely dark (it is important to note that this won't work on [Level 6](level-6.md)).
- Food and water (it is advised to take inventory of consumables and look for food and ration for yourself).
- Clothing (if you have supplementary clothes, you can try to evolve more into exterior environments for example).

##### P - Perimeter your environment

Before going into exploration, make sure to define a landmark, preferably near your base. Even if it is a rudimentary one, it will ensure you have a place where you can sleep and strengthen yourself. Moreover, creating a map of the area is also very important so as to not lose yourself in the repetitive labyrinths that are most Backrooms levels. Whatever the situation, take as few risks as possible, and make sure to always remain secure. You can take more risky actions that lead to a safer outcome if you have plenty of information about the level, such as going to an outpost. Be cautious, however, as overconfidence is one of the biggest precursors of death.  
  
Whatever the case, *watch your steps.*

### Theory 1: "Model 38" or "Four-dimensional superposition"

  
  
As its name indicates, "Model 38" is a theory linked to the strange nature of [Level 38](level-38.md). This level seems to consist only of the 37 previous levels which appear to converge in a precise place, giving form to Level 38. The theory supports the idea that the Backrooms finds itself entirely in a four-dimensional physical space. Levels would be overlaid on top of each other in the same space, organized in tridimensional "layers", moving around without a goal and sometimes touching each other at one or more points. Although humans would not be able to notice or even perceive it, it would theoretically be possible to see fragments of another level that came in contact with the level they are on, by noclippable walls or ground. However, due to certain levels' irregular nature or infinity, those could "touch" others via precise points or just occasionally, creating a passageway between these layers. As those layers are in perpetual movement, the entrances and exits may change places within each level, although generally bringing the wanderer to the same locations.  
  
This theory has the merit of explaining entrances that no longer exist, like the case of [Level 15](level-15.md), or the fact that we could noclip roughly through liquids, like in [Level 37](level-37.md). This also opens up the possibility to make additional assumptions on the function of the [Blue Channel](blue-channel.md), which allows navigation between different layers of the Backrooms' levels.

### Theory 2: "The Simulation"

  
  
It is possible that the entirety of the Frontrooms could be an ultracomplex IT simulation where we and other species live for an indefinite amount of time. The goal is unknown, but the Backrooms could be a remote zone from the "principal map". It may be a "test" zone of environments, physics and time. This would explain some "glitched" levels or objects, the Backrooms' abnormal properties or what they hide. It also explains the vast diversity of levels in appearance and behavior, or, on the contrary, how some loop. It is likely that the test zone will expand further as time passes and humanity and/or the Earth or the entire universe grows, which would give a timeline to this evolving zone and add "content" each time a technological, architectural or historical prowess (natural or human) happens.  
  
Test diagrams and machine replicas of installations or infrastructures could be modelized in "beta" in this environment to pre-study its physical behavior in the Frontrooms. This would explain the vehicles and unusual buildings' presence like on [Level 2-FR](niveau-2-fr.md), [12-FR](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/niveau-12-fr), [33-FR](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/niveau-33-fr), [11](level-11.md) or [61](level-61.md). Noclipping would be an occasional and accidental physical phenomenon similar to the one found in our video games. This theory however does not explain the presence and nature of entities in the Backrooms. The hypothesis on this subject is that they are species who noclipped perhaps thousands of years ago and evolved within this 'dimension', some of them acquiring "abilities" because of the abnormal environment. This hypothesis, however, fails to win unanimous support in the eyes of research staff for noclipping and wanderers.

### Theory 3: "Collective Consciousness"

  
  
The Collective Consciousness theory argues that the Backrooms is in reality the result of humanity's collective consciousness. This theory is not based on scientific facts but simply on a collective reflection of the Backrooms' nature as well as a limited and relative knowledge of human psychology. The Backrooms would then be a deformed and anthropologic vision of reality. We can justify this by the presence of intrinsic rules in the Frontrooms (gravity, fauna, peers) colliding with malformed human concepts or thoughts. The perfect example is Level 45, which is a cluster of human-made skyscrapers in the middle of a spatial void gradually causing the level's destruction. The interesting point is that the skyscrapers' interior respects Earth's gravity like a human would experience it. But as soon as they leave a skyscraper's interior, the void's spatial gravity catches up. Here, we can interpret a conflict between two different individuals' interpretations of gravity. This conflict of concepts, interpretations and mèmes is very often found throughout the Backrooms.  
  
This can let us elucidate almost all of the entities and objects' origins. They are only simple creations of human consciousness based on existing elements from the Frontrooms. Each consciousness has a unique vision of reality. A spider won't be perceived the same way by the totality of humanity, thus the present conflict on the levels of the Backrooms ends up in the middle of entities and objects. The strange and familiar side of entities and objects is caused by the human collective consciousness' interpretation.  
  
The most tangible and known effect of the Backrooms is noclipping. This phenomenon can be compared to a change of thinking, going from one thought to another instantly like human consciousness would. The limits of noclipping seem very similar to those of the ability to focus. For example, focus can be changed from one object to another due to a shift in attention: "I am thinking about an apple… I notice a door… I think of what is behind that door." If we add this pattern of reflection to noclipping effects, then the only fact of thinking about the door makes us go through that door. The previous reflection works too with the environmental noclip. Only the initial noclip does not find a specific answer within this theory.  
  
*What about us? Who are we in this reality made from scratch from our consciousness ? Is it possible that we exist in translations of our very selves in the Backrooms. To make it simple, we would be copies of our consciousnesses, letting us do things that would be impossible in our reality, like noclipping. The impossibility of leaving the Backrooms then is absolutely logical. We can cross our consciousness via noclipping but **we can not leave the Backrooms because we are the Backrooms.***
