**Ok, hello here is the criticism towards your level:**

---

[Click Here!](javascript:;)

[Inside the Level 231!](javascript:;)

**Introduction:**

> Level 231 is the 232nd Level of the Backrooms. It takes the appearance of a small shopping mall similar to the ones found in the Frontrooms. This level was discovered on 22/11/2017 when a wanderer took an elevator to this level.

At the beginning I recommend that you put Level 231 in **bold**, then put Frontrooms with [square brackets](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/square-brackets) to create a link to that level.

---

**Image:**

> A Photo taken of the second floor of level 231.

Capitalize the first L in Level 231, and lowercase the first P in Photo.

---

**Description:**

> Level 231 is a small mall complex that consists of 8 floors, a parking lot, and an outside area.

Put in **bold** Level 231.

---

> Each one of these floors have a moderate amount of stores, three elevators, and an escelator.

Here, I removed the comma before the AND.

---

> The parking lot is an unsafe area, as Smilers have been reported to reside in this area.

Add [square brackets](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/square-brackets) to Smilers, to create a link to that entity.

---

> This level has no day-night cycle, and is always stuck at daytime.

Here, I removed the comma before the AND.

---

**Entities:**

> This effect is one of the many factors affecting the safety of this level, and wanderers are advised to stay away from facelings as much as possible, should they find themselves in this level.

Capitalize the first F in Facelings.

---

> can also be found throughout the level, and while they are more aggressive towards wanderers in this level, they appear to act more docile towards facelings here, and are often kept by facelings as pets.

Capitalize the first F in Facelings.

---

> Other Reported Entities are Smilers, Deathmoths and Death Rats. They only inhabit dark and seemingly abandoned parts of the mall, however, such as out of business shops.

I recommend that you delete the Smilers, since I had previously mentioned it. Lowercase the first R in Reported and the first E in Entities.

---

**Effects:**

> For Example, Facelings that work in this level's cafeteria may not serve wanderers or serve them harmful drinks, such as Liquid Pain or Cashew Water.

Lowercase the first E in Example, then put the words Liquid Pain and Cashew Water in [square brackets](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/square-brackets) to link to that object.

---

> In some rare occasions, Entities with this effect may also attack and be extremely hostile and vigilant towards other entities.

Lowercase the first E in Entities.

---

**Entrances and Exits:**  
I recommend that you put an asterisk in each sentence:

```
* No-clipping through the enterance to a mall complex in Level 11 will send you here.

* Some wanderers reported no-clipping through a cinema screen in Level 68 sent them here instead of Level 94.

* Going to the ground floor of any level using any modern looking elevator will rarely cause wanderers to be sent here.
```

  

```
* Pressing the 6th, 7th or 8th floor buttons inside any elevator will lead you to said levels respectively.

* Exiting the mall has a small chance of sending you to Level 11. This method is highly discouraged, however, as the area outside the mall is insfested with hostile entities.

* Entering a parked car outside the mall and closing your eyes in it for 15 seconds will lead you to Level 69. This method is yet again highly discouraged, as the area outside the mall is insfested with hostile entities.

* Taking the Elevator to the underground parking lot will send you to L3v3l 71.
```

---

> Taking the Elevator to the underground parking lot will send you to L3v3l 71.

Lowercase the first E in Elevator and I recommend that you change the word L3v3l to Level, so that there is no confusion or misunderstanding.

---
