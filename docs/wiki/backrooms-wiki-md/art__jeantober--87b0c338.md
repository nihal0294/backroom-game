# Jeantober

![aww](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/art:jeantober/aww)

I am doctrinator AKA jeanmeierfan1092884772 and this is my awesome collection of [Jean Meiers](http://fr-backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/metro-boulot-dodo) that I made during October 2025. Despite multiple attempts over the years, I've never been able to complete a single October art challenge past like the 3rd day. Doing Jeantober made me realize that the reason I couldn't do so was because none of the prompts or challenges were about Jean Meier. Once I committed to Jeantober, it felt like the dam halting my motivation to draw came crashing down and I was able to reach my full Jean Meier artist potential. I think my hands started glowing and my eyes changed color too. Probably a side effect.  
Anyway all that to say I love Jean Meier bless my beautiful Jean Meier life.  
Enjoy the Jean Meier art page.

Note: You might see some other faces here that aren't Jean Meier. Don't panic. Those are his coworkers. The guy with the mask is Pythéas, one of the founders of [Cercle Ariane](cercle-ariane.md) and leader of his own eponymous team. The guy with round glasses is Esdras, he's one of Jean's few coworkers that actually somehow like him. Georges Bonnot from [Level 44-FR](niveau-44-fr.md) is also there. He's tall and scary.
