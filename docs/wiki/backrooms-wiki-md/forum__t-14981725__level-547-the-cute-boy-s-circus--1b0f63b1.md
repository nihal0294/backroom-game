Re: Level 547 - A cute boy's circus

05 Aug 2022 06:45

> Level 547 is the 548th Level

The second use of "Level" shouldn't be capitalized as it's not a proper noun.

> 548th Level of the backrooms.

"backrooms" should be capitalized because it's a proper noun.

> Level 547 is a very large circus ground. It is made up

I'd change the period (.) to a semicolon (;) as shown below:

\*Level 547 is a very large circus ground; it is made up\*

> and the floor is made up of damp soil.

I'd add "constantly" before "damp".

> and there's There's a little bit of cheery music throughout the circus grounds.

Don't repeat "there's", remove the second use of the word. (make sure not to capitalize it!)

> a little bit of cheery music throughout the circus grounds.

I'd rewrite this as:

\*the faint sound of cheery music can be heard throughout the circus grounds.\*

> There's a little bit of cheery music throughout the circus grounds.  
> There is a large wooden stage in the middle, surrounded by numerous seats.

Put a linebreak between these two paragraphs.

> There is a large wooden stage in the middle, surrounded by numerous seats.

I'd rewrite this as:

\*Located in the middle of the level is a large wooden stage, surrounded by several other smaller seats.\*

> If you sit in your seat,

How does a wanderer know which seat specifically is their's? Could some more elaboration be made on this part? If there isn't a specific seat, just say "If you sit in any seat,"

> If you sit in your seat, the circus will start in 5 to 10 minutes.  
> The circus is nothing short of a normal circus,

Put a linebreak between these two paragraphs.

> you can't see the faces of the actors.

Could you elaborate on what you mean by this; I'm having a hard time imagining that?

> And all the actors are children who look about

Remove "And" from this.
