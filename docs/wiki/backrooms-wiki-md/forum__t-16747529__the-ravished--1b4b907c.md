The Ravished

11 Mar 2024 03:56

First time author here.

The entity is a tall humanoid in some accounts, while being described as more animalistic in others. It is very rarely seen when it is not acting out its 'duties', but when it is, it quickly escapes from sight. The entity has a small chance of appearing when a wanderer is near death due to starvation. It skulks around a bit, just observing the wanderer, before hunching over and removing a piece of its flesh, placing it beside the wanderer. It then disappears, leaving the wanderer alone. The flesh tastes completely awful, almost unbearable. However, it is extremely high in nutrients and can pretty much completely undo all malnutrition the wanderer was facing. The article would include an interview with a wanderer as he describes his encounter with the entity. It is not known if there is multiple of these entities, or only one.
