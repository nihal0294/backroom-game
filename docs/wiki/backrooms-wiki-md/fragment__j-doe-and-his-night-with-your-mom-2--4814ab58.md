# fragment:j-doe-and-his-night-with-your-mom-2

rating: +1[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

Two men stood in the hallway, between some of the offices and the computer lab. They were part of Level 4’s MEG base, and were on break for the moment. The two usually help maintain the website and clean up archive records.

The short brown haired man, Fritz, held a printed file, waving it around to catch the others' attention. The other man, a man with longer black hair called Micheal, had been scrolling on his phone. They were supposed to print out online articles in case something ever happened with the website, though it was rare since the printer was always breaking down.

> “Lookie here, I found something in the filing cabinet.”
>
> “What? Looks pretty thin. What is it? Like one page?”
>
> “Yeah, weird right? What's weirder is that there's basically nothing on it.”
>
> “What? Give me that.”

He grabbed the file out of the others hand and flipped it open. True to his word, there was practically nothing on it. Just a bland description and single witness interview. Who in their right mind uploaded this and who thought to print it out?

> “This seems like a waste of paper.”
>
> “No doubt, but like, didn't you notice anything weird?”
>
> “Not really.”
>
> “Look at the interview and the description, nobody can describe much about them. The guy interviewed barely even said anything about their encounter!”
>
> “Yeah… bit odd I’ll admit, does it say who wrote it?”

They both skimmed through the file, one pulling out his phone just to double check.

> “Nothing… Damn. Bit strange no account is linked to it's original upload.”

Michael showed his phone, on it was the history of edits to the page.

> “Found edits, but it's just minor things like formatting. What the hell?”
>
> “Don't even say it's by an anonymous account, it's just blank. You think it got scrubbed?”
>
> “Nope, they usually leave a big annoying black box instead of just removing it all.”

They both stood in silence for a moment, not really sure what to make of it.

> “Maybe it's like uh… what's that term the kids use. Y’know when they talk about their silly internet horror?”
>
> “Meme?”
>
> “No! But that might be close. Memes are like things that get spread around right? So what if this dude is like a reverse one?”
>
> “Now you're just being ridiculous. He's not some creepypasta thing, dude.”

Fritz shrugged his shoulders.

> “Why do you think they're like that?”
>
> “Who knows! Maybe some old backrooms god sent it as a messenger and its like too perfect to comprehend.”

There was a dead silence between the two.

> “Are you high?”
>
> “What! No! I was just joking! Jeez dude.”
>
> “Ok ok, what if they are just some guy who's so boring that the backrooms gave them an anomaly to make it 10x worse?”
>
> “Pfft-” he punched his friend on the shoulder. “That's stupider than my thing!”
>
> “What if it was actually an entity trying to disguise itself as a normal human being but was so good at being bland it went right back around to weird?”
>
> “Oooo good suggestion. What if… it's a mind goblin?”
>
> “What's a mind goblin-”
>
> “MIND GOBLIN DEEZ NU-”

Someone they didn't know walked into the hall with a grimacing stare, they were holding a plastic tray with several hot cups of coffee on it. The cups smelled uncharacteristically good, maybe the guy got them some fresh cups from Level 11? Any coffee at this base was commodity, especially from Level 11, it was actually the best tasting coffee you could realistically get! The coffee on this level, especially in this base, always tasted horrible. You know that one game beanboozled? Imagine if you took all of the nasty flavors like vomit and dead fish then stuck them in a blender and added all of that into a coffee alongside a good amount of blood. That's how it tasted.

> “Oh, sorry dude, didn't see you there.” Fritz looked away sheepishly, embarrassed by being caught off guard.
>
> “Did you get us the drinks? That's nice of you dude!”

The man, was he a man?, silently handed them both a cup and quickly walked away to hand out more. The two shrugged, a bit weirded out by the guy but what can you do y’know?

They both took a gulp of the coffee, and immediately began wheezing and heaving.

The unbearable taste of the coffee hit them, it was worse than they thought. It was bitter, sour and disgustingly metallic, they somehow tasted the smell of a rotting fish. Micheal ran to his office to chug from his water bottle while Fritz dumped both the cups on the ground. The taste wouldn't leave their mouths, if nails on a chalkboard or hitting metal on your teeth was a taste this would be it. Agonizingly disgusting and annoying. Why do they even have that coffee machine here if this what it makes!

A nondescript person watched from the corner, a smile snaking a way up their cheeks. That's what you get for talking shit about someone while they are listening.
