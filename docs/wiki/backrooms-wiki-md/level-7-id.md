# **Description**

**The Gloomy Library** is a modern library whose area mostly consists of aisles of single bookshelves with a few larger areas. The total size of this library is unknown as it is quite rare for survivors to visit this level. This level has no entities and no usable objects. Since this level is a library, there are a large number of books in various colors, sizes, and thicknesses that can be found here. However, the books within the level cannot be read due to their abstract text and chaotic images.

This level consists of two main structures: narrow aisles of single bookshelves less than a meter wide with good lighting, and slightly larger areas containing rows of tables and chairs. Posters, signs, and ornaments appear randomly along the aisles, but there is a small chance of survivors finding doors—all of which cannot be opened. However, similar to the books, all text within this level is randomized.

Survivors who have entered this level state that it contains nothing but emptiness; consequently, staying here too long induces feelings of depression and loneliness. Furthermore, survivors do not experience fatigue nor require sustenance such as food or water, allowing them to explore the level continuously. However, this leads to mental exhaustion due to the level's monotonous structure and its silent atmosphere, devoid of any signs of life.

The cool temperature and the level's notoriously dull appearance can induce drowsiness. However, since survivors remain physically refreshed and do not need sleep, these conflicting sensations are known to cause frustration. There are many testimonials describing the emptiness and isolation of this level; below is one such account from a survivor who has entered the level.

[Open Testimony](javascript:;)

[Hide](javascript:;)

Ah yes, the *Grey* Library, was it? I've been there before. Why did I enter? Let's just say it was out of sheer curiosity. It looked like an ordinary library, yet it consisted entirely of aisles, forming a single-track maze with complex turns. In my opinion, there's no need to map this place; it isn't as disorienting as you might think. However, the main issue with this level lies in the psychological effects you feel upon arrival. And it is because of this effect that the level earned its name.

It is empty, cramped, and you won't find anything here but yourself. The sensation of the Backrooms capturing and consuming you is palpable here; the claustrophobia and loneliness pressing in from all sides make you feel as if you are inside a monster's gullet. Time seems to stand still, making you question whether you should keep moving forward. The unnatural vigor that persists makes you wonder if this inability to tire is a blessing or a curse.

For some reason, you are compelled to walk down those narrow, monotonous aisles endlessly. You lose track of how long or how far you've walked because your energy never depletes; your only thought is to push forward, hoping to find a way out. Slowly, you start to crave interaction with other beings, especially humans. You miss their voices, you want to go home immediately, you want to be part of civilization again.

The isolation is intense, yet so is the sense of familiarity. This level somehow reminded me of my school days back on Earth, wandering the aisles of bookshelves in search of something interesting to read or to complete an assignment. It was quiet, yet bustling. There were boring moments, and fun moments. That library was a colorful place where people from diverse backgrounds gathered for one common thing: books.

But when I arrived in the Backrooms, I thought this library would be the same as the one on Earth. It wasn't. Everything was silent and desolate. There was no reading material, just me and the emptiness within. And then, what happened? All those colorful experiences turned *grey*. I was frustrated, bored, lonely, and I kept walking incessantly with a vacant stare. That mental torture finally ceased when the floor pulled me out of that unpleasant level; I was relieved to finally escape.

Ah. There are many similar levels out there—empty voids that isolate and test your mind to its limit. This level is one of them, so those of you accustomed to such places probably won't complain as much as I did. But to those new to this world: you must fortify your mind at all costs, because the weak-minded will be 'consumed' by the Backrooms… *not that I recommend visiting levels like this often*. Well, it is more or less a testing ground like other empty levels; you can visit if you wish.

— *Hinge A.*

# **Bases, Outpost, and Communities**

There's no bases, outpost, and communities in this level.

# **Entrances and Exits**

#### Entrances

Level can be entered by looking for doors labeled "Library" in both settlement-themed and indoor levels, but sometimes this access may lead survivors to another library-themed level. However, survivors can still recognize this level just by peeking at the indoor design from the door—if it's appearance different from what it supposed to be, then it's another library level.

#### Exits

Survivors can only exit this level by noclip into the floor, but this event occurs randomly—it is unknown where and when it will occur, and will send survivors to another level randomly as well.
