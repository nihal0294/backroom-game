02 Oct 2023 19:03

1. Make a wiki site that is blank
2. Copy and paste the code from [NuLiminal](theme__nuliminal--b088b523.md) into the customize your theme page wikidot makes for you
3. Past that, it's just tweaking what you want and don't want on the site. I would read up on a lot of the wikidot documentation before making your own sandbox site, just in case.
