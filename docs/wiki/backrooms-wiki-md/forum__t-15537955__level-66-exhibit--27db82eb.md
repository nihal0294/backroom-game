No-vote

18 Jan 2023 14:55

As I critiqued this, I was inclined towards a horror slant. I really like this Level's idea, but I really don't think the vibe was expressed sufficiently through the writing.

- Insufficient "show not tell". A lot of words TELLING me about paranoia and anxiety. Not enough words EVOKING that paranoia and anxiety inside me.

> Due to the lack of evidence to back these claims apart from witness statements, these incidents have been deemed audiovisual hallucinations.

Especially this. This… really ruins it for me. By presenting a conclusion at all, it reduces the sense of uncertainty in your writing about whether or not something is actually there. That and the fact that this line is very clinical really takes me out of the immersion.

- SPaG nitpicks annoy me. You clearly misspelt "don'ts", and it's "**lose** their way", not "**loose** their way"!

- Do's and Don'ts section is interesting for a Level article, since it breaks the format… but it's not formatted properly. Please use asterisks to form proper bullet points instead of copying in what I typed over to you on discord lol
