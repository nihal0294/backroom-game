Level Idea: The Penitentiary

03 Jul 2021 05:43

Hi everyone, first idea. Just looking for some CC on this and whether or not this idea is a winner or loser. Posted this on the Reddit, so this is the trimmed-down summary.

**Page Type:** Level description w/exploration journal

**Genre (Optional):**

**Page Layout (Optional):** As of right now, I'm thinking of writing this as a level description and guide from the perspective of someone who has lived in the level and well acquainted with how the level functions, as well as the personal observations he makes about the more unusual and unsettling elements of the level.

**Elevator Pitch:** The level is a prison, staffed by entities called 'Wardens.' Upon entering the level, a person is immediately 'sentenced' to a random period of time, not less than a week, not more than a year. The level is almost entirely free of entities except for the 'Wardens' which are extraordinarily protective of the level and those who inhabit it. They are not having it when it comes to shenanigans, be it a human or an entity. They see to it that all those 'incarcerated' in the facility are protected as they serve out there sentence. However, you have to follow the rules to live peaceably, and unfortunately the rules are not entirely clear. Any human who breaks those rules are immediately sent to solitary confinement, a place where no one has ever come back from.

The 'Warden's' themselves are uncanny, all appearing to be normal humans, except they all look, sound, and act identically. The only way to tell them apart are their name tags.

Once a person's sentence has been served, they are allowed to leave, but one can stay in the relative safety of the level by donning a uniform and becoming a 'custodian,' even getting a nicer dwelling and a seemingly normal existence within the level. It soon becomes clear though that something isn't right with the custodians, and it's later noted that custodians that stay for too long begin to become more and more deranged, seeing the level as something they have a stake in that requires protecting. Eventually, the connection is made when we find that the name tags of the Wardens are all people who once stayed in the level as custodians.

**Central Narrative:** It's just a level guide with exploration log, but I kind of want the log to imply that the level was some long lost person's attempt to try and police the Backrooms and instill some form of order in this world outside of the world and make it a safe place to live, because he himself could not escape the Backrooms. In the end though, the Backrooms eventually thwarted him, and distorted and corrupted his vision.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** It's a case of humanity's desire for law and order in a place where neither exist, and when you get down to it, no different than the law and order we seek to establish in our own reality. What we call law and order don't really exist in the first place, they are constructs. Just like the Backrooms, the universe could throw us a curveball, distorting those concepts.

**Additional Notes:** I just want to know if this idea is a winner worth pursuing and expanding on, as well as input as to whether or not it's something that can fit within the lore of the Backrooms.

The Reddit post is here:  
<https://www.reddit.com/r/TheBackrooms/comments/ob99jc/idea_for_new_level_the_penitentiary/>  
It's not really an explanation of the story and it's more a description of how the level operates and why it's a level worth exploring and worth treating with an air of caution.
