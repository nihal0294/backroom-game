This is an okay article with a great concept.

The article repeats itself (because the test's results are in all previous sections), it mentions Entities and multiple people in Level 0, and it doesn't explain why there are tales that do not use Babel Balm. The page isn't as good as it could be, but it **is** okay. It is not the peak of Backrooms writing and we shouldn't expect it to be. Admittedly, the idea of holding down an Entity and applying a lip stick is *hilarious*.

The actual concept is amazing. It fills a niche no-one knew needed to be filled (as in why people of other languages can work together). It also introduces multi-lingual outposts, and as a byproduct shows why making the M.E.G. English exclusive and the Ariane Circle French exclusive is actually a stupid idea. The direct concepts of Babel balm are pretty useful and it is a good Object.

My only problem with the concept portion is that putting Babel Balm on the corner of a page doesn't really make sense. It is lip balm, why does it work on pages? If you want it to make it work, make it have to be slathered on the page, since that is both funny and explains why people don't do that to every unknown book. Plus that's why Blanche doesn't use it, since I doubt she'll take kindly to putting lip balm on ancient relics.

Anyways that's my thoughts on Babel Balm. It's an okay article with a great concept.
