I'll just put all of the necessary sentence restructures here, since this is basically uncritiqueable without it.

"**Level 297** is usually a non-euclidean space and it's full of an unknown kind of gel" should be "**Level 297** is a non-euclidean space filled with an unknown kind of gel".  
"We really advise wanderers not to try noclipping there because all wanderers did that haven't come again" should be "Going here is advised against, as all who did so never returned".  
"Get into a gel pool inLevel 37" should be "Enter a pool of gel found in Level 37".  
"Well,I'm sure the link really started this time.I think I has just noclipped to a new level.Let me describe this fucking place" should be something like "I think I just no-clipped into a new level, let me describe it".  
"A level filled with gel,I think.but why do I feel the gel a little harder?Perhaps my hallucination" should be something like "It's completely filled with gel. It feels like it's hardening around me, but maybe I'm hallucinating".  
"No,it isn't! It covered my skin and hardened clearly,but why I can still breathe" can be something like "Forget that idea, it's covered my skin and is hardening around me like concrete. Fortunately, I can somehow still breathe".  
"Ouch.I almost can't move or do anything others.It's much harde--" should say something along the lines of "I can hardly move anymore, I'm trapped in pla-"  
You could just get rid of the Kat quote.  
"Harden Space" should be "Hardening liquid".  
"It's too dangerous to get into **Level 297**,we wish you not to try it" should be restructured to something like "**Level 297** is too dangerous to enter. Do so at your own risk".  
"**Level 297**is usually a 450-cubic-metre-big closed space that wanderers in it can see the edge easily and the level is full of gel" should be something like "**Level 297** is a level filled with gel, measuring 450 cubic meters. Wanderers can see the walls through the gel".  
Restructure the next sentence the same way as I mentioned previously.  
"When a wanderer come in the level,the gel beside him or her will begin to harden in about 10 minutes and wanderers can't move finally" should be "When a Wanderer enters the level, the gel will harden around them. Within about 10 minutes, they will be unable to move".  
"However,the ones who are trapped there can't suffocate in any ways" should be "However, those trapped in the gel do not suffocate".  
"Like Level 0,wanderers who was in**Level 297**can't meet each other at all.But now the phenomenon was invalid" should be "**Level 297** once had Level 0's isolation effect, but it has since disappeared".  
"Noclipping at any level dominated by liquid has the probability to take you here" should be "No-clipping into a pool of gel in any level has a possibility to take you here".  
"Terribly,I fell in a gel pool.No hope here,I can just record here and help wanderers like me" should say something like "I fell into some kind of gel pool, and now I'm stuck here. There doesn't seem to be any hope of getting out. I guess I'll just record my time here".  
The sentences after should be something like "My water bottle fell out of my bag, now it's turning the gell yellow. It's not hardening anymore, either. I'm going to use more water on it".  
You could just remove the other speech, it's kind of inconsistent and I can't tell if they're answering to Wende or not.  
"**Level 297**is usually a 450-cobic-big space and it's full of yellow liquid.We really advise wanderers not to try noclipping there because all wanderers did that haven't come again.Now it becomes safe,here's ever  
filled with gel and it beside wanderers would harden to trap them.But now you shouldn't care it,because safe liquid is instead of gel.The level is always safe,the only disadvantage is the lack of objects.In this level,wanderers can't suffocate in any ways and they never feel hungry or thirsty,so here is also called"A heaven corner"in somebody's words" could be significantly shortened to "**Level 297** is a 450 cubic meter area filled with yellow liquid, which is safe to traverse. It used to harden around people until they died, but this is no longer the case. The level lacks resources, but Wanderers do not need to eat or drink while in the level".  
Edit the entrances as I mentioned before.  
"Noclip.You'll get to a random safe level soon" should be changed to "No-clipping anywhere will take you to a random safe level".
