*This is the first known document of the [manila room,](manila-room.md) digitized for preservation.*

I made it.

I told you I would.

The… ["tutorial level"](level-0.md) was just as lonely as described, but I found this room, just as the page said.

But there's nothing here. Just this desk and a chair. There should be filing cabinets filled with information, but there's nothing. There's not even wifi. Was I mistaken when I read about the Manila Room…?

I remember there was an [endless city.](level-11.md) I'll go there. If things go smoothly, we'll meet there. If not, well— doesn't matter.

I'll find you.
