Re: potential new (colourful) sublayer for level 1

05 Aug 2022 05:34

> Level 1.3 is the 3rd sublevel

"sublevel" should be hyphenated here.

> sublevel of level 1 of

"level 1" should be capitalized and a link should be provided to its page.

> of the backrooms.

"backrooms" should be capitalized here since it's a proper noun.

> A sublevel of level 1,

"sublevel" should be hyphenated and "level 1" should be capitalized. (It's not needed to link it after the first time.)

> level 1.3 is a series of brightly lit,

Capitalize "level 1.3" here since that's a proper noun.

> rebar, loud fluorescent lighting, concrete

Ughhhhhhh, "loud" lighting is so overdone at this point. Give it a unique flair; maybe it makes a montone continuous noise, maybe the noise fluctuates between being really quiet and then really loud, maybe the lights are really high pitched. Anything besides using that Level 0 shtick over again.

> strains of the disease have been discovered

Add some more elaboration on what "disease" actually is, since it's introduced as a major element here but doesn't seem to be the case.

> drinks are replaced with cashew water or liquid pain,

Capitalize "cashew water" and "liquid pain" in this instance.

> containing almond water or lucky-o-milk and greasy marshmallows

Capitalize "almond water" and "lucky-o-milk" and "greasy marshmallows" in this instance.

> foods from the frontrooms can be sometimes

Capitalize "frontrooms" here.

> However, some supply boxes, as well as any seen in dirtier areas are to be avoided at all costs,

Change this to:

\*However, some supply boxes, (especially those found in dirtier areas) are to be avoided at all costs,\*

> normal adult and child facelings, although pink dress facelings (often in pastel pink shade dresses) are frequently encountered, skin stealers (sometimes also wearing pastel coloured clothes), death moths and death rats

Capitalize "facelings" and "skin stealers" and "death moths" and "death rats" here.
