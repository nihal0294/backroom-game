Now see, the way I interpreted the rules is that it's allowed *to a point*. If it's in a formal article, it should be within a data entry, such as a journal or an interview. Within the information itself? No. Don't do that, as it will severely diminish the clinical tone that we the authors strive for here at the wiki. On the other hand, if it's a story/tale, go right ahead.
