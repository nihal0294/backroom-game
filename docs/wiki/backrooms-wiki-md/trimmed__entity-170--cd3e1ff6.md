### Entity Number: 170

### Habitat(s):

Majority

### Description

Pyrochelicera are 14-meter-long reptiles that somewhat resemble Frontrooms caimans. Their flesh is slightly acidic and can survive extreme heat.

### Behaviors:

Pyrochelicera seem to randomly noclip in and out of walls, ceilings, and floors. They will attack most living organisms, with the exception of an instance of [entity-136](trimmed__entity-136--9445b9ef.md). Most Pyrochelicera are able to spit flaming hydrochloric acid up to a distance of 20 meters through two glands located in its cheeks. Pyrochelicera minds are animalistic in nature, constantly seeking food and mates. Their favored prey is [Entity 130](trimmed__entity-130--daea3dd9.md) and [Entity 66](entity-66.md). Their overall intelligence varies from individual to individual. It should be noted that a dead Pyrochelicerate will attract other Pyrochelicera.

### Biology:

Pyrochelicera are large reptilian creatures, with black skin resembling that of [Entity 187](entity-187.md), and a reddish brown underside. They have inch-long, interlocking teeth in their small mouth. The species has two pairs of eyes, one located above the mouth and one located below the mouth, and four 12-meter-long wings on their body. Their body itself is quite round and ends in a short tail . They have two limbs that end in three talons underneath their wings, used for basic object manipulation. Entity 170's facial structure ends in four long tusks. Pyrochelicera are simultaneous hermaphrodites, and must reproduce with another of is kind. Entity 170's internal biology is similar to that of [Entity 187](entity-187.md), with black organs and cyan blood. They have two large hearts, four small lungs, and no stomach.

### Discovery:

The first confirmed sighting of a Pyrochelicerate was made by Abraham Lena. One dead Pyrochelicerate was also discovered on Level 8 on 11/11/2020.
