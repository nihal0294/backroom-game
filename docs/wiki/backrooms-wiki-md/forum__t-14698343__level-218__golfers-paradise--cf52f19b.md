**Level 218** is the 219th positive level of the backrooms. It is an infinite golf course with an infinite number of trees, lakes, golf clubhouses, and golf holes.

# **Description:**

**Level 218** is an overall unsafe and dangerous level, and the safest parts of it are inside the clubhouses. When you first enter **level 218**, you will spawn inside a randomly inside the level, and it is recommended that you immediately start to look for a clubhouse, because you are not safe until you are inside one.

## The Holes:

The golf holes of these level are harmless, except they are infested with the level exclusive entity known as a golfer (see entities section). The holes are recommended to be avoided at all costs, unless you really want to get out of the level.

## The Woods:

The woods is a safer, but still dangerous area of **level 218**. There are multiple undocumented entities scattered throughout this area of the level, and most of them seem to be hostile. When looking for the clubhouses, this is the safest area to be in. Staying in the woods too long will drain your sanity, so it is recommended not to stay in the woods for more than 3 hours.

## The Lakes:

The lakes are the most dangerous area of **level 218**. They are completely made up of Cashew Water, and going near them is ill-advised. When you start to get within a 50 yard range of a lake, you will start to feel like you need to go towards it. When you get close enough, an unknown force will knock you into it, and nearby entities will finish you off. Luckily, the lakes are uncommon, and seeing one is very rare. Unless you need cashew water, stay away from these lakes.

## Clubhouses:

The clubhouses are the safest areas, and probably the only safe area, of **level 218**. They are rare, but if you find one, it is recommended to go to it immediately. The clubhouses provide the most reliable exit in the level. The golfers in the clubhouse are passive, and will happily engage in a conversation. There is food located throughout the clubhouses, and it is recommended to eat some before leaving. Attacking or provoking a golfer in the clubhouse will cause them to become hostile toward you and attack you.

# **Entities:**

## Golfers:

Golfers are a level exclusive entity of **level 218**. Golfer are extremely hostile outside of clubhouses, and will run at you if they see you. If they get close enough to you they will start to hit you with their golf clubs, and if they knock you out, they will run you over with their golf carts. These entities seem to be indestructible, as attempts to hurt them have all failed. They can run up to 25 miles per hour, and have inhuman strength. The only way to get away from a golfer is to get out of their sight. This is why you should stay away from the golf hole.

# **Bases, Outposts, and communities:**

Because of the difficulty of getting into this level, and no reason to stay, there are no bases, outposts, or communities on **level 218**

# **Entrances and exits**

## Entrances:

Entering **level 218** is unreliable, and the main way of getting to this level is by no clipping in an outdoor level. There is also a 20% chance of coming here from the frontrooms by no clipping while golfing.

## Exits:

- Traveling too far into the woods may result in a door appearing, which will lead to a random outdoor level.
- If you politely ask a golfer in the clubhouse where the exit is, they will show you. The exit leads to a safer much safer level.
- No clipping in the woods can take you to level 42
