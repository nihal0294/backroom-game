# Level Background

[![A tunnel leading to a mine.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/fragment:level-155-0/quartz_image/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-155-0/quartz_image)

A tunnel leading to a mine.

Level 155 is a newly discovered level that was first brought to the B.N.T.G.'s attention in early February, 2024. The level is an infinitely winding complex built out of refined quartz slabs.

Architecturally, the rooms themselves are monolithic in size, with ceilings that ascend for hundreds of kilometers. Stairs and large ramps connect the various floors of the level creating a labyrinthine system of chambers, corridors, and colosseum-like rooms. Level 155 is pristine, with few imperfections or signs of structural damage.

[![An expansive, deep ceiling, common in Level 155.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/fragment:level-155-0/quartz_mine/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-155-0/quartz_mine)

An expansive, deep ceiling, common in Level 155.

The quartz— which most of the level is made of— has been graded to be fit for architectural use. The composition has been determined to be approximately 89-92% quartz crystals, and 7-10% resin.

This composition is evaluated as durable and high-quality, with potential usage as building material, luxury jewelry, coins, and furniture. However, its viability in industrial and manufacturing sectors (semiconductors, electrical insulating) is rather dubious due to the resin content. In addition, extraction of the silicon within the quartz that the level produces for other purposes demands a logistically complex process of melting and collection, which has long dissuaded attempts to do so.

While other access points may exist, the most frequently used and well-known entrances to the level have been demarcated in Levels [1](level-1.md), [2](level-2.md), and [11](level-11.md), identified as large doors made of quartz. The most popular entrance is from Level 11, located only 23 kilometers away from the epicenter of [New Times Square](the-b-n-t-g.md).

Exiting the level is as simple as going back through an entrance door. Each exit door (to the knowledge of the B.N.T.G.) has been marked by an official notice which denotes the specific level which it will bring individuals back to.

Despite being safe in structure and quality, Level 155 is not capable of sustaining life for prolonged durations due to a lack of food and water. To address potential food insecurities, any base or outpost residing in the level for extended periods of time should seek to find reliable means to obtain food and drink from outside of the level, preferably by establishing connection with an external base or outpost. Commonly, outposts have maintained close ties with organizations located within Level 11, as the two levels share numerous access points.

# Establishment of Asset 155

[![A large, monolithic room with a white ceiling. To the right, a glimpse of a settlement of Asset 155.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/fragment:level-155-0/quartz_base/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-155-0/quartz_base)

One of the large rooms which have been settled by Asset 155.

Asset 155 was originally a small group of wanderers who resided in Level 155 prior to its discovery. In early February 2024, this small community reached out to the B.N.T.G., requesting aid. Recognizing the potential of the resources in Level 155, after a long, arduous process of negotiations and disputes, the B.N.T.G. leadership acquired the community— now known as Asset 155— in mid-March.

With the help of several investors and financial backers, the B.N.T.G. pooled together a significant sum of funds and resources towards a corporate venture capital, creating Asset 155 as a result. In less than a week, a small community blossomed into a company of over 80 miners.

At its peak, Asset 155 had five large dormitories: large rooms with rows of tents with ample space and small community centers, as well as sixteen vertical mines that extracted quartz from the expansive ceilings within most rooms.

## The Vertical Mining Strategy

The principal concern of the early settlers was considering where to establish a settlement. The second concern was exactly where and how to mine. The vertical mining approach, developed by the B.N.T.G. upon acquisition of the asset, sought to create a system of extracting quartz which minimized damage to structural integrity of the level, and maximized quartz yield.

Instead of mining into the floors of the level or the walls, which could potentially lead to regions of the level collapsing in on itself, the vertical mining strategy made use of the expansive and dense ceilings of the level. By using steel ramps (donated by the B.N.T.G.), miners could access the deep ceiling, carving tunnels into it. Although at first seemingly unconventional, this strategy has effectively left Level 155 fully pristine, easy to traverse, and unlikely to collapse. In turn, productivity and quartz output skyrocketed, with there being few safety incidents and workers being able to navigate the level safely.

With more than 400,000 presses invested into Asset 155, and thousands more invested into advertising and branding, the revenue earned by Asset 155 in its first month was significantly less than expected. It was predicted that the markets of New Times Square and the Traders Keep would each rake in at least 85,000 presses within the first month. However, combined the two largest markets as well as several other smaller markets only brought in approximately 22,000 presses. Adjusting for operation costs, only about 14,000 presses were actually earned.

Despite initial market failure, there was still hope— largely pushed by the marketers and advertisers— that quartz would find its place in the market eventually. By late May, Asset 155 was provided another loan of 20,000 presses in order to begin a campaign of mass advertising and marketing, with ads on BackNET, posters distributed to bases, and pitches to other groups to purchase large quantities of quartz. While these campaigns brought some success as evident by growth in July, the markets eventually stagnated.

In early August, investors of the project issued a demand to the B.N.T.G. to explain the disparity in alleged claims of what would be earned by Asset 155 versus what was being earned.

With rising pressure, the B.N.T.G. spent the last of its resources attempting to look for other valuable resources in Level 155 in hopes of finding something else profitable. This venture failed, leading to the eventual financial ruin of Asset 155.

# Dissolution of Asset 155

In the first week of September, profits from Asset 155 fell more than thirty percent, a result of the failed mining expedition and an exhaustion of every last solution. With more than 400,000 presses still owed to investors, the B.N.T.G. officially terminated its contract with the mining company in Level 155. After several days of notice, the B.N.T.G. officially withdrew all assets back to official bases.

The withdrawal from Level 155 was peaceful, with little disruption. Official higher-ups, such as managers and executives, left Level 155 on B.N.T.G. orders, and the remaining miners— counted as 75— were left within the level.

# Addendum

Although Asset 155 will be considered a failure due to the amount invested for a product that ultimately lacked demand, it can also be seen as a case study. Asset 155 was an attempt to take what the Backrooms has given us and turn it into the next sold commodity. It was an attempt to engineer the next "big thing" that would break through established markets and innovate. The results of this attempt conclude that not everything can be engineered to make success.

Any remaining assets of the project (leftover quartz, power tools, and utilities) are currently being auctioned off at discounted prices to large groups in order to scrap any remaining money which can be made from the remnants of Asset 155.

For now, the B.N.T.G. will no longer affiliate with Level 155, nor the mining company that resides within the level. The B.N.T.G. **[will not restrict other groups from entering and conducting their own business within the level, either.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-155/offset/1)**
