a pleasant test, a nice mistake

15 Nov 2024 15:38

To some, the autumn's breath is cold. To others, it's warm. The wind is just starting to chill, but the forests turn a new shade of orange, and there is always that lodging-place, a home or a coffee shop, a roof or a hot drink.

Why is autumn so beautiful when everything is dying? It's an often-asked question by bearers of a somber mood, but to be honest, there's always something to retain the leftover warmth. Within the life and the death, there is a bittersweet allure.

But you would be the judge of that, no? We all have our own seasons.
