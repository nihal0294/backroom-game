**Entity 227-X : "Death Angels"**

### Location

Any outdoor levels?

### Description

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-771/entity227X)

Entity 227-X photographed by a night vision camera

We have secured and recovered a plane wreckage in a remote area of level 11. This confirms the existence of the entity mentioned in previous reports; the “variant” of the already documented Entity-227: “Model Fighter Squadron”. This “variant”, which we call Entity 227-X or "Death Angel", is a 1:1 scale replica of an F-15 fighter jet. The wreckage we found was in surprisingly good condition, with little damage.

Throughout the inspection, we weren't able to locate where the ammunition and missiles were stored. It likely generates them only when needed. We only found flesh and organs each time a compartment was opened. The cockpit, completely opaque from the outside, reveals what looks to be a brain when opened, with many blood vessels linking it to the different parts of the aircraft. Additionally, we found out that the propellers use blood to function, thus explaining the "red smoke" left behind their path. Precautions and proper equipment are required when handling Entity 227-X since we found out the blood was infected with [the disease](entity-19.md).

As expected, this aircraft is fully autonomous and based on a "flesh and blood" system, just like any living being, without any sign of electronics found inside. Even its lights seem to be powered by blood. Only its "skin" is made of metal.

### Properties

According to the testimonies and footage provided by survivors, the entity may appear out of nowhere and suddenly disappear at any moment. It has been noted that they fly in a single direction and do not perform any maneuvers and may also appear in succession. Once one of them appears, other instances of the same entity will likely appear in several directions. This fighter jet emits a blinding red searchlight near its center with two other red lights on the wings, assisting the entity in spotting ground targets. The blinding searchlight, the deafening engine noise, and the "red smoke" that emits from its afterburners leaving behind its path, an infected blood rain, make the entity extremely easy to spot, thus making it easier to prepare for its arrival. Its top speed is also way lower than an actual F-15, reaching only about 300km/h instead of 3087km/h. It is said the entity is extremely hostile and will attack any non-entity target it finds. When spotting a target, the entity will brutally slow down to the point of being almost stationary and shine its searchlight at the target's exact position. Then, the entity will fire a burst of self-guided missiles and projectiles before flying away at top speed.

### Do

- If you're in a group, divide and regroup later  
- Avoid any lights emitted by the entity  
- Immediately find a hiding spot if you hear a loud engine noise coming from the distance

### Don't

- Breathe or interact with the blood left by the entity's afterburners.  
- Immediately come out of your hiding spot as more instances of the entity may appear at any time

### Conclusion

The entity may be easily avoidable if alone, but in a situation of chaos and despair involving large groups of humans like the attack of December 25th, this entity proved to be the most deadly of all. Were those entities naturally attracted by all the fuss caused by the attacks? Since when do they even exist? What are their motives? We still have a lot of questions to answer, but one thing is clear: someone is behind that. Maybe it's Liberation, or, maybe something bigger is lurking behind the scenes. We must solve that conundrum; the answer could provide us with crucial knowledge.

#### Lead Scientist Dr Archange, Team Apollo
