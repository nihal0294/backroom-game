I probably can't say much here which hasn't already been said by others but I will state some specific grievances I have.

The strikethrough text. I don't think it adds much to the article and it feels like a gimmick.

Many others have said this before but this level is pretty much guaranteed to just kill you and there's no way people could have explored so much of it because they would have died due to various reasons.

Along with that, explanations as to why they wouldn't kill (They still would because of water pressure. You go down too deep, you will just die.) feel more like excuses and not logical reasons.

The bases section uses a cult that never has its beliefs actually explained. It's just a hostile cult group that worships a big fish. Why? Who knows.

To go further, who is the person behind the explorer's note? The use of a personal pronoun implies it's a specific person but who is it and why do their notes even matter?
