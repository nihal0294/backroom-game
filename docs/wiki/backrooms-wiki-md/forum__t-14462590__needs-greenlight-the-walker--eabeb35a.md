Entity 00000 - "Walkers"  
Entity Number: 00000

Habitat - majority of levels

Description

Walkers are very hostile, There has been only one sighting of them,There is only one characteristic that is noticeable and that is the immensely long legs that are capable of running at a wanderer(s) at top speed of 3 MPH.

Behaviors

The Walker is attracted to sound, light and fear, They will chase and will kill if it senses: Sound, Light and fear. Using almond water will repel it (hopefully).

Biology

Walkers have long legs, Short arms and knife sharp teeth that are capable of tearing through bone and chewing it easily. Nothing else is know about the Walkers biology.

Discovery

it is unknown on how the Walker was discovered there is no registered info about the Walker by the M.E.G

Do's and Don'ts:

Do:

splash the Walker with almond water  
walk away quietly  
Don't:

run away  
make loud noises
