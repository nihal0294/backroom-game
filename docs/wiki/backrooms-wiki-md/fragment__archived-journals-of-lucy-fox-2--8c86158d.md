# Archived Journals Of Lucy Fox 2

**Entry One**

  
  
  

**Just in case this is the last anyone hears from me, and someone finds this and not me, I'm keeping this journal to document my observations of these [strange masks](object-24.md) that have been popping up recently. I've been walking along the [endless city](level-11.md) for a few hours now. I just stumbled across one hanging on the wall of one of the stores — I noticed it instantly. It looks similar to an old, stereotypical theater mask, but it's painted a beautiful color of gold. The — what's their name — [MTG](the-m-e-g.md)? I have no idea. Whatever their name is, they say these things are dangerous, but they refuse to tell me about it when I ask. I'm taking matters into my own hands. I'm gonna approach the mask and see what happens. I'll write back once I'm in.**
