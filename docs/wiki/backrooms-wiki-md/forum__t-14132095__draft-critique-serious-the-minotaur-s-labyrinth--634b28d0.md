# **Description:**

Due to the nature of the Level, all data was obtained by a single male survivor who discovered the level, and as a result the information about the level is limited. Due to HonestLier445 being mute and deaf, all information may be inaccurate.

**Level 63** ostensibly is a large, but not infinite, labyrinth of Greek origin. The Labyrinth apparently resembles the famous Cretan Labyrinth in both its intact state and its dilapidated counterpart, depending on the person viewing it. The Level apparently has several small to medium sized holes in the ceiling, revealing daylight. This appears to be the only source of light in the Level. Escape through this way is seemingly impossible. It is unknown if the Level has a day/night cycle. **Level 63** has an only 2 distinct areas, the entrance and the exit. The entrance is a small room with a door leading into the labyrinth. The chosen youth supposedly used the twine on the door, an event that took place in the legend.

The exit is another large room, but with copious amounts of skeletons in the area. Within this room, there is a locked door that seemingly cannot be opened until the Minotaur is killed. Every other area within the level is a part of the labyrinth. Multiple galleries and doors within the Level are designed to mislead people within the Level. According to HonestLier445, a male Wanderer that claimed he managed to escape after he discovered the Level, the doors in **Level 63** are tunnels and the galleries are either very derelict or absent entirely. HonestLier445 signed that the chosen youth repeatedly stated the doors and galleries were in pristine condition, although allegedly no one else in the Level could see what the youth was seeing.

**Level 63** seems to correspond with the legend of the Minotaur with some major differences. **Level 63** seemingly causes 7 maidens(young woman) and 7 youths( young men) to appear in the Level every year, though this is only based on the legend and not confirmed as of now. All 14 individuals appear in different areas within the level, with only the chosen youth appearing at the entrance. All individuals present within the Level will seemingly don a Greek robe, and all items originally on the Wanderers will disappear. The one youth will take on the appearance of Theseus, and obtains the weapons and the ball of twine Theseus received from Ariadne in the legend. The youth will apparently gain heroic qualities, even though the youth affected apparently did not possess these qualities. The youth took on a leadership role, and attempted to help the Wanderers escape.

According to HonestLier445, he was the only survivor, which is much different from the legend. The other deviation is that the youth that killed the Minotaur apparently turned into the Minotaur. This process causes the youth to mutate, reportedly gaining :

- Approximately 2 feet in height and and an undetermined amount of weight;

- A long bull's tail;

- Large smooth horns;

- A very misshapen and squashed face;

- Short brown hair that covers his entire body;

- Hands much larger than normal;

- Hooves;

- Dark and leathery lips;

- Huge, sharp and crooked teeth;

- And bright red eyes.

HonestLier445 claimed this process appeared to be very painful to the youth.

The main threat of **Level 63** presumably is the Minotaur. This entity is apparently extremely aggressive. The entity is slow, but can charge for short periods of time, though the exact speed and amount of time it can do this is unknown. The entity reportedly has enhanced strength and sense of smell, and its preferred methods of killing is goring or crushing its victim. According to HonestLier445, the creature can only be killed by the chosen youth, with the sword they possess. All other means of attack proved futile. HonestLier445 signed that The Minotaur could not kill the chosen youth, though this is unconfirmed for now.

This entire Level could be fictitious in nature, but the amount of detail and HonestLier445 not having anything to gain from lying makes this unclear. Due to this, HonestLier445 is currently under examination.

## Bases, Outposts and Communities

There are no known Bases or Outposts on this Level.

## Entrances and Exits:

### Entrances:

It is unknown exactly how to get into **Level 63**, but it is speculated that once a year, 7 random youths and 7 random maidens will appear inside the level, though this is only based on the Legend of the Minotaur.

### Exits:

According to HonestLier445, there is currently only one known exit within **Level 63** and that is to escape the labyrinth via the exit door. Once you have escaped, you will appear within the town in [Level 10](level-10.md), but it is currently unknown you will appear in [Level 10](level-10.md) consistently.
