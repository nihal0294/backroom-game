Hello, nxrvousbun! Welcome to the wiki, the staff, and members are all here with open arms. You don't need to apply to anything to start a draft for your level idea! Just head to the wiki sandbox to start [http://backrooms-sandbox.wikidot.com/](index.md) ! Before you set your heart on your idea search the main ideas (location, desired room number, etc.) in the search bar of the main wiki to make sure we prevent over-lapping ideas! We have many templates to help those unfamiliar with coding, <http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/contributions-guide> , feel free to check it out. You can always PM me or any rotating staff if you have any questions. Happy to see you here and I'm sure you'll do great!
