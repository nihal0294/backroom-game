# Blurb Contest 2026

To combat the issue that was the original Backrooms blurb being non-CC, Blurbcon was launched to decide on a new CC-compliant blurb. There were a total of 40 submissions, but of course, only one of them could win.

The winning blurb was **Blurb 38** with **19 votes**! Made by and . Congratulations on winning!

---

*They say that it's an afterlife–not for people, but for places. For every chain storefront demolished, every retail outlet bought out, every dead mall razed to the ground. A limbo for every building without purpose, those in-between spaces. Endless structures so unfathomable, yet so clearly man-made. Edges in the curtains of memory, corners that open up at the seams of reality to let the unlucky wanderer in.*

*Be careful out there, for the footsteps that fill these halls may not only be your own.*

---

Of course, we also have to mention our runners-up: **Blurb #9** by with **16 votes**, and **Blurb #17** by with **12 votes**.

# Valentine Blitz 2026

![Anthonyx_hand_holding.png](http://praetor.wikidot.com/local--files/blfogkzixkziige/Anthonyx_hand_holding.png)

As Praetor once famously said: "*Love is in the air? WRONG! Writing is..*"

Okay, fine, I'll give context. In February, a writing blitz was held where two people would collaborate together on writing a romantic article, all to celebrate Valentines Day. The event saw a total of **7 new articles** by **14 authors**. Funnily enough, all but one were posted on the same day, February 28th.[1](javascript:;) Don't ask why…

# Series Hub

![This.PNG](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/pnn-wepm/This.PNG)

There is now a [hub](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/series-hub) dedicated to the many tales with multiple parts, which are referred to as a series. Some of these series had their own central hub containing all parts of the story, and the new series hub acts as a hub to these hubs.[2](javascript:;)

Currently, there are *four* different tale series that have been added to this hub, with more on the way.

![banner](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-36/banner)

also rewrote the second part of the previous feature, [Level 36.1 - "Astral Airplane"](level-36-1.md). These are the airplanes that fly out of Level 36 to transport passengers to other levels.  
![cabin](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-36-1/cabin)

And here we have the very first article of 2026. In [Prince des vaches](prince-des-vaches.md), shows us the strange lives and deaths of Jean Meier. But… what's even going on?  
![cow1](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/prince-des-vaches/cow1)

In [Knocking On Heaven's Door](knocking-on-heaven-s-door.md), explores the afterlife of Overseers Justin and Andrew after the events of Entity 246. But what happens in this place after the two seemingly died?  
![Waiting%20room](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/knocking-on-heaven-s-door/Waiting%20room)

Get ready to read something tragic. In [Unnumbered Entity - "My Mom"](my-mom.md), the author shows us the gut-wrenching story of someone losing their beloved mother. But if that's all there is, why is this an entity? Only one way to find out.  
![mother.png](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/my-mom/mother.png)
