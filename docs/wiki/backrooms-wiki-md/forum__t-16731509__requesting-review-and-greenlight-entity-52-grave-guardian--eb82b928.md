Entity 52-"Grave Guardian" (REQUESTING REVIEW AND GREENLIGHT)

19 Feb 2024 03:18

Entity Number: 52  
Habitat: Levels with a large amount of 'room-like' structures.

# **Description**

This entity takes on the form of the corpse of a human man in his mid-twenties, sitting in a chair or slumped against the wall, based on the room he appears in. He will never appear outside of a room with a door. *THIS ENTITY IS TO BE AVOIDED AT ALL COSTS, especially if you are alone.*While race, height, facial features, and posture will all differ, the entity will always have a few things in common between manifestations:  
-One eye will always be closed-the other is always open and will always be staring right at whoever is closest to it in the room.  
-The corpse will always be as far from any entrance to the room as possible, pointed away from any way to see into the room.  
-The corpse will always be holding an electronic device capable of displaying video and audio.  
-The corpse will always bear an uncanny resemblance to someone you care about.  
-The corpse will always be in pristine condition, aside from a trident[1](javascript:;) driven through his chest[2](javascript:;).  
-The corpse is invincible above the neck.  
-The corpse will always be wearing a blue business suit.  
-The corpse's eye cannot be closed.  
-The corpse will always be smiling.  
-The corpse will always be as warm to the touch as a living human.  
-The corpse will always be surrounded by dead instances of [Entity 15](entity-15.md).  
It is unknown if there are multiple instances of this Entity.

# Behaviors

This entity will not activate until there is a Wanderer inside the room with it. Once there is, all forms of entrance to the room will close and become both unopenable and indestructible from the inside. From there, the Wanderer(s) inside are doomed unless someone else frees them from the outside. Every three minutes, one of the corpse's fingers will break, serving as a timer. Over time, the following effects will occur:  
-Immediately after entering the room  
The Wanderer will feel a sense of dread fall over them as the exits become inaccessible, and their heart rate will drastically increase.  
-After three minutes  
Any lighting in the room will begin to flicker-however, the corpse will always be lit by an unknown source. The Wanderer will start to hear faint breathing while standing near the corpse.  
-After nine minutes  
The breathing can now be heard throughout the room, and the corpse's chest will rise and fall in sync with it. Wanderers have reported feeling abrupt flashes of terror, as well as a sensation of wetness on their hands.  
-After twelve minutes  
The device in the corpse's hand will turn on and begin playing a silent video. Wanderers have reported seeing many things on the screen, such as glimpses of other levels, the outside of the room, and themselves from the corpse's perspective.  
-After fifteen minutes  
The Wanderer will begin to hallucinate the corpse moving out of the corner of their eye. They will feel an extreme sense of paranoia and will likely begin to panic at this time. The lights will begin going out for extended periods of time.  
-After eighteen minutes  
The video playing on the device will shut off and be replaced with audio. This audio alternates between agonized screams, silence, [a maddening hum-buzz,](level-0.md) and heavy pounding. The corpse will begin to cough, and blood[3](javascript:;) will spill from its chest and mouth. The wet feeling on the Wanderer's hands will return, and they will hallucinate blood if they look. It is at this point that the Wanderer will begin showing symptoms indicative of the [Wretched Cycle](entity-15.md)'s first stage.  
-After twenty-one minutes  
The device will resume playing video along with audio. Whatever you do at this point, *DO NOT LOOK AT THE SCREEN.* What is displays is unknown, because every Wanderer that saw it instantly went mad and devolved into a [Wretch](entity-15.md) soon after.  
-After twenty-four minutes  
Every light source will go out for good, and the room will turn pitch-black[4](javascript:;). The Wanderer will begin showing symptoms of the [Wretched Cycle](entity-15.md)'s second stage. Failure to escape the room at this time puts you past the point of no return. Suicide is advised if you believe there is no way to escape the room.  
-After thirty minutes  
There is no account of what happens after thirty minutes. All Wanderers inside the room are to be presumed lost at this time. After this point, the Entity will vanish from that room.

---

# **Do's and Don'ts**

# Do:

- Save anyone you can from this Entity.[5](javascript:;)
- Spread the word about this Entity.
- Write notes warning people about this Entity and place them by the entrances to the room it is inhabiting.

# Don't:

- Fall victim to this Entity.
- Look at the screen after twenty-one minutes.
- Attempt to confront this Entity alone.
- Be a grave robber. The audacity. They got what they deserved.

Footnotes

[1](javascript:;). Tests have concluded that the trident is made from pykrete and painted over with silver.

[2](javascript:;). You are able to retrieve this trident, and it is an effective weapon-however, this is not to be attempted unless you are in a group.

[3](javascript:;). The DNA in this blood is unidentifiable due to heavy radiation damage.

[4](javascript:;). It is rumored that no-clipping into the walls at this point has a small chance to send you to Level 6.

[5](javascript:;). It has been observed that this Entity is only triggered by human presence.
