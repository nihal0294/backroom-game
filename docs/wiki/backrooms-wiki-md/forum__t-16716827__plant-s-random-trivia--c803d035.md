This is just random fun facts about stuff you'll likely never use except while you're in a party and trying to seem smart to that one girl  
The Amen Break, a 6-20 second break from the 1969 track "Amen, Brother," has been sampled over 6,000 times, appearing in many famous songs today. It created half of the EDM genres, but has appeared in pop and metal as well. Breakcore is basically based off this break as well. (Here's the break: <https://youtu.be/NkU4hsaFglU> )  
My favorite use of it is in hkomori's "anybody can find love (except you)" (yes, the "breakcore in nutshell" song. <https://youtu.be/-NEGsRc3fbA> ) or DM DOKURO's "Scourge of The Universe" ( <https://youtu.be/-Syn1mJVt7U?t=188> )

Calvin Coolidge is USA's 30th president. He did a few officially passed things, including giving US citizenship to all Native Americans born in USA from now on and restoring public confidence in the White House after the Harding scandals

When asked why he went to so many parties despite being so quiet and seemingly uncomfortable with them, he replied with "got to eat somewhere"
