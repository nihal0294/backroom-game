Heptaphobia

29 Jul 2022 01:09

"Why was 6 afraid of 7?" A math joke that, by this point, I'm sure everyone's heard. See, the humor of the joke comes from the fact that, when read sequentially, the numbers "7", "8", and "9", sound similar to "seven ate nine". Now, I don't know about you, but if someone ate somebody else I'd be scared shitless of them too. It doesn't help that 6 looks like an inverted 9, making them an easy next target of 7 considering how close they resemble them. It's like when you can't get the brand of cereal you like so you just go for an off-brand version. It's not the same, but it's good enough.

This page has virtually nothing to do with what I just said. Enjoy!
