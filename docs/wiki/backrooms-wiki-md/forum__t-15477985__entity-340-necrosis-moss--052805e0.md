# Entity 340 - Necrosis Moss

## Habitat(s): Majority of Levels, Mostly

### **Description: Necrosis Moss are red phosphorescent invasive moss hive colonies with tendrils that stick to walls, floors, and ceilings that hunt wanderers, items and entities for food, once that they find esculent prey they will start eating their prey via absorb with highly corrosive acids on its skin or surface. Necrosis Moss can be dispatched by using [firesalt](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/firesalt) on Necrosis Moss to set them on fire.**

### Behaviors: Necrosis Moss are highly territorial and aggressive with other creatures and will unlatch itself from the surface and chase you relentlessly without exhaustion, they have high human intelligence with being able to talk in Morse Code when clicking their tendrils against walls and roofs, saying things like "go away" "we want you to go the other way" and "we will find you".

**Do & Don'ts.**  
**Do**  
**Do Avoid Necrosis Moss.**

**Don't**  
**Don't Touch Necrosis Moss if in area.**  
**Don't ignore its threats.**
