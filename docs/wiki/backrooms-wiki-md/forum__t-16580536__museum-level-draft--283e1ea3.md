Level Museum???

29 Dec 2023 01:27

Hello, here is my review about your recent idea, you can contact me anytime if you want any critic or help.

**Criticism:**

> Level n is the n+1th Level of the Backrooms.

Here you must put the number of your level, and please do not put "X" back.

---

> There are no known bases, outposts or communities on this level

After this sentence there must be a final period.

---

> Add any entrances and exits to other levels.

Delete this.

---

> The only recorded entrance to level X involve No-Clipping during prolonged interaction with facelings

After this sentence there must be a final period, capitalize the "F" in the word "faceling" and the "L" in the word "level X".

---

> Attempting to steal, tamper with, touch, or in any way disturb exhibits will immediately lead to ejection from Level X

After this sentence there must be a final period.

Level Museum??? (2)

29 Dec 2023 09:48

Hello, here are my reviews but updated.

**Criticism:**

> Level X is a small level consisting of interlocking hallways consistent with the layout of a museum.

Here you have to **bold** the word "Level X".

---

> The only recorded entrance to Level X involve No-Clipping during prolonged interaction with Facelings.

Here you have to **bold** the word "Level X".

---

> Attempting to steal, tamper with, touch, or in any way disturb exhibits will immediately lead to ejection from Level X.

Here you have to **bold** the word "Level X".

If you don't know, you have to put two asterisks between the chosen word, in this case "Level X", and you will automatically see that the word is in **bold**. And the *three bars* work to add a link to an entity, object or level that you have mentioned in your draft.

*Example:*

```
> The only recorded entrance to 
**Level X** involve No-Clipping 
during prolonged interaction with 
[[[Facelings]]].
```
