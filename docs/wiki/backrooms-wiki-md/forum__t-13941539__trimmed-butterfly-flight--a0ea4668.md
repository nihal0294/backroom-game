This was one of the fun ones to write, and it was originally based on "What if I wrote Jerry poetry?" From there, I made a flight of butterflies. I'm sure you can guess the story here, but for reference, here's some spoilers

[spoilers](javascript:;)

[u were warned scrub](javascript:;)

This tale is written in a storytelling format for a reason. In this tale, the story presumes that Jerry  
A. Is a species which died off  
B. Jerry is the last of its kind  
C. Jerry is dead.

The poetry is meant to signify his rise and his inevitable fall, The "Beam of light" is insignificant to describe, because ultimately, all that matters is something came and killed the bird. The poetry doesn't actually tell the way he died, it simply signifies how Jerry came throughout his life.

The other bits are a bit of an in-universe explanation to why you are actually seeing the poems, since the perspective was meant to be read.

In any case, super huge thanks to , who helped me make sure my syllables matched up, made sure it followed proper poetry format, and helped me ensure the proper ideas were engraved into each poem!
