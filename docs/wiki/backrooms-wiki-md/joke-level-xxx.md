**Level XXX** is a very dangerous Level for no explained reason.

# **Description:**

**Level XXX** is an infinite landscape, which is unconfirmed to have an end[1](javascript:;). **Level XXX** has a random type of theme which is common and has relation to the real world. **Level XXX** also features an unusual amount of something entirely random, which needs explanation through a footnote[2](javascript:;). The lack of clinical tone present really shines through, and naturally, the Level is only partially understood, because that draws the reader in more.

There is a high amount of Entities present on this Level[3](javascript:;). These only include things such as [Deathmoths](entity-4.md), [Wretches](entity-15.md), and [Smilers](entity-3.md), because those are the only Entities that people care for anyways. Naturally, you should bring Almond Water whenever visiting this Level, in order to defend yourself from the Entities. Also, there is a completely unique Entity to this Level, called [A Funny and Ambiguous Entity Name, Which Could Mean Virtually Anything](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/joke-entity-xx|). This Entity does something completely unique, which will only be vaguely described in this article[4](javascript:;).

[Almond Water](object-1.md) can be found in high quantities on this Level, to the point where it causes major lore implications. However, we won't go into that, and avoid it by making a very vague entry way[5](javascript:;). Naturally, Almond Water also is rather useful to have, which we will state here, even though it is heavily implied before now.
