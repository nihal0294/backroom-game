New site theme!

29 May 2023 00:33

The site theme just got updated to its newest incarnation: NuLiminal. It comes with some new changes to the visual elements of the site theme.

We will let you know when implementation is complete in this thread. After that, you should come to us about any remaining bugs or suggestions, but until then Technical Team is working behind the scenes to finish fixing all themes!
