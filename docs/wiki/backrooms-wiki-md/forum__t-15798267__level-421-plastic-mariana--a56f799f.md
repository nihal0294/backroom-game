11 Apr 2023 20:48

A veeeeeery hesitant +1. The writing and description is vivid and almost completely clear, with some slip-ups disallowing complete understanding of what the surroundings actually *feel* like. Vocabulary is sometimes strangely chosen. Some SPaG errors exist, but none enough to affect comprehensibility.

My main problem arises in the conclusion and sort of perpetual[1](javascript:;) cliffhanger you've left this on. There's no effective closure to what you've presented here, and its narration is also confusingly strange on that note. I see in comments above that the author is supposed to be ambiguous… but that still doesn't really sit right with me.

The vibe and overall environment you've painted here do make up for these points with some intense heavy lifting, though, so I've left an upvote. It may teeter to a novote or downvote based on future rereading and thoughts.

Footnotes

[1](javascript:;). I'm assuming perpetual at least.
