I have a couple that stand out in my mind for a number of reasons.

- [Level 66](level-66.md). I couldn't sleep one night, so I decided to read on here a bit. Happened to read Level 66. About halfway through, I did a cursory glance around the room, then I turned on the lights.
- [Phenomenon 3](phenomenon-3.md). Good lord, someone needs to put a content warning on that page.
- [Entity 97](entity-97.md). Just the mental image I got from that page of one of them feeding… just no.
- [Entity 123](entity-123.md). Do I even need to explain this one?
- [The Hadal Zone](the-hadal-zone.md). I am deeply thalassophobic.
- [Level 735](level-735.md). This one's got it all. It's an infinite ocean (thalassophobia), one where you're separated from everyone else (monophobia), may be vertically infinite (apeirophobia), possibly operated by some kind of mechanism (submechanophobia, erasophobia), and inhabited by a f\_\_\_ing massive creature of some kind (megalophobia, megalohydrothalassophobia).
