Summary:

The entity 175, also known as "Cameraheads", being tall, humanoid, territorial, solitary and agressive beings which heads were replaced by a head-sized reflex camera. Their way and style of hunting humans is animalistic and "kill" their prey via bright flashlights which hardenly calcify the bones of their victims almost instantly, resulting them in being stuck in their current position like in a "photo"

Entity Idea: Entity 175 - Cameraheads

04 Jul 2021 01:51

Page Type: Entity

Genre (Optional): Horror, Psychological

Page Layout (Optional): There will be no special presentation, design or layout of the page.

Elevator Pitch: The core idea is to introduce an entity from classic horror and psychological horror tales with a twist of creating a "monster" in a hybrid scheme between humanoid and an everyday object.

Central Narrative: None.

Hook/Attention-Grabber: What will stand out is the abstract way of the entity, using anomalous and inexplicable methods and ways on how it acts, how it's using its "tools" to kill and being an observative predator.

Additional Notes: If the idea is too predictable, old, un interesting and/or common. In case of reviewing the sandbox draft, I'd like to focus on if the page is too long, way too detailed for it to be interesting anymore and/or "boringly" written.
