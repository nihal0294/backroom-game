### Answer 1

When writing in Sandbox, you can find your link at the top of the page. (or bottom if on Safari iOS)  
It appears your sandbox is this: <http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/volcanic-torchlight>

You can find the Draft Critiques Forum [Here](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/forum:new-thread/c/6898687), in which you can post that link, along with a description of your page.

### Answer 2

It's best to create only one sandbox, as the Sandbox Website itself has a limited amount of storage. We do allow extra sandboxes, but please keep them to a minimum.
