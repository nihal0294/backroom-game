Yes, you can write this. There are other articles on site that have a backrooms exit. However, while your allowed to write it, you should be asking, "Will the community like this idea enough to not downvote my article into oblivion?"  
On site, there is currently an article being downvoted due to the majority of reader's dislike of the exit to the frontrooms in it. I suggest if you decide to include a backrooms exit, you be careful with execution and not make it too easy to trigger it.  
Edit: Oh, it's an entity…Oh well, similiar rules apply. If this entity banishes you, where would you go/ what would happen to you? I don't recommend having an entity sending people to the frontrooms unless you're aiming for something emotional…
