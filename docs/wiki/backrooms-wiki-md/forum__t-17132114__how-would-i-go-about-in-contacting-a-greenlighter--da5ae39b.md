How would I go about in contacting a greenlighter?

17 May 2025 05:38

I have written a page and received three pieces of critique, over 100 words each. I think it's in its best state at the moment and want to present it to a greenlighter. However, I'm a bit confused about how exactly to do that. Particularly in actually showing the relevant criticism. Do I simply copy and paste the critique and include it in a wikidot message? Do I use screenshots? The greenlight policy page doesn't specify, and it'd be helpful to know so the process goes along smoothly.

You can PM a staff member or greenlighter on wikidot! If you send them a link to the sandbox page, along with either links to or screenshots of the critique you received, they’ll clear the page.

My PMs are open along with many other staff members and greenlighters, so please feel free to reach out!
