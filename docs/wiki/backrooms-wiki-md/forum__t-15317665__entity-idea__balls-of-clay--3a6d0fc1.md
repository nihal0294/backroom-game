Entity Idea: Balls of clay

20 Oct 2022 11:41

These entities would be a shapeshifting[1](javascript:;), childish, floating, balls of clay that wander [Level 0.2](level-0-2.md), [Level 11](level-11.md), and [Level 6.1](level-6-1.md). They are passive and will try to use their shapeshifting ability to entertain any wanderers passing by. If there are multiple present, then they will do a group performance. And that's basically it. This kind of needs expanding so gimme some suggestions pls.

Footnotes

[1](javascript:;). Their forms would keep their clay-like texture.

1. Well no, I'd like them to be like performers who entertain people on safe levels. But if they were to get in contact with a hostile entity (Mysterious monsters on [Level 6.1](level-6-1.md)) or a hostile person, then they would get the heck outta there.

3. Weakness? Well none at the moment, but I would like to think that their child-like nature is sorta one? Idk really.
