A Different Kind of Hell

03 Sep 2022 12:55

**Page Type**: Level

**Genre**: Psychological Horror

**Core Concept**: The level is full of several seemingly abandoned prisons across a wide landscape, and they are the only buildings in the level. Within these prisons are locked cells, all solitary confinement, where wanderers can hear crying, screaming, mumbling, begging, any variation of noise. They are unable to be accessed. Being in the level for too long creates a feeling of prolonged isolation, and causes the wanderer to slowly lose track of time, space, and their own self.

**Additional Notes**: This level is meant to show how being isolated with your own thoughts for so long can drive a person crazy and they can lose every sense of life and self.
