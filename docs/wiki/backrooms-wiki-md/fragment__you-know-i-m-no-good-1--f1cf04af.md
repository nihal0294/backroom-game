# fragment:You Know I'm No Good-1

June 12  
   
Had to sleep in Alexis's room last night. Some dumbass broke his bed— wonder who that could have been.  
   
I'm going to see if I can convince him to go back today. It's time for us to stop dodging the issue. At this rate, we'll never leave.  
   
As fun as that could be, I can't live like that.

That asshole is hogging the shower right now. If he uses all the hot water, I SWEAR I'm going to choke him out. Jesus H. Christ this man has no sense of how to be a courteous roommate.  
   
But look who's talking. Shit.

---

A few hours later, the two find themselves sitting in the lobby of the hotel. The vibe in the air is somewhat lethargic. Spec is lying on his back across Alexis's lap, messing with his phone. Alexis seems barely able to stay awake. Whether it was the serene atmosphere of Level 201, mounting mental tension, or something else, both of them had been completely sapped of energy.

  
Just as Alexis feels himself drifting off, he feels Spec poking him in the cheek.

"Hey. Wake up. I have something to ask you."

*"Ask me? What could he possibly—"* "What do you want now, you parasite?"

Spec smiles thinly, sitting himself up. "Your number. I'm not letting you go *that* easily once we get back."

*"Fuck."* Alexis returns the expression, taking Spec's phone and making a new 'Backdoor' contact. "There you go. Now, I was about to fall asleep, and I'd LIKE to get back to it please."

Spec clicks his tongue disapprovingly, standing up and pulling Alexis along with him.

"Sucks for you then, 'cause we're leaving today. I'm getting some *serious* cabin fever, and you look like you've been one step from tuning out to the VHS effect for at LEAST the past three days. It's time, Alexis."

*"Shit. I'd have liked to have more time alone with him…"* "I suppose you're right. Do you want to leave now, or…"

Spec nods, pulling on the strap of his backpack. "I packed this morning. And I know you don't have anything you need to grab, so…"

Alexis stands up and stretches, looking around the lobby one last time. "All right. Where are we headed?"

"Back to 11." Spec replies, pointing at one of the television sets broadcasting an image of the Capital. The pair walk over, taking one last pause before leaving 201 behind.

"So I guess this is goodbye…" Alexis says quietly, staring at the screen.

"It doesn't need to be." Spec replies. "We can always meet each other again— it's not like we're going to suddenly lose all contact."

*"That would be in your best interest at this point. God, why did I even—"* "Yeah."

The two stare in silence at the flickering image in front of them. Neither really wants to be the first to leave.

Spec finally speaks up. "Goodbye Alexis… I— you know what? Fuck it."

He leans in close, giving Alexis a quick kiss on the cheek.

"See ya."

Spec touches his left hand to the television screen, leaving Alexis alone as he vanishes in a flicker of static.
