Level Paranoia

13 Mar 2025 23:00

**Central Narrative and Description:** The level at which I haven't come up with the physical space yet will be completely safe with no entities or environmental hazards. But instead will induce paranoia and the fear of being watched the longer someone stays in the level. This will continue to grow until the wanderer(s) are so full of fear that they freeze up and are unable to move, eventually leading to starvation. I'm thinking a week is the maximum time.

**Hook and Purpose:** This idea stemmed from how it feels to play peaceful mode in Minecraft. While there are essentially zero dangers other than falling, burning, and drowning, there's still this fear of "What if something is there?" or "I'm alone but feel like I'm not." I think that inherent fear of danger when there is no danger is a cool concept to write on, and as far as I know, this concept isn't written about too often.

**Additional Notes/Remarks:** Any ideas of a space that would feel like there's something there but there isn't? I don't think it should be an open space like a beach or a flower field. Maybe a lightly wooded area or something of the sort.

All thoughts are appreciated!

Re: Level Paranoia

1. The concept is good an creative, but how can it be safe if it has a certain undirect threat "freeze and unable to do anything"? There are 2 options: The first option is that the level is unsafe. And the second is that something like that happens rarely, but for some readers something that happens rare is "boring".  
2. It's not directly about a level but also about a phenomenon which is just okay, even though that the phenomenon like this would be greater in my opinion.  
3. Some readers get bored after just 1 paragraph of reading, so the page as a whole should be written in an interesting way, immediately introducing the concept before explaining it completely. It's just a suggestion but it's still a good thing.  
4. Most of the new writers write a little bit and then get told that the page is unoriginal because it's too short.  
5. Don't forget about the clinical tone in your page, some people think that the page is bad, only because of a personal tone in the page.

Good luck!
