Hey there; quick tip.

After you've made an article, try going over it a few times on your own to check for choppy flow or sentences that do not make sense. Think really deep; if you read this article on the site as a reader, not the author, what would you fix?  
If you are not sure whether or not you need to check over your article a few more times, try reading a greenlit article of the same genre to compare the tone of the author(s)' to yours. For now, I skimmed through the article and I noticed a plentiful amount of SPaG errors.; it's a pretty big issue.

> Level 379 is the Level 380 of the Backrooms. This level was discovered on 03/10/2020, it is quite relaxing for those who visit it, but the curious thing is what is behind the wall.

This is being phased out so if you aren't doing something with Level linearity I recommend removing this.

> Level 379 it consists of a small 30m² beach where the depth of the water is about 3m deep and where the coast is you will find small pieces of stones embedded in the sand of the beach.

- remove "it"
- "where the depth of the water is about 3m deep" change to "with the depth of the water being around 3 meters deep"
- Add a . after deep and remove "and"

> The only thing you will be standing on is beach sand, there is nothing more than a small beach and nothing more since there is a large wall of thin concrete that until now no one has been able to see what is behind the wall, this is because the wall has properties which prevent anyone from crossing the wall, it can cause dizziness, fear of heights and other effects which seem to have no name. Many rumor that there is a hidden area while others say it takes you to another level.

This sentence seems to repeat a few words every once and while "there is nothing more than a small beach and nothing more since there is a large wall of thin concrete".

> The Level 379 is completely silent and the only sounds that can be heard are the sound of the sea on the beach and some sounds of pelicans, but most of the Level 379 is completely silent, there is also a sound that can rarely be heard the which seems to come from the other side of the concrete wall

- Remove "The".
- How can the Level be completely silent and also not be? I recommend removing the completely silent part and say "Level 379 can be completely silent at specific times".
