My Recent Research...

13 Nov 2023 19:39

Many say that Backrooms are NOT real, but maybe they ARE real. Let's see, there is a channel on YouTube where the first videos were from the Backrooms. The creator is from Spain and the places where he records do not seem like they happened in real life, so you could assume that the Backrooms are real but not in the way we imagine them. Before starting, what caught my attention is that the creator said "Backrooms are like a dimension that we cannot see", when I asked him about something.

I collected several things about these videos, so now I put them one by one so there is no confusion:

# **The Entry Way is Different**

Removing everything, it is the same as the noclip but with small differences, the first is to necessarily be in some comfortable place for you in your house, for example: Your bedroom, the kitchen, the living room. Depending on what you recap, there are different parts where you are more likely to enter. The bottom corner, the door and the top corner have 50% - 75%. The wall, a piece of furniture, between a piece of furniture and the wall, a chair and the middle part of a corner have 21% - 49%. Any part of a wall, the floor and the bottom of a door has 10% - 20%.

# **The Design is Different**

The design of each level is different from what we are used to seeing and thinking…

#### **Level 0**

[![flickr:53326124103](https://farm66.static.flickr.com/65535/53326124103_d1ccb9502b_b.jpg)](https://www.flickr.com/photos/199383394@N08/53326124103/)

  
**Level 0** has somewhat narrow hallways, doors, fire extinguishers, small stairs and grates. The floor, instead of being carpet, is made of mosaics, the walls are smooth and the ceiling is flat like a sheet of paper and has no tiles. In one of the videos it was seen that there are pipes above the ceiling, there are also strange rooms with different furniture (I forgot to mention that the sound of the lights is like static).

And the way to exit **Level 0** is to find a door with an "Exit" sign.

#### **Level 1**

[![flickr:53325888671](https://farm66.static.flickr.com/65535/53325888671_1838f18836_b.jpg)](https://www.flickr.com/photos/199383394@N08/53325888671/)

  
**Level 1** is the closest to its wiki version, as it has pillars, rebar, louder fluorescent lights, slightly narrow hallways, and doors. But **Level 1** looks more like a parking lot, although I couldn't get much information about this level, it was seen in another video of **Level 1** that there is an entity in the shape of sticks.

The exit is through another door but without the "Exit" sign, and is located inside the hallways.

#### **Level 2**

I couldn't get much information from **Level 2** since there is no video, but what I know is that it is quite similar in appearance to **Level 2** on the wiki.

The exit is stairs that lead down and lead to **Level 3**.

#### **Level 3**

[![flickr:53326124018](https://farm66.static.flickr.com/65535/53326124018_129c79e999_b.jpg)](https://www.flickr.com/photos/199383394@N08/53326124018/)

  
Firstly, **Level 3** does not have the appearance of an electrical station at all, but it has more of an appearance of a subway station. The lighting is lower, the walls are smooth and orange, the floor is smooth, the hallways wind around in a circular fashion, there are stairs, and the ceiling is weird looking. And at the end of the video there seems to be an encounter with a **Smiler**, although it seems very fake.

The exits from **Level 2** seem to go downwards, since the exit from **Level 3** is through another staircase that leads down and leads to **Level 4**.

There are more levels but I only put four because it is a lot of information, what I also noticed is that they are only from **Level 0** to **Level 10** and the other "extras" are **Level 3999**, **The End** and **The Basement**. But most are different.

# **Entities**

**These entities are well known to many:**

•Smilers.

•Skin-Stealers.

•Hounds.

•Insane.

•Partygoers.

# **Final Warning**

**Do Not Enter In The Backrooms If You Are/Have:**

•Heart Problems.

•Breathing Problems.

•Schizophrenia.

•Autophobia.

•Thalassophobia.

•Sensitive Skin (some levels are very wet and humid).

# **Final Conclusion**

Entering the Backrooms is more different than just running into a wall, also the levels are only **Level 0** to **Level 10** and the others like **Level 3999**, **The End** and **The Basement** are just extras for me. The entities are the simple and best known, the objects are different and the design of each level is also different. It is not known what is under each wall, ceiling or floor, but it is probably pipes and cables. But to finish this investigation of mine I have to say something about one of the videos, which is one on how to make no-clip. The channel is called "Backrooms Scientist" and lastly… Backrooms are quite dangerous…
