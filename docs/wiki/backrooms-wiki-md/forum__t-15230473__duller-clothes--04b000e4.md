**Level 807** is the 808'th Level of the Backrooms.

# **Description**

**Level 807** appears as an infinate clothing store. The store is split into two parts: **Duller Clothes** and **The Warehouse**.

## **Duller Clothes**

**Duller Clothes** is the name of the store and the starting area. It takes the appearance of long isles made of store clothing racks. However the clothes aren't for normal wanderers but are instead for different entities. They vary from hats for [Hounds](entity-8.md), to jeans for [skin stealers](entity-10.md), sunglasses for [Smilers](entity-3.md), etc.

Occasionally you can find changing rooms throughout the first area of the level. These changing rooms seem like average changing rooms with no special properties. The estimated distance of this first area is 20 miles. After 20 miles a door can be found. Going through this door takes you to the next area.

There are alot of entity types in **Duller Clothes** but entities themselves are quite rare. Entities on this level include [Smilers](entity-3.md), [Hounds](entity-8.md), [skin stealers](entity-10.md), [facelings](entity-9.md), [Crawlers](entity-17.md), [Nguithr'xurhs](entity-16.md), [Photoshops](entity-104.md) and [dullers](entity-6.md).

## **The Warehouse**

**The Warehouse** is reached after venturing 20 miles into **Duller Clothes** and finding a door. Through that door is an infinate warehouse that seems to have a ceiling 2 kilometres above the normal height of **Duller Clothes** ceiling. The warehouse is full of Crates with clothes. But instead of just clothes for entities, any type of clothing can be found. There are even reports of stab proof vests and a Skull Mask killer costume from Fear Street.

Although this area has some good clothing it is recommended to keep away from this area at all costs. This is because of the danger **The Security** provides. **The Security** is an Entity exclusive to this level. It manifests itself as a drone with a human eye in the front of it. Attached to the bottom of the drone is a robotic arm that has the ability to extend up to 56 meters. The entity is extremely hostile and if it sees you, it will pick you up with its arm and fly you up to the ceiling. From there it will drop you to your death. Nobody knows what the entity does with the body afterwards.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

## **The fashionable**

- The fashionable are a group of sixteen wanderers who are obsessed with trying on clothes in the level.
- The group doesn't have a set place but is normally seen around changing rooms
- The group is friendly but will become Hostile towards wanderers if said wanderer insults how they look.
- Other than this you can trade with this group if you give them clothes from outside the level. In return they give Almond water and Smiler repellent

# **Entrances And Exits**

### Entrances

- Enter a clothing store on [level 11](level-11.md) called Duller Clothes will lead to **level 807**
- Noclip through a wardrobe on [level 806](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-806) to get here

### Exits

- No clip through the floor to get to [level 1](level-1.md)
- Walking out of the front door leads to [level 11](level-11.md)
- Taking a black or pink Teddy bear will take you to [level 974](level-974.md)
- No clipping through a changing room will lead to the changing rooms

The **Changing Rooms** are a room within the Backrooms located somewhere within [level 807](level-807.md).

# **Description**

The **Changing Rooms** are a room accessed by no clipping into a changing room in [level 807](level-807.md). In total the room is 20 meters by 20 meters.

The room takes the appearance of a corridor with four rooms connected to it. Two rooms are on the left, the other two on the right. The rooms have no front wall or door. They are instead closed off by a curtain.

In each **Changing Room** There is a 8ft tall 4ft wide mirror reaching from floor to ceiling upon the right wall. On the left wall there is a coat rack and there is a bench pressed up against the back wall. On the bench a [Wall Mask](object-24.md) is lying. Each of the four rooms have a different [Wall Mask](object-24.md). It is highly unadvised to pick up, wear or even touch the [Wall Masks](object-24.md).

# **Entrances And Exits**

### Entrances

No clipping into a changing room in [level 807](level-807.md) will lead here

### Exits

Leaving the corridor the way you entered will lead back to [level 807](level-807.md)
