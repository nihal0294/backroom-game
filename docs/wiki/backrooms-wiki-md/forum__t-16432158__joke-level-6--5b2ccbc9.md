Joke Level 6

30 Oct 2023 18:05

Page Type: Joke Level

Genre: Mysterious

Core Concept: There’s this really suspicious level that only a few people really know the truth of, but whenever they try to tell someone, they forget. Nobody really remembers any concrete information, which makes the person assigned the write the article quite upset. People were sent to the level to explore, but when they returned they all died within 30 minutes of Terminal Lucidity and didn’t supply enough information to describe the level.

Additional Information: It’s Joke Level 6 because Post Awareness Stage 6 of Dementia is the final stage. I originally wrote this article a while ago not very seriously, and recently decided to bring it back. Why? I dunno.

The article is written and can be accessed upon asking.
