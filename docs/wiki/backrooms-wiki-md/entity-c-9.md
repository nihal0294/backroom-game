## Habitat: [All](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/how-to-write-an-entity)

# **Description:**

**Backlight Bird** are entities with intelligence similar to that of humans. They are rarely seen in daily life, so their appearance is usually regarded as a symbol of good fortune.

> ##### *"They call us saviors."*

## Behaviors:

**Backlight Bird** and [Deathbird](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-4) seem to be in an adversarial relationship. When a living [Deathbird](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-4) appears and flies toward a target, a **Backlight Bird** will suddenly appear, fly toward it, spread its wings, and hover in front of it. As a result, [Deathbird](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-4) will cancel its target lock and leave reluctantly. Although sometimes the intervention fails, **Backlight Bird** continue to try their best to save lives that have been locked onto from [Deathbird](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-4).

> ##### *"They call us traitors."*

## Biology:

**Backlight Bird** are typically described as ethereal white bird-like creatures with extremely high flight speed.

> ##### *"The [Lord](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-99) has granted us new life."*

### Discovery:

**Backlight Bird** was initially often regarded as a meteor streaking across the sky, but after research on [Deathbird](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-4) gained importance, **Backlight Bird** was also included in the archives.

> ##### *"The [Lord](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-99) has entrusted us with a mission."*

### **Do's and Don'ts:**

### **Do:**

- Protect **Backlight Bird**.

### **Don't:**

- Attack or hinder **Backlight Bird**.

> ##### *"We are the [Lord](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-99)'s most faithful servants."*
