rika's flowery commentary

22 Jul 2021 07:43

This theme is pretty solid, there are a lot of little nitpicks that I have, but it's alright. Not anything too spectacular so my consensus for this is a no vote.

I like the colors used, but not the color palette. The green for the tabs and stuff is really nice, but it doesn't sit well with the background green, which is a bit too vivid in my opinion. This theme looks very similar to the Kalag Institute theme, but what makes the Kalag Institute theme better and one of my favorite themes is the contrast and easy-on-the-eyes color palette. This theme doesn't really have much of that. It's focused around one color with not a lot of variation in the shades, and that makes the elements blend together too much. The blockquotes and the styled quotes and the tabs look so similar that there's nothing that stands out or jumps at me. There's no pop, and so the theme is the visual equivalent of stale toast for me.

The lack of contrast is also apparent in the text, especially with the monospace font. The fact these fonts are all serif fonts with a thin style, in addition to the darker green and the lack of complementary contrast outside the article, makes the text harder on the eyes to read. I really like the bolded version of the font used in the image block, but unbolded it isn't appealing to the eye.

The monospace font and the cursive font don't really fit well with the base form. The monospace font is too thin and clunky to read (just look in the theme code block; it's almost dizzying) and the cursive font feels very out of place with the rest of the theme. This is because everything about your theme panders to a uniform style. The colors are all shades of green, the elements are similar, everything is rectangular and perfectly arranged, there's a lot of 90 degree angles in your theme, and even the other fonts that you used are very rectangular. Add in extremely slanted, rounded cursive letters for the headers and you got something that sticks out like a sore thumb, and in a distracting and confusing manner. I feel that it would be a lot better if the header font was rectangular like the rest of the theme.

One thing that I can say is that I really like the inclusion of survival difficulty classes for this specific theme. I thought that was a nice touch. The color of your links is also pretty solid.

The least favorite thing about this theme by far is the top menu. On my phone, the photo is extremely squashed, and on my computer, the theme is extremely stretched. It does not look realistic nor appealing, and it conflicts really badly with the top bar's text. The cursive subtitle is especially hard to read against the white flower. It also sticks out much like the cursive does in that it's so organic in composition in comparison to the monotone uniformity of the rest of the theme. It feels very out of place, and I think that a solid color (preferably dark) would fill the gap much more effectively.

Overall, I feel like this theme is pretty bland, and it doesn't do anything new or interesting. It doesn't feel like a lot of work or care was put into this, and it feels like a rehashing of the Kalag Institute theme and the base Liminal one. I can liken it to a piece of stale toast for how interesting it is. That being said, it's stale, not soggy or moldy, so if I saw articles using this theme, I'd be able to put aside my misgivings and enjoy the article by itself. Using this theme for an article would not detract from it, but it wouldn't be any different eye candy-wise from just rolling with the basic theme. This is why I'm giving this theme a no vote for now.
