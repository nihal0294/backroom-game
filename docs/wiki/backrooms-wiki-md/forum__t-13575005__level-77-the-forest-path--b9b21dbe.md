Level 77 - "The Forest Path"

22 Jul 2020 23:53

**Page Type** : Level

**Elevator Pitch** : A mysterious level with multiple paths for theories, from how it was discovered, what causes specific phenomena in it, to why parts of it behave as they do. What I believe to be a great level has substance, ability for expansion, and a solid overarching idea, which I believe this level has.

**Central Narrative** : The big part regarding the story is the location, This level takes place in an infinite forest with a path of dirt and leaves that stretches far through it, possibly forever. However the substance of the story is how people react to it, going too far down the path causes even a large group of people to turn back for seemingly no reason, attempting to leave the path causes instant sanity loss, getting far enough away from the path allows the individual to stop feeling the sanity loss and be able to explore the forest in peace. Far enough out from the path, there's more to see and experience, lakes of Almond Water, cabins which had been recently abandoned with fire pits still smoldering, but not a single entity, or an animal for that matter, *not even a mosquito.*

**Hook/Attention-Grabber** : What I think is unique is the individual experiences and the creeping feeling that something isn't known about this place, but it's just so close. Just like a forest, you can be so close to the truth, but it can be just right out of sight.

**Additional Notes** : Perhaps the stories within the level can be enhanced before becoming a full level.
