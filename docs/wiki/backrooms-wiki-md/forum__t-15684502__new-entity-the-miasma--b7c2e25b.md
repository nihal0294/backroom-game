New Entity - The Miasma

23 Feb 2023 10:44

The miasma is an entity composed mostly of a thick, black vapour emanating from a sickly looking humanoid at it’s center. The vapour creates hallucinations in wanderers and other entities, luring them deeper into the miasma. The vapour is also strongly acidic and will slowly digest anything within it. Cameras are not affected by hallucinations and thus are a reliable way to tell what is real or not.

24 Feb 2023 13:54

- How are illusions created:  
The Miasma learns over time which illusions work best on which creatures (E.g for an entity, it might start making human like illusions which the entity would be drawn to attack) Most is learned through trial an error, Miasmas that don’t succeed or adapt will be killed by other entities.

- Can the illusions be interacted with physically:  
No, they are created by the inhalation of the fog and thus are not actually there

- Do the illusions react dynamically:  
The miasma can strengthen the potency of the illusions, this makes them more clear. However this requires lots of energy and thus only used as a last resort (E.g The Miasma’s illusions are not working and it is about the be killed by another entity) this also increases the potency of the acid.

- What are the physical abilities of the humanoid  
The humanoid at the centre is frail and thus cannot put up much strength, thus it hides behind it’s illusions. However, it is relatively fast, being quicker than the average human. The miasma also acts as a sort of sensory device for the humanoid, if something is in the miasma, it can tell and will move away from the danger.

- What if wanderers meet the humanoid before digestion  
As said, the humanoid will use it’s speed to keep distance from any wanderers in the miasma. However, if the humanoid is somehow cornered and/or interacted with before digestion it will attack as a last ditch attempt to save itself though this is ineffective due to it’s frail build but can take wanderers by surprise.

- Does rain effect the miasma  
No, the rain is not enough to neutralise it’s strong acid. However, strong winds are effective at dispersing the miasma faster than the humanoid can produce it.

- What does the miasma dissolve specifically  
Flesh, bone, cloth, plastic, wood, some types of soft metals. The levels themselves are even damaged due to the sheer acidity of the miasma. Some of these will take longer to dissolve than others. It will be pretty much try to dissolve anything it touches.

- What materials are more or less affected.  
Steel and other commercially used metals are effective at not being dissolved, even materials that can be dissolved, such as wood and plastic, will still take some time to dissolve. Flesh and cloth are the most effective. Due to it being inhaled it can damage internal tissues very quickly. Eyes are also susceptible and many will go blind before death.

- The Miasma appears around the entity due to the fact it is produced by it. It will slowly spread out over time and is hard to disperse.
