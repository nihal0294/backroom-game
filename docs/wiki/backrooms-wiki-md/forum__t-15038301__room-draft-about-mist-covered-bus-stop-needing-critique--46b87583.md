Hi Total\_Travisty!

Wow, I like this! I'm gonna cover the SPaG first, then the actual level.

Every so often, you missed, or should I say, "mist" a few periods, capitals, the usual stuff. The spelling looked good, but even though the majority of your article is in a very clinical tone, you may want to hold back a *little* on the "storytelling".

Now, the good stuff! Tbh, this was amazing! You definitely started off strong, with a highly detailed piece of art, but that feeling did begin to fade. After mentioning the bus, me being who I am, began to felt kind of bored. This article was very very short, so I think you could elaborate further, but the simple design of the level is well-done, as this is hard to pull off.

If you have questions/concerns or have updated the page, feel free to Pm me anytime! Happy writing!
