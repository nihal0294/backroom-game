I don't like what I made but thanks for taking the time to translate it here

01 Mar 2025 13:39

I don't really like what I made, although it's a year old now (actually a year and a half wow), so it's not really surprising.

The tone isn't wonder-y enough, really. Outside of the Kelvin quotes, it just feel very cold, which goes against what it was supposed to be. It was meant to be something personnal and more poetic, but the presence of clinical concepts and typical phrases breaks it It's less present in french because of the vocabulary I used there (even if still an issue), but in english it's far more blatant because the way the sentences are translated stripes away the few wonder there is because of the vocabulary and sentence structure (english writing marvel with a different style than in french in my opinion). I don't wanna bother on that but at the same time I think it's worth noting.

The concept is kinda wonky and should have been tied together more. I wanted to make a space cathedral and I didn't put enough concept-wise. It's basically just "this place has stories and people see what they think is canon yay" in a very redundant way (hey did you see there are lore statues? And lore mosaics! And lore constellations!"

It's a "good" example of a page I wrote because I wanted to write about a space cathedral (warhammer spaceship vibe) and didn't really manage to make its "canon-breaking" concept interesting both in worldbuilding or format. It's a remnant of when I was less experienced and I do value it as a showcase of how much I learned and will (hopefully) continue to learn.

Don't get me wrong, I highly appreciate the time and effort you took to translate it, and I wouldn't have critted it otherwise, but I still wanna clear things up as the author about what I think of it in retrospect.
