06 Feb 2024 23:52

Hello there! I am new to this wiki and I already came up with an idea for an entity. I already wrote down the entire article in sandbox but I wanna know if it was a good idea to begin with. My idea is an entity made up entirety of ice cream or an ice cream-like material that manifests into Explorer Colonies. They are very small, around the size of a basketball and have tiny eyes. And what they do, is that whenever every Human in the area goes to sleep, they will raid the food storage and consume all the food while the Humans are sound asleep. I call them 'Swirlies' because it fits them, and it is a cute name for them. There is much more that I wrote down that is worth mentioning, but the main idea is that they mainly chill (No Pun Intended) with Humans until Night strikes where they consume all the food.
