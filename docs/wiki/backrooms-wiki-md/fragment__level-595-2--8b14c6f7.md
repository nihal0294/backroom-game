# The Euclid Device

This device was created with the sole purpose of manipulating the environment of the Backrooms. The main achievement of the device was creating an [infinite apartment](level-314.md) out of nothing. Any geometric landscape can be programmed into the device, and when activated, it will create that landscape in the desired location.

  
The Euclid Device Protection Program, or E.D.P.P. for short, is a failsafe program in place to stop the backrooms from flooding. This program was implemented as a secondary defense mechanism in case the Dam reconstruction Program in **Level 595** fails, and the dam breaks.

The way the E.D.P.P. works is that it utilizes the Euclid Device to create a no-clipping zone that spans directly in front of The Blue Channel. This no-clipping zone leads back into the lake, which, in the event of flooding, would cause the water to eternally fall back into itself, effectively saving the Backrooms.

The M.E.G. still has the Backrooms Remodeling Co. working on revitalizing the dam, so part of the E.D.P.P. involves a laser sensor that detects micro movements in the dam. The sensor is engineered to detect any eternal movements that occur in The Dam and compute if it's the dam breaking. If it senses that the dam is breaking, the sensor initiates an alarm that tells everyone in Level 595 to evacuate.

## The Proposition of the E.D.P.P.

### Diplomatic Meeting — M.E.G./Boveta

---

**Excerpt of an Audio/Video Transcript from the main meeting room in the Bovetan National Congress.**

**AUDIO/VIDEO LOG**

---

**DATE:** FEB 12th, 2025  
**MEMBERS PRESENT:** M.E.G. Diplomat Mitch Buvare, President Pottinger  
**TOPIC:** The E.D.P.P.  
**LOCATION:**

![A large, primarily tan meeting room full of hundreds of chairs facing the middle of the room.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-595/Boveta%20Meeting%20Room)

The main meeting room of the Bovetan National Congress, the table used in this specific meeting is seen in the middle of the room.

---

**»BEGIN LOG«**

*[Video captures Mitch Buvare, sporting black dress pants and a blue dress shirt, walking into the meeting room. He scans the empty room until he sees President Pottinger sitting at a table in the middle of the room. President Pottinger gestures for Buvare to approach the table. The president himself is wearing a black three-piece suit, dark red tie, and has a Boveta flag lapel pin on his left suit collar.]*

**Mitch Buvare:** It's an honor to finally travel to Boveta and meet you. I wasn't expecting you to conduct your diplomacy personally, but it's a very pleasant surprise!

*[Buvare reachs aross the table and shakes President Pottingers hand]*

**President Pottinger:** The honor is all mine, and yes, I like to personally meet the people I do business with.

**President Pottinger:** Please, let us sit.

*[President Pottinger and Buvare take a seat directly across from each other]*

**Mitch Buvare:** So first, I'd like to hear about why you called me over here.

**President Pottinger:** Yes, the solution to your problem with **Level 595**. My scientists do a better job explaining than I do.

*[President Pottinger pushs a folder labled "E.D.P.P." towards Buvare. Buvare opens the folder and takes 3 minutes to read the contents.]*

**Mitch Buvare:** Do you think it will work?

**President Pottinger:** I have unbreakable faith in my research team. It'll work.

**Mitch Buvare:** If you have confidence in them, I'll just have to follow suit because this is our last hope. After Mr. Cuttler went rogue, we've been grasping at straws.

**President Pottinger:** I read the report you emailed to us, and I have to ask, what caused him to commit those horrible acts?

**Mitch Buvare:** From my sources, he had complaints with the safety of the Reconstruction Program as a whole, and M.E.G. ignored his pleas. I think that, coupled with his prior hatred of the Backrooms Remodeling Co., caused him to go off the deep end.

**Mitch Buvare:** If you were to ask me, I fully agree with his complaint. The Program is very dangerous to everyone in the level, but M.E.G. believes it's a necessary evil to fix the dam.

**President Pottinger:** I hope that our Program can fix your issue since the Reconstruction Program will become fully futile once the no-clip zone is active.

**Mitch Buvare:** I'll talk to the higher-ups at M.E.G. and try to have your program be the only one active, if it works.

**Mitch Buvare:** Well, I don't know what else to say but thank you.

*[Buvare stand up, President Pottinger does the same.]*

**President Pottinger:** No thanks needed, I simply want to keep my state and the other people inhabiting this labyrinth safe.

*[President Pottinger shakes Buvares hand.]*

**Mitch Buvare:** What do we owe you for the Program?

**President Pottinger:** Oh, don't talk like that. Like I said, I simply want people to be safe, and your compensation by offering us the trees in Level 595 is more than enough.

**Mitch Buvare:** Thank you again, Mr. President, and I wish you the best of luck with your growing state!

**President Pottinger:** Thank you for trusting us, and safe travels back home.

*[Buvare walks back up to the National Congress meeting room entrance and leaves through the far left door. President Pottinger can be seen sitting in the same meeting chair as before, lost in thought.]*

**»END LOG«**

*[Ruby's bodycamera is turned on. He is in warehouse 4, standing behind six technicians who are holding the laser sensor on their shoulders. **Police Chief Henry Calibri:** Alright, lets move out.

**Officer Ruby** Bovetan Police, put your hands up!

*[The figure with their back facing Ruby throws their hand in the air. Ruby approaches the figure, grabbing their arms and handcuffing them. He spins the figure around, revealing that it is Sam Cuttler. Ruby grabs his radio]*

**Officer Ruby:** Clo, find the Chief, and get over here. I found a camp, and the suspect the M.E.G. warned us about.

*[Ruby places Cuttler on the ground and explores the camp while waiting for Clo and the Chief. He starts off by searching the tent, finding nothing but personal belongings. **Police Chief Henry Calibri:** Whats going on here Ruby?

**Officer Ruby:** The subject M.E.G. warned us about was spying on us, so I followed him to this camp and found this.

**Police Chief Henry Calibri:** What were you planning on doing with this number of explosives?

**Police Chief Henry Calibri:** I'm guessing you were going to blow a part of the dam up again, weren't you? And judging by the number of explosives here, it would have most likely knocked out the entire thing.

*[Cuttler stays silent]*

**Police Chief Henry Calibri:** Fine then, you want to play the silent game? We'll see how silent you'll stay when you get to the detention center. Ruby, Clo, grab him!

**Officer Ruby:** Should we inform M.E.G. that we have their missing employee?

**Police Chief Henry Calibri:** No, what they don't know won't hurt them. Besides, we could get a lot of information out of this man regarding the M.E.G.

*[Ruby, Clo, and Cief Calibri walk Cuttler into the no-clipping zone and once inside the warehouse Ruby turns off his bodycam]*

### **Post Arrest**

---

The dam was checked after the arrest, and at multiple points, explosives had been placed at the base of it. All of the evidence found in the campsite was collected by the Bovetan Police and is being kept in an evidence storage unit.

Sam Cuttler was sentenced to life in jail on charges of a universal explosive threat and first-degree murder.

Hello, Mr. Pottinger,

I just wanted to reach out and thank you again for the E.D.P.P. I also wanted to say that Sam Cuttler has not been sighted in weeks, and I hope it stays that way. Whether he is still in hiding here or if he left the level after hearing about the E.D.P.P., only time will tell.

The last thing I want to address is the safety issues we discussed in our meeting. M.E.G. agreed to end the Reconstruction Program, but the BRC refused to leave the level because they wanted to "finish their best remodeling project yet". Because of this, M.E.G. has decided to keep the program to make sure no one gets injured by the BRC. The good news is that they have made major safety changes to ensure that no one else, both within and outside The M.E.G., gets hurt. Changes include:

- Having volunteers work in separate groups from the BRC.
- Volunteers now work at least 2 Km away from all BRC employees.
- The M.E.G. has a perimeter around all BRC workspaces and bases that disallows any non-BRC employees from entering.
- M.E.G. members are not allowed to get more than 15m from any BRC employee.

This is a big step for M.E.G. Boveta relations, and I hope we can continue this healthy diplomacy. I can't wait to visit Boveta again; it truly is a beautiful city.

Grateful,  
**Mitch Buvare**
