SEEKING GREENLIGHT: A Large Church

01 Mar 2022 03:13

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre (Optional):** Wonder

**Core Concept:**  
This level resembles a large Church with a lot of benches, there are also some sculptures of angels, the cross in the ceiling, and stained glass with a lot of religious drawings (Which would probably be the "Windows" entity).  
There’s almond water on the holy water fonts, You get into the next level by praying or confessing sins,  
Some entities that would appear here would resemble angels/fallen angels or some other religious figures.

Additional Notes: I think that this level would be interesting because the backrooms are about liminal places that are familiar for some reason, and since there are a lot of religious people in the world then a place like this would be very familiar to some people. and the large church with a lot of benches would probably work.

I hope this doesn't make me look like I'm a Religious Extremist (I'm not) and I'm trying to make Christian propaganda. it's just that I think this concept of a large church with a lot of benches would work as a level of the backroom since it can look very uncanny (especially at night) and also because of themes like the exorcist.

If the concept of a modern church doesn't work then probably I'll make the one of an old gothic church

Re: SEEKING GREENLIGHT: A Large Church

01 Mar 2022 03:34

Honestly, I don't like this idea much. There isn't anything liminal about religion itself, this is just a church with some Backrooms entities and objects. Unless you're *extremely* competent in your abilities to describe the space, it's most likely going to come off as lazy. I'd encourage you to look deeper into religion if you wanted that to be a part of your level, there's a lot you could take from it that could be implemented into a level without feeling shoehorned in there. Currently, a Backrooms church doesn't sound too appealing.
