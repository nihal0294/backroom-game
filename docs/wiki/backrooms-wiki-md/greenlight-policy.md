# Greenlight and Posting Policy

This page will explain our wiki's critique guidelines and the process of posting a page; please read the entire thing carefully before attempting to contribute to the site. If you have further questions, please take them up with [staff](meet-the-staff.md), open a ticket on Discord, or raise your questions in the discussion section!

---

# Greenlight Policy

**There has been a major change to the greenlighting system.** Due to the removal of posting restrictions, the greenlight system has been revamped to only affect new authors and how they interact with our critique ecosystem. **While posting may be open to with a successful article, it is still highly recommended that you use our critique system to its fullest before posting to the site!**

### *So what's a greenlight?*

If a new author would like to use our draft critique resources[1](javascript:;), then they must receive two greenlights on their concept in the Idea Forum on Discord. These greenlights are a stamp of approval on the concept, showing that the author has put sufficient thought into the structure, theme, and literary value of the piece—thus allowing them to move on to the next phase of writing/critique: drafting.

### *How do I get a greenlight?*

The first step is to come up with a concept for your page, then make a thread on the Idea Forum with the tag "Seeking Greenlight". When making your thread, please use the pinned thread format while keeping the information succinct and relevant. Reviewers will likely ask follow-up questions to better understand your concept; this will allow you to fill in any extra information that may be pertinent to the concept. If you need help with the ideation process, check out the [Guide Hub](guide-hub.md).

[+ Idea Thread Format](javascript:;)

[– Idea Thread Format](javascript:;)

> **Concept Title:** [The name of your idea; this should include the page type.]
>
> **Sensitive Content:** [Specify mature themes. Optional.]
>
> **Central Narrative:** [A 1-3 sentence summarization. Describe the major points without focusing on little details. What is the story you're trying to tell?]
>
> **Brief Description:** [A brief description of this work, or an excerpt. Include some of the minor details here without going too in-depth with them. This space should mostly focus on the actual physical aspects without touching on subtext too much.]
>
> **Hook:** [Describe what makes your concept different. Why should the reader care about this idea as opposed to any of the other work here? What will make this memorable?]
>
> **Purpose:** [Describe what you want your concept to do. What emotions do you want your reader to feel? How does the work build onto the setting of the Backrooms? What other pages will they view differently after reading this? Optional.]
>
> **Additional Notes/Remarks:** [A place to bring up miscellaneous things. Questions for the reviewer, sources of inspiration, and so on. Optional.]

### *Who can greenlight my concept?*

Currently, three types of members may greenlight your concept.

**Greenlighters & Greenflame Trialees**: These members of the GLMP[2](javascript:;) have the ability to "double greenlight" a concept, allowing the author to move directly to the drafting phase without the need for a second greenlight.

**Star Critics**: Any member of the Crit Crew who has received the star critic role may greenlight ideas; they can easily be identified by their "Star Critic" role on Discord.

**Authors**: Any author that has written and posted three successful pages may greenlight a concept thread; they will have the "author" role on Discord.[3](javascript:;)

---

Once a new author has received the required greenlights, they will receive the "Forum Access" role, which will allow them to use the Draft Forum on Discord.

---

> #### Role of Critics:
>
> The Crit Crew is a non-staff team of critics that are a core part of the page posting process, and it's important for aspiring authors to understand that. Although not all of them can greenlight your idea, it is almost always recommended to run your writing through them before you contact a Greenlighter.

#### Role of Greenlighters:

The GLMP is the official non-staff team responsible for critique, as well as other site quality related matters. They work directly with the Crit Crew in order to ensure as many authors are helped as possible. Additionally, they have the ability to "double greenlight" your idea so that you may move to the drafting forums without a second greenlight.  
  
For a list of our current Greenlighters, check out the ["Meet the Greenlighters" page](meet-the-greenlighters.md).

> #### Role of Authors:
>
> The role of authors both new and experienced, is to not only write entries, but to actively engage in the critique process, reading of newly posted pages, and voting on those pages. Our critique ecosystem is reliant on those aspects just as fully as the writing, put simply, our site cannot maintain a healthy critique environment and a good level of site quality without your participation.

# Page Posting

  
  
This section will explain the basic process for posting a new article to the wiki. When posted, your article will subject to peer review and will be at the mercy of our [Deletion Policy](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/deletion-policy). Once again, **it is highly recommended that you use our critique system to its fullest prior to posting a page**—while it may not guarantee a successful page, it will greatly increase the possibility of having one.
> If you are posting a rewrite please contact staff before posting, the old page will need to be archived before you post your page.

### *My idea was greenlit and I finished my draft, can I post my page?*

That depends, if you have not posted a sucessful page before then you have one more step—if you have you may want to skip to the next section.

**Important Info for New Authors:**

If you are a new author you must have your draft "cleared" before you may post to the wiki proper. *What's a clear?* A clear is an affirmation by a member of Staff/GLMP that you have received **three critiques** of at least ~**100 words** on your draft. Once confirmed, you will be given the password to post your page.

**This is by no means a recommendation that this is the total amount of critique you should receive, this should be thought of as the bare minimum.**

### *I've gotten critique/cleared and I'm ready to post. What's next?*

First you will need to find where your page will be posted; you can do this in two ways:

- Go to the Level/Entity/Object/Phenomenon list and find your corresponding slot.

- Directly type the slug into your URL.[4](javascript:;) For unnumbered, GOI hubs, tales, and other specialty pages, this is the method you will need to use.

That will bring you to a page that looks like this:

[![first](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/greenlight-policy/first/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/greenlight-policy/first)

Scroll down until you find this section:

[![second](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/greenlight-policy/second/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/greenlight-policy/second)

Now you will need to input the posting password; this can be found in the "Info" channel on the wiki's Discord. The password may change unexpectedly, so refer to this channel if the one you know doesn't work.

It will then give you a second input bar in the same space:

[![third](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/greenlight-policy/third/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/greenlight-policy/third)

Here you will input the URL slug for your page; if you need help or are unsure of what your slug should be, please ask staff or a greenlighter for help.

Then you just paste your draft and update the title[5](javascript:;) and save your submission. Any images you'd like to use must be saved directly to the wiki using the "File" option at the bottom of your page; further details on image use can be found in the [Image Use Policy](image-use-policy.md). Then double-check your submission using the format check found at the bottom of this page, and don't forget to add your tags.[6](javascript:;)

### *Is that it? Is my page on the wiki now?*

While it is on the wiki, it is currently in the "submission" category; staff still needs to review and move your page to the wiki proper. Before staff moves your pages a few things will be checked:

- The final format check (found below), which includes any formatting requirements.

Ai check: Ai use in writing or images is **strictly forbidden**, any suspected use will be reviewed by the Disciplinary Team, and those guilty of using it will be banned.

- Tech Check: Any new or extensive technical work will need to be reviewed by the Techlighters to ensure they comply with the site's [Tech Policy](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/tech-policy)

Once these have been addressed, the page will be moved out of submission and onto the wiki proper, where others will be able to interact with your work.

# Final Format Check

Part 1 - Final Format Check

# Ai Check

Use either [Copyleaks](https://copyleaks.com/ai-detector) or [GPTzero](index.md) when checking. Other AI checkers are too unreliable and should be avoided. Don't copy the page formatting, however, as it can create false positive.

Images should also be glanced at if possible in case one of them looks AI-generated.

# Rating Module

Pages should not directly use [[module rate]]. They should instead use [component:rate](component__rate-bar--1983f524.md) or derivatives of this component (for example, [component:open/close](component__open--798a671f.md)), as this allows Technical Team and Maintenance Team to change things about all the rating modules at once.

All pages need a rating component somewhere, but it can be put anywhere in it.

# Image Blocks

Moving forward, pages should not use the [[image]] base wikidot module, but instead using the [wiki image block component](component__image-block--eb47f2b7.md) (the image block guide can be found [here](component__image-block-example--c4e93ead.md))

Most [[image]] wikidot modules can be replaced using the following parameters:

[[include component:image-block  
|name=  
|alt=  
|width=100%  
|align=center  
|captionless=a  
]]

All images should also have an alt text so that they can be read by screenreaders. Alt texts are one or two sentences explaining what the image looks like. This is NOT an in-universe explanation of its context.

- For example, Level 0 original image would have the following alt text *"Yellow segmented hallways, with fluorescent lighting."*

# Backlinks

No hardcoded wiki link (ex: [https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-0](level-0.md)) should be used outside of offset links.

This also include links to pages that aren't translated yet, or haven't been written yet. For example, a Level 200-FR link should still be [Level 200-FR](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/niveau-200-fr) and not [Level 200-FR](https://fr-backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/niveau-200-fr).

# Footer Format

First of all, the **license box has to be present** even if there are no other pieces of media used in the page, as it shows users how to cite the page. It should be at the **VERY BOTTOM OF THE PAGE**, since it's meant to look as if it's a part of the UI, below the page content.

It should be laid out like:

- FOOTNOTE BLOCK (use component:footnotecol if more than 7 footnotes)
- ---- (horizontal rule)
- WIKIWALK NAV (If applicable. Necessary for numbered pages)
- LICENSE BOX

An author collapsible could be placed between the horizontal rule and the wikiwalk nav, though the info button on the rating module should be recommended instead.

**Check that the license box only has a ===== sign on the top and bottom, NOT between each blockquote and also that there is nothing other than blockquotes within the ==== part.**

# Licensing Checkup

Two things should be checked in the licensebox:

- The licensebox format
- The licensing itself

### Licensebox Format

The licensebox follow the following format. It has the ===== even if it has no images.

- "Filename:" is specifically for the name of the file as it is uploaded on the wiki
- "Name:" is the specifically for the name of the image as it appears on the source. If the source is discord, or a sandbox site, it should be the name of the file.

This is the correct format:

[[include :backrooms-wiki:component:license-box]]  
=====  
> \*\*Filename: (Optional)\*\*  
> \*\*Name:\*\*  
> \*\*Author:\*\*  
> \*\*License:\*\*  
> \*\*Source Link:\*\*  
> \*\*Derivative of:\*\* (If applicable)  
> \*\*Additional Notes:\*\* (Optional)

> \*\*Filename: (Optional)\*\*  
> \*\*Name:\*\*  
> \*\*Author:\*\*  
> \*\*License:\*\*  
> \*\*Source Link:\*\*  
> \*\*Derivative of:\*\* (If applicable)  
> \*\*Additional Notes:\*\* (Optional)  
=====  
[[include :backrooms-wiki:component:license-box-end]]

- **If images have been edited to add something on them** (a black box on a face, a collage…) it should be handled as a derivative: the new image is licensed, with the original licensed in "Derivative of:" using this format:

> \*\*Derivative of:\*\* [link of the original Title of the Original] by Author of the original licensed under License of the original

For example:

> \*\*Derivative of:\*\* [https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Noun-handshake-3720225.png Noun-handshake-3720225.png] by Vectorstall licensed under [https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/deed.en CC BY-SA 4.0], [https://backrooms-sandbox-2.wdfiles.com/local--files/trailmixncocoa-meg-theme/IMG\_3130.PNG M.E.G. Logo] by Kosef Sturre licensed under [https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/ CC BY-SA 3.0]

- **If the image has been edited but nothing was added**, the changes are listed under "Additional notes:"

For more information, please refer to the [Licensebox component page](component__license-box--969e0688.md).

### File Licensing

All images should be creative commons compliant. Usable licenses are all listed in the [Image use guide](image-use-policy.md)

Remember that the Pixabay, Pexels and Unsplash licenses are all NOT ALLOWED on the wiki.

- Unsplash images uploaded after 5 June 2017 CC compliant and are unable to be used due to license changes.
- Pixabay images uploaded after January 2019 are not CC compliant and are unable to be used due to license changes.
- Pexels images uploaded after July 2018 CC compliant and are unable to be used due to license changes.

In addition to this, the only allowed AI image generator is [thisperondoesnotexist](index.md). Images generated by it fall under public domain.

Part 2 - Moving Pages out of Submissions

Before checking anything, make sure the page has been cleared or greenlit! You can check in the [#greenlights](https://discord.com/channels/695664941247627274/828153749464809492) discord channel.

**Warning! The submission moving should be done by a different person than the one who greenlit/cleared to ensure at least two people checked the parameters.**

For translations, check the Backrooms INT staff server in the translation greenlights channel.

# AI Check

Use either [Copyleaks](https://copyleaks.com/ai-detector) or [GPTzero](index.md) when checking. Other AI checkers are too unreliable and should be avoided.

# Quick Format Check

To make sure the format check has been followed, check the following parameters again (you can see how to do so in part 1 above)

- Rate module block
- Image module block
- Backlinks
- Footer format
- Licensing

# Verify Title Format

For numbered categories (levels, entities, objects and phenomena, including sub-pages and unnumbered pages), the format should be **Category XXX - "Title"**. An example can be found in the table below:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Right | Level 184 - "Field of Forgotten Forts" |
| Wrong | Level 184 |
| Wrong | Field of Forgotten Forts |
| Wrong | Level 184: "Field of Forgotten Forts" |
| Wrong | Level 184 — "Field of Forgotten Forts" |

Instead of **Category XXX**, unnumbered pages use "Unnumbered Category", for example:

- Unnumbered Level - "The Frontrooms"

If a page has an in-canon reason for why the title should look different, you can use a meta title:

[[div class="meta-title"]]  
Displayed Page Title  
[[/div]]

The actual page title can be hidden using the following code:

[[module css]]  
/\* Every page technically needs a title! This hides it. \*/  
#page-title { display: none !important; }  
[[/module]]

# Image Links

All images/files should be uploaded on the page, and these links should be used on the page (no discord, imgur or other places' image links). Additionally, image file size cannot exceed 1mb. If an image is too big, you can use the [Image Resize Widget](component__image-resize-widget--f10e2ed5.md) to bring it below the size limit.

# Tags

Once it is certain that all images are CC, tag the page with \_cc and \_licensebox, as well as \_title if the title has been checked. For additional tags, the author should put them on the page before it is moved away from submissions. Ask the author if the page hasn't been tagged or hasn't been tagged properly (for example if non-existent tags have been used, of it no category tags have been put).

Make sure the following tags are present:

- Category tags (required) (level, entity…)
- Sub-category tag (sub-section, unnumbered level…)
- Genre tag (romance, action…)
- Character/setting/group/canon/setting (nyx, level-5, ariane-circle, multiverse-canon…)
- SD tag (sd-class-5…)

# Add Title to the Main List

If the page is a numbered article, put the title of the page in its designated spot.

In the case of a group without a tag/canon without a tag, put in in the "other" list of its respective hub (the list is in alphabetical order)

In the case of a POI/group with a tag/canon with a tag, ask the author to add it on its respective hubs with the needed blurbs.

### Then move the page out of submissions and let the author know so that they can make an announcement in the page-announcements channel.
