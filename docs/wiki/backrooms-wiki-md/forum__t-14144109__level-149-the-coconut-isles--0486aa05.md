**Idea**  
Creativity: 8/10  
*Surreal in nature, creative in pure concept. The idea of the whole place of moon gravity space islands on a bunch of wind is unique to say the least.*  
Expansion on the idea: 5/10  
*I feel like more could've been done with this idea. What if drones were sent out past the barrier? What if the islands could split apart; after all, what is holding them together exactly? Do birds travel towards the barrier?*  
Intrigue: 6.5/10  
*Intrigue is built with the community of Lost Suns and the Barrier — Which is why I wanted to see some more information about it — but there isn't really anything to build off a sense of story or wonder on here. It's essentially a base for people to write for rather than a story that builds up a sense of feeling to explore and the such on its own.*  
Worldbuilding: 6/10  
*The islands and the life on them could definitely use some more character. How do people get from island to island? How is the culture of the Lost Suns influenced by the fact that they are essentially trapped in this level forever? Are there any other settlements in this level other than that one tribe? I'd assume there would be a form of overpopulation as well if no one can exit this level, or there would be more M.E.G. outposts/bases.*

---

**General Writing:**  
Execution: 9/10  
*Wonderfully executed as a level. Followed a clean and concise description which was easy and fun to read through, which was as much as was needed to be done with such a concept. Nothing much to say here.*  
SPaG: 5/10  
*The sentences have a fair amount of periods and the such, which breaks the flow of the article up a lot due to how short and stout it sounds. There are also some minor spelling mistakes, but it is overall ok in this regards.*  
Structuring and formatting: 9/10  
*Seeing as the Barrier is such a small part of the article, I doubt it needed to have a designated header. Other than that, there isn't much I have to nitpick on.*

**Did I enjoy reading this?**  
Yes.

Overall verdict:  
It's the average Ferra level. Nothing too groundbreaking, but it's not bad either. It can stand on its own well, but it leaves a bit to be desired; it is essentially a blank canvas upon which one can build their stories on. Seeing as it is the first of your group's GOIcon articles, I assume that there will be expansion made on it, so I'm looking forward to what you do with it in that regards. I'm leaving my vote as a no vote for the time, as the level is currently just *ok*. Not bad, but not good either.

rika's flowery commentary

18 Jul 2021 17:48

The main gripe that I have with this level that's preventing me from upvoting it is the treatment of the Iroquois tribe within the level itself. There are no specific items, ideas, or structures that are completely unique to the Iroquois themselves (For example, what about the six major traditional festivals that happen throughout the year based on the seasons, like the mid-winter festival Gi'-ye-wä-no-us-quä-go-wä? How is that influenced by the Backrooms?)

Because of the general description, you can really replace "Iroquois" with the name of pretty much any other Native American ethnic group and not really lose much of substance. To me, it stands out that you didn't really go too much into researching this group, and it feels like an afterthought rather than a central focus of the level. It feels like you wrote the level first, and then added the group because you *had* to for Goicon, and they don't feel integrated in the level because of it. Otherwise, description is solid and enjoyable, but that major part (which is super integral to your level seeing as this entry is for Goicon and is supposed to focus on the Goi) is what's holding me back from an upvote.
