In the Backrooms and the Frontrooms, the brain is the most confusing to all. Does it have more control over us than we suspect? What if it is trying to show us the beyond, but we disregard it? Do we control the Brain, or does it control us?

We do not truly understand how it works. As said above, it’s confusing to all. The way it operates is clear to us, but what truly gives it thought? Do we take the old stories, such as Προμηθεύς[1](javascript:;)stealing the Fire from the Gods and giving it to Humanity? Or do we go with the more modern approach, believing that modern cognitive evolved in Europe approximately 40,000 years ago, even if it is just a guess?

In my take, it’s both correct. 1.5 million years ago, one of our *Homo Habilis* brothers lit a fire, and the world was given thought.

Now, we wish to know less.

There is a study from the American Mensa Society that having more IQ can lead to more mental illnesses. The more we know about reality, the less we want to know.

In this way, the Backrooms is Humanity’s greatest enemy. The Backrooms gives us a kick in the Brain, reminding us that we are not immortal. It gives more reality to us, and we try to reject it. It reminds us that we are not alone, that we are not the only species to have thought. And it horrifies us.

We endure each entity and level because our brain requires it. That we need to get out of this place as soon as possible because we want to know less.

The Brain gives thought, and now it regrets it.

Have we been awoken?

  

Well, yes, one human questioned the mind.

  

A rather annoying time to be awake. Why do we?

  
To make sure our students do not find something they aren’t supposed to find.  

For now, we have to watch this human with…curiosity.

  

First we select the alternative reality for em, now we have to watch them?

  

They’re a rather smart group. Their mind is strong, yet, their bodies are fragile.

  

So why are we watching them? They can’t witness the Beyond.

  

Such a mind of it’s kind is interesting. We have not seen a species such as them in a long time.

  

Perhaps one day, they will observe the beyond.

  

I believe that is highly unlikely.

  

Even with that statement, you still have a bit of questioning in your voice.
