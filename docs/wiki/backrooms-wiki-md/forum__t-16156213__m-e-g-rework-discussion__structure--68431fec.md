M.E.G Rework Discussion: Structure

12 Jul 2023 05:17

So as part of the rework of the M.E.G., I want to give the group a new, defined structure that will help the group make more sense and be much more functional given how big it is. This will be the present-day structure going forward. Of course, it will have evolved and changed much over time before reaching this point, looking very different in the earlier years.

New M.E.G. Structure:

Oversight Council /// Overseers & Underseers

- 5 Overseers are responsible for managing the 7 Sectors of the Major Explorers Group.

- Composed of the Overseers at the top of the chain of command, and the Underseers directly beneath them.

- Can give orders to any level of the group’s hierarchy, and can veto orders lower down in command (basically they have the final say in things, and have the power to do whatever they want within the group, including make changes to the structure if necessary)

- All decisions made by this Council are made by majority vote, so that no one individual can have complete power over the group.

Sectors /// Chief Directors

- Each Sector carries out an essential function of the group.

- Each is headquartered at one of 7 major bases (Alpha on Level 1, Gamma on Level 3, Omega on Level 4, Beta on Level 11, Delta on Level 230, Epsilon on Level 283, and Zeta on a level yet to be determined)

- Each Sector is led by a Chief Director, who manages the branches within their sector, and report to/takes orders from the Oversight Council

- The only group of people that make multi-sector decisions is the Oversight Council, and under normal circumstances, one sector does not have any authority over others.

- The Sectors have complex relationships that aid each others’ functions and allow the Major Explorers Group to run smoothly.

- Chief Directors can form and disband Special Teams and Outposts in addition to managing the branches. These are assembled for a variety of reasons, such as for carrying out non-vital functions of the group, conducting operations, or for emergency circumstances. These allow the Sectors a degree of flexibility not provided by the branches.

Branches /// Directors

- Each Sector is split into multiple branches, each of which manage specific areas within the Sector (ex. In an Exploration sector, there could be a cartography branch).

- Each branch is led by a Director, who reports to/takes orders from the Sector’s Chief Director

- Branches will only contain Teams with vital functions to the group (former divisions and regiments, as well as new additions). Special Teams will remain separate and instead report to the Chief Director.

- Directors can form and disband Teams within their branch as they deem necessary.

- Branches themselves are stationary within the major base the sector is headquartered in, while Teams can be sent out to wherever they need to be, depending on their purpose.

- They replace the old Regiments and Divisions (giving the group a much more fleshed out central command structure).

Teams /// Outposts

-Directors can form and disband Teams as needed.

-Teams within branches report to / take orders from their branch’s Director instead of a Chief Director.

-Outposts and special teams, such as Level-specific ones, are an exception, reporting directly to their respective Chief Director.

-Outside of highly unusual / extreme circumstances, Teams and Outposts never report to the Oversight Council directly.

-All currently existing Teams and Outposts will be assigned to a Sector, but will maintain lore compatibility

The goal behind giving it this structure is to better reflect the goals of the group and give it more identity. It will also take it away from the militaristic and SCP-like aspects that the old Major Explorer Group had. What are your thoughts on this?
