Hoax Entity Concept

09 Feb 2024 19:34

So, I have been doodling potential entities recently, the Swirlies being one of them (I talked about them in a different post). And I have came up with an idea of an Entity that doesn't actually exist, but people think it does. And the more people talk about it, the more mentally unstable they become. Any thoughts?

I like it, it honestly reminds me of the concept of [Entity 27](entity-27.md) or the **Athenian Ducks**. Thinking about them too much makes your head explode and then another duck pops out of your head. Some ideas I give are that you give them some type of mortal or non-fatal danger, such as cardiac arrest, stroke, dizziness, vomiting, fainting, loss of consciousness, among others similar. You could also add they go crazy or lose their sanity and cannot be cured with **Almond Water**. Those are some, although I can give you more ideas if you want.
