03 Jul 2026 00:16

The entries into the "Panda series" have always been interesting to me due to how isolated they are from the rest of the Backrooms. There's no advanced lore or complex political interactions; the only group mentioned is the M.E.G. It almost feels like seeing a better written version of a 2020 article (for instance the "Level x is the x+1th level of the Backrooms" is a long-outdated practice), which - given that this is rewritecon - seems oddly fitting.

I think the advantage of that old-style is it makes the Backrooms seem more alien and underexplored: you truly feel like there's only a few people down here, trying to figure out what the hell's going on with this strange place. This ends up playing really nicely with the themes of this page, what with all the gentle emptiness of these endless corridors.

I also really like the lore detail that animals that noclip into the Backrooms end up in Level 241. It's always bugged me as to why the Backrooms isn't like covered with ants or something.

No vote - it's nice but it's not much

06 Jul 2026 22:29

My comment kinda describes everything already. Looking at the comments, apparently this kinda page is the author's M.O. I like it, I can appreciate the intention, but it's not for me. Maybe it's because these are the pages I found the Backrooms on and I feel we're far moved past from these types of basic pages, but I'm left thinking "yeah that was a page." It's short, so it doesn't outstay its welcome, which I think helps massively (I didn't even finish L230 for that issue) but the page isn't… much. I guess that's by design.

This page is clearly well liked, and I'd say keep at it for your next pages, but I do wish it was a tiny bit more cohesive. If you cut out the dark, evil, edgy, fucked up part of the level I would rate it a lot higher. If this was a very small, short, and sweet level where the gimmick is "the Backrooms finds lost pets and gives them to the pure of heart" I'd like that! Make that a 2000 word shortpost, I'd enjoy it. But there are many little details that go unexplained and things that feel incongruent where this just feels like a collection of ideas with no thought. If that's the intention to invoke, that's fine, but it wouldn't hurt.

The thing about younger animals being immortal feels like it goes against the entire idea behind the level. Like you have magically spawning animals to take home with you, they get old and die, and there's presumably a graveyard for them. But why do the babies live forever when they grow old? The explanation for the page is that there are supposed to be parts that don't make sense ofc, it's a 2020-esc page, they had those nonsensical details, but in emulating that you kinda ruin what you had going. If it was a brief story about pet mortality, how it's really sad but there's always the chance to experience the love of a pet again, that would be great! but the dark fucked up evil half of 241 goes a little against that. As does the baby immortality thing.

Overall, it's a fine page, but not necessarily for me. One no-vote on a +29 isn't going to affect anything
