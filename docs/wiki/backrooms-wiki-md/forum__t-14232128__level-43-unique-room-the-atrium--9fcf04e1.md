Level 43 Unique Room - The Atrium

19 Oct 2021 16:23

General Pitch: Recently discovered, habitable, indoor sub-layer. Sub-layer is presented as a large Room within an existing level, not an independent Sub-Level, as I thought presenting an indoor, habitable sub-level would create too much of a "totally perfect utopia" vibe, rather than that of an "isolated oasis". Although, I am open to changing this if it'd work better as a Sub-Level (in which case it'd be 43.1), instead of a Room.

Seeking critiques, and eventual approval, if I receive the blessing of the Author of Level 43.
