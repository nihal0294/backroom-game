## Description

Entity 80 is hypothetical, with only a handful of people claiming to have encountered it. It is a being that is described as a bright white light that is human-like in shape. The exact details of the entity differ between each report, so a general description is the only suitable method for depicting it.[1](javascript:;)

Entity 80 is said to only appear to those on the edge of death. It is assumed that the entity is harmless and exists to "guide" those who have died. What it guides people to is unknown, but it is hypothesized to be the afterlife by those who think the entity exists.

## Behavior

![Painting of a white glowing angel in the void.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/entity-80/Angel%20Entity)

Artistic rendition of the entity.

Despite the varying accounts, all have given the same description of the entity's behavior.

When Entity 80 manifests, it always attempts to calm the person who has just died. Even if the person is already calm, it will still attempt the same ritual. The entity will slowly approach the person.[2](javascript:;) If the person starts to panic while Entity 80 is making its approach, it slows its movement down. When reaching the right distance to be able to interact with the person, the entity will stand and watch the subject; all reports say that after some time of Entity 80 watching them, they calmed down. The entity will then do one of two things.

If the person is on the floor, it reaches out one of its hands to help the person to their feet. All accounts say that they took the entity's hand when it was offered to them. They explained that when touching the entity's hand, they felt like life returned to their body.

Once the person is standing, Entity 80 will then travel past the person and situate itself behind them. It then places a hand on their shoulder and begins to guide them. Every account becomes inconsistent after the entity places its hand on the subject's shoulder.

## History

![Painting of a white glowing angel looming over a bed with a man in it.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/entity-80/Angel2.jpg)

Painting of Entity 80 from the 16th century.

The history of Entity 80 goes as far back as the 15th century, but no documented encounters took place until 1948.[3](javascript:;) It is unknown where the true origins of the stories came from. The name people gave the entity in the 15th century was "Ante Somnum Aeternum," which means "Before Eternal Sleep" in English.

A transcript of the first text mentioning the entity is found below. [The Lost](the-lost.md) hold the original Latin manuscript in their archives, and they date it to be from around the 15th-16th century. The bottom of the manuscript is torn off, leading most to believe that there is a missing section.

### Before Eternal Sleep

Shining the brightest light

There is a being  
Eating the night  
Removing every fright

There is a being  
You meet after death  
After your last breath

There is a being

With you every step

There is a being  
A guardian true  
Watching over you

There is a being

## First Reported Contact

![A black and white photo of an old man in a suit.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/entity-80/AF)

Axle Fridel, circa 1949.

Axle Fridel is the first person to document an actual interaction with the entity. Before his account, the entity was simply a campfire story told by wanderers in their spare time when they weren't trying to survive.

Axle Fridel no-clipped into the Backrooms in December of 1946. His encounter took place on May 14th, 1948, when he was exploring [Level 7](level-7.md). Fridel was taking a long dive with the goal of finding unexplored parts of the level. He and a group of friends dove for 20 minutes before his friends decided to surface for more air. This is when his friends realized that he was not with them. Fridel had gone too deep into the water and drowned on the ascent. His friends rescued his body from the water and resuscitated him. His full account is found below.

**My Testimony**

---

I, Axle Fridel, swear that everything I am about to say is not fiction. All things I list in this document are real experiences that I had after I drowned in 1948.

Do you ever think about death? Because I do. Sitting there thinking about what lies beyond and how everything that I strive to build in this life means nothing compared to what happens after it. Then the existential fear crawls in because you aren't supposed to be thinking about that. About something you don't understand in the slightest. This fear stopped after my experience with death.

I wish I could give a reasonable explanation for why I didn't swim back up with my friends. Maybe I wanted to find something without their help and claim sole credit for what I found. The one thing I do know is that I did find something they did not.

Before drowning, I remember losing my breath and panicking. I tried to swim back up to the surface, but my lungs ached for air, and I opened my mouth. This stopped my accent, as water was all I was inhaling. That's when everything started to go black, and within a moment or so, I lost complete consciousness.

I had died. Just as suddenly as I lost consciousness, I found myself in a sort of blank, dark plain, lying on a transparent floor. I looked down at myself, and I almost looked "inky." I never spoke a word while there. To this day, I don't know if something was stopping me from speaking or if I was stopping myself.

This is when I saw it, a bright angelic light that appeared no more than 5 meters away from where I was sitting. The light was shaped like a person, thin, with long hair and broad shoulders. The only features I could point out were those of its silhouette because its whole body was producing a warm white light. It never spoke a word; all it did was offer its hand, which I took. It then placed its hand on my shoulder and started to guide me.

I blinked, and we were in a different place. It looked very similar to a normal level. It was coated in fog, and I could only see about twenty feet in front of me. The ground was dirt, and in the fog were shadows that looked like unfinished buildings and landscapes. My guardian angel was still guiding me, and after a short passage of time, we came upon a house.

It was my childhood home, and inside were my family and myself as just a small boy. This was an exact memory I thought of from time to time while wandering the Backrooms. There were stockings lining the mantel over our crackling fireplace, and Mom had just made us all hot chocolate. She always did this before we opened our gifts. I turned around, and there was that sunset that always peeked into the window before I opened the first gift. I saw myself tear open my first gift and felt the same joy that he, or I, was feeling. Childish, clean, innocent joy.

That's when my guide pressed on my shoulder, and we walked forward into the fog. We traveled for some time until we came upon a park. Sitting there was my sweet Laura. This was the first time I saw her. Sitting there in her grey sweater, reading, with the autumn leaves rustling above her. When I laid eyes on her, it was love at first sight. I remember being so nervous walking over to her and asking if she was seeing anyone. When it showed me this memory, I could feel the same butterfly feeling I felt back then.

Into the fog I went again. This time, we came upon a hospital. This was the happiest day of my life. Watching my baby boy being born. No other feeling before or after that time has come close to it. Reliving seeing my baby for the first time was the best thing to happen to me in this labyrinth.

I felt calm the entire time I was in this expanse, not an ounce of fear in my body. When we journeyed into the fog again, I felt like where it was leading me was a place that I was destined to be. I never got to see it, though, because I blinked and I was back to reality. I regained consciousness while in a fury of choking up water. I was lying on my back, and my dear friend Chuck was performing chest compressions on me. My friends had told me they went back to find me, and by a miracle, they saw my body floating in the depths. They took me back to our raft and immediately started to perform mouth-to-mouth and compressions.

What I experienced was nothing short of a divine interaction.
