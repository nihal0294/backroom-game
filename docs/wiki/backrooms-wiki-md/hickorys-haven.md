Hi! I'm Hickory, and thanks for reading my author page!

I used to write here, but I've since moved on and won't be doing much more for the Backrooms. I've had most of my old articles taken down, for their concepts will have new homes in different projects of mine, though articles I've rewritten remain.

Feel free to message me for any reason if you do need something. I'll respond when I see it. Just please be nice to me.

I also did daily Backrooms pixel art for a few months, which I posted on my [Tumblr Blog](index.md) and in our Discord's art channel. I will not be returning to it.
