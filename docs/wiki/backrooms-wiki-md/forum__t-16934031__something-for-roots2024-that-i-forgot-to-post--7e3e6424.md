This is very nice. I like how it flashes out the world and people living in it.

One thing I do recommend is having some small indicators of when you jump in time within each entry. It's a bit confusing to read and the indicators would help a lot.

There are also some places where I'd recommend a paragraph break:

> This house seems eerily quiet.

> Oh no. I haven't been perfectly quiet while I was here.

But overall, it's very nice.

Just a reminder, our discord is more active, so if you want crit, I would join there. :)
