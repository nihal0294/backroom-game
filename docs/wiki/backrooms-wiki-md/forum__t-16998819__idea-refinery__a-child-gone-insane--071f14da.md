Idea Refinery: "######; a Child Gone Insane"

02 Dec 2024 22:43

Page Type: Tale

Genre (Optional): Psychological horror

Core Concept: I have observed the effect of **abuse on children,** and it thoroughly messes with their mind. Likewise, the backrooms is this weird flavor of an "incorrect" feeling. When I read tales and articles detailing the backrooms, there's this off feeling that will never go away when you are stuck in the backrooms, just like when people are subjected to psychological torture.

###### is a child that went through severe abuse, and in the cruel, painful sense, the type of degradation that leaves permanent scars. She ends up in the backrooms and is constantly reminded of what she went through while traversing the backrooms.

The tales track her journey through the backrooms and the effect of her scrambled psyche on her perception of her experiences. The entire tale series is meant to disturb people to their core; not by shock value(really what I want to avoid is shock value storytelling), but just by frustration and pity of the way the rest of ######'s life unravels with no one to help her navigate her hardships as a wanderer with trauma. She, eventually, will become hostile and entity-like.

Additional Notes: The problem is, I do not know how to interest people with this idea enough to keep them hooked. The hook is my first issue, and my second is the way I will reveal her damaged mind. Through flashbacks, off-hand comments?

And, finally, I was thinking of making her character nonbinary in an involuntary way. The reason her name is ###### is because she does not remember her name(her noticing she lost her name will become a chapter!), so it might be bit fitting if she lost connection to her gender identity, as well.

I appreciate your time!
