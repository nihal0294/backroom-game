## Description

[![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/entity-297/Sovereign_Sketch/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/entity-297/Sovereign_Sketch)

A theorized sketch of the Dark Sovereign drawn by a wanderer

The Dark Sovereign is an entity native to Zone 4 of Level 197. The entity has a largely unknown appearance, being [ERR404:DATA\_NOT\_FOUND] and comprised mostly of smoke. Two orbs follow the Sovereign, which are presumed to be its eyes, and an item appears to hang above its head, which to the few who have seen it, appears to be a crown-like object, signifying royalty or power.

[[ERR410:GONE]](javascript:;)

[He is here...](javascript:;)

The surrounding environment begins to grow dark; cold, and lonely. Take a look around. Where are your friends? Did you ever have any? Where are you exactly? Do you really know? Are you in Level 4? 11? Maybe the Hub? Can you really tell? How do you know that in one second it won't all go to black?

The Sovereign operates off of what some wanderers have called a "respect basis," which entails that The Sovereign will reciprocate respect it is given. The few who respected the Sovereign escaped Zone 4 with their lives, but said that their exploration parties were not so lucky. Wanderers taken by the Sovereign have not been seen since their exploration into Zone 4.
