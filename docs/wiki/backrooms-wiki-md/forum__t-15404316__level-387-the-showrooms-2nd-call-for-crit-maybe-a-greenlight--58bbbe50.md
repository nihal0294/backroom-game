This actually really cool. While reading this, I got an idea for an Entity that might exist there, though most likely an enigmatic one. I haven't written it down though. Basically, there's a rare chance of encountering it when leaving the showing room after 10-20 minutes. It's form is unknown, as it is constantly "glitchy" and has short range teleportation and can cause object to "glitch", making the unusable for about 20 minutes. If it makes contact with you, you will similarly glitch, often times flickering in and out of existence, before either the effect stops, or you simply cease to exist. You will also uncontrollably teleport a max of 5 meters on occasion. The "Glitch", as it is often called, will also cause any sceens near it to glitch until leaves. The entity can speak, but is often sounds, distorted, warped, and in some cases has to be played backwards to understand. There is also a strange "static" sound emanating from it. The entity hostile to those who leave the showing room too early, which is believed to be around 10-20 minutes after the start of a movie, which then resets when the next movie starts. He is docile unless attacked if found by other means. It states that has no idea what happened to it, but believes that it was a "glitch in the matrix". While the entity is docile, it does seem to have emotions, and always speaks in a (distorted) clinical tone, and does not use words related to emotion. It does however feel pain, and is in constant pain, though it is not unbearable, and can be ignored if no attention is payed to it. It claims that while it has memories of watching movies, and has extensive knowledge in many of them, it does recall any personal memories outside Level 387.

Im not too much of a crit-er but here we go:

First of all i like the idea of the level and the execution was pretty good, these suggestions are mostly my opinion so don't take some of these too seriously

Instead of saying x in each direction you can say the level is x kilometers large.

Also add a metric to imperial conversion in a footnote for the people in the states.

The data missing can be changed to a cross out followed by what was in the footnote like this like this (that is 100 percent my opinion but keep it like that if you want to keep the mystery).

When you said the level 387 effect, all I thought was that several other levels had an effect similar to that, so you can change it to something like the safety effect or just gives you a sense of safety (that should really be a phenomenon).

In the community section you put: "This group has numbered 103 members at the time of writing" the word numbered is not needed in it and makes the sentence pretty awkward.

One more thing, go into more details about the movies. Are they the exact same, any weird cryptic glitches in the middle of the movie, do the credits have the same actors, etc.

Other than that, I really like this level and I can't wait to see it in the main page before to long.
