To clarify, the issue here is not with wikidot, but with the formatting for the page. Basically, one should only place stuff they want to appear on all pages in the main page. Everything page-specific should be in fragments. Also, the user isn't meant to ever visit the fragment pages by themselves. Instead, they visit the main page, which will automatically featured different content based on stuff at the end of the url.

To fix your issue, put the content other than the listpages contained in collab:larn in a new fragment page. Instead of linking to the fragment pages, link to the main page with /offset/[number here] at the end. Ask if you need clarification.
