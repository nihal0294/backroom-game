## **?? Days since we found the outpost:**

I finally found it! I have no idea how long I've found myself stranded out here, but I know I found it! Almost exactly a replica of the images shown in the level log. I believe I got into the sublevel 2 days ago.

About three days ago, I a little less than half my food supply left. I was planning on heading back soon. However, I was starting to notice my surroundings steadily changing as I was walking forward. It was very odd since I was so used to the buzzing fluorescent light "sheets" and the light bulbs were quite startling. It was that moment I realized, I had found it!

Oddly, the topmost layer was no longer so 'thin', at least to the severity described in the N.T.G. entry, as I walked through there for a full day, barely seeing any changes. My educated is that this sub-level is still growing, even now. However, I must not get sidetracked. Once inside the second to top layer, I started hearing some sounds. It started just as panting, soon changing into footsteps. I thought I was going insane at first, then I realized it must be an entity.

That was the second I started to run. Of course, it followed. I looked back and saw it, a strange monkey-hound creature, similar to the one described prior. Luckily, it lost interest very quickly; since it was likely just protecting it's territory. Soon after that incident, I realized I should stop wandering deeper in, as my luck would likely run out soon. Of course, I was lost at that point, but I knew it wouldn't be too long if I could identify the exits as per my training.

It took another day, but I finally made my way back to [Level 0](level-0.md). In fact, I've already made it back to [Level 1](level-1.md). By now, they must've done at least something to console the public. Farewell, and till next time.

**P.S** I'll put these logs into the level data; our citizens need to understand what the truth is.
