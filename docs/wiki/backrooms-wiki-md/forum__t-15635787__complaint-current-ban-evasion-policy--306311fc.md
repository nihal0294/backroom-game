[Complaint] Current Ban Evasion Policy

11 Feb 2023 04:27

Current rules regarding ban evasion prohibit even talking to banned users about article ideas, which is not only an overreach of staff determining who can and cannot be spoken to outside of the server, but also an extremely vague and difficult to enforce rule. The rule prohibiting the use of banned users' ideas should be dropped, as it brings no benefits that I can see and only serves to create a headache for staff and users. Additionally, I do not understand why staff wishes to prevent proxy writing at all — the purpose of a ban is to protect the community from a dangerous user, and if a banned user wishes to donate articles to the site without interacting with the community, I see no harm in it. I would like a clarification from staff regarding the exact boundaries of what this rule does and does not forbid, and the reasoning behind it.
