The Entry Entity

04 Jun 2021 15:30

**Page Type:** Entity

**Genre (Optional):** Horror/Supernatural

**Page Layout (Optional):** Don’t know yet. Probably normal

**Elevator Pitch:** The core idea is that the Entity is the Entry itself, no one knows who made the entry or how it got there, no one can edit the original writing of the entry.

**Central Narrative:** At the moment I don’t know, because I have a lot of different ideas. All I know is that I might spread some warnings throughout the article. Some maybe hidden and some not so hidden. If I feel like putting in the work then I might try to create some kind of a puzzle as well.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** If you’ve finished reading the entry, it’s already too late…

**Additional Notes:** I’m mainly just looking for ideas on the story/supernatural element. Maybe add some spooky thing towards the end. Because so far this is just a concept in my head with a whole bunch of nothing in the middle. And yes I know the concept sounds a bit cheesy

Re: The Entry Entity

05 Jun 2021 09:04

Hi! A classic idea you got there. Here are some points I'd give:

- Since the Entry is the entity itself, I'd say the page layout would actually be a format screw.
- As there are already a few Entry Entities, it's great that you're considering the idea of adding a puzzle of sorts. It would definitely make it stand out more. However, if you are to pursue this concept, I highly encourage making sure this Entity still has an identity of sorts. A few ideas may include: an Entity who just realized they're an Entry and wants to change the page up a bit (changing the theme); an Entity who realizes they're an Entry but does NOT like it and wants to leave; and an Entity who explicitly finds pleasure in screwing up other pages so that Wanderers would get hurt. I leave the plot up to you to decide, though I think with these extra concepts it would help even a bit.

I have to say, I'm intrigued! Do come back to me on this one. I'd love to see this concept grow.
