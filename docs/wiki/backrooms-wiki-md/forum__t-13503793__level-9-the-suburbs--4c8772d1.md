Quite good, but could be a little better

23 Feb 2021 06:02

This article is pretty good, one of my favourites so far. I do have a few criticisms though.

*The items that require a power source are unfunctional due to the missing requirement of a power source.*

This is a little wordy/redundant, perhaps you could simply say that the homes are unpowered

*Another anomalous property of this Level is that you can find two houses weirdly clipped inside of each other when travelling too far, which is physically impossible.*

I think the fact that two houses are merged together speaks for itself! I also don't think you need to say "another anomalous property is". In my opinion, this sounds unorganized. It should be clear to the reader that we're essentially describing anomalous properties of the level.

*The wet asphalt roads are unpainted, and are covered with leaves in some areas. The puddles in some spots of the roads indicates that the Level had once rained before. The stone sidewalks are normal, and don't seem to have any anomalous oddities.*

I really love the characterization of this place as apparently having weather, but also having no direct evidence of that! All the leaves and puddles suggest there was a storm, but does it ever actually storm while someone is there? Maybe you could elaborate on this. How do these streets smell I wonder?

*The stone sidewalks are normal, and don't seem to have any anomalous oddities*

Once again, I think this is a bit redundant, but I think it's interesting that the sidewalks are stone. Maybe you could simply say the sidewalks are made of stone (what kind?), but do not seem anomalous.

I know this probably seems like a lot of criticism, but I do like your article :)
