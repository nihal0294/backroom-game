Level XXX: The Locker Rooms

31 Mar 2021 22:18

**Page Type:** Level

**Elevator Pitch:** The core idea is that this is a basic high school/gym locker room, but unlike a regular Locker Room, it's width and length are both twenty miles. You can find showers, lockers, and an unnerving feeling that the next locker row you turn, there will be something there. You can find almond water, food, and other items inside lockers. You can also find skin stealers on this level. The Locker Rooms also house many secrets, entrances and exits, if you can find them.

**Central Narrative:** This level is discovered by a 17 year old boy after he walked into a building labelled "POOL" in level 11. He explores and charts the locker rooms until 2 months later, he escapes and gives all the data he collected to a M.E.G outpost in level 11, but he seems like he is hiding something. As M.E.G enters the locker rooms and discovers (and documents) it's secrets, the Locker Rooms will be fully uncovered.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** A new level that can bring a new storyline, new paths, and new choices for people throughout the backrooms, but you better watch your six because the skinstealers won't.

Additional Notes: Thanks for reading this! This is my first idea, so it might be a bit bad, but i have been lurking on the wiki without joining for the last 6 months and I wanted to join and submit my idea. Thanks again- T.
