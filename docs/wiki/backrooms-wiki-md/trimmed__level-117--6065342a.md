**Level 117** is the 118th Level of the Backrooms

# **Description:**

**Level 117** consists of a seemingly infinite number of rooms. These rooms are connected directly through doorways and resemble school classrooms. The rooms are very similar in design with slight variations, often featuring chalkboards, desks, and cabinets. Occasionally school supplies such as pencils and binders can be found around the level. The level is lit by lighting fixtures in the ceiling, some of which are burnt out.

**Level 117** has an 18 hour day-night cycle with 7 hours dedicated to day time and 11 dedicated to night time. During the night time all of the lighting fixtures will switch off and **Janitors** will begin to roam the halls. [Smilers](entity-3.md) can occasionally be seen in the darker corners of **Level 117**, but are rarely seen at night. Some reports say that this is because they are afraid of the **Janitors**, but this is not confirmed.

# **Entities**

Aside from Smilers, there are 2 main entities that can be found in **Level 117**: The **Janitors** and an entity who calls himself **[Mr. Freeman](trimmed__entity-91--e8fa81ac.md)**.

## Mr. Freeman:

**Mr. Freeman** can occasionally be seen during the daytime "teaching" in empty classrooms. This usually consists of the entity giving lectures about topics found in High-School level Algebra and Geometry classes to the empty classroom. Some say there is more than one identical **Mr. Freeman**. These reports are unconfirmed. **Mr. Freeman** is friendly to Wanderers, may invite them to listen to his lectures if he sees them.

## Janitors:

**Janitors** are the most common entity found in **Level 117**. They appear as shadowy figures wearing navy blue uniforms and carrying flashlights. **Janitors** also often carry around large brooms and trash cans and will sweep the floors of the classrooms. They will not react to wanderers unless the wanderers get very close. When a wanderer gets close to a **Janitor**, the **Janitor** will quickly engulf that person in shadow. Afterwards the **Janitor** will return to cleaning duties with the person no longer there. It is unknown what happens to people engulfed by Janitors, but there have been several unconfirmed reports of these people reappearing as students in [Level 52](level-52.md). Because of the **Janitors**, it is inadvisable to visit **Level 117** during the night time.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities:**

There are no known bases, outposts and communities in **Level 117**. This is likely due to the danger that **Janitors** pose during the night time.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

### Entrances:

The primary method of reaching **Level 117** is by entering classrooms in [Level 83](level-83.md) which very occasionally lead to **Level 117**. Some people have also had success entering though [Level 18](level-18.md), but this is not reliable.

### Exits:

Some rooms in **Level 117** contain cupboards labeled "calculators". When entered, they lead to [Level 4](level-4.md). There are also reports of no-clipping through the walls to reach [level-190](trimmed__level-190--ce90e00a.md), but this is not yet confirmed. Red doors can also be found rarely throughout the level. When entered, these lead to [Level 74](level-74.md).
