# Level 668 - "Hor·eye·zon"

rating: +75[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

> ##### "The eyes are just a false mirror. It mirrors not the world itself, but its mirage."

•  
•  
•

> You know it too well ─ your eyesight is failing. It won't be long before your world goes completely black.  
>   
> You arrive at the [Art Gallery](level-57.md) and ask the Artist to draw a colorless painting for you- she calls it ***Le faux miroir***[1](javascript:;).  
>   
> You no-clip into the painting and find a hospital. Without hesitation, you ask the oculist there to conduct an optometry test.  
>   
> An autorefractor covers your head. The sight you see through the aperture attracts you: *a field of wheat, a path, and a colorful balloon dreamily floating at the end of the path. It reminds you of your childhood dreams.*
>
