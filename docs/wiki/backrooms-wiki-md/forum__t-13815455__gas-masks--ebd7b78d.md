Gas Masks

14 Oct 2020 18:57

<http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/gas-mask>

Description:  
Gas masks are somewhat rare items found across several levels. They can be found with filters already plugged in, or laying near them. They are found in different types, with several of note being full versus half faced masks, and models that take one versus two filters at a time. Most gas masks also have a speaking membrane built into them, which allow for clearer enunciating.

Sources:  
Gas masks can be found in several levels, the best sources being the bombed-out plane in Level 66 or trading for them in the Traveler's Vault on Level 2. However, other sources of Gas Masks are also there for people who find them.
