An unofficial challenge for the site!

10 Jun 2023 23:11

A while ago, I joked in the wiki's discord server that I should host an unofficial contest for self-insert fanfiction involving the Actor. More than one person thought it was funny, and from there I decided to make a more serious challenge as a fun exercise. So, to the creators of the wiki, I issue a challenge: **create a work that heavily involves the Actor in some way.**

This is an unofficial challenge (as opposed to an official contest), and is run entirely by for fun. To sign up for the challenge, please click **New Post** in this thread and post "I will participate" so that I can keep track of sign-ups. Once you finish your submission and post it to the wiki, link it in this thread.

# Ranking

Ranking will be done via total **upvotes** on each page in order to prevent bad-faith downvoting. Standard deletion threshold rules apply. There are no tangible prizes for this challenge, other than bragging rights.

# Rules

1. Don't be an jerk
2. One submission per person
3. Participants must sign up by July 1st, and submissions must be completed by July 16th (under “anywhere in the world” rules that official contests run on) but can be posted at any time until then
4. Submissions can be in any format or medium that can be posted to the wiki, and can include drafts started before the challenge was announced
5. If you think your submission won't be posted to the site by July 16th due to lack of GL or critique checks, let me know so we can work something out
6. I am off-limits as an idea or draft critic to challenge participants, but you can ask me questions about the Actor as much as you like
7. No funny business to get around site posting rules (ex: no giving each other critique checks without actually critiquing the drafts)
8. Don't be the reason why unofficial challenges get banned from the site

# Not rules but requests from me

- Please preserve the fact that he's a men-liker and Japanese-Uchinanchu. You're obviously allowed to change this under CC-BY-SA 3.0, but I made him that way for a reason. You don't need to focus on these aspects, but please do not actively erase them.
- Other than the above request, I encourage everyone to get creative with writing the Actor. Feel free to ask me if you're concerned about an idea being too "out there".
- Please treat this as a challenge. Nobody can force you to put effort into this, but I do want this to be a fun higher-effort writing exercise for everyone.

## Other suggested material:

A writing guide for the Actor (from my author page):

[+ Show](javascript:;)

[– Hide](javascript:;)

> Inherently a character who can do, has done, and will do anything as required by the narrative. Anything can happen to him (including permanent removal from a narrative) based on the writer's choice without it affecting the Actor himself, since all he plays is a role. The Actor is exactly what he is called, and is willing to fill any role position needed (protag, antag, etc) or use any name that fits for that role. Bring up the metanarrative if you like, but it's not necessary to do so when writing him. He can be just Some Guy.
>
> The flower attached to him can be changed to whatever type of flower, since the flower and vines represent the narrative. Make them symbolic or not, whatever you choose. He moves in a way that suggests the vines/stems all over him control his movements, but ignore this detail if you feel like it's too distracting.

About his weaponry:

[+ Show](javascript:;)

[– Hide](javascript:;)

> I've given the Actor two weapons, which are effectively the same object in different forms: a standard rain umbrella, and an ieku/eku. An ieku is an Okinawan kobudo weapon based on a wooden rowing oar, and so was associated with fishermen. One side of the rowing blade is sharpened, and can inflict decent damage in a fight. The umbrella and ieku occupy the same "existence" when I write them, and so they're never seen together at the same time. Either it's an umbrella, or it's an ieku.
