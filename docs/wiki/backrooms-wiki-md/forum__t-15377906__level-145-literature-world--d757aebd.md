Re: Level 145 "Literature World"

12 Nov 2022 02:16

Idea is pretty good (if a bit similar to Level 906), but there a bit too many issues for me to say it's a great page. Here are the ones I noticed:

> Level 145 resembles a library, presumbaly 800 km² big.

You misspelled "presumably".

> The library houses an incountable amount of bookshelves.

"Incountable" is a bit of a weird word, I would write "a large amount" or something like that.

> The books are all completly unsorted. Neither after language or genre.

Once again a bit of a weird collection of sentences. I would write "The books are not sorted in any realizable pattern."

> The level is a good place for wanderers to meet, as everyone entering this Level appears in the same area.

Appear in what area? Give some additional information on this starting area.

> Even though this occurs rarely sometimes pictures or level keys are found in Level 145.

Nothing wrong with this sentence per se, I just think it would read better if you wrote "Sometimes pictures or level keys can be found in this Level, but this is quite rare."

> Trying to use dynamite in Level 145 to open the to this day locked basement of Level 145 has shown that it is impossible to light fire in this Level.

Uhh…what? I don't know what this is trying to tell me. Can you rewrite it to show what this means?

> The M.E.G has forbidden any not authorized movement 10km outside of the starting point to all sides. Anyone breaking this rule will be brought outside of Level 145 or will be eliminated if

neccesary.  
Three things wrong with this one. First, change "any not authorized movement" to "any unauthorized movement". Second, "necessary" is misspelled as "neccesary". Third, there's no explanation on *why* this rule is in place. Most articles are written from the M.E.G's knowledge of the subject, so there should be no secrecy on why they are doing this. Give a reason as to why they want to do this.

> **The Readers**

You should make this a Heading 4, like this:

#### The Readers

> The Readers are the only entity found on Level 145. Readers are humanoid creatures. When Readers were discovered, they were first counted as Facelings, but when they were observed more thoroughly they were classified as their own entity.

You should combine the second and third sentences into one for easier reading. I would write "Readers are humanoid creatures that were initially classified as Facelings, but have since been deemed their own entities after study of their behaviors and appearance." Also, I don't believe these should have their own entity page. Most level-exclusives don't unless there is a lot of detail about them that cannot be summarized in an article, and I don't believe they are interesting enough to warrant this.

> They will read any book they find loudly.It is not known if they can speak human languages when they are not reading. After they finish a book they enter the "Sleeping Phase" in which they reside into the book they rust read.

There should be a space between "loudly" and "it" and "just" is misspelled as "rust".

> Wanderers finding these books often report halluzinations telling them to open the book.

Once again a simple change. "Hallucinations" is misspelled as "halluzinations".

I thought that these entities were exclusive to Level 145? How can you know that if they don't go outside Level 145?

> Level 145 is easy to exit and can bring one to various Levels of the Backrooms.

This sentence should probably not exist, the Exits section is exclusively for a list of exits out of the Level and any other information should be put in the Level itself.

In conclusion, I think you have a solid and interesting idea, but it does overlap with [another level](level-906.md) already on the wiki and there are quite a few technical issues. Take my advice and fix all the issues I mentioned, then go to the Discord server for a more in-depth critique than this.

-*QuestionableRose22*
