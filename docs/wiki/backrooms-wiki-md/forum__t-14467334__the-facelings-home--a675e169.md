I think it is really cool one, there is a lot of content and it is well written. But, I think in those enigmatic levels should be more vagueness and less concrete details. I'm not saying that this is a 100% minus or disadvantage, but in my opinion you should not directly say that this is the facelings home, it would be cooler if there were hints about it, just without a direct statement of this fact.

Anyway, it is just my opinion, maybe the opposite of it (direct statements) will be way better.

To sum it up, I really like your draft, it is really cool!
