**Entity Number:** 15

**Survival Tips:** You can Save one with [Almond Water](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/almond-water) before they develop into a full **Insanity**, if your near one that is fully developed, you'll need to listen to it's noises to determine if it is dangerous or not.

**Description:** These creatures are zombie like humans that went insane in the backrooms. Only one picture was taken when they were terminated. They are usually found in the lower Levels, and are very rare. This usually starts when a Human goes insane in The Backrooms. after a while they will start to attack people, and they will start to act like a Skin-Stealer, their skin will become more reddish, and they will develop holes on their body, and develop a Large smile over time. Some will have weapons, or items, but most are very slow, but still be careful, as the more insane they are, the more speed they will have. They usually make low grunts and moans, but some will make High pitched grunts and Maniacal Laughs that are very loud, you can take this sound info and listen to see if they are dangerous or not. Rarely you can save one, or yourself with "Almond Water", before they turn into this state. But, if all of their bodily fluids have developed into "Almond Water", Before your intervention, which at that point, they cannot be cured. There are only have been one sighting of a "friendly Insanity", but this is not confirmed. The M.E.G. (A.k.a Major Explorer Group) "Team Wild Warriors" are researching more about this entity at the moment.
