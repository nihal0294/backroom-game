# Object 227-X - The World Breacher

---

### Description

![Missing alt text.](http://delameuh.wikidot.com/local--files/fragment:level-772-0/World-breacher)

Object 227-X

The World Breacher is a machine of wonder, 15 tons of metals, capable of transporting large numbers of people directly into Liberation's closed paradise network. This APC[1](javascript:;) features a camouflage system and virtually indestructible armour, while being powered by an all-electric motor, ensuring stealth and protection!

### Rift Technology

We have developed a new technology based on [Object 227-A](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-772/offset/4), whose main properties have been studied by the Apollo Team of the Ariane Circle. We have discovered, however, that the destination is based on the frequency emitted by the liquid silence. More precisely, we can regulate its frequency to choose a specific location. As a result, we can now choose from several destinations instead of just getting one at random. Thanks to this technology, we can now, at the touch of a button, teleport the vehicle and all the personnel inside to the chosen destination.

### BR-550-K Camouflage System

Even before Liberation's threat emerges, we were already working on a top secret project involving an undetectable camouflage system. This system generates a reality-altering wave field through a dome of predefined radius, allowing everything inside the field to become invisible from the outside thanks to waves that perfectly mimic the surrounding environment. The wave field also counteracts all types of ultrasounds and radar systems. The BR-550-K camouflage system is finally operational and has been successfully implemented after years of development and prototyping. This technology was particularly useful for experimenting with the World Breacher's “Rift technology” while remaining invisible to Liberation's eyes.

### Composite Armor

The World Breacher is also equipped with an almost impenetrable armor made from materials taken from the composition of [Entity 503](entity-503.md)'s armor. As a result, the vehicle can resist all kinds of environmental hazards and most hostile attacks.
