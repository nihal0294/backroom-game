[I'm Sorry You Need to Go Through This Process One More Time](javascript:;)

[I Really Am I Swear](javascript:;)

First Tab:

> "The outpost was destroyed by Memory Lurkers, do not listen to them, they are under the level's many effects and trances."

- You should separate the first part into its own sentence (specifically, end it after "Lurkers."

—-  
Second Tab:

> "There are only two (2) entities here: Memory Lurkers, and the level itself."

- 2 choices: I recommend you either take away the comma or replace it with double dashes (—).

> "We have weapons, and we have Almond Water."

- I'm not sure if this was a stylistic choice made by the supposed writer - but if it's not, you could shorten it to "We have weapons and Almond Water."
- Linking "Almond Water" to its article would be nice.

> "I think we are safe, I will update this later."

- I recommend replacing the comma with a dash.

> "They are just floating around, some floated close to us, though."

- I think it would suit better to replace the first comma with an ellipses (…).

> " I managed to hit one with a crowbar, and it's just flopping around like it's a fish."

- No comma needed.

---

Third Tab

> " The false form is shown to people via one of the only entities that inhabit this level, Memory Lurkers."

- The comma should be replaced with a colon.

> "These entities will kill you instantly, and make you think you're still alive, which is one of the many reasons this level is a dead zone."

- Curious. How do they do that if you're dead?
- First comma is unneeded.

---

Final thoughts: I like it a lot! I like the ironic realization in the third tab that the entirety of Level XX's true form is fake. I don't see why you wouldn't keep it.

Welp, I have no other nitpicks nor narrative bones to pick here, so take this delicious Greenlight, my friend. Happy writing!
