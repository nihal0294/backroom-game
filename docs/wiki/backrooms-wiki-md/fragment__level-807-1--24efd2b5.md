# **Description:**

Level 807 consists of a partially destroyed pine forest, spanning approximately 200 square kilometers[1](javascript:;). The level has a tropical climate, with temperatures ranging from 29°C to 35°C[2](javascript:;), despite the flora of the level only existing naturally in temperate climates in The Frontrooms. Level 807 is non-Euclidean, as going in a straight line for long enough loops wanderers back to where they started.

Level 807 follows a 24 hour day-night cycle with a Sun similar to that of the Frontrooms. However, the level does not have a Moon.

One important thing to note of the Level 807 are the acid rains. These rains occur very frequently in the entire level, and can cause major damage to one's body if not under shelter. One can determine that an acid rain will occur if the clouds are pointing towards the sun. Wearing corrosion resistant clothing can reduce damage from the rain. However, the best way to survive these rains is to stay inside a properly constructed shelter, made from stone or bricks. The rains of the level are corrosive enough to melt through leaves, and can leak through wood.

Level 807 has mostly flat, soiled terrain. However, the soil of the level is too infertile and acidic to sustain any form of agriculture, unless limestone is applied. Despite this, the frequent acidic rains of the level make agricultural development next to impossible. Level 807 also contains several natural springs. These springs are incredibly acidic, as well as containing traces of toxic metals such as mercury and lead. Because of this, **drinking or using the water in the springs for any purpose is strictly forbidden.**

# Resources:

Level 807 contains a wide variety of metals underground. These include common metals like iron, nickel and copper, as well as rare earth metals like scandium, which are traded with the [Backrooms Robotics](backrooms-robotics.md). Timber is theoretically collectible, but it is no longer harvested due to the acid rains.

# **Bases of note:**

Level 807 used to have a lot of minor settlements. However, the acid rains caused most of them to be abandoned, including Base Cedar, which was given to the [B.N.T.G](b-n-t-g.md) by the [M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md).

### Description:

Jean Kepler is a 29-year-old woman of French descent. She was previously a member of the [M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md)., having first joined 2 years ago. She was at first a junior researcher in [Level 10](level-10.md), before being transferred to Level 807 as the Senior farm researcher of the now-abandoned Base Cedar.

Jean Kepler was last seen in her travel clothes, which consisted of blue denim jeans, a Hawaiian shirt with a yellow-black color scheme, and black sneakers. She has blue eyes, neck-length brown hair, and pale skin.

On June 13th,[data corrupted], she defected from Base Cedar along with most of her research team. Jean Kepler eventually created and lead the group known as The Ecology Protection Agency, or E.P.A for short. She has also caused many of our most skilled operatives to defect to the group.

This group is particularly threatening to the Collection Outpost as most members of the group know a lot about the place's secrets being former operatives of the [M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md), as well as our operatives. The group has infiltrated our outpost's defenses, sabotaged the smelters and stolen our mining equipment, hindering our capability to extract resources from Level 807.

If seen, it is imperative that she is captured immediately, and brought back to [Office Space EL3A](office-space-el3a.md) for questioning. From there, we will be able to take down The E.P.A, and resume mining activities mostly undisturbed.
