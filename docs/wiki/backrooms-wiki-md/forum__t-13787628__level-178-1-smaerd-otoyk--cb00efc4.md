Level 178.1- Smaerd Otoyk

05 Oct 2020 04:58

**Page Type:** Sub Layer

**Genre (Optional):** Lore dropping?

**Page Layout (Optional):** Standard entry for floors

**Elevator Pitch:** The basics of this floor is the explanation for lack of entities, specifically, [insanities](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-15|). The lack of entities in its whole is definitely present in this article, and this'll provide a minor explanation for this. The floor will be largely harmless if you've brought almond water from the original 178. As it is very difficult to access this floor by becoming an insanity, due to an abundance of almond water, and lack of things to make you go insane, this floor will have little insanities, even less of which will be hostile. There will be other entities as well, specifically, any entities that wander into level 178. To enter, swim down one of the springs, making you fall through to level 178.1. To exit, find a ladder (which I plan to add to this floor), and go back through. Without a ladder, even crawling onto an entity will not be able to make you go back, meaning the ladder will be your key back. I plan on making the ladders somewhat difficult to find. For another, a new object will be located here, known as "cashew water." Cashew water looks, smells, and taste the exact same as almond water, but with newer properties. For one, entities can "sense" cashew water, and will strive to go near it. If cashew water comes into contact with any entity, they typically become improvements of their original selves. The exception is insanities, but only ones located inside 178.1. If an insanity comes into contact with cashew water whilst inside 178.1, they will undergo extreme pain, and then become non-hostile versions of themselves, similar to facelings from level 11. If outside 178.1, they will simply become stronger versions of themselves, like other entities. If it comes into contact with wanderers, it will cause extreme pain, and if drank, your insanity will rapidly deplete, causing a high chance of turning into an insanity yourself. Cashew water is found rarely in some of the springs on 178.1, and entities will hang below it, unable to reach it. Similar to leaving, to obtain cashew water, get a ladder and any container for liquids, and obtain some.

**Central Narrative:** Like stated above, 178 lacks entities, so this will be the floor where the insanities go. Whenever someone goes insane, they are sent to this floor via no-clip. This floor will be an upside down version of the original floor 178, defying some laws of gravity/physics. This floors main narrative is providing an explanation for a complete lack of entities on this floor. Beyond that, cashew water adds some interesting mechanics to pre-existing entities around.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** Mainly, the explanation for the lack of entities on this floor. Cashew water adds on, and potential for recovery of entities could be explored.

**Additional Notes:** Quick note, the floor name is just gonna be the original floor names backward, supporting the inverse floor idea. Huge thanks to @bread, @liitausy, and @vivamusludio on discord for ideas pertaining to cashew water.
