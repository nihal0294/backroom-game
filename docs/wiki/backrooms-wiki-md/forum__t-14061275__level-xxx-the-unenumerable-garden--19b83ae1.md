Level XXX - "The Unenumerable Garden"

12 Apr 2021 09:16

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre (Optional):** Horror, I guess, since it's a paranormal space.

**Elevator Pitch:** It's a basic countryside house garden. A bench, trees, a chicken run, a trampoline, a shed, a pond an a vegetable patch, but everything has a strong red and orange hue. There are trees around the edge of the garden and high walls, though there are houses around it, all of which have the same colours. The garden is situated on a downwards sloping hill, however, and at the bottom of the hill it is connected to a perfect replica of the garden. The garden will loop forever. However, at any point in time, if you turn around, you will be the position you are in on the first garden (e.g if you are on the trampoline on your 18th garden, turning to face the entrance will reveal you are only on the trampoline of the first garden from the entrance.)

**Central Narrative:** None

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** It is a somewhat normal looking area, being a seemingly friendly garden with no entities, only being made abnormal by it's strange colouring and it's main catch, being that it functions on a non-euclidean space and is infinitely looping.

**Additional Notes:** This is my first layer idea and I would very much like criticism on it :)

Maybe they all tell a story about the level?
