Making a completely new theme is difficult for those that don't know how to code. What I recommend is finding a theme you like already and play with the colors. My [author page](navyeod-24.md) and my [group's page](the-eternal-repository.md) both use themes already on the site, but I've played around with them and made them different.

If you have any questions let me know. I recommend looking at the ones I provided and peek into the source code.
