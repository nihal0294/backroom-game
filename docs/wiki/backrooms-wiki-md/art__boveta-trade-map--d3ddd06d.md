# Boveta Trade Map

rating: +21[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

![map.png](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/art:boveta-trade-map/map.png)  
  

When traversing the Backrooms, it is important to know where you are and where you are going. Thus, the Republic of Boveta Department of Infrastructure has prepared this map for your reference. The map labels important Bovetan trade routes, which contain some amount of Bovetan presence. However, do be warned that just because there is a trade route doesn't mean it is safe to traverse some of the levels. Bovetan trade convoys are equipped with specialized equipment to survive these levels, equipment that most civilians and wanderers do not have. Thus, we labelled the survival difficulty of the levels so you can choose the safest path.
