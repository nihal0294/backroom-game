02 Feb 2025 14:12

Someone else is already rewriting Level 13. If you wish to continue with this, you must change the level to fit in an empty space. This won't necessarily ruin the concept, though.

Some suggestions and critique:  
"Level 13 is Level 14" is inaccurate, and is supposed to be written as "Level 13 is the 14th level", reflecting position instead of identification. This opening is often considered redundant altogether, though.  
"The disappearance of this famous level impacted the MEG" how so? I would elaborate on this, maybe explaining how the level was beneficial prior to its erasure. This would work better as the focus of the whole article, actually giving it some meaning within the rest of the Backrooms lore.  
"Now the level no longer exists, it will cease to exist forever" Which one? Does it already not exist, or will it stop existing in the future?  
Doing all of this would make the page much less boring than just "the level is gone".  
You may also want to use a grammar checker like Quillbot to make the writing of the level more consistent.

So, really, this page needs a complete expansion and overhall in all regards. Putting everything into "this level is gone now" results in an unsatisfactory and unfulfilling read. Adding more information all around (and especially about the effects on everyone that a whole level disappearing would cause) would, for one, fit it better into the lore, and two, make the page more interesting altogether. I may check on this again if these changes are made.
