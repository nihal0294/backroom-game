Idea: Level-XX; The Wine Cellar

17 Feb 2021 02:35

So, this is my first idea, and I'm relatively new here, but I'm basing this off exactly what the summary says. My grandparents' hotel, a place I swear is haunted. Lots of large, empty rooms, and there have been ghost sightings in the wine cellar.

Basically, this seems like the most pleasant of the Backrooms, being where you can just rest and relax with no worries. There's even a receptionist. There are food and drinks. Great, right? As long as you ignore the ghosts, it is…

Except not really. See, the receptionist? Is not what they appear. They're actually unlike every other entity on the floor in being an actively malevolent, sapient creature with an agenda all on its own. It lures you in and keeps you there, siphoning off your hopes, your dreams, your fears, everything, until you're ripe for feeding. After all, the food and drink come from somewhere, right? And those ghosts? People who tried to escape after seeing through the trap but failing, kept there by the receptionist. And it's not even a case of doing this over again and trying again, because you only get one chance to escape, and then boom, that's it, the receptionist grabs you once you fail and leaves you begging for a chance to become the food and drink, if only so that you end up dead.

What do you guys think?

This sounds cool, actually. From what I understand, the idea is that you'd rather choose death rather than whatever fate the receptionist might provide. Something like that can really show how dire the situation is, and plus, if you're going to base the appearance of this room on a place you've been to in real-life, you can get pretty creative and/or go into detail.

You could try to elaborate why the receptionist is like they are, considering every other creature seems to be docile when compared to the receptionist.

I'm actually going to leave much of the receptionist's backstory unexplained and indicate that it's just doing this out of very human reasons; hunger, a desire for power, control, ego, and ultimately, sadism. And yes, this is basically a "damned if you do, damned if you don't" scenario, where you absolutely need to leave before the receptionist gets their claws into you.

I've decided that the only way out is through the wine cellar, and again, you have one chance to get out before the receptionist catches you.
