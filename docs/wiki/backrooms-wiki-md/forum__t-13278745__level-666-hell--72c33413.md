Level 666 - "Hell"

29 Apr 2020 20:48

This level would essentially be a maze inhabited by many demonic creatures/entities and scorching temperatures. There would be frequent lava pits and caverns.

The main feature would be that the only people sent there are those that murdered other Wanderers in cold blood. When the people sent to the Level spend long enough there, they begin to decay into a 'demon'.

if something like hell existed in the backrooms, specifically for the sinners who inhabit the backrooms, it shouldn't be just the standard depiction of hell. It should be way more unique!

with that being said though, i do think some kind of awful afterlife could be done well in the backrooms, but really when you look at it, the backrooms pretty much *is* hell. it would make more sense if the horrible people become entities.
