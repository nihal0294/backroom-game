Bident's lore will be expanded upon in future articles, and yep, it does have to do with the Gods of Griots. (Also, you may have noticed there's currently a dead link to Level 799 — that link will be filled in next week, probably!)

Also, fun fact: A lot of my ideas go through heavy revisions and changes in my head before they're something I can actually write. The design for this creature originally came from me seeing the word "bident" (aka a 2 pronged spear), and thinking, "Hmm, what if 'Joe Bident', a 2-headed version of president Joe Biden?"

Also also, I think I'm going to start explaining the meaning behind the numbers I use in my articles in the author post, if there is a meaning, because that would be fun, I think: this one is 202 because Bident kind of has a "two" theme going on, so I decided to use two twos.
