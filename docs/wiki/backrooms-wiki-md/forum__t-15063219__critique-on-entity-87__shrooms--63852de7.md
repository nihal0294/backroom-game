"entity number:" capitalization. entity and number here should be capital.

"moist" Would be better as *Moist areas*

"moist levels of the backrooms " Backrooms should be capital

" moist levels of the backrooms with a height of 4 meters." Confusing. Having a with here implies the two sentences are connected; however, they are quite unrelated. I would put the bit about the height after this "Shroomz resemble mushrooms in various ways" and reword it too *Shroomz are typically around 4 meters tall.*

but this is believed to be a convenience" Confusing wording. Convenience typically relates to comfort so unless you are implying that they look like mushrooms for comfort, you may want to find a more suitable term.

" hounds. The umbrella of a male Shroomz has bright, vibrant colors to attract mates." The flow here is poor. It rapidly switches into another topic without really much transition and it ends up feeling unnatural.

"Males can also attract mates by using their rectangular feet to tap dance." A bit of tonal whiplash here. It goes from "entity that drinks human blood" immediately into "funny tap dancing mushroom" in the matter of a few sentences.

"is more than one male trying to win a female over all of the males will enter a dance contest of sorts to see who can dance the longest." Again, the tonal whiplash going from drinking human blood to funny tap dancing mushrooms sorta just makes this article read like a joke article which might be a problem if you want an article to be more serious.

"The winner gets to mate with the female. These contests can last from a few minutes to weeks." You should reverse the order of these sentences.

"shroomz" Inconsistent capitals

There are more instances of poor flow throughout the article

" they can turn into an adult at any second." How does this work? It makes little sense and should really be explained better.

"and have no resemblance to ears at all; how they function is unknown. " So how do they know that those things are ears?

" unknown. If you see or hear a shroomz try and sneak around it." more bad flow, uses second person point of view, and should be in the do's and dont's section.

The section about the 6 arms lie feels a lot like fluff.

The hide block show block should be an author collapsible
