mystery multiverse canon idea

17 Aug 2022 10:47

so this was the theory I wrote out. I am still new to the lore and canon stuff behind the backrooms, so this might be a standalone canon or it could slot in with other POI/GOI/canons. I hope people will see this, enjoy it and please give some criticism. =)

"If it's a multiverse, then there are infinite versions of yourself. Therefore infinite universes, infinite where you exist, and infinite where you have never existed and never will. One version of each individual across the multiverse at any one time must reside in the liminal frontier known as the backrooms to ensure the stability of the individual's presence in the remaining infinite universes.

However, upon an individual exiting the backrooms, where they would return to their original universe, or dying trying to survive, resulting in permanent death, an alternative version of that individual from another universe will be randomly selected to replace the original person in the backrooms, fulfilling the role of ensuring stability for their remaining multiversal counterparts. This is where the instance of 'noclipping' can take effect, as the unlucky individual would just randomly glitch out of reality.

But what if the barrier between the multiverse and the backrooms became thin, and more alternate versions of the unlucky original were unfortunate enough to end up in this mono-yellow hell? Would they be willing to work alongside themselves, or would the survivability decrease, as one individual will do anything for their survival alone?"
