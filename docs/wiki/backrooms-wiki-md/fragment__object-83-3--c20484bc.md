Robovac Sightings: What You Need To Know

Over the past few weeks, the growing number of robovac sightings across the Backrooms has dominated the headlines. Here's what's been going on.

Robovacs have never been an uncommon sight within most settlements. While Backrooms Robotics is often regarded with suspicion, the presence of Robovacs in settlements has long been tolerated by most major groups due to the convenience they provide. In recent weeks, though, sightings of Robovacs have become increasingly common, and many wanderers have begun to express their concern. Protestors have formed a picket outside Backrooms Robotics' Level 11 office demanding an explanation.

"I didn't have any worries about Robovacs in the past," said one protestor. "They helped keep our bases clean, they were unobtrusive, and they didn't go anywhere private. But that's changed now. I see dozens of the things every day, and I caught one trying to follow me into my flat. Something's clearly gone wrong with them, and we should get an explanation from Backrooms Robotics now - rather than an apology after they end up like RoboPets."

Prior to the recent increase of Robovac sightings, most bases reported encountering a single Robovac once or twice a month. By the start of this month, over a dozen Robovacs are sighted daily within and around bases. Many have been sighted attempting to enter restricted areas of bases.

Relationships between many groups and Backrooms Robotics continue to fray in the face of what is regarded by some as a blatant attempt at mass surveillance and by others as a malfunctioning product left unrepaired. The B.N.T.G. recently announced that they would temporarily withdraw Backrooms Robotics products from sale, and the M.E.G. has issued an official statement advising wanderers to avoid using Backrooms Robotics tech. Both groups have signed a joint statement urgently requesting an explanation from Backrooms Robotics.

A B.N.T.G. spokesman stated, "Backrooms Robotics has done a lot to win our trust over the years. They have been a reliable business partner and we have been proud to stock their products in our numerous stores. That is why we are greatly dismayed by the recent behaviour exhibited by one of their products and why we are deeply concerned by their failure to address our concerns. For the safety of our customers, we feel that we have no alternative but to withdraw all their products from sale until an explanation is provided."

The Daily Rooms has reached out to Backrooms Robotics for comment on the situation, but no response has so far been provided.
