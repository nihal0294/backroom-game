level 450 "unfinished"

19 Jul 2021 21:03

# page type:

a level description with bases and communities, entrances and exits

# genre:

horror

# page layout:

basic, with description, bases and communities, entrances and exits. an additional document will describe all glitches observed in the level.

# elevator pitch:

the level is another basic infinite apartment level, but everything is unfinished and glitchy. the walls, floors, and ceilings are placeholder textures. parts of the level are completely missing, and no-clipping is much more common in the level. the entities are glitched and broken. smilers face lights flicker on and off. hounds pathfinding is broken. deathmoths are present in the level, but there are no deathmoth hives. skin stealers wearing a human's skin just have a placeholder texture. performing the mountain king ritual brings you to an untextured room with 4 walls. the only way to get into the level is by no-clipping.

# central narrative:

level 450 is a basic level turned on its head with glitches. the log of every glitch present in level 450 will serve to expand the concept of the level and make it more interesting.

# hook/attention grabber:

its a level expanding on the concept of the backrooms being a simulated/video game type of thing. by adding a level based on the concept of unfinished games, it can add another element to the video game aspect.
