Core Concept:  
The Musician is a tall, slender entity that can be found in the majority of the rooms, including his own level (not made yet). He is docile, or passive unless provoked or angered. In 95% of encounters, they can be seen wearing a sort of black pinstripe suit with a tophat and many other themed items. Occasionally, he can be seen wearing suits of other patterns or colors, including floral, star-themed, striped, red, and many others. On very select encounters, he has worn a different, more casual style altogether(?).

You can tell when he's near based off of the sound of playing music. It's very important to pay attention to what music is playing, as it's possible it could be another entity pretending to be him (based off of another entity that I'm debating making, but idk yet). When encountered, he has a very formal and polite way of speaking and interacting with wanderers and anyone else who may be in the Backrooms. He will help those he encounters in any way he can. To date, this has included:

- Providing them with Almond Water or food of some kind
- Leading them to an exit or secret room that would help them or be safe to stay in
- Protecting people from dangerous entities

and several others.  
His constant music has been found to soothe certain dangerous entities, making them docile or passive. Some entities will be deterred by the music, avoiding him entirely. On the occasion that a dangerous entity cannot be soothed or deterred by his music, he will protect one through other means. He is acutely aware of where said entities are at most, if not all times, and is able to direct wanderers and personnel through avoiding them or taking protective measures against them. He will eventually leave after helping someone for a while, but there is a chance they will encounter him again.  
He seems to have a very wide knowledge of the Backrooms and things within it and is very willing to answer as many questions about it as possible.
