I don't like the change with the enigmatic categories

16 May 2025 19:14

Putting Enigmatic level/entities to normal ones just doesn't really fit as being normal. The mods didn't even just pay attention to the characteristics those have. But don't get me wrong I think some levels do deserve to be put as normal ones because they are just levels that are the same as the other normal ones. But I'm not happy about a few levels (not sure about the entities) not most of them

And for the definition change. It's just stupidest things I've ever heard. ''Unnumbered levels/entities are put on the unnumbered section because they are unnumbered or don't need a number". Like c'mon man at least change the definition from enigmatic levels/entities being a rumor to how do they work why are the enigmatic levels/entities put as enigmatic and why are they so vague. Speaking of vague I remember the mods saying the reason of why the definition was changed because it was too vague. Well guess what you guys made it way more vaguer

And for the rooms, like it's really cool concept about independent rooms but isn't it too harsh to make them to be forced too be put as levels? And I have a similar argument as the enigmatic ones. Because they are to small too be a level.

And I EXPECT the people to tell me that the change was needed but guess what, it's becoming fucking tiering to hear the same answer. Just change it again but to make more sense and put some levels back as enigmatic. AND I don't think that I am the only one who has this problem and I think that there are some people who have the same problem as I have
