There’s an ongoing reform to the branch system, so becoming an official site might not be relevant anymore. They will be replaced by Linked Language Wikis, which will have roughly the following requirements, with undecided numbers in brackets:

> [some amount] pages.  
> If a language wiki does not host translations, but only hosts originals it only needs [smaller amount] pages.  
> [some amount] members.  
> If the site has any NSFW content it has to be behind adult content warnings so our <18 members cannot access it if they visit your wiki. The age limit of the site need to reflect this as well.  
> The wiki must not be obviously broken in some way e.g. a broken home page.  
> The wiki must have existed continuously for at the very least 3 months.
