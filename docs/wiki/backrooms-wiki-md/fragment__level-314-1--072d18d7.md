Ever gotten confused by non-Euclidean geometry? Don't worry, we got you covered with our state-of-the-art apartments and convenient access to major levels.

# Description

Level 314 is the first artificial level in the backrooms, made using an experimental device called the Euclid device as a part of the Eden Project. Our experts at the C.T.G. figured out a way to create new geometry out of nothing! Yup nothing! As many may remember, the previous state of the level was literally filled with a vacuum. With the Euclid Device, we were not only able to procedurally generate an infinitely tall apartment building with a diameter of 100m, but also create custom exits to all major levels. We have made efforts to ensure that the geometry of the level is completely stable, meaning you do not have to worry about no-clipping or glitchy environments. We have even accommodated the level with a natural day-night cycle lasting 24 hours[2](javascript:;)!

### The Community Center

The community center occupies the first 5 floors, allowing inhabitants to interact with each other and access other levels. The dining area is located on the second floor, and we provide unlimited free almond water and food for residents.

### Apartments

Our level consists of 4 different categories of apartments for all budgets, each coming with purchasable Bestrooms™ furniture from Backrooms Remodeling Co.

#### Category 1

Category 1 flats are the smallest but also cheapest, while only containing 1 room, it does offer all needed features for basic survival[3](javascript:;). We also provide a option to purchase the [Level 0](level-0.md) style wallpaper we bought from the C.T.G. It unfortunately doesn't have any windows, so you won't be able to enjoy the view outside. On the plus side, these flats are closest to the elevators and come at the lowest price of only 250,000 presses.

#### Category 2

Category 2 flats have 5 rooms and, more importantly, a balcony! Allowing you to enjoy the views outside. Category 2 flats are on higher floors, which creates a beautiful view of the forest below on clear days and a serene view of the clouds on cloudy days. And don't worry, our innovative elevator system can bring you down to the ground in just 10 minutes, all for just 620,000 presses!

#### Category 3

Have you ever wanted to have a multi-story apartment in the Backrooms? Now you can get one for just 1,400,000 presses with Category 3 flats. Featuring 10 rooms spanning 2 floors, connected with stairs. You can now accommodate your entire family (if they noclipped here with you, of course)! Category 3 also offers free Bestrooms™ wallpaper of any design that you want. Of course, we also include a balcony to let you enjoy the view. Category 3 and above purchasers will enjoy free food and water at the community center.

#### Category 4

Too lazy to share a floor with others? You're in luck because Category 4 flats allow you to buy an entire floor on Level 314—yes, the entire $2500πm^2$[4](javascript:;). You can customize the design of your apartment however you like and even purchase multiple floors and combine them if $2500πm^2$ somehow isn't enough for you. We reserved the first 200 floors for Category 4 flats so you can get the quickest access to the ground floor and the best views. Category 4 apartment owners also get access to our exclusive Premium Club in the community center. All of this comes at the price tag of just 6,000,000 presses.

# Entrances And Exits

#### Entrances

- Entering a door labelled 314 on the 1st floor on -823rd Avenue, 32nd Street on [Level 11](level-11.md) leads here.
- Entering a black door that opens to a wall on [Level 4](level-4.md) will noclip you to this level.
- Entering a door that opens to a dark space on [Level 1](level-1.md) leads to this level.
- Walking down a designated path marked by the M.E.G. on [Level 39](level-39.md) leads to this level.
- The [Hub](the-hub.md) door is always unlocked; walking through will lead to [The Hub](the-hub.md).

#### Exits

- Going back to any entrance to the level will lead back to where you came from.
- Walking into a grassy room on the level will noclip you to [Level 369](level-369.md).
- There is a small wooden shack near the main building that leads to [Level 10](level-10.md).
