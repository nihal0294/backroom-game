First critique really.

I think the level is well written and described, though I feel that despite its large level of description is rather lacking in unique classrooms (excluding the principals office or the one full of partygoers.)

What I mean is that a high school (I am Canadian so I may be incorrect) usually has;

- Woodworking class, which is full of woodworking equipment
- A gym
- Teacher's lounge
- Cooking class
- Janitorial closet
- Library
- Cafeteria
- Science lab

To name a few.  
I think it could probably be done in a tabview, which states the different class types.

That's just a suggestion though, and otherwise the level is quite well made.
