**Level 655** is the 656th Level of the Backrooms.

# **Description**

**Level 655** is a tiny cottage with a garden surrounded by wodden fence, repeating infinitely on both sides of the road, the road is also repeating, making infinite loop. The houses are randomized in furniture, but they are always identical in structure. One person reported seeing huge mansion in this level, but this is unconfirmed and probably fake. The gardens are overgrown with lavender, that is safe for consumption and has the effect of almond water. Some people say that there are hoards of entities at the roads, but they can't enter the gardens, others say that level is completly devoid of harmful entities. However, one entity inhabits **level 655** she calls herself **"Lavender"**.

# **Lavender**

The only entity in the level, called Lavender, is a women wearing purple dress **(made out of violets)** and shoes, with violet-green hair **(growing like the plants in her garden)**, seems to be the owner of the houses. She's very friendly, **(because she has to be punished)** and she help's all wanderers that need help, she also will let everyone stay in one of houses as long as they want, even forever. She made the only rule in the level: don't take photos. If someone will break this rule, Lavender will lead him to the exit, and will attack him everytime he will enter the level again. **(as her should)** She looks like normal human, but she dosen't seems to show standard life functions. She can only move andt talk and "create" an exit from **Level 655** anywhere, **(by creating a no-clip border)** and grow plants nearby.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

## Lavender's lovers

- Open for new members.
- Live forever in **level 655** in houses close to each other.
- Has 36 members.
- Open to trade
- The founder has a book titled "The backrooms ultimate guide" but he doesn't show it to anyone.

# **Entrances And Exits**

### Entrances

The only method to enter **Level 655** is to enter a cottage in any city level, and sleep in it. You will wake up in the same cottage in **Level 655**.

### Exits

You can just ask Lavender to lead you to exit, after exiting you will find yourself in the outside of the cottage that you used to enter.
