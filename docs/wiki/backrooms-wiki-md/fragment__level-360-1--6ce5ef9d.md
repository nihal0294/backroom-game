Long Ago

#### ***Time Died***

Here

---

Funny story, right: I was walking up the [Forsaken Stairs](level-922.md). The actual part of me walking up is not funny. The word ***exhausting*** came around rather often. But that place **kept** me alive: Nothing solid for 5 fucking weeks, and I'm still walking up like a tin soldier.  
But one day. Ooh, just that one day I tripped hard and came crashing down the few dozen steps I've climbed until suddenly I was lying on a solid bed of stone. When I got up, I saw arches, and so many, my head spun like a damn hurricane. I walked around, bored **Out Of My Mind.** Tried to loop back to the **Entrance**: Instead, I faced a set of stairs that I've never seen in my 32 years of life.  
Got nothin' to lose, so I went in deeper. **The Best Mistake I've Ever Made.**

I Saw Him. Saw ***Myself***.

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/fragment:dr-andrew-pie-2-offset/Sighting.jpg)

My Reflection. Past, Present and ***Future***.

*My Old Self Whimpered In Fear.* I ***Rejoice*** In Memory Of This Moment.

It- ***I*** Came Closer.

An Otherworldly Haze Floating Around Its Silhouette.

***Something Lesser Than Fog, Yet Thicker Than Smoke.***

My skin crawled like the walls had fingernails.

And then, gone. As if I never existed.

Just then…

***Came The Screams***

The Walls Were Screaming, But The Floors Were Whispering.

Whispering Secrets Untold, And Forgotten By ***Time*** Itself.

I could hear the breath, ***my*** breath. *Labored. **Punctured.*** Like I had forgotten how lungs work. The eyes? They weren’t mine anymore.

***They Were Filled With Time, And Time Itself Was Raving.***

***My Old Self Gazed In Terror At The Wraith In Front.* The Very Moment Of  
*My Salvation*.**
