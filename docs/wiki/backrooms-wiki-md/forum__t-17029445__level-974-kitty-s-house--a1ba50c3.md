-1, bordering no-vote

20 Jun 2025 16:21

I think the premise for Level 974 has potential to be emotionally evocative, especially when the nostalgia of the reader is catered to. The imagery of a pink and innocent house with a strangely friendly horror figure inside of it is a very good idea to work of off, but you didn't capitalize on making the piece appeal to the reader's emotions and didn't put enough care into making the page a cohesive one. You just stuck a grocery list of things people associate with good parts of their past without putting in any work to make the reader actually immersed in the homely smells or familiar sights of the level—the poor implementation of clinical tone only furthers this issue. Secondly, the pacing of the work had me struggling to make any connections and came off as very jarring; it was very difficult to keep up with the article due to it bouncing from subject to subject without much care. Both of these issues combined make giving this page anything other than a **down-vote** feel like I'm giving too much credit to the original idea rather than your stab at it.
