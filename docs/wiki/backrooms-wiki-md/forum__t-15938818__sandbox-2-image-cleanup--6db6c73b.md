Sandbox 2 Image Cleanup

04 Jun 2023 12:32

Hey everyone, just letting you know I will be trying my best to go through Sand2 pages and delete any images exceeding 800kb for the sake of freeing up storage! (And also to comply with a new tech policy we're working on releasing soon.)

If you know you have images on your pages that exceed this filesize, please shrink them down at your earliest convenience, or better yet, simply delete them if you're not using them!

We'll keep a log of all the files we delete, so if you end up missing something from your page, reach out to us!
