Entity 109: Hodgepodges

23 Jul 2022 04:19

Depression. Anxiety. *Madness*. Hodgepodges are tall, yellow entities that only reside on the most liminal and childhood-themed levels. Frightening one is likely an early grave. They're uncontrollable, reckless, and want nothing more than to make it stop. Have fun reading, critters, and greenlighters all! (Just remember to stay quiet.)
