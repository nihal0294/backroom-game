Level -Fun is a copy of Level Fun but black and white, but with many different things:

- Instead of just a few entities, every entity may be here, except some rarer than others. All level exclusive entities from other levels aren’t here.
- If you wander far enough, you will be sent to level “You Cheated.”
- Some random areas could have no ceiling, but if you climb out, you’ll be sent to Level 72 and on rare occurrence Level 666
- Killing a hound will send one to Level 0; quite a reset.
- You enter from a door with a black and white picture on any level; this picture is commonly Level Fun, but could be any picture.
- Surviving for 88 days without leaving will send one to the Frontrooms, however the entities get stronger and more aggressive every day, so I don’t know how you go about surviving for that amount of time.
- Level exclusive entity called ‘Insanity’ which is a wanderer in which went insane here and somehow got power over the level. It looks like a normal person but with clawed out eyes, and it is very powerful (being able to adjust entity count or own stamina/strength at will.

It was a normal day; the sun was shining bright and vividly, its colors blinding. As John went to work, he had a feeling that someone—something was watching him. It is unknown how this happened or which entity it was, but what is known is that the entity started chasing him and eventually caught him, but instead of killing John, the entity somehow forced him to no-clip into Level -Fun. Yeah, Insanity caught him, he died, but the partycreators at the level somehow resurrected him as a partygoer. He couldn’t control himself anymore, his story was over.
