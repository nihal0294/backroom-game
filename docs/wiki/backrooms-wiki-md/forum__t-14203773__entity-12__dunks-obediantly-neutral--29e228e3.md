Entity 12: Dunks, Obediantly Neutral.

22 Sep 2021 03:13

These are Dunks! Strange creatures with a simple taming process, they follow any commands told to them by their owners. They carry out orders to the best they can, even if they must give up their life. Strangely, they do not attack anyone with an older appearance, this is tied to their discovery. They attack the average opponent by using their horns and grabbing them with their arm-shaped trunk. However, they are actually very weak as they only have a single weapon to use.

These entities do not pick favorites and are entirely neutral, they are somewhat built to serve their owners.

This page is a bit long as I wanted to plot down all of my ideas, but hopefully it is not too cluttered with information.
