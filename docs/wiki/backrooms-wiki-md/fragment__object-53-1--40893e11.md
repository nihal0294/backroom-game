# fragment:object-53-1

# 14th of February

# 2025

Rain yawned.  
  
It had only just turned noon and already he felt like he'd done three all-nighters in a row.  
  
At least he was resting, which was nice, although it was hard to say whether he could actually sleep like this.  
  
Rain was vaguely lying on the flat's sofa, his head resting on a pillow wedged on the armrest.  
  
His partner Lucius was lying on his chest, wrapping his toned arms around Rain's waist in a tight grip.  
  
Rain had folded one arm over Lucius's neck as much as he was able, and thus a cuddle was created.  
  
It was very one-sided, but neither of them really minded.  
  
Lucius had dropped off to sleep almost as soon as they had sat down.  
  
He had been out for most of the day on business, and likely deserved the rest.  
  
Rain curled the young man's fluffy hair in his fingers and contemplated sleep for a moment.  
  
Strangely enough, Lucius could get up almost instantly when called while sleeping, and could seemingly sleep almost anywhere, no matter how uncomfortable.  
  
Rain was starting to think that Lucius was either a really light sleeper or he was putting up a facade most of the time.  
  
Either way Lucius didn't want to disturb him, so Rain just lay there thinking.  
  
After a few minutes of thinking about not a lot, he was thoroughly bored.  
  
Trying to keep as still as possible, Rain reached towards his phone that was on the table next to the sofa.  
  
As soon as his questing fingers touched the case and lifted it gently, it buzzed violently.  
  
Once, twice, three times, the notification sound made Rain's hand jump.  
  
He tried to hold his arm steady, hoping that the movement wouldn't wake Lucius.  
  
Thankfully, he still seemed to be resting peacefully.  
  
Rain tapped quickly through the lock screen and navigated swiftly to Discord.
