There's a fair bit of capitalization issues here:  
- every mention of "The Whiteout" should have both The and Whiteout capitalized  
- every mention of "The Blackout" should have both The and Blackout capitalized and should be bolded  
- the names in the list of Entities found from the first paragraph should be capitalized, and consider cross-linking them to their individual articles  
- the mention of "The Maker" should have Maker capitalized  
- every mention of "The Bride" should have The and Bride capitalized

- The final cross-link to The Whiteout should extend to "The" as well as "Whiteout"

But I enjoy the implied story of The Maker and The Bride, which I think is made much better because of it's vagueness. The overall atmosphere of this level is really nice and eerie, and it's a nice touch to not have The Bride be some sort of murder monster that hunts Wanderers down.

+1, this article is underrated
