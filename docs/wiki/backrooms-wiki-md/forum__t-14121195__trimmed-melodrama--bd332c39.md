Here’s another piece made for the ARENA storyline, an elegy to be exact. Something I’ve always found off putting, yet oddly intriguing, was the concept of prepared graves. Preparing for your own death seems odd, as it implies you already know how you’re gonna die. I ran with that for this piece, and I think it turned out somewhat well.

Special thanks to for their crit+greenlight!

Melodrama is a fine line, think about that.
