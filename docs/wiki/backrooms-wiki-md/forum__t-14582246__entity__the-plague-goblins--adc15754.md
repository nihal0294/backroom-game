Entity: The Plague Goblins

28 Mar 2022 15:04

**Page Type:** Entity

**Page Layout:** The layout is typical of most entity entries.

**Elevator Pitch:** A fairly common species of entity that is located in the majority of levels, preferably ones with high populations. They're cat-sized creatures covered in dark matted fur with large corvid beaks concealed beneath a wooden/leather mask vaguely, with their pupilless yellow eyes emanating from within. The entities have 4 long avian legs which allow them to grasp and manipulate objects as well as escape from enemies with ease. They have fairly long dog-like tails covered in sparse fur. They tend to wear tattered capes for reasons unknown, possibly for insulation, to hide, or just basic modesty.

These creatures are highly social and incredibly intelligent, traveling in packs from 5 to 10 on average. They have a tendency to scavenge off of corpses of survivors and entities alike for supplies, food, and almond water. Sometimes they will steal from survivors utilizing coordinated strategies to distract, restrain, and escape, but almost never inflict lethal harm. They are capable of producing portals to escape to unknown places. However, you can bargain with them for items they collect in exchange for almond water.

**Central Narrative:** The Plague Goblins on the surface appear as typical pests and harmful nuisances for explorers traveling through the backrooms. However, there is far more to them than meets the eye as they seem to have a deeper understanding of the backrooms than most other beings and seemingly can provide you with items almost impossible to find otherwise.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** They are strange quirky entities that add a bit of levity and mischief in the otherwise surreal and horrifying backrooms. Ranging from incredible allies to a massive thorn in your side depending on your interactions with them. They are basically some of the most human entities, establishing communities, fighting other entities, and being surprisingly empathetic.

**Additional Notes:** Portals are made by a designated "shaman" of the squad who draws an invisible circle on the wall.
