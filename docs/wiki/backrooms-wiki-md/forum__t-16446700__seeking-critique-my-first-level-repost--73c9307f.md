A couple things I'd like to say:

[– hide block](javascript:;)

> - Various beverages from The Frontrooms brand, such as Yuanqi Forest and Coca-Cola.
> - Various brands of candy from The Frontrooms, such as White Rabbit and Hsu Fu Chi.
>
> 2. There may even be some beverages that are not available or have been discontinued in The Frontrooms, such as Feichang Cola and Jinping Valley Soda.

Assuming that most of the users here on the main site are native English speakers, I don't believe most will know what any of these are (besides Coca-Cola, obviously). As such, I'd appreciate it if you had an in-article explanation for what these products are.

> - Converted into safe Cashew Water.

I'm going to assume that this is meant to say that you can find safe and consumable Cashew Water on the shelves, so I'd suggest you change the wording a little.

> In this level, stairs or elevators rarely[3] appear

Move the footnote to after "appear"

> In this level, a few Facelings affected by the 11th effect will respawn

Firstly, I'd suggest changing it to "the 11 effect." Secondly, I'd prefer a more in-depth explanation about these specific Facelings. You say that a few of them respawn, implying that there's only a select few present, and you specifically state that their appearances don't change. I'd assume that if they come back whenever they die and look the exact same each time, the M.E.G. would have ample opportunity to document these specific instances. Maybe give some names, or describe their appearance. How many are there? You could do a lot with just this part.

You forgot to remove the placeholder in the Bases, Outposts, and Communities section.

Who is Peter? If you want to specifically mention him, at least give a reason *why* he's specifically mentioned.

Also, given that this level is classed as Habitable, I'd suggest that you add an actual, large community, not just two M.E.G. Outposts and whatever Peter's doing.

> After repenting for all your mistakes in Level 999, you may either proceed to Level 1000 or remain in the current level.

what

> There is a small probability of arriving at the current level when cutting through any level with a number ending in double zeros.

I get what you were going for with this, but it's a bit too wordy.  
Add more entrances, preferably some with more typical methods of entry.  
Remove the "More entrances/exits need to be added" things.

> The image is a self-portrait.

I assume that this is meant to be "the image was taken by me", so I'd recommend changing it to that.
