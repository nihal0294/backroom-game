If it helps, I'll give some suggestions.

[+ show block](javascript:;)

[– hide block](javascript:;)

> The majority of the Level is filled waist-deep in a dark fog that cannot be seen through

Change underlined section to "covered in a dark, waist-high fog"

> **with the only exception being crudely made shacks** that can be seen in the Level. These wooden cabins can be seen throughout the level

Not sure what the shacks are an exception *to*. Maybe change to "with the only visible structures being crudely made shacks." Also, remove either "that can be seen in the Level", or change "in" to "throughout" and remove the second "can be seen throughout the Level".

> **can be seen throughout the level in varying conditions** that can, depending on the condition, be used as a temporary shelter.

If you pick the second option above, then replace it with "can be found" so it says "can be found in varying conditions." Also, remove "depending on the condition".

> According to first-hand encounters with the wooden cabins by wanderers, the cabins are

Replace with "accounts"

> The temperatures within Level 305 can be minus five degrees Celsius to minus twelve degrees Celsius.

Change "can be" with "range from". Use "negative" instead of "minus".

> The Entities within Level 305 consist of Smilers and Skin-Stealers.

Add "found" before "within". Possibly change "consist of" to "include".

> They have been seen commonly

Change to "They have commonly been seen"

> similar to tall grass

Change "similar" to "similarly"  
For the excerpts, don't use blockquotes *and* quotation marks. Use one or the other.

> Entities I've seen throughout this Level, have seemed to keep a distance from humans and, appear to stalk via the fog that is constantly located throughout this Level.

Remove the comma after the first "Level." Change "have seemed to keep a distance" to "seem to keep their distance." Either remove the second comma or move it to after "humans." Add "them" after "stalk." Change "that is constantly located throughout this Level" to "that's found throughout this Level."

> hanging off the branches of the tree.

Remove "of the tree."

> nearly instantly the tree will begin

Move "nearly instantly" to after "will".

> as a warning for approximately 10 seconds

This is fine as is, but I feel the word flow could be improved by changing it to "for approximately 10 seconds, as if it is a warning."

> When this time is up, the tree will

Change "when this time is up" to "Following this"

> If the wanderer still proceeds to approach

Change to "If the wanderer doesn't back away"

> inside the trunk,

Change to a period.

> then the tree will proceed to

Change to "The tree will then proceed to"

> a specific range though, I must admit

Put a period after "range," make "though" the beginning of a new sentence, and move the comma to after "admit"

> have reported to be able to hear

Change to "have reported being able to hear"

> a water current, then after walking 10 more meters

Replace the comma with a period and remove "then"

> A wooden bridge circa 1800s appearance crosses the river

Change to "A wooden bridge, appearing to have been constructed in the 1800s, crosses the river"

> who made it to the river had given their first-hand account

Change "had" to "has"

> dissolve and become part of the river as the substance.

Change to "dissolve into the substance and become part of the river."

> A wanderer had consumed the substance from the river. They described the taste as bland and dull, but still horrid. After ten seconds, they described a burning feeling within their throat. At this point, they were unable to speak, so the M.E.G. relied on said wanderers field journal. After another twenty seconds, they describe a sharp amount of stomach pain, and five minutes after consumption, they said they felt like their body was shattering. A puddle of the substance was found next to the notes.

This part is a bit confusing. I can't really tell whether this is supposed to imply that they drank the substance while in M.E.G. custody, or if they had drank it alone and all of this information was gleaned from the journal. Based on the last line, I assume that the latter is the intention, so I'll give what I think is a good rewrite of the paragraph based on that.

"A testimony of a wanderer who claimed to have consumed the substance was found written in a field journal found near the river, next to a puddle of the substance. The wanderer describes the taste as 'bland and dull, but still horrid.' They state that 10 seconds after consuming the substance, they felt a burning sensation in their throat that prevented them from speaking, followed by a sharp stomach pain twenty seconds later. They also describe that, 5 minutes following consumption, they felt as if their body was shattering. The current location of the wanderer, if they are still alive, is unknown."

> Many insist it is being supplied

Change to "One insists that it is being supplied"

> by an unknown source or some kind of Entity or Entities

Remove "an unknown source" and "or." That unknown source is what these hypotheses are *about*.

> More theories speculate of it coming from

Change to "Another theory speculates that it is coming from"

> as if the unknown Level is sentient and

Remove "is sentient and"

> had No-clipped into a dead tree

"No-clipped" shouldn't be capitalized.

> Currently it is assumed that Samantha and Daniel are dead as no reports

Add a comma after "Currently" and after "dead"

> Wandering Level 6 until no-clipping into the floor to enter Level 305.

Change to either "Wander Level 6 until no-clipping into the floor to enter Level 305" or "Wandering Level 6 until no-clipping into the floor will lead to Level 305." I'd suggest the second one for consistency.

> No-clipping into a dead tree on Level 1, Level 9 or Level 11 to enter Level 305.

Same here. Change to either "No-clip into a dead tree on Level 1, Level 9 or Level 11 to enter Level 305" or "No-clipping into a dead tree on Level 1, Level 9 or Level 11 will lead to Level 305." Again, I recommend the second one.

> Finding a hatch on the floor to enter Level 306.

Firstly, I'm not sure if Level 306 existed when you wrote/posted this or if you're planning to make Level 306, but as of writing this, it doesn't, so I'd suggest removing it until the slot is filled. Second, if you decide to keep it, change it to either "Find and enter a hatch on the floor to enter Level 306" or "Finding and entering a hatch on the floor will lead to Level 306."

> No-clipping into a tree within Level 305 can lead to Level 1 or Level 11.

While I know that the word "can" is there to show that this method can result in you ending up in either Level 1 or Level 11, I feel this would be clearer if you word it as "No-clipping into a tree within Level 305 will lead to either Level 1 or Level 11."  
I'd also suggest putting the Entrances and Exits as bulleted lists.

Edit: Thanks for incorporating my suggestions!
