31 Aug 2023 20:01

(So i decided to use this template because i feel like this is pretty useful ^^" !)

**Page Type:** Level.

**Genre:** (Not sure, I would says horror but it's more mysterious… )

**Core Concept:** So this level would be a seemingly at first an infinite walkway made of wood.

The wanderers would appear in a random place in this level.

And since this place is infinite, they would see the wooden walkway going forever in front and behind of them, it's impossible to walk off since if you do that, you would directly pass through the "floor" were the path is, and no one know what happens to the wanderers who walk off the wooden path…

Sometime the path would split, making you chose between different types of wood paths.

The type of wood mean things, like indicating that you will go to a certain level.

if you take the dark wood path to give an example, you would go to a dark level with not much or no light at all.

There would be one type of entities on this wooden path.

I call it the Termitenated, it's a joke with the word "Termite" and the word "Terminated".

(I am also going to present to you this entitie ^^" !)

This insect looks like the termite found in the FrontRoom, but they are much bigger and aggressive. they are also more intelligent, creating paths with the wood they eat, to confuse wanderers, and create trap for them since they could fall through the hole made by the termitenated in the wooden path, and so disappear through the floor instantly!

**Additional Notes:** The mysteries part is that I wanted to point out some things, and make the readers ask the question to themselves:

Where do the different's paths goes to? And what really happen to the ones who get off the path, where do they go? Where does the termitenated entities came from?

Are their a secret ways to explore out of the wooden walkway?

Thank you for reading, I will wait patiently for your critique of my ideas!

Well, since I welcomed you, I reckon I'll give you some critique.  
"So this level would be a seemingly at first an infinite walkway made of wood."  
Do you mean the pathway is infinite, or the whole level? What do you mean by "seemingly"? Are you saying it isn't actually infinite but just extremely large? Or that there is no way to tell for sure that if it is infinite? Perhaps clarify.  
"And since this place is infinite, they would see the wooden walkway going forever in front and behind of them, it's impossible to walk off since if you do that,"  
Okay, so it *is* infinite… I have two conceptional problems with this bit here. First of all, what are you walking off of? The walkway? What's on the side of walkway? Second of all, you say it is impossible to walk off of if you do **that**. What is that? Why does it make it impossible to walk off to the side of the walkway?  
"The type of wood mean things, like indicating that you will go to a certain level"  
No problem with this, I just think it is creative to make the types of wood mean things, which is something I wouldn't have thought to do if I were the one writing this.  
"There would be one type of entities on this wooden path."  
Well, I'm really doing more conceptional critique here, but I really am a stickler for grammar sometimes when it comes to other people's writing (yes, Pen, it's okay for *you* to make misteaks, but not others)(I've ignored a few mistakes you've made above, but I **had** to point out this one in specific for no apparent reason). Ignoring my jokes, I pointed this out simply because this may cause confusion to the reader seeing that you've only mentioned one entity and you should decide this before you get to your draft. If there is only one entity, there is no need to make the "entities" plural.  
"I call it the Termitenated, it's a joke with the word "Termite" and the word "Terminated"."  
Always nice to have a bit of humor around here.  
"This insect looks like the termite found in the FrontRoom, but they are much bigger and aggressive. they are also more intelligent, creating paths with the wood they eat, to confuse wanderers, and create trap for them since they could fall through the hole made by the termitenated in the wooden path, and so disappear through the floor instantly!"  
I'm going to give you some advice that an english teacher gave me: Ask yourself why over and over again like an annoying little kid who got told they couldn't do something until you've explained yourself so much you can't explain yourself anymore. **Why** does this entity do this? Maybe take some different approach than "Hmm, yes, terrifying monster bug that goes around trapping people for no reason and confusing people" After all, you did say they were intelligent, and made it seem like you were saying they did this on purpose. Is it just for their own amusement? Do they not want people in the level?  
Also, you say the make holes in the path for wanderers to fall through, but I'm pretty sure that most people would notice if there was a giant hole in the floor, and would do their best to get around it. Perhaps come up with a way these creatures hide the holes they make from humans?  
Furthermore, if these creatures eat the wood, doesn't that mean it is gone into their digestive system for good? You'll probably have to dive more into their biology in your draft, and how they live, etc.  
"Where do the different's paths goes to? And what really happen to the ones who get off the path, where do they go? Where does the termitenated entities came from? Are their a secret ways to explore out of the wooden walkway?"  
It's fine to try and make your reader ask these questions, but one major problem I have as a reader is a person who tries to make me ask questions and then leave them unanswered. So try to come up with your own responses to these questions and see if you can hint at the answer in your draft.  
Regarding the first question, if the place is infinite wouldn't the paths lead to…other paths? I could also understand if some lead to exits, as you said above in your idea post.  
With the second and third questions, I would just like to reiterate what I said above about trying to provide an answer to them.  
As for the last question, I think this fine and dandy and all, but you said above that you **couldn't** go outside the path, or at least if you did, you would dissapear through the floor. Clarify whether you meant you couldn't go outside or whether you would dissapear for me, please. If you have decide to say you can't go outside at all, you've got to come up with a way to say that you can without contradicting yourself, say that they've found a way to get out and the information above is inaccurate, or hint/say that there is a rumor of a possible way to get out.  
That's pretty much it.  
Good luck,  
~ Pen.
