I'll be completely honest, I feel like the name could use a change. Although, putting that aside and talking about the actual concept, I'd recommend making it a little more unique than them just being researchers. I say this for 2 reasons.

1. It would be called out as an M.E.G. Ripoff. The M.E.G., despite being a group dedicated to leaving, they also very much are dedicated to researching the Backrooms as its main researcher. If you make them too similar, it'd basically just have a new face over an already existing concept.

2. It'd get compared to the S.C.P. foundation. Something we don't need any more of on this wiki is to get compared to the S.C.P. foundation. For a good while here, people have compared the two and while some of the dust has settled, we really don't need more stuff drumming on up. I'm not saying we absolutely shouldn't do stuff like this, if executed right, it could be a unique alternative to the M.E.G. I'd recommend showing a page with them, they're tone, etc, before trying to release a page like this.
