"The Shadow Stalker" Entity Concept

23 Jun 2022 03:12

**Page Type:** Entity  
**Genre:** Horror  
**Core Concept:** The base of this idea is the classic bump-in-the-night, monster in the dark basement with you fear- nyctophobia, if you will. I want to introduce the fear of arms pulling at your neck from out of the dark closet with this entity. At the same time, however, I want the reader to understand that this creature is intelligent and can easily outsmart them. Beyond that, I integrated an underlying story about some mysterious boy (whether he was an unlucky child from reality or some Backrooms reflection of one is unknown) who ends up unleashing this homicidal, nyctophilic nightmare-dragon onto the Backrooms.  
**Additional Notes:** Really I'd just like to know whether this idea is unique-enough material for the official site and if the page successfully executes the core concept. To those who read this far, thank you.
