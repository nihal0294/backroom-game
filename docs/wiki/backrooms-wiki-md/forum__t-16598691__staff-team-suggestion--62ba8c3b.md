Staff Team - Suggestion

02 Jan 2024 05:32

I have seen that apparently it takes at least three (3) reviews from any member to receive the **Green Light**. So, I thought and suggest that for those new to the wiki, who have to receive critiques, that they receive three (3). For those who have already published an article, for the next one they receive two (2) reviews and for those who have already published three (3) articles, they could only have one (1) review.

Obviously you can receive more criticism from two (2) or one (1), but I say this because if someone becomes aware of their errors in their draft, perhaps they need few criticisms and it is also so that the critics are not so saturated and at the same time you see that new articles are published a little faster.
