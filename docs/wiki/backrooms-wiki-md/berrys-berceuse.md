# Berry's Berceuse

rating: +22[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

Although we may be apart,  
your love and care are in my heart.  
Despite my unexpected leave,  
Don't lose hope and don't you grieve.  
Even though I'm not alone,  
I'd prefer to be with you in our [home](level-906.md).  
   
I miss the window in which I laid,  
between shelves amongst I played.  
The food I get is hard and dry,  
but I'll still be seeking [you](entity-140.md) in the sky.  
   
This message may never see you,  
but I hope to reunite with whom,  
sheltered me from the dark,  
and kept me happy as a lark.  
And though we still may be apart,  
I will keep you in my heart.
