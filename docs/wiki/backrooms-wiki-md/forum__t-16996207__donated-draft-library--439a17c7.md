The Donated Draft Library is an initiative aimed at fulfilling a specific goal:  
Allow people who can't finish their drafts to donate them and allow them to have a second life

Donations can come with conditions if the donator wishes to do so.  
The concerned pages must be copied to [Backroom West](index.md).  
It's an excellent and functional site made by who can also be used to work collaboratively on drafts.

The site already has some pages. Feel free to check it and claim if you desire to…
