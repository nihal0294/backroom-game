> Level 811, also known as "Titanis Sepulcrum" or "Titans' Deathbed," is a recently discovered level for the M.E.G.

for —> by

> There is a day/night cycle identical to that of planet Earth, as well as a magnetic field, though no explanation has been found for it's existence.

it’s —> its

> Most notable, however, is the level's namesake feature: Numerous colossal metallic structures resembling armor and weaponry are spread throughout the level, all possessing strange, circuit-like lines running through them, which pulsate with faint light at regular intervals.

Numerous should not be capitalized (you don’t capitalize words after dashes or colons)  
Comma after “colossal”  
Remove the comma after “through them”

> Some of these artifacts have been documented in greater detail below, as they are key landmarks of the level and have helped Wanderers orientate themselves since the time of the Lost, and are featured in their maps.

Wanderers should not be capitalized

> Zehuti is the name attributed to a massive helm-like structure resting on its side atop a small hill, about three kilometers (3 km) northeast from Excalibur.

Remove the comma

> It is estimated to measure 9-12 meters in length.

This should be an En (–) dash not a hyphen

> Atlas, like Ikaros, is a vambrace linked to a gauntlet, located on a hill seventeen kilometers east of Excalibur. It

Remove the comma after gauntlet

> Gungnir is a large, twenty meter long spear, found hovering above a large crater surrounded by a river, about twenty-five kilometers north of Excalibur.

**Gungnir is a large, twenty-meter-long spear found hovering above a large crater surrounded by a river about twenty-five kilometers north of Excalibur.**

> Upon further inspection, a small cockpit-like area was found within Aegis.

Comma after small

> I was exploring the area, I found this cockpit and decided to go in…

Run-on. Either add a conjunction or separate the sentences.

> You've been asleep for nearly 5000 years, it's only natural.

Another run-on. Change the comma to a semicolon or add a conjunction

Visuals are top notch, unfaultable tbh. Really A+ distinction star star etc for those! /gen

I would say though, that it kinda ends there for praise.

The physical landscape of the level without the mechs is incredibly boring. I understand that it's to host the mechs(which… I'll get to), but I think the level would've benefitted from *something* else in it to describe before launching our sorry selves right into lore. It's just an earth-like place, which really doesn't facilitate much intrigue.

Now, of course, this *could* be overlooked as not being needed. Which yeah - you could argue. But I feel that the page just does far too stark of a shift from "yeah okay here's two paragraphs of fodder about how boring this place is" to "woah paragraphs of M E C H." Personally, I think it would've been good to give the level other properties as a method of slight subversion. Just so you're not reading thinking "Eh where's the weird shit"

---

Secondly, the mechs! Yes, those…. I don't like them! Now, are mechs cool? Yes, 100%. Undoubtedly so, in fact. Mechs are a cool concept that can buy in large be injected into virtually any setting that has a fantasy setting and have it feel…. not too out of place. It's probably part of their wide appeal(that, and they're usually designed quite nicely).

However- I personally feel that the addition of mechs to the good ol' Wikidot supply of story and plot ideas(I'm referring from calling it canon) is kinda silly. Silly is good, don't get me wrong. Whilst I personally don't like all the silly, I feel that The Backrooms is allowed to try out crazier ideas and I feel like I'd like them, but this ain't it. Mechs in the Backrooms, to me, feel like a bad idea conceptually. Much like the Pantheon, it feels like other media injected into The Backrooms. To me, not much work has been made to make it feel like part of other Backrooms mythos.

For example:

- Level these things are being hosted on is boring, I already went over that.
- Uses a pretty normal, standard group and hosts onto that the idea of ancient mechs, which I'm sure is a trope. It doesn't recontextualise the idea of mechs in a Backrooms fashion, whether they come from an original Backrooms GOI or something like that, it just takes the idea of ancient people building advanced technology and kinda keeps it at that.
- The idea of mechs on their own, especially at the sizes people usually expect of even mid-sized mechs, feel really out of place in other Backrooms content. I mean - when compared to the various entities, levels, objects, and what have you, mechs feel like the "+The Simpsons" on every Disney+ advert you see. It's an odd addition which adds to the content of The Backrooms, and can make some amount of sense if done right, but ultimately feels like a very jarring and out of place addition.

I can tell this was written well, and with genuine care, but to me it just ain't it. It's a lot of heart put into something with a jarring concept that worked great in the visuals(and by that I specifically mean the page theme and layout, I've spoken about the level and I think the ruined mechs aren't that interesting), but it didn't exactly excel at much else, besides the writing.
