Given that this was the only rewritecon entry I'd already upvoted before I started this series, I think you know my stance on this.

If each entry to this contest was a meal, this would be a baked potato: it's simple, it's not groundbreaking, I doubt it'll win the contest, and it's *really* good. It feels like reading about a real organism, something that's just crazy enough to make you think it *could* exist, somewhere on the hairy edge of possibility. If we have lizards that shoot blood out of their eyes, why not a bird with a floatsac?

Detractors may say that the page really doesn't do anything new when it comes to Backrooms entities. Indeed, the deathmoths article itself has a very similar approach to this rewrite, but to that I say: *does everything have to be so high concept?* Does every Backrooms page need to push the boundaries of technical sophistication, and be some never-before-seen idea straight from the mind of the next James Joyce? Or - perhaps - when you get home from a long day at work, you don't want caviar, you just want a nice, warm baked potato.

**Easy +1**.
