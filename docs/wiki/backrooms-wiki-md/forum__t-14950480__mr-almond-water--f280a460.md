Mr. Almond Water

29 Jun 2022 17:21

Page Type: Entity

Genre: Wholesome, Mistery

Core Concept: An old man with vintage 1920's clothing, walking around the majority of levels randomly no-clipping without a problem, giving **Almond Water** and simple gifts to anyone he comes across. Besides having a talking voice and understanding of different languages, his order of words usually has no meaning and it's just random words about sports and work.

he sprays whoever finds agressive with **Almond Water** on an atomizer until they calm down or kill them, whether it is an entity or a human. He's able to appear out of nowhere several kinds of containers, from plastic bottles to metal barrels, filled with fresh **Almond Water**; he also can summon various objects with a brand of **Almond Water** on it, most of them beign clothes with an 1920's aesthetic, and sometimes he can give a **Vanilla Coin**

His appareance is of an 40 years old man, wearing a white shirt and a tie, beige pants with suspenders, and some glasses; his hair is brown and a little white, combed backwards, and with some thick mustache and eyebrows; weights 90kg and height 1.79m. He doesn't have any facial hole such as eyes, nostrils or mouth, he doesn't even have ear holes, so it's unkown how is he able to talk, listen or see. Until now, there is only one of this entitiy on all the backrooms

Additional Notes: he may be related with another entries i'm working on, i hope that this first idea would then grow with other entries
