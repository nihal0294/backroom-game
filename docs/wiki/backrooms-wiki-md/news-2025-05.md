# Anti-AI Policy Reminder

---

Due to an increase in bans related to AI usage in writing. Staff would like to remind the community that the use of generative AI[1](javascript:;) is strictly prohibited in all forms. The use of non-generative AI for the exclusive purposes of spellchecking,[2](javascript:;), and translations[3](javascript:;). AI writing goes against the spirit of this writing site, which is creating our own unique space with our writing. Let's celebrate the Backrooms together through originality and collaboration.

# The Crit Crew

---

We now have a *new* Discord based critique team! [The Crit Crew](critic-crew.md) is an official non-staff, non-GLMP team that will hopefully help streamline the critique process and reward our critics for their hard work. With an additional focus on helping our critics improve, we are hopeful that this will help cultivate an even better critique environment going forward. If you are interested in joining, check out this [page](critic-crew.md) before contacting a Mod or Greenlighter on Discord.

# The Gleeful Return of GLE

---

***What is GLE?*** A Greenlight Exemption is when an author is granted permission to post any of their drafts without a greenlight or clear.

***What are the requirements??*** All applying Authors must have a minimum of three pages with +20 or more upvotes (+15 for tales), not including joke pages, themes, author pages, or components) and a minimum of five pages posted to the site in total.

***Where do I apply???*** You can learn more about GLE on our [Greenlight Policy](greenlight-policy.md) page, and the application can be found [here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfDFKpLmmQgNv8FBgewQed3aDSXR9DO1bdr-HBWsW1WE0Q3Vg/viewform).
