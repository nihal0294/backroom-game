Re: Level 307 - The Den of the Basilisk

06 Mar 2025 01:02

Well, I must say, that this level in it's current state would not survive on the site. I can offer some ideas, however — you aren't required to follow them exactly, but they may give you some inspiration nonetheless.

So, first off, the things that aren't included in the plot but should be. "Singula complentia fabulam", I will call this from now on.

So, you describe this level as an exhibition filled with various reptiles from around the world. What types? It may prove beneficial to explain, even if briefly, some kinds of the creatures that can be found here.

Second, the basilisk itself. It's described as this terrifying beast, yet there is much information lacking about it. The page currently does not give it's height or speed, nor does it explain the entity's abilities. Generally, basilisks are supposed to have the ability to kill anyone who makes eye contact with them. So, what else can it do?

And for the parts of the plot that don't make sense as of now:

Continuing with the basilisk, why is it described as a monster? From what I can tell, it is no less scary than any regular entity. The inhabitants of the Backrooms are bound to be used to entity encounters enough to not be frightened by the basilisk specifically. I would give it some way of being uniquely threatening.

Additionally, why are none of the exhibits broken? I feel like a giant reptile would eventually damage something by throwing enough people around. It may add a little extra to the description, and to enhance the basilisk's fear factor.

Third, is there anything in the trapdoor? Given how the exploration groups in the Backrooms are, someone is bound to try looking inside it ,or take cover inside when the basilisk is distracted.

And, an overall suggestion: I see you wish to make the main entity of this level seem scary, but it isn't quite there yet. The descriptions of it as this gross, rotting, ancient creature do help with this, but more could be done to enhance this part of the article. Firstly, as I mentioned before, explain the basilisk's power. With that, there must also be a reason why not everyone can escape the entity — being able to simply run away makes the creature seem much less threatening. Third, enhance those gross descriptions of the basilisk some more. For example, what sounds does it make, and what does it smell like?

Well, for now, I believe that's all I have to say about this page.
