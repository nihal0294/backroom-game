# fragment:spec-9

There's not really an easy way to put this. If you're reading this, I'm either dead, or someone's hacked my laptop.

Anyone that knows me by this point has probably figured out I'm only *slightly* paranoid, so me writing an "if all else fails" last-case 'eulogy' for myself probably won't be much of a shock. It's like… Batman's justice league contingency plans, except depressing. If there ever comes a time where I fuck up to such a degree I end up looking anywhere from ground beef to a stain on the wall, I want to get the last word in. For the record, I'm writing this in about 2030— so whatever the G.P.D. throws on my page, I can confirm that it is 100% absolutely true or heinous lies from the deep state. Remember kids: it was a horrific crime, except for when I did it (because then it's a necessary evil.)

Anyways… the topic at hand.

The Backrooms. Kind of a shitty place to call home. Depending on your perspective, I'm either one of the lucky ones who was able to experience the outside world, or I'm one of the cursed wanderers who lives in perpetual longing for a place to which they can never return. There's a thinker for you… is it worse to never experience it, or to have experienced it and never be able to again?

So yeah. Contrary to the beliefs of [some](the-lost.md), I'm not a god. I didn't just *spawn* out of the Aether. I noclipped in here just about 10 years ago, been kickin' it ever since. Like a solid chunk of those who entered via noclip, time… stopped for me. I haven't aged a day since I fell in here.

This may sound awesome, but ask anyone else cursed with the Backrooms' many methods of pseudo-immortality: This shit is god-awful. You try being trapped in the body of a 19-year-old for almost a decade. At a certain point, my brain is going to overflow with information I simply won't be able to handle anymore. Yknow that one Dr. Who episode where that girl keeps writing stuff down because she's been alive too long to keep the memories?

That would have been my future. Fun. I suppose it could have been worse— I've seen people age backwards, I've even seen people frozen in time completely. Like, if you break your arm, it's just going to \*be\* broken for the rest of your life. Your cells aren't regenerating, your organs aren't working, yet you're somehow still alive.

Good times.

What else is there? I guess I should probably apologize to the people I know for being stupid enough to get myself put on a t-shirt. So… I'm sorry.

A good portion of my investigations have been stored on this computer. Use them to your benefit. As of writing, I still haven't uncovered the mechanisms that "run" this place, but hopefully I manage to come to a conclusion before someone inevitably ends up reading this to a group of all 5 of my closest friends.

Sooooo yeah. That's about all I can think to say.

Alexis— I'm sorry.

This is Spec signing off, for likely the last time. Hopefully no one ever has to hear this.
