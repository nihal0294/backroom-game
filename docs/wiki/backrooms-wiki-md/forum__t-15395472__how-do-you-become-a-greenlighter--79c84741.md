Hi! to become a GL, you first have to become a GLT! To apply for glt, simply send three of your crits to a greenlighter, and then there will be a vote held to see if you are up to the requirements. If you are, you will be sent an invite to the GLMP discord server. Then, you just do crit as a GLT under a mentor until they decide you are ready to be a gl. (btw, if you have three crits I can send them to my mentor for you as I am currently a GLT.)
