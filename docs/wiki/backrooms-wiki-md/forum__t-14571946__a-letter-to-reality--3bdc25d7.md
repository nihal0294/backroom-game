A letter to reality

20 Mar 2022 12:22

Two weeks after the investigation and the return of [REDACTED], we received a letter from the Backrooms, there are some entities claiming themselves as "the [Partypoopers](entity-68.md) Colony", talking about their new life after the departure of our own operatives:

```
Please enter access code
```

```
Access confirmed
```

---

Dear friends of the frontrooms,

It has been weeks since you guys left. How're you guys? We have finally found the MEG and a place to stay to raise our lovely blue civilization once more. Level [REDACTED] is great! There's an arcade with a lot of games to play, plenty of tasty food and refreshing drinks, and many outposts here for fellow wanderers to stay and enjoy the last stop of their journey back home.

The people here are the best! The waiters here are very caring and taught us how to play the games provided. The other entities and humans are also friendly with us, and we occasionally hang out to have a meal and play some games. The environment here is so better than the snackrooms ngl!! Speaking of the [Snackrooms](level-6-1.md) , it is now called the New Hub. The friendly entities helped us connect multiple paths between other safer or habitable parts of the Backrooms, such as level 11, and our colonies, Sadly we can't connect to most of the main 9 from the Snack Hub, as no safe exits lead to it directly.

On one occasion, one of the humans asked us how did we end up here. We told them we were searching for a new home when we met in our old home. We told many stories about our journeys throughout the Backrooms. Everyone was fascinated about how heroic you guys were, but the most popular and the most loved one is how we met and got rid of the partygoers. “No wanderers ever have been so brave and united to dare to challenge even one! Let alone the whole colony!” A MEG operative once said.

This party may have been over for long, but unexpected incidents are still happening. There was once a baby [Faceling](entity-9.md) raid on level 11 near our outpost where they had a food fight and pulled pranks on everyone! Some of them put whoopie cushions and fake explosives in every corner they find, and they totally scared us! Others squirted bottles of sauce on all the walls and poured chocolate fondue all over the ground! Luckily, we took care of it swiftly and no one was hurt. We still find it somewhat funny even now.

To achieve our new goal— 'making the Backrooms a safe place for travellers' — we have reconnected the Backroom's Internet with your world, and we have sent guides to your friends of the frontrooms to help those who have set out their unexpected journey here.

What is it like in the frontrooms? What are the entities there? Are they friendly? What kinds of activities do you recommend in your world?

At last we hope y'all are safe out there. Keep in touch.

Love,  
Aaron, Perry and Tristan  
From the [Partypoopers](entity-68.md) Colony
