+1, but some notes

01 Jul 2026 04:50

This article is pretty good, and I like that the “Addendum” section helps against the entity being overpowered, but I do have some thoughts:

—  
1. Considering the controversy surrounding The Keymaster (as well as the fact that The Keymaster just generally doesn’t seem that well-liked among other community members), their reference in this article should probably be replaced with something else.

2. Even with the “Addendum” section, I still feel like Elevator’s may be *slightly* overpowered, and I feel like there should be some other kind of restriction towards using them (limited to only buildings, inconsistent button options, rarity, etc.)

3. It is mentioned within the “Behavior” section that Elevators are capable of direct communication, and I feel like it would have been nice for an example of this to be included within the article itself (such as through an interview or wanderer account). Also, the line “Due to this, many believe that all elevators are sentient” feels odd, considering that their sentient qualities seems largely specific to the Backrooms, and the way elevators are constructed and operate is entirely explainable for the Frontrooms, since they are just human technology in those instances.  
—

Overall, still an incredible article! Those were just some things I felt during/after reading.
