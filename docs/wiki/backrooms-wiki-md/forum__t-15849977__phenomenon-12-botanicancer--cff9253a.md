I always think of the Backrooms as a living hell for wanderers. Everything they do is a compromise. Imagine if the Backrooms is filled with large cities built by humans, which, from my perspectives, is a boring copy of the real world. You just can't make their lives as easy as those in the Frontrooms. And what's the opposite of construction? Plants, I guess. So I wrote this. Hope you guys enjoy it.
