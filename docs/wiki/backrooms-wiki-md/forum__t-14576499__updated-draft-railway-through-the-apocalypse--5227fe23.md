05 Apr 2022 20:58

Well, the reason for the Class 0 difficulty is due to the fact that the hostile entities will never board the tram, so by staying on the tram, you are perfectly safe. But since leaving the tram will result in instantly being attacked by countless entities, the entity infestation is something that should be mentioned.

Now as for the Unsecure status, I wasn't sure about whether the level was secure or unsecure. Since anything left on the tram when it departs from Final Destination is never seen again and the entity infestation outside of the tram, making a colony in this level is impossible. In the end, I felt that calling it secure would imply that the level (or rather the tram) is always stable, when no one knows what happens to people that stay on the tram past Final Destination. In other words, since you can't stay in the level forever, I concluded that the level, as a whole, is unsecure.
