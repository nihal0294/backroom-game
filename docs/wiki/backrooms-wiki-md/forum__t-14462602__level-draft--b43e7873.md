I'm the kind of person who likes to just let their work speak for itself, but if I have to put some kind of marketing babble here to sell you on taking the time to read this, then I'd like to personally say that horror is something I really take to heart. I believe that there's a subcategory, a niche genre or type of horror that's been almost totally untapped. Not by just this website and the pages, but in the world of artistic expression as a whole, and I believe it's due to a majority of people's lack of experience with this level of horror. My aim in the following page, and most likely any pages I may write in the future, is to explore and try to express this kind of horror. I tried to put a decent amount of content in here, so please sit and take time to go through it all. As is the point of this forum, if there's anything that I may have missed, any typos or oversights, anything, please let me know.
