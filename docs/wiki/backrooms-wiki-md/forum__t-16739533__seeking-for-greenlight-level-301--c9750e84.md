This level will need some reworking before it'll be ready for a grenelight.

Concept  
Tbh there's nothing particularly new with the level that really stands out. It's just another level where death is gonna be immediate. The part stating that people who enter will be considered dead is effective but that's about it in terms of what had impact. I don't see why anyone would bother putting so much effort to get into this level, even sending drones. I'm not really that interested in the level itself based on what I've read.

Dimensional cracks are also very uninspired. They're very hectic based on their nature but ultimately they're not used in a very interesting way. It reads like you had an idea, put that idea in, then just sorta left it contained to one small section and an image caption. It doesn't feel like it fits at all besides just being naother thing to kill you on a level that's just gonna kill you some other way anyways.

The parrot is kinda confusing. First of all, the title is "parrot" but the page talks about a species. The evidence they exist is bones but also these people know lots about them, particularly things that wouldn't be known from looking at bones, so they have other evidence for this entity? I feel like I've missed a lot when I read this section but I've reread the page and can't see any context I overlooked. Tbh I don't really leave this section feeling like I understand this entity better or have much of a reason to care about them. Also, you don't say the word "parrot" once in the entire paragraph dedicated to talking about parrots.

"According to the Parrots" what? I'm so lost now. Aren't the parrots gone and the only way we know about them is their bones? Also, you never said these birds could communicate. You say they "exhibit a level of intelligence comparable to that of humans" but this is so vague and essentially meaningless that it reads more as a get out of jail free card that lets the parrots be whatever is most convenient for the plot.

I'm not gonna go any further than this. The page is confusing and lacks intrigue. I have some issues with format too. Information is just stated rather than being presented in a natural way. I'm just being told thing rather than learning them.

This all needs to be worked on first.
