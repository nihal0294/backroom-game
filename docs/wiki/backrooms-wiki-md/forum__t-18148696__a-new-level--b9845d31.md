I liked the idea.

A movie theater that only plays movies and TV shows the wanderer never finished watching is a cool concept.

I also like the fact that the level has rules the wanderer actually has to follow.

The entity is interesting too. I can't really put my finger on it, but there's just something about it that feels a little off to me. I honestly can't explain what it is.

The entrances and exits work really well. I'm not a fan of levels where the entrances and exits have absolutely nothing to do with the levels they're connected to. The Backrooms are random, sure, but that randomness still needs to feel intentional.

Overall, you've already got a solid foundation.

That said, I'd recommend refining the article a bit more before publishing it.

It's got good mysteries, good ideas, and a lot of potential, but I feel like some of the explanations come across as a little awkward or unclear.

Honestly, I think this level just needs another editing pass. The concept is already there—you just need to polish the writing a bit.

Re: A new level

18 Jul 2026 00:59

Right off the bat, I think the level should have a proper title. Most Wikidot articles are introduced as something like "Level 190 — [Level Title]", and that immediately gives the page more identity.

I also noticed that the introduction refers to "ushers" in the plural, but later the article establishes that there's only one employee in the entire theater. I'd keep that consistent from the start.

Another thing that stood out to me was the claim that it's permanently midnight. How did the researchers figure that out? Are there clocks? Signs? If there's no way to observe the outside world, how can they verify that it's actually midnight instead of that simply being part of the anomaly? I think that section could be reworded to make it more believable.

The article's structure could also be a bit clearer. At one point you introduce three main areas, but later you describe two more. If those are actually subsections of the original three, I'd make that explicit so the page flows better.

I really like the idea of movies that wanderers feel like they've seen before but can't actually remember the name of. The examples are good, although I'd probably present them a little differently.

The entity is great. I don't really have any complaints there. I also liked the inclusion of the audio log.

One thing I kept wondering, though, was what the theater is actually trying to accomplish. The premise is interesting, but I think the level's purpose could be communicated more clearly.

As for Phenomenon 2, I think there's a small inconsistency. The article establishes that movies never reach their ending, but then mentions auditoriums that "aren't currently showing anything." If the movies never end, then every auditorium should theoretically always be showing a film. That part probably needs either a better explanation or a rewrite.

The rest of the phenomena are really solid, especially the fourth one.

I actually think the outposts make sense here. The entity is easy to avoid as long as you have a valid ticket, the level seems reasonably safe for a Class 2, and the theater itself provides more than enough space and infrastructure for groups to establish a presence.

The exploration log, on the other hand, felt a little short and didn't really add much to the article. Personally, I think a good exploration log should reveal something new—a phenomenon that hasn't been documented yet or some detail that the main article couldn't naturally convey.

I don't really have anything to say about the entrances and exits. I think they work well.

One suggestion I'd make is to connect this level to other movie theater-themed levels. It might also be interesting to expand on the idea that certain films can transport wanderers to levels related to whatever they're watching.

Overall, I think it's a good level. I genuinely like the concept, and I think it has a lot of potential. In my opinion, what it needs most is clearer presentation and a bit more work on the article's structure. I'd send it in for Greenlight. I doubt it'd pass on the first try, but I'm sure the reviewers would be able to point out areas for improvement and help polish it.

Oh, and one last thing—I'd do something about those images. It's pretty obvious they're AI-generated, and the moderation team tends to be pretty strict about that.
