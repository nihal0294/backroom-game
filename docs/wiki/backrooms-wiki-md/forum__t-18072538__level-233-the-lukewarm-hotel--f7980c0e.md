Reading, Voting and Commenting on Every Rewritecon Entry #1

02 Jul 2026 00:43

The first randomly generated page brought me here, so I guess it's where we'll begin.

What can be said about this page? As others have pointed out, the writing is most certainly a bit rough; some of the sentences feel really cramped, like the clauses should be lengthened a bit more. For instance,

> I remember, since my mother's passing, when I was so little, my father and I were so poor, he couldn't afford to take me places, let alone waterparks.

has 5 commas in it, breaking the prose into 6 pieces that stop-start kind of jarringly.

Unfortunately, these kinds of flow issues cannot be solved in the span of a single page comment: practice is the only real way out, so other than pointing out the problem there is little I can do to aid in preventing these issues.

As for the page itself, the story given is serviceable, if a little similar to Level 357; I particularly like how the level itself is almost "lukewarm" in how it handles the wanderers inside: it's just kind of "nice", but not so much so that major groups would give it any mind (e.g. there's no food in the cafeterias, but a "perfect" level would have such provisions). The background music was as-always a nice touch, and as a rewrite the page works fantastically: leaning into what worked in the previous version whilst abandoning that which didn't. In fact, the old version of the page is so much worse than this that if this page doesn't survive rewritecon it may take a long time for it to be properly rewritten (yes this did bias my decision a bit).

Call it novelty bias because it's the first page I'm reading as part of this series, but **this gets a tentative +1** from me.

P.S. Why is there a P.S. at the end of the email if there is no postscript that the P.S. is referring to?

+1 I like it when the Backrooms is personal in a very detached way

06 Jul 2026 22:39

I think this was the last level we did for Asian crit night, and I remember liking it! It's a little rough around the edges, the story is *very* slow, which while slow stories are great sometimes that can be to a detriment, but it's enjoyable. I remember Scutoid said the origin of the main character is vague, and some kind of home town or city or county/state/regional variant would've helped a ton, and I can't help but agree. The story is a little… vague, and while I can appreciate that fact, given how *long* it is, some kind of indication on where our MC lived before the Backrooms would be swish.

I have always been a fan of 233's aesthetics, and to inject a nice story that actually mirrors the look of the level is nice. The Backrooms *is* about feeling like elements of home in a very detached, isolated way, and I can really appreciate doing that here pretty well.

Is the page perfect? No… I wouldn't say so. The vague details, as stated, can be to a fault, and the writing is sometimes a little too slow. There's also clearly not a lot to say about the level itself, so when the description veers off into very personal stuff sometimes, you can *kinda* tell its to cover for that fact? But, like, it works. It characterises someone who very clearly isn't good at writing this stuff, but wants to, and is sentimental. I feel for the guy by the end.

I don't have much to say as I usually do, but that's because you did alright! Nice work

Also congratulations on being the last page I read for this con technically. The last one was Level 122, but this one's the last one I left a comment on.
