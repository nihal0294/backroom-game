I’m gonna go out on a limb and say that maybe a joke article written about a real person like this isn’t the best concept. The whole joke hinges on one running joke about a pngtuber who isn’t particularly widely known outside of that sphere, the UEC gets mentioned for no particularly good reason other than to kill people, the images are all unusable because of licensing issues, and there’s nothing much of substance here beyond a reference to a funny incident that happened about this person.

You could pivot the concept into something much more removed from its origins, like having a digital entity that fights the UEC through taking on the exaggerated persona that pngtubers are notorious for, being a thorn in their side that manages to distract them just enough to cause real disruptions in their operations but not enough of a direct threat to be considered anything but an annoyance. I don’t think your draft will work in its current form, but if you want to rework it, you’ll need to heavily distance it from any specific person as the core of the joke.
