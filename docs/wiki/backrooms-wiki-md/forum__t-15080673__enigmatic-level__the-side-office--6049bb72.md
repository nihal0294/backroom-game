# **SPaG:**

---

"The Side-Office is an enigmatic level that represents an office building"

  
Crit:  
Add a period to the sentence.  
"represents" may be substituted with "resembles".  
Please bold "The Side-Office".  
Like this:

```
**The Side-Office**
```

---

"The Side-Office is a strange office-like level that exists outside of the regular backrooms, although connected to it. It represents an office building with desks, chairs, computers, and other objects normally found in office buildings."

  
Crit:  
Again, bold the level's name.  
"backrooms" should be written as "Backrooms".  
Again, "represents" shall be substituted with "resembles" or "takes the appearance of".

---

"The Hazmats are a small group who always wear the hazmat suits found in the Side-Office. They are friendly and help wanderers escape the level, and will give you almond water if you ask, as they have a seemingly infinite supply. They have no recollection of their past, or why they joined The Hazmats […]"

  
Crit:  
The interview and outpost seem…pointless.  
Like, why are they there? Why didn't they move away? I feel like this group was added only to make the draft longer, which is still way too short. You also put no links at all. As for the entrances and exits, "level" shall become "Level" and "front rooms" shall become "Frontrooms".

---

# **Concept:**

None of this reply is supposed to sound disrespectful, but you didn't do a good job. All I gotta say is that it's too short and wasn't ready for a full crit to begin with. Maybe expand this a bit, fix the SPaG and remove the purposeless additions. Send me a PM if you need anything and I'll be more than glad to help.
