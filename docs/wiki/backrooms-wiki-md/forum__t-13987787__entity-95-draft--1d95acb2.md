31 Jan 2021 20:02

> hives that are home to multiple genetically modified bees.

How does the M.E.G. know that they're genetically modified and not just a weird backrooms creature? Does it make sense for them to be genetically modified?

> causing bizarre and disturbing image manipulation to occur

"hallucinations" might be a better description than "image manipulation"

> Entity 095

You do this a few times throughout. Should just be "Entity 95"

**Conclusion:** This is decent, but I would highly suggest adding more meat to the article. An idea could maybe be exploring more how the M.E.G. reacted to someone dying in that test. Also maybe give the M.E.G. more of a reason to want to capture these entities, because right now they don't really have too much of a reason to go to all of that effort. Maybe they can be weaponized? Or, if they are genetically modified, maybe capturing them can help the M.E.G. figure out who exactly modified them and why? Let me know when you make some changes, and I can take another look!
