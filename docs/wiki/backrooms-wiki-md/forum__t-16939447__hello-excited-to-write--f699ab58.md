Hello! Excited to write!

16 Sep 2024 23:56

Hi everyone! Thank you for welcoming me to the wiki- I'll just go by endless\_waters.

I've been fascinated by liminal spaces for most of my life, and making art about them. I'm also a complete social media hermit who's never really existed online. For a long time I didn't know there were entire communities around liminal spaces (I thought I was alone in having this weird obsession)- so when I finally found them I was thrilled. Didn't take long to fall in love with the Backrooms (though perhaps I'd feel differently if I were trapped there XD)  
I've been silently reading along and following the lore for a while now, and getting lots of ideas, so I decided I wanted to write. I'm also an artist and plan on doing some some illustrations.

I'll just say this is all kind of new to me- I'm a neurodivergent adult who never joined Tumblr in the early aughts, and I suspect I might be a generation older than most people here. This is my first time participating in a community like this, so forgive me for adjusting. But I'm very excited to be here and share in writing this story with everyone else.
