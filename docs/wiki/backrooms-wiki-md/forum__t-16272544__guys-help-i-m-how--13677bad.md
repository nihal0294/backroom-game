I'm assuming you're talking about collapsibles? They're not javascript, they're just built into the basic wikidot markup engine !! And they're not too bad to use

This bit of code here:

```
[[collapsible show="+ Click here to open" hide="-  Click here to close"]]
This is the hidden text !! Ain't that neat
[[/collapsible]]
```

…gives you this:

[+ Click here to open](javascript:;)

[-  Click here to close](javascript:;)

This is the hidden text !! Ain't that neat

Of course, you can put almost anything in between the "[[collapsible]]" and "[[/collapsible]]" lines—plain text, extra divs, images, tables, whatever !! I do know putting collapsibles *in* collapsibles requires a little extra work, but other than that this bit of code has you covered. You're free to replace the bits after "show" and "hide" in the quotes, too.  
(Also—there's a couple bit of extra collapsible pointers [here](wikidot-syntax.md) if you'd want to have a look.)
