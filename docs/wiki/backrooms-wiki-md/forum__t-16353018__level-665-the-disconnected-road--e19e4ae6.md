Level 665 - "The Disconnected Road"

21 Sep 2023 10:44

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre:** Mystery and wonder

**Core Concept:** Imagine there's a snowy level with an infinite road but walking off that road is dangerous. If you walk off that road (onto the sidewalk or into a property) a black humanoid entity emerges from behind a tree or window of a house, or from the snow ground and sprints at you and violently beats you to death with it's superhuman strength and speed.

Since the road is infinite the environment around you changes as you walk on. Weirdly there seem to be cars driving around you on other roads and humans walking around but there is never anyone on your road. If you scream at them they don't respond either. Even in some buildings, the lights are on.

**Additional Information:** I haven't thought about all the concepts yet but this is the core concept and I'm wondering what you guys think about it and if I could have some conceptual critique. Thank you!
