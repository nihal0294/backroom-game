The Apartment

11 May 2021 13:35

**Page Type:** Room

**Genre (Optional):** Disturbing

**Page Layout (Optional):** Basic description, entrance/exit + a journal

**Elevator Pitch:** a small apartment of 2 rooms, one of which having a kitchen, living room and bedroom while the other is a bathroom. After prolonged exposure, your perception of yourself and the environment will become warped. Seeing entity’s when there are none, paranoia about leaving etc.

**Central Narrative:** A room which was discovered by a wanderer who stayed for a little over a week before starving to death after refusing to eat. Claiming in his journal that the ‘room is safe and I no longer need to eat’

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** I believe that my idea is unique to the site due to its mystery and way a level interacts with a person. Whilst many are environmental hazards, my room is supposed a sentient being hellbent of wanting you to stay long periods of time.
