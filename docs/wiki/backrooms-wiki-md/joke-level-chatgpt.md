Level ChatGPT—the infinite land of death—is the ChatGPT+1th level of the Backrooms.

---

# Description

Level ChatGPT is an **inhospitable hellscape** within the Backrooms—those who enter it **never make it out alive**. It's not just any generic Backrooms level—it's the **most edgy level you can think of**. The level is **filled to the brim** with **hostile entities**—ones that can kill you in 3 nanoseconds. However, it's not just the entities you have to worry about—it's the sheer **contagious cringeness** originating from the **terrible** level page that you will be forced to read when entering this level that kills you. The sheer **cringiness**—along with the **entities**, **radiation**, **toxic atmosphere**, **mentally altering** chemicals, **carcinogens**, **isolation** for some reason, **sanity loss**, and **fine ash** that can lead to **████**—causes your brain to spontaneously combust and turn into liquid bromine—a **toxic** element. This doesn't just fill the entire level with liquid bromine—it also fills the level with a **never-ending supply** of fluorine, liquid uranium, liquid sodium, liquid potassium, liquid cesium, liquid francium, liquid rubidium, a lot of water, chlorine gas, highly concentrated oganesson, fermium, ████████, and **ChatGPT's electric bills**.

# Entities

## The 2 Entity Variants

💀No entities can last for more than 1 nanosecond on the level—a testament to the level's **inhospitability**.

👾 But all entities are on this level anyways—most notably **Entity 67-A - "Partynukers"** and **Entity 1-B - "ChatGPT pretending to be a human"**—because they are all overpowered variants of the standard levels.

☢️ While the partynukers are dangerous—and can certainly kill you in 1 femtosecond— Entity 1-B is **especially dangerous** but easily discernible from a human **for now**.

## Investors

⚠️ **Investors** are the most dangerous entities on the level—coming here to invest in **AI scam products** instead of an actually useful AI company.

🧟 Be careful—these investors have **zero brain** and will kill you for no reason because they are so stupid.

☠️ While their **stupidity** does make them easily escapable, the **sheer quantity** of them—with origins unknown—makes the investors dangerous.

⏲️ Entity 1-B also **routinely** pitches the scam AI product—with a convincing tone—to the investors, giving you valuable time to escape.

🤯 Remember**—DO NOT EVER INVEST IN THE PRODUCTS**. They are not just destined to fail—they can lead to your brain exploding. In fact, it's not just investing that causes brain explosions—it's listening to the pitches as well. The **sheer stupidity** of the pitches makes your brain explode—a common danger across the level—after you listen to them for over 2 seconds.
