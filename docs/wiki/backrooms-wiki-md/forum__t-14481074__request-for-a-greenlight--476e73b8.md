11 Feb 2022 19:15

Safe  
Secure  
Low Entity Count  
Level #( i haven't chosen yet), also know as "The Mans forest" contains one entity, and is located on the # level of the Backrooms.

Description  
This level of the backrooms contains a seemingly infinity forest with man made buildings in the the trees. These building seem to be tree houses. Some are the sizes of drift store, or apartment rooms. other's are the size of your average 3 story building, sometimes a sky scraper but this has only been seen a few times.

In the building, if you can find a way to get up, you will see that they are fully furnished and complacently decorated to the brim with Christmas lights and Halloween mask. you may also fined in the refrigerator's, bottles of almond water, food, and cheese.

The Entity  
The entity that lives on this level is a a humanoid creature that speaks and communicates with every traveler that happens to fall in to its forest. The entity is a humanoid creatures that has, green fur, a mask on its face, and a green leaf sporting from it head. It likes to go by the name of Frigg, but it commonly goes by "that green thing".

Bases,Outpost,and Communities  
\*\* The slackers\*\*:  
There name doesn't imply they are slackers, they thought it sounded cool. The bass contains 40 people and they are open for trading.

Entrances And Exits  
Entrances  
You can enter level # by falling of a tree and no-clipping in to the floor.  
You may also have a chance of getting in from level 1 or level 2

Exits  
You can walk through a door that found a bass of a random tree.  
You can also ask Frigg to send you to the next room
