Facelings: Their anatomy’s akin to humans (other than no face and no sapience), so their blood is obviously red. That’s a given.

Smilers: Do they have blood at all? If so, what color?

Wretches: They’re former humans, so their blood color might be red, but that’s not a given.

Hounds: Black or red blood?

Clumps: Maybe red blood?

What do you think? If you can bring up any other entities who you know the blood-color of, let me know! :)
