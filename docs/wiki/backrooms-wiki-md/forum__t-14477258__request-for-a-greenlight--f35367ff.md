Description:

Level 890 is composed of randomly segmented rooms, each of these rooms are dark and ressemble a toy shop.The toys appear to be from reality and videogames and other machinery in this level does not work (exept for flashlights and objects from other levels). This level is non-euclidean and time in this level works backwards.
