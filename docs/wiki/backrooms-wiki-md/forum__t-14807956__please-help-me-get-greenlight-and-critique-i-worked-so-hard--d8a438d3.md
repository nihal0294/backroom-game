I will specify that I liked many things about this level, but no published or unpublished level goes without its mistakes. When the level was first introduced, I instantly was reminded of a video game, which I liked, but whether or not that was your intention, I am not sure. I also like the lore present in the background, revolving around an entity overseeing the level, and the whole "its a trap!" kind of situation. I feel that this is very common in many levels, but if you further elaborate on this lore, it may begin to seem less generic. A nice touch I personally would add, is some unique entity such as a kind of soldier, or idk, living tank? It never harms to add more. Finally, review the article for SPaG errors. I noticed a few in the entity section, (no space), and in your M.E.G. log, where you had incorrect punctuation. I am not a greenlighter, but this level seems like it could be given the go in a short amount of time! I enjoyed!
