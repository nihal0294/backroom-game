[+ The Actor](javascript:;)

[- The Actor](javascript:;)

Inherently a character who can do, has done, and will do anything as required by the narrative. Anything can happen to him (including permanent removal from a narrative) based on the writer's choice without it affecting the Actor himself, since all he plays is a role. The Actor is exactly what he is called, and is willing to fill any role position needed (protag, antag, etc) or use any name that fits for that role. Bring up the metanarrative if you like, but it's not necessary to do so when writing him. He can be just Some Guy.

The flower attached to him can be changed to whatever type of flower, since the flower and vines represent the narrative. Make them symbolic or not, whatever you choose. He moves in a way that suggests the vines/stems all over him control his movements, but ignore this detail if you feel like it's too distracting.

As far as core, fixed traits about him, he's Japanese-Uchinanchu, he likes men, and he's not bothered by his fictional nature (most of the time).

[+ The Moon Hare](javascript:;)

[- The Moon Hare](javascript:;)

A non-human sapient being who learns information from the moon. Also pounds rice cakes and medicine with his pestle and mortar. He has paranoia and anxiety, which is core to his character and should be treated with care in depiction. As a concept, his existence is to answer the question of "what happens when people are given love and acceptance for the things that many would abuse them for?"
