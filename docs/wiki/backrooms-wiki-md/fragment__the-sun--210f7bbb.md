# The Sun

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment%3Athe-sun/NEWsplithead)

The SPLIT(!)HEAD found within Level α.

### Entity Number: 0

### Habitat: [Level α](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-alpha), Omnipresent

### Hazard Level: Tempestuous

### Shared properties:

**Entity 0** refers to a system of interconnected creatures, phenomena and environments which share many similar characteristics which define anything within this specific type of categorization. Typically, all of the items within this category are titled either SPLIT(!)HEAD, SPLIT(?)HEAD, or SPLIT(‽)HEAD depending on how grounded they are in reality; their corporeality being categorized from greatest to least when previously mentioned.

### SPLIT(!)HEADS:

SPLIT(!)HEADS refer to the "hosts" who contain SPLIT(‽)HEAD and specific types of SPLIT(?)HEAD. These hosts take on a physical form, usually with the appearance of a humanoid, and serve as a gateway into the SPLIT(?)HEAD. Research regarding SPLIT(!)HEADS is difficult to actually extract due to how limited contact with them can be; individuals who have managed to communicate with it seem to have been picked completely at random. It is currently theorized that SPLIT(!)HEADS only exist in specific rooms that they control entry to.

### SPLIT(?)HEADS:

SPLIT(?)HEADS refer to multiple different entities that all have extremely similar properties. Environmental SPLIT(?)HEADS seem to hold a limitless amount of space within them that is filled only by the creatures that have managed to stumble within it and an unbearably bright light. Despite the entity forming a present location, it is theorized to be made up of an organic substance based on accounts of those sent here. There are rare times in which small "windows" can be found whilst inside the entity which show you a space within the Backrooms and can allow you to exit the SPLIT(?)HEAD; due to this, it is currently theorized that the entity exists throughout the entirety of the Backrooms.

SPLIT(?)HEADS are also able to form humanoid shapes for brief periods of time, hinting at possible sentience. Humanoid SPLIT(?)HEADS cannot remain in their state for longer than three minutes at a time, though are able to audible communication with individuals around it. They've been described as being extremely calm and reasonable, giving wanderers who make contact with them feelings of uneasiness.

### SPLIT(‽)HEAD:

Currently, only a single SPLIT(‽)HEAD has been found, though information surrounding the entity is very unreliable due to limited contact with it being made. Visually, it appears as a jellyfish composed of pure light with tentacles extending outwards that trigger rapid mental degradation to a wanderer when contact between them is made. After fifteen minutes of time have allotted, the individual affected will suddenly experience splitting within the frontal portion of their skull, causing their death. This entity is believed to have killed 89 wanderers within Level α; any documented information surrounding the deaths will cause those who read it to perish. See ascension.odp for more information regarding this subject.
