Phenomenon: Linear No-Clipping

30 May 2026 00:04

**Concept Title:** Phenomenon: Linear No-Clipping

**Central Narrative:** While, it technically serves as a demostration of M.E.G's technological prowess, it also serves as both a in-universe and meta solution for levels who do not exit into the level directly after them, which is something that has bugged me for a while.

**Brief Description:** Linear No-Clipping is a phenomenon that allows wanderers to exploit traditional no-clipping to launch themselves through 'Outer Level' (basically the space between levels) and travel further depending on their speed. Going faster can have unintended consequences.

**Hook:** While I want the level to contain all the information for the story to be complete (obviously), I also want it to feel like a sort of entry for beginner wanderers that teaches them the ropes for Linear No-Clipping. It'll have a quiz, little side notes, and a bunch of other things that makes it feel like a lesson.

**Purpose:** I want Linear No-Clipping to be the solution to the issue of levels that do not lead to the level directly after them, as well as a wider solution to the issue of logistics. All these outposts on all these levels need their resources (food, water, etc) somehow, and right now, it seems a bit more like an afterthought.
