Hello! I am not a permitted greenlighter, but I would still like to give feedback!

[+ show block](javascript:;)

[– hide block](javascript:;)

> Level 288 consists of interconnected hallways, rooms and ramps going on for an unknown amount of length.

A better way to phrase this would be: **Level 288 consists of interconnected hallways, rooms and ramps, continuing to a length of which is currently unknown.**

> using an elevator in [level 0](level-0.md) or [level 15](level-15.md) will lead to level 288

An important aspect of level 0, is that the only way you can escape is by noclipping, which will subsequently lead to [level 1](level-1.md), so you cannot enter your level from level 0. You can, however, exit your level *into* level 0.[1](javascript:;)

The writing needs to be heavily polished, but other then that and the other things I have mentioned, I don't have any other problems with the level. This seems like an interesting level that fits with the backrooms-vibe. I like it, and I hope that it gets greenlit after you improve on the writing.

**good luck!**
