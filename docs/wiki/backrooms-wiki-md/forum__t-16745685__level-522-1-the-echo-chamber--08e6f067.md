This page did not take a lot of effort; I created it in around 12 hours. There are a lot of inspirations for the sub-level, from [SCP-6376](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-6376) to [The Adventures of A Knight and His Disciple](the-adventures-of-a-knight-and-his-disciple.md) to even games like God of War and Fear and Hunger. It's a scattered mess with almost no connecting thread. The main point of the page was supposed to be a very light description of the sub-level, followed by a ton of logs that make up 90% of the page. But, as I scroll up and down the logs I've written, I feel as though I didn't create enough. I will probably release a lot more for this. I'd like to eventually have this contain fifty logs.
