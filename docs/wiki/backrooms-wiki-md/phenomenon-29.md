# Description

**Whispers** refers to a rare phenomenon characterized by temporary loss of hearing concerning all external stimuli, accompanied by auditory manifestations. It affects wanderers who have spent an extended period of time within the immediate perimeter of certain naturally occurring flora within the Backrooms. The duration of the phenomenon is typically limited to a few minutes, after which manifestations subside, and hearing is restored.

Confirmed instances have been verified with audio recordings to rule out alternate (psychological) causes.

# Addendum

Below are a series of compiled logs detailing confirmed incidents of **Whispers**.

[+ Incident Grey](javascript:;)

[- Incident Grey](javascript:;)

**Flora Type:** *Salix matsudana* (pekin willow)  
**Duration:** 78 seconds  
**Contents:**

> *Wind rushing. Footsteps on asphalt.*
>
> "The road—"
>
> *Indiscernible.*
>
> "The road back home has some branches growing."
>
> "Kinda look like long, spindly fingers in the night. I try not to look when I'm walking home."
>
> "I know what you're going to say. Ah…"
>
> *There is a soft exhale.*
>
> "No, it's okay, really. I'm fine. Really."
>
> *The scrapping of tin on asphalt, the sound of wind in the trees.*  
>   
> *The voice laughs a little.*
>
> "There goes a can."
>
> *Steady footfall on pavement continues uninterrupted for some time. Branches creak like garden gates of some location lost to time. Buster of traffic in the distance.*
>
> *A distant thunderclap crashes down in the dark.*
>
> "Sounds like a rainstorm to me. I think it's gonna be a long time before I get any sleep tonight."
>
> *Indiscernible.*
>
> "You know, I went to the park this morning."
>
> "Wild, huh?" *The speaker huffs.*
>
> "So I got to the tree and hung up the paper you showed me. Basically spilled everything I put here."
>
> "I don't think I'm superstitious… but who knows?"
>
> *Footsteps halt.*
>
> "Is it too much to hope?"

[+ Incident Pale](javascript:;)

[- Hide Incident Pale](javascript:;)

**Flora Type:** *Prunus persica* (peach blossom)  
**Duration:** 51 seconds  
**Contents:**

> *The rustle of wind through foliage and distant chirping.*
>
> *A rapid succession of clattering, followed by an abrupt cut.*
>
> *Silence.*
>
> "I'm up early again. I mean, I stayed up late. God."
>
> "I promised that last time was the last time."
>
> "Woke up in time for Nate the other day. He said a lot, but his voice was practically dripping with disappointment our entire session."  
>   
> "Like 'oh, I understand, I'm trying to be helpful here but do you understand'—"
>
> "Like, shut it. You…you are a bunch of things I would never say to your face."
>
> *The voice barks out a harsh laugh.*
>
> "Here, look."
>
> *Sound returns like a crashing tide. The wind howled, accompanied by the irregular clinking of glass.*
>
> "I mean, you can't see but the trees in the garden are pink! After so long that it's completely slipped my mind."
>
> "I was hella excited. And they're here."
>
> "And you…"
>
> *Howling subsides. Clinking settles to give way to ever-persistent birdsong.*
>
> *The voice yawns.*
>
> "The flowerings in spring are so short-lived. Maybe next year then."
