Level 98: The Virtual Level

17 Jul 2020 19:14

Page Type: Level

Elevator Pitch: Level 98 is a small room seeming to have no doors and no way out, but it does have a desk with a computer on top of it. Turning on the computer will reveal the real level, which looks similar to Level 0. You can move around in this virtual level with the arrow keys and the mouse.

Central Narrative: None

Hook: Everything that happens to your virtual self also happens to you. If you go insane in the virtual level, your sanity will also start to decay in the real world.
