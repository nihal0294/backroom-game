So, I´ve been browsing around the tales and entities lately, and couldn´t help but notice the **¨what-if-con¨** tag. So I read a few of them, but one that sorta clicked off the question for me was Project Gaia. This then led me to think:

**Why aren´t governments trying to figure out where the Backrooms are?**  
or to be more clear,  
**Do they know the ´noclipping´ issue?**  
Me, personally, I would think so because:

**A:** The U.S. has nearly 45-70 million surveillance cameras, so at least one of them had to catch someone or something noclip.  
**B:** The families of those who noclipped would probably be searched all the way around by investigative agencies (SIS, ASIS, FBI, etc.), including their neighborhood.  
**C:** The same skyrocketing cases of people going missing all around the globe would probably be a threat to society, and thus, research would have to be conducted in the search for answers. Probably by testing particle phasing. This leads me to my next point.  
**D:** Let us consider this image:  
![340px-Limitcycle.svg.png](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/91/Limitcycle.svg/340px-Limitcycle.svg.png)  
In quantum mechanics, there is an important factor in *p* and *q* which is the phase space. If possible, could a large collider be used to confirm the Backrooms do indeed exist if the particle isn´t detected after passing through the floor?  
I rest my case, you can post your answers in the comments.

1. Photoshop, CGI, and video/photo editing

2. People in some stories no-clip at different points in time, so ancient people don't have cameras, and it may be a rare occurrence from the perspective of people in any given year. This could also in theory apply to people coming from the future, or even alternate realities. So if it were a single person in modern times in each universe from across the multiverse, there is no way they would discover it.

3. Even if they had noticed and saw it as real, they would know nothing about the Backrooms, and not even know what was happening to those going missing.

4. If they did go as far as to use a hadron collider or something, that wouldn't necessarily prove that the Backrooms exist. For all we know, there are other realities like I said earlier, and it could be proof of alternate universes. Though particles behave weird at that size and with that kind of force acting on them, so it would not be understood, and even if someone thought that, it would be speculation at best. Even if they in the most extreme case somehow went as far as to develop travel to another universe, it wouldn't necessarily take them to the Backrooms.

5. A government discovering the backrooms was the original intention behind the Insurgence GOI, though I'm not sure if that ever got expanded on or explained. In that story, the U.S. government found a way to send soldiers there, and did it as a test, only to realize they couldn't retrieve them back. Like I said, I have no idea if that information was ever published, and the author who came up with it is permabanned, so I'm not sure if that will ever happen now, if it hasn't already.
