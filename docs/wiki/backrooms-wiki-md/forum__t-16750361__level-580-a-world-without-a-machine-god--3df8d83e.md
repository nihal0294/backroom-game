This page took a surprising amount of effort, and I'm not sure if I executed it right. To many, this will look like a lazy attempt at a page, and I'm fine with that. I think this is a very risky thing to post, as the "depth" that I intended may just not come across well at all, but I think it would be worthwhile to at least see how it would fare. This page has been in the drafting phase for far longer than the current contest has existed, and is a level that's been in my head for around maybe a year now, but this contest really gave it the purpose it needed to flourish. I think it is evident how much I hate sheep. If this page fails, I will not be surprised, but I will be disappointed.

For all of my works on the Wikidot site, I had vowed that, if a piece were to ever receive a down-vote, I would delete it and rework the page from the ground up. This is the only exception to that self-imposed rule.
