Level Idea: Dead Forest

02 Sep 2021 22:04

**Page Type:** Level

**Elevator Pitch:** The level is a large forest where many individuals shelter within. There are many areas within this level, the most well known of the people of the backrooms is the "Storage Unit", an area where people can buy and trade items for valuable items used within the backrooms. They have so many items inside of the level because there are some scout groups who venture through the backrooms to find extra items for the people of the Dead Forest.. Other areas are towns constructed for people to live within.

Most of the time in the Dead Forest, it snows, so it's best to bring equipment appropriate for this occasion.

There is an entity that lurks on this level called the Skinless Ghosts. They aren't hostile until you start attacking then. A very neutral species.

**Central Narrative:** This area is where people can just break out from the horrors of the backrooms, and genuinely, it's for the people who want to stay sheltered in a safe place where there are no hostile entities to worry about.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** It's not a level with dangerous entities lurking around (although the skinless Ghosts are hostile when attacked, they won't show hostility). It would be one of the safer levels of the backrooms, which most levels really aren't.
