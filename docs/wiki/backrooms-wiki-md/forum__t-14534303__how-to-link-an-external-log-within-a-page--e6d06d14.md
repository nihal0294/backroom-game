Ah, gotcha. I must've misread, sorry! It's like this.

```
[[[(link here) | (name of clickable here)]]]
```

If you want it to automatically go into a new window (my preferred method), you can do this.

```
[*(link here) | (name of clickable here)]
```

Hope this helps!

Edit: Yes, I am slow. XD

I do not know of a way to link to a certain log. Like the example you showed above, it's best just to link the article and the user can read the log by going through the article you linked.

Let me recap.

So for your entity, "The Musician," you want to create an outfit log, yes? The easiest way to do that would to go simple; make a bullet point list or collapsible. You don't necessarily need a separate webpage just for an outfit log, and SCP does it in tables. I would say go for one of those three options.

Of course, if you *need* to create a separate webpage for this outfit log, then it's simpler than you think.

Make a new page that encompasses the outfit log and expand upon it. A new page just for outfits, as I (think I) stated above, is not very practical. Then you follow the reply above yours and link it.
