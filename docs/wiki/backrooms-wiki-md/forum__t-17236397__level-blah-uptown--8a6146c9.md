01 Sep 2025 02:12

Soooo,,, I've been here before. No, seriously, with the same article. Here's the sitch':

I have had this article run through the works of critique before, three years ago by now, and it was greenlit, although it shouldn't have been as it was very much in the rough. It got downvoted off of the site, but it came with much needed feedback! So, I wrote here and there on other sites, and I decided this was an opportunity missed.

So, I'm back to try and give this concept another shot. I think you'll agree it has some potential.

Continuing from the summary, all is not well in this level. Multiple references to a flesh monster that can be conjured up by pure imagination are referenced to throughout the article, ending in a crescendo in the middle, and leaving the reader with many questions by the end(you'll see what I mean). I meant for there to be conflicting dialogue throughout the article, but just subtle enough not to overpower the feeling of it being a part of the description of the level.

As for the entity, I want to create an entirely separate page for it, as well as for the backstories behind the strange phenomena in the level and the communities(two of them are crucial to the narrative!).

This level is an intro to a larger canon. If it seems like it lacks information, it does! The point is to leave the reader grasping for bread crumbs. Maybe not ravenously, like not being super hooked and obsessed, but just interested enough to think "Huh, I wonder how/where this will go."
