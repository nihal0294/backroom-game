# Poetryrooms

rating: +21[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

Welcome to the Poetryrooms! Here you can find poetry about any Backrooms article here on the site, and you can add to them, too!

If you wish to add to the article, simply do the following:

1. Open the edit option all the way down at the end of this page, and go all the way down to the end before the licensing syntax.  
2. In bold lettering, quote the article you wish to write a poem about.  
3. Put a space between the quoted article and the poem.  
4. (Optional) Credit yourself!

And you’re done!

[Level 27 - "The Bunker Springs"](level-27.md)ⓘLevel 27 - "The Bunker Springs"Level 27, also known as "the Bunker Springs," is a relatively peaceful and small level completely devoid of entities. The level appears to be finite infinite, with the only accessible part being the approximately 200 square feet (18.6 square meters) hot spring that houses both the entrance and exit to this level that houses all entrances and all but one exit. The water in this hot spring is potable and mineral-rich.-

Beneath a hellish place lies  
a quiet spring with silent skies.  
No creatures, no trouble,  
no plants, no glow—  
yet life still flows…

Two waterfalls in silence fall,  
from an unknown source they call.  
The waters clearly fall,  
leaving stress and worry to drown…

But stay not too long inside,  
for even peace has a side.  
Even perfection, something stings,  
the splash of endless, unseen springs…

[Level 469 - "The Bluebonnet Fields"](level-469.md)ⓘLevel 469 - "The Bluebonnet Fields"Level 469 is a bluebonnet field bisected by a single road. The weather is consistently sunny and warm, but ambient and apparent temperature can range due to changes in wind and cloud cover. The shoulder on either side of the road is lined with gravel, with some areas showing depressions consistent with motor vehicle tire marks.-

Kiss me softly, wind,  
for as your blue petals spin,  
my exhaustion dims.

Cradle me in sleep,  
held in bluebonnets warm deep,  
the fields I’ll keep.

Once I wake, I’ll laugh,  
I need not a wanderer,  
just these winds and plants.

[Phenomenon 71 - "The Counting Plague"](phenomenon-71.md)ⓘPhenomenon 71 - "The Counting Plague"My name is Mark Anderson. I am, well, I was, a Deputy Underseer at Base Kappa. If you're reading this years from now and don't know what Base Kappa is, that's good; this message found its way to the right person. If you do know… I'm sorry we couldn't stop it.-

One one, two three, three seven:  
can you feel your brain leaven?  
With the blood on the wall  
And the terrors befall't  
Kappa, it's best if you give in.

[Level 0 - "Tutorial Level"](archived__level-0-2020--ebb68d25.md)ⓘLevel 0 - "Tutorial Level"Level 0 is a non-linear space, resembling the back rooms of a retail outlet. All rooms in Level 0 appear uniform and share superficial features such as a yellowish wallpaper, damp carpet, and inconsistently placed fluorescent lighting. However, no two rooms within the level are identical.-

You’ve fallen into,  
Something outside of nature,  
Run if you hear screams.

Reeks of wanderers,  
With no hope of escaping,  
Then I hear a voice.

Whispers of the lost,  
Mixed in with the light’s hum buzz,  
Now, forever damned.

**???**ⓘ???Coming soon!

I can hear the faint tune,  
Of the waltz you played at noon.  
I know I'll leave this world soon,  
But I know you're resting too.

[Level 7 - "Thalassophobia"](level-7.md)ⓘLevel 7 - "Thalassophobia"Level 7 is an impossibly large ocean that seems to stretch infinitely in all directions. Despite the absence of fixed light sources within the level, a dim natural light is present across the level. Detailed exploration of this ocean is sparse due to the extreme danger and the amount of preparation it requires to navigate.-

Chance entails I bail  
The depths I shan’t hail  
For mariners joust  
I shall face my oust

And yet, still I race  
For a beast gave chase  
Spread all throughout  
I just wanted out

More sea, I will see  
And forevermore  
I’m shook to my core  
Of the Thing I flee

I won’t look behind  
Perhaps I will find  
A moment’s respite  
Earned life through my fight

I am a wanderer in a kingdom of walls  
And in this kingdom, I have lived to see many of its duchies  
But despite my wandering, I have found no lord or king  
So I wander ever more -from nobody going to nowhere

[Object 41 - "Electrical Outlets"](object-41.md)ⓘObject 41 - "Electrical Outlets"Object 41, known by everyone simply as outlets or sockets, is the single most common source of electricity within the Backrooms. Like their baseline reality counterparts, electrical outlets are receptacles fixed in walls that supply electricity to any electronic device that is plugged into them. Instances of this object appear in the Backrooms with the same varieties that can be found on planet Earth in a roughly equal distribution.-

Positioned low on the wall  
Constrasting the rest in these halls  
Three of them bunched up if you counted them all  
Had decided to sit down to plug in my device  
It was like rolling the dice

Who knew what I was going to receive  
It might have just been best to leave  
Random files and cries of help  
Watching them all had me let out a sigh  
Oh welp

As time goes by  
Its finished having to charge  
Now getting up to continue going through these rooms so large  
For I had been told to move on

[The M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md)ⓘWho are the M.E.G.?:  
The M.E.G. (a.k.a. The "Major Explorer Group") is a group created by the people of the Backrooms. The group started out as an exploration outpost in 2012, but it grew to become an exploration group by 2014. The M.E.G. tries to keep wanderers safe from entities, traps, and other groups such as The Insurrection, which they are at war with for territory of levels like Level 3. To ensure the safety of the wanderers, the M.E.G. maintains multiple teams for different purposes. This faction is extremely friendly and will allow new members into a team known as the "Volunteer Squad" until they are ready to be in a team of their own.-

Through thick and thin,  
We unite the brave and the weak,  
We save the foolish and the wise.

Division is a foreign concept known only to those,  
Who’ve spent too long a life allowing the darkness to guide them,  
And shunning the light that begs for their salvation.

Unity is praised and practiced,  
Lining every hall ‘til our blood depart us,  
We’ve no time to waste.

But between and betwixt lies a common goal,  
Among us all no matter our stance,  
We must move forward,  
We, the Major Explorer Group.
