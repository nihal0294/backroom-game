Error012 isn't able to give pages a greenlight, so they won't be able to help you in that regard. For a full list of Greenlighters, look at the [Greenlight policy](greenlight-policy.md). However, I will say this is very far from being completed. As a first step on your path to finishing this up, you should first work on how you format the draft. If you compare your draft to any work on the actual website, you'll notice that they're wildly different in appearance. This isn't something that will magically be fixed once it's posted; you need to fix this before you can get a greenlight. You should put your sandbox side by side with a posted work and see how close you can get to looking like it. Here's a small list of guides that should help you with this and a page that you can take inspiration from in terms of formatting: [Level 410](level-410.md), [templates](templates.md), [image citing](image-use-policy.md), [images, headings, lists, and more.](wikidot-syntax.md)
