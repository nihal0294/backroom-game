Hey hey hey, I like it!

You did an excellent job of conveying the message, and the theme you used, while not typically a fav of mine, fits very well. I'm gonna go over all of my opinions on this, as well as any errors spotted.

First of all, you happened to call the level "Level 989" later on in the page, so shift+cmnd+F and you'll see what I mean. I didn't see anything more though, nice.

The idea… I'm going to be brutally honest here: This wasn't very original on the surface. When I began reading, I instantly connected this level with 2 others: an indoor water park, and a play place. While I hadn't seen these combined, that play place level also referred to a section as "The Tubes". I thought I'd add that, so no one tells you this again.

As for the actual piece itself, it looks very good. As I already mentioned, the theme looks good, all formatting is correct, and it just looks nice. You went into much detail on the level, but not *too* much, as to not clutter it up. I will give you an idea for the Pool Boys, however. There is no pool. Maybe you will add this later, but maybe call them "The Lifeguards" or something, as this makes more sense.

ALSO: I noticed what I thought to be a cryptic code near the bottom. I am assuming I am correct, so I just wanted to check that this is not just random. I was really confused with "💣︎✌︎☪︎☜︎"; is it a code?

I don't have anything else! If u have questions/concerns or any draft updates, feel free to pm me!  
Happy writing!
