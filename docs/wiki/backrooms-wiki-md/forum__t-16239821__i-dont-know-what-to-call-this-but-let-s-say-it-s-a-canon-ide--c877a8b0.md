Several problems with this before I can contribute, but each of them have several solutions.  
Okay, first of all, if M.E.G. never existed, how can the database exist? In canon, or at least to me, the M.E.G. database is the the wiki itself.  
Second of all, if M.E.G. just up and dissapears, there has to be an explanation on how that happened.  
Third of all, if there is already a M.E.G. timeline being established by . This would really contradict his work.  
Solutions:  
1. If you decide to go with M.E.G. never existing, then there would have to be an explanation to where the article/the database has come from. Even if you had a smaller number of people in the M.E.G., that would mean it would have less people to contribute to creating the database, and fewer people mean fewer articles. Perhaps make it to where everyone/anyone in the Backrooms is able to contribute to?  
2. The M.E.G. *does* have several powerful enemies in the form of both G.O.I.'s and entities. Perhaps find a perfect one to fit the bill of destroy the M.E.G. Or perhaps make the The Enigmachina malfunction or The Gearmaker mess up in it's task and the M.E.G. dissapear temporarily(if you are seeking to have it fit somewhere in Mr. Crafter's timeline).  
3. Another thing you can use to escape the contradictions with Mr. Crafter's work is making the whole thing an alternate timeline. Then you don't have to worry about having to pm him and sort things out (if you are actually going to do this).  
Tell me what you decide, if you decide to do anything at all.
