Entity 79 "Bloodwagons" (Need critique, article in a finished state)

04 May 2023 15:44

<http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/ottawashington3>

In short, Entity 79 is a Bloodsucking parasitical organism that is shrouded within an exoskeleton resembling a 1960s Cadillac ambulance. it pursues and attacks wanderers until they are incapacitated, and then extends a tendril from its underside to siphon the victim's blood. This is used to further the entity's survival as well as nurture its offspring, who are essentially used as a failsafe protocol in case the entity withers away if deprived of enough nutrience, and will take over from the original organism's place.

Need some critique, article is nearly finished, some details and minor things need to be fixed. let me know if you notice anything worth pointing out be it grammar or concept related.
