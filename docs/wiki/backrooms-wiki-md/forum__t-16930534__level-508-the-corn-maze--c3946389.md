+1 great formatting completing a fun story

08 Sep 2024 10:23

I like this! While the concept isn't groundbreaking, the format complements the narration very well. Having those enclosed collapsible really gives that feelings of a field where it's difficult to see ahead of you. Like the protagonist, we discover what is hidden in the cornfield as we advance, which really adds to the atmosphere.

The way the collapsibles also showcase different contradictory sentences everytime really adds to the idea the protagonist is doubting of what he is seeing or hearing. It works really well near the end when each action becomes smaller and smaller and the overall course goes faster, adding to the feeling of dread and anxiety.

Overall, nice work with this! It's a very small page, but the narration and the format really works well with each other, creating a great experience. I could even say it does a similar job to the original creepypasta in a sense, building up to a moment where we doubt the fate of the character, yet feel uneasy about it.
