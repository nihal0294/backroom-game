Tentatively declaring this my favorite article from the con

30 Mar 2022 19:02

This article has the certain advantage of being the first one I read for the con, so I had a bit of time for it to ruminate in my head. This was an instant upvote for me when I went back and realized that the line:

> we discovered a disoriented, black-haired man, likely in his mid-twenties, wandering around the office area of the Gaia System. Agents detained him and asked how he discovered this area, but the man provided no answers. He only said that he “fell through the ground” while jogging and identified himself as Dr. Michael Dawson while in federal custody. He is currently receiving medical treatment for inexplicable amnesia and injuries consistent with claw marks.

Had been sitting in my head without me even knowing it was from this article, it's just such a perfect and genuinely creepy image. I just love everything about this article, it takes a trope one could consider cliche or overused and just plays it perfectly straight and does it really well. The "what if?" of this article is obvious and in your face, and it feels like the entire article was actually built for this contest, unlike some other entries.

There is nothing this article does wrong, except for the banner with the stretched out american flag that looks absolutely ridiculous, please fix that it sucks so bad.

Anyways, enthusiastic upvote and this will always be the winner of the contest in my heart.
