So, SPaG:

As an overall comment, I would reduce the amount of contracted words (e.g. "it's") if you're writing in a formal tone.

"Discovered by" can be "Discovered through".  
"The only thing that makes a difference" would work better as "The only thing that varies".  
The table would work better with the durations of below the time of day.  
"an strange figure" should be "a strange figure" ("an" is only used if the next word starts with a vowel).  
The sudden perspective shift to 1st person should be fixed; replace the first "we" with something like "The M.E.G." and "One wanderer accidentaly fell into a hole, we never know what happened to the wanderer after that." to something like "A Wanderer accidentally fell down one of these holes, never to be seen again."  
If you want more emphasis on "*he*", you could replace every instance of "it" in that section with the entity's name (except in the last sentence).  
The entity list is fine, but you could add a period (full stop) after each entity name.  
"If you go too far and surviving the glicthes in the level" should be something like "If you explore too far, surviving the glitches in the level". I'm also not sure where the glitches part comes from; nowhere else in the page mentions them, so you could either add more mentions or remove this one.  
"There is only one shack in this towns, it's very useful for supplies" should be restructured to something like "There is only one shack in each town, which is loaded with useful supplies".  
"Many wanderers say that you can get sent to an random level in the shack for an unknown reason." should be restructed to something along the lines of "Many Wanderers have reported being sent to a random level by the shack". The whole "unknown" part is kind of a given in the Backrooms, and doesn't need mentioning. Similarly, I would remove "We can't confirm this somehow".  
"The only form" should be "the only way".  
" noclipping into" should be "no-clipping through" and "noclipped into" should be "no-clipped through".  
"Falling accidentally" should be "Accidentally falling".  
Capitalise "i" in every use of it in the div block.  
I would list an entrance to the level, as it has been explored and documented thoroughly.  
Remove "somehow" from "Somehow staying".  
"an virus" should be "a virus".  
Remove "starting to" from "and starting to".  
"other wanderers" could just be "others".

Conceptually, everything on the page is kind of too disjointed for me to make many comments on. I would focus on connecting everything together more. Once that's done, I would probably explain the origin of the virus and its effects, attempts to rescue people from the holes, and generally more about "he".
