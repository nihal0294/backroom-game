Entity idea: Shadow people

20 Aug 2022 22:04

**Page Type:** entity

**Elevator Pitch:** A shadowy creature resembling any organism that has been infected. These entities are always different humans or entities except with a shadow like body and two white glowing eyes. These entities show no aggression unless you interrupt what they are doing. If you are able to trigger there aggression multiple of these entities of all different variety will manifest from different realities too track you down and kill you. These entities are commonly shown attempting to watch any human in the backrooms and have been shown scratching at thin air for an unknown reason. These entities have been sighted on nearly every level. Any attempt to kill the entity will most likely succeed however it will cause multiple other of this entity to swarm you. This entity is said to come from a completely separate reality and exists in the backrooms from cracks in reality discovered. Information about these cracks other side is unknown as no one has ever left.

**Central Narrative:** this entity was discovered on level 0 of the backrooms while an explorer was attempting to flee from an instance of entity 10. Entity 10 immediately stopped to hunt the larger prey which was my entity. Entity 10 attempted to lunge at my entity but missed. Multiple of my entity began manifesting and dragging the entity 10 through a crack in reality. Eventually, more and more instances of the entity appeared being sighted on nearly all documented levels.

**Additional Notes:** This is my first backrooms entity and I hope you like it
