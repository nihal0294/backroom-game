# THE ETERNAL REPOSITORY

### FINDING THE FORGOTTEN, FORGING THE FOUND

We are **the Eternal Repository**, a collective founded by **the Master Administrator** that is governed by the principle of anonymity. Induction means stripping away identity, reducing it to a codename, and erasing all personal characteristics, until all that remains is function and purpose. The self is set aside herein, for the collective alone benefits.

Our ultimate mandate is to achieve the state of informational omniscience, where all knowledge of **the Backrooms** is catalogued and accessible only to the Repository, with external permission granted solely through equivalence. Every request must be matched with a contribution of commensurate worth, whether material or immaterial. Exceptions are not granted; solely deals, and we are full of them.

Established across extradimensional planes devoid of conventional authorities, we maintain order according to our own standards, guarding archives with measures suited only to us. Therefore, we are compartmentalized by specialized mandates — defense, analysis, exchange, concealment, acquisition, preservation — each distinct, yet inextricable from the whole.

Collectively, we are equilibrium in obscurity.

#### BRANCHES

The Repository functions through a network of compartmentalized branches. Each branch is engineered to execute a singular yet irreplaceable role within the broader mechanism.

Each branch operates autonomously, solely coordinating through structure rather than communication. Some remain without knowledge of the others' existence or the scale of their contribution, if any. Cold efficiency is the lifeblood of our system, and it is sustained by ignorance as much as knowledge.

To impose order upon function, all branches have been divided into four distinct hierarchies, outlined below:

## THE STALWART SENTINELS

"In silence we ward, beyond all ends."

**The Stalwart Sentinels** are our elite military force, bestowed with the finest armor and weaponry available through our highest-tier trades. Their operational versatility allows them to function as soldiers, vanguards, and even emissaries permitted to interact with external entities in the name of the Repository. As such, under controlled conditions, select Sentinels may even be contracted by external parties under brief, costly, and strictly supervised arrangements.

Structurally, the Sentinels operate hierarchically, with Regiments led by Marshal Sentinels containing Battalions commanded by Command Sentinels, which divide into Cohorts under Lead Sentinels. The smallest unit is the Fireteam — four to six Sentinels headed by a Spearhead Sentinel that executes independent or coordinated tactical maneuvers.

## THE ARCANE ANALYSTS

"Truth grants the scepter none see."

**The Arcane Analysts** are responsible for the research, experimentation, and documentation of classified assets under the Repository's jurisdiction. Acting as conduits of communication across all branches, their role both mirror that of [Team Pytheas](cercle-ariane.md) and the [General Public Database](index.md), with the current **Acroamatic Analyst**, **"Oracle"**, rumored to formerly hold stewardship over the Database.

The Analysts are organized into numerous compartmentalized subdivisions focused on a distinct field of research, which may expand into full divisions if warranted. Each one is overseen by a Superintendent, whose authority is maintained at a level comparable to **the Acroamatic Analyst** himself, rendering him more a unifying figure rather than a single point of control amidst the Analysts’ broad mandate.

## THE HANDS OF TRADE

"Sooner or later every war of trade becomes a war of blood."

**The Hands of Trade** are the first branch most will encounter before entering the Repository’s fold, maintaining outposts and other lowly posts where non-members may approach. They are tasked to carry out negotiations or exchanges between the Repository and external entities, requiring frequent interactions with outsiders. Value of goods is assessed through a tri-axis system: scarcity (how unique is this information?), applicability (how immediately useful?), and risk (what exposure does sharing create?). Disputes are resolved through arbitrated blind auctions, where both parties submit sealed counter-offers until convergence or abandonment.

At their head stands **the Godhand of Trade**, whose office accompanies **the Master Administrator** in matters of diplomacy and acts in his stead when required. The present incumbent, **“Faust”**, has cultivated a reputation for his unflinching pragmatism, revolutionizing the branch collectively through the abolishment of the previous strict hierarchical structure.

## THE INFORMANTS OF INTEREST

"Watch your back…"

**The Informants of Interest** are covert personnel embedded deep within external [Groups of Interest](groups-list.md) that assess potential threats by acquiring intelligence that otherwise cannot be obtained traditionally. As their discovery results in near-certain exile or death, any individual coming to suspect or uncover the identity of an Informant must swiftly be silenced permanently.

At any given time, multiple Informants may both menially and influentially operate within every major external party of note; at present, no fewer than four Informants are active in each targeted party, whose identities are unknown even to one another. This renders them the highest kept secret of the Repository, with their existence unpublicized and unacknowledged, even to our highest-valued clients.

While Operations engage the external, Initiations serve as genesis and foundation. They initiate members into our fold while constructing the enduring systems that sustain our totality.

## THE CLOAK CRAFTERS

"Mend anew beneath unending dark."

As the sole branch permitted to see the faces of other members, **the Cloak Crafters**' craft consists of shaping and preserving the Repository's image, from cloaks, vests, coats, and armor to the masks that conceal our identities. For this, the Crafters are regarded with uncommon respect, despite their relatively low hierarchical position.

Because their duties necessitate exposure to identities, the Crafters are subject to routine administrations of varying degrees of **Memoricidics**, which fragments their recollections of members’ faces to be irretrievable. As a result, exposure induces chronic disorientation and episodic memory fragmentation, and they compensate through comprehensively written logs and reliance on procedural memory, which remains intact.

## THE CACOPHONOUS CONSTRUCTORS

"We wrought as sinew, as bone, to its name."

Despite their increased chance of discovery through their cacophony, **the Cacophonous Constructors** are the primary builders of all facilities and infrastructure necessary to the Repository in any specified environment, who are additionally charged with the ongoing and perpetual maintenance and logistical management of them.

Their organization follows a simple hierarchy of Foremen commanding and adapting specialized Crews, with each Crew assigned to a level or area and becoming responsible for both immediate construction projects and, consequently, their disciplined measures of concealment and decommissioning protocols.

## THE SCOURGES OF SCRAPS

"Today's scraps for tomorrow's products."

**The Scourges of Scraps** scour through innumerable levels as our gatherers and preliminary refiners of salvageable, raw material, often through the use of machinery. Each month, they reliably distribute gathered materials evenly to all branches of the Repository for their own purposes, such as crafts and shelters.

The Scourges are constantly motioned in small groups or alone, their routes meandering through dangerous and overlooked levels that contain more resources to scavenge than others. Their low profile grants them operational freedom and an unrestrictive hierarchy of solely Scavengers and Refiners.

## THE DATA DIGGERS

"Forever loyal, forever eternal."

Representing the entry point of service within the Repository, **the Data Diggers** are charged with the documentation of insufficiently recorded levels, entities, objects, and phenomena. They are then compiled and refined by higher branches before entering our internal archives.

The Diggers are mostly deployed singly or in small, inconspicuous groups, often responsible for erecting newly established outposts. Despite the impracticality of strict centralization given their individualist nature, leadership is bureaucratically provided by **the Diligent Digger**. Without them, the Repository itself could not endure.

Divisions are specialized branches derived from the broader framework of the others when a singular function demands autonomy and focus, each assigned a unique Glagolitic letter indicating its function and hierarchical standing. Few for now, but they will multiply as needed.

#### PROJECTS

A project is enacted when a fragment of knowledge is deemed of sufficient value to warrant pursuit. These undertakings may last hours or forevermore, their scope as narrow as a single summit or as vast as the restructuring of an entire concept that persists through decades of reinterpretation.

Each project serves the Repository’s continuum, not the inclinations of its curators. We pursue and dissect until comprehension is attained and rendered stable. Once a project is complete, it is dismantled without sentiment, and its results are absorbed into the archive.
