hmm, conflicting, conflicting. this piece has a helluva lot going for it, definitely. it has a *smacktastic* gif, a thrilling tale told in a mere 36 words, and plenty of depth surrounding it, but i found a few issues. for one, though it may be me, that gif has a lot of trouble loading, though that's probably wikidot. the main issue i found was much more related to the monospaced text, mainly where it fit into the rest of the story. generally, i tend to try to see what it would look like should the monospace text be unmodified, showing it's raw wording, but here, it felt very difficult to find the right flow in that manner. it felt more, substituted in the wrong direction. instead of the monospace feeling a little off place, it sort of felt the other way around. maybe it's just me being invested in the general aptitude of nostalgi gaius, but it felt weird to read that one layer deeper.

no-vote for now, leaning towards an upvote and may change my mind depending on where the contest goes. i've decided seeing how the leading factor for my novote was the compatibility thing, which is just wikidot anyways, to change my novote to a tentative upvote.
