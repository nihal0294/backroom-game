Hiya!

So, there are two ways to upload an image:

First, you can put the following text in your article and replace the link with the one to your image file.

```
[[image linkgoeshere.com]]
```

This will just put an image in your page, plain and simple. No caption, not anything else, just the image.

Secondly, you can use the image component by putting the following text in your article and adjusting accordingly.

```
[[include component:image-block 
| name= /* Enter the link for the file here. */
| caption= /* Enter the text you want to show up in the caption here. */
| align= /* Enter either left, right, or center here. This is for where the image shows up on the page. */
]]
```

This will specify where you want the image to show up and will have a caption.

And to host an image on the site, click the “files” button at the bottom of the page and then click “upload file from computer”, and then just select the image you want! It’ll upload and if you click “info” on that file, it’ll show you the link.
