> ## Interrogation of "The Reaper" - RECOVERED
>
> ---
>
> #### Date: July 04, 2024
>
> ##### Location: [REDACTED]
>
> **<BEGIN RECORDING>**
>
> **E.O.A. interrogator** : [After pouring a water bucket] Wake up.
>
> **The Reaper** : Blyat'..
>
> **Chifuyu Konosage** : I will get straight to the point. You will not get out of this place until you answer all of our questions. And we do have a lot of questions, "Reaper".
>
> **Chifuyu Konosage** : First, I want to know what you and your little friends from Liberation were doing the past 3 months. We are aware that you're plotting something with this so called "[Paradise 997](level-997.md)" but I want you to know more about this "[Operation Liberation](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-997/offset/3)".
>
> **The Reaper** : [While sarcastically laughing] Don't make me laugh. Is that all you know about us? As expected, you are all bunches of incompetent rats. We are way ahead of you. We made many discoveries that will insure the completion of our mission. Poshel na khuy, Suka!
>
> **E.O.A. interrogator** : We don't have time with your nonsense. Activate the electric chair.
>
> **The Reaper** : AHAHAHAH! IS THAT ALL YOU HAVE, MUDAK?!
>
> **E.O.A. interrogator** : You psycho… Turn the power to the maximum!
>
> **The Reaper** : [Visibly convulsing] …
>
> **E.O.A. interrogator** : Turn it off. I think he got enough.
>
> **The Reaper** : [Quickly regaining his senses] Pfiou, it's been a while… It reminds me of my times in the gulag. But they were slightly more intimidating. You, you are just bunch of clowns compared to them, eheh.
>
> **Chifuyu Konosage** : You won't answer our questions? Fine, this is your last chance before we use our ultimate weapon. What was that earlier. You're not a god are you? So, why weren't you affected by any of our attacks??
>
> **The Reaper** : Oh, that. You know, I had a lot of fun crushing your agent's head one by one. While they were tickling me with their miserable blades and bullets. I had the power of God. Very lucky of you to have knocking me down. Bravo!
>
> **Chifuyu Konosage** : ENOUGH! Sister, bring me the mask of truth[1](javascript:;). I hate the fact about using this monstrous object but at least he will be forced to give us at least some information. He left us no other choices.
>
> **The Reaper** : Listen to me, Suka. You will n e v e r, defeat Liberation and the reverence! The inevitable will occur. The judgement day is coming. Your pathetic houses of cards[2](javascript:;) will collapse under the chaos we sowed. All humanity will kneel before God and its creations, and all that will be left for you to do is beg for forgiveness!
>
> **Chifuyu Konosage** : Is this your last words?
>
> **E.O.A. interrogator** : What's that glowing thing around his neck?
>
> **The Reaper** : Do you hear me, father? Liberation Aeternum.
>
> **E.O.A. interrogator** : Fuck, this must be a bomb! This bastard tricked us! ALL PERSONNEL EVACUATE IMME-
>
> A loud explosion interrupt the recording

**The B.N.T.G. soon learned of his death and wasn't happy about it. Apparently it was one of their best trader. Little do they know we know we made them a bigger favor.  
Rest in peace my fallen brothers. We will avenge you in due time. Justice prevail.**

***- Leader of the "Justice Seekers"***
