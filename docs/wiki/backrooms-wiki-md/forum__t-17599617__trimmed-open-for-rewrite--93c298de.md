*Real* late reply but this is notice only applies to articles with that notice as far as I’m aware. Articles listen under the trimmed category are articles that haven’t been updated in some time that just don’t match with the complexity of the current articles on the site.  
Now that’s not to say there’s simpler articles on here that are fairly similar to older ones. The notice in the trimmed article hub states

> This category is reserved for pages deemed uninteresting, unsalvageable, or lacking sufficient value to warrant further attention.

Basically it means that the level or page that hasn’t been updated in a while (like level 0 for some time) that would have to go under significant changes— to the point of being unrecognisable from the original —to fit the modern standards. From my understanding a lot of the older levels were pretty shallow and were just lists of entities attached to a creepy image. So anything that didn’t have nuance, if any at all.

In terms of your writing I think it’s best to look at drafts and ask yourself this: “if anybody was to link to my page, would they be able to?” Think of it this way, somebody is writing a level that is being investigated by the M.E.G and they mention a level the M.E.G previously explored, one of the levels is yours. When people click on the level out of interest you want them to be able to understand why it links to the level instead of leaving your page thinking “why is that there?” Of course you’re level isn’t always gonna be linked into some big massive lore filled story arching over many pages but it’s still good to keep that possibility in mind.
