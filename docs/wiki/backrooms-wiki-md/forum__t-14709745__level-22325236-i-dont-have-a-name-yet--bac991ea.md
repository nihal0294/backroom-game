Level 22325236 is the 22325237'th Level of the Backrooms.

### Description

Level 22325236 is a 4m wide, 4m tall brick hallway that stretches out 50 miles north. there are branching paths on both sides every mile that go directly forwards relative to the wall they appeared on. Each branch has 50 branches, which continues infinitely. due to this, the size of the level cannot be measured. Noiseless, Light blue, LED lights are lined along the ceiling. The level has an artificial Day-Night cycle, Abruptly turning the LED lights off every 8 hours, and turning them back on 6 hours later. Travellers who enter this level will only enter halfway through the "Day" section, and they will enter with all of their possessions.

### **Entrances And Exits**

Entrances

### **Exits**

Noclipping through the wall in any location will send you to a random level , but has a 20% chance to send you to the void  
Falling asleep exactly halfway through the main section or any branch will send you to level 1  
Falling asleep within 6 metres of a glowing red exclamation point will send you to level !
