# **[The Wrong Stage](the-wrong-stage.md)**

Posted on 19 March 2026.

> ##### Sneak Peek
>
> *Regardless, the Comedian has a job to do, so xe shuts the door, descends the stairs, and strides onto the stage, doing xir best to hide xir uncertainty. Xe looks at the guests sitting down, and they feel a little familiar, but something about them still feels wrong. On the stage are two seats — useless to the Comedian of course, jokes aren't funny enough sitting down — and a microphone within its holder. Despite the uncertain feelings xe has, stepping onto a grand stage always gives Comedian a sense of purpose, enough to give these feelings a backseat in xir mind. Instead, xir mind was now racing with ideas to make the people gag. Xe grips the mic, picks it up, and, wasting no more time, begins the performance.*

---

[Show commentary](javascript:;)

[Hide commentary](javascript:;)

> Oh boy, this thing, I have quite a bit to say on this. This article was written as part of Secret Santa 2024, and was my first attempt at writing for a canon, that being the Terror Hotel Canon. It was a gift for Praetor who asked me to, and I quote:  
>   
> *"I would love to see a page that takes two other articles and brings them together, linking their lore with each other, as well as expanding each other's lore in a meaningful way. I don't want to give any article restrictions, so surprise me with the choices! With no contradiction with the rest of the site lore of course :P"*  
>   
> So I had to chose two unrelated articles and make a page that combines their lore into one, effectively linking them together. Now I'm very bad at Backrooms lore, so this was difficult, but after a day of brainstorming, I had two ideas for unrelated pages that were perfect to link to each other. These were Level 74 and Entity 185. So I got to work writing this page, which I enjoyed quite a bit.  
>   
> I sent the gift to Praetor, and he loved it, but even after that, I still wanted to get it posted, but… the fear of posting pages was pretty real at the time, so I didn't muster up enough courage to ask Praetor for crit, something he offered at the time. It wasn't until March 2026 when I decided to get crit and post the page. It didn't get quite as popular as I hoped, so be sure to give it a read if you like Terror Hotel comedy.
