# A New Era for Humanity

Throughout the history of Backrooms, many humans have been unwillingly transported into this paranormal dimension.

Alone, humans were at the mercy of the Backrooms' environment and entities already established long before the arrival of the human species. But, human nature, which is to unite and cooperate for survival, was preserved.

They founded various communities with the common goal of survival, and progressively gained power until they eventually reversed the balance of power between humans and entities. It started with ancient communities who founded the Collective of the Lost, then medieval communities founded the Eyes of Argos until more modern communities built the M.E.G.

Humanity, whose fate was determined by an accidental no-clip from their homeland, ended up in a dimension they were never supposed to be in. Knowing they were trapped against their will until their inevitable death, some humans thought they should establish their domination in this newly discovered land, while some decided to explore alone.

However, a small part of humans yet enough was convinced of the opposite. These humans wanted to return to the past times before humanity developed into what it has become today. They wanted the entities and the harsh environment of the Backrooms to remain dominant over humans, just like it originally was.

That's where corruption originated.

Corruption is a parasite that starts from a single host. The parasite then multiplies, again, and ever again until it infects all its surroundings.

But despite our greatest efforts, we weren't able to contain the corruption.

After Liberation's takeover, they established an oppressive regime fueled by terror. They use propaganda to discourage and create internal conflicts within the resistance. It is mentioned in their description that they tolerate wanderers minding their own business. Still, in reality, they persecute and manipulate absolutely any wanderer they find, regardless of their status, or their age. They will not hesitate to eliminate children or even babies if they are ordered to. They execute anyone at the slightest suspicion without bothering to involve justice.

Because of the mass influx of people defecting from their groups to join theirs. Several patrols of Liberation were deployed on many levels to maintain "peace". They do not wear any recognizable uniforms, so we would recommend you stay alert at all times and avoid contact with strangers.

Furthermore, they settled outposts in several Levels to coordinate their operations against the resistance.

But humanity's fate is not sealed yet. There is still hope to save humanity.

That's why we decided to set apart our differences and unite under one coalition. To fight a common enemy: to eradicate corruption.

# A New Coalition for Humanity

The first group fighting this corruption and the founder of the syndicate: us, the [Masked Maidens](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/the-masked-maidens/offset/2), plays a key role in our coalition, with our precious knowledge about our enemy. The [Eyes of Argos](the-eyes-of-argos.md) joined up not long after, supporting our various operations against corruption. We then managed to rally the [H.D.F.](u-e-c-team-homelanders.md) and sub consequently the [U.E.C.](the-u-e-c.md) into our cause. While reluctant at first, they were surprisingly attentive to our claims and understood the severity of the situation. The [Backrooms Robotics](backrooms-robotics.md) who closely followed the news volunteered to join the coalition. After the attacks of December 25th, the [Eternal Repository](the-eternal-repository.md) and the [The Ariane Circle](cercle-ariane.md) were willing to support the resistance. Finally, it was the [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) turn to join our coalition. Seeing that the U.E.C. was working with us and our historically poor relations between us and the M.E.G., they thought more than twice before joining the coalition. But they suffered the most losses of all the other groups, so the situation didn't allow them to work alone.

With our collective efforts, we were able to gather crucial information about our enemy. Their Headquarters are hidden in a closed network of levels, named "the lands of Liberation", cut from the rest of the Backrooms. The problem is that the only access is through Level 997 and reports warn us about a dangerous cognito-hazardous entity possibly controlling the premises.

But, Liberation is nothing without its leaders: The Patriarch and the Saint Mother.  
Eliminating them both would shatter the whole organization and hopefully bring an end to this corrupted era.

The attacks of December 25th were certainly not their last move, so they must be plotting a final strike.

We are the last front of resistance, failure is not an option.
