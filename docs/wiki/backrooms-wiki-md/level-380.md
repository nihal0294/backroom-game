# Description

Level 380, often known as "The Plight of the Pedestrian" consists of a dual carriageway bordered by boreal forests.

The roadside is a dirt footpath going both directions, alongside the dual carriageway. The road extends for 50 kilometers, before looping back to where it started, while remaining entirely straight.

Do not try and cross over the road to the other side, as any attempts invariably result in death. Despite no cars being visible in either direction, trying to cross causes a car or other vehicle to appear outside your view and hit you at highway speeds, before both the vehicle and the corpse vanish. The vehicles are assumed to be controlled by the level itself and not by separate entities, as they do not appear outside the level and cannot leave the dual carriageway.

# Bases, Outposts and Communities

### The Cross Coders

The Cross Coders are an offshoot of [The Completionists](the-completionists.md), obsessed with crossing the road despite the fact that this is impossible, as they believe some "great secret" lies on the other side. The group was originally started to try and complete the new achievement to cross the road on level 380, but over time members started to think that the other side was more important than was previously thought. While they still work with mainline Completionists, the Cross Coders do not try and complete any other achievements.

There isn't much shelter in the camp, but it doesn't matter as there are few sustained residencies. While there isn't any food or drink in Level 380, long-term Cross Coders survive off supplies brought by newer members.

In mid-2024, the MEG decided to entertain the Cross Coder's idea of their secret in case it did turn out to be important. The Cross Coders were assigned a M.E.G. researcher and provided with requested materials at no cost.

> ### Addendum:
>
> It is now known to the M.E.G that level 380 maliciously targets wanderers, and that its method of murder always tries to cause the most casualties possible. Fortunately for wanderers, none of it's vehicles can leave the road, and it cannot attack if you stay off the road. For your own safety, and the safety of others, do not cross the road, or join the ranks of the Cross Coders, no matter how sensible it seems.

# Entrances and Exits

#### Entrances

Level 380 can be entered by taking a tunnel in an underpass that is at 90 degrees from the main passage, in any level where such structures appear.

#### Exits

Walking into the electric substation next to the Cross Coder base will lead to [Level 3](level-3.md).
