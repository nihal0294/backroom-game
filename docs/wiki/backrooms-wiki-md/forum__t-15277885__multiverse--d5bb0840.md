Multiverse?

07 Oct 2022 15:16

In the wikidot, there's approximately (I assume) 289 levels in determination of written levels(plus the courtroom that's in re). In the fandom, there's a *lot* more levels that are unique.  
Now, my question is: *How the hell are the backrooms fitting all these levels*  
If we take Level 9223372036854775807 and assume that it is the final level of the backrooms and no more rooms go after that, then there are 9223372036854775806 other levels. EXCLUDING the negatives  
Now, I know it's out of reality and all that, but that's more than 18 zeros. 1 quintillion seconds is approximately 31,688,738,506.8 years. That's older than the universe, and we have *9* quintillion.  
What I'm proposing is that the backrooms is a multiverse, and not just a singular array of levels. It would make sense with the noclipping still be effective as transport here, and different levels have completely different laws of reality.  
And before you say "That's fandom tho," that would bring up more evidence for the multiverse if we say that the fandom and wikidot lores are different.  
Am I thinking too hard here or is my one brain cell coming up with something smart.
