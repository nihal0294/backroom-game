Idea: liquid pain rework

03 Nov 2023 12:41

Type: object

Core concept: a rework of the old Object 12, liquid pain.

So we all know the liquid pain right? The old object 12, an liquid that is very deadly and made By Skin stealer blood and almond water. Well, since the author of the object (I forget their name) got banned from the site, we they had to delete their pages and one of them was liquid pain. Well, liquid pain, in the first times of the backrooms was one of the most iconic objects of the community. However. Deletion of an object like this of course made many people disappointed, including me which really likes liquid pain.

To fix this, I got an idea to rework the liquid pain and add it again. However, it will not be the same thing as the old liquid pain was. It has been a long time since it was removed. Here are some things that I have in my head about the rework

**THINGS THAT WILL NOT CHANGE**  
-The color of the liquid  
-The way to form the liquid  
-It being deadly (the liquid is literally called liquid **pain**
