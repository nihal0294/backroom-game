Re: Level 128 (64 was taken)

23 Aug 2021 23:57

I think the concept itself is unique in the Backrooms story, however I do have some concerns about the execution of it…

> Supposed to represent video games, but every time someone goes there, their false memories of video games are added into the level

Um…what? I really do not know what you mean by this. Can you clarify?

> Hook/Attention-Grabber: it's video games i guess?

That is not a hook. A hook is something that is unique about one idea. It could be a special way to enter and/or exit, a special colony/group inside this level, or a special object (not the only options, of course). Just "video games" is a theme, not a hook.

> Central Narrative: none

It's really not a good idea to make a level with no story. How was it discovered? What entities/groups lurk within it? More importantly, how does it fit into the Backrooms as a whole?

In general, I think there needs to be some more backstory to this level. I would recommend getting into specifics about the discovery of this place. If my time at the SCP Wiki taught me anything, it's you can't just not have a story.
