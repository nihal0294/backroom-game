Level 890 : ''The toy shop''

Description:

Level 890 is composed of randomly segmented rooms, each of these rooms are dark and ressemble a toy shop.The toys appear to be from reality and videogames and other machinery in this level does not work (exept for flashlights and objects from other levels). The entities here appear to be extremely aggressive (hounds, wranglers, facelings, smilers etc). The entities here can be repelled by splashing liquid pain on them. This level is non-euclidean and time in this level works backwards.

Communities and Outposts:

There are no communities or outposts in this level.

Entrances and exits:
