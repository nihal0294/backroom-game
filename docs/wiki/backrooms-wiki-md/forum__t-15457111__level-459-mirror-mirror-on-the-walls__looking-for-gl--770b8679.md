12 Dec 2022 18:25

For draft critique, go to the draft critique forum.This is for ideas, like your summary of the thread.  
  
I do, however, have some critique. This is something I myself did in my sandbox (using "you"), and it doesn't match the clinical style we're supposed to use. Instead of "you", perhaps use "Wanderers". Is there anything else about the level? It seems pretty empty of information. Are there any locations or other things of signifigance aside from the hallucinations?  
**There are no known entities on this level.** Instead of that, consider using "There have been no reports of Entities on this Level."  
Capitalize Backrooms in the introduction.  
I've seen this mentioned several times by other critters. There are several types of madness. What kind, specifically? Schizophrenia? DID?  
**These hallucinations will cause the wanderer's voice to change and they will begin to see twisted reflections of themselves in the mirrors. These reflections appear monstrous and often causes the wanderer to panic, their distorted screams only adding to their already panicked state. These hallucinations can only be combated by staying calm and closing one's eyes.** Again, the tone is more personal than clinical (I have the same problem, don't worry). Instead of referring to the reader or giving the facts like this, perhaps state that surviving Wanderers have reported these feelings.  
**Level 459 can exited by no-clipping through a hallucinatory door, bringing you to Level 460.** So, how do you no-clip through a hallucination? Perhaps you can no-clip through something else, but a hallucination seems a bit far-fetched.  
  
As Gustave Flaubert said, "The devil is in the details." Specifics, my friend. Overall, I like the idea. Maybe you could call it "The Hall of Illusions".

PS: Most of what I told you are common issues for me that I'm working on.

Of course! On another note, there are still some tone corrections that need to be made, but better! Here's what I found:  
**According to a survivor of Level 459 that goes by the name "[DATA CLASSIFIED]", claimed the hallucinations will cause a wanderer's voice to change, and they will begin to see twisted reflections of themselves in the mirrors.** Easy fix. Just take out "claimed" and you're good.  
**She also stated that the hallucinations can be combated by staying calm and closing one's eyes.** Perhaps say that the Wanderer reported that they calmed down when they closed their eyes. In this paragraph, perhaps you should say "they" instead of "she", as this gives a hint to who te Wanderer may be, and the name is classified.  
**Although, multiple reports of a monstrous-like roar has been heard.** Perhaps say something along the lines of "However, there have been multiple reports of loud, distorted roars throughout the level." After the Entity speculation, you can put down, for humor, "We have also theorized that these roars are the screams of other Wanderers, though distorted by the psychological effects of this Level." I don't know, I just thought it was funny.  
**The deeper you go into the level, the more severe the hallucinations become, eventually leading to Schizophrenia.** Another easy fix. Just lowercase schizophrenia.  
Much better on the exits section. More concrete and workable.  
Still wondering if there are any signifigant locations to this Level, like a room of doors that lead nowhere, or some other thing (idk where that came from).  
  
  
I think the new changes are coming together, and this could shape up to be something pretty good.
