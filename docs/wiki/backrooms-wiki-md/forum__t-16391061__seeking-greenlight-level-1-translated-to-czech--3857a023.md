Greetings.

As this is a translation to Czech, we will not be able to host it on our site, as we're an English-speaking community. However, it can easily be posted on the [ULI](index.md), without the requirement of a greenlight, though make sure to follow the rules stated on [this](http://backrooms-uli.wikidot.com/translation-rules) and [this](translation-hub.md) page.

As for the image block code, it is functional, though you have transcribed the image format differently for both images,(i.e. the first one is "JPG" and the second is "jpeg", but this is up to personal preference.). Note that there's a spare equals sign at the bottom of the page that isn't utilised by anything. In addition, the second image block lacks a period at the end of its description. Lastly, you have not translated the details in the license box, but that isn't too important.

May your journey in writing be fruitful.
