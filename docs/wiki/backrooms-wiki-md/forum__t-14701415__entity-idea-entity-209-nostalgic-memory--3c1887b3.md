Entity 209 “nostalgic memory”

[![flickr:3314800722](https://farm4.static.flickr.com/3476/3314800722_0c1fc75b17_b.jpg)](https://www.flickr.com/photos/deviant1/3314800722/)  
this entity manifests it self differently depending on the wanderer.This entity will manifest as a nostalgic object like your favorite childhood video game. And when approached by a wander the victim will go into a nostalgic trance, as an example if they manifest as say Minecraft, the entity will will spawn a tv a console and a chair, everything needed for a session of Minecraft there saves everything will be there. the victim will play with the manifested object for a variable amount of time until a hostile entity comes and the victim now unable to stop playing and run will be ruthlessly killed by said entity. Though it’s nostalgic affect is strong it can be fought. On some harmless levels like level 5 they are completely non capturing and will not put you in the nostalgic affect. Though this is rare. When you see a nostalgic object walk away swiftly and think the past is the past. This is the only known way of surviving this entity. If you start playing with the nostalgic object you are doomed. Do not attempt to save a person in this nostalgic state in fear of either being sucked into the nostalgic affect or being killed by the person in the nostalgic trance out of fear of never being able to use there favorite object again.  
Note this is an idea I will clean it up and make it good if it’s acsepted
