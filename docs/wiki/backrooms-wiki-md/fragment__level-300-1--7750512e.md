# fragment:level-300-1

*Spits*… "Shit boys, we're getting mighty close to the taste of eternal life! I wonder if these little angels are just gonna let us have it!?", said Daryl, his southern accent just as thick as when he first clipped.

"Who are you fooling? We've still got a solid few hundred feet to go. Don't let your guard down — I have a feeling those vixens are gonna come crashing down at us any minute.", Albert replied, as he usually does, to shoot Daryl down.

*The group, six in number, was tied together, using warps in the tree's bark to tie them somewhat safely to the tree. They each were adorned in jean-jackets with wings stitched into their backs, along with harnesses.*

"Live life a little, Bert. You'd think eith how badly you wanna live forever you'd at least enjoy it a lil'", Daryl cackled.

"Just focus on making it to the top before you start celebrating.", Bert barked sternly.

*Time passed relatively silently, though the sound of rope gliding along their harnesses, their laboured breathing, and intermittent grunts filled it somewhat. The wind whistled violently, and the group's gaze darted to their side. An angel was approaching, and it wasn't to greet them kindly.*

"LET THE SLACK GO, GODDAMNIT, DROP!", warned Gilson.

*Three of the six men let go of the tree and fell several feet to avoid the angel's attack, one man failing to react, and the other two leaping to the next piece of tree trunk and ascending. Barry was cleaved clean through both sides of his abdomen, his body tugging on the ropes of the rest of the group tightly. Daryl and Gilson acted quickly, severing their respective ends of rope that were connected to Barry. His blood that stained the tree was quickly absorbed as the angel flew away with his body.*

"Naw man, we can't keep going after that. We have to go back down and warn the others — it ain't worth it no more!", Gilson bargained. He was at the head of the three men below.

"Suit yourselves then, pussies! Me and Bert here are gonna keep on keeping on; y'all can see Barry in hell!", Daryl exclaimed.

*Bert observed the others arguing, then without a word, resumed his ascent. Daryl followed close behind him, while Gilson and the others hurriedly descended back to their group.*

"You know, I always liked you better than those folks anyways. You and me buddy, we're gonna live forever! Next time one of those pretty little ladies flies by I'm gon' rip 'er wings out!", said Daryl.

"I'll never be your buddy… I just don't wanna die down here in this place.", Bert said sternly.

*As they finished speaking, and amidst their rushed climbing, the wind began to whistle once more. This time, several angels surrounded them on each side. The men both paused; they were only a couple dozen feet from the top. As the angels swooped in, Daryl reach for the machete at his hip and took a well-timed swing, after which Bert dropped down a short ways and leapt off Daryl's shoulders, simultaneously severing their rope and avoiding the onslaught. Daryl succeeded in wounding one of the angels, but they weren't safe just yet.*

"Maybe you'll be the one seeing Gilson here soon.", Bert remarked.

*Daryl grunted in rage, grasping onto each piece of the trunk as he desperately wriggled his way up. As Bert looked up to resume climbing, an angel speared him straight through the head. His body plummeted, snapping the rope attached to him and flying right past Gilson and the others. Daryl grimaced as he heaved himself over the edge. A flat area was sprawled out in front of him, with hundreds of bushes of the fruits described in legends emerging from the treetop. Sunlight glistened brilliantly through the thick leaves and branches, painting a picture of the Garden of Eden.*

"HAHAH! You son'bitches didn't think I'd make it! Now look at me, all on my own!".

*Daryl darted towards the first bush he found and began devouring the fruit, unaware as to just what he was about to subject not only himself, but everyone to.*

*Slurp*, "God, the taste of eternal life is so damn sweet! Don'tcha wish you would've made it so here with me, little brother?"

*Daryl paused, looking down at the left side of his chest to see that an angel had used its wing to pierce straight through his heart.*

*Belch, gurgle*, "Heh, you've got me, but it's too late now–"

*Daryl's feet were **planted** to the tree as a torrent of vines entangled their way up his body, swiftly encasing him in a cocoon as his muffled screams and the sound of his bones and organs being turned to mush like he was being juiced echoed out.*

He hurried back out the door, a look of disdain plastering his face as he resumed the evacuation.

While we have taken a monumental hit to our resources, what is important is that we all made it out of there alive. We have established a blockade on all known entrances to **Level 300**, and we will move forward having learned a valuable lesson — never get too comfortable with what this realm has to offer us, for it could all be taken away in an instant.
