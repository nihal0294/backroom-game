Anomalous broadcast  
We recovered the footage of our "Emergency Alert Scenario" broadcast. The EAS was broadcast to every television in the Backrooms network and the giant screens present on level 11, until it was hijacked by what we strongly suspect to be our aggressors. The broadcast was meant to inform, prevent, and reassure the public, but it completely backfired on us. We believe the broadcast of our aggressor may possess anomalous effects as reports say that this broadcast caused mass hysteria, later followed by paranoia and distrust, leading many people to turn against each other.

This footage may contain anomalous effects, view at your own risk:

M.E.G. Base Beta, Level 11, 25 Dec 2024, 00:09
