Am I late?

> Level 736 is a wooden, flimsy, and extraordinarily high rope course. Adding to the height of this level, the wood is also very weak and flimsy, so breaking this level is quite easy. 736 is also covered in moss and fungi, though neither of them add any extra challenges in this already hard and dangerous level.

This sentence is written very well, but I think it would be better to change it this way: "Level 736 is a wooden high rope course.". Long term corruption has made the environment here fragile. The floor plan and facilities of Level 736 have reached a fragile balance, and the arrival of any wanderer may disrupt this fragile balance. Mold and moss are spread throughout the floor of Level 736, making the already fragile environment even more vulnerable. “

> Level 736 is an infinite, wooden rope course.

As you mentioned earlier, delete this sentence.

> And if you fall, you will no-clip through the floor. However, instead of ending up in another level, you will fall down thousands of feet (or meters) down until you hit the real bottom of this level and die.

No-clip will definitely go to other levels. I suggest modifying it this way: "Falling in Level 736 is very dangerous, and for some reason, the person who fell in Level 736 ultimately did not catch the platform of Level 736 again.". But instead fell into the abyss below Level 736. This kind of decline may even last for several years. However, Level 736 does not have a bottom - but all wanderers who came into contact with the rocks at the bottom of Level 736 have lost contact, and there are currently no records of survivors. “

> Despite being a rope course, there are no harnesses preventing you from falling down. This means that, if you slip from the course, then there’s no chance of you surviving any longer. So, if you’re clumsy, it's highly recommend that you NEVER visit this level, it's a death sentence for anybody without good coordination.

These words can be further simplified: "Level 736 does not have any safety protection facilities, which exacerbates the already unstable situation in a few areas."

> Now, even if you’re not clumsy, or slip easily, this level is not easy to traverse. As well as that, the structure is believed to be hundreds if not thousands of years old, and things begin to wear down by that time. The only thing holding each platform above the abyss is a thin, crappy old wooden pole going thousands of feet to the ground.

This sentence can also be modified as follows: "Level 736 is still in constant wear and corrosion. Now, every rusted screw in Level 736 may be the culprit that caused your death."

> If you thought you could just not try and sit still

There is a blank line in this sentence and the following paragraph, please correct it.

In short, this level is done very well, continue to work hard! I really want to see it appear on the website. You only need to make some small formatting changes, such as changing the title of each section to the font of the title. I believe it can be released quickly, and I enjoy reading this :)

I hate to critique a critique, but even your fixed version of the first paragraph is still very repetitive, as you write "fragile balance" twice in the same sentence and repeat the word "fragile" four times in the same paragraph

. I would suggest to not tell authors who you are critiquing exactly what to write (as I have seen in my experience that they usually just copy and paste it) and instead just give them broader instructions such as: "See if you can make this sentence tighter" or "how can you write this sentence more formally." This forces them to come up with their own solutions, which in turn helps them develop their personal voice and skills as a writer. It's a form of the "fish for him // teach him to fish" parable

## SPaG and Formatting

> Level 736 is a wooden high rope course.

Should be written, first of all, with the Backrooms signature “Level 736 is the 764th level of the backrooms. It is…” I’d also call it a “high wooden rope course” or if you want it to be super fancy, “wooden rope course at a high altitude”

> **Description**

For headers, use a + and a space before the text. This makes the headers more visible, making it easier to differentiate the parts of the text (tip: when using the +, it automatically bolds, so the \*\*s aren’t needed.)

> Each part you walk across leads to a larger platform. All of the larger platforms are circular, not very big, and have at most 4 connections to another part of the course.

I’d add “Level 736 is an infinite expanse of platforms and obstacles.” If you do this, change “ 4 connections to another part of the course” to “ 4 connections to an obstacle or another platform.”

> Some parts are small circles you need to…

Change to “Some obstacles are small circles you need to…” and so on.

> The ground has grass, and even some flowers here and there, and lots of trees

The ground has grass, lots of trees, and even some flowers here and there.

> Falling in Level 736 is very dangerous, and for some reason, the person who fell in Level 736 ultimately did not catch the platform of Level 736 again.

Falling in Level 736 is very dangerous, and for some reason, a person who falls in Level 736 ultimately will not catch the platform of Level 736 again.

> Now, every rusted screw in Level 736 may be the culprit that caused your death.

Could be simplified to Now, every rusted screw in Level 736 may prove fatal on contact.

> These poles are not very stable, and can easily fall if too much weight is placed on the platform or course part they connect to.

There’s no mention of poles before this, so this should be changed to “The poles in this level are…”

> The highest weight you should place on these platforms depends on how big they are.

Should be changed to “The greatest amount of weight”

> 1: Do NOT stand still. 2: Be careful and cautious of where you walk. 3: Just don't enter this level.

This shouldn’t be in a footnote. Dos and Don’ts aren’t in levels, but I’d recommend finding a way to enter it into the description. I’d also change 3 to “Don’t enter this level if possible”

## General Critique

> However, Level 736 does not have a bottom - but all wanderers who came into contact with the rocks at the bottom of Level 736 have lost contact, and there are currently no records of survivors.

This is confusing. It says there isn’t a bottom to the level, but wanderers have come in contact with rocks at the bottom. This could be fixed by saying the illusion of the grass isn’t the ground, but sharp rocks lay far beneath or something similar.

> Person and Wanderer

This article switches between calling the humans in the backrooms people and wanderers. This may be a personal thing, but committing to one label will possibly make this easier to read

## Note

This is honestly an amazing idea for a level. It’s a great read and I look forward to seeing it official.

The use of the traditional phrase:

> "Level [BLANK] is the [BLANK] level of the Backrooms…"

Is one that's falling out of use and is actively being phased out within newer drafts. Although you're not barred from using the expression, you should not enforce or suggest its use if it's against the wishes of the author. I haven't read more than the first paragraph of your post, but "high ropes course" is technically correct. A high ropes course differs from a low ropes course in that (in the real world at least) the course is set substantially high the air, requiring participants to be suspended from harnesses for maximum safety, whereas a low ropes course presents no such danger due to the course being set close to or touching the ground.
