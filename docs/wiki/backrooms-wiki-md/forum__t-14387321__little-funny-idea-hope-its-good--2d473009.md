# Description

The liminal archives is a soft [blue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color) codex of many mysteries and many places. It holds a similar first few levels and then branches off with unique places and a unique idea of limspaces. limspaces are spaces out of regular reality, levels in the backrooms are limspaces, however when limspaces are bunched together they are called limspace systems. the backrooms is an example of such a limspace system.

### Limspace systems

Some limspace systems are the crimson, the midden ground, and the abyss. The crimson system appears to be connected to level "crimson [forest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grass_(disambiguation))" due to the fact that they are the exact same thing.
