[You open a bottle, hoping to find Almond Water.](javascript:;)

[Instead, you find a handwritten critique notice.](javascript:;)

As always, I'm writing as I go, so apologies in advance if it sounds like I'm pulling this from thin air. Just know that I haven't read it completely until the overall takeaways. I also apologize for the bluntness.

> Should a light source shine onto the walls floors or ceiling the substance will immediately begin to Harden and leak blood after forty seconds of being shined onto the surface being shined onto to shatter with the force of a hand grenade.

The idea is pretty good, but it needs to be expanded on. What is the substance made of? Are there exceptions to the light shining and making things explode?  
Going purely based on grammar, I would rephrase the sentence to "Should a light source shine on the surface of the walls, floors, or ceilings, the substance will immediately begin to harden and leak a blood like substance. After another forty seconds, the surface that the light is shining on will explode, sending shrapnel out with the force of a hand grenade.[1](javascript:;)" Include the footnote there.

> This will leave an open hole leading into a black void which takes explorers to Level 6.

To be honest, I don't think this should be a guarantee. Make it a possibility. Instead of "will", I would put "may". Put a comma after "void".

> Should the Level Succeed in doing so the blood of the person will be removed via the wound and absorbed into the Level1.

Lowercase "succeed" and put a comma after "doing so". My other issue is this: how does the M.E.G. know this fact? Is there a survivor account?

> After the person's blood has been removed the corpse will be melted down into the Level.

Put a comma after "removed". Again, how does the M.E.G. know this?

> After traveling 152 meters into the Level the walls will have parts of rusted metal pushed outwards in an attempt to harm Wanderers. Should someone travel 213 meters into Level ### The walls will begin to block off sections of the halls with rusted metal and all surfaces will begin to harden into their more solid form making them more prone to exploding. Currently none have made it past the 213 meter mark as the combination of the metal with the surface hardening and more often exploding.

All in all, This is very interesting, though I still have the same issue as the last two crits. How do they know? This is very specific, and the trials required to obtain this information are not worth the loss of life.

I stopped right about here to give you these last points. First, add a lot more information to this. Treat it like a story of facts, theories, and evidence. Once you've finished updating, run this through a grammar checker, as there were a lot of spag mistakes here that should not have been made. For now, add some meat to this, and then season it. Let me know when you're done with it!
