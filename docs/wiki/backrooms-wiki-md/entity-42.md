### Description:

**The Memory Wyrm** is a colossal creature, bearing great similarity to the Frontrooms species *Arenicola Marina.* The wyrm exhibits a wide variety of metaphysical abilities, which it uses mainly for pursing its prey.

The entity's reality-warping abilities are highly potent, often causing wanderers near the wyrm to report intense and unnatural feelings of comfort or nostalgia, even by the standards of the Backrooms. This "passive effect" that the wyrm exudes likely exists to pacify its potential prey, in order to make consumption easier.

The mouth of the wyrm opens to reveal a vortex of razor-sharp teeth that seem to lead endlessly down its gullet. Victims caught in the wyrm's spell are almost always lured in, and quickly devoured.

---

### Biology:

Accounts of the Memory Wyrm's biology vary from telling to telling, as sightings of this creature are considered extremely rare, and survival of encounters are rarer still.

The wyrm is always described to be a mammoth of an entity, with an estimated size anywhere between 300-600 meters in length. While of impressive size, it typically has at least 3/4 of its body buried underground at any given point. It is thought to use this extra length for leverage while chasing its prey. With a girth of ~5 meters, its size is, overall, comparable to an extremely long subway or passenger train.

The exterior of the entity is composed of a dense, yet flexible, layer of hardened skin which is a disturbingly human shade of pink, unlike the normal off-brown color of regular wyrms. It is thought to be dry in texture, with visible cracks on its surface, and it provides the creature with what could be compared to natural armor. Conventional weaponry has been proven to be ineffective in piercing this hardened shell and dealing any meaningful damage, making combat with the entity extremely difficult. In addition, the wyrm's exterior layer has been known to "glitch" or "stutter" when its full body is in motion, which is assumed to be the combined effect of its titanic size and extremely low stability in local reality.

The mouth of the Memory Wyrm is most commonly described as a gaping maw, filled with razor-sharp teeth that make a spiral shape down its gullet. It has been observed that these teeth have the ability to rotate inside of the its mouth, allowing it to tear through prey like a chainsaw. Each tooth is around 16 inches in length, and extremely dense.

*It should also be noted that the Memory Wyrm may have the ability to physically alter its bodily appearance. Some rare accounts of survivors describe the entity with drastically different features than what is most frequently observed during encounters. For a list of documented alterations, see the supplemental documents at the bottom of the article.*

### Behavior:

*Heed not the call of The Spiral.*

*The beast of the disgraced bears only false truths, a distraction from our meager existence. The lecherous scourge shall not overcome our will — not with its Siren song, nor its unbridled fury.*

*Fear the spiraling abyss of lustful disorientation! Ne'er shall one live to see the day in which we slay the beast, but for that reason alone we must endure.*

*Brethren, even the cruelest of horrors still hold value, though imperceptible to most. We must perceive the beast's truth. We must learn from it not to dwell or contemplate upon that which is past, for living within our memories shall deprive us of the beauty of the now and the hope of the hereafter.*

---

— Excerpt from το χαμένο βιβλίά του χώρου (to Chaméno Vivlíá tou Chórou), volume VI.

The Memory Wyrm is driven by one thing, and one thing only: its eternal hunger.

The entity is plagued by an insatiable starvation, its famishment only ever temporarily negated by the constant consumption of memories. It has been observed engorging itself on almost every sentient creature, and will frequently swallow its victims whole. Notably, it also seems to possess an extreme appetite for humans, and most curiously, [Memory Jars](object-7.md).[1](javascript:;) This particular craving is so great that it will even stop a hunt if it senses any nearby. Currently, this is one of only two known ways to reliably "lure" the Memory Wyrm to a given location, or to interrupt one of its rampages.[2](javascript:;) Given the wide availability of Memory Jars across many circles, wanderers are advised to carry some on their person at all times as a distraction, in the unlikely event of a wyrm attack.

*See attached file 1-A for more information on the Memory Wyrm's feeding habits.*

Another of the Wyrm's notable characteristics is that it possesses some semblance of sentient will. The entity has been shown to hold grudges against wanderers that harm or escape it, as evidenced by the attached interview.

- The Memory Wyrm's size was drastically reduced, being able to fit inside of a small room.
- The wyrm appeared to be growing a wispy type of hair on its back, near the front of the "head."

Wanderer Sarah Cratser reported the following deviations:

- The Memory Wyrm's body was significantly thinned, and it appeared to be much longer than normal from her point of view, having encountered this creature before in its normal state.
- All of the wyrm's teeth were missing, leaving only a gaping hole at the front of its face.

---

Wanderer Atlas Bruce reported the following deviations:

- The Memory Wyrm's entire body was entirely a shade of pure vantablack, seeming to absorb the light around it to an extent so that it appeared almost two-dimensional.
- A massive, glowing-white symbol in the shape of the Greek letter "Omega" was placed directly in the center of its forehead area.

## Feeding Habits of Entity-42

---

Unlike most members of Phylum Annelida, the Memory Wyrm does not need to consume nutrients to survive. Instead of consuming prey for the purpose of restoring energy, the main purpose of the Memory Wyrm's hunts appears to be to sate a purely psychological hunger.

The Memory Wyrm prefers to hunt in a slow-paced ambush style, often favoring having its prey come to it than vice versa, similar to that of a Venus flytrap. The entity is blind and hunts off of vibration and sound. Considering the esoteric structure of the Backrooms, this allows it to take more unique approaches to hunting, as the wyrm does not need to be in the same area as its prey to sense it. While animalistic, the Memory Wyrm is highly intelligent, comparable in intellect to that of a common cephalopod. It possesses great problem-solving capability and adaptability. If a wanderer or entity is caught by the Memory Wyrm in this state, its life essence and memories will be completely drained. A telltale sign that one of its 'hunting nests' is close by is the sopping, moldy flesh-pile of partially-devoured corpses, fused together by the toxic saliva of the wyrm.

While preferring the aforementioned method of hunting, this by no means implies that the Memory Wyrm is incapable or unwilling to hunt prey in a more conventional manner. If deprived of memories for an extended period of time, it will enter a state of blind hunger-driven madness, using all of its energy to barrel through whatever level it currently inhabits until it hunts down and consumes a victim. Considering its gargantuan size and reality warping abilities, encountering it in this state is extremely dangerous and to be avoided at all costs.

> *The following log is an audio-visual transcription of an interview between M.E.G. Agent Jackson Brown and a young wanderer named Avalon Adams.*

---

> **Agent Jackson:** "State your name and occupation for the record, please."
>
> **Avalon:** "Avalon… Avalon Adams. Is it even possible to have an occupation here?"
>
> **Jackson:** "Well, it depends. If you associate with a particular group, people often have specific jobs. For example, I could say that I'm a 'M.E.G. Field Agent.' Most people just say 'wanderer' though."
>
> **Avalon:** "All right. In that case, I'm Avalon Adams, wanderer."
>
> **Jackson:** "Thank you."
>
> **Jackson:** "On to business: It's come to our attention that you've had an encounter with Entity 42, or the 'Memory Wyrm,' yes?"
>
> **Avalon:** "That's correct. I unfortunately crossed its path a few times in my days here."
>
> **Jackson:** "More than once? Allow me to offer my sympathy for the experiences you had."
>
> **Avalon:** "Thanks— that *thing* is rather… unpleasant."
>
> *She chuckles nervously.*
>
> **Jackson:** "What was your first encounter with the wyrm like?"
>
> *Avalon pauses for a moment, letting out a deep breath as she recalls the jarring memory.*
>
> **Avalon:** "I was in [Level 1](level-1.md), travelling to [The Hub](the-hub.md) in order to get somewhere a bit safer. I was coming back from meeting an old friend at his house in [Level 11](level-11.md), when I stepped on some unstable ground and got noclipped into the good 'ol concrete zone."
>
> **Avalon:** "After letting out a little bit of… 'colorful language' from the situation, I ended up walking down a pathway that led me straight into the hallway where the Memory Wyrm was hunting."
>
> **Avalon:** "As I was walking, I started getting a very calm feeling of nostalgia. Being here for a while, I was quite accustomed to this type of feeling. You know how whenever you end up in [Level 0](level-0.md) you get that weird calm uneasy sensation in your gut? It was like that but stronger. Thinking nothing of it, I pressed on straight into the trap it had set for me."
>
> *Jackson scribbles a few notes into his spiral-bound notepad.*
>
> **Jackson:** "Do you think it's possible there was an extranormal effect preventing you from recognizing the danger?"
>
> **Avalon:** "Truth be told, I really don't know. I think of myself as generally observant, so it's possible. However, I could have just been an idiot."
>
> **Jackson:** "I see. Carry on."
>
> **Avalon:** "After a few more seconds, maybe minutes— I don't really know how long it was, time seemed to flow inconsistently when I was around that thing, almost like a dream. I was completely out of it when it was happening, but afterwards the absurdity of it all hit like a truck."
>
> **Jackson:** "So let me get this straight: so far, we have artificial nostalgia and time alteration, yes?"
>
> **Avalon:** "Correct. This cycle continued in intensity, so far to the point that I didn't realize where I was anymore. I had gone from an orderly concrete hallway straight into where that thing was waiting. Its hunting nest, if you will."
>
> **Jackson:** "Can you describe this 'hunting nest?'"
>
> **Avalon:** "It was… gross. The walls, floor, and ceiling were completely covered and dripping with this thick, gooey, warm and viscous substance. It was opaque and white, and I swear I could see these small stringy things inside of it. It looked like clam chowder from hell."
>
> *Jackson laughs.*
>
> **Jackson:** "Clam chowder from hell? That's a new one. This material usually shows up at Memory Wyrm attacks, and it has loads of atypical properties that would definitely cause the things you experienced. Did you happen to get any of it on you by chance?"
>
> **Avalon:** "I was standing in a puddle of it."
>
> **Jackson:** "That'd do it. The Memory Wyrm's saliva is one hell of a drug."
>
> **Avalon:** "I noticed. I was there."
>
> *The interviewee chuckles at her own joke. Jackson reciprocates with a small grin.*
>
> **Jackson:** "Anyways, carry on. If you could remember how you snapped out of it or escaped, that would be very helpful information."
>
> **Avalon:** "Now that you mention it, there was something that sobered me up, aside from being inches away from a vortex of teeth the size of a subway car. By some miracle, a loose pipe of [Almond Water](object-1.md) burst above my head and ended up drenching me, sobering me right up."
>
> *Jackson tilts his head, frowning.*
>
> **Jackson:** "Really? That's new. We'll need to conduct further testing, but this could prove very useful in the long run if it can counteract the trance effect."
>
> **Avalon:** "Happy to help in any way I can."
>
> **Jackson:** "Your cooperation is always appreciated. What happened next?"
>
> **Avalon:** "Well, the wyrm definitely seemed to notice I wasn't under its control anymore, and it got *pissed*. The ground started rumbling, and I wasn't about to stick around and see what happened next. I bolted for my *life*."
>
> **Avalon:** "I ran down the corridors, and the wyrm chased me through. Luckily, I was in an area with very compact halls because it seems the wyrm definitely had a hard time squeezing through to chase after me. It was practically ripping up the concrete as it slithered behind me."
>
> **Avalon:** "Eventually, I came to the entrance to [The Hub](the-hub.md), and I turned around to see its maw slam into the door frame behind me. I'm not being hyperbolic when I say that the entire lobby tunnel of The Hub shook like it was in a tornado. It seems something else was stopping The Wyrm from entering because otherwise there isn't a doubt in my mind it would have—"
>
> *Avalon pauses.*
>
> **Avalon:** "*Level-ed* that whole place."
>
> *Jackson facepalms, trying to hold in a laugh. That joke was awful.*
>
> **Jackson:** "Anything else? You did mention a second encounter with the wyrm. Was it pretty much the same?"
>
> **Avalon:** "No. It was something out of a fever dream, even in comparison to the weirdest stuff in this place."
>
> **Jackson:** "Well, don't let me stop 'ya."
>
> **Avalon:** "I think that thing holds grudges. After I escaped, in every level I entered, I'd always start to feel that same feeling from the first encounter. I'd always leave after that occurred, not wanting a repeat of the first incident. But one time I was asleep."
>
> **Avalon:** "I was on some beach level… I can't recall much about it, but when I woke up, I felt a rumbling underneath the sand. The water in the distance was frothing, and I could make out something enormous over the horizon."
>
> **Avalon:** "Still trying to shake off the drowsiness, I started walking across the beach, to find somewhere to get out. After a few minutes, I checked back to the ocean to see the most enormous creature I've witnessed in my entire life."
>
> **Avalon:** "This thing had its entire body above the waves, stretched out like an ornery sea serpent. This thing looked like Jormungandr and one of those sandworms from *'Dune'* had the most angry baby you could possibly imagine."
>
> **Jackson:** "That's quite the analogy. What happened next?"
>
> **Avalon:** "Well, I thought I was about to become this creature's lunch, until two beings stepped out of a spatial tear behind me."
>
> **Avalon:** "One was wearing glasses, a black trench coat, and was carrying a scythe. He looked like if they cast a younger Elton John as Neo in *'The Matrix'* and then gave him even more combat gear. More interestingly, his arms were *completely* covered in bandages."
>
> **Jackson:** "Interesting. Did you manage to catch a name?"
>
> **Avalon:** "Not exactly, but the other figure called him '[Argos](entity-74.md).'
>
> *Jackson scribbles something down in his notebook.*
>
> **Avalon:** "[The other figure](entity-71.md)— he was dressed in what looked like the clothes of a medieval knight."
>
> *The furious scribbling continues. The agent's brow is furrowed.*
>
> **Avalon:** "Are you ok? You seem confused— do you want me to repeat anything?"
>
> **Jackson:** "No no, it's fine. Just a long day, that's all. Please, go on."
>
> **Avalon:** "All right. Anyways, like I was saying, these two individuals came up and started fighting this thing. The one called Argos led me away from the fight and force-clipped me to [Level 6.1](level-6-1.md). Before I left, I could see the knight and the wyrm locked in an intense battle. That guy must have been ridiculously strong because the wyrm's entire body seemed to shake whenever he struck with his sword. Even so, the wyrm was keeping up with him. I don't know what happened after, as I had begun to clip."
>
> **Jackson:** "Is that all?"
>
> **Avalon:** "Yes. The wyrm hasn't bothered me since, and I still have no idea what I experienced."
>
> **Jackson:** "Neither do I. This is *way* above my pay grade. I thank you for your time, Avalon. Please help yourself to a bite to eat on the way out."
>
> **Avalon:** "Thanks Agent Jackson. It was a nice meeting you."
>
> **Jackson:** "Likewise. Take care, Ms. Adams."
>
> *The feed cuts here.*

# **Do's and Don'ts:**

### **Do:**

- Slowly move away the *moment* you think you see it. It hunts primarily off of vibration and sound.
- Pay heed to any strange underground rumblings you hear while in outdoor levels.
- Use [Memory Jars](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/memory-jars) to distract the wyrm and make your escape.
- Keep [Almond Water](object-1.md) on your person to prevent becoming entranced.

### **Don't:**

- Approach the Memory Wyrm intentionally for any reason.
- Fall for its illusions.
- Linger within a location whilst experiencing feelings of unnatural nostalgia.
