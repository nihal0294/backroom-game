The line between Entity and Level

20 Feb 2022 16:47

Hello all,

Years of real-life work experience with local authorities in horrible storage rooms have inspired me to write about several concepts. I mean, this is before the days of video games where you could "fall out of bounds". God, the atmosphere there was so, bizarre and oppressive. I really wish I took photos of the places where I once worked - they were horrible areas that made Level 0 feel "palatable"; without any windows or natural light, tripping hazards, sealed up doors, corridors that repeated on themselves with no ventilation; you'd deem these buildings fire-hazards, surely?

I have an idea for a seemingly benign/morally ambiguous creature that can randomly leave clues, or invitations for any wanderer from any other Level, to follow it, and come into its Level.

I suppose the inspiration for that, is "Alice in Wonderland" where the main character follows the White Rabbit, oblivious as to what she's about to get herself into. Most often, a wanderer who has ended up on a hazardous Level, or being pursued by a dangerous Entity, would jump at the opportunity (wouldn't you if you were facing certain death?).

Once there, the entire Level is an endless, barren, sterile void, that somehow seems "alive". Its as if the Entity is both everywhere, and nowhere, at once. It's virtually impossible to escape - there's no exits beyond an infinite horizon. An "avatar" of the Entity materializes before you calling itself the Entity/Level that saved you. The "neat freak" creature, seems to want nothing from you, apart from polite conversation. It seems kind, after all, it helped you out of a jam. But it hates the dust and mess humans seem to always make.

The longer your sojourn inside this being/place, the smaller you become. At first you don't notice, but eventually you can't deny you're shrinking. The avatar of the creature ignores your plights. You get tinier and tinier until the apparition of the entity before you is colossal, now regarding you as not much different from the "human dust" it despises so much, and decides "you must be cleaned up at once". It puts you away, but you find yourself not thrown out of the Level but going deeper inside and further into the recurring entity, eventually you're placed into its sub-levels, where the creature has put away various other "messes", human or otherwise.

But I wanted to ask - when writing about said powerful Entities that essentially control an entire Level, to where these beings could be considered "dimensional lords" i.e., having complete mastery over that physical plane and anyone who enters, to where it's difficult for us to comprehend or distinguish between Entity and Level - what would you call them? What is their category?

Are they Enigmatic Entities/Sentient Levels or something else entirely?

That's a difficult question, in all honesty.

Levels are usually just planes in this void we call the Backrooms. They usually aren't alive, sentient, or sapient. They just exist as places you can walk on or exist in. However, that doesn't mean that they can't be that way. A level that thinks on its own would be an Enigmatic Level, where the ground can shift in response to a wanderer.

An enigmatic entity, however, is something that is powerful beyond our realm of comprehension and is most likely aggressive. There are exceptions, but they are few and far between. They rule over their domain and can control anything that happens there, like the controller of a computer.

Hope that clears up the confusion between the two

Thank you for the response. It helps clear up the uncertainty of the two. It was tricky to define them both when there's overlap - especially when an enigmatic entity has such power or influence over a plane, it behaves just like a level.

I suppose a level is more abstract, and an entity in backrooms is more of a personification. I didn't mean this to be a debate about epistemology or ontology. Perhaps therein lies the idea for a new level/entity to write about - a Schrödinger's cat thought experiment. Is the cat alive or dead?

Perhaps I could try a creature like Ouroboros. An entity that thinks its a level, or a level that thinks its an entity, and the wanderer exploring it is none the wiser.
