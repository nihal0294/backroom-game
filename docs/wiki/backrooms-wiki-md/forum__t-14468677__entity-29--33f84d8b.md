**Entity** 458

**Habitat** level 0

**Description** Mothman is a 12 foot tall man with the wings, arms, and head of a moth.

**Behaviors** Mothman will fly to the closest light and grab it until a hostile entity walks under him, then he will let go of the light, and it will tear it until it is in small chunks and devour it. if a wanderer walks under Mothman, he will fly to a light far away from them.

**Biology** Mothman has 16 arms, and his intestines go through this brain like a giant squid, all so like a giant squid, he is really dumb.

**Do's and don'ts**

**do**

- walk with good posture and stand far apart to look human.
- yell loud to scare Mothman.

**Don't**

- huddle close, *he will* drop down
- crawl on all fours. he could mistake you for a hound.
