The original is quite good imo, but this translation just can't quite match, sorry. Phrasing is awkward at some places and it has SPaG issues. Also, are the CN type punctuations intentional?

> Because your arrival became richly colorful

Because of your arrival, it became richly colorful (?)

> Your departure will make this place lose color again, you should not leave this place.

The original has an implied conjunction, but English is different, to better fit the original vibes you might want to use a period instead of a comma.

> This is all thanks your arrival.

This is all thanks to your arrival. (?)

> That place is your warm bed.

"Warm bed" is a very direct translation and is not super appropriate here imo, a better phrase might be "seedbed" or perhaps even "beginnings"

> this place is your forever-staying affection…

I'd rewrite like "this is your eternal home", but there's probably some phrase that mimics the love aspect better

> and you will luckily arrive here!!!

You generally wouldn't define someone else's action as lucky from their perspective, idk translation is hard

> Attempts to edit this file will be ineffective

I'd just say "this file seemingly cannot be edited"

Anyways there's way more things I'd change but then it'd be more subjective than anything, these are just the blatant ones I've found. idk. If you are not related to the creation of this page go read [https://backrooms-wiki-cn.wikidot.com/level-c-5](level-c-5.md) if you can
