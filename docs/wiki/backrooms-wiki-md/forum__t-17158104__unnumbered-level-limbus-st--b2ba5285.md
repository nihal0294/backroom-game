Alas, the execution doesn't do it for me -1

29 Jun 2025 15:24

Ah, I'm not sure I would have used footnotes the way you did, if at all. I see the attempt at provoking intrigue, but I don't believe it suceeds, I kind of groaned upon reading the first footnote to be perfectly honest. It's like the author is continuously poking me with a stick while murmuring "Hey, hey, there is *something wrong with this place!*", which rapidly threw me off.

I also don't think the footnotes particularly justify themselves? See, [L343](level-343.md) pulls the same "gimmick" as this page, but it works because the page itself is an anomaly, a part of the Level. It's also clearly written like an advertisement, so the footnotes turns into an effective subversion of the format. This isn't really the case here, the author of the in-universe page is *also* the author of the footnotes, so why wouldn't he just… directly writes his thoughts *into* the page itself? It's clear that the character is in a precarious situation, so him breaking the clinical tone or even not following it at all would make sense.

I will say the ending works pretty well! It's subtle enough, yet hits hard.
