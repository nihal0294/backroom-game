# Timer Collapsible Showcase

### timer-collapsible

**timer-collapsible** — Clickable text with a countdown timer. On the first click, the content expands and the countdown begins. When the timer reaches zero, the active block is replaced by the expired block.

---

**Usage:**

[[include :backrooms-wiki:component:timer-collapsible  
|timer=60  
|title-active-closed=[CORRUPTED DATA]  
|title-active-open=[READING…]  
|title-expired-closed=[DATA EXPIRED]  
|title-expired-open=[DATA EXPIRED - READING]  
|content-active=Text of the active block.  
|content-expired=Text of the expired block.  
|marker-closed=▶  
|marker-open=▼  
|color-active=#3a6dc2  
|color-expired=#888888  
]]
