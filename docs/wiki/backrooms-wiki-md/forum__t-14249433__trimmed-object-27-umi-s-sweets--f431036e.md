+1 in 2025; Underrated

14 Apr 2025 14:44

This is an excellent article. There are other articles that fill the niche such as Candy, Dumb Gum, and Greasy Marshmallows, but this is by far the best.

As an article, it fulfills its goals excellently. It is obviously based on Trick or Treating, and in this case it keeps to the initial premise in an elegant Backrooms-like way. You literally have to survive being tricked to get a treat. That is combined with an unknown Entity that is described comprehensibly while allowing some room for interpretation, on a Level that fits the Halloween aesthetic perfectly. It also introduces anomalous candy which takes something normal to our world and twists it on its head.

I love this article for all the reasons mentioned previously. I have also played a Christmas one-shot in my D&D campaign, and my players *loved* opening boxes to find Umi's Sweets and Candy. They spent 2 sessions experimenting with them and having fun. That is how good this Object is.

This article deserves vastly more than 20 upvotes, I wish I could give more.
