> Human scan

Having your entire page in a collapsible is kinda bad practice and I'd heavily discourage you from doing so. There isn't really a need for it and it overall doesn't look very visually well; if you want something like this, you could do it, just try using other CSS things than a collapsible. Though this may just be my hate for collapsibles talking lmao.

> We both know what its for.

In this instance, "its" should actually have an apostrophe and be written like "it's" since "its" is the possessive use; "it's" instead would mean "it is", which would be fitting in this case.

> I wake up in a red hall full of pipes, i then

The second use of "i" needs to be capitalized here.

> then find a dead skin stealer with a note

"skin stealer" should be capitalized since it's a proper noun and there should also be a link to the Skin Stealer page so that the reader gets

> saying “Follow the blood stains (=” I remember

These quotations (") should actually be half quotes ('\_) because it's been placed inside of another set of quotation marks. For more information on this subject, you can look at this this resource: <https://www.grammarbook.com/blog/commas/quoting-a-question-within-a-question/>

> is a Sub-Level to

"Sub-Level" shouldn't be capitalized in this instance. Only capitalize the word if it's used with a number.

> Level 2.1 “Hot steam” is a

"steam'" in this instance should also be capitalized.

> is Non-Euclidian and

"Non-Euclidian" shouldn't be capitalized here.

> and can change shape

Punctuation is needed here.

> Euclidian and can change shape  
> Every 30 Minutes, Entities

Add a linebreak in between these two paragraphs.

> Every 30 Minutes,

"Minutes" shouldn't be capitalized here.

> 30 Minutes, Entities are

"Entites" shouldn't be capitalized here.

> are more Hostile than

"Hostile" shouldn't be capitalized.

> than they’re original version

"they're" should be "their".

> A group of Hostile Friendly Party goers

"Hostile Friendly" neither of these words should be capitalized; "Party goers" should be one word.
