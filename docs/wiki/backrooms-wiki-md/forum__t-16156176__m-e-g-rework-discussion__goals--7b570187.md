M.E.G. Rework Discussion: Goals

12 Jul 2023 04:55

So I'm thinking of making the goals of the M.E.G. in the rework the following:

M.E.G. Goals

1. Find an exit to the Backrooms (The reason the group was founded. While not as commonly seen in the present, it is still the primary goal of the group)

2. Help and protect wanderers (A goal adapted as the group grew. Gained more focus over time, since there are no leads to finding an exit up to this point)

The group was formed by the original three overseers and some others lost with them in the Backrooms, so the original goal ofc would be to find a way out, to get back home. Over time, an exit wouldn't be found, and the focus would shift to helping other lost wanderers, though the original goal remains. The goal to find an exit was even stated in the tale "The Beginning of the M.E.G.", which is what I was originally going off of.

Thoughts?
