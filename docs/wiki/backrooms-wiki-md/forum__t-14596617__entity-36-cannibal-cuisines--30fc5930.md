Love the concept, great execution. The addiction/enslavement angle was an excellent choice.

Although the amateur chemist in me questions the nature of, what reads as actual dopamine, entering the body from the products and somehow crossing the blood/brain barrier. In most cases of medicine or addiction, dopamine is produced by the body, triggered by chemical signals. Taking dopamine orally wouldn't reach the brain under normal conditions. Maybe some properties of the entity make it effective in the backrooms :-) That's just me nit-picking though and it was a worthwhile read.
