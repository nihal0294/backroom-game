could use a glow up here's some ideas i had

09 Feb 2022 11:05

This level i feel could use i little glow up so here's my ideas.

This level would have a more unnerving atmosphere if the outside was pitch black, this would add a "after hours" feel to the level. I feel there could be more elaboration on what the businesses "sell" and the other things that they may offer, perhaps some anomalous products for wanderers to find?  
I don't like that the food kills you, I feel it could be expanded on like maybe some foods have differing effects (elaborate on these effects).

The out of the blue mention of Level 703 is rather out of place and should just be removed. The fact that looking up your location shows that your in Houston is really cool and should definitely be expanded on, it would give the lvl a lot of lore to work with. regarding the exit gates instead of leading to an unknown place maybe they could lead to an outside area that would be very dark and dangerous, maybe a small list of dangerous entity's or hazards to add to the danger factor of the outside.

I like the idea that some of the gates can lead to different levels of the backrooms perhaps even enigmatic levels like the end. The basement could be described a bit better, tell me what is the basement like, have small or big is it? what are the walls and floor like? is there any decorations? The tram system is quite interesting but again it should be elaborated on more. The 914 outpost should be changed to a fictional plane instead of a irl one, it could come across as insensitive having it be in…
