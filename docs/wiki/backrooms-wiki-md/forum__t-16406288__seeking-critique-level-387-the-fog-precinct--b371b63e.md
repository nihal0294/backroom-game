**Name:**Level 387 - The Fog Precinct

**Seeking:** Critique only

**Genre:**Bittersweet, mystery, horror

**Description:** Level 387 is a cold, foggy morning level consisting of a somber forest, road, and busy apartment building. The level is surrounded by a border of fog that seems to be linked to disappearances. The level is densely populated and all the inhabitants make up a developed and complex community known as The Fog Scouts. Throughout the article, it is slowly hinted and revealed that this community has many dark, morbid, and non-human secrets…
