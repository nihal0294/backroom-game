I like the idea some quick fixes I would do would be to fix the header (i just copy the code from a working page and edit it), there also seems to be some spelling, spacing, and grammar mistakes (not putting spaces after periods), I would delete this section "The level has its own entity named "Quartzies" and add a new header "Entities" and have your Quartzies as a subsection under it (so it matches the rest of the formatting of the wiki.), and a few of the almond water links seem to be broken.

For some of the bigger changes I would be more detailed in this part "The light source is considered to be the quartz itself." giving some reasoning of why the light source is thought of like this if that a wanderer explaining or listing the properties of the light. I would also go into more depth on the tunnels and with the width tunnels in general can get very wide so for this section"its width is much wider than possible" I would be more specific like if the tunnel width is infinite (like a infinite plain) or if there's something strange with the top of the tunnels. If you wanted to add more to your page you could also have the fog have a strange effect on wanderers.
