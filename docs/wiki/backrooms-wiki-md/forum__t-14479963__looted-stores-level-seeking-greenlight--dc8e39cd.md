So, all you have is a couple of capitalization errors, and the entrances/exits also have grammar issues. I can fix entrances/exits, and if you want to know where the capitalization errors are you can just ask me.  
Entrances  
When crawling between the ceiling plates in level 0, there is a chance you may find a hole in a concrete wall. This will lead to one of Level 25's stores which contain large concrete plates.

Finding a manhole cover on the surface of Level 24 will lead to a manhole cover on level 25. What is strange is that no porthole has been seen on Level 25, even in stores, and once the porthole isn't in direct line of sight, it disappears.

Exits  
Find a store that has exit signs and grab one. This will create a hole below your feet, and bring you to a variety of levels, but most likely Level 39 or Level 15.[Add Links]
