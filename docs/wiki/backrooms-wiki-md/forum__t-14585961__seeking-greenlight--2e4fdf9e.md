---

[+ show block](javascript:;)

Generally, there are a lot of capitalization errors, specifically at the start of sentences.

> Level ! is a fan made level of the backrooms, and does not have a number.

These kinds of levels are called [Enigmatic Levels](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/enigmatic-levels) here; hence, this should be changed to:  
Level ! is an Enigmatic Level of the Backrooms.

**Description**

> Level !, also referred to as Level "Run For Your Life"…

Level !, also referred to as "Run For Your Life"…

> be a hospital hallway approximently 10 kilometers long.

approximately

> the only natural light source in this level are the constant bright red exit signs you will encounter throught this level.

The only light found on this level are the red Exit Signs found throughout the level.

> this level is very dangerous, and should not be underestamated, as only one person under the username "Ivansuelo12345" has managed to survive without any major injuries.

This level is extremely dangerous, and should not be underestimated, as only one wanderer has been confirmed to survive this level without major injuries.

**Colonies and outposts**

We use the phrase "Bases, Communities, and Outposts" on this wiki.

> No known colonies exist within this level

There are no known bases, communities, or outposts on this level.

**Entities**

> The amount and types of entities are unspecified (other than the Clumps you will find in front of you), as the survivor of Level ! did not have enough time to look, although its rumoured to house Smilers among the many other entities within this level.

The current entities found within this level are unknown, with the exception of [Clumps](entity-5.md) that must be avoided.

If unsure how to create hyperlinks, use the following code.

```
[[[Page-Link|Anchor Text]]]
[[[entity-5|Clumps]]]
```

**Entrances and Exits**

> There is only one specific way to enter this level, other than being transported to this level when you least expect it, and that is to noclip through Level Error, as it has a chance of bringing you here.

There is no [Level Error](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-error) on this Wiki, so you'll need to change this.

> you will see an exit sign that will bring you to a random level, and The Backrooms won't send you here again.

After 10km, a door with an exit sign above can be found. Entering this door will lead to a random level.  
After leaving Level !, it is impossible to re-enter it.

---

In total, this page needs a lot of work, as this is almost a direct import, and relies on articles that are not on the current wiki.  
You're really close to having a good level though!  
Don't give up, and this may be on the wiki soon.
