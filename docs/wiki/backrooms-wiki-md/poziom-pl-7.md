**Level PL-7** is the seventh level of the Polish Backrooms. It was discovered on November 3rd, 2002.

# **Description:**

Level PL-7 is a supposedly infinite bridge made of wood and steel, that hovers over a deep chasm. It has many branches leading in different directions. This level is completely shrouded in fog, with visibility limited to 10 meters. The temperature in Level PL-7 hovers between 7 and 10°C.

While staying in Level PL-7, you will not feel hunger or thirst. There are no potential sources of food or water here. In Level PL-7, you will feel uneasy. The feeling of déjà vu that accompanies wanderers throughout the Backrooms is stronger here than anywhere else. This is not a coincidence. You have definitely been here before. Just like everyone else.

There is a very high chance that you won't encounter any entities here. Although some wanderers have reported a dark figure watching them from a distance, no real entities have been confirmed in Level PL-7. Here, nothing is real. Even if *He* exists, it is not real. He cannot harm you. *Right?*

There is no chance of encountering other wanderers in this level, even though many reports suggest that Level PL-7 is one of the most frequently visited parts of the Backrooms. *He* wants to see us individually. *He* invites each one separately.

---

# **Colonies and Outposts**

On this level, there are no known colonies or outposts. No one stays here for long.

---

# **Entrances and Exits:**

## Entrances

Everyone gets in here by chance. No one remembers how. And even if He does, he won't share it with others. That's too private. It's *His* problem.

## Exits

There is no single, clear exit from Level PL-7. Sometimes you come back right away, sometimes a little later. Sometimes you don't come back at all. Although, jumping into the misty void beyond the bridge seems like a good solution. Or maybe instead of scheming, just open your eyes?

# **Additional Documentation**

> ### **Regulation of the Polish Discovery Committee**
>
> Due to the lack of progress in the exploration of Level PL-7, all research work related to this location is hereby suspended. We must come to terms with the fact that Level PL-7 will likely remain a distant and unattainable dream for us forever. After 10 years of research, we still know practically nothing. Wake up, wanderers. This level simply does not want to be understood. Just like *Him*.
>
> — Ryszard Czarnecki, director of the Polish Discovery Committee
