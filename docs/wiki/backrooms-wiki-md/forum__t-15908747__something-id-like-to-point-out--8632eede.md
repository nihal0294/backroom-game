ive noticed a lot of ppl use the word "phenomena" when referring to a singular one. however, thats actually wrong. "phenomenon" is the singular and "phenomena" is the plural.

examples of usage of "phenomenon"

- "Phenomenon 19"
- "This is a very dangerous phenomenon"

examples of usage of "phenomena"

- "There are lots of phenomena within the Backrooms"
- "Liminal Echo and Frontrooms Reflection are two different phenomena"
