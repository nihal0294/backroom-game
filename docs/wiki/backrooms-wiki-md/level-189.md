# Description

**Level 189** takes the appearance of a common convenience store. However, the level itself is much larger than your day-to-day convenience store. Everything inside this level manifests a barcode, even if it does not originate on the level, or normally contains one. The handheld scanner in the Checkout is able to read these barcodes. Despite it being insisted to scan all items before leaving and that there is indeed an existing currency for these items, everything on this level is free of charge. All items on this level seem to automatically refill or restock themselves on where they originated before they were consumed. Most importantly, **Level 189** contains a set of rules wanderers are insisted to follow. This level is separated into four extremely different sections known as the Checkout, Aisles, Storage, and Server Rooms.

### Checkout

The checkout is a foyer zone at the entrance of the level. It consists of a singular vending machine, cash register, and handheld scanner. The checkout is the smallest of the four zones and the first place wanderers will find themselves at once entering the level.

### Aisles

The Aisles are the main focal point of **Level 189**. It consists of multiple shelves, signs, and products. Mainly, The Aisles are an area for wanderers to collect resources to check out. Most of these items are displayed on shelves that are organized in neat rows. These shelves have every existing product for any use or problem. Around the aisles multiple spots can be found broken down and vandalized. Exposed wiring and fungi can be seen growing in these spots.

![A dirty storage area with a fire exit.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-189/RubbishRetail2.png)

A picture of the Storage with a fire exit.

### Storage

The Storage is one of the few things in **Level 189** not openly talked about by staff or in the message. This area is accessible through a set of double doors located at the back of the Aisles and is the largest area of the four. The area is a complex of tight hallways and large rooms with gray spotless floors. Similar to the Aisles, this section contains multiple dangerous or broken down areas. Bits and pieces of lumber, cardboard, and plastic trash bags can be found here, all with barcodes on them. This area is also unable to receive signals or any form of digital communication outside of the Storage. Fire exits can be found in the storage which lead to other levels. These doors disappear behind the wanderer after exiting.

![An open space with a server and computer.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-189/ServerRoom.png)

Only known Picture of the Server Rooms

### Server Rooms

The Server Rooms are multiple interconnected rooms containing industrial pipes and high-tech computer appliances. They are located in the deepest area of the level and is the most dangerous out of the four. The rooms located here can be extremely tight or unusually large. Rooms have exposed industrial pipes, wiring, etc. Temperatures in the server rooms can reach to extremely low temperatures causing severe hypothermia. All appliances inside the server rooms are accessible and completely powered on. All these appliances are connected to a database that tracks multiple different subjects. These subjects include:

- Amount of wanderers that have entered the level.
- Which items are checked out.
- Wanderers breaking rules.
- Deaths on **Level 189**.

This database can be accessed through many of these appliances. This database cannot be accessed outside of the Server Rooms. Appliances originally in the Server Rooms break down once brought elsewhere.

It is not advised to enter or explore the server rooms.

### The Security Office

At the backmost area of the Server Rooms, a security room can be found with a black door for its entrance. This door has a sign stating "Restricted Area, Do Not Enter Under Any Circumstances". It is a small room consisting of multiple security monitors connected to the cameras found in the Aisles and Checkout. The database can also be accessed on these monitors. The room is covered in deadly open wiring and temperatures are insanely low.

# Entities

> ## Meet the Staff!
>
> Sadly, we have a small amount of staff, so this is a quick section to get to know them just a bit better.

- [*The Register*](javascript:;)
- [*The Assistant*](javascript:;)

The register is a humanoid entity in black and yellow attire that reads "BACKMART". They are always present at the checkout behind the counter. They are a helpful entity warning wanderers about items they forgot to scan or when they are close to breaking a rule. It is heavily implied by the Assistant that the Janitor is a creation of theirs. It is still unconfirmed however.

> The Register is our friendly guy who will help you check out your items, and greet you into our store. Please be nice to him as he works hard and is extremely welcoming.

The Assistant is a mysterious entity that apparently used to operate the cameras on this level. These cameras have been seen to move and follow wanderers. The Assistant also seems to be able to induce certain phenomena onto wanderers at her will, and also has a hatred for people and other entities. The Assistant has only been seen once and it is theorized she may be encountered somewhere in another level.

> Now as for me, the person writing this document, I am the Assistant. I operate the cameras and keep our lovely store up and running in the back. You will never see me, but I'll always see you!

> That's it for our staff. It is a shame we have a small group, but we make do with what we have and will still be able to give you a warm welcome here at **BACKMART**!

# Rulebreak

Unlike the entities present here, **Level 189** holds a phenomena inherent to itself that is mentally hazardous and unforgiving. This phenomena is referred to as "Rulebreak". This phenomena occurs once a wanderer breaks one or more of the rules listed on the poster in the checkout or listed in the message. It is in basic terms an enforcement system to them. Rulebreak seems to cause wanderers to experience hallucinations, and ask for forgiveness from some unknown entity. Rulebreak can vary in intensity based on which rule was broken, or who broke it. This may lead to wanderers running away, usually into the Storage.

> ## Rules
>
> Obviously, we must tell you to follow the rules to have a fun time here. Rulebreaking is unnecessary and will not be tolerated here. Please do not do anything along the lines of:
>
> - Disrespecting other customers or staff
> - Vandalism or destruction
> - Shoplifting or stealing
> - Trespassing
>
> Friendly reminder to follow the rules in our store.

# Operation Sold Goods

This operation, hosted by the M.E.G. was a long and tedious exploration of unknown parts of this level. The experiment was kicked off after the recovery of operator Hank Tillman, with his information proving **Level 189** was of interest to the M.E.G. This operation included breaking rules, exploring unknown areas, and studying the unusual sections of the level. The operation's data logs can be viewed below. The two most important logs can be viewed below.

Members of the M.E.G. that participated in these stunts are now suffering from severe mental trauma and extreme injuries. It is advised to not break any of the rules or explore deep into **Level 189**, as it can result in fatal consequences. This is for your safety.

# Bases, Outposts, and Communities

## BACKMARTTM

After the Assistant's leave and the end of operation "Sold Goods" Hank Tillman now works at **Level 189**. He, along with others took over the store. They Warn others of phenomena and helping them with shopping. This is their humble home. Welcome to **BACKMARTTM**.

# Entrances And Exits

#### Entrances

Finding brightly lighted double doors on [Level 11](level-11.md) will lead you here.

#### Exits

Leaving through the front double doors lead you back to [Level 11](level-11.md). Fire exits in the Storage area lead to [Level 3](level-3.md) or [Level 141](level-141.md).

> ## Closing.
>
> That's really all there is to it. We know how inconvenient our store may be. However other than a few things, there's not much that *isn't* great around here. So come on down to **BACKMART**, and start shopping with us today!
