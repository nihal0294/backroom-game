Object 64 - The Dangers of Safty (Help Refining)

11 Oct 2024 15:53

*(I accendently posted this in Help: Ideas Critique. But cannot delete the thread. If an admin reads this please delete that thread. I'm really sorry for messing up)*

**Page Type:** Object

**Genre**: Nostalgia, Wholesome, Horror

**Core Concept:** The object in question provides safety and the *illusion* of well-being to its user. The user cannot move location and has a desire to keep interacting with the object.

A reader's curiosity can be peaked thanks to a subtle joke in the object's selected number and a not-so-subtle reference to many's childhood. As they follow the clinical writing which contrast with the subject of happiness they will be introduced to the seemingly innocent/positive properties of the object. However, the more they read, the more they come to realize there is another, scarier, side to the coin. This object may protect the user from outside threats, but it addicts them with a unconditional feeling of happiness, enclosing them in a safe and sequestered environment. Prompting them to forget about worries and stay hooked for hours on end. Their body, growing hungry, thirsty, and tired.

**Is the ultimate safe haven worth the risk?**

All is done to achieve a Fridge Horror effect. As such the information is laid out on the page. But, it is up to the reader to realize the darker undertones.

**Additional Notes:** I have an addiction to writing, so I've already written up a complete Sandbox page for my idea. However, I require an outside's and professional's perspective on my work.

I'm trying to achieve a Fridge Horror effect with this piece and have ordered the elements of the page accordingly. I would like to know if it is effective, if a detail is given out too quickly, if elements should be reordered, if information should be kept for or elaborated on later?

I also lack a lot of wikidot syntax knowledge. Although I've posted my question in the designated forum. But, if you think something would look better in another, I'd love to hear it!

I'm currently not looking for a greenlight (although if you already wish to give me one I'd be more than happy). My present goal is to refine my Sandbox page. Here's the link to my draft <http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/pumgumgum>

I hope I've peaked your interest and welcome any and all critiques :D

A note before I start: I'm leaving the log for now. I think it'd be better to focus on the main page first and then work on the log. Just wanted to say so you don't think I've forgotten it.

### Description

This page hangs on the description of the object for a bit too long. It takes us multiple paragraphs to find out what makes this deserving of an object page and even to realise what makes this belong to the Backrooms. This would work on SCP but here it's most likely just gonna kill attention.

I think this page would benefit from a mini introduction. The first sentence works fine and can stay but it should be followed by a sentence or two giving an overview of the object's stranger properties. You don't have to hint at anything negative here but it'd be good to prevent people from thinking this is just another 'thing but Backrooms'.

The bullet points below are long and drag the description on longer than needed. There's information here that we don't really need to know and doesn't add anything to the page.

> Japanese cartridge-based video game console

You can remove "video game" because we know it's related to video games as you've already said. Also, "cartridge-based" gives it away.

> (also produced in 1997. Theorized to be the original ones bundled with the console)

This can be removed. We can assume its the same year and that extra bit of information doesn't add much.

> These items show no signs of hardware or software issues. However, underside scratches, internal dust build up and severe RCA cable twisting can be found on the console.

The first sentence can removed. We can guess this from the fact that it's useable. If you wanna give a mention tho, instead make the sentence something like "Underside scratches, internal dust build up and severe RCA cable twisting can be found on the console, but the console remains useable."

> This suggests these items have been used extensively.

This can removed. It's something that can be inferred.

> (No notable additional remarks can be made about this item.)

This can be removed. If there were additional remarks, you would have said them.

> It is possible to remove these plugs in order to use their sockets with other electrical devices.

This information isn't needed but still good to have so I recommend making it a footnote instead. There's a section on footnotes in the syntax guides

> It is unknown where the electricity powering the sockets originates from.

This can be removed

Not description but

> Interacting with any of Object 64’s items independently yields no known effect.

This could be made into a footnote attached to the sentence after this one.

### Concept

As I said, I like this concept. I think it has interesting potential for the Backrooms. It makes sense with the items used for the object and reflects sentiments about gaming and such from the real world.

I see what you're going for but I think it's currently too vague and falls into stereotypical Backrooms territory.

For example, staring out listing the good properties, then there's a warning followed by the dangers. You do try to work the negative effects into the properties section of the page but it's undermined by the uses section. The points raised could be included in the properties section alongside the positive effects.

> Descriptions of the experiences vary from nostalgia, peace-of-mind, euphoria, disappearance of stress, and relaxation

Here you could expand on how these manifest and then give an idea of how it feels for people after they stop using 64 (beyond memory loss). This can introduce the idea of becoming essentially addicted. This could then be made into its own paragraph exploring the emotional side of effects.

Following this, I think the physical effects should be their own paragraph. That way, you can incorporate "Object 64 does not stabilize one's metabolic needs, erase environmental dangers or treat psychological and/or physical injuries. Object 64 only suspends and/or suppresses these conditions." into the properties.

> It is incentivized to only interact with Object 64 in an aim to momentarily escape from dangerous entities, similar to hiding or escaping. Individuals experiencing metabolic or cognitive distress are advised to seek solutions to these issues instead of pacifying them with Object 64.

You could keep this as its own paragraph but tbh I think you'd be able to make it part of the central text in properties. Expanding on withdrawal and symptoms can help. Maybe also adding some advice for alternatives (especially for emotional solutions) such as saying to find social support or drinking almond water. Idk but something like this can imply that 64 isn't a favourable solution but should only be a last minute choice without explicitly saying this.

Another point for concept is that nostalgia is extremely common with Backrooms pages. I think you could elabourate on the nostalgia point to instead show what is meant by nostalgia here in a show don't tell kind of way. Does the nostalgia aspect make certain people more vulnerable to 64? Does 64 impact people differently based on age?

Only other thing I have for now is how does 64 impact people who don't speak English? This is based on the cartridges.

That is all from me for now. I like the layout of this page and nothing else to say about syntax. Despite how harsh I may sound, I really like this page. Let me know when you've made the changes and feel free to say or ask me anything. Looking forward to seeing how this page works out!
