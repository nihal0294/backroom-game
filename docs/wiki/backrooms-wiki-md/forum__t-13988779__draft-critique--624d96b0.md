Please put all drafts in your Sandbox page. I know you've used one before, so I'm guessing that the reason you didn't this time was because there's already another draft there. If you want to include more than one draft, you can either use collapsibles, or tabs.

Collapsible work like this:

```
[[collapsible show="+ Name Of Your Draft" hide="- Name Of Your Draft"]]
The text of your draft.
[[/collapsible]]
```

  
and look like this:

[+ Name of Your Draft](javascript:;)

[- Name of Your Draft](javascript:;)

The text of your draft.

Tabs work like this:

```
[[tabview]]
[[tab The Name of Your Draft]]
The text of your draft.
[[/tab]]
[[tab The Name of Your Other Draft]]
The text of your other draft.
[[/tab]]
[[/tabview]]
```

And look like this:

- [*The Name of Your Draft*](javascript:;)
- [*The Name of Your Other Draft*](javascript:;)

The text of your draft.

The text of your other draft.

Also, if you want your tabs to not have that yellow background, you can put this code at the top of the page:

```
[[module css]]
div.yui-navset div.yui-content {
    background: #D7CE93;
    border-left: #D7CE93 outset 0px;
    border-right: #D7CE93 outset 0px;
    border-bottom: #D7CE93 outset 0px;
}
[[/module]]
```
