Entity Idea: Seers

14 Oct 2022 02:40

Seers are small creatures about the size of Dachshund dog. Their bodies are round, white with black stripes in a zig-zag pattern, 4 stubby legs, a small mouth and one large eye. The eye comes in 3 colors, blue, green, and brown. However, when they feel threatened their eye will turn red. Seers have a friendly personality and even act like dogs! They can fire a powerful red beam from their eye for defensive purposes that can set whatever it hits on fire. They are also capable of moving quickly, even though their legs are short and stubby. Seers also have a phobia of lemons and onions. Their diet consists of almond water, and meat from other entities.
