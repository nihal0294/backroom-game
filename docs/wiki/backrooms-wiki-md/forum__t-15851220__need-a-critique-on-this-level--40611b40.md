**SPaG**:

- When used with a number (ex. Level XXX), the word "level" needs to be capitalized. When you are just referring to the general concept of a level, you don't need to do that. You forgot to capitalize Level 242 a few times. Backrooms should also be capitalized.
- You sometimes mention Level 14 when you probably mean Level 242.

> If approached, these sounds can gradually rise to 190 decibels.

> The source of the noise is unknown, and it's unclear if it even originates from this level.

> The stairwells on Level 242 are unique in that they tend to only go down one floor of the level at a time.

I am not sure if this is worth mentioning.

> The stairs themselves are made of concrete, and the steps are covered in a thin layer of moss, making them slippery to walk on.

> Despite the eerie atmosphere, there are occasional signs of life on Level 242. Small patches of grass can be found growing in cracks in the concrete, and birds have been known to nest in the vines that line the walls. It's a reminder that even in the Backrooms, life can find a way.

The text before already mentions vines and moss, so there also being grass shouldn't be on note.

**Formatting**

Having everything be in bullet points is awkward. The interview should be formatted with a quote block or something similar. Its opening section should outline both the interviewer and the interviewee. Other than that, there are no major issues with formatting.

**Content**

There isn’t enough in the level to grab the reader’s attention. It is simply an industrial area with a loud noise. The Soundbreakers aren’t shown doing anything interesting with their ability.

The dialogue feels stiff. Having some variation in the line length would be a good way to avoid Marcus spewing paragraphs at the interviewer.

Speaking of: the Interviewer isn’t characterized well. They don’t even have a name. All of their lines are essentially the space-filling “fascinating, continue.”

I am not an expert on dialogue, so I would suggest getting aid from somebody else.
