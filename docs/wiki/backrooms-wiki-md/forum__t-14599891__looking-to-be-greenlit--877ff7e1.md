Basically, the best way to describe it is that if you post this without staff permission(already a big no no) and don’t credit the og author of the fandom work, only putting your name in the author collapsible, then you’ll be plagiarizing. *And plagiarism is not taken lightly anywhere(seriously, don’t plagiarize.)*  
In response to your second question down below, just because it ain’t here doesn’t mean it isn’t written. In fact, [here’s the fandom Level !](https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Level_!) in case you’re confused on what I’m talking about.  
You have to credit the og author, and you have to get a staff’s permission to rewrite the level if you want. You also have to write the code of the page itself which isn’t in the page(like the SD, pictures, maybe even a CSS if you know it enough).  
If you want to learn more on how to add the fandom import on here(or if I confused you too much on a small rant, sorry bout that), [here’s the rewrite policy.](rewrite-policy.md)  
Oh, and when you do get permission to rewrite it, I’ll gladly critique it. It still needs work(you’ve written “fan made level of the Backrooms,” and since most of our articles are first person, you’ll be saying that people who are the fans of the Backrooms made this level brick by brick.)
