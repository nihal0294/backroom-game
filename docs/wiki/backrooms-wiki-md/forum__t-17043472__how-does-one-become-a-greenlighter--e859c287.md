If you have a discord account, you can join the Backrooms discord server.

The best place to start is by reading critiques, especially ones given by greenlighters. There's a few on this site, for example [https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/forum/t-16714096/pituriasis-fungi-looking-for-critique#post-6439717](forum__t-16714096__pituriasis-fungi-looking-for-critique--cb76d19f.md)  
Giving critiques is also pretty much a requirement before applying. Being active in forums, regularly giving feedback and returning to give more feedback when your critique is applied.  
We do have a greenlight policy you can read throught to familiarise youreself with [https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/greenlight-policy](greenlight-policy.md)  
If you think you're ready to apply, you can use this form <https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSf08xmWvT4mNe0u5kwSdZa4HLFlYqsqLVMH9mPNSYXU9n9COQ/viewform>
