[+ Stewart's Bottles Edit](javascript:;)

[- Stewart's Bottles Edit](javascript:;)

Stewart's Bottles are twelve-ounce glass bottles, which are meant to hold the many sodas made by the Stewart's Fountain Classics in the Frontrooms. Yet, in the Backrooms, there is never any liquid within the bottles, and they are completely dry within. They also don't have any cap on, and no caps to Stewart's Bottles have been found.

The main property of Stewart's Bottles is that they are incredibly durable. It's currently believed that it takes 3,000 psi (pounds per square inch) to break Stewart's Bottle. It's almost impossible for an average human to break Stewart's Bottle in the Backrooms on their own.

All ten flavors and label designs of Stewart's soda that Wanderers have remembered in the Frontrooms have been spotted in the Backrooms. But there have been consistent rumors of two other designs unique within the Backrooms. The first flavor has a black-colored design, and is named: 'Grinner Potion'. The second unique flavor is blood red, and is named: 'Cannibal Fluid'. Wanderers have claimed that encountering Grinner Potion bottles means you are in a level with an abnormal amount of Smilers; while encountering bottles of Cannibal Fluid, means you are in a level with an abnormal amount of Wretches. This has yet to be confirmed by the M.E.G.
