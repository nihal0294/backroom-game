The entity commonly referred to as a Smiler, or [Entity 3](entity-3.md), is extinct. As this entity emits its own [artificial light](light.md), many reports have come in witnessing this entity combusting into flames before promptly expiring. While there has never been a recorded count of how many existed within the Backrooms, it is heavily theorised that the effects of both the light and Synthesial Reversal have rendered these entities entirely eradicated.

While it is always a shame to classify a recorded entity as extinct, no matter if harmful or harmless, this is a good sign for our survival - another means of people falling victim to the light has been eliminated.

Humanity must persevere. We *will* overcome the light.

Once formerly classified as Extinct, [The Sun's Grin](entity-3.md) is an amorphous being of mass and light that evolved from Smilers. While recorded to be very slow moving, their reach is not to be underestimated - If you can see the creature, then you are too close. If you are caught, you will be dragged into the mass, where the many faces of the creature will show itself. There is no escape from that point — your body will undergo an accelerated process of the symptoms of artificial light infection.

This being is recorded to be only present in small amounts, a maximum of one or two on a level. However, unlike their former form, they are present within levels with an active daytime, no longer simply residing in the darkened areas of indoor levels.

These beings are to be avoided at all costs. They are highly dangerous, they are highly infectious. Do not attempt to set this creature aflame, you will die before you even light your match.
