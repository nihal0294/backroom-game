Object 78 takes the appearance of a light blue gelatinous substance composed of anomalous material. At room temperature and 1 atm[2](javascript:;) of pressure[3](javascript:;), Blue Gel has a density of 1130 kgm-3. Under maximum compression, Blue Gel has a stiffness comparable to concrete.

The most defining property of Blue Gel is its ability to store electrical energy as chemical energy and release chemical energy stored as electrical energy. While Blue Gel by itself cannot store any energy, Blue Gel made into a battery cell has an extremely high energy density of 2573 Whkg-1. This, combined with its stiffness,[4](javascript:;) makes Blue Gel batteries powerful and durable. The extremely high energy density of Blue Gel allowed it to make its way to around 60% of electronics across the Backrooms.

The production of Blue Gel requires an anomalous substance called Blue Paste, which is extracted from geysers on [Level 614](level-614.md). The extraction of Blue Paste is highly costly and convoluted. Firstly, a sensor array is to be lowered into one of the geysers, which detects when an eruption is about to happen. When an eruption does happen, the contents of the eruption will be sent into reverse osmosis, which will remove salts in the geyser solution. Then, the solution must be kept at a supercritical state for 6 hours before yielding blue paste at 2% of the input mass. Blue paste is then added to almond water to create Blue Gel.[5](javascript:;) Currently, 58% of the Backrooms Blue Gel market supply is from the Bovetan firm Gelco, with the remainder from the B.N.T.G. and Backrooms Robotics.

### Blue Gel For Batteries

The most common use for Blue Gel is for high-capacity batteries. As with all batteries, Blue Gel batteries involve a cathode, an anode, and an electrolyte. Blue Gel itself takes on the role of both the cathode and the anode. The electrode is made with 4-6% [Firesalt](object-15.md) and [Liquid Pain](object-48.md).

Due to its stability, Blue Gel batteries rarely experience failures and accidents, which are caused by damage to cells. These accidents are almost always due to mishandling. However, these mistakes are exceptionally dangerous due to them not only causing fires and explosions from the Firesalt but also releasing toxic hydrogen cyanide from the liquid pain. Additionally, accidents occur quickly, often just 3 seconds after a battery failure[6](javascript:;). The most common cause of accidents is overcharging batteries. A Blue Gel battery cell can be discharged down to 2.5V and charged up to 4.2V, and cells charged or discharged beyond this range will be damaged and will pose a safety risk. Battery packs with multiple cells must also be charged with balance plugs, as if the difference in voltage between cells is too large, the cells will also be damaged.

Examples of other less common causes of accidents include damage through brute force[7](javascript:;), heat[8](javascript:;), and accidental short circuits[9](javascript:;).

> A Blue Gel battery failure is often telegraphed by the following signs:
>
> 1. Battery swelling
> 2. Battery being hot
> 3. Sweet smell in the air[10](javascript:;)
> 4. Visible fumes
>
> If any of the signs are present, stop what you are doing and make as much distance from the battery as possible.

Blue Gel batteries do not lose capacity over many charge cycles and do not lose any energy when charged. Thus, Blue Gel batteries became the main way of energy transport across different levels, as running wires through different levels is usually not possible. Blue Gel is often transported using large autonomous vehicles carrying Blue Gel batteries across various levels. Recently, copper cables within levels are starting to be swapped out with Blue Gel cables because they have lower resistance, but the Blue Gel market price is still too high to make this a profitable initiative.

# Startup Invents Revolutionary Blue Gel Battery

Author: Amie Cindy  
Date: 2019/04/02

  
Yesterday, Bovetan Startup firm Gelco announced the creation of the first-ever Blue Gel Battery. With an energy density 10 times the most efficient batteries on [Earth](the-frontrooms.md), these batteries promise to power the entire Backrooms.

![A bag of four vials containing a blue mixture.](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/object-78/4bluegel)  
A batch of Blue Gel ready to be made into batteries.

The battery pack created was a 4s1p battery pack that had a mass of 200 grams and was able to supply 519Wh of energy from fully charged to empty, giving it the highest energy density of any battery known to humanity. The cherry on top is that the battery does not lose performance over charge cycles, which allows it to be used indefinitely. This invention was based on an earlier discovery of Blue Gel's property to turn into both a cathode and an anode depending on the direction of flow. This allowed Gelco to create a battery cell by charging what they call a base cell, a cell that has regular Blue Gel for both the cathode and anode, which causes the cathodes and anodes to form. To create the electrolyte, Gelco used the proven Firesalt and Liquid Pain reaction. This prompted some scientists to warn about potential toxic and explosive Blue Gel Battery failures. While Gelco acknowledged the existence of this safety hazard, they emphasized that Blue Gel batteries are extremely stable and would only experience a failure in extreme conditions or short circuits. According to Gelco: "The battery is undeniably dangerous if handled wrong, but that doesn't stop it from being much more reliable and stable than lithium-ion batteries".

Gelco has made its intentions clear to "push Blue Gel batteries into serial production", which received support from the Bovetan government. The Zephyr 0 colony, previously used for research of [Level 614](level-614.md), has been converted to a dedicated Blue Gel Extraction plant. Additionally, the Department of Infrastructure commissioned the Zephyr 2 colony, which, alongside Zephyr 1, will become the new backbone of the Bovetan Blue Gel industry.

![New Zephyr Logo](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/theme:boveta/zephyrlogo)  
The Logo of New Zephyr

This prompted the President to come up with the solution of creating New Zephyr, a large-scale merge of the Zephyr Outposts. New Zephyr is a town acting as a central operations center for harvesting Blue Gel and housing for extraction employees. The new town is expected to become a pillar for increasing Blue Gel production efficiency: the organized structure of the town allows more effective communication between various plants and logistics systems, allowing more efficient transport to major levels. The Blue Gel Pact has announced that they have switched their central meeting hall to New Zephyr, which is symbolic of more equal operations between the member groups.

On December 1, President Pottinger, alongside representatives of all 6 member groups of the Blue Gel Pact, personally oversaw the commissioning of New Zephyr. The president said he was "shocked by the astonishing efficiency of the town" and encouraged local managers to "expand the purpose of the town beyond harvesting Blue Gel". Head Administrator Jason Rayne stated that the next step for the town should be to help the local population, named the Zealous, by providing space for them to meditate.

# Precautions

  
Due to safety concerns, the Bovetan Government advises you to follow the 4R guideline below when handelling Blue Gel batteries.

- **R**outinely inspect all Blue Gel batteries for damage
- **R**esponsibly dispose Blue Gel batteries by putting them in designated government collection sites.
- **R**un if a Blue Gel battery is about to explode
- **R**eport if you find someone mishandelling Blue Gel batteries
