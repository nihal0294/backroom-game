You should *probably* have that disclaimer on the first offset. The decoded message is quite graphic after all, so the warning is going to be most effective on the page where it starts.

Reading the decoded message again, I'm not sure you took my advice of toning it down just a touch. It's kind of teetering on the edge of body horror/gore for the sake of gore territory. Anyway, I personally don't mind it, but I think this is one of the more extreme examples I've seen, so I'm just giving you a heads up.

Also… while I'm a big fan of the cipher, I'm not sure how familiar people would be with the pattern. You probably should add a few more hints, especially since the decoded message is a key part of the page. If DMing has taught me anything, you can't be too subtle.

> Buildings outwardly depict themed small businesses, restaurants, and a variety of other structures found in a residential uptown. These buildings advertise a manifold of products, such as food, convenient goods, hardware, and other supplies. Thus far, no building has been proven to contain anything of what was showcased on its signs or billboards. The interiors of these buildings, alternatively, do not have much volume.

This bit is worded a bit funkily, and I stumble reading them aloud. While it does give me details about the level, they don't feel like important details, mostly fluff. I recommend removing them or rewording them to not feel so stiff.

Second offset page still, that first footnote is redundant. The sentence immediately before tells me that light fixtures in the level are recessed. Remove it.

> counters; all

Remove that semicolon. You really only use them when connecting two independent clauses. It adds a weird pause if you do keep it.

> Previous to the sub-level's proper documentation, reports from numerous wanderers depicting what could only be a level shift were submitted to the M.E.G.'s record database.

This part doesn't quite make sense logically. We're going to tweak some of the sentences and move the information around a bit so it makes sense:

> Previous to the sub-level's proper documentation, most reports stated that Level 11.2 was in a state of constant day. However, after the level shift, the sub-level suddenly adopted a day-night cycle. The M.E.G. has been investigating the level shift, and will continue to until an explanation is discovered.

> The night-cycle lasts for precisely three hours.

That's a nice clue. Hm, probably expand that more. Have things come in threes. *Really* hammer it in.

> The smell of ozone and metal intensifies at night.

Nice tonal switch in this paragraph.

> The Meanies

Alright, full disclosure. I *heavily* dislike this name. With all the description and tension you've built up in this, I'm immediately taken out of it when I read it. I'm reading and imagining body horror and then I read a schoolyard insult that's never matched in tone again :/ It's a sudden tonal whiplash that's not working very well since everything else in the page is going along with the overall creepy vibes. I know you have ideas for them, but do they *really* need this name? Especially when compared to "The Emporia." Now *that's* a menacing and intriguing name.

And going through it again, I'm left on your last page, which ties everything together nicely.

With that, I think that's my final crit. If you feel like it's ready, let me know, and I'll give it a greenlight. :)
