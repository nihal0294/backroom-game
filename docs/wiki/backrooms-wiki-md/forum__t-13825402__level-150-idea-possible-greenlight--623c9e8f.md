Level 150 Idea (Possible Greenlight?)

18 Oct 2020 05:06

Level 150:  
-Threat Level: 0  
-Description (Will be expanded): Level 150 is a series of (possibly) never ending hallways made of a variety of materials lined with various doors of varying style. The hallways of Level 150, if mapped out, do not follow Euclidian geometry, and seem to intersect. (Example image made, unable to attach). Each door can either lead to another hallway of Level 150, or to another Level. If the door leads to another Level, the appearance of the door with somehow relate to the level it leads to. For example, a door leading to Level 2 would probably be a steel maintenance door.  
-Entrances/Exits: No-clipping from another Level could have you end up at Level 150. Doors leading to another Level, which are prominent, are exits.
