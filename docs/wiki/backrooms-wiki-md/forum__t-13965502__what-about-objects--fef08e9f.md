There isn't really an official template. Right now it's just, a description heading, and then any other headings that you want to add. Some also have a Object Number subheading, but that's really up to what you think works best.  
Here are some examples:  
[Scarabacks](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/scarabacks) has a pretty standard format that can apply to a many objects.  
[Smiler Repellant](object-3.md) has it's own format with some additional information, and uses headers to showcase relevant documents.  
[Madison Street Mannequin](object-65.md) follows a simple Description/Supplementary Documents format similar to smiler repellant.  
You can look at other objects or just do what works for you!
