Level 29

29 Jan 2022 05:10

**Page Type:** Level.

**Core Concept:** This level is a infinite castle with a day or night cycle where you need to find the exit with day being safe but no exit while night is where the exit spawns. There's vampires too and they bite you making you be one of them and be doomed to walk this castle forever! The level is in black and white and there's no sound.

**Additional Notes:** This idea is a way to break level 29's curse of being coldposted.
