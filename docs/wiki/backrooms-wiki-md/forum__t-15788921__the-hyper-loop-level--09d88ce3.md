The Hyper Loop Level

06 Apr 2023 03:06

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre (Optional):** Action, bittersweet

**Core Concept**

The Level is a concrete tunnel in the shape of a circle or an oval, with magnets on the ceiling of the tube like lanes. Though there are no stations on the level, maintenance doors are what allows Wanderers to enter and leave the level, although theses doors are quite rare.

Entities are also rare to encounter except for the Hyper Loop Trains themselves.

The trains of this level are very common and used to go up to speeds of hundreds of miles/kilometers an hour; but as Wanderers entered the level, there has been accidental deaths from these entities which seems to show both curiosity and remorse.

As for outposts the M.E.G. manages to use these trains to setup a outpost while a group of train pirates who utilizes the entities to steal resources from those who are unfortunate enough to get caught by them.

**Additional Notes**

I already have a partial draft of it, I’m just wanting to see where this idea will go.
