**Object R-53** is a seal fur from a currently unknown type of seal, theorised to be that of a *Selkie*, or *maighdeann-mhara*.

## Description:

**Object R-53** is a full hide of a seal, with all extremities such as the head and arms still attached. This is unusual for furs, as they usually have the arms, tail, and head removed when being skinned. The inner surface of the skin is made of an unidentified biological material not matching any known type of flesh[3](javascript:;). The pelt can be vaguely worn by a person — it seems to be designed for this purpose — but for unknown reasons it cannot be fully sealed and actively resists attempts to do so. It is presumed[4](javascript:;) that it can only be used by its owner, through some form of DNA recognition.

## Discovery:

**Object R-53** was originally brought to the attention of the U.E.C. by senior researcher Loretta Murray, and she showed a group of other operatives[5](javascript:;) the location of the object on the condition that they would not ask how she knew of it. It was found three layers deep inside an abandoned building's collapsed basement, on the outskirts of the Capital. Due to the aforementioned condition, little is known of why this object was there and what its significance is to L. Murray.

## Physical Properties:

In basic appearance, the pelt seems to be that of a Harbour Seal (*Phoca vitulina*), but does not show any evidence of being attached to a living seal[6](javascript:;).
