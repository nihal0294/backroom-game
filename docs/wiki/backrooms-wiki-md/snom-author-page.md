# Snom's Author Page

rating: +64[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

Oh, hey! Welcome to my-

> Ahem.

Whoops, sorry!

Anyway, I'm ! You can call me Snom, though (or Tom, if you prefer or want to be needlessly formal). I've joined on March 17, 2021. I discovered this whole project, looked at a character I had written in need of a place to be…

> That was me, correct?

Yup. You've probably met Blanche, a character that has been with me for a long time, now with a place to be. Level 906 was my first page, and one I'm pretty proud of. After that, there was Entity 140 - Blanche, and from there…. well, I got myself entangled in a ton of projects.

Turns out, if you make a character that people like, people will want to use her in their stuff! Fascinating, I know.

> Not that I mind, dear. I do enjoy this newfound popularity.

Guess this is where I talk a little more about myself? I'm from Portugal, I'm bisexual, I'm a massive nerd, I like Magic The Gathering, the Lancer TTRPG, fighting games, aggressive music (Darksynth is my favourite genre, with metal as a close second), writing (duh), retro games (Specifically PS2 and PC), board games, tea, crabs, Cyberpunk, and lots of other things.

If you want to reach out to me, find me on Discord or Wikidot PM's. I'm back from a two year hiatus, and I'm still open to others using my characters and levels, just talk to me about it! I'd be happy to lend a hand if possible. Someone should be able to point you my way, if you can't find me otherwise.

[Level 714](trimmed__level-714--abfc8a26.md) - Future Funk Radio!

[Author's Notes](javascript:;)

[Hide Notes](javascript:;)

There's a fun story behind this one. Future Funk Radio is the product of my favorite interaction ever in the Backrooms Tech Support Server's #idea-critique channel:

> **person 1**: what if there was an entity that didn't like noise  
> **person 2**: what if it was a musician trying to record music  
> **person 3**: what if the song lyrics have hidden prophecies, giving you a reason to stay and listen  
> **my one braincell**: what if…. ***F U N K Y***

This was a lot of fun to write, I got to ditch the clinical tone, and just write a stylish article, soaked in early 2000's vibes. Or at least that was the goal :p… Do yourself a favour and listen to some Hideki Naganuma while reading this. There's a few nods to both his work and Jet Set Radio on the page!

[Level 811](level-811.md) - Titanis Sepulcrum

[Author's Notes](javascript:;)

[Hide Notes](javascript:;)

What if the BACKROOMS had GIANT FUCKING ROBOTS that looked COOL AS HELL?

On a more serious note, I've been in a bit of a mecha kick lately. I've watched a ton, and I mean a *ton* of Mobile Suit Gundam stuff lately, and I have also grown interested in Zone of the Enders, one of the main inspirations behind this page. At sometime during the writing process I also learned about the Marathon triolgy, a series of first person shooters developed by Bungie for Machintosh in 1994, 1995 and 1996. Before the smash hit that was the first Halo, before the term "First Person Shooter" was even a thing (they were called Doom clones at the time), Marathon pioneered a lot of concepts that would later be seen in many other titles in years to come. I was hooked to the way Marathon tells it's story– a large-scale space conflict between rival rogue AIs trying to escape the heat death of the universe. Terminals spread throughout the various levels give you everything you need to know, and I thought i'd emulate that here. It was a fun endeavor.
