So, I can tell what you're doing here, but I do have a few suggestions to make the page more engaging.

Overall, despite it being the main concept of the level, the whole "paranoia" thing doesn't have much depth right now. While, yes, the characters do start panicking, all they really do is sit around and let themselves get more scared. The fact that they explored the whole level and found nothing would, realistically, give them no reason to fear the place — as you wrote yourself, the more courageous are less affected by the level.

I think what could work better is structuring it with the characters arriving and setting up camp first, as they see no immediate danger, and becoming more paranoid as they stay. They could then try to search the level, but as they are too afraid, be unable to, leading to the effects getting worse. This just seems like a more realistic sequence of events.

Also, except for a small section at the end, there is no mention of what the characters think there is to fear. It should work well to explain what the level is putting into their minds to haunt them — you could even make it different for each person, which would provide more insight into their personalities. It could amplify their phobias and/or bad memories, for example. You have already done a little of this with one of the characters being paranoid because they're so used to dangers, but aren't seeing any; this doesn't have to be changed outright, but it could do with the extra details.

This is a much smaller problem, but "AWOL" should be "MIA" (missing in action).

Anyway, while the good ideas are already there, restructuring them this way should make it even better.
