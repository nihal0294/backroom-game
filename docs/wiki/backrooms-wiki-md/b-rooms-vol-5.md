# B-Rooms Magazine, Vol. 5

rating: +27[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

  
Issues of the following magazine were found scattered in various parts of [Level 11](level-11.md). It is not known at this time how they were distributed or who wrote them. Investigation is ongoing as to the significance of the contents.
