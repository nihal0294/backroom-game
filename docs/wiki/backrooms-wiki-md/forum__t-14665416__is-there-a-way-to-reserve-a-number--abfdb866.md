Re: Is there a way to "reserve" a number?

Numbers do not matter unless they have importance in the article, like if something is mathematically tied to the number 96 for some reason. If there is an importance about the number in the text, whether that be mathematically, story wise, required for plot, or in a poem, then I say that's grounds for reserving a number. If you just want a number, you can't reserve it.
