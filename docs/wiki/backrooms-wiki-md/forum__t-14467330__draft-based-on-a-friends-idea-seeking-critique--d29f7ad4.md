Hello! I am here to offer critique! Let's get right into it, shall we?

> The level consists of a mysterious hallway, which consists of an unknown amount of segments.

Try to rephrase this in such a way that "consists of" doesn't repeat. You can use "is composed of", "is made out of" or other synonims. Also, there are a lot of segments over segments then more segments… I can go on. Try to make the article easier to read. Maybe it's because of the use of the word "segments" too many times, or maybe because of the over-use of complex and compound-complex sentences, which turns this whole article into a world jumble. Try to shorten the sentences (some of them, not all of them!) to improve ease of reading. And finally, I may be getting this wrong, but you say that there's an unknown amount of segments, but then you list the doors, which are different segments of the level, so wouldn't the amount of segments be equal to the amount of doors?

Now, with the list, why did you have to describe colours? Why are you telling me white is the colour of a sheet of paper? Why are you telling me that red is the colour of blood? I mean, these comparisons are very good usually, but right now you are milking them dry. Just use the colours as they are and don't use comparisons. (At least not right now)

> Completely black walls, ceiling, floor, and door.

I like the use of the Oxford comma there, but why do you need to re-specify that the door is black?

> These doors are colorless, appearing similar to beige.

Beige **is** a colour. There 2 colourless "colours": black and white.

> Segment where the walls are completely filled with miscellaneous objects.

What are said miscellaneous objects? You don't have to list all of them, just some special apparitions like a wolf head, a boat, an atomic bomb, anything!

> Segment filled with sounds of crying, screaming, and other sounds of pain.

Instead of saying "sounds of crying, screaming, and other sound of pain" you can just say "crying, screaming, and other sound of pain"

> Extra long segment, where the temperature progressively gets hotter and hotter inside.

You can say "hotter" only once, but if you want to leave it like this I won't condemn you.

> Segment completely filled with entities chasing you.

What entities? I guess I understand the logic behind why you don't say what entities there are, but I just consider that the names of the entities should be added.

Also, bold all of the doors from the list so they can stand out more.

> Another way to access the level is to walk backwards on Level 1 for fifteen minutes.

Replace the "on" with "in"

You should also add a discovery segment with a date when the level was found and a interview/log/note about the level because, right now, the only thing about the level is a description, nothing that would keep people reading it. Anyways, this is it for Revision number 1, I'm waiting for the update and revision number 2.
