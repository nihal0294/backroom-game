***Level 780- Level Facade***  
![S1z3unpraqGLCcZGirWcmnPRsiGsmIFUJCUKF-OIQWnLfOVMGpa5PzRwPqikVu6EPEIYCdFSchg-qvyD3Q-G1DL6y58qm78nndXeXOTJ](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/S1z3unpraqGLCcZGirWcmnPRsiGsmIFUJCUKF-OIQWnLfOVMGpa5PzRwPqikVu6EPEIYCdFSchg-qvyD3Q-G1DL6y58qm78nndXeXOTJ)  
Level 780 is an abandoned army headquarters building. Once entered, the Wanderer will see a dark corridor, covered in mould and cracks and it only lit by fluorescent lights. ![ROjUpdK5W4d5-igY1Uj5g6nGC-U_5yQt5pQ7WT8uECccLu2kqDG7jqCeAIr1kfSz1Qc1YYbHphs-IOYiPtEOIec9wN0WJd3rT82Gepg](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/ROjUpdK5W4d5-igY1Uj5g6nGC-U_5yQt5pQ7WT8uECccLu2kqDG7jqCeAIr1kfSz1Qc1YYbHphs-IOYiPtEOIec9wN0WJd3rT82Gepg)

Above: the corridor seen when entered to Level 780

The wanderer can enter the rooms to find a bunk bed, a closet that has supplies like almond water, flashlight or an energy bar. The sanity of someone is very quickly lowed while in the facility. Other entities like Smiler, Hounds, Wretches, Skin-stealers and more

![UcIrIpL_VzJNj4aBMhQuObA8d0bbprQeUAIFLewAnXUQDMogGXqJqSB1502oweHOSVHZQG6eas9MqzPSVllMfIknolKX7F6TicxW1C7c](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/UcIrIpL_VzJNj4aBMhQuObA8d0bbprQeUAIFLewAnXUQDMogGXqJqSB1502oweHOSVHZQG6eas9MqzPSVllMfIknolKX7F6TicxW1C7c)

Above: one of the rooms found  
***Entities***  
Entity 250 or Entity Commando is a male wearing army uniform but his skin is bloody and missing an arm. His face is grey and have red eyes and flesh missing from his mouth.  
If you hear the sound of a leg dragging itself on the ground, better hide or run! The Commando can whip out his radio once he sees you and the radio will teleport entities within the area behind the Commando and start chasing you. The Commando will also join the chase whipping out his knife and using the radio to teleport in front of you. If you encounter one before he uses the radio, take out an energy bar and once he sees it, he will stop and stare at you. If you give it to him within 5 secs, he will gobble it up and will teleport away. If you don’t, his knife will be in your skull.

![tltam72rZkBgPW65dBmyQeiAeyT57osl5G33U-3cZZJFA9SavOIWw7vzhjufQABLoZDOb_sM__Hn-LRKwODuyRwMJPtAtCx-Tgiku7Rp](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/tltam72rZkBgPW65dBmyQeiAeyT57osl5G33U-3cZZJFA9SavOIWw7vzhjufQABLoZDOb_sM__Hn-LRKwODuyRwMJPtAtCx-Tgiku7Rp)

Above: The last thing a wanderer recorded before the wanderer was killed

Entity 251 or Entity Medic-bot is a friendly entity that is a small word-down robot and has a ‘+’ shaped eyes, and a red cross on its chest. It can only be found in the Medical room located somewhere in the building. A Medic-bot can heal any injuries and quickly restore your sanity along with giving you food supplies.

![fa1SubWz5PLW5flfw1gYzEF-Arv0eZkEVu44lZwzOIt-oMor7qj_-L1FDQfG9S3XomizIet_pWYDMnEJCjguD79a2Ly3fH2OmzuFAqJ0](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/fa1SubWz5PLW5flfw1gYzEF-Arv0eZkEVu44lZwzOIt-oMor7qj_-L1FDQfG9S3XomizIet_pWYDMnEJCjguD79a2Ly3fH2OmzuFAqJ0)

Above: A Medic-bot resting

***Medical room***  
The Medical room is a room that is guarded and occupied by the Medic-bot. All other hostile entities cannot enter it due to the Medic-bot protecting it and the presence alone. This also suggest that the Medic-bot can easily take down any hostile entity. How it is done, is unknown. The room contains a pool and 2 hospital beds. The room is brighter and cleaner than the rest of the building. The room also has another door that leads to a lot of medical, food and cleaning equipment and supplies. Jumping in the water fills up your sanity also. You can see some friendly entities like Aidens. You can also see a computer researched on partypoopers with notes next to it, saying Where? Still alive? Must find.  
![FEL0Hgm3sQHJyalqVH9jVVbdv4cmq9R8rQEEioi7eQZA33Z8LQAksJASQm5F0UPk-TKbGtdlNOaLzVeDOPj2sOc9v7A69galmxM4kgcj](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/FEL0Hgm3sQHJyalqVH9jVVbdv4cmq9R8rQEEioi7eQZA33Z8LQAksJASQm5F0UPk-TKbGtdlNOaLzVeDOPj2sOc9v7A69galmxM4kgcj)
