Good, but unorganized

23 Feb 2021 06:41

I enjoyed reading this article and I don't have any major problems with it, but the structure is a bit messy.

Criticisms  
*These Entities are extremely dangerous, but are also very rare to encounter.*

I don't think this really belongs in the description. Maybe the fact that they're dangerous could go at the top of the behaviour section, and then you could list their dangerous behaviours/properties.

*These Entities only appear when a Wanderer has been inside Level 9 for a few days. During these days the Wanderer would see large sections of fog randomly in Level 9. This fog is not harmful, but can damage hearing for a short amount of time, due to the large amounts of distorted wind noise emitting from the fog. This fog also can go down to temperatures below zero (0) °C, so it's freezing cold. After the Wanderer has encountered these fog clouds enough, The Mangled will start to appear.*

If I'm being honest, I don't really like the way you introduced the fog. In my opinion, it is mildly confusing and less effective when you mention the fog **first** and then tell how it relates to the entity. Instead you could immediately state how the entity relates to the fog: *"Mangled seem to manifest from a freezing white fog which only appears after a certain period of time is spent within Level 9."* You could then describe the fog a little.

Overall I like the article I just think it has a few structure issues
