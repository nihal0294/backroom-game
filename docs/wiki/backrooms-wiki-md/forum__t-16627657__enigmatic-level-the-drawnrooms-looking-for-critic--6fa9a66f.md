```
[[>]]
```

> The drawrooms level consists of a lobby-like level with an infinite space of walls

I just realized that between the word "draw" and "rooms" you have to put an N, because in the name of your thread and your draft it is written like that.

---

> The drawrooms level consists of a lobby-like level with an infinite space of walls, corridors and rooms completely designed as if they were unfinished, the walls, floors, objects and everything within this level consists only of traces of the edge of each detail of the objects as a cartoon. Nothing within this level has color or texture, the floor, ceiling and wall only have solidity and collision like a normal wall, objects and walkers that enter this level are completely scribbled as well, but the properties of each are not affected. It is possible to find food and Almond Water in abundance on this level, there are lounges and rest corridors that are rarely found, as well as fully designed kitchens, living rooms and even cinemas, TV screens and any equipment electronics are partially affected, devices also turn into sketches, but their screens and properties remain exactly the same.

You have to leave a space for this entire paragraph with the other paragraph. What would be like this:

> The Drawnrooms level consists of a lobby-like level with an infinite space of walls, corridors and rooms completely designed as if they were unfinished, the walls, floors, objects and everything within this level consists only of traces of the edge of each detail of the objects as a cartoon. Nothing within this level has color or texture, the floor, ceiling and wall only have solidity and collision like a normal wall, objects and walkers that enter this level are completely scribbled as well, but the properties of each are not affected. It is possible to find food and Almond Water in abundance on this level, there are lounges and rest corridors that are rarely found, as well as fully designed kitchens, living rooms and even cinemas, TV screens and any equipment electronics are partially affected, devices also turn into sketches, but their screens and properties remain exactly the same.
>
> It is not possible to record, photograph or make detailed drawings about the level due to its mysterious effect, but it is possible to make simple drawings on sheets of paper that represent the level. It is also possible to find sketchbooks and sheets of paper with pencils for the walker to kill boredom, sometimes a sheet of paper can be found with a drawn map of the level showing the position of the walker who has the napa. It's possible to infinitely extend the level whenever someone moves around the map, but all items beyond the walls, ceiling, and floor are generated completely randomly. No one knows to this day who the wanderer was who reached this level, but reports say that he left voice recordings where he himself describes what this enigmatic level is like. It is also described that this level has no sense of direction, causing everyone who enters to end up getting lost wherever they went much faster than normal.

If you do this, I assure you that it will look much better and comfortable when reading it.

The title section is used to change the title of the topic that can be seen at the top of your draft. The subtitle is used to add a small text below the title, each subtitle of each topic is different, for example the main topic of the wiki has the subtitle "You've been here before", so you can add anything to it. And the one in the image is only used to add a logo of some group or random image, for example the one on the wiki has the logo of the group known as MEG. Although I mostly recommend that you delete the image section (because it only works for a png image or one with a transparent background.)

If you have any other questions just tell me and I will respond as quickly as possible.
