Idea Review

17 Apr 2021 08:24

**Page Type:** Entity

**Genre (Optional):** Condolence? (Not exactly sure, if misc is a genre then that)

**Page Layout (Optional):** I'll use the regular entity format, maybe with some footnotes.

**Elevator Pitch:**

- A cat polarising [King Rasputin Bartholemew III](entity-84.md)
- A format similar to King Rasputin Bartholemew III
- No level attached to it
- Name is Queen Napu Wemer I
- Mystery in the form of unknown knowledge regarding King Rasputin Bartholemew III and Queen Napu Wemer I's relations

**Central Narrative:** Queen Napu Wemer I is confirmed to be originated in the backrooms, with anomalous properties (level transportation, mental barriers, fur that takes you to King Rasputin Bartholemew III, intelligence) without a potential origination level. She was found around the time of King Rasputin Bartholemew III being put into protection by the M.E.G, but has used her level transportation to avoid them. She is also a friendly cat.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** For the same reasons as King Rasputin Bartholemew III, a condolence page is usually rather rare in the backrooms, but helps remind people that its not 100% bad in the backrooms.

**Additional Notes:** I need help with the name, narrative, and ideas.
