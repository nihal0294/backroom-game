I dont really do crits like these but i think it would be most useful in your case (Please don't take this in a negative way)

1st thing is that this isn't structured very well. Im not good at structuring stuff myself so you should copy this template and follow from there. <http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/how-to-write-a-level>

2nd is that this changes writing style every few sentences from clinically to personal. Going on the link i showed earlier will also show you an example.

3rd is that you described the whole level in a single paragraph, splitting it up will make it feel more professional.

Finally is that there are a bunch of grammatical and punctuation errors throughout this whole article, just reading this again will fix the mistakes you made.

I don't want you to think that your idea is bad, on the contrary. The idea is very good and i would like to see this turned into a level. Ive only been in this fandom for a week but I have made 4 and 1/2 levels (In sandbox), Ive read 70+ levels, and the only thing I think it needs is a little polishing and it could become a good level.

Happy writing! -Firekay225
