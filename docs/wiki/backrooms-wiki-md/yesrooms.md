Tale! My first tale! This idea started when a friend asked for me to write a joke script for them and I came up with the idea of a heaven waiting room where one person knows the other and the other just knows nothing. It was meant to be comedic and have a stark contrast where the amnesiac just becomes a new, chaotic, deranged person! Alas, I figured the idea was so good I had to reuse it, so, here we are! Andrew and Justin sitting in a waiting room. K-I-S-S-I jokes aside, I just got that idea and then left it. For 7 months. Writers block straight. Then one Thursday it all clicked and I just started writing and it just evolved to this like on the spot after i got the andrew and justin idea. I like the horrors of humanity, being conciousness without purpose. And that's scary. Conciousness is scary. Also, betrayal is evil too, especially when they didn't mean it whatsoever and genuinely meant the best or were just misguided.  
Anyway, if I had a nickle for everytime I wrote a sequel for a page I didn't write, I'd have two nickles. Which isn't a lo-
