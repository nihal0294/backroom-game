"Writing and Posting and Votes, Oh My!"

18 Oct 2022 08:45

With more than twenty entries for the Object Contest 2022 now posted, the voting begins in earnest. Personally speaking, two criteria drive most of my votes:

"Is this object genuinely useful in the Backrooms?"

"Will this object add to the lore of the Backrooms in an outstanding way?"

These are the criteria that I used to develop my Object 365 - “Seer Tea.” This Backrooms Wiki is an amazing writing opportunity. It is an honor and privilege to contribute to this project. Winning this contest, for me, is not as important as securing my creation in its rightful place and establishing the Level 365, Entity 365, and Object 365 triad. All of my best to everyone for everything that they do. You are appreciated.

---

Your friend and colleague,
