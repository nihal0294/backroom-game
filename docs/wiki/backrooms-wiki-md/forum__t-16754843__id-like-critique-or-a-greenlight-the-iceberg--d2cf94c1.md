Re: (I’d like critique or a greenlight) The Iceberg

15 Apr 2024 09:06

There are a lot of small grammatical errors within the draft that can be easily fixed by running this through a grammar checker. I would recommend [Quill Bot](https://quillbot.com/grammar-check) personally, but any mainstream one should suffice. I don't have a lot to say, as this is a pretty small draft, except for the following things:

> This room is a large iceberg in Level 7.

You don't really need to include this bit of text as you immediately repeat this in the description (although worded differently) and this information can be inferred from the image.

> The image was taken by Hubert Neufeld and the image is licensed under unsplash

When citing an image's sources, make sure to use the [license box](component__license-box--969e0688.md).

> Hopefully whatever the tentacle belongs to won’t be getting out of the iceberg. The Iceberg seems to be slowly melting so Level 7 could have a new inhabitant soon

Alright, so you try and set something up here which is a good sign! It's better to go somewhere than nowhere within your draft; this is a really nice sense of direction. However… you don't really go anywhere with this. You can't just include, what could be, a major narrative focal point and then immediately drop it. You should focus the page around this and actually take this somewhere.

Adding onto that point, I think you should actually explore the environment of an iceberg further. If it's as large as you say it is, you can easily go into a set of logs detailing exploring a system of winding tunnels/caves that lead into the heart of the iceberg, which could help add a more smooth transition into the addition of the tentacle.

> The Ice Climbers  
> Only recently created.

This outpost actually needs to be involved within the page somehow. You can't just introduce them for no reason, because you're then wasting the reader's time. Everything on your page should have a reason for being there that ties into something else.
