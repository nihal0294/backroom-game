Ladder of humanity

18 Feb 2023 16:20

Page Type: Level (Ladder of Humanity

Genre (Optional): Action, Existential horror, Experimentation, Mutations

Core Concept:  
Somewhere, two sky facing each other. An upper one being nocturnal while the lower one would be a day sky. Immenses structures floats in the atmosphere looking like chains made out of amalgamations of homes. There is some kind of frontier between the day and the night section that are delimited by abrupt changes in lightings.  
The wind is blowing and from time to time the structures will move and bump into each others.

The term ladder of humanity come from two anomalous effects associated with the level.  
On the daily zones, a majority of non humans gains humanity as their body shapes start to change to match a more humanoid form. Dumber monsters get more intelligent. etc…  
The final step of the process happens when the skin is shed to transform into clothes revealing a brand new human.  
The process isn't 100% perfect, as the primary instincts and some abilities can partially be conserved.

The reverse process happens in the night zone where human mutate into horrifying monsters of all shapes and abilities while conserving barely some humans characteristics.

Once a part of the transformation is done, it can't be reversed, and the transformation will resume when the person or the creature get back to the area of effects.

Also humans or former humans can't be affected by the gain of humanity while the Non humans or former Non humans can't be affected by the loss of humanity.  
Therefore, the transformations are completely irreversible

It is also to be noted that entities of human origin such as hounds, clumps and wretches are completely immune to these phenomenons and roams freely in the level.

Additional Notes:  
Can have a list of experiment being done there  
And explorations being done by former Non-Humans

Also big philosophical questions about the nature of humanity.

As a characteristic ?  
As a force ?  
What does humanity means ?  
And what happens when someone gains it ?

Does a monster will feel remorsefull of their actions ?  
Does a former human can still have sparks of it left ?

Does empathy and sentience automatically a part of humanity ?

Does completely Aliens mindsets still be counted as a form of intelligence ?  
Think about plant or blob who have a non neural intelligence.
