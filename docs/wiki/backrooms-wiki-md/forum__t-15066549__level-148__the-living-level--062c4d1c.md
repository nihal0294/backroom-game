I haven't tried giving criticism before, but I'm happy to give it a try.

I loved the article! The level was extremely well-detailed, the theme was on point, and there was amazing use of clinical tone! I also like how the level is actually alive (although i'm pretty sure level 30 already did that). However, there were a few tiny things I found wrong:

- If you didn't know, the page for memory worms got deleted, so I think you should replace that entity with something else.
- Level 154's whole purpose is to escape by beating the laser games, not just no-clip through the floor to get to another level. Although the argument of 148's dangers is valid, Level 154 doesn't deserve an alternate exit that makes the laser rooms kinda pointless.
