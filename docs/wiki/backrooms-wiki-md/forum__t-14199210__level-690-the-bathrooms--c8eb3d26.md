Level 690 The Bathrooms

17 Sep 2021 23:16

"The bathrooms" the 690th level of the backrooms

Danger level 0

The bathrooms resemble a small public bathroom that would be common in the frontrooms, white floor tiles etc. However the thing that makes the bathrooms different is two things. One, the fact that the sinks will produce a seemingly infinite amount of almond water, however they will shut off automatically as soon as you sanity and thirst are filled. Two, any toilets, sinks, doors, or mirrors with blood or a yellow glue like substance on them should be avoided at all costs (these will produce liquid pain or a substance that will turn you into a partygoer).

Entities  
None, as it is a personal poket dimension.

Colonies/outposts  
None, as it is a personal poket dimension.

Entrances  
The bathrooms can be reached by any level via finding a bathroom, stepping inside the toilet and flushing, at which point you will pass out and wake up in the bathrooms.

Exits  
The only way to leave the bathrooms is via the exit to the left of the sink, however the only way to get somewhere is by thinking about the level you wish to end up at, however any level after 899 cannot be reached (Please note that any thinking of a place in the frontrooms will immediately take you to level 11)

Level 690 is the 691st level of the backrooms

Danger Class: 0

Level 690 resembles a small public bathroom that would be common in the Frontrooms. These are common floor tiles, sinks, and toilets. But the thing that makes The Bathrooms different is two things. All sinks in this room produce an infinite amount of almond water, but these will shut off when sanity or thirst are filled. Be aware of any toilets, sinks, doors, or mirrors with blood or a yellow glue-like substance on them, as these should be avoided at all costs.[1](javascript:;)

Entities  
None, as it is a personal pocket dimension.

Colonies/outposts  
None, as only one person, can exist in this level at a time.

Entrances  
Level 690 can be reached from any level via finding a bathroom, stepping inside the toilet, and flushing, at which point you will pass out and wake up in Level 690.

Exits  
The only way to leave Level 690 is via an exit to the left of the sink, but the only way to get somewhere is by thinking about the level you wish to end up at, but any level after 899 cannot be reached.[2](javascript:;)

Footnotes

[1](javascript:;). These objects produce either Object 12, or a substance that will turn you into Entity 67)
