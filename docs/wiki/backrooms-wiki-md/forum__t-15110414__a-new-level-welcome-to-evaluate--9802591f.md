I recommend using more wiki code, as there aren't any images, headings nor links provided. Out of all things to fix first, I would recommend that. I also recommend using clinical writing style, not personal. Remove words like "please", as a fix, I recommend to go along the lines of: "It is not recommended to walk off course" or something like that. The page also needs better grammar, as commas and periods have spaces after them, as a big grammar problem I have noticed.

Here is a helpful link that may help you make better pages, and possible get some on the wiki: [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/how-to-write-a-level](index.md).
