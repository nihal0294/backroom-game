[+ Critique](javascript:;)

[- Critique](javascript:;)

> This article has been locked to protect it from being deleted by external sources.

"external sources" sounds odd… I suggest rewording the sentence as follows:

> This page has been locked in order to protect it from being deleted.

---

> As such, certain parts of the page may be outdated in the near future.

I get what you tried to say here, but it may not be extremely clear to others. Suggesting to substitute "As such" with "Due to the inability to edit the page further".

---

> and are the only usable platforms in this level.

It's best to say that they "are the only platforms to travel onto within the level's river". This part of the sentence kinda broke the clinical tone.

---

> Although one can find rock on either side of the river

If you are planning to implement the previous sentence, omit "of the river". I also suggest substituting "rock" with "an unknown substance" given the flammable nature mentioned afterwards.

---

> Even though one will spend lots of time dangerously close to lava in this level, the temperature of the level is fixed at 77° F (25° C), which prevents death from extreme body temperatures.

Remove "of the level". Omit the statement after the second comma.

---

> The lava has also been discovered to be much colder than usual, staying at around 932° F (500° C). However, it is still obviously not recommended to touch.

Reword the first sentence. Suggestion: "The lava is, furthermore, colder than normal, with its temperature being around 932° F (500° C)." Semicolon before "however". Reword the last statement as follows: "it's still ill-advised to ever make contact with it".

---

> Level 187 does not have a fixed day-night cycle. For example, the sun may be out for a few hours on one day, but on the next one, it may not even last a few minutes. Night time is often much brighter than usual, while at daytime, the sky has a deep red color to it.

I'd say that 187 has a *varied* day-night cycle. The second statement shall be omitted. Correct "Night time" to "Nighttime". Reword the last statement as follows: "Additionally, nighttime is usually relatively brighter than expected. Daytime, on the other hand, gives the sky a red hue".

---

> Although it is rare

Add "to occur".

---

> This level is useful, even if dangerous

I'd say "valuable despite its hazardous conditions".

---

> Special Objects and Materials  
> There are two special objects and one special material native to Level 187.

I'd say "Special Objects and Anomalies". This is because there is a single anomalous material, not multiple.

---

> This suit will not only protect one from the lava, but

Omit the comma.

---

> However, these suits are very hard to make, as even a single miniscule hole in them can let lava seep through and burn the user. It is advised to find an M.E.G. operative in the level that is skilled in crafting them.

These two sentences are useless. Just say that, due their fragility, it's best to seek the help of the M.E.G. for crafting.

---

> They have refused to share any information about them, other than the fact that they did not create the shoes.

I don't know what you are trying to say here. Please only state that this Reddit user did not create the shoes.

---

> can either propose serious hazards, or can be benevolent for wanderers.

Remove the comma.

---

> can be found if one goes into a dead end in the river and finds a peculiar hole. The hole will then

Clinically incorrect: elaborate on what you mean by “peculiar hole”. Semicolon after “hole”. Substitute “The hole” with “it”.

---

> which is a replica of the average river found in the Frontrooms

Better explain what you mean by “avarage river”. This statement is also clinically incorrect.

---

> a temporary strength and stamina boost

Reword the sentence like this: “a temporary boost in strength and stamina”.

---

> can only access this location once, and once they leave it, they will never be able to return.

Omit anything after the first comma, it’s redundant.

---

> given the difference of terrain. However, further research has proven this rumor false.

Semicolon before “However”.

---

> if not, thousands

Remove the comma.

---

> the Frontrooms

Capitalize “the”.

---

> entities. However

Semicolon.

---

> and Level 14

Omit this. Nobody truly knows what’s happening there, even though psychological effects indeed take place.

---

> or dehydration

“or” shall be replaced with “and/or”.

---

> the Barrier

Capitalize “the”.

---

> sometimes even causing blindness

Reword like this: “having a chance to result in blindness”.

---

> Although setting up a base or outpost in this level is unfeasible, a community still exists within this level.

Contradictory statement. \*infeasible

---

> M.E.G. Community: The Magma Association

\*Base

---

> an entrance

\*entry

---

> This is incredibly rare.

\*The likelihood of this to occur is incredibly tiny.

---

> After the drone had been airborne for 15 minutes, the team monitoring the drone

Do not repeat “the drone”. Use the pronoun “it”.

---

> The interview was as follows:

\*Here is the audio transcript of the interview.

---

> So what do

Add a comma after “So”.

---

> and already something was… off.

\*and there was already something off

---

> There was a sudden fog

\*Fog appeared all of a sudden

---

> it wasn't there before, it just.. showed up

**Mind you that I am not the best when it comes to dialogues**, but I’d reword it, saying something like “it happened… instantly” or something.

---

> with constant geysers shooting out red-hot magma.

Geysers erupt water and steam rather than the rock and ash that comes out of a volcano, according to [knowablemagazine.org](https://knowablemagazine.org/article/physical-world/2018/thar-she-blows-what-why-and-where-geysers#:~:text=No.,volcanoes%2C%20and%20erupt%20more%20frequently.), at least. No, I’m not advertising it, I was just unsure…

---

> the drone saw some structure

The drone *filmed* some structure.

---

> There was no footage. Just a green silhouette.

The first period shall be a comma.

---

> Oh, and by who? Well, we don't know, but maybe [REDACTED]

Uuh, I’m not exactly the best when it comes to format-screws. (I know, I know, I’m a hypocrite, considering that my drafts are most likely semi-format-screws, but I just have too little experience for that. No, , I’m not making fun of you. It’s just that, normally, I’d crit format-screws from those with the greenlight exemption, for easier management.) Anyway, the “redacted” thing is a little overused, in my humble opinion. I suggest replacing that entirely.

---

> Reply “No”

This part is a bit confusing, and weird. The scream and the fact that this looks like it comes from an audio transcript just confuses me more. I suggest you make a normal conversation with a bit of corrupted text, but nothing else. Keep the ending, though.

---

Alright, it’s time for the author collapsible. (YES, I check those frequently when giving critique. This is done so that the staff doesn’t have to do it for you.)

> Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0

You did not give a link to the license. It’s required for this kind of use, y’know?

# Offset 2

Okay, expect me to be a little less picky, mostly due to the fact that I myself suck with dialogues and logs. At least, that’s what I believe to be the case.

[+ Critique](javascript:;)

[– Critique](javascript:;)

> why make it so boring?

I don’t get why having to travel onto literal lava is boring.

---

> these shoes aren't normal!

This entity just kinda stated the obvious. I suggest you make it say it in another way.

---

> So anyways, Im minding my own business, walking around the empty terrain of this place. Maybe thats why this ground explodes.

What do you mean?

---

> But then, I finally see smth new. Smth… strange.

You repeated “smth” way too many times. Please fix the redundant sentencing.

---

> Its like one of those kids TV shows with way too much dumb luck.

Substitute this statement entirely. I myself found it a bit difficult to understand.

---

> and the place just cant stop having those damn earthquakes.

This sentence makes the earthquakes seem a bit too violent to be able to type this.

---

> . In fact, Ive almost died here so many times. When does this end?

Semicolon, not a period before “In”. The second statement and the question make this log a bit… boring. I’d say “; in fact, Ive risked my life so many times. When will this end? Will it?”.

---

> Now, bcs of that, Ive surpassed what I think is the end of paleozoic era. Walking through a mass extinction, at least without dying, is pretty awesome.

Omit any statement referring to the draft below the logs. Do not mention something that will be talked about afterwards.

---

> for whatever reason.

For *some* reason. I get that this dude didn't care about punctuation, and that's fine. But please, check grammar, at least.

---

> Bcs for once in a lifetime, this place feels… interesting.

This statement is… confusing if you compare it to the rest. "once in a lifetime" seems out of place. Don't use "feels", use "seems".

---

> But whatever it is, its stopping me from what I want. What I love.

Several statements like this make it necessary for the reader to, well, *read* from the begging. In other words, if I were to show this to a totally random person and asked what they thought about it, they'd say that the lack of punctuation is simply… not fitting. It goes from narratively written to… something with the same amount of SPaG as a Reddit post. (Yes, I am aware that this dude *is* a Reddit user, but please consider my suggestion.)

---

> Oh my goodness.

The paragraph that follows feels like… a flow of thoughts, not a log. I can say the same for pretty much the rest of the journal.

---

> a presumably infinite portion of Level 187.

The reader will most likely think "how can it be a portion if it is infinite". That's because it seems a little… contradictory. I suggest saying that it is a formerly discovered portion of 187 or something.

---

> It has only been thoroughly explored once, the person being the Reddit user u/Aethereus333.

Grammatically incorrect. Reword like this:

> It has only been explored thoroughly once, with the explorer in question being the Reddit user u/Aethereus333.

I also suggest that you add a link to the person’s profile, if they allow it.

---

> four of which he has managed to document.

\*four of which were able to be documented.

---

> Upon leaving the Barrier, one will find that the area in front of them is a volcanic wasteland;

\*one will stumble upon a volcanic wasteland,  
It sounds a bit weird otherwise. No, no semicolon, use a comma here.

---

> the level will protect one from any fatal threats they come across

\*the level will protect the explorer from any lethal hazards they may come across

---

> Upon traveling

\*After

---

> the first signs of water

\*the first signs of the presence of water

---

> hosting the first ever forms of life

You made it sound like these were the first life forms that have ever existed. Reword:

> hosting the first life forms that are to be witnessed.

---

> This does not include mass extinctions.

What does this mean?

---

> land plants

Using “land” here is a bit unnecessary.

---

> Biological activity involving these creatures will continue for dozens of kilometers

This sentence makes little to no sense. I get that you want it to feel like a, well, *walk through time*, but the way you write this kind of statements makes it feel like it shifts over time and not that you are travelling. Do not use the term “mass extinction”, as it’ll simply make it harder for others to understand what you are striving to describe.

---

> Several natural disasters will occur

From this point onwards, you don’t describe any threshold area, which is kind of required to make it clear what’s happening. If in this area constantly happens a mass extinction and occur these natural disasters, make it explicit.

---

> ignored him on his journey

\*ignored him during his journey

---

> they had all seemed to ignore him

\*they all seemed to ignore him

---

> he had felt a sudden *clip*

“clip” doesn’t sound right here. Suggestion: he had suddenly fallen through the floor.

---

> that throughout the whole walk

Add a comma after “that”. Afterwards is a complement.

---

> he was entirely unable to go in the opposite direction.

\*completely  
If this suggestion is to not be accepted, don’t use an adverb at all.

---

> The only exit from The Walk Through Time is to continuously walk for an unknown period of time.

\*for an undetermined amount.

---

I suggest you also give a link to ’s Reddit profile in the author collapsible.

---

# **Notes:**

Overall, it was very difficult for me to properly visualize what the parent level/main area looked like. I suggest you try to base the description off the pictures and then you add what you already specified. If this is not your thing, I suggest you expand the description of the main lava river a little bit more before adding the rest. As for Offset 2, well, the description is just overly short. You could add a bit of details (e.g. the entry’s author’s interpretations, presented in an objective manner).
