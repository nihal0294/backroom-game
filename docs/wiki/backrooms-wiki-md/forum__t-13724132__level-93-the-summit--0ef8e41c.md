Level 93 - The Summit

13 Sep 2020 10:40

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre (Optional):** Adventure

**Page Layout (Optional):** Typical Format. Standard Stuff.

**Elevator Pitch:** Level 93 is a baseless mountain alike in appearance to Mt. Everest. Level 93 is a risky level to survive in due to it’s harsh weather and it’s weak composition.

**Central Narrative:** Level 93 was discovered by an M.E.G. search team named “Search Team 09” examining Level 8. Once Search Team 09 received an answer to the types of entities in Level 8, they attempted to exit Level 8 and enter Level 9, however, entered Level 93 under unknown circumstances.

Search Team 09 happened upon the exit of Level 93 however died of hypoxia before they could document the exit of Level 93.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** This is the first mountainous level of the Backrooms.

**Additional Notes:** If there appear to be any nitpicks or plotholes you want me to fix, notify me and I’ll revise the concept.
