> [[>]]  
> [[collapsible show="Author(s)" hide="Hide author"]]

It should be at the near-bottom of your code source.  
On the page, it is the part that appeared as:

> [Author(s)](javascript:;)
>
> [Hide author](javascript:;)
>
> [info on the production of the article and/or the authors behind it]  
> ~   
> [Please also place your image source links here!]

I assume, with your history of more than 100 days being a Wikidot member, that you already know what to and how to put in this box. It is a qualification, a watermark that tells people who made the page, which is extremely important.

[Or that you don't know?](javascript:;)

[I see...](javascript:;)

First, we'll break down the code into small parts, each line being one.

> [[>]]  
> [[/>]]

These two lines should appear at the top and bottom of your author box.  
It tells your page to put the contents included in the middle to stay on the right (">" looks like an arrow pointing to the right).  
If you're fine with it, leave it be. If not, then you have 2 other options:  
- Put it on the left by changing the ">" to "<".  
- Put it in the middle of the page by changing the ">" to "=".

---

> [[collapsible show="Author(s)" hide="Hide author"]]  
> [[/collapsible]]

These two lines should appear at the 2nd-to-top and 2nd-to-bottom of the code.  
It is a "collapsible" that allows text to be hidden unless clicked on, which reveals them.  
The contents put in the middle of the 2 lines are what will appear when opened.  
I don't recommend changing anything in this portion, but:  
- When it is hiding all the texts, it will appear as what is stated in …show="something here"… and when opened will appear as …hide"some other thing here". You can put just about whatever you want in them, but it is not recommended to do so.  
- You can't put 2 collapsible inside each other, which is a bit sad but well…

---

> ~ [[\*user username]]

This line will appear right in the middle of the code.  
You don't actually need the "~" at the start, just remove it.  
This code will show the username along with the avatar of the account with their username inside.  
For example:  
With my usernamein the code[1](javascript:;), it will appear as .  
With yours in[2](javascript:;), it will appear as .

Concept Critique

13 Jan 2024 01:02

Hm, well, I'm struggling to see how this is Backrooms related. At the moment, it seems that the page is comprised of the basic tropes that are found in magical girl anime with very little adaptation to make them fit the Backrooms setting. In the current state the idea is in, I doubt that it'll last long onsite, for a couple reasons:

- Tonally, magical girl anime and the Backrooms are very different. With the exception of one that I know of[1](javascript:;), magical girl anime is very optimistic and fantastical in tone. Through the power of friendship, enemies are defeated and everyone lives happily after. The Backrooms is very much more horror-like and existential because people have been trapped in another dimension.
- The description of the level isn't very immersive. The wording is too clinical, and doesn't grab my attention.
- There are too many named characters, and I can't keep track of them. Because there's so many, there isn't a lot of detail that endears them to me or makes me interested in them, so they all blur together. They also don't have personalities. They have character attributes (hair color, clothing style, etc.), but nothing that tells me who they are as a person.

Now, this isn't to say that you can't have magical girls in the Backrooms. It's an interesting concept that *could* work, but there's just going to be a lot of work before this draft is ready.

My suggestions are these:

- Describe the level, but only give me the details I need. Within those details, every word picked should evoke a specific feeling.
- With that, it's a bit lazy to describe things as "magical girl." Like yeah, that is the vibe you're going for, but rather than telling me it's magical girl-esque, *show* me. What colors are those? What motifs? What if I've never seen a magical girl anime? How would you describe that?
- Cut down the characters to just… one: Storyia. She's the one who created this level. She's the one making magical girls. She's the most interesting. Flesh *her* out a little. Why is she doing this? How is she doing this? Can the people she empowered go rogue? Is she human? and so on and so forth.
- The idea I'm *most* interested in is the idea of empowered wanderers in pastels fighting entities and saving people. I think that's an interesting idea and it genuinely makes me smile.
- **Change the tone to fit the Backrooms.** I think this will be the hardest thing to do because it'll require a lot of revision in your draft. If *I* had to write this, I would actually be inspired by Madoka Magica. Madoka Magica is a very cynical take on the ideas of magical girls. These girls are *children*, empowered by beings who don't care about them, and will fight until they die or until they turn into monsters. That interpretation of magical girls would work better in the Backrooms: a life of neverending war.

Good luck, and let me know if you have questions :)
