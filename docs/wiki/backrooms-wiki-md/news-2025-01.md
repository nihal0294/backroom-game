# New year, new contest!

---

We’re kicking off the year with a brand new contest: **Characterizacon2025**! This contest focuses entirely on the POIs we currently have on the site and expanding on them to create a diverse range of lore. Very few of these POIs are actually utilized in pages and are often left at just their introduction page, and this contest aims to combat that!

Similarly to Canonicon2024, this is a team contest! We have 4 teams participating as of right now, all of which were organized by the Staff Team based on a list of requests each participant submitted. While we wait for these teams to release these pages, let’s learn a bit about the teams and their plans for the POIs!

## Androgynous Aliens

---

This team, consisting of , , [1](javascript:;), , and , is focusing on the lovable researcher alien that is [Nyx](nyx.md)!

Nyx is a researcher with the [Ariane Circle](cercle-ariane.md) who has an ability to manipulate levels to their liking. They’ve harnessed this ability to be able to control almost every aspect of their current home, Level 523-FR. While they are incredibly smart and knowledgeable about engineering and technology, they aren’t too great at social interaction with humans.

This team plans on expanding on Nyx’s relationship with the Backrooms and their struggles with fitting into human society, while also providing a bit of backstory for what life was like for Nyx before they fell into the Backrooms and Nyx’s biology, along with the slightest hint of romance!

> “Who needs lore when you have gay alien fanfiction”  
> ”Why not both?”

## V for Valentin

---

This team, consisting of , , , , and , is focusing on the mysterious director of a mystical group, [v-valentin](archived__v-valentin--0291b1f1.md)!

Viktor Valentin is the leader of the shadow-y syndicate that is [the-eternal-repository](archived__the-eternal-repository--16af9bf0.md)! He founded the group in 2016 and it has since had a large hand in the trading of information. Highly valuing anonymity, no one truly knows how the group operates or who they are. Valentin deeply cares about the group he administrates, but that care may cause him to go too far.

This team plans on expanding on the Repository’s relationship with the other groups in the Backrooms, whether that be allyship or rivalry is up for debate, along with a glimpse into Viktor's character outside of the Repository. If you like thriller mysteries and uncovering the truth behind the cloaks and masks, this may be the read for you!

> "We are going to expand on this Russian guy who's the leader of a super secret group."

## Furry-Curious

---

This team, consisting of , , , and , is focusing on the master of the hunt himself, [Herne](herne-the-huntmaster.md)!

Herne the Huntmaster is the leader of the widely feared entity, the [Wild Hunt](entity-398.md)! This… furry[2](javascript:;) has existed long before humans’ earliest ancestors were born. While he sure looks like he’s friendly and kind, don’t be fooled. He does, in fact, bite.

This team is reportedly still brainstorming on what they would like to focus on! However, there is a rumored W.I.P. home level for Herne and the Wild Hunt, along with some hints at how life was for Herne as someone who’s been around since the beginning of time! Be sure to stick around to see what this team has planned.

> “Snapshots of the life of an immortal.”

## What Team?

---

This team, consisting of , , , and , is focusing on… which POI is this again? Oh, right. [J. Doe](j-doe.md)!

J. Doe isa eccentric individual who is always on the move! They’re incredibly recognizable for looking like… something. Now that I think about it, I can’t really remember, though no one else can either. This not-so-interesting Person of Interest has the effect of having every aspect of their person or interactions with them completely erased from the memory of those who interact with them! Because of that, not much is known about this indifferent individual.

This team decided “why take one route when you can take all of them?” You’ve heard right, folks. Everyone knowing nothing about you might as well mean that everything about you is true! If you’re interested in complete and utter absurdist nonsense, make sure to stay tuned with this team’s shenanigans!

> "Our plan is to make up as much bullshit as we can about J. Doe. You put together a team of jesters so we plan to hold an entire circus act!
>
> Also who?"
