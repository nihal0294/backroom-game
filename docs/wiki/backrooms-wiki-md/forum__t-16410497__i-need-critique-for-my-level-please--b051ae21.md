I’m not the best at critiquing but I have a few suggestions, mainly to do with SPaG and the flow as I personally love the concept.

The first thing I will say is, whenever you write the name ‘Tom Hounds’, Tom and Hounds both need to be capitalised as in most instances on Tom is.

The second thing is the starting description jumps around a bit going from the core to outside back to the core.

*“**Level 859** has two sections, the core and the outside. The core is the inside of the restaurant and the outside is the outside of the restaurant and resembles a city. The core is where wanderers who enter **level 859** will always end up. However, there is no known way to exit the core as Windows and doors are unbreakable and won't open thus making travel to the outside impossible. The outside has little known about it other then its modern city appearance. However, when observing through the windows of the core random monuments of different countries cities can be found.”*

I think that you should stick to describing one area at a time rather than switching after each sentence, it would make it flow smoother. A better way to word it would be:

“**Level 859** has two sections the core is the inside of the restaurant and is where wanderers who enter **level 859** will always end up. However, there is no known way to exit the core as Windows and doors are unbreakable and won't open thus making travel to the outside impossible. The outside is visible from the windows and resembles a city. This are is outside of the building and little known about it. However, when observing through the windows of the core random monuments of different countries’ cities can be observed.”

This makes it flow a lot smoother and it doesn’t jump around to much. For the second paragraph there is some minor SPaG errors. These are just a quick fix.

*“**Level 859** is divided into different floors of cores and outsides accessed via an out of place elevator in the hallway leading to the washrooms. The amount of floors **Level 859** has is unknown”*

- ‘cores and outsides’ doesn’t flow too well and should be “divided into multiple core floors each with their own outside”. This is just better grammar and makes it sound more professional.
- Also the first sentence is a bit too long, I would shorten it into too sentences by putting a full stop after ‘outside’ and before accessed.
- Due to the Full stop being added reword ‘accessed via’ to “they can be accessed via”
- After unknown a full stop is needed

*“Flags of sub divisions and cities can also appear however, they often are inaccurate to the actual areas the flags represents along with the signs following the flag.”*

All I’ll say for this section is after appear there needs to be a full stop or a semi colon to divide the two main clauses. Other than that this section is really good.

Due to this and the windows being indestructible, wanderers like to observe entities through the Window.

Window doesn’t need to be capitalised

*“**Level 859** has a day, night and seasons cycle. Every 10 hours it switches between morning, afternoon, evening and night and every week it switches between spring, summer, fall and winter. The season often decides which entities appear in the outside.“*

This is just personal opinion here but I think doing this helps with world building. Add a collapsible table showing which entities come out at what season instead of just saying it decides the entities. This will help readers with visualisation of your level.

# ***Bases, Outposts and Communities***

### *[M.E.G](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/m-e-g)(major explorer group)*

### *base soda*

- *Consists of 5 floors*
- *Dedicated to researching **level 859***

### *The onion folk*

- *A group of 36 people consisting of humans and some intelligent docile entities who have taken refuge in **Level 859***
- *Takes up 28 floors*
- *Has an exit to [Level 4](level-4.md) on one of the floors they own that they use to get valuable recourses from [Level 4](level-4.md)*
- *The onion folk are known for celebrations and a culture revolving around joy*
- *Have a tradition called "Entity fight betting" were if they see entities fighting in the outside they will gather around the window and place bets giving a reward to whoever bet correctly.*

Mainly, most of what’s wrong here is capitalisation errors. ‘Major Explorer Group’ needs to be capitalised as it is a name. The same applies to ‘Base Soda’ and ‘The Onion Folk’. Also it should be ‘Take up 28 floors’ rather than takes. Also when linking groups you need the URL to the group page otherwise the link will not work.

I hope this critique helped as I’m not the greatest. I genuinely love the concept here and I look forward to seeing it get posted to the site. Good luck getting greenlit!

Ok, we are back for round 2. After a quick read through it already looks a lot better. However, it is not yet perfect.

*“Level 859 has two sections the core is the inside of the restaurant and is where wanderers who enter level 859 will always end up. However, there is no known way to exit the core as Windows and doors are unbreakable and won't open thus making travel to the outside impossible. The outside is visible from the windows and resembles a city. This are is outside of the building and little known about it. However, when observing through the windows of the core random monuments of different countries’ cities can be observed.”*

- I would put a semi colon between “sections” and “the core”
- “Windows” does not need to be capitalised as it isn’t referring to the entity and is in the middle of a sentence.
- ***“This are is outside of the building and little known about it.”*** The first thing wrong with this sentence is “are” should be ‘area’. Secondly, “and” should be followed up by ‘has’ otherwise it doesn’t make much sense
- ***“ However, when observing through the windows of the core random monuments of different countries’ cities can be observed.”*** This sentence is far longer than needed. You could probably shorten it down to ‘ However, when viewed, monuments of different countries’ cities can be observed.’

*“Each core of level 859 varies in colour and structure. For example, one core could be an octagon with orange walls and a light brown floor, and the next could be a rectangle with red walls and a grey floor. Each core is the size of an average fast food restaurant.”*

- I think it sounds better as ‘Each floor’s core varies in colour and structure.’ There is no need for “of level 859” and it flows smoother with ‘floor’s’ before the word “core”.
- The rest of this paragraph is pretty much fine. I would just replace the word core in the second and last sentences to floor as it flows smoother. Plus we already know that you’re describing the core.

For the seasonal entities section, make it into a collapsible table rather than a collapsible list. It would make it look a lot more organised. If you don’t know how to create a table here is a link to the [wikidot syntax](wikidot-syntax.md).

Just a quick and brief thing here. For the Onion Folk I think it sounds better as ‘takes up 28 floors’ rather than “take up 28 floors”

*“Finding a fast-food restaurant in Level 11 with a sign saying "come eat at Tom Hounds, the best restaurant in all the backrooms!" can lead to Level 859”*

- “come” in “Come eat” should be capitalised.

I cannot spot much more wrong; however, I have already stated I am not a professional critic. I would recommend fixing these mistakes and coming back for a quick third revision. The third revision will probably be the last.

Still I really love the content this level provides and I feel it deserves a spot on the site.
