12 May 2021 23:41

Alright, I have a few questions and problem with this concept. According to your entrance, you gave a possibility that entities will abduct you and drag you into this level which inadvertently suggests that the entity are a hivemind. It also infers that this level is the base for them. Not only does this effect other articles but it's vague enough for people not to take it seriously.

As for the level number, there are hundreds of free articles that haven't been made and its highly improbable that the article wouldn't be published for a few months.

In my honest opinion, this is a very ambitious and empty concept. While the thoughts are clear on what you expected to make this level, the way it's written doesn't reflect it. I believe you need to explain more things in this article. What is the red object in the sky and why is it special to the level? Why is there a random flame which warps you to a random level?

You need to make more connections with these loose ends or else it'll seem more like filler. If it doesn't add to the article, it subtracts.

Another thing is with the interview log. The people involved seem emotionless and robotic. I believe you should make the interviewee more hesitant and more distraught before recounting the events. If you need help with dialogue, I always like to imagine the conversation in my head like a movie before writing. The interviewer is also pretty emotionless as the man is close to becoming an insanity, sounding like becoming an insanity is more like an inconvenience rather than a bad thing.

Sorry, Hope this helps!

Hiya! I saw you left some friendly comments on my draft so I wanted to return the favor and help you out.

> Level 1000 is a pitch-black expanse, with nothing but the whispers of ghosts in your ear.

Replace "whispers of ghosts in your ear." with "whispers of seemingly invisible entities in your ears."

> Do not touch the pole, of the flame, as you will likely be warped to a random dangerous level.

Replace all of this with "Do not touch the pole or the flame, or there is a high chance you will be warped to a highly dangerous level."

> as you will likely be warped to a random dangerous level.

Remove all of this, as the sentence before it already explains that the level you may be transported to is random.

> The only current confirmed way to enter this level is via being "forgiven" in Level 999.

"The only current confirmed method of entering this level is being "forgiven" in Level 999."
