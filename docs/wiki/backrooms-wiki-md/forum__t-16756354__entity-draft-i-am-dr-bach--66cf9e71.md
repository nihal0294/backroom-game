Entity Draft - "I am Dr. Bach"

23 Mar 2024 19:07

**Seeking Greenlights:** Yes

**Page Type:** Entity

**Genre:** Sci-Fi

**Page Layout:** Normal page structure with addenda, no offsets

**Word Count:** ~1752

Spectrous entity that exists only as writing on a piece of paper. A result of an expierment gone wrong. I don't want to spoil too much, so I won't go into much detail here. I believe I have a good handle on SPaG, so that shouldn't be much of an issue. I am mainly looking for critique regarding my narrative, flow of my writing, and in particular if the article drags or is too verbose in certain places. This is my first serious attempt at writing a page, and so I definitely need help with tone and pacing.
