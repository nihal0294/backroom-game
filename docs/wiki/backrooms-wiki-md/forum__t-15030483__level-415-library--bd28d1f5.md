Hi RichardSongTT.

I have seen *MANY* posts of your level, so its about time I give you my opinion, Starting off with SPaG.

> Listening to the sound for a long time may cause the homeless person

You should probably replace "homeless person" with "wanderer".

> such as dizziness, and nausea .Any outside…

Should be "nausea. Any outside…"

> look at the books on the shelves to easily determine that.

Should be "to easily determine which floor they are on."; It's more clear.

> Small circular dots will grow in size over time until the homeless person falls asleep

Again, "homeless person" should be "wanderer".

> considered by the Wanderers

Unless you have a specific group called "The Wanderers", just call them wanderers. "considered by wanderers"

> this list lists the characteristics

Hmm… maybe "this list displays the characteristics"

> 1F and 2F No entity

Correction: No entit*ies*

> few Marking camouflage, some The Book Spider

Correction: *F*ew Marking *C*amoulage, some Book Spiders

> Some Marking camouflage and few Smiler, many The Book Spider

Pluralize entities, capital letters, yk.

> becoming a kind of the entity

Correction: Becoming one of the entity's kind.

> Marked disguise

Is the entity's name not "Marking Camouflage"?

> but marked disguise wins without

"Wins" should not be plural

> When the wanderer actively touches or is attacked

Correction: Touches the entity. (This makes it more clear what they are touching)

> . But if you take a glass of almond water

This is incorrect grammar. Change to either "If you take…" or rewrite the sentence.

> The Book Spider moves slightly faster and often moves in groups.

Does it move fast, move faster in groups, or move faster than (…..)? Please rewrite.

> Also, books disguised by book spiders are usually green or red ,

Delete the space before the comma, and switch the comma to a period.

> The viscous liquid tasted like cream

"Tasted" should be "tastes".

> You can also enter here from The Hub if you have the level key.

Either switch this to the last entrance listed, or just "You can enter here…"

> FINALLY: Remember to fix all of your broken links! (Unless you are referring to an undiscovered level, of course.)

…And that's all! Now for my crit on lore/desc.  
Okay. Remember, I am one of the friendliest guys, so when I give some tough crit, it's not supposed to be offensive, but constructive.  
Concept: 7.5  
Actual Work: 6.5

My initial thoughts on the level were that this definitely reminded me of Blanche's level (I forgot the level #). What sets these apart is that your level is a much more dangerous one, which I appreciate. I think that a massive modern library is a pretty decent idea, getting a nice 7.5 on the concept. After your description of the level, it's the entities I was interested in.

Marking Camouflage and Book Spiders. Ignoring SPaG errors, I liked them! I am a little against Marking Camo turning u into one of them, as that is an overused thing, but nothing I'll be disappointed about. The Book Spiders weren't *as* good, as I would have enjoyed a biology report on them. All you mentioned were their outside looks, behaviors, and the liquid they secrete. Could use a little fixer-upper, but still a strong idea.

My Summary:  
If I were to rate the current state of the article in general on 1-10, I would give it a 6.5 or 7. I could see the creativity behind it, but the written stuff just didn't hold it up quite right. You should improve on your clinical tone, get rid of all SPaG errors, and look for ones I may have missed. The size of the page is simple and nice, but adding a little bit more detail definitely won't hurt. Just a reminder, *I really like the ideas* but I just need to see these beautiful ideas in a more stable, well-arranged setting.

Whew! Took me 30 mins to write this *plus* reading ur level. Anyways, I would really enjoy seeing your improvements soon. If you have questions or are ready for me to check your next draft, I'm only one PM away at . Cya!
