> Content Warning:

I'd add some blank spaces (do so by typing ) below this as the image can be seen very easily currently; this would give the reader a bit more leeway.

> so take extreme care.

I'd rewrite this as:

\*so proceed with caution whilst in the presence of an instance of the entity.\*

> or childlike surroundings.

"childlike" should be "child-like"; hyphenate it.

> A digital recreation of a Hodgepodge

Add punctuation to this caption.

> on surfaces such as a wall,

I'd change "a wall" to simply "walls".

> This is for unknown reasons,

Add "done" after "is".

> meet other Hodgepodges and have even been spotted attacking one another.

I'd rewrite this as:

\*meet other Hodgepodes—to the extent where they've been noted to attack each other once contact had been made.\*

> these bones are always found broken

I'd add "almost" before "always" here.

> peanut, and even further causes their idiocy.

I'd remove the information after this comma; doesn't add much to this sentence.

> on a group of seven(7) wanderers

Add a space after "seven" to give some space for "(7)".

> discovered about three(3) hours after the

Add a space after "three" to give some space for "(3)".

> All four(4) survivors of the

Add a space after "four" to give some space for "(4)".

> Connor Drew has added Dr. Henry Argall

This yellow is **very** difficult to read; try and find another suitable color so that this may be just a tad bit more legible.
