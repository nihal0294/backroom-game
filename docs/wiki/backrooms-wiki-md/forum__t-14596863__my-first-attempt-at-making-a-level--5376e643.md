**Level n** is the (n+1)'nd Level of the Backrooms.

# **Description**

Level -y- appears to be a claustrophobic crawlspace, approximately 1 foot in height. The level contains stone brick walls, a floor that feels like cold steel, but looks like a flat dirt texture from a poorly made video game, and wood planks with support beams as the "roof" of the level. The level appears to be a randomly generated maze, and it is infinite in width but not in length.

Upon entry to this level, the wanderer that entered will have all belongings removed and replaced with overalls, a bright blue shirt, and a mining helmet. They will be forced to crawl the 10 mile long path to the exit. The wanderer will be stripped of all food items upon entry, so it is advised to get to the exit as fast as possible. Upon exiting the level, all belongings will appear back on the wanderer and the mining helmet and clothing given to them will be taken. Since all items are taken from wanderers who enter the level, photography is impossible, therefore there are no existing images of this level.

Entities in this level do not appear in the first mile of the level. They begin to appear more frequently as the level goes on. Entities in this level include Smilers, Hounds, Skin-Stealers, Dullers, Clumps, Crawlers, and Faultcrawlers.

Bottles of almond water can be found in the later segments of the level, most often found on the 8th mile. It is reported that small puddles of liquid pain can be found rarely at the 10th mile, but such claims have yet to be proven.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There are no outposts on this level. However, it is possible, but not advised to set up a community, due to the scarcity of almond water, numerous entities, and claustrophobic nature of the level.

# **Entrances And Exits**

### Entrances

In any level, a crawlspace door on the wall may appear. Upon entering the trapdoor, the wanderer will appear in level -y-. Upon sleeping in a crawlspace in the frontrooms, there is a small chance that the wanderer will wake up in level -y-. A crawlspace door may manifest in the frontrooms on walls at completely random times, and entry will lead to level -y-It is advised to never enter a crawlspace door leading to level -y- unless a dire situation is at hand, and there is no other way out. Make sure to avoid hunger before entering level -y-

### Exits

After traveling 10 miles of the level, the same trapdoor used to enter level -y- will appear, and upon entering the trapdoor Upon exiting, the wanderer will walk out of a trapdoor on a completely random level.
