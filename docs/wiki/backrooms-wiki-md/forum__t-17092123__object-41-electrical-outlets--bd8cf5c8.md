Electrical outlets I think could have been a really interesting object, yet I feel this page is very underwhelming.

If you were exploring the Backrooms, you would want to power your portable electrical equipment, and you would presumably plug it into one of the outlets. All the different outlets would have different voltages and shapes, so it would be very hard to find the right plug outlet. This could be matter of life or death, because with no electrical devices you cannot access the General Public Database.

There could have been a section talking about how to successfully power your equipment such as sticking wires directly sockets, and talk about doing it safely.

Other things that could have been talked about drawing too much power from outlet, tripping power across sections of the level, maybe relating it to outages some levels experience, or trap outlets with extremely high voltages that are dangerous to plug into. These are just suggestions, but my point is that much more could have been done with this page.

I also don't like the idea of a transportable outlet that can output infinite energy. That is really overpowered.

Not really an issue, but I will mention this page does somewhat contradict Object 50.
