Chloe's Move to the Backrooms

01 Feb 2023 19:45

My idea is that a cat gains telepathic and telekenitic powers after she no-clips into the backrooms with her owners. She is now able to express her (rather annoying/interesting/funny) opinions. The main question throughout the parts is whether she and her owners can survive (and possibly escape) the Backrooms. It'll be a long series, but it would be fun to try something humorous for once.
