# ARENA

### Meghan Remis

rating: +18[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

---

The haunting vocals of hit debut single "adage" sent Meghan Remis, teen sensation from Philadelphia, to new heights. Through her jubilant dismissal of the common lifeline for celebrities, she was universally acclaimed for her relatable lyricism. After moving to L.A., Meghan Remis released ARENA, her debut EP, shortly after. with her combination of minimalist indie-folk rooted in pop undertones combined with her sheer lyricism, you'll be left feeling like a new person after your first listen. With hit singles like "melodrama" and "ARENA," alongside the melancholy, heart wrenching, soul crushing verses, you'll be surprised this all came from someone with so much youth.
