Of course, the details are these:

•They are mostly named levels, although they can also be named file, paradise and redacted if they have important, altered or unknown information.

•They have a varied appearance such as old (old or architecture from the past like 1890), current (like current structures) and new (half futuristic or completely futuristic).

•The names of each level never have quotes, those that are files, paradise and redacted have altered names (optional).

•The entrances and exits have more to do with the architecture of the level, for example: "You have to find a service door to enter Level 2" and "To exit, find a window that shows an office and enter it."

•Your "Security" table looks like this:

| Entities | Objects | Groups |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You can use the score (1/5) or a small piece of information. You can also name or add more to the table such as the exits or the level environment. | | |

•Only "Communities and Bases" are used.

•It is not mandatory but it is recommended that you always set the level discovery.
