**Level 8** is the 9th Level of the Backrooms. This level seems to be an infinite cave system with caverns and dangers around every corner.

# **Description:**

**Level 8** is made of huge caverns and small cave systems that twist and turn like normal underground systems. **Level 8** is very damp, with [Almond Water](object-1.md) flowing from the walls and ceiling. Stalactites and stalagmites appear to be very common in **Level 8**. Sounds echo throughout the Level, so it is relatively easy to listen for potential danger, or possibly even attract it if not careful. **Level 8** is normally devoid of natural light, but light sources from an unknown origin shine on the damp walls, making the Level slightly glow in select areas.

Vegetation appears rarely on **Level 8** in the form of dried vines and small shrubs. Large pools of [Almond Water](object-1.md) can be found around the level, which is not safe for consumption due to large amounts of minerals. Some pools that are found are made of the viscous tar-like substance, the same substance found in [Level 2](level-2.md) and [Level 7](level-7.md). Entering the goop will result in groups of humanoid hands covered in tar reaching out and grabbing the Wanderer, dragging them into the pool. Another variant of the pools is one made from distilled water, and entering one will lead the Wanderer to [Level 7](level-7.md). Small veins of ore exist within **Level 8**. This Level includes many types of ore minerals such as iron, copper, gold, and more. [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) have created public travel routes in the safe area of **Level 8** for safe travel for Wanderers.

Many dangerous Entities reside in **Level 8**, that being [Smilers](entity-3.md), [Skin-Stealers](entity-10.md), [Wretches](entity-15.md), [Camo Crawlers](entity-31.md)[1](javascript:;). [Transporters](entity-13.md), Male and Female [Deathmoths](archived__entity-4--35e09fc6.md), Hostile [Facelings](entity-9.md), [Hens](entity-80.md), [Hounds](entity-8.md), [Death Rats](entity-24.md), [Paralies](trimmed__entity-85--10570987.md), [Clumps](entity-5.md), [Reviooks](entity-14.md), [Wranglers](entity-75.md) and rare sightings of Stalkers. [Crawlers](entity-17.md) have been reported due to the vents of [Level 2](level-2.md) connecting to **Level 8**. [Windows](entity-2.md) have been reported to appear in **Level 8's** walls, however this claim has yet to be corroborated.

Due to the danger presented by the Entity known as [Wranglers](entity-75.md), it is highly advised to be aware of their abilities before traversing this Level. Above all, if any rumblings are heard or felt, evacuate the area immediately.

This report details an event that occurred that created massive changes to **Level 8**'s structure and public routes. A [Wrangler](entity-75.md) had died during No-Clip throughout **Level 8**. This Wrangler is the largest ever recorded, being approximately ninety miles in length. This caused many public routes to be blocked off by organic mass, and parts of caves collapsed and trapping Wanderers inside. The Teams of M.E.G. Regiment "Quick Match" have been dispatched to rescue as many Wanderers as possible. Here is an interview with a witness of this event:

During the mission of saving the Wanderers trapped in **Level 8** a discovery was found. Many new cave systems were uncovered after the event occurred. These systems go miles deep underground, and it is unknown how many new anomalies and Entities can reside there. Evidence shows that the [Wrangler](entity-75.md) that caused this event originated from these deeper systems. [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) will begin to traverse these new systems once every route is back to working condition.

# **Bases, Outposts, and Communities**

Setting up an outpost or community within **Level 8** is not advised. However, this does not mean it hasn't been done.

## [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) Outpost "Cave Raiders":

- This is an Outpost set up by [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) during 2015 and finished by 2016.
- This Outpost is tasked with terminating hostile Entities and mapping out routes for public transportation.
- This Outpost has fifty-four members.

## The Town of Kavragost:

- This now-defunct group was reportedly made up of an unknown species of vaguely humanoid Entities that have long since disappeared without a trace.
- This community was said to have seventy-eight members.
- These members lived in a remote area of **Level 8** which was not accessible to outsiders before its abandonment.
- The leader of this town was reportedly more welcoming to humans than the rest of the group.
- There is evidence to suggest that this group existed as a means of supplying other communities with unique minerals hidden deep within **Level 8**.
- These minerals appear to have also been used to create crude weapons.
- Members of the group are said to have been adept hunters, often skirmishing with the more hostile Entities roaming near the town.
- It is unclear what became of these Entities or this town, but there have been no signs of life within its walls since it was discovered.

# **Discovery:**

**Level 8** was discovered when The Explorers Group found its way to this Level in 2011. The Group had enumerated many encounters and discoveries until their disappearance in [Level 9](level-9.md). Since this discovery, many Wanderers traversed the dangerous caverns of **Level 8** until 2015, when [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) had set up an Outpost to ensure the safety of the Wanderers who decide to traverse the Level.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

### Entrances

- To enter, finding cave entrances in [Level 7](level-7.md) is the main way to enter **Level 8**.
- Walking through a dark hallway in [Level 103](level-103.md) will lead to **Level 8**.
- An as of yet undocumented exit in [Level 64](level-64.md) leads to **Level 8**.
- Jumping through the well in [level-135](trimmed__level-135--e2f606c5.md) can lead to **Level 8**
- Small tunnels in [Level 52](level-52.md) most often lead to **Level 8**.
- Crawling through The Tubes in [Level 283](level-283.md) will lead to a point where the multi-colored playground tubes transition into **Level 8's** small jagged rocky caves.

### Exits

- To exit, you can randomly fall through the floor of **Level 8** to enter [Level 9](level-9.md).
- Rare tiny silvery passages of **Level 8** lead to [level-75](trimmed__level-75--fc93a5d8.md).
- Vents can be found in some areas that can lead to [Level 2](level-2.md), but this is not advised.
- Entering the distilled water pools has been rumored to drag you back to [Level 7](level-7.md).
- Entering the tar pits in **Level 8** will cause you to be dragged into [Level 91](level-91.md) if you are lucky enough to survive. This is also not advised.
- No-Clipping through a corner of **Level 8** or a rare chance through exiting **Level 8** will bring you to [Level 93](level-93.md).
- No-clip through **Level 8**’s ceiling has a small chance to bring you to [level-205](trimmed__level-205--6732fe45.md).
- Finding random sets of old televisions and No-Clipping through them will lead you to [Level 104](level-104.md).
- Falling through the floor randomly can also rarely lead to [Level 69](level-69.md).
