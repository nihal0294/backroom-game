The Nullifier (Seeking GreenLight)

18 Apr 2022 06:12

Page Type: Level

Genre (Optional): Horror

Core Concept: Wanderers have minimal chances of falling into this Level. Level-Ø, or what its name suggests, is a level devoid of all concepts, devoid only until someone thinks of something within their mind. If a Wanderer manages to reach this floor, depending on what they are thinking inside of their head, if this Level ever felt one's thoughts, it would make that thought become a reality by creating an alternate universe to encase the said person.

The moment when you have already fallen into the trap of this Level. No imagination nor desires could get you out of that universe the Level created.
