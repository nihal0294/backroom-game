I did a little research, and I think I understand what the license plate means, at least the main part of it.

I think that it's a reference to the fact that there have been a number of unsolved disappearances on Interstate 80 in northern Nevada. Here's why I think that.

> Level 50, aptly named "The Moribund Highway" is a seemingly endless desert highway.

This is a very accurate description of the Nevada part of I-80.

> Notably, all vehicles have Nevada license plates

Mention of Nevada

> I80L2WV

The first three letters definitely mean I-80 (Interstate 80). The last four I'm less sure of. The L2 could maybe mean Lovelace which is the town nearest to the disappearance, but I'm not sure.

> it appears no vehicle models date past 2011, and some date back to 1978.

The first I-80 disappearance was in 1978 and the most recent were in 2011, as far as I can tell.

> Lastly reported, around five miles in, the explorer found a highway exit, exit 205

Exit 205 is the place on I-80 where at least 2 of the disappearance took place
