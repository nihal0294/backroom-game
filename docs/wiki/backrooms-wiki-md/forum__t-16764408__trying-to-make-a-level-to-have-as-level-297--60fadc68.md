A large issue that you'll face when trying to receive critique for this is that **this doesn't have enough length at current.** Now, there obviously isn't a minimum word count for your sandbox pages, but you should try to give your critic something to work with. Right now, you have an alright **central idea** but not much else. By central idea, I mean a couple sentences that explain the core of what your draft is going to be based around. So, just working with that, try and expand things more. If you want, **picture it as a tree**; add new ideas connected to your central one like branches. A big part of your central idea is that the level is a winery. Okay, you can work with that. Who made the winery? What group of people was the winery intended for? What thematic elements can a winery contribute to your writing? Your level is set in the 1900s, so some questions that could be asked are: Why is this an important detail? Is it just something meant to help tell the reader what your environment looks like? Do people conform to the time period of the space when they enter, speaking in Enligsh from the good old days and stuff like that? Does even technology go back in time, making things like cameras turn into their vintage counterparts? Why is there a Faceling here and why does it give out Almond Water? Does it take its craft to an extreme and brutalize anyone who doesn't appreciate his finer tastes? Do those who drink the Almond Water go and tell anyone else they meet about the level so that more and more people come here? **There's just a lot of different directions you can take this, so I wouldn't advise playing it so safe.** Write a lot more.
