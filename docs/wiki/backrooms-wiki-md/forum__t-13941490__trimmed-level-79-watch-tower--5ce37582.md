OHHH no that’s the application for joining the wiki. When it’s accepted, that means you can start commenting stuff. Check the [Greenlight Policy](greenlight-policy.md) for how to post a work.  
Editing is when you are given specific permission from the *author* when you largely rewrite it or adding components that completely change the article. The only time you are allowed to edit another’s work without permission is when there’s a minor SPaG issue. For example, say someone made a typo and put, “the,” as, “teh.” Unless all others are like that typo(meaning it was purposely put there by the author), then you can change *only* that and put a note explaining what you did.
