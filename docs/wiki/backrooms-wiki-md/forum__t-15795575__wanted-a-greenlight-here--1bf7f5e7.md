It still needs a good copy edit. For example:

> The only known entity in this level is The Gift. The Gift is a invisible entity with a 3.3 meters tall and can only be seen using a night vision goggle. The entity will be always stares at a person and have a very bright eyes. The current body form of The Gift is currently unknown, but it is presumed to be that of a 34-year-old male with tentacles for arms. The Gift will give anything they want, but you can only ask 10 times. The Gift will be visible if a person request the entity, or is asked to show himself.

I would rewrite:

> The only known entity on this level is The Gift. The Gift gives a person almost anything they ask for, but will do so only 10 times. It is an invisible entity 3.3 meters tall and can only be seen with night vision goggles. The Gift will make itself visible if asked to. When visible (or viewed with night vision goggles), The Gift has the body of a 34-year old male with tentacles for arms, and always stares at the person with very bright eyes.

(This could be improved upon, but I think all will agree this rewrite flows much better.)
