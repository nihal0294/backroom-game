Level 207 - "Sprouts of Wonder"

17 Dec 2022 07:55

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre:** Nostalgia

**Core Concept:**

- A large row of hallways that are painted in many different colors and flowers.
- Separated into two areas, one habitable and the other infested with entities.
- Flowers in the dark zone can suck your almond water out of your bottles or body.
- A group of Wanderers use the native "ocularists" to find other wanderers in danger in exchange for a childhood memory.
- Connected to [Level 165](level-165.md)
