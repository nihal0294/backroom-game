  # Liberation

  ---

  ![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-997/Liberation-Eagle)

  Liberation

  ‎  
  **One of the faction of the Reverence, the Liberation was founded on the purpose of eradicating all groups and communities of humans, by bringing chaos to those who think they own this place.**

  **We have been convinced by the Reverence and their ideologies : a world without factions, a world without conflict where humans don't fight for their meaningless interests, a world where nature and its creations rule above all, where no humans dare to call this place home, where wilderness owns these paradises now spoiled by human exploitation; exactly as in the past. Our ultimate mission is to restore the world to the way it was before humans arrived.**

  **They don't know yet, but we are planting our seeds, and when comes the harvest day, all they can do is watch them grow, as chaos will bring true liberation in this place we call the Backrooms.**

  **Nature is claiming for its due, and we're going to give it back.**

  **We will return to the past.**
