(I critted 292 because seeing u think it is in greenlight territory, I was just checking it. This is all my opinion as a reader, so use all of it, completely disregard it, who cares.)

Hi, Unhacked!

Bcs I see you've been pretty active with critting stuff purdy thoroughly, I should return the favor 😁.

> Level 292 can be described as an cold

**an -> a**

> Stay safe out there.

**Imo, this seems unnecessary and breaks your clinical tone. Either delete it, or further emphasize the dangers.**

> Making any of the above is impossible

**Took me a sec to realise what "the above" were. Change this to "bases, outposts, or communities", for better clarity.**

> will guarantee you wind up in this level

**I don't know about this… "wind up" sounds more playful and relaxed, contradicting your clinical tone.**

> a utopia that you will never forget.

**This is simply an opinion of mine but adding "{{text}}" would make it look better.**  
a utopia you will never forget. {{a utopia you will never forget}}  
**v.s.**  
a utopia you will never forget.

> ### **Your Civilization**
>
> Anything that you want them to be

**Either pluralize "Civilization" or change "them" to "it".**

> SUB-LEVEL FOUND

**I understand where u were going, but I would flesh this out a bit more. Instead of a little collapsible, how abt an interview log of some sort? As you obviously change the tone here, why not use a classic scenario where the MEG are talking, and stumble into this, or get hacked, if that is what u were going for.**

> FYI, PNGs don't work when trying to put them on an article. Convert these to an actual site link first. An easy way is by adding the images to the page's files, and then pasting *that* link in the image box. It should work.

Alrighty. Now that I've gotten SPaG stuff outta the way, here's what I think of ur article in general, along with some suggestions.

I thought that the slide was a decent concept, but if it is *infinite* then why can I *restart it?* This should be explained. Next, it is kinda unoriginal. When opening your "sub-level file", all I could see was yet *another* copy of level 404. This may not have been intended, but the whole "*stay here, it's safe, all your imagination* bla bla bla, just doesn't cut it now. Try branching out your creative mindset! You can **totally** base this off of that idea, just give it something to make it stand out, or something that I wouldn't expect. Plus, using this idea gives me cheesy "I'm a level whose sentient 'you're safe" vibes.

You also cut this thing pretty dang short. Add some more details! What do you mean by a "glitched ocean"? What is the sub-level about? What other scenery can you see? Can you explore the scenery? Do you spawn on the slide, and if so, how can you go multiple times? I would love to see at least 1 or 2 of these get answered, and more!

I'll prolly crit ur other 2 pages tmrw, but for now, I think its good. Of course, this is all constructive criticism, and I hope to see your level take some steps in the greenlight-able direction soon!
