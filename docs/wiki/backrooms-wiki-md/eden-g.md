## Description:

Eden G. was a former Head Field Researcher of the M.E.G. and close friend of Former Senior Archivist [A. River](a-river.md). Eden was last heard from in a call with Overseer B, which is logged on this database entry.

Eden was a light-skinned woman of Japanese-American descent. All pictures of her that were taken before her trip to heaven have since been lost, so her exact details are only documented through written reports. She had short pink hair, and would often be seen wearing traditional clothing and hairstyles. She was an entity expert, and even learned how to communicate with certain peaceful entities after intense study. She was promoted to Head Field Researcher after teaching multiple [Facelings](entity-9.md) how to use sign language, which she was greatly congratulated for. If not on a mission, she would spend most of her spare time studying in the [Cygnus Archive](level-906.md).

Eden was one of the main field mission leaders. She would often lead missions focused on entity documentation. She always had a specific philosophy and personality that made her feel trusted by the entities she studied. While many see entities as monsters, Eden always looked at them as her equal — even the dangerous “murder-monsters” of the Backrooms. She resided on [Level 11](level-11.md) before her trip to heaven. She would often be seen going on research missions with A. River, and would spend lots of time with hir outside of missions as well.

A log is attached below of Eden’s final documented communication with The M.E.G.

  Another possible identity of Eden was spotted one week after the Level 5 sighting on the wheat fields of Level 10. The M.E.G. was in the middle of conducting a field research mission to attempt exploration beyond the main path. In the middle of this mission, a small child of seemingly Middle Eastern descent approached the group of agents. Images of the child were captured by the body camera which was being used by one of the agents for the exploration mission. The agents attempted to interact with the child, but the child did not respond to any sort of stimuli. It only stared at the agents blankly.

  The agents proceeded to continue the exploration mission while the child followed them. After about 30 minutes, the child finally spoke. In later interviews, the agents only described the voice as “otherworldly”. The child spoke softly and only said, “I know what happened.” The agents turned and attempted to ask the child what it meant. It ignored the questions and only responded with, “Tell Kat that she caused this.” After this, the child turned around and left the agents behind. They attempted to follow it, only to see that it had disappeared.
