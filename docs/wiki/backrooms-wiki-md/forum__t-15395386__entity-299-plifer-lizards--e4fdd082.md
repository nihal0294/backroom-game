I think the idea s pretty cool and the entity is definitely dangerous. I do have a couple of suggestions, but not too big.

- I'm not quite sure how they would forget to breathe. Perhaps it's due to some form of brain degeneration or the Wanderer entering a vegetative state.
- For the effects of looking at the young lizards, perhaps you could say **"Surviving Wanderers have reported feeling an extreme ecstasy when viewing a juvenile Plifer Lizard for more than 5 seconds."**

I think the idea is great overall.
