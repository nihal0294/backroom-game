We've noticed an increase in suspicious activity from the M.E.G. recently. Initially, we suspected their “Trial Testers” division, but it would appear that a more far-reaching threat is developing within the organization. According to reports from our masked agents, the suspected M.E.G. members can be recognized by their badges and the black military uniforms they all share. Apparently, they are a division of the M.E.G.'s secret services; a certain Task Force “Sierra”. We have also identified two major suspects, believed to be their superiors.

**Appearance :** Overseer S, whose real name is Richard Simmons Delacroix, is a Franco-American male in his 40s, and 1.78 m tall. He is, however, systematically referred to by his code name: “S”, since only a few peoples know his real name. He has black and gray hair, his left eye is dark gray and he has a robotic eye in place of his right eye, the lens is an almost fluorescent purple and it's likely that he acquired it even before he ended up in the Backrooms. He is a mystery as it's hard to predict what he might be up to or how he might react. As for his attire, he's always been seen wearing an all-black suit and tie, including his shirt. Over it, he wears a long trenchcoat, also black, decorated with the M.E.G. Overseers insignia.

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-997/Team-Sierra)

A badge belonging to the Task Force "Sierra"

**Description :** The perfect archetype of: “By any means necessary”. Richard joined the M.E.G. in November 2021 and currently holds the position of Overseer S, who is in charge of the M.E.G.'s black operations. As a result, he has set up his own response team, named “Task Force Sierra”, made up of over-trained elite operators with military backgrounds, all devoted to him. Overseer S has earned the trust of the M.E.G. thanks to his outstanding intelligence and the fact that he has never failed a single operation under his leadership. Reinforced by his composed and cold-blooded nature, he has forged an unmatched charisma which has allowed him to quickly rise through the ranks within the M.E.G. Overseer S is also known to be ruthless and will lose all notion of empathy for the sake of the Operation, which is often the subject of controversy, even within the Overseers Circle. However, his honor and reputation were always left untouched.

**Background :** We have not been able to trace anything before his position within the M.E.G.

**Appearance :** Chinatsu Mutsuki is a Japanese woman of Russian origins, she is only 20 years old and is 1m67 tall. She has silver hair, probably due to a hereditary disease, and blue eyes. Her expression is generally cold and emotionless. Finally, she's easily recognizable by her all-white outfit, consisting of a shirt and a tailored skirt with a lab coat decorated with a gold M.E.G. badge.

**Description :** A genius scientist who has made many discoveries for the M.E.G.; some good, some bad. Indeed, during our investigation of her case, we discovered that she played a major role in the [object 24](object-24.md) researches. She may seem innocent at first glance, but deep inside, an uncontrollable thirst for knowledge corrupts her “pure” heart. Mutsuki will use any means at his disposal without hesitation or remorse, no matter how many human subjects are needed for the sake of his experiments. We don't know how long she's had a relationship with Overseer S, but it seems she obeys him strictly and even calls him “Father”. The reason for this is yet to be determined, but we believe this information would be crucial to our future investigations.

**Background :** We found out she had relationships with [Blanche](entity-140.md) prior to the M.E.G. Here is a transcription of our discussion with Blanche:

**Blanche** : Chinatsu Mutsuki… Of course I remember! How could I forget such a person. The first time we met, about 5 years ago, she was still a teenager. She'd landed in my library and seemed lost between the endless shelves of books, but she was very interested in them. So I asked her to become my student, and she gladly accepted. Like a shining star, illuminating this empty world, I saw an incomparable wisdom and innocence in her. Deep down, she wanted to know the truth. The truth of this world full of mystery. Her thirst for knowledge never ceased to impress me. I enjoyed her presence every day, every hour, every minute and every second. She had a remarkable enthusiasm, which is quite rare here. Until one day, with apologies and thanks, she decided to leave the library because she wanted to use her knowledge for the common good of her own species. I so hope there would be more people like her… Besides… I miss her. I haven't heard from her in 2 years. Do you have any idea what happened to her?

  
Telling him the truth was probably a bad idea, but a necessary evil. After telling her what Mutsuki was really up to, she couldn't believe a word we said and systematically refused to listen to the horrible reality. In the end, for everyone's sake, she gently kicked us out of her library…
