The Audacity.

23 Feb 2024 22:13

Vince stared dully at the screen, suppressing a yawn. *Did they really have to put me on database maintenance? There's already a dozen others on this and those [Partyfucks](entity-67.md) can't be doing that much damage.*  
*Well, if you really don't want me to read about "[The Numbed Man](entity-50.md)" I guess I won't.*  
*[Heralds of Rust](entity-51.md)…sounds cool. Hmm…nope, no smileys.*  
*[Grave Guardian](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-52-grave-guardian)…Oh, that is a gruesome picture. Should probably censor that. Not the worst shit I've seen, but-*  
"Wait, what the fuck?"

> # Don't:
>
> - Fall victim to this Entity.
> - Look at the screen after twenty-one minutes.
> - Attempt to confront this Entity alone.
> - Be a grave robber. The audacity. They got what they deserved.

*Okay, is someone messing with me?*  
He highlighted the final line and deleted it.  
*"Actually, no."*  
Vince whirled around, drawing his knife while leaping out of his chair.  
*"Put that scrap metal down."*  
There was a [man in a blue suit](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-52-grave-guardian) standing there, leaning on a silver trident with its prongs driven through the ground-the *concrete ground*. His chest was a mangled mess of blood-oozing puncture wounds. One eye was closed, and the other was fixed on Vince, shining blood-red.  
The door to the room slammed shut.  
Vince scrambled back, feeling his heart pounding in his chest and scrabbling for the wall. *Just break the glass and pull out the pistol, pull out the-*  
The man just stood there, smiling gently, as Vince stabbed through the glass, pulled the gun from its rack, and fired twice. Two bullets carved smoking holes through the man's stomach. Another four flattened against his face and dropped harmlessly to the ground.  
"What the fuck are you!?"  
The man just yawned and strode towards the terminal, yanking the trident out and planting it in the ground like a cane as he went. Vince scrambled away to the opposite corner of the room, the gun slipping from his hand and dropping to the floor.  
*"Quite impressive, what [your people](the-m-e-g.md) have accomplished. Just look at this database. Spectacular, truly!"*  
The man leaned down and hit *ctrl-z.* The line Vince had deleted reappeared.  
Vince's hand latched onto the doorknob, twisted, and pushed. The door didn't budge.  
The man turned at the click. *"I'm who you call the Grave Guardian, if you wanted that question answered."*  
The two regarded each other for what felt like an eternity.  
Vince swallowed. "Are you-"  
***CRACK***  
He flinched, hands going to his ears. The man simply regarded his left hand with an annoyed expression. His thumb was hanging at an odd angle. *"Go on."*  
"Are you going to kill me?"  
The man looked him in the eye. *"Possibly."*  
*Oh, FUCK. FUCK FUCK FUCK.*  
"W-why are you here?"  
*"Well, I was here to restore my statement after you wiped it, but now we're having such a nice conversation. I don't have much reason to leave yet. It's been so long since I got to talk to anyone."*  
Vince backed away more. "O-okay. Okay! What do you want to talk about?"  
*"Well, how about this. You do love to document your 'Entities'. You may consider this an interview."*  
The man pulled an old cell phone out of his pocket and dropped it on the floor, then kicked it across the floor. *"Record this, if you wish."*  
Fingers shaking, Vince picked up the phone and hit '[record](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/interview-52)'.
