> \*\*Level 444 is the 445th

Add two more asterisks (\*) after "444" to actually bold your level.

> the 445th Level of the Backrooms.

"Level" shouldn't be capitalized here since it's not a proper noun.

> Office space in Level 444

I believe this was intended to be a caption for an image, though that image isn't currently present. Remove it as it currently has no place here.

> and the 2nd most dangerous level

Alright, just straight up remove this. There isn't a quantitative scale on how dangerous levels are so this is a very arbitrary claim, and there are also many levels with a higher SD class than this one.

> dangerous level of the backrooms.

Capitalize "backrooms" since that is a proper noun.

> It’s infinite and it is more dangerous than Level 3

Eh, I don't like comparing aspect's to other levels (excluding very specific cases) I'd try and cut all of that out of the level.

> than Level 3 due it having

Change "it' to "to" in this instance.

> has nearly all the Common Entities in

"Common" and "Entities" shouldn't be capitalized here.

> Entities in The Backrooms.

"The" shouldn't be capitalized here.

> look like level 0 but

"level 0" should be capitalized; also, this was already stated???? Remove this, no need to repeat it again.

> level 0 but its a bit tweaked

Change "its" to "it's" since "its" is the possessive term.

> a bit tweaked, like trap rooms, all the rooms

Change this first comma to a semicolon. (;)

> lead to The End and some

Add a link to the level.

> in the backrooms. Also,

Capitalize "backrooms" here.

> Resources are very sparse. Food is impossible

Change the period to a semicolon. (;)

> find and almond water has

"Almond Water" no longer exists on this wiki; remove references of it from your page.

> to be quieter than level 0’s infamous

Capitalize "level 0" here.

> Hallucinations and insanity are common due

"sanity/insanity" is no longer a mechanic used on the wiki. Please remove all mentions of it from your page.

> No major colonies have

"colony" is no longer a term used on the wiki; please remove all mentions of it from your page.
