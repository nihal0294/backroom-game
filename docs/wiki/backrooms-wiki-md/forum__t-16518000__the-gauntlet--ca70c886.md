15 Dec 2023 14:33

The level is itself quite challenging, as you will need to either make it to the exit or fight other anomalies in an arena that resembles the Spartan combat style. The arena has been overrun by monsters and humans, which creates a violent outpost, that is meant to be a statement on human nature.

To complete the level, you will need to engage in combat, and if you are unable or unwilling to do so, a volunteer will replace you. However, if the volunteer dies, you will also die, which adds to the thrill and excitement of the game.

The level offers rewards for completing it, such as Almond Water, food, and weapons, which can be quite helpful in future levels. You will have time to prepare before the level starts, and weapons and armor will be provided to you to ensure that you are adequately equipped to handle the challenges that come your way.
