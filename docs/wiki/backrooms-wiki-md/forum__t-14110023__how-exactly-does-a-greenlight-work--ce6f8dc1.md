This level resembles a very large apartment building with unlimited floors. Kind of like level 132 but more modern, with a lot more entities. These entities are mostly facelings, but partygoers can get in, but are usually terminated right away. The facelings are very human like and friendly unless you break "the code of conduct". Which then they will go crazy and try their best to get to you. They will not harm you if you try to exit, but if you try to exit by harming the apartments in some way they will become hostile. if you hear anything in your apartment just press the red button near your bed and the entity will be terminated. failure to do so will result in consequences. You arent allowed to knock on a facelings door or interact with them in any way while they are in their respected apartments. A few humans reside here but they are rather hard to find. You are allowed to call anyone in the apartment complex by pressing their floor and room number on the telephone near the desk of your apartment.

# Code Of Conduct

1. You Cannot interact with Facelings while they are in their apartments.

2.You cannot interact with a Faceling for over 5 minutes.

3.You have to press the red button if an entity is in your apartment.

This is all we know about the "code of conduct" at the moment but more info is currently being discovered.

# Colonies and outposts

## The Employees

They wear a white shirt with a tag that says "Employee". Facelings that live there wear black shirts with gray sweats at all times, anyone who wears anything different is most likely a human.

## Front Desk

The people who work at the front desk act neutral towards humans and are willing to help with your apartment or assist you with any other issue, and will give you a small cup of almond water if you need. To call the front desk dial 404e into the telephone.

# Entities

### Facelings

### Partygoers

If partygoers get in this level they are usually terminated right away, if somehow they aren't dial the front desk and they should be gone immediately.

### Exits:

Hi! I'm quite interested in this Level concept. However, there are few things I think would help you a lot.

- About the Greenlighting process, you first and foremost must have your draft in a [Sandbox](index.md). Once you have that, you can contact Staff or Greenlighters via the [Forums](forum__c-6898687__help__draft-critique--555ceb34.md), pming them (contacts found [here](meet-the-staff.md)), or even going on the [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/2fXuFbB). From there, someone will be able to give you critique and eventually after fixing up your draft, you'll be given the Greenlight to post. However, since you're already here, I'll be happy to critique this for you!

- I'd love it if you could explain what the apartments look like in-depth. The thing you'd want is not to compare them to similar Levels, but to make it stand out from them. What do the rooms look like? Is there a lobby? I of course wouldn't mean to over explain something, but giving good detail would help tremendously in showing readers just what makes this Level important.

- On top of that, what makes the Partygoers come here? Is there a Party Room of sorts? A mini lobby for them? What are the signs that show that Partygoers are in a certain room? Adding more information on that will show that the Partygoers actually have a station of sorts here, rather than a simple, normal addition for fear factor.

- About the humans who live there: who are they? I suggest writing a little bit about them within the "Colonies and Outposts" section to further give this article more depth.

- I can see the Level is meant to be a Level Class 2. Here's the code for it!

As a first, remove the mentioning of colonies as that is a term that is no longer allowed. I would also organize the main paragraph describing the level to help break up information.

I like the idea of a code of conduct, but the level itself lacks details that I feel could bring more depth into the level. An idea I had is about the behavior of the entities and which entities are present on the level. Given that you said partygoers do not survive for a long period of time on this level, why? Do the Facelings kill them? Do the partygoers not have enough resources to survive?

Other details could also be added to the environment. What does the apartment look like? What are its faculties? Do these faculties play into the level's anomalous properties? I'm saying this because it seems that you're going for an overall unsettling theme here. My rule of thumb is this: explain as much as you need to so that the reader can come to their own conclusion without them being unable to get anything out of it. Like a meal, you don't want your guest to become too full or become too famished by the end of their meal.

Lastly, since the formatting/guide is going to be changed, there are many different ways this can be relayed without a stereotypical formatting. Perhaps it's from the perspective of one of the entities, or a wanderer passing through?
