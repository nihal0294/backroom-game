Reluctant -1 (sorry, kinda)

16 Dec 2023 20:58

To start with the positives, this page is much much better than the Roblox one. Sure, I downvoted both, but downvotes are restrictively binary and annoying and the only way I can elaborate the nuance of my decisions is here. The actual look of the level is better, I s’pose, as much as some word choices are odd (saying “spending time in the sun” in the page is very colloquial and advert-like, for example), and some of it is kinda clunky in that the information being given doesn’t always flow well into the next point. Particularly the very start. And the second audio log bit is, I think, good. If I were to pick it apart, I’d question why someone is being a bit nosy to someone and recording them for so long, and the dialogue is a little eh, but I was drawn in with the surprisingly descriptive parts to set the scene. I liked that. Did it do Gaius and River good? Who fucking knows, I don’t know or care about the character. I feel what it did was fine though, as much as it maybe dragged a bit towards the end :/ but hey, that’s okay.

However, this level is… underwhelming, to say the least. And has a major logic hole which I’ll bring up at the end. This level, in terms of what it *is*, is boring. It’s a nothing place, just a basic and dull liminal space with some vague weirdness. It’s not a place that has any unique theming or ideas except for the fact that Nostalgi Gaius stayed there momentarily. Now, I like the implications from the first log that people lived here and Gaius’ very presence caused them to scatter, but without any idea on what the level itself was like before it’s ultimately shallow. This place is nothing, so it’s very obvious from the start that something else is going to substitute it. This can work, take my Level 82 for example, but here it’s different because there’s no state that the level used to be like, it’s just dull.

As well as that, it’s very tropey! It has the format screw idea which makes no logical sense if you think about how page editing works for even a second, the stuff with Gaius is ultimately very minimal (just one thing where ze talks to hirself and River for a bit in a mirror), and the first audio log sets up the people that used to be there but does nothing with it. It doesn’t imply anything beyond what it’s actually said because of how basic it is. There’s tents and a general old little base, the camerawoman doesn’t detail much beyond that to my memory.

So, this level put a lot of its time into the wrong areas basically. The whole level is clearly meant to be for the bit at the end. It’s probably why I like it, I can feel the inspiration from it! But… the rest is filler, and padding. Paragraphs of paragraphs of basic content farm equivalent fodder to get to the bit that was inspired. This actually leads me to my logic hole, actually! …why is this page altered in any way?

Like yeah, I get it, Nostalgi is le spooky bad who can edit into the database. But… why would ze do it *here*? This is, from what I can tell, just somewhere ze was at for a bit. It’s not a home, it’s not a page about her, it’s not a major moment in her life, it’s an incidental moment. Those edits had to come from *somewhere*, they wouldn’t just appear, but then that also implies a degree of importance to this level which it very obviously doesn’t have because it’s not important to the Gaius storyline.

The above point is why I call the actual level *filler* and *fodder*, because it’s going through the motions. Besides the link to the YouTube video, everything follows the mould without deviating in any way, leading to logic holes and a pretty boring level. Like, this could’ve taken place in any other outdoors, semi-urban level and have someone finding the glitched areas. Hell, then you could give some character to the cameraman too!

Idk, I can’t upvote this just because of a good end bit, and the mehness of the rest of it sways me to a downvote. It’s an improvement over the Roblox level, yes, but we’re not quite in the good realm yet
