Searching Greenlight for Dereck Doheny (POI)

09 Jan 2022 07:21

I have been writing this for months now but it was little by little that I did not put the truth high priority. I left it unedited and seeing that it seemed my creativity was not going to give me something to enrich it, I have decided to leave it for greenlight.  
My English is not my first language so excuse me if it sounds weird or very like a google translator.  
It will sound silly but I can't quite figure out how to put a description on the photos so I put them inside a shabby table, if someone can explain me how to put a text on the photo I would appreciate it.
