No Shame in Being Popular

28 May 2024 16:46

I would love everyone reading this to take a deep breath, let it out real slow, and brace themselves for the wild ride ahead. Summer is coming for many of us; **new posting rules** on this Wiki are imminent for **all** of us. This might mark the dawn of a new Backrooms era.

To be honest, I write for the casual reader, who is not looking for Shakespeare, but is looking for a **brief** and **enjoyable** read that sparks the imagination and, perhaps, sends a chill down the spine.

Let’s all support one other, from **contributors** old and new, to our **staff** and **Greenlighters**, and **everyone else** whose unpaid toil makes this virtual place shine. We need to focus on and appreciate those who will soon visit us for the first time, both curious and eager to add to our amazing lore.

**Let’s roll out the welcome mat.**

Your friend and colleague,
