Welcome to the hub of hubs! This is a list of all pages with the "[hub](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/hub)" tag organized into the following sections: Categories, Canons, Classification, Contests, Groups, Series, Translations, and Other.

This list was created and is regularly maintained by the wiki's staff team, but if you have created a new hub, and you want to add it yourself, you are free to do so. If you are not sure in which section you should add your hub, you can also contact a [staff member](meet-the-staff.md) and we will gladly do it for you.
