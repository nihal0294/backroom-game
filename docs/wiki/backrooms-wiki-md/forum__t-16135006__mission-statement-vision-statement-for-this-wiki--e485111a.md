Mission Statement & Vision Statement for this Wiki

06 Jul 2023 22:15

Mission:  
**To win the hearts and minds of readers and authors wandering the Backrooms.**

Vision:  
**Giving a diversity of aspiring minds their opportunity to help create the Backrooms of their dreams and nightmares.**

Mission and Vision Statements offer focused benefits to understanding a business or organization. These statements explain why something exists and how it plans to achieve its goals. **They unleash the power of purpose!**
