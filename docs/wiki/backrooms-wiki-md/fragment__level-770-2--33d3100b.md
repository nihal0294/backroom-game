After we failed to convince the M.E.G. executive board about both Overseer-S and Senior Researcher Mutsuki's suspicious activities, we can't rely on the M.E.G. anymore. So, we decided to shift to a more physical approach. The secret organization run by Overseer-S should absolutely not expand any further. Each hour, each minute, each second that lapses is a threat to the whole of humanity.

We formed an unexpected coalition with The [Eyes of Argos](the-eyes-of-argos.md), who are taking the case more seriously. They share the same interests as us and the same disregard toward Overseer-S. The Eyes of Argos are the ones that retrieved and gathered information about Overseer-S' organization, referred to as "Liberation", and its members.

Until now, Overseer-S always had a step further of us but we are determined to turn the tables.

The Enforcer

**Full Name :** Elizabeth Graves

**Codename :** "201"; "The Enforcer"

**Status :** Alive

**Affiliation :** [M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md) (Team Sierra)

**Last known location :** [Level 4](level-4.md) (M.E.G base Omega)

**Charge(s) :** Conspiration, Multiple war crimes

**Threat Level :** 4/5 [SENSIBLE]

---

**Appearance :** Commander Elizabeth Graves, the leader of the M.E.G. "Team Sierra" is a 32 years old English woman and 1,75m tall. She has one blue eye and has relatively short black hair stopping at her shoulders. The most noticeable features about her is her black eyepatch on the left eye and her robotic left arm allowing here to carry an absurd amount of weights as well as dealing powerful blows. She is dressed like all the other Team Sierra members but usually carries an imposing double-barrel heavy machine gun, apparently modified for both entities and armored targets extermination. Approaching her carelessly would result in a death sentence if not prepared…

**Description :** *"The concept of empathy is understood by her, but irrelevant…"*  
If we rely on the little information we got about her and her records, we determined that she was the most ruthless and cruel member of Liberation, even more than Overseer-S…

On the public side, she is Overseer-S' personal bodyguard and the leader of Team Sierra. She and her team are the ones that conduct the M.E.G. black operations such as interrogations through psychological and physical torture as well as hitmen tasks, spying and counterintelligence operations. According to a certain record, she didn't hesitate burning to death the entire family of a C.B.S. scientist right in front of him after he lied during an interrogation… Other records involve multiple war crimes that makes her even more despicable than the U.E.C.

Behind the scenes, she is serving as "The Enforcer" of Liberation, seemingly performing the same function as "Team Sierra" does within the M.E.G. side but for different interests.

**Background :** By digging into her classified records, we found out she lost her eye and left arm after an encounter with unknown rogue M.E.G. operatives. A knife was stuck in her left eye and her left arm had to be amputated due to severe blood loss from her arm. It is mentioned a Backrooms Robotics member found her and decided to help her by giving her a robotic arm. Unfortunately, the damage caused in her left eye was too important for a robotic eye to work. This led her to have a strong grudge against the M.E.G. although the rogue operatives isn't something the M.E.G. could control.

Even though she always wanted to stay away from the Groups of Interests and prefered exploring the backrooms by herself, Elizabeth ended up joining the M.E.G. in March 2022, in hope to take revenge from the inside. She was assigned to Team Jack, one of the teams belonging to the "Entity Removal" Regiment, the Wild Warriors. While attempting to sabotage the M.E.G.'s base Omega, she was caught off guard by Overseer-S who, instead of reporting her to the M.E.G. leadership, took her under his wing and ended up founding his own personal team in October 2022, with Elizabeth Graves as commander.

We believe her initial motive was strongly influenced by Overseer-S with her grudge now extending to all humans groups and communities.

---

**Conclusion :** With the attentive look of Madam Evangeline Holmes and [REDACTED] from the Eyes of Argos, Elizabeth Graves should be taken down as soon as possible. Her position makes it clear that she plays a huge role in Liberation's schemes. However, she is considered extremely dangerous and always sticks with Overseer-S wherever he goes. We're waiting for the perfect opportunity to eliminate her, and if possible, Overseer-S at the same time.

Jacqueline Guzman, *Leader of sub-sector Volto*

The Shepherd

**Full Name :** Andrei Defalco

**Codename :** "301"; "The Shepherd"

**Status :** Alive

**Affiliation :** [H.D.F](u-e-c-team-homelanders.md) (6th Battalion)

---

**Appearance :** Major Andrei Defalco, the leader of the H.D.F. 6th battalion was a male native American from Utah before he ended up in the Backrooms. He is 57 years old and 1,88m tall. He has short white hair and red eyes probably because he wears lenses. His uniform is comprised of a red beret, black military pants, and a long black leather trench coat with a U.E.C. armband and all sorts of military decorations. Under which he has a red shirt topped with a black military jacket with a belt, tightening it all together. Judging by the only few records we were able to obtain of him, he remains calm and composed in any situation he may be involved in, which makes him a highly respected leader. One thing we could also notice is that Major Andrei Defalco and Senior Researcher Chinatsu Mutsuki are the only "sane" members of Liberation.

**Description :** Because of our poor relationship with the U.E.C. in general, information about him is very limited. We even attempted to hack into their servers but the U.E.C. themselves haven't filled in any relevant information about him.

On the public side, he is the leader of the H.D.F. sixth battalion. However, we learned that he is secretly "The Shepherd" of the Liberation : the leader of a certain "Team Salvation" that we never knew existed until now. Apparently, Team Salvation would be equivalent to a state's army, conducting war operations and territory defense tasks.

**Background :** It is highly likely that he already got a military carreer before he ended up in the Backrooms. And, we assume he joined Liberation after the multiple interactions between Overseer-S and the U.E.C.

The Reaper

**Full Name :** Unknown

**Codename :** "401"; "Team Leader 401"; "The Reaper"

**Status :** Terminated

**Affiliation :** [B.N.T.G.](b-n-t-g.md)

**Last known location :** [Level 11](level-11.md) (Eyes of Argos annex building)

**Charge(s) :** Conspiration

**Threat Level :** 3/5 [SEVERE]

---

**Appearance :** All we know about him is that he was a Russian male in his 30s. And that he had the annoying tendency of partially speaking Russian on top of his poor English.

**Description :** "Team Leader" was the only name related to him. This is a title he gained during his days within the B.N.T.G. that he later reused within Liberation. No one even within the B.N.T.G. knew a single thing about him, not even his real name. On top of his borderline sadistic demeanor, his efficiency and fearsome charisma made him one of the best traders of the B.N.T.G., so nobody even dared to ask him questions. However, we do know that he belonged to Overseer-S' organization and that he was the leader of a certain "Team Sapphire", apparently in charge of resource exploitation, under the name of "The Reaper".

**Background :** With Overseer-S's vast network, it's not surprising that he's managed to rally a high-ranking member of the B.N.T.G. to his cause.

---

**Confrontation :** With the help of the Eyes of Argos, my team led by one of my most talented agents, Chifuyu Konosage, planned an ambush near [Level 11.1](level-11-1.md) not long after "The Reaper" exited a building belonging to an *unknown group*. His guards were easily dealt with but Team Leader 401 himself was a pain to deal with. He was immune to any of our melee attacks and gunshots from the Eyes of Argos. The reason of his near immortality is still unknown and we suffered several losses from both The Eyes of Argos and us. But luckily we managed to knock him out with a sleeping gas bomb. After all that fuss, they brought him to an annex building owned by the Eyes of Argos on Level 11 for interrogation. Please refer to the following [document](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-770/offset/3) for more details.

---

**Conclusion :** God damnit! They tricked us… Before blowing up everyone including himself, this "Reaper" psycho mentioned something about the reverence, God and Judgement Day. Until now, we only heard rumors about "the reverence", a group of crazy fanatics who wish to return to the past… In other words, they want to bring the End and eradicate all groups and communities of humans, leaving humanity at the mercy of the Backrooms environment and entities. And it seems they've finally decided to act. Liberation; Reverence, there is something going on between them… All of that makes sense now!

The fact that Overseer-S, Senior Researcher Mutsuki, and Commander Graves still occupy their position within the M.E.G. as if nothing is happening makes me sick. Well, now they lost one of their key members from the B.N.T.G., but that's nothing to celebrate compared to the losses we've suffered in this confrontation. Rest in peace sisters. We will never give up our fight against these monsters, I promise. We'll pull out the roots of corruption one by one, until the mask drops.

Jacqueline Guzman, *Leader of sub-sector Volto*
