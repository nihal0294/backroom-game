I'm not that good at English, so there are probably some mistakes there (nor am I a good critic).

Okay, here's something for you…

[I'm glad to know that the Prank Levels section still lives.](javascript:;)

[I don't know...](javascript:;)

These are the things that are a little wrong for me, so correct me if I'm wrong.

> Jon Willis was instructed to, roughly, draw what he saw. He was given 5 minutes and a digital drawing tool. Unfortunately, he forgot to save his drawing, and it was never added to this article. Great! (I hate this)

Remove the comma before "roughly" and add a period at the end.

> There are ways to exit the level, but it is impossible to remember them, due to EVERYONE we sent to this GOD FORBIDDEN level FORGETTING right before they tell us! I’m so sick of this! Scrap it all, man! We don’t need to know this one! God, I hate this article! Why did we need to write this anyway?

Eliminate the comma before "man" and change the question to "?!" or "‽", would be the same.

You'd be better off adding a little more text if you're open-minded, but that's just an idea. Also, making me laugh a little is difficult.

Slight corrections:

> Remove the comma before "roughly" and add a period at the end.

Remove the comma after "roughly" as well.

> Eliminate the comma before "man" and change the question to "?!" or "‽", would be the same.

You can keep the comma before man since "man" is offset from the sentence and is adressing the reader or whoever the speaker is adressing.  
As for the punctuation, you don't really need to change it for *that sentence* since it isn't a question and an exclamation. The suggested puntuation would better apply for this sentence:

> Why did we need to write this anyway?
