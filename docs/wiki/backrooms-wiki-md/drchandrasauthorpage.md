### A Bit About Me

Hello, I'm Wikidot author DrChandra, and in addition to my posts on The Backrooms, I've contributed nearly a hundred articles to the SCP Wiki, which you can find [here](http://www.scpwiki.com/drchandra-s-author-page).

I've also authored several dozen creepypastas and other short stories under the name The Vesper's Bell, and I'm currently a Featured Writer for Odd Directions. You can find these stories at [r/TheVespersBell](https://www.reddit.com/r/TheVespersBell/).
