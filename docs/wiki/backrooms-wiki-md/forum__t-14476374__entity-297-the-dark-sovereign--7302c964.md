Check the source code for the CECS code. I will link what I use below.

```
[[<]]
[[include :backrooms-sandbox-2:component:cecs
|number=297
|habitat=Unknown
|danger=4
|intel=B+
|class=Itemic
|properties=DMN UNQ custom
|custom-name=RYL
|custom-fullname=Royal
]]
[[/<]]
[[module css]]
:root {
     --cecs-accent: 230, 23, 68 !
important;
     --cecs-dark: 255, 255, 255 !
important;
     --cecs-light: 30, 30, 30 !
important;
}
[[/module]]
```

The "—cecs-accent:" area is what the red lines are in my article, and there are many different options for Danger (0-5) and Intelligence (A-E). Intelligence can also have a "-" or a "+" sign, meaning that the entity has slightly lower or slightly higher intelligence than its class, but not enough to completely change it.

Here is the webpage for more information (<http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/collab:entityclasssystemproposal>)
