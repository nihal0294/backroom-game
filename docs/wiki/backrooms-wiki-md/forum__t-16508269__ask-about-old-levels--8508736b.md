Re: Where's The Enigmatic Levels?

30 Mar 2024 03:28

Enigmatic Levels have been changed to Unnumbered Levels after numerous internal discussions with our contributors. Any levels that are no longer on the list have been moved to the numbered list after reaching out to their respective authors. The majority of Negative Levels were moved upon author request, with the last few being moved by the staff team to adhere with the new definition. You can find the new category and its definition in the same place Enigmatic Levels used to be. I'm willing to answer any questions you might have, so don't be afraid to ask.

Here's is the list of all levels moved: <http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/new-numbered-levels>
