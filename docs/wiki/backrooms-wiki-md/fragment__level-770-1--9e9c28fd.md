# The Ascent

![Missing alt text.](https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/53874632615_04210dd3ce_b.jpg)

Mount Seela

Paradise 770 consists of a single mountain, that we named "Mount Seela." The mountain is about 2 kilometers high and is surrounded by a ravine about 20 meters deep, with a small river flowing inside. On the other side of the ravine is an unreachable everlasting plain of clouds.

On top of its height, Mount Seela is also very spacious, housing an entire cave system inside. On the outskirts of the mountain, entire snowy plains and trails make the ascension of the mountain effortless.

We noticed the frequent presence of a mysterious matter throughout Paradise 770 that we named "Mystical Matter." The matter has a shiny, glowing, blue color. On the surface, the Mystical Matter looks like an aqueous "cosmic" substance flowing in every direction. The pattern of the matter can be compared as a cosmic space made of stars and interstellar clouds… Despite its appearance, the matter feels like a crystal when touched.

Paradise 770 is part of the cluster of paradises only accessible through [the gates of heaven](level-997.md). Thanks to [Entity 997-A](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-997/offset-1)'s efforts, this paradise has been protected from humans for all this time.

  

# Heavenscape

There is a day/night cycle. However, the day only lasts 6 hours, while the night lasts 18 hours. A mysterious star acting like a sun suddenly appears in the sky, announcing the day cycle. With the joyful sounds of the birds playing in the background, the light emitted by this star has a light blue color, which explains the bluish atmosphere during the day.

During the night, Paradise 770 offers a cosmic landscape with frequent blue aurora borealis. However, we can still recognize the void of [Paradise 997](level-997.md). We believe that an atmospheric layer similar to the [frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md) may surround the entire level, altering the appearance of the purplish void.  
  
The temperature is always ambient, around 15 to 25 C°, surprisingly warm for such an environment. It is worth mentioning that the snow and the ice on Paradise 770 does not melt through any means, even when exposed to extreme temperatures. Lastly, the weather can turn snowy. The snow that falls melts the snow on the ground, thus renewing the snow.

# The Mountain's foot

The foot of Mount Seela is mainly covered by dense, snow-covered forests, where passive wolves inhabit the premises. Some trees can be seen emitting glowing particles in the air, indicating that they grow berries[1](javascript:;) that can satisfy your hunger for a whole day, similar to the [royal rations](object-16.md), but they are far more common. When adventuring near the edge of the ground floor, you will sometimes encounter naturally formed slopes that lead directly to the ravine. The mountain's foot extends up to 2 kilometer before being cut by the ravine.

# The Ravine

![Missing alt text.](https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/53874225071_a209483096.jpg)

Entrance to the sacred chamber

Access to the ravine is relatively simple since occasional slopes lead directly to the bottom. There is not much to say about the ravine itself. However, if you're heading for the river's source, a large cave that we named "The Sacred Chamber" can be found, hidden behind a waterfall.

The Sacred Chamber consists of one single large cave. In each corner of the cave, four tall statues made of stone resembling guardians all face the statue at the center. The statue at the center is smaller but resembles a divinity sitting on a throne surrounded by orange crystals. Inside those crystals is an element that looks like the Mystical Matter, but the cosmic like substance is replaced by what appears to be lava flowing in all directions. Just in front of the divine statue is a mysterious and imposing pit, with several rocks levitating above. The depth of the pit is completely black, but we assume that falling into it will result in death, or maybe worse…

# The Mount's Interior

The main characteristic of Mount Seela is its interior.

There is one single entrance at the front side of the mountain.

Upon entering, you will witness a haven of peace with a system of large open caves linked by tunnels or naturally formed paths.

There are several types of caves that we have listed :

#### *Open caves*

Some caves have holes at the top to let in the light from the sky. Open caves can sometimes be mixed with watery caves, thus naturally generating mesmerizing light reflections.

#### *Glowing caves*

They are the most common type of caves. The glowing caves are mainly populated by flora, such as trees, grass, glowing mushrooms, and crystals of multiple colors that light up the whole cave with several colors. The different entities of Paradise 770 frequently wander those caves. The most common entities in these caves, and all the other caves in general, are butterflies made of Mystical Matter that fly around and rest on the stone. Additionally, you can come across trees that vary in size, reaching about 20 meters for the tallest ones. Just like the trees at the mountain's foot, some regrow their fruits every 24 hours and contain large amounts of nutrients. This allows entities and small groups of humans like us to be fed for eternity.

#### *Watery caves*

Some caves contain shallow lakes in which three-tailed foxes usually swim while koi fishes made of mystical matter brighten the water with shiny colors. Nothing special can be found in those caves, but they are a good place to relax and meditate.

#### *Crystal caves*

Another type of cave that contains a large amount of object 770-C, better known as "The Crystals of Chaos". For more details, report to the Objects section.

# The Hearth of the Mountain

At the very center of the mountain, after 1,5 kilometers of ascension. A majestic tree imposes its root and ascends to its peak. Just in front of the bottom of the tree stands a carved stone pedestal with mysterious scribed sigils shining purple. The pedestal house a dark purple orb, through which you can see the interior of Paradise 997 space station. Holding contact with the orb for 10 seconds will transport you out of Paradise 770, but that's not the wisest choice. From the bottom of the Hearth of the Mountain, trails on the side forming a spiral allows to easily ascend to the mount's peak. While ascending, you can view stones made of mystical matter floating all around the tree.

# The Mount's Peak

After walking several kilometers through the caves, trails, and plateau we finally reached what seems to be the highest point of the mountain. The branch of the Hearth of the mountain extends up to the sky where white ravens stand while the tree's leaves emit shiny particles that float in the sky. These branches also grow small amounts of Object 770-B as well known as "The Divine Fruit". Additionally, a shiny white crystal is embedded inside the wood. Holding contact with the crystal for a few seconds will allows you to preview your destination. After making a choice, you will be transported to the destination you chose.

# Entities

Pure sustaining wildlife lives on this mountain. Created by God itself and proud of its creations. This is exactly what we aim to spread.

![Missing alt text.](https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/53873321152_0a07a38dd4.jpg)

![Missing alt text.](https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/53874224651_1efda8839d.jpg)

The entities closely resemble frontrooms animals such as :

- **Three-tailed white foxes**, mostly found on the snowy plateaus or the open caves inside the mount.

- **White wolves with glowing blue eyes**, mostly found in the forests at the bottom of the mountain.

- **Deers with horns made of Mystical Matter**, found pretty much anywhere.

- **Butterflies made of Mystical Matter**, mostly found inside the cave systems.

- **Koi fishes made of Mystical Matter**, found in the multiple rivers and lakes of Paradise 770.

- **White ravens with glowing blue eyes**, mostly found at the mount's peak, on the branches of the Hearth of the Mountain.

- **White doves with glowing blue eyes**, singing during the day and found throughout the entirety of Paradise 770.

The most fascinating characteristic of these entities is that the food chain relies exclusively on the fruits growing from the trees, as we never saw any entity feed off another entity, whatever the species.
