Hello, this is HyperNervie from Chinese branch. I'm translating CN articles to EN recently. (but I haven't made any progress for a few days because I'm busy with other things)

We don't have a group that is specially designed for translation, but we still actively translate EN articles to CN, which is spontaneous. On the contrast, there are just few CN members that are determined to translate CN articles to EN. I'm one of them. You can check out my progress in my [sandbox pages](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/hypernervie).

Apart from that, we do have a group for discussing literally everything that happens in CN branch. You can join us on our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/backroomscn), where it's okay if you're not a Chinese user, but members are less active due to some evident reason. If you are able to speak Chinese and access QQ, it's better to [join our CN site](system__join--bbc7e40d.md) and then our QQ group: 607835817, where CN members are much more active. We'll be happy if you can give us a helping hand.
