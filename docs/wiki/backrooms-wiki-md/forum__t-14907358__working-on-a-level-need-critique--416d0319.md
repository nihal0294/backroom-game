Re: Working On a level need critique

05 Aug 2022 07:14

> Level 573 is the 574th level of the backrooms

"backrooms" should be capitalized and punctuation should be added onto this sentence. Generally, you'd want to add more information to the introduction of your level; provide a brief overview of your level's environment and abnormal properties.

> The kitchen in duplicate 1,000

Punctuation is needed on this caption.

> Level 573 is an old small house

Add a comma after "old".

> house with a wooden floor that looks to be dated back to 1980 and 1990,

The floors in the image are made of tile—not wood.

> Once you enter level 573

"level 573" should be capitalized here since it's a proper noun.

> Once you enter level 573 you will immediately smell laundry detergent, and no matter where you look, including in the laundry room, you will never find any trace of it.

This section caught me off when I first read it, but it doesn't seem to be actually relevant in any way to anything else in the level. I'd like if you could tie this to something existing.

> After looking around in the level for a little bit you will notice that where you

Add a comma after "bit".

> you will notice that where you spawned on the level there was a door behind you and by opening that door you will see a porch with a set of stairs on it that leads to nothing but a void, despite there being a window outside that is clearly showing you the stairs are outside and lead to a driveway.

Change this section to what's shown below:

\*you will notice that where you spawned on the level, there was a door located behind your area of entry; by opening it, you will see a porch and set of stars that lead to nothing but void, though windows in the house show the same staircase leading to another driveway.\*
