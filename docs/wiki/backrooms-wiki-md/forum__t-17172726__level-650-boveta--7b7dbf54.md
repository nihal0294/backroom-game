Sorry to say, but I am giving this page a down vote.

This level has no resources beyond trees. Where are they getting their food from? Nothing on agriculture was talked about in this level. Where are they getting the resources to build all these structures? Importing it from other levels? Boveta does not have bases in other levels to extract resources (At least in 2016 when they were first founded) and if they are purchasing the materials from other groups, what is Boveta buying it with? There are no resources in Level 650 that cannot obtained from other levels. There is nothing unique to sell.

The only unique thing Boveta has is glue gel, but glue gel is made with blue paste that is not even found in Level 650; it is found in Level 614.

Why did Boveta build their capital in Level 650 instead of Level 614? Level 614 has a good supply a food, structures that are already pre-built, and of course, the highly valuable blue paste. The founder of Boveta even found Level 614 before Level 650. There are so many levels with more resources than Level 650. I don't understand why Boveta chose Level 650 level of all places to put their capital beyond making a fictional city real.
