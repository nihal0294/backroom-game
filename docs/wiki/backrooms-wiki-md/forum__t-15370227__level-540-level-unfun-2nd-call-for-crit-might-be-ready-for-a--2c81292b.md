This is cool!

[Just a few things](javascript:;)

[Just a few things;](javascript:;)

Remember to bolden the level name, and every other level mentioned should have a link to the level, so instead of:

> no-clip you to level 109.

It should be

> no-clip you to [level 109](level-109.md).

This level could be rather disturbing, but it is lacking a bit on the classic liminal horror expected of a backrooms level. Also, the structuring of the article is a bit dense. you should include space between the paragraphs.

So instead of:

> Salts, and small cubes of Royal Rations.  
> In hall B, your will find another infinite hallway lined with workshops where various equipment can be > found.

It should be changed to:

> Salts, and small cubes of Royal Rations.
>
> In hall B, your will find another infinite hallway lined with workshops where various equipment can be found.

**EDIT:** Another worry is that the level doesn't really align with the 'party pooper' aesthetic, meaning that the article is at risk of poor reception. You should probably add an extra room, or element to pre-existing rooms, for where the party-poopers came from that better matches their theme. Explaining something as popular as 'party poopers' is something that is going to have to be carefully executed. I would recommend changing it to be a spawn-point type thing, where it isn't **the** 'origin', as much it is **an** origin. But at the end of the day, it's up to you.

Good luck!
