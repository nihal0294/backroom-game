***Welcome!***

My name is Gustavo, but you can call me Gus Rout, I'm 15 and I am an author and a translator on the Backrooms Wikidot. I'm a ex-moderator on the Russian branch and the master-admin of the German branch (yes, it actually exists and is not a myth). If you were an active TS member before September 2022 you are probably wondering: "Who tf is this guy?", well, you see… I got in the Backrooms community when I was 13, so joining the EN branch wasn't an option. Instead, I was doing stuff on the Russian wiki and was one of the translators, who translated Russian articles to the English wiki. But now, I turned 15 and here I am!
