This page reads like you've inserted as much Backrooms stuff as possible. It's like you had a checklist of every stereotypical Backrooms thing and were checking them off as you went along. It's all over the place.

My first recommendation is to read pages written in 2023/24. This page feels very much inspired by 2020/21 pages. The site standard has changed significantly since then and it'll do you a great load of good to see what makes it in more recent times.

A lighthouse is a really interesting setting for a level. I've been in one before and it is super cramped and the ladders and stairs were so steep. It was hard to feel completely safe at all times. This level does nothing with the lighthouse setting. It feels like you just picked a random thing to make infinite. An ifnite staircase isn't anything interesting either. It's been done before and doesn't do much with the fact that a lighthouse is made up of small sections, include the room with the big bulb in I recommend you focus more on the setting and ways you can explore it. It's such a unique setting, don't waste it.  
Some things you could focus on relating to lighthouses:

- Claustrophobia: As I said, lighthouses are small. Google "lighthouse interior" and you'll see what I mean. Some of these pictures might look big but this is someone's whole living space. They're pretty cramped. You could work with this, especially with the fact it gets smaller as the building continues.
- Loneliness: Working in a lighthouse is known for being a lonely job. They're often far from places where people live meaning the ligghthouse keeper ends up being alone often.
- Horror: You could make use of the ocean, winds, and height. You can't run away (if something dangerous walks in through the front door and you're upstairs, you can't just run away). It makes a great horror setting. There's even a horror movie set in a lighthouse.

The alarms confuse me. Why use alarms for dramatic lighting effect when ligthouses have a huge light bulb at the top? You use a picture of what looks like an old fashioned light house too. Black outs is an interesting addition but it's not being used effectively. Also, having it occur after a specific number of steps makes it boring. It's predictable so why should people be *that* scared of it?

Rn, this level is trying to be scary but it really isn't. The phenomena are just a loose list of random things with 0 connection to each other.

"Several children (of any gender) are hanging from a noose. The rope itself is believed to be infinite."  
This is just being edgy. What connection does this have to anything else in the page? What purpose does this serve? Why is it even here?

"The step or railing will disappear before reappearing in the subsequent darkness."  
So? This isn't expanded on at all.

"An unidentified, shadowy, but lengthy humanoid figure with seven fingers is observed hanging on the railing, staring at the wanderers."  
This is just trying to cover all areas of horror but again, so what? There is nothing else to this character. You're basically just telling the reader to be scared by inserting senseless horror.

"The items of furniture in the bottom area will float. Serval wanderers report that during that manifestation, the television will only play static. Attempting to touch the TV will cause the wanderers to become static before disappearing."  
This is interesting. I think you could make something interesting out of this. Make this the only thing that happens with the blackout and expand upon it.

The next two are just uninteresting and don't do anything for the page.

"The wanderer's sanity will vanish and return during the next blackout."  
Sanity isn't really real. This sounds like a game mechanic. Don't use sanity/insanity.

"The temperature will change immediately. Between 4000°C and -4000°C."  
This will immediately kill someone. That's boring. Don't make your level an instantly die level because that wastes all your potential.

The core is whatever. The trope of "only one person makes it to x part of the level" is old and overused. It's also boring. It doesn't serve any purpose for this page. The core isn't interesting at all tbh. Why would people try to get to the top of some random level? It doesn't do anything for them.

Tbh, this level being infinite has no meaning either. It's just there cause *Backrooms*. I don't think this level should be infinite but it's up to you. If you're gonna make a level infinite, do something interesting with it.

The entities.  
I'll first talk about Harold. I don't care about him. He's a very basic horror monster and adds literally nothing. He is meaningless to the overall narrative.

The rest of the entities, there's way too many of them. Look at entities on recent levels. It seems like you've just inserted every entity you like. This is boring. But also, why does the level need entities?

Instead, take a new approach. First ask if entities are even needed? If yes, why? Do they compliment the level? Do they amplify an aspect of the level? Do they make readers interested? Really think about why you're adding entities and what impact you want them to have.

As a whole, I like the very basic idea of this level. It's not at a great place rn but it has so much potential. Like loads of potential. This critique is harsh but you have so much to work with and so many directions you could take. I rwally encourage you to work with this and improve the level because I would love to see it on site if it reaches its potential. You have a way to go but I genuinely think this could make it.

Feel free to ask any questions or even just start a conversation. I'm happy to walk you through anything if needed.  
Good luck!
