Backrooms are a fictional location that people end up at when they "no-clip" (don't make physical contact with an object, most commonly, a wall or a door). Backrooms are not real, and we are writing for it together. Backrooms are mainly made out of [Levels](normal-levels-i.md), along with Sub-Levels, Rooms, Enigmatic Levels, etc. There are [Entities](entities.md) wandering in most of them. Some levels are habitable and have sustained communities, some are pure hell — this is what makes the Backrooms interesting. It is a good place for creativity, meaning that you can easily write for it.
