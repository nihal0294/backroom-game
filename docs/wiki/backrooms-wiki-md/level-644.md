# Level 644 - "Trying to reach you"

Truth, what is it? (163 words.)

rating: +17[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

  
   
   
   
 

**Reality #644** is flat and oval-shaped. It has not been measured, and it will not be. Whether it contains a human corpse(?) or a living being, we don't know.

**Reality #644** hosts an unmarked grave in its geographical center. It has not been measured, and it will not be. Whether the human corpse(?) or a living being is located in it, we don't know.

**Reality #644** is found in the human collective consciousness of the entirety of [Base Omega](level-4.md) after a cassette was shared around and viewed by its employees. The emotions that it causes have not been measured, and they will not be. Why it cannot be forgotten, we don't know.

**Reality #644** is freely accessible. Its entry has not been found, and it will not be. Why that is, it knows. We don't.

**Reality #644** hasn't received any flowers in years. The reasons for this have not been determined, and they will not be. Who the living corpse(?) is,

we know.
