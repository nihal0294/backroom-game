# SPaG, Clinical Tone and Syntax

> In a horrendously remote railway system, lies a miles long train

1. All of the level links were not linked correctly. Here's an example of how you can link them correctly:

> [[[level-72|horrendously remote railway system]]]  becomes [horrendously remote railway system](level-72.md)

Although this will not work in the sandbox, it will work once the article is greenlit.  
2. The links are very ineligible. Here's how you can change the color of links:

> **Firstly, implement this line of code anywhere in your page:**  
>   
> [[module css]]  
> .name {  
> color:#000000;  
> }  
> [[/module]]  
>   
> **Note that you can change the "000000" part to whatever hexadecimal value you want.**  
> **Next, go to your link.**  
>   
> In a [[[level-72|horrendously remote railway system]]], lies a miles long train  
>   
> **Wrap this code around the link:**  
>   
> [[span class="name"]][[[level-72|horrendously remote railway system]]][[/span]]  
>   
> **There ya go! It should work.**

> Description:

I suggest that you connect the two lightblocks (the one before the 'Description' header and the one after it). It makes the TOC look like it's badly placed 'on top' of the page.

> The picture was taken this way so that a Clump wouldn't obstruct it.

This detail is pretty unnecessary, please remove it.

> made of *"Allocasuarina luehmannii" wood*

De-italicize 'wood'.

> At the train's pilot is a single Faceling who's completely unresponsive.

Replace "who's" with "who is".

> For instance, as stated above, the journey is presumably infinite, meaning that the tunnels have to continue forever, however, the best estimation of the level's area, prior to this recent discovery, was 500 square kilometers, which is unsuitable for the "infinite journey" hypotesis.

big block o' text. I'd split this into:

> For instance, as stated above, the journey is presumably infinite, meaning that the tunnels have to continue forever. However, the best estimation of the level's area, prior to this recent discovery, was 500 square kilometers, which is unsuitable for the "infinite journey" hypotesis.

> False entertainment

Capitalize 'entertainment'.

> Level 77 is well-known to be a quite boring location, as most of the time is spent in a single, nearly devoid of entities section unless one decided to leave it; however, on the passenger seats it is possible to stumble upon magazines and newspapers; all of them share common factors, as they all lack text on the first two pages, showing solely the headlines, however, the rest is comprised of oftentimes scarily accurate, peculiarly graphic descriptions of kidnappings, torture methods, pornography and brutal murder.

another big block o' text. Although I like the use of semicolons, I'd change this to:

> Level 77 is well-known to be a quite boring location, as most of the time is spent in a single, nearly empty section unless one decided to leave it. However, on the passenger seats it is possible to stumble upon magazines and newspapers; all of them share common factors, as they all lack text on the first two pages, showing solely the headlines. In spite of that, unfortunately, the rest is comprised of oftentimes scarily accurate, peculiarly graphic descriptions of kidnappings, torture methods, pornography and brutal murder.

> quickly drain sanity from the explorer, oftentimes irreversibly trasforming them into their wicked, starving selves.

'Sanity' is no longer a mechanic used on the wiki. I'd change it to something like:

> quickly cause schizophrenia and other mental issues, oftentimes irreversably transforming them into their wicked, starving selves.

> shade of beige, the poles on either side stay red, however.

Change this to:

> shade of beige, although the poles on either side remain red.

> It's unknown

I suggest that you write "It is" instead of "It's". Terms like "it's", "they've", and "he'll" often break clinical tone.

> lead to Level 59.

Link "Level 59" here.
