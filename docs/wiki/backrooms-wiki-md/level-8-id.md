**Level 8-ID** is the 9th level of Backrooms Indonesia. This level is a roundabout road shrouded in fog.

# **Description**

![Missing alt text.](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/32/Raymonds_Hill%2C_the_A35_crosses_the_border_in_fog_-_geograph.org.uk_-_3361372.jpg)

Level 8-ID

**Level 8-ID** is a roundabout surrounding a traffic island of unknown diameter. Both the inner and outer perimeter of the roundabout are filled with dense trees, although the trees on the outer side are not as numerous as those on the inner side. The road is made of asphalt, and is wide enough for two cars to pass each other.

This level is shrouded in fog, so visibility is severely limited. Survivors traveling in groups must stay close to each other, or they risk separation. Thus far, survivors separated in the fog have been unable to find their companions. The level's atmosphere is constantly like a foggy morning.

Although the road is circular, survivors will not recognize their surroundings no matter how far they travel. Turning around does not help either, as the surroundings continuously change. The level clearly suffers from spatial distortion once a survivor is no longer able to observe their immediate surroundings due to the fog.

![Missing alt text.](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c1/Lochemse_Berg%2C_omheining_en_bomen_aan_de_zuidkant_van_de_berg_IMG_3200_2020-01-01_10.34.jpg)

An intersection to another level.

After walking on the road for a while, there will be an off-ramp (or exit) that, if taken by the survivors, will lead them to another roundabout. Furthermore, after walking for a sufficient amount of time, survivors will encounter an off-ramp whose roadside drastically differs from the rest of the level. For example, if an off-ramp’s verge is filled with bushes instead of trees, it is highly likely that the off-ramp leads to [Level 16-ID](level-16-id.md). The fog quickly dissipates as the survivor proceeds down the exit. Survivors typically encounter such an off-ramp between the 10th and 17th junctions (or exits).

If survivors attempt to walk into the inner side of the roundabout (the center island), they will generally encounter another road on the opposite side after walking for a while, regardless of the direction taken. This is considered normal because the level is, in fact, a roundabout. Survivors will unfortunately not be able to locate an exit in this manner. However, on certain roundabouts, after entering the tree-lined area within the roundabout, survivors may come across certain striking buildings. One example is the case where a survivor found a locker room in the middle of a roundabout that led them to [Level 22-ID](level-22-id.md). This is considered a rare case and is not recommended to be sought out.

The longer a survivor stays in this level, the more fatigued they will become, even if they spend the entire time sitting. When the level was first discovered, it claimed quite a few victims who suddenly suffered cardiac arrest due to exhaustion. It is therefore recommended that survivors move as quickly as possible in this level until they find an off-ramp to another level, regardless of whether they have a vehicle or not.

# **Base, Outpost and Community**

There are no known bases, outposts or communities at this level.

# **Entrances and Exits**

#### Entrances

- Going through a narrow alley in [Level 10-ID](level-10-id.md) can lead survivors to this level. The condition is that the alley must be only as wide as the survivor's chest. Survivors are usually startled by a bright light that suddenly appears before they realize they are already standing in the middle of this level's road.
- Entering a grove of trees in [Level 24-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-24-id) can lead survivors to this level.
- Driving on [Level 11-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-11-id) can bring survivors to this level. Survivors need to be wary of roads that begin to be covered by clouds, as they may suddenly find themselves transitioning to this level shortly thereafter.

#### Exits

- An off-ramp leading to a seaside directs the survivor to [Level 4-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-4-id). Upon stepping onto a pool of water, the survivor will suddenly fall and drown, only to emerge 5 meters above the sea level of that level. If mangrove trees are present in the vicinity of the exit, the destination level is [Level 24-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-24-id).
- An off-ramp that begins to be covered with sand can lead survivors to [Level 5-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-5-id).
- Generally, continuing through an off-ramp will lead survivors to a level that features roads.
- Buildings are very rarely found in the middle of the roundabout and are very risky to search for, as survivors quickly become exhausted. So far, the following have been discovered: a locker room that leads survivors to [Level 22-ID](level-22-id.md), a house covered in drizzling rain that leads survivors to [Level 23-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-23-id), a large temple similar to Prambanan Temple that leads survivors to [Level 5-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-5-id), and a tall unpainted building with exposed concrete rebar on top that leads survivors to [Level 41-ID](level-41-id.md).
