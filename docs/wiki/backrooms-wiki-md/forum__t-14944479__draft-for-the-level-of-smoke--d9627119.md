Hello Custodian Funderful! I will provide some critique and criticism on your level so far, yet you will want to update it further.

### SPaG and Clinical Tone:

Looks good as far as I can tell, and I am impressed by your clinical tone and descriptive words.

### Formatting/Coding:

You want to link the levels located next to this one, but don't worry abt that rn in the early stages. You do however need to provide image sourcing.

### Desc./Idea:

Frankly, I find your level quite bland. In a nutshell, your level is just level 1 but with toxic gas and lore. I think you could expand the details about the level to make it something that does not look as similar, but that's all.  
Now, about the gas, it doesn't rlly make sense. You said it was very toxic, but all you mentioned was that afer 3 days u would be screwed. What about the other 95 hours? Is it an instakill with no warning, or a slow process you feel throughout the hours? And if this gas is as dangerous as it seems to be, maybe you should notch the SD Class 1 or 2 higher.

### Lore

I like the lore to this level, but I don't see much too it. I assume it is because you have not updated it, but you never mentoned anything relative to "fusing with the level." My assumption was that the man in question became the fog after passing, but how did he die? Each of the documents also doesn't provie much detail to what is happening. I enjoy the mystery, but total confusion is not tha aim.

+++Overall:  
I think you have done a good job on this level, however many updates will be needed before greenlight. Who is the secret man? Why did the gas appear? Why has the M.E.G. (assuming that is the "author"), not mentioned anything about its appearance? Good luck on your next update! PM with questions. -Iamalemon
