Hi, I'd like to add a critique for your level idea.

Overall, it is well-written, but it is very short, and the details are very scarce. I have a few suggestions for you.

- Add more details. What sensations do your bodies experience in the level(like smell, touch, and taste)? Are there any anomalous properties? Just add more to this than what is there.
- Add more entrances and exits. Just one entrance and exit is very bland. My sandbox has a wide variety of entrances and exits, for example.
- Possibly add more images, and there's some good resources in the Guide Hub.
- Get rid of the usage of an offset. It can get frustrating if you're just wanting to look at the article.
- If the image you *do* have is from the internet, **please add the source in a dropdown.**
- This is a very overused concept, of the level being destroyed.
