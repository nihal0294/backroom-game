Q: How do i enter into The Backrooms?

A:  
1) Noclip  
Definition:  
*In video games, noclip mode (also known as "noclipping") is a cheat that prevents the first-person player character camera from being obstructed by other objects and permits the camera to move in any direction, simply removing all physics so the player is granted the ability to pass through such things as walls, props, and other players. Noclipping can be used to cheat, avoid bugs (and help developers debug), find easter eggs, and view areas beyond a map's physical boundaries.*  
There is not a very documentation about doing it in real life, while in video games is easy.  
But it can be through anything material, so be careful.

2) Touching something that seem "glitched"  
Items that can seems weird, misplaced, unnatural.

3) Entering through misplaced door etc…  
Entering in doors, stairs, weird elevator floors… that seems misplaced, that you dont recognize.

Q: What are "levels, sub-levels etc."?

A: Levels are the most recognizable parts of The Backrooms, is the environment around you.  
Some are safe while some not.  
[Level 0](level-0.md) is probably the safest one.  
You can find a full list [here](normal-levels-i.md)  
Clusters (you can see references on various levels pages) are series of levels that usually go from 0 to 999 (if the Cluster is higher than 2 it will look like X000 to X999, X is an undefinied number).  
Right now there is only 1 Cluster.  
But the wiki is evolving fast, it may reach Cluster 2 soon.

Sub-layers:  
*Sub-Layers are locations that usually add more detail to an existing level. There are two types of Sub-Layers — "Sub-Levels" and "Rooms". Sub-Levels are levels that are related to their parent level, but are still their own distinct level. Rooms are locations that are too small to be considered their own level. Usually, they are places in an existing level, but that is not always the case.*  
The Backrooms wiki.

Sub-levels:  
*Sub-Levels are intended to add lore, or "depth" to an already written level. Think of them as spin offs of the level they can be found in, or levels within the level, or a Level between two other levels. A Sub-Level is usually smaller, however more detailed - but not always. A Level can have as many Sub-Levels as you can imagine, as long as they maintain the loose theme of their parent Level.*  
Still Backrooms wiki.  
You can find a full list [here](sub-layers.md)

Rooms:  
*Rooms are special locations that appear on specific Levels. Rooms typically follow the theme of the Levels they appear on. Rooms are not infinite, nor are they very big which is the main reason why these are labeled as Rooms.*  
Again Backrooms wiki.  
You can find a full list [here](sub-layers.md)  
(Scroll down until you see Room Mainlist).

Enigmatic levels:  
*The Enigmatic Levels are areas in the Backrooms which do not have an ordinal placement, and are very mysterious. Some Levels here are safe as far as we know, but some can be dangerous, though it is less likely. These Levels have stranger properties than most Levels, giving them unique names and/or numbers. A lot of these Levels were recently discovered; it is theorized there are still many more Enigmatic Levels to be found.*  
Backrooms wiki.  
You can find a full list [here](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/enigmatic-levels)

Q: What are Entities?

A:  
Entities:  
*Native to the Backrooms rather than our own reality, the term "Entity" covers a wide variety of beings, from friendly inhabitants with human-like intelligence, to mindless beasts, to creatures whose motivations and abilities are beyond our understanding. Each entity has unique behaviors, habitats, and physiological needs, catalogued by eyewitness account in our database, but when in doubt, a good rule of thumb for interacting with entities is to leave them be, for your sake and theirs. Conflicting accounts may exist in the database, and trusting your gut is the best way to survive.*

Q: What are objects?

A:  
*Objects, also known as items or artifacts, are tools, weapons, consumables, and other things used by people in the Backrooms. Many of them follow the rules of normal reality. Others are changed by or affected by the Backrooms, and some are created by (or exclusively found in) the Backrooms. Some of them are dangerous, while others are crucial to your survival. They key difference between an entity and an object is that Entities are alive.*  
You can find a full list [here](objects.md)

Q: What are canons?

A: In a nutshell, they are stories about The Backrooms, very simply telled.  
You can find a full list [here](canons.md)

Q: What is a Tail Archive?

A:  
This archive is designed to organize the Backrooms tales by author name, and as such is organized alphabetically. If you discover that a tale is absent, please contact either the tale author, or someone from technical team for assistance.  
You can find a full list [here](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/tails)

Q: What are groups?

A:  
*While some authors portrays the Backrooms as place of isolation, others see the Backrooms as populated by myriad of unique groups, factions and communities. These are groups of humans or entities that have banded together to achieve some goal, whether that goal is survival or something else entirely.*  
You can find a full list [here](groups-list.md)

Q: What are POIs?

A:  
*As odd as The Backrooms is in itself, some people still manage to stick out of the crowd like a sore thumb. Persons of Interest, referred to commonly as POIs, are both local and alien to this dimension, both hostile and peaceful to others, and they all have their different motives and drives to do what they want, often times using outlandish methods to reach those mysterious goals.*  
You can find a full list [here](poi-s.md)
