" They have a heavy disliking for hostile entities, such as Facelings and Skin-Stealers, and often kill them for their own enjoyment."

-Heavy disliking seems like a odd word choice for this, I would suggest just saying hate as its more to the point-

"Humanoid genes mutate exponentially. This results in new variants being created quickly."

-This really belongs in the biology section so I suggest putting it at the end of the biology section then putting the variants below the biology section-

"but have a power mental stability"

-I'm guessing you meant 'powerful'-

"dwindling population"

-Why is the blue humanoids population dwindling? Is this suppose to be a mystery or is it a looked over feature? If you intend to explain this I suggest explaining it in their description-

"After the Incident, all but five of these variants were left in the backrooms. The locations of these variants are unknown due to multiple organizations relocating them to a safer area. Records of what these variants are capable of have been lost for years on end. If you encounter one, do not attempt to communicate."

-This is probably intentionally vague but just incase, Why should you not attempt communication? Why were the records lost?-

"Biology"

-you should probably explain why their faces change color here, unless you wanna keep it a mystery-

"The BDC"

-Mention that the humanoids did not create the BDC to avoid future confusion concerning our group-

"Sightings of black-faced variants have been reported throughout the backrooms. Little is known about them but if encountered, avoidance is the best method."

-This should have its own variant section, even if most info is unknown it is still a variant nonetheless-

"Humanoids cannot develop immunity from The Disease."

-This may be my personal preference but this belongs in the biology sections if you ask me since it has to do with their immune system-

"aggressive, smiler-like entities if fed the substance."

-You should give this its own variant spot as this seems very important to the safety of wanderers and would likely be a priority to ensure wanderers understand the need to not feed them memory juice and the dangerous consequences if they do-

Conclusion: This is a very good article that could use a bit of re-organizing and expanding to improve the flow of things and expansion on some of the above mentioned concepts but it is in pretty good standing! Hope you take this crit into account, see ya
