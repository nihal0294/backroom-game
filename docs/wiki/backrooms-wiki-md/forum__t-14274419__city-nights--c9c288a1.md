This had a really strong opening couplet, and for a second I was prepared to read some thoughtful and musical poetry. Unfortunately, I feel like the end of the first stanza and the off-kilter rhythm to it threw me out of the reverie, and from there, I couldn't help but take notice of every little thing I felt could be polished with the poem. While it has somewhat interesting imagery, and I think the emotion it tries to point to is poignant, I just don't think it's as tightly cut and prosodaically sound as it needs to be for the incredibly short length. I can give props and say that it does feel like a non-poet's first draft, but I do feel like the poem being good should be prioritized over realism in microfiction.
