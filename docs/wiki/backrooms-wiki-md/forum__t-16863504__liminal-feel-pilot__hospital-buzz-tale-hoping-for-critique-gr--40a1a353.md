Liminal Feel Pilot: Hospital Buzz (Tale) (Hoping for critique/greenlights) :3

15 Jun 2024 02:56

Here's a pilot tale of sorts to what I'm hoping will be made into a full level series. It is about a teen finding himself in a eerie, albeit somewhat familiar place. As he explores the corridors of what he finds to be a hospital, he starts to notice and experience strange phenomena and becomes further aware of his loneliness, and starts to feel that something worse may be going on here. (The level referenced is a WIP level draft)

Here's my draft (I can't publish links yet sadly):

*My eyes are closed…I should really open them…hmph, the bed feels really different…well, anyway, I need them to open…*

When his consciousness came to be, and thoughts started elapsing through his mind, his eyes slowly gave way to the somewhat blurry at first, bright, white, light, accompanied by some, sort of buzzing.

Soon, it would became clear what the source of those two were. But first, his vision was still unclear, obscured by the ‘dust’ or whatever you call it, hiding in the crevices of the eyelids. He lifted his arm, but noticed that it was…stiff. How? Waking up was obviously going to bring with it some rigid elbows and shoulders, but it wasn’t to the extent that he was feeling right now. They were almost frozen in place, but, with great force, he brought his arm to the eyelid and removed the specks of dust from there.

As everything came into focus, he realized that the bright white light and the buzzing Zzzs emanated from the rows of fluorescent, blinding, lights. He slowly tilted his neck up, with a great chunk of his willpower, till his eyes were facing the bed that was on his opposite side. The empty bed. But that wasn’t of worry now, he still had to get up.

After struggling to even slip his feet to the tiled ceramic floor, he flung his body upright, and at that point, his mind finally started to wake up. He remembered that his name was Mark, he was 14 years old, and…wait, why wasn’t he in his home in Kiwi Town?

Mark started to wonder why he wasn’t in the place that he normally resided in, before further realizing that he wasn’t wearing pajamas. Heck, he wasn’t even wearing his own clothes. Instead, he was outfitted in a cyan tee, with light purplish tiny ovals dotted around his wear. He looked around this place, only to notice that-

Nobody was in the room.

Even more ominously, part of it looked somewhat decrepit. Most of the lights were still shining bright, but some were either significantly dimmer or just completely stopped emitting its light, with most of them being located close to other beds.

Speaking of which, whilst some of them were perfectly tidy, with their bedsheets neatly tucked in, others were crumpled and messy, with a few of them lacking pillows, blankets, or mattresses entirely. Strangely enough, when Mark’s attention was drawn to one of the more untidy ones, which, on top of it, read ‘Josh Hutchinson - D’.

He scanned the other beds, with the ones in better shape containing blank name tags, whilst the ones in worse condition contained name tags that had what presumably was it’s occupants' name, along with the symbols ‘A’, ‘D’, or the rare ‘C’. He looked up his own bed and saw his name tag, which read ‘Markus Johnson - A’.

Mark then turned his attention to the window, which was someway, somehow, blacked out. Why would people even want to do that? He thought. Mark staggered out of the room, and only then, only then, did he realize where he was, with the room labeled ‘Ward 1001 - Specialized Care’ - He was in a hospital. And he was still alone. *Alone. **Alone.***

*Specialized Care? What care do I even need?* Mark walked down the narrow, claustrophobic corridor, and the ward number increased as he went on. And he still couldn’t see or hear anybody. It was still just the buzzing and the shining coming from the lights. At times, the wards’ lights were completely off. At others, there was a pungent, rotten egg-like smell, and in one of those specific rooms, it was SO disgusting that he had to look into it to check its source. And when he did, he’d wished he hadn’t.

Blood. Dry, maroon-colored *blood.* On one of the messiest beds that he saw, blood was spread all around it. Even more gruesome was the decomposing arm that was under it. Quickly, not wanting to think about it, Mark ran out of the room, and seconds later, started to feel more *fear. A pure, growing fear.*

And suddenly, everything became worse. The lights quickly flickered off, and Mark’s fear only grew. He started to brisk walk through the pitch-black environment, feeling his way through the rough, concrete walls. When he felt the corridor’s end, he turned to his left or right. And still, he was alone. *Tap, tap, tap, tap…*Suddenly, Mark’s shoes tapped themselves against some metal. After several more seconds, his hands felt a metal button laid back on the wall. He quickly smashed his right index finger against it, and it glowed a dim red.

However, the fact that soon, Mark would be simmered in light, didn’t resolve the fear in him. No, it only exacerbated its festering, and he didn’t know why. He remembered that one time, when he was 7, when a power outage would transpire when he was alone at home, at 9pm, before curfew. At that point, he still had his bedroom lamps turned on while sleeping, and he used to have a phobia of the dark. Mark remembered crumpling his body in fear in the bathroom, shivering from fright. Even those feelings that were felt years ago paled in comparison to what he was experiencing right now. And then-

A growling sound could be heard in the distance, slowly growing louder and more menacing. Mark immediately started pressing the red button faster, just in case it wasn’t working. Rivers of sweat dripped down his face, as the growling slowly grew into barking, and banging sounds got louder and louder.

Finally, after a period of time that seemed like an eternity, the elevator doors finally opened. Running in, Mark, without care, pressed one of the buttons, and repeatedly started vibrating his index finger against the ‘close’ button. *Oh crap, oh CRAP, OH CRAP, NO NO NO NO NO PLEASE DON’T.* Never mind the fact that he looked much older than he knew he was when looking in the mirror. ***JUST CLOSE.***

And as the elevator started closing, a loud, stretched-out growling sound could be heard, and Mark could barely see the luminescent eyes and that exaggerated smile of…whatever that thing was. And it flung itself towards the lift.

His scream could be heard in the distance. All was silent.

Especially since this will be my very first article if it gets greenlighted (and indeed it's also my first piece of writing I have dedicated to this wiki and the backrooms in general), I do have quite a few concerns:  
- Pacing, might be a too abrupt at points?  
-Length, idk if I should make it longer but at the same time I'm worried about the tale being too stretched out?  
-Lack of imagery, would like to make it more detailed but also doesn't want to make the tale, once again, too stretched out?
