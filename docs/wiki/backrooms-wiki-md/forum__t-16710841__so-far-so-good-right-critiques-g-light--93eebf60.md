**Critic:**

---

*By the way, I recommend that you change the font to not bold, since it can be confusing (and only leave the parts that say "Level 93.2" in bold.)*

[click me!](javascript:;)

[yes!](javascript:;)

# introduction

> Level 93.2 is a sub level in between level 93 and 94.

sublevel goes all together and you need to capitalize the first L in Level 93.

---

> It is understood as the base to level 93's mountain.

Capitalize the first L in Level 93.

---

# Description:

> Level 93.2 is a mountain range with massive mountain in the middle.

After this sentence you should change the full stop to a comma, since you are talking in these parts of the range.

---

> The land near the mountain is always snowed on and will never stop.

After this sentence you should change the full stop to a comma.

---

# Climate:

> Level 93.2 and level 93 are both renowned for their weather.

Capitalize the first L in Level 93.

---

> 93.2 has more tame and calm weather compared to level 93.

At the beginning type Level and then capitalize the first L in Level 93.

---

# Any extra information

> Several wonderers are allowed in this level at one time.

It is correctly written as Wanderers with a capital letter in the first W.

---

# Bases, Outposts and Communities:

### The Climbers

> The climbers are a group of wanderers whose main goal is the help people in the level.

Capitalize the first W in Wanderers.

---

# Entrances/Exits

#### Entrances:

> Walking through any doorway in level 387 has an extremely low chance of taking you here.

You have to capitalize the first L in Level 387 and the code is poorly done.

```
Walking through any doorway in [[[Level 387]]] has an extremely low chance of taking you here.
```

---

> Touching any picture with a mountain range on level 57 will bring you here.

You have to capitalize the first L in Level 57 and the code is poorly done.

```
Touching any picture with a mountain range on [[[Level 57]]] will bring you here.
```

---

#### Exits

> Level 8, Level You Cheated, or level 220. Burying yourself in snow will send you back up to level 93.

You have to capitalize the first L in Level 220 and Level 93, you must also delete the "Level You Cheated" part and change it to Level 363. The link code is also wrong:

```
[[[Level 8]]], [[[Level 363]]], or [[[Level 220]]]. Burying yourself in snow will send you back up to [[[Level 93]]].
```

---

  
**I hope this critic has helped you.**
