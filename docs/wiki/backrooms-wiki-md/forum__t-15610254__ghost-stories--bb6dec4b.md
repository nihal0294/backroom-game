# **The Masterpiece**

After a long day of gaming, I turned off my computer and took a nap. I slept for a while. Then I woke up with tape over my mouth, unable to move for some reason. I was afraid, and I tried to scream, but it was no use. I then saw the shadow of something. It looked purple or something like that. I did not know what to do. It got closer to my bed, and it then said something. It was something about a masterpiece, but I could not hear it very well. It got closer to my face, and then I blacked out.

My room was covered in purple liquid. The walls had "It's not a mistake, It's a MASTERPIECE" on them. I don't know what it means, but I have had nightmares of it almost every night. [I hope it is no longer watching me.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4954We5rtWU)

Welp, here goes:

She was lying in bed half asleep while listening to music. After three minutes of trying to get into her favorite song, she gave up. It was pointless trying to enjoy anything now that *she* was gone. Her dad seemed to be ignoring her now. Like she wasn't even in the room with him. Sometimes she came home to find that he had stripped her bed of it's blankets and covers, and she had to go and replace them. Why? All this because her mom died in the crash? Why couldn't he realize that his daughter was still here for him?  
Her thoughts were interrupted by a loud knock on their apartment door. She stood up and went to her bedroom doorway, but stopped. Her dad had already beaten her to the door. He looked through the peephole, unlocked the door, and flung it open. She looked in shock. Her mom was standing there very much alive  
"I'm so glad you've finally been released from the hospital. But she's doing it again. I heard music from the bedroom  
"Dear, if it's too much, we can always leave this house."  
Her mom seemed to be looking directly at her as she said this. This wasn't possible. Her mom was dead. She had died in the crash. So what was going on? What did her dad mean by "She was doing it again?"  
She walked up to her parents and waved a hand in front of their faces. They suddenly stiffened.  
"I think we should. I can't be living in this house with several reminders of losing her."  
"Alright."  
Over the next few weeks, she listened to her parents make arrangments to move. When she told them she didn't want to or tried to start a conversation in general, they just ignored her. When moving day finally came, she dressed and walked out the door with her parents. They got into the car and pulled out of the driveway. And then a car going well over the 10 mph speed limit smashed into them.  
She opened her eyes. She must have fallen asleep listening to the music. After three minutes of trying to get into her favorite song, she gave up…

It is suggesting she is a ghost. The reasons her parents ignored her was because they couldn't see her. Her mom was in the hospital after being injured during the wreck that killed the girl. Her dad was at home and kept hearing the girl's favorite song playing in her room, but no electronic device was seen in the room. When the mom came home from the hospital, the ghost of the girl is shocked to see her, as she thinks her mom died and she lived. Eventually, the family leaves the home, and the girl follows them into the car. There was another wreck in which we don't know what happened to the parents.  
The girl then wakes up with her earbuds in and trying to get back into her favorite song. I'm suggesting here that she's going through a loop. Maybe not the best written story, but I was typing it up within twenty minutes and had no time to edit.
