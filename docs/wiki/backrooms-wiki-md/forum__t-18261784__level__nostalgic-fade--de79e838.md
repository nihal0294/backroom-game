Level: Nostalgic Fade

02 Aug 2026 17:20

**Concept Title:** Level: Nostalgic Fade  
**Brief Description:** The wanderer makes his or her way across many backyards that are surrounded by white fences. That is very similar to the yard they had when they were a child. The yards end, and eventually open up into a long field. After trekking, the wanderer happens upon a ghost town and in this town there is an office building blocking any path forward.  
**Central Narrative:** The story is told from a second person’s point of view. This explains what happens throughout the different sections of the level as the character is being hunted by wolf-like creatures–and their own imagination: which seems to get away from them sometimes. All for a long-shot hope for escape.  
**Hook:** You enter yet again another level; it’s difficult to see due to the brightness of some glaring object in the sky. After a little while your eyes adjust and you start to look around. It’s almost identical to your backyard from when you were a kid. The only difference is a large white fence surrounding it. Not caring too much you make it towards the fence gate. You swing it open on its rusted hinges and it squeaks into the air.  
**Purpose:** This concept should bring the nostalgic energy to the next level by not just bringing the reader back in time, but also jar them back and forth due to the encounters in this level. The reader should hopefully gain the feeling of being trapped in their own memories. This work will add on to the old and nostalgic feel of the Backrooms as a transition to following levels.  
**Format/Execution:** We plan to write our article in a way that successfully conveys all of these emotions and keeps the readers hooked. We would like to really give a questioning tone throughout the story leading to a growing uneasiness: Building on psychological horror.  
**Additional Notes/Remarks:** My friend and I are hoping to create our very first Backrooms page. But, we would like to do it the proper way. Thus, we are seeking feedback to do so. Thank you for your time.
