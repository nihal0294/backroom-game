Re: Level 445 "Languageless"

11 Jun 2022 02:32

Hello! I think that you did not add more than necessary, and if anything, less than. I understand the concept of the level, but I think you could really go into greater detail about it being "languageless". Asa reader, I was left wondering what would happen if you were to bring a writing utensil? Could you write words in this level without text? I also think that you could go into a much more brief desc. of the day/night cycle, or put that later on, as some readers may get bored if it is very early and so long. On the bright side, the level really intrigued me, as I have not seen any levels relatively similar. The appearance can be compared with other levels quite easily, but your languageless function makes it stand out. Make sure to check for SPaG errors, and I see this as a level with much possibility of being greenlighted. Good luck!
