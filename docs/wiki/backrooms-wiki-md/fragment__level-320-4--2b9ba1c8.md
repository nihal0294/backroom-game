# fragment:level-320-4

Hello?

You just opened up a direct line with me, who are you? If this was an accident, say so. I'm busy.

Hi, sorry! I didn't mean to interrupt you.

That's very clearly not true, because you did. Just because it's a Saturday doesn't mean I'm not working. I get a lot of emails and direct lines, just tell me what it is you need, please.

Just to make sure, you are Holly Clove, right? Level 100 and boulders Holly Clove?

Are you really trying to get on my bad side here? I have more recent and better achievements, you know.

Oh, I know you do! Sorry, I suppose. I just wanted to make sure I had the right person, Holly Clove's a common name.

Yep.

Okay, good!

Now that's out of the way, I'd like some help. A mutual friend of ours isn't having a great time with things right now, and you're an influential woman in the M.E.O.D., I figured that you could help me do something for her to lift her spirits?

Who's this mutual.

Oh, uh. It's Esther? Esther Hanks. I know you and her mom were close, I figured you were close with the rest of her family too.

You're friends with Esther? Wait, who are you? How old are you?

I'm Magnus Grand? I'm sure I've been mentioned by Esther before, we're close.

…Oh. Right. Yeah, okay, your name is familiar. It's been mentioned.

You're from [29](level-29.md), right? Esther mentioned that.

Yep! I'm a Hyperia baby.

Right. Well, fine. What is it you want me to do?

Well, you're a woman with hands in a lot of pies, and I imagine some of those pies are somewhat involved in the new BRC conglomeration? right?

…Yes? Technically? I'm a Blogrooms reporter, so I can't exactly get you hooked up to Cap'n, but yeah, I know people from my more active days.

That's completely fine! I was wondering if you could maybe do some kind of page based on the levels that have gone missing or died? You know, like one of the old Level 42's? Maybe the last level that took Level 32's slot?

What's Level 32 again? Actually, I'll just check myself.

The one with the Skeleton Queen? Why there? No one's been there in over a decade.

No, no, the level that held its slot before that. The theme park one, do you remember it? I think it vanished around 2021.

Oh, that one, right. I remember it vaguely, I didn't even realise it was gone.

Wait, how the fuck does writing about this level make Esther feel better? She lost her mom, dude, B-Tier amusement park attractions aren't going to fill that hole.

It's one we both went to once when we were younger. I sorta figured with her losing her mom, she could at least have something back?

… In the form of an article where it isn't even the focus. Have you thought this through?

Well, I was hoping that after the article, Level 32 would get popular again, and… Well, maybe it could be revived? Maybe by the Renovations Division?

Dude, that's way out of my pay grade. I can't call up the Renovations Division and ask them to spend insane money and resources bringing back a level that's been dead a decade in the vague hope of making Esther superficially happy for, at most, a day.

I think you're being a bit too harsh on the concept, but that's pretty much the idea yeah.

Oh my god. You do know I don't get to pick what I write about, right? We have a writers room and advisors for a reason.

Well, I know you can at least suggest it? Please? I saw you posted something today when I got the news of Esther's mom dying, surely you need another article to write?

Well, I guess I do. I'm not back in the office until Monday, but I'll pitch the idea. Don't expect it to go anywhere, though, and don't expect credit. There's also no way in hell I'm contacting mutuals from 5 years ago to revive an entire fucking level, no chance in hell.

That's okay! I think I know how to do it myself.

You're, like, 19. How the hell do you plan on doing that.

I'm 20, actually. But I think I have my ways.

Right… Whatever, man. I don't think your idea will help Esther in the slightest, but you seem hell bent on it. Thanks for the idea to pass by the board on Monday at least.

No problem!

Oh, you already closed the chat.
