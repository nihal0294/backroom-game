You cross the [threshold,](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-355) from a world of black and white, to a world of greys.

![hallway.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/hallway.jpg)  
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
 

Boundaries blur; you don't quite know where one shade ends and another begins.

![Liminal-Space_Flickr_Douglas-Muth_CC_BY-SA_2.0.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/Liminal-Space_Flickr_Douglas-Muth_CC_BY-SA_2.0.jpg)

At what point does left become right?

When is right left?

Is the way forward behind you?

![长廊_Flickr_pear82_CC_BY_2.0.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/%E9%95%BF%E5%BB%8A_Flickr_pear82_CC_BY_2.0.jpg)

It doesn't matter.

   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
   
![Aisle_-_Flickr_-_Stiller_Beobachter.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/Aisle_-_Flickr_-_Stiller_Beobachter.jpg)

It all blends together.

![Royal_York_Hallway.JPG](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/Royal_York_Hallway.JPG)

The halls move ever onward.

![hallways_vanishing_point.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/hallways_vanishing_point.jpg)

And so you walk.

And walk.

![Lorain_High_School_hallway.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/Lorain_High_School_hallway.jpg)

Stuck between [beginning](level-0.md) and end,

![black-and-white.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/black-and-white.jpg)

Hoping to see black and white again,

![Stasi_Basement_Hallway.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/Stasi_Basement_Hallway.jpg)

You wonder:

At what point have you met your destination?

![school.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/school.jpg)

You do not know.

![walkway-museum-hall.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/walkway-museum-hall.jpg)

You cannot know—

![columns.jpg](https://signetsandbox.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-356/columns.jpg)

[Until you have passed the threshold.](level-357.md)
