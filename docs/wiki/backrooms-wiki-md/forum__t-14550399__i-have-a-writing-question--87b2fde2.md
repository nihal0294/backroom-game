Best way to write a collab is to ask who you want to collab with! If you wanted to use any of someone's work in your own article, then you definitely need to ask them. That's why I went to when writing [the Serpent's Void](the-serpents-void.md) because I didn't know how to write like the M.E.G..

Ask away and you'll get an answer!
