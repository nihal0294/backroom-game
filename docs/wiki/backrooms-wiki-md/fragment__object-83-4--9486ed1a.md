## Dear Reader

We at **Backrooms Robotics** want your data, and we need it.

I Accept.

Official Statement Regarding Robovacs

We are aware that many groups and individuals have recently been experiencing significant concerns relating to the behaviour of one of our numerous world-class products, the Robovac. We understand that our silence up until this point about these concerns has been detrimental for our valued relationships with our various group, corporate, and individual clients. It is our hope that this statement can begin the process of repairing these relationships and restoring trust in us.

Ever since the first complaints about the behaviour of Robovacs were brought to our attention earlier this year, we have been hard at work investigating the causes of this behaviour. We will be transparent here - the investigation was initially complicated by the fact that the Robovac project had been mothballed since 2015. While Robovac units remained active throughout the Backrooms, we had internally pulled the plug on the project due to it being deemed no longer viable and not matching our standards for operational excellence.

Some may question why we did not make the decision to send out a shutdown order to all Robovac units after we discontinued the project internally. The answer is that we were looking for a win-win solution where the Backrooms could continue to benefit from the services provided by Robovacs, and we could continue to benefit from the resources gathered by them even after the project was mothballed. In retrospect we should have touched base with stakeholders, considered the end-user perspective, and run it up the flagpole before we made the decision to move forward with that initiative.

After reactivating the central oversight system for all Robovac units, we fully began our investigation into the aberrant behaviour exhibited by Robovacs across the Backrooms. We quickly discovered that they were the result of unauthorised access by an unknown threat actor. The security vulnerability they were utilising to take control of hundreds of Robovacs was previously known, but its severity had been greatly underestimated by previous employees.

We chose to keep this investigation strictly internal for the past six months due to concerns that if we disclosed it, the threat actors targetting us might escalate their attacks against us. Earlier today, a security patch was rolled out to all Robovacs across the Backrooms. This update will prevent Robovacs from being hacked again in the future. It is the result of months of hard work.

We are also reaching out to our group and corporate clients for assistance. While we have resolved the security vulnerability in the programming of the Robovacs, we are still unaware who was responsible. We are requesting assistance from our clients in tracking down the perpetrators of this cyberattack, for the sake of the security of the Backrooms.

We are aware that this statement alone is nowhere near enough to repair the broken trust and frayed relationships that have formed over the past few months. All groups and individuals affected by the aberrant behaviour of Robovacs are eligible to apply for compensation in the form of a Backrooms Robotics discount voucher.
