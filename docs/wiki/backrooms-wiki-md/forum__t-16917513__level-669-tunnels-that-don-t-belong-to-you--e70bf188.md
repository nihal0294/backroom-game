+1 sweet, sweet worldbuilding

23 Aug 2024 07:45

This is an excellent worldbuilding piece and story, that also constructs the Sim lore very well. The use of Backrooms historical elements, whether they are established like Hoofstad or created for the page, also give the page some serious credibility by showing how ancient and everlasting the influence of the level is. The Victorian part, for example, was very interesting, depicting an era that isn't that used in the wiki. Some random bits of lore, like Level 9 shifting form, were also quite nice and refreshing to see.

In concept, the level is extremely simple, but it is its implications over history that matters. This page also, interestingly enough, introduces the canon timeline quite well. The future part at the end, presenting the PA as a history piece, was also very clever and felt meaningful. It reminded me of those Encyclopedia Galactica parts in the Foundation series (The one by Asimov, not the SCP wiki). It makes what we felt like the present, with characters we knew about, feel more distant, which helps seeing this period with a new interesting angle. Kind of a nice "twist".

Overall, great page. Not that much of a rewrite seeing the old one didn't really have a concept as far as I remember, but not really an issue nonetheless. The clinical parts were also concise enough for everything to feel interesting, and not some endless (and useless) level description.

Two things I noted and wanted to respond to

- I found the comment about the level number being high quite funny, as I was thinking about this on the wiki just yesteday. If that was a meta comment, I liked it. Perhaps we should close some number ranges for now on most of our numbered categories indeed.
- Phenomenon 1-FR actually does contain a name for defining the noclip that makes one enters the Backrooms, being Initial Noclipping. Do what you want with this information, but at least there is a word somewhere.
