**Disclaimer :** We are still unsure about entity 997-A's properties, so we suggest you remain cautious at all times, until we find a way to get rid of it. *That's not possible, forget about it*

**Description :**  
A cognitohazardous[1](javascript:;) entity, nicknamed "the Whisperer," wanders in the space station as if it were the guardian of that place. It also seems like the entity is feeding on your fear. We have resumed its properties in 3 states.

**State 1 - Hallucination :** Many testimonies indicate that the entity is merely a hallucination, devoid of any physical form, emitting strident and incomprehensible whispering noises. The volume of these whispers depends on the distance between the subject and the entity, becoming louder the closer the entity gets to you. In theory, Entity 997-A will only spot you if you show signs of fear within the radius of its whispers, which is roughly equivalent to a 200-meter radius. What's more, when the entity passes, the surrounding lighting will flicker and the display screens will darken before glitching, while the loudspeakers will emit powerful static noises.

**State 2 - Transcendence :** Once its prey has been spotted, the entity will hunt the wanderer and intensify its whispering. When in this state, the entity is still devoid of any physical form, but its prey will develop an awareness of its exact location. Unable to perceive danger but able to sense it, an alarming sense of panic will be added to the terror already felt by the wanderer. *Run, don't ask yourself questions, it's too late, that thing knows where you are, at any moment. This thing will hunt you down until it catches up with you or until you leave its realm, so run until your legs collapse and your heart stops…*]

**State 3 - Appearence :** If the entity catches a wanderer while in State 2, it will enter reality. The entity will then *dismember* its prey and intentionally leave it in a living state so that it can suffer in agony. After this, the entity returns to State 1.

**Characteristics :** According to several sources, the entity's appearance varies from subject to subject, taking on the appearance of what you fear most. Because of the entity's ability to alter video footage as well as people's perception of reality, it's impossible to draw a picture of what Entity 997-A really looks like.

**Additional Properties :** According to operator Seras, who is the only official survivor of an attack by Entity 997-A. The entity can take control of nearby electronic devices such as display screens and loudspeakers, which it will use to its advantage in order to destabilize its prey.

—Moreover, a pattern of encounters cannot be determined, as the entity wanders randomly. You may encounter it within minutes of entering the level, just as you may wait days before crossing its path.

Recent research has proven that the video surveillance system has a direct link with the entity. More precisely, they are the entity's “eyes”. Indeed, when a camera spots a wanderer, there's a good chance that the entity will head for his or her location within minutes. What's more, despite the use of multiple means, it's impossible to disrupt these cameras in any way. So please stay out of their field of vision at all costs. *Your research leads nowhere. The entity already knows where you are… It's just playing with you.*

**Conclusion :** In theory, staying calm or simply ignoring it would be the only way to avoid Entity 997-A completely. Feeling fear or concern will only make the situation worse. To date, only one case, out of hundreds of subjects sent, has succeeded in repelling the entity.
