Level 251 - Taken for Granite

01 Jan 2022 08:02

Hi! I’ve been thinking of a twist on the “countryside” setting; a Backrooms version of a small island located off the coast of Singapore. [Info on the real world location](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulau_Ubin) . The island has no day/night cycle (its sky is either a perpetual cyan blue or greenish hue) no humanoid entities (except sentient monkeys) and infinitely regenerating flora and fauna. It has the strange property of including geographical and architectural features from every point of its history, so wanderers often find a building has vanished into the wilderness of the woods, sudden cemeteries springing up, or a lake has turned into a quarry. The only stable locations are those marked with QR code stickers for entry (from the 2020-current day policies for COVID in Singapore) and placing them on a building probably makes it stable. Singapore and other islands are visible from it, although sailing just makes you float at sea and never reach these locations.

I still have no ideas for points of entry to this level, as well as exits. Thank you for reading!
