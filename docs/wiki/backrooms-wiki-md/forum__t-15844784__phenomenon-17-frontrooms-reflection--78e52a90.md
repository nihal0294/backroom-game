Liminal Echo = Frontrooms Reflection

08 May 2023 20:40

This is the first time I've seen two people write the same concept with two different presentations. Both phenomena 7 and phenomena 17 are the same thing, down to what they are and do. I guess in the case of phenomena 7, it's this and more, like an expanded version of this. This is the first time I'm not going to be able to acknowledge both articles in my canon lore, since both pages' very existence is contradictory with each other. I just find Liminal Echo the more interesting of the two.

Conceptually, I wasn't as impressed with this one, though that might be because I think phenomena 7 did it much better. It's not that yours is bad. It just has a very similar competitor that is hard to compete with. I also don't have the same interpretation of how things are that you seem to have. I don't think the Backrooms looking like the Frontrooms is just a trick of the human brain, since you can physically feel the shapes of what you can interact with, and it's very hard to mistake writing especially. Not to mention that the things we're seeing can interact with us back (entities) based on the shapes we do see (Among many other pieces of evidence to the contrary). Phenomena 7 may not have directly called out things like voices or radio signals, though it's implied there through the Backrooms mimicking the Frontrooms being the main focus of that. The very internet/Wi-Fi in the Backrooms is an example of Liminal Echo/Frontrooms Reflection.

In this case, it's probably more a bias against the concept as it was presented, rather than it being bad, as well as because of what Robert made. If he didn't make liminal echo, maybe I would have been more interested in this. There is nothing wrong with people making two different interpretations of a concept, though that may be difficult to do in a way that people generally like. I'm just going to have a diminished interest in pages that do the same concept as one I've already seen. And if I think one does it better, I tend to use/form my headcanon around the one I like more.

I also see people trying to use a phenomena rating system. If I use a system, I'd want it to be a consistent one people use. I even planned on possibly making one later that the M.E.G. could standardize as an optional rating system.

Hi Crafter,

Thank you for this really long feedback, it's very helpful.

First is a little clarification in case anyone has doubts. Basically this idea itself — the whole connection between real world and the Backrooms thing — is not a new one, and to some extent a cliche. I had this idea as soon as the phenomenon category was introduced, and the contest thing made me decide to actually carry it out. I do recognise Robert, as an established author both on site and off site, writes way better than me, and in this specific case, his entry and mine could seem and maybe actually are conflict.

As a person who didn't write but read much, both regarding SCP and the Backrooms, I never thought of it as a big deal to have inconsistency on a collaborative site. By nature, a large number of people tend to have similar or conflict ideas. So when I saw Robert's contest entry was basically depicting the same phenomenon (though possibly with different interpretations), I did not drop the idea, but I did expand a bit more on the emotional part, and hope to have done it right. With that said, I know some people see consistency as an important thing and are to downvote or dislike my entry for that, and I accept that.

As for the writing quality thing, I wholeheartedly understand that you think Robert's entry is better than mine (as I said in the second paragraph), and the reason why you liked his entry better is also valid and I have nothing against that. Still, I wish to explain that I did not post this entry to compete with Robert's (I know I can't, at least now) or anyone else's; I posted this simply because I put efforts into this entry and had fun, and thought those efforts would be lost if I drop it. I don't blame anyone who decide to novote or downvote this because Robert's entry is better; it's the risk I decided to take when posting this entry.
