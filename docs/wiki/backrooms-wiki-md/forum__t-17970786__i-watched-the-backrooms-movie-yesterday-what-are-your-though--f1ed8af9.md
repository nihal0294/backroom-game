I watched the backrooms movie yesterday! What are your thoughts?

30 May 2026 12:06

I watched the movie yesterday and I am seriously impressed. It is definitely one of my favorite movies.

I could be biased because I am a huge backrooms fan and someone who is very sensitive to the eerie effect of liminal spaces, but I seriously loved it. Even though the kane parsons canon is fundamentally VERY different from our canon, and I love this canon more (I've authored levels here), the movie NAILED the nostalgic, eerie liminal space effect better than any backrooms media I have seen. The whole time I was glued to my seat, feeling the same way I felt when I discovered this wiki for the first time.

The acting, cinematography were of phenomenal quality, and the wider implications and implicit meanings of the movie were so beautiful because of they were so psychologically disturbing and related to what makes the backrooms so unnerving. Incredibly thought provoking. These meanings were hard to capture, but those who are smart and attentive enough to understand them definitely would appreciate it. It left many questions unanswered (obviously), and eerie confusion but I think the backrooms should be that way and I'm glad they did that.

There are quite a lot of criticism on social media, and of course its fine to not like the movie, though a lot of the criticisms I found unfair. Understanding the implicit things in the movie requires a certain attentiveness and cognitive ability I think a lot of people did not have, with many people saying they were confused about the message, and did not like how many questions were unanswered. I know this is part of the point though and compliments the eerie awe the backrooms should establish. I also think if you feel no nostalgia/curiosity/disturbance when viewing liminal spaces you may not appreciate the movie as much, and you're not the target audience. Many people commented on that it was slow, and thats a criticism I do not really understand because I was shocked the movie ended when it did, if it was 3 hours long I would not have noticed. It is a slower burn horror movie (in a nice way though), I do think people may have quite a short attention span in todays age, and expected the viral AI backrooms tik tok content type of backrooms.

That are my thoughts, for those of you who have seen it, what did you think?
