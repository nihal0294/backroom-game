**The Catalyst**, real name Unknown, is a violent instance of Entity 68 who is dedicated to terrorizing[1](javascript:;) [the Unbound Explorer's Coalition](the-u-e-c.md) and other similar anti-entity groups in order to prove some unclear moral point. They can be identified by their unique frowning husk mask — which depicts the scars underneath it — and the complex armatures they wear on their hands to facilitate usage of deadly weapons[2](javascript:;)[3](javascript:;).

Very little is known about the Catalyst, and most of what is known comes from a single source, that of the U.E.C.'s small amount of public databases[4](javascript:;)[5](javascript:;). It is undeniable that they target groups that believe humans are superior to other sapient entities, but the damage that they do is inconsistent, with the U.E.C. sometimes reporting simple theft, but sometimes reporting large-scale digital vandalism. Their personal history is similarly unknown, as very few people claim to know them before they started their guerrilla warfare. Their current location is unknown too, it is assumed to be in [Level 11](level-11.md) as most of their raids take place there, but they have also been spotted in [Level 466](level-466.md) and [Level 502](level-502.md).

They were first identified by the [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) after a U.E.C. notice declaring The Catalyst an enemy of the organization was sent out in late 2023. After some investigation it was discovered that The Catalyst had been damaging U.E.C. bases and equipment for months, before identifying themselves when caught in [Level 502](level-502.md). After this point, their actions either increased or became more attributable to them.

It is unknown why they attack the U.E.C. at all — when asked they simply said: "I'm sure you will work it out eventually."  
As of now, no satisfactory theories have been proposed.

Reggie glared at the pixels on the screen in front of them.  
  
They ought to have got more data, that was for sure. He would have been happy to do an interview, had they only asked, but it seems that bureaucracy got in the way.  
  
Reggie checked the chat again, and saw that another file had been uploaded. An audio file. Surmising that it might give some more useful information, they took the file, throwing it into the digital media player.  
  
Before playing, Reggie decided to check the metadata on the file. It was recorded on a standardized M.E.G. audio recording device — the type that were distributed to all roaming personnel in case of noclipping into undocumented levels, for logging purposes. They recalled that Ruby had been posted at a new base in some class 0 or other, before being transferred to 11 to do authoring.  
  
He gently tapped the mouse button, and the log played through his headphones.

---

*All sounds in the log seem to be muffled, like hearing through fabric.*  
  
**Ruby Reverchon:** "Okay… This seems to be recording."  
  
*A click of a door is heard.*  
  
*There is only the sound of moving feet and distant voices for the next thirty seconds.*  
  
*A short knock is heard.*  
  
**Unknown:** Come in.  
  
*Another door click.*  
  
**Ruby:** Mr Smith, uh, Walter, I came to discuss that person of interest page we were working on…  
  
**Walter Smith:** Whatever for? You said the writing was going fine.  
  
**Ruby:** It's about this source we got for the page.  
  
**Walter:** What about it?  
  
**Ruby:** I don't see why we should trust the Unbound, we've had wars with them for God's sake. They're one of the most biased groups to get data from, and in any case, isn't it standard to get multiple sources?  
  
**Walter:** I was told to use this source and only this source by an Administrator.  
  
**Ruby:** Which administrator? how? they don't usually come down here.  
  
**Walter:** I received an email containing the data set from administrator Locke on… the second of January.  
  
**Ruby:** I still don't think this is the best course of action.  
  
**Walter:** *sigh*. Remember it's not your job to fault the sources, only to facilitate them into articles. We would be much less efficient if the likes of us had to do research *and* writing.  
  
**Ruby:** Okay. I will return to my writing.  
  
*The door clicks open again.*  
  
*Corridor sounds continue for the next twenty seconds before the end of the file.*

Reggie removed their headphones, and stared at nothing for a while.  
  
This administrator thing seemed rather suspicious, as did the data source.  
  
He would have to do some digging online, before heading over to Beta themselves.  
  
Reggie sighed, picking up their headphones again, and got back to work.
