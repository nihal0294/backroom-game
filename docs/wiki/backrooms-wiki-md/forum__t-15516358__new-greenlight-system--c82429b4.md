New Greenlight System!

07 Jan 2023 21:12

2 weeks ago, the new greenlighting system finally arrived. Here are the new details for this system:

- Create a sandbox at [http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/](index.md)
- Brainstorm ideas, either in your sandbox, or in the [idea critique forums](forum__c-6898686__help__ideas-critique--c0cfab6f.md).
- Work on article in your sandbox.
- Post a thread in this forum with a link to your sandbox and follow the template to describe what your article is about.
- Wait for someone to critique your work within the thread.
- Modify said work according to critique.
- Get three or more critiques within these three categories: **SPaG, Concept, and Formatting.**
  - **SPaG** critiques are where a user points out errors involving things such as spelling, punctuation, grammar, capitalization, and other such mistakes.
  - **Concept** critiques are where a user talks about the concept of the article and how to improve on it in that regard.
  - **Format** critiques are critiques where a user helps the writer fix issues with syntax, CSS and other, similar problems that may not give the article a nice visual look.
- One of these critiques **must** be from a greenlighter or greenlighter trainee.
- Keep track of who critiqued your article in what category.
- Once polished enough, take your article to a greenlighter and give them a link to your draft in order to review your work.
- If your work is good enough/has taken on the crits you received from critics, you can receive the password to post your article!

*People with drafts that have already been critiqued can still go through the old system if they wish, but feel free to migrate your pages to the new system if you want to!*
