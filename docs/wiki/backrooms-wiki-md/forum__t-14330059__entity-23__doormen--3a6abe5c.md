Entity 23: Doormen

20 Nov 2021 22:30

**Page Type:** Entity

**Elevator Pitch:** vaguely humanoid and highly intelligent creatures with hotel bellhop attire. They have long limbs, resulting in the creatures' height being around 7 feet. Their head is a spherical shape with a bellhop hat floating above it along with two mouths on the top and bottom halves of their faces with one asymmetrical eye between them. These creatures tour the Backrooms in search of wanderers and other entities to feed on. These creatures are also capable of speech and use that to lure their victims. When on Level 5 these creatures experience the 11 Effect and act as hotel employees, helping wanderers that pass through Level 5 with common rations.

**Central Narrative:** none

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** These creatures can turn any door into portals to go to and from anywhere that also has a door, even being able to travel across levels. On Level 5, some will occasionally offer wanderers with a portal to go to any level of their choice.

**Additional Notes:** As I have not been writing horror stuff like this for a long time, I'd like to see if my choice of words is on point or not.
