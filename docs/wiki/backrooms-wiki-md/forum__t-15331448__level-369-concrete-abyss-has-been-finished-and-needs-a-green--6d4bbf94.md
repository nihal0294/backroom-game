yeah i dont think ill be able to get crit to you. all you need to know is that its **not ready for a greenlight just yet.** there are spag (spelling punctuation and grammar) mistakes, some pieces of information shouldnt be where they are, and you shouldnt be using the term "you" in these pages. for example:

> There is wi-fi in this level on a network called “grass”; if you use this network you and your device device may proceed to noclip to a random level.

change to:

> There is [[[object 21|Wi-Fi]]] in this level on a network called “grass”; if one uses this network the wanderer and their device may proceed to noclip into a random level.

also, please dont get ahead of yourself and think that your article has absolutely zero errors. there are people that are better than you at writing that you can seek for help.
