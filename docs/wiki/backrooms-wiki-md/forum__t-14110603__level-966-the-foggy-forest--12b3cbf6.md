Hi! Quite a bit of potential in this article, would love to give it some critique.

There will be two parts to the crit: one for grammatical, punctuation, etc etc, another for more (and optional) narrative points/suggestions. Do forgive me for the length of the critique.

[Show Critique](javascript:;)

[Hide Critique](javascript:;)

---

Regular Critique:

> "This level is a foggy desolate forest that is seemingly infinite."

- A comma should be placed after "foggy."
- The word "seemingly" is added before infinite but the second instance of "infinite" lacks that same hesitance. I recommend removing it.
- This is more so a suggestion - older authors tend to capitalize certain words such as "Entities" and "Levels," even when by themselves. Whether you choose to keep them capitalized or not, it would be best to keep it consistent, please.

> "Due to the high possibility of disorientation within this level it is highly recommended that wanderers avoid this level."

- There are two instances of level which may sound wordy to some, so I suggest removing the phrase "…within this level" and adding a comma after "disorientation."

> "Level 966 is an infinite forest with an unusual amount of fog present on the level."

\*Same point as earlier, I suggest simply removing "on the level" to appear more concise.

> " The trees in the forest are randomly dispersed throughout the level with no apparent order."

- "With" should be replaced with "in."

> "However, other supplies such as food are recommended though not necessary."

- A comma should be added after "recommended."

> "There is a large ornate gate on the west side of the forest (compasses do work on this level) that has a large lock on it."

- A comma should be added after "large."
- This sentence overall feels rather… off? How would one know, then, which side was west? Perhaps there's a directory of sorts? If so, do describe it.

> "The list of entities is low for this Level however for some unknown and yet-to-be understood reason, animals present on earth are here. Though the animals presents are just the occiasonal fox, this is the first appearance of familiar creatures in the backrooms!"

- Contradictory phrases here. You mention there are animalS present but the only present are simply foxes. It may be more efficient to simply refer to them as instances of Entity 119, Volpes. If you do insist on keeping this, please refer to the next set of bullet points.
- The first sentence would be better written as "The list of Entities is low for this Level - however, for some unknown and yet-to-be-understood reason, animals found in the [Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md) are present.
- The second instance of "present" has an extra s. This point is self explanatory.
- "Occasional" is misspelled.
- The "backrooms" should be capitalized.

> "Bases, Outposts and Communities"

- This is more so a nitpick than an actual point but I suggest adding a colon (:) at the end of it.

> "The only mostly guarenteed way to access this level is through an elevator shaft present in a undisclosed and unrecorded area of Level 1"

- I recommend removing "only" as it's rather wordy and unneeded.
- "Mostly" should be changed to "most."
- "Guarenteed" should be spelled as "guaranteed."
- "…a" should be "an" as the next word starts with a vowel.
- \* "…and unrecorded" seems unneeded. I recommend removing it.
- A period is missing at the end of the sentence.

> "Just ask one of the wanderers at the Base of Hearts if you can talk to the Mayor, when you meet him tell him Daniel sent you and you want to exit the level."

- This would be better off sectioned into two sentences, the first ending after "Mayor."
- "When you meet him" should have a comma at the end.
- I recommend adding what you put earlier about the gate sending one to Level 20 in the Exits section.
- A nitpick - this is the only section that actually uses "you" which sort of breaks the non-personal flow. I recommend rewriting it so it stays consistent with the rest of the article.

> "Agent Kyloshian"

- I think putting the following code down would be a nicer touch to this article:

> Note: Add another bracket to the start and end of the code so that it works properly.  
> [\*user Agent Kyloshian]

---

Narrative Critique:

> "Due to the high possibility of disorientation…"

- You mention disorientation though you never come back to it. I suggest adding a little something that goes over any mental and physical effects the Level may have on wanderers, to further delve into the level of safety the Foggy Forest has.

> "The trees in the forest are randomly dispersed…"

- There seems to be an odd level of importance given to the trees. Why is that? Also, I recommend specifying how exactly the trees are similar to Engelmann's Spruce trees.

> "The mayor of the base has the key to the gate mentioned earlier."

- Why is he considered a mayor if what he governs isn't a town or city, but a base? Wouldn't he instead be referred to as a manager or leader?

> "Just ask one of the wanderers at the Base of Hearts if you can talk to the Mayor, when you meet him tell him Daniel sent you and you want to leave the level."

- Adding something like "tell him Daniel sent you" seems… rather random. Who exactly is Daniel? Why is he relevant to the Mayor? If you like, you could even add a recording log of sorts that features him and shows why he's meant to be important.

Once you've changed the things you wanted to change, feel free to get back to me or ask another for critique again! With luck, you should be able to get your first Greenlight! Happy writing!

> " The trees in the forest are randomly dispersed throughout the level in no apparent order. The trees are all very similar in size (rough approximations suggest no higher than 35ft tall)."

- I feel as if these two could be put together in one sentence to reduce wordiness.

> Note: There seems to be more inconsistencies when it comes to the capitalization of "Level." Perhaps to make it easier, I recommend pressing Ctrl + F and typing in the word so you don't end up missing any instance of it.

> "Compass required for navigation with this Level."

- Perhaps this would better fit in the first sentence of the second paragraph. Something like "…wanderers that decide to enter the level should bring compasses and various fog lights."

> "Due to Level 966 being a dense forest with a copius amount of fog present. Wanderers can easily lose their sense of direction, as such, its is recommended that wanderers bring a compass with them to at least maintain some form of direction."

- I can see that this was written to make the article seem lengthier but many will find this to be repetitive and redundant. I recommend taking this and the last point and putting them together in the 2nd paragraph.
- "…copius" should be spelled as "copious"
