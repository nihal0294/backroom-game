# SPaG

> Entity Number: 251
>
> Habitat - Various levels and environments

Following the format of most pages, I'd recommend making these both into a title.

> Various levels and environments

This could be me nitpicking, but this is just a fancier way of saying "anywhere". Are there any specific examples of where this entity lives, or where they are more commonly spotted?

> The soldiers wear airtight uniforms

Woah, Woah Woah. You jumped right into it, starting it off with "soldiers". Before describing these soldiers, let me know that *this is a Backrooms entity.* Saying soldiers implies that these are regular human beings. Try "Entity 251 resemble soldiers… so and so."

> airtight uniforms and gas masks and full head covering helmets leaving no skin exposed

replace the first "and" with a comma and add a comma after "gas masks".

> ; Their

Words after a semicolon should not be capitalized.

> 251 entities breaching a hostile G.O.I stronghold

Add a period to the end. There are 251 entities? This is the way it sounds. Replace with "Instances of Entity 251 breaching a hostile G.O.I. stronghold.

> look like a X and

Put quotation marks around "X" and make "a", "an".

> and their native country’s flag on their right.

Do these entities coming straight from the Backrooms have a country on Earth? This makes zero sense, as they have never left the Backrooms. Unless you replace this with maybe the different faction's flags (an idea), I would delete it entirely.

> They wield their country’s standard issue rifle.

It seems odd to have this standalone fact, no? Try adding this into another sentence.

> no clipping

Either delete the space or add a dash in between, as this would mean "you cannot clip".

> We can't use their weapons either,

During clinical tone writing, never refer to the reader or yourself, as this is incredibly informal and breaks the clinical tone. You also want to avoid contractions as it will appear low-effort. Replace it with "Humans cannot".

> except an entity 251

Capitalize "entity" as this is a proper noun, referring to a certain creature.

> either, they seem to jam

Replace the comma with a semicolon.

> are sometimes found on the carrier,

Instead of "the carrier", put the level number.

> the carrier, medic

Add "and" after the comma.

> the Military Hospital level but both are rare.

Again, put the level number instead of "Military Hospital level" and add a comma before "but".

> We are the shield

This is a very uneccesary collapsible, as it only contains one sentence. You can include this elsewhere. Also, add a period.

> Drawing of the B.D.C. Symbol

Add a period.

> They are incredibly friendly

Who are "they"? Specify that this is the entity.

> non hostile entities

Add a dash between "non" and "hostile". "non-hostile".

> if it’s

Change to "it is", as again, contractions are messy and low-effort.

> ; They

Uncapitalize "They". Never use capitals directly following a semicolon.

> They have been seen even offering their weapons to wanderers who didn’t have one.

Huh? You earlier mentioned that the weapons will not work for anything but Entity 251. Change one or the other, but I would highly recommend that this sentence be removed. Also, *most* wanderers do not have weapons, unless it is a squirt gun, knife, etc.

> incredibly hostile to hostile entities

Change "to" to "towards".

> won’t

Remove the contraction. "will not".

> about putting the entity down

This could just be me, but I would change it to "killing". Putting something down is kind of different, as it is usually used to describe ending something's life for the better, and not gunning something down.

> They are found in only small groups

Specify that this is Entity 251. "They" could mean anyone.

> small groups, around 6-15

Change the comma to "of".

> However, they can move in groups up to 300 but this incredibly rare and only when they are going to war. They also can suffer from mental conditions, the most common being PTSD but they can be mentally broke or driven insane, you should approach lone entity 251’s with caution as they could be driven insane, killing any you entity 251 alone and without a weapon is the best option.

This entire paragraph does not make sense, and I could not understand anything. Please remove and rewrite this.

> They are us, I mean they have our same biology, there is no difference between us and them in way, shape, or form.

"They are us" sounds incredibly bad. For one, I shall restate that you never refer to yourself or the reader in a clinical tone-based page. Please remove any instance of "us", "our", "you", and "your" from the page, as well as any other terms that may break the tone in this manner. I would rewrite this as "Entity 251 have a biology almost identical to that of a human being's.", and then go on to describe whatever else.

> They bleed like us, they have the same internal organs, their skin and bones appear to be the only difference as it seems to be tougher to withstand stronger attacks making them less fragile than a regular human.

Again, don't. Use. "Us". I cannot stress that enough. "They bleed like us, they have the same internal organs" Remove this entirely, as you are attempting to go into detail on an already completed subject, and the effort is fruitless. "as it seems to be tougher to withstand", change "to" to "in able".

> They eat and need to use the restroom as well.

"Need to use the restroom" made me laugh. In order to keep this serious-sounding, change to "need to defecate and urinate".

> dose not appear

"dose" should be "does".

> The wretched cycle dose not appear to affect them.

This is oddly worded. Also, capitalize "wretched cycle". Change it to "Entity 251 is unable to experience the Wretched Cycle."

> Variants

For the entire next section, before listing the characteristics of that variant, add a colon.

> The most versatile of all variants and most common they use

Change this to "The most versatile and common of all variants." Add a period at the end before the next statement.

> they use swords a lot of the time

What kind of sword? Also, "a lot of the time" sounds informal. Use "frequently".

> keep entity 251's friends

Capitalize "entity".

> keep entity 251's friends in one piece,

"in one piece" is informal, like I'm chatting with a friend. State this in a more formal manner, such as "contained", "under control", "protected", or whichever fits the best. I do not know what you meant by keeping them "in once piece", but protected sounds the closest.

> they wield pistols, SMG’s or short swords,

Change to "they wield pistols, SMG's, and short swords".

> their Red Cross on their helmet.

"Red Cross" should not be capitalized, as you are not referring to the actual medical group.

> A semi common

Correct to "semi-common", with a dash.

> and above usually)

This is the end of a sentence, so add a period.

> aggressive, it’s best to stay out of their way,

Replace the comma to "and". Delete the contraction and replace with "it is". Change the second comma to a period and end the sentence.

> Usually dressed in aquatic gear they are usually found on aquatic levels but are sometimes utilized as spec ops, identifiable by the strange scuba like combat armor that allows them to be effective in water and on land making them a good shock trooper in levels with deep water and land.

I would like you to rewrite this paragraph. It is not clear what you are describing, your sentence layout was somewhat messy, and "spec" should be "special".

> These guys are CQC experts and usually wield axes or shotguns and carry explosives, Identifiable by their extra body padding.

"These guys" sounds very informal, so change it to the title of the variant. Change the comma before "Identifiable" to a period. Also, I thought that Heavy Assault had extra body padding? Add some differences so they would be distinguishable from one another.

> Pretty rare unless since most of the Backrooms are CQ and narrow hallways, Identifiable because they wear little to no armor.

"Pretty" is informally and breaks clinical tone, so replace "Pretty rare" with "Less common". "unless since" makes no sense. Delete one of the two words. I may be dumb here, but I do not know what "CQ" and "CQC" stand for. I would recommend removing the acronym unless this is very common knowledge I do not know. The comma before "Identifiable" should be a period.

> A 251 marine

Replace with "A marine variant of Entity 251." Remember to add periods to the end of photo descriptions in the future.

> Found in hot places they wear armor suits that keep them cool and hydrated in bleak conditions

Please rewrite this. There is no proper grammar or punctuation.

> Ditto desert but opposite functions keeping them warm and allowing easy mobility in snow

You cannot say "Ditto this, ditto that," about things. Include a full description, no matter how similar, unless there is a specific reason. Also, never use the word "ditto", as it is another clinical tone-breaker. Add a period to the end.

> Flame thrower wielding pyromaniacs, they are crowd control and are regularly deployed against the masked maidens, identifiable by the big ‘fire’ tank on their back.

Please rewrite. This is not well written with grammar and uses odd adjectives.

> A rate variant

Correct "rate" to "rare".

> these guys

For the second time, change this to the variant title (capitalized).

> masked maiden

For the rest of the page, capitalize "masked maiden", as it is a proper noun.

> guard the files, they also know a lot about the masked maidens and are therefore frequently targeted by them,

This should be 2 sentences with a period at the end of each, as they are unrelated.

> They fly helicopters and planes and are armed with pistols or SMG's and a combat knife for CQC,

Make this into a list separated by commas. The comma at the end should be a period.

> Keeping everything running these guys are critical to entity 251's operations, wearing full on protective gear a lot of the time since they handle explosives they are easy to spot, the rest of the time they are wearing a variety of colored jumpsuits depending on their specialty.

As much as I would like to, this is just a very poorly written paragraph in general. Rewrite this.

> Wielding scout rifles and short swords these guys never forgot a path or a face and are highly reliable in any situation, they are identifiable by their light and highly flexible combat armor and futuristic looking helmets.

Same for this one, please rewrite it, as it is extremely difficult to understand.

> Just because we sport a good relation with entity 251 dose not mean they are at our beck and call people! Im tired of getting complaints from entity 251 about this. We don't control them. They can easily leave us to our fate if they want but luckily for us they have morals. Treat them as equals or don't talk to them. They are not our personal kill squad.

Why is this in a collapsible, and why is it relevant? This should be outright deleted, as it seems to be a random filler used because "you felt like it".

> They were discovered in

"in" should be "on". "They" should be "Entity 251".

> specifically, in the middle of a group of entity 251.

Remove the first comma and capitalize "entity".

> But reports from the formation

Remove "but".

> wether they still

"wether" -> "whether"

> Relations with G.O.I.

The same as the "Variants" section. Add colons before each description.

> It’s a good relationship with entity 251 and M.E.G. as our interests have interlinked before

The wording is unclear, "entity" is not capitalized, add a period to the end, and this also seems to break clinical tone, as you used "our".

> It’s iffy,

Did you actually just use "iffy"? *Never* use that again, as it is *very* informal.

> they will attack masked maidens when they go after M.E.G. But won’t attack them if they happen upon them when their out and about resulting in a uneasy peace, it appears the masked maidens are attempting to defuse the powder keg between the two.

Another *very* poorly written paragraph. Please rewrite this in general.

> entity 251 have launched raids against U.E.C. Compounds.

Capitalize "entity" for the who-knows-nth time, and uncapitalize "Compounds".

> The lost neutral relations.

Besides adding a colon after "lost", "lost" should be capitalized, as it is the name of a group, and this section is also pointless. Either come up with more detail or delete it.

> Eyes of Argo

"Argo" should be pluralized.

> A increasingly

"A" should be "An".

> Interview Log 8/9/22

This entire section needs a rewrite, sorry. Poor grammar can *sort of* be excused, but not ignored completely. You also need proper punctuation, and this really didn't go anywhere, and seemed like a filler. I wouldn't delete it, as an interview is a nice touch, but I'd start from scratch on this bit.

> Do’s and Don’ts

Add periods to the end of every one.

> Kimiko has proven a problem……….

Delete this whole section. It's useless and doesn't progress the lore at all.

# Overall

This did not impress and is nowhere near a GL. Your SPaG was abysmal, and while I would love to provide specifics, you did not do well in *general*. A few things you will need to remember are periods, not leading sentences too long, capitalizing proper nouns, adding colons, and having a good sentence flow that makes sense.

The concept is another thing. You informed me after I critiqued Level 251 that you had made changes to the entity so that this would fit Backrooms lore a tad better. I am unsure where that was. You did mention that the guns cannot be used by anything other than Entity 251, but this doesn't matter because there are *still guns*. Guns do not adhere to Backrooms lore. Period. I would rewrite the page in a primitive style, not an armed-to-the-teeth SWAT team gunning down entities. This would drastically affect everything else on the site, and I cannot see this even reaching a GL without MAJOR edits. I would rewrite almost the whole page, IMO.

I did not aim to upset you, but the truth is the truth. Please contact me if you would like fresh ideas, have questions, or have updates to the page. I will not accept a crit request in the next 1.5 weeks as this may take a while to recover. Take this time to jot ideas down on a piece of paper, pitch them to someone, and work from there. It is my recommendation that you join Discord. I do not know what conflicts you may have with this, and if there are not, please do so! We'd love to have you on there, and it would benefit you so much.

Happy writing!
