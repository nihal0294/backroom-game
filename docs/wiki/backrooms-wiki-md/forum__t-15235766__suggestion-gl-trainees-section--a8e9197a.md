[[Suggestion]]GL trainees section

26 Sep 2022 17:21

What I'm proposing here is to add a list of greenlight trainees to the greenlight policy page, if they so wish to add themselves and got staff permission to. This will provide benefits for the site's quality and maybe release some weight off the active greenlighters.  
Benefits:  
1. People can get multiple rounds of critique before going to greenlighters. This can release greenlighters needing to critique almost everything in an article, and can give greenlight trainees more practice with critique itself.  
2. It can shorten the wait time for greenlights. This is self explanatory for both parties, as a work with multiple rounds of crit can be easier to decide if it should be greenlit or not, and if not, you do not need to provide much crit as too why.  
3. A chance to train nondiscord trainees. As a greenlight trainee myself, I often find myself waiting for a good article I can take critique on. The forum section isn't enough for us to train. If we were getting pms of those afraid to post in that forum, we could find more articles that could train us better.  
I await staff responses. Good day.
