Hi!

For those who are like "why is the B.N.T.G. being so sussy?", the version of the B.N.T.G. that I write is a lot more morally questionable and capitalistic than it has usually been portrayed on the site, but I think that's also about where the upcoming rewrite of the group is going to take it!

Also, I made a new B.N.T.G. format. It's not official or anything, but I'm going to be using it in at least one other draft, so I figured I'd explain it here in case anyone wants to use it in their own B.N.T.G. stuff:

[+ Show Format Explanation](javascript:;)

[here it is!](javascript:;)

So basically, my idea is that instead of separating things into levels/entities/objects/etc, the B.N.T.G. just files things into "Assets" and Liabilities". Assets are things that benefit them, such as objects they own or important contacts, and liabilities are things that hinder them, such as entities that attack their bases.

Then they have three headers + any other header that you want to add:

- Value/Danger (depending on if it's an asset or a liability) — ranges from "None" to "Very High". Pretty self-explanatory.
- Priority — ranges from "None" to "Very High". How important it is to allocate B.N.T.G. resources to maintaining or neutralizing it.
- Description — the description of the thing.
- Any other headers that you want.

Also, idk if the lore behind why this sublevel exists was explained that well, so lemme that rq.

[+ Show Lore Dump](javascript:;)

[hi](javascript:;)

So basically, this sublevel works on the same rules as [Deleted Level 14](deleted-level-14.md), in that it's a meta thing where when the level is deleted from the site it and all memories and records of it physically disapear from the universe, but it still existed at some point. Out-of-universe, Level 998 was recently rewritten, which meant that the old version of the level was deleted. In-universe, this deletion process was hindered by the fact that the Backrooms Remodeling Co was working on remodeling the level as it was deleted, and they had used an object called "Reality Fresheners" inside the level, which have the ability to "halt the fluid reality of the backrooms" and essentially prevented that small portion of the level from actually disappearing in the deletion. So basically, this sublevel is just a piece of the pre-rewrite Level 998 that refused to be deleted. Pre-rewrite 998 is functionally almost the same as it's rewritten counterpart (though not as well-written lol), but one of the big differences is that instead of there just being a bunch of entities, the level creates clones of existing entities, including unique ones, hence that being a thing that happens in this level.

Also, if anyone wants me to make an collaborative log for this, I can, but I've found that collaborative logs tend to be pretty hit or miss for whether people will actually contribute.
