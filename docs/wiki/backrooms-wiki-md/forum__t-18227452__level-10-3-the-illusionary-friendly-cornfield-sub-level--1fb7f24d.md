Level 10.3 — "The --Illusionary-- Friendly Cornfield" (Sub-level)

28 Jul 2026 19:33

**Concept Title:** Level 10.3 — "The Illusionary Friendly Cornfield" (Sub-level)

**Sensitive Content:** Psychological manipulation, body horror/mutation.(Note: The recovered logs will touch upon themes of losing the will to live and giving up to the level's anomaly, handled carefully via psychological horror without violating the site's content policy regarding self-harm).

**Central Narrative:** A wanderer trapped in an endless cornfield must resist a powerful anomalous illusion of false nostalgia and voices of loved ones. Upon firmly refusing to stay, the illusion shatters, forcing him to escape a sudden horde of horrific Wretched to find a way back to Level 10.

**Brief Description:** An endless cornfield where the stalks grow well over human height, completely blocking any horizon. The entire level is illuminated by a heavy, unnatural moonlight, though no moon or stars are visible in the pitch-black sky above. Upon entry, an anomalous localized EMP effect occurs: all forms of electronics, digital devices, and battery-powered light sources instantly lose power and shut down permanently. Concurrently, all human physiological needs, such as hunger, thirst, and physical fatigue, disappear completely, distorting the wanderer's perception of passing time. The rows of corn are populated by entities that look like standard, peaceful Facelings. They do not attack; instead, they utilize powerful telepathy to project the exact voices of the wanderer's real-world family and loved ones directly into their mind, gently persuading them to sit down, rest, and remain in the field forever.

**Hook:** Most Backrooms levels just throw monsters at you right away, but this one plays with your mind. It hits the wanderer where it hurts most—their emotions. The level uses the voices of your actual family to trick you into staying, making you feel safe when you're actually in extreme danger. The real hook is the sudden jump scare: the exact moment you say "no" and try to leave, the beautiful illusion instantly turns into a terrifying, high-stakes chase with the Wretched. It’s a tragic trap that only attacks when you try to survive.

**Purpose:** I want to give the readers a rollercoaster of emotions—starting with eerie, peaceful comfort and ending with pure panic. I also want to expand the Backrooms lore by showing that Level 10 isn't just a 100% safe zone; it has hidden, dangerous sub-levels. Plus, it gives a tragic backstory to how some people turn into Wretched, making those entities feel more human and sad rather than just mindless monsters.

**Additional Notes/Remarks:** The final article will feature recovered journals and diaries from past victims to show their slow slide into the illusion. On top of that, it will include an interrogation log of the only survivor conducted by M.E.G. researchers, where he describes the exact moment the illusion shattered and the chase began.

I'd love feedback on whether the escape method (finding a rare patch of Level 10 wheat) feels fair and earned. This is my first article, so I would welcome any criticism and suggestions on what I should correct or change!
