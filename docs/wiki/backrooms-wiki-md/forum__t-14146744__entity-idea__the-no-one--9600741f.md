Entity idea: "The No One"

22 Jul 2021 17:07

**Page Type**: Entity

**Page Layout (Optional)**: entity description, behaviors, biology, and discovery.

**Elevator Pitch:**  
The entity lives in images. There is a 1 in 10,000 chance of the entity appearing in the image at random spots, mostly in the background. It shows the entity in the image but it wasn't in the cameras view. Some images have caught the entity screaming or weeping. It represents a human-like creature with full-body clothing, changing every image.  
There's more small details, just summarizing here.

**Central Narrative**: none

**Hook/Attention-Grabber**: Like all of the other creatures and levels, it's distinctive in its own way. Because no one has ever seen that style before, it immediately draws attention. I believe my entity has the potential to attract the same degree of attention as the other entities.
