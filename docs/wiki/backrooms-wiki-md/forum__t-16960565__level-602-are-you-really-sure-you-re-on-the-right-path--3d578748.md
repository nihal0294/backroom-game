Re: Level 602 - "Are you really sure you're on the right path?"

13 Oct 2024 10:37

An enjoyable read. Here are a few points that stood out to me and could be elaborated on:

I was very intrigued by this concept of "no harm, but pain", similar to a Prometheus myth. The psychological implications of people undergoing never ending agony I think could be explored a bit more. It's an interesting facet, I feel, you don't really expand upon.

I was slightly confused by the sudden apparition of the "white trees" in the article. In the brief description you refer to the roads and dark trees, but fail to mention the existence of white trees. I was wondering if this was done intentionally to startle the reader into asking questions? If so, it was very effective. If not, I'd think it would be a good idea to include all aspects of the level in the brief description and then expand upon them in the "highway" and "forest" sections.

I enjoyed the visual distinction you got going with the notes an messages, dark ones for more primal fearful thoughts and light ones for the clearer civilized mind. Its a nice touch.

Otherwise, I picked up on a few SPaG errors and noted you have a tendency to repeat words a lot. for example in the "Highway Area" you repeat "area" once to twice a sentence. Perhaps you could reformulate around the words "section", "zone" or "territory"? More diverse language will help your article flow better.

I hope this helps, keep up the good work :D

Re: Level 602 - "Are you really sure you're on the right path?"

19 Dec 2024 17:56

Hello! Gave it a read. Congrats on sharing your first level!  
I like the creepy, oppressive atmosphere and the question repeated throughout- it’s like some frightening entity of doubt is haunting the reader. It’s a good start to the level, with lots of distinct ideas to build on. The details of the level and story in the Notes just need a more expansion, as a few things don’t make sense yet. These were questions and comments I had from reading:

CONCEPT

- Because the central idea is “are you really sure you’re on the right path”, I think it would be good to make it easier to get lost, and have more self-doubt in wanderers’ minds. I would emphasize that the roads are like a maze. For example:

*Level 602 is a **sprawling maze of intersecting forest roads**, that is always in a midnight state*

A question: Are the roads in this level winding, bending, and all over the place? Or are they all straight, in a grid of intersections? It says that the distance between intersections is regular, so an infinite grid makes sense. Personally, I think that’s scarier. If all the roads are straight, and the grid is infinite, it would be very hard to remember which road you’re on, how you got there, where you’ve already been, and where you are inside the level. And because they’re all the same, choosing a road that might lead to an exit would feel hopeless. People *would* be constantly asking themselves, “am I on the right path?” And it would makes the panic and loneliness people experience make even more sense. This could also be developed more- maybe focus on wanderers experiencing intense and debilitating doubt, and losing their ability to make decisions. That alone could trap someone in the level for good- having a mental obstacle to overcome raises the stakes.

Making the roads all the same would also make the white trees even more tempting and dangerous. Wanderers would instinctively head towards them just because they’re different- either out of desperate boredom or in the hopes that it could be an exit.

- ***"All entities entering this area will, for unknown reasons, gain a relatively clear self-awareness and a normal set of values for humans."*** What is a “normal set of values for humans”? Is this saying that entities in this area start acting like humans, and no longer want to kill them? If so, I would word this differently. Just say that entities become friendly towards humans, and will even try to communicate if they can. *A normal set of values for humans* is a very complicated, philosophical thing to define, because humans have no “normal values”- we all believe different things. Also- what specific entities can people encounter here?

- ***"Confirm that you are moving forward, not circling around"*** I would explicitly advise wanderers to try and mark the paths they’ve been on, to help them stay calm and not get lost. (Unless this impossible- which would be very cruel and terrifying indeed. Maybe marks and signs disappear or move around?)

- ***"Any light within the forest will be completely absorbed by unknown things."*** If this is the case, then I imagine wanderers will be bumping into trees and tripping over things. Actually walking through a forest in complete darkness would be very difficult. And probably painful, even if people can’t be harmed. I would add a warning about this.

- ***"When entities enter here, their original self-awareness will gradually become blurred."*** ***"Wanderers/entities entering this area will experience strong fear and confusion. Therefore, wanderers/entities often become extremely dependent on those around them after entering this area."*** This is a contradiction- if entities lose their human-like self-awareness in the forest and go back to normal, they wont rely on human wanderers for support. They’d probably go back to stalking and attacking them (if they were predatory to begin with.)

- ***"If you find anything clear in the forest area, please immediately close your eyes and stay away."*** What does “clear” mean, in this case? The forests are pitch black, so are “clear things” trees, objects etc. that are suddenly visible, for some reason?

- ***"All entities in the area are considered communicative, even if they do not have basic vocal organs. If necessary, it can be accompanied by entities to weaken the abnormal impact of hierarchy."*** ***"Find a black octahedron floating within the hierarchy, and contact it to leave this hierarchy. You will be liberated."*** What is the “hierarchy” in this level? This needs an explanation.

- Who are the notes written by? It sounds like an MEG operative- they’ve witnessed survivors of level 602 and how they behave oddly, and now they’re exploring it for themselves. If this is correct, I have some further points and questions:

- Notes written by MEG personnel would probably have a little more context, in this situation. This doesn’t sound like an official log, so maybe this is the operatives’ personal journal? If it’s a personal journal, the page would say something about it, ex “The following is an excerpt from the journal of MEG Opererative [Name]”

- Why would an MEG operative head off to explore a level on their own, especially when people who escape 602 are clearly messed up? Usually the MEG explore in Teams- which makes way more sense, for everyone’s safety. If this person undertook a personal mission to level 602- something extremely dangerous and probably against the MEG’s rules- there needs to be a good reason. ***"The people in the archives department asked me to make a trip when I had time."*** I don’t think this is something the Archivists would do, especially since Compass Point is in charge of exploration. They wouldn’t tell a lone operative to go off on their own- unless maybe it was a very experienced Team Leader, and there was something nefarious going on. Develop this part of the story more- why did this person go to 602?

- In the first two messages, where are the operative and the level survivors? Are they in one of the major MEG bases? Did the MEG pick up a party of wanderers who escaped 602, and have them hospitalized? Provide some more details about where this starts off.

- ***"Surprisingly, those entities that emerge still retain self-awareness and normal values, which is truly unbelievable. But they seem to have lost their memories before entering that level"*** Putting this in Message 1 doesn’t make sense. In the first two messages, the Operative is observing survivors of the level 602, but they aren’t in the level themselves. So how would they know how the entities are behaving? Or did a party of wanderers emerge with some entities that befriended them? This needs to be clarified.

- ***"However, before leaving, someone anonymously sent me a message, which was very interesting. He seemed to know that I had been to Level 602…"*** This last bit is confusing- did they get the message before leaving *to enter* level 602, or before *exiting* 602? And how was this message sent to them?

- If wanderers are suffering personality changes and phobia of the dark after escaping the level, should the level be classed as Psi for mental hazards, rather than N/A? Regardless, I wouldn’t say this level is “safe” or “secure”, because of the effect is has on people, the ease of getting lost, and whatever it is the white trees do to people.

- What do the white trees and flash points do? I like that it comes up suddenly, with no explanation- it’s eerie and makes me wonder what’s going on. But when a level has a phenomenon like this, that’s clearly dangerous but unexplained, it needs to play a greater role in the story. The page needs to “imply what it is without telling us what it is”, or “show what it does”. We need to see the consequence, or else it doesn’t matter to the story. Develop with phenomenon more, and drop hints about what happens if you go towards the light. I think it would be good for the person writing the Notes to have an encounter with the white trees, and write about what happens to them. First-person encounters are a great way of giving more detail about something while keeping it mysterious- because the person can have the experience without knowing what’s going on.

LANGUAGE

- Are the trees around the highway black because they’re like silhouettes? They’re just all black, with no details? Or are they “black” because the level is dark and they’re hard to see? I think this needs some clarification

- “Homeless person” is used throughout the page. I would not say “homeless person"- we’re talking about wanderers, so it’s better to say “wanderer”. Saying “homeless person” implies that we have homeless people living in the level, which is an entirely different matter.

Alright- I know I say a lot and meticulously pick things apart, but don't be discouraged! A lot of "road" levels feature one straight road heading into the distance, so I like the maze-like interpretation of this and would love to see it realized. Just work on the details of what makes sense in context of the lore, and within the page itself. And develop some of the ideas further, because they have a lot of potential.

Cheers!
