*My canon! (is a lie).*

Personally, the canon that I liked the most so far is the **Desolation Canon**, since it is not as difficult to understand as the others that I even need something summarized. But if I were to write something for the **Desolation Canon** (which I'm doing with *Level 10*), it would be easy for me to describe a desolate, abandoned, or long-forgotten environment. So the one I like the most (but not a favorite because I don't have one), would be the **Desolation Canon.**
