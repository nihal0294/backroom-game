[You open a bottle to see if it has Almond Water.](javascript:;)

[Instead, you find a handwritten critique notice.](javascript:;)

Just a quick note, I'm one of the only people who will critique spag. I also apologize in the fact that I am straightforward. Some might call me blunt.

**Level 850 is the 851th Level of the Backrooms, is a level just discovered by a wanderer,  
as he tried to break through the level 149 barrier.**  
Rewrite it to "Level 850 is the 851st Level of the Backrooms. It was recently discovered by a Wanderer trying to break through the barrier on Level 149."

Another helpful note is that you can have a link with the text! Here's the text, replacing the url and title as you want:

> [[[your url | page title]]]

> Level 850 it's a set of snow-capped mountains that are of random size, some reaching over 2,000 meters in height and others under 50 meters, temperatures range from -5 °C to 1 °C being a stable temperature.

"Level 850 is". Put a period after "50 meters". Reword everything after that period to "Temperatures are stable, ranging from -5°C to 1°C." This keeps the meaning of your sentence without the awkwardness included.

> The day and night cycle is something present in the level, however having a random time for it to occur. A maximum of 50 hours for dark and 34 hours for dawn were reported.

It's a bit unwieldy to read. You could say "The day/night cycle is random. The longest day/night was 34/50 hours, respectively, while the shortest was x/x hours." You should include a shortest recorded time for the day/night. I apologize if I got the numbers mixed up, it wasn't completely clarified.

> The level has 2 reported structures which are: Celestial Wall and the City in Ruins.

Now, this could be interesting, but the wording is a bit off. Perhaps use " Wanderers have reported two structures on this Level, which have been called the Celestial Wall and the City in Ruins."

> The Celestial Wall is a great wall located to the north, which separates the City in Ruins and the mountains.

I don't think "great wall" works with the clinical tone we're supposed to use here. Great for a story though. I would use measurements detailing how wide ad tall it is.

> The Wall is seemingly endless, azure blue in color, abandoned but oddly in great shape.

"The wall is theorized to be limitless in length. It is an azure blue in coloration. It seems to have been abandoned, but is sound enough for travel of large groups across it."

- For the color sentence, include a footnote detailing the hex code of it. Here's the text:

> [[footnote]]The hex code is 007fff.[[/footnote]]

If you ever want to make a footnote, replace the sentence inside the opening and closing of the footnote with whatever it is you want there.

> The wall has an inner part where you can find chests, boxes and barrels, which contain supplies such as canned food, bottles of water in perfect condition for consumption and other types of materials, and it is also possible to find axes, swords, bows and arrows and other battle weapons.

"The wall is hollow, with the inside housing several chests, boxes and barrels holding useful supplies like food, clean, bottled water, and first aid supplies. It is also possible to find several kinds of weapons, including swords, axes, and bows and arrows.

- My question is, do the supplies regenerate themselves? What Backrooms-specific items are there, if any? This is a good chance to dive in and do a little research, while also getting to know the site better.

> The top of the wall may contain cannons, some of which are loaded, all the cannons that can be found are facing the side of the mountains, and trying to move or turn them around has proved ineffective.

Does the top of the wall have them or not? If it's liminal(limitless), then it either does or it doesn't.  
Put a period after "loaded" and capitalize "all".

> Any attempt to take a picture of the Wall turned out to be unsuccessful.

"has turned out to be".

> The City in Ruins is on the other side of the wall, it is also abandoned, but unlike the wall it is completely in ruins.

Put a period after the first "wall". After that, reword it to something like "The city is also abandoned, though it appears to have been neglected, unlike the wall."

> In the city it is possible to find the same battle weapons as in the Wall of the Sky, except that most of the weapons are broken.

"city, it is possible".  
"Celestial Wall, though all of the weapons have broken."  
Why did you call it the Wall of the Sky when you already named it the Celestial Wall?

> Going north of the city it is possible to find a sea of ​​fresh water, which has never been discovered if there is an end or not, until then it will be considered infinite.

"Travelling North of the city will take you to a sea of fresh water. No one has discovered an end to it, which has led to the theory of a liminal sea."

> No entity has been documented, however wanderers claim to have seen Light Guides near the Celestial Wall leading them to the sea and then disappearing.

"No entities have been documented. There are claims, however, that there are Light Guides leading Wanderers to the sea and then disappearing."

For the MEG base, give it a base name, like "Starlight Base" or something. Relate it to the name of the Level, as the MEG are the ones that document Levels and pretty much everything else here. That's at least the way the lore goes.

> Only 12 members destined to explore the level further.

"destined"? Very prosey. You should say only 12 have "decided" to explore the Level.

> Eles são pacíficos.

You accidentally switched to Portugese here, so change that to English. "They are peaceful."

> Crossing the barrier in the level 149 takes you to level 850. (although not recommended due to risks).

Take out the parentheses and rewrite it. "This method is not reccomended due to the risks of travel."

> No-clipping on the level mountain 93 will bring you here.

"level mountain 93" => :Level 93".

> Finding a house in good condition will take you to the level 90.

Delete "the".

Just go through your article and make sure every instance of the word "level" is capitalized. THat's all I have for now!

> There are huge valleys between one mountain and another, but it is also possible to find one mountain next to another.

This bit doesn’t make much sense to me. The valleys are large, but also the mountains are adjacent? Mountains do tend to be adjacent to one another, and that is exactly why valleys exist. Not sure why there’s a “but” there suggesting that valleys can’t exist where mountains are next to each other.

> The City in Ruins is on the other side of the wall, it is also abandoned, but unlike the wall it is completely in ruins.

> They live in the City in Ruins.

These two sentences actively contradict each other. The image you use for the City in Ruins also shows buildings notably \*not\* in ruins.

> a sea of ​​fresh water

When referring to water, “sea” specifically means a saltwater body of water connected to the ocean. I’d suggest you call it a body of water instead.

> Eles são pacíficos.

Missed some translation.

Is this a translation of a page already posted somewhere, or did you write it and then translate your draft into English? If you’re translating an existing work, you’ll need to cite it in your author collapsible.

Nothing much to go off of here. There’s mountains, a big wall, and the ruins of a city, but none have much focus here. All of them sort of… exist, but none get the attention I’d expect and there’s not much compelling here to grab my interest.
