Level 184: Iron Dawn

14 Nov 2022 01:31

A massive liminal space made of metal bars repeating a cubic-pattern across the sky. The longest time period here is dawn, making a nice aesthetic for the level. If you go far enough down the level, travelling many miles, you will reach the bottom, which is a long-grass field with occasional daisy fields and willow trees.
