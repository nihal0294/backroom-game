## Description

Entity 404 is suspected to be a neutron star that possesses a supercharged electromagnetic field capable of changing in strength, causing Entity 404 to be considered both a magnetar[1](javascript:;) and a standard neutron star depending on the strength of its magnetic field.

Entity 404 is capable of rotating at extremely high speeds which directly correlates to the strength of its magnetic field. Along with this, Entity 404 increases its magnetic field's strength and uses anomalous energy to suppress [Null-06](entity-52.md)'s behaviors and abilities.

---

## Behavior

When distant from Null-06, Entity 404 exhibits the behavior of a typical neutron star. However, when a wanderer enters Entity 404's vicinity, they report hearing a female voice of unknown origin. From recent investigations, this voice cannot be traced to any previously documented entity. Due to this, it's believed that the voice belongs to Entity 404 itself, despite it being proven to be devoid of any life force, consciousness, or living characteristics.

Recent interviews state that wanderers who have experienced the previously mentioned voice have also experienced a calm and euphoric state where they "lose all concerns and worries". Wanderers also stated that Entity 404 spoke phrases that promised protection from *The Void*, which was later identified as Null-06.

When Null-06's energy output and gravitational pull increases in strength, Entity 404 has been observed to move towards its event horizon and increase its rotation speed to approximately 100,000 rotations per minute. By doing so, Entity 404 increases the strength of its magnetic field to approximately 1211T, and projects the anomalous energy into Null-06 and surrounds Null-06 with its magnetic field and produces massive pulses of energy, measuring at 1260 J, causing Null-06's cataclysmic abilities to cease until it's next cycle. As a reaction to these events, Null-06 tends to launch Entity 404 out of its even horizon at high speeds.[2](javascript:;)

However, when entering Null-06's event horizon, Null-06 consumes Entity 404's matter and energy, causing Entity 404's abilities to weaken over time. At the time of writing, Entity 404's mass has decreased to about half of its original mass. As well as this, its abilities only slightly delay Null-06's growth and weaken its abilities.

## Biology

Entity 404's structure is identical to that of its non-anomalous counterpart, containing an outer crust, inner crust, outer core, and an inner core. However, the inner and outer crust of Entity 404 has been observed to be composed of an unknown form of plasma used to protect itself from its own magnetic field and anomalous abilities.

Entity 404's electromagnetic field has a strength of approximately 1013G, about 200 billion times the strength of Earth's magnetic field. Recent studies show that Entity 404's magnetic field is also composed of energy similar to the anomalous energy *The Clouds of Creation* uses when it produces celestial objects, allowing Entity 404 to suppress Null-06's cataclysmic abilities and to allow its magnetic field to be visible to the unaided eye.

## Entity 404's Consumption Incident

***Incident Date:** 01/18/2022, 13:00*

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/entity-404/quasarnew)

Image of Null-06 consuming Entity 404 while exuding its anomalous energy and gas.

On 01/18/2022, Null-06's gravitational pull and energy output began to increase. Entity 404 was unable to suppress Null-06's abilities with the little mass it had, causing it to be engulfed by Null-06's event horizon.

After approximately 45 minutes of being trapped in Null-06's event horizon, Entity 404's suppression attempts began to cease due to its lack of mass and energy output. From then, Null-06 began to consume Entity 404 at an expedited rate while launching the anomalous energy out into Level 78.

During this this event, wanderers within Entity 404's vicinity report hearing the previously mentioned voice saying phrases such as; "I'm sorry", "I failed you", "He was too powerful", and "I really tried". Shortly after these phrases were broadcasted, its luminosity dimmed, and all current measurements of its attempts at suppressing Null-06 ceased. From then, Entity 404 was consumed by Null-06 which initiated the hyper-growth period.[3](javascript:;)
