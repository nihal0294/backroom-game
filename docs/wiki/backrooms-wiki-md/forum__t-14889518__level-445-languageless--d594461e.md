> Level 445 is the 446th level of the Backrooms

Punctuation is needed here.

> many things that are found in suburban cities

I'd rewrite this as what's shown below to improve the flow:

\*many things characteristic of such a location\*

> Level 445 resembles modern suburbs on the outskirts of a city.

You've already said this information; there's no need to repeat it. Remove this section.

> The size of Level 445 is estimated to be approximately 8.5 thousand square kilometres.

This is a commonly known problem that needs to be addressed more, but how is it know that it is this exact size? It seems very improbable that a M.E.G. agent would measure it by themselves, so it makes this just feel very unrealistic; I'd suggest removing it.

> Examples of this phenomenon are; street signs do not show

This semicolon (;) should be a colon. (:)

> Level 445 can prevent wanderers from writing and drawing1. Writing devices will disappear when out of sight or dropped.

I'd change this period to a semicolon.

> The lead from pencils will break when using them.

*Actually, pencils have graphite in them, not lead lmao.*

> Cars can often hold almond water,

"Almond Water" no longer exists on this wiki; remove references of it from your page.

> Facelings allow wanderers to come into their homes to rest and replenish. Facelings are passive unless they see wanderers stealing items within the buildings.

I'd change this bit as what's shown below:

\*Facelings allow wanderers to come into their homes to rest and replenish; they're passive until a wanderer steals any items from the Faceling's abode.\*
