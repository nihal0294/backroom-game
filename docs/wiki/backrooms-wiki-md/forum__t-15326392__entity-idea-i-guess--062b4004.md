Entity idea I guess

23 Oct 2022 03:14

Page Type: Entity

Genre: Horror

Core Concept: The main idea is that the entity is a highly infectious virus that upon catching, will make the person extremely weak and will feel these symptoms: Vomiting, nausea, dizziness, diarrhea, sore throat, loss of taste, and severe coughing. After an hour, people will start to change in appearance and behavior, losing hair and color and becoming aggressive. After another hour, the person will have completely transformed into a husk which attacks non-infected people to also infect them by vomiting on the person.

If you forgot already, the entity is a virus, I guess.
