# Description

Entity 116 is a bacterial infection capable of transfiguring human victims into biological vessels, colloquially known as "Dentists".

## Bactal Properties

The Entity 116 infection occurs in three stages, concluding in the transformation of its host.

- [*Stage I*](javascript:;)
- [*Stage II*](javascript:;)
- [*Stage III*](javascript:;)
- [*End Stage*](javascript:;)

Stage One | Day 1 - 7

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/fragment:entity-116-1/b)

116 infection; day 6.

During the initial stage of infection, the bacteria will rapidly expand a preexisting cavity[1](javascript:;). It is also capable of surviving on the gums for weeks, especially when inadequate dental hygiene is occurring. Individuals may experience toothaches, itchy gums, bad breath, and towards the end of Stage One, jaw pain.

**Treatment:**

- Removal of infected teeth.

- Application of a semi-corrosive agent to the entire mouth.

- A daily regimen of penicillin and use of an isopropyl alcohol oral rinse.

**Stage One survival rate with treatment: 78%**

Stage Two | Day 8-15

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/fragment:entity-116-1/c)

116 infection; day 13.

In the second stage of the Entity 116 bacterial infection, nearly all of the victim's teeth will show signs of severe infection and decay. The frenulum will begin to lose elasticity, causing the lips to sag slightly around the mouth. The victim will experience frequent headaches, unbearable toothaches, and irritable gums. It is common for swelling of the inner cheeks and tongue to occur as well.

**Treatment:**

- Quarantine the victim.

- Remove all teeth and cauterize the sockets.

- Application of a semi-corrosive agent to the entire mouth.

- Antibacterial medicine, when available.

- A daily regimen of penicillin and thrice-daily use of an isopropyl alcohol oral rinse.

**Stage Two survival rate with treatment: 54%**

Stage Three | Day 16-20

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/fragment:entity-116-1/d)

116 infection; day 16.

The initiation of Stage 3 is marked by the start of uncontrollable salivating. Most if not all of the victim's teeth will have fully decayed and/or fallen out. Other symptoms of Stage 3 include fever, joint pain, headaches, delirium, aches, and a pronounced sagging of facial tissue surrounding the mouth. Any remaining teeth will begin to soften and expand rapidly, usually tearing in the process, causing extreme pain in the victim's mouth and jaw. Cranial swelling will also begin during this stage.

**Treatment:**

There is currently no feasible treatment for late-stage Entity 116 infection; all victims who reach this state must be euthanized before transfiguration occurs.

**Stage Three survival rate with treatment: 0%**

Transfiguration Event | Day 19-22

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/fragment:entity-116-1/e)

Cranial collapse caused by transfiguration.

During this stage the bacteria will soften and consume the upper jaw and eventually the skull itself. Victims report severe, debilitating migraines prior to cranial collapse. At some point during the transfiguration process, the individual will lose consciousness and, ultimately, control of their body. The afflicted individual will remain in this state until the transfiguration process is complete.

Any individual not treated or euthanized prior to transfiguring into a vessel is further classified as "Assimilated" or "Dentist".

## Dentists

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/fragment:entity-116-1/f)

Dentist consuming its victim.

Dentists are the human victims of Entity 116 that have been assimilated by the bacteria. The bacteria alter their host's DNA to prompt the epidermis of the victim to begin creating epithelial cells, giving its skin a gum-like appearance. The entity's exterior gingival tissue continuously secretes saliva that accumulates and trails behind it. This saliva often carries a high concentration of the Entity 116 bacteria. Due to the near universal collapse of cranial structure, the Dentists' only remaining sense is taste, which is greatly enhanced. It is theorized that these entities possess highly developed gustatory capabilities, allowing them to perceive their environment with enough detail for effective predation.

Dentists are osteophagic, showing a particular affinity for human teeth and jawbones. The entities swallow these structures whole and assimilate the material through digestion, regrowing scale-like teeth along their outer surface. Once they have consumed enough material, the teeth that grow become an armor, protecting their sensitive gingival tissue from external harm. Generally, these formations first occur over vital areas, then spread to the arms, and lastly, the legs. After years of consumption, older Dentists will form such a density of tooth formations that their movement will become hindered, making them slower and, in extreme cases, fully immobile.

Dentists use a wide variety of hunting tactics, differing based on age and the level of teeth growth they have achieved. Younger, less armored Dentists use ambush tactics or wait for their prey to fall asleep—at which point they use blunt force trauma to kill their victims before consuming their teeth and lower jaw. After a few months of consistent feeding, the Dentists begin to show more aggressive behavior, actively seeking out prey and using brute force to incapacitate them. These Dentists will often remove the lower jaw of their victims and affix it to their bodies as a trophy or possibly as additional armor. After a year or two of active predation and when their movement becomes hindered by teeth, they will once again revert to ambush tactics. They remain hidden within the environment until prey approaches, then attempt to physically overwhelm the victim. In all cases, Dentists typically target solitary individuals and rarely risk group encounters.

# Protocols

Over the years, the M.E.G. has developed procedures to prevent, mitigate, and eliminate the threat posed by Entity 116, they are known as the M.O.L.A.R.S. Protocols:

**M - Maintenance**

Good dental hygiene is the first step in preventing infection. Always perform routine dental inspections, and consult medical personnel on any abnormalities.

**O - Operate**

Perform immediate dental removal and oral decontamination of all teeth with cavities, breaks, or fissures.

**L - Lockdown**

Quarantine affected individuals and seal contaminated areas. This mitigates the spread and demobilizes any assimilated entities.

**A - Avoid**

The average wanderer should avoid assimilated entities at all costs. Report their location to local groups and warn other wanderers of potential danger.

**R - Respond**

As with all entities, avoiding Dentists is not always possible. Respond to the situation as it develops and defend yourself if need be. Aim for unarmored areas, especially the legs; immobilizing the threat should be your #1 priority.

**S - Sanitize**

Whenever possible, incinerate remains and sterilize affected areas to prevent bacterial persistence.
