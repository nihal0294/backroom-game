"The [Manila Room](manila-room.md) is an oasis in this ever-changing yellow madness. Beige wallpaper (interior and exterior) gives the square one-room sanctuary its drab moniker. Normally furnished with a table and a few chairs, this nondescript room may feature from one to four doors. **This is the only place where people can find each other.** Veterans of the Backrooms are capable of stocking the table with helpful documentation. Finding the Manila Room, more often than not, leads the wanderer to [Level 1](level-1.md).
