No, it still has to be licensed under CC BY SA of some sort.

What that meant was that if someone made art of a level or entity, you can't use it unless it's CC BY SA. For example, if someone took a really cool picture or created a really cool render of a place, unless it's CC BY SA, you can't use it for creating a level page here.
