# Bliss of Finality

| Missing alt text. **The Augur**.  **DATE:** 2008/02/14  **INTERVIEWER:** "Viktor Valentin," Master Administrator, the Eternal Repository;  **INTERVIEWEE:** Nara "[The Augur](the-augur.md)" Kagan;  **FOREWORD:** This transcript is sourced from a hidden recording device placed near **the Augur**'s stand by [the Major Explorer Group](the-m-e-g.md). The extent of **the Augur**'s awareness of this device remains undetermined. |
| « BEGIN TRANSCRIPT » |
| **AUGUR:** Ah, privet, "Viktor Valentin." What a fascinating codename.  **VALENTIN:** Privet. Please, begin promptly.  **AUGUR:** Your wish is my command. *<Closes eyes.>*  ***<The Augur places a hand over the crystal ball.>***  **AUGUR:** …You arrive with a purpose — one you have not shared with your underlings. A clandestine meeting, how thrilling.  **VALENTIN:** You know exactly why I am here.  **AUGUR:** *<Opens eyes.>* Do I? Or is it merely the natural conclusion of events? Ask what you must.  **VALENTIN:** I have read the logs. You predict deaths with unerring accuracy. You foresaw their demise, yet you let it happen.  **AUGUR:** I let the world unfold as it should. Do you blame the ocean for the drowning man?  **VALENTIN:** I blame the tide that pulls him under.  **AUGUR:** *<Chuckles.>* So, what do you seek? Confirmation? Assurance?  **VALENTIN:** I want to know when I will die.  ***<The Augur places both hands over the crystal ball and exhales slowly.>***  **AUGUR:** That is an answer I must not give.  **VALENTIN:** You mean: you cannot give it?  **AUGUR:** If that brings you peace, then yes.  **VALENTIN:** You knew the other's death down to the second. Why am I different?  **AUGUR:** There are answers even I should not seek. Some threads of fate are woven into infinity. Would you wish to see the horizon if it never ended? Would you chase the dawn if the sun never set?  **VALENTIN:** So, you do know.  ***<The Augur looks away, silent. He then stands, signaling the end of the session. The Master Administrator closes his notebook without writing a word and exits.>***  **AUGUR:** …One must imagine "Viktor Valentin" happy. |
