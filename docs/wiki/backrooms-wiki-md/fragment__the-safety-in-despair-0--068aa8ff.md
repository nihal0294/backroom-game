# The Lucky Ending

*Trapped in a dimension of despair and a heart full of hope, luck is your only saving grace.*

You've run five blocks already and desperately crave a break, but stopping for a device you don't recognize is beyond even you. So, continuing to run, you escape the dangers lurking around you and find a staff door.

Living to die another day.
