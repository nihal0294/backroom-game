Before the description while saying the level number it needs a brief description

I would get rid of doomed in this section ". If this is not overcome quickly, the wanderer will lie down in one of the rooms and become incapacitated, doomed to rot for eternity." It makes it seem like your writing a story. So I would use "… and become incapacitated, rotting for eternity" I'm also not completely sure about the use of eternity and later "the seal is broken" but I think its fine. I would also describe how a wanderer could get out of the nostalgia trance but this is not needed.

You should also break up the 4 main areas into subsections under the description and I would make a new section called Entities: and put the matron as a sub category and make a short description about them.
