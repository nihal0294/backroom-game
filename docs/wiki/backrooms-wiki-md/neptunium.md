![Neptunium](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/neptunium/Np)

Neptunium

Neptunium is the 93rd element on the periodic table, it has an average atomic mass of 237 AMU and a density of 20,450 kgm-3. Neptunium has no non-fissile isotopes; thus, it is a bad idea to clump large masses of neptunium in a sphere. Neptunium is commonly used as an intermediate in the synthesis of plutonium, in scientific experiments, and theoretically, in the production of nuclear weapons. Do not eat neptunium or else you will die of radiation poisoning.
