> Level c-offee ୧⍤⃝ is a

The "c" in "c-offee" should be capitalized. Also, the three symbols after "c-offee" aren't visible on all platforms?

> is a secret level of the

"secret level" is no longer a term used on the wiki and you should replace it with "enigmatic level" instead.

> of the back room

"back room" should be "Backrooms" instead.

> and the ୧⍤⃝ symbol comes

Again, these symbols aren't visible.

> comes from Mr. coffee bean

Capitalize "coffee" and "bean".

> 描述

This header is still in Chinese.

> A photo of this level

This caption needs punctuation.

> There is no diurnal variation in this level, and the temperature is about 25-28 ℃.  
> This is a coffee shop that looks ordinary,

Add a linebreak in between these two lines.

> similar to the coffee shop in many

Remove "the" and make "shop" into "shops".

> production area, in which several cold

Remove the comma.

> In front of it is a metal ordering table. "Welcome to the

Make this period into a semi colon.

> in a dream, Canon, clock

"Canon" shouldn't be capitalized in this instance.

> piano of level c-offee.

"level c-offee" should be capitalized here.

> and the signature of Mr. coffee bean is

Capitalize "coffee" and "bean".

> Mr. coffee bean will grow in

Capitalize "coffee" and "bean".

> —— Mr Coffee Bean

Add a period after "Mr".

> proved by spectral analysis to be a simple substance of XR element

Capitalization and punctuation is needed.

> / / \* \* quick clip player Association \* \* / / caution: do not use \* \* the curtain wall at this level \* \* to complete the challenge, and this behavior is regarded as cheating

Italics and bolding doesn't work if you put spaces between the asterisks/slashes. Also, punctuation is needed here.
