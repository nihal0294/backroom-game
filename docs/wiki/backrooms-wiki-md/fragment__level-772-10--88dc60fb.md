##### July 14th, 2025 [06:00]

A macabre representation of a bygone paradise worthy of Pandemonium, the capital of Hell. The Heavenscape of [Level 770](level-770.md), once peaceful, has now become a war scene, torn apart by the cries of pain and the rain of fire pouring down the landscape, sparing no souls: madness is the only word to describe the situation. The horrors of war emanate from the battlefield: World Breachers are now used for crushing Liberation's soldiers alive, while the latter release [deadly chemicals](object-20.md) on the Resistance's fighters, ensuring their slow and painful demise. After hours of hardened battle and countless losses on both sides, the Resistance finally manages to break through Liberation's defenses, pushing them to the brink.

Amid the chaos, Jacqueline takes the opportunity to chase Mutsuki, Liberation's chief researcher, to the Mount's peak. Sensing that there is no point in running any longer, Chinatsu stops and turns towards Jacqueline; holding a bayonet visibly heavier than her frail hands, while the snowflakes falling from the sky land on her already white attire.

  

**Jacqueline: *[Using a reassuring tone]*** Lower your weapon and surrender Chinatsu, it's finished. Your madness finally comes to an end.

**Saint Mother:** Finished? No… I don't think so! You'll have to go through me!

**Jacqueline:** Come on, Chinatsu… You're not cut out for this. I have no reason to kill you anymore… Listen, corruption took control of you a long time ago without you realizing it… But there's something we can do. You're still young and have your whole life ahead of you. So, be reasonable and come with us.

**Saint Mother:** What are you talking about!? And why should I trust you!?

**Saint Mother:** I've never felt better than the day Father Delacroix reached out to me. I've been freed from the corruption of M.E.G. and humanity! I'LL NEVER GO BACK! SUCH SHAMEFUL BEINGS DON'T DESERVE MY SERVICES. SO BACK OFF!

**Jacqueline: *[Rising her tone]*** And your beloved Father Delacroix will abandon you! I suppose he never told you, but we know exactly what he's up to. He doesn't care about you, or anyone else but himself! He's already gone as we speak! He ran off without you! You're all alone, you're fighting for a ridiculous ideology doomed to fail from the very start. Witness your surroundings. Don't you see? Liberation is falling apart! Excuse me, but it's time for you to face reality Chinatsu; you have nothing left to fight for!

**Saint Mother: *[A tone of utter despair can be felt as she speaks]*** Father… leaving me? No… Why would he… He told me I was his dearest child? All alone? …You're just… a LIER! YES!! WHY WOULD I TRUST A CORRUPTED BEING LIKE YOU IN THE FIRST PLACE!?

**Blanche: *[as she remove the mask that covered her face]*** This is the truth! Open your eyes, I beg you, Chinatsu! This psychopath of Delacroix has been manipulating you all along! He's the corrupt one, NOT YOU! I watched you grow up as a kind and innocent girl full of hope who wished for the common good. You've been my student for years; I know that better than anyone. Your achievements could have made the Backrooms a better place for all of us! You could have become the Saint Mother of humanity! So why? Why have you become like that? You are not yourself Chinatsu… The horrible experiments about the [wall masks](object-24.md) and those human "test subjects" used all along… You were blinded by his misleading rhetoric… Isn't it!?

**Saint Mother:** Blanche?… Is that- really… you?

**Blanche:** Please Chinatsu, you have to put an end to this madness! Free yourself from the devil's hands, I'm begging you…

**Jacqueline:** Look, we can only save you if you come with us. We'll forgive you if you do, I give you my word. We can blame it all on Father Delacroix after all.

**Saint Mother:** I…

Commander Elizabeth Graves, severely wounded and deprived of her robotic left arm as a result of the battle, emerges from behind. She fires the [Prometheus cannon](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-771/offset/8) at Blanche, narrowly missing, but the ultrasonic projectile's hiss is enough to temporarily stun her. She then rushes to Jacqueline and grabs her by the neck.

**Enforcer:** YOU!!

**Jacqueline:** Urgh…

**Enforcer:** DIE!!

**Jacqueline:** …

**Enforcer:** !!!

Elizabeth suddenly stops as she feels something on her back. When she turns her head around, she is surprised to see Chinatsu holding the handle of her bayonet planted deep in her back.

**Enforcer:** Chinatsu… You bitch…

**Jacqueline:** Get off me now!

**Jacqueline:** Argh. Anyways. I should be grateful to you, Chinatsu. You just saved my life, I guess I owe you one now… Well. It's all over now, we can go back home.

**Saint Mother:** I can't…

**Jacqueline:** Chinatsu?

As Jacqueline turns her head, she sees Chinatsu directing the bayonet to her throat.

**Jacqueline:** ???-

**Blanche: *[As she just regained her senses]*** NO! STOP!!

After a brief moment of hesitation, and at the sight of Blanche rushing towards her, Chaintsu finally decides to plant the knife deep into her own throat, slicing it wide open… Burning blood cascades down, melting the snowy ground as she collapses.

**Saint Mother: *[Agonizing]*** Urgh…I… I-am……so…so-rry… But… This is… for the good… of all of us…

**Blanche:** What do you mean "for the good of all of us"!?… This doesn't make any sense!!

**Jacqueline:** Damnit…

**[REDACTED]:** Jacqueline, Blanche, are you two alright? We heard a loud gunshot in our way.

**Blanche: *[Sobbing]*** You didn't have to do that… Why??

**[REDACTED]:** For Argo's sake… That's not a pleasant view.

**Overseer Adrian:** Sorry for your loss but I'll have to disrupt the mood… We should probably check on Seras; I saw her going straight to Father Delacroix as we entered the Level. And it's been almost four hours already, who knows what is happening out there?

**Jacqueline:** That's indeed a wise decision, Adrian. Blanche, stay there; the others, let's head to that "[Sacred Domain](level-771.md)".

**Amadeus: *[As he inspects the Prometheus Cannon that was lying on the floor next to the Enforcer's body]***  
Looks like it's ready to fire another shot… Hmm… Gentlemen, it would be a shame not to use it, wouldn't it?

### Left alone with the lifeless body of Chinatsu, Blanche finally places a rose between her motionless hands, pressing the flower to her chest as the snow slowly cover her body.
