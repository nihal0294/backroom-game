22 Aug 2022 04:43

Hello, welcome to my rant about the modern backrooms levels not feeling liminal. Many modern backrooms levels dont feel like the backrooms, or really liminal, in that sense. I feel like this wiki has turned more into "make a level, with a liminal space image prompt" rather than "make a backrooms level, being liminal in its own ways". Even with the liminal space usage, it doesnt feel liminal. The liminality of most levels, is low, if not zero. This is the main reason I like the old wikidot. Not for nostalgia for old levels, rather than the levels actually felt like they are liminal spaces.

The entire first backrooms 4chan post was just wandering in endless, slightly memory-inducing yellowed halls. Now, its turned into "oooh modern level: hello i am entity, welcome to my level, heres weird anomaly thing now". For those whom make levels, make them liminal feeling, not a modern dumpsterfire of emotions, with the unique colored text and different font level names. It just doesnt feel like the backrooms anymore.

TLDR: It isn't about liminal no more

14 Sep 2022 03:37

Liminal spaces are kinda hard to write, and with the fact that sanity has been removed, there wouldn't necessarily be any dangers. And when there are no dangers, writing gets boring, reading gets boring, everything just sorta dies. The only tales would be "Hey, this seemed familiar to like my life in the front rooms!!" or just stories about different fractions or groups.  
If we didn't branch off of liminal spaces, we would only have boring levels, some may be interesting with the correct story, perhaps barely any entities since all the liminal goes into levels and images, maybe lots of groups, and no danger other than oneself and maybe if the level has its own twist.  
That would get boring pretty quickly in my opinion. While it could get good stories out of it, new writers would struggle a lot, and what brings the backrooms alive is new writers. Inspiration, because there's so many good pieces that comes in different genres and writing styles that someone is bound to like a lot. Then, that branches out more and more, each with a unique theme, until soon you won't get bored of this website cause every article provides a new feeling.

#### Short version

Liminal Backrooms can get boring. The Backrooms is considered infinite, filled with many different realities. Therefore, it'll make sense for the backrooms to be branched in many different ways, and that brings in many different new writers to help expand the backrooms even more.
