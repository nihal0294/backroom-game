15 Mar 2023 23:26

This level was real fun to read. It evokes this simplistic feeling I'm such a fan of. There were certain sections of liminality that made me like It even more as well.

Runners are interesting. They are this kind of see-and-kill beings, but since you made them match thematically with the level nature, they feel very much on point (and kinda scary, imagine seeing a black shadow 20 buildings away, it suddenly begins chasing you and is super agile lmao).

One personal favourite of mine is the detail that those who fall through streets clip through it never to be seen again. Overall, it's an article that makes me recall the early, more simple stages of the wiki, but written in a better way, with much care put into it. The length was not a problem to me either, I got engaged for most of the article.

I thank you for reminding me that this community still hasn't lost sight of the conceptual foundations of the Backrooms. +1 From me.
