1000Dumplings said not to rewrite their pages, and I'm personally not going, as in repect to their wishes(Until 1000Dumplings says I can rewrite it I'm not going to), but it is possible to rewrite pages **that are deleted** without the author's permission.  
According to the Rewrite policy:

> You may rewrite an article that is no longer on the site without going through any of the above processes. This means that you don't need to get permission from anyone, and you are not subjected to the time limit. These articles also don't count towards the limit of articles in rewrite. Even so, please make sure you inform staff that you wish to rewrite a deleted article, just so we can keep track.

So, I *could* still rewrite Level Fun without 1000Dumplings permission, or someone else can. However, if I want to use the original source code, I have to ask for his permission, as it says in the Rewrite Policy:

> There are two options when it comes to posting your article.
>
> - Ask staff to delete the original article, and then post your rewrite in its place.
> - Edit the original article.3

The three is a footnote that says:

> This option may only be done if the author of the original article allows it.

Therefore I would have to start from scratch if I wanted to rewrite it without the author's permission.
