Hey, GLT here. I'll do a quick crit for you on this.

> DISPLAYTITLE:Level #:179"Anemophobia"

This is not how you display a title. To display your title, enter your desired title in the prompt that says "Title of the Page". I would also recommend just putting "Level 179" instead of "Anemophobia".

> # Content Warning
>
> This article contains blood.

Eh… is this really needed? As long as you do not have any particularly triggering topics or excessive gore, mentioning blood can be bypassed and let loose without a content warning.

> Level 179 is the 180'th Level of the Backrooms..

Delete one of the periods at the end of the sentence, uncapitalize the second instance of "Level", and remove the apostrophe in "180'th".

> Level 179 consists of seemingly never ending vents. The vents are dark, since no light sources are in the level. So, it is advised you bring a light source.

This section is way too choppy… You spat fact. After fact. After fact. Replace this with "Level 179 consists of a seemingly infinite amount of ventilation systems that are all covered in darkness, as no light sources are present. Because of this, it is heavily advised to carry a light source with you before entering the level.

> The vents are quite small, so you will have to crawl on your hands and knees to travel through the level.

In any clinical tone-based writing, *never* use "you" or any personal words that refer to the reader directly. This is also another choppy sentence and I would suggest rewriting this as "The vents that are present on Level 179 are quite narrow and tight, requiring wanderers to crawl on their hands and knees throughout the level." I would also add an actual measurement for the space, as to bring more detail.

> Both wet and dry covering the all sides of the vents.

This sentence was written very poorly. Include the fact about wet and dry blood being on all sides of the vents in the sentence prior to this.

> The vents are quite similar to ventilation vents, as there is a distant sound of a fan spinning as you traverse through the level.

"Vent" is short for ventilation, so you are basically repeating yourself. Delete this sentence and mention the fans' noise in another sentence.

> When crawling through the level, make sure you keep your grip on reality, because as you travel through the level, you'll start to hallucinate and hear things.

Again, do not use words that refer to the reader. You can replace this with "wanderers are advised to concentrate on reality as to not be driven insane… bla bla bla…"

I am afraid that I am short on time right now, so I hope to get back to you later. The concept seems somewhat generic, but I am hopeful that the rest of the page will tell otherwise when I check later. In the meantime, fix up the issues I mentioned and happy writing.
