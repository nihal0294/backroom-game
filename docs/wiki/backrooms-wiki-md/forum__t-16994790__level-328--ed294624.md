I like the use of pictures and the similarity to level 0 is interesting to me! The descriptions are great, however I noticed there's a lot of run-on sentences in the sense that commas are used more than they should be. I would try making use of semicolons, em-dashes, and just generally compiling descriptions into either more compact or just generally more divided sentences because otherwise it just seems messy, and if a real person were reading that they'd probably be out of breath by the end of the first sentence. for example, I would write "Level 328 consists of a group of buildings similar to an infinite condominium, the buildings are always the same size and width. The level has no day cycle, it is stuck in a state of eternal night, and eventually it can start raining, the rain works very similar to the frontrooms, no anomalous phenomena or events have been reported, but due to lack of studies it is not recommended to stand in the rain or drink or reuse the water in any way." as "Level 328 consists of a group of geometrically identical buildings resembling an infinite condominium. As this level has no day cycle, it is stuck in a state of eternal night; and as for weather, at times it can begin raining. So far, these weather patterns have been typical when compared to the front rooms, but consumption of the liquid is ill-advised due to underdeveloped knowledge of the level."

of course, I probably used a lot of flowery language and vocabulary as I find it makes descriptions easier to section, but really all you need to worry about there is watching your use of commas. to be fair, though, you can section these descriptors in many different ways; I just love seeing semicolons and em-dashes in writing. overall, though, for such a simple concept I do really like how the level is presented and the descriptions will be perfect once the punctuation is fixed. the entrances and exits also speak to me for some reason? I just like those parts of articles since it makes it more interesting when connected to different parts of the backrooms, mostly because I like to ask myself WHY this level connects to these levels, so on and so forth. happy writing!!
