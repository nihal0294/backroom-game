Okay, here goes:

When the letters spell out a pronounceable word, the mind leans in that direction. Words like F-B-I and C-I-A are usually spelled out, because they aren't easily pronounced.

I say Meg and M-E-G interchangeably….usually saying M-E-G if there is a "the" in front of it…. (The M-E-G) or just Meg if there is no "the" in front.
