**Level 2.1** is a sublevel of [Level 2](level-2.md), estimated to be around ten (10) kilometers wide and long.

# **Description**

**Level 2.1** resembles a myriad of complex but Euclidean halls, similar to its parent level, but differs in terms of architectural recency. Tunneling patterns in the area are far more lenient compared to its main level, with junctions being less organized. **Level 2.1** appears to serve a storage role for its parent, containing janitorial supplies and standard work equipment throughout its chambers.

![A dirty white room with various metal lockers lining the walls, lit by fluorescent lights. Atop a row of dark brown lockers is a set of messily placed boxes. A doorway to the left leads to a restroom, and a doorway straight forward leads to another similar room with brown wooden walls through a checkered hallway.](https://backrooms-sandbox-2.wdfiles.com/local--files/penutbuteraples2/2.1pba2)

An image of **Level 2.1** prior to June 2020.

Room "types" vary significantly throughout the sublevel, holding lounge areas and restrooms notably different from [Level 2](level-2.md). Generally, floors are grimy and squalid, periodically covered with threadbare maintenance carpeting. Walls, however, appear to be in mediocre to adequate condition, lacking the expected peeling, cracking, and leaks compared to the flooring. Sufficient lighting for most operational procedures and basic navigation is present throughout the level in the form of white fluorescent lights that occasionally flicker and buzz. Machinery in **Level 2.1** is much rarer than in its parent level, with an abundance of lockers, cabinets, and storage containers, rather than industrial equipment. Containers within the sublevel mainly contain industrial and janitorial supplies, but have also been found to have weapons, medical supplies, clothes, and other objects necessary for survival (excluding food and water).

**Level 2.1**'s air is tolerable at best, as widespread pollutants are common, and a poor ventilation system is present. The level has been described as "hot and humid" compared to its parent, with an average temperature between 27 and 32°C (81-90°F) depending on location. The level lacks significant environmental factors and dangers, making travel throughout easy and efficient. The walls, floor, and ceiling are remarkably thin, covering a layer of concrete presumed to barricade the surrounding spatial perimeter occupied by the level. Entities are encountered semi-frequently, with a population similar to that of **Level 2**.

**Level 2.1** occupies a space above [Level 2](level-2.md), disconnected from the entire level if not for a single access point. As of August 2021, it is unknown if **Level 2.1** is possible to be accessed by any other GOIs. See **M.E.G. Base Alef** for more details.

**Level 2.1** consists of two main areas:

- [*The Halls*](javascript:;)
- [*The Edges*](javascript:;)

![A brightly lit white hallway with rows of exposed pipes lining its ceiling, viewed through a set of pushable wooden doors under an exit sign. Down the hallway, more wooden doors can be seen on the sides, as well as a locker and blue crates before a "DANGER" label at a door at the end of the hallway.](https://backrooms-sandbox-2.wdfiles.com/local--files/penutbuteraples2/2.1pba1)

An image of "The Halls."

The Halls are the main area of **Level 2.1**, taking up approximately 99.8% of the sublevel's floor area. As stated before, The Halls contain storage containers and act as janitorial areas. They contain the majority of resources and entities within the level.

![A large, cavernous, subterranean hallway with exposed rock on the left, and white brick walls on the right. Exposed pipes and periodic fluorescent lights hang overhead. Lining the right edge are multiple windows and doors opened outwards to the view, along with other electrical machinery, red signs, and occasional columns of exposed rock.](https://backrooms-sandbox-2.wdfiles.com/local--files/penutbuteraples2/2.1pba)

An image of "The Edges."

The Edges are the outskirts of **Level 2.1**, acting as its boundary and taking up approximately 0.2% of the sublevel's floor area. The Edges can easily be accessed by navigating far enough from the "center" of **Level 2.1**, generally making up the edges of the sublevel, hence the name. The area is partially made of rock, limiting the area of the sublevel to its 10x10-kilometer perimeter. Excavations of rock have thus far failed to discover any exits or signs of finity.

The Edges are generally cooler and cleaner compared to the rest of the facility, but have shown an increase in entity counts in recent months. Lights in the area fail more frequently and buzz more often than in The Halls, suggesting inconsistent power usage in The Edges. It appears as more of a tunnel than an outside perimeter, as the rock also acts as covering for the outside edge of The Halls.

# Entities

**Level 2.1** boasts a variety of entities native to Level 2, albeit generally rarer, and with certain species glaringly absent. Confirmed entities are listed below:

- [Windows](entity-2.md)

Windows in **Level 2.1** reside in a similar fashion to those in Level 2, rarely appearing at the end of hallways.

- [Smilers](entity-3.md)

Smilers are most commonly found within The Edges, but can also be occasionally found within The Halls in darker areas. Most Smilers found within **Level 2.1** are presumed to have migrated from Level 2, as no major nesting areas have been located.

- [Deathmoths](entity-4.md)

Deathmoths are an extremely rare sight in **Level 2.1**, with less than five reports to date. Other than their rarity in the sublevel, no other differences in appearance, hostility, or behavior have been noted.

- [Hounds](entity-8.md)

Hounds are likely the most prevalent entity within the sublevel, with population density similar to that of Level 2. Hounds show equal, if not more, aggression compared to their parent level counterparts.

- [Wretches](entity-15.md)

Wretches can occasionally be found in the sublevel; however, they appear to be slightly more sensitive to auditory and visual stimuli compared to normal.

- [Death Rats](entity-24.md)

Death Rats generally tend to avoid humans and all other entities. Their hairless appearance remains almost identical to their Level 2 counterparts. Death Rats in **Level 2.1** pose virtually no danger and can essentially be neglected.

# Discovery

**Level 2.1** was discovered in January of 2020 by M.E.G. officials in Base Alpha after receiving an anonymous tip from an unknown wanderer. Upon exploration, **Level 2.1** was considered a possible tenth level in the "Main Nine." This idea was quickly dismissed with the discovery of no exits other than the single entry point located in [Level 2](level-2.md) and its high resemblance to its parent.

# **Bases, Outposts, and Communities**

### **M.E.G. Base Alef**

M.E.G. Base Alef was the supposed first of many "Sublevel Bases," intended to act as backup outposts for Levels in close proximity to the main Greek Bases (ex, Base Alpha, Base Beta, Base Epsilon, etc.). The concept was accepted on February 2, 2020, and work began on establishing Base Alef in June of that year.

Base Alef was designed primarily as a backup storage and living-quarters outpost, employing 53 constituents upon its founding. After approximately one year of steady maintenance, communications with Base Alef abruptly cut off, and all natural entrances to the level were sealed, preventing access to either side of the exit/entrance.

Unfortunately, due to the severe lack of resources required for human sustenance within **Level 2.1**, personnel began to perish due to hunger and thirst or succumb to ever-frequent attacks from both entities and other desperate individuals. Only twelve individuals are currently known to have exited the sublevel since, with the majority of them exiting within the first 48 hours of the incident.

On April 4, 2022, after almost a year, M.E.G. operative Frank Arellano exited the level via [Warpberry](object-74.md). Arellano was extremely malnourished and dehydrated, as well as displaying symptoms consistent with Stage 1 of the ["Wretched Cycle"](entity-15.md) when retrieved from [Level 10](level-10.md). After recovery, he had accepted an interview, transcribed below in **Addendum 2.1-A**.

On June 19, 2025, after nearly four years without contact, the twelfth and last M.E.G. operative, Jared Banks, exited **Level 2.1** into [Level 2](level-2.md). Upon retrieval, Banks had suffered severe injuries and was unable to be resuscitated properly. A journal, multiple stacks of sticky notes, a camera (inoperable), and an audio recorder, among other items, were found in Banks's backpack, serving as a second perspective of **Level 2.1** post-incident. These can be viewed in **Addendum 2.1-B**.

## **Addendum 2.1-A**

**Preface**: Below is a transcribed interview between Dr. Sargsyan and Operative Frank Arellano on May 29, 2022, in [Level 4](level-4.md)'s Base Omega.

**[BEGIN LOG]**

Sargsyan: Firstly, we'd like to thank you for joining us here today.

Arellano: No worries. I'm just happy to be alive.

Sargsyan: Now, I'd like to ask you to tell us about your experience in 2.1. Before "The Lockout."

Arellano: That's what you guys call it? Sure then. I was on Exploration Team-A, second in command to Team Leader Gray.

Sargsyan: Yes, we have that information on file. Keisha Gray, M.E.G. operative, missing, presumed dead.

Arellano: July 11th, right?

Sargsyan: Operative Gray led a full team operation that day. Coincidentally, it was also the date of Incident 2.1.

Arellano: Yeah, it was also the day Keisha died.

Sargsyan: I beg your pardon?

Arellano: We were out exploring The Edges again. Normal, routine stuff, you know. We're just about to go back when half the lights go off, so of course, we pick up the pace. I forgot who it was, but someone trips up, and Keish goes back for them. And then the light above us pops, so we keep going. I took the rest of the team back, but didn't see either of them alive after that.

Sargsyan: My condolences.

Arellano: Thanks.

Sargsyan: Now, do you remember how you got the news?

Arellano: Of The Lockout? When we got back, we just put our gear away like normal and went to log the expedition. Nobody told us anything until I realized the database wasn’t working anymore and went to the IT guy. He told me there was a “temporary connection loss” between the rest of the Backrooms and us, and it’d be fixed quickly. Whole lotta bullshit there. It still hasn’t been fixed, has it?

Sargsyan: As of now, the entrance remains closed.

Arellano: They told us a door appeared, but they’d get it fixed soon. Ben really started freaking out there. If anyone could tell you this place has no other exit, it’d be us. We’d been looking from the start, and still no luck. No clipping just injects you into the floor, doors lead to nothing, you get the idea. If the one stairwell in and out of the level got blocked off, we’d last, what? Three weeks as a whole? Not to mention there’s no food and water, like at all.

Sargsyan: Did you make any notable observations during the first 48 hours of the incident?

Arellano: No, not really. I think I saw a bunch of those admin guys running into the exploration room. Probably stealing those berries, huh?

Sargsyan: A total of ten administrative personnel escaped from Level 2.1 using warpberries twelve hours and twenty-one minutes into the incident.

Arellano: Entitled bastards. Killed off everyone but themselves.

Sargsyan: You stated before that you thought Base Alef would last a maximum of three weeks. Did anything notable occur during these first three weeks?

Arellano: I’m saying at maximum efficiency, we’d last three weeks. Hell, Alef’s the most unorganized base I’ve been to since I got here. We all knew that door wasn’t opening anytime soon. We were stuck, and there were only two ways to go from there. Stupid and dead or stupid and alive.

Sargsyan: What were the general actions taken by most individuals within Base Alef?

Arellano: As soon as the higher-ups realized what was happening, they set up a strict rationing system. If they hadn’t put me in charge of that shit, I’d be dead.

Sargsyan: Are you implying you stole M.E.G. property?

Arellano: I wouldn’t be here without it. Plus, I’m allowed to take “actions against command,” and you guys would review it later, right?

Sargsyan: You are correct. Carry on.

Arellano: Start of week two, we had almost no food. Jared, Alice, Max, they all left. Probably thought they’d find an exit, but none of em came back. It was around Week 2, maybe 2.5, when we cut rations in half, people started complaining, and the entities heard. That night, [Smilers](entity-3.md) ate up like half the base. After that, everything started falling apart. With the higher-ups gone, I tried my best to save at least some of them, but no one wanted help. All they wanted was escape, and I couldn't give it to them. More people left. The rest died slowly. One of them blew a pipe or some shit while looking for water. Heard the pop and scream from inside the base. Week 4, no one left but me. Was real sad to see the place crumble just like that.

Sargsyan: And you didn’t feel guilty at all?

Arellano: This is an interview, right? How about we stay on topic?

Sargsyan: As of “Week 4,” how was your resource supply?

Arellano: I reckon I had enough for maybe two months. Four if I really stretched it. At that point, I made a habit of checking the door every day. Would be a shame to starve when the exit opened the day after I last checked it.

Sargsyan: With your food supply up to that point, how did you survive the next three months?

Arellano: Month 5, I went to the exploration room and slung two bags on my back. No need to stay in that place, and I quit checking the door. No use when it's really never gonna open. I got so fucking lucky after that. Walked out for a few hours and found a body. Looked like Connor, I don’t remember, though. Slumped on the wall with a can of beans in his hands. Emptied his bag, found a fuck ton of food, but no water. Enough to last me a month or two. Not sure how he had so much, but I was thankful.

Sargsyan: Did any other similar experiences occur?

Arellano: Nope. Two months later, I was hungry as hell. Had enough water to last just a bit longer. I think it was around then that my dumbass realized that exploration teams have extra rations. Next few days I spent tracing The Edges, looking for Keish’s body or something. When I ran out of food, I pulled the first aid kits and started chewing the pills. Aspirin or Tylenol or something.

Sargsyan: I assume it was around now you found the warpberries?

Arellano: Yep. Woke up one day, and the first door I opened was a body. I saw a bag on it, ripped it offa whoever it was. The pocket I pulled was tiny, but it had the container. I almost choked on that berry. Anywho, that’s pretty much the end of the story.

Sargsyan: How would you describe your experience within Level 2.1 before the Lockout?

Arellano: Nothing too special off the rest of the Backrooms. I'd been to most of the main Meg bases, and this was about the same.

Sargsyan: How about your experience after the Lockout?

Arellano: Hot. Felt like a Florida summer in there.

Sargsyan: In terms of how you felt about the singular exit?

Arellano: No one really cared. I didn't for sure. People take things for granted until they lose it.

Sargsyan: Hmm. That should be all. Is there anything you’d like to add or say?

Arellano: You guys shouldn’t give up.

Sargsyan: Excuse me?

Arellano: There might be someone still alive up there. You shouldn’t stop trying to help.

Sargsyan: As we’ve said before, utmost efforts are being put to "unlock" Level 2.1. We will consider your request.

Arellano: That should be all.

Sargsyan: Thank you for your willingness and participation, Mr. Arellano.

**[END LOG]**
