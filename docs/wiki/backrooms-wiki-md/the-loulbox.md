My Children.

And hellur, to pay respect to social convetions I will introduce myslef:

I'm , Author/Translator from **Corsica**, I'm trying to substitute the staff and take control of the site contribute by making article as well as translating the ones from our **marvellous** anglophone neighbors. So yeah, I'm from the FR branch and that's where I create stuff.

As for myself, I like games, astronomy and cats. Upthere are my cats, they're my children and I love them more than anything.  
i can also do crits' on FR and I'm mercyless (That's not true).

*Me kind.*

Old junk from the SCP Foundation, I recycle my "writer" experience from there and continue to improve it by sharing lore and ideas from my tortured mind. If you want to join me I'm right here :  
**\_loulou**.

Okay, now done, time for the real thing.
