> Level 741 is the 752nd level

Okay, Super, listen: **this mathematically makes no sense.** If you level is Level 741, it should be the 742nd level of the Backrooms; please make that change.

> 752nd level of the backrooms.

"backrooms" should be capitalized since it's a proper noun.

> The Hallways are painted white

Okay, pause. There is no real introduction to what the level is; the reader has no idea what hallways you're currently talking about! Slow down, take your time, and properly explain the environment without assuming the reader already knows something. If you go speed through like this, then the reader will have absolutely no idea on what's going on in the page.

> to more rooms, the floors

Change this comma to a semicolon.

> are dark oak wood

Add "made of" before "dark".

> "Zig-Zag" Pattern on it

Don't capitalize "Pattern".

> The lighting system and switches seem to work and will light up those rooms and hallways while inside.

I'd personally rewrite this as what's shown below:

\*The lighting system seems to be functional—lighting up rooms and hallways once activated via a switch.\*

> while inside. there are kitchen rooms,

"there" should be capitalized.

> are kitchen rooms, with very little food

Remove the comma here.

> and all drinks are "Boiled Almond Water",

"Almond Water" no longer exists on this wiki; remove references of it from your page.

> Water", Regardless of brand.

"Regardless" shouldn't be capitalized, fix that.

> There will be a few Closets in the halls,

"Closets" shouldn't be capitalized here.

> The Bedrooms have beds,

"Bedrooms" shouldn't be capitalized in this instance.
