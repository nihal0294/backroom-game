I mean, why wouldn't they?

Level 16, Level 26, Level 70, all the previous Level 42's, I'm also sure that Hyperia's 29 slot was previously taken by another level that died before [2017's exodus](level-587.md). Other people get to have that claim to fame for way less, it's not like I'm sitting at desks in the low levels and arguing or whatever the meg overseers did when they were relevant. Oh, and alive, I just checked and they're dead.

Oh, and obviously this will help Esther feel better, but that goes without saying. That's why I didn't mention it until now. Anyways, I'm going to drop in with Holly and check Esther's Instagram to see if she's posted anything yet. The fact she's posted nothing yet isn't a great sign, I admit, but there's still three days until I can stop by, so that's still time for her to feel better without my help.
