If you were to be a MEG operative, at what outpost would you like to work at the most? Personally it would either be Base Alpha, Beta, or Epsilon. Base Alpha because it's the main one with most people so I would feel most interconnected and important. Base Beta because I love level 11 and I think the working and living conditions would be ideal, leading to a happier life. Base Epsilon because I can be a kid again and jump around in that playland! :3
