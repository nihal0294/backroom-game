Entity Concept: "Worm off a String"

09 Aug 2022 00:32

**Page Type:** Entity  
**Genre:** Horror comedy  
Core Concept: The entities are known as Riglurs, basically resembling giant monstrous living versions of the "worm on a string" toy. They come in a variety of shapes colors and sizes, though they are all large, omnivorous entities that typically range between 6-18 feet in length. They appear to be sapient and are fully capable of speech, being incredibly intuitive and eloquent despite their uncannily derpy appearance.

The only notable exception to the rule is the monochromatic Riglurs which are basically eldritch abominations that are the propagators of the species. They come down from the ceiling in the form of a black riglur and a white riglur entwined to one another before unraveling to search for potential prey. They tend to search a level in a radius of 100-300 meters, each headed in opposite directions. The black one is silent, stealthy, and prefers to attack one victim at a time while the white one is more vocal and will lunge at anything that moves. They do not kill their prey, instead, they simply bite them and leave, this bite will have different results depending on whichever riglur bit you. The Black riglurs bite will cause the victim's flesh to gradually break apart into thousands of tiny riglurs, leaving only an empty skeleton behind. The white one's bite, on the other hand, displays more rabies-like symptoms, the body will gradually shrivel and bloat the skin becoming necrotic before a full-sized riglur carrying similar features to its host erupts from the mouth and slides out from the hollowed corpse like a snake shedding its skin.

Additional Notes: The entity is basically part of the "worm on a string" trend that's been coming up the past few days, so I decided to capitalize. This is meant to be disturbing, but in a way that makes people laugh.
