**Level 112** is the 113th Level of the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

**Level 112** is a large room (approximately 800 by 800 meters) filled with extreme fog. Wanderers who have visited this level report that it's impossible to see more than one foot away from you. The floor here is made of a thick carpet of dead Venus Flytraps. The walls seem to be the typical white drywall, and the fog is too thick to know what the ceiling looks like. The only entities here are **Thinkers** and the occasional [Strider,](entity-96.md) and you can find some [Almond Water](object-1.md) and [Smiler Fluid](object-10.md) here.

### Habitat(s): [Level 112](level-112.md)

**Thinkers** are exclusive to **Level 112**. There are estimated to only be 50 in existence, which would easily put them on the "endangered" list if they lived in the [Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md).

### Description:

**Thinkers** are human brains[1](javascript:;) with tentacles spread around the brain stem. They use these tentacles to walk around and attack wanderers.

### Behaviors:

If a **Thinker** spots a wanderer, it will fling itself at them and cling to them with its tentacles. The tentacles will create huge welts, which if left unattended, can start bleeding and cause major blood loss.

### Biology:

**The Thinker** is an average-sized human brain, with all of the normal parts and lobes. The only notable difference is the tentacles arranged around the brain stem. **Thinkers** do not seem to have any sort of eyes, ears, or other sensory organs, and yet they can detect wanderers quite easily.

### Discovery:

**The Thinker** was discovered on 11/1/2020, along with **Level 112**. The wanderer who first discovered this place reported that she was "just looking at the brain thing, trying to figure out how it worked, when suddenly [she] was teleported to this big library!" The library mentioned here is presumably The End.

### **Do's and Don'ts:**

### **Do:**

- Avoid it
- Peel it off as soon as possible if it attacks you
- Bandage your welts

### **Don't:**

- Approach it
- Ignore your wounds
