Hey no worries! It's been a bit rough, but staff is handling things at the moment. Sorry for the radio silence, but the wiki itself is fairly calm compared to the Discord, so all our attention got sucked there. Don't be afraid to reach out to me. It's a bit harder to get staff attention on the wiki than in the discord unless you PM us, but my inbox is always open! It just will take me a second to get to it.

Univ pretty much got the jist of most of it though. But here's a bit of quick rundown.  
-Prominent member was banned due to an anonymous report. People were in a flurry of wanting proof or debating the reason with each other.  
-McToran came out and said that he sent the report. I think it's at this point that the Discord imploded.  
-We had lots of old staff resignations, but we got a lot of new staff as well  
-Staff started to regroup and streamline our processes. One of these was starting to do disciplinary discussions on our staff Oversight page, so that we could be more transparent with the community.  
-There were so many disciplinary situations happening as a result of all of this. They take time to get through unfortunately.  
- As you can see on our Oversight site, we were suspicious that McToran was tampering with votes to get a certain page deleted so that another author could rewrite it. This is the second time he's done something like that, which is why we voted to ban him.  
-Generally, community members were not happy with McToran. All of this caused so much drama and as he was banned, he released a document confessing he got people banned so he could take over their pages and then deleted every mode of contact with him.

As you can see, it's been quite eventful. BUT, things have calmed down now.
