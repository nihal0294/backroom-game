Gonna echo some points here, namely that the visual presentation is really nice, and the writing is very well done too. In some ways, I wish that the orange of File 1 could turn red upon opening File 2, but I don't know if that would go against any intent you have regarding the separation of the two. To bounce off of KAMAMO, I am curious why File 2 exists story-wise. The impression I got was that the Level was corrupted somehow, turning it red and making electrical poles and things levitate. I guess I would like something of a hint of what caused that to happen - did something happen at the firesalt mines? Maybe the call log at the end could help give some insight into the shift. I do feel like the log could use some better explanation, i.e. where it came from and why it's being appended to the File. Otherwise, though, it's looking really good, I hope it gets greenlit soon!
