# Description

Level \_\_, called The Tradin’ Post by its inhabitants, is a 100-acre cement cube with a 15-by-15 hole in its center. As far as we know, this hole is bottomless and anyone who has attempted to jump in has never been seen again. There is also the occasional groan from outside The Tradin’ Post, suggesting entities. Many people call The Tradin’ Post their home and have ended their Backrooms exploring careers here. All around, there are crowded stalls of people bartering and trading items such as but not limited to: food, almond water, books, pens and paper, clothing, wood planks, weapons, mattresses, and animals. These stalls also double as their homes, with mattresses under the counters being a common design.

Once a week at a random time, a crate with supplies will come out of the hole. It is a first come first serve basis and many people die fighting to get these items. Due to this, The Tradin’ Post has a self-sustaining ecosystem and many children have been born here.

The average life for a citizen is to try to barter there items with others at stalls. For example, one may need medical attention after grabbing supplies while another needs food. They could trade a bandage for food and be off with their days. They also keep their children close with them for kidnapping and rape is common in The Tradin’ Post. At night, most of the gang violence happens where most hide at there stalls, trying not to get involved. However, hostages such as leaders could be kidnapped if the gang needs food, and could barter as such.

However, it isn't always so safe due to gang violence. There are many gang factions, but here are the main three:

### The Red Snake

The Red Snake is built upon the idea of total anarchy and loyalty to the gang. Many of the members, using a knife, will make a permanent scar on their left shoulder with their symbol:  
[[include component:image-block  
|name=<http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/awt23/40BFB806-8492-43C3-B826-772EB259752D.jpeg> |caption= The Red Snake Symbol

]]

### The O.B.R.S.

The Orderly Backrooms Society, or OBRS for short, is the direct opposite of The Red Snake. They believe in a government to run The Tradin’ Post. They are represented by a shark, but no official symbol has been used.

### The No-whats

Now, technically they are not a faction, but because they have no faction and the others target them, they stick together for that reason. Many reasons one might stay in this group could be a language or physical disadvantage. Their name is just a nickname from others in the previous 2 factions.

### Entrances:

•In level 40, there is a slight chance that when playing Yoinkers II you can teleport into The Tradin’ Post  
•You can no-clip into The Tradin’ Post through levels 1-4.  
•You can no-clip through any level to get here, but your chances are significantly less than through levels 1-4

### Exits:

•On the eastern wall, there are doors with signs saying levels 1-200, each leading to the corresponding level
