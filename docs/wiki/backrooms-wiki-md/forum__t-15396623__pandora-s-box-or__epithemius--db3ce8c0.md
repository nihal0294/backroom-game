Hi draco! I'm writing this as I read your draft so you can see what I'm thinking as I go through (:  
First thoughts are a lot of 'Frontrooms this, Frontrooms that' so maybe try and link some of those sentences so you do not have to keep repeating… it makes the draft slightly clunky. The entities I am not aware of existing anywhere else so you should describe them! It will help bulk out the draft a bit and make it interesting for the reader. I think the line "There are more that will eventually be documented" is a bit weird because it implies that the writer KNOWS that there are more and different entities while also detracting from the cold, analyst-style writing which Backrooms entries are written in. Ditto for the style of "At the moment of this writing". Maybe try getting rid of that adverbial phrase and just state that there have been multiple wars. Try to call the "people" adventurers/wanderers to keep the style of the Backrooms. I am not keen on the "unusual resistance to being shot by bullets" because there is no logical explanation for this and if these wanderers are more resistant, they should be to other things as well? Again, I feel like this is a strange addition and would take it out and maybe replace it with an anatomical difference to other wanderers. How new is "newly**-**discovered"? Maybe use a footer to link the interview rather than stating it. If you give diameters for the blast, try and use a smaller margin of error. I would just write "up to 7 kilometers in diameter". I like the interview and the outposts. You mention that resistance is met by "lethal force" in Armin Vrante. However you go on to say aggressors are arrested. Which is it? One cannot arrest a dead person (: I enjoyed the note in Ryftani Munitions :D Instead of writing "they started", use "There is". Makes it more analytic. Instead of "you", use the wanderer/adventurer. Is the party you end up in Level Fun, perchance?

Main takeaways:

Write a section on entities.  
Make sure to keep the cold, analytic-style writing up to make it authentic.

Overall, very nice (:
