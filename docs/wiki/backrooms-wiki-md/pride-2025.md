**TS Pride Art Blitz**

We'd also like to encourage our artists to join in on the celebration with our **TS Pride Art Blitz**. Posted through Discord, these pride-themed art submissions will be added to the wiki's Pride 2025 Showcase and our various social media platforms. Just add "Pride Blitz" to the title when posting so we know to add it to the showcase.

**Rules:**

• Each piece should be in some way both Pride and Backrooms related.  
• Art must follow all site and Discord rules regarding NSFW/gory imagery  
• Any non-original assets used must be CC compliant.  
• The use of AI is, as always, strictly prohibited.  
• Entries will be posted to the Backrooms-Art channel any time before July 1st.  
• Don't forget to add "Pride Blitz" to your title when posting.

**TS Pride Crit Night**

As a part of our Backrooms Wiki Pride Showcase, we will be hosting a Pride-themed Crit Night on our Discord server!

Crit Nights are events hosted by Staff members and the GLMP, where we read through previously posted pages and sandbox drafts that follow a certain theme, and we offer feedback to the author! Crit Night serves as an opportunity to get your draft looked at by someone on the Greenlighter team AND counts for one of the 3 required critiques to post a draft!

This June, we’ll be hosting a Crit Night all about LGBTQ+ representation! Both posted pages and work-in-progress drafts are welcome! We’ll be hosting 3 Crit Nights for each respective timezone cluster:

SEA - **Friday, June 27**

NA - **Saturday, June 28th**

EU - **Saturday, June 28th**

**Times to be announced via Discord Events**

We hope to see you there! If you have a page or draft that you’d like to recommend, feel free to [fill out this form](https://forms.gle/unbbwfWoUG9QbwgPA)!
