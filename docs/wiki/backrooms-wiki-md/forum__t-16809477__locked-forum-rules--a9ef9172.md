Forum Rules

10 May 2024 22:44

1. **One thread per draft.** Making multiple threads for one draft is spam and clogs up the forums.
2. **Messages must be substantial and on topic.** Messages in this forum are meant to help writers get their page published to the site. Critique should be at least 500 characters (roughly 100 words) and must follow our [Criticism Policy.](criticism-policy.md)
3. **Rather than waiting for critique, be proactive.** There are many writers with drafts. If you want eyes on your page, it is better to help other writers by offering critique for critique. Tips and tricks for critique can be found in our [How to Critique](how-to-critique.md) guide.

**Failure to abide by these rules may result in disciplinary action.**

Do not reply. This is a staff post.
