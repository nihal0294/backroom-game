Hi!

[You open a bottle, hoping to find Almond Water.](javascript:;)

[Instead, you find a handwritten critique notice.](javascript:;)

> The main area of the factory is a single large fifty (50) by fifty (50) ft room with large machinery that has four levers on a control board each labeled with a setting:

First and foremonst, we use the metric system. You should convert it to meters. In addition, you should replace the colon at the end with a period, as this a complete thought, and you list the others as complete sentences.

> "Burn" will cause any items or beings placed in the machines to be incinerated to ash, "Trade" will change the item to something of equal value, "Infuse" will place a random item inside a being or item placed inside, and a set of words scribbled over with pen that all testing has been redacted.

Replace the commas after "ash", "value", and "inside". These are all complete thoughts, and you can just use them as their own sentences. Delete "and" in the last part and make it a sentence on its own.

> Level ### now has four undocumented entities that roam the halls and one documented known as "the mistake".

Capitalize "mistake".

> On 12/23/22 The S.T.O. was abandoned due to the hostility of the entities on level ###.

Earlier, you said the Level was abandoned on 12/22/22. Consistency is better unless something is actively trying to be inconsistent. I doubt this is one of them.  
  
**[INFORMATION REDACTED]  
Due to the hostility of entities within level ### all ways of entering have been redacted sorry for the inconvenience.**  
It's best not to redact the entrances so that Wanderers know how to *avoid* the entrances.

As for the footnotes, the only one that really needs to stay is the second one. The third is unneeded in an actual article, and the first, people who have read the Writing Guidelines should already know.  
  
  
I'm going to be honest, I was kinda disappointed. The detail in this Level is pretty low. I would appreciate more detail, what's the story behind this Level/ What's the story behind the STO? Things like that add a little extra character to the Level. That's just my opinion. I'd like to hear what others think on this as well, so feel free to play around with this idea.  
  
  
With regards,
