06 Jun 2022 21:44

It's okay. It does put a vivid image in my mind, and it seems to accomplish what it sets out to do.

However, it does feel a bit underwhelming. The scene it displays on its own is fine, but the lack of context prevents it from having much impact. It says nothing as to how one reaches the level or how one escapes (which presumably they can, judging by it claiming that floatation devices give a slim chance to.) As no entrances are defined, including any that wanderers might take accidentally or involuntarily, it feels pointless and like there's no reason one should enter it in the first place and face that fate.

The vivid imagery of the scene that the words paint and visual style of the page help significantly, along with context of why it was written, which ultimately leads me to upvote it. However, it could definitely be improved with context surrounding the scene.

It feels to me like an electric chair with the controls on the armrest. Why would you go in?
