# **Description:**

**Level 310** is a series of severely damaged urban areas that extend across two facing flat planes[1](javascript:;), separated by approximately three kilometers. The two planes are positioned vertically, with one suspended above the other, giving a hazy view of the opposing city in the far distance. Wanderers can appear on either side upon entry; however, determining precisely on which — especially when entering as a group — proves difficult, with an equal chance of ending up on either.

---

Labels mark "city B" and "city A," as well as the distance between them, "3 kms."](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-310/illustration310/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-310/illustration310)

An illustration showcasing the two facing planes.

---

The entire expanse of the level is covered in an abundance of lingering dust and smog that bears a noticeably warm hue. This dense haze heavily obstructs wanderers' view of their surroundings, as well as imparting a distinctive yellow color to the atmosphere.

#### \* Gravitational Shifts \*

Each plane has its own gravity, creating an opposing gravitational pull. This results in a cycle where one side gradually gains strength as the other weakens concurrently, leading to a fluctuating balance between the platforms. Over time, a persistent layer of waste had accumulated, indicating the anomaly existed years before **Level 310** was discovered. The repeated lifting and dropping of this layer onto the opposite plane causes further structural deterioration and increases the risk of wanderers being caught in flying rubble or crushed under the accumulating debris.

[![Narrow alleyway between tall, decaying brick buildings, filled with fallen debris and rubble, notably metal paneling and bricks.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-310/Level310/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-310/Level310)

An image of a typical street within Level 310, taken by a M.E.G. operative.

These gravitational shifts seem to happen at random intervals. Signs of an impending shift include environmental changes, rapid vibrations from objects, and sudden changes in the direction of falling debris. Despite its effects on objects, wanderers and entities appear unaffected by it, experiencing standard gravity comparable to that on Earth.

Urban areas of **Level 310** are interconnected by a series of branching streets with a non-Euclidean layout, where some streets extend infinitely while others loop back on themselves. Because the streets are open to the sky, they are densely covered in debris, often piling up to several meters high, making some buildings inaccessible. Despite the destruction, intact objects can be found among the debris, including personal items, as well as blank road signs, benches, bins, and other objects typical of cities from [the Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md).

#### \* The Day/Night Cycle \*

The level experiences a day/night cycle of approximately 12 hours, during which nighttime extends for over 8 hours. However, measurements have shown that the length of nighttime appears to be gradually increasing, rendering the currently recorded time potentially unreliable.

A large object that replicates the sun — resembling an enlarged incandescent light bulb — floats between the two planes, illuminating the entire level. The sun is visibly cracked, possibly due to debris that has been constantly impacting it for long periods of time, leading to the hypothesis that this may cause the night's gradual lengthening. Regardless of how far a wanderer travels, the object seems to remain in the same position, always appearing to be in the center of **Level 310**. As night approaches, its luminosity gradually diminishes, eventually vanishing completely and plunging the level into darkness.

#### \* The Buildings \*

The buildings on **Level 310** vary in style and form, resembling a typical urban landscape. However, they are heavily damaged, with some rendered nearly unrecognizable.

---

[![Large pile of broken wood, plastic containers, fabric, and other debris scattered across the ground.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-310/Rubble/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-310/Rubble)

The presented view upon entering.

---

Despite their decayed state, these buildings remain the safest refuge from the falling debris. Therefore, it is recommended to use them as temporary shelter, especially at night. Consequently, we have created a short guide with essential tips on how to safely settle within these structures.

General Precautions:

**1.** The buildings on **Level 310** are prone to collapse; find a location that offers a stable structure, such as corners or rooms with sturdy construction.

- These include structures with a solid roof; however, it is important to check for any structural deterioration that may cause it to collapse on itself.

**2.** Avoid any buildings and rooms that:

- a) Have an open roof.

- b) Have a large amount of debris stacked on their roofs; it may collapse under the immense weight, or the debris may suddenly give way and fall.

**3.** When taking cover, only move to another room or building if the current area becomes compromised due to debris accumulation.

- If one finds oneself trapped, it is crucial to remain calm. Wait for a new gravitational shift to occur; this may either lift the debris or create visible openings leading to an exit. Otherwise, do not attempt to force an escape.

# **Entities:**

Numerous entities can be encountered within **Level 310**, including [Smilers](entity-3.md), [Clumps](entity-5.md), and [Death Rats](entity-24.md). However, sightings of previously unobserved entities have been reported on this level. These entities are rarely seen and are only vaguely described. While no hostile interactions have been recorded, it is advised to avoid approaching them.

#### \* Light Seekers \*

[![Semi-realistic drawing of a tall, emaciated humanoid figure standing in smog, having a glowing lantern in place of its head.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/level-310/LightSeeker/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-310/LightSeeker)

A wanderer's artistic depiction of the entity.

Light Seekers are tall, emaciated humanoid entities inhabiting **Level 310**, typically standing between 6 and 8 feet in height. They are easily identifiable by the lampshade or lantern-like protrusion at the end of their necks, which houses a single light bulb that varies in appearance and is encircled by swarms of male [Death Moths](entity-4.md). Their bodies are encased in a thick layer of rusted metal on the outside. While this protective shell effectively shields them from falling debris, it is not impervious, and visible dents appear on their surfaces. The shell appears to be extremely heavy, making the entities struggle to support their own weight, as evidenced by the unnatural shaking of their legs. Occasionally, some entities are seen without limbs, revealing a glowing material that makes up their insides. This suggests that they have no skeletal structures, internal organs, or tissues.

# **Entrances and Exits:**

#### \* Entrances \*

**Level 310** can be accessed by no-clipping through a wall on [Level 44](level-44.md) or through the floor in sections of [Level 11](level-11.md) obscured by dense smog.

#### \* Exits \*

The most efficient and reliable method of exiting the level is to identify and exploit unstable areas that allow for no-clipping through, which leads directly to [Level 255](level-255.md). However, if this option is unavailable, navigating through a sewer hatch provides an alternative exit route to levels such as [Level 34](level-34.md) and [Level 76](level-76.md).

**Subject:** Observational Report #1

**Conducted by:** Operative, Dorris Ayers

**Number of days (in-level time) passed since infiltration:** 3 days

**Findings:**

The skies of **Level 310** are lit by unknown light sources other than the sun. The anomaly is especially visible during the night. Larger and smaller light particles, appearing as thousands of specks, are floating towards the center of **Level 310**, flowing into the cracks on the sun's surface. Since my arrival on **Level 310**, the anomaly has not ceased.

Further investigation is being conducted.

**Subject:** Observational Report #2

**Conducted by:** Operative, Dorris Ayers

**Number of days (in-level time) passed since infiltration:** 5 days

**Findings:**

Upon finding a suitable refuge for long-term observation, I was able to document, albeit vaguely, a new recurring behavioral pattern between Light Seekers. Currently, they appear to seek out other members of their kind, forming large hordes, trying to gather as many of themselves as possible before wandering off to a currently unknown location. I suspect this is not a coincidence, as all instances appeared to walk towards the same direction before disappearing a few hundred meters down the left side of an old hospital building.

**Subject:** Observational Report #3

**Conducted by:** Operative, Dorris Ayers

**Number of days (in-level time) passed since infiltration:** 6 days

**Findings:**

Inconsistent data from the digital time-recording equipment suggests that the days are shortening. Though this number is very subtle, proper time documentation within the level will be unreliable in the relative future. In addition to this, cracks on the sun appear to be worsening by the day. It is unknown whether the light anomalies observed prior are a causal factor in this phenomenon or are meant to slow, hinder, or possibly reverse it.

The entities also appear to be affected by this phenomenon, as more and more subjects have been observed of having slowed movements relative to others or no movements at all, where they remain stationary, staring at the sun. Other specimens have been observed showing signs of what can only be described as stress and/or agitation, aimlessly pacing up and down alleyways.

**Subject:** Observational Report #4

**Conducted by:** Operative, Dorris Ayers

**Number of days (in-level time) passed since infiltration:** 7 days

**Findings:**

I managed to follow a smaller horde for up to several hours before being seen and having to flee from the area. Upon doing so, I had come across a significant finding.

I had observed a group of Light Seekers congregating in the yard of what’s left of a school building. They were holding hands, forming a circle around what appeared to be a pile of lightbulbs of various sizes and designs.

Observation was cut short due to being pursued by the same Light Seeker that had spotted me earlier.

**Subject:** Observational Report #5

**Conducted by:** Operative, Dorris Ayers

**Number of days (in-level time) passed since infiltration:** 8 days

**Findings:**

Upon returning to the site where the ritual-like gathering had happened, I managed to find several carcasses spread across the ground, partially buried by rubble due to a gravitational switch that day. They all appeared to be the same subjects from yesterday. Reason of death: undetermined.

**Subject:** Observational Report #6

**Conducted by:** Operative, Dorris Ayers

**Number of days (in-level time) passed since infiltration:** 9 days

**Findings:**

While attempting to find refuge for the night, I spotted a lone Light Seeker walking towards a relatively intact building and entering. At the same time, a gravitational shift had occurred, which forced me to retreat into the same structure.

The Light Seeker, upon spotting me, did not show hostility immediately, even when my weapon had been drawn. That is, until the entity grabbed my hand and forcefully removed the object from my grasp.

No additional behavior happened until I looked back at the entity’s head.

Slowly, the entity pointed towards a hole in the ceiling, drawing my attention to the lone, failing celestial body above. Bright spots dotted the night sky, drifting towards it. It then lightly tapped its lamp-like head, its light dimming and flickering for a moment before releasing my hand. I retreated to a corner of the room, attempting to interpret the entity's non-verbal communication.

The entity walked under the open sky and put both of its hands against its head, slowly opening its cavity and unscrewing the single lightbulb situated there. It held the bulb before its face and then destroyed the object. From the object, a bright particle emerged, levitating through the hole in the ceiling, and the Light Seeker fell to the ground as nothing but an empty husk.

—————

Shortly after, I was forced to deploy the shield due to the increasing amount of debris that started accumulating around the structure. Once placing the box flat on the ground, I stepped on the induction lever. Immediately, its upper lid opened, extending numerous wing-like appendages from inside, which formed a circle, leaving just enough space for a human to lie under it. I was able to fit, but I had to leave my rucksack behind.

While multiple larger dents appeared on it afterwards, the shield proved to be sufficient in providing protection; however, my luggage and supplies did not make it. Note this as a request for approval to return to base due to a lack of resources.
