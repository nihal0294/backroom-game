+1 and constructive criticism

01 Nov 2022 18:04

This tale is definitely one of the best ones I’ve seen on site. I knew it was going to be good the minute I saw Spanglish included (algún artículo que incluye ambos inglés y español probablemente va a ser muy bueno ;).

One thing I think could have been improved upon is the exposition and the side character. The opening is very good with imagery and characterization, but I notice the protagonist’s companion appears to be quickly cast to the wayside after the protagonist’s entry into the Backrooms. I feel like this would’ve been a more prominent plot point given how close the two appeared to be. Comments here and there concerned about what she’s thinking, if she knows she’s even gone, etc. I also may have missed it, but it appeared to be unclear exactly who said companion was. Was it the sister? A friend? I wasn’t sure.

This is a very minor, insignificant issue however that I only noticed after my third read through and pondering the article for a bit on a walk. Overall, the imagery and storytelling is fantastic. I’m in extremely strong opposition to minimalism and short stories as a concept because they’re almost never done right and come off as either underdeveloped or dry in nature, but I think this is probably one of the rare exceptions where it is executed near perfectly. Very good job. You definitely earned my upvote.
