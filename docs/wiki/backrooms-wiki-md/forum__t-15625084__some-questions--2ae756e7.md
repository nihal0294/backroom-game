Some questions

09 Feb 2023 14:22

Do not talk about taste, just talk about quality: in the case of similar cultural background and the same audit system of the website, the current lower limit of article quality of fandom EN is much higher than that of wikidotEN.

Netizens on the fandomEN side point out the fact that the Fandomen side audit requires higher overall quality than for a particular style.

In addition, I think there are two points: first, wikidot EN uses a prior censorship system, so the style of writing depends to a large extent on the green light, which in turn leads to homogenization. Second, wikidot EN lacks a downvote culture, and many people are unable to face up to the meaning of downvote, resulting in poor quality articles that cannot be reviewed twice by members and remain on the website for a long time.

[You've Got Mail.](javascript:;)

[I lied, just some facts combined with opinion.](javascript:;)

We have downvotes. If we dislike an article, we downvote it. If the author edits the article to be better, we change our vote, simple as that. If an article is deleted due to downvotes, the original code is saved by the staff so a person can rewrite it if they want to. There are also plenty of good suggestions in the discussion forums for that page if the author wants to see what they did wrong. You said that the downvote system leads to homogenization, but that's not true either. There are plenty of different and diverse articles out there. If your saying people from other cultures can't introduce a different style of writing, that's also not true. There are plenty of users from different cultures on the wiki, as well as different backroom branches for people who speak different languages; that's what the language branches of the EN wiki are for.  
I'm not talking about taste or quality, I'm talking facts as best as I can.  
If the staff were to increase the amount of required greenlights, that would mean contacting two reviewers. As of right now, there are too many reviewers who are yellow or red status for that. I'm still waiting for a reviewer to look at my article and it's been over a week since I pm'd the reviewer. Oh, did I mention I've already contacted two different reviewers during that time period? Of course, I'm doing the process via the forums, which is bound to be slower than discord.  
You're also forgetting the plenty of good articles on the wiki that have resulted from this system. If we require two or three greenlights (let's take the SCP Foundation Wiki for example), it would take a long time for a new time author to get the required greenlights. You can't compare two different communities based on their system of getting articles approved. You judge them based off of the quality of their community and of the articles themselves. You'll notice that any articles that aren't well-written will quickly be downvoted below the negative vote limit.  
Not to sound rude, but these don't seem to be questions, they seem to be your own opinions. If you have actual questions, feel free to ask them at the question desk.
