Entering hir password was more than just muscle memory these days. Hir hands felt purpose-built for clawing and clacking at the keys — ze imagined hirself pulling the keycaps right out and revealing the pure unadulterated data underneath. It was those types of thoughts that made hir feel like a drone. One with a remote control buried in the sofa.

Ze had tabbed over to the LOG IN button, but hir hands froze into marble before ze could press enter. Hir train of thought no-clipped and landed on the tangentially related memory of hir teenage life on the web back home (though [home](level-200.md) wasn't one of hir favourite words). Hir old username was "islandshelly" — and what a great username it was, it served hir quite well. From forum to forum ze used to bounce, ze remembered again those fond memories messaging from dawn to dusk, how they used to think hir real name was Shelly, and how much joy it brought hir to—! Ah. Ze was supposed to be working.

After that trip to [heaven](level-404.md) last month, this nostalgia business happened to hir at least once an hour. Ze already had some sort of undiagnosed executive function problem before entering the Backrooms, and this just added to hir problems getting work done. Truthfuly, ze felt like ripping hir memories out with a toothpick.

Ze changed hir username on the database from "islandshelly" to "the-a-river" the other day to stop hir teenage self screaming in hir microphone.

To hir, nostalgia was like… if you had a favourite program your motherboard used to run you, but you'd cut off your power supply with your manufacturer ID due to major incompatibilities of your SAMEORIGIN policies and now, though you still loved her, you could never really enjoy it again.

Ze pressed enter. Was everything normal again? Ze pretended it was.

What was ze doing again?

Oh, right. The M.E.G. had, years ago, used the term 'Colonies and Outposts' as a heading in their level template to designate human communities. This was changed to 'Bases, Communities and Outposts' for a variety of reasons. Good reasons, probably. Ze didn't… remember why? What?

Ze did know why, it was hir decision in the first place. There was just… some fog in the way. What did it matter? Ze knew what job had to be done… so that meant everything was fine. Just a simple mass edit. Didn't even need a regular expression to pull off, which was a good thing because ze didn't know if ze was grounded enough to construct a regular expression.

Ze could see the data right before hir eyes. It was all there. All of it. Just… needed to edit it. Mass edit it. Right now. Alright.

Ze pressed apply, and began to think back again…

Back in the early days, nobody knew what to do about the M.E.G.'s stacks of paper. They had built up an immense archive of level, entity, object and even phenomena pages. Stacks and stacks and stacks and stacks and stacks of documents scrawled by Overseer-A himself. Ze just knew him as "Justin".

This archive became difficult to manage, ffffffragmented and stack overflowing out of the realm of reasonable searchability. That's when ze discovered a technological breakthrough — the [General Public Database](index.md).

The database was accessible via any device in the Backrooms. Why? There was something uniquely digital about this place. Something fundamental. Although ze now knows why, hir lost love was syntax error near `🮲🮳🯃🮳🮳🮲🯁🯁🯂🯃🯁🯂🯃🯃🮳🮲` back then. Ze wished she still was.

Ze came up with the database infrastructure hirself. Master admin — hir dream as a computer scientist… well, ze was no professional, but it was still hir dream through and through. THE dream. And ze did a damn good job.

Ze was pulled back to the present by hir collar when the mass edit finished running. It had been an hour of reminiscing. Fuck.

Ze wanted to check that the top rated pages were still alright. When had ze last checked the top page ratings..? Maybe it would be interesting.
