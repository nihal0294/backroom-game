Level 601: "Totally Non-Existent"

07 Aug 2022 00:52

The level is about an entity (I'm gonna write about it later) that accidentally created Level 601, a level that's habitable by humans. One of his minions decides to create a document about it, and in its attempts to clutter the database with useless information, it accidentally uploaded Level 601. The entity boycotts the minion (it didn't want the word spread about the level), even after deleting the level's page.
