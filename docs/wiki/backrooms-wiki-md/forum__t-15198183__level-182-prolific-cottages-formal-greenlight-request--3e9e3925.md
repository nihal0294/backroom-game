Alright. Keep in mind that I'm not a GL or GLT, so my crit won't be the best.

# SPaG and Syntax

> Class Mushroom

I'd prefer to shorten this to Class M, as the word 'Mushroom' is not mentioned in this graphic.

> The Cottagerooms appears to be an infinitive forest

1. I do not think 'The Cottagerooms' would be a good name. I'd change it to 'Prolific Cottages' or something like that.  
2. The word 'infinitive' is not the same as 'infinite'. Please replace that word with 'infinite', or a synonym of it (such as *endless*, *boundless*, etc.).

> The water is varied in type.

Change "is varied" to "often varies".

> It can be either of the following: Almond Water, distilled water (like in real life), or non-harmful Liquid Pain. The Liquid Pain is devoid of the properties that would make it dangerous, and has a taste that varies by wanderer. It can either taste like ambrosia (a cherry -like flavor) or like a fruit punch that reminds you of before you were no-clipped.

Change this to:

> It can be either Almond Water, normal freshwater, or Liquid Pain; all of them being completely harmless. They also vary in taste depending on the wanderer's personality, sometimes being like ambrosia, or bearing resemblance to fruit punch.

> All are safe to drink, but it's best to purify it first, whether by boiling or other methods.

1. Change 'but' to 'although'.  
2. I would prefer if you do not use shortened forms of certain words (such as "it's, "they've", or "they'll"). They make your article look less formal.

> The temperature of the air is around 45-60 degrees Fahrenheit (7.2 - 15.6 degrees Celsius), and has a mild climate. The day/night cycle is similar to real life, but with one major difference: There's no sun or moon, but the light is there, similar to Level 998. Wi-Fi and cellular data are very strong throughout the level.

Make this information its own paragraph under the 'Description' header.

> The day/night cycle is similar to real life, but with one major difference: There's no sun or moon, but the light is there, similar to Level 998.

1. Change "There's" to 'there is'.  
2. Change "but the light is there" to "but light is still visible during the daytime".

> The entities here are animals like you would see in a forest in real life, but they have mild differences.

Do not use 'you' in a clinical tone based page. Use the pronoun 'one' instead. If you want examples of this, check out one of my [sandboxes](level-82.md).

I'll finish the crit later. please be patient :)

You know what, I don't feel like critiquing anymore. The fact that nitpicking every single thing takes so long is just way too annoying.

That being said;

- I do not think your article is ready for a greenlight. Although I kind of like the idea, the grammar and the clinical tone may need to be worked upon.
- The Cottagerooms is still not a good name. "The (attribute)rooms" is meant to be a collection of rooms with said attribute. Take [The Paper-Rooms](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-409), for example. It is a collection of rooms made of paper. However, your page does not suit the name as the level is a forest and not a collection of rooms. Please change it.
- I do not think this level deserves to be an enigmatic level. Enigmatic levels do not have a number solely because they are incredibly mysterious and strange. Your level does not have that. I suggest you give the level a number again. It just isn't that mysterious at all.
- Please apply the changes that I have suggested. I'm trying to improve your article, after all.
- The term 'infinitive' still has nothing to do with the term 'infinite' and should be removed.
