# Godhand 3

# No Cost Too Great

*Many months had gone on and on, streets emptied and stores closed. Streets littered with sleeping bodies and stores painted with red colors. It was a time of silence and stalking, where no one spoke so much as a whisper lest they be noticed by the hand that mocks. Behind forged walls, man thrived on and on as well, on top of them were three seer-heads disgruntled at the hand way above them, with its five fingers that squeeze on their necks each time they breathe a sigh of relief. But this was not the end yet, the seer-heads' plan is only in its second stage. Far from expected success, their current conflict stands in a stalemate, with the hand only growing more powerful from excessive violence and hysteria instilled in the minds of most living creatures, and the seer-heads' eagle only tiring itself into paralysis and laziness. What should the seer-heads do? Do they have naught but the rushing of their plan? These thoughts lingered long in the seer-heads' minds as they desperately stand prepared.*

---

*Overseer's Note*

*Steele stands tallest amongst the fingers of Godhand, in a literal sense the middle finger of the damned group. He may not be as strong as the rest, but it is evident who commands Godhand; a cursed hand with a taste for blood has a working mind — I would never have guessed such irregularity had it not been for Steele's sole existence. He is quite the charismatic enigma for one such of his kind, a negotiator and a politician through and through. Steele oozes a sinister mysteriousness about him, as if ready to pounce like his mindless minions. Of all the vessels he is the most well-known and most controversial. I could only compare him to a dormant volcano, calm and serene but ready to erupt in anguish and hatred at any moment. I wonder, is he the result of violence being aware of itself? A rare breed of controlled rage? Or has his mysterious benefactor given him this miracle? I would advise one such as you to be wary of this mystificator.*

---

*Director's Note*

*Alas, a year had gone by in a flash and most had grown accustomed to life within cells, inside walls of makeshift yet durable sturdiness, and most importantly life in constant bloodshed. The radio plays endless news accompanied by old-fashioned music, a throwback to some and a mystery for most as they have been born in this forsaken place. Nevertheless, the bitter stalemate relents as the Overseers look for any opportunity to call an offensive. But each time one cuts down a Godhand minion, two take its place. It is gradual, yes, but it would eventually overwhelm the seemingly endless resources of the M.E.G. and its allies. However, Godhand holds the same problem, for it is running out of people to influence — most have been shut out behind gates protected by only the best weaponry available and well-trained men. It will not be long before either side collapses from exhaustion.*

---

*Wanderer's Note*

*What is the meaning of all this? I refuse to place my faith in the truth that this is all but a means of displaying violence unbound, done in the name of Godhand. A nomad such as I find no place of solace in this desolate place, where the air is strewn thick with blood-mist, its smell so potent that one could taste its iron dread. Now I walk the streets toward apparent salvation, inside the walls of the M.E.G., within the wings of its eagle. But I hear not even they will be able to hold any longer against those red-veined berserkers and their leaders. A part of me believes that this prolonged truce will soon come to a blazing end.*

---

August 12, 2027  
Disaster in Level Eleven

After a long-drawn stalemate between the M.E.G. and Godhand, on August 12, 2027, at 11:00 PM, Godhand berserkers breached one of the two major gates of Base Beta. Casualties are estimated to be at least 154 as of current, with 120 surviving wanderers and M.E.G. personnel. The stationed Overseer and directors have been safely evacuated from Level 11 while the rest of the survivors await further support from available teams and regiments in the area. M.E.G. Special Division "Cerulean Edict" (External Operations) will be tasked with saving and destroying all information in Base Beta and declare the base to be officially defunct and overrun by Godhand berserkers. Therefore, Level 11 will no longer receive any support from the M.E.G. after this completion.

August 16, 2027  
Breakthrough in Level One

After months of grueling trial and error, M.E.G. Special Division "Cerulean Edict" (Internal Operations) officially announces the full development of an anti-Godhand weapon in an M.E.G. public conference. According to Dr. Cain Bartholomew, Head Researcher of the anti-Godhand project, the weapon contains an agent that forcefully puts the body into a state of lull and calmness, nullifying the violent nature of Godhand effectively. Dr. Bartholomew states that this agent is non-lethal and is more akin to a cure than it is, for example, poison. He has said that this weapon has already been tested on different subjects in varying stages of Godhand influence, and believes that it is capable of completely eradicating any Godhand influence in the body and mind. There is no specified date for when the weapon will be distributed, but Dr. Bartholomew states that it shall be as soon as possible.

August 19, 2027  
Massacre in Level Four

During an anti-Godhand weapon testing conducted in Level 4, an unnamed Godhand Vessel and approximately 250 Godhand berserkers laid waste to Base Omega after successfully breaching three of the five major gates to the base. Only the stationed Overseer, Directors, and personal bodyguards were safely evacuated. 329 lives were lost in the massacre and Base Omega is condemned to be defunct as of August 19, 2027. Level 4 will no longer receive any support from the M.E.G. and its allies.

August 27, 2027  
Eradication in Level Nine

The M.E.G. enacts Phase Three of Operation "Severance" after confirming the decline of Godhand berserkers in Level Nine, one of the main hubs for Godhand. M.E.G. Forces were able to eradicate all Godhand berserkers in the area and almost captured another Godhand Vessel whose identity is also unknown. The investigation will continue on the current state of Godhand and efforts into declaring an official counter-attack have been considered by the Oversight Council. All forces of the M.E.G. and its allies will rally in former and defunct M.E.G. bases and await further orders.

The conflict lasted a decade. And the M.E.G. is its pyrrhic victor. Godhand and its vessels continue to exist, but not its berserkers, its people of violence. The deviant leaders of the U.E.C. were executed. Steele is nowhere to be found, and so are his fellow vessels. Godhand is the true victor. This will not be the last of it. It will not be long. The glory of Godhand remains impure. Godhand requires a pure vessel. It will not be long. Godhand will await those bold enough to partake in the ritual of violent truth. This will be its end for the moment.
