Entity Idea: Hearlings

21 Oct 2022 04:32

Description: Hearlings are bat-like entities, except they have 4 human ears pointing in 4 directions. Because of these ears, they have exceptional hearing and their echolocation is double that of normal bats.

Abilities: Hearlings are capable of producing powerful sonic blasts of sound and music. Different genres of music have been heard being played when they attack such as country, classical, rock & roll, and death metal. These blasts can cause hearing loss, dizziness, bleeding of the ears, and nausea.

Hearlings are physically the weakest creature in their level unless they group up. Once gathered together, they will combine their sonic blasts to cause massive damage.
