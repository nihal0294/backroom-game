# Level 250 Extended Document

As the night sky folds in around us  
My heart stutters to see your shining face once more  
Such a miracle that you had found us  
I'd never imagined this happening before  
O my child, product of the ages,  
Your love brings wonder to a world so dark  
That smile's healed this book's ripped pages  
But no memories seem alike, or so stark  
As you the light, the world's culmination  
Bring forth, through night, my life's continuation  
Stay with me, child  
Stay with me
