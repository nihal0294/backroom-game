- Level 1 is a Class 1 and should be destroyed however, it is inhabited by humans  
- Crimson Forest is "Class: Habitable" and thus should not be destroyed  
- Level -4 is "Class Undetermined" but evidence suggests that entity(s) live there and since it is not suitable (clearly) for human inhabitation, it should be destroyed.
