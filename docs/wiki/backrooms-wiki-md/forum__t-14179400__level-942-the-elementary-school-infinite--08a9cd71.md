Level 942 - "The Elementary School Infinite"

28 Aug 2021 11:34

**Page Type:** New Level Idea

**Genre:** Other

**Page Layout:** In this Level's page, it will use the customary normal level template, but add a "Areas" Section.

**Elevator Pitch:** This Level transports the reader to an elementary school with various entities and few Maps with fill information about level in library. Includes a few well known entities such as "The Artist" and "The Hikari". Also includes well known groups such as B.N.T.G and the M.E.G.

**Central Narrative:** When you enter this ominous level, you are aware something is different. the classroom fill education book, school bags, and shoes. a hallucination show blood appear in random hallway.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** A school in day night. a stalker entity around school area watching the reader A sleepy settlement vigilant in the night.

**Additional Notes:** For full level article, please visit the Level Page at this link: <http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/global092>
