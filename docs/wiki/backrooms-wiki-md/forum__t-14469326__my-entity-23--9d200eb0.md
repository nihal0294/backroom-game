Interesting idea, but I feel the execution needs work. For starters, the description should have more detail. You wrote that they are "bipedal humanoid entities, tall, cruel, intelligent, deadly, and with a large population". You should give them more detail, what do they look like? How tall are they? How big is their population, and does it vary from level to level?

Also their tracking ability needs to be fleshed out more. Give them a specific radius of which they can sense you or even show how they do it to give a bit more depth or leave it as a mystery.

As far as basic spelling and format I would simply refer to wanders who fall victim to them in your methods section as they or them as it gives a bit of a formulaic approach. Also lessen your bolding as when you use it for large chunks of your article it can be distracting and you need to work on capitalization.

If you need any more information of my thoughts please respond and I will be more than happy to reply.
