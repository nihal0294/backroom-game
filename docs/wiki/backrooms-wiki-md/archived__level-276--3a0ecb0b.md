**The Hive** is a level that seems to be the primary abode of various entities.

# **Description:**

According to **u/T0ygU1a**, who found the level, **The Hive** seems like an endless maze of caverns and caves where fleshy inanimate tentacles cover the roof, floor and walls. The rooms are dotted with alien looking eggs, that can be at least 1.5 meters big. The eggs cover columns in the middle of large caverns. A pastel blue fog coats the air. **u/T0ygU1a** stated that it is hard to breathe in **The Hive** (probably due to little oxygen), and he also stated that he witnessed some eggs hatch [Hounds](entity-8.md), [Crawlers](entity-17.md), and [Deathmoths](archived__entity-4--35e09fc6.md) that seemed to act docile for a few minutes before turning hostile.

This gives the impression that some entities in the Backrooms reproduce. **u/T0ygU1a** has also said that howls can be heard coming from all directions. He has also said that there are a lot of hostile entities in this area, including a previously undiscovered entity known as the [High Queen](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/high-queen). This makes **The Hive** the most dangerous level discovered to date. **u/T0ygU1a** who discovered the area eventually escaped a few days later, as he says, “by wandering through tunnels that seem to lead to different levels.” He found an entrance to [Level 0](level-0.md), but when he walked through it, the tunnel disappeared behind him.

A theory 'making it's way around' states that the eggs may be the origin of the entities of the other levels of the Backrooms, or at least an origin point from which they exit, although this has been disproved with further testing. It is currently unknown where the entities of the other levels of the Backrooms originate from.

# **Log:**

So it began when I leaked some secret information to **u/hamster\_damster**, and then the M.E.G. began hunting for me. I escaped to [Level 6](level-6.md), and I no clipped through the floor. This is how got to The Hive. I've seen lots of creatures, these include fish without their outer layer of skin, a giant bag of flesh, and something that looks like an embryo of the thing **u/DerpyEpic\_Craft** calls a Strider. It managed to control my mind, and told me that I should find a worthy candidate for “the gift” (Most likely a human with compatible DNA) and he would kill me fast. After that, I snapped out of it, and stomped it. I then escaped to [Level 8](level-8.md) through a tunnel.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

**u/T0ygU1a** states that 'he fell through a hole in the floor in [Level 6](level-6.md) and landed somewhere completely different.' It has been noted that **The Hive** is very difficult to enter. It is not particularly difficult to exit **The Hive**. The most common exit route is through the tunnels that are usually in the walls of a cavern, although there is a possibility of no-clipping into negative levels.

There’s also a possibility of entering through [Level 8](level-8.md).
