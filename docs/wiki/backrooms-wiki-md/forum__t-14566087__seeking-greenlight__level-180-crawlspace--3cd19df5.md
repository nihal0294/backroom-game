SEEKING GREENLIGHT: Level 180 / Crawlspace

16 Mar 2022 15:58

Page Type: Level

Core Concept: The foundation of the idea is pipes (they give you enough room to stand, but not much else) that lead into cubby holes (I imagine that they aren't much bigger than a Large Home Depot cardboard box) that lead into other rooms. Unlike Level 2, it's not open, it's completely closed in, The story behind it is that the M.E.G. has it as a sort of subway between rooms to locate wanderers more quickly, but is being fought for control by other groups, with the added difficulty of various entities. It'll draw and keep the reader in by having the reader think about where THEY would go and giving them a "why did you do that" feeling when they go a different way.

Additional Notes: This is my first idea, so it won't be amazing :| If you have any suggestions, let me know. If this idea already exists, let me know.
