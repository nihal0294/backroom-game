So it seems this page has too many down votes for it to stay on this site. It doesn't seem like anyone provided a reason why they down voted, but here is why I think they did:

The level was generic. It was just Level 0 but with tight corridors. Now the tight corridors is an interesting concept, but it is not utilised in any interesting manner. When I started reading the page, I thought it was going to be focused on the theme of claustrophobia, but it was not really. The environment was not particularly compelling to read as it was empty corridors and rooms without any unique feel and the storyline was also very vague and did not tie into the level. You could move those narrative portions to pretty much any level and it still will be applicable.

If you want to have a primarily clinical description of a level, you want the stuff the page to be describing to be interesting. Level 369 is a good example of this. It is entirely clinical, but the environment is sufficiently interesting that the page is still enjoyable to read. If you want the level to be empty corridors and rooms, then you want a compelling narrative. A good example of this is Level 127.
