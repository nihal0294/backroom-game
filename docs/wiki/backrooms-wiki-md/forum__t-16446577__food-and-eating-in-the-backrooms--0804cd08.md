How does eating work in the Backrooms? I don't see any articles really address this. I'm really curious what a meal in the backrooms would be. Is there even hunger in the backrooms at all or is hunger slowed in any way? If this is the case and hunger doesn't exist in the backrooms, shouldn't there be a phenomenon about it? Maybe [Almond Water](object-1.md) also satisfies hunger? Or maybe random food items from the frontrooms can be found in levels. I think if that was the case it would be more talked about though. Same if wanderers have farms that they use for food etc. There is the [Backrooms cookbook](object-56.md) which has a bunch of delicacies that contain items from both the frontrooms and the Backrooms. What do you guys think about all this? Any theories or thoughts?

This is my canon, views may differ, but this is my thought on it.

I'm pretty sure you need food, if you go back to the rewritten almond water, it has a nutritional label, in the past almond water water was mainly used to restore sanity, but the wiki has been leaving this sanity phase, leaving sanity as more of what it is like in the frontrooms.

As for if food can be found throughout the backrooms, yes, it would just be kinda sitting there. In several articles there are food farms, im pretty sure people make (or used to make) bread in level 10.
