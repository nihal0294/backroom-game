Hey, check our [Image Use Policy](image-use-policy.md) page before selecting an image. It should answer *almost* all of your questions.

As for inserting images into a page, here's my tutorial;

1. Find your image (based on the guidelines in the [Image Use Policy](image-use-policy.md))  
2. Right-click and open the image in a new tab  
3. Copy the link to the page  
4. Insert the link into this code;

[[include component:image-block  
|name=examplelink.com|  
|caption= super awesome amazing image look at it right now  
|align=(you can align in right, left, top, bottom, etc.)  
]]

5. Fill in the rest of the information. If you're not sure how, experiment with it a bit.  
6. Enjoy your new image

Hope this helps.
