**The following parts are where you have to put *bars* in the name "Parrot".**

> There is only one kind entity in this level, called Parrot, which are very friendly to humans and willing to help people.

---

> More information about the Parrot has been recorded in the next section.

---

> The number of Parrots varies, but they always cannot fill the entire living room.

---

> In the two surrounding rooms, one of them emits blue light and human voices, but Parrots do not allow wanderers to enter.

---

**And here you should also put the *bars*.**

> You can go back to your home now.

---

I failed to mention the entity section, since some readers may be confused there. So it is better that you put the name parrot in **bold** or in *bars*.

---

> These levels all have the characteristic of "nostalgia" and are generally dangerous, and it is currently unclear why Parrots are stationed in them. It is worth noting that the Parrot has exceptional combat power and can easily defeat most entities. At present, many wanderders individuals who have strayed into the above levels have been rescued by Parrots. It seems that Parrot cannot be killed, and most entities are afraid of the Parrot.

**Throughout this sentence you repeat the word "parrot" a lot, so I recommend that you reduce it and use other similar words.**
