Trying something a bit different

11 Jan 2024 20:00

(Posted with GLE)

For this page, I wanted to delve into the idea that every page on the wiki/General Public Database is written by someone, and of course, everyone has their own personalities while they're writing. I wanted it to feel like the character themselves is bleeding through the page, especially as you get deeper into the offsets.

I've had this character in my head a lot: an older man who's been around the block a few times. He's just a regular guy, wandering the Backrooms. Might make him a POI page, but I also might just pepper him through my works. (You're also welcome to use him. If you need a name and someone to go into stupid places, Erik's your guy :) )

Also, I wanted to play around with offsets a little bit, see how it works. They're a pain getting set up properly

This is also my first page with GLE, so we'll see how it goes.
