Level 56 (By Dr.SWC)

02 Oct 2022 05:02

**Level 56** is the 57th level of the backrooms and takes the appearance of a non-Euclidean storage facility.

**Description:**

Level 56 is what seems to be a storage facility with non-Euclidean properties, being much bigger on the inside than the outside. On the outside of the facility, there are 5000 by 5000 square feet platforms by each side of the building. The building on the outside meets the length and width of these platforms and seems to be about 125 feet high. If you try to go off the platform, there will be an invisible barrier stopping you. If you look off the platform or into the sky, you will only see a black void. The temperature of the outside seems to be around 75 degrees Fahrenheit, or 23.9 degrees Celsius. The inside of the building seems to be potentially up to 15,000,000 square feet with a height of potentially over 5000 feet. The temperature of the building is the same as the outside. The inside of the facility has a fog effect because of the huge size of it, when you go inside the facility you will likely immediately realize this.

Inside you will be greeted to very high and long shelf’s. These shelf’s will almost be completely full with boxes varying in size depending on the content inside the boxes. These boxes do not have labels of what are inside of them. While the boxes do not have labels, how far you are into the facility will effect what can be in the boxes. Beside each shelf, there is a ladder that is easily portable despite the huge size of them. The walls, floor, and ceiling of the inside and outside of the facility seem to be made of concrete. The inside of the facility is well lit with sporadic “dark spots”. Even with the immense amount of light inside, there seems to be no visible light-source to cause this, there is also no explanation for how the “dark spots” occur as they can be in the middle of an open area between shelf’s, in corners, etc.

The content of what is inside the boxes is almost entirely dependent on how far you are into the facility. When you first enter, the boxes are usually empty, or have scrap paper, and other useless things inside of them. The deeper you get into the facility, the better the items inside the boxes will get. Eventually the scraps and many other useless relics will be replaced by blankets, pillows, and even stools. Eventually even very valuable things like precious gems, bottles of almond water, bags with food inside, and even full luxurious furniture will appear inside the boxes.

Going inside deeper in the facility to get better items does come at a cost though. The deeper you go into the facility, the more dim the lights will get, the amount of dark spots will gradually increase, and the amount of entities in the area will also gradually increase. At the entrance and surrounding areas, there is no entities, and very few dark spots, the lights are very bright. As you go deeper, lights will get dimmer by 1% every 150 feet, this may be part of the reason why entity numbers increase. 15,000 feet in, it will become pitch black, there will be very high entities counts. This is where the best items will be, but it is a very high risk to try to get items in these areas, as most entities in these areas are on the shelf’s. The only good news is that the level has an effect on entities to make them slow enough to the point where an average person has much higher chances of outrunning the entities. It is still not suggested to go into these areas as because of the darkness, there is a good chance you will run into entities, there is also still a lot of entities, with a decent chance still that they can catch up to you. As well as, the deeper in the level you go, the more the building absorbs sound, and the less likely you are to hear entities near you. The buildings effect on your sanity correlates with how dark the area is, the darker the area it is, the faster your sanity will drop, it is suggested to exit this level within 5 hours of entering as even very light areas drop your sanity considerably fast.

**Entities:**

The most common entities on this level are smilers, hounds, and skin stealers. The deeper you go in, the more entities that will start to appear and the faster they will get. For the first 150 meters within the entrance of the building, there is no entities, nor can entities go into this area. From 150 meters to 5,000 meters within the entrance, entities will appear sporadically. They will become hostile if you get close enough to them. From 5000 to 15,000 meters within the entrance, while entities will only attack if you get close to them, there will be much higher densities of entities in these areas. In the pitch black area past 15,000 meters within the entrance, entities will be the most common, they will be in usually 2 out of every 5 shelf’s, with many of them hoarding into groups. While they still only attack if you get close, they will run much faster towards you if they start chasing you to attack. It is very likely this will happen as the shelf’s are usually not that far apart.

**Bases, Outposts, And Communities:**

There is no documented bases on this level, as the majority of the level is the pitch black zone, as well as it is very rare to get sent to this level.

**Entrances And Exits:**

**Entrances:** The only entrance to get to this level is to no-clip through a concrete wall on Level 162 after exactly 5 hours (the suggested time limit to stay on Level 56).

**Exits:** To Exit This Level, jump off the top of the shelf that has a painting on the side of it, it is around 10,000 feet directly in front of the entrance. This will send you to level 57. Dying on this level by a smiler will actually bring you all the way to level 998. This is a desperate way of exiting the level if you cannot find the shelf with the painting, the reason why you cannot fully die on this level is unknown.
