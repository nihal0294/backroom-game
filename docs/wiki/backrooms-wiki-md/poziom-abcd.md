**SCANNING…**

Welcome **Human**!

**Species:** Human  
**Origin:** Earth

I have prepared 10 questions for you to answer. A question will appear on the top panel in front of you, with four buttons below it containing the answers to the question. If you answer correctly, you move on to the next question. However, if you answer incorrectly, you will be teleported to another level. The further you go, the safer the outcome becomes. For example, answering the first question incorrectly will take you to a level where you would not want to be, but if you answer the ninth question incorrectly, you will be transported to a level where it is not difficult to survive. Remember, there's a reward waiting for you at the end! Good luck!

**Level ABCD** is an unnumbered level of the Backrooms. It was discovered on September 6, 2001, when a member of the [Polish Committee of Explorers](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/pko-hub) stumbled upon a door marked "ABCD" on [Level PL-15](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/poziom-pl-15). After leaving **Level ABCD**, the explorer reported the incident to the nearest PCE outpost on [Level PL-5](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/poziom-pl-5).

  # Description:

  **Level ABCD** is a white room with a screen on the wall and four buttons below it. The screen always displays a question, and the buttons display answers, one of which is the correct answer to the question.

  What is the chemical symbol for mercury?

  - - \_

    [Hg](javascript:;)[Congratulations! Time for question number 4.](javascript:;)

    The level seems to be controlled by an artificial intelligence, which asks wanderers to answer questions. At the very beginning, when an explorer enters **Level ABCD**, they will be scanned by a green beam of light. After scanning, the AI will tell the wanderer their species and origin. For non-human entities, the data displayed will be appropriate to the creature in question.

            # Entrances and Exits:

            ## Entrances

            You can get to **Level ABCD** through the door marked "ABCD" on [Level PL-15](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/poziom-pl-15), but sometimes this door appears on other levels. However, the door is only open when no one is on that level.

              ## Exits

              The only way out of **Level ABCD** is to give the wrong answer to the question on the screen. The entire list of levels that can be accessed through this level is not known, but it is theorised that the level can teleport the wanderer anywhere. In rare cases, even outside the Polish cluster.
