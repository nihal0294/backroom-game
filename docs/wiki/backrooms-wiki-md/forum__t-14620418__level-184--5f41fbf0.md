Level 184

12 Apr 2022 13:53

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre:** Emotional

**Core Concept:** The level is a sentient level that wishes to help out wanderers, having no interior motives. It starts holding weekly 'giveaways' where it generates a bunch of resources (frontrooms food, almond water, maybe toys for the kiddies) for people to freely take. As the weeks go on and the level becomes more popular, people start getting violent over resources. Tension rises, and each time, there's more people to split the resources between. Eventually people snap and a full-on brawl occurs, resulting in serious and even fatal injuries for some. Saddened by this, the level decides to close it's doors indefinitely.  
The level will focus on the perspective of a M.E.G agent sent to do study the level, who makes friends during the giveaways only to lose them in the final fight.
