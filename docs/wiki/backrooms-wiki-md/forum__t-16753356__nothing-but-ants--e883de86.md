Novote and here’s why

20 Mar 2024 03:24

I’m going to ignore the SPaG errors and strange word choice, because that’s not what I want to focus on.

You do an INCREDIBLE amount of worldbuilding here. You bring back the horror of the original levels and add another layer of monstrosity to the chaos, which I love. The glimpse into Blackout life is truly fantastic, however, I cannot ignore the issues with the narrative.

I didn’t feel anything while reading this. I didn’t feel the terror the protagonist felt when they ran for their life, I didn’t feel their sadness as they reminisced about the incident. The descriptions of emotions that your character feels is key to the story, and the execution just didn’t sit with me right.

Also at some points, the protagonist’s reaction takes away from a LOT of the horror. They witnessed SOMEONE DIE AND A CREATURE OF PURE DARKNESS EMERGE FROM THE BODY, and they were just like, “cool. Who are you?”.

I really think that this article could be improved(although it’s not like I could do any better), and I overall liked this tale to the point where it was almost an upvote, but it’s those few things that leave me neutral.
