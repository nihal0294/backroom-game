Definitely replace the numbers with hashtags until you are ready for a Greenlight.

I have read Level 944 and never once mentioned an entity within the Level maybe switch it to majority or various.

> Entity has been shown to have no legs or limbs that work like legs. Instead the entity has a long serpent-like tail, which is about 50 feet long and 30 meters in width, covered in black scales. The scales are also thanks to testing with shedded skin samples that are shown to be harder than regular steel. the Entity is also shown to be(or appears to be) female shown by the feminine body structure but also the female anatomy on the midsection. the entity also has long humanoid arms

. for movement around its level though it cannot move beyond 55 mph thanks to its heavy body. the entity also seems to be the only one in its level  
Probably add the number of the entity when saying entity such as "Entity ### is known to hunt in Level 2" this is an example.

> The entity was discovered when an exploration team had found the level. after walking for what seemed like hours one of them seemed to say they heard beautiful singing, though the rest of the crew seemed unable to actually hear it, then the man began to run towards the direction of the noise in fear of losing their member the rest began to follow but then lost the man when turning a corner. after searching they began to hear screaming. the leader, named cooper, began to follow said screams only to discover the Entity ripping apart the man they had been searching for. the rest of the team managed to escape while the entity was eating.

Quoted from Level 944 "We can't really confirm anything about this level, aside from the fact that no one who goes in comes back. Pretty much all of what we have has been provided to us by an individual known as Gani. Although I can't verify any of this, given my past experiences with them, I have no reason to doubt it."  
Aside from that there are loads of SPaG errors that are very noticeable when reading over make sure to also capitalize Level, Wanderer, Entity and names of people.  
Overall I kinda like the idea though it can be improved. Feel free to PM me again when you finish revising it.
