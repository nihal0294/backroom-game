Hi! I will be providing concept critique! Please wait a little bit while I read your draft and see what you've got.  
I'll admit, having a pretty harmless, innocent entity is a nice idea, and changes things up a bit from the normal, bloodthirsty entity you see. However, I feel that you will confuse the reader with the different varients. You make it seem there is one specific "Timothy" who changes forms, and make it seem that there are multiple versions of "Timothy" at the same time. I think it would be better if you clarify which you mean. If you mean there are multiple versions of the same entity, you should clarify. If these variations are entirely separate species, you should point that out. I know you said at the beginning of the article "It has made friends with Yellow and is often seen with him." But then you go on to say "Entity 682 mostly looks like a spider but can look different if it wants to." Definitely confusing.  
I feel that the bit at the end is unnecessary. You already have a good concept. You don't need to copy the concept of the Followers of Jerry. Jerry already has several opposing cults.  
You also said above in the passage the wanderer could tame the entity. That is completely different than them being indoctrinated by your entity. Consider finding a different way to make your entity more interesting that doesn't take of off another article. Use your imagination. Make an interview with it if it can talk. You can do other things than make multiple variations, etc.
