Level Idea: Submechanophobia

28 Sep 2024 01:17

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre:** Horror

**Core Concept:** The Level is essentially a network of tight concrete tunnels filled with stagnant murky water littered with various debris. The water at the beginning of the level is warm and shallow, but becomes extremely cold and deep further out. Only 1 entity type lurks here, known as *‘Animatronics’* or *‘The Submerged’*. They take the form of decaying and rotting animatronics and lurk beneath the surface of the water, waiting for the perfect moment to ambush their prey. If they catch their prey, they tangle them in any way they can before dragging them under the surface. They are rare near the beginning of the level, but become more common further out. The level really plays on the fear of submechanophobia, pulling the readers into an eerie place where they can barely move or see, and are confronted by these terrifying animatronics. The Backrooms is all about exposing raw and primal fears, and submechanophobia is a truly terrifying concept that hasn’t really been tapped. The level can be entered by: unintentionally tripping on level 39, touching a poster for an ‘Ocean Exhibit’ on Level 5, or no-clipping into a broken shark animatronic on Level 9. When a person with submechanophobia falls asleep on any level, they have a small chance to be sent here. The level can be exited by: by walking outward so far that the water levels reach your neck, you have a chance to fall into Level 7. Though this exit is ill advised, as being this far out will allow ‘The Submerged’ to attack you without the need to breach the surface. No-clipping into a mural of a zoo found at some dead-ends will lead you to Level 81.

**Additional Notes:** The only trouble I’m having is finding a copyright free photo I could use for this level. Could anyone help with this?

Love it! Agreed that submechanophobia is a good concept to explore. It combines the eerie unkowability of ruined, submerged things with the basic anxiety of not being able to see danger under water. That would be a truly nerve-wracking place to get trapped. (I say this as someone who, personally, is very afraid of underwater machines XD) Have you had a look at Level 183, the "Clean Sewers"? It seems similar- a network of flooded tunnels- but tiled and well-kept. Maybe your level and this one could be connected? They seem like logical extensions of each other.

On another note, your level made me think of this short story- if you like submechanophobia content, I would recommend ;)  
<https://www.reddit.com/r/nosleep/comments/ke21i6/submechanophobia/>

I wanted to mention that I also have a draft for a related idea in the works- rather than a network of tunnels, I'm creating a level that's an infinite, underwater shaft. (Playing with the specific submechanophibic fear of being sucked down a giant drain, or having an infinite abyss with unknown contents below you.) It seems like we have some similar interests and personal anxieties, ha ha. Maybe we could crit other's drafts when we're ready?

PS. If you're looking for free images, have you tried Morguefile.com? Great name- but it's a site of free images by creatives for creative use. The major restriction is that you can't use images as-is without attribution and you can't sublicense them- but if you modify the photo (ie. photoshop it into something) you have a lot of freedom. You can read their license- personally, I would email them and double-check that remixed work can be released into the public domain- but this might be a good image resource.

<https://morguefile.com/license>
