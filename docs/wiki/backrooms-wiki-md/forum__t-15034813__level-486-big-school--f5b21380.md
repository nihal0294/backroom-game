**Level 486** is the 487'nd Level of the Backrooms.

# **Description**

**Level 486** is an ordinary junior high school. The time is 24 hours. 19 o'clock is the demarcation point between morning and evening. The school is divided into four parts: teaching building, indoor field, basketball court and football field. The teaching building is the main activity place for the explorers. There are four grades in the school, and each grade has five classrooms, an office and a men's room and a women's room. Each classroom has complete materials, neatly arranged desks and chairs, blackboard newspapers drawn by students on the blackboard, and each student has his own cabinet, which can be obtained with the permission of his classmates, usually a few bottles of almond water, or a small amount of royal rations.

Half of the chalk on the platform is edible. The office is the teacher's activity place. There are four rows of desks and chairs. There are almond water, purple pens and a book on the table. Occasionally, there are coffee and cakes. Again, with the permission of the teacher, the supplies can be taken away. The dining hall is usually only used by the eighth and ninth grade students, and food and drinks will appear regularly on each table. On the fifth floor of the teaching building, there are auditoriums, dormitories, libraries and other facilities.
