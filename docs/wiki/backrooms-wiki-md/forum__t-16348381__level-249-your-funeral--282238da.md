Little Rooftop

23 Jul 2026 23:30

Yesterday I took a seagull to the vets to be put down. It was only a fledgling, maybe 3 or 4 weeks old. I've seen it around a few times with its sibling. But yesterday it's wing was broken. The bad kind of broken. It was fully snapped and the seagull was dragging it behind it on the floor. The pain it was in is unimaginable. Its wing and belly was covered in its own shit. We had to separate it from its sibling and catch it. I've picked up seagull hatchlings twice before but never a fully grown seagull. I was scared of it and it was scared of me. It fell when running from me and struggled to get back up. Eventually I caught it and I put it in a cardboard box covered with a towel. I could feel it breathing on the way to the vets. The vets were nice about it. I was worried they'd be annoyed we brought in a seagull but they thanked us. I named the two seagulls I saved before and I named this guy too. I called him Little Rooftop.

I know how I should feel about this. I should be glad that I was able to help it and prevent it from more pain or being killed by a cat. But I still feed sad. I wish there was something else that could have been done. That happens a lot in life though.
