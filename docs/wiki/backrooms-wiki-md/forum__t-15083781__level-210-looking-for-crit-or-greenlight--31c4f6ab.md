Good idea. You also did a good job at turning it into a level. Have some additional crit.

[+ Critique](javascript:;)

[– Critique](javascript:;)

"Level 210 takes the shape of an road at night that stretches as far as the eye can see. There is no sun or moon in the level, but stars are usually visible. On each side of the road, one can see certain features on some grass, but do not try to step on it. Most features other than the road are completely holographic and leaving the road will make you fall into a massive pit."

  
Crit:  
The first "an" should be "a".  
Okay, but what are these features? Holographic? I'm sorry, but I can't tell what you are trying to explain. I get that you explained it afterwards and that this additional sentences fit the initial description, but I suggest omitting the sentences regarding the features and to simply let the "Phenomena" section do its job. Why shouldn't I step on the grass?  
Good clinical tone.

---

"These entities' insides consist of eight seats, an operating system that seems to be corrupted, and non-functioning driver's controls similar to those in The Frontrooms."

  
Crit: no comma after "corrupted". There is no need for a pause.

---

What is "NTR"? (Referring to the IETS.)

---

"However, acting hostile towards them can cause them to attack you with whatever they have, although in most scenarios, the sustained injuries are not that dangerous."

  
Crit: semicolon after "have". I feel like it requires a longer pause.

---

"Not much is known about Entity 250-A, except that they come from Entity 250, and that they are dangerous humanoid creatures."

  
Is Entity 250 something you are working on?  
Also, I suggest expanding this section more. What do these entities do that makes them so dangerous?

---

"No-clipping into the path of Level 9 or Level 166."  
"Entering the driver's seat of a living car will put you in Level 69."

  
Crit:  
I suggest substituting "path" with "road".  
I feel like it's better to say "Sitting on" rather than "Entering". If that's the case, however, than entering any of these entities might nearly always result in entry into Level 69, then. Might just be me misunderstanding.

  
Hope this helps and happy writing!
