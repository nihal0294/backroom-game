Okay that’s pretty funny.

VERY clever use of links to set up the immersion in this one. Even the start with the age question sets up a funny atmosphere if someone decides to check what happens if you’re under 19, so you’re already in the right mindset going in to the actual article.

My favorite part of this is how the level itself can entrap you by being there too long, not necessarily psychologically preventing you from leaving but because doing so after a certain point will definitely kill you. A sort of “you can never go back” that you risk by entering. The audio log transcript is perfect, and having the interruptions like the kid checking their age was great at establishing not just the properties of the level, but also the interconnection it has with the lives of wanderers. Other than that, it sets up an appropriately growing dread for the fate of these characters.

A few visual issues:

- The offset links with the pink text with pink drop shadow on pink background is kind of hard to see. I’d suggest changing the div background color to make the neon light look stand out more.
- The header title overlaps on mobile. I don’t think there’s much that can be done about this, though.
- The licensing box’s title is pink text on pink background. You’ll probably need to edit specifically the licensing box’s CSS to fix that.

Partying has never really been my cup of tea, but this is a solid article!

Thoughts:

1. HOO BOY there was a lot of effort put into the theme and CSS. Major props. Excellent atmospheric storytelling.

2. The unnoticed consequences of partying could have been expressed with more subtlety. For example, this is pretty on-the-nose about the grave danger, rather early on:

> The risk of becoming one of the All-Nighters is especially high among wanderers who are emotionally afflicted, desperate, or hopeless.

You don't particularly need this statement in the first place; the following sentence about bringing a friend flows naturally from the preceding sentence about losing track of time and awareness.

I'd have preferred a more gradual sense of worry building up through Danna's words, with less explicit mention of the danger in the body text. Then a sudden 'snap', with a change of theme for the toilet offset (a jarring white theme could have been very fitting), along with the clear formal explanation of the danger which is already present in the offset as it stands, could have brought across the idea of a 'hangover' more effectively.

3. Could have been grittier. Casual mention or unnervingly positive portrayal of illicit substances and such could have pushed the narrative that judgment is impaired in the level, and help to show readers the dangers of partying here.

4. Structurally speaking, flow could be improved somewhat. You occasionally mention concepts offhand, before you have introduced them — e.g. you name-drop all-nighters before explaining who they are properly. This is not wrong, but it can be distracting for readers. A simple (see below for more) can do wonders for this sort of thing.

Apart from perhaps no. 4, I think that these express more of my personal preferences and nitpicks than objective critique! Overall I enjoyed this piece! Solid stuff!
