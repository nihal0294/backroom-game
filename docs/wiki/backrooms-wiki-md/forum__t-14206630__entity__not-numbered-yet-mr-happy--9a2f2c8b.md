[+ show block](javascript:;)

[– hide block](javascript:;)

Alrighty, so an entity that basically comes by to cheer people up who are sad or distressed, interesting. Honestly we could use an entity like that in the backrooms haha. Overall nice premise. However I have some questions and nitpicks:

1: >Entity XX, also known as Mr Happy, is a humanoid entity who wears a white tuxedo-like suit, along-side white shoes, a white top-hat, white gloves and a black-and-white mask, covering their face.

You do not need to really delve into detail like that, I would refer to doing something like this : " Entity XX, also referred to as "Mr. Happy", is a humanoid entity adorning several articles of white clothing, such as a tuxedo suit, gloves, and a top hat. The only article of clothing that sticks out is the anomalys face with appears to be covered by a black and white mask. Areas not covered by any clothing articles were observed to be pitch black in color and soft to the touch. " Y'know stuff like that.

2: other than him offering people items, how exactly does it behave? I know it can speak, but what does it say? Does it speak often or does it resort to actions that speak for him?

3: There should be a little more to the discovery log. Like maybe an interview following the events regarding it's discovery? Interviews really do add to an article and make it stand out.

4: you say that there is nothing that can be done to piss him off. I'm sure there's something that may scare him, but not annoy him. He is Mr happy after all. Are there ways to deter him or make him give up on making someone happy? There has to be something.

5: the biology section is slightly bleak. There has to have been someone who got spooked and punched him on the groin or face or something like that. I'd be spooked at first. Really lean into the biology aspect here. Like other than it's height, delve into it's anatomy a little more. What's it's build like? Strong and broad shouldered? Or lean? Stuff like it's physiologically.

Overall I like the main premise here, but it needs a little bit of a touch up to be a memorable article.

Kind regards

Wrackk

I think this one’s a dead end. It reads off like a Fandom entry and maintains none of the professional, clinical tone that is associated with most entity pages.

Keep readers interested. The immature language used here will only drive readers away.

The concept itself is generic and uninteresting. It makes people happy. Who cares?

You need to expand more on the concepts that haven’t been done before. I’d suggest reading other entity pages and noting your emotions during it. Try to mimic their style of writing without doing a full-blown copy of someone else’s writing syntax.
