*If you specify what type of entity, but I'm going directly to the question.* No, since the biology part would be more for your formation of the body or what it is made of. Although the "Biology" part can also be used for the same purpose of describing physical appearance, but "Appearance and Diet" makes more sense so yes, I hope this helps you.

Note: It is worth clarifying that the texts that appear when you choose level or entity are not mandatory and can be changed as you wish.
