# **Description**

![Missing alt text.](http://id-backrooms-sandbox.wikidot.com/local--files/sandbox:marlin-paskalis-s-sandboxs/Lorong)

A photo of one of the hallways on the 5th floor with Piero.

**Level 3-ID** is a single building with an infinite number of floors with each floor having a similar structural form. The area of one floor on this level is 200 square meters containing 20 rooms and a staircase on one side. Each wall is painted in white and the floor is tiled in white. There are neon lights only in the hallways[1](javascript:;). At the edge of the structure, it is only limited by a wooden fence to separate the outside of the building which is only a void.

No resources or features can be found on **Level 3-ID**[2](javascript:;), therefore it is expected to bring a supply of resources when exploring the level. To date, no dangerous entities have been found and the level is classified as safe.

In the stairwell section, there are black *pylox* graffiti on the wall in the form of numbers indicating the floor level. To date, the deepest floor explored is -87 and the highest floor explored is 1,000.

Exploration of the 1,000th floor was not possible due to temperatures reaching -45°C. The exploration was an accident due to entering one of the sealed level entrances and caused six fatalities. The temperature drop on each floor is inconsistent and different. It is known that snow can be found on floors 650 and above. A warning board has been installed on the 350th floor that forbid entering the next floor.

Explorations on the -87th floor reported the floor temperature reached 33°C. The temperature rise of each floor also remains inconsistent. Some wanderers have reported a more shrill [Hound](entity-8.md) voice although it has never been found. It is still suspected that the deeper floors have less extreme temperatures and are inhabited by many new entities.

![Missing alt text.](http://id-backrooms-sandbox.wikidot.com/local--files/sandbox:marlin-paskalis-s-sandboxs/Kucing)

Hallway on the first floor.

The first floor is the most different from the other floors. The light source on the first floor is only in the area around the crematorium room. The walls are also painted in cream and white colors. Sometimes on the first floor there is a random phenomenon in the form of fog that will cover all parts of the floor and the smell of incense that will be smelled.

#### **Crematorium**

The crematorium was in the centermost room on the first floor. It is the only room on the first floor that has a light source. In the room there are features that can usually be found in a crematorium. There are two incinerators that can be found in the room. The incinerators have anomalous properties where there will be no trace of combustion left. A note can also be found in the room.

[Read the contents of the note?](javascript:;)

[May you understand.](javascript:;)

This world or reality is so shitty and sickening. Watching my friends die at the hands of dogs or god knows what other creatures. No one here likes this place, this place that separates us from the people who love us. My desire to speak my true feelings pierced me. Who was to blame? Myself? My careless friend? Or that bastard wanderer society?

My best friend died by that damn moth entity. He shouldn't have tripped that time. Why didn't I help him who had been torn to shreds back then? Why should I be afraid and run even faster leaving him behind? It wasn't all my fault It was all my fault, my fault, my fault. Now he's just flesh and bones that will crumble soon.

Now this is all I can do, burn you in this incinerator. Hoping that your ashes and soul are freed from this suffocating world. Hoping that this is your only way of freedom. This letter is just the contents of my heart that goes out to you. This is all I can do, just this. ~From your bastard friend

Possibly due to the anomaly of the incinerators and the letter, some wanderers come to **Level 3-ID** just to go to the crematorium room. Wanderers will usually burn the corpses of their friends, family, or animals, although sometimes wanderers just burn a letter or item they own as a sign that they are still alive to [Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md). Unfortunately this is still unknown.

# **Piero**

Piero is the name given to the tuxedo kitten living in **Level 3-ID**. A kind and cute cat who sometimes spends his time following wanderers from a fair distance. He'll sit quietly in his last position when the wanderer stops for a break or to descend the stairs. Don't you dare approach him, he'll hate it and leave you behind.

There are reports that Piero was found on floors 657 and -70[3](javascript:;). Unfortunately he was never found on the first floor. Unfortunately Piero also never wanted to eat or drink. All the food given by the wanderers was just skipped and ignored. No wonder he was never big from the beginning of his discovery.

# **Bases, Outposts, and Communities**

## **Crematorium Office**

- Located to the right of the Crematorium room.
- Guarded by 2 crematorium staff.
- Focuses on processing the crematorium room.

# **Entrances and Exits**

#### Entrances

- Entering the tv on [Level 15-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-15-id) which depicts a hallway with Piero will lead to the 35th floor.
- Entering [REDACTED] will lead to the 1000th floor.

#### Exits

- Entering a random room on the tenth floor has the possibility of leading to one of the [Level 10-ID](level-10-id.md) houses.
- Exiting the Crematorium room can lead to [Level 22-ID](level-22-id.md).
- One of the doors on the 25th floor can lead to [Level 14-ID](level-14-id.md).
