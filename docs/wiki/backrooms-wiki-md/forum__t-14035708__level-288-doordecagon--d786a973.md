Level 288 - "Doordecagon"

26 Mar 2021 21:44

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre:** History, somewhat, but hopefully with low-key unsettling Horror.

**Elevator Pitch:**

- Nexus point from which number of other levels can be accessed through summoned doors, but only if they have dodecagonal numbers (1, 12, 33, 64, 105, 156, 217, 288, 369, 460, 561, 672, 793, 924, 1065…), and only if the one calling the door has been there before, so it can't be used to explore every level of the Backrooms.
- Doors often plain with stainless steel knob and embossed level number, but color, material and condition of door often indicative of accessed level's nature. The occasional door will stray outside standard template.
- Doors one-way, and you can't travel to this level from levels it can send you to (with the exception of itself), and methods of reaching the nexus are presently limited.
- Can't pass through door called by another unless you've been there, but can look through.
- Doors can't be used to reach secret, sub or negative levels, this being a normal level system, which indicates conscious design behind floors available, if the number sequence didn't already.
- Doors always exit into a level at the same location or one of several fixed rotating locations, and vanish/are invisible once someone reaches the other side.
- Door will stick around til one who called it leaves area, dismisses door or passes through.
- No hostile entities, but people alone/in small groups report feeling watched, some seeing something shadowy in the corner of their eye that vanishes when faced.

**Central Narrative:** Evidence gathered indicates that 288 was originally a dangerous level worthy of Class 5, and wasn't even originally a nexus as it is now. The layout of the level is that of a small town surrounded on all sides by impenetrable fog, centered on a large, 12-sided building of slightly unnatural design (from the outside it almost looks like something "forced" the building to take that shape, rather than it being constructed that way). Arrivals on the level usually turn up at one of the central building's entrances.

A plaque over each entrance arch leading to the central areas of the building uses words from a wide variety of languages (including some that aren't known to modern scholars) roughly translates to a cryptic message indicating that some powerful entity/wanderer that came before somehow "banished" most/all hostile entities from 288 and forced it to become a sort of "nexus" point of their own design that connects to other levels. Whoever they are, they seem to be long gone - or, at least, their creating the doors was eons ago - as the plaque is visibly eroded despite the surroundings being very dry, words only just legible.

The being that put the plaque there and made the doors signs its name with a dodecagon outline (M.E.G. researchers call them "Dodec", because of their seeming to like that shape/number, though nobody's sure who came up with the name), and infers the nexus is the last of 12 great experiments they carried out in the "first fold" (i.e. the first Cluster), and what they view as their finest early work, due to its ability to "evolve with those who use it". They themselves claim to have traversed on to levels with numbers well beyond what M.E.G. has currently explored, though, possibly even going up into the 10s of thousands, if not more (it's almost certainly a dodecagonal numbered level), and may still be waiting there.

One of the non-descript rooms at the center of the building serves as the "nexus area", an object in the room serving as the "activator" which can be used to call doors, which usually appear in or in front of a nearby square arch/passage. It's architecturally quite modern, and not the sort of place you'd expect to function as a nexus, inferring this wasn't the original purpose of the level.

Whether "Dodec" would be friendly, hostile or apathetic in either direction to the M.E.G. is unknown, their apparent ability, though likely limited, to impose their will on fabric of the Backrooms in such a way to ease passage, being a concern. Given their lack of modern presence, though, it's considered a low risk, the whereabouts of their other 11 "experiments" being of greater importance.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** This Level is intended to be interesting to read for two major reasons. The first is that it is able to evolve and grow as more levels with relevant numbers are created, serving to tie certain areas of the Backrooms together with a non-standard choice of number sequence. The second is the implied presence of "Dodec". The idea that someone, be they god or man, was able to persevere long enough not only survive in but also master the Backrooms enough to force alterations that have mostly stood the test of time is both a hope spot in the darkness of the setting, as well as a subtle implication of horror for whether they are friendly or simply do things at their own whim, possibly being just as much of a danger to your average wanderer as any other entity should someone one day be unfortunate enough to meet them.

**Additional Notes:**

- Most consistent way for M.E.G. to access this level is through a painting on Level 57 - though as the painting is a very abstract representation of the level (literally just a black and white painting of a dodecagon), entry only succeeds a small amount of the time, often routing to Level 0 or 2 instead. Level's existence was originally revealed in meetings with other factions, who are postulated to know of a more consistent/stable way of getting there that they haven't shared.
- Level number, 288, is both a dodecagonal number and the lowest positive multiple of 12 available for page creation.
- I have an image put aside for the level already, but will wait on approval but I put it forward.
- Given the Exits section will have door descriptions for every level the nexus theoretically links to, it's the sort of list that may be updated further as more level pages are created. Whether I or someone else does this may depend on how the page is received.
- M.E.G. efforts to establish a more permanent outpost are still ongoing, as are attempts to determine if there is an equivalent location in the negative levels.
