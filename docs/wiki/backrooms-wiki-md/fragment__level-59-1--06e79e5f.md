# fragment:level-59-1

!["The moon reflecting light towards the sea."](https://live.staticflickr.com/8540/8688777139_ba594cb609_b.jpg)

How embracing is the sea and the moon?

# **Description**

**Level 59** is the brink of when the moon and the sea embrace. The moment of embrace, the dim nights, bringing a temporary respite for their hands to interlock. Here is how it begins: A shoreline. One seam of wavering blue at our feet. Salt sticks to our skin—my skin, your skin—touching, brushing, playing. We slip away into the tide undiscovered. Seabirds line the horizon. The way they tilt their bodies makes their white feathers into sails. Touch me, everything inside of me. The water drags at my ankles, fingers pulling me under, and oh—how exciting, to drown with you. The breath in your lungs, my lungs, take a deep breath, take that breath from me, take it all. All of it? All of it.

It’s yours.

You are certain this is where we belong. My mind fails me. I trust yours. It’s so cold at the bottom of the sea. Little pinpricks on my skin. Sometimes we dance to the static on the radio, too dark to tell where our footsteps land, (y)our body moving blindly, without care, without hesitation. Hold me? Hold me. Of course I want to be held.

I remember everything about you. Things you’ve never said. I remember nothing. Well, you remember what I remember. It’s a beautiful thing, the ocean. Every time you blink, you’re looking at a new beach. A new beginning. Begin. This is how it begins.

# **Additional Content**

Does my love for your love of me of me loving you need an explanation?

#### Entrances

Find that warmth in yourself.

#### Exits

Why would you want to turn a blind eye to the self's touch? I love you, do you want to kiss me with your lips that are mine? They're wet with the spray of the ocean, which is where we found ourselves where I was illuminated by you and where you influenced my push and pull.
