# Description

Level 252, upon entering is a vast and expansive room with food and super almond water lying around the entire area.  
However, the walls slowly begin to close in, making people with claustrophobia lose their sanity with it. Photos have never been taken due to the strict time frame of entering and exiting the level. However, If unlucky, the room will resemble [Level 6](level-6.md) and wanderers will be clueless as to where the food is, aswell as how fast the walls are closing in. There are only few documentations of people ending up here, as the two entrances to this level are either in a level that is very hard to get to, or be completely luck-based. Unless running low on food or sanity itself, do not attempt to enter this level, as you can easily be crushed by the walls at any time. Smilers have been documented in a lights-out situation, and hounds are rarely found within the level. Level 252 is set to be fully documented soon by M.E.G.

# Entrances

-Noclip through a mannequin with a wide face in level !.1  
-Find a door labelled "Food Storage" in [Level 2](level-2.md) and walk through it to be teleported here.  
-Find a door labelled "Food Area" in [Level 0](level-0.md) in order to enter this level.

# Exits

-Picking up all food will open a guaranteed door to [Level 998](level-998.md)  
-Noclipping through the walls will lead to [Level 36](level-36.md).  
-If entered via [Level 2](level-2.md)/[Level 0](level-0.md), Walk through the door again to return.
