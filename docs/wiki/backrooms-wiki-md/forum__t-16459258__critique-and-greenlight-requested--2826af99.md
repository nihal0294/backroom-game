I’ve completed a full critique covering all 3 sections (SPaG, format and concept). I shall go over each of these sections in the order they have just been stated.

To start with SPaG!!

“will be less noticed.” - Here the wrong form of “notice” has been used. instead of saying “noticed” it should be ‘noticeable’.

“The Xavar is a mangled up pile of bones,” - “mangled-up” needs to be hyphenated.

“or walls that nobody normally looks at.” - Here the wrong determiner has been used. Instead of saying “that”, it should’ve been ‘where’. Changing it to ‘where’ also allows you to shorten the sentence as “at” can now be removed from the end of the sentence.

“will vary in different sizes.” - “Different” is redundant and should be cut.

“Xavar on a routine research trip to level 23, it was found” - If they found 3 at once, “Xavar” should be ‘Xaven’ and the pronoun “it” should be the plural pronoun ‘they’.

Moving onto Format!!

> This entity can get distracted easily if an object is thrown. It will hide anywhere where it will not easily be noticed.

I feel like this section is in the wrong place. This part should really fall under the “behaviour” subheading due to it describing actions.

All instances of “Entity 59”, “Xavar” and “Xaven” need to be in bold.

We don’t use the “author” box at the bottom of the page anymore, the template needs to be updated. Instead we use the licensing box, the code for which is just below:

```
[[include :backrooms-wiki:component:license-box]] ===== > **Name:** > **Author:** > **License:** > **Source Link:** > **Derivative of:** (If applicable) > **Additional Notes:** (Optional) ===== [[include :backrooms-wiki:component:license-box-end]]
```

Just copy and paste this code into the article and remove the authors box. You don’t have to fill in any of the information unless you add an image.

Finally, concept:

I feel this page is too short, I’m left with a lot of unanswered questions and the points don’t really link together.

Think of ways you can expand upon the idea; go in-depth with the entities description.

You could answer some of these questions:  
Why do they not like light?  
How do they attack and kill their prey?  
Why do they get distracted when an object is thrown?  
Why do they ambush their prey?

You could also add a M.E.G. report or a newspaper article describing the first encounter with **Xaven**

Anyways, that’s all from me, BlockHead out.
