I personally think an object would be easy to write if you’re good at describing what something does, like if you did this, then that happens, if not, then this, in a way?  
levels are more of describing scenery but also describing sections of a level and if there’s any entities or objects specific to that level or if entities are affected by that level, and also if the level had any effects on memory, hearing, vision, etc (imo levels sound the hardest to me)  
entities are mostly just visuals and behavioral descriptions but can be more like describing sentience/emotional values, and a lot of stuff that i’m too tired to really explain right now and because i can’t remember half the words for what i’m trying to say :)
