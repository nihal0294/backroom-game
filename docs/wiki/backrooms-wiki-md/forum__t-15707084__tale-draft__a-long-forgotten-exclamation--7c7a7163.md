I realize you did say everything is in lowercase, but when doing SPaG critique, I have to point out that it seems like they are communicating via text and you aren't a skilled writer(and I know you are). I suggest you change that once you get the chance.  
The theme also cuts off some parts of the discussion.  
If you haven't already, I suggest you use a free spelling and grammar checker such as Grammarly.  
My first problem lies with this sentence:

> "i am though!"

You either need to get rid of "although" or put a comma between it and "am". I imagine the person who said this didn't say "I am, though" in a rushed manner, without a pause, so the comma is needed.

> "you think something probably put together by a nine-year-old is real?"

I suggest you change "probably" to "most likely". It just makes it sound better; you don't have to.

> "i mean i thought it was fake but– look, just come find this door with me."

There needs to be a comma between "mean" and "I". You don't need the hyphen(-). Replace it with a comma instead. Change "find" to "see". I'm suggesting this because the person who said this already knows where the door is, and aren't having to "find" it.

> "yeah but can't you see that faded mark on it?"

A comma is needed between "yeah" and "but".

> "looks real scratched up and faded… like someone's tried to remove it…

Since the speaker who said this was interrupting the other, there should be some kind of interjection. I suggest "But it…[rest of sentence]".

> "i guess so? i mean considering all that we've seen so far, doubt it's gonna be anything specky… but—"

I think you were trying to type "spooky" instead of "specky"…? Specky, as far as I know, isn't a real word. Clarify or fix what you meant here.  
I didn't notice any other errors. As I said earlier, I suggest you run it through a spell checker such as Grammarly.

  
By the way, I hope you are an author who can doesn't require a greenlight to post, because it can take time getting an acual reviewer to look at it.
