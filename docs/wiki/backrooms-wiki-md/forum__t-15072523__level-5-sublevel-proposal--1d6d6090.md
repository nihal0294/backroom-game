Hi puppybork,

I could not find any immediate errors in your writing, like SPaG stuff, but tbh, this article was quite boring. I enjoyed the theme, and stuff having to do with Level 5 is always pretty cool to me, but this was just *plain*.

You described it as a bunch of hallways that had near-white walls and… yeah. I think the idea of a part of the hotel that was "left out of construction" is neat, but it wasn't done properly. Here are some ideas of mine for an "under construction" area:

- It was "left out of construction"? How about a more "under construction" thing, as the first makes not a lot of sense?
- Some construction-like details. (Half-built walls, construction equipment, machinery)
- Some details from the hotel. (Paintings, furnished rooms, boxes of decorations not quite set up)
- Some things "the Terror Hotel left out". Maybe there is an indoor pool or kitchen that we can't see from Level 5?

So, that's my wrap-up for now. Now that I look, your lightbox at the top has 2 SPaG errors I'm sure you'll see. If you don't write this idea, I think *I* may, because it could turn out as a cool concept. Ik I've just written a sub-level for you, but try playing around with the concept. Pm me if u have questions or concerns.
