Here is the crit I offered.

There are a lot of comparisons to Level 11. This would work better as a Level 11 sublevel. However, constant comparison isn't a good idea. Pages should be able to stand on their own without wholly relying on another page's existence or else it is really obvious that you read Level 11 and wanted to make your own version of it. This also makes it really hard for the page to stand out. Even if your idea if 'Level 11 but opposite', people are still going to see this as just another Level 11. This page lacks indepence and uniqueness.

Instead of thinking about how this level compares to Level 11, think about what makes this level stand out. What themes do you want to exist in this page? What do you want people to remember about this page? What emotions do you want people to feel when they read this page? What will make people remember this page more than other Backrooms city levels? Questions like these will help this page have its own sense of identity.

This page doesn't seem like a Backrooms page. Sure, it has entities but it still doesn't feel apart of the Backrooms. It's very similar to New York City. There isn't really anything on this page that makes me think of the Backrooms. Instead, it feels like something from a young adult novel or typical cyberpunk show. People are here for the Backrooms. People are unlikely to be interested in something that is essentially the Frontrooms with a few entities in the mix.

What makes you want to write for the Backrooms instead of other things? What made you decide to use this setting? What are some of your favourite Backrooms levels? What Backrooms levels inspire you to write? These kinds of questions work as inspiration. These in addition with my point above will help make this page feel more Backrooms.

These are two big concept critiques. Hopefully they can help you with writing. While the page doesn't feel very Backrooms-esque, I do think you have a good understanding of the Backrooms based on how you've written the page and the inclusion of the narrative.  
Feel free to ask anything or talk about anything if you want to.  
Good luck!

The concept for this level is very interesting, I think it's be exciting to have a bustling utopia in the Backrooms!

However, the story in the beginning uses both "your" and "his". Please decide on second or third person.

For the list of differences between the Neon City and the Endless City, ending with

> - And other things.

is a bit abrupt. Instead, you could remove that point and start the list by saying:

> The differences between The Neon City and the Endless City include, but are not limited to:

and then listing from there.

I also notice that the phrase "with a notable difference" or "with very notable differences" is used a lot. Try alternating or replace some of the instances of these phrases with "except". (A is like B except C)
