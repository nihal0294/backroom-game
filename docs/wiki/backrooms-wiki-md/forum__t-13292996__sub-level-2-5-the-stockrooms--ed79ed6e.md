Sub-Level 3.5- The Stockrooms

05 May 2020 22:17

---

**Page Type:** Sub-Level, the 2.5 Slot (if possible) Switched to sub-layer 3.5.

**Genre:** Mystery

**Elevator Pitch:** Little is known about the stockrooms and it’s contents, except that it is a sub-level of the backrooms that resembles a warehouse and shopping center of sorts. Many useful items can be found, but there are also dangerous consumables that are scattered and disguised to look fresh. There are also hostile entities (both new and current entities) that roam the floors. It will also be a relatively safe level, but the explorer must be careful to not touch a lot of items, as mold that spreads “*the disease*“ can be commonly encountered.

**Central Narrative:** u/ErikWithaKnotaC had noclipped into the backrooms, and noticed that none of his surroundings had matched any level that has been explored in the backrooms. Soon after, u/QueasyInvestment3 had also clipped inside this section of The Backrooms. He too noticed that the looks of this area haven’t been seen before and realized it was a new floor. Because of its appearance, he called it **The Stockrooms**.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** The hook grabber for this floor is the various new and mysterious items and entities that aren’t seen anywhere else in the backrooms. Level 3.5 will also have over 3 different sections of the entire level (the checkout section, the warehouse, and the resupply aisle) . There will also be only one way to leave: the stairwell. The stairwell will lead you to either level 3 or 4, depending on which way you go up.

**Additional Notes:** This is my first time writing a backrooms level. Please be as **harsh** as can be. I’m also doing a thing where I’m stuck inside this floor in the subreddit community (I know, roleplay is cringe but what the heck).
