> Level 682 is the 683rd Level of the Backrooms.

The second use of "Level" in this instance should not be capitalized since its use is not a proper noun here. I'd also just generally add more information to this introduction; it's supposed to serve as, well, just that, an *introduction*. Just saying what numeral is assigned to the level is kind of lame; briefly go over the level's environment and abnormal traits as well to really hook your readers in.

> First ever photo taken of Level 682

Add punctuation to this caption.

> gates, and hallways. All of these fit together

I'd change this use of a period to a semicolon.

> varying from Almond Water to hostile entities

"Almond Water" no longer exists on this wiki; remove references of it from your page.

> also a chance, although rare, that you

I'd remove ", although rare," from this, it's an unnecessary buffer word that does nothing but slow this sentence down.

> dangerous to Wanderers and can

"Wanderers" shouldn't be capitalized here, since the term is not a proper noun.

> and can result in bodily harm, or death.

I'd remove the comma here.

> strange embodiment of the backrooms, or possibly

"backrooms" should be capitalized here, since the use is a proper noun.

> just walkin' through level 428 and I decided

"level" should be capitalized here since it's a proper noun.

> thingie one of the facelings dropped

"facelings" should be capitalized here since it's a proper noun.

> "Any way, I found this door thing

"Any way" should actually be one word here.

> not a big deal but I decided to record anyway"

Add punctuation here.

> "Ill start keeping track of

An apostrophe is needed on "Ill".

> however I still have no idea how I got here

Punctuation is needed here, as well as end qoutes.
