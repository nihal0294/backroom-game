> entity number: 87

Capitalize "entity" and "number"; you can make this bit and the one below it into actual headers by putting +++ and a space before it, doing so will make it look more professional.

> Habitat: moist

Okay, I get that this is in the entity guide, but just putting "moist" shouldn't be done and caught me off guard in a funny way. I'd change "moist" to "Moist Locales".

> A sketch of a male and female shroomz

Since this is plural, remove the second use of "a" and capitalize "shroomz".

> moist levels of the backrooms with a height

Capitalize "backrooms" since that's a proper noun.

> 4 meters. Shroomz resemble mushrooms

"Shroomz" should be bolded here.

> to be a convenience and no

Change "convenience" to "coincidence" as I believe that's the word you meant.

> but this is believed to be a convenience and no biological connection to mushrooms other than appearance has been discovered.

I'd just rewrite this to what's shown below:

\*but this is believed to only be a coincidence and nothing more.\*

> Text and drawings have been blurred out for containing false information

I'd remove this since it wouldn't make since for almost the entirety of the image to be blurred; at that point why would M.E.G. even show it? It also just plainly doesn't look like it's been blurred.

> somtimes be seen eating entities such as hounds.

"sometimes" should be "sometimes" and "hounds" should be capitalized since it's a proper noun.

> a male Shroomz has bright, vibrant colors to attract mates.

"Shroomz" should be bolded here.
