**Level 139** is the 140th Level of the Backrooms. It was discovered on 8/11/2005

Level 139 is mental torture.

# **Description:**

level 139 is a seemingly infine mirror maze, with the walls and ceiling being made entirely out of large mirrors. The floors are made of a hard and shiny white tile. The halls twist and turn like a maze, however the height and width of the hallways never changes. The true size of level 139 is unknown. Level 139 often reaches low temperatures, sometimes reaching that of bellow 32 F°. It is very dimly lit in level 139, with the maze only being lit by a moonlight type darkness. The source of the moon light is unknown however it fills the entire area. bringing a flashlight and warmer clothes are strongly recommended. There are no known food or water sources in level 139, making is seemingly uninhabitable for humans.

Level 139 is completely devoid of noise and very dimly lit. If you find yourself in level 139 it is advised to find an exit immediately, as the effects on a person are devastating. The mirror illusion will slowly begin to worsen as time passes in the following ways:

- 1 day your reflection begins to become more distorted. Your facial figures and proportions seem to become unreasonable.
- 2-3 days. The reflection you see gains pail skin. And your teeth begin to become mangled. Eyes, teeth and ears become severely misplaced across the face.
- 3-4 days and your reflection skin becomes completely gray. All facual features will become completely lost. The creature in the mirror will being to move on its own no longer copying your movements. At this point you are mentally and physically exhausted from the days of dehydration and hunger. This is when the mirror entity attacks. Grabbing wonders through the mirrors. What's happens to the wonderer in the mirrors is unknown.

It is believed that the only entity's on level 139 are the mirror creatures themselves. Only 9 known people have been to level 139 so far all and only 5 live to tell the tale. Those whom suvived reside in level 6.1 the snackrooms. 1 survior has shared there story as of so far.

> so it all started in level 5. Me and another mate had been exploring level 5 when we both stopped to take a rest. Leaning against the wall was this…gold framed mirror. But when you looked at yourself it was just….off. anyways we begain talking in front in the mirror and talking about it being off and then I just woke up surrounded by hundreds…thousands of halls of the same fucking mirror. And the longer you looked the weirder and weirder it got… I begin to panic and started to run. It didn't help that I couldn't hear my own footsteps. I was trapped in there for days possibly less though. My reflection had become unrecognizable my features were….I looked like a demon. What made me lose it…was…was I found someone. I don't know who. But he was talking to himself in the mirror..when something grabbed the poor bastards and drug him into the fucking mirror would I panicked and began to run like hell. Eventually I came across a completely dead end. And as I looked I noticed the mirror was cracked. So I saw two of the same mangled mirror faces I saw for days in one mirror. The thought just….enraged me. So I punched the mirror. And to my surprise it shadered leaving only a gold framed door way back to level 6.1

# Additional notes

If a person Is exposed to the mirrors is level 139 for more than 96 hours there reflection will then become the entity know as "the mirror snatchers". If you reach the point your reflection becomes a mirror snatches it's already to late to escape.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

#### Entrances

To enter level 139 you can:

- find and stare into any mirror with a gold frame In [Level 5](level-5.md) for more than 2 minutes.
- One can also enter level 139 by staring into any mirror found in any floor number divisible by 39 in [Level 13](level-13.md).
- There is also a very small chance you can also end up in level 139 by smokeing any of the weed from [Level 420](level-420.md).

#### Exits

To exit level 139 you have to find a cracked mirror and break the glass and go through the frame. You will most likely enter [Level 6.1](level-6-1.md) but there is a random chance of ending up on [Level 0](level-0.md), [Level 2](level-2.md), [Level 6](level-6.md) or very rarely [Level 8](level-8.md).  
If your extremely unlucky lucky smash the mirror with balloons for a party at [Level fun](level-fun.md). =)
