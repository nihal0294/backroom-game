I'll write up a brief list of similarities and differences. This isn't with the intention of you changing everything that's similar but rather finding ways that make it interesting in its own ways.

Similarities:

- Fungi
- Exist in run-down areas
- Infect using spores
- Takes over the body with mushroom structures
- Takes over the brain
- **Can only be dealt with if brain is destroyed**
- This isn't a TLOU similarity but you call it "the walking body" which is similar to "walkers", that being what they call zombies in The Walking Dead

Differences:

- 72 takes controls brain of dead bodies; infected are living
- 72 injures body so that it can replace it; growth just happens naturally with infected
- 72 is a slow process with symptoms present beforehand; infection happens much sooner in TLOU with mostly absence of symptoms
- 72 has no hivemind; TLOU does
- AFAIK, cordyceps don't kill plants (at least not in the real world)

Some ideas I have:

1. I think you could incorporate the idea of 72 taking over the whole nervous system (brain, spinal cord, and nerves). This would mean that it could continue to operate the body by sending its own signals that mimic neurons to individual parts of the body, forcing them to move. This would mean the control over the brain only refines motor skills but 72 can continue to move the body with the destruction of the brain.
2. How does the body react to stimuli? The individual is dead meaning such would be taken over completely by 72. Can 72 understand and respond to sensory information?
3. Can 72 use skills that the host had learned? Not to the level of the body but, for example, could a host that learned guitar still be made to play guitar after death in a way similar to how it had while infected?
4. How strong is 72's control over the body? What are the body's motor skills like when completely controlled by 72?
5. What is the behaviour of the host like when controlled by 72?
6. I think it'd be worth changing the aesthetic of where the fungi grows. It's very similar to TLOU's
