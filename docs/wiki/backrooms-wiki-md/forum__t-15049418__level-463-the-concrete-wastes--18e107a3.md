I'd thought about that, and I actually had details about the M.E.G. testing hazmat suits on this level originally! I chose to remove it after talking with some folks in the discord, because the consensus was more or less that the M.E.G. probably wouldn't have access to that kind of equipment.

But short answer is no, radiation suits wouldn't help on this level. My overall vision for this level was that this radiation is unlike anything studied in the Frontrooms, and so precautions for radiation created in the Frontrooms wouldn't help on this level.
