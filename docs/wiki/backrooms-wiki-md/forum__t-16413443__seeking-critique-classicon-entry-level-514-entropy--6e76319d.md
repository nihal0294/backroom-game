Hey, quick tip; try not to outrightly state the level is creepy or gloomy, sere, ethereal, etc. Use descriptions to put your reader the state of mind of fear and apprehension. Try describing or highlighting the fact the wanderer is all alone, or that there is nothing but silence, or other creepy things. In all, ***describe*** an eerie setting; the fear should. naturally, kick in.
