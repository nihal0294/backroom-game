I didn’t find the beginning part that discusses wanderers who aimlessly wander very interesting, I get that this is meant to tie into what is essentially a resting point for wanderers but it didn’t really say anything that people haven’t already thought of, there just wasn’t anything super novel about it.

Also the level itself isn’t very interesting because the idea of a resting point for wanderers has already been done before only this time it’s a truck stop.

I like the theme of meeting someone or visiting somewhere one time and quickly forgetting about the experiences that came with that, I think the level would’ve been more interesting had that been incorporated throughout the whole page as opposed to it just being briefly touched upon a handful of times.

I also kinda liked the fact that the place went from safe to dangerous, you would expect it to be the other way around so although it’s simple it’s a nice subversion.
