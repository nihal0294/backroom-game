This effect is one of the many factors affecting the safety of this level, and wanderers are advised to stay away from Facelings as much as possible, should they find themselves in this level."*

*"The Apartheid Effect is a phenomenon known to affect most entities on this level, namely Facelings and Hounds. This effect causes the entites to act hateful and discriminative towards other entities."*

**Working so very hard to banish "apartheid" and racism in the Frontrooms, is it honestly necessary to invite this into our Backrooms?
