Entity Number: # 895

Habitat(s): levels with music  
Description:  
radio rash is a radio that plays three different song that cause different effect on entity and humans, on first glance it seems like a normal radio but when you look at it closer you see there is only three button. if you look at at the back of the radio it says "use the radio rash to prank your friends =)".

Biology:  
unknown since you can't damage it

Behaviors:  
If damaged the buttons turn into eyes then the buttons come out of the radio connected to the radio by human flesh. the eyes look around and if they detect the human or entity that damage it it will blast music at 180 decibels until the humans or entity's eardrums burst. if you press the first button it will play never gonna give you up. after 2-3 days of hearing the song you will see a tall slender walk in after in the corner of your vision and you will develop extreme anxiety. if you let the entity catch you it will grab you, pull into the blue channel and it will start whispering never gonna you up over and over and over again it will only let you go if you die. if you press the second button it will start playing daisy bell right after the song ends you get the uncontrollable urge to become a partygoer or a smiler if you find a knife you will carve a smiler face or partygoer face then you walk straight into the entity. if you click the third button it will play it's just a burn memory 15-20 days after the song finishes you will get a burning rash around your head. every time one of the bumps pop it will make you forget one random memory. when all of the bumps are popped your brain will catch on fire

Discovery:  
entity 895 was first discovered by a M.E.G team of 7 people when they where exploring level 92 the M.E.G team brought it to M.E.G base alpha when they click the first button it played extra loud and it was heard by 20 days later they all went missing

Do's and Don'ts:  
Do:  
don't press the buttons  
don't damage  
don't bring it to public places  
Don't:  
press the buttons  
kick it, punch it or through  
press the buttons when there are people around
