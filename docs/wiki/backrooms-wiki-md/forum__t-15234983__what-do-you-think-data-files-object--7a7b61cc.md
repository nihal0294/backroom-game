I personally think its a really good idea and would make a great new object. However, I noticed a couple of mistakes in the writing.

> This however causes them to weight around 5-10 pounds

There's a small mistake here where it says **to weight around**. I think you meant to put causes them to weigh around 5-10 pounds

> They do have one odd feature that is unusual

The problem with this sentence is it repeats the same point twice. I would say instead of using both **odd** and **unusual** in the same sentence, use one or the other.

A small suggestion is instead of writing this section like this:

> **M.E.G.** They currently use Data Files to catalogue new levels, entities, and resources to a better degree than their old methods but because they must be made by entity 251, the M.E.G. has a small amount available.  
> **U.E.C.** They use data files for cataloguing new inventions and discoveries similar to the M.E.G. but also document experimentation on entities with them and the use of weapons on entities. They have as precious few as they must find them due to their hostile relations with entity 251.

Change the group names to a sub heading instead of bold writing in the same line so it appears like this

> ## **M.E.G.**
>
> They currently use Data Files to catalogue new levels, entities, and resources to a better degree than their old methods but because they must be made by entity 251, the M.E.G. has a small amount available.
>
> ## **U.E.C.**
>
> They use data files for cataloguing new inventions and discoveries similar to the M.E.G. but also document experimentation on entities with them and the use of weapons on entities. They have as precious few as they must find them due to their hostile relations with entity 251.

To do this write it like you would a normal heading but put two + instead of one.

I personally think this is a really good idea and look forward to seeing it on the site.
