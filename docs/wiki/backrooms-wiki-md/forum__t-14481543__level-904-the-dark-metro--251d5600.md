The read was thoroughly enjoyable. I enjoyed the simplicity of the article - it's a murder monster, but one that's done in a really interesting way, unlike most of the older entries or its previous iteration.

With this said, there's one big issue throughout the article that I think should be pointed out, and that's flow-related mistakes. The way some things are described is confusing as a first time reader - for example, Galena. Had I gotten, at the minimum, an indication of who Galena was before the interview (I had to work on assumption), it would've helped me more. The way some information is presented is also weird, particularly relating to the living entities in the level. Rather than it being concentrated in one part, the information is spread across multiple large parts, and it does get disorienting sometimes (an example of this would be the explanation that there are three entities, but then only mentioning two things - readers don't know the shadows are two people at that point!!!). There's also some SPaG mistakes which, whilst not so noticeable, could have been corrected by reading it more thoroughly (though I don't blame you for this - I find mistakes in my articles to this day)

From a storytelling standpoint, the only real big issues I could find were that the means are a liiiitttle bit silly, almost comical in a way. There's also the fact that the latin is translated in in one log but not in the other, which was a bit strange imo. The logs are also a bit… odd. The way they describe things is a tad bit disorienting, but I'm placing the blame here on the surreal and hard to describe situations here rather than a lack of writing on your part.

I digress however. I'm naming just the negatives here, without mentioning the positives - which are delightfully abundant, may I say. The atmosphere you build during the description is immaculate, and it sets the mood which you're going for perfectly. I'm particularly enthralled by the sheer presence the Dark Metro has in this article. The image with just 'It.' as a caption is so powerful, it made me shudder when I read through it for the first time. The way it's described as 'alive' near the end is also a bit of a spin on what I'd envisioned it to be prior to reading, and the way that people inside seem to see it differently is also pretty cool.

Overall, there's some small parts of this article that detract from its enjoyment (which I know you can fix if you revisit this a bit), but the atmosphere more than makes up for them. This was thoroughly enjoyable and had me at the edge of my seats the whole time.
