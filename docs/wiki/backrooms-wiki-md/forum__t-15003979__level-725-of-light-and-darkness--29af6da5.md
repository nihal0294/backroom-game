Even if this isn't your best work in your eyes, it's still a very cool level!

I was shocked to learn that at least some of the misbehaving reflections are nothing more than the wanderers themselves who wandered into the void, somehow displaced in time and space. The tale part was certainly what made it great for me. If the wanderer didn't already go crazy at the perception of infinity, this certainly did it! I thought that was a pretty well executed twist.

The actual description itself was pretty interesting as well. Mirrors that project infinite reflections, but slowly become more decrepit as you walk through them… Sounds like it would be enough to drive most people crazy.

It's similar, yet different to Level 365. That level already had some spots where infinite reflections occurred, but it wasn't to this degree of frequency, and probably not to the same level of psychological torture as Level -3 (Not due to the infinite reflections themselves anyway).

The reflections in both levels are trickery, but the purposes appear to be completely different. In Level 365, it was so Sightless Seers could scare prey and use it as food, while here, the reflections trick wanderers into dangerous traps, or simply are people trying to escape from the void.

It makes perfect thematical sense why the entrance is level 365. It gives it the impression that you are diving deeper into mirror madness.

I also really like the page theme. I wonder how it will all look after Scrapcon? There were some really cool ideas here already.
