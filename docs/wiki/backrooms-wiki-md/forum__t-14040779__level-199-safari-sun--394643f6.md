# **The survival difficulty has not yet been chosen.**

**Level 199** is the 200'th Level of the Backrooms

# **Description:**

Level 199 is a vast spread of dead trees and grass, very rarely a house can appear, including at least 1 litre of almond water. Almond water is extremely needed on this level, as it is extremely hot. Level 199 has only 1 entity : [REDACTED]. It is very likely to meet another person on level 199, as all cobble roads in it eventually meet up at a watering hole full of almond water. Parts of level 199 show to be traps, which will lead to death.

# **Colonies And Outposts**

## The Park Rangers.

- Have guns.
- Like to deal almond water from the watering hole.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

### Entrances

You can enter level 199 by climbing into a safari truck that can be found on any level (this truck does not work).

### Exits

If you jump in the watering hole you will end up on any random level.
