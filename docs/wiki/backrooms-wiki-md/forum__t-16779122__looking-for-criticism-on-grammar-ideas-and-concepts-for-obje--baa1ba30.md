I see some SPaG Errors, mainly due to capitalization. You should use capitalization letters at the start if a sentence,nor when referring to a name, important object or place, like "the servant disillusioner" should be "The Servamt Disillusioner" or "The box" should be "The Box". Others SPaG I see include:

1) For the 1st paragraph, change "it containing" to "it contains"

2) Change "has later been modified" to "has been later modified"

3)Change "types of chemicals into chemicals listed below" to "types of chemicals listed below"
