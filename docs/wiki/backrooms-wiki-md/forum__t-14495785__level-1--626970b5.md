Level ±1

16 Feb 2022 19:44

**Page Type:** Level.

Genre : Horror, Mystery.

Core Concept: Level ±1 is a metaphysical space beyond human comprehension, it's a place where you can vaguely discern that there are things around you but you can't identify what they are, similar to what it feels like when you're having a stroke. On this level, you exist not as you are but in the form of a glowing orb that floats, you still have your sight while like this but you float instead of walking. Exiting this level doesn't transform you to how you were before you entered this level, but you transform into an entirely new person, with new memories and a new body.

There's a single entity on this level who keeps a physical form, known as "Amarok". It takes the form of a glowing giant fox, and speaks to wanderers who are on this level. It tells them that they were always destined to give up their body and allow another to take their place. It claims that humans have many creatures crawling beneath their skin, and that when you die, one of these creatures will use your consciousness to birth a new body for them to inhabit.

In the page for this level, Amarok will be directly communicating to the reader and telling them to "expand their horizons" and to break free of their consciousness in order to come to Level ±1, and that doing so will free them of their mortal shell. There will be a collapsible in which the level's details are described, which Amarok claims to be a lie. Most of this level's features will be told to the reader through interviews with people who've gone to this level and now have a new body.

Additional Notes: If this were to be a fully fleshed out page, it'd be extremely large, may make it a bit of a hassle to read. To summarize: Reincarnation, the level.
