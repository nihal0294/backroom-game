rika's flowery commentary

14 Jul 2021 07:10

I really enjoyed the concept and overall description of the entity itself, as well as the drawing. Despite a number of logical inconsistencies and such, the description of your article is pretty solid. The thing that caused me to downvote is the number of spag errors in this article. While normally one or two spag errors wouldn't interfere too much with reading, this article is riddled with them, and in some places there's at least one in every sentence.

> The flesh within its maw is slick, colored hauntingly close to Vantablack as any texture definition cannot be viewed.

The flesh within their maws is slick, colored hauntingly close to Vantablack, as any texture definition cannot be viewed.

This is an example of a sentence with multiple spag errors, and there are lots more in the sentences around it.

This overall makes the article very hard and jarring to get through, and turns it from an enjoyable experience to an unenjoyable one. I strongly suggest double-checking grammar, especially plurality/singularity agreements and problems with subjects. Other than that, though, it's a good article that's just unfortunately ruined by the amount of spag errors. Once these are fixed, this page would deserve and upvote, though.

Interesting Idea, Poor Execution

05 Apr 2022 01:55

The idea is interesting, but the execution feels overall lacking. From how their skin remains so strong, to the very nature of feeding or basic anatomy, too many "unknowns" are brought up, making it feel like a cope-out. In-lore, it makes complete sense for M.E.G. to not know every detail of the entity, but crafting one that deliberately is riddled with so many of these unknowns makes it difficult to feel particularly interested. And with all those unknowns, the rest of the entity feels rather unrealistic. It can't be killed it seems, and it does next to nothing to actually feed. Also, the anatomy (or, what little of it we're told of) makes no sense on a basic level. Why does it have eyes in its mouth— a grimy place where they're likely to get damaged? I've found nothing that actually makes me want to read further.

I'd suggest reworking it to not rely so heavily on unknown variables, and perhaps indulge on its ability to remove the bones of entities in its sight. Maybe it could be covered in eyes to capitalize on this ability?

Also, what's with the font?

(Also, if I've misunderstood, feel free to tell me)
