Object: Dragon's Breath

09 May 2023 11:56

**Type:** Object/Entity?

**Core concept:** The nature of this object is very vague, some wonder if's actually a living, a cursed artifact, or some sort of machine, but no one really knows for sure. The object in question is a fairly large dragon statue that spontaneously manifests in an open-air environment, always perched on a slightly elevated position be it a lone pedestal, atop a wall, and even the side of a cliff, its face a perpetual snarl. These statues never stay long and usually, vanish when left unobserved for roughly 5 minutes or more. They can't move nor do they impede wanderers in any meaningful way, usually being out of the way and otherwise appearing as just a strange fixture in an already strange place.

However if one were to give direct eye contact for longer than 10-15 seconds, its eyes will glow a firey orange or blood red depending on the statue, in which an affliction is formed. Over the course of 8-12 hours, the individual's core temperature will rise exponentially, display feverish symptoms, mind grains, heightened agitation, and extreme dehydration. The moisture in their body will be expelled from their mouths in the form of steam as their internal temperature rises ever hotter yet they won't die. Culminating in the wander littering breathing puffs of smoke and fire usually they're roasting from the inside out until expiration. The only cure is to drink a gallon of almond water before the more serious symptoms manifest.

**Additional Notes:** I'm not sure what to call these things right away or if they should be entities instead, but I figured that since most threats in the backrooms are things that attack you outright, one that preys on curiosity and isn't even confirmed to be alive would be a nice change of pace.

I hope this concept hasn't been taken already.
