Hello.  
I've read your level and from what I've understood the main theme of the level is "Military desert".  
Am i right ?  
I've noticed several qualities of your level as well as some points of amelioration:  
Organisation: The layout of the page is well organized  
Local entities: So the principle of theses entities is that they look similar to wanderers and have access to anomalous capacities. The concept is interesting yet need more flesh-out:  
-Do they talk ? in regular language ? telepathy ?  
-What differentiates them from regular people aside from the anomalous abilities ? What does that imply ?  
-Why do they walk in convoys across the level ? Trade? defense ? Pure programming ?  
-What characteristic of them may be uncanny if they have one ?  
-Is the upgrade process to superior unit dependent on a specific ritual ? under conditions or not ?  
As you're talking about war against factions:  
Is this behavior purely an anomaly ?  
Is this conflict linked to some kind of history ? to resources ? to ideologies ?  
What are the motivations aside from the main ones of each faction that pushes them to attack the others ?

Trivia:  
You forgot to remove:  
Add any bases or outposts you'd like to add.  
If there are no bases, outposts or communities please write:  
"There are no known bases, outposts or communities on this level"

Glad to have red your page.  
Cordially,
