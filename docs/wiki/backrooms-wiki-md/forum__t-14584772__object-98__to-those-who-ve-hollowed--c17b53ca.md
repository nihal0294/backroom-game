OBJECT 98: nb78ui0p To Those Who've Hollowed

30 Mar 2022 01:20

I weep unto those with empty sorrow.

Your blood allows me to feel alive.

I won't believe those who's skin I borrow.

Your love for me is yet to arrive

But your touch leaves me forever hollow.
