I would ask a member of the image team to confirm if it is allowed before you use anything from here, but I believe it is.

<https://accomplice.ai/terms>

"Content

Our Service allows you to create original text, graphics, videos, and other materials (“Content”). Any Content you create is yours to post, link, store, share, sell, use for private or business purposes, and otherwise enjoy with free use. Accomplice does not own any of your Content. As such, you alone are responsible for all Content that you post on or through Service, including its legality, reliability, and appropriateness."

It seems that the content it produces is entirely owned by the person who input the prompt. Thus you would have the ability to license it as you wish, if I understand this right. Regardless of whether this means you can license it, or the photos count as public domain since an AI made it, the company takes no ownership over the things you create with it.

I don't know if you can modify existing images with this AI. I didn't look through all the things it can do (there's a lot), but if that's the case, be careful of the original license of any images you put into it to modify.

Edit: Someone from the licensing team confirmed that it's useable.
