# Valentine Blitz 2026

rating: +24[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

It is a cold, rainy winter. Perhaps not cold enough, the barely frozen tears of the sky splattering onto the concrete floor in a formless snow tainting the few colors still around.

The mood is muddy, the state of the world reflecting the ice melting on the pavement, forming a brown putrid sludge near the park. The sun is distant, abandoning the landscape. It is a rough day.

Yet, despite the cold, there is a warmth in your heart, for whatever the struggles you have to face, you don’t have to do it alone.

## Guidelines

> In this writing blitz, you have until **February 28th** (included) to write an **article containing romance**, collabing **with another writer** of your choice. It is encouraged for the romance aspect to involve a character you made and a character your teammate created, although this isn't necessary. Be sure to be original! Love can take many shapes and form, so don't be afraid to get creative.

- This event is NOT a contest. It's just a thing for fun, to encourage people to write something fast they wouldn't usually do

**Your entry must respect the following points:**

- Be co-authored by you and your teammate (don't forget the **co-authored** tag!)
- Qualify for the **romance** tag (also don't forget this one)
- Be posted before March (as long as it is February somewhere in the world, you are allowed to post without a clear/greenlight using the event tag **valentine2026**

## Rules

- You may submit only one article for this event.
- Your entry has to be co-authored by a maximum of two people (you and your teammate). A co-authored article will count as each author's one allowed submission.
- You may coldpost your article. While you can technically post it as soon as you see this page, it is still heavily recommended you get critique.
- You may participate with an article that began development prior to the official announcement of the event.
- Editing your article after it is posted is allowed, if needed.
- You may use the code "valentineblitz2026" to post entries for the duration of the event. Use of this code for other coldposts will be met with disciplinary action.
- Please tag your entries with **valentine2026**, but also **romance** and **co-authored** since these are mandatory requirements for the blitz. All articles tagged as such will automatically show up on this page.

## Posting Period

As said earlier, you have until the end of the month to write and post your co-authored page. After that, if you want to post your page, you'll have to go through the regular posting process.

- This date is based on "Anywhere on Earth".
- Pages posted through this event still are subject to the [Deletions Policy](deletions-policy.md).
- Please set the parent page of your article to this page for the duration of the event by going to + Options > Parent on your page. Staff will remove the parent after the event has ended.

## One Final Word of Advice

Remember that this event is just for fun. It's not a competition and is meant to be an interesting exercise for you to try, if you're up for the challenge!
