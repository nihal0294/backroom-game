New concept/feature of an interactive backroom cartography

22 Jun 2023 23:06

So I got bored lately and got the best idea of my existence (wrong) to view every levels pages of the wiki and map them all.

This contain every entrance and exits of every "normal levels" "sub levels" and "enigmatic levels" of this wiki with addition of extra ones (some former lvls)  
Each level image will redirect to the corresponding wikidot pages.

Regarding of the third party site used it's called miro app, just a whiteboard app which you can interact with too (that wouldn't fit in a pdf), there also may be a way to embed it via CSS. Also run decently on mobile.

[Board link] : <https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVMDi_u5Q=/?share_link_id=742990535282>
