I really liked the idea behind this level.

Overall, I think you nailed it. Most levels have some kind of defining gimmick or a clear objective for wanderers, so making a level that's intentionally just… boring was a pretty clever idea.

What I liked even more is that you didn't just make an empty room with nothing going on.

Instead, the level has its own anomalous effect centered around boredom, which makes the concept much more interesting.

I also thought the writing was really solid.

That said, I'm not sure whether this was intentional or just an oversight.

The level doesn't have a number—not even something like "Level XXX."

If your goal was to make this an unnumbered level, I don't think it quite follows the usual style those articles tend to have.

Unnumbered levels usually have something very distinctive that sets them apart from regular levels.

That isn't really a criticism, though. The whole point of this level is to be boring, so when I say it "doesn't have anything special," I actually mean that as a compliment.

Now, if you left the number out simply because there's no way to know what number it'll get if it's eventually published… then I just embarrassed myself. Kkk.

I also really like the image. It's memorable and fits the concept really well.

Personally, I think this article is already in good enough shape to be submitted.

That said, don't take my feedback too seriously. Lol.

I read a lot of Wikidot articles pretty much every day, but I'm still fairly new to actually interacting with the community.

So I'm not sure how much weight my opinion really carries.

I'd definitely recommend sending it in for Greenlight. Even if it doesn't pass right away, the reviewers will probably be able to give much more useful feedback than I can and point out anything that still needs work.

Because honestly, I think the article is already really good.
