19 Dec 2022 20:03

I will fully critique this later, but for now you should know two things: you break clinical tone a lot on this page, and there a lot of continuity errors.

First, the clinical tone. A helpful tip for clinical tone is to act like you're a professor who has studied a lot about the subject in hand (in this case, the subject is the Level) and writes as such. I doubt a professor would write "No known outposts—- although be on the lookout! You never know!" on a very important document, especially one that could save the lives of other people. If you find a sentence that doesn't match that clinical tone, change it.

The other thing are the continuity errors. One thing that stuck out to me while reading was this section here:

> If you stay on the small floating island, the island will start to crumble into fragments that are too small to stand on. As you fall, you will see the island reform, as if nothing happened.
>
> There is only theories on what happens to the fallen, the ones who stay on the island, only theories.

I was thinking to myself "Well, if there are only theories as to what happens to those who fall on the crumbling island, how the f\*\*k do they know that you can see the island reform itself while you fall?" You should always avoid these scenarios. Say that that's another theory. Remove it entirely. If your reader has to ask the question of "how do they know that?", you need to rewrite it.

Anyway, I hope that helped you. As I said, I will do a more thorough critique later, but please fix these before I come back to here.

-*QuestionableRose22*
