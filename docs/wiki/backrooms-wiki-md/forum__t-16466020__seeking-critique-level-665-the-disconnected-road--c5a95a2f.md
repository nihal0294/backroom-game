**Article Link:** <http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/univ-wise-explorer-3>

**Article Name:** Level 665 - "The Disconnected Road"

**Brief Description:** Level 665 is an infinite snow-covered level, however, many disturbing facts lie beneath the surface. There is a mysterious black and dangerous humanoid entity that appears after you stray off the main road of the level, mysterious un-interactable people on other roads and other signs of life, and also disappearance periods that seem to wipe people out of existence.

**Genre:** Horror, Mystery, Wonder

**Seeking:** Critique only, I do not believe this is ready for a greenlight yet. I would love any type of crit, even just general remarks and comments.

**Additional Notes/Remarks:** Some of the content can be a little disturbing around the part when the interviewee goes crazy, but if you can handle levels like [Level 909](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-909), it is fine.
