Fair, honestly. This got me to remember what my thought process for some of it was and I feel like rambling about it, I won't have anywhere else to so I might as well.

I don't have an excuse for the hallucinations, I went ahead and gave those a second mention in the Electric. It's still just a brief mention, but at least it's something.

The weird stuff this level does were meant to foreshadow the reveal at the end. For example you get cold and paranoid if you go near windows because you're, though unaware, looking at the beast dead in the eye. My idea with the hallucinations was "Uh, you hear a voice because something is with you at all times, and you see snow because this level is a cold planet. Yeah, sure." I think having them become more frequent in the Electric ties it together with everything else better, since the Electric is meant to mess with you more.

The info hazard stuff was meant to be vague. The implication was that since you know about the entity after reading, it would do something to you if you entered Level 20. I wanted to leave what the beast actually did up to the imagination. Even if I did want to expand on that, I don't see how I could do so without adding a whole new offset or something like that, and at that point it be more of a rewrite than me just editing stuff to fix up an unreadable article. Might make a tale to expand on that, who knows.
