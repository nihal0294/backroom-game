# Fia

This is my entry for **mascotartcon2024**! I worked super hard on this piece and I really hope you all like it(though I didn’t have the motivation to give the piece a background). Her name is Fia, she’s a reindeer. I looked at our site logo one day and thought “huh. The little wing thingies would make great antlers”, and this mascot idea just came to me! If you want to draw her, feel free to reach out to me if you need any help or references(regardless of how well this does in the contest).

Also huge thanks to for the name suggestion!
