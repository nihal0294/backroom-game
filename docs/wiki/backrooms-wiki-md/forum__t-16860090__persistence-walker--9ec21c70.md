**PERSIST** —

At 72 years of age, I have learned this word. I take the trash to the alley once a week. And, once a week, I fill my little medicine dispenser with a seven day's supply of pills. It seems, to me, as if I do these things every other day. I have slowed, but time has quickened. It is rushing past me! Years happen in a little over two weeks now. Grandsons blossom into men before my very eyes, like time-lapse photography. I want to do more, be more, taste more… but I move slower and slower. Every step that I take is uphill. A struggle. There is pain. And still, I must — persist.

A +1 for you, my new friend and colleague, while I still am able.
