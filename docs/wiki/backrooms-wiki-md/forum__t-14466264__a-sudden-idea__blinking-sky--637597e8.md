A sudden idea: Blinking Sky

25 Jan 2022 13:22

There is such a level: the wanderers entered the level will not be able to look up at all, and will feel a great sense of oppression and fear above. But beyond that, there is no danger. People can just walk to the exit with their heads down.

It's always night here, but there's Moonlight lighting, so it's not invisible. But occasionally it turns completely dark for a moment.

Finally, in some way, someone saw the sky overhead: a very ordinary night sky and a moon.

Then he found that it was not the moon, but an eye pupil.

*(I am a BODer!)*
