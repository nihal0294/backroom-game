# The Kingdom of Nowhere

The Kingdom of Nowhere

rating: +17[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

![a-heart-to-be-won.png](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/collab:mothbunnyfilestorage/a-heart-to-be-won.png)

I see a path before us, one that is carved in chalk.  
I see a bold red traffic cone, and a path that ends in dust.  
I see a sign before me, warning me not to go down this road.  
I see a darkness beyond the trees, and I pass a sign on a pole.

I see paste-white branches, and the blue mist of the night.  
I see the woods so dark and deep, and the morning nowhere in sight.  
I see sunset tulips, before a purple sun.  
I see a newfound gift for me, and a long-lost heart to be won.

![shrines-to-good-and-evil.png](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/collab:mothbunnyfilestorage/shrines-to-good-and-evil.png)

I see mornings in silent snow, and frost-jammed escalators.  
I see blocked-up chimneys, and the blurred-out date on this picture.  
I see turbines in the wind, and the wind wipers swishing.  
I see fingerprints on glass, and the first raindrops hitting.

I see busted-up hotels, and graffiti scrawled on roads.  
I see a fire starting, where it's warm and where it's cold.  
I see lightning that strikes, beyond beaches without people.  
I see altar statues on planes, and shrines to good and evil.

![whatever-i-desire.png](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/collab:mothbunnyfilestorage/whatever-i-desire.png)

I see tattoos faded, mall carts deserted,  
Ghost towns flooded, love it or hate it.  
I see stories so bleak, old tech that reeks,  
Suburbs asleep — set aside for you and for me.

I see nails on iron, beehives on wires,  
Digital liars, mechanical pyres.  
I see rupturing glaciers, leaning pillars,  
Mountains of tires, anything I desire.

![the-kingdom-of-nowhere.png](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/collab:mothbunnyfilestorage/the-kingdom-of-nowhere.png)

I see the roots of ancient trees, cracking through concrete walls.  
Amidst the ruin of my house, where one day I shall fall,  
I watch the ghastly pale moonrise, a prison for our weary eyes,  
Tracks that run off to our right, my resting place for when I die.

I am told the track is closed, I am told the PC is sold,  
I am told my model is old, I am told I can't walk this road.  
I seek the attic above the stairs, I seek the fun house at the fair,  
I seek the castle in the air, I seek the kingdom of nowhere.
