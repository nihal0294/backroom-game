I don't quite remember when we had enigmatic rooms? Unless I'm forgetting. Regardless, the point of the 'room' descriptor is that it denotes specific areas that are a part of larger levels. Levels don't really have a minimum or maximum size so a small level that isn't really connected or dependent upon other levels is, at least technically, just a small level, not a room.
