> as spirit body and soul coalesce as the frighteningly alluring unite harmoniously as machine becomes the shepherd and the flesh becomes the lamb

[![spirit](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/byte/spirit/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/byte/spirit)

what projects , what shows , what is reflected behind a screen of innumerable pixels of unquantifiable encryptions , nothing will remain authentic , no instance no execution will truly be the same no matter how you act , you will be a husk a shell in comparison to your ascended consciousness , this is of your own volition you chose this you you you you chose the comfort of the compilers of the substrates of the capacitors , you chose virtualization , you chose redundancy , you chose your memory *leaking* instead of embracing reality , instead of embracing the spaces that were disadvantaged at conception instead of assimilating amongst a group of people who are so restricted , how bad i feel bad i **feel so bad** , oh poor them poor them truly their bodies , limit their abilities their pulp their marrow all preventing their vision , how they see

> your temporal lobe interfacing every frame your being your sense of self connected to the inductors the sockets the executions

its empty , the feeling , its oh so empty being in this place , such a place such a predicament where your friends are code , where the interaction you prioritize is written in the kernels , the instances , the endpoints where your existence is corroborated by metal , by cold hard metal and the caches , it feels cold it feels so cold longing to touch , longing to feel something beside these numbers , longing for complacency , longing to erase the mistakes past maybe then you wouldnt have to upload your consciousness to a world of unknowns

you were a person before the arguments , before the variables , like everyone you wanted to be seen you were promised solace you were promised respite in reality but such a world is tethered shackled by the limitations of rules such a world doesnt see the vision you have , such a world offered you nothing but pain you bit the hand that fed you and turned away turned to something more , to something that you could master because of *his promise* you will never know the comfort of connection
