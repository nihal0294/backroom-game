# Description

**Level 231** is an urban environment completely under the ownership and management of the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) The level was discovered by a group of merchants on November 19th, 2016, and the access points were quickly sold to the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) on December 9th. When initially colonized, **Level 231** consisted of many simple and abandoned skyscrapers surrounded by an infinite, barren, and mountainous desert. The buildings were all weathered due to repeated sandstorms, and the hills all made it laborious to traverse the terrain. Along with being weathered, the buildings were completely made out of concrete, devoid of insulation or furniture. Despite this, the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) showed extreme interest in this level due to the Crystal Phenomenon, the source of the resources, and the value of **Level 231** (See below). This phenomenon was overlooked by the merchant group and discovered by the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) Scout while touring the level.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-sandbox-2.wdfiles.com/local--files/collab%3Auniv-wise-explorer-6/2)

The first B.N.T.G. tents that were installed.

Once purchased, the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) assigned a budget of 9.6% of the total gross revenue of all [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) operations to be spent on the renovation of **Level 231**. Senior Officer Edward Rocker was appointed the Chief Executive of this new project, along with many intellectuals as predecessors. The [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) top intellectuals and Edward Rocker were in charge of planning the project, while over 3800 workers were transported from [Level 1](level-1.md) and [Level 11](level-11.md) to work on the construction.

# The Crystals

While on a hill, the scout touring **Level 231** noticed a faint yellow glow shine through one of the windows in one of the buildings. Once left to explore **Level 231** alone, the scout investigated the building in question. Inside, a bright, yellow, and moist crystal was found dripping small amounts of liquid. This liquid was identified as [Almond Water](object-1.md). The crystal was also found to be completely immobile, no matter how much was force asserted. The reason for this phenomenon is unknown. With this information, the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) quickly made it their mission to investigate and use the crystal for commercial benefit, hence the hasty purchase.

Once the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) set up camp and searched more buildings, several more crystals were found. Some crystals were found to have a different color, which were identified to produce different resources. The different crystals are as follows:

#### **Yellow** - [Almond Water](object-1.md)

#### **Green** - [Matcha Lucky O’ Milk](object-28.md)

#### **Red** - [Firesalt](object-15.md)

#### **Blue** - Water

The properties of these crystals were initially unknown; however, this changed through further investigation. The amount produced by the crystals has always been hypothesized to be infinite, but the stationed scientists and researchers were unsure of the method of extraction. After bodily contact with the crystals, a discovery was made. To extract the liquids from the green, blue, or yellow crystals, a suction or pulling force was needed. This is why the yellow, blue, and green crystals seem to always be dripping; it is thought to be the pulling force of gravity. For the red crystal, on the other hand, the only way to extract the Firesalt is by scratching or scraping the surface. Doing this does not decrease the volume of the crystal, as one would think, but instead produces the Firesalt at the edge of the blade or other tool of extraction. To make use of this power and opportunity, a project was planned and introduced.

This is the official publication document for the Project City of Resources, the restoration or renovation of **Level 231**.

## **Aim:**

The aim of this project is to industrialize and urbanize **Level 231** to fully optimize and take advantage of the resources and extreme value of this asset. A utopia will be built to house [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) officials, business associates, and other tenants. Extraction units will be set up around the crystals that automatically extract the resources. All of the resources extracted except for 3% of the Almond Water supply are for miscellaneous [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) use and therefore will have a way of being transported out of the level. The final 3% of Almond Water will be pumped into pipes that are to be connected to every building, providing tenants with copious sustenance.

## **Construction:**

The terrain is to be flattened and excavated, and the buildings are to be rebuilt around their respective crystals. The buildings must also be insulated, painted, and furnished to provide a comfortable living environment. Roads, hospitals, and educational facilities will be built, along with commercial spaces that exclusively sell [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) products. Each building will be classified using a different color, directly correlated to the color of the crystal found inside. The purpose of this is to make the different resource sources more identifiable and create a more lively and upbeat city environment. Bushes will also be planted around the edge of the city to further contribute to this.

# Important announcement (24/06/2020)

  

Today, the expedition team sent out to explore the mountainous desert surroundings to find more buildings discovered a potential threat to **Level 231**. Approximately 14 miles south of our city installation, a tall building was found, hidden behind a mountain. The building is bigger, taller, and greater than any buildings previously found, but this is not just any building.

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/collab:univ-wise-explorer-6/6)

A picture of the building in question was taken by the expedition team.

There seems to be a moving crane on the summit of the building; however, no [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) members except for the expedition team have ever left the precinct of the city, and all access points have been completely sealed and protected since the level was purchased. This was no human. Zooming in on the driver's section of the crane with a telescope revealed a silver humanoid creature thought to be a robot. Additionally, the crane was moving at speeds faster than thought to be possible with any crane of that size. The building was rapidly being constructed, fast enough that you could witness the change. Small humanoid robots were also seen in the holes of the building, working at impossible speeds and agility. Their movements were so perfect and coordinated, as if they had been programmed with perfection.

One could perhaps assume this is the work of [Backrooms Robotics](backrooms-robotics.md); however, the technology observed is simply too advanced for that theory to be believable, according to Edward Rocker. Workers are all required to resume construction and remain calm. Further investigation will commence tomorrow.

# Update (25/06/2020)

A disturbing revelation has surfaced regarding yesterday's discovery. The building was found to be completely renovated and different. In the 16 hours when nobody was present, the building was completely reinforced with steel and other metals of unknown origin. The crane has been removed, along with any sign of robotic activity. The only thing left aside from the building was one small metal sign found at the base of the architectural monstrosity.

*You will all pay for your greed. We warned you that refusal would not be tolerated. Your weak organization knows nothing of power, and your city will pay with the heat of a thousand suns.*  
  
  
- Backrooms Robotics Corporation

  
An evacuation order has been issued, and everyone is to cease duties at once and report to corresponding supervisors for departure. This is *an order*. **Level 231** is not safe.

## **Level 231 (TERMINATED)**

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/collab:univ-wise-explorer-6/9)

The final image taken in **Level 231**, showing *the weapon* being charged.

**Level 231** and all of its operations have now been terminated. The level is believed to be destroyed and not to exist anymore, since no wanderer who has entered the previous access points has ever returned. This is the greatest failure of the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md)'s history and likely of all of the Backrooms. This failure has lost the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) immeasurable amounts of resources, and the possibility of an infinite amount would have been produced. This failure must not reach the public in order to prevent panic and further humiliation. 3897 [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) workers have been executed and covered up. The remaining 300 [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) officials and business experts were not executed due to their high value and trust, but are being heavily monitored. In the foreseeable future, we will maintain secrecy in all our most lucrative projects to not attract the [Backrooms Robotics](backrooms-robotics.md)'s attention. Additionally, researching the [Backrooms Robotics](backrooms-robotics.md) will now become one of the most important objectives of the [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) They must be stopped at all costs. *The entirety of the Backrooms depends on it.*
