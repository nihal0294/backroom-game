First of all, I am not a greenlighter, but I will give you my honest opinion on your level. So, the most blatant thing that stuck out to me was that none of your linked pages were correctly linked, and instead lead to sandboxes. Your link to almond water should be corrected, as I see an incredibly low chance this will be greenlighted if the link leads to a page called "almond wata, the water smells nutty". You also need work on your clinical tone, mostly in this paragraph:

so it does have an edge. The edge is located exactly 7.79 square kilometres away from the centre. The edge is more unstable than the rest of the level with objects and furniture glitching through walls and bookshelves, you should avoid the edge at all costs if you want a chance of survival since it's infested with entities and furniture glitching can crush you or suffocate you.

As for the lore and design, I think it is quite plain, and in my opinion, this is not a bad thing, as many levels seem to be overcomplicated. If you add anything else to make it stand out, I think this level could be greenlighted in the future. GL!
