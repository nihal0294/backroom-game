# Object 48 2

![Blood](https://backrooms-sandbox-2.wdfiles.com/local--files/collab%3Areyday-liquid-pain/Blood)

Oh God, please make it stop. My breath is short, and my heart is racing, I can clearly taste that sick iron flavor of blood. It’s eating me. It’s eating me from my inside out. I should’ve just been satisfied with what I found. Why did I drink it? Why did I have to do this to myself?

I’m stuck out here in the desert, with no one to help me or even see me die. No one will know where I am. I’ll die and no one will even know. I never thought I'd be alone at my funeral, bleeding out and buried in the sand by my own hubris. I'm going to die alone. I'm going to die alone because I went too far.

Please, whoever finds this, avoid it at all costs. It knows when you’re desperate. It knows you're prey. It knows you need it, but you can’t consume it. It will consume you. You need to make this known, finish what I couldn’t.

Olivia, I’m so sorry. I failed you when you were my biggest supporter. I hope I can see you again someday. Thank you for believing in me.
