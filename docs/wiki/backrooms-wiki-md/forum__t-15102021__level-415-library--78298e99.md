The idea is very cool as is the concept of more book/ more entities the further you travel up.  
One thing though is that the writing sounded slightly awkward at times. For example;

> Some Marking camouflage and few Smiler, many The Book Spider

Perhaps remove 'The' from the book spider or word it like And many instances of The Book Spider  
Also this in the description of the book club:

> They are very friendly, but they won't help.

Maybe They are not hostile but will not provide aid to wanderers  
  
I just felt like this sort of thing happened several times throughout the article.
