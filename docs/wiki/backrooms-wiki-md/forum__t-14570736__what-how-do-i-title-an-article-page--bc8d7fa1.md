Specify where exactly you want to title your page. Are you meaning on the mainlist where you can access different entities (<http://http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entities)?> If so, you need to  
- Upload the page to the number you want (<http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-137>)  
- Go to the main entities page (<http://http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entities>)  
- Scroll to the bottom of the page and press the "Edit" button  
- Scroll through until you find Entity 137  
- Replace [NO DATA] with "The Musician"

If this isn't what you needed, reply please.
