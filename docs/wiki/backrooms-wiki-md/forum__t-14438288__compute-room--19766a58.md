compute room.

09 Jan 2022 09:39

**Page Type:** Room+Entity

**Genre (Optional):** Other (survive)

**Elevator Pitch:** the computer room where the light this room have rain and thunder sound in low quality loops, the temperature is always muted, in this place, your stats are random, hunger thirst resistance… has random values over 1 day (24 hours)  
his only company is an unknown brand all in one computer with the name daisy scrawled across the bottom side of the screen.  
daisy only has a 1/64 chance of appearing in a room, other computers are broken or showing eyes or mouths across their screens always moving along with their movements.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** this is a quiet backroom without too many dangers.  
but it's a backroom focused 100% on survival.  
each person's status will be changed every 24 hours  
these 24 hours can be extreme or extreme thirst, extreme health, extreme joy or depression.

**Additional Notes:** daisy is a computer that has an avatar of a daisy flower across the screen that computer has 75% or 99% of the human personality that looks like a npc  
this computer can give you a maximum amount of 10 items, the items can be from food to firearms but you can only choose 1 item category  
price to pay is counted by the number of checkpoints that are added to the number of windows that the person has gone through, the waiting time until the next purchase varies from 19 hours to 1 week  
you cannot find the daisy computer until the countdown is complete.
