Lovely first entry!

I really like the small details, like using the term Zenith, or describing unreliable entrances as ‘isolated incidents’. These seem small, but they really make the article’s feel more immersive!

Minor nitpicks:  
- Would be nice to explain a little bit about what the Kowloon walled city is first (maybe link to the Wikipedia page). It’s a place many people are unfamiliar with. You could also use the opportunity to provide a more vivid description of how the surroundings look (**build atmosphere**)!

- The explanation about carrier shadows could use work… it’s a bit confusing. I don’t understand what they’re supposed to look like, or how they work. You say that Wanderers who cast a shadow will have their shadow replaced by a Carrier, then no-clip back to the start of the Level. But everything casts a shadow under light, so wouldn’t Wanderers be forced back to the beginning continuously?

- I think an SD class amended might be more appropriate for your level! See [Level 721](level-721.md) for reference.

Good point on the atmosphere! I actually got a bit scared that the article was a bit too long, so I was scared to add too much detail on that. I'll make sure to keep it in mind!
