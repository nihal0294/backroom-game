Level 148 is split into 2 sections, the Crimson Hallways and the Hallway of Infinity[or the Infinite Hallway]. Both sections have a lot in common, with the only difference between them is that you might stumble upon a plant in the Crimson Hallway. The plant is called the, “Crimson Plant.”[1](javascript:;) When exploring this level, there are doors and windows[ capitalized Windows mean the entity, [Windows](entity-2.md) ) which take you to different levels(Put what levels they send you to).
