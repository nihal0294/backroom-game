Level 155: aperature laboratories

02 May 2021 20:21

This level will consist of the entierety of aperature laboratories, and the way to get out would be by either A) defeating GlaDOS or B) completeing all of the tests that she will be put through. there will be a total of 1000 tests that get progressively harder. around level 150, you will get an entity called the portal gun. all levels before 150 will not require the portal gun. with said portal gun you will be able to place 2 portals on surfaces covered in a paste that is ground up moon rocks and water. it is advised that if you ever want the entity "portal gun" to work on any other level that you will have to find hidden caches of this "moon rock paste". around level 300 the entities called "turrets" will start showing up, more frequently the farther you go. at level 1000, there will be an elevator that comes out of the ground that will take you to level 156. you can ride this elevator back up if you ae able to find it again. you would also be able to find cans of soda and cake in hidden spots of some of the levels. this floor would be a class 3 safety rating. there is also a small chance that you find a portal gun part that you can put into your current portal gun. this part would allow you to travel the 4th dimention. yes, time travel. this would make it easier to complete the levels, possibly defeat glados and help you escape the backrooms. this portal gun part would be in one of the weighted companion cubes, making it a moral gamble to either kill the companion cube, or have the possibility of not finding said portal gun part. and yes, the companion cubes are sentient, even though they may not look like it. if you choose to, defeating glados would be pretty easy. all you would have to do is find the entity called "wheatley", but make sure to help him, because he may betray you. once you bring "wheatley" to glados's lair, an automated voice would announce "additional core detected, do you want to transfer core?" in which you and wheatley must say yes.
