**Entity Number:** XX  
**Habitat:** All Levels (most commonly found on level 0, level 9, and the fog on level 69.)

# **Description:**

Entity XX, also known as Mr Happy, is a humanoid entity who wears a white tuxedo-like suit, along-side white shoes, a white top-hat, white gloves and a black-and-white mask, covering their face. Mr Happy has very little exposed skin, with only their neck being visible. The little amount of skin visible appears to be pitch-black, not allowing any light to reflect. Mr Happy is estimated to be around six feet and three inches tall, but this is not confirmed. The chances of encountering Mr Happy is slim, as there is only one confirmed instance of Mr Happy existing, but your chances increase the more upset or sad you are.

# **Behaviours:**

Mr Happy is a docile entity, who is often found on levels that have a large quantity of sad or upset people, such as level 0. Mr Happy will approach upset people and offer them emotional support, as well as items such as food, weapons, and almond water. These items are completely normal, and sometimes even work more efficiently than most normally acquired items. Mr Happy' s presence has a calming effect on humans, and it is unknown whether this is intentional or not. Mr Happy is most commonly seen roaming around random levels, or comforting distressed wanderers. Mr Happy has the ability to instantly teleport between levels, and to other places in the same level, allowing them to reach many wanderers in a small space of time.

# **Biology:**

Mr Happy has extremely dark skin, believed to be true black, not allowing any light to be reflected. It is unknown weather their clothes are a part of their anatomy or not, but they have a texture similar to cotton fabric. Mr Happy is estimated to stand at six feet three inches tall, but their real height is unknown. Mr Happy is capable of speech, having "Pleasant conversations" with wanderers and members of various groups. It is unknown weather Mr Happy can receive damage or not, because nobody has ever tried to inflict any kind of injury onto the entity.

# **Discovery:**

Mr Happy was discovered roaming on level 9 by a wanderer, who told the MEG about the encounter in 2004.

# **Do's and Don'ts:**

**Do:**

- Ask for items
- Have a conversation, as it may help your sanity
- Talk about your problems, as they can help you find a solution

**Don't:**

- None, Mr Happy is not a threat in any way, no matter what you do.
