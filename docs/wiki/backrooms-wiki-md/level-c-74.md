Level C-74

**Level C-74** is the 74th level of the Backrooms C cluster.

# **Description:**

Level C-74 is a forest. Like most other Backrooms levels, Level C-74 appears to have no border and does not have linear space stability. Any attempts to record paths in Level C-74 would be declared pointless.

Identifying whether one is within Level C-74 is quite easy: all humans who no-clipped into this level will be in close proximity to a large tree with the words "No pedestrian passage"[1](javascript:;) written on a traffic sign.

**Beware:** after confirming oneself to be within Level C-74, please rapidly escape (the escape method is located at the *"Exits"* section below). As of now, there are no humans who remain contacted/alive(?) in Level C-74 past 10 minutes after entry. Although most of them have never been seen alive or dead, there are no cases of anyone returning safely after 10 minutes. Like what is described above, the longest current recorded contact time is 10 minutes; wanderers remaining at this level for more than this time limit have unknown outcomes.

At the same time, within Level C-74 wander large amounts of entities; this includes but is not limited to: [Cleaners](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-2), [Clumps](entity-5.md), [Dullers](entity-6.md), [Hounds](entity-8.md), and [Laughers](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-6), all of them falling within the "dangerous entities" category. Notably, the aforementioned entities can exhibit erratic behavior which suggests some level of psychological differentiation from their common counterparts[2](javascript:;). The creatures do not abide by their regular behaviors but instead frantically move around while shrieking and acting combatively. This aside, almost every entity was reported to react "surprised" and "crazy" to people who recently entered Level C-74, trying to approach them while ignoring everything else. At the same time, the wanderers, for some reason, will experience a supernatural fear, shock, and even exhibit sadistic tendencies as well other abnormal behaviors[3](javascript:;) towards attacking entities. This often leads to fierce counterattacks until the entities are neutralized[4](javascript:;).

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There are no bases, outposts, or communities on this level.

# **Entrances and Exits:**

## **Entrances:**

- All forest levels have the hazard of accidentally no-clipping into Level C-74; if you have discovered a *traffic sign* on a tree similar to what is mentioned above, you are deemed to have entered Level C-74.
- Further entrances pending investigation.

## **Exits:**

- Finding a wooden door on a branch will take you back to the level you were previously at[5](javascript:;).
- Further exits pending investigation.

Footnotes

[1](javascript:;). Referring to the sign's design. Combining Level C-74's realistic situation, more people believe the real meaning is "No humans allowed"

[2](javascript:;). Some people who rapidly escaped and survived reported the yells to be similar to "crying."

[3](javascript:;). The current hypothesis is that Level C-74 has a psychological effect on humans. Fortunately, this has a positive effect, as it propels humans to conduct powerful counterattacks on entities, even torturing and killing them.

[4](javascript:;). Level C-74's entities lack their physical strength and special abilities and seem to constantly attempt to use their unsystematic language/strange noises to communicate with humans.

[5](javascript:;). There are wanderers who have reported finding multiple corpses beside the door. According to an autopsy of their stomach, their guts were filled with tree bark; currently, they are deduced to have died of hunger.
