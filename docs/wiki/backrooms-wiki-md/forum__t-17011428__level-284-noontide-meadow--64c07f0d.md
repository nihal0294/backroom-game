I'll give some SPaG and general fixes.

Things to fix:  
The image on the page is broken. This can be fixed by taking your image and uploading it onto the sandbox page, then replacing the Discord link with the one Wikidot generates.  
Whenever you mention Level 284, it should be styled in bold like "**Level 284**".  
When you first mention a level, entity, object, etc it should be hyperlinked. An example format of this would be [[[level-284|Level 284]]].

SPaG:  
Comma after "faint".  
"cuts" could be "cuts off".  
"after several meters" could be "a few meters in".  
Whenever mentioning a level with its number, start "level" with a capital ("Level 284" for example). Do this in all cases.  
"although" can just be "though".  
Put "the" before "level's exclusive entity".  
The comma after "into the forest" isn't necessary.  
"after entering an" would work better as "after discovering an".  
"Pasture keeper" could be "Pasture Keeper", with the start of both words capitalised.  
"Only one of them made it alive to level 288." did you mean to say Level 284? If so, that can also be fixed.  
"backrooms" should start with a capital ("Backrooms").  
"In 2019, grain was imported from Level 10" should be "In 2019 grain was imported from Level 10,".  
"agroculture on" should be "agriculture in".  
The comma after "plots of land" is unnecessary.  
Again, start both words of "pasture keeper" with a capital ("Pasture Keeper").  
"attack the wanderer" can just be "attack them".

By the way, I don't think the barn would work as both a base and an exit via sleeping. It would mean every Barn Keepers member eventually leaves the level.

No comma necessary after the Level 288 exit.

In the image caption, change "A picture taken by a Barn Keepers member of level 284" to "A picture of Level 284 taken by a member of the Barn Keepers".

I think that's everything for the structure of the page.

Now, for the writing itself, this falls into the category of what people here call a "nothingburger". There isn't anything going on with the page besides basic descriptions of everything in the level. Those parts are fine in a page, but it needs more depth to go with it.

For example, with a clinical (that is, a page focusing more on level description that story) page I'm currently writing, I'm also being descriptive about how the people in the level survive and live their daily lives. A lot of pages could benefit from this kind of thing, including yours, but there are also other things that can be focused on. The Pasture Keeper, for example, is quite glossed over. Really, in a situation where something like it exists, tales and myths about it might spring up. It may prove beneficial to include things like that. Additionally, further describing the members and structure of the Barn Keepers group will likely make for a more interesting read. People like to read about people, after all. With this in mind, the page's blandness can be cured in several ways.
