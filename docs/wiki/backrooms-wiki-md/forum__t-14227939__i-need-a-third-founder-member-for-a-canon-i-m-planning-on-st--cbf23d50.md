I need a third founder member for a Canon I'm planning on starting.

15 Oct 2021 15:33

Basically the Created Ones' society collapsing is bound to affect other GOIs and anybody who has family living under the flag of the Commonwealth. I want to explore that in a Canon called "The

The background for the Canon is the Commonwealth attempted to mitigate social unrest after an outbreak of tuberculosis killed 1 million out of the 6 million by lifting restrictions on moving to the Commonwealth's territories in Level 10 I'm planning on writing into the Level 10 article soon. Long story short, 4 million of the remaining 5 million people leave because they're sick of still living like it's the Middle Ages when humanity has outpaced them technologically by centuries.

The vast majority of who's left still in Level 321 are the clergy and most of the nobility, and almost a million angry peasants.

The Commonwealth completely collapsed after the mass migration of so much of its population and a power vacuum has appeared. there's now the potential for a violent Russian Civil War style conflict to arise in Level 321, with potential gain from interference by other GOIs.  
I will be fleshing out the Commonwealth in five stories that will portray the events as described here, and those will set the stage for everyone else who finds this of any interest to contribute. All I need is a third member to join me and Argonmighty in making this happen.
