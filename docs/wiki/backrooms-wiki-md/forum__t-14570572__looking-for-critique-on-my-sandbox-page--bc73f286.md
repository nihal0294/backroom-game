don't use the word like "kind of" or "sort of". Any expression of "uncertain" can be changed to be slightly more formal. *"Seem" is OK.*

> "but if you attack it or your seen as a threat to it or its cult then it will send you to **the smile land.**"

I feel like it would be better to replace it with:

> "But if its cult or itself is considered threatened or attacked, the entity or person it sees as an attacker will be no-clipped to **The Smile Land**"

It's better to use "you" less often. It might not be so…clinical tone.

Also, the relationship between some sentences confuses me a bit. It's best to read it yourself from cover to cover.

Finally, there may be some words that will not be checked by the machine:

> entrances and exists:

Some more grammar problems to fix

---

> Smiley face has two states. Canvas and active.

Change period to comma and correct capitalization

> Smiley face has two states**,** **c**anvas and active.

---

> When active the image on the canvas disappears and smiley face appears on a level of its choice.

Add commas and capitalize.

> When active**,** the image on the canvas disappears**,** and **S**miley face appears on a level of its choice.

---

> Smiley face is normally docile to wanderers and has a low temper, But if its cult or itself is considered…

Lowercase and punctuation

> Smiley face is normally docile to wanderers and has a low temper, **b**ut if its**'** cult or itself is considered…

---

> …the entity or person it sees as an attacker will be no-clipped to the smile land, If not…

Again, punctuation and capitalization, along with wording choice

> …the entity or person it sees as an attacker will be no-clipped to **Smile Land.** If not…

---

> .be rude to smiley face.

Instead of using a period, use a bullet point by using \* and a space after it (e.g. below). Also capitalize.

- Be rude to Smiley face.

---

There are more, but I don't want to make this too long,

Happy writing!
