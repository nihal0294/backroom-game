This story is going into one of my future pages, titled “The Music Box”

When I was around 9, my siblings and I had this toy keyboard. It was about medium-sized, not a full-size keyboard but it had about 3 octaves of notes, though it was always slightly out of tune. Well, we would play with it often and it resided in our basement. Fast forward a few months, we’d all forgotten about it, but in the middle of the night, I’d wake up, on numerous occasions, to the sound of it playing(my house has really thin walls). I’d go downstairs and it would just be playing random notes that barely resembled a song. The keys wouldn’t move, and the piano was completely still, but it’d play anyway.

It’s either rotting in our garage now or we donated it to Goodwill, and it’s now someone else’s problem.
