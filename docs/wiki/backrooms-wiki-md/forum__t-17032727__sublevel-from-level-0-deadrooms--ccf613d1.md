First off, I'm going to guess that everything mentioning groups of people is only about the sublevel and not Level 0, since the latter isolates every individual person.

But, still, this currently reads like a purposeless 2020 page. Right now, the sublevel seems to just be a Level 0 reskin. But there are fixes to that.

Personally, I would suggest adding further explanation of the Wretches and why they're there. I would also mention the exit before the group information, since as it seems so easy to leave this sublevel, there would be less need to directly act in sending wanderers back. But, really, if these are "Dead"rooms, I would change the whole area to be significantly more dangerous.

The danger doesn't have to come from entities, but can from the environment. Instead of higher carbon dioxide levels, maybe there could be no atmosphere altogether, for example.

That's really everything I have to say for now.
