Hmm, alright, lemme just see what I can fix here.

“ The temperature does not seem to affect them.”  
So, this in habitat is either not needed or really confusing. Is it either the Wolf’s temperature that doesn’t effect the forest, or does the forest’s temperature not effect them?

“ They typically travel alone, but up to groups of three have been spotted.”  
You don’t need the up to here, it can just be, “They typically travel alone, but groups of three have been spotted.”

“ …to kill the wanderer that attacked it”  
Replace the “wanderer that attacked it” to Attacker.

“These entities seem unaffected by temperature, as they are found in levels of all temperatures.“  
Two things here:  
1: To sound more fancy and clinical, you could do, “These entities seem unaffected by temperature, as they can be found in the more cold levels, and the more hot levels.”  
2: If the statement above is true, then the habitat should be Majority.  
“These entities have the ability to howl.”  
You just said that these entities are similar to the frontrooms wolf, and well, it’s a wolf. I don’t think you need this.

“[[Level 121]]]”  
To put a link, you have to put the website link on it, have 3 [, and to name it you can put |. So, for example, [Level 11](level-11.md), and for source link [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-11](level-11.md) | Level 11(without the brackets, if you need the page, include the brackets.)  
“(Optional)” Not needed, near the Additional Info.  
“it seems to sink into the ground and vanish. Attempts to locate the body have been unsuccessful”  
Two things:  
1: Does it literally sink to the ground? Like, when killed, it’ll slowly no clip into the ground?  
2: The second sentence, “Attempts to locate the (bodies) have been unsuccessful.”  
That’s all the critique I can give
