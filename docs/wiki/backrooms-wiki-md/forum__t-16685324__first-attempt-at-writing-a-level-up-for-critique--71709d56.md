Hello and welcome! Just to let you know, our Discord is more active than our forums when it comes to critique, though we have a small group who prefers our forums. :)

I think you're right in thinking that there are too many ideas going on at once. Going off this brief blurb, I was a bit disappointed that the level wasn't in more ruins, especially since it *moves.* Don't think I've seen that before! How cool! Rather than this vague entity threat (which you could recycle for another page), the idea of a meteor screaming through space dragging along poor wanderers and bits of other levels is a very strong image and has potential for lots of different themes: survival, natural disasters, uncertainty, etc.

And one more thing, as a wiki, we're moving away from requiring pure clinical tone, so I recommend looking at more recent levels to see what other authors have been doing and what our Greenlighters expect.

I have to say that the idea is good and I don't think it's too long. Furthermore, there are already articles on the wiki with absurdly long descriptions such as [Entity 999](entity-999.md), [Level 999](level-999.md), [Object 1](object-1.md) and others. A recent and long one is [Level 228](level-228.md), which at first seems short, but then you see that it is very long. And also that I have already written very long, extremely long articles.

---

> Its nature leads to the Level being surprisingly dangerous, with the numerous Events being a major cause for concern for any Levels affected. Should you find yourself here, try to walk as far away as possible. You don't want to stay.

You have to move this paragraph to the description section, since any reader will see that it seems something torn from the level description.

And you also have to fix the capitalization issues in the parts that say "Level", "Levels" and "Events."
