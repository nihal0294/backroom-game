So, I like levels that execute the personalized writing thing(could never do that fancy science stuff for fun lol). However, it’s hard to pull off that kind of writing in a level and still make it seem like it belongs in the Backrooms wiki.it’s why I respect those able to pull it off. What I’m saying is that you can’t have one note that describes the level and that’s it.  
What I suggest is to add more notes, talking about their time there and probably have the last note to be about a janitor.  
Now, two things I suggest removing/changing.  
Since this is a paper note, I don’t know why [REDACTED] would be there. If you need to know where it is,

The Building Has 7 Floors, But May Have More, And I Finally Found Out What The Source Of The Water And Electricity Is! It's [REDACTED].

  
Unlike the SCP Foundation, The M.E.G. has almost every information open to everyone, so those who end up in levels they don’t know at least has a chance of survival. Another thing I may suggest changing is the source all together, since earlier you wrote

Anyways, It Appears That The Building Generates Its Own Gravity, Electricity, And Running Water. It's From An Unknown Source.

  
I might actually suggest adding dates to the paper notes, and splitting some paragraphs off.  
I’m not gonna do SPaG(I already have a large page I’m doing now), however, I recommend you put this through a grammar checker. Grammarly is most suggested.

OH ONE MORE THING  
Did you get permission from the actual author to put their article in here?
