Who was on that radio? Sometimes it's lit, sometimes it's not. There's a strange cult that say they offer "protection"...but what kind of protection? Let me leave. Oh God, let me leave.

Level 148 - "Strange Catacombs"

07 Jul 2021 12:55

My only concerns are that people might think it's unoriginal, and that I might have overdone it. I wouldn't want it to be boring, but I went into quite a lot of detail, so I'm thinking I might have to scale it back a bit.

I also explained a unique entity for this level, which I think might be better suited as its own entity page. I know creating too many entities that are unique to one level is a bad idea, so I only went with the one. The radio receiver entity thrives on the idea of paranoia — of something coming to get you, but it never really does. All it does is take away your ability to scream.

Anyways, here's the link: [my draft for Level 148](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/alkrolyd)

I'd love to know the thoughts! Obviously, I won't get my hopes up about it getting greenlighted lol, but it's worth a shot.
