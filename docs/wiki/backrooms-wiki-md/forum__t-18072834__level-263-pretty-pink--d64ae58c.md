Novote, but I do like the idea behind it

30 Jun 2026 23:05

I'll just be blunt here. I found the page pretty tropey, even for a format screw rewrite. While I do appreciate the choice to do the format screw in this way, a lot of it came across pretty dry. Granted, this type of thing is pretty challenging to write, so I can't hold it against you too much. I think the biggest thing keeping this from being a downvote is flipping around the "please come to me I love you teehee ;)" story by… having Pink actually be genuine and chill

However, the presentation is what's keeping it from an upvote. The "please please please pl-" rendition is an example of what I mean when I say "tropey." I can give the standard "Level description" page at the end a pass, because it serves as a fine conclusion to the page.

This next part is just a personal opinion, so feel free to ignore the next paragraph

As far as suggestions go, if I were writing this I probably would have focused more of the page's content in the chat logs between Blair and Pink. With the amount of things that try to "hack" the databases of any of the 3 letter GoIs, I think seeing there be more exchange between them would give the page a lot more punch. There's a lot more I think you could do with the concept, and a lot of different directions you could take it.

I'm known as "the guy that hates Levels" though, so maybe I'm just being a hater. All in all, the effort and ideas of the page make it far from a downvote, but the pacing and execution keep it from an upvote.

I would like to see more with this one though. Whether it's just a dialogue touch up, full rewrite, or something else entirely
