hello backrooms community, call me T.V.  
before I start please correct me if I have the wrong information and my writing is horrible.

(For me) The backrooms have lost the horror the backrooms held. Now I think of the backrooms as something to explore, an adventure of sorts. The backrooms started off with an eerie image (everyone reading this should know about it.) on 4chan I think? Anyways that inspired this wiki and many articles were written adding their idea of the backrooms. All the articles were all unique and meshed well in the fabric of the backrooms. When I first started getting into the backrooms many levels brought fear, but then it got too big too fast, then dinnerbone 777 (I think) made the first video of the backrooms. Then made more. then kane made his first video on the backrooms (Again I may be wrong) which at the point of writing it has gained over 40 million views. This is where I think they lost the dread of being in the backrooms. Many people started to jump on the bus to "milk" this new idea for content. Most people made it look like some internet joke. I like the backrooms as this place where there was nothing except solidarity, with you and whatever entities lived on that level. It was a simple horror, with a small portion knowing about it. Soon ruined by the internet, by how popular it got .

I still love the backrooms, but as something to explore more than to dread.  
anyways that's all, thank you for reading.  
Dr Television out.

Hello,

I've read your post and i think there is something really interesting here.  
Therefore, i've reread the old levels and compared it to the new ones.

If you think about it, the old levels :  
- are liminal, close to reality without being completely real  
- closed instead of being an open space  
- Seemingly infinite  
- Highly dangerous or surreal  
- Absurd by nature

Well,  
if you think about it, the main problem can compare to films series:

How do you create something albeit coherent with the tone and the setting of the Backrooms ?  
Therefore, the main reason that came to my mind while writing is that not everyone have the same interpretation of the backrooms, thus deviate a bit with the new entries.  
This lead to new Backrooms material.

While i'm certain that Backrooms have lost a part of their eeriness, i don't think this is automatically a bad thing. Because in this collaborative piece of work, good and bad are to be defined.

Howewer, there is multiples canon and multiples sites for the backrooms (fandom, wikidot).  
Therefore, why don't create a compilation of eerry levels ?
