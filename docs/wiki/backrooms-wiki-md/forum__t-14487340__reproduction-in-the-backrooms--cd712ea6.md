With how time works in the Backrooms, it's hard to establish a solid answer. Humans don't die from age, we know that, but it hasn't been confirmed whether they *can* age or not. If they're stuck in the age they entered as, then I see it nigh impossible for children to be born and grow. If other people can't age, then why would an embryo?

Now if time works just *very, very* slowly, then it's feasible for children to be born and just age super duper slow. Another way is that babies are born and reach a certain age normally, let's say 18, and then they stop aging. Why this happens is up for a headcanon, as that's personally mine.

For entities, I think they just spawn in. I don't think they have to reproduce, as the Backrooms created them in the first place. It can just make more if it needs to, or maybe it just passively makes more entities.
