I was looking for something to create a map with recently and came across something that I could use, but it used the [MIT License](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT), which I wasn't sure if we could use.

The program I found specifically has a slightly altered license, which is linked [here](https://github.com/Azgaar/Fantasy-Map-Generator/blob/master/LICENSE).

The program's QA page says the following on it:

> ## Who owns the maps created?
>
> You. The Generator is licensed under MIT license and derivative works such as maps are free of charge. You can sell them or make them available for free.

So I think it's alright but I just want to be sure.

MIT is one of many software licensing terms that is widely used. Compared with other common licensing terms (such as GPL, LGPL, BSD), the MIT license is almost the most relaxed copyright agreement, which means that others can do whatever they want as long as they include the above copyright notice in the content.  
(including "use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense and/or sell copies")
