Level 225-"cold conditions"

09 Apr 2026 15:35

Hello dear members and moderators, this is the first level I’ve created; I hope you’ll find it acceptable and give it a read.

Brief summary: Level 225 takes place in a forest on a snowy night. In this level, snow falls continuously and never melts. There are mountains in the level, but climbing them is impossible due to the weather conditions. There may be caves around the mountains. These caves contain stalactites from which almond-flavoured water drips. There is a discovered entity in this level called the snowman. These snowmen appear to be snowmen, but when the traveller turns their back, they approach and attack. The bodies of the deceased travellers have never been found, but according to one theory, the travellers killed by the snowmen turn into snowmen themselves (this theory is based on the increase in the number of snowmen). There are two interesting groups found in this level. The first group once lived in a cave, but the group fell apart for unknown reasons. The second group, however, has been reported to constantly attack travellers, and very little is known about them.

Also, the level I’ve created so far is a bit short, but I’ll be adding more content as time goes on.(There are many things I haven’t mentioned in the summary of the level I’ve created; you’ll only be able to see these once the level is published)
