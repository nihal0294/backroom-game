Hello, people of the internet! I'm AlmostEternity, and I've been watching this site for a while now (about 2 years, give or take.), but haven't bothered to join until now.  
My favorite things to do are listen to music, write, and draw.  
I'm best at that those last two when I've got a reference, motivation, and/or and inspiration.  
If I had a goal in writing for this site it would be to get my articles actually noticed on Youtube (Did I actually say that aloud…? Umm…).  
I'm currently writing my own non-Backrooms-related story, doing a collaborative with another person, and working on starting on a second one with a different person.  
My writing will normally have inspiration from one of four things:

- Things I head(Music, sayings)
- Things I've experienced
- Feelings I have
- My imagination

One thing I like are stories with multiple layers to them. So a lot of my stories may have something extra to them hidden. I hope to get to know you all.  
See ya around.
