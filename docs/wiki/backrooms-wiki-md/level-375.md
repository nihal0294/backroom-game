# **Description**

My name is Jess. I'm 25.

My family moved here a long time ago, and ever since then, these potatoes have kept my family going.

This land is an infinite, beautiful place with clear, bright blue skies, trees scattered around, and delicious potatoes that contain all the parts of a balanced diet. This is due to the dirt, which I have found to give the same benefits to other crops I've cultivated. These crops include onions, garlic, ginger, turnips, and radishes.

I sleep peacefully here, with the gentle silence and wind rustling the leaves.

Sometimes, though, I have nightmares.

Nightmares of that time oh so long ago…

I was quite young, only seven years old at the time.

We had just arrived in the Backrooms, and we were wandering in the [Abandoned Utility Halls](level-2.md) when we were chased by a [Clump](entity-5.md). I was so terrified. My brother was lagging behind due to him being younger. The [Clump](entity-5.md) proceeded to chase us into the [Electrical Station](level-3.md).

We ran as fast as we could, for as long as we could. However, we were getting tired. My brother lagged behind again, and the [Clump](entity-5.md) grabbed him. The [Clump](entity-5.md) was gaining on us. Just as we thought that all was lost, we found a door. I remember this door vividly. It was made out of green leaves, all intertwined, with dirt and potatoes as the doorframe. My mom told my dad to get me through the door, and then she turned back to save my brother. I heard my brother scream, and I imagined the worst. Right then, my mom burst through the door with my brother, bloody axe in hand. My mom had shut the door, but we could hear the [Clump](entity-5.md) scratching at it. Amazingly, as fast as the [Clump](entity-5.md) tried to tear down the door, the door just kept growing back! This door saved us from the [Clump](entity-5.md), and after the [Clump](entity-5.md) gave up and the scratching stopped, we all sighed in relief and looked around in wonder.

This was a paradise. Trees swaying in the breeze, the sweet smell of earth. My parents eventually built a log cabin with the wood that was there and with the axe my mom always had on her belt. My brother and I rolled around in the dirt, laughing and playing. Something about the area just felt so… relaxing. Even after our horrifying encounter, we just felt… at peace. Everything was so calm.

  
Being in this peaceful place has helped me work through the fear and trauma that the experience gave me. I've noticed that the nightmares are more infrequent now.

# **Bases, Outposts, and Communities**

It's just me and my family living here, but you're free to join us. We're open to trade, and we're accepting of everyone. I believe this level can be a healing place for those afflicted in mind, body, or spirit.

# **Entrances and Exits**

#### Entrances

Finding a door made out of green leaves, all intertwined, with dirt and potatoes as the doorframe in the [Electrical Station](level-3.md) will lead you to us. There may be more entrances; let me know if you find them.

#### Exits

Exiting through the same door will lead you back to the [Electrical Station](level-3.md)
