please greenlight level: level 3.5

11 May 2022 15:05

survival class 3  
unsafe and unsecure  
high entity count  
lots of supplies.

Level 3.5 is a level that is in-between level 3 and 4. it is filled with rooms that look like level 3. however, the walls are different, as they are the same color as level 4.  
there are twice the amount of entities, and the same ones, as in level 3. also, there are twice the amount of the same supplies in level 4.

Entrances and exits: you can enter by breaking a bottle of almond water on level 4. to exit, you must do the same, and you will end up on level -1, with all supplies you own. you can also exit to level 4 by pressing a randomly appearing red button, which will appear on rare, red colored walls. you will pass out and wake up on level 4, with all your supplies beside you. This means that, if you wake up next to a wall, the supplies may clip through and disappear.

The outposts here are:  
Meg base Risk: a generic meg base,  
The Suppliers: who live on this level and will sometimes send people to leave the level to trade with others for supplies, then they return.
