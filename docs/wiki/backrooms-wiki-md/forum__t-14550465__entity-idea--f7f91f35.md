Entity Idea

04 Mar 2022 23:37

Page Type: Entity

Core Concept: CLOVER is a sentient ai that resides on a Windows 95 desktop. Although the desktop has a range of applications on it, CLOVER's chatlogs (I was thinking their chat app looks like a slightly edited version of ICQ.) appear constantly and cannot be removed.  
CLOVER is a very friendly entity and is completely willing to help wanderers whenever they can. I'd like to assume that they'd work with the M.E.G.  
They do their best to answer any questions and their knowledge roughly spans to that of a Backrooms veteran; they can help others survive, but they can't answer anything too OP like how do the Backrooms properly work or anything.

Additional Notes: I'm currently brainstorming a level for them, as well as a fully fleshed-out personality. (My current level idea is like a computer lab, although that's definitely up to change.)

This sounds very similar to [Entity 82](entity-82.md), and I don't think it would really work too well if they went with the M.E.G.. If you wanted this to work, I suggest making it a bit more interesting. A new origin might be that CLOVER was an old Koko model and was disregarded for newer equipment, or maybe it just came into existence and is now trying to learn about who its creator is. That always makes a good story, in my opinion.
