ObjectCon2022 Object 12: Taste Trees

09 Sep 2022 05:59

Page Type: Object

Genre: Mystery

Core Narrative: Object 12 revolves around 7 trees. 5 of the trees produce food that corresponds to the different levels of taste: Salty, Sweet, Sour, Savory (Umami), and Bitter. The last two trees were only seen producing meat and vegetables. All food is safe to consume. These trees are guarded by giant worms with human teeth called Tasters.

Black Tree: Produces only coffee. The tree appears burnt and damaged. The bark on the tree is warm to the touch. (Bitter)

White Tree: Produces Salt, French Fries, & Pizza. Physical contact with the tree may increase blood pressure. ( Salty).

Yellow Tree: Produces Citrus Fruits such as oranges and lemons, Vinegar, Sour candy, and Sour Cream. Food should not be consumed for at least 1 hour to reduce Sour levels. (Sour)

Pink Tree: Produces Sugar/ Sugary foods such as candy, soda, donuts, & chocolate. No leaves have been seen on the tree instead, cotton candy appears to grow in place of leaves. A sweet scent wafts from the tree as well. (Sweet)

Tan Tree: Produces a variety of food such as popcorn, hamburgers, peanut butter & beef jerky. The food produced are not always solid as soups can be obtained from this tree as well. (Savory)

Red Tree: Produces only Spices and Meat. Meat varies from personal preferences. The tree appears to be made from meat as well such as red meat, chicken legs and barbecue ribs. Meat collected during the day will already be cooked, meat collected at night will be raw.

Green Tree: Produces only vegetable and certain fruits. This tree is considered sacred to tasters as vegetables helped them stay healthy for years. Food consumed helps the body stay physically fit.
