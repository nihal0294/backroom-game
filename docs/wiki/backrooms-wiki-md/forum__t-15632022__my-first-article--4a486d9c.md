For a person who doesn't speak English very well, you have very good grammar, just based on this paragraph. I've seen worse SPaG mistakes from new users.  
Focusing on "There are some rooms by the hallway, There are usually some substances and furniture or household items in the room, But some rooms are empty inside and there is nothing inside.", as it is the only sentence in this paragraph that has errors in it.  
This is what we call a run-on sentence, in which you are connecting three independent clauses(a group of words that contain a subject, verb, and express a complete thought. They can work as sentences on their own). You need to change your punctuation and wording a little here. Change the comma(,) after "hallway" to a period(.) With the independent clause that starts with "There", you have a few options. You can turn this into a compound sentence:  
"There are usually some substances,and furniture, or household items in the rooms, But however, there are some rooms that are empty inside.and there is nothing inside"  
This, without my edits, would look like this: "There are usually some substances, furniture, or household items in the rooms, however, there are some rooms that are empty inside."  
I suggest using Grammarly.com or some other spell checker before submitting an article, as it will find many mistakes you missed while typing. I even use it on my critique.

> At present, a variety of substances have been found at this level. Common substances are Almond Water, Firesalt, and Babel Balm. Sometimes, you can find Squirt Gun and Royal Rations in the room.

I suggest replacing "substances" with another word/other words to avoid repetition, such as using "useful items" instead. You can combine the first sentence with the second sentence. You can say:  
"At present, a variety of substances have been found at this level, such as Almond Water, Firesalt, and Babel Balm." In the third sentence, you need to make "Squirt Gun" plural, as "Royal Ration" is plural.

> Level 764 has a small number of entities. Common entities are Facelings, Deathmoths, and Dullers. They usually don't actively attack wanderers unless you provoke them, On extremely rare occasions encounter The Smiler, They actively attack wanderers, When encountering them, hide in the room until they leave.

You can combine the first two sentences here to avoid repetition:  
"Level 764 has a small number of entities, such as Facelings, Deathmoths, and Dullers."  
The next sentence is an extremely long run-on sentence due to the commas. I'll do the same thing I did with the last one.

> They usually don't actively attack wanderers unless you provoke them, On extremely rare occasions encounter The Smiler, They actively attack wanderers, When encountering them, hide in the room until they leave.

I'm just going to correct it:  
"They usually don't actively attack wanderers unless you provoke them. On extremely rare occasions a wander may encounter a Smiler, who actively attack wanderers. If you see one, hide in a room until they leave."

> According to wanderers who entered this tier, They always feel like they're being stared at, Although there is nothing around. The longer the wanderer stays at this level, the more pronounced this feeling becomes, Therefore, we do not recommend staying on this level for a long time.

This paragraph can be deleted, it's just an unneeded extra detail.

> It's worth mentioning that Level 764 has Wi-Fi, And the Wi-Fi signal is strong. This allows the wanderer to update and modify the information at that level at any time.

You can add the detail of the wifi being strong with your statement that the level has Wi-Fi.  
"It's worth mentioning that Level 764 has strong Wi-Fi."  
Change "the wanderer" to "wanderers", as multiple people may be on the level.  
With the description of the M.E.G. base, there are two sentences you need to correct:

> Almond water will be provided to the wanderers.  
> Highly open to trade, Willing to trade with wanderers.

Get rid of "the" in the almond water statement. With the trading statement, just say they are highly open to trade and be done with it. No need to repeat what you already said. Get rid of "Willing to trade with wanderers."  
The Cleaner's description is fine.  
With your entrances, you need to specify how you will get to the level by going into these places.

> Find a door in Level 33 that says a safety exit.

For instance, when you find a door that says it's a safety exit on Level 33, and open it, will 764 be on the other side? Or will you no-clip into 764? Be more specific.

> Enter a long corridor on Level 3.

Same here. You could make this into a situation in which when you turn around after walking a certain distance, you find the way you entered is no longer there, as you are now no longer in Level 3, but now in Level 764.

> Enter a Chinese restaurant at Level 11.

I assume the wanderer will no-clip into your level from this restaurant.

> no-clipping a boat model and you will find yourself on a small boat (you need to add "on" here)Level 97.

The first letter needs to be capitalized here.

  
That's all (for now) folks!
