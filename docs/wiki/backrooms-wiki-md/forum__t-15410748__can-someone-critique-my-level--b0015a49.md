Over all this is a good article, there are some stuff that you can add/touch up on:

Adding metric to imperial conversion in a footnote

The supply boxes, do they restock? If so how often? How large are they?

In exits it says that one of the exits is going through a hole, can you go into more detail, how often do they appear? How large are they? Etc

Can you go into detail about how wide the path is.

Is the level consistent? Like when you re-enter it is it always going to be the same trees, grass, etc?

Other than that it is a really solid level.👍
