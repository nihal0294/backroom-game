> **To [The Followers of Jerry,](followers-of-jerry.md)**  
>   
> **For the last time, I assure you that I am, in fact, a "follower of Jerry" just like you lot are! Despite your dismissive remarks, I'd like to make it perfectly clear that I certainly do follow that blasted blue bird, thank you very much. Actually, if you must know, I've followed [Jerry](entity-7.md) through just about every area of this bloody place for the past fifty years in an attempt to study him. Now if that isn't dedication, then I don't know what is!  
>   
> Therefore, I believe that I am more than qualified to be considered one of [Jerry's](entity-7.md) followers. Not to mention that it is imperative that I study this confounded macaw! Of all the birds I've observed, [Jerry](entity-7.md) has proven to be as much of an anomaly as he is an annoyance, but I will not let that stop me from pursuing him. So please…would you all just indoctrinate me already? I refuse to accept anymore of your refusals!**  
>   
> **Sincerely,  
>   
> Enoch Roddingham, Ornithology Enthusiast**
