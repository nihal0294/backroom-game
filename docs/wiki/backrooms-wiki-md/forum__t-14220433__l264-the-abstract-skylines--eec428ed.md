L264 - The Abstract Skylines

08 Oct 2021 10:34

**Page Type:** Floor

**Genre:** Mystery, Cosmic Horror

**Elevator Pitch:** A large skyline filled with floating geometric shapes (Cylinders, Cubes, Spheres, Pyramids) that have holes or trapdoors into them. They are sometimes connected by ropes or merged together into more complex shapes. The level itself is separated into four levels, each of which with its own ecosystem of 2-4 unique animals and an increasingly hard to traverse area. Each shape can lead into this deeper area, but also can be filled with smaller versions of other levels, museums, storehouses, houses, or nothing at all.

The first level is an open blue sky, whose main predators are venus fly trap-like ropes that feed off of herbivorous flyers. The second layer is a cloudy sky. The third layer is a cloudy sundown, and is filled with living clouds and shapes. The fourth layer is only described by one person who entered - fleshy, wet, windy, and dangerous.

**Central Narrative:** The first three layers have an overall 'Off' feeling to them - like there's something strange. Small hints at the overall structure, which comes to a head at Level 4. The level is implied to be the heart of the level, being alive and sentient. At the end of the story is the reveal that the microphone that the explorer who entered is still transmitting one message to this day. Said message is a constant repeating argument about how the complexity and self-infatuation of modern society kills ourselves on a mental level.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** As far as I know, there aren't that many levels of the backrooms that are vertically oriented. The reveal that the floor is alive is not unique, no, but the fleshy and vague nature of the final level gives a sense of cosmic horror that makes the dimension itself much more menacing in retrospect.

**Additional Notes:** None.
