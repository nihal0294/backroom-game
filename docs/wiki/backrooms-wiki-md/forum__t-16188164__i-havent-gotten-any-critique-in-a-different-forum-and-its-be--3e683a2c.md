You don't need to contact staff for this. Remember that a lot of people are awaiting critique too. It depends on who sees your forum post and who decides to critique it or not. Even after they decided to critique it, it can take some time to do it.

If you don't want it to be purely luck based, i highly recommend asking a specific person, because just like they say "Don't ask for help from a group of people, they'll just think that someone else will do it".

If you'd like faster critique time, you can also join our discord server which is more active than the forum.
