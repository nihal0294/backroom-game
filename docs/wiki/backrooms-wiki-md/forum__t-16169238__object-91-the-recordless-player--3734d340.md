Additional things since I crat this and also it's desocanon

16 Jul 2023 11:41

- it says located on lvl9 because it's as opposed to its current location in the hospital or it's other former location with the meg in lvl4
- the significance of the meg not having cylindrical records is that they cannot test what happens when one is inserted into the player and it is then cranked.
- they can't tell if it's a boy, but they can describe it as feminine. it really depends how young the child is anyways.
- they recommend trying to use the turntable later as in "if you want it to function properly and get you the voice of someone from your past, then you should wait a bit and try again instead of immediately keeping on going."
- they're a very very small group of 22 people. their best shot at getting any clues on who this kid is would be to get people to crank it so they could hear it again. make a bit of a profile. and anyway, they want to make sure it's personalised to everybody AND that nobody in particular causes strange behaviour in the object.
- it's delivered back because it's going back from 9 to 4 — 4 isn't where the object came from, it's where the person doing the delivery came from.
- the log demonstrates its functionality just fine.
- Dr. Park is meant to be characterised as stubborn, slightly eccentric, and relatively confident. So, he'll call machines that don't work right 'stubborn' — you're meant to think he's kind of weird in a little bit of an endearing way so when you find out tragedy's occured in his past you'll feel bad for him.
- to be honest I might ask century to remove the date, time doesn't really work like that in desobackrooms
- Mr Mason is perfectly correct. Mr. Mason would be preferred in american English and Mr Mason would be preferred in English, but neither is incorrect (it is an abbreviation, but one that uses the first and last letter of the word so some wouldn't put in a period.)
- It got transferred to Adam mason because they're all in one base on level 4. He just gave it to him.
- Mentioning that Dr Park is not a psychiatrist clues the reader in on this M.E.G. not being the massively capable and well-staffed M.E.G. of the base canon.
- Things in the Backrooms here aren't meant to make perfect sense all on their own. Hopefully we'll tie up the loose ends eventually, but this is an M.E.G. that is very confused about the nature of the Backrooms.
