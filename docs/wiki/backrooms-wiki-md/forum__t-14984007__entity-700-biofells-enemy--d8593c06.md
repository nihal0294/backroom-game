Hi there Zenzick,

I think you have an interesting entity idea coming on. I would have a lot to say, but unfortunately, this article is very short. If you could add some more content, I would be glad to go into further detail, but for now, here's what I have to say:

I couldn't notice any glaring SPaG errors, so we're good on that unless I missed them as well.

As for the concept of the entity, I think it is a very intriguing idea, but at the heart of it, there isn't all too much. I would sum this up as "Human flesh and bone arranging into the shape of a mammoth. That's all there is. I think it would be cool to see it have some sort of supernatural power, besides its creation. Following along with how silent the entity is, what if it was so outrageously loud, it deafened prey, causing them permanent deafness? I haven't seen anything like that, (other than the scp wiki).

Great job writing, and I hope to see the next update soon! PM me if u gotta question. -Iamalemon
