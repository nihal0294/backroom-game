[I am going to put in italics what you originally wrote and my corrections in bold](javascript:;)

*"The reaper only appear’s when a wanderer is close to dying."  
**There is no need to put an apostrophe in appears. Instead of "appear's" just put appears*** "The reaper will search for wanderer’s who are hurt or deathly sick" ***No need for an apostrophe in wanderers. Instead of "wanderer's" put wanderers*** "The reaper look’s like a skeleton in a cloak. He doesn’t appear to have feet or legs but some wanderer’s believe he does." ***No need for an apostrophe in looks or looks or wanderers. Instead of "look's" put looks and instead of "wanderer's" put wanderers.*** "The reaper was discovered a very long time ago when a wanderer’s friend was attacked by a death moth. The wanderer and his friend barely escaped but once they did a skeleton in a cloak appeared. The creature approached the wanderer’s friend, took out a scythe, and rip the soul out of him. The creature then vanished." ***Again, no need for an apostrophe in wanderers, instead of "wanderer's" put "wanderers". Capitalize Death Moth (proper noun). Instead of "rip the soul out of him" say "ripped the soul out of him"*** "Say goodbye to those you are travelling with."//  
**You spelled traveling incorrectly- you put "travelling" and the correct spelling would be "traveling."**  
Overall: A lot of the SPaG errors are pretty basic (mostly unneeded apostrophes but a little spelling and capitilization.) I would reccomend proofreading your work a little closer, I'm assuming some of this is typos or not double checking wether your apostrophes are gramatically correct. This article feels a little bit informal, this is your choice but the majority of the articles on this site are written more formally and so wording some of this differently might help change the feel of this article.

The concept of the article is intriguing, but in my opinion the excecution could be improved, also some things just feel unclear. For example, does The Reaper kill people who've had near death experiences or just people close to death? The article makes it clear The Reaper kills people near death, but this remains unclear to me. The article mentions that it was originally discovered when a wanderers friend was killed by The Reaper after a close encounter with a Death Moth, but doesn't say wether the friend was injured or not. If not, this implies that The Reaper also attacks those with near death experiences, but you should specify.

You put in "Additional Information" nothing except not to run if you see him but this is not needed and seems repetitive to me because you also said in the Don't section not to run so unless you put something else in the Additional Information section this section feels unnecessary.

The article itself just feels- lacking. Each section has short and not very descriptive sentences. The article really needs more to it, and I would really reccomend making it longer and adding onto it.
