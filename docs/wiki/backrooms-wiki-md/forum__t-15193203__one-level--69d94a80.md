I am going to do spelling, punctuation, and grammar line by line.

> This level is represented by a townhouse area of a construction faction in southern China, which follows the normal rules day and night, but specially, the interior of the villa at this level is a non-Ocreidian structure, which leads to when entering the villa, you see the hall, but when you walk over, you come to the balcony.

1) Do you mean resembles instead of represented?  
2) What is this construction faction or is it of the fashion common of southern China/  
3) Rules for day and night.  
4) What does "but specially" mean?  
5) Remove "at this level".  
6) I think that it is non-Euclidian, not non-Ocreidian.  
7) Put a period after structure.  
8) Probably word " which leads to when entering the villa, you see the hall, but when you walk over, you come to the balcony." like Entering the Villa leads to a hall, but traveling left leads to an upper floor balcony.

> At the same time, the entity in this level is also There are so many, but their intelligence level is extremely high that they will not appear during the day, but even at night, they will not make it easy for you to detect their existence.

1) Start it like this This level is populated by many entities.  
2) Remove "but"  
3) Put a period after "high".  
4) Remove "that".  
5) Replace the comma after day with a period.  
6) Write the last sentence something like During night on this level they are extremely stealthy.  
7) **Give a description of this entity type.**

> In the test, their voices are only 3.32 decibels, so it is highly recommended to move at this level at night, so the best time to spend the night.

1) Probably just remove this sentence.

> The strategy is to hide in the villa.

1) Say how the entities do not often enter the villa and how it is safer inside.

> Although the internal space structure of the villa is messy and there are many windows, as long as you rest in the living room, there is a high probability that nothing will happen.

1) Is it messy or is it loosely attached to reality/  
2) Say how the living room is a great opportunity to get much needed sleep.

> **M.E.G Hana Base**  
> Camp a villa garden 45 meters from the entrance  
> Open trading  
> 23 members

1) Probably word like Stationed within a villa garden not too far from the entrance.  
2) They are non-hostile and open to trading with wanderers.  
  
  
I will add more later when I have time.
