# Revelations

**Revelations**

  

**It goes back to the very origins of the Backrooms.**

**The very first god, the very first creator of this dimension.**

**He was proud of its creations and the world he built on his own.**

**Like a father, he carefully watched over his creations.**

**He eventually decided to create other gods and grant them a fraction of his power, to maintain a perfect equilibrium in the world he built.**

**Everything was fine, the god was satisfied.**

**He never got bored watching the evolution of its creations and what they would become.**

**That is until a foreign species ended up in his world.**

**He was puzzled at first, wondering how such a thing was possible.**

**He questioned his own powers, He went crazy over the fact that he couldn't control something.**

**As a way to comfort himself, he persecuted the humans who ended up in his world against his will, which he qualified as intruders.**

**He never directly caused their demise but rather enjoyed watching them suffer.**

**From the pain caused by the entities he himself created for this sole purpose.**

**To the various deadly environments and structures of countless levels he himself conceived for the very purpose of torturing humans both physically and mentally.**

**However, even if we were his sons, we pitied the humans and secretly disagreed about what our father was doing to them.**

**We had to watch them suffer in the cruelest ways all day long.**

**Some of my brothers couldn't take it anymore.**

**The suffering of these humans was transmitted indirectly to ourselves.**

**We couldn't let that continue forever, these humans haven't done anything wrong in the first place.**

**This was all about his ego.**

**One day, when our father was distracted, we all sneaked behind him, and all together, we unleashed all of our power on him.**

**To his misfortune, he gave us enough of his power to actually beat him.**

**That was because he never thought a single second his own sons he himself created would betray him.**

**That was because of his ego - because he thought everything he made was perfect.**

**But the consequences of his actions caught up to him.**

**Even as a god, he wasn't invincible after all.**

**He lost nearly all his powers and was severely injured, but he survived our attack.**

**Because he was still our father, we decided to let him live, but we imprisoned him in a secluded place apart from the rest of the Backrooms universe.**

**A place that he could never escape from.**

**Or so we thought.**

**Having witnessed what happened lately,**

**It seems our father is trying to break from his cell with the help of a group of your own kind.**

**We must put an end to all this and restore peace once and for all.**

**But, because he was the one who initially granted us his power, ours decreased as well as a consequence.**

**We can still create some entities, objects, and levels that could be beneficial to humans but we can't undo the "torture devices" nor the "monsters" our father created from the start.**

**All this has taught us a valuable lesson,**

**that fate should be determined by one's own acts and decisions.**

  
  

**My brothers are opposed to the idea of involving humans in this affair. They think humans have no chance against our father, so they want to settle this matter themselves, but I think otherwise; I believe that you, humans, are the ones who can truly stop our father.**

**I will do my best to provide anything you need, I count on you, humans.**

**Sincerly,**

#### Evi'ka
