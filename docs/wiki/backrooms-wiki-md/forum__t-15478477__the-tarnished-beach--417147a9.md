TheFloridaLord (Me!)  
Level 207 “Tarnished beach”

[[include :backrooms-wiki:component:level-class  
|class=unknown  
]]

This level takes the appearance of a Wheat-Field and a beach.The wheat fields are Dotted with Trailer-homes,which should not be entered unless  
As a last resort,as these are inhabited by wretches and facelings,which attack on sight.The Wheat fields are also dangerous,as they Have an infestation of entities.It is recommended to enter this level with a firearm.The Beaches Are made from Silver and tungsten powder,Which Is just Barely Tarnished. The sand shimmers in the sun,As Soon as it stops shining,Start running towards a safe-place,as the entire level will essentially turn into a death zone.The full day is actually roughly 39 Hours,And try avoid the last half of the day…  
An unidentifiable Piano Opera will play faintly in the background at day,And at night, ‘Its just a burning memory’ Will Play,which drains sanity **extremely** fast  
And the Pier Area plays an unidentifiable 1950’s song  
The beaches are,Effectively,the only areas that should be stayed in for Any Period of time.  
These beaches are,however,geographicly,extremely unstable. I would say…Try to avoid The castle to **say the least.** This is due to the fact that There is a level-specific entity on this level,This is the one you -should- Must hide or run from.  
**you cannot accept her gift or trade,as these are in-fact,Often at least 50% iron oxide,Giving any wonderer who drinks ‘The Funny orange-Juice’ a painful death.**

If you see ships on the sea,It is advised to Swim as fast as you can towards them,as they Are  
**usually devoid of entities** and an exit from this hellhole.

Bases and outposts:  
•No **human** Colonies,bases or outposts have been discovered.  
• There is a small milkshake,dairy and Coffee shop at one of the beach piers,Pay the faceling with Almond water,And you may get some cheese,Milkshake,coffee,As well as a boat if you ask him **Kindly**,Have a nap here,You need it. :)

Entrances:

It is possible to enter this level by Suffocating to death in sand on Any beach level.  
Consume 10-Litres Of Coffee And Banana Milkshake,Topped with whipped cream and silver/Tungsten powder In 10 Minutes,Inside Any level With A Supply of wheat,And the level with transition slowly into This level over the course of 10 Hours.  
Sleep inside a trailer-home or sandbox inside level 9.

Exits:  
A Ship **may** Send you to level 103  
Falling into a sinkhole will send you to level 8.

Survive Consuming 10-Kilograms of the Sand To be sent to another beach level,Mainly to level 100.

You can Actually Give A Faceling or smiler a Milkshake,which causes You To Teleport to A Well-Documented,Safe level,This is the Only Confirmed Way in order To Safely Go One-Breath without being in fear of Not having another,apart from the coffee shop.
