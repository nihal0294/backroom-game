Hey Justice (Didn't u want me to call u that?) I will be harsh, will not be rewriting sentences, and will give as much crit as time allows.

# SPaG:

> Level 251 is the 252nd floor

"Floor" should be "Level" and add a period at the end.

> THIS PAGE HAS BEEN MODIFIED BY ENTITY 140 WITHOUT AUTHORIZATION

Add a period.

> installation 12

Capitalize "Installation"

> installation 12, it is giant featuring 3 levels above ground and 10 below.

Change the comma to a period. Change "it" to "The building" for better clarity. Add a comma after giant. Swap "levels" with floors, for clarity.

> Level 251 has a constant supply of electricity and almond water.

You should capitalize "Almond Water", but as the object does not currently exist, remove it. If and when it is rewritten, make sure to link it.

> nuclear power plants found far from cities that are.

That are what? You cut off the sentence.

> Unoccupied water treatment plants produce a unknown water type, It is safe to drink however.

"a" -> "an". Change the comma to a semicolon. Add a comma before "however".

> Expect military checkpoints, in fact you should seek them out if alone since the soldiers inhabiting them will always help.

Delete a space before "Expect". You totally broke your clinical tone here with "Expect", "you", and "in fact". Replace them with other words, but imo rewrite it. You capitalized "Soldiers" earlier, so keep it capitalized.

> The cities they guard look more like New York and Washington DC, but some look like London or other major global cities, you can ask for a escort to one of these cities and you will be sent with the next convoy.

"they" -> "the Soldiers". What do they look more like? "a escort" -> "an escort". Its pretty poorly written sentence in general.

What is? The cities? The outposts? The convoy?

> photo's

Remove the apostrophe.

> levels 9 and 10 are off limits, they contain the base reactor and a Blanche book, they appear to hold her in high regard, see Entity 251 for more info on the possible connection

"levels" and "book" should be capitalized, but "levels" should be replaced with "floors". Tbh, I don't really know what you were telling me. The collapsible seems unnecessary for such little information, and it is a very weird combo of 2-3 sentences. Rewrite this.

> Around 20 known military installations the most significant will be listed in order below

Begin the sentence with "There are" and add a period after "installations". Capitalize "the" and add a period to the end. Change "will be" to "are". You could also rewrite it as "They will be ordered by significance". Ik I said no rewrites but I couldn't let this sit.

> The main military installation on this level, is operated by a unit known as “Hells Servants” for their use of pyrotechnics

Remove the comma and add a period to the end.

> Operates using a hydrogen reactor to power everything and the surrounding city despite no civilians except wanderer's living in the city

Run-on sentence. "Wanderer's" should have no apostrophe. Rewrite the sentence. Add a fricking period.

> A large arsenal of weapons for their common ‘extermination’ runs where they kill all hostile entities they find, this has become very rare due to their interaction with Blanche and her pacifism and their attempt to have a good relation with her despite the fact their soldiers and not pacifist in any way

Pardon me, but what the hell. This is a massive sentence and should be rewritten. Period.

> Garrison of 4,000 entity’s

Remove the apostrophe, add a period.

> This PVT 1st class Williams, this is a restricted site and is not open to the public, thank you for your time  
> Main unit is “Biological”  
> Garrison of [REDACTED] entity’s

None of this makes any sense. Please rewrite it in general.

> The main cargo base where trade happens and items are stored, they will gladly give their items away for free to people in dire need of it but will attempt to trade for rare items such as tech of unknown origin or books of Blanche  
> Garrison of 2,000 entity’s  
> Main unit “Hammer Down”, a tank unit using M1A3 Abrams1, 9 tanks to be precise, they have been seen using T-14 Armata’s but this is rare and T-14’s are usually used by “Blazing Winter”, a Russian tank unit  
> [Abandoned MEG base]  
> A MEG base that appears to be have been built long ago, time frame is murky but they estimate not long after the founding of MEG due to what items that were left behind  
> Believed to have been a sizable community of 200  
> Investigation ongoing, believed to have been led by a man called Jackson Davis, no record of him exists he likely came here before the database or he was never logged  
> [INSTALLATION 13]  
> Found in the middle of a plateau this base closely resembles a air base  
> It has various fighter jets and bombers from across the 21st century even experimental ones yet to see service  
> They allow easy recon for the soldiers and will shoot flares near lone wanderer’s to signal their location  
> Surrounded by a electric fence and barbed wire to keep out unwelcome guests

I'm sorry, but it feels unnecessary to go over this again. No sentences have periods, "wanderer's" should have no apostrophe unless a singular wanderer possesses something (same with others,) if before a word beginning with a vowel, "a" should be "an", and they are generally poorly formatted and run-on. I would *highly* recommend going though this an either rewriting entirely, or correctly formatting it with SPaG and general structure.

> This is 2nd Lt Harris, I have been told to write a detailed report on Installation 12 for your convenience specifically level 9 and 10, and detail the first encounter with Blanche as some MEG operatives were curious.  
> Level 9 holds a book that leads us to Blanche, the first friendly entity we found, Level 10 holds the hydrogen reactor, yes, it is basically a hydrogen bomb contained in glass mid explosion, you can see why we don’t let people down there

Same here. You made 2 sentences last 1 paragraph. I do not understand what you are trying to tell the reader, and this got very messy. Rewrite it.

> A military steel door on Level 1  
> No clipping through a random wall in Level 132  
> A painting on Level 57 that depicts a military base

Add periods to your entrances.

> Sleep in the only red bed in the barracks building and you will awake in Level 1 near M.E.G. Base Alpha

Add a period.

> Thanks to my friend Davis for posing

I am assuming that is your friend Davis in the picture. Make sure to mention that you took this photo and that it is licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0 (If you *did* take it). Also add a period.

> Exterminator-1

# Conceptual Crit:

Ehh… I think this whole military type level inhabited by the Soldiers is a fine concept, but no one really likes guns in the Backrooms, including myself. I was told to remove a revolver from Level 148's audio log, in fact. I bet you could change the guns to, oh idk, a gun that only works for the entity? Maybe the gun is built into the entity? Just not too fond of the concept of having assault weapons in the Backrooms. It would change your concept a lot, but it may be worth it.

The way you described Blanche was unusual. Blanche would likely never edit a page without authorization, and I can't see her working with a bunch of violent beings wielding assault weapons. She is very anti-violence and would *never* assist them. Just because she is so friendly doesn't mean she'll take out an AR-15 and start mowing down entities. Also, why is there a whole Blanche thing going on in the first place? You added this for convenience, it seems, and no real storyline purpose. I think you should scrap the Blanche parts because they seem unnatural and unlikely.

So if you try to take a pic in this level, they will be censored… [where have I seen this before](level-12.md)… Get rid of it. It isn't an overused concept, but simply a bad one that only really could have been done once until it died. Imo, I don't like the concept in general.

# Final Comments:

This is *very* far away from a GL. Throughout the *entirety* of the page, you forgot commas, and periods, had run-on sentences, and it just didn't work. Your concept could work if you give it some tweaks, but before you get brainstorming, you need to seriously get the SPaG under control. [This](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/writing-tips)] could definitely help. It may seem dumb, but at least give the links a chance, and maybe you'll be ready to rewrite your draft. As aforementioned, scrap the random additions, like Blanche and self-censoring, and work on good looking formats.

You can PM me as always when you get these under control in a week or 2, but I still recommend getting discord. It's helped a lot since I've gotten it. I'm actually a GLT rn, so working towards GL. Happy writing.
