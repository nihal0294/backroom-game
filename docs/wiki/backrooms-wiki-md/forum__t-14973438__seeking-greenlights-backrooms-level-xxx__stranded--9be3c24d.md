**Danger Level:** Class 2

Level-XXX was first discovered on Jan 15, 2002.

Level-XXX is the XXX level in the backrooms

## Description

Level-XXX is a giant island that is approximately 212mi2 in its area. On the outer parts of all sides of the island, there are very shallow waters leading up to a direct drop-off. Currently, the estimated depth of the drop-off is about 45 meters down. There is no visible light source as of the current moment but there is light similar to that of what the Sun could produce. Its day/night cycle is similar to that of earth. Here's what we know about Level-XXX:

The area covering the island is covered with dense, tropical, forests with trees similar to palm trees. **Level-XXX** also has a mountain-like rocky terrain that has an overview of the island. Another terrain seems to be a desert-like place that's best avoided because there are sandstorms are a constant thing there; even though these sandstorms don't affect the environment they can cause a bit of damage to wanders or explorers. Other than the explored terrains, the ocean surrounding the island seems to be infinite in all directions.  
There is one other area in this place explored other than the terrains and this is an abandoned pirate ship that crashed on the west coast of the island. Inside the ship, there is nothing on the ship or in its rooms other than the broken canons placed on either side of the ship. The flag is ripped to absolute shreds.

This level has one big noticeable thing about it. It's able to seemingly make explorers feel stranded if they are there for a long enough time. Though this time is different for everyone, and some it doesn't happen whatsoever.

Something explorers or wanders should keep note of is no piece of technological equipment works here, meaning being able to have pictures of this place and its inhabitants are practically impossible. This is because any technology that explorers have on them when they no-clip into level-XXX is damaged via tons of water.

It should be noted that there practically are no natural Ocean waves.

There are two "zonas"

**Zonas:**

**The Surface:**  
Danger Level: Class 0

The Surface Zona is anything above the trench and consists only of the island and the abandoned ship.

**The Trench:**  
Danger Level: Class 4

The Trench Zona is much more mysterious. While it's practically pitch black after you get 12M below the surface; Explorers can see things moving. This Zona is ecologically dead, except for one Entity species.

## Entities

There are only one known species of entities living on **Level-XXX**. These creatures are called Thalassans; they are described as giant Whale-like entities with many rows of sharp teeth and many, many Whale-like eyes covering their bodies. They are only found in the trench and, when looking into the pupils of any of these creatures' eyes, said person loses the ability to swim or control their body in deep areas of water. The babies are approximately 7M in length and the adults, as lengthy as 24M.

According to many wanderers, there is one bigger than all of them with an unknown, unestimated length, width, and height. In legends surrounding this one, it's apparently 50M long.

One thing to note about these entities is that their eyes have been seen in other water backrooms randomly no-clipping through random deep water areas of the backrooms and looking around with their eyes.

## Exploration/ Survival

**Level-XXX**'s island is easy to survive and explore.

One thing Wanderers and explorers should note is that every so often things will come drifting to shore, these things include Supply Crates, Bottles with random things inside them, Snack Bars, and even Almond Water. The Supply crates come with a mixture of lots of Snack Bars, Almond Water, and Bottles. The Bottles can come with papers, usually reading random messages with random things, sometimes it's even someone's incomplete notes. The final thing is a pirate ship with looks familiar to the crashed ship on the west coast of the island. Even the trees, which look like palm trees have fruit that acts almost like almost water but is not as effective. The most dangerous thing on the island is sandstorms in the desert area with can cause damage if in one long enough.

**Level-XXX**'s Trench on the other hand is quite dangerous to explore, on the account of the Thalassans living there and the incredibly dangerous pressures that can crush someone if they go down far enough. So, avoid the trench as much as possible.

## Bases, Camps, and Communities

**Thallasons**  
This is a small, though growing, community of 6 that worships the Hypothetical Giant Thallasan as a god.  
They are known for making sacrifices every once in and while; though they can be negotiated out of the sacrifice with food, supplies, and hospitality among other things.

## Entrances and Exits

**Entrances:**  
The only known entrance as of the moment is looking at one of the no-clipping Thallasan eyes on other levels.

**Exits:**  
There is one way to get out of this level, no-clipping through one of the Palm-like trees in the forest. Doing this will result in the explorer no-clipping to the place they no-clipped from to get to **level-XXX**
