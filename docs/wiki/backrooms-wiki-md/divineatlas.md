### About the Author

Hello, I'm Atlas and I'm a writer and teacher. I teach music and English and hold a Master's in English Education. I found the Backrooms through YouTube (Congrats on your film, Kane!) but quickly fell in love with the writing here on the Wikidot. I'm so grateful that I've been able to contribute, please enjoy perusing my articles!

I had the enormous honor (and stress) of rewriting Level 0 for the Backrooms community. This was a project driven by our membership, I built the article based on their feedback. While I did a majority of the writing, I could not have done it without the invaluable contributions from and . They are also wonderful people and writers, please check out their work!

A special thanks to our Greenlight team who helped bring this to fruition, much love to all of you!
