A Grassy Plain

02 Feb 2023 08:10

**Page Type:** Normal Level

**Genre:** Liminality, psychological horror

**Core Concept:** The idea is that there's a 1km by 1km square of grassy plain, with no discernible landmarks and a couple of buildings. There's an uninhabited factory on the plain and a small village. There's also an elderly man constantly walking along in rows across the whole map while mowing the grass. He is friendly to talk to and can speak every language, but under no circumstances will he talk about why or where he is. This goes the same for the 23 other people in the small village.

More things will be added once I know this idea is worth moving forward with.

**Additional Notes:** I am not looking for a greenlight now.
