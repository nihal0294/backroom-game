##### [𝓣𝓱𝓮 𝓟𝓮𝓽𝓻𝓲𝓯𝓲𝓮𝓭 𝓖𝓪𝓻𝓭𝓮𝓷](level-23.md)

Upvotes: +121

[[+] Commentary](javascript:;)

[[-] Commentary](javascript:;)

Ah, my first collab. I was never really the best at code or making my writing seem cohesive and intriguing, so I came to Rika for help. I had an idea, what if the world ended. I know, unique totally, but i feel the kicker lies in how. Most apocalypse movies or books we read in literature tend to show the end of the world as, well, life ending as we know it, but i felt that was cheesy, overdone, and honestly quite boring. So i thought, what if humans as we knew it were gone, but nature thrived and grew to inhabit all of Earth, but in a less traditional way. Nature overpowered the Earth. This lead to the inspiration of the level, concept, and general writing. Rika did some major help with the code, as well as a tiny bit of the writing and I can't say much in her favor due to personal situations, I can say that what she helped me with kickstarted my interest in writing for the backrooms as a whole. I wouldn't be here without that push. Overall though this level is one I'm proud of.
