"Floor =): A party awaits on the other side. But this time, it is safe."  
Okay so what really unnerves me about this line is while it does look like something the partygoers would typically write to lure wanders into Level Fun, it lacks some of the key components that a typical partygoer message has (namely poor-ish grammar, ends in '=)', lots of exclamation points, etc.). Also, by saying "this time, it is safe" it's (kind of?) admitting every 'party' before was a trap or otherwise dangerous, which doesn't coincide with the usual deny everything energy of partygoer's other messages. Something about this makes me think it wasn't written by a partygoer - at least, not the kind you see scurrying around Level Fun trying to transform wanderers. 'Floor =)' could be a mimic or bootleg of Level Fun that people are too scared to explore (and rightfully so). Or possibly even a separate population of partygoers that reside in this level for whatever reason? Maybe even a subspecies of partygoers?  
…Then again, I could just be staring into a kiddie pool and acting like I found the Mariana's Trench. Oh well! Still fun to overanalyze tiny details for all they're worth! Nice article too
