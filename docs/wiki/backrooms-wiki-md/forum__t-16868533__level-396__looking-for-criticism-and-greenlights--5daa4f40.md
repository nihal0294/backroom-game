Hello Dang Nguyen, I just read your article and the following is my conceptual evaluation of the article (I am not good at SPaG)

---

I think the description in this article is simple, it seems like a livable outdoor level without significant features, and each region in the regional section is only described in one or two sentences. You can write more about other parts that contribute to livability, such as what resources are available here. Additional things need to be added.

Some associations between these groups can be added to the base, outpost, and community, or research reports on Lv396 can be included

> - These robbers often hide in the forest; they cover themselves with a moss green shirt to camouflage themselves from wanderers.
> - "Falcon" is the group's leader, followed by "Hawk," "Eagle," "Vulture," "Crow," "Dove," and "Sparrow." The "Sparrows" have the weakest power in the group; they are often forced to stay at the bases to watch over them.

I think this idea is a bit interesting, you can try to supplement it in detail.

> There are quite a few entities inhabiting **Level 396**, mainly Deathmoths, Crawlers, Volpes and Ants, who pose virtually no harm to wanderers.

Entities can try their living conditions at that level, etc., this is just my personal suggestion.

> Entering a dense fog area at [Level 165](level-165.md), [Level 205](level-205.md), and [Level 184](level-184.md) will lead the wanderer to this level.

The entry conditions for the entrance are a bit simple, you can enrich them a bit more

---

I hope my suggestions can help you, have a pleasant writing experience! (Note: My native language is not English, I use a translator, and I also use a translator to read articles)
