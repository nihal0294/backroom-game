Entity 38

03 May 2022 23:22

**Entity Number:** 38

### Habitat(s): [Level 4](level-4.md)

### Description: Entity 38, referred to by Wanderers as False Chairs, are seemingly normal office swivel chairs at first glance. If sat upon, they will instantly enter an "attack" stage, lashing out at anything living. When in a "roaming" stage, Entity 38 grows long spider-like legs and tries to find a spot where it can enter a "hibernation" stage, reverting back to its original form.

### Behaviors: Unless sat upon, Entity 38 is immobile. They have been documented hunting in groups, and tend to make groups no larger than 12-14 others. They can only be found on Level 4, but reports of Entities similar to it have been seen on other Levels.

### Biology: Entity 38 appear as office chairs that vary in quality, some have been spotted with chairs that are 87% rusted, and others as if the chair was brand new. The chair Entity 38 takes the form of is most commonly Herman Miller branded, though other unknown brands have been discovered on deceased subjects. The legs are powered by hydraulics, like normal spiders. However, unlike spiders, the legs lack an exoskeleton, instead having an endoskeleton. Entity 38, despite being an office chair, can consume most matter by virtue of a large mouth filled with sharp teeth on the underside of the chair. Entity 38 can most commonly be discovered if it is doused with a mixture of one part [Object 1](object-1.md) and two parts [Object 15](object-15.md), which, when combined, are fatal to it. Once an instance of Entity 38 has been terminated by any means, the legs will curl up and will rapidly decompose, often taking mere minutes for them to completely rot away. The chair will be left behind, and seems to have returned to a normal state.

### Discovery: Entity 38 was first discovered by [the M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md) when Wanderers began to vanish from areas once thought to be safe. A small team of six Wanderers, and two M.E.G members were sent to the room where the most of these vanishings occurred, and came into contact with Entity 38.

### **Do's and Don'ts:**

### **Do:**

- Carry around the mixture of Almond Water and Firesalt at all times while in Level 4.

### **Don't:**

- Sit on any suspicious chairs without sprinkling them first.
- Enter a room alone.
