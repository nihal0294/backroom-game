This is the last CutTheBirch article. I haven't been around awhile — or rather, I've been present, just elsewhere in the real world because like all Backrooms authors must do eventually, I've moved on to another chapter in my life. But I never did say goodbye to a community that was very important to me for a very long time, so I have reprised my role of Backrooms author CutTheBirch one more time before I return to the return to the real world.

This is a draft that I started before my long hiatus. It was originally going to be for Object Con if you can believe how long ago that was, but in actuality, I've wanted to rewrite Super Almond Water ever since the original was deleted. SAW has always been viewed as an untouchably bad concept, the platonic ideal of the argument that some concepts are unsalvageable. I've never agreed, and I've even felt like the original SAW article took the concept in a more interesting direction than people give it credit for, just lacking a more mature execution. There's always beauty to be found in a story; you just need to pull it out!

This community was very important to me for a very long time. I joined at age 17 when it was much smaller, and as the community grew around me, so did I. In the Backrooms Tech Support, I matured both as a writer and as a person, and I think my life would be very different if I had never been apart of this amazing experience. The Backrooms Wiki will always hold a very special place in my heart, but now it's time for me head out.

My final message: change da world Find beauty in a world that changes around you, and don't be afraid to let go.

Goodbye everybody!

What's the "coherency" like now? I left the wiki and Discord a good while ago because of what i would say is the same reason. It became "cool" to take a type of article, usually put in a specific section because it was formatted a certain way, and about a certain topic, and then ignore all that and write whatever. It felt low-effort to see so many articles where the whole gimmick was that woah, it's an object article in the phenomenon section! A tale in the level or entity section! The tales were alright, but had they been posted in the tales section they would've been just a good write, not some masterpiece that breaks the mold as people would tend to see it as. And the tales often didn't come with the proper content of the article too, it wasn't 20% level/entity/object 80% tale, it would be 100% tale, sometimes not even **referencing** a level or entity or object, just put into the wrong section i sometimes assume to boost its popularity.

Is this the same thing you mean by "coherency with tone and lore"? Even if it's not, i think they're related, the wiki got too addicted to trying to be the new biggest concept or gimmick or outlier and forgot that alot of what makes the original 10 or so levels so massively popular is that aside from Level 0, they aren't anything entirely unbelievably spectacular and important on their own. Playing a background role in some lore or game or community can be cool too and often makes it all feel more rich. Maybe the community's no longer like that, it's been over a year since i interacted with it in any way, but it was definitely a huge peeve of mine back when i was a part of it.
