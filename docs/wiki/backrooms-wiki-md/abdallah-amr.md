# [Phenomenon Threat Scale (PTS)](component__pts--f4475e75.md)

My first page on-site, and my first component. Designed by while discussing my then upcoming phenomenon (now P32, more on it later), PTS is a highly customizable classification scale designed and tailored for phenomena of all types.

# [Phenomenon 32 - Level Velocity](phenomenon-32.md)

My first posted article, this phenomenon is centered around the dynamic of the levels in the [Blue Channel](blue-channel.md). It's a mix of astronomy and string theory in a way that makes it feel unique. Also fun fact I picked 32 because this phenomenon was going to originally be called "Level Shift" and I decided to pick 1 bit-shifted 5 times as a way to honor that original name, also an homage to [Level 15](level-15.md), the level that sparked this idea whole.

# [A Wanderer Journal](a-wanderer-journal.md)

A collaborative article documenting the journey of a Backrooms wanderer. Participated in the writing of this, specifically pages 13 and 14, and is technically my first writing project on this site, even before P32.

# [Offset Timeline](component__offset-timeline--eec3fc1e.md)

One of the components I am most proud of, this small yet elegant component lets you navigate pages with multiple offsets easily and quickly, without taking too much design from the page.

# [Font Toggle](component__font-toggle--fa61adf0.md)

Inspired by the original component by and a redesign by , this component is aimed at enhancing accessibility by giving the ability to toggle hard-to-read fonts.
