I will say that this is some truly incredible art. You did an absolutely fantastic job! The one nitpick I have with the art itself is that the text is very low-contrast with the background and doesn’t allow for much readability, especially for dyslexic and colorblind people.

Now, the main issue I have with this is what it depicts. I don’t feel that this is a proper representation of what our site is and who we are. This doesn’t showcase any of the identity we’ve built for ourselves, and while I wouldn’t have been as judgmental under different circumstances, the fact that we have a [site mascot](art__tesseract--7a9923e1.md), which was even mentioned in the contest page, makes showcasing our identity incredibly easy. I don’t even think you could tell that this is a depiction of the Backrooms had it not been for the text. None of it is recognizable to the naked eye, and I don’t even know who the character featured in the center is.

Overall, great art, but I don’t think this is fitting for going on all of our site socials and the new home page. It’s not enough for a downvote from me (for now), but I don’t exactly feel comfortable upvoting this either, given that’s how the winner is chosen.

P. S. The text surrounding the art didn’t exactly help your case. Far too edgy.
