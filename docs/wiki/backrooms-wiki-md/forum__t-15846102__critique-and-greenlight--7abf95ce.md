Guess who? I swear I’m not actively searching for articles. I also want to say that this forum is for ideas, not sandbox and greenlight. But whatever.

> Backroom Level Zen State is literally just a white room with healing powers.

Get rid of “Backroom.” This doesn’t sound professional. Get rid of “literally just.” You can rewrite this as; “**Level Zen State** is the (an) n+1th (enigmatic) level of the backrooms. It is a white room that has positive effect on a wanderer’s mental and physical state.

> Level Zen State is basically a white room that heals the wanderer's needs and helps put the wanderer in a better state of mind.

Replace “is basically a” with “takes the appearance of”.

> Entrances  
> The main way that is believed to enter Level Zen State is to get caught up in a bad situation in the backrooms and you will end up being sent here until you are mentally and physically better.

This feels generally unprofessional. A better way to put this would be; “The main way to enter **Level Zen State** is if one is to find themself in a dire situation, they have a chance finding their way here”

## General stuff + final thoughts

This article’s tone is pretty unprofessional and the level feels unfinished. I like the idea though, there is a lot of potential. But it just isn’t executed that well 4/10.
