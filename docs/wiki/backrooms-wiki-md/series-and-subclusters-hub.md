# Series and Subclusters Hub

## **What are Series?**

*Series are when a group of pages create one continuous story. They are usually within the same storyline, and consist of two or more pages. Note that even though some of these series only consist of tales, that, by no means indicate that it should only be tales. You can create a hub with the tag "[series](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/series#pages)," and link your pages that are involved in the page!*

## **What are Subclusters?**

***Subclusters** are collections of levels, entities, groups, etc. that are in their own self-contained sector of the Backrooms. They are still part of the Backrooms, but typically have some sort of designation that sets them apart. You can create a hub with the tag "[subcluster](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/subcluster#pages)," and link your pages that are involved in the page so long as your subcluster contains a minimum of 3 pages.*
