Click the arrow next to your name in the top right of the page, click Activity, click Recent Activities.

Your sandbox page names and urls should be something that you can easily remember, like your own username. If you’re naming it after the specific type of page you’re writing (levels, objects, etc) then it’ll be harder to use your sandbox page again down the line for a different draft with the confusion between page url and content.
