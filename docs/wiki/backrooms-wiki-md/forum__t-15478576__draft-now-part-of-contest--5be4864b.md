Re: [Seeking Greenlight and Critique] Phenomena: Radio Waves of the Past

27 Feb 2023 06:34

Overall, I think you have a very interesting general concept. You have incredibly fun concepts in how you format the offsets, and the general concept, as I mentioned, is incredibly interesting. My main complaint lies in the actual formatting and organization of the page. The switching between the recorded occurrence and the commentary on it is incredibly back and forth and hard to keep up with—for the sake of improving readability, try to keep these separate rather than interjecting between each other. The way the information is presented may be a little plain, but if it were organized better it would be a much easier to digest if the formatting were improved.

> Tips moving forward:
>
> - Reference how other Phenomenon pages are formatted. Yours doesn't need to be the exact same, but having a point of reference can be beneficial so that information doesn't feel "jumpy".
>
> - Avoid repetition, for the most part. You repeat information a lot—when done in an impactful manner, information will stick with readers, so you don't need to really drill it in unless it serves a greater narrative purpose.
>
> - Establish a timeline so that you can pinpoint specific instances and separate them from others. Personally, I felt it was really easy to lose track of where one instance began and the other ended—creating a timeline and breaking up the article to focus on separate instances might aid you in organizing your information.

Overall, that's all I have. I can't offer a greenlight, and I can't offer SPaG crit given that the changes I suggested might wind up overriding that crit. I hope that my suggestions were helpful, and I do wish you luck with this!
