Technically, there isn’t a hard and fast rule about it. It’s mainly about the connotations of the word or the group it’s connected to.

Here’s how I personally parse them:

- Outposts - Tend to be connected to groups, implying a small or temporary location set up by said group that’s far away from the “main” core of that group.
- Communities - More “civilian” in connotation, vs a more “military” or “official” connotation given by the other two terms. Less directly connected to a group.
- Bases - Tends to have a somewhat “military” connotation, and tends to give the impression of a more well-established or permanent location made by a group.

You don’t need to adhere to these terms, either.
