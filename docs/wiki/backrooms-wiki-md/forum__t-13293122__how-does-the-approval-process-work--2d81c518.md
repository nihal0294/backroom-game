Re: How does the approval process work?

06 May 2020 14:50

At the moment we haven't gotten the chance to get our policy on it properly written, though our system generally works like this:

- Anyone can post in both forums for critique as much as they desire, as long as it's not spamming
- First time authors need a draft greenlight in order to post their article to the site; idea greenlights are generally only *symbolic* of approval, but you are advised not to continue to the drafts without one
- Successful[1](javascript:;) authors can bypass the critique process, though it is heavily suggested to receive critique anyway

Once a policy is created in writing, I'll post a summary and sticky it.

Footnotes

[1](javascript:;). meaning that at least two of their pages (on here *or* the SCP wiki) stayed above 0 for 24 hours
