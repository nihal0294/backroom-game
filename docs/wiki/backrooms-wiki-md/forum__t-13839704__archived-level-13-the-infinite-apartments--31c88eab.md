Level 13 is… A Level with an interesting concept that can solve the housing problem, but I think it needs a re-write. Here's why:  
1. If the Level is infinite then wouldn't it have more than an estimate of 1000 floors? Looking at the numbering system you can guess that there can be up to 9 sections and 99 apartments on each floor. If the Level is infinite, then there should be infinite floors, not an estimate of 1000 floors.  
2. In the image, the walls aren't white, and the white walls & brown carpet design is consistent throughout all floors. I think that since the image is good then you should change the visual description of the Level.  
3. Since the description says that the Windows are less hostile in this Level, it would be better to remove the 'very' from 'very dangerous'. "The Windows are less hostile but still are dangerous" sounds better than "The Windows are less hostile but still are very dangerous".  
4. You just ask for an apartment key and that's it?  
The Faceling just gives one to you without asking for payment?! Well, at least you get to have unwanted Entity guests as a punishment for not paying your rent. Open your cabinets to find a Clump living in there. Nice…  
5. I thought the only confirmed way to exit Level 12 was to do that specific exit ritual thing? Now you can just noclip through the floor?  
6. The description says that all of the walls are white. How would you find a red wall to go to Level -1? Even the image dosen't have a red wall.  
…and I think that's it. Hopefully I didn't miss anything and someone will rewrite this.
