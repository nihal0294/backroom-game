# fragment:object-83-5

Lifting the Mask

For those not currently aware of our existence, we are the Masked Maidens. We are those who have been hurt by supposedly "good" groups within the Backrooms, and our allies are those who have seen the truth and cannot abide quietly tolerating such injustice. Many lies have been spread about us and about our mission by those who would rather the truth not be unmasked.

We seek justice for the crimes committed against us and numerous others by corrupt members of various groups. We seek to unmask the truth. We do not care about group allegiance or inter-group politics - we only care to unmask the corrupting rot lurking within the hearts of numerous groups and to see that they face justice.

Over the past few months, we have been conducting an extensive espionage campaign against various groups across the Backrooms. We have uncovered many of their secrets, some of which we shall share with you today. We achieved this by turning a tool of a foe into an asset. We discovered a vulnerability within Robovacs that allowed us to take control of them, and we used this to our advantage. With the sheer number of Robovacs we had under our control, we only had to get lucky once to successfully infiltrate settlements across the Backrooms.

# UNMASKING THE M.E.G.

While M.E.G. leadership claims that few knew about the unethical experiments being conducted by Team Trial Testers and parts of the Research Department, we can reveal that knowledge of the unethical experiments was widespread and that their attempts to claim otherwise were a calculated deception. For your consideration, we present an excerpt from a recent meeting of the M.E.G. Research Committee.

**AUDIO TRANSCRIPT**

**Date:** 2021-08-23

**Location:** Conference Room 5, Administration Wing, Base Alpha

**Overseer Donovan:** I don't see why you needed to talk to me in person about this. In the past our research teams had a lot of independence and little oversight and they tragically abused that. That is our stance, and there's no reason for me to be here to reiterate that.

**Senior Researcher Petrovich:** We called you here because we don't think that stance is working any more. I've heard what people are saying. People are becoming increasingly sceptical of the idea that we could have failed to notice everything happening under our noses.

**Senior Archivist Hahn:** And what's the alternative, Ivan? We did all this for the greater good, but the people out there wouldn't understand it. They wouldn't understand that sacrifices need to be made. Admitting the truth would tear the M.E.G. apart, and tear apart humanity's best hope in the Backrooms with it.

**Senior Researcher Anderson:** Did we really do it for the greater good or were we just sticking our nose into things for the sake or sticking our nose into things?

**Senior Researcher Vance:** We have a duty to investigate anything that has a chance of improving our chances of survival and our understanding of this place.

**Senior Researcher Petrovich:** We are not the Coalition. There are lines that we should not have crossed and that we did not need to cross. We chose to cross them anyway.

**Overseer Donovan:** And what of Seer Tea or other successful experiments? Did we cross lines we should not have crossed there, or does that only apply when we failed? If the Wall Mask experiments had succeeded-

**Senior Researcher Anderson:** Overseer Donovan, with all due respect. It should have been obvious that the Wall Mask experiments were doomed to failure once we started getting reports about the mental instability they were causing.

**Overseer Donovan:** Then you should have exercised your veto. Or did you forget that as a member of the Research Council, you have a veto you can use to pause or even terminate any research being performed by any research team? You all did, and none of you exercised it once.

**Senior Researcher Petrovich:** Every time any of us entertained the idea, we'd be told that the team was close to a breakthrough-

**Overseer Donovan:** It doesn't matter whether you consider yourselves responsible or not. It doesn't matter if you think I forced your hand. If we reveal the truth, the people out there won't care and they'll be calling for your head the same way they'd be calling for mine. The blood is just as much on your hands as it is on mine. Unlike you, I have the mental strength to be honest with myself.

# UNMASKING THE B.N.T.G.

The B.N.T.G. likes to portray itself as an organisation thoroughly disconnected from the criminal underworld of the Backrooms. We have uncovered evidence that the B.N.T.G. has covert relationships with numerous Backrooms criminal groups, and engages in both the purchase and sale of illegal goods.

For your consideration, we present a recording of a meeting between a B.N.T.G. manager and a senior member of the criminal underworld of the Backrooms - captured in a B.N.T.G. warehouse.

**VIDEO TRANSCRIPT**

**Date:** 2021-07-04

**Location:** Warehouse 2, B.N.T.G. Outpost EL3A

A man dressed in the suit enters the camera's field of vision. He is pacing back and forth and checking his watch. He is muttering something indiscernible. He has been identified as M. Smith, a mid-ranking B.N.T.G. manager within the exports division.

Approximately two minutes later, a middle-aged man walks into the camera's field of vision. The suit he wears resembles those worn by citizens of the Leaders in the late 1980s.

**Smith:** Mr. Rochester, a pleasure to see you as always. Time is of the essence here- we have half an hour before staff return to work.

**Rochester:** I didn't realise the B.N.T.G. were in the habit of rushing their valued clients.

**Smith:** Not at all sir, not at all. As we both know, this entire thing is off the books. Half an hour is all the board could get us without leaving a substantial anomaly in the records.

**Rochester:** I understand. To business then.

**Smith:** We are willing to provide a negotiable quantity of Memory Juice as well as a limited quantity of Royal Rations in exchange for Pixie Guns. I have been authorised to negotiate on behalf of the B.N.T.G. within certain limits.

**Rochester:** The first question is how many Pixie Guns do you want?

**Smith:** How many have you got? If your asking price is reasonable, we are willing to purchase your entire stock.

Rochester looks surprised for a moment before he speaks again.

**Rochester:** I understand that the B.N.T.G. has been making many such deals. Word spreads fast in the circles I move in.

**Smith:** We are not desperate for pixie guns, if that's what you want to insinuate. We are simply taking advantage of good deals involving them when they occur. We aren't going to make bad deals.

**Rochester:** Very well. We have forty-three Pixie Guns in our inventory. Six of them have already been bought by other clients and are awaiting delivery. We can sell the rest to you for five barrels of memory juice.

**Smith:** How about three barrels of Memory Juice and a crate of Royal Rations?

**Rochester:** Not quite enough. I hear that the B.N.T.G. has developed something pretty potent from Agrugua Fruit. Add some of that to your offer and I'll take it.

**Smith:** You mean Distilled Agrugua Extract? You're damn right it's potent. We're developing it as an entity sedative. Still in R&D right now, but we can give you six flasks. Tell your customers only to inhale it- a corpse isn't going to turn into a repeat customer.

The two men chuckle.

**Rochester:** Then it's a deal. In exchange for three barrels of Memory Juice, a crate of Royal Rations, and six flasks of distilled Agrugua extract, my organisation will provide yours with thirty-seven pixie guns. I'm not going to ask what you intend to do with them because I know you won't tell me.

**Smith:** You are correct, Mr. Rochester.

The Masked Maidens have been able to identify "Mr. Rochester" as George Rochester, leader of a prominent criminal organisation based out of Level 9.2. Other recordings, which can be found [here](javascript:;), show that the B.N.T.G. has a pattern of making deals with criminal organisations.

To the wanderers who were not the targets of our operation, we understand that what we did caused you alarm, but we hope that you understand now that it was necessary for the greater good. Your secrets were not included in the information we released, and they have been removed from our records.

To the corrupting rot that lurks within the M.E.G. and other groups, hurting innocents - know that we are watching. Know that we will turn your tools against you. Know that someday, your truth will be unmasked.
