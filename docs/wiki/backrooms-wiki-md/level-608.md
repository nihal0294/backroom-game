# **Description**

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-608-0/288precedent.png)

A Precedent Class locomotive, specifically number 790 "Hardwicke" in original LNWR black.

Level 608 is an indoor level comprised of several large brick, prefab, and corrugated metal locomotive roundhouses and general locomotive sheds. These spaces are connected either directly to one another by large doors, or by hallways which contain commercial utilities such as unisex toilets, restaurant spaces, or shelves of model locomotives by a variety of leading manufacturers in [the Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md).[1](javascript:;)

Each of the rooms in Level 608 contain a myriad of tracks, most of which are standard gauge.[2](javascript:;) Depending on if it is a roundhouse or shed, the tracks will either be built around a central turntable or built in organised rows parallel to the longest wall. Along all of these tracks are countless locomotives, trams, rolling stock, small train consists, DMU’s, DEMU’s, EMU’s, and other categories of railway stock that fit on two rails.[3](javascript:;) Most of the example stock are specifically of British origin, with examples of locomotives across the former British Commonwealth. Most items from the collection come from between the second and ninth railway eras, although outliers do exist in the form of a handful of era 1 coaching stock and a few 2030's era EMU's.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-608-0/288gladstone.jpg)

Gladstone, one of 36 LB&SCR B1 Classes of steam locomotive introduced in 1895.

These various trains and locomotives are all in a completely static state. Not only are they not in steam, not fuelled, or have dead batteries, but they are also completely unable to move by more supernatural means. Any attempt to move a display piece, even something as small as a Wickam Trolley, will result in it feeling incredibly heavy, and therefore immovable. Some of these locomotives even get "opening days" where new exhibits are adorned with balloons or period accurate crests on their first day of entering the level. This is most frequently done for locomotives and consists which have a level of fame or historical importance in the Frontrooms, the most recent of which being the British Rail Battle of Britain 34051 "Winston Churchill".

It is known that Level 608 originally did not contain any locomotives until at least 1829, which is the date in which the Stephenson's Rocket, one of the original pioneering locomotives, was built. The level, in its old state, was much harder to exit and far more non-Euclidean, as well as being built out of just brick and wood rather than its wider variety of modern day materials. Although elements of the Euclidean nature still persist in the modern day, these lapses in third dimensional space are appropriately signposted to avoid confusion. As well as this, many of these signposts—and some randomly found kiosks—provide physical and digital maps which are multi-layered to help navigate the sometimes obscure layout.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-608-0/288renovating.jpg)

37 258 undergoing mechanical overhaul, pictured here in BR Brunswick Green with a half-yellow end.

Although undoubtedly large, Level 608's atriums are far from infinite. They take up a space of 23 miles across and 28 miles long, and are surrounded by several more miles of storage rooms and workshops for restorations. Whilst many of the exhibits within this level were previously acquired in nearly pristine condition, certain more recent pieces are scarse or harder to source, and sometimes are instead delivered to Level 608 in scrap condition. As such, many units have to undergo intensive restoration, as well as the many inspections that the exhibits have to go under to ensure they stay in either cosmetic or operational condition. Although being repaired to operational status is the preferred, many exhibits are unfortunately not in this state, mainly due to age.

These spaces are differenciated mainly by their more industrial appearance, having noticealy high ceilings with gantries, cranes, and ceiling hooks. As well as this, these back spaces often showcase even more non-euclidean layouts, though this is seemingly so even more exhibits can be maintained. Various workstations, tools, and equipment of varying sizes can be found dotting the walls and floors of these back areas as well, adding to the general clutter. As they are not intended to be seen by the public eye, and are deliberately made restricted from the public exhibition spaces, there is far less regard for safety, and it is incredibly likely that one can trip or get injured on the various industrial parts and machines.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-608-0/288storage.jpg)

Two class 47's sitting deep within storage. One of these Class 47's, D1693, has been kept in its original 1957 style of numbering, wheras 47 376 utilises TOPS.

These industrial workspaces are spliced with much more neat and tidy storage rooms which house the various exhibits that are currently not on display, or any recently restored units. Though tidier than the workshop rooms in that they have less ground and wall clutter, these spaces are usually filled to the brim with museum pieces, and are usually stored with some level of categorisation in mind.[4](javascript:;) Although they are never coupled together, these pieces are usually paired up buffer to buffer,[5](javascript:;) making it extremely difficult to move between them. However, as these back areas are strictly out of bounds, this is never an issue for visitors.

---

Although most of the level is devoid of entities, some do travel from other levels into this one. These kinds of occurances, whilst uncommon, can serve as a risk to the visitors and exhibits in Level 608. As the level is not home to any unique entities, only Backrooms-wide species such as [Smilers](entity-3.md), [Deathmoths](entity-4.md), [Hounds](entity-8.md), [Facelings](entity-9.md), [Skin-Stealers](entity-10.md), and [Wretches](entity-15.md) that find their way into the level can be found.[6](javascript:;) However, the threats of these beings is dealt with by the owner of this level, Blake, who swiftly removes them from the premises.

# **Blake**

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-608-0/288blake.jpg)

Blake sat atop of a Highland Railway Jones Goods Class, specifically No. 103.

Blake is the somewhat anomalous entity that runs the entirety of Level 608. They, similar to the employees of the as-of-yet unamalgamated [Backrooms Remodeling Co.](backrooms-remodeling-co.md), are completely black in hue and lack any kind of details on their body besides white and emotive eyes and mouth, the latter of which is unique to them in comparison to the rest of his species. In the rare instances they are found wearing clothing [at all], it is usually related to railways. Blake is also a sexless individual with an androdynous looking figure, and whilst they frequently refer to themselves as "he", they are also more than willing to wear, present, and be referred to as things which are not traditionally masculine. Though this is usually done at themed events they run within the level, they have also been seen to dress in bygone attire from both genders, and have used both traditional binary pronouns in the past.

Though seemingly related to the employees of Backrooms Remodeling Co, their physical abilities differ from them somewhat. Not only do they have a mouth, but their entire body is incredibly fluid, able to bend and contort and take on the appearance of multiple different body types, which they usually uses to fit into specific period clothing. Their malleable form is also used for more trivial matters, such as reaching things from long distances or utilising multiple limbs to aid in restoration of their trains. Their form is so fluid, in fact, that it has brought into question what the "default" appearance is for most BRC employees, a query which has prompted the M.E.O.D. to begin formal discussion with the group.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-608-0/288blakeposter.jpg)

An advertisement for Level 608 that was distributed across [Level 9](level-9.md), [Level 11](level-11.md), [Level 13.1](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-13-1), [Level 29](level-29.md), and [Level 797](level-797.md).

Blake is a rather excitable character, if not somewhat introverted as well. Although they are quick to speak about their level in incredibly excessive detail, they oftentimes struggle with more basic communication or questioning. Most topics brought up to Blake usually loop back to their level, or more specifically their railway collection, something which is seemingly done to aid in their understanding and explanation of their views and opinions of what is being talked about. Their excitability when it comes to their knowledge also bleeds into various references to Level 608 online, as Blake has frequently been found making edits to pages that include hyper-specific details on their exhibits.[7](javascript:;) Whilst opinions are mixed on these edits, recent discussions with Blake have permitted them to remain, especially on this page.

Despite them being tough to work with on a professional level, tourists to Level 608 speak highly of Blake and their general enthusiasm. Ever since Level 608 was listed as a destination in the vacation division's website, it has brought similar rail enthusiasts from across the Backrooms to behold these sights, which has seemingly brought great glee to Blake. It has brought them out of their shell a lot more, giving them the confidence to detail more about their behaviours and life.

### <Begin Recording at: 12:51PM>

**Date:** 20/07/31  
**Location:** A crowded atrium within [Level 608](level-608.md).  
**Participants:** Alex Patr(Interviewer), Blake(Interviewee).

---

**[Context]**

*[It was incredibly busy in Level 608, although that would be far from a surprise to anyone who knew the occasion that was being celebrated. It was the one year anniversary of the level being officially amalgamated into the M.E.O.D. and being classed as a vacation destination, and Blake had opted to put on a creative display for it. They were utilising many of their supposedly Victorian-era locomotives, piloting a small six-wheeled steam engine on a turntable in the middle of the atrium itself. The demonstration was gradually coming to a close, seemingly having been popular given the size of the crowds. Though countless members of the BlogRooms journalists were present to document the occasion, a member of the M.E.O.D's Interviewing Division was also present to speak to Blake not for the event itself, but instead to ask some questions about Blake themself.]*

*[After reducing steam pressure and shunting the locomotive back to its display, Blake left the locomotive to one of their new employees whilst they paced off to one of the hallways to relax for a moment. Seeing Blake on their own and approachable, Alex took the opportunity to walk up to them, holding a recording device in their general direction as they did.]*

**Alex Patr:** Hey, Blake, do you mind answering a few questions for me?

**Blake:** Huh? Oh, really? Another person? A lot of people have come up to me, but sure!

*[Blake seemed a little worn out and exasperated, no doubt from speaking in front of a large crowd for hours by this point. They created a bottle of water, either from their locomotive driver overalls or from thin air, and chugged a fair bit of it before speaking to Alex again.]*

**Blake:** Do you wanna know about the event? I can tell you a little bit about the locomotives I've been moving, if you'd like? No one's wanted to know about that yet, and I think it'll make a good piece for the, uh, BlogRooms or whatever.

**Alex Patr:** Well, I'm sure it would? But I'm not here on behalf of journalists. I'm here on behalf of the M.E.O.D. itself, actually.

**Blake:** Oh, so… You're *not* here to discuss the locomotives like everyone else?

**Alex Patr:** No, no… I want to ask questions about *you*, not about today's event. Sorry.

**Blake:** Oh! Well, that's… Different, at least? It can get a bit boring answering the same questions over and over again. Shoot yours to me!

**Alex Patr:** Well, hang on, let me see…

*[Pulling out a small tablet, Alex begins to shuffle through his apps to find the list of questions. He seethes a bit upon rereading them, but ultimately follows through with his questioning, albeit in a more reluctant tone.]*

**Alex Patr:** Are you affiliated with the [BRC](backrooms-remodeling-co.md)-?

**Blake:** Wait. *THAT'S* the question you're leading with??

**Alex Patr:** It's the first one listed! Believe me, the others are far more uh… intrusive than this one.

**Blake:** *Well, that's not really making me want to answer your questions! What even are these other questions, how bad are they?*

**Alex Patr:** Uh… They ask about how you collect your trains, how your anatomy works, how much of this level is modified by you, and uh… *ngh,* asking about your intentions and allegiances.

**Blake:** *I don't want to answer any of those.*

**Alex Patr:** It's what I've been asked to do, I'm sorry. Between you and me, I… I wouldn't want to ask you these anyways. I know how you feel.

**Blake:** *Would you like it if I grilled you, huh? What if I asked you about where you came from or implied some maliciousness as to why you were here?*

**Alex Patr:** I wouldn't like it, you're right. Could you at least answer one or two of them? I-I'll go after, it's not like I can get demoted further from this position anyways.

*[Blake pauses momentarily, supposedly thinking over Alex's words a little more before speaking.]*

**Blake:** Oh? *You've been demoted before?*

**Alex Patr:** Many times… I was a field investigator once? [But... I got injured once from it](level-206.md), and got demoted to a PA and AA "interrogator". I didn't do well at it, so I've been pushed further and further down until I got here.

**Blake:** So your job right now *isn't* interrogation?

**Alex Patr:** It rubs off at interrogation sometimes, but it's technically not. I'm a stock standard… entry-level… interviewer. Getting information on you is pretty low priority, it's only being done because the people above me are contemplating some negotiations with the BRC.

**Blake:** That is… kinda sad.

**Alex Patr:** Mhm! Sure uh… sure is.

**Blake:** *If I tell you all that stuff, will they please stop asking me any further questions?*

**Alex Patr:** I… I can't guarantee that? I'm technically just a rookie.

**Blake:** *If you can find a way to guarantee it, I'll tell you. I-I… it's the only way I will, I don't feel comfortable telling anyone any other way.*

**Alex Patr:** Will you answer them on the recording now, then? I promise I'll make sure they don't bother you ever again about this sort of stuff, I'll wipe the file if they don't comply.

**Blake:** I don't want them bothering me about other things, too! I just want to be able to show off my collection a-and run events… please. If you can get them to leave me alone on stuff like that, I'll answer the questions.

**Alex Patr:** It's not like they'll have a reason to pester you when they have the questions answered anyways?

**Blake:** *Yeah, I suppose you won't.*

*[Suddenly, Blake reached forwards to Alex, snatching his tablet and bringing it closely to their chest. They looked down at it, bringing the top of it outwards just enough to see the questions.]*

**Alex Patr:** Hey! I could've just read them to you?

**Blake:** *I don't want to draw this out, uh… Alex Patr? Let me just read this, please.*

**Alex Patr:** Just hand that back to me when you're done with it, I really don't want to break a second tablet.

**Blake:** I won't break it, don't worry…

*[Blake begins to hum to themself, scrolling down the list of questions with an annoyed and disconcerted expression. After reading the list, they cleared their throat and looked up to Alex.]*

**Blake:** *Yes, I'm… Kind of a part of the BRC.* I'm one of the rarer species in their group, I'm kinda related to their basic pitch-black species, whatever they're called. They used to use me and some others for their organisation quite frequently because of what I can do. For some reason, one day they didn't want us anymore. They employed questionable ways to get rid of us, but… thankfully I got out early. They replaced us with [Reality Fresheners](object-32.md) not long after.

**Alex Patr:** Shit, sorry… Sounds like you went through a lot? *How long ago did this happen, though? I thought they always had those fresheners.*

**Blake:** I dunno? Does it matter? Ages ago, if your reaction is anything to go by. *This* place is where I ran off to, and I sorta uh… Found a love for trains, they really did something for me.

**Alex Patr:** Did the ones in this level help distract you from what happened?

**Blake:** *Well, there weren't any in this level? I brought some here, kind of on accident.*

**Alex Patr:** You did? Where from?

**Blake:** [The Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md), obviously? Or, uh, a handful of them at least…

**Alex Patr:** Wait, what? You can do that? And what do you mean *a handful of Frontrooms'?*

**Blake:** Oh, you don't know about that?

**Alex Patr:** *No?* Uh, so I don't get lost on a… strange tangent, I'm impressed you can just pull stuff from the Frontrooms? How does that work?

**Blake:** I… don't even know myself? I can manipulate a lot of stuff as it is, and I don't know how I do *that* either. It kinda strains me to do it, especially when I pull stuff from random earths, but I can. I began collecting these things back when they were new, but now I have to get scrapped ones or uh… just more modern ones. *I don't really like the modern stuff as much though, electric trains are boring.*

**Alex Patr:** Oh, uh, okay… *You being able to do that is, uh, kind of powerful?*

**Blake:** For a human, I guess it is? What, do you think I'm dangerous…?

**Alex Patr:** No, not at all? But you can do a lot, could anyone else like you be dangerous?

**Blake:** *Rude.* But no, there's no one else like me anymore. It's probably been a long time since what happened. I feel I would've found another person like me. I won't hurt anyone with my abilities, I don't even know how I could? I just like having a collection of trains…

**Alex Patr:** *That's… oddly sweet.* Does that cover everything on the tablet, though? I don't actually remember each question.

**Blake:** …Mostly, yeah. There's some stuff about my anatomy, but it's not like I know anything about that? I've never cut myself open, or… cut myself at all, actually.

**Alex Patr:** In that case, I'll leave you alone now, okay? As will everyone else at the M.E.O.D., unless it's for your fun events.

**Blake:** Right… thank you. Actually, hang on, there is something more I'd like to tell you.

**Alex Patr:** Yeah?

*[Blake doesn't verbally reply, and instead just hands over the tablet back and suddenly snatches the audio recorder at the same time. The distant sounds of Level 608's crowds become obscured by the sound of Blake's fast swipe, followed by the abrupt ending of the audio recording itself. Alex Patr refused to elaborate on any further details about their conversation.]*[8](javascript:;)

# **Entrances And Exits**

#### Entrances

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-608-0/852288entrance.jpg)

An example of an empty shop that leads to Level 608 in Level 852.

Despite being a popular tourist attraction for many, Level 608 is unfortunately not connected to many majorly inhabited levels. Individuals travelling to Level 608 are urged to utilise [Level 605](level-605.md) on their journey to cut travel times, or to follow dedicated travelling groups when on booked vacations.

One of the main entrances into Level 608 is from [Level 26](level-26.md), and can be utilised by entering any large and industrial-looking garage doors that can appear in random rooms. Another similar door can also be found in [Level 248](level-248.md), although this exit should not be used in travel groups due to the isolating nature of the level in question.

Another entrance is found in [Level 255](level-255.md) by noclipping through the walls of any large, empty rooms within the factory. The same is also applicable for large empty rooms in [Level 852](level-852.md), [Level 853](level-853.md), and [Level 979](level-979.md).

#### Exits

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:level-608-0/288exit.jpg)

The front of a British Rail Class 401, also known as a 2-BIL, pictured with an exit to Level 797 off in the distance.

Under the laws put over any level classified as a holiday destination, all level exits that are known about are signposted accordingly, coupled with scannable QR codes to give visitors directions to a handful of populated levels. Unfortunately, much like its entrances, Level 608 does not directly connect to any major cities or towns.

Entering any open shed berths which have a bright white light emanating from them will lead to [Level 566](level-566.md) or [Level 797](level-797.md) depending on the opening's corresponding signpost. Any smaller glowing exit, such as an open doorway or archway, can lead to [Level 153](level-153.md), [Level 284](level-284.md), or [Level 699](level-699.md).

The level's other remaining exits, being exits to [Level 374](level-374.md) and [Level 402](level-402.md), are both accessible by utilising signposted doors that otherwise do not stand out within the level itself. Other exits may exist, but an appropriate amount have been found to comply with the vacation division.
