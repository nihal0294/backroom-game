> Level 997 is the 998th Level of

Don't capitalize the second use of "Level" as it isn't a proper noun.

> 998th Level of the backrooms and one

"backrooms" should be capitalized since it is a proper noun.

> area,which is approximate two

Add a space after the comma, change "approximate" to "approximately".

> two hundred (200) square meters.

I'd personally remove the "(200)" as that isn't helping unless the number is much larger, though that's just my personal choice.

> will appear; Entrances located in other

Don't capitalize "Entrances" here.

> retreated to various bases/outposts/communities indicated that

No need to say each synonym for base, just put one down since they all mean the same thing.

> It is not clear whether this is part of Level 997's self-defence mechanism.

Change "whether" to "if" and "defence" to "defense".

> of rich materials except bottled lightning will be refreshed. The refresh

Add a comma before "except" and after "lightning".

Replace "whether" with "if" and "defence" with "defense".

> at the resource refresh point, if the resource is

I'd replace the comma here with a semicolon to make it not a run on.

> However, the special exit at this Level

Change "at" to "on".

> Unlike entity 81 "elevator",

Capitalize "entity 81" and remove "'elevator'". There's no need to state the entity's name twice here.

> stand there and do nothing, enjoy the happy beautiful music.

Capitalize "stand" and change the comma to a semicolon.

> normally in Level 997,except users

Add a space after this comma.

> have been trapped in the lift,so if you have

Add a space after this comma.

> Object-96.1,please utilise it immediately

Add a space after this comma; change "utilise" to "utilize".
