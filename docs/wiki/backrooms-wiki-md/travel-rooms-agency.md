# The Backrooms Travel Agency

*Are you tired of the Backrooms rat race?  
Want to settle down in a peaceful, habitable level?  
**Contact the Backrooms Travel Agency!***  
*Your stay in the Backrooms doesn't have to be miserable.  
**Tell us the level of your dreams. We'll take you there**—quickly and safely.*[1](javascript:;)

Backrooms Travel Agency is dedicated to chaperoning wanderers wherever they want to go; safe and secure levels are our speciality! We work with absolutely anyone—aging wanderers, families with young children, wanderers with injuries or disabilities; we pride ourselves on helping anyone who wants to go to a better place.

At the Agency, our mission is to give a helping hand to wanderers in need. Our Travel Teams consist of experts who are highly skilled in level navigation, with backgrounds in the M.E.G. and other exploration groups, so they have unmatched experience. Read the profiles of our trusted Travel Guides below!

## The Backrooms Travel experience

Traversing the Backrooms across unknown levels can be daunting, dangerous and complicated. That is why so many wanderers trust Backrooms Travel to make traveling as simple and breezy for you as possible.

**Travel better with us.**  
Our seasoned Travel Guides will chart the optimal routes to your destination and confidently lead you from level to level. Your Travel Team will be equipped with essential gear, packed food and refreshments.[6](javascript:;) Detours are also possible for overnight stays in comfortable levels. For Standard Class and above, we will provide you with a [Worn Sack](object-87.md)[7](javascript:;) for your luggage.[8](javascript:;)

**Your safety is our priority.**  
Although our Guides try to arrange the safest routes, danger may always lurk around the corner. Our Travel Guards support your travel team to protect you from any external threats.[9](javascript:;) These experienced wanderers are armed with gear to ward off entities or other hostiles, and objects such as [Squirt Guns](object-19.md) and first aid kits.[10](javascript:;)

###### Silas (Founder)

*Silas has been the manager of Backrooms Travel since its founding. Although he doesn't personally lead expeditions, he currently works at the Main Agency in Level 11.*

> Hello! I was in the Mapping and Pathfinding team back in my days at the [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md), so I'm still passionate about charting routes through levels and optimizing travel in such a foreign world. My girlfriend says I'm a bit of a nerd, and well, I do in fact approach this as a giant puzzle—but solving this puzzle potentially saves lives and helps wanderers reach habitable levels. I believe a sense of community and mutual aid is crucial in the reality that is the Backrooms.

###### Yates (Lead Travel Guide)

*Yates joined the BTA and was instantly commended for his friendliness, availability and cheer. He appreciates every journey that life has to offer, especially in the Backrooms.*

> Hi there! I was an avid wilderness enthusiast in the Frontrooms—trekking, backpacking, rock climbing, you name it! Falling into the Backrooms was unnerving but posed a somewhat familiar challenge to me. Of course, I know that isn't true for everyone, and now I pride myself in helping wanderers find their way. People have told me I look like I could carry a person on each shoulder, and, well, that isn't false haha! I'm here to fully support ya on your journey!

###### Macey (Premium Travel Guard)

*Macey has blessed BTA with her insight and skills. She values life and safety above everything, and leads our Travel Guards to ensure that no harm comes to customers.*

> Hello. I was modestly high-ranking in my previous career, where I honed my skills in entity removal and self-preservation. The philosophy of being unbound by fear stuck to me to this day. Here at Backrooms Travel, we've successfully escorted prominent figures and persons of interest before, and nothing deters our Guard force: not entities, environmental hazards, hostile groups, nor political enemies. Hence, to our Standard and Premium customers: please feel at ease, we are here for your safety.

  
*We would like to remind you that our employees exclusively work in uniform. Do not follow anyone else claiming to be from Backrooms Travel. We don't know who they are.*
