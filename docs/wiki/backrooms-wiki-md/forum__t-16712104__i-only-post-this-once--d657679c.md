Only doing concept crits at the moment. Anyways, this level’s structure reminds me a lot of [Level 53](level-53.md) which is one of my favourite levels.

The calendars are a common theme in the level, yet they serve no purpose. Why are there so many calendars? Also, how do they date back to 7500BC? We didn’t even have the sundial until 1500BC!

I’d include the house shrinking in the main article and in the logs. Speaking of logs, I’d expand on the process of the shrinking, maybe include some experiment logs to show it.

You could also expand on the layout of the home a bit to help with world building. It isn’t even stated how many floors or bedrooms the house has. Are there different rooms in the basement or is it one massive one?

Hope this helps!!
