[You open a bottle, hoping to find Almond Water.](javascript:;)

[Instead, you find a handwritten critique notice.](javascript:;)

Most of what I do is spag related, but I'm going to branch out with a bit of formatting related crit.  
**Let's begin.**

- To start, I would separate different entrances and exits with bullet points, marked with a single asterisk "\*" at the beginning of a line. A useful page to check out for helping with general syntax is [The Syntax Page](https://www.wikidot.com/doc-wiki-syntax:start).
- I agree with with the paragraphs. Put a space between the paragraphs to make it easier for people to read.
- I'm going to be honest, you could probably elaborate on this Level. Are there places on this Level that are actually dangerous to go to? Are there rooms/a series of rooms that somehow distinguish themselves? Is there an entity that controls the level? I wonder what the story behind this Level is, as what you have here is basically the [Snackrooms](level-6-1.md). Without a story, you should scrap the idea, as it's basically the same thing.

I know this was really blunt, but I'm being honest and making sure you don't accidentally do something that could get you banned for something akin to copyright. My biggest thing is add some more story to the Level. After that, I'll be happy to look over your spag. Have a great day.
