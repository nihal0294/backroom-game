# An Average Author Page

rating: +15[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

Hello, dear reader. I'm (Cart on Discord), and I'm glad you are here to check out my works! Before we get to those, I'll tell you a little bit about me. I'm just a guy who loves reading and writing who discovered the wiki in my teenage years and never looked back. I live in the beautiful United States of America, listen to a LOT of country music, I'm a strong Christian, and my favorite element is copper.[1](javascript:;)

As a writer, I'm always looking to collaborate with others because group work is a great way to unite people. If you see my name pop up in many different GOI pages or canons, that is why. Now, enough about me. I hope you enjoy the articles I've written below.

### Entities

---

[Entity 80](entity-80.md)![AA](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/entity-80/AA)Entity 80 is hypothetical, with only a handful of people claiming to have encountered it. It is a being that is described as a bright white light that is human-like in shape. The exact details of the entity differ between each report, so a general description is the only suitable method for describing it. Entity 80 is said to only appear to those on the edge of death or who have died. It is assumed that the entity is harmless and exists to "guide" those who have died. What it guides people to is unknown, but it is hypothesized to be the afterlife by those who think the entity exists. - "Before Eternal Sleep"
