05 Aug 2022 06:37

> filled to the brim with a hundred different stores and restaurants but that time had long passed.

Add a comma after "restaurants".

> here with his friends, without a care in the world.

I'd change this comma (,) to an em dash (—) as shown below:

\*here with his friends—without a single care in the world.\*

> in new towns, he had been left behind and forgotten.

I'd change this comma (,) to an em dash (—) as shown below:

\*in new towns—he had been left behind and forgotten.\*

> and then he saw something, below him was an endless expanse

Change this comma (,) to a semicolon (;) as shown below:

\*and then he saw something; below him was an endless expanse\*

> As he regained his consciousness the first thing he noticed was an incessant buzzing

Add a comma after "consciousness".

> As Jonathan opened his eyes he was hit with a wave of confusion.

Add a comma after "eyes".

> incandescent halogen lights on the ceiling

Level 0 doesn't have halogen lights lmao.

> his surroundings a second time but it made less sense than before.

Add a comma after "time".

> after the fall, that was the only explanation.

I'd change this comma (,) to an em dash (—) as shown below:

\*after the fall—that was the only explanation.\*

> he called out, nothing but a faint echo answered him.

I'd change this comma (,) to an em dash (—) as shown below:

\*he called out—nothing but a faint echo answered him.\*

> After running through an uncountable number of cookie cutter rooms he stopped dead in his tracks as an expanse stretched

Add a comma after "rooms".

> The room was still the same height as everywhere else but this time rather than

Add a comma before "but".
