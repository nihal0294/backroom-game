12 Oct 2022 04:14

So I was thinking about the entities, and how we have Skin-Stealers and Bone Thieves, and I was thinking, what if a third entity existed to complete this trifecta? This third entity would feast on the blood of wanderers, naturally, since it's one of the only things the other two entities don't touch. I even had a name ready: Blood Bandits. It was a perfect idea. Perhaps I should give this hypothetical new entity a more parasitic design, some special way to lure in prey-  
And then it hit me I was just reinventing entity 161, Leeon. Damn it.
