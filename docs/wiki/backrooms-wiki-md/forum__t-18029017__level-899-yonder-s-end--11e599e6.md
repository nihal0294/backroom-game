+1 What if you were in the Backrooms and depressed

06 Jul 2026 17:43

I never know if I'm getting the right interpretation when I read a page, but I really liked this from what I gleamed! To me, it's a story that follows somebody incredibly depressed, and who thinks they got a taste of something that would fix that depression. It's irrational, they can't explain *why* it will help, they just know it will. It has to be better than right now, right?

I kinda see Level 899 as an allegory for an addiction, as are the people (penumbras?) are those on the drug. They sit around and be happy, even though we can see from the outside it's not a good place. But at the same time, you can understand why the main character wants to (and eventually *does*) move there. His life is objectively terrible, but if he becomes obsessed around this one place and becomes dependent on it, I mean… it works for so many others?

You almost wish the people around him could recognise he wasn't doing so well mentally and could help him out. But the M.E.G. is too busy for that. If he had a lifeline, maybe he wouldn't have been so tempted
