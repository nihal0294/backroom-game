**Level C-1801** is the 1081st level of the Backrooms C Cluster.

# **Descriptions:**

**Level C-1801** is a an indestructible and small space like a church made of marble. Statues, murals and some other things about [Entity C-99](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-c-99) are displayed. The entrance of **Level C-1801** is buried 10 metres deep in the ██ Town in the east of Level 11 and has been dug by the M.E.G., the corridor to the entrance of the level can be found somewhere under Mount ██. The entrance door cannot be open inside and has kept powerfully closed since the M.E.G. found the level for the first time.

The characteristics of **Level C-1801** are stopping the metabolism of wanderers in this level and making them never die that they will keep their minds no matter how big hurts they get. Therefore, if a person dies near ██ Town, a naked copy of their body will be created in **Level C-1801**, minds of the deceased will be moved to the copy.

After investigating local residents, we found out that the earliest record was in 18██, mentioned in a letter of the local resident named Luied Coral:

> *My dear old friend:*  
> ‎  
> *Last week my courage let me drink up a bottle of poison again, the disgusting death that burned my throat made me get close to the god again. As my sight shaked like the boat light in the storm, I paid attention to the sliver dome on the other side of the reed plain, there the Lord of Life stood highly that wait for its people.*  
> ‎  
> *I walked up, with my forehead knocking at the ground:"Please commiserate we the lost lambs fogotten in the wasteland and lead us out of the hurts and death."The god didn't say anything and looked like hating me about breaking his words by returning the place with life and death. I dear not speak and the god took a bone that shone with the pearl luster out of its robe then drew three concentric circles with it on the sand. After the last arc closing, reeds on its handwriting all struggled like dying people, entangled together and spit out four white pebbles with blood. The pebbles expanded into four obelisks and then formed a.square. Walls and altars grew up from the ground. The god put his deep sights on me with slightly nodding, immediately I returned home and all of the hurts recovered.*  
> ‎  
> *Last night I visit badly sick Old Jacob and his murky pupils suddenly reflected a weird radiance. "Look!"He use his thin finger powerfully pointed to the ceiling covered with mold stains and Knocked over the apricot dew in the maid's hand, "The deity in the dark robe is now walking around the corridor pillars made with white jade and the blood lamb meets the light again…" He passed away with an unfinished sentence, but his smile was so noticeable. As the most bizarre, my wife said that she heard her husband's teenage voice like her loving mate whispering to her just this morning. I'm really sure the god has showed its great power to give his followers deserved blissful place…*  
> ‎  
> *Attach my hand drawn sketch of the shrine that I saw in the Realm of Reincarnation.[1](javascript:;)Best wishes for you and Masie.*  
> ‎  
> *Yours,*  
> *Luied Coral*

When **Level C-1801** was originally discovered, it was filled with a high-density mixture of human tissues and its gene was the same as people who had been dead since 18██ in ██ Town and it kept wriggling. However, after moving the mixture out of the level, it completely stopped all of the movements. Now about 90% of the mixture has been moved out and manage as the Typical Biochemical Waste.

# **Bases, outposts and communities:**

There are always two M.E.G. workers from ██Town going around the entrance of **Level C-1801** periodically to prevent door from accidentally closing and trapping the wanderers.

# **Entrances and Exits**

###### Entrances:

- Enter from the entrance in Level 11.
- Die in the area of ██ Town

###### Exits:

- exiting from the gate is the only known exit.
