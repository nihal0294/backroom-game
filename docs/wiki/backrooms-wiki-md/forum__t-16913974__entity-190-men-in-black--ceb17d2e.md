+1 a good feeling of importance

25 Aug 2024 12:28

I really enjoy the concept here. The mystery and importance set up by their existence and their action make them feel extremely meaningful. from the start, we feel that the entity is much more than anything we have seen before ("they are not native to either realm").

The way it got structured is also quite interesting. The way their behaviors between the Frontrooms and the Backrooms are different first makes them feel disconnected (as of, why are they doing two whole different things depending on their location?), but later on, everything comes together as something that does make a lot of sense. As their mission got unraveled, I got that "oooooh so that's why!" moment that felt really good.

The mention of Level 234 alone made the entity feel entitled to the Backrooms universe way more than anything real-life mention, as well as creating a real sense of danger and threat other than their simple raw power.

There's just two things I'm not a big fan of. First, how many people died. Like Partygoers, the addition of a lot of death didn't really add any feeling of threat to me, as those deaths didn't feel meaningful. That's mainly why the mention of the Prison was necessary for me to really see them as something important AND threatening.

While the structure of the page and the variation of formats is nice and welcome, I also found the logs to go quite fast on their content, which didn't make me as compelled by their implications as they could have. Thankfully the clinical parts of the page convey the importance and the links with the Backrooms enough for me to feel good about the page.

I like the implications that the page has, truly. It showcases them well, and it uses the Frontrooms and the Backrooms as a way to give them importance, something I didn't find in Orbs. I just wish the page took more of its time for the more narrative part, as they can go a little fast for me to really be able to care about their intend, but the page is overall very interesting. The page showcases lore, and I like the lore. So good one!
