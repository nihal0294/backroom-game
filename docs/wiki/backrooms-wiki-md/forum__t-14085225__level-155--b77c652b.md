Level 155

04 May 2021 19:32

**Page Type:** Level

**Elevator Pitch:**

- Level 155 Consists of a shopping centre, filled with many different stores that sell varied items such as basic essentials like food and water or entertainment devices.
- There is an outside but it is just seemingly endless streets and houses.
- all aspects are controlled by Object XX, a computer that can be used to control Level 155's interior using Adobe Photoshop.

**Central Narrative:** I haven't got any ideas for a narrative at the moment.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** I don't think there are any rooms currently currently controlled by outsider actions. That idea could be used to explore what people would do presented with the option to change a world, would they make it easier for people to live inside or would they mess it up without thinking of the consequences for others?

**Additional Notes:** I haven't expanded much on the object as I'm still deciding if it should be its own article or if it should be worked into the levels one, Also sorry for the short pitch, it's a basic idea but I think it could be expanded upon well in a draft.

I feel like this concept currently is a bit bland. Perhaps some of the stores can sell items that aren't for humans? Like alien products, or silly stuff. E.g. here's a keychain with a small universe inside.

I don't think including the Adobe Photoshop section is needed. The computer is enough in my opinion to implicate that there is something else afoot.

Narrative wise, I think you could work with the computer maybe abucting people to become workers at these various shops, the story being:

**We need to get out of here. Now.**

The story can be told from the sole survivor of a group that landed in level 155.

When it comes to the outside, I think what could be even more creepy— is if it was a desert with some abandoned buildings. Perhaps the computer took over this area and made its home. Or this is an interdimensional store that resides in an area void of life.

My go-to for something interesting is this:

- Complicated isn't always good. If the soup has all the vegetables, it loses the main flavor and becomes less tasty.

- Implication. You don't need everything figured out, let the readers do that for you.

- Fonts can set the mood.
