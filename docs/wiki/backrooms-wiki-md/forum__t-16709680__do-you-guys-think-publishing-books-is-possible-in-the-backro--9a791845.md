Re: Do you guys think publishing books is possible in The Backrooms?

09 Feb 2024 16:07

If I remember correctly, in [Object 56](object-56.md), it talks about how the B.N.T.G. distributed copies of the Cookbook, so I think that it would be possible to *get* a book published. I'm not sure about *publishing* a book, though, as in self-publishing. Although, considering the fact that Level 4 exists, I don't think printers are the most unusual sight, so if someone printed up a couple copies of something they wrote and started selling them, I think that'd count.

Now I'm wondering, what kind of books would people publish in the Backrooms? I think it'd be interesting to see a character who's just a humble author who goes around writing stories about the places he visits.

Also, what about CHILDREN'S books? Since animals aren't super common in places like Level 11, would there be children's books that use *Entities* as characters? *That'd* be an interesting tale to read.

Would there be books based on real stories of people in the Backrooms? Would people write about the Frontrooms? Could an Entity write a novel and publish it? What about biographies? Could someone in Sim Canon write a biography about the now-dead Overseers?

I should probably stop now. But, long story short, yes, getting a book published is definitely possible, and there's a lot of storytelling potential to tell *about* stories (see what I did there?) that are written by people in the Backrooms.

It's possible, but not necessary

12 Feb 2024 14:37

This might come across as cynical, but distributing a newspaper or a book physically would be impractical due to the difficulty of traveling across levels. Its not like in real life where everywhere is easily reachable by plane or truck. Computers and mobile telephones are very prevalent in the Backrooms, and the MEG already has a public database that serves as their mouthpiece, making print mediums redundant and unecessary.
