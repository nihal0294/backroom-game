# Hey there, I'm Endrew!

I joined in 2022, after being introduced to the Backrooms by Broogli and similar YouTube videos. I like everything liminal, mystery, thriller, with some nostalgia and comedy. My main interest is levels (as you can see by my published pages lol.)

My pages are closely related to my interests and life experiences, plus I try to use my own photos if I can, cuz that's neat. I strive to create a particular ambience and unique vibe with each page. Admittedly, most of my pages are rather short, but that's not a bad thing! I like to leave room for a little mystery.

[Staff history](javascript:;)

[Staff history](javascript:;)

I was Staff Helper momentarily in 2023, but got demoted since I was too busy with school. I properly rejoined Helper + JStaff in late 2025. I'm basically the image replacement wizard, as I've found and edited replacements for a TON of non-cc images. Using my 3D art degree for good ig.

Fun fact: even before joining staff, I pioneered the modern tag system that includes genres, settings, and attributes! Years ago I was independently classifying a ton of levels on this very page, and staff saw that and used it as the basis for a proper detailed tag system.

[My vision of the Backrooms](javascript:;)

[My vision of the Backrooms](javascript:;)

- To me, the setting is king; this whole thing is about liminal SPACES after all
- I ignore almost all entities and POIs, especially overpowered ones
- Despite that, I'm fascinated by the interactions of groups and of humanity as a whole and how they grapple with this reality
- Yea I like clinical tone BUT I very much appreciate the creative depth and diversity of modern pages
- Imo a level can work purely based on strong vibes and ambience, without necessitating narration, characters, or plot. A great image can be worth a thousand words, but since this is a writing website, we still ought to build on top of these images, not be carried solely by them

Because of my opinions, I may tend to downvote more than I upvote. Please rest assured that it's not personal at all! Whenever I read and/or vote on a page, I only check the author after I'm done reading it, to not let myself be biased (I can hardly recognize usernames anyway lol)
