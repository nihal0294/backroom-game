Level idea - Drive alone

17 May 2022 15:52

![road-g32b0265d9_1920.jpg?width=914&height=609](https://media.discordapp.net/attachments/860818026454843415/976149020483387462/road-g32b0265d9_1920.jpg?width=914&height=609)

This level is an irregular road about 100 miles long.

The explorers who enter this level for the first time will sit in front of the driver's seat without even realizing it. The interior of the car looks old and old. The navigation does not turn on, and the light in front of it blinks as if it's about to go out. Explorers cannot open or break the car door on their own, and even the seat belts that are firmly fastened cannot be undone. The sky seen through the window is mostly a night with a crescent moon, and it is not easy to drive because there is not much light.
