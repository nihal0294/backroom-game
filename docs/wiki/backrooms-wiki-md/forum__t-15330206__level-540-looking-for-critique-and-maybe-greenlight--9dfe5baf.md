"Hall E is the final hallway, consisting of a multitude of kitchen and food storage rooms. Consuming of these foods is not recommended as it is all expired and has large amounts of mold."  
-I suggest expanding this as it seems more like a after thought than a actual part of the level-

"Hall D is a hall that appears to be collapsed or closed off. Entry of this hall is not possible as it seems to be non-existent."

-Same thing here, I get it's collapsed but maybe we can peek through the rubble to see the other side? Seems like a after thought again-

"The Partypoopers stay in this level and frequently stay in the unlocked prison cells. They can be seen crying on the cot in a fetal position, because of mental trauma. upon eye contact, a Partypooper will quickly accelerate at speeds that exceed humanly possible, and rip you limp from limb. Some Partypoopers appear to have been infected with the Crawler Fungus."

-I would suggest to run this by other critique people since this is kinda gray for me area since party poopers have been extinct for a while and I don't wanna say wether this is good or bad but the concept seems good-

"Cryos Base Icarus is a small base consisting of 4 members who have barricaded themself into a kitchen in Hall E, and have been hiding away from a smiler who has trapped them in the room."

-This seems kinda redundant in my eyes since someone would have likely saved them by the time this article was published, its only one smiler after all, I suggest removing the part about the smiler and simply have it as a sort of research base-

"The majority of prison cells are locked."

-Can we see inside them? Is there anything visibly inside? Expand upon this as it could definitely be more interesting, or at least say the cells are pitch black so you can't see inside-

"Machines are the one constant in these rooms"

-You can expand upon this part by describing some machines found in prisons, this would make the workshops seem more detailed-

"upon eye contact, a Partypooper will quickly accelerate at speeds that exceed humanly possible, and rip you limp from limb."

-This may just be me but this seems oddly specific and dose not elaborate, why does eye contact cause them to go berserk?-

"There are no known Exits"

-Then how do we know about it?…-

Conclusion: It is a pretty good concept but suffers from being short and not very detailed at certain spots or does not elaborate on certain things that would have likely been discovered, I hope you take this crit into account and I look forward to your next version of this article.
