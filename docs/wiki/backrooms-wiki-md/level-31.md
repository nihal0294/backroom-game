# Level 31 - "The Roller Rink"

rating: +31[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

The Roller Rink  
The lights, the sounds, the smells… they draw you in… That feeling of familiarity, the yearn for something familiar. Within these dazzling, comforting lights. How much would you give up to stay in your comfort zone?

# **Description:**

Level 31 is a infinitely looping roller rink. The area most explorers end up in when entering this level is the actual roller rink area of the level, the arcade being rarer to enter. The roller rink section is the largest part of the level.

Muffled radio pop songs can be heard playing in the main area, and the DJ station at the south side of the rink can occasionally be seen having a docile [Faceling](entity-9.md) sitting inside selecting songs. Other sounds, such as skates hitting the rink's wooden floor and laughter, can be heard. The scent of cheap pizza and soda pop radiates from the direction facing the food court.

Entry 1  
Arrival  
When I arrived, all I could remember was the wooden door within the brick walls of a long-forgotten electrical station. Familiar music came through the barrier, something I've heard back home. Better than the numbing buzz of the lights I had experienced beforehand. I took no time to push the door open, arriving at what was a rolling paradise. A roller rink that was just like the one that was in my old town back before falling into this infinity. The lights and sounds were all the same, like I was back at home.

Maybe there is more of home around here. But for now, I'm tired, and I've roamed around for way too long. Hopefully, I can find a spot where the light isn't too bright and where the garbled noise from the arcade won't be too loud.

The main area of the roller rink is what one would expect from a more modern interpretation of a Frontrooms roller-skating entertainment building. With smooth wooden floors for the main skating section of the roller rink, set in a loop that curves around infinitely. The exits of the rolling area are separated by a short wooden gate, with flaps in said gate for wanderers to easily change from the smooth wood to the rough black carpet.

The rink is well-lit, with many colorful fluorescent lights lining the ceiling in various patterns, as well as the star-shaped light fixture at its center. Most of the time, the lights will be dimmed, just bright enough for the skaters to see where they are going.

![Picture of an arcade.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-31/Arcade-level31.jpg)

A portion of the arcade inside of **Level 31**.

# **The Arcade:**

Following the sounds of various arcade games from the eastward side will lead you to The Arcade. It resembles a modern arcade. Just like the main roller-skating section, it loops around back to the entrance at the north side of the arcade. All the games are free to play as long as you have a green card, which you can get from a device in the entrance of the arcade. The device is a simple, rectangular, metallic box with printed graphics that match the arcade's current aesthetic. Featuring the words "Please press the button to get a card" over a large blue button in the center, with a card catcher below. Once the button is pressed, it will give you a green card, which drops into the dispenser holder below the button. The arcade games in the arcade all resemble modern arcade games, such as Ski-Ball tables, big game wheels, air hockey, and many other modern game machines.

![Picture of a Fix It Felix Jr Arcade Game.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/level-31/Old-Arcade-level31.jpg)

An old game cabinet inside of **Level 31**.

Pinball games are also included among the previously mentioned machines, each resembling a pop culture movie, TV show, or video game prominent in the modern era. There are a few old 1980s-style box-like game cabinets still littered about in the arcade, such as Q\*bert, Donkey Kong, and Space Invaders, but those are few and far between.

Entry 2  
Discovering the Arcade  
Once I woke up, I noticed that my compass surprisingly still worked in this level. It was pointing me east, so I decided to follow its guidance. From there, I managed to find the arcade, the source of the noise I kept hearing besides the music. While it was in a smaller section compared to the rest of the place, it was just as nice. There were all sorts of games, from old arcade cabinets to even ones that I don't recognise. But one I did recognize was Fix-It Felix Jr., a game I used to play all the time at my local skating arcade. Even if I wasn't tall enough to reach the controls at times, I still liked to try to play it.

I played around with some of the arcade cabinets for a bit before I felt my stomach growl. I needed to get some food soon. I don't know when I last ate, but I know the smell of cooking meat and fries somewhere.

Just need to follow the trail.

# **The Food Court:**

Travelling towards the scent of pizza and soda in The Arcade will result in the food court appearing. The food court is a small section with a dining area, pizza, and soda that is safe to eat and drink, and a soda fountain dispensing almond water among various other [Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md) sodas. The menu at the food stop is rather limited: pizza slices with either cheese or pepperoni, chicken wings of varying spiciness, and foot-long hot dogs. The food court's sole entity is a [Faceling](entity-9.md) wearing an employee uniform with a blank name tag. This entity is docile and will serve food and drinks for free if asked. However, it will refuse to answer any questions about the Backrooms. The dining area of the food court is rather simple, with various square tables and orange plastic chairs scattered around the center. Orange and green metal park-like benches are screwed into the ground next to the walls, made for larger groups to eat. A condiment stand is centered next to the food desk queue. Before someone leaves the food court, there are trash cans next to the exit, with a taped sign above them on the wall that reads, "Please throw away trash and uneaten food here!" If any food or trash is left on the tables or the floor of the food court, the [Faceling](entity-9.md) behind the counter will begrudgingly clean it up and will refuse to give free food and drinks to whoever left it.

Entry 3  
Time to Eat!  
So there's a food court here. I knew something like this would be here with a roller rink, just like the one back at home. It had the usual cheap food selection most rinks had, especially the foot-long hot dogs and large sodas. I expected to have to trade some of the supplies I gathered for a bit of food, but the entity behind the counter simply gave me the hot dog and soda for free, without asking for anything in return. Free food is free food; I’ll take what I can get.

Sitting down at one of the few tables, I dug into the food. They weren't the best-tasting, but they were still better than nothing, yet familiar in a strangely welcoming way.

As I ate, I realized just how empty this place was. Yeah, there was that one faceless guy that was manning the DJ station and here at the food court, but that was about it; no one else was really here. It was strange; it felt off, despite how at home I felt here. The lack of other real living people kind of sat at the back of my mind. At least, that's what I thought, before another one of those strange, faceless people approached, speaking to me.

Behind the counter located in the Skate rental of **Level 31**.

# **Skate Rental & "The Coach":**

The skate rental is easily reached from the food court by heading away from the dining area toward an area with blue walls. The skate rental offers a variety of roller skate sizes and a counter with a US size chart. The rental is run by the native entity at this level, known as the Coach.

#### **"The Coach"**

The Coach is a [Faceling](entity-9.md) that is incredibly complex and intelligent, appearing as a faceless brown-haired man in a striped coach's uniform. He is friendly, very smart, and will offer a free roller-skating lesson. Accepting will lead him to give you a pair of skates in your size and explain how to live safely on this level. Declining will cause him to sadly sigh and ask you to come by if you ever want a lesson.

He is known for being able to hold various conversations with wanderers, from casual employee talk about asking for skating lessons to more emotional, in-depth conversations about someone's life experience. The Coach is very emotionally intelligent, able to understand human emotions and expressions, and able to act accordingly when helping said wanderers with whatever issues they may have in the rink. The Coach is also an advocate for helping wanderers push past their comfort zones, often asking them to do things they would otherwise say no to, while still being safe. They often push for the safety and well-being of the wanderers, wanting to improve their overall mental health above all else. He can even remember people's names, even long after they've left.

He can also lead you out of this level if asked. If you have stayed here for a prolonged time, the Coach will attempt to engage you in a conversation about not staying in one place too long. The Coach will then attempt to lead you to an exit and tell you that you're always welcome back.

Entry 4  
A New Friend.  
The being that approached me called themselves “The Coach,” or at least that's what their nametag said. Said he had noticed me entering the level some time ago but didn't really know when it was a good time to approach me, worried he might scare me off. I don't blame him; it's not that often you get to see a fairly welcoming and friendly entity in this hellscape. I probably would have been weirded out, too, after all the creatures I've seen before now. We talked a bit longer. I don't remember much of what we discussed, probably about our lives within these endless places, maybe our likes and dislikes, the usual first-time discussions. But I do recall him saying he offered some skating lessons, and I did take him up on it.

The rental section wasn’t that far from the food court either, just around the corner from the court. He gave me some skates to try on from behind the rental counter and helped me get out into the rink, even as I was struggling to stay steady.

It's been so long since I've been roller skating; the last time I remember going was when I was much younger, still struggling not to fall on my face, much like I am now. Tripping over myself, falling over, getting back up just to do it again. It was embarrassing, to say the least. I felt like a kid again, a frustrated, emotional kid.

But yet, Coach never got mad at me, nor did he rush me; he even came back whenever I had fallen down to pick me back up. Even when I couldn't understand what he was trying to tell or show me, he still helped me keep it together until I finally found a good rhythm and flow to keep steady. I was so happy, so proud of myself, that I nearly ran into one of the walls trying to go faster.

Coach told me he was proud of how fast I learned and that he could see me being a great skater one day. It was a bit silly; something so small made that feeling of being at home come flooding back, being that young child again, getting praised for the smallest achievements. It felt… Nice.

Although I couldn't enjoy that feeling for much longer, exhaustion was setting in from all the times I'd fallen over. Coach probably noticed this and directed me towards a place called the “Cinema”. He said it was one of the darkest places on the level, quieter too, minus the movies playing. So it would be best for me to go rest there before I leave.

So I did, but I didn't feel like leaving just yet. Hopefully, after a nice rest and a movie, I could explore more. I hope there's more than just this.

# **Cinema:**

Recently, a new section of the level has been found. By walking northward and finding TV screens, one will stumble upon the Cinema section. The Cinema section can play seemingly any movie, and it tends to play movies based on the viewer's favorite movies, or if they've never watched one, an age-appropriate movie. The cinema is small, with a large main television and three rows of benches in front of it, all sectioned in a small staircase fashion. Each bench is a single, continuous metal piece, all the same size and length. One can simply leave this section by travelling southward until one can no longer see the television.

Entry 5  
Something New.  
I never thought a roller rink would have a cinema. All the ones that I had been to back home were often just the rink itself, a small arcade, and maybe a food court, but never an actual theater. More or less, one that was so small. It was playing a movie when I got there; it was one of my favorites, All Dogs Go to Heaven, something I hadn't seen in a very long time. It was still in the first part, when the main character just got to heaven, and I knew the movie from start to finish, so I didn't miss much. I'm just happy to see something else so familiar and new.

But I knew I couldn't enjoy this much longer; my eyes were getting heavy, and I was having a hard time staying up. So I reluctantly decided to go over towards the corner of the room and just lie there. The sound of the movie replaying in my ears.

I just hope there's something new to do later.

# **Bases, Outposts, and Communities**

While it is possible to have communities in Level 31, the Coach discourages them, and as a result, no permanent communities have been formed. [The Venosha](the-venosha.md) have set up temporary camps in the level, however.

# **Entities:**

Most entities in this level are docile [Facelings](entity-9.md) who are either designated as employees for the roller rink or as lone customers wandering the looping rink.

The Facelings in this level have various tasks. One Faceling runs the food court, serving food and drinks and cleaning the tables and grounds as a regular human employee would. And another has been seen as a sort of music jockey for the roller rink, set inside the DJ station on the south side of the rink, where they select music to be played throughout the level. Other Facelings have been seen doing smaller tasks, such as playing the various arcade games, wandering around the rink, and even seemingly conversing in the food court and the Cinema.

Entry 6  
Why Isn't There More?  
I've been here what feels like forever, but according to my calendar has been a few weeks. I've played all the arcade games several times now, and while they're fun, I just want something new. I know that this place loops, as The Coach told me, but I wanted to try to find something anyway.

Maybe going back to the food court will be helpful. Try something else again.

# **Entrances and Exits:**

#### Entrances

Finding a wooden door in [Level 2](level-2.md) or [Level 3](level-3.md) that has music playing on the other side and going through will send one to this level. These doors are infrequent in appearance.  
Consuming one of the Least Favorite/Hated Pastries on [Level 67](level-67.md) may lead to this level.  
Touching a hyperlink leading to this level in [Level 210](level-210.md) will send one here.

#### Exits

No-clipping into any working game in the arcade portion will transport one to [Level 40](level-40.md).  
Finding a white door in this level with the number 70 engraved on it will take you to a building within [Level 70](level-70.md).  
Finding a door labeled "Employees only!" in the food court will send you to [Level 232](level-232.md).

Entry 7  
I don't really have much to say here. I guess just saying, "I hope for something new"? But I feel as if I've said that at nauseum already. But it's true. I've felt as if I've just been going through the motions again. Arcade, skating, food court, cinema, repeat.

Sometimes I go to Coach’s rental area to talk to him, but even then, it feels as if we are just reciting the same few topics and sentences over and over.

“How are you doing?”

“Have you beaten your high score on Fix-It Felix yet?”

“Do you want to try this new skating technique?”

Blah Blah Blah.

I like Coach, I really do. He’s felt like the first actual person I've met and talked to this whole time. Like an actual friend, someone who remembers my name just like I remember his. But even still, it's getting old. I want that feeling to stay, just like what I first felt all those weeks ago. But yet it's not coming back; it's only disappearing.

And I'm scared. Maybe a few more trips around will help.

It has to.

Entry 8  
I think Coach has noticed that I am not often coming around to his section. He met me in the arcade, asked if everything was okay and if something was wrong. I told him that I was fine, that I just needed to play more games, to do something to feel what I felt back when I first got here.

Guess he caught on that I slipped up. He asked what the feeling was, and I explained it. I don't want to rewrite it here. Familiarity and that sort.

He did bring up that maybe stepping outside the level would help get those feelings back and that some fresh air outside this place might help.

But would it really? I mean, there has to be a way to feel at home here again. There has to.

I thanked him, but I said I would be fine.

He made me promise to come back to his rental area again. He misses seeing me there and wants to resume lessons.

I said I would, but honestly, I don't think I will. The lessons don't do anything for me anymore.

I hope he understands.

Entry 9  
The Coach came by and talked to me.

He called my name as I sat in the cinema and asked me if I wanted to take any more skating lessons, since it's been a while since I've been away from the rink. I told him that I was fine, that he taught me all I needed to be a skating master. He said I could always learn more, but what else is there to learn? Last time I had lessons, he ended up reteaching me how to spin around without falling over. The same lesson he taught me some cycles ago.

He did say he wanted to talk to me, and I expected it to be about the same dialog choices from before, something I would have to just mindlessly talk through again. Instead, he asked me if I had met anyone else down here before I got to the roller rink. I didn't, or at least not personally. I've met a few agents of “M.E.G” but that was only fleeting and only for supplies before I left again. He then wanted to tell me that “connections are the most important thing you can make in this place. Friends, partners, families, any sort of connection is better than nothing, especially when you have been alone for as long as he has.”

He said he was sorry if his constant nagging and pushing me out of my comfort zone felt as if he was trying to push me away after all this time. He just doesn't want to see me suffer like the others he’s failed to help; he only wants to see me go home.

I wanted to ask what he meant by “others” and by “failed to help”. But yet, the null sense overtook my own curiosity. Maybe it's best I just focus on what I'm trying to do now; I need to feel at home again. I don't know what coach means by any of this. Yes, he’s a friend to me, but nothing about that is new or familiar.

I just need to keep going in here.

Entry 10  
Why do I feel like this?  
I don't know what else I'm supposed to be doing here. I've explored every inch of this place, eaten what I could from the food court, played every game in the arcade, watched the same movie on repeat in the cinema, and yet I don't feel safe anymore. This is the safest level I have been in for a long time. It felt like my old roller rink back before I fell down here. And now it doesn't feel right anymore. What is going on?

Maybe if I stay in here a bit longer, the feeling of home will come back? I just need to go back to the arcade and play some games again. Play that old Q\*bert cabinet again, like I've done before. It's always made me feel like I'm home again. It'll get rid of that nagging sense of unease.  
Right?

Entry 11  
Nothing is working. Nothing feels good anymore. I've tried to play those old games again, I've tried to eat my favorite foods here, but nothing is working. I've seen other people come and go from the rink; some had tried to talk to me, wanted to join me in playing games or watching movies. But that didn't feel right either. They don't know how much this place has meant to me; they don't know the hell I'm in now, they don't need to know. They are lost here, just like I am, but they don't want to stay; they don't feel the same way I do. So why bother with talking to anyone anymore?

It's not going to help me anyway.

Entry 12  
The Coach had been talking to me a lot more than they used to. Most of the time, he would just say hi to me when I passed by the Skate rental, and offer me those skating lessons he always had available. I tried those once out of boredom after the feelings started fading. It was a lot of fun, and I managed not to fall once I got the hang of it. But even that wasn't enough to bring the feelings back. He’s been finding me almost everywhere: in the rink, in the cinema, honestly, anywhere outside of the rental area.

He says hi, asks how I'm doing, all the usual small talk that other wanderers have with me when they see me. But with Coach, he wanted to show me something. I followed him, not having much of a choice, to what I assumed was a back area of the arcade, where many machines were turned off or covered with cloth. He showed me a white door with a 70 engraved on it. The Coach said he thinks it's time for me to move to the next level. That I've done a lot at this level, that it's time for me to go and spread my wings, something like that, I don't remember. I know there are doors that lead to different places, and those places will never feel like this one; it will never be as safe as the roller rink. I told him I'm not ready yet, that there's so much I want to do, so much I want to try and feel again. He still tried to push me to go, saying that he had seen me grow so much since I had been here and that he wanted to see my efforts not go to waste. I don't know if he truly meant what he said. But at this point.

I don't care.

Entry 13  
The Coach found me today as I was sitting in the food court again. He asked me how I was feeling, and I gave him the usual "I'm fine” answer I gave every time he asked. He doesn't need to know; I doubt they even want to know anyway.

He said he noticed I wasn't eating much anymore and that he was worried about me. I didn't need to; I wasn't hungry anyway. I just wanted to sit here, maybe try to feel that same comfort from the food court again, if it was even possible at this point.

He said there was more food behind the employee-only door and wanted me to see if maybe getting up and helping the employee make some food could improve my mental state. Coach reminded me that even if I don't get anything out of this, he would still be proud that I would try and that he would still do what he could to help me. But I've seen other people go in this door and never come out. It was an exit; another exit he was trying to push me through.

Why is he trying so much?

Why does he keep trying to get me to leave?

Doesn't he see that I just want to feel at home again?

That I want to feel safe?

Why is he trying to push me out of here?

Yes, I don't feel safe anymore, but I did, and I want that back more than anything! Why can't he see that?

I just told him I'll think about it and to leave me be. He reluctantly agreed, saying if I needed anything, I could always ask for help. Then he finally left.

Not like it matters.

Nothing does.

Entry 14  
He keeps talking to me, trying to get me to leave.

I don't care

I genuinely don't care.

I just want to feel safe again.

I want to be okay again.

Please let me be okay.

I just want.

To be okay.

Entry 15  
I don't care.

Entry 16  
W h y .

These entries have been retrieved by MEG operative Daniel on October 23. After Daniel had entered Level 31, they were approached by “The Coach,” who asked them for immediate assistance. Following the entity, Daniel arrived at the food court to find an unknown wanderer lying in the corner, unresponsive. “The Coach” had explained that the Wanderer, Jonathan, had been unresponsive to any attempt to communicate, get food or water, or even move from their spot. The entity also asked the operative if they could take the wanderer out of the level, in hopes that they would be okay once they were gone.

Daniel obliged, being able to pick up the unresponsive wanderer and the notebook of the entries listed above. As the operative left, “The Coach” thanked Daniel, telling them they are doing not only him a service, but the wanderer a great service as well, and hoped Jonathan a speedy recovery. Daniel noticed “The Coach” looking disheartened right before leaving the level.

Unfortunately, Jonathan had succumbed to dehydration long before MEG operative Daniel had entered the level.

Daniel theorized that [Liminal Echo](phenomenon-7.md) was to blame, but to this day, the cause is unknown. Whatever you do, listen to The Coach, and most importantly, do not stay too long.
