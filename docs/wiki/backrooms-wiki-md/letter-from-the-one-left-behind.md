I haven't forgotten you. Not for one day. Not ever.

It’s paid off. You're alive. You’re *alive*.

I found this database, and every word you've written is a beacon of hope. Every word is a breath of relief I take, a night of restful sleep, because I know you're alive.

This letter is a promise to you. For as long as I live, I won't stop looking for you. There's no barrier I won't tear down, no darkness I won't illuminate, and no star I won't snuff out to get to you again. I will find you.

Wait for me.
