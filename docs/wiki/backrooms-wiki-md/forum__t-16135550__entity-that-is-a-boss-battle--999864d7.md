Entity that is a boss battle

07 Jul 2023 00:09

So, as a sort of reference to Room 1000 in Spooky's Jumpscare Mansion, this entity will sort of be a boss battle. It is a red-colored humanoid that has omni-directional limbs and a detachable head. If you beat the entity, you get teleported to the Enigmatic Level [The Broken](the-broken.md). The entity is called the "DEADRED" and attracts nearby corpses so that it can absorb them. When you are nearby DEADRED, all other entities seem to "despawn", as well as other Wanderers. So that means that you are on your own with this gigantic skeletal monster which is so large that if the level has a ceiling, it breaks right through it.
