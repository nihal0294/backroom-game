# Description:

**Entity TH-1** appears as hand-shaped dents on various surfaces and is usually found in groups. When alone, it appears as only a single pair of hands. They can appear on almost any surface but will not be below shoulder height.

# Behaviour:

**Handprints** will move by fading and reappearing in the vicinity. They mostly move to dark areas to capture their prey easier. They will wait for a humanoid entity to touch them before engulfing them into their surface. If successful, they will multiply and form into larger swarms.

**Handprints** are not particularly dangerous entities. They do not chase and solely move to suitable surfaces[1](javascript:;) using instinct. They do not engulf inorganic material, and wearing gloves can prevent accidents involving them, which makes them a low-risk entity.

However, **Handprints** cannot be killed. Destruction of the wall does not affect them. They will move to the floor and then move back to their normal spot.

# Biology:

As mentioned above, **Entity TH-1** are the shallow dents on various surfaces, shaped like an adult human hand. The surface of these entities have a property similar to quicksand, making it difficult to pull out once in contact. Observations indicate that one **Handprint** will only have a single pair of hands, and this pair cannot be separated very far from each other.

Beyond this information, the biology of **Entity TH-1** is not well known.

# Do's and Don'ts:

### Do's:

- Wear gloves before entering any indoor levels.
- Try to avoid them.
- Pull your hand off if it gets captured by them.

### Don'ts:

- Lean or support yourself against walls.
- Walk into dark areas.
- Touch them directly.
