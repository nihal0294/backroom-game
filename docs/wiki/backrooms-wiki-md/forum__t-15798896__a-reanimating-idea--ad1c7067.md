this is a tough question, I am not the most knowledgeable when it comes to human nature, but I am interested in the topic:

first of all, there are already several infection-based entities, so we will have to assume that this zombie apocalypse changes how people survive in the backrooms. Ill be using base alpha as an example, Seeing that I can’t find any clear size or population of base alpha, ill make a few assumptions: it has about 1000 permanent people and is ~10 square miles (idk if that fits 1000 people but lets roll with it). The reason why governments collapse in zombie movies is because it is hard to control hundreds of millions of people across thousands of miles. Having small communities means you have better control over everyone to prevent chaos and collapse.

As for teaming up, i feel like that is a large possibility because in hard times like these can cause unlikely truces or alliances, though the opposite can be very true because you know what is good at uniting people: getting everyone to be against a common enemy. Also, maybe you need resources and your neighbor might have that resource and is not willing to hand it over. Or you know the good ol rising tension between communities cause not everyone likes to be allied with a bird cult.

As for levels, the only levels that would have lots of life would be ones with communities with some form of law or order. because the saying strength in numbers means something. As for levels with a centralized community but no order, those would fall quickly because again you need some force to keep everyone together and not scatter. Base alpha would have no problem keeping everyone together due to some form of order being present, while level 11 would mostly be a game of hidenseek to the infected because everyone scatters with no one to keep everyone together.

In the wiki's early days, a lot of the older entities, such as wretches, hounds, and partygoers, were able to turn you into another one of them. A surprising number of these entities did it via biting (partygoers, hounds, etc.). Wretches did it when you came into contact with the brown liquid that oozes off of them. There were plenty of others, but this just shows you how in the wiki's early days, an entity fell into one of two categories: Regular murder monster, or zombie-style converting murder monster. There are some other disease-type things like that as well that spread like crazy, so it makes me wonder if a zombie scenario played a big part in the inspiration for early entities, whether people were aware of it, or it was subconscious.

I want to shift the focus away from this, and since I own the page for wretches, and the rewrite permissions for hounds and partygoers, I want to make them something better. I'm at least working on partygoers, and both hounds and wretches will be rewritten by someone at some point to be better. That I want to make sure of.
