Hello, dear reader! I hope you've enjoyed this article.

[author's note](javascript:;)

[hide](javascript:;)

I understand and accept the Wiki Staff consensus that this page should be posted as a joke article. I recognize the many possible and real merits of doing so. I recognize that this article is tonally and conceptually lighter than most genuine articles. I recognize that a joke article may still be considered 'canon'.

Even so, as the author and creative arbiter of this article, I find that the 'joke' connotation may affect the perception of my article in ways I do not want. Hear me clearly. I do **NOT** want this article to be removed from the joke section — in fact, I am in favour of it *staying* here. It even fits thematically, to give them a kind of 'mythical' status. Even so, I'm writing this to hopefully change some of the unwanted perceptions you might have of this article.

Firstly — I hope, dear reader, that you realize that this was not written casually or carelessly. I had this carefully put through numerous rounds of critique (thank you especially to ArgonMighty), writing and rewriting sections again and again till I was satisfied. I was careful to ensure that (as far as I am aware) the article does not clash with lore given in other articles, to create an immersive experience. I even took the liberty of introducing footnotes to explain concepts that appeared contrived or contradictory. I hope that the effort put into this will be recognized as genuine, even if the article itself is not.

Next… creatively, I'm concerned that the consensus would be that the Society does not really exist in the Backrooms. In an environment as unpredictable and incomprehensible as the Backrooms, would it be so far-fetched for a group such as this to really exist? Absurd as they may be, I sincerely hope and strongly advocate for the Society to be accepted as a real GOI in the wiki's genuine canon (of course — the only ruly 'universal' canon to exist on the wiki being general consensus). **Even so, with my full blessing, this will remain a joke article, and you are free to perceive the Society however you wish.**

Finally, feel free to contact me at Kai4Christ#2063 on Discord if you would like to add an Incident Log to this page, or even to feature this GOI in a genuine article. It would be my pleasure.

Thank you very much for bearing with my creative concerns.

May my Lord Jesus Christ bless you.

Sincerely,  
Kai
