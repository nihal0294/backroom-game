Re: An level, or perhaps more than that

02 Dec 2024 00:37

The concept is very captivating to me- I love personalized levels, levels that make you question your sense of self and your place in the Backrooms (from the perspective of a wanderer, of course). The theme is also a great choice! I feel that the black and white give it an emptier look, which is perfect when your level is so focused on being personal to the person there. At first, I was going to say something about the use of the word "you" a lot, since for some reason I assumed it was a normal document about the level, but once the "me" was slipped in there (that's amazingly subtle by the way) I understood and it made the level even better. First correction though- you spelled "only authorized people" as "only autorized people". easy fix! second, I was wondering how you'd go straight ahead if there's only two doors, left and right. are they sitting in an outdoor space with no doorframe, like a park or the city, or are they in a room? would you be able to clip through the space in between the doors, and that's how you go straight ahead? I would try clarifying that part. also, in the "right" section, when it says "When I looked back, I saw something different on the other side, but strangely similar. " the word "familiar" would fit better than similar. similar implies that its being compared to something; and there are no direct comparisons to be made, as that is the first sentence of the paragraph. so really, you could go two ways (hah) with that, by either replacing similar with familiar, or lengthening the description to ADD something to compare it to, like saying its similar to a specific place, time, or event. if not, familiar would make it more vague- like you've seen it before, definitely, but not anything specific. kind of like deja vu. those are really my only peeves upon my first read. otherwise, the fade from "you" to "I" and "me" throughout the page is really well done, and I love how the description of wanting to feel normal and feel at home again when you you know you cant is written. honestly? Ive felt like that before and I'm not even in the backrooms. that's how you know the narrative is superb. the exit to level 0 is also a good choice, feeling like you're back at square one. once again something I've felt many times before!
