# A Small Place

You have made a great travel through areas that are both desolate and inhabited. Did you feel cold? Did you feel like your life is falling apart? Do you feel like you will never reach the final frontiers? It is common. Many have suffered losses, injuries and sufferings unbearable for a human being.

This all seems to be a psychological and physical test, to test your limits. Do you think you can even withstand the barrier? No one has been able to. The reason why many say goodbye to their friends and family is a tale that never ends. Arrival of peace in your body, a rare level.

Many gave you a warning, but you ignored it. You feel nostalgia, is it familiar? Very liminal, is it normal? Planet Earth is but a small world. A small place.
