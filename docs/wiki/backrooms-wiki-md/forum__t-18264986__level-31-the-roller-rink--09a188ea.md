-1 interesting story towards the end, but driven down by clinical

12 Aug 2026 15:43

The clinical feels very bland. It's a place that is weird with details and information that doesn't feel important. The repetitions, as well as the narrative bits cutting the clinical, makes it really tedious to get invested into. The different parts of the levels seems to be here not because of narrative significance, but due to a check-list of zones to add, which make them lack uniqueness.

The longer narration after the end of the clinical is more engaging and has an interesting message of trying to find refuge in nostalgia, but the rest of the page diluted that meaning with unnecessary clinical tone and description.

In the future, try putting the information in the story, instead of having description that would then be used in the narration afterward. This helps making the flow feel more natural, since every information would have a purpose as they're being mentionned, as well as making the story easier to get into.
