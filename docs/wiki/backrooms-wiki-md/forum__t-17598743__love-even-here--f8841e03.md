Usually I don't explain why I upvote content. I only really do that for downvotes *but* I did kinda say in the staff server that I would read the page and upvote it if I liked it to help it escape deletion. So here's my honest review of the page to prevent it from looking like I upvoted the page to save it from deletion to help one of my staff buddies out.

Firstly, it targets my weakness, people falling into the Backrooms. I love this trope, I know this is the BACKROOMS wiki, but I love seeing how people feel into the Backrooms. It makes it feel more natural almost and gives inherent backstory to the character(s). Good trope, new authors if you can, use it. Please.

Secondly, yeah I'm Aroace and irl don't give a shit about love. But I love me a cute little story. it does have some pacing issues I feel. Love at first sight is an over used trope, but it's cute. It's a cute lil' queer story. I love queer fiction since most companies either pretend we don't exist or pretend to care and do rainbow capitalism bullshit from Midnight June 1st to Midnight June 30th.

So yeah, reading cute queer stories from (I'm assuming real queer people. JTC I know for a fact is queer, not sure about Oreo) is a nice change of pace. +1 from me!
