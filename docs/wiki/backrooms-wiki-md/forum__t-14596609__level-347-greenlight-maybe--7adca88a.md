Obligatory not a Greenlighter, or a critiquer, really

This is an interesting concept (I actually recently made something similar) but there is a noticeable lack of clinical tone. For example, using emotion and the word, 'you'.

Additionally, I would try to add a few more details; not necessarily a story but things like: Do the shoppers or cashiers have any particular motives? How did they come into existence? Where does the human flesh come from? Is there some sort of meat factory? I found that asking myself these kinds of questions helped flesh out (pun intended) my ideas.
