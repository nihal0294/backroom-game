I'm not downvoting cause of the art itself, but for the absolute failure that this overall thing has ended up becoming. It's a nice idea in paper, but staff should have taken into account whether or not they could have kept up updating it. As I feel it this comes off as an attempt to make the site more interesting / polished, only for it to just be a lame bait in the end. So far staff explanations on the matter just sound like excuses at this point, there's no way any kind of issues that may have arisen on the first one-two months haven't had a chance to be resolved yet, 6 months later.

I'm very disappointed. Though I'm open to switch back to a novote if this is actually picked up again, I don't think I can ever upvote this at all. I just wish our site was a bit more professional, Backrooms deserve it.
