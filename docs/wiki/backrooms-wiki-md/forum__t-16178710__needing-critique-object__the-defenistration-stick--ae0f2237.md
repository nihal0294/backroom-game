Ha! Love the name. Like, I love the name an unhealthy amount. Such a fun word.

As for the concept, Just stick to one idea. There's a few things bubbling around right now:

1. Sending someone to another level  
2. Taming deathmoths  
3. It's connected to Object 29

Frankly, 2 and 3 are the least interesting aspects of the object. They feel like they were just tacked on. I would focus on the first aspect: the fact that this stick, when it hits an entity or person, forcibly no-clips them to another level.

It's a rather simple idea, but think about the implications. What could people do with a stick that forcibly no-clips someone? No-clipping is dangerous, and being forcibly no-clipped is even *more* dangerous. Being transported to a random level without sufficient preparations could be a death sentence. These are hooks that interest me, but you'll have to come up with your own.
