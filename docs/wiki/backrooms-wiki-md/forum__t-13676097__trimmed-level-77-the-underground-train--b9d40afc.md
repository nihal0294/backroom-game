I wish you were to wait a bit for me to come back, because I wasn't able to mention a few critical errors that you could've fixed easily. For the sake of this being a final draft, I'll only point out a few examples:

[And those are...](javascript:;)

[– hide block](javascript:;)

> The reason for this display has been hypothesized to be due to the sign not having a secure connection.

This sentence, clinically, is written incorrect. Reading it aloud will show reasoning.

> Periodically, the exit door will open on each car simultaneously.

This sentence feels… off… and could use some rewording

> Within Level 77's sections, a different kind of Entity is present.

I know what you were trying to say here, but it sounds wrong. Suggest: Within every colored section of Level 77, a different type of Entity is present.

> On Level 72, you must go within a train that is abnormally long. This train is Level 77, and it comes out from an underground tunnel periodically

These sentences are off for entrances. They are also clinically incorrect.

---

Stated before, I don't go in depth-crit on final works. These are lighter examples.

I enjoyed the train concept a lot, it was well done, and honestly deserves recognition. The theming of writing is inconsistent (this happens commonly with rewrites), and the times in which the theme is consistent, your clinical tone still isn't present. My vote is heavily weighed by the clinical tone, but as this stands, it's gonna be a downvote from me.
