SC

Good afternoon M.E.G. Reports,

The project is on schedule and is making steady progress, but the main reason I am filing this report is because of the circumstances surrounding the Backrooms Remodeling Co. I'd like to start by saying that the B.R.C. employees do not listen to me at all; I've just become a glorified babysitter with a clipboard. I know that the employees are known for their lack of awareness, but I assumed that I would at least be able to converse with them.

Any time I try to tell them what to do or, God forbid, try to just talk to them, they just hand me a business card and walk away. The second complaint I have is that it is very hard to keep other M.E.G. members and the volunteers out of their way. Even with all of the precautions set up, someone always manages to get hurt. In the last week alone, I've had three people get run over by excavators, four people get trampled by BRC employees, and a person get a pallet of concrete dropped on them. There has to be a way to deal with them or a way to just replace them with volunteers or M.E.G. members. It would be greatly appreciated if anyone could advise me on this.

Sincerely,  
**Sam Cuttler**  
Foreman Of The Dam Reconstruction Project

## **Notice**

---

After the email labeled "[Blank]" was sent, the person of interest, Sam Cuttler, went missing along with 3 boxes of BRC explosives. The letter below is presumed to be the last piece of writing Sam made before he disappeared. The letter was found on a table in one of the Backrooms Remodeling Co. conexes on February 4th, 2025, surrounded by 4 mangled bodies of B.R.C. employees. The cause of death of the employees was found to be blunt force trauma. This was further proven when a shovel with the DNA of the B.R.C. employees was found under the aforementioned conex.

  
  

![A handwritten note on a manila envelope with a lamp shining on it.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-595/Sam%20Cuttler%20Note)

The note, now stored in The [Base Alpha](base-alpha.md) Archives

### **Transcript**

---

Destruction is life. To destroy is to make anew. If nothing is destroyed, nothing changes and everything stagnates. Stagnation opposes living. Stopping destruction is foolish and pointless because to stop destruction is to stop life. So why do we try to patch up something that is meant to fall apart? When natural disasters happen, we don't try to stop them because they are natural. The same goes for this damned dam; its natural progression is to decay. Let it. Let the blue flow into the blue. Let the other levels be quenched. Let it become an infinite waterfall, what it was always meant to be. The flow must be unbroken and wild as it travels this infinite labyrinth.  
This breathtaking plain.  
This endless abyss.  
This wondrous expanse.

-Sam Cuttler
