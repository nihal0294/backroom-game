I mean I critted it so makes sense.

I've been saying this for a while now, but I think the Pantheon is at its best when it uses characters to serve a mythology, inspired by real beliefs set up within the Backrooms setting.

Here, I believe the page is a great example of how character story and interactions with the wider universe can serve to hint at something greater than itself. The tale is told through the gods, beings who don't understand mankind, which allows us to interpret the actions like you'd expect from an abstract legend, while still trying something different.

The page gives context to Argos and Philia's current mindsets and actions, while still being ethereal enough for it to feel like something old yet significant. It could be seen as mankind first sin in the Backrooms, after all.

Overall an enjoyable read and I hope to be able to use it as a reference point for future Lost works. Keep it up friend!
