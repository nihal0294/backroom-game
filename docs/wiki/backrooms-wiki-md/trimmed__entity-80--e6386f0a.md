**Entity 80: ”The Hen”**

### Habitat(s): [Level 1](level-1.md), [Level 8](level-8.md) [level-147](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/trimmed:level-147)

### Description:

**Hens** are bird-like creatures somewhat resembling normal chickens that are bred for their meat and eggs on the stage. They are not dangerous if you do not approach them or touch their eggs. **Hens** live exclusively on the **[level-147](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/trimmed:level-147)**. The **Hens** eggs are very valuable due to them being nutritious and also due to the possibility to grow them domestically.

### Behaviors:

They are very protective of their eggs and will not hesitate attacking anyone even if the perpetrator is much stronger and bigger than them. **Hens** are known to organize themselves in packs of five or more it becomes extremely dangerous to engage the **Hens** because four of them can and will grab you by the limbs and the fifth one will bite through your neck. The **Hens** have been observed to be extremely aggressive with the **[Clumps](entity-5.md)** going as far as tearing them apart and then consuming them.

### Biology:

The biggest and most noticeable difference from a normal chicken is that the **Hen** has a beak that can open up to 160° revealing a mouth filled with razor sharp teeth placed in rows going around the mouth. The throat is separated from the beak by a membrane opening when the **Hen** is swallowing their food. They are also known to be completely blind but have keen hearing and can hear the slightest disturbances around them. They appear to be communicating using echolocation producing a sound resembling clucking followed by a series of clicking. The **Hens** can be tamed by using the almond water, as they are very attracted to almonds and almond water. They are also hermaphrodites which was discovered after doctor Keshbekov's autopsy of a tamed **Hen**.

### Discovery:

The First sightings of **Hens** were recorded by a small group of **[The Lost](the-lost.md)** passing through the **[level-147](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/trimmed:level-147)**. The official discovery of the **Hens** was made by **[M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md)** explorers.

### **Do's and Don'ts:**

### **Do:**

- If they become aggressive, spill **[Almond Water](object-1.md)** near them.
- If you see a pack of more than 5 hens retreat slowly producing the least amount of sound possible

### **Don't:**

- In any circumstance break their eggs
- Try to pet/touch the hens even if they appear passive and calm
