Level 219 is the 219th Level of the Backrooms, and it's one of the safest backrooms level you can encounter.

# **Description**

Level 219 appears to be a single room that resembles a high school gym. The gym itself is decorated with prom decorations, such as banners with words like "prom night" or "high-school prom". There are also tables of food and drinks that you'll normally see in a normal prom in the front rooms scattered around the level. There are no doors or windows and the lights are set at a dim setting. Romantic music is head playing from an unknown source.

Determining on the couple, level n+1th can appear different sizes. The smallest level n+1 can be is 50ft by 84ft, while the largest is 110ft by 60ft.

Level 219 is completely devoid of entities except for facelings, who inhabit the level. The facelings are usually in a trance like state as they are commonly found slow dancing in pairs out on the gym floor. Wanders can't break up the pairs the facelings are in, nor can anything else.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There are no known bases, outposts or communities on this level.

# **Wanderer Count**

Only two wanderers at once can enter level n+1 from any level. The wanders don't even have to be on the same level to get in. For example, one wanderer could be on level 4 while the other could be on level 0. They could noclip at the same time and end up on level 219. The wanderers who noclip into the level will feel some romantic attraction towards each other, and have reported feeling the same connection after exiting the level. Even couples wandering the backrooms together can enter this level.

# **Entrances And Exits**

#### Entrances

Two random wanderers can noclip into level 219 seemingly at random. The wanderers might know each other, or they might not.

#### Exits

The only way to exit this level is for the wanderers to dance with each other to the music. After some time of dancing, the wanderers will eventually noclip to a random safe level together.
