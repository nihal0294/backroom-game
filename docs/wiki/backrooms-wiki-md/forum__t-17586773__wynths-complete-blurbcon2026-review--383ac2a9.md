Critters:

So, this was a lot of fun to write!

I admit, when I voted for the entries the first time I barely glanced at most of them. After all, the average person doesn't[1](javascript:;) have the requisite time to go through all of them and give them the fair shake-down that they deserve.

That's why I've created this page! ^^

I know the feeling of not getting recognition for your work, and as someone who *does* have the time to go through all BlurbsTM and give them at least some crit, I felt that it would be a fun idea to go through and write a review for the entire contest.

Also, I must give credit to , as their page [Fish's SCP Wiki Themes Complete Tier List](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/fish-s-scp-wiki-themes-complete-tier-list) was a MAJOR inspiration. Plus, with their page ranking over *200* elements, it really gave me the encouragement I needed to complete my mere 40 :P

Hope y'all have a good day — see y'all when March rolls around![2](javascript:;)

As you can see by the tag, it's an essay. This page examines and analyzes why Wynths thinks certain blurbs for the contest should win, or if the blurb shouldn't win, why it's a good/bad piece of writing.

Essays are meant to showcase a particular viewpoint and analysis. They are, at the end of the day, opinions, meant to help bring a different form of thinking to Backrooms writing and meant to help you think. Of course, you don't have to agree with the opinions put here. But at the end of the day, I think this essay is a fun bit of writing that helps readers understand how Wynths thinks and can show the blurbs in a new light.
