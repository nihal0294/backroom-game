Ah, another day…

Another load of problems.

Memories.

Liminality.

It’s the simple things that make me smile.

Just smell the roses, and all will be well.

That’s what they all told me to do, at least.

Don’t worry. It’s only temporary.

That’s what they keep telling me.

But is it true?

…

Wake up and smell the pain.
