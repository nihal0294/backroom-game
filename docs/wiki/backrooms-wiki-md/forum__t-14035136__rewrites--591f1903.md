Re: Rewrites

25 Mar 2021 21:44

The current rewrite guidelines is basically if you get permission to rewrite something, you can essentially do whatever you want with it. As for getting permission, here are the possible scenerios:

- An author is listed, and it doesn't fit into any of the below categories.

In this case, contact the author. A Wikidot pm should work, and ask them for permission to rewrite. If they say "yes", go for it. If they say "no", you're out of luck.

- The article has a negative rating

In this case, just ask staff, and we'll probably let you no matter what unless someone else wants to rewrite it since it would be deleted soon anyways in that situation.

- The author doesn't respond to a pm, is a banned user, or is listed in the author collapsible as a "Fandom User"

In this case, just ask a staff member for permission. We'll probably give you permission if you already have some content on the site that shows you can write, or just show us a promising draft.

It should be noted that rewrites still have to go through the greenlighting process, so read the [Greenlight Policy](greenlight-policy.md) if you haven't already (ignore the "Idea Greenlight" part, we've been meaning to remove that section)
