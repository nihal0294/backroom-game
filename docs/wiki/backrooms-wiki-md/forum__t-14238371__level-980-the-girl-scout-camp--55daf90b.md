> There is a vast assortment of Girl Scout tents pitched in the area all colored a bright yellow.

Comma after "area"

> The Girl Scouts never appear to age; they are friendly and have been known to help wanderers.

The Girl Scouts should probably have a whole paragraph dedicated to them in the description

> This room is infinite and contains luxury furniture and childrens' things.

This should also have a few more sentences describing it because right now the description leaves a little to be desired.

> However, such properties come at a cost sometimes causing the person great pain

Comma after "sometimes"

> or the sacrifice of another person.  
> There is a girl scout food and drink

You need to press enter twice between paragraphs

> There is a girl scout food and drink product in each area with a girl scout symbol cookies; most of the products are of an unknown brand.

If the brand is unknown, probably don't describe them as "girl scout products", or at the very least, just describe them as similar to frontrooms girl scout products

> the super almond water can be found on in each area.

This is kind of a big thing to introduce and do nothing with - I'd recommend just not including this sentence

> The field is comprised fully of fruits and vegetables. Fruit trees are also present in this area in large quantities. One such fruit tree on the farm has yet to be identified. The farm contains many varieties of animals. There have been rumors of an unknown dinosaur appearing in the area.

This whole paragraph is made up of really short sentences which make it feel kind of choppy. I'd recommend combing some of them, like: "The field is comprised fully of fruits and vegetables, with fruit trees also being present in this area in large quantities."

> Burner Chicken is a chicken-like entity that is known to constantly be on fire

is known to constantly be on fire -> is constantly on fire [kind of wordy]

> on fire, it is not hostile to the Wanderer.

Comma splice - replace the comma with a period, semi-colon or em-dash

> When the Wanderers kill a Burner Chicken, it will turn into grilled chicken safe for eating.

When the Wanderers -> When Wanderers [sounds more natural]

> Red Bull

This entity is kind of boring, maybe add a couple more details to it.

> with electric shock on its coat.

This is worded kind of strangely

> If the Wanderers touches the entity

the Wanderers -> a Wanderer

> then they get electrocuted and death soon follows.

Remove the "then"  
Comma after "electrocuted"

> if wanderers throw water at the entity, then they will explode instantly.

wanderers -> Wanderers  
remove the "then"

> Dr. Rexorus Tyrant

This one should probably be mentioned in the description too since it's kind of important  
Also maybe expand this paragraph a little bit

> The Origin of the Girl Scouts

This is cool. I like it, but it could use a little framing. Like, maybe say that this is information told to the M.E.G. by the Girl Scouts. You could also probably expand on this part a bit. I think this should really be like the "meat" of the level, so if possible, I think you should expand the story by at least a couple more paragraphs, or like turn it into an interview log or something

> turning all female children into humanoids

idk what you mean by this
