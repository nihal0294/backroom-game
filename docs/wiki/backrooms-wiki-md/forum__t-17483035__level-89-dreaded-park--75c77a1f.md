Not quite there -1

09 Dec 2025 06:13

Overall, you have a good grasp on tension, which is something that a lot of new authors struggle with.

The reason why I downvoted it was because the characterization felt… off. Bryon didn't really feel like an 11-year-old, and the characters made dumb decisions because it felt like the plot *needed* them to rather than it making sense as part of their character. Like why would Megan split up when she knows that's a bad idea and acknowledges it as a bad idea? Also, how the hell did the person who pulled a gun on someone in custody not get reprimanded? That's absolutely insane and escalates the situation *incredibly* fast.

I think this article would have been much better had it stuck to just Bryon's POV, at least in the beginning. Portraying the M.E.G. from the eyes of a child who has STRANGER DANGER ingrained in them would be a unique and interesting twist, especially with the idea that *somehow, they are watching him write.* Honestly, that was truly terrifying, so I really want to see you push that idea.

Overall, a good try! If it's deleted, good luck with the next draft.
