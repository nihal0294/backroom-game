You have to go find a greenlighter yourself and wait for them to like greenlight you or not greenlight you.  
Idk about average time, depends on the person. You could check the guide hub under greenlight policy, there's a list of greenlighters, it also shows if they're currently accepting  
ideas critique is running your idea past other ppl, draft critique is for critting drafts that are finished but not posted yet.  
Critique I think is completely up to you, it's not like a forced thing, you could choose to not get any crit and go directly to a greenlighter, but it would be best to get crit first if you want your entry to be accepted better by the community.

Answers are not in order and I am not a veteran, if there's anything I got wrong feel free to correct me
