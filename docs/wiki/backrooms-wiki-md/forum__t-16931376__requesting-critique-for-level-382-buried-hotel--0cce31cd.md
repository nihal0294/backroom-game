I think you did a good job on nailing the clinical tone, it's a very well-described Level and definitely very visually interesting. I do feel there's some possible room for expansion if you wanted to deepen the world a little bit. A few questions come up for me, namely: what do the Hazmats do when visitors aren't around? They seem friendly, which implies to me that they like living on this Level for some reason. Are they working on something? Are they just a gang of buddies who like chilling around radiation? It's not something that needs a ton of detail, I'd just like a bit more of a glimpse into their world. I also think it might be worth mentioning which specific entities manifest in the lower floors. Overall though I like the idea of a hotel buried in stone, it feels very dreamy - nice draft. (:

Re: Requesting Critique for Level 382 - Buried Hotel

01 Oct 2024 23:30

Nice- I really like what you've done here. The idea is visceral- all those rooms enclosed by stone and imagining the weight of it above you, possibly being trapped inside a solid mass… it feels claustrophibic and makes for a distinct atmosphere in the level. You've given the hotel idea a unique feeling and a unique set of challenges for visitors to overcome.

Now some points:

I second adding more information on the Hazmats. There’s a story there- 5 people surviving on a radioactive level? I want to know more about them- why they chose to live there, what it’s like for them, survival advice they have, etc. I feel like this is a rich opportunity to add some interview material, to hear from these people in their own words. Especially since they’re friendly- maybe they have things they want to share about the level? At the very least, maybe the paragraph about the Hazmats should have more information about where they live in the level- a specific room? Are they nomadic?- because if a group is friendly the M.E.G would probably try to provide information on how to find them.

Another point about the radioactive floors- I like the increasing danger of heading deeper into the hotel, but it almost seems like a moot hazard because the dangerous rooms are marked, and any travller who reads this article will know that they shouldn't go deeper than Floor 57. In other words- the danger is there, but it's completely possible to avoid, so it almost feels pointless from a plot/tension point of view. I would add something in the lower levels to make it more tempting to go down there- so Wanderers have a risky choice to make. Maybe the Front Entrance Exit could lead to a safe, well-populated level, instead of the Caves? That way people might be tempted to risk the radiation for guaranteed passage back to safety… You could have testament from a wanderer about enduring the radiation and falling sick in their desparate attempt to reach the exit…  
Same with the stone hallways. Maybe there's something in there, valuable mineral like Firesalt perhaps? A reason people might enter the halls and get lost…

Also, I feel like I want the stone to have more presence, since it's one of the characteristic features of the level. What about the radiation being caused by a substance in the bedrock- maybe radon? That's a real thing- homes with bedrock basements are at higher risk of natually-occuring radon exposure. You could do something like this, pumped up to Backrooms-level intensity. Basing a radiation source on a real-life phenomenon would be nice touch and put the level even more in the uncanny valley. Another idea- maybe the stone could become more visible the deeper you go? Maybe walls start to crumble in and the stone is exposed? Maybe hotel features start to be made of stone? Just some thoughts.

On another note, some SPaG suggestions:

“These almost always resemble hotel rooms; they can very rarely also resemble rooms from other buildings, such as office buildings or hospitals” Use semicolon here.

"Notably, these rooms will often appear lived in or in a state of use…"  
No new paragraph. This is still more information about the previous idea, so it doesn’t need a line break.

“These rooms are to be avoided and have usually been marked by other wanderers in the level.”

“All wanderers start on floor 100… though they quickly die due to the radiation.” I would re-write this section and change the ordering of the information. Also, I think it can be combined with the previous paragraph.

“These abnormal areas are areas within the level that display abnormal qualities compared to other areas in the level.”  
Suggested wording: "These are areas which possess anomalous qualities compared to the rest of the level." It's already stated that these areas are abnormal in the heading, so there's no need to state it again in the body text.

“Upon entry of the level, all wanderers will wake up in a random place within an area named the Entry Zone. This zone has few irradiated rooms and is located on the highest floor of the level. No entities appear here.”  
Suggested wording: "Upon entering the level, wanderers fall asleep, and wake up in a random place within an area named the “Entry Zone”. This zone has few irradiated rooms and is located on Floor *\_*, the highest of the level. No entities appear here."  
I would mention that entering this level induces sleep, since that's uncommon. Also, I would give this floor a number, since floor numbers are mentioned in an earlier paragraph. If this is the highest level, people should know what number that is.

“This area is located on Floor 1and is the only non-irradiated area on that floor. The area resembles a hotel lobby and is entirely empty of all furniture and decoration except for the front desk. Leaving through the doors located in this area will take you to Level 8.”  
- Be consistent about using digits for the floor numbers- that will make it easier to read. Also I think it makes sense to capitalize the Floors.

“This area is located on Floor 12. It is a collection of hallways that resemble the ones normally found in Level 382, but entirely comprised of bedrock. No rooms appear here. These hallways are all dead ends, but due to the winding nature of them it is very easy to get lost. Due to this, it is not recommended to enter this area."  
Repeated phrase- not necessary.

“Going to Floor 1 and leaving through the Front Entrance will take you to Level 8, though this is dangerous due to the radiation and should not be attempted."  
Capitalize Front Entrance, since this is a distinct area and feature of the level

“Finding a staircase going up on Floor 100 will take you to Level 11.” [[span style="color:red"]]Capitalize Floor (This last inline keeps breaking, for some reason.)

Anyways, if you got through all my rambling and picking- I like it! I'd maybe want a little more material/story, but regardless the level has a good feel and a distinct personality. And for me the tone is spot-on. Cheers!
