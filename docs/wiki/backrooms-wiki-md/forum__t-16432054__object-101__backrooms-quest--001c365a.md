Object 101: “Backrooms Quest”

30 Oct 2023 16:20

**Page Type:** Object

**Core Concept:** Anybody remember those old 'choose your path' books, The ones where it would be something along the lines of:

> *You come across a crossroads. To the north you can here a distant scraping sound. To the east water can be heard far off, it drips at a slow, continuous pace. The hall leading west is well lit and short, a dilapidated wooden door sits closed at the end.*  
>   
> *If you want to head NORTH turn to page 36*  
> *If you want to head EAST turn to page 38*  
> *If you want to head WEST turn to page 35*  
> *If you wish to head back SOUTH turn to page 21*

If you don't remember you're missing out; that, however, besides the point. I thought it would be cool to have one of these books as an object that you can actively play through on the wiki. Using the backrooms as a concept would be perfect, it has the same maze like dungeons, deadly entities and a range of objects going from being the cure to everything to a liquid that makes everything completely silent!

**Additional Notes:** I understand it would be hard to write up and would often become outdated due to new levels being green lit, or older levels being rewritten or deleted entirely. I recon its possible to find a way to work around this, whether it be an update every six months to a year or giving permission for the author of whatever new page to add it into the book. I personally see it as a fun little idea and quite unique as I am yet to see anything similar on the site.

**I want to hear you're feedback on this and if you think its a good idea I will work on it as my next project.** If you genuinely have never heard of these books, Ian Livingstone is a great introduction and a big writer in this space. You should definitely check him out if you want to know more!

That's about it, look forward to hearing the feedback!!!

Also I wouldn’t mind a better name. Thanks in advance!!
