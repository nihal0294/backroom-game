A group of lost Herberts.

### Description:

Instances of Entity 113 look like wanderers who wear Hawaiian shirts and call themselves "Herbert". To distinguish Herberts from wanderers with poor fashion sense, examine their eyes since Herbert irises look like Frontrooms globes.

After spontaneously appearing, Herberts seek out wanderers with their map and ask for directions. Once asked, you won't be able to move until you assist Herb-

***"Excuse me! Could you give me directions? I can't find an exit anywhere!"***
