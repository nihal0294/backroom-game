**Page Type:** Entity

**Genre (Optional):** Supernatural, some Comedy/Humor?

**Elevator Pitch:** A seemingly normal cat with some not so normal psychic abilities- though, she’s a bit lazy, and I think that adds a bit of charm/humor to her.

**Central Narrative:** There is a vague connection between Samantha and a Traveler character that I would like to expand on later in future Tales/Logs/etc. but for now, it’s just this page!

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** She’s extremely powerful and could be used to the M.E.G’s advantage; she just has a lot of potential as a concept and I believe she could definitely be featured in certain narratives/storylines.

**Additional Notes:** I have all of it written in Sandbox right now, and I wanna see what people think. English isn’t my first language, so I tend to misuse punctuations or be rather verbose in my writing at times. Any and all critique is welcome!
