Sky, you went on a writing hiatus for half a year and all you could muster up was 333 words?

Yes. In fact, I literally wrote this in a span of an hour yesterday.

This page is, of course, a reference to Fandom’s Level 9223372036854775807, more known as the “True End” in some instances. It was this overtly grandiose level that would strike fear and awe via its description alone. It was so incomprehensible that your brain would rot after mere seconds of experiencing the level. It was so significant and so famous in the Backrooms discussions.

Needless to say, I did the exact opposite.

It’s just a staircase. Nothing more. Subversion of expectations. I am fully aware that this will be anti-climactic and controversial, but I just felt like breaking my writing drought—writing stories because I want to write them and not specifically to appeal to anyone else.

Storytelling is quite a powerful tool. It shapes objects based on our perception of some events. Use it.
