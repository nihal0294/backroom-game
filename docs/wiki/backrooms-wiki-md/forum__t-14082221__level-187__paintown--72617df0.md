Level -187: Paintown

03 May 2021 01:38

**Page Type:** Level

**Elevator Pitch:** Level -187 takes place in large suburban area, much like Level 9. However, there are a lot of differences - The sky in this level is a deep red, much like liquid pain. Speaking of liquid pain, there is a lot of liquid pain present on this Level. It can be found in lakes, tubs, sinks, even the water supply on this Level is made entirely of Liquid Pain, meaning that that showers and sinks will spill liquid pain instead of water. Also this level is really hot - the temperature stays at constant 56 degrees C (132.8 degrees F).

All the houses on this Level are locked but can still be broken into. This is not recommended however, as there will be hostile Facelings and Hounds (and the occasional Skinstealer and Smiler) filling the role of the family which will attack on sight. Occasionally if you survive these attacks, if you go to the bathroom, there could be a skeleton laying in a pool of liquid pain in the bath tub. These skeletons could possibly be the wanderers that went insane on this level and took a bath on the liquid pain and died.

Not only are there Hostile entities in the houses, there are also hostile entities on the streets of Level -187. There are Facelings dressed in gang outfits and will usually have some sort of weapon on them. They will also attack/shoot any wanderers on sight. There are also instances of Smilers, The Neighborhood Watch and Mangled on the streets of the level, but they much more common then on Level 9.

Additionally, there is an Entity native to this Level, It is called the Bandit. It measures 70 feet in height. It is a humanoid shadow-like figure and has a bandana over where its mouth should be. If it finds any Wanderers, it will chase them relentlessly and step on them. It runs really slow, so there is time to run.

Usually every few days, it will rain liquid pain on to the ground. DON'T TRY TO DRINK THE RAIN. It will result in headache, skull fractures and eventually, loss of function of the limbs.

Overall, this Level is real dangerous and difficult to survive.

**Central Narrative:** A group of wanderers found a shack on Level 9. The shack was red in colour and looked abandoned. They entered the shack and the environment started to morph into a red hellish suburb. They gave the level designation -187 because 187 is a sign of killing and torture and the dangerous nature of the Negative Levels, which pretty much sums this Level up.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** The fact that it rains Liquid Pain on this Level. This rain can completely paralyse you on the spot. Also the fact that all the drinkable items on this level is replaced with liquid pain. I don't any other Level has these features.

**Additional Notes:** This took more than an hour to write. really appreciate that this gets the greenlight

O would highly recommend removing anything in regards to sanity, as it's a concept that is being phased out. In general, no Greenlighter would greenlight this with sanity remaining. There are a lot of things going on with this level, I think this could be narrowed down a bit more.

Don't spell everything out, leave some parts up for interpretation.

I think what could work for this level is the level being an abandoned town that seemingly was destroyed by a weather phenomenon of liquid pain.

The entities, I would recommend sticking to non-corpeal entities personally. Why? For me, it wouldn't make sense that liquid pain would only affect humans. Since Smilers are basically shadows, they could bypass this.
