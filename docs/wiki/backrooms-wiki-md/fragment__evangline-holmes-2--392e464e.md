## **Description:**

Evangeline Holmes is a 24-year-old woman of Filipino descent. She was previously a member of the M.E.G., having first joined 5 years ago alongside her late adoptive father, Lorenzo Holmes, and late twin sister, Rosaline Holmes. She was the former Junior Archivist of her father's now-departed team, Team Veracity Searchers.

Holmes was last seen in her travel clothes, which consisted of slightly ragged, beige pants, a black turtleneck, a red windbreaker, black gloves, and black boots. She was also seen with a large, red [mask](object-24.md), the top of which was sprawling in golden feathers. She has brown eyes, shoulder-length black hair, and warm, ivory skin. Her left hand is prosthetic—a limb lost prior to experimentation.

On October 26th, 2020, her father would lead the [Object 24](object-24.md) experiment—an experiment conducted with the goal of controlling the effects of the Wall Masks—which she herself would be a part of. Three months later, due to neglect and delirium, she would escape. After the events of March 15, Holmes escaped the facility along with a group of other escapees after killing her sister, eventually creating and leading the group known as the [Masked Maidens](the-masked-maidens.md).

The Masked Maidens are ruthless, going as far as to infiltrate our facilities and kidnap our most capable agents under the guise of "removing the corrupted." Though the Maidens are often seen exploring the first ten Levels, their abilities to escape have made it difficult to track them down.

If seen, it is imperative that she is captured immediately and brought back to [Base Alpha](level-1.md). From there, we will be able to take great leaps in bringing down the Maidens. However, one must take caution in pursuing her, as she is capable of harming others—an effect of the mask itself.
