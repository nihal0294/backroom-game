Level idea for 1000 “ Nexus Point”

22 Oct 2022 17:23

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre (Optional):** bittersweet

**Core Concept:** this level is filled with cubed shaped room’s with space in between them, the rooms them selves are completely lit by an unknown source and the hallways between rooms are dark with the rooms being the only light source.

Each room is about 5 by 5 meters and will have a single door facing at a random duration, the type of door also being random from normal to sliding to no door at all and above the doorway with an inscription that reads “Universal Coordinates” and then a bunch of numbers after that.

The level is going to have some entities roaming around due to them no-clipping into the level from above.

The only way into this level is by going through Level 999 which you’ll wake up in a room of your Universe or by no-clipping back here from level 1001-1999.

The only way out is by no-clipping in the hallway which would lead you to level 1001 or by no-clipping in the room leading you to the cluster 1 levels though if you want to still find your way back home it is advised to use the some room that you came out of.

**Additional Notes:** The reason why I wanted this as a level 1000 is because if we filled up on levels in the first cluster, then this level connecting to the multiverse would probably make a fine introduction to cluster 2 and have some interesting things to come after.

Also I’m not going to be working on the sandbox until we do fill up the first cluster so I’ll take plenty of suggestions for the time being.
