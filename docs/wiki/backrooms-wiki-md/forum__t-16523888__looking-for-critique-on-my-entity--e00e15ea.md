Looking for critique on my entity

16 Dec 2023 00:04

Entity Number: 1396 - The Wood Monoliths  
Habitat(s): N/A  
Description:  
Entity 1396 is relatively unknown, as the one of the only pieces evidence of its existence is a pile of polaroid pictures found in level 23. The pictures are covered in various unidentified liquids, one of which seems to be some sort of paralyzing venom. The pictures are of a forest, with a tall wooden statuesque creature in the background. The creature seems to be 70-90 feet tall with a glowing orange eye slightly to the left of the center of its head. Its head is an odd shape, similar to the shape of a boomerang.

Behaviors:  
According to victims on this entity, it is an ambush predator. It stands stationary blending in with its surroundings. It has highly sensitive roots underground which can detect the movement of prey. Once it notices its prey, it's secondary roots, which are covered in microscopic spines, stab into the victim, paralyzing them by injecting an unknown liquid into their bloodstream. After neutralizing it's prey it drags them under the ground to be digested.

Biology:  
Entity 1396 is 70-90 feet tall with one glowing orange eye. It is a tall wooden statuesque creature with 2 primary types of roots. The first of which are heavily sensitive to vibrations in the ground, allowing the creature to detect prey. The second type of root injects it's victims with a venom capable of disabling their nervous system. After they are neutralized, the second roots drag the victim underground to be digested.

Discovery:  
Entity 1396 is extremely rare, only sighted 4 times since it's discovery. The first recorded sighting was in 2008, when a wanderer stumbled into an unknown level. The wanderer survived its attack by throwing his backpack onto the ground far away from him, which alerted its roots there.

Dos and Don'ts:  
Do:  
Stay still in the case of an attack.  
Throw all jackets, backpacks, and other heavy items far from you and others.  
Slowly and carefully walk away from the creature after throwing said items.

Don't:  
Run.  
Panic.  
Shout.
