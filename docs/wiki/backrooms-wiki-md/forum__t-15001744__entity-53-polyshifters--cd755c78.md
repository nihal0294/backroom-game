Entity 53 "polyshifters"

10 Jul 2022 04:05

ENTITY NUMBER: 53

DESCRIPRION: Entity 53 is a species of polymorphic creatures that is rarely seen in level 37. They appear to be symbiotic, defending its owner and are extremely fierce when protecting their owner. They are very friendly, and are tamed by simply interacting with it in a playfull or protective fashion. They typically imprint on the first individual to show kindness, but will become distrustful if treated poorly. They will slowly absorb small amounts of nutrition from its symbiotic partner, and in return will perform any actions it is trained to do, but by default they will protect their owner, or if they detect that they are not healthy, will attempt to find whatever they think will help. Using their abilities to figure out what's wrong, They will often be able to find medicine rather quickly. They have an average intelligence of 150 and can teach each other tricks they have learned within a week. However what makes them so special is their ability to transform into a functional replica of almost any object they encounter. They have the ability to innately sense the interior of any object and reverse engineer these items, and use that information to become the object in question. They will even be able convert into the same materials and use the abilities of said object, for example, a deulcidator. If any of it gets damaged as an item, or separated from its main body, it will lose small chunks of its body when transforming back.

BIOLOGY: entity 53 instances are long, skinny, furry creatures, about 3 feet long, with a head similar to a green anole, but covered in fur instead of scales. They have a long tail and four short, stubby legs. Polyshifters don't have any known organs, simply absorbing nutrients from their hosts. They have a dark purplish blue color and can be albino in rare cases. However they can still change color despite having no pigment if they are albino. They appear to have both male and female, but can use their abilities to transform into the other gender if needed.

BEHAVIOR: polyshifters have a very curious nature, and are typically friendly, unless you attack them or their partners. They can be trained rather easily, due to their intelligence, and are extremely loyal to their owners. They become distrustful if abandoned or treated poorly/abused.

DISCOVERY: Entity 53 was discovered by a wanderer named James Quin. After entering level 37, he ran into one and they became symbiotic partners ever since. He has name his "poly".  
Poly generally takes the form of a fuzzy ribbon-like object wrapped around James's left arm. Most information on polyshifters has come from poly.

DO'S AND DONT'S

DO

tame one if you find one.  
start training yours as soon as possible.  
DONT

aggravate a polyshifter.  
attack someone who owns a polyshifter.  
abandon/abuse a polyshifter
