# Don't Bring Me Back.

rating: +16[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

Don't bring me back.

Mum's leading me to the [door](the-frontrooms.md).

I ran- running, like in a dream.

So close…

But… something pulled me [back](level-520.md).

Wrapped me round like a [mask](object-24.md).

Let me live in the [illusion](level-153.md). Please.

Five more minutes, Mum.

Don't bring me back.
