Overseer A and Overseer-1 would not get along I imagine. The overseers of SCP take things extremely seriously, while Stretch, the current overseer A, founded this group as a highschooler freshly no-clipped, and isn't the kind of guy to take things too seriously, often getting into conflict with the other M.E.G. overseers. The M.E.G. and the SCP foundation have fundamentally different goals. The SCP foundation would contain anything that tries to go to our reality, which is the M.E.G.'s primary goal. I agree that they would try to contain members of the M.E.G. should they try to escape the Backrooms. And if you base it on a meeting between Overseer a and 1 alone, it would not go well for the M.E.G.
