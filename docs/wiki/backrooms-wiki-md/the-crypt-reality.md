**The Crypt Reality** is an unnumbered Level of the backrooms, formerly was Level -998.

It is very different from other levels in the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

The Crypt Reality is much different than other levels. As said by Entity 100, it's not *in* the Backrooms. Instead, it is outside of it, similar to the [Blue Channel](blue-channel.md). The level seems to be created by Entity 100 by getting minerals from thousands of levels and turning them into his home. There is debate regarding whether or not this level can be considered a level, seeing as it could very well be an alternate universe or false reality. Whatever it is, it's something Backrooms related. The actual answer is unknown, but as of now it is classified as "altered reality", altered by Entity 100.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/the-crypt-reality/Level-9982.jpg)

Outside of an abandoned factory.

This level seems to have its own world, with countries, cities, continents, etc. Only one country has been documented, and its name is still undecided. The country is abandoned, littered with train tracks[1](javascript:;) and abandoned factories. At least 15 cities of varying sizes have been found, all of them deserted. It is recommended that you avoid them as they can cause static on the drone, breaking it.

Most things in this level are completely overgrown with dark red bark and purple leaves. The grass is usually an earth-like green. The trees are similar to those in the Frontrooms, but are a little more dull. Water is scarce, and the little water that can be found is boiling hot. The color of the water isn't much different than in the Frontrooms. The sky seems to have a dull, calming sunset with purple and red clouds.[2](javascript:;) clouds. However, there can also be normal clouds. (See image labeled "Core 2")

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/the-crypt-reality/Level-998.jpg)

Inside one of the facilities sub warehouses, taken by a drone.

Among the buildings in this level, there are two main factories that are were operational.[3](javascript:;) , Core 1 and Core 2. Core 1 exploded at an unknown time due to lack of security to keep it sustained. As a result, toxic gas spread throughout the country, and possibly other areas, making The Crypt Reality inhospitable to humans. However, Core 2 still exists. It needs to stay intact for more drones to explore this mysterious level. For a while it was difficult for humans to keep it from exploding, but intelligent robots were deployed to operate and secure the core. Some are ran by humans behind a screen, some are operating by themselves. Core 2 has multiple "sub warehouses". Inside of them are various useless items.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/the-crypt-reality/Level-9986.jpg)

Core 2.

# **Entities**

The entities found here are The Whispers, Entity 100, and The Crypts.

# **Discovery**

The Crypt Reality was discovered when Entity 100 showed it to M.E.G operatives during an interview.

# **Communities And Outposts**

Despite the extreme conditions, the M.E.G. managed to create some robots that could stabilize Core 2.

## [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) (A.k.a The "Major Explorer Group") Base Robotic:

- This is the 16th base of [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md)
- The base is still under construction, and is most likely the most unstable base yet.
- The base is 100% created and ran by humanoid robots created by [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md)
- This community's main goal is to sustain Core 2 from exploding.

## The Crypts Base

- Location and member count remain unknown.
- Holds friendly relationships with the M.E.G due to the sustenance of Core 2.
