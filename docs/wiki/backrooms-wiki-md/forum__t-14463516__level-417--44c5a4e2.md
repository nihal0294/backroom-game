**Level 417** is the 418th level of the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

**Level 417** appears to be an abandoned indoor playground going on forever. There are hordes of entities here, such as [Smilers](entity-3.md) and [Deathmoths](entity-4.md), though they are unusually passive. Some people claim to see weird glitches throughout the level such as the ground changing color, or the level changing shape entirely. There is still a lot that is unkown.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There are no known bases, outposts or communities on this Level.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

**Entrances**

- You can enter by no-clipping through [Level 18](level-18.md) or [Level 16](level-16.md).

**Exits**

- Unlike most levels, you can simply go back the way you came.
