[POLL] Entity 1 Slot

01 Sep 2023 01:31

Hi, everyone! BlueSignet here announcing that staff is holding a poll of what to do with the Entity 1 slot. I've created a strawpoll to let the community decide! It's anonymous and each IP address only gets a single vote.

In addition, results will not be available until the poll ends on Sunday. Some of the responses have been tweaked, but they're essentially the same. You are able to choose from 1 to 3 options. The most popular one is the one staff will implement. To see our reasoning for why, see [here](http://backrooms-oversight.wikidot.com/forum/t-16297105/entity-1-discussion#post-6190837.)

Go vote! Do your civic Backrooms duty!

Thank you for voting! The vote has concluded and option 1, Moving Humans to be entity 1, has won! A total of 149 people from the forums and Discord participated for a total of 249 votes.

Y'all should be able to see the votes, but I'll post them here for those unable to see them for any reason.

Whatever text to show/hide.

- Move the "Humans" enigmatic entity to the Entity 1 slot. (Currently being rewritten by a group headed by Niku): **77 votes (30.92%)**
- Hold a contest for the Entity 1 slot. The highest rated page gets the slot. Other entries are assigned numbers or are "Enigmatic" according to the author's wishes.: **60 votes (24.1 %)**
- Hold a contest for the Entity 1 slot. The highest rated page gets the slot. Other entries are assigned numbers or are "Enigmatic" according to the author's wishes.: **44 votes (17.67%)**
- Entity 1 will become a collaborative page helmed by Wikidot staff: **22 votes (8.84%)**
- Do not include an Entity 1 slot. **14 votes (5.62%)**
- Open Entity 1 up as a normal entity slot. Only a new greenlit page can fill it.: **13 votes (5.22%)**
- **Open Entity 1 up as a normal entity slot. Any new page can fill it. : \*\*10 votes (4.02%)**
- Open Entity 1 up as a normal entity slot. Any page, including an existing one, can fill it. **9 votes (3.61%)**
