An Idea

28 Nov 2023 15:56

So, if you've been on the SCP Wiki, you might know about achievement badges: little things that authors can put on their author pages that show that they've done certain things (Writing a certain number of articles, writing different types of articles, being active for a certain number of time, etc). They're just for fun and not moderated by staff. I wanted to see if y'all would be interested in seeing something similar here. If you do, then maybe give some suggestions for what exactly could be achieved. I've linked the SCP Wiki's achievement badges below, and given my ideas as well.  
  
[SCP Achievement Badges](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-achievement-badges|)  
[Foundation Cool Achievment Casefiles](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/dark-achievement-badges|)  
  
Side note: I wasn't exactly sure what the specific numbers should be for these, and I also wasn't sure what the name of one should be, so if you could give some suggestions for those as well, I'd appreciate it.  
Thanks to for everything in green!

[My Ideas](javascript:;)

[– hide block](javascript:;)

- Archivist of the Year: Had 6 pages published
- No Borders: Had a page translated into 3 languages
- Middlesorter: Wrote an article that crosses over 3 canons/Written for 3 canons
- Mr. Helpful: Gave 20 crits
- My Role Model is Blanche: Collabed with 5 authors
- Formats are for Losers: Wrote X format screws
- Knight of the Cygnus Order: Wrote 5 tales
- Playing For Both Sides: Wrote an in-universe collab between two characters or groups that probably wouldn't get along.
- PEOPLE of the Backrooms: Wrote one of each- **P**oI, **E**ntity, **O**bject, **P**henomenon, **L**evel, **E**nigmatic
- Fun on the Forums: Participated in non-contest site activity (e.g. Holiday Exchange)
- Hospital Society Remembrance Award: Rewrote an article, or incorporated a no-longer-existent article into your article.
- Honorary Eye: Voted on 100 articles
- Friend of (Level) 57/(Entity) 41: Have 5 articles with your original media (The articles don't necessarily have to be your own; you could provide the page image for an article written by someone else and that would count for this.)
- crouton: You know what to do. (Joke)

I think we should implement this. It would be fun to be recognized for one's achievments on the wiki and to have goals to stride towards.  
I know I probably have the "Mr. Helpful","Honorary Eye", and "Fun on the Forums" achievements already.  
Suggestions:  
"Archivist of the Month" should be "Archivist of the Year", and you need to get six pages published.  
I recommend "No Borders" to require 3 languages.  
For "Middlesorter", I also recommend 3.  
Mr. Helpful, I recommend twenty.  
I would name the collab one "My Role Model is Blanche", and requires 5 collaborations with other authors.  
I can't give a gauge on the format screws one.  
Eh, I say for the tales one, require 5.  
Honorary Eye should probably require 100 articles.  
For the original media one, I say 5.
