**Level 18446744100000000000** is the **18446744100000000001st** Level of The Backrooms. The number is the 128 Bit Integer Limit.

# Description

**Level 18446744100000000000** 's contents change depending on the area.

Part of The Level contains the layout of Level 0 but devoid of any color. This will always be the first area that wanderers will enter  
Part of it is a long hallway, made of a material like concrete but way stronger. The Hallway contains some doors that lead to different levels and/or different areas.  
Part is a complete white void.  
Part is a complete black void  
All areas are Non-Euclidean and can be segmented in multiple ways.  
There are multiple different lights on **Level 18446744100000000000** with lamps being the most common, lining the floors of The Hallway and The Entry.

There are multiple Wi-Fi signals throughout **Level 18446744100000000000**. They each have different names such as:

- The End
- Backrooms
- F#ck@ng Hell

After staying for an extended period of time in Level 18446744100000000000, Wanderers will start to experience multiple illnesses such as Blindness, Hearing Loss, Memory Loss, Scurvy, and Infections.

# Entities

The entities that reside are only found in The Hallway and The Black Void. The entities consist of Smilers, Skin Stealers, and Hounds. They will appear in "packs" with other entities of their type.

# Entrances And Exits

**Entrances**  
The only confirmed entry is by going to Level 9223372036854775810 and touching the Earth Orb. This will send you to this Level. A hypothesized entry is if you climb to the top of the stairs in Level 9223372036854775807 and jump down.  
**Exits**  
There is no confirmed exit. It is hypothesized that if you noclip in the Black Void, you will be sent to Level -0.
