Level 977 - Back when nothing mattered.

13 Feb 2022 03:01

**Page Type:** Level.

**Genre:** Nostalgic, Wholesome, Bittersweet.

**Core Concept:** This level will be an old family entertainment that features singing and dancing animatronics, similar to an establishment such as Chuck E Cheese, though the ceilings in the main building will be unusually tall and the staff only sections of the building are seemingly infinite, though are off limits to wanderers. The level has around 200 staff members, which are wanderers who've been recruited by the main entity of the level, who maintain the building and cook food for wanderers on the level. These staff members act much like a cult, and worship the entity of this level who is known as "Daedalus".

Daedalus is a single spirit who controls the all the animatronics at once, letting them put on shows even if damaged to the point where it would be unable to. Daedalus attempts to communicate to the reader while they read the page via hidden hyperlinks that lead to separate pages in which Daedalus is speaking directly to the reader. The main thing Daedalus talks about is how he was an entity who existed in the backrooms without a purpose, so he creates a child. Daedalus lives with the child for several years, teaching the child all it knows, however the child is lost through unknown means. Daedalus attempts to find the child, but is unsuccessful, and comes up with a new idea. It decides to take control of the animations on this level and put on a show that'll bring the child back to him.

**Additional Notes:** No sandbox as of this time, apologies if this is sloppily written, but as a quick summary: A spirit makes a Chuck E Cheese to get their lost child back.

Also, the level "977" was chosen specifically because Chuck E Cheese was founded in 1977.
