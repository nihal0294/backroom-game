I don't see why this is getting upvoted

25 Aug 2024 17:25

I like the pictures, I like the plexal idea. But not only this is poorly written, the main idea of the numbers is pretty bad in the way it currently is.

The idea of the numbered directions currently written is just a SCP-914 article. Level 273 is a mathematical function that takes an input, then outputs something. For example, 1874859532693820 gives Almond Water and 11118902235468760 gives Crowbar. This doesn't makes the level interesting…

I suggest making numbered directions like the entrance of the castle in B3313. The numbers shown on the signs may change when going in one. It seems random, but it is consistent.  
A storytelling could be written where a narrator write down on paper the numbers written on the signs. Noticing that he is going in circles this way because he happens to write down the same numbers.

Even if I believe in [nuclass](component__nulevelclass--45cf99e3.md) supremacy, I don't understand the SD class of this level. Why mysterious while the sighting of the entities are confirmed ? Is it because the "Mysterious undocumented entity" turned the SD-3 to SD-U ?  
Ok I don't always understand the SD-U class of most backrooms levels, but the reason why it is SD-U is really mysterious. Just the fact that it's implied that wanderers constantly get there due to people finding stuff on Reddit there, 4 men seeing a undocumented entity, the Lost Log and the Reddit user taking picture, the SD class should at least be known.

The writing in the second person "you you you you" really feels like a very old level on the wiki that hasn't been rewritten in ages. The clinical tone noclipped in the backrooms I suppose 😭🙏 ?

Obviously, every bad level of the backrooms has its "Mysterious entity that inhabits the level that appeared once" cliché. As other entities inhabits this level like the classical Smilers/Deathmoths/Hounds/Skin-Stealers (still surprised that Partygoers aren't listed), this would not be logical to write about a mysterious entity, as it could just be interpreted as an undocumented entity that can be found on other levels.

And lastly, the origins of the level and the history of the documentation is non-existent. We hear about a Reddit user named LcY2K07 taking a photo, then a Lost Log has been found, then we hear about Reddit users claiming to find new inputs. This is again, information that goes from nowhere that seems to just be filler content.

To conclude and summarize, we have filler content, "SCP-914" writing, the classical 5 entities, the innapropriate SD class, irrevelant characters, mention of Frontrooms social medias, missing potential for narration.  
This level is probably from 2020 but I criticize it because it's cool and it serves as an exercise for me and other people to see what should not be written.
