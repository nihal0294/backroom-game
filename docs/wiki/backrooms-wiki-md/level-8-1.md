Level 8.1 was a conglomerate sublayer of [Level 8](level-8.md). This level was often described by wanderers to be a complex amalgamation of claustrophobic halls resembling tight cave systems; however, it was susceptible to multiple changes.

# Description:

Dissimilar to other sublayers within the Backrooms that are seemingly located in discrete dimensions with no correlation to their parent level: Level 8.1 has shown certain anomalies that suggest an indefinite interrelationship between [Level 8](level-8.md). Certain passageways of this sublayer that connect to the cave systems of Level 8 are frequently witnessed during specific occasions but are often reported to be subtle. Due to the contrasting properties of both spatial dimensions of Level 8 and Level 8.1, it is theorized that the two spaces are partially fused, causing visual and auditory distortions to appear in certain areas on Level 8 and Level 8.1. It is advised to avoid all exploration of these spatial and auditory distortions as tangible and intangible interactions with them have shown to cause fatal consequences.

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-8-1/Level8.1)

Continuous hallway. Photo taken: July 28, 2010

Level 8.1 is a complex of twisting and intertwining hallways that frequently lead to dead ends, causing consistent navigation to be impractical. The structure that composes Level 8.1 consists of unnaturally durable bedrock that constantly leaks receding almond water and a red substance that emits a rancid odor thought to be caused by a sulfuric compound. Structural collapses frequently occur due to erosion of bedrock caused by the percolating almond water; this has shown to gradually weaken the overall structure of Level 8.1, leading to recurrent incidents of massive sinkholes and collapses.

Ominous auditory disturbances can often be heard throughout the hallways due to the acoustics throughout the halls. The auditory presence of physical impact that is frequently accompanied is theorized to be the local disintegration of bedrock; however, certain odd resonances heard from the walls indicate noticeable whimpering that, when tested, often causes a temporary loss of consciousness in multiple individuals. Recorded frequencies of these sounds inside the walls using specific apparatuses show a constant negative frequency. Ceramic objects with a strong molecular bond have been witnessed to collapse into small, discrete pieces when in close proximity to the mimicked frequency. Conscious behavior of delusion and derealization has shown to manifest in all subjects after a ten-minute direct exposure to these negative frequencies[1](javascript:;). These noises have no confirmed origin, however, it is often thought to be a result of distortion between the two partially fused realities leading to distorted auditory presences that seem to include the suffering voices of several other wanderers.

## Fused Hallways

Similar effects of distortion have been reported influencing and provoking physical locations in Level 8.1 where certain sections have been documented to be in a periodical state of combination/superposition between two spaces. The visual anomalies created in affected areas often show vague abnormalities where the environment rapidly oscillates between Level 8 and Level 8.1. Ambience and lighting of the environment has also been documented to be inconsistent in these periods causing many to lose touch of reality due to objects and matter having a 2-dimensional appearance. During these spatial phenomenons, any animate or inanimate bodies directly influenced by the effects through touch will instantly be transferred inside the bedrock structure of Level 8.1, causing an immediate casualty from compression. The distorted sections in Level 8.1 caused by these events are frequently reported to be transient with a presumed duration of thirty to sixty minutes; however, during special circumstances reported by wanderers, the environmental distortions can open up a type of spatial gateway that leads to an obscure second layer of Level 8.1.

  "Layer 2" refers to the physical space existing at the intersection between the two dimensions of Level 8 and the sublayer[2](javascript:;). This region is often described to be a self-similar version of Level 8.1 with a subtle, conglomerate appearance of Level 8 without the presence of distortions that are frequently seen in the first layer. The auditory abnormalities of screaming and whimpering are noticeably more distinct and can frequently be perceived without discerning it through the resonance of tangible mediums. The reason for the heightened amplitude of these sounds is unknown; however, it is widely hypothesized to be the cause of the overall area existing in closer proximity to the indeterminate source of these negative frequencies.

  ![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-8-1/Layer2)

  Narrow hallway resembling cave systems.

  The second layer of Level 8.1 features long, echoing hallways, that in contrast to the first layer, don't contain any recognizable light source. A red substance whose texture is similar to the fluid found on the first layer constantly leaks in high volumes from the stalactites causing noticeable puddles to form on negatively curved surfaces. Due to the commodious and impenetrable structure of Layer 2, noises and other audio can easily be distinguished from distant locations; however, determining local hazards is reported to be impossible as the amplitude of sound remains eminently consistent. The number of entities and what type exists within the second layer cannot be announced due to there being no official or authorized information on this subject.

  Currently, as of October 4th, 2017, there have been no reported instances of people escaping the narrow cave systems due to no inherent openings nor distortions found on the second layer along with the unnatural toughness of the structure. All information gathered from wanderers has been sent through a method of analog communication via the use of certain devices. In theory, presumed by travelers and personnel from specific foundations, there exists a third layer beyond the bedrock walls of layer 2; however, there has been no documentation of anyone being able to phase through to that space.
