Entity 232: The Plunderfoot

08 May 2022 13:15

Page Type: Entity  
Page Layout:

Elevator Pitch: The Plunderfoot (Struthiohippus Pisces) is a strange entity that resembles a cross between an ostrich and a sea horse that roams the more open-air environments of the Backrooms. They are roughly 8-10 feet tall and are generally omnivorous, eating fish, small invertebrates, and plants. Their typical behavior is very similar to ostriches and they are skittish and very ill-tempered, males especially. Plunderfoots get their name as they are highly territorial and will often attack wanderers with bone-shattering kicks and ear-splitting squeals. The creatures can also change their colors like a sea horse to reflect their mood. They also have a bizarre life cycle in which they typically start out as microscopic aquatic larvae in the dark, water-based levels of the backrooms. Then they develop into a transitional phase that moves and behaves like frogs before becoming fully terrestrial. How they jump levels is unknown.

Central Narrative: none

Hook/Attention Grabber: While they lack the more eldritch and mystical properties of other entities, their sheer wackiness, bizarre biology, and mysterious life cycle make them a very peculiar animal to come across.

Additional Notes: These entities are not meant to be groundbreaking, mostly just a silly concept that feels like something that you would see in a surreal environment such as the Backrooms.
