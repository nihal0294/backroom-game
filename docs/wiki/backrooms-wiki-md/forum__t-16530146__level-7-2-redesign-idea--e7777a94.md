Level 7.2 Redesign Idea

17 Dec 2023 22:32

Hello, this is my idea to redesign **Level 7.2** since currently it already reached a score of *-9*. I know it's being rewritten, but I also wanted to show this idea to you in case they delete the level due to the rating.

**Page Type:** *Sublayer*

**Genre:** *Mystery*, *History* and *Aquatic*

**Core Concept:** *In **Level 7** you can find a sunken ship by chance, which upon entering, you find yourself inside a new level. Welcome to the interior of the titanic, but more dangerous. It is a series of rooms, hallways and stairs that go up or down each floor each time you use them. The entire boat does not have an exterior exit to the side, but it does have entities. It is mostly of indeterminate difficulty, due to its properties and unknown information. In other words, it is the interior of the titanic but non-Euclidean, and full of furniture from the 70s, 80s and 90s. You can be inside the level for a certain amount of time, as it may become completely flooded like the original incident. It is not possible to survive beyond the flood and the only thing you can do is find a way out as soon as possible or drown.*

**Additional Notes:** *I only put some things that the new **Level 7.2** may have, but when it is finished it will have even more things and mystery. This is just an idea in case they delete the current **Level 7.2**.*
