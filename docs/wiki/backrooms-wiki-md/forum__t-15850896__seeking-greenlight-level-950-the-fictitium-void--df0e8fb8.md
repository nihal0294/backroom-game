This is well written, & if I had any say should be seriously considered for greenlighting.

However (yes, however), there is a flaw in this. A flaw in logic. At various points you write, "Level 950, also known as the Fictitium Void", & " it's called the Temple of Strife", & similar wording in different spots. All of these passages suggest that people have experienced this level & have returned to share what they found, & people discuss this … only no one ever leaves this place. Which, speaking sincerely, is unfortunate because these labels help the mood. The person who stumbles into here is doomed to experience something they cannot share with anyone else. Sorta like death: something everyone experiences, but no one can report what happens.

But I have a suggestion to fix this flaw: why not write instead (at these points) "Level 950, which we will call the Fictitium Void", & "what we will call a "Beam of Light", & "what we will call here the Temple of Strife". I know using that phrase repeatedly can be annoying & ruin the mood you have created, but I think with a little subtlety you can change this wording & avoid the problem of how others could know this.

Good luck with this
