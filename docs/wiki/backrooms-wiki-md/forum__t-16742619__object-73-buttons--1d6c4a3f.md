# Object 73 - "Buttons"

**Buttons** are small buttons that can appear around various levels. They have different effects based on their color/type.

[![flickr:53560862875](https://farm66.static.flickr.com/65535/53560862875_15e335ed83_m.jpg)](https://www.flickr.com/photos/200136497@N03/53560862875/)

*An artist's rendition of the Blue Button.*

# Description:

**Buttons** are somewhat common objects scattered throughout the majority of Backrooms Levels. Most buttons have the appearance of the typical, bright red 'press me' button, only varying in color.

# Properties:

When encountering one of these buttons, a Wanderer will feel a need to press it.[1](javascript:;) Every type of button possesses seemingly magical effects that only occur when they are pressed by a human. The effects are listed below.

| Name | Notes | Effects when pressed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red Button | The Wanderer will suddenly feel the need to press this button in order to receive rewards. | The button will generate a random, semi-helpful item[2](javascript:;), like [Almond Water](object-1.md), [Memory Jars](object-7.md) and [Scarabacks](object-14.md), or, on rare occasions, high-value items such as [Royal Rations](object-16.md), [Level Keys,](object-2.md), [Pockets](object-51.md), and once even a [{REDACTED}](object-99.md). Upon use, the button enters a six-hour cooldown phase. |
| Orange Button | The Wanderer will suddenly feel the need to press this button in order to leave their current location. This is one of the most highly valued variants of the Button. | The button will instantly cause the user to no-clip to a different Level[3](javascript:;). The user has some control over their destination-for example, by wishing to go to a certain Level, the Button may leave them in a Level that leads to the Level they wished to go to. Upon use, the button enters a five-hour cooldown phase. |
| Yellow Button | The Wanderer will suddenly feel the need for violence.[4](javascript:;) | The button will immediately spawn a common Entity, such as a [Child Faceling](entity-9.md), [Partygoer](entity-67.md), or [Skin-Stealer](entity-10.md). This button has been mentioned as a hypothetical method of reviving any species of Entity that goes extinct, such as the [Partypoopers](entity-68.md). However, all such attempts have thus far been fruitless. Upon use, the button enters a nine-hour cooldown phase. |
| Green Button | The Wanderer will suddenly feel the need to press this button in order to revitalize themselves. | The button will immediately heal *all* injuries and illnesses, physical and mental, that is afflicting the Wanderer. All malnutrition, dehydration, and exhaustion will also be cured. As a result, this button is the most valuable variant of this item and a highly sought-after commodity in the Backrooms. Upon use, the button enters a nineteen-hour cooldown phase. |
| Blue Button | The Wanderer will suddenly feel the need to press this button in order to progress technology. The button is different from the other buttons, as it has a cable attached to it and a 'power' symbol printed on it. | The button will immediately fully charge any form of electrical device or battery that the cable is plugged into.[5](javascript:;) Additionally, it will repair all physical damage and degradation to the battery/power source of said device. |
| Purple Button | The Wanderer will suddenly feel the need to press this button in order to hide **hide HIDE *HIDE HIDE*** at all costs. | The button will render the Wanderer completely invisible to any and all forms of detection. They become unable to be seen, heard, smelt, touched, or tasted. Additionally, they become unable to make physical contact with any walls, doors, etc. Due to some unknown factor, they are still able to navigate their environment-i.e. climb stairs and ladders, drive vehicles, or operate elevators. However, they cannot carry any other items, even clothing, besides the button. This effect lasts for twenty-four hours. Upon use, the button enters a thirty-six hour cooldown phase. |
| Black Button | The Wanderer will suddenly feel the need to press this button in order to die. This button is very common. DO NOT PRESS THIS BUTTON. | The button will immediately explode with the force of ten tons of TNT. It is the second-most valued button due to its widespread use as a form of land mine-however, it takes an extremely strong mind to resist the urge to press this button. It is advised to avoid this button when encountered. |
| Golden Button | The Wanderer will suddenly feel the need to press this button in order to test their luck. | The button will use one of the effects of one of the other buttons at random. Regardless of the effect chosen, the button will only have a one-hour cooldown phase. |
| Rainbow Button | This button is only rumored to exist. | There have been many varying reports of this button's effects-as such, it is impossible to confirm what exactly it does. |
| White Button | The Wanderer will suddenly feel the need to press this button in order to become immortal. | dont press this button. you dont want to end up like [me](entity-56.md) |

Footnotes

[1](javascript:;). This urge seems to be strongest in the presence of the Golden Button.

[2](javascript:;). This button will ALWAYS create a [Pocket](object-51.md) when used in [Level 9](level-9.md). As such, it is advised to not use this button in that Level.

[3](javascript:;). This is currently the only known method of safely escaping a 'dead-end' Level.

[4](javascript:;). In certain instances, if the Wanderer does not quickly press the button or leave its vicinity, they became violent towards their companions and had to be knocked out.

[5](javascript:;). The cord will change based on the device the Wanderer wishes to use it on. If there is no port, the button is used by touching the bare wire to the battery.

I'm *very* sorry for not seeing your reply until now.

Here are my suggestions:

Firstly, although I love simple articles, this one is just too short.

Secondly, let's look at this sentence:

> Buttons are small buttons that can appear around various levels. They have different effects based on their color/type.

In this sentence we know that the buttons "can appear around various levels". But "various" is a very vague concept. If the article actually passes greenlight and is published on the website, the discussion forum will become a Q&A forum. You should make it clear at what levels, or what kind of levels, the Backrooms would be filled with buttons if The Backrooms actually existed.

Thirdly, let's look at the description part:

> Buttons are somewhat common objects scattered throughout the majority of Backrooms Levels. Most buttons have the appearance of the typical, bright red 'press me' button, only varying in color.

I don't like boring objects that only change colors in appearance. Especially also as entity variants. And it's too short for a description, you're not in the Minimalism Contest, so feel free to write!

In the Properties part and the table below, we can only know that: the buttons will attract wanderers to press them and will have different effects when pressed.
