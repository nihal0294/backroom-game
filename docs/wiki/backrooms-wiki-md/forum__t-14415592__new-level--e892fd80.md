Okay well, where to start. If I understood correctly your idea was that this laboratory is used by Wanderers to conduct research on the entities and objects of the backrooms. That concept on its own is very original and unique and one that I love, but there are a few snags.

First, be specific on *who* is doing the research: Is it an established group of people, maybe one like M.E.G or an entirely new group specifically for this level, or maybe you want to take it in an entirely different route by having the dissected entities and other experiments already set up by the Backrooms itself when it generated this level. Or as a third option by having this lab be abandoned with only half-completed experiments and research left behind by some long-forgotten group of scientists. It's up to you but you need to be detailed about this stuff.

Second, I don't want to be rude about anyone's writing skills, but the level is confusing and hard to read. The grammar is inconsistent with typos left unfixed, as well as the overall writing being messy and choppy. I understand that English may not be a language you are used to or the fact that you are simply inexperienced, so please don't take this as an insult.

Fixing these things will definitely improve your chances of being green lit and having a great level. :)
