An Introduction

23 Oct 2023 19:14

Hi. I'm JSX87, or, as I'm known in the real world, Justin. I've read articles on SCP since 2018, lost interest in that around the time the Backrooms rose to prominence, read articles here in early 2022, left during the summer, migrated back to SCP, got out of the groove of it in late May 2023, remembered that this site existed, migrated back in June, then finally decided to make a Wikidot account and join in mid-October.

As you can see, I'm kind of all over the place. I have ADHD, so while I do enjoy reading an actual book, the article-by-article structure of this site is perfect for me. I also have social anxiety, so I'll probably take a while to work up the nerve to respond to you if you do message me.

I'm a fan of pretty much anything sci-fi, and love cyberspace, cyberpunk, synthwave, OutRun, 2000s techno/neon, and retro/pixel/chiptune aesthetics. I'm also a fan of fictional esoterica (obviously), time travel tomfoolery (when it's not super confusing), and multiverse shenanigans.

I am also a gamer™. Some of my favorite games include Subnautica, Sonic Mania, TF2, Pokemon Ultra Sun, VVVVVV, and Rivals of Aether.

I'm probably not going to write any articles due to anxiety and procrastination, but who knows, maybe I will.

That's about it. See you on the flip side.
