Object Contest Idea - UnBrellas

09 Sep 2022 16:36

UnBrellas are sentient objects that when opened, provide time reversing/reality-bending effects, such as healing wounds, making you completely dry, or bringing a weapon back from the verge of breaking. Where their sentience comes in is that they each have a different set of things that they like or dislike, and it'll affect what they do to you. If they dislike you, they can open up past wounds, dull a weapon, or, for comedic purposes, summon a raincloud on you when you raise it above your head. Inspired by Fallout tbh, in the way that along with protection, companions can also add a unique perk.  
I am writing this idea rn, though I'm currently dried out of ideas. What ideas/suggestions do yall have? Open to anything.
