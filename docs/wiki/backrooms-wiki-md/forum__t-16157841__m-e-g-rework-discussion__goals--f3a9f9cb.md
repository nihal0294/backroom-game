M.E.G. Rework Discussion: Goals

12 Jul 2023 18:06

So I'm thinking of making the goals of the M.E.G. in the rework the following:

M.E.G. Goals

1. Find an exit to the Backrooms (The reason the group was founded. While not as commonly seen in the present, it is still the primary goal of the group)

2. Help and protect wanderers (A goal adapted as the group grew. Gained more focus over time, since there are no leads to finding an exit up to this point)

The group was formed by the original three overseers and some others lost with them in the Backrooms, so the original goal ofc would be to find a way out, to get back home. Over time, an exit wouldn't be found, and the focus would shift to helping other lost wanderers, though the original goal remains. The goal to find an exit was even stated in the tale "The Beginning of the M.E.G.", which is what I was originally going off of.

Thoughts?

Here are my two cents: the group is called the Major *Explorer* Group for a reason. I definitely agree with goal 1, but goal 2 on its own almost seems defensive and passive. But the thing is, the MEG is actively exploring, expanding, forming connections, and researching the Backrooms as a whole. Of course they want to get out of there, but (at least in my eyes), there's an important element of scientific curiosity and determination to uncover the inner workings and secrets of the Backrooms. So I would put a third goal of "Discover and learn as much as we can" or something better phrased

anyway im a fan of the MEG so excited for the rewrite

To me, the "learning about and researching the Backrooms" fits under goal 2, since the purpose of it is to inform wanderers, usually about dangers and ways to safely get from place to place. That's the point of the database. I should probably elaborate what each point means, and the measures taken to follow those goals.

I don't disagree that it'd be a focus. Heck, we were even thinking of making an entire sector dedicated to research. I may make it a goal, or merge into the existing goal 2 somehow. They are definitely an Exploration group, although they've grown to become far more than that over time.

And of course, we'll word them far better in the actual rewrite. These are just to present the ideas.
