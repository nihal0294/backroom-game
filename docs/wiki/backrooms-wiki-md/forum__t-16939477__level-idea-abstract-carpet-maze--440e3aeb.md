Level idea- abstract carpet maze

17 Sep 2024 01:38

Currently developing a concept for a new level. Am I the only one who finds carpeted boxes oddly mysterious and alluring? When I think about liminal spaces I’ve encountered in real life, one that tends to get me are public seating areas- like amphitheatre-style steps or sunken lounges- that are completely covered in carpet. Another one that gets me are cat trees- those series of carpeted boxes and platforms people set up for their pets. I’ve always thought, what would it be like to shrink down and explore one of those? Crawl around in this weird geometric maze of carpeted rooms and tunnels where everything is hushed and insulated and full of static electricity? I like the idea of permutating this into a backrooms level, and haven’t seen it yet.

I’m picturing an infinite vertical space, like a large shaft, filled with complex clusters of geometric forms that are all carpeted, which must be climbed upon or crawled through. Claustrophobic tight squeezes, shocks of energy, oppressive, dry silence from the carpeting on every surface absorbing sound, and nothing but an endless climb either up or down in vain hope of finding something different. Platforms you can rest on, bottomless carpet-pits you can fall into and dangerous heights, tiny squeezes where you can get stuck. Potential for energy-generation using static electricity and interesting electrical phenomena. (I’ve also been playing with a minor plot involving partygoers hanging out it this level, and some disturbingly stained carpets.)

I have a draft in the works, but wanted some feedback on the concept before opening my sandbox. Appreciate all thoughts.

I think you've definitely got something here. Its a very bizarre concept, so it works greatly in your favor in a backrooms setting. I do like the ideas you present with the static electricity being a problem, and I'd definitely lean into that. It could make for a very unique Level, as I don't think anything like this has ever been done before. It's pretty out there, as it's a "random expansive material" as opposed to "big house lol"

I can't speak on behalf of the partygoers plot, but I'd be careful with it: with things like the Partygoers, its very easy to fall into the trap of "I'm going to include them in my page so people will read it!" I'm not saying that's what you're trying to do here, but it is merely a suggestion to not take lightly. My general rule of them when referencing other pages is:

"What cool idea do I want to do? What page fits that criteria the best to help me execute it?"

Definitely give this one some thought, I think it could really come out well.
