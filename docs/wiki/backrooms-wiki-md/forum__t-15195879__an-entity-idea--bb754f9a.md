An Entity Idea

19 Sep 2022 00:44

I have been thinking about an idea for a new entity. It would be cute but with a twist. The twist would be that its cuteness is a disguise for a monster and can switch back and forth from cute to terrifying. The entity may take on certain forms that appear cute to many individuals such as rabbits or puppies. It would also have poison to either paralyze and kill, or mainly slow you down to eat you.
