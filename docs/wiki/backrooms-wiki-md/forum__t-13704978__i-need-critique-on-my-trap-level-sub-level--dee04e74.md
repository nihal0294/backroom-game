Hey! I'm not a Moderator or critique staff member or anything, but I thought I could give my opinion to be helpful to other new writers like me. This is just my opinion, you don't have to take my word for anything.

This is certainly a good idea to start out from, but the simple concept can easily be expanded on. You should probably add quite a bit more depth to the Level (no pun intended). For example, make a feature for this Level that has some kind of feeling that is expressed to those who enter, like paranoia, fear, sadness, or greed, for example. Adding small pieces of lore and mystery might be helpful to appease the reader, because right now the Level is technically just a big pit of spikes (no offense). I wish you the best of luck!
