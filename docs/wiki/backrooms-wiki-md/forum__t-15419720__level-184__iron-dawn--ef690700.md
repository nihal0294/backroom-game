Thank you for your feedback!

The reason there are so many bases is because the structure of the level and the large metal deposits allow large buildings to be erected in the area.

You raise a good point about the large housing amount. I still think that, all things considered, this level is rather safe, and that many travellers will be wary trying to go to a new level, not knowing where else they could end up, meaning that there would still be a settled population within the level. That being said, 2000 people does seem quite extreme. I will lower the number on the two housing units to a more realistic number.

The reason that the SD is 3, is because the level does have a relatively decent amount of entities, the constant risk of falling off the level makes the level unsafe, and the occasional, albeit rare, fog events make the level unsecure, meaning that low entity count, unsafe, and unsecure is the most accurate description for the level.

Once again, thank you for the feedback!
