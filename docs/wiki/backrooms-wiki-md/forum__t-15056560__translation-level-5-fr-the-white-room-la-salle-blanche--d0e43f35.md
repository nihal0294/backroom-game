Hello again, Fulbion.

So I understand that you have translated this from an article that was written by another author. If you are to GL this on *this* site, then here are some changes you may want to put. Not many, but I am limited on time and this is not so hot.

Almost this entire article seems like it was a clinical tone that just wasn't clinical. While there were factual statements arranged in a clinical tone format, this seemed to be talking to the reader. There was the frequent use of "you", "your", etc. This is a big no-no with this kind of stuff.

The idea is bland. Okay, so there is a furniture store, and if you walk too close, you fall into a hole, big whoop. This could be built upon, especially with more than just white rooms. Maybe some *actual furniture store decorations?*

Finally, the layout was very choppy. It was fact. fact. something related. fact. This should be an easy fix.

Alrighty. This article was not written by u, so I am not blaming you for any of these mistakes. I am not sure how you are communicating with this author, but either edit this yourself or maybe ask them if they are allowing you to use this but with their ideas/crediting. Also, this article is very short. Some short and sweet articles do well, but this one wouldn't.

PM me with any questions or updates, as always! (p.s. I have just registered to Discord and can be contacted at Iamalemon13#6339, if u have it.)
