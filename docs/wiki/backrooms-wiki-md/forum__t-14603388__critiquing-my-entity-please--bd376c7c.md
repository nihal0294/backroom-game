Quick read-through(I didn’t go through every SPaG issue) shows multiple stuff I find weird/isn’t good. I’ll go ahead and tell you about them below

[What’s Wrong?](javascript:;)

[Needs more work](javascript:;)

First thing first, the image. If you go to the very bottom of the page, and that bar(should be the place where you edit your page), go to files. There, you can click “upload a file from your computer.” Then, you can select your drawing to add the photo. After doing that, copy the website for the photo and put it in the name section of the image block. Here’s the code for it:

```
[[include component:image-block
    |name=Thebackrooms.jpg
    |caption= Backrooms confirmed?!?!?
]]
```

  
Next, the subtitles(I.E. “Cycle,” “Gem,” etc.) aren’t capitalized.  
There isn’t any Behavior for them, nor biology, which I think would be interesting to read through.  
Another thing I noticed is that a 3rd of the article is written about taming them or something related. While taming is a pretty important thing, it’s not the best thing for your article to ride on. Even though most entity articles have something that tells you how to fight/tame them, those same articles are written to inform the people on *what they’re seeing,* and not just how to fight them.

  
Now, through a full read-through, I can say one thing; it’s filled with SPaG based issues. Please take this article through a SPaG checker, like Grammarly. I would do it but I don’t have any free time right now.
