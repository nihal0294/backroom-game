to answer ur questions: the machine god isnt meant to be malevolent or benevolent, its just that its idea of love is fundamentally incompatible with man's. the sacrifice is, primarily, the followers' individuality, and the machine god actually considers this as love. christ sacrifices himself for humanity whereas the machine god makes its followers sacrifice themselves into the machine. and those followers gradually converge toward the center, *becoming part of the machine*
