Stating staff's deletion policies here from my time as admin a short time ago, as I remember them:

- All deletions, including ones that happen "automatically" due to a page falling into deletion voting range, require a witness. This means at least two staff members should be present and looking at the pages when a deletion happens, with one doing the deleting and one confirming the deleting.
- Staff are not allowed to write that a discussion with staff was had in official staff Wikidot PMs unless a discussion was actually had, for the obvious reason of "that would be lying". I'm sure any staff member can take screenshots of the discussion in staff's discord server to supply proof of discussion.
- The record of the page being deleted can be found [here](http://backrooms-oversight.wikidot.com/forum/t-14227932#post-5658183). Deletion reason and the original code of the page are included. If the link doesn't work (as wikidot forum links often don't), it's the latest post in the thread as of posting my message.

A few theories as to what might have happened:

1. Staff felt that a page translated into English from another language does not fit the rules of the contest, even if the page was never posted on another language branch before. *[ghostchibi opinion: this is dumb and this page could reasonably count as a collab submission between Null0 and ZCOM12 for the contest, given that they also seemed to have uploaded it to CN as an EN->CN "translation"]*
2. Staff felt that the page was being presented as *only* Null0's contest entry, not a collab entry giving ZCOM12 proper credit as a "co-author". This would make it unfair to give Null0 full credit in a contest where other users are doing all aspects of writing. *[ghostchibi opinion: the wording of Bun's PM makes me think that this was the reason for deletion]*
3. There was a misunderstanding as to when the "original" was posted, and the contest entry was mistaken for a translation of an existing CN page. *[ghostchibi opinion: yeah I can see this easily happening]*

I don't think the page should have been deleted, since most of the issues could easily be fixed by just stating that the page is a collab between Null0 and ZCOM12 (barring reason #1 above, but I reiterate that a reason like that is stupid). With that being said, this is an oddball of a submission and I think Null0 should have talked to staff before posting to make sure that a) this is allowed, b) staff understand the situation beforehand, and c) the rules of the contest were be updated to include entries like this as a type of collab. This situation isn't fair to Null0 and ZCOM12, but I think it isn't fair to accuse staff of malice here. It doesn't seem like anyone checked with staff before posting to make sure that the article was OK under the contest's rules. This was a needlessly confusion situation all around.
