Entity conceptual idea: Kilarocs

23 Aug 2022 02:47

**Page Type:** Entity page

**Genre:** Thriller

**Elevator Pitch:** Kilarocs are gigantic avian-cervine entities that can be encountered in open-air levels, particularly prairies, rural countryside, and suburbia. They are flightless and instead use long, powerful legs to pursue their victims, knocking them down with their horns and using their hooked beaks to feast on juicy innards. They are cooperative and intelligent, with the supernatural ability to perfectly imitate the silhouette and voice of humans, namely loved ones of their intended target. This trick is used specifically in heavily populated areas as well as when dealing with a large group of wanderers, luring individuals away to kill in silence.

However, Kilarocs don't start out as towering murder chickens, in fact, it's questionable to consider them either birds or deer. Faunlings are the juvenile form of the Kilaroc and from a glance look and behave very much like human children, even being able to take on their likeness. However, they have digitigrade feet, large avian eyes, sharp beaks beneath their lips, long silky soft down for hair, and small underdeveloped antlers covered in velvet. They can be in the vast majority of levels and are generally no more violent or gentle than typical children, albeit in a more detached and partial way. They can be bonded with and this can even transcend past their visceral transformation into adolescents, however, their conscious minds are eventually overshadowed by their avian instincts and predatory desires.

**Central Theme:** These entities are heavily based on the mythical creature having the head, body, and forelegs of a deer and the wings tail, and hindquarters of a bird known as the *peryton*. They're said to cast a human shadow until they kill a human. I also took loose inspiration from other mediums such as the film *Hatching* and some illusionary yokai from Japanese folklore. They're pretty much the backrooms equivalent to a yokai spirit or mythological creature than a basic monster like a hound or smiler. the nuance of their nature making it harder to decide the moral correctness of simply killing them.

**Additional notes** I was imagining that the Faunlings would often integrate into child facelings groups as well as enjoy mutilating blub cats (as the latter can't be harmed and enjoys the violence).
