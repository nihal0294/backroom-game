Endless Falling Elevator

04 Feb 2022 00:57

**Page Type:** Other

**Genre (Optional):** Horror, Dark, Psychological

**Core Concept:** It's basically an elevator located somewhere in the backrooms. When somebody goes inside and press any button, the door closes and the person is stuck in there, but they just might notice it a little bit later. After some minutes, the elevator mechanisms (that would make a normal elevator go up/down) break, but instead of hitting the floor, the elevator keeps falling down forever.

This elevator also makes the person inside immortal-ish. Basically, a person's regenerative abilities are highly increased, along with their lifespan. They also have immunity over hunger, thirst and other bodly needs. But on the otherhand, they'll still feel like they're hungry or thirst, even though they don't need to eat or drink anything. They can also feel pain and negative psychological effects.

I haven't thought much about the story, which is something I'm open for ideas.

**Additional Notes:** This idea was supposed to be an SCP, but I chose to put it on this forum for two reasons. First, the Backrooms sounded more fitting to have a random elevator that never stops falling. And second, because the Backrooms is a concept that really hooks me on.
