Level 182, also known as Prolific Cottages, is a draft level about a forest that seems abandoned and spooky, but is really safe and a wonderful place. I have spent quite awhile revising and adding onto based on critique and ideas with a few reviewers, so I'm feeling pretty darn confident about it. It's about medium in size, with some original content, including an entity exclusive to the level. I'm mainly looking for help making sure that the overall feel of the level isn't too , and I quote, "cheesy and soft". I hope this gets a greenlight, so I can get this published.
