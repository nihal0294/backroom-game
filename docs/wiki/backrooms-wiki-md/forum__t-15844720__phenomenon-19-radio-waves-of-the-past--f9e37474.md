Note: This is just me personally. Main grip I have with this article is that it does a *lot* of telling.

> Why weren't these messages heard in the Backrooms during the years they were made? Why are they altered? Who is transmitting them, if anyone?

> I know you said that the Backrooms might be on a different timeline than the frontrooms, but how would these radio waves reach us even then? If it were possible for radio waves to reach the frontrooms, we would try communicating with them. How would our radios recieve these messages?

> Could it be that these are disembodied voices trying to talk to us? Based on some of the contradicting messages, there might be two beings fighting for control. Are they on our side? Are they entities or people from the future?

Just a lot of this within a short period of time, so much that it becomes a block of text to skim through. It could be better to cut down on the questions? Sometimes the unsaid speaks volumes.

Rather than asking many questions in succession, it could benefit from allowing the reader more space to think about the implications of the ideas presented themselves. As of now, it doesn't leave much room for readers to engage mentally.

Hope this helps!
