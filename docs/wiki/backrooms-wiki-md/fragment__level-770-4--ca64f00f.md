# HOMELAND DEFENCE FORCE

### July 13, 2024

---

***[knocking noises]***

E. Triggs : Grand Marshal, may I talk to you for a moment ?

C. McNamara : Come in.

E. Triggs : As the H.D.F. secretary of order, it's my duty to check on our personnel. And without implying anything, I do have some questions about Major Andrei Defalco and our 6th battalion…

C. McNamara : Andrei. My comrade Andrei. We discussed face to face 3 months ago. He told me that some M.E.G. members were plotting something quite… unusual, and the U.E.C. is pretty busy with other issues at the moment. So, I allowed him to carry out a long term Operation with the entire 6th battalion. Whatever the M.E.G. is plotting, we must know about it.

E. Triggs : …

E. Triggs : Why didn't I know about this?

C. McNamara : Oh! Yeah… I should have told you Miss Triggs. It slipped out of my head.

E. Triggs : Well, I'm asking all that because we received a mail from a certain "Masked Maidens" yesterday.

C. McNamara : Who are they already?… Aren't they the mysterious peoples that wear masks all the time?

E. Triggs : The message was suspicious. It talked about Major Andrei and conspiration against humanity and that they wanted to meet us…  
We left no response and blocked their mail address.

C. McNamara : Conspiration against humanity? Andrei? I doubt about it. Andrei is part of our family since the very beginning of the H.D.F. and has proved overtime to be one of our most loyal member. This is some conspiracy bullshit, I have no doubt about it…

E. Triggs : Well, I will not bother you any longer. I hope he will return very soon. I had things to look with him.

***[As she leaves his office]***

C. McNamara : Hmm…

C. McNamara : Miss Triggs! One last thing…

E. Triggs : I'm listening. Grand Marchal.

C. McNamara : Transfer me that mail. I will arrange a meeting with them. Even if it sound like conspiration theories, I still want to hear what they have to say…
