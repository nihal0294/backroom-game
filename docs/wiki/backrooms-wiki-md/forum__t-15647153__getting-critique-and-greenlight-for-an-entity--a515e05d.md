> The Kind One is a mysterious dark humanoid entity with some particles surrounding him as well and disappear when they float away from it, referred by the name "Wanderer".

This seems worded wrong. I suggest you break this up into two sentences instead of having a big clump of words. The adjective "dark" is unneeded here. I suggest you run your article through Grammarly or some other spell checker if you haven't already.  
"The Kind One is a mysterious humanoid entity that prefers to be reffered to by the name "Wanderer". It is normally seen with dark particles surrounding it that dissapear when they float away from it."

> It is a male and stands as 5'7 feet tall. Its face is described as completely dark with only white eyes and a white mouth visible. The body is dark as the face.

This has some wording problems:  
It is a male that stands at 5'7 feet tall. Its face is described as completely dark with only white eyes and mouth. Its body is as dark as its face.

> Some people claim that it appears to have Middle Eastern features and is estimated to be around 21 years of age. Upon physical contact with The Kind One, individuals report that he is wearing a shirt, pants, a sturdy shoes, a sturdy armor, and a sturdy helmet.

Two things, first of all, I don't understand how they can tell if it has "Middle Eastern features" if it's body and face are practically pitch-black. You say, "Upon physical contact[…]individuals report that he is wearing[…]" How can they tell its features if it is wearing armor that is majority of its body? In fact, how do they know it's wearing a shirt? You would also think that a person wouldn't have to be in contact with an entity to know what it is wearing.

> However, the design of these items is impossible to discern.

Why?  
Weapons appear from some pocket dimension, The Entity consistently employs a dark sword, the design of which remains elusive  
"Weapons appear from some pocket dimension." isn't needed here. Get rid of it, or reword it.  
Once again, why is the design "elusive"? You also don't need to italicize "Entity" every time it comes up in your sentence.

> According to the Entity, this weapon is the least powerful among its arsenal, which purportedly includes a variety of additional armaments. The specifications of these weapons remain unknown, but he claims that he every single weapon that a warrior would need.

I don't know what word you were trying to spell when you typed "purpotedly".  
You need "has" between "he" and "every".  
You keep swapping between "he" and "it". Please decide which one you will be using.  
How dangerous is this entity, exactly?

> This Entity is only one and none of its kind were seen, no information is known about any people from its kind yet it keeps searching for knowledge that we don't know, but the most we could say is that he is looking for information about himself, nothing is known aside from that.

You could reword this better:  
"This Entity is the only known one of its kind, as none others have been seen. No information is available about others of its kind, yet it keeps searching for evidence of them. It is also said that he is looking for information about himself."
