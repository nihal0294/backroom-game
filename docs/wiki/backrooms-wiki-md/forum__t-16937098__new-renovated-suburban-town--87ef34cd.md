Level 310 critic:

First and foremost, you don’t have the rating module.

On a concept level, this is like a basic example of a level renovated by the backroom Remodeling Co.  
I don’t think this bring really much compared to level 90.  
It’s mostly, we have done some renovation, don’t pay attention to the details.  
You also lose yourself in not really useful details. We have this specific detail also this other specific detail, etc…

Some parts are not developed at all:  
“Well, it's a bit weird how we discovered the original level”  
No further explanation.

I would recommend to rework on your level by asking yourself the following questions:  
-What are the themes of the Backrooms Remodeling Co?  
-What parts of the Remodeling Process do I think are interesting?  
-What part of the Backrooms Remodeling Company can I expand on or explore or bring a new approach to?

Format is pretty much decent.

Time for some nitpicking:  
Well, you certainly need to check your SPAG with a dedicated SPaG checker software.  
Some sentences are confusing, some are awkward in their construction. But, level 90 seems to have the same kind of tone… So, I guess that’s a special case.

Here what I found (I’m not a SPaG specialist):  
“Ok, so before we renovated this level, there was a bit of a problem he had before-um any construction could be made.”  
Before any constructions could be made

“After their entitie” entities (plural)  
“After their entitie clear our they told”  
After having cleared the entities, they informed us that the renovation process could be continued…

“then our crew got straight to work. “  
Our crew got straight back to work

“even close to done yet”  
To be done yet

“really,really big.”  
really, really big.

“600 miles in”  
Precise also the distance in meters, please

“are still working strong on building”  
Working hard

“Well besides are workers living here after their hard work.”  
Okay well or besides  
Besides, Workers are living here after their day of hard work

“anything that's puts people”  
Anything that puts people in danger

“there's some nicely done houses”  
There are some

“I know it's a bit weird, but trust me, it's worth it to make it to our lovely new renovated town.”  
Split the sentence

“to connect our other”  
To connect to

“No hard feelings”  
Final dot, please
