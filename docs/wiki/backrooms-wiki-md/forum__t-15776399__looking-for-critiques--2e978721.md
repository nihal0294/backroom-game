Looking for critiques

31 Mar 2023 22:07

First, I want to admit there's a lot of material here that is not only good, but probably better than anything I could come up with. Which is one reason I haven't seriously tried to write one until now.

But the other night I was reading a book about one of my favorite artists — Giorgio de Chirico — & I realized as I studied his early paintings from his Metaphysical period how much they fit into the Backrooms. Surprised that no one seems to have used de Chirico as inspiration, I wrote this level. (And according to US copyright law, all of his metaphysical pieces are in public domain.)

This is a working draft, with parts obviously unfinished. I admit that I haven't added any links from this level to other pages, that I need to write out Prof. Savinio's essays, & meanwhile I'm struggling to embed one of his paintings in the page. (If I solve that problem, I'll also add some pics to illustrate some of the mannequins of the Synod of the Heretical Atheists.) But I feel I've got this stable enough to solicit feedback & find out if I have something others might find worth reading. I'll worry about getting this Greenlit later on.

Thanks in advance for looking over my contribution & offering criticism.
