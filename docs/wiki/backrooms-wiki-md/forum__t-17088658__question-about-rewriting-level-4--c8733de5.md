Yes, you are correct. You do not need the original author’s permission. However, I recommend posting an original page to the site or rewriting a less popular page first before applying for a big rewrite like Level 4. The GLMP is very hesitant with Level 4 (and rightfully so) since it is one of the primary pages that represent our site, so we want to ensure that the person rewriting it will create a quality product.
