Level 0.9 - "Contained Endlessness"

20 Jul 2022 05:57

**Page Type:**

Level.

**Genre:**

Wonder, Mystery.

**Core Concept:**

*What is the foundation to the idea?:* Level 0.9 is the gateway through the empty, endless paths and patterns of Level 0, and the relative safety and communities of Level 1, it is almost a fusion of the two, having properties that resemble both, with the iconic buzzing of Level 0 still being prominent, but yet the stone and brick walls of level 1 seemingly penetrating through the nostalgic yellow. The lights flicker at times, which always occurs during a blackout event in Level 1, further pushing their connection. There is some resemblance of entity activity, with hound footprints on the carpet floor, that has lost its colour and dampness from Level 0, however even with evidence of entity activity, there has never been a sighting. Maintaining the void of entities that Level 0 offers, while almost taunting wanderers with tracks and traces of entities scattered around the halls. (Possible introduction if shortened, or a first part of the description.)

---

*What is the story and how does this build on the premise?:* I'm thinking of making the story of this level shrouded in mystery as the genre suggests. I want to make the reader believe this is just some fusion level, and then build upon a bigger story through emails and messages sent through to different characters about a series of events that take place in the level, building on the concept of the level and adding layers that will make readers wonder about the level.

---

*What will hook the reader into the idea?:* The initial emails sent through that will be "encoded" by the senders, making only some of the message visible, and then as you read on through the details of the level, the emails will become readable and the bigger picture will be revealed slowly.

---

*How will you keep them hooked?:* Through small hints at the major concept of the level. These will be done through, as said before, emails sent to different characters, small details in images, and more.

**Additional Notes:** This is a very early concept I've just recently come up with, and any ideas on which way to go on expanding the concept in the future would be very helpful.
