If you were given the option to enter the backrooms, would you?

21 Nov 2023 14:56

A lot of people in the real world suffer. Whether they have financial problems, disabilities, stress, social anxiety, or depression, they all may not enjoy life or feel like they *belong* in the real world. A lot of people seek an escape from this world and that's something I theorize got a lot of people interested in the concept of the backrooms. A lot of people may just want to go to a beautiful world of wonder, a surreal world of infinite magnitude where they can be alone and forget about all their worries and problems from the real world. That beautiful world of wonder being *The Backrooms*.

The question is, are *you* one of those people? If you had the option to spend the rest of your days exploring the infinite realms of the backrooms, would you take it? Or would you need a guarantee you won't get hurt? What else?

I would love to hear your thoughts! : )
