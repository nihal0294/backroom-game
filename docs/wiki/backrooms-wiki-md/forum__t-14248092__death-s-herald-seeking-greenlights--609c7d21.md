"Death's Herald" SEEKING GREENLIGHTS

30 Oct 2021 16:10

**Page Type:** Entity

**Genre (Optional):** Horror/historical

**Page Layout (Optional):** Typical entity layout page.

**Elevator Pitch:** An entity that acts follows one until death.

[+ show block](javascript:;)

[– hide block](javascript:;)

So Basically it's the Irish folklore creature, "The Dullahan. " It seeks the souls of the living, however with a twist.  
It is an entity that serves a God named "Cromm Dubh".

1) The Dullahan is a humanoid dressed in medieval knight armor. It rides a black steed to hunt those who run from it and weilds a whip made of a human spine. The most prominent trait is that it carries its own head, or sometimes has it strapped onto its neck.

2) it does not outright kill people. This isn't a murder monster after all. It simply picks and chooses who it deems as " worthy" for sacrifice. After it finds someone(usually with high sanity) , it proceeds to stalk and follow them until they die, to which it takes their soul and gives it to it's dying God. However, sometimes it grows impatient and will forcefully execute the victim.

3) Nonviolent in nature unless provoked. Another person simply witnessing the Dullahan on duty will provoke it. It will proceed to blind the assailant, leaving them to presumably perish in the backrooms.

4) How to spot the Dullahan is off duty is quite easy. If it simply has it's head strapped onto it's shoulders, it is off duty. Usually the dullahan will carry it's severed head when searching for it's victims. Coming into contact with the Dullahan in this scenario is mostly safe. However make sure that you're not wearing any gold jewelry and such. Since it has an irrational fear of gold, it will see you as a potential threat.

5: To temporarily scare away the Dullahan, the victim must wear some type of gold jewelry or accessory. It has an irrational fear of the precious metal, and simply having the entity come into contact with it will singe it's skin and armor.

**Central Narrative:** When Saint Patrick introduced Christianity to Ireland in 433 CE, the followers of Cromm Dubh dwindled as they converted to Christianity. Sacrifices became scarce and the God grew weak and forgotten, without the souls it was being offered, thus it and it's army ending up in the backrooms somehow.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** Well, I think it's unique since it's an entity with a **motive**. It doesn't blatenly kill people for the sake of being scary. I think something following you until you die is eerie. I figured taking something that exists and giving it a unique twist would make it appealing. Nothing is original after all.

**Additional Notes:** Irish folklore is cool. That's all.
