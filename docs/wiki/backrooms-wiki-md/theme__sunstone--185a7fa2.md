# Sunstone Theme

rating: +24[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

This theme is brought to you by [by literally re-skinning the [Lazuli Theme](theme__lazuli--1fda8e80.md) by ].

Special thanks to for helping with the logo and for the greenlight/techlight.

I made this theme for the people who like red or orange or both, excluding [Critical Status](theme__critical-status--09885a83.md) and canon-oriented themes with red colors.

The gradient page banner is from Unsplash by Luke Chesser released under the Unsplash License.  
The Header font is Space Grotesk.  
The Body font is PT Sans.  
The Title font is PT Mono.  
The monospace font is Ubuntu Mono.
