level 3.5

26 Oct 2023 15:26

Description  
Level 3.5 also known as “the main switch” is a sub-level of level 3. Its difficulty class varies throughout. The whole level looks like a hybrid of level 3 and level 4. The walls are made of brick painted white and the floor is made of concrete. Throughout the level, there are pipes and wires on the walls, ceiling, and floor that obscure more of the hallway the closer you get to the switch-room. The temperature is a cool 45 degrees fahrenheit and it can get to about 32 when you get closer to the switch. The reason for the heat fluctuations is simply the amount of fans to cool the electronics. The level also gets more and more dangerous towards the switch room, so it’s advised to stay towards the edges of the level. The level is theorized to be capable of shutting off the whole power of levels 0-999, we at M.E.G. I think it takes the power generated in level 3 and all of the power is directed towards the main switch.

The switch room  
The switch in question is located in the exact center of the level in a 7ft by 7ft room. The room has a desk that is covered in notes in an unidentified language. On the walls, there are fuse boxes. If you flip one of the switches in the breaker boxes, that level’s power will shut off. Note, there is no switch for level 6. The switch is located in the exact middle of the room, If that is flipped, levels 1-999 will lose power. There has only been one time that it has been turned off, In 2006 a wanderer found himself in the switch room. Upon flipping the switch to off, levels 1-999 lost power for 14.6 seconds, with just 14.6 seconds the smiler population increased 37%.

The switch  
The switch in question is more of a lever, it has a metallic mount that is 10 inches wide and 20 inches long. The lever’s stick is approximately 14 inches long with patches of leather wrapped around the end, on the mount the words “Main Power” are engraved with black text. The power shut off process has a built-in safty feature where the user of the switch has to twist the tip of the handle to pull it down. The breaker boxes also have a safety feature to the door, in which the user has to input a three digit code.

Entities  
This level contains smilers, skin stealers, hounds, clumps, death moths, windows, dullers, facelings, wretches, striders, and Nguithr'xurhs. As mentioned before, the danger increases when you get closer to the switch (also known as “The Center”), so it’s advised to stay towards the outer edges of the level.

Safety zones  
As long as you stay 50 miles away from the center, there will be no entities, but when you get within 30 miles of the center, there are more entities and more dead ends, making a difficult and dangerous maze. Within 20 miles of the center the halls get tight and there are an overwhelming amount of pipes and wires littering the walls and floors, there are now entities around every corner. Within 10 miles of the switch, the walls are very, very tight. There are now even more wires, some even completely blocking the way, there are now even more entities in the halls. If a wanderer somehow makes it through all of this hell, there will be a long hallway and at the end of it, the switch room is right there. The switch room has no entities, so you will be fine. In the room, there is a one way door to the hub.

Entrances and exits  
To enter this level, you can no-clip into a power box in level 3, you can also find a large, concrete pipe on any level, break it open and climb in, and follow the wires until the pipe’s wall composition becomes brick and breaks out again. To exit, you can enter the one way door in the hub, you can climb back into the pipe and follow the wires, or you can wander away 100 miles from the center and you’ll be transported to level 3. If you no-clip into a power box on level 3.5, you’ll go back to level 3.

Exploration  
The M.E.G. Has planned several explorations have been made to reach the center of level, only five have made it, team sevan was ripped to pieces by a horde of hounds, team six suffocated when they were trapped behind a wall of wires so thick it resembled nanocarbon alloy. The five teams that did make it to the center set up a base camp called “The Regulators”, they propped open the door to the hub so they can go on food runs, no one is allowed in that is not in the group, the group contains 34 people and 1 monkey.
