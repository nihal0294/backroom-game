**Level 10-ID** is the 11th level of Backroom Indonesia.

# **Description:**

![Missing alt text.](http://id-backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-10-id/IMG_20220417_040838.jpg)

One of the photos taken of the **Level 10-ID** when it was first discovered. The photographer is unknown.

**Level 10-ID** is a residential settlement situated within a non-linear industrial zone at night. This level is composed of houses, shops, convenience stores, factories, and narrow residential roads entirely paved with cold, dry paving blocks. **Level 10-ID** possesses a darkness scale similar to [Level 45-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-45-id), with a moderate danger rating. The architecture varies, though it is dominated by simple structures typical of Indonesian industrial areas in the Frontrooms. It appears that **Level 10-ID** has a consistent water supply and lighting, though the status and origin of these sources remain indeterminate.

Most buildings within **Level 10-ID** are unfurnished. Infrastructure such as public toilets, gas stations, roadside fuel kiosks (Pom Mini), and ATMs are largely inactive. However, some function as shops and minimarkets providing limited quantities of Almond Water and food for survivors, which respawn instantly every 24 hours. There are also reports of shops sporadically selling retail gasoline, activated charcoal, and 3kg LPG canisters. Three known Wi-Fi networks exist within the level: Mahira, Dinandra, and Agus; their passwords remain unknown.

Furthermore, a spatial anomaly affects residential structures. Any house out of a human’s direct line of sight will vanish and be replaced by a completely different building. This anomaly, however, does not affect buildings designated as shops.

Prolonged exposure to **Level 10-ID** (44 hours or more) triggers acute paranoia and hallucinations. Victims perceive eyes within the windows of every house, some of which appear to maintain direct eye contact. In severe cases, this culminates in a dissociative disorder where the victim refuses all sustenance, often leading to death by starvation or malnutrition before they can exit the level. Survivors are strictly advised to stay indoors, avoid all windows when outside, or head immediately to the main outpost before the onset of anomalous symptoms.

# **Entity**

The Runner (Pelari) is the only known entity currently inhabiting **Level 10-ID**, maintaining a nomadic existence. Due to its nature, attempts to facilitate direct interaction or communication are conducted by first incapacitating the entity’s legs with a kitchen knife. Survivors are warned not to obstruct the Runner’s path unless they wish to risk total erasure from existence. It is theorized that this entity migrated from another unidentified level before settling here within the last decade.

# **Bases, Outposts, and Communities**

## **Abiprasada Cêmani**

![Missing alt text.](http://id-backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/level-10-id/IMG_20220420_051830.jpg)

An effort to take picture of the main base station from outside.

- The group actively contributes to guiding and training newcomers on survival strategies within the Backrooms, specifically within Level 10-ID.
- This group operates as a Forward Outpost, headquartered in a 115-meter-tall building surrounded by a complex of smaller industrial factories.
- The outpost is equipped with adequate facilities and resources to monitor and safeguard survivors from anomalous symptoms, providing medical care, psychological therapy, food supplies, and antibiotics.
- It maintains a permanent staff of approximately 122 members. A portion of this personnel is deployed throughout the level under strict security protocols to provide field assistance to survivors.
- All members have successfully adapted to the topography and anomalies of **Level 10-ID**. They possess comprehensive data derived from initial discovery research, enabling them to remain resilient against the level’s inherent cognitive hazards.

# **Entrances and Exits**

## **Entrances**

- Building at KM 111 on [Level 11-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-11-id) has access to one of the houses
- Entering a random room on [Level 3-ID](level-3-id.md) on the tenth floor may lead to one of the houses.

Currently, these are the only confirmed and known main entrances to **Level 10-ID**.

## **Exits**

- To exit, follow the paved bridge, which will lead directly to [Level 11-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-11-id).
- Entering a teak doors that connect to another door on [Level 9-ID](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-9-id) in one of the structures presumed to be a school. Level 9-ID can also be accessed through an alley marked by the Abiprasada cêmani group.

Proceed with caution and care.
