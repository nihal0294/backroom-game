Hello, I put this so I can free myself from the great weight of having many drafts that are not so complete and others that are just starting. Mostly I recommend that you take it as a group of drafts that I created myself, in case you don't know what to read or see. Also, you would do me a great favor if this is not removed, since for me this would be like at least publishing an article or several articles that have been in a warehouse for a long, long time (gathering dust). In short, I prefer that you grab this to read if you have free time, or if you want to know more spelling errors (I didn't understand myself).

  

##### I have to warn you that I am not looking for any criticism of any kind, it is only in case you want to read my work.

### I wish you happy reading!!!
