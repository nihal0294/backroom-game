The Hourglass Collection: Entity 998 (Hourglass), Level 401 (Vremya Clock Tower), and a Tale

27 Oct 2022 19:49

This project has three pages: The Entity 998, called "Hourglass", its home Level 401, called "Vremya Clock Tower" and a currently unnamed Tale to give extra story. I designed it as sort of a "final boss" entity, if you know what I mean by that.

**Gist of Entity:** Entity 998, also known as "Hourglass", is a tall, jet-black humanoid entity which wears a mask similar to a human man, but with a crude drawing of sharp teeth on it. It also is known to hold an object in its hands that looks similar to an hourglass, hence its name. It is an extremely hostile entity that can appear in any level, whenever it wants to, but has a particular fondness towards Level 401. Unable to be photographed or recorded, it stalks its targets silently but swiftly, and marks its victims death location with an hourglass and takes them to an unknown location.

**Gist of Level:** Level 401, "Vremya Clock Tower", is an infinitely ascending clock tower, that is filled to the brim with strange machinery and pictures indescribable in language. An odd property is that no matter how far up or down you go, the clock will always be the same distance away from you. This is where Hourglass spends most of its time, and where it is always hostile to every wanderer or Entity inside. Those trapped must survive 24 hours inside to escape.

**Gist of Tale:** A story about a man and a woman attempting to survive inside of the Vremya Clock Tower and Entity 998.
