Level 75-“After the rain, the sky becomes overcast”

22 Aug 2025 13:11

![IMG20250628191853.jpg](https://i.postimg.cc/8zcLPkpd/IMG20250628191853.jpg)Level 75 is a small space consisting of a group of long-abandoned warehouses and a dirt road. The road is about 3 meters wide and 50 meters long, which is also the length of the level. It is separated from the outside world by iron fences and woods on the north. On the south is a group of warehouses, arranged from one to nine from east to west. The warehouses here are about 70 cm above the ground. Each warehouse has a corresponding door painted red. The walls are ordinary cement walls with propaganda slogans painted on them, but they are usually written by party guests. Once the ground is wet, it will become very muddy and difficult to walk.

It's worth noting that while this level is home to numerous dangerous entities, they remain silent during rainy days. When the weather dries out, the entities become increasingly aggressive and are able to detect explorers. Conversely, during rainy days, they remain quietly within the warehouse. Fortunately, this level appears to be in its rainy season, with rain occurring approximately one week out of every month. If someone is lucky enough to find a cell phone, the weather system above can reveal a month's rainfall. However, this also means the phone's Wi-Fi will be constantly on, so discard the phone after viewing the weather log.

However, there are safe places. For example, Warehouse 8 is absolutely safe and houses Meg's headquarters. It's separated from Warehouses 7 and 9, and entities never enter. Warehouses 1 and 7 restock supplies like almond water. Warehouses 4, 5, and 6 are connected, and though invisible from the outside, there are no walls separating them. Warehouse 6 is overgrown with weeds, Warehouse 5's door is locked, and Warehouse 4 contains "Smiler" , but it never leaves. Warehouse 9 has “window”. Warehouse 3 is surrounded by balloons and the message "Come in =)." Entering the door leads to Level Fun. Warehouse 2 is inaccessible, and Noclip Warehouse 1 takes you somewhere in Level 1.

Warehouse 8 on this level serves as the M.E.G. base, with a permanent staff of five. The duties at this level are quite simple. One person greets explorers at the entrance and determines whether to admit travelers based on the risk level. The others simply check that the warehouse door is closed. Due to the uncertainty at this level, all objects other than essentials will be transported to Level 11.

The entrance is 5 kilometers southeast of M.E.G. Headquarters at Level 11. Exits are Level 1, Level 11, and Level Fun.
