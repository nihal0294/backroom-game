# Description

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/object-c-57/binoculars.jpg)

A Binocular.

In terms of appearance, **Binoculars** are near identical to their Frontrooms counterparts. The left and right barrels of the object are engraved with the **B.H.A.A.** group icon and the **B.H.A.A.** number corresponding to the Binoculars' owner.

**Binoculars** already began production on the 7th of July, 2022 by the **B.H.A.A.** However, only small quantities were produced, and those were restricted only to **B.H.A.A.** members.

---

# Functions

One of the uses of **Binoculars** is to act as an identity card of any **B.H.A.A.** member. Every encrypted document by the **B.H.A.A.** is able to be decrypted using a pair of **Binoculars**. **B.H.A.A.** members are able to identify other members by using the object to check if others possess **Binoculars** as well. If a person possesses the object, a green light will be flashed from it, indicating that person is a **B.H.A.A.** member.

Another use for **Binoculars** is to function as a portable observatory, allowing users to be able to use it to observe the constellations of [Level C-337](http://backrooms-wiki-cn.wikidot.com/level-c-337), without having to use the observatory. Additionally, this object be used to observe stars or skyscrapers in other levels with tall buildings or a night sky. This allows users to be able to locate **B.H.A.A.** bases in Level 11 and Level C-337.

**Binoculars** are also able to be used against entities sensitive to eye contact, such as [Smilers](entity-3.md). Using the object to observe these entities will daze them for a period of time. While the initial effectiveness of the object against Smilers was inferior to that of [Smiler Repellent](object-3.md), the latter soon became completely ineffective against them; thus, it became the main method of defeating Smilers for **B.H.A.A.** members.

One last use of **Binoculars** is that their effects are only activated by their "owner". Thus, this object has become the best method to identify their "owner".

---

# Materials

The lenses of the **Binoculars** are made of a special crystal that is extremely rare to find, with the only sources being from [Level 11](level-11.md), where a few shards of it were found. Researchers of the **B.H.A.A.** have discovered special properties from this type of crystal and produced a batch of **Binoculars**, passing them out to **B.H.A.A.** members. But they found out that the total quantity of the crystals was not enough to produce a single pair of **Binoculars** for every wanderer in the Backrooms. Currently, the M.E.G. is searching Level 11 as well as other levels for the crystal, hoping that, soon, mass production of **Binoculars** can be achieved.

> Thus, after a day, the **Binoculars** you hold in your hands were born.  
> However, considering the rarity of the **Crystal Constellation**, no plans on mass production have been currently made.
