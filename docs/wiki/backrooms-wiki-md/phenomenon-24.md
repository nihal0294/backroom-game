# Description:

Phenomenon 24, dubbed "No-Clip Derailing", is the process of being "derailed" so-to-speak, in the middle of no-clipping.  
On a very rare occurrence, while no-clipping, the teleportation can become increasingly unstable to the point where the person, object, or entity doing the no-clipping, will be “derailed” from said no-clipping, stopping them in a void. This void is dubbed "midway" as it seems to be the middle reality between point 'A' and point 'B' during no-clipping. This Midway is where every single thing in the backrooms briefly passes through to transport it to the next point.

# Frequency:

To this date, there have only been two recorded instances of a derailing. The first of which was from the aforementioned letter, and the second one being a M.E.G. operative, sent to discover the truthfulness behind this phenomenon, then only known to exist on the piece of paper.

# Discovery:

The following is a letter, written by an anonymous wanderer located floating about on [Level 128](level-128.md).

> May 4th, 2011
>
> > I don't know how to explain where I am, but it's some sort of void. It’s pitch black here, but I see flashing lights. I'm not sure how I got here, BUT, I do remember I had just no-clipped my way out of that arcade place, level 40 I think. Suddenly I'm here. I see flashes of people zoom away, at least that’s what I’m pretty positive the lights are, as well as random objects, and what I had feared the most, some weird entity zipped right past me. The only logical theory I could come up with is that I am in some sort of no-clip traveling space. Maybe this is a level, I don't know, but damn this is a weird place.  
> > Even though this void is not like any other level, I still feel things like hunger and sleep, I still feel all of that. I'm scared I will starve to death here, and my corpse will zip around like the other things here.  
> > Anyways, I'll be holding onto this paper for now, as something I can just look at, hopefully it will leave my hands and fall into someone else's someday.  
> > As a side note, this paper also zoomed right past me but suddenly got like blown away from its path or something. I couldn’t move to get it, the dimensions of this void are strange, but after enough reaching, I grabbed hold of it (All I know is that I am standing on solid ground, but it feels like the shapes of this thing are impossible. Whatever, I’ll figure it out).  
> > Sincerely,  
> > [Smudged out, illegible]

On the back of this paper were random doodles with seemingly no correlation to The Midway, No-Clip Derailing, or anything similar. If wanderers hold any theories or possible explanations to these doodles, be sure to contact M.E.G. through any method.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/phenomenon-24/doodles.jpg)

The doodles on the back of the wanderer's note

This paper was found by a M.E.G. operative, James Bozzelli and although it was first thought to be a joke, the same operative tried no-clipping until he reached this void. With a radio equipped, he was able to transmit a message lasting just short of five seconds to a M.E.G.Outpost.

The following is that audio message.

> **Date Recovered:** September 2nd, 2013  
> **Begin Audio Transcript**
>
> ---
>
> **James:** Hello? This is agent James, Can you hear me?  
> **Outpost Operator:** Yes, we can hear you.  
> **James:** I’ve got good news and bad news. Good news, I finally got to the midway. Bad news, this is a lot more annoying than I thought it would be  
> **Outpost Operator:** James, is The Midway treating you ok? I’m going to get someone else on the line, it seems the connection is failing.  
> *(Muffled talking can be heard in the distance.)*
>
> ---
>
> **End Audio Transcript**

James Bozzelli has never made contact with this, or any known outpost to this day.

# Do’s and Don’ts:

If you find yourself stuck in The Midway, here is what you should and should not do during, before, and after the event.

### Do:

- **Before:** Have sufficient supplies on you at all times, especially when you plan to no-clip.
- **During:** Stay calm, do not panic.
- **During:** Ration your supplies, and find any way to communicate, whether that be via paper, radio, or anything else.
- **During:** Comfort yourself, and if possible, sleep, you want to keep a sharp, focused mind.
- **After:** Immediately find another wanderer, if you cannot on the level, do not be afraid to no-clip or simply exit through another one.
- **After:** Locate more supplies, weapon, and if possible, take a moment to ease your mind.

### Don’t:

- **Before:** No-clip excessively, this will heighten your chances of derailing.
- **Before:** Waste supplies.
- **Before:** Get injured.
- **During:** Waste supplies.
- **During:** Panic, in other words, do not try to lose focus on your task of surviving, it will not be helpful.
- **During:** Make frantic decisions, they could end in unpredictable ways.
- **After:** Continue no-clipping excessively for no apparent reason.
- **After:** Forget about scouting for wanderers and supplies.

Ever since the date 11/07/16, new evidence of No-Clip Derailing has been recorded. The M.E.G. Information Department has provided a list for the safety of other wanderers. These updates unfortunately were not able to be added to the regular article, and were instead stored in the Updates Log. Below is the aforementioned list provided in the form of a Tabview.

  

- [*11/07/16*](javascript:;)
- [*07/08/17*](javascript:;)
- [*10/12/19*](javascript:;)

**Discovery:**  
On November 7th, 2016, a M.E.G. operative found a video camera containing a video clip of what seemed to be The Midway. The video lasted only 53 seconds, with no audio, and severely damaged footage.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/phenomenon-24/camera1.jpg)

The video camera that was found

**Contents:**  
The video showed, in grainy detail, a pitch black void, with lights flashing by the camera in different distances and different paths. The video also showed a glimpse of the ground in The Midway, the same color of pitch black, but seemingly wet all over with a yet unknown liquid. The wanderer taking this video bounced a ball in front of the camera, which seemed to randomly hit invisible forces, confirming that the placement of walls and floors around The Midway are peculiarly placed.  
**Information Gathered:**

- The base surface of The Midway is coated in an undistinguishable liquid.
- The placement of other walls, or bases, have a structurally impossible design, and seem to be invisible, though also coated in the same liquid.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/phenomenon-24/bottlew.jpg)

The same bottle, placed on a different patch of grass to recreate the discovery.

**Discovery:**  
On July 8th, 2017, a plastic water bottle was found lying in a patch of grass on [Level 186](level-186.md) by a random wanderer who was in need of water. He would later hand this over to a M.E.G. agent, declaring:

> *"It tastes strange."*

Before moving on again, not to come into contact with this agent again.

**Contents:**  
The water bottle itself did not provide much, but it was the wrapper around the bottle that was truly intriguing to agents. On the inside of the wrapper, contained a barely legible note, it read:

> *"I suddenly opened my eyes to see this strange void with weird lights, shooting stars maybe? There is some weird type of water or something of the sorts on the ground. I bottled it up and am letting it float away. Hopefully someone [illegible] t[h]is."*

When agents inspecting the bottle became aware of The Midway, they immediately made the connection and got to work to figure out what the sparse amount of liquid in the bottle was. With very little tasting equipment at hand, the results gathered were minimal, and some, useless. These results were compiled in the **"Information Gathered"** section.

**Information Gathered:**

- The liquid is most likely **not** water.
- The taste of the liquid was mainly described as that of walnuts.
- From all testing done, this liquid has never been harmful or fatal.
- This liquid evaporated at an average of 103°C (217.4°F)
- This liquid froze at an average of 2°C (35.6°F)

**Note: These temperatures were slightly above that of water's (100°C, 0°C) in every trial.**

**Discovery:**  
On October 12th, 2019,a wooden box containing a few peculiar items was brought into a small M.E.G. Outpost. The wanderer who brought it in stayed around for the investigating done on the box, but promptly left in pursuit of The Midway, something that M.E.G. Operatives warned her not too.

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/phenomenon-24/boxw.JPG)

The wooden box upon initial investigation and discovery.

**Contents:**  
Within the box were a note, a wine glass, and a pen.  
**The Wine Glass:** The wine glass lying inside the box was nothing strange, but there was residue of strange a foggy liquid, resembling the one mentioned in the last entry. There was also hair in the cup, which will be explained later.  
**The Pen:** The ball-point pen inside the box was what was likely used to write the note. The pen was out of ink, likely due to the overuse of it on the back of the note paper.  
**Note Paper:** The note paper was written by a wanderer named Michael. This is where the ties to The Midway comes from.

> Don't know the date, but this is where I would have written it.
>
> > Not too long ago (although I can't really tell how long it's been), I was no-clipping out of this level, and suddenly, I began to uncontrollably bump my head onto something in this black void while no-clipping. I thought it was a "glitch" or something, but I'm still here, so if it is, it ain't a good one. I was in here alone, until I found an older woman named Fran. Because of the weird ways the invisible walls work here, it took me very long to get to where Fran was. She had this note paper I am using in her make-shift purse. She was the only company I had in this strange void, and although she calmed me down at first, we both started to panic soon enough. With a lack of food and water, maybe some other factors, Fran passed not-so-peacefully while I was asleep. I have nothing here now, and feel I will meet the same fate. I have seen things no-clip out of here randomly, and am hoping that these pieces of our memories will find someone too. I put in a wine glass we shared, three pens, a scissor, used to cut some of our hair I left in there, and of course this note. Hopefully someone finds this and believes every word I say, Be weary.  
> > Regards,
> >
> > > Michael

**Information Gathered:**

- Multiple individuals can be present in The Midway at once.
- Even if contained in some sort of container, not all the contents will be transported.
- Death is fully possible in The Midway.
