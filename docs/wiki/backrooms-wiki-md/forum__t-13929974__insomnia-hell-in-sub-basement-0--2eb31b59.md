**Conclusion:** I like this a lot. The only real issue I can see with it is that in the first half, the dialogue kind of disrupts the tone. Maybe look at how you did dialogue and inner monologue stuff in the second half, and see if you can replicate that for the first half. That being said, it isn't that big of an issue, and I'm not going to make you do structural changes like that if you don't want to, so I'm going to give it a greenlight (provided you fix the grammar issues). If you do end up making larger changes and want me to take another look, I'd be happy to.

Also, quick grammar lesson since most of your grammar mistakes were based on one very specific rule. So, you only have a comma before "and" if the things on both sides are complete sentences. What you can do is ignore the "and" and everything before it, and ask yourself "is this a complete sentence?", and if it isn't then no comma  
Ex: I found a hallway with dead lights, and I got an hour of sleep before they turned back on.  
"I got an hour of sleep before they turned back on." is a complete sentence so there should be a comma  
Ex: I slowly get up, and sway unsteadily.  
"sway unsteadily" is not a complete sentence, so no comma.

Also, it isn't a problem that you posted it on another site.
