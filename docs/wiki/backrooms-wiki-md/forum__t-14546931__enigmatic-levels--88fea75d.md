Recently in the Discord we've had a discussion about whether we should merge Enigmatic Levels with the mainlist, and there have been arguments thrown left and right about why they should or should not be merged. Personally I see both sides and do not have a preference, though I lean towards keeping them separate slightly.

What are your thoughts? I thought I would bring this up to you forum users who can't access Discord.

Re: Enigmatic Levels

01 Mar 2022 18:32

I think keeping them separate would be much easier for several reasons. For starters, they're not meant to be proper "levels," in a sense. Many of them are better off on their own because they give their own stories. Yes, normal levels can do the same, but the enigmatic levels do it in such a way that they deserve to be their own separate thing.  
In addition to this, the entire concept of enigmatic levels is that they *aren't* actual levels. If you were to merge them with the normal levels, I think it would cause a lot of confusion and unnecessary clutter in the list.  
Keeping them separate also makes it much easier to find them. Basically, I just think they should be kept separate because they're not meant to be the same thing lol.
