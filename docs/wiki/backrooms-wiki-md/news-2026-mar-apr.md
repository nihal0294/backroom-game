# April Fools

![Missing alt text.](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/local--files/collab:site-news/Praetor)

Live photo of Praetor after his successful operation. Thank you, Praetor, for sacrificing your kidneys to save us from the wrath of Pane Kixels!

It was another stressful April here at the office! A23 broke down the office doors and Pane Kixel’s himself said he’d feed us to the Bactaria Monster if we don’t pay him for the rights to his IP. Thankfully, thanks to some quick thinking from , with the help of , we got a subscription service up and running to generate revenue while the rest of us drew straws in the breakroom to determine whose kidney we were selling on the black web!

Please give a thank you to when he comes back from his dialysis appointment for selling his kidneys, and thank you, the reader for paying to access content you thought was protected under Creative Commons licensing!

# Subclusters

April saw the introduction of Subclusters! Not quite canons, but moreso groups of levels that share similar lore, entities, traits and more! Like a canon, a Subcluster must have 3 or more pages to be added onto the [hub](series-and-subclusters-hub.md)! We hope this will improve your reading and writing experience!
