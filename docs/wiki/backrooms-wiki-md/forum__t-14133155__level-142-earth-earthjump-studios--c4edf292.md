1:00 am binge-read session

22 Nov 2024 21:23

great concept, but it could be executed better. offset 0 is serviceable, but prose from offset 1 onwards stumbles into many of the same pitfalls that plagues early 2020 levels.

> Do you remember the heartbeat of the sand caressing your unblemished toes and the freshness of the undulating ocean, back when discovery was a friend and not a monster?

stellar opening. the entire dream segment is illustrated beautifully, and provides precise, visceral descriptions for the reader to dwell on.

> Wanderers who enter Level 142 will appear at an empty reception area with several hallways in all directions leading to the VR rooms.
>
> Each VR room is equipped with a periodically-refilling water cooler, as well as a refrigerator with frozen food. Each room contains two stools and a table with a VR headset on top of it. The model is consistent across all rooms and the headsets never seem to run out of power.

unfortunately, right after hitting offset 1, we revert back to the basic structure of classic levels, and all momentum is lost. there's a missed opportunity to incorporate some of the delightful imagery of 0 into 1, but as of now, this feels like a decent tale tacked onto a 2020 nothingburger.

**-1**
