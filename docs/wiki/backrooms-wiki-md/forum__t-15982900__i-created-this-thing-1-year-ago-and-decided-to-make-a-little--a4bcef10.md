Well I’ve read it over and here’s what I found

SPaG: “the level is infinite and very confusing to wanderers” Make the t in “the” uppercase. “ the level looks like a garage” Make the t in “the” uppercase.

Coding: Anytime you say level 1.9 bold it (you can bold things like this: \*\* 1.9 \*\* just don’t do a space between the stars and the number). You need to link your author page, who made the image, and what the image is licensed under.

Concept: I think your concept is good but a few things could be worked on. The incident could definitely have some more info on it. Your description could have included more info (what you have written is still good though I just think more could be added to it).

Other then that I think that your level is very good and I hope that it someday gets a greenlight.
