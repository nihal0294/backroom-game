05 Aug 2022 08:08

> This level was discovered on June 18, 2012.

This information doesn't have much relevance to the actual page; why should this matter to the reader? If there isn't a real reason for this to be here, I'd suggest removing it.

> The Shop/The Outside

Both of these sub headers don't really need to be here; imo it'd look a lot visually better if they were removed.

> It has random Poles that are not in perfect arrays, and Almond Water, and sometimes, random bottles. near The Poles.

Rewrite this entire section as what I've shown below for clarity:

\*There are random poles located throughout the level that usually have bottles of Almond Water near the base.\*

> and Almond Water, and

"Almond Water" no longer exists on this wiki; remove references of it from your page.

> The supermarket has white tiled floors, and tiled roofs, with fluorescent lights.

Rewrite this as what's shown below:

\*The supermarket is characterized by its white tiles that cover both the floor and roof, with fluorescent lights overhead.\*

> The hobbit holes have wooden poles holding them up

There are no previously mentioned "hobbit holes", please include some introduction on them earlier in the page.

> probably from Level 4

I'd change this to:

\*most likely from Level 4\*

> The hobbit holes have wooden poles holding them up, and old computers and TVs, probably from Level 4, and a singular mattress, as there are reports of random mattresses in this level spawning, this theory is proven.

I'd say this paragraph highlights my main issue with the page; you bring up one detail as if you're about to explain it, only to bring in two completely other things that complicate everything even more. I'd suggest that your really take your time to explain these; as it is, it's incredibly convoluted.

> Random blankets can be found too.

Similarly to the date, why does this matter and why should the reader know this? For more information on this, I'd suggest you look at this resource: <https://www.masterclass.com/articles/writing-101-what-is-chekhovs-gun-learn-how-to-use-chekhovs-gun-in-your-writing>
