Replace the code above with below (in the source code of the Toggle Font Component) and the font will work.

```
.Holmes {
   font-family: Italianno;
   font-size: {$size}rem;
   line-height: normal;
}
```

```
.Holmes {
   font-family: Yesteryear;
   font-size: {$size}rem;
   line-height: normal;
}
```

Good god the Masked Maidens are old. This page started in 2021, and the last forum message was **3 years ago**.

Regardless these are my thoughts on this page:

This page is good. It has a unique concept and explains how a group that's on the fringes of society organizes, but it makes no sense. How do you use Maiden's Ink on web pages? Do you write on a tablet with a ballpoint pen? And even if it does work, why doesn't it allow things to be painted over it, or does some poor worker of the Masked Maidens sit on the internet all day undoing the M.E.G.'s attempts to write over this. Also what does the G.P.D. think of this? Their servers are literally being controlled, overwritten, and deleted by a group that they have no knowledge of or beef with. Also how is this ink invisible? I understand the aura stuff means magic [note to add this to the magic discussion later today], but c'mon it's still present. Put it on a wall and you'd surely notice that there's a clear liquid on there, even if it's incredibly hard to read exactly. You'd also notice people literally writing on walls, paper, or tablets with transparent ink!

This isn't as overpowered as it should be to give the M&M's a chance against the M.E.G., and yet I don't think it's underpowered either. It is simply an Object that exists. The writing and style is pretty good, but there are still SPaG errors (4 years after written btw). Overall I cannot downvote it because it's not that bad, and I can't upvote it because of it's problems.

A resounding novote. Thanks for your time.
