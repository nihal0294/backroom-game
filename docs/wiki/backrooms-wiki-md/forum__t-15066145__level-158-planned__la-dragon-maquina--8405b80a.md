> M.E.G online staff are currently

Add another period after "G".

> incidents, and no culprits have been found.

I'd change "and" to "though", although this change isn't required by any means.

> NOTICE: There have been multiple unauthorized edits to this page which have been found to contain harmful information. M.E.G online staff are currently investigating the incidents, and no culprits have been found. Please be cautious of the information on this page.

I'd rather that this was above the SD so that this is the first thing that the reader sees; makes it a more alluring hook.

> A picture of the town, taken by one of the discoverers of this level.

This isn't the proper image block code, the correct code can be found here: [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/templates](templates.md)

> by a group of Wanderers exploring Level 129

Don't capitalize "Wadnerers" in this instance.

> village that seems to be covered in snow,

Remove "seems to" and make "that" into "that's".

> dubbed The Town, The Market, and the Castle

I'd put quotations around each of the section's titles; also, capitalize the third use of "the", the one beside the word "Castle".

> Each section takes up about 1/3rd of

If you put two carrots (^) on either side of "rd" you can make it into a super script which would look a lot better here.

> - A small sketch of the layout of Level XXX, created by an old friend.

Again, this isn't the proper image block code, the correct code can be found here: [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/templates](templates.md)

> The Town is the smallest part of the Level

"Level" shouldn't be capitalized here.

> and the section where most Wanderers end up

"Wanderers" shouldn't be capitalized here.

> when arriving to the Level

"Level" shouldn't be capitalized here.

> Items such as Firesalt and Almond Water

"Almond Water" no longer exists on this wiki; remove references of it from your page.
