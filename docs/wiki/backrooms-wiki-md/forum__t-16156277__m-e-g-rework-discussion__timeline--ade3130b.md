M.E.G. Rework Discussion: Timeline

12 Jul 2023 05:48

For lore compatibility, as well as to improve the quality of the M.E.G. going forward, the M.E.G. timeline will be split into two parts: The "pre-rebirth" M.E.G., and the "post-rebirth" M.E.G. The transition period between these two will be referred to as "The Rebirth".

"Pre-rebirth" (aka Legacy M.E.G. era)

- All events prior to the new structure being created (i.e. The past M.E.G.)

- From the group's origin, until the "Rebirth" takes place

- During this time, the old M.E.G. structure will still exist, with regiments and divisions

- Most of this period is a struggle for the group to keep up with its immense growth

- Justin Rivers (Stretch), Katherine Rivers (Kat), and Andrew Donovan (Andrew) are Overseers A, B, and C respectively during this time

The group's history will be preserved to allow lore to stay compatible, and it will even be expanded upon. The expanded past lore will serve to give the overseers and other important characters more personality. The struggles and drama these characters faced will better explain the many decisions that were previously made, as well as the separation of other groups from the M.E.G. (such as the B.N.T.G. and U.E.C.) This will also better flesh out the old structure as it evolved.

The "Rebirth"

- A period of major reform in the M.E.G., where the group's structure is entirely re-organized into sectors (a transition period from pre-rebirth to post-rebirth where the group is undergoing the changes they intend to make)

- Overseer A (Stretch/Justin Rivers) will be removed from his position as overseer

- Overseer C will also be removed from his position

- New overseers will emerge to fill the power vacuum left behind by Stretch's and Andrew's removal, including overseer A and C replacements (Going from 3 to 5 overseers)

The numerous hardships and conflicts taking place in the group will eventually lead to the decision for reform, which is what this event is. With the group having grown incredibly large at this point, it can afford to change how it operates to even better pursue the goals they've laid out.

"Post-rebirth"

- This is anytime after the new structure is created (The new, present-day M.E.G. going forward)

- The 7 sectors structure will exist going forward

- At this point, the group is stable and able to “safely” sustain itself

- With the new structure, the group can much more easily keep up with its immense growth, and it marks a new era for the M.E.G.

-The nice thing about having these two distinct eras for the M.E.G. is that people can have their canons take place in either time period, or even evolve it along with the group. These two time periods can work separately for canons, and together act as one unified canon.

Thoughts on all of this?
