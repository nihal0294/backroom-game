Hello from an online newbie.

07 Oct 2024 17:08

Greetings!

A little introduction: I've been writing for some time now, either to satisfy my brain's own dopamine rush or for academic clubs. I have since developed, what some my call an addiction, but what I prefer to call a fascination for clinical writing, liminal spaces and subtle horror. In a word, the cold, scientific description and examination of paranormal objects.

I have only recently (a few years prior) found out about these big online communities, entirely dedicated to this passion. I have refrained for some time now to join, out of fear, as I'm definitely in the top 10 least tech-savvy human beings. However, my curiosity has finally won me over. Though I still find all of this very daunting.

In a word, I am most enthused to enter your world and excuse myself in advance for my lack of experience in the digital realm.
