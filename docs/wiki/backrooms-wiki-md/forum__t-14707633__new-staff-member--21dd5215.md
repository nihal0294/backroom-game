I'm currently working in the following teams.[1](javascript:;)

---

## Tech Team

The technical team is tasked with all the ins and outs of the site's code. Whether that means maintaining site themes, important components, or the general presentation of the site's design, members of this team make sure that the site runs smoothly and that the code is neat and organised.

## Rewrite Team

The rewrite team is responsible for keeping track of rewrites. Their duties include maintaining the list of active rewrites, approving rewrites that require staff permission, and contacting rewriters after their permit expires.

## Guide Team

The guide team is the team that makes sure that the information present specifically in the guides is up to date. Mostly focused on the writing up of these guides, this team makes sure that new members get a good idea of what and what not to do in the time they spend in the community.

---

If yall have an issue with anything related to these teams and aren't available on the discord, contact me via Wikidot Private messages.
