Good Item page

19 Jun 2022 03:00

I really like the idea of an indestructible vial with various liquids/crystals of various effects. Not only are the various liquids useful in various situations (I imagine you can use the helpful ones on yourself and harmful ones on dangerous entities/your enemies), but the vials themselves could be incredibly useful.

Think about it. Once you use up the liquid inside and empty the crystal out of it, you could rinse out the vial and use it to store extremely dangerous substances. These vials could replace the storage for substances like liquid silence and pyroil (molten firesalt), among other dangerous liquids/small items.

Maybe you could even use the vial as a defense against certain entities/weapons, if you really think creatively with how to use it. If it can't be destroyed, than nothing can cut or smash through it…Maybe you could even cleverly find a way to use it to hold open doors and similar things that normally can't be forced back open, since it won't break the vial… I think outside the box, and this vial alone seems super useful with a lot of potential.
