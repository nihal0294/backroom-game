I'll change my vote to +1 as the temperatures are less unreasonable. Though, I still doubt that someone could survive 48°C for two days.

The core human body temperature is 36.5–37.5°C. The only way to keep your body cool when the outside environment has a temperature above this level is by sweating, but this can only work effectively up ~40°C. Above this temperature, you body temperature will gradually rise until you die of heat stroke. I don't know exactly how long it would take, but when you are exposed 50°C, your body temperature takes an hour to rise 1°C, so you would be dead in a few hours at that temperature. 48°C is only slightly below that.

Not to mention that sweating would use up fluids, so you would die of dehydration quicker.
