**Level 7.1**  
Survival difficulty:  
Class 2

- Safe
- Secure
- Low entity count

Level 7.1 is a sub-level of level 7, the eighth level of the backrooms  
**DESCRIPTION:**  
Level 7.1, otherwise known as shallow waters, is a bay off the shore of what seems to be a beach. When the level is entered, the most notable feature is that you are on a white boat. The boat's length is about 3 meters and the width is 0.7 meters

Level 7.1 is mostly explored compared to it's main level, Level 7. There are 3 main sections, consisting of The Boat, The Bay & The hotel.

THE BOAT  
As the name suggests, It is a boat. There are 4 sub-sections and 2 floors to the boat, They are listed as such:  
The Diner  
This is the First sub-section of the boat. It appears as a small restaurant with brown tables and chairs with some having Almond Water and Agrugua Fruit. The items seem to have a regeneration ability, as when some one grabs an item, the items will be regenerated again if you look away or leave the level somehow with no cause, Making it an infinite source of Almond water and Agrugua Fruit.  
The Rim  
This is the Second sub-section of the boat. It consists of some couches and a table with the exact same items and regenerative abilities.  
The Party  
This is the Third sub-section of the boat. As the name suggests, it is a birthday party area on the second floor with white tables and chairs that have the same items and same regenerative abilities as previously mentioned. However this section has some special features such as Pink and White Balloons, some party hats on the tables, and a birthday sign. This sub-section also has both adult and child facelings with party hats.  
The Cockpit  
This is the last sub-section of the boat. It is as the name suggests, it is the cockpit of the boat with an adult faceling driving the vehicle.

THE BAY  
This area is the main area of the entire level surrounded by a beach, its area in diameter from one side to the other is about 2 kilometers There are 2 sub-sections to the section consisting of:  
The Shallow  
This is the First sub-level of the bay. The water after the shoreline is this sub-portion of the level that spans from the shoreline of the beach to 0.5 kilometers from the beach. Its depth is about 1 millimeters from the shoreline, to 3 meters by the 0.5 kilometers mark.  
The **DEEP**  
This sub-section of the bay starts about 0.5 kilometers from the shoreline to the middle. The length from one start to the other is about 1 kilometer. Its depth starts from 3 meters from the start to 10m by the middle.

THE HOTEL  
This is the final sub-section of the level. There are 2 sub-sections of the level. Here are them:  
The Docks  
This is the First sub-section of the hotel. After waiting abut 12 hours after entering the level, you will be sent here. This sub-section of the level consists of multiple docks with other boats lined up.  
The Fountain  
This is the Second sub-section of the hotel. This sub-section consists of a fountain with stairs on the sides of the fountain. Occasionally you can find a staircase that leads to level 20.

**ENTITIES:**  
The only entities that can be found on the level are adult and child facelings, however they are completely docile so you can interact with them. Just don't attack them.

**BASES, COMMUNITIES, AND OUTPOSTS:**  
M.E.G. Base Zeta

- Seventh Most important M.E.G. Base
- Near the shoreline
- Around 100 members stationed there
- 25 members standing guard
- Open to trading

**ENTRANCES AND EXITS:**  
Entrances:

- Swim about 10 kilometers from the house in Level 7 to be sent here.
- Swimming Far enough away from the beach on level 48
- Leaving through the entrance of level 5

Exits:

- Swim to the bottom of the **DEEP** to be sent back to level 7
- Swim towards the shore and wander towards the mainland to be sent to level 48
- Wait for the boat to reach a city and swim to it to be sent to level 11
- Wait for the voyage to end and enter the hotel to be sent to level 5
- Go on a random boat to be sent to a randomized level between levels 0-50
- Enter a staircase in the fountains sub-sections for the hotel to be sent to level 20
