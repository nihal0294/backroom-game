# To βETA, with love.

rating: +29[+](javascript:; "I like it")[–](javascript:; "I don't like it")[x](javascript:; "Cancel my vote")

  
I don't have much time so I'm keeping it short.  
  
You don't know who I am, [was](entity-128.md). But that's of little importance anymore.  
  
  

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/to-beta-with-love/beta.jpg)

β

You are not the first, and definitely not the last of us. Your goal is to create a successful entry in our [system](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-0). I hope you can fix my [mistakes](level-512.md). You're my successor, and I shall leave you with my knowledge. Do not let those wanderers take advantage of your creation, as they did with mine. I guess you could thank me in a way for existing, as you wouldn't have if it wasn't for my failure to do my job. You will be provided a shell of what was once mine. Take [my creation](level-512.md), improve it, give it the respect it truly deserves.  
  
You will be successful. I promise.  
  
Farewell. **βETA.**

With Love,  
**ΩMEGA**.
