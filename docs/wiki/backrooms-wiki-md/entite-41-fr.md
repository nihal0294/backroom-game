## **Description and Biology**

[![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/entite-41-fr/Snake/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/entite-41-fr/Snake)

An image of a basilisk in daylight, it is to be noted that it is missing its maw.

[![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/entite-41-fr/light/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/entite-41-fr/light)

A bioluminescent basilisk present in a dark environment.

Rosko’s basilisks are entities similar to snakes that we can find in our world, though most have distinct abnormalities that would lead to eventual death. This may include missing jaws, eyes, nostrils or other anatomical parts. Additionally, these parts may be duplicated on the snakes’ bodies rather than wholly absent.

Some extreme cases of abnormalities exist, with some basilisks having multiple heads[1](javascript:;), bodies, and in even more extreme cases, missing skeletons or having a tail in places of their head.

Individuals greatly vary in size from one to another, ranking from a few millimetres to around 7 metres for an individual discovered in the sewage system of [Level 11](level-11.md). In most cases, their size matches that of the species of snake they resemble: A basilisk that resembles a viper will likely have a length of around 50–70 centimetres.  
Their eggs appear similar to that of a normal snake, though they emit a slight blueish glow that can be observed in the fluid within the eggs. This light gain in intensity as the embryo grows and is strongest during hatching—a powerful burst of blue light is produced when the shell is broken.  
These eggs are a popular dish for their taste and the exoticism brought by their nature. On the 27 August 2022, the [B.N.T.G.](b-n-t-g.md) estimated that the value of an egg of a Rosko's Basilisk is approximatively equal to 4 litres of [Almond Water](object-1.md).

## **Behaviour**

Whatever their appearance and abnormalities be, all basilisks have a commonality that differentiates them from a regular snake: If the area surrounding a basilisk reach a certain threshold of luminosity, they will suddenly start to shine brightly.[2](javascript:;) This ability is also used when a basilisk is in danger or wants to blind a prey.

Rosko's Basilisks are omnivorous, their habitat has forced them to adopt a more diverse diet.[3](javascript:;)  
Though, they have an obvious preference for meat, their favorite prey being [ants](trimmed__entity-89--f18eacb5.md), they don't have a lot of means to defend themselves from this predator.

In general, their behaviour match the one of the snakes of our world, you can learn more information about them by search "Snake's behaviour" or

## **Discovery**

Rosko's Basilisks suddenly appeared in late March of 2021. Their name derives from Rosko Mens, a wanderer that arrived the in a community within [level 10](level-10.md) on March 19, 2021. He brought with him the first eggs and specimens of this entity that he discovered "in a field, not so far from here".

Sightings of and interactions with this entity have been relatively rare since their discovery. Multiple groups have tried to trade their eggs, exuviae[4](javascript:;) or remains without much success during the first few months after their discovery. Their popularity increased when Tom's Diner—which can be found on [Level 1](level-1.md)— popularized their consumption.

As of early August 2022, their population had drastically increased, increasing from a few sightings per month to several per week. No explanation was yet found for this sudden increase; however, it is to be noted that this happened soon after Rosko Mens went missing during a solo exploration of [Level-45-FR](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/niveau-45-fr).
