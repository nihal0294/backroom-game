Hey there, Harvich. Your level doesn't look quite ready for greenlight, so lemme help you with that.

> "Killing a friend and regretting it so much you commit suicide will have a 50 percent chance of bringing you into this level"

- Please add a content warning at the top of the page; this shouldn't just be thrown in a reader's face.

> "Dying isn't recommended"

- I understand that you were referring to the *different* death on your level, but this is still laughable and should be removed.

**Overall Critique**  
There were too many SPaG errors and improper formatting that I cannot give a full crit on this page at the moment. Your idea is half-decent, but its just another "ooh ima level and ima drive u cra-cra" kind of things. You did not keep a consistent clinical tone, and even if you *were* going for a more informal and storytelling-type tone, you switched between tones very frequently. You barely provided any details, and even though this is meant to be a barren and non-descript level, it just didn't sit well with me.

PM me after you have fixed these, and I will gladly give a full critique on this. Happy writing!
