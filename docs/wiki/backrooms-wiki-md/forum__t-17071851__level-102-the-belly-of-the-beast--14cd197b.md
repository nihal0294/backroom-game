I might appear negative in this response, but it would be a shame if you'd just throw it all away just like that, but the article is pretty much okay (it has got its flaws and its good sides). (Very short summary: Sorry if this message is a mess, it's kind of difficult to judge such a long page like that since you've already written a lot about the biology, but not about Persons Of Interests , or introduced more unique ideas to make lots of unique dynamics.)

So it's a page about a complex sorts-of-bloody (organic matter) cave. The main problem of the page is not that it's just long, but the fact that it introduces new species/groups/people, and it explains them for a bit, before completely stopping and ignoring them until the end of the page, maybe mentioning them a few times when they are not needed? It also feels like you're writing about species, while also introducing new species without having clear ideas. The problem with the page is that all of these things feel unnecessary, because there are already lots of environmental levels talking about similar concepts, but yours isn't unique but has the potential it needs. There is still a page on the wiki about a cave made of organic matter inside of a creature with M.E.G workers feeling hopeless that they will die. And almost the same forest-based deleted page by which didn't focus on one exact thing, but rather only on many almost non-related ideas with many groups' interactions (well, it seemed like that, because it was feeling at least a bit important unlike the rest of the page), so it's almost the same case. So, if the page will get more creative, it'll be really awesome, but just describing it in clinical tone for 7,622 words is a bit too much, really. There are some people on the wiki who get bored by reading 5 paragraphs, and these 7,622 words are just.. Too long?  
At first reading all of it was interesting, but then it started repeating without giving no new proper ideas except for repeating, and slightly changing the old ones, people really don't like such long pages with an actual point but no clear purpose just as I mentioned before, but you can introduce an idea not immediately, but you can try to take your time and mix the whole thing so that it makes sense in the end, because the dialogue is already well-written. When it came to giving ideas, you decided to give the P.O.Is long names, but you didn't expand on them like actual people, but rather as just more text. The problem is that the three groups aren't different from each other, except for one that is slightly more perfect with an unknown reasons of why they survived (or at least it seemed like that when I was reading the page). But there wasn't enough information about the groups, nor any unique ideas about the groups (All of the groups are just M.E.G outposts rather than different organizations with their own diplomacy towards each other or unique P.O.Is). But then you focused only on one thing, finally. It was the dialogue.. It's pretty much perfect and it doesn't need any improvements unless you want to make it even more complicated which pretty much won't change that much since the person who is "talking" about the dialogue feels like an actual person of interest (also mentioned by me before as P.O.I). The exits and the entrances are pretty much perfectly-written as well as the licenciation.  
Your spell and grammar combined with clinical tone is so perfect, that I couldn't find any mistakes, or words used incorrectly out of the 7,622 words used! But the execution just ruins the whole thing, hope you improve this page rather than just quitting!  
Also, sorry for the wrong link I mentioned before the message was edited.
