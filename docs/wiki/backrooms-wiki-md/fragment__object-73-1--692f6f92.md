# Welcome to The SuperGreen Company![1](javascript:;)

### About Us:

Here, we sell our signature SuperGreen SuperApples! We're a small company based in [Level 11](level-11.md) who seeks to sell rare, quality items to our customers! Ever since our CEO no-clipped into the backrooms, he wanted to create a company after losing his last one, and he's sought after only the best and most unique items to sell in his new company At The SuperGreen Company, our goal is to make our customers happy with healthy, organic, pesticide free, and fresh apples. We even recycle old paper to make our advertisement posters! We also donate to charity! You can fund our charity by following the arrows on our advertisement posters in [Level 11](level-11.md) to find us at our store and buy now!

### Our Team!

At The SuperGreen Company, we're a family. Our family consists of hard and devoted workers. There's Our CEO, who wishes to remain anonymous. Our security staff are our first and last line of defense and deal with anyone trying to mess with us. Our Runners are our pride and joy, with them courageously going out and gathering our apples so we can sell them to you! They also occasionally do other tasks around the company. Our Tech staff are the ones running this page! Last but not least we have our Health experts keeping our apples super!
