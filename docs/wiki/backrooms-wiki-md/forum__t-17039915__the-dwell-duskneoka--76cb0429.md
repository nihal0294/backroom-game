This draft doesn't actually need SPaG or format critique; after reading through everything looks fine, although you could do with replacing the bold titles with proper subheaders (put 1 to 6 plus symbols before them). What it does need is concept critique, so I'm doing that anyway.

So, first off: "Habitat: Majority". This doesn't actually explain anything. The majority of what, I ask?

"leading explorers into believing the Dwell were cryptids" You could do with elaborating on this much further; in fact, this could actually be the main setting of the page. As the focus of the page right now is purely on the entity, and the entity has little to be said about it, it makes a short page which many will find uninteresting. Changing the plot would make it far more unique and allow for the page to be extended without bloating.

"Use or open an occupied item" if the entity uses itself as a weapon, it would probably be easy to predict its movement and stab it midair, so this doesn't seem like an accurate warning. Are all objects that Dwell inhabit empty? If so, it would make more sense to describe fighting the entity as a waste of time.

Along with all of these, in my opinion, the entity's appearance is kind of out of place compared to its purpose. Why does it look like it would fit in an entertainment show? And, you know what? If you think you could do it, that might actually be a better theme for the entity. Maybe, when summoned, it would try to perform tricks for whoever encounters it - the shapeshifting part could be useful for this - but if received negatively, it would then turn hostile.

Anyway, it's up to you to choose which plot you go for here, but I do strongly suggest choosing one.
