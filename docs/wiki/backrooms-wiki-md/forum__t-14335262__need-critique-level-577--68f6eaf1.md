I think that the 2nd last paragraph in the "Exits" Column needs something. Where it says "Go up to the 2nd floor, pace in a circle about 19 steps, go back down, then back up again, enter room 200, and then exit, and then re-enter, this will then bring you to Level 200." Merge "enter room 200," and "and then exit,". And try changing "And then re-enter" to "then re-enter". It's not a major change, but it may better the professionalism of the article.

Also, I think that the tone is fine. It may be a lack of complex words, perhaps, but the tone is good and has a slight professional sound to it. If you truly feel like something's wrong, then give it a good read a couple times.
