The Japanese Backrooms Wikidot community is, rather different than other language wikis. It does not accept translations from the English Wiki and any other language variants except for Level 0. It is better regarded as a sister site to [the Japanese Backrooms Fandom Wiki](https://backrooms.fandom.com/ja/wiki/Backrooms_Wiki), which accepts translations from both Wikidot English Wiki and Fandom Wikidot Wiki.
