This is my first time giving critique on here so I apologise if it is a little lacking, if you need anymore detail on what I've written please let me know. Now, my first comment is that I like the idea of villages connected by wooden walkways and the general aesthetic of darkened forest where light doesn't shine, this is helped by the choice of good images to convey this. It almost makes me think back to the Myst level The Channelwood Age if you want a little bit of extra inspiration for the looks of the level.

I have a couple of points that I think might improve the level however:

- Expanding on the entrances to this level would be helpful, are there specific levels you can no clip from or a particular method of no clipping that increases the odds of getting there?
- I'd also recommend adding a bit more danger to the level. I'm not one of those people who demands every level be flooded with entities however I think maybe adding some environmental hazards could make it more interesting. As it currently stands this level has infinite food and water making it practically a paradise in the backrooms so maybe doing something such as adding liquid pain in certain parts of the waters, poisoned fruit on the trees etc. could add a bit more tension for wanderers stuck in this level
- In expansion of the previous point I think adding more detail on the environment as a whole could help flesh out the entry e.g. do the waters occasionally flood? Are there are mysterious noises heard from the forests in the dead of night? etc.

Finally, I like the idea of Murkwood being this level exclusive material and I feel that it could be expanded upon by having people trying to get to the level to trade for Murkwood to take back with them to the rest of the backrooms as I'd imagine the wood be very valuable for people in the other levels. Maybe a faction could be set up to handle the gathering and export of Murkwood.
