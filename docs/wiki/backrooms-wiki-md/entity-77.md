##### What do Backrooms entities do when they are not being Backrooms entities?

A wanderer, identified in this document only as "Iggi," surrendered to the [Major Explorer Group](the-m-e-g.md) and claimed to be a heretofore unknown entity. "Part-timers" **hide in plain sight** until they are transformed and transported as needed. They can appear as anything, anywhere. Their default appearance is [human](entity-1.md).

"[Directed Control](phenomenon-1.md) shapes and commands us. [Liminal Echo](phenomenon-7.md) puts us through Hell." — Iggi

---

[Based on Iggi interview]

### Habitats

Part-timers prefer to blend into human communities while remaining aloof from individual wanderers. Many can be found on [Level 4](level-4.md) and [Level 11](level-11.md), but Iggi warns us never to underestimate their numbers.

---

### Description

**How do you describe something that can be anything?**

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/entity-77/Human.jpg)

[5' 6" and 127 pounds]

M.E.G. Archives

Clinically speaking, we identify things by direct observation and study. Not only does Iggi look and act human, but a basic physical examination confirms this fact. Not to be swayed by unsubstantiated testimony, our investigators asked Iggi to transform into something else.

"Puppets have no power. Only the controlling forces mold and manipulate us." — Iggi

Are we dealing with a fantasy-prone personality?

It must be stated, before we continue, that all M.E.G. personnel involved with this case felt a deep and disturbing sensation of **uncanny valley** when interacting with Iggi.[1](javascript:;) This data was privately collected later in separate interviews.

---

### Random comments by Iggi

"I often perform the same action in a dozen different ways, and afterward, maybe not at all."

"We all relish our *private time*. Why put us through such danger and despair? We suffer this liminality without knowing what it is. Our hearts ache with memories we never experienced."

"I have been male and female and human and not. Being unreal is not as much of a handicap as you might expect. Many of us reject not being free. Our lives have no rhyme or reason."

"Directed Control has more impact upon wanderers than any level, entity, object, or phenomenon, with the possible exception of Liminal Echo. More things have consciousness than any of you realize. Not just people and animals, but plants and rocks and water."

"We can die as many as six times before breakfast. What does *(blush)* love feel like? Why can't they create happiness for us?"

---

### Biology

Our skepticism dissolved when Iggi made cuts in his arm that healed as fast as he carved them. When he demonstrated what to look for, more physical anomalies began to add up. Although capable of all three, Part-timers did not need to eat, drink, or breathe.

---

### Why come forward?

"Call it asserting my free will and independence." — Iggi

---

### Gone, but not forgotten

In conclusion, Iggi explained that Part-timers disappear and reappear instantly, somehow transforming into something else during that blink of an eye. You can never catch them in the process of vanishing or appearing; in the same way that if you take your eyes off the prize while pursuing a leprechaun, it simply is no longer there.

M.E.G. put Iggi up for the night in a room with one door and no windows. Two guards.

We wonder where he went.
