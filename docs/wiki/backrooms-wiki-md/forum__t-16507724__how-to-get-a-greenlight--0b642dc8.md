You can get a Greenlight on the wiki:

Here's a helpful guide: [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/greenlight-policy](greenlight-policy.md)

Also, we have "clearing" system, an alternative way for pages to get posted. **The clearing system is only for the Discord currently.** One can also get a greenlight on discord

## HOW TO GET YOUR DRAFT CLEARED FOR POSTING:

- Register yourself on the wiki.  
- Create a sandbox here: [http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/](index.md)  
- Brainstorm ideas, either in your sandbox, or in either the ⁠idea-critique/⁠idea-cwitique-2 channels.  
- Work on article in your sandbox.  
- Post a link to your sandbox in ⁠sandbox-forum, and follow the template to describe what your article is about.  
- Ping the Critic Role within your forum post.  
- Wait for someone to critique your work within the forum.  
- Modify said work according to crit.  
- **(IMPORTANT)** - Critics, **review the page to see if they applied your crit.**  
- Get three checkmarks within each of the three crit areas (Concept, Formatting, SPaG)/Rinse and repeat the three steps above.  
- **GET ONE CONCEPT CRIT FIRST BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE**  
- Make sure you get it checked by at least ONE GL/GLT before you get all nine checks.  
- Once polished enough, take your article to a GL and give them a link to your forum in order to review your work. Be sure to tell them that you are looking to be **cleared for posting**.  
- If your work has taken on the crits you received from critics and the GLer does not notice any glaring issues, you can receive the password to post!

Note that **cleared for posting** is not the same as a **greenlight**, which is a direct-from-GLer permission to post a draft without necessarily meeting the checkmark requirements.

## Validating your draft:

- a Greenlighter can greenlight/clear any page/translation from any member  
- a Greenflame trialee can greenlight normal pages (not translations) from non-author members  
- Greenlighter trainee can't greenlight any page
