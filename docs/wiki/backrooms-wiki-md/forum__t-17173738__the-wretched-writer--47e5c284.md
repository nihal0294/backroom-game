-1

04 Aug 2025 00:15

I feel like this page just relies on the reader to insert themselves into the position of the protag, and doesn't really support the emotional arc that it wants to create within itself. While I understand the conceit of the page is that those who read it will be able to empathize with the exasperation of writer's block, it doesn't impress me personally because I think that good "metafiction" should also have a more concrete plot. I would have liked a longer descent-into-madness, and more development of this person you write about as an *actual character*. In its present state, all I see in this is a slightly cheeky nod to the expected audience, and I don't *personally* like writing where that is the only thing presented.

Hey thanks for the feedback! I think I struggle with adding detail because I personally hate when pages drone on, so it's heartening to hear that I'm not boring people reading my pages.

I'll be honest. With this page, it wasn't meant to be a piece on writer's block, but more a way for me to channel my frustrations about my real life job becoming monotonous and the sucky hellscape that is job searching. I'm not sure how I could add more plot when the purpose of the work is to just be about a breakdown and release. Perhaps I could have added more characterization, like you said, but alas, the headspace I was in didn't really allow for it, and none of my critiques mentioned anything about it.

Oh well. Next time, I'll branch out. Thanks again for the feedback!
