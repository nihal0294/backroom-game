Author commentary

29 May 2022 03:38

This is the only time I will ever give an in-depth commentary on any of my articles, so here we go:

This was my first article for The Backrooms. I wrote it months before the wikidot was created, way back when levels and entities were written on The Backrooms Fandom wikidot. That was almost three years ago now, give or take a couple months depending on when the Fandom wiki was actually made.

I don't think this level has held up particularly well. It's at the very least readable and understandable, which is actually quite high praise for my older articles. The version which is currently on the wiki is one of my later revisions— the original fandom article was only two or three paragraphs long and was hosted on a page with a variety of other, equally short and equally incompetent levels. That's just how we did things back then, I guess.

A lot of my reasons/inspirations for writing The Whiteout were very subconscious and very personal at the time. I don't want to go into too much detail because a lot of it is still too close to me, but The Whiteout and The Blackout were both levels unintentionally based on my living condition at the time of writing them. I had just moved into a partially renovated white house which hadn't even had its electricity turned on yet. So, towards the end of the day the sun would set and the rooms would kind of "devolve" into this nightmarish silent-hill looking place because of the low lighting. I remember it bothering me a lot at the time.

I wrote The Whiteout on a whim on fandom after reading a couple of levels, and then promptly forgot about the backrooms and the fandom site for several months. It wasn't until later, when I remembered that weird copypasta and decided to check if my tiny little contribution had gotten any response, that I saw how well received it was when I first released it. It was because of that initial reception that I decided to continue to keep tabs on the fandom, and eventually saw a post asking for fandom users to join a new backrooms-related project.

The goal of that project was porting the fandom and all of its articles over to wikidot, and starting a new website entirely. I joined it rather hesitantly, and the rest kind of fell into place after that.

If it wasn't for this shitty, poorly thought out article which I pumped out in one afternoon, I would never have worked on or written for the wikidot. I would never have been chosen as it's admin after Warpez's abrupt departure.

If none of that had happened, I don't know where I'd be now.
