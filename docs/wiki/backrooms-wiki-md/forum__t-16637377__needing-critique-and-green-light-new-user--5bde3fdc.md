**Entity Number:** 52

### Habitat(s): [Level 23](level-23.md)

# **Description**

**Entity 52**, also known as **The Gardener**, is resembles an adult female human. **Entity 52** has no knowledge of The Backrooms. If someone asks **Entity 52**: her "*Can you help me out of The Backrooms?*", she'll respond with confusion by saying: "*What’s The Backrooms? I’ve never heard of it.*" If someone or asks about **Entity 52's** her real name, she'll respond by saying: "*Hmm… I don’t really know*." she doesn’t know her real name and can’t remember. **Entity 52** has a camera to take photographs of the beautiful plants and mushrooms in [Level 23](level-23.md). If anyone goes through **Entity 52's** her pictures without permission, she’ll get mad. **Entity 52** just randomly walks around the [Level 23](level-23.md) anywhere. **Entity 52** knows where the exit in of [Level 23](level-23.md) is.but she doesn’t know what the Backrooms is.

# **Biology**

**Entity 52** appears to be as an adult female human. She is 163 cm tall. with She has dark brownish brown/orange hair and bluish blue/green eyes. She has is most commonly seen wearing yellow boots, a green jumpsuit and a white undershirt, although sometimes she can appear in wearing ripped jeans and a black shirt. When **Entity 52** is asked about that her clothing she will say: "*Hmmm, I don't think it matters.*" and **Entity 52** will think that it is not relevant.

# **Dos and Don'ts**

#### **Do**

- Ask **Entity 52** about anything.
- Be nice to her.
- Treat her well.

#### **Don't**

- Be nasty to **Entity 52**.
- Audibly judge her.
- Call her names that she’ll never like.

When you someone follows the do's and don't's  
and treats **Entity 52** well, she’ll give you show them a  
way out the exit of [Level 23](level-23.md) and might also give you them food  
and [Almond Water](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/almond-water).

These are just suggestions, and you don't have to add them to your article. I tried to keep all of your stylistic choices while fixing the grammar. I do have some other suggestions though:

[+ My suggestions](javascript:;)

[– hide block](javascript:;)

- Your entity is a woman that takes pictures of plants. As this stands, its not very interesting. However, you can make it more interesting. You could try to answer some questions about the entity like: "Why does she take pictures of the plants?", "What's her origin story?' You could add an interview log that establishes her responses (quotes) to questions without including them in the description. You need to flesh out your entity and think of something that makes it interesting and add that in the article. What's the hook, what captures the reader's attention about your story? Your article has promise, but it needs a lot of work as it stands to make it more interesting and compelling.
