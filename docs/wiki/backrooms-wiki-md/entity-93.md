### Entity Number: 93

### Habitat(s): [Level 480](level-480.md)

# Description:

Entity 93, dubbed "The Night Stalker", is an entity rumored to exist in [Level 480](level-480.md). Its existence is unconfirmed, but many wanderers have reported seeing an entity which takes the appearance of a large spider roaming the aisles of the level. Witnesses have also reported that the entity tends to appear and disappear at random, making it difficult to catalog. One theory as to why there are so few witnesses is that the majority of wanderers who encounter the entity do not make it out alive. As such, wanderers are encouraged to not go searching for this entity as to ensure their safety.

# Biology and Behavior:

Research into the nature of this entity is still ongoing, but the Night Stalker has been reported to be a large, spider-like entity whose appendages end on sharp, dagger-like points. The Night Stalker has reportedly been heard making sounds similar to humming, the significance of which is as of yet not known. It isn't clear what its role in the level is, but some have speculated that it has something to do with the "anti-shoplifting" measures that are advertised in a few signs around Level 480.

# Do's and Don'ts:

## Do:

- Avoid it if possible. The potential dangers are not worth it, and the M.E.G. warns any wanderers to run away if they see it.

## Don't:

- Make any sudden movements, or loud noises; it is unknown what would happen if you attract its attention.

### Entity Number: 93

### Habitat(s): [Level 480](level-480.md)

# Description:

Entity 93, dubbed "The Night Stocker", is an entity rumored to exist in [Level 480](level-480.md). Its existence is unconfirmed, but many wanderers have reported seeing an entity which takes the appearance of a large spider stocking the shelves of the level. Witnesses have also reported that the entity tends to appear and disappear at random, making it difficult to catalog. Due to this, the Night Stocker has ‘attained the status of folklore for the Wanderers passing through Level 480. Many consider seeing it to be a sign of good luck.

# Biology and Behavior:

Research is still ongoing, but the Night Stocker has been reported to be a large, spider-like entity which can use its appendages to stock shelves. The Night Stocker has reportedly been heard humming and mumbling to itself as it stocks the shelves. It is not known where it comes from, or where it gets the products it uses.

# Do's and Don'ts:

## Do:

- Try to get a closer look. Who knows when you'll have a chance to see it again.

## Don't:

- Make any sudden movements or loud noises; you might scare it away.
