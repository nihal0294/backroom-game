Entity: "The Seraphim"

12 Oct 2021 03:16

**Page Type:** Entity

**Page Layout (Optional):** Standard presentation

**Elevator Pitch:** This is a single entity that appears to be an ordinary survivor at first glance. However, such uncanny abilities, such as presumed no clipping at will, disappearing, changing entity behaviors, helping or hurting other survivors.

**Central Narrative:** M.E.G. stumble upon this entity's existence as more stories of similar occurrences appear of people being helped by the same survivor in mysterious ways. However only through indirect means

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** Despite appearances, this entity is assumed not to be human due to its bizarre abilities. Basically, he helps kind survivors through indirect means and hurts those who've crossed him or others. Forms of helping include: pointing or suggesting them in the direction of their desired location. Helping find supplies. Pulling them to safety, and rarely redirecting other entities. Hurts others by the same means but in a negative way.

**Additional Notes:** I really like the idea and simply wish for some smoothing over of the idea or more specifically, problems with the concept I haven't seen. Thanks for the help
