Obligatory not a greenlighter, nor am I a professional critiquer, but I want to throw in my two cents.

The concept sounds fairly unique; sand isn't something I really see in many levels. However, I do think some more could added.

Using percentages is a bit iffy; how does the M.E.G know that something has exactly 36% chance of happening? The 14 minutes thing I'd believable, but it would likely require a ton of research.

I like the idea of the hands, but I think they could've been expanded upon as well. When trying to kill you, do they crawl towards you and onto your body, or do they float by an unknown source?

I also think adding a bit of reason would possibly help a bit. Why are there sandstorm that replenish the sand? Is it to prevent a certain thing from happening? Are the hands animalistic, or do they have a specific goal? Etc.

Still, this seems promising. I hope things go well for you.
