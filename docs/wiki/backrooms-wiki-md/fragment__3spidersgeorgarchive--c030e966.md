### Condition Name: "Spider-Mania"

**Description:** Spider-mania is a rare psychological disorder induced upon exposure to venom secreted by [Entity 16](entity-16.md).

**Incidence rate:** 0.08% of recorded cases of Nguithr'xurh Paralysis

**Prognosis:** Full recovery in 3 - 6 weeks

## Symptoms:

Sufferers of **Spider-Mania** are generally identified by delirium, panic attacks, and severe arachnophobia. These occur in conjunction with regular symptoms induced by Entity 16 venom (fatigue, intoxication, slurring of speech, etc.). All symptoms persist in patients for weeks after venom has been cleared from bodily systems.

Patients' inane ravings have been noted as oddly specific. These tend to center around being pursued by a non-existent spider-human hybrid entity. Repeated denial of the existence of this creature may also cause patients to turn aggressive and attack those around them verbally/physically.

## Cause:

The cause of the condition is not known. The few documented cases vary widely in age, ethnicity, lifestyle, and overall health. Entity 16 venom samples collected from patients also bear no significant differences from regular Nguithr'xurh venom. An environmental factor is suspected, though this has not been verified.

## Treatment:

Bed-rest and mild sedatives are recommended. Recovery generally occurs naturally, within 3 to 6 weeks. Patients are noted as statistical outliers, and treatment is to be considered low-priority compared to more urgent cases.
