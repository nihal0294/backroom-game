A few things

- I'm pretty sure we have a Level -3 in the Discord server that's very close to being greenlit, as well as a -9, so I'd start going for the 11's or higher.  
- This definitely needs some more work. Grammar and flow errors ridicule the article, and it makes a bad read.  
- Personally, I think it would fit better as a normal level. There's nothing particularly interesting or out of place that makes this different from other levels. It actually reminds me of a theme park that might've flooded.

However, the concept is really good! Don't let me crits discourage you from writing more. I only hope to improve you ^^
