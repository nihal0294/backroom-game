# **Description**

Lorum Ipsum

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

## The Suppliers are a group of those who who live on this level and will sometimes send people to leave the level to trade with others for supplies, then they return.

# **Entrances And Exits**

### Entrances

you can enter by breaking a bottle of almond water on level 4.

### Exits

to exit, you must do the same, and you will end up on level -1, with all supplies you own. you can also exit to level 4 by pressing a randomly appearing red button, which will appear on rare, red colored walls. you will pass out and wake up on level 4, with all your supplies beside you. This means that, if you wake up next to a wall, the supplies may clip through and disappear.
