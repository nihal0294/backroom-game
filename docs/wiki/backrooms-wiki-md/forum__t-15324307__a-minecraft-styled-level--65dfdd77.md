Concepts alright, though some slight issues.

I believe it needs something to stand out a bit more, beside it being literal Minecraft and the unique layout. Perhaps some unique property native to it? Perhaps include some mechanics that originate from minecraft? I believe this can go well if executed correctly, just make sure to keep the reader hooked by a possible property or uniqueness that is exclusive to this level.

Also the "still use dead memes" kind of breaks the clinical tone of the article, would highly advise to remove it.

Wouldn't take my criticism to heart however, I am quite new to this wiki.
