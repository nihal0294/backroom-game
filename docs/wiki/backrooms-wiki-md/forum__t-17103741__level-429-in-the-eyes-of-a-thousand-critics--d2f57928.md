not for me

30 Apr 2025 12:28

I think that the idea behind this page is one that is extraordinary. I’ve wanted to see a page like this for a while, where a level just is… a level. It’s an idea that I think is perfect for surrealism and abstract absurdity, which I always love to see.

However…

I don’t really like the way you went about executing this idea. The flowery language dancing circles around your head is great and all, it adds to the fun and carefree nature of the page and complements the “this level is a level” aspect very well, but it’s just that: this page feels so light that it’s weightless to me. I didn’t really feel anything when reading this. You somehow found a way to remove all the gravity from the idea of a level just being a level. The thing about absurdism is that it has to acknowledge the fact that there is gravity to the ideas it toys with, and the carefree and ridiculous nature of it is a way of defying expectations with the reader. This just felt a bit too plain for it to really work in my opinion.

Poems are really a hit or miss for me, and unfortunately, this was a miss, so it’ll be a downvote this time.
