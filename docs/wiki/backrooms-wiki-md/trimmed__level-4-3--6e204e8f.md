# **Description:**

**Level 4.3**'s entrance is found within [Level 4](level-4.md). This Level has visual similarities to [Level 3](level-3.md) yet it still technically being in [Level 4](level-4.md). The total area of **Level 4.3** is unspecified. This area has been explored briefly, and information and recounts of people’s experiences with it are limited. It is described as being generally more decayed than most previous levels and contains an extreme lack of light sources; this warrants the use of a flashlight or other means when navigating in this level. It is known to be exceptionally dry and to contain a strong smell of mould and dust. This Level is a solid contrast to its entrance in [Level 4](level-4.md), Although it does not share a lot of the key features found within [Level 4](level-4.md).

**Level 4.3**’s layout is composed of multiple medium-sized rooms, similar in design to an industrial basement. These rooms contain many concrete pillars, exposed wiring (note: no running electricity has been found) and rusted metal piping throughout. A noticeable unopened door has been observed in the top left-hand corner of one of the larger rooms, the only one of its type.

## The Lost Cloak:

This Entity is a dark mass that has rarely been documented on **Level 4.3**, it is generally aggressive but has been observed to not attack on sight. The physical description of this entity varies, though it is usually seen as being a large (1.5 m across approx.) soundless, floating mass with a purple tint around its outsides.

Its aggressive label and common nickname has stemmed from the entity's tendency to surround those who observe it in a flowing cloak-like fashion and kill them quickly and subtly. When being covered, the entity may appear darker than it previously seemed and its purple tint may increase its brightness. It is unknown what method the Entity uses to kill its victims, or why on occasion it does not attack. The Entity has not been sighted on other Levels. There is no known procedure to prevent attacks from the Entity. No other Entities have been documented in this area.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities:**

No bases, outposts, or communities exist in this area.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

Entering and exiting **Level 4.3** is very simple. **Level 4.3**'s entrance/exit point seems to stay in a consistent position, using this is the most effective way to enter and exit. Exiting will most likely take you out into [Level 4](level-4.md).
