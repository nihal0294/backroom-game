Okay! Wow, I see potential here. (Not ready for a greenlight yet though). Consider this a concept check :)

> Level 243 of the backrooms takes the appearance of an average American media center, and is about 2000 square feet. It contains books and other objects that you might find in a library, however, the books are random and do not exist in real life. Some of these books even have mind-altering properties that can cause paranoia, sudden anger, and uncontrollable mood swings. If someone takes a book then after 3 days of the levels day and night cycle. **It will be returned.** The levels day and night cycle last about 48 hours. There is an activities board with ads for a Pool club, Party place, a city, and a neighborhood watch program.

There's a lot of information here that I want answers to. First, the page tells me that there are books and other media that have mind-altering properties. Great! How can I identify them though? Remember, the whole reason (or rather the best reason imo) why these levels are being documented is so wanderers in the Backrooms can avoid danger. Is there something that connects all these materials together so a wanderer will avoid them?

Secondly, this bit is so ominous.

> **It will be returned.**

There's a story there. Why the bold text? (If it was an accident, keep it because it's so intriguing.) What happened? How was this discovered? This is absolutely a space for there to be more explanation of how this level works or include some creepy lore.

> There is an activities board with ads for a Pool club, Party place, a city, and a neighborhood watch program.

I love this! But what happens when someone tries to go to where the ads are ? Are you trying to link to other pages? Do new ads show up? What happens when they do?

Oh, and do be aware that the [Faceling](entity-9.md) page has been rewritten. If you're wanting the Facelings in your page to match the Facelings there, you'll have to rewrite a bit.

I think this level is incredibly intriguing, and answering the questions I posted above will make it even better.
