Level 807 consists of a destroyed forest spanning approximately 200 square kilometers[1](javascript:;). It is one of the most dangerous levels in the Backrooms.

# Description

Level 807 is an incredibly threatening level, and should not be entered under any circumstances. This is caused by a wide variety of reasons. Firstly, the level rains acid frequently. This causes vast amounts of flooding throughout the level, despite it having an almost flat terrain. The acid in the level is incredibly corrosive and can dissolve almost anything. This makes base building completely impossible.

Secondly, Level 807 has incredibly high temperature, with records reaching up to 57°C[2](javascript:;). Thus, just staying in the level for prolonged periods of time can lead to death from heatstroke.

Thirdly, Level 807 is infested with hostile entities. Deathmoths and Hounds are seen very frequently in the level, despite it's hellish landscape. These entities are not native to the level, however, but enter the level from an entrance in The Hive instead.
