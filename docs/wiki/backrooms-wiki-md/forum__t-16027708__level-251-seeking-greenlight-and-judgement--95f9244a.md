# **Description**

Level 251 or “Abandoned but not Forgotten” is an old house which appears to be covered in a green-black mud-like substance. The substance in question is slightly corrosive. While not deadly, it may sting and break off a small portion of the skin. The house itself is seemingly unstable, and random parts may fall out from underneath wanderers. Touching the wood with bare skin will give you a splinter, even if you did not touch a sharp piece of the wood, so it is recommended to wear some sort of skin protection, if you have accessed any.

The house itself is often uncomfortably moist, and the wood will fall apart if pressed on too hard. The actual size is unknown, and most attempts to wander the entire house have ended fatally. Doors that lead to rooms often appear, and will lead to cabinets and drawers which may contain useful supplies, like Almond Water, knives, gloves, tools, and rations. The gloves are very useful for protecting against splinters in this level.

The level is usually inhabited by Facelings, Hounds, and Smilers. Although, in rare cases, Windows are sometimes seen. These entities are few and far between, but they seem to be more aggressive than usual at this level. The cause for this is currently under investigation, but unknown. If you see stairs, you are either unlucky or lucky. If you go down them, one of two things may happen. Either one, you walk right into a pit of Hounds, or two, you may enter the basement of the house, which is made of concrete similar to that in Level 1, and the same green-black substance mentioned above. In this area, cabinets and drawers are much more common, and sometimes Almond Water fountains can be found. The downside is, long halls may appear, which Dullers sometimes inhabit.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There are no known bases, outposts or communities on this level

# **Entrances And Exits**

#### Entrances

A doorway with stairs that lead up may sometimes appear in Level 1, which will bring you to the basement of Level 251. If the stairs lead down, it will most likely lead you down to Level 3.

Level 123 will sometimes contain a hallway that you cannot see the end of. Walking down this hallway will bring you into Level 251.

#### Exits

A door in the basement may appear with stairs leading downward. This will bring you to Level 1.

Sometimes a door will appear in the house that leads you to a white room with pipes. There will be a door to Level 3 somewhere inside of the room.
