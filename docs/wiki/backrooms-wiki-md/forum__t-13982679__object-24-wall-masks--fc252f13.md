+1 in 2025

11 Apr 2025 19:21

I've been doing postcrits of the Objects, and I have done it for the first 23 Objects. Now I will do it for Wall Masks. I don't have any experience with the M&M canon (beyond Akimoto's Kimono Phenomenon article which I did a crit of), so give me some leeway in the lore department.

I feel like the page is good. It introduces an Object and helps explain it pretty well. The hidden messages add some flavor that is much needed, even if they are a *bit* contrived. It might help if you explain the relations and what happened instead of being vague, although this might have been explored elsewhere.

My main complaint with the article is that the scenarios surrounding the Wall Masks are incredibly contrived. They involve people questioning or being suspicious and things generally unrelated to the intent of the mask. This feels incredibly boring in comparison with the good parts of the article. The point is that it makes these people into killers (according to the M.E.G. at least), yet this goes contrary to the point as they will only kill given **VERY** particular circumstances. All around it feels contrived, boring, and contrary to the point.

This article is still good. Not as good as it could be, and I can see why a rewrite is taking place, but it is fine nonetheless. Good luck with your rewrite, best of luck.
