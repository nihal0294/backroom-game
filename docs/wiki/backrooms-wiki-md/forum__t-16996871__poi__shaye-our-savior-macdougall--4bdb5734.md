General critique:

The semicolon after "second-degree burns" is unnecessary.  
"due to the nature of his concerns" would work better as "due to concerns about his safety".  
You should have a space before and after dashes (like those in "—assumed to be a part of a single group—").  
"although speculation has been made" what speculation? I would elaborate on this, even if only a little.  
The dialogue parts of the interview and recovered sources are fine, but people might question why MacDougall is writing with an accent. It's not a required change, but people don't usually do that.  
Page 11 doesn't have a div block around the text, you might want to fix that.

Honestly, this is one of the better pages I've seen on the forums, and I would be happy with seeing this expanded. I would like to see more about this cult in the future, they seem quite focused on whatever they're doing, and it's something new to the site. Do you want me to put this on the Discord so more people see?
