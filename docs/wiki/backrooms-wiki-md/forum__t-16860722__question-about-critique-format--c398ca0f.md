It's as simple as leaving comments within the forums like I did [here](forum__t-16037915__level-xxx-not-numbered-yet--b75046bb.md). Or you can copy the article into a Google Doc and leave comments on areas of interest/improvement (although I would be careful of revealing personal info like a personal email address). You can even send it in a private message to the person.

Really, so long as it's constructive criticism, you can format critique in whatever way you're comfortable with.

As for SPaG, lots of people use the quote function by typing > to offset the problem text and then comment the fixes down below. For example:

> This is the text that there's a problem with.

This is the text that fixes the problem.

If you have more questions, please feel free to reach out to staff. Additionally, our Discord is much more lively than our onsite forums, so if you're interested, I recommend joining the Discord :)
