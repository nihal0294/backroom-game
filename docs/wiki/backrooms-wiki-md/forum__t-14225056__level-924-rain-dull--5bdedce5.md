12 Oct 2021 15:31

Level 9244 (or Rain .Dull)  
Survival difficulty : 4  
Unsafe  
Unsecure  
Medium entity count

Rain .Dull is a 3rd world country suberb area with a lot of rain.(it rains 6 days in a week)  
The level is approx 6500 squaremiles in area and the streets are mostly cracked and often have sinkholes that leads to the sewers that have wormlings,death rats and very rarely there can be packs of hounds in the sewers and there is also acidic liquids coming out from the sew³er walls.

There is day and night cycle and 8 hours of day and only 2 hours of nightime.  
There is no sun and the only source of light is the dim "sunlight" coming through the clouds.  
Even if the rain stops,the clouds don't go away the sunlight just gets a little brighter.  
There is no "evening" it just goes from day to night immeidately.

You can hear footsteps behind you when you walk in the rain,Making you anxious.  
If you look back a couple of times,it will become worse and you will begin to feel the breath blew in your neck ,so just try to ignore it.(There aren't any footsteps when it's not rarining)

There are 1 colony and it's called "The end of spring" and it consist of 12 resulkas and 8 humans.  
They live together in harmony and some of the people starts to resemble the resulka's skins as the rain slowly eats away at their skins in course of years.

There are 3 zones,  
(Rain,Tempest and conflict zones)

The Rain zone is 1000 squaremiles in area and mostly safe if you are cautious about your surroundings except for the sewers underneath.

The tempest zone is 1500 square miles in area and the streets and buildings looks a bit more worn down and partly destroyed.  
The sewers are more destroyed as well and there can be intersections of the sewers where there can be hidden outposts of partygoers.But there is less acidic liquids in this area of seweage.  
(In the transition from tempest zone to conflict zone,there is bandit resulkas that roams around the city edges,they mostly stays in groups and resulkas in the city don't like them aswell, sone of the bandit groups even tamed hounds)

The conflict zone has a large tidal forest yet there is no sign of an ocean or sea in this level.  
The trees in the forest are a bit saturated in blue.  
There is rain-silkworms in the forest on the trees and you can harvest silk from the worms and they are said to be strong and soft and lightweight aswell.  
It can be used to make armor out of the sliks and it also acts as a currency for the resulkas.  
The silkworm it self is a bit of poisonous or be careful while hervesting the silk.  
But there are packs of hounds that seems to be more intelligent than normal hounds but not by much.  
There are way less infastructure and mostly wild terrain and some odd looking brick houses in the middle of the plains or in the middle of the forest.  
There is a recently discovered farm house in the pains northwest from the center of the level but there is an unidentified entity living in there.  
Noone knows if the entity is hostile or neutral.  
Some has seen it's silhouette while it's out of it's house in the night.  
(There are no grass in this zone so the plains just looks like a dirt ground) the light here is very dim aswell.

There are 10 entities including both surface entities and sewer entities  
(Windows,Households,The drains,Resulkas and Partygoers,farmland entity,death rats,hounds,wormlings ​and rain-  
slikworms)  
a bit of food and water in the street sides and inside some buildings.  
You can drink the rain but it's a bit bad for your stomach as it contains low concentration of various acids.  
Boiling it has no effect.

You can go into the houses made out of brick or concrete while it has stopped raining.(if it's raining the doors to the buildings close shut and it's reported that it's impossible to break down the doors)  
Inside the building there can be food and almond water.(in very rare cases there can even be memory juice inside the 2 storey brick buildings).There can be one entity in the buildings and it's called "the household".  
They look like normal humans but looks a little shorter than humans and they can have one more organ or limbs. if it's female it's not hostile and aggresive but will attack back if you attack her  
If it's a male it can be a bit aggresive but by giving him food,water or a makeshift rain harvester it will make him neutral or even become friendly towards you.  
(There is no effect in giving items to female households).They will just accept it and mind their own business.  
If you wanna take items from the house,you must have approval from the "household".  
Giving items to the households might help with getting approval but it seems that it's random for the most parts.

If raining starts,the door to the buildings will close shut and the households will go towards their frontdoors and vanish. If you happens to be in the building,the door will close on you no matter what and you have to wait until it stopped raining.  
The light in the buildings will shut off and you will hear and see things that you havent even seen before.  
(in rare cases,some people have even seen partypoopers in the hallucinations and it's reported that seeing them makes their sanity a bit better.)But the good news it that all the items in the house are now in your will.  
After 4 days your sanity will be very low but you don't get turned into a wrench no matter what in this level.  
But the headache and the stress wil linger on for days or even weeks if you don't drink enough almond water.  
The the raining stops and you happens to be in the room, you will be thrown outside the building you are in by a mysterious force.

There is another entity roaming in the streets and It's called "Resulkas"  
They roam around the streets in all 3 zones.  
They are humanoid but they are dark blue skinned with no facial details like noses and eyes.  
They wear ripped and worn down clothings.  
Their clothings are hard and seems to be made out of rain-silkworm and they can even defend against attacks with sharp wepons and pistol shots.  
They are hard but they can be bent easily and water can go though them.  
They are a bit aggresive and will attack when provoked or if they feel threatened.  
They moves in akward ways and their actions are ragdoll like.  
They sometimes have battle scars in the battle with partygoers in the conflict zone.  
If you give the resulkas with battle scars the partypooper masks or their clothings,they will take it and will look a bit sad and they will bury the mask or anything you give them and wisper something from their "mouth" in an unknown language.  
Some suggests that they are comrades with partypoopers and some people suggest that the partypooper hallucinations are the partypooper's souls.  
You can trade with Resulkas and they will give you weapons or other equipment if you give them almond water or other valuable things.  
You can even befriend with them and they are just as intelligent as you and they can also fight very well.  
They might even become your partner along your journey through the backrooms. But their diet contains a lot of water to survive.( it does not require to be an almond water, even if you give them water full of acids, they will sort out the acid and drink the remaining water themselves.But there are some levels Resulkas can't go to like level 52 and other levels with no liquid substence at all.  
If they do they will either thirst to death or they will just glitch out of existance.

The drains are hostile entities that looks like drains but they have white pair of eyes and you can sometimes even see their hands sticking out of the "Drain"  
They cannot get out of their drain but will try to suck you in because they can control the water exactly 3 meters around them.  
They can be in the sides of the streets or in the middle of it.  
The drains changes their positions every time the rain stops. They are in all 3 zones but they are more common in the Tempest zone.  
The drains can seemingly suck in endless amount of water and that's the main reason why there is no flood in this level despite raining 6 days a week.

The rain zone consist of 3rd world suburban area with a lot of rain and low amount of drains,resulkas,windows.  
But there are a lot of households and no partygoers.  
The resulkas in this zone seems to have scars and this zone might be the retirement zone for resulka fighters.

The Tempest zone consist of medium amount of drains,Stronger wind and rain.  
And there can even be thunderstorms in this area and the lightning seems to strike near the drains.  
There are medium amount of resulkas and drains and low amount of househoulds which means less houses you can go in.  
There is no windows in this area and sometimes partygoers can be seen here but mostly they can be seen fighting with other resulkas.

The conflict area consist of medium amount of resulkas and some partygoers and they can be seen fighting each other.  
The footsteps that you normally hear will not follow you to this zone and you can hear the footstep slowly going further and further away from you.  
There is also low amount of drains but the drains here seems to be a lot bigger and can control up to 8 meters of it's surrounding water.

Sometimes there can be waves of partygoers coming from the entrance in the conflict zone and attack the resulkas.  
But the Battle seems to always end up in a stalemate with a few casualties on both sides.  
But very strangely, there are buildings on fire but even though there is a lot of rain,it seems that the rain does not extinguish the flame.  
The rain in this area have more acid in them so being out in the rain for a long time can make your skin turn red and burn.

You can make a new juice called "Battle juice" with almond water found only in this level.  
To brew the juice,you need 1 litres of almond water and special water which is made from filtering the rain in this level with a piece of clothing from the fallen resulkas.  
Then boil 800ml of almond water and 150ml of special water together.  
If you see the juice starting to turn gold you can stop boiling,the more you boil,the thicker the liquid and more powerful it is.  
But if the juice truns orange,it's not drinkable anymore.  
By drinking the battle juice, it will make you stronger and can give you regenerative abilities for a short while.  
But never drink the battle juice in it's entirety.  
Instead drink small sips slowly over time.  
And also never drink more than 200 ml a day are your muscles will literally tear apart and there will be organ failure.  
Partygoers are scared of this juice as well and you can repel them using it.

The entrance : Go to the "strategy room" in the level 52 and put your hand into one of the drawers in the room.  
It will suck you in and will teleport you right into the battle with partygoers and resulkas if you are unlucky.

Another entrance is to type in  
"01010010 01100001 01101001 01101110" in level 9087 or in "the dev room" or in the most computers in the backrooms.

To exit you must not touch any form of liquid other than you internal ones for 3 hours in this level and you will be teleported back to level 0.

Other exit is by going in to the 2 story building and killing the household inside.  
Do this will send you to "the flooded subways" and lvl 170.

And the safest exit is to find a house with a red refrigerator inside.  
There will be a random vegetable inside and you need to boil it with the rain in this level and then eat it.  
Doing this will teleport you to the Crimson Forest which is a very safe level.

Are the last exit is to go into the tidal forest in the conflict zone and perform the raincaller dance.  
You can find the paper about the raincaller dance inside the partygoer outpost in the sewer intersection in the tempest zone or you can get it by trading with the resulkas,(the price is a bit expensive)
