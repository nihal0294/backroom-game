> Although Level 41 is confirmed not to be an infinite space, traveling throughout the corridors within it can prove to be both difficult, and in some cases, completely disorientating.

"and in some cases" isn't really needed. You could say "and possibly disorienting". The part with the disorientation is a bit prosey and you could say Wanderers have reported disorientation instead.

> Previous documentation of this level proved to be somewhat challenging to achieve due to non-linear spacial factors present, however, much has been discovered by reports from Wanderers as well as multiple reconnaissance missions conducted by M.E.G. personnel.

This should be listed as two separate sentences. Put a period before "however" and you should be good.

> When in Level 41, it is possible for a Wanderer to find themself stumbling across a small Diner.

In this particular sentence, you should lowercase the "Diner" in the sentence.

> This Diner is operated by the group that resides within this level and will serve Wanderers in exchange for various trinkets and bric-a-brac.

This sentence contains a little redundancy. Trinket and bric-a-brac basically mean the same thing, so you may want to delete one of those.

> The Diner itself appears to be somewhat old, with worn leather seats in each booth, and faded vintage posters plastered haphazardly across numerous walls.

How old? What time period does it look like it's from? Also, how many walls are there? This could help readers imagine the Diner.

> The entity's physical features are yet to be discovered due to its facial area being covered by a black cloth.

You should say "facial features" instead of "physical features". Physical features encompass more than just the face. Height, weight, skin color; those are examples of physical features. Facial features is a subtype of physical features.

> Although it resides in Level 41 "The Collector" has been documented in multiple other levels of the backrooms, carrying a large rucksack.

Put a comma after "Level 41". Always capitalize "backrooms", as it is the name given to this.

> For the item a Wanderer wishes to receive, an item of equal value must be given to "The Collector"

Put a period at the end of this.

> If a wanderer does not have sufficient enough items for a trade, an item of great personal value, such as a family photograph, can be traded to the entity instead.

You've been capitalizing Wanderer throughout your article like you should, and you kinda messed up here. Capitalize it for consistency.

In the Bases, Outposts, and Communities section, you could combine all the points into one sentence. "There are no M.E.G. outposts or communities on this Level due to an agreement made between the M.E.G. and the entity that resides on this Level."

The Subway Dwellers section should have a smaller heading if it's part of the Bases section.

> This group consists of around 12 - 15 members and is noted to be exceedingly friendly toward others.

"…approximately 12 - 15 members…" is more clinical. "exceedingly" is not not clinical, and you should delete it. That would fix the tone.

> (For example, if a Wanderer boards train number 011, they will be sent to Level 11

Put a period and a closing parenthesis at the end to make a proper note. You could also use a footnote or just make it part of the normal body.  
  
  
That's all I have for now! It's an interesting idea, though the beginning gave me some Level 0 vibes. You fixed that with description about the specific points on the Level. Good article.
