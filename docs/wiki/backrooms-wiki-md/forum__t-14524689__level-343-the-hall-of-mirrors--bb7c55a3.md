So a few quick things

- We do have someone making a hall of mirrors level already, but don't let this discourage you! As long as you make it different, then you can post it!  
- Zalgo text is no longer allowed, as it messes with mobile users.  
- Colonies are no longer allowed because of the connotation that they bring, which can offend some readers.  
- The article definitely needs to be expanded. There are two big, beefy sections but then it goes to short little sentences. Make the length last if that's what you want, and if not then I suggest finding points in the large blocks that you can turn into paragraph breaks. Large blocks are also hard to read consistently, and personally I lose my place when reading huge walls of text.

That's all I got! Other than that it's a neat concept.

This is just… generic, overdone, and empty.

The language and formatting used is outdated, and the level does not represent the description you give it. It feels like a pain to read because it abandons the database feel. In my opinion, it reads off like a Fandom entry—like a child around the age of 14 wrote this.
