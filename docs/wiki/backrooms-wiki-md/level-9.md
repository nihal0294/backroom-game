**Level 9** is the 10th Level of The Backrooms, This Level seems to be an infinite Suburban Area.

# **Description:**

**Level 9** is an infinite suburban area at midnight. This Level has darkness similar to that of [Level 6](level-6.md), although not as dangerous. The houses vary in design and size, and each are completely different. Although there are reports of spotting two houses near each other that are the same.

The houses of **Level 9** appear to be furnished and fairly new, although there is no power source for the lighting systems to function. Some houses have a chance of being empty. Many useful objects, such as [Pockets](object-51.md), can be found inside these houses and areas similar to them. The furniture of these houses is what a person would expect from a normal house, such as sofas, televisions, beds, refrigerators, etc. Items that require a power source are not functional. Some of these houses have fully furnished yards at the back of the house. Another anomalous property of this Level is that you can find two houses weirdly clipped inside of each other when travelling too far, which is physically impossible.

The streets of **Level 9** are the most dangerous area of the Level. The wet asphalt roads are unpainted and are covered with leaves in some areas. The puddles in some spots of the roads indicate that the Level had once rained before. The stone sidewalks are normal and don't seem to have any anomalous oddities. Any walkways that lead to grass fields will lead to [Level 9.1](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-9-1). Wandering off the walkway into the fields will for some reason lead to [Level 10](level-10.md). The street lamps are usually powered off, and are inactive, although some flicker off and on and are sometimes even powered. It is unknown where the power originates. Be aware of the foggy mist that can appear, due to the fog being the spawning mechanism for [The Mangled](entity-63.md).

Entities that can appear in **Level 9** are [Deathmoths](entity-4.md), [Smilers](entity-3.md), [Skin-Stealers](entity-10.md), [Jerry](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/trimmed:entity-7), [Hounds](entity-8.md), [Transporters](entity-13.md), [Death Rats](entity-24.md), [Warning Kites](entity-22.md) and many [Wretches](entity-15.md). Sightings of [The Frayed](trimmed__entity-21--dc9362bc.md) and [The Arachnids](entity-39.md) have been reported, but it seems that they have wandered into **Level 9** from [Level 8](level-8.md). **Level 9** also includes some of its own special Entities, the most notable ones include [The Neighborhood Watch](entity-96.md), The Observer and [The Mangled](entity-63.md).

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There are a few Groups that live and roam **Level 9**, as Groups can be set up in **Level 9** although this is a very dangerous task, and none have been recorded for this Entry at the moment.

# **Discovery:**

**Level 9** was discovered when a post published by "u/Bart0nius" one of the members of the missing Explorers Group. After he and the rest of the group fell through the floor on [Level 8](level-8.md), he claimed to have been dreaming and claims that this Level doesn't exist. Although the claim was false, it is still unknown how the Explorers Group disappeared. Here is what the post reads:

> I fell through the floor.
>
> I sunk and landed in an unfamiliar place. There were no spiders, but I still felt like I was being watched. Around me, there was a street, houses, and dead trees everywhere. My head felt, and still feels… fuzzy. Like TV static in my brain. I lost consciousness but apparently had an outburst, according to Centurion. I was talking about death, pain, weakness… all kinds of awful things.
>
> **Level 9** is Level 6 on crack. I can tell there are going to be Things, but the effects on your mind are bad. Almost, ALMOST as bad as Level 6.
>
> ---
>
> The Explorers were never found after the publishing of this post.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

### Entrances:

- To enter, falling through the floor randomly in Level 8 is the main method of entering **Level 9** for most Wanderers.
- Crawling up the sewer grate in [Level 34](level-34.md) will lead to **Level 9**.
- One of the many doors inside [Level 92](trimmed__level-92--cb3388d4.md) can lead to **Level 9** as well as other Levels.
- The revolving door in [Level 40](level-40.md) will lead to **Level 9**.
- The yellow arcade cabinet in [Level 25](level-25.md) will lead to this Level.
- Entering one of the doors in [Level 54](level-54.md) will lead to **Level 9**.
- The animated houses of [Level 94](level-94.md) can rarely bring you to this Level.
- Break the window in [Level 87](level-87.md) to enter **Level 9**.
- The backyard of [Level 104](level-104.md) will lead to **Level 9** after exiting the
- An arcade machine in [Level 25](level-25.md) can lead to **Level 9**

### Exits:

- To exit, following the arrow street signs will soon lead to [Level 11](level-11.md) after one-hundred (100) to two-hundred (200) miles, although this will only be the case when following the signs.
- Walkways that lead to grass fields will lead to either [Level 9.1](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-9-1) or [Level 10](level-10.md).
- [Level 60](level-60.md) can be accessed by No-Clipping through the street floor in **Level 9**. Entering a house will have a chance to randomly transport you to [Level 53](level-53.md).
- Following the electric powerlines has a chance to bring you to [Level 113](level-113.md).
- An airport can be found while following the signs to Level 11.
- Enter the airport to be transported to [Level 36](level-36.md). No-Clipping will lead to the first few Negative Levels.
- Finding a playground and crawling through any kind of tube structure with white glowing interior windows will commonly teleport to [Level 283](level-283.md)'s Tubes.
