The level is located on a different habitable planet Ina separate galaxy which has The Charted (or explored) region and The Uncharted (or unexplored) region. The Charted region is called the Continent and is an island surrounded by The Coast in all directions. The Continent is further divided in multiple geographical regions and has multiple human colonies. I have put a lot of detail in the sandbox page, and as such it may take a while to go through.
