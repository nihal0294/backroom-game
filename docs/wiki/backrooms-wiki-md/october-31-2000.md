How long has it been since I've been home?  
How long has it been since I've felt the cool breeze of fall?  
How long has it been since I've seen my family?  
My mom?  
My dad?  
Time has never felt the same down here.  
Everything merges together.  
From memories of the past to even time itself.  
It's hard to recall certain times.  
All I remember was being outside.  
In the dark of night  
Illuminated by my neighborhood's festive lights.  
Orange, green, purple.  
With people all around me.  
Door to door, they all went.  
Costumes and candy everywhere.  
Halloween.  
The time of the year I look forward to the most.  
The time I feel like a child again.  
The time I'm finally with my parents again.  
The time I feel happy once more.

Then I fell.  
I fell into this place.  
A place where those festivities and warmth give way to cold nothingness.  
A place where memories and love go to die.  
I still hold the pumpkin pail my mom gave me that day.  
Even if I was considered too old to go door-to-door.  
I still wanted to be a part of the festivities.  
I wanted to be that kid again.  
But instead of holding plenty of candy.  
It now holds plenty of tools.  
The last remaining memory is now ruined.  
Just like the rest of my mind.  
I never understood how long I've been down here.  
How long has it been since I've been taken?  
Since I've left home.  
I just wish I could be back home.  
Even if it's for a moment  
Even if it's false.  
Even if I'm thrown back to the dull nothing again.  
I wish to be home for Halloween.

I never really believed my wishes would ever come true.  
In the darkest of times.  
In the hardest of moments.  
All I could do was wish.  
Even if I knew nothing would change.  
And yet.  
I was proven wrong.  
As the concrete walls and parking lot floors merged in my mind.  
As all the other places have.  
I could see something sticking out.  
Something wooden amongst the stone.  
A door in the wall.  
Decorated with orange, black, purple, and white.  
Patterns of old Halloween.  
Black cats, pumpkins, bats, witches.  
All cut out and stuck to the wood with tape.  
Something that my mom would do every year.  
It was special to me.  
Even if it was something so small.  
And yet here it is.  
A brazen interruption in the monotony of nothing.  
Something I never thought I would see again.  
A more than welcome change.

I touched the door  
Feeling the paper folds.  
The wooden notches.  
The cold metal knob.  
It was all real.  
Like it was taken from my own home.  
From my own memories.  
Like I never even left.  
My own wish is finally coming true.  
I pushed it open gently.  
And fell into unconsciousness.  
Before I could see what was on the other side.  
Before I could even process the world changing around me.  
All I knew was that-  
[I was finally home for Halloween.](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/october-31-2000/offset/1)
