Simple Idea

05 Mar 2021 12:04

Essentially, this Entity would be the "eyes and ears" of the Backrooms themselves, changing depending on the level it manifests on. It has human-level intelligence, nothing more or less, and is only hostile if provoked. The exception to those two aspects of it? Level 1.5, if it manifests there it chases you and calls you an idiot. It poses no physical threat on most levels, but can easily wear down your patience and will to go on. It need to eat, sleep, and breathe, just like you (assuming you're human like I am). It has no control over its transformations, and is more a part of the Backrooms than an inhabitant of them. Anyone like this idea?
