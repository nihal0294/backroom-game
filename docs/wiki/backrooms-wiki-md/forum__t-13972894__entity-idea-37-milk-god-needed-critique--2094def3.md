Entity idea- #37 Milk God

08 Jan 2021 17:35

Today I want to give an idea to my draft that I have worked on. It was originally uploaded on draft critique, but now I feel like I need this on here. So here is the idea:

Genre: Surrealism, obscure

Main idea (Elevation Pitch): A milk carton who is seen as god with fantastic abilities and has a religion perfect for mostly anyone.

Page layout:  
This will have the same page layout, but with a few additional features

Milkism:  
Milkism is a religion only in the backrooms that is about drinking milk and becoming a better person. The religion is for anyone who doesn't have a religion to follow. Joining requires you to call the Milk God2.

Rules of Milkism:  
Drink milk as much as possible  
Don't drink chocolate milk. This is considered a sin by the Milk God  
Don't kill another human being, unless being attacked  
Sacrifice chocolate milk if found  
If you don't follow the rules, then the Milk God won't consider you a true member of Milkism. It won't get mad, it'll just be disappointed in you.

Central narrative:None

Attention getter: "I came to bring a light to the cold darkness. i am your calcium, not sodium. This light will burn for all eternity for generations to look upon. I shall bring this damned hell alone in the darkness where it belongs were it cannot see the light anymore. And what is this light i speak of? i am the light. i am your hope. i am here.'

(Imagine all of that speech said by a milk carton.)
