Level 162: Transporting Level

09 Nov 2020 00:34

**Page Type:** Level

**Elevator Pitch:** Level 162 consists of rooms that consist of multiple hallways that are connected to one large 10m x 10m x 10m room. Entering through one of the hallways will bring the person to one of the following rooms:

- The person has entered through another hallway connected to the same room with the hallway that the person went through.
- The person has entered an endless hallway.
- The person will be brought to a new level.
- The person has been brought into an empty 10m x 10m x 10m room. The hallway exit will be covered by unknown means, and the person will be trapped forever.

**Central Narrative:** Basically, the chances of these scenarios are based off of luck. Two people can enter the same hallway and both can get a different scenario. For example, Person 1 enters an endless hallway and person 2 enters a different level.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** Its a luck based level where you get a completely different scenario every time you enter a hallway. Its unique because I'm pretty sure it hasn't been done before.

**Additional Notes:** I need criticism.
