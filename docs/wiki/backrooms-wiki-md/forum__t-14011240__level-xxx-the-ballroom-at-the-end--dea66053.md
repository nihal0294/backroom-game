Level XXX "The Ballroom At The End"

24 Feb 2021 21:07

This level takes the form of an old ballroom. In the center, there is a record player that emits disturbing old ballroom music. It seems distorted. By the record player, there is a woman humming along and dancing as though she is with an invisible partner. If you manage to block out the noise of the music, the ballroom becomes broken down and trashed. Some wanderers think the sound is causing the hallucination of the ballroom being well kept. The woman appears to be under this same trance and has been driven insane. Leaving the ballroom leads to a run-down mansion. Minimal entity count.

(I have lots of details and more in-depth stuff for this level, but it's all just around the paragraph above, no new topics.)
