**Article Title:** Entity 290: Friend

**Sensitive Content:** Mild Gore

**Brief Description:** Appearing on Level 91 inside the house, Entity 290 takes the appearance of an item, animate or not, that had the most sentimental meaning to the wanderer. Even if the item it takes the appearance of is not animate, Entity 290 will be animate and lively no matter what. The appearance is in the viewers head because it appears as different things for all people, even when recorded by camera. If the viewer acts "appropriately" (does anything in the Do section) Entity 290 will act as a source of comfort to the viewer. If the viewer acts "inappropriately" (does anything in the Don't section) Entity 290 will act violently, similar to that of a rabid animal. If the viewer does not have anything sentimental to them, it simply will not appear to them.

**Critique Focus:** All types.

**Additional Notes/Remarks:** The name "Friend" is still being workshopped so name suggestions would be greatly appreciated. If I do choose one of you guy's name suggestions, I will give credit in the final article. I did also post this in the ideas critique channel on the Discord under the same name.
