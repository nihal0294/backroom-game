I have expanded my original *Temporal Canon* idea into something glorious.

# Multiverse Canon

Earth and our vast universe are but a single drop. Beyond this observable cosmos, there is a literal ocean of other universes. The Backrooms reality is, apparently, an important "hub" for portions of the multiverse.

There are many ways in which alternate realities might influence the Backrooms. Other storylines must exist. Feel free to discover (or create) the treasures of the multiverse.
