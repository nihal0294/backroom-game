Level Fun and it's old lore.

29 Oct 2023 01:05

Ah, the old backrooms… Not quite well writen, but surely memorable.  
Level Fun and the Partygoers were recently removed so they can be rewritten, and I agree that the lore was outdated and not really connected to the backrooms as a whole, but after Level 999's rewrite and the wiki fandom's rework of partygoers, I am afraid that the new lore might be disapointing, not because of it's quality, but because of how different it might be.  
And I was disappointed when level 999 was reworked, because now it does not have the same impact on the story of the backrooms as a whole, and now it's more of a forgettable level, mainly because it's not used to enter cluster 2 or even the frontrooms anymore.  
The old level 999 was not as confusing and empty as the new one, but not as well designed, and because of it's massive change, I don't think it's a good final cluster 1 level.  
With that in mind, you see where I am going? Level Fun is getting rewritten along with partygoers, and I'm afraid we might get something too far or even completely different than the OG story.  
Don't get me wrong, a rewrite is very welcome, but I think we should keep the main design and characteristics, and improve the overall writing and relations with other levels and entities to update the story, without just ignoring most of the original idea.  
Fandom's Partygoer rewrite is an example of this, it has some of the characteristics, but other than that, it's a completely new entity, good written? Yes. But a complete change on the original concept, and I think it's just a lame reason to remove roleplayers from the wiki, even if they are literally just kids having fun, and I'm sure most of us already did some cringy thing as a kid or teen.  
I hope you guys can understand my point, and if there are any questions about anything, feel free to ask me :)
