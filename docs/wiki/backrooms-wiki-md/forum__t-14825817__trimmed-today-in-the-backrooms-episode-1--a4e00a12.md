Honestly speaking, I really want to like this article, but I just can't. Even though I love the idea and the overall concept, I don't believe the execution is up to par.

My main problem is the dialogue. It just feels… unnatural and robotic, to say the least. The hosts feel cold and disconnected with no real discussion being held about the cryptids themselves. It feels like they're reading off a list. This problem becomes especially prominent in the last part. The guest is in this weird in-between state of being scared, but unnecessarily objective with the way they talk; it's just off-putting and uncanny. In addition to that, there are SPaG mistakes spread throughout the entire article. While they are minute, they do ruin the overall experience, if I do say so myself.

The premise of this series and theme of this specific article is spectacular. I do really enjoy it. However, I cannot bring myself to upvote this.

The writing itself needs a good amount of polish and touching up. There are some errors such as "and those who have checked under the carpets" repeating unintentionally and some bits that feel awkward to read, plus most of it is just either host reading off a brief description of an entity for a good chunk of text that's utterly devoid of personality before they make a couple quips about it and move on.

I'd really suggest a greater focus on the hosts' personalities first and foremost as well as breaking up the longer, blockier sections in ways that make them more interesting and give the article some substance.
