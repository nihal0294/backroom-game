Back from Tchernobyl (not a joke)

20 Jun 2023 18:50

Hello everyone.

My name is David, I am French, and I come from Nantes.

I apologize if my introduction was a bit messy; it's been about ten years since I last delved into a forum. Please forgive me if my English isn't very fluent.

I recently discovered the backrooms, and damn, I've been looking for them since my childhood.

It all started with a dream in a strange place. The geometry was bizarre, inconsistent. Then there were others, with places I knew. These dreams were strange but so real that they disturbed me for years, and since then, I've been spending my life searching for strange places. It led me to have one of the biggest urban exploration forums in France, and then to organize trips to the Chernobyl zone (I work with the NGO Tchernobyllab, now called Chernobyl Family, and here's a link to their YouTube page: <https://www.youtube.com/c/ChernobylFamily>). Today, I am a tattoo artist, but I have also been a concept designer and worked on a personal project: [My personnal project](http://www.modelers.fr/galerie).  
In my univers, we speack about "others frequencies", that is not an alternat réality, but another frequence of our own reality.

And then there's also this kind of visit that I do ([Vidéo of my child backrooms: I'm penrose Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hqAAiR_w3J0&t=4s)) (these caves are literally accessible from the basement of my house), and I occasionally organize visits to the catacombs in Paris.

But nothing will ever replace those strange places that I've been trying to find until today. And today, I would like to actively join the adventure.

Finally, if you want to connect and follow my work, you can find me on Instagram:  
[my tattoo univers](https://www.instagram.com/david_chardon_)  
or  
[sometime y draw](https://www.instagram.com/david_chardon_illustration/)  
If any team members would be willing to help me get involved in the project, I am highly motivated and eager to contribute!
