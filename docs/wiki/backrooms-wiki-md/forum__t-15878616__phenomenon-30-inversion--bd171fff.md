Phenomenon 30 - "Inversion"

21 May 2023 03:00

Next time you find yourself outside below the open sky, give a quick glance to the imposing heights that constantly look down upon us. Then, wonder what would happen if the only link one has to the surface of our beloved Earth, gravity, were to suddenly cease functioning.

Phenomenon 30 - "Inversion". If you happen to see the colours invert, watch your feet! You may be in for a good chunk of trouble.

I recently got this entry greenlit. It is also the first greenlight I've ever gotten. A lot of effort was put into it, so I hope anyone who wants to give it a chance enjoys it as much as I enjoyed writing it. It's such a simple, yet frenetic and deadly concept, I figured it had to be on the wiki one way or other. This is Backrooms! The result is, in my opinion, a fun, cool page, that gets more serious and uneasy the deeper you get into the read. Or so I hope.

Let me know what you think about this article, all opinions matter!
