Re: Level 179: The Lazy River (Seeking Critique And a Greenlight)

30 Dec 2022 06:04

\*ahem\*

[Here's what I got...](javascript:;)

> **Level 179** is a series of corridors with an appearance that mostly always matches each other.

"mostly always" is a contradiction. You could say it generally matches other hallways on this level.

> The wallpaper on the walls is a grassy area with palm trees and a bright blue sky.

I think you mean to say that it *depicts* that scene, not actually *is* that scene.

> In the corridors are platforms on each side constructed of cobblestone with water in the middle.

Instead of saying "in the middle", you could say "running through the halls". You could also day "cobblestone platorms" and save some extra words that aren't needed. Brevity is encouraged here when possible.

> The pumps in the water will push you in different directions if you are in it. You may find floaties in the water as the water takes you in random directions.

In the first sentence, use "random" instead of "different". As for "as the water takes you in random directions", use "as you are floating along the waterway"

> Throughout the entire level, music is present within.

You don't need "within", as it creates a redundancy.

> Throughout Level 179 are water parks, water parks vary in size.

Delete the comma and everything afterward. In its place, say "of varying sizes" and include a footnote of the largest discovered one and the smallest.

> They could be only one or two structures you'd usually see in a water park, up to huge water parks with slides and play areas.

Never use "you" in an article for the Backrooms. It's far too personal and we want a professional tone. Also, there are structures in there, but they aren't the structures.

> Wanderers had to rely on small water parks, bridges, slides, and other structures in Level 179 to navigate around the level.

"Wanderers have relied on…"

> The boxes with supplies were how wanderers survive within Level 179.

"Survivors trapped for extended periods of time have had to rely on these crates for survival."

> Sometimes, weapons could generate three times the power as regular weapons, which is very effective against the entities.

"Occasionally, weapons could be found in the crates, and they generate three times the power of their standard counterparts. This has proven effective in dealing with entities."

> Shortly before, a member of the B.N.T.G. no-clipped into Level 179 and reported it to one of the members they were in contact with through their radio, they described the area as being aesthetic or somewhat uncomfortable too.

Put a period after "radio" and begin the next sentence as normal. "as being aesthetic as well as somewhat uncomfortable."

> Wanderers Paradise use to be a M.E.G. base settled within Level 179 8 months after being found, but after 2 weeks of being settled inside one of the waterparks, the inhabitants started fighting **other** a room which has crates that could generate resources.

I think you meant "over". I bolded the mistake.

> After taking over the waterpark, the group had enlarged by a lot.

"Since taking the waterpark, the group has substantially grown, now housing X members."

That's all I got!
