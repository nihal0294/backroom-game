Hello RichardSongTT, I just read your article and the following is my conceptual evaluation of the article (I am not good at SPaG)

---

I really like this idea, especially the pictures, which make me feel very good.

> The **Level 415** area is exclusively illuminated by streetlights, as previously mentioned. However, investigations conducted among wanderers who have ventured into Level 415 reveal diverse perspectives on the color spectrum emitted by these streetlights: they can appear blue, white, yellow, green, purple, or even random switch among different colors. The researchers assert that there is no discernible pattern in the colors observed by wanderers. Furthermore, when multiple wanderers simultaneously observe the same streetlight at a specific location and time, each individual perceives different colors. Similarly, photographs of these streetlights captured using device1equipped with photography functions also demonstrate this phenomenon when viewed by different individuals.

I think you can try something else with the color of the light, such as adding conditions for different people to see the color of the light.

> [Level 175](level-175.md): Find a relatively dark corner within this level, curl up your body in the shadow of the corner, close your eyes and wait for a few seconds. The wanderer will feel their body moving rapidly, and after about a minute, they will feel like they are slowly stopping. The wanderer will successfully reach **Level 415**. This is also the only stable entrance to **Level 415**.

I feel that the exit of [Level 175](level-175.md) can be replaced with something else. Level 175 seems to have nothing to do with the forest trail.

---

I hope my suggestions can help you, have a pleasant writing experience! (Note: My native language is not English, I use a translator, and I also use a translator to read articles)
