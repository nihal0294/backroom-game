# The Rolling Dreams

![7v1Kbi7.png](https://imgur.com/7v1Kbi7.png)

"The Rolling Dreams" is an aerial view of the roller coaster tracks of Level 834.1. The calmness of the level can be reflected in the image. The tracks shine under a strange dim glow that follows someone through this level, and beyond this glow lies darkness.
