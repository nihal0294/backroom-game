Introduction to offsets  
For a while, offsets generally have a bad reputation for being very difficult and finicky. Many authors desire to use the trick, but are too intimidated by the long process and finicky process. Some of the most popular pages on the wiki use offsets and have very high upvote scores from it. Some articles with amazing coding magic use offsets in order to make said magic work. It’s easy to look at such articles and think that you could never do such a thing. This guide intends to change that.
