11 Jul 2022 18:18

Nate’s Grand Return to Writing has arrived everyone! First article in 4 months babyyyyyy

After spending 5 months working on a comic I have returned to writing with some funny jellyfish. This was originally a draft by which I rewrote for scrapcon2022. This idea was actually devised on a whim a few years ago. Me and some other people all made a server for a big collab project which… unfortunately fell through. When I saw the opportunity to bring these guys to site, I took it. Now here we are. Almost a year and a half later, The Saberzoa finally have a home. I hope you enjoy!

Thanks to for concept Crit and for SPaG
