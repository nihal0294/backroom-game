1. I have a feeling it would be better if the person who embarks on this adventure knows these people, beforehand, if you decide to keep this format. Who would go on a quest based on the premise of someone they do not know?

2. No, I'm referring to something ***along the lines*** of documenting the level in level format along with the commentary. To elucidate, it should look a little bit like this:

> **yowai\_mo\_1:** woah brudda dis level be creepin man

Level *n*'s architecture is abstruse, and its structural foundation is heavily corrupted. The walls are smeared with a translucent, red substance, and slowly creep to black once the smears reach their end. The wanderer may be overwhelmed by the lack of light and texture, as the entirety of the level is concrete.

3. I suppose that can be assumed. However, I see this is a facsimile of The Basement by Dr. Bierre. The Attic somehow mirrors The Basement, I assume. But, if this article can be published, you will need to make it more *original*. For now, it looks very, *very* inspired by Dr. Bierre. The fact an attic exists to The Basement of Level 0 is a great idea. However, you need to gain more **conceptual critique** on it.

Overall, I believe everyone on the site would love to see this blossom into a fully-developed concept. You just need some help to get there. If you have any more questions, I am all ears. For now, do some thinking! Get the gears going and think about how this idea can stand out from the rest. I will be right over here every step of the way.
