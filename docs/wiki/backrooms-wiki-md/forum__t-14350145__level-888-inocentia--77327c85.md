Inocentia is a corruption/portmanteau of two separate Latin words that respectively mean "innocence" and "guilt, harm, or transgression". Ava Alis is a corruption of the Latin words for "bird" and "wings". Lynn Odagrau is a corruption of the phrase "lake of tears" in Welsh.
