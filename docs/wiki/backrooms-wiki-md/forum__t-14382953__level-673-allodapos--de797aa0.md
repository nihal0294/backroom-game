Level 673 - Allodapós

12 Dec 2021 18:13

Page Type: Level

Elevator Pitch: The level looks like another world.

Central Narrative: None (As of yet).

Hook/Attention-Grabber: The level is alien-like.

Entities:

Tencles: It latches onto a victim's mouth and fuses with them.

The shapeshifter: Is the result of a impostor latching onto a victim's mouth, they can shapeshift into their original forms .

Big mouth: They camouflage in the ground and once someone steps on top of them, then Big mouth eats them. If there's a slightly dark cycle on the ground RUN.

Squid dog: They act as a dog but can breath underwater.

Birdbat: They are a hybrid of bats and birds, nutritious and on the food chain

Ischyrós: The big bad entity that is the leader of the level
