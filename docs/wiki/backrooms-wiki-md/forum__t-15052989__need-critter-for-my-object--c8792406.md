> Object 58: death masks.

Remove this qouteblock; when this is on the main site, the page's title will tell the reader what page this is.

> confined spaces in level 0.

"level 0" should be "Level 0".

> Eerie masks that are in very small, confined spaces in level 0.

I'd rewrite this entire section as what's shown below:

*Object 58 are small, eerie masks that are located within small and confined spaces within Level 0.*

> What the masks do to you:

I'd change this header to something like:

*Abnormal Side Effects:*

> These masks will drop your sanity to zero

"Sanity" is no longer something that is allowed to be mentioned on this wiki. Remove all mentions of it from your page. You can substitute this by saying that it causes lethal mental deterioration.

> it, the causes are unknown.

Change this comma to a semicolon and change "the causes" to "the causes for this effect".

> when their damaged,

Capitalize "when" and change "their" to "they're".

> You can use it as a shield because if an entity breaks it, they will be sent to a dangerous level.

This is actually a really cool aspect, very nice!

> let's just say… It isn't going to scream no more!

I'd personally remove this. This is extremely unprofessional for what is to be considered a M.E.G. written page.

---

The real issue with this page is exploring any depth. Why are they found in Level 0? How do they kill you? Why do they kill you? How does this effect any GoIs that decide to use them? Why does it cause you to no clip into a dangerous level? How are they made? Who made them? Why were they made? These are all ways you could expand your page; it is currently very short and there isn't much for me to really grab onto as a reader.
