Level 521 - "Foggy Light Show"

17 Mar 2026 20:13

**Article Link:** <http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/researcher-jay>

**Article Title:** Level 521 - Foggy Light Show

**Brief Description:** A class 0 level about an audio-interactive environment inside a thick fog. A laser show that's highly reactive to any form of sound input. Very tame environment.

**Critique Focus:** Polish, and maybe also modernization? (Considering the age, but the clinical tone should still work fine.)  
I remember someone nitpicking the partially very long footnotes. The footnotes are how I both like to go into high detail, and provide fairly unimportant scientific info. I don't believe that this is common practice, but I think fondly of it.  
The one minor entity is the result of my first critique. I didn't originally plan on adding any native life to this level, but I now think it's fine.  
Also, in case you aren't familiar: this level should be pretty obviously based on the VR game Beat Saber, partially known for its stunning VR light shows.

**Additional Notes/Remarks:** This level is something that I started almost four years ago, and have revisited once over a year ago. Both times I had gotten one feedback each, so this would be my third.  
I've finally chosen re-revisit it and make some more minor adjustments. I believe after a third 500+ character critique I may contact a green lighter?  
Also, I remember asking for feedback on the discord server, and probably getting it, but I don't seem to find it anymore.
