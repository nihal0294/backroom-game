It does seem a bit cliche, but with a bit of reworking and passion, this level could shine in the partygoers/partypoopers lore. Maybe change some things up, make a mystery. Sorta like "Reports of a single partypooper has been reporter here multiple times, even by highly trusted members." Or make it sure the Commander doesn't talk, yet he saves wanderers from entities before disappearing.  
And hey, don't think down of yourself. Every article sounds bad at first, maybe even 404 or The Fool. Now, they're good reads for the backrooms, and many enjoy them. Even if you think you can't expand on the idea, someone else can. Report it to the scrap con list if you can't write anymore, as it's been turned to a general dumping grounds. Keep writing, and always let that light shine, mate.
