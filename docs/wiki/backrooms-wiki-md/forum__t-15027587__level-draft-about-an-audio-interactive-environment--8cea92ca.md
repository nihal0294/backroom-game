Re: Level draft about an audio-interactive environment.

20 Jul 2022 03:53

I see promise in this draft but I *definitely* don't see a level called *Beat Saber* coming up…

At first, I was thinking "This is neat! Seems like there was inspiration from Beat Saber.

Then in the second half, you just described the whole game to me. Thaaat isn't very good.

So, I like the idea. A level *based* of Beat Saber sounds really cool, but the Backrooms isn't meant to be a video game world. If you rewrite the second half, as I can see that this level is in its early stages of writing, I would be glad to go over it again.

If *I* were writing this level, I would keep the first half of the desc, but maybe add an entity? One unique to this level, like a DJ. I would also reccomend using a cusom theme. Critical Status could work well.

Pm me for any questions/updates. Iamalemon
