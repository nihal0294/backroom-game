hooooo boyo the grammar errors  
1 abandon**ed** hotel  
2 according **to** "the Grade"  
3 same error as 1 but under the Description header  
4 A period would have been better instead of an awkward run-on sentence  
5 Opposite problem at the end of the sentence, a comma would have been better  
6 "some rooms can be trap**s** that lead to Level 210" would read better (no bold s)  
7 the pool is filled with **a** water-like substance  
8 Deathmoths, Skin-Stealers, Death Rats, and Smilers are common**ly** found  
9 **on** this level  
10 no comma needed between lamps and sunroofs  
11 The words rarely, sushi, rice, and chicken should not be capitalized  
12 and should appears randomly  
13 kitchen**s**, seeing as there are many rooms and thus many kitchens.  
14 fresh should be lowercase  
15 the word In should not be used  
16 hotel rooms should be lowercase  
17 although should be in between material and it  
18 the whole sentence is also run-on, cut it up a bit  
19: a part, not apart. apart means separated, like a piece of paper torn apart  
20 summer camp group should be lowercase because it is not the name of the camp group but its category  
21 entities, not entitys. this happens with all\* words that end with a y.  
\*except monkeys because english

You also need some entrances and exits, but you probably know that part. Pretty cool concept otherwise!
