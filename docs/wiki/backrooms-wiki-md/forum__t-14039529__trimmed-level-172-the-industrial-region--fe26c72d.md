Hello there, sorry for the late response. But the streetcars appearing in this level aren't necessarily of single type. What I imagined when writing this level, that any kind of streetcar, bus, etc. that existed outside of the backrooms can appear here.

The streetcar on the image representing this level is a 1950's T1 model from Czechoslovakia but any other kind of streetcar can appear on this level. Thus the models used in Toronto are technically possible to appear as well.
