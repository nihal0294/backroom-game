I am here to give critique! I finally got time, and Im happy that I have it! I saw the fact that you upgraded a lot since last time I read this, but it's still rough around the edges, and I am here to polish it!

[Here we are again](javascript:;)

[idk what to write here](javascript:;)

> Level 285 resembles an outdoor high school campus, the buildings inside the high school are in good condition.

I don't consider this change necessary, but I recommend the following change: **"the buildings inside the high school are in good condition"** to "with the buildings inside the perimeter being in good condition." to avoid the repetition of "high school" and to sound a tad bit more formal

> The outside of Level 285 is surrounded by a purple fog making it impossible to see your surroundings.

You should write a comma between "fog" and "making".

> it's not recommended to stay outside for too long as the gloom could take over take over your mind and have you join the ones with the gloom; so it's best to go outside when you need to.

This sentence has a massive contradiction, it being that you say "it's not recommended to stay outside for too long as the gloom could take over […]" and then you say "so it's best to go outside when you need to". You should change it to make it sound like you *can* go outside, but only if you really, *really* need to. Also, you repeated the "take over" and the semicolon isn't needed, just put a comma. Also, in this case you can do as you please, but you should make "the ones with the gloom" into that purple font that you use for mysterious text. Also, it wasn't really mentioned that the gloom has any mind-bending effect (not yet at least) so you should find a way to add some context to what the gloom does after, or just add this part later on, in the "gloom" sub-section, or remove it entirely.

> With your class schedule from the 1st to the 6th period, you must go to your first-period classroom, as the 1st period will start in 10 minutes. If you can’t make it, find the nearest classroom. If you don’t make it to class on time, all the classroom doors will be locked, and everyone who doesn’t make it to the classrooms on time must find the library, as the library will always be unlocked.

I don't wish to be rude, but you can barely understand what you are trying to say here. In my opinion, this whole sentence should be spaced out with full stops to improve ease of reading or just rewrote entirely, those are the only options. To be more clear, this whole part feels like a word jumble, with most words being over-repeated and having such long sentences.

> There are 5 buildings and they have 10 classrooms each, they are the safest place to be as in there, you can meet up with other students, the students can study or do whatever they want, Chromebooks are also present within the classrooms, with these Chromebooks, you can access websites like The Backrooms Database, CoolMathGames, and other unblocked websites to keep yourself sane and prevent boredom in this level, there are also board games to play and books to read to keep yourself companied, you can also talk and trade with other students in the classroom.

Wanna know what is wrong with this whole block of text? Punctuation. From hidden commas to full stops, punctuation. There should be a comma between "buildings" and "and", there should be a full stop instead of a comma between "each" and "they", there should be a comma between "be" and "as", there shouldn't be a comma "there" and "you", there should be a full stop instead of a comma between "students" and "the students", there should be a full stop between "want" and "Chromebooks", you should also add a "and" after the comma before "with" and after "classrooms" and there shouldn't be a comma between "Chromebooks" and "you", there should also be a full stop between "level" and "there" instead of a full stop and the word companied doesn't exist. Finally, there should be a full stop instead of a comma before the last "you".

> Masks are also provided in the classrooms so the fog won’t enter your mind or if you want to go outside, but only near the building.

You were reffering to this fog as "the gloom", so change that, and also remove **"but only near the building."** because it doesn't make sense in the sentence.

> In the hallways of each building, you can access other classrooms within the building, there are also water fountains that dispense almond water so you can have a refreshing drink or fill up your water bottle for later; so there's no need to go outside and get almond water.

The first comma isn't needed, you should replace the second comma with a full stop, and you should remove **"so there's no need to go outside and get almond water."**

> The Multi-Purpose Space is a Cafeteria and Gym combined into one building and is very habitable as the cafeteria serves food and you can play sports to sustain your sanity

In my opinion, everything after the end is not needed. It is just bonus information that is further explained later in the subsection, meaning it's useless/

> The cafeteria is where you can get your lunch and socialize with other students, even though the gloom is everywhere on the campus, there are still lunch ladies serving food to students, and you can also trade with other students in the cafeteria. There are also vending machines that dispense almond water.

The striked part is not needed, you should instead merge the two sentences into a phrase. An example how you could do that: "The cafeteria is where you can get your lunch, usually served by ladies in their late 40s, and socialize and trade with other students."

> The Gym is where you can play sports and hang out, the Gym is also where most athletic students play sports, such as Basketball, Football, Boxing, etc; it is best not to provoke them because they will physically harm you.

You should merge two of the sentences to create something like this: "The Gym is where you can play sports, hang out and the place the more athletic students play sports such as basketball, football, boxing, etc. It is best that you do not provoke these students, as they could physically harm you."

> The Library is where you can read the books in the library, you can read any of the books found in the library but you aren't allowed to check out any of the books because of the gloom and the lockdown. Abigail is also found in the Library.

This whole section should be rewrote from top to bottom and left to right. The worst mistakes are "The library is where you can read books from the library" because of repetition and redundancy and "you can read any of the books found in the library but you aren't allowed to check out any of these books […]" because of repetition and contradiction.

> The Front Office is where most of the school staff resides, you are not allowed to enter the front office as the staff would bring you back to your classroom, there’s also a nurse's office where the nurse can treat you with almond water.

There should be a full stop instead of a comma on the first comma and you say that there is a nurse's office but you don't add a separate description for it?

> The students are humanoid entities who mostly reside in classrooms, they can also be found in the library, cafeteria, or the GYM.

Remove the first comma and the "they" and instead put a "but"

> There's another variant of Students known as Jocks, they are mostly seen in the GYM playing sports like basketball, it's best to ignore them and not provoke them as they would physically harm you.

Change the second comma to a full stop.

> The Teachers are seen in classrooms teaching students or entertaining them, they are mostly docile unless you go outside the classroom without their permission.

Once again, change the comma to a full stop.

> Abigail is an entity exclusive to this level and is the most intelligent student in the school, Abigail is a teenage girl whose height is average, has long brown hair, and has hazel eyes, she wears glasses, a light blue blouse, and a long skirt. She can be seen reading a book in the Library, She is very friendly to wanderers and will give the wanderer a bottle of almond water and snacks & supplies to aid them in their Journey.

Change the first comma to a full stop. **"Abigail is a teenage girl whose height is average, has long brown hair, and has hazel eyes, she wears glasses"** I brainstormed a tad bit on how I could change this sentence, and I think I found how: "Abigail is a teenage girl with an average height, long brown hair, and hazel eyes covered by a set of glasses. She wears a blue blouse and a long skirt.". Also, the comma between "Library" and "She" should be a full stop. You should also change "the wanderer" to them because it's understood from context and avoids repetition. Finally, you should change the "and" between "water" and "snack" to a comma and the "&" must be changed to a "and" with a comma before it.

> She will help you with any problem answer any question, and give you tips on how to survive any level and counter any entities, she won't be able to answer questions like how to get back to The Frontrooms, She will also lead you to a safe level and make sure you make it out safe.

**"She will help you with any problem answer any question"** this sentence is wrongly formed. I would recommend that you do something along the lines of "She will answer almost any question". You should remove the first "and". You should also add a "but" before the "she" and after the comma and, also, replace the comma between "The Frontrooms" and "She" with a full stop.

> The Ones With The Gloom are very hostile entities, they used to be students and survivors who succumbed to the Gloom, they now roam outside of the campus looking for anyone to attack, they should never be approached, as they will attack anyone they see.

Instead of the first "they" add a "who" and remove that comma before it, replace the second "they" with a "and" and remove the comma before it. Finally, instead of a comma, add a full stop between "attack" and "they".

> The School’s Salesmen consist of exactly 23 members. They trade stuff like Almond Water, TMIG, normal snacks like chips, soda, and candy bars, they can also sell you weapons like slingshots and Squirt Guns; most members reside in classrooms while the other 6 stay at the cafeteria.

Instead of a semicolon you need and full stop.

> The Campus security consists of at least 12 members, they wear gas masks so the Gloom doesn’t corrupt their minds, their duties are to make sure any student stays inside and make sure they are not outside, they will escort any students back to their classroom and take down anyone that succumbed to the gloom.

All commas must be replaced with full stops.

Musn't the gloom be in a phenomena sub-section?

> You can get to this level by finding a school bus on Level 147 and you along with the other passengers on the bus would be taken to the entrance of the campus

There must be a comma between "you" and "along" and between "bus" and "would". Also, add a full stop at the end.

> Finding a classroom with a chalkboard and no clipping to the chalkboard will bring you to Level 202

Shouldn't the "to" be changed to a "through"?

> You could leave Level 285 by going outside which will take you to Level 147 or Level 9, it's very dangerous as the Campus security will take you back to your classroom or entities outside the campus will try and kill you, so use this escape route in your own risk

You should add a "but" after the comma after "Level 9" and "it's very" and the "in" must be change to a "at"

PHEW! That was all… for now. Your most reacurring problem is commas instead of full stops, as those were most of the mistakes when it came to punctuation. I would recommend that you do another revision yourself and spot some mistakes that I didn't by taking my critique and analysing it. Either way, can't wait for revision number 2! C'ya!
