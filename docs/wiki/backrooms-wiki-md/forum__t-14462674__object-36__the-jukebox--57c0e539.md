Object 36: The Jukebox

20 Jan 2022 05:07

**Page type:** Object

**Core concept:** A jukebox that plays a song and attracts Wanderers and potentially causes some sort of adverse effect. I'm not married to the idea, but my working idea is that it transforms them into Wretches, sort of like how ingesting Cashew Water can have the same effect.

**Additional notes:** I also have a paragraph explaining how Backrooms Robotics is attempting to conduct research on the Object, because if weaponized it does have the potential of being used as a sort of trap, idk. I'm not really married to this idea either.

I have been a longtime reader of the site, but this is my first post, and I really just want to see what some other people have to say about my idea because I think it has the potential to be a really cool idea. I have created a rough draft, so if anyone would like to take a look at the Sandbox, I have no issue with that at all.

It's an interesting idea for sure. I like the idea of luring Wanderers with its music, but maybe it could work better with more harsh consequences. Just throwing ideas out there, what if instead of immediately turning the Wanderers into Wretches, it could lure them into the same room as it and then use its music to create the illusion of the entrances and exits to the room becoming walls, leaving the Wanderer trapped. This could be the thing to turn the Wanderers into Wretches because of the increased anxiety and confusion and non-stop music(maybe the illusion would fade once the transformation is complete).

But I don't know. The idea has potential, keep working on it, it could be really good.
