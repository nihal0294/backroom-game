# **Description:**

Level 251 is a familiar looking level occupied by humanoid entities. The level appears to be multiple large cities with military installations every so often with varying sizes. The first and possibly main military base you come across is in the middle of a city and is called Installation 12. The Installation is giant, featuring 3 levels above ground and 10 below. Level 251 has a constant supply of electricity and unknown water. The power comes from unoccupied nuclear power plants found far from cities that are unoccupied. Unoccupied water treatment plants produce an unknown water type, It is safe to drink, however, any water that has not come through the treatment plants is dangerous to drink as it appears to show high amounts of radiation. The cities the soldiers guard look more like New York and Washington DC, but some look like London or other major global cities, you can ask for an escort to one of these cities and you will be sent with the next convoy. It is inhabited by [Soldiers](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-251).

# **Bases, Communities, and Outposts**

There are around 20 known military installations. They will be ordered by significance are listed in order below

## [INSTALLATION 12]

- The main military installation on this level is operated by a unit known as “Hells Servants” for their use of pyrotechnics.
- Operates using a hydrogen reactor to power everything and the surrounding city despite no civilians except wanderers living in the city.
- A large armory of weapons is stored here for safe keeping.

[Concerning Entity 251 Weapons](javascript:;)

[Weapons report](javascript:;)

Entity 251 weapons only work when being used by Entity 251, So we can't just get an infinite supply of weapons, they are rendered useless when in the hands of anything except an Entity 251. They also don't have infinite ammo. Many appear to wield melee weapons, around 35-40% wield melee weapons to be exact. But all have pistols for desperate situations.

## [INSTALLATION 4]

- A massive sprawling complex that researches and studies any new items brought in by expeditions.
- Features a massive underground section as well, filled with vaults for the most dangerous items they have found there is even a wall mask that we haven’t see before.
- Home of the research and bio weapons division
- Garrison of 4,000 entity's
- All experiments take place in this installation clean white halls
- Attempting to take any item especially a vault item is ill advised as all entities near will you become hostile and will demand you to gently lay down the object and back away, do as they say and they will simply ask you to leave the installation, refusal results in being stunned by some sort of electric coil gun, minor burn injuries.

## [INSTALLATION 7]

- This is the main trade base of the level. The B.N.T.G have set up a small building where they temporarily store trade items.
- Garrison of 2,000 entity’s.
- Main unit “Hammer Down”, a tank unit using M1A3 Abrams[1](javascript:;), 9 tanks to be precise, they have been seen using T-14 Armata’s but this is rare and T-14’s are usually used by “Blazing Winter”, a Russian tank unit.

## [INSTALLATION 13]

- Found in the middle of a plateau this base closely resembles Area 51.
- It has various fighter jets and bombers from across the 21st century even experimental ones yet to see service. The most common jets and bombers are American.
- They allow easy recon for the soldiers and will shoot flares near lone wanderer’s to signal their location.
- Surrounded by a electric fence and barbed wire to keep out unwelcome guests.

## [Abandoned MEG base]

- A MEG base that appears to be have been built long ago, time frame is murky but they estimate not long after the founding of MEG due to what items that were left behind. The items include early and crude weapons and armor plus some early fire arms.
- Believed to have been a sizable community of 200.
- Investigation ongoing, believed to have been led by a man called Jackson Davis, no record of him exists he likely came here before the database or he was never logged.

# **Entities**

There are few entities present here since Entity 251 calls this level home. But some can be found if you look hard enough. Entities found include [Smilers](entity-3.md), [Deathmoths](entity-4.md), [Facelings](entity-9.md), and [Hounds](entity-8.md). If Hounds or smilers pursue you it is best to flee towards a nearby base or patrol as they can deal with them easily, Facelings are usually left alone and child facelings will behave when they see soldiers, they will even stop attacking or playing tricks on a wanderer when the soldiers arrive.[2](javascript:;)

# **Entrances and Exits**

##### Entrances

A military steel door on [Level 1](level-1.md).  
No clipping through a random wall in [Level 13](level-13.md)[3](javascript:;).  
A painting on [Level 57](level-57.md) that depicts a military base.

##### Exits

Sleep in the only red bed in the barracks building and you will awake in [Level 1](level-1.md) near M.E.G. Base Alpha.
