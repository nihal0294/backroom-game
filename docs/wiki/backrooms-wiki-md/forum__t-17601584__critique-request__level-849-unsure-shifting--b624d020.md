So I read through this and it’s a really solid concept! Love the tie in to TH3 SH4DY GR3Y and the usage of the void. I don’t have much to say since the concept is really cool but I do have a bit of SPaG critique

> some of the flourescent lights work normally, while others are flickering or not working. are broken or flickering

There’s nothing necessarily wrong with this sentence I just feel it flows weirdly.

> but they are locked fortified with a strong glass

You can’t lock something with glass, but you can fortify. Assuming with the phrasing you wanted to say the glass can’t be broke or is hard to break.

> Even though the void is pitch black, a light from something bright can still shine the level, it's unconfirmed if there is a sun or a moon in this level. The void appears to be pitch black, however, the inside of the level is perfectly lit. Where this light comes from is unknown.

The usage of the word ‘level’ so many times throws off the rhythm, the phrasing of the light source felt a bit redundant too.

> Level 849 has [unique] rooms and corridors whose behavior are different from the other rooms, which behave differently from others.

The whose sounds strange in this context as the pronoun is used towards living things, unless this is what you’re going for it’s not needed.

> It is possible to enter a room while the shifting is happening, it can lead into more rooms, or it can lead to nothing but the void. Even though it is possible, it's not recommended, as it can cause getting stuck, lost, injured or worst. It’s unadvised to do so as there is a chance of falling into the void. Due to the nature of how the rooms move it’s also highly possible to get injured by doing so. No-clipping on the Shifting Rooms is possible, though it isn't recommended, as it have a big chance of making you fall to the void, falling into the void doesn't kill you, but it takes you to the place you spawned. If you do so happen to fall into the void, you’ll be teleported back to where you entered the level.

Ok this one’s bulky, there’s several ideas going on here, it sounds like you can only fall into the void as a danger, but you can also get injured as a danger, but it’s actually not dangerous at all if you respawn. The sentence builds up to nothing in a way. So the alternative provides a bit more clarity.

Also it’s a bit of a stretch for a SPaG critique and I may be out of my depth here but if you still want to make it a danger to fall into the void (because at the moment there’s really no reason people should be afraid of falling other than having to be mildly inconvenienced) you could add the possibility that it’s extremely disorienting once being teleported.

> Level 849's rooms tend to start shaking, making walls crack and break, tables fall over, paper scatter, and other things, it happens because Level 849's stability is very low, causing it to shake sometimes, people theorize the level may collapse on itself, but is still not confirmed if it will fall on itself or not. has a tendency to start shaking, which can cause damage to anything inanimate within the rooms, its theorised— due to how the level acts —its quite unstable, no obvious hints lean towards evidence of its collapse as of now. Level 849's rooms can also have holes in it, the holes, leading to the void aren't a problem, as the majority of the holes are small enough to step right across it. Further evidence of this instability shows in holes throughout the level, though majority of these aren’t hazardous due to their small size.

Ooh boy, another bulky one. So I found this confusing as I thought this was in relation to the shifting rooms with how the article flowed, so I tried playing around with the wording. It seems like incorporating the instability level throughout this while segment would work quite well, as the original sentence seemed like an afterthought.

> Another thing that can happen is the blackouts, Blackouts are common in Level 849, they can happen every 8-15 minutes,. When the blackout occurs, usually the level gets really dark, but not as dark as Level 6, no light source can shine the level, only the "sun"/"moon" light can. the darkness can be described as pitch black however it is confirmed to be brighter than Level 6. Because of that, traversing the level becomes almost impossible without relying on the "sun"/"moon" light, as the chance of falling into the bigger holes is very high. After 30 minutes, the power comes back, making the level bright again.

A couple more sentences seemed like afterthoughts so I tried to incorporate them more. The sentence stating the blackout gets “really dark” is kind of obvious and redundant.

> The void on in this level has a special proprety that makes whatever any photo you took close to the void corrupted depending on how close you are to it. taken close to the void corrupt. This can be avoided by being far away from the void or/and being infront a wall or a window. A photo far from the void is normal, a little far from it makes the photo have a tiny glitch, close to it makes the photo have a lot of glitches. In regard to the distance the photo is taken, the closer one gets to the void the more Corrupt the image becomes.

The same issues as the last point

> Going far from the center of Level 849 and looking out the window, it's noticable a presence of a black hole, going far also changes the level's stability, getting lower each 10 kilometers. there’s a notable presence of a black hole. Every 10 kilometres the level appears to degrade in stability.

> The black hole looks like a normal black hole with the photon ring, but the clouds doesn't seem to get sucked up by the black hole. Getting close to the void with the black void, gets you sucked up slowly, getting close to the blackhole makes you pass out, and makes you go through it. The black hole doesn’t seem to affect the surrounding area, it’s not recommended to get close as it yields the same results as that of one in the front rooms.

It feels a bit tedious to list what happened at every distance you are from the black hole, it’s better to state that the black hole acts like it’s known.

> 7 people [reportedly] got sucked up by lost in the the black hole, 6 of them didn't respond to M.E.G's message, nobody knows where did they go, presumed dead. six of these people have been presumed dead. Only one person responded to M.E.G's message, the person was exploring Level 849 when a blackout happended and he fell through one big hole, after sometime without signal, he responded, saying he got into a 5tr4ng3 pl4c3… The one person who did survive, was able to reach out to the MEG, he reported he was in a 5tr4ng3 pl4c3…

That’s all I could find that stood out in terms of SPaG, once again in terms of concept is great and as far as I can say doesn’t need too much revisiting. Let me know if you need any more help. Sorry for stretching the corrections a bit, some parts are just fully rewritten but I tried my best to keep it close to what you wanted.
