Level 127 is the 128th level of the Backrooms.

---

Level 127 is composed of a series of corridors and rooms. The walls of Level 127 are either made of plywood or cinderblocks. These walls are covered in a substance that was later identified to be human bodily liquids and tissues along with other substances, while the floors are constructed of concrete or tiles depending on the room a wanderer is in and coated in that same/a similar substance. Along with this, Level 127 has been described to smell like fish and rotting meat.

Silver pipes run through every room and hallway across the ceilings. Although these pipes vary in size, they are usually empty except for an occasional Human Blob attempting to traverse through them. These pipes, when they are empty, can be used to create weapons and/or tools to help defend against the native entities on this level.

Structures like household water heaters, furnaces, and radiators can all be found within Level 127. These structures are quite common within this level and are fitted within nearly every room and hall. They are anomalously powered and cannot be turned off. If destroyed, an instance of any of these structures will repair itself rapidly once it leaves a wanderer's line of sight, regardless of how damaged they may be. Also, these structures cannot be affected by the produced heat in any way.

This level shares common attributes with [Level 2](level-2.md) by producing lethal heat over time. The main culprits of heat production are the aforementioned structures. They produce heat that can climb to approximately 93.3o Celsius (200o Fahrenheit). This is hot enough to melt human skin and bodies down to a blob-like entity. This heat will continue to climb during the time a wanderer is within this level. However, if multiple wanderers enter Level 127, the heat will climb at different rates for each individual.

The entities on this level have been described as a human blob or a fleshy human amalgamation, aptly named Human Blobs. These entities can be found in abundance throughout Level 127. Human Blobs can be heard to sob and whine while endlessly traversing through the level's pipes and hallways. When contact is made with the entities, they will cause severe burns and blisters on the wanderer's skin. Upon further contact, the wanderer will combust into flames, later turning them into a Human Blob. However, Human Blobs have not usually been seen to aggress towards wanderers but rather attempt to seek help and mercy from the level, though in some cases they can become aggressive for unknown causes.

## Description

Level 127's native entities, commonly known as Human Blobs, are vaguely humanoid creatures that resemble a fleshy blob or chewed gum and endlessly roam Level 127 in hopes of escaping. Human Blobs communicate by a series of gurgling, crying, whining, and coughing noises, however, words from the Human Blob's past language can be understood on rare occasions.

## Behaviours

Human Blobs tend to wander Level 127 in hopes of escaping it by travelling through the silver pipes inside, entering furnaces and heaters, and trying to enter cracks in walls. This is always unsuccessful due to all these places leading to nowhere. When wandering Level 127, Human Blobs can be heard to make noises in hopes of catching the attention of nearby wanderers for help, but to wanderers, these often sound like random noises.

If a Human Blob spots a wanderer, it will slowly slide and squirm over to said wanderer and plead for help. Occasionally, they also tend to purposefully harm wanderers to hopefully regain their original bodies.

The ways Human Blobs attack are:

- Wrapping their bodies around the wanderer.
- Attempting to consume the wanderer.
- Dismemberment.

After a Human Blob successfully terminates a wanderer, they will then climb onto the body and assimilate the carcass for energy and to attempt to regain a normal body.

## Biology

Human Blobs are fleshy human amalgamations of their living counterparts with several limbs, organs, and faces being displayed out of different regions of their bodies. It is noted that most of these limbs, faces, and organs are fully functioning and do not have a limit on their functionality no matter how many bodily structures there are. Human Blobs bear minimal bodily hair and skin on their surface, although their hair and skin can often be found on their inside.

The immense heat within Level 127 gives the Human Blobs' fleshy exterior and interior a deadly temperature. Contact with its exterior instantaneously inflicts several 3rd degree burns and boils.

A Human Blob's exterior secretes an oily substance that is brown and translucent, and it boils when secreted. This contributes to the harmful effect when touched. In addition, this liquid tends to stay on the wanderer's skin until it is washed off. This causes more harm the longer it is on the skin.

These entities produce a pungent odour that smells like that of rotting and rancid fish. This odour can be smelled throughout all of Level 127. The odour is so pungent that no matter the breathing aid a wanderer wears, this odour can still be smelled through it. This odour has been studied to have a high vapour density causing the entirety of Level 127 to be humid. The liquid that can be found in its condensation is the same liquid that is secreted off from the Human Blobs.

## Do's and Don'ts

###### Do's

- Ignore them.
- Attack if being aggressed.
- Avoid bulging pipes.

###### Don'ts

- Touch.
- Respond to their noises.
- Consume their carcasses.
- Be noticeable.

## Bases, Outposts and Communities:

There are no known bases, outposts or communities on this level.

## Entrances and Exits:

###### Entrances:

Level 127 can be accessed through [Level 2](level-2.md) or [Level 64](level-64.md) either through a hotter area of the level, through a door on a lower floor of the level, or a plywood door.

###### Exits:

To exit Level 127, a wanderer must find a hallway fitted with multiple sets of crematorium furnaces. After finding this hallway, the wanderer must climb through one of the furnaces. This will lead back to [Level 2](level-2.md) or to [Level 90](level-90.md).
