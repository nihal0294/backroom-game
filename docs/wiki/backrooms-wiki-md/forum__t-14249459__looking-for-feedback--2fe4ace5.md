Alright, so overall I do like the main idea here. The whole "the entities are non hostile " in the erea is a unique concept. However I do have some nitpicks and questions:

1) What makes this level so special? How will you portray this in the context of an article?

2) the anomales are friendly at a certain time??? Why? And how so? Is there another greater cause for this? Almost like some kind of curfew? What makes the entities suddenly become aggressive? I think a unique twist here instead of those loosing sanity is **to have the creatures suddenly turn aggressive**

3) what's so significant about the number 9 here? If anything I'm shocked you didn't pick the witching hour here to be midnight since that would mean 12 hours of peace and 12 hours of terror. Fair and equal.

4) By saying "not a permanent level", how so? Is there clipping that happens ? How does one escape the level?

5) Setting. You lack a setting here. I have no visual as to what the place looks like here. When writing an article, you'll need to go into detail to describe the appearance and nature of the place. You cannot miss out on any details, make the article as informative as possible. I've seen way to many bleak and short articles, so keep that in mind.

Overall I do like the main idea, but there's work that needs to be done.
