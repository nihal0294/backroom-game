**Level 599** is the 600th level of the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

Level 599 appears to be an endless void, with nothing but dust and faint lights that leads to nowhere but dead stars. The oxygen here is thin, so you can easily suffocate here due to a small amount of air. You are able to breathe by a really small light entering your throat, and eventually going to your lungs. But, if you move too fast or hard, that will make the light start to shut off, making you suffocate.

The second you enter this level, you immediately lose control of your own body. That’s because no surface actually exists on Level 599, however, if you somehow relax, you are able to keep yourself still and you can explore this level with no problems. No entities are actually present in this level, but dying is still possible (like lack of oxygen, triggering a large star explosion, etc.), the stars here however, are in an immense amount in size. Triggering them will be immediate death, so it is really recommended to stay at least 50 feet away from one.

At some random times, wanderers may experience a “Reality Time Flashback”, which means that they will see all the good things they did in the past, but experiencing this long enough may lead one to dark flashbacks. [The M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md) stated that only a few people have actually made it out alive on Level 599, their cause of death was unknown. There’s also another occurrence that a wanderer may experience, this time, it’s another hallucination but they will actually think they’re in reality, also known as [The Frontrooms](the-frontrooms.md). This will make the person wander around aimlessly until they are about to trigger a large star explosion, which causes death as fore-mentioned. The only way to get out of this is by controlling your mind, it may hurt, but it’s worth saving your life. Most theorized that corpses don’t rot in this level, except some disgusting fungus eats their body until it’s gone, but, this theory was proven to be false.

# **Colonies and Outposts:**

Colonies and Outposts are unable to be established due to the nature of this level.

# **Entrances and Exits:**

## Entrances

- It is possible to enter here by noclipping through [Level 63](level-63.md) the second you fall off.
- Finding a door marked with morse code translating into: “The Void of Nothingness and Forgiveness”, once you open and enter the door will disappear behind you, and you will be here.
- *Remembering all your sins.*

## Exits

- The only confirmed way to exit this level is to find a large beam of light, in form of a crack. It will take at least 35 minutes to get there, after that, you must go through it, it may be risky but you did escape. This will take you to a much more easy and safer level.

# **Archived Logs**

*The following log was revived from [The M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md), the wanderer who recorded their experience is unknown.*

> **COMMENCE**  
> **Wanderer:** Jesus, like, where the hell even am I? I see nothing but, HOLY SHIT-, ok, lost control for a second. But, I see completely nothing.  
> **5 minutes elapse**  
> **Wanderer:** I keep following these faint lights, but everytime I do they just lead to nothing. Why the fuck is this level so confusing?  
> **20 minutes elapse**  
> **Wanderer:** I’m still wandering around, no entity has been in sight yet. But, I sure hope that they will not be anywhere.  
> **2 minutes elapse**  
> **Wanderer:** No god damn exit yet? Why? What the- FUCK-  
> **15 minutes elapse**  
> **Wanderer:** I keep fucking losing control, I’m staying completely still just so it won’t happen again. I think I can’t breathe either, because I feel less air coming through my body.  
> **41 minutes elapse**  
> **Wanderer:** Is that… a light? It’s in form of a crack thing or something, but fuck this. I am going there right now, I’m just gonna try to run as fast as I can, and I don’t give a fuck if I lose control or something.  
> **END**
