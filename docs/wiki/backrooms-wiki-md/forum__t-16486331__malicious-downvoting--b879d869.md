Malicious Downvoting

01 Dec 2023 15:29

So, I've seen this a lot with some of the newer users:  
They will have their main reason for downvoting an article be simply because the article does not have an image. I've even had it happen to one of my articles.  
The lack of image should not be even considered a proper reason for downvoting an article, in my opinion, simply because an author does not have to include one. That is their choice. Furthermore, if a person has enough descriptory details, then an image would not be needed. In addition, finding an image that perfectly matches something, even a level, can sometimes be difficult if one is not basing the article on an image.  
If you want me to write an essay, I will, but this essentially summarizes my thoughts.
