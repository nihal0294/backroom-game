Break

28 Sep 2023 13:40

I'm planning to take a nine day break starting on this sunday. There are couple of reasons for this. The main one being that I have fall break then.  
However, I have been feeling what I would describe as burn-out regarding writing in general. I have regarded this site essentially as a job that I really love doing. But that doesn't mean that I can't get tired of doing it. This, combined with school, hasn't been giving me much time to enjoy doing other things I like doing, like reading books for fun(yes, I know, I'm a nerd). Two things that I consider work taking up every minute of my life really isn't doing me good for my mental health, and I figured that a break would do me some good, and get me feeling a lot better in giving critique (whether as a normal user, trainee, or as a greenlighter) in the future.  
I apologize for any problems this may cause for others(specifically my rewrite collab partner, the user I'm working with to create a canon, the user who I sent my greenlighter application into recently, and the user who is helping me get one of my article posted and who agreed to train me through pm to become a greenlighter if I got denied).  
Thank you for understanding,  
**~** Pen.

Hey, im glad you looking out for your mental health, but i can't help but feel like you might of found yourself in a predicament.  
First if all i assume you joined as a hobby, but now you are considering it a job, this may not be the best, i do understand that you are looking to do alot here, but i feel like it should be considered at most like a side gig.  
The place you find yourself now is balancing between a dozen projects (some with other people so you feel more obligated to work on it), you have bit off more than you can chew and is causing this exhaustion.  
The problem is that one day you might not feel burned out, but sick and tired, but still feel obligated to continue, while this may sound a bit unrealistic, it could happen. And i don't want to see you fall down this rabbit hole  
So please remember, you are not obligated to anything here, and think before you start another large project think, "do i really have room for this". Im not saying to stop half of your projects, just be a bit more wary before starting alot of stuff at once.  
Though i could be overlooking everything, but as a online friend i don't want to see you fall down this rabbit hole of stress and obligation.
