It reads like a fever dream, which is quite endearing. There's a certain surreal nature to it, where the Level is just explained and we have to take it at face value, as if the wanderers stuck inside the Level just innately knew a sound they heard dates back from *decades ago.*

It is and short and concise, while also remaining quite faithful to the classic aesthetic of the Backrooms.

I will say that the one issue here is the suspension of disbelief this page requires: the information is given away with no clear explanation of how "we" know it, especially since there seems to be no real way out of there.
