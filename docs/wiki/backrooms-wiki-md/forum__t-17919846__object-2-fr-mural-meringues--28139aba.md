i think the page itself has aged a lot (especially the partygoer reference but to be honest i find that charmingly of its time, i can't knock it)

"mural meringues" is an in interesting choice but i think it's probably worth the alliteration to sound slightly unnatural. Mural is usually not an adjective but a noun in english so « meringues murales » would just be "wall meringues". In lieu of an actually good replacement pun of which I believe there is none, keeping the alliteration is the kind of decision I can get behind.
