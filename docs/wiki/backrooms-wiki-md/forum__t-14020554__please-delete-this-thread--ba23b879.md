\*\*Level 1000 is the 1000th Level of the Backrooms. \*\*  
This level is similar to an American style coffee shop from The Frontrooms. Only native to one entity who seems to be human that no clipped in the frontrooms while selling coffee.

# **Description:**

Level description. This level is an American styled coffee shoe home to only one entity who’s name is “Manson”(more on him in ENTITIES), the coffee shops is the size of a normal coffee shop upon first look, however the more you walk the more the level grows spawning in more tables and chairs. Behind the counter there is a door that leads to a hallway that is infinitely long with just as many doors lining the hallway. Inside the doors are rooms that can only be accessed with “Mansons” permission. The rooms contain cabinets that are fully stocked with Royal Rations, Twin fingers of all sizes and flavors, and plenty of almond water. The closets are full of weapons and clothes that suit whoever enters the rooms style. There are doors that lead outside of the room that lead to a pool that expands to people’s preference and is completely safe to swim in. Photos of this place become corrupted for unknown reasons.

# \*\*Colonies And Outposts\*

There are no known colonies or outposts due to the extreme difficulty to access this level, although it is highly advised to make colonies here due to the seemingly infinite amounts of supplies

# ENTITIES

There is only one known entity on this level, he is the barista of the coffee shop. There is quite a bit known about this entity because of how kind he is and willingness to be interviewed.  
**Interview log 1,000- Date 3/6/2021**  
Preface: this is an attempt to interview the entity named ‘Manson’, in an attempt to learn more about what comes after exiting level 999.

---

Begin log 2:04 Pm  
Interviewer: M.E.G Researcher Pablo  
Interviewee: Entity 197- “Manson”

---

**Pablo**- This is Researcher Pablo can you please state your name and why you reside here?  
**Manson** My name is Manson and I serve here to help keep the balance of the backrooms and help anyone who enters this place.  
**Pablo** What do you mean by keep the balance?  
**Manson** When somebody enters my shop I can see what they will do in their life and how their actions will affect the backrooms, if it is bad then I try to help them as much as I can by giving them a room and just being helpful in order to put them in the right direction.  
**Pablo** I see, now can you tell me about the liquid ‘comensate that you put in your drinks for your customers?  
**Manson** I would be glad too, it’s a tasteless liquid that helps aid the bodies regeneration rate by about 50% and increases how much weight one can lift up by 50%,(ie, if you can lift 100 pounds, you will be able to lift 150 pounds with relative ease).  
**Pablo** And can you tell me what the pure form of liquid comensate does?  
**Manson** Sure, if any Entity other than me consumes it, they will have a heart attack and die, even if I consume it I pass out for a week, so I heat my ring up and quench it with the stuff, this makes it so the normal effects of it compound infinitely, ie normally if you could only lift 100 pounds, you would be able to lift 200 pounds, then what would happen is it would take that amount and make you able to lift 400 pounds and so on and so on. The same thing happens with the regeneration rate making me entirely immortal.  
**Pablo** I see, and is there any way to exit this level?  
**Manson** Absolutely, just tell me what level you would like to go to and you will pass out and wake up there with plenty of supplies, this also works with just thinking of the level, level one I see, goodbye.  
//There is a thud heard and the audio cuts out and resumes presumably on level 1, Researcher Pablo woke up with a worn backpack full of royal rations, food and almond water. //

# \*\*Entrances And Exits

#### Entrances

There is only one known entrance so far which is to enter from level 999 by repenting all of your sins.

#### Exits

If you wish to exit you simply ask Manson which level you would like to go to, you will pass out and wake up at said level with a worn backpack full of supplies
