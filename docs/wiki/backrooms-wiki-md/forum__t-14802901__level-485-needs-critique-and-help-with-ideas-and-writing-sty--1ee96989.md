Level 485, Needs critique and help with ideas and writing style

08 Jun 2022 07:52

(Note Level's class is Unknown)  
Level 485, known as "Face Yourself" is a supposedly endless void with a white colouring.

DESCRIPTION: Level 485 is a white void with random buildings, landmarks, and very rarely fragments of other backrooms levels, it has been known to have been described as being "similar to The Blue Channel", the only differences apart from the void's colours being different, are that there is ground, although it is invisible to the naked eye during a supposed 'day' time, during the 'night' time it is slightly lighter than the sky, 'day' here lasts around 7-8 months, while 'night' lasts for around a month. When people make it to level 485, they will often mistake it to be The Blue Channel

SETTLEMENTS: Level 485 has a supposed 60,520,000 inhabitants, no known colonies have been set up.

ENTITIES: Many entities inhabit level 485, Smilers, Facelings, Partygoers and many more have been seen here. The 'Master' entity is a shapeshifting creature that takes on a nightmarish version of the person that encounters it. How deadly the form is depends on what you have done over your life, the creature is essentially judging you. Renditions include but are not limited to: Your Faceling, a faceling version of you. You get this weak, hostile, faceling if you have been a kind person, but have committed a crime in the past; Cerberus, a version of you that has the body of a dog, and has three heads, the middle one is yours, the left is a close family member, and the right is a person who has had a big impact on your life, you get this by being someone who disrespected your family and friends, this form is incredibly powerful and should be avoided AT ALL COSTS. Your Worst Nightmare, the form is the person fused with their greatest fear, this form is usually seen by people who have committed a horrible crime like murder, this form is the most deadly with odds of someone surviving being extremely slim.

ENTRANCES: You can get here by noclipping into a smashed window on level 4 or by finding a standalone white door in The Blue Channel, which will disappear after it is entered

EXITS: To leave here, you must defeat a nightmarish rendition of yourself, known versions are explained in the entities category, or enter one of the levels found around the void

NOTES: it is known to have creatures from the frontrooms fall into this level and stay for 2-4 weeks before noclipping back into the frontrooms, no one knows how or why they come here.
