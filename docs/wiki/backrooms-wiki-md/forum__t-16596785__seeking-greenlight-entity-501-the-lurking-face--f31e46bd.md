Seeking greenlight Entity, 501 the lurking face

01 Jan 2024 17:11

~~~~ Entity 501 or the lurking face resides in levels 1 2 but has been spotted in others. The sightings in other level’s are not confirmed.  
Entity 501 will only be seen in dark places, and it must be dry enough in those dark places or the entity will not appear in that section of the level.

### Description:

A lurking face is a tall, green figure with a green smile. it is almost always hostile.  
The Best way to escape a lurking face is to move slowly away, and to not make a noise.  
Lurking faces, appear on level 1 and level 2 or in places within an abundance of smilers

![IMG_2087.jpg?ex=65a3144c&is=65909f4c&hm=c824ab7ca349997847b12e3dfe42d007be631e9a96ef20c6bd1515185f4fcacf&](https://cdn.discordapp.com/attachments/897250716626857984/1190789667826704424/IMG_2087.jpg?ex=65a3144c&is=65909f4c&hm=c824ab7ca349997847b12e3dfe42d007be631e9a96ef20c6bd1515185f4fcacf&)

### Behaviors:

Lurking faces are almost always hostile to any human that enters there sights  
They always reside in dark Places and become sluggish and tired in light.  
lurking faces, will attack you if they hear a noise coming from you or if you stay in their line of sight for too long.

### Biology:

Lurking faces are tall, green, humanoid figures wearing cloaks they have what looks like a mask on, but is actually their face. The mask has two glowing eyes and a unhinged almost manic smile. Smirking faces are seemingly part machine and that could be the reason why they like to stay in dry places. Although it is unconfirmed.

### Discovery:

An anonymous Reddit user discovered this entity on level two after multiple smiler attacks, they eventually saw a lurking face learned of its patterns, and managed to avoid it, but they say that in its presence they felt nauseated, and almost to the point of throwing up

Lurking faces, although friendly to most entities, absolutely despise death rats And will kill them on site

### **Dos and Don'ts:**

### **Do:**

Avoid at all costs  
Use water as a weapon

### **Don't:**

Stare at a lurking face  
Stay in the presence of a lurking face too long  
Make any noises
