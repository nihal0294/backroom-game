I find the idea of a pathway that branches off into three various pathways interesting, but I have a few problems and critiques.

1. Sometimes the description mentions us the reader in a second-person point of view.[1](javascript:;) While it can be used correctly in a story by luring the reader into it by mentioning them like something knows they are reading this, in the text, it just seems like lazy writing or something meant for kids and not some secretive file on a sublevel of what is essentially a different dimension.

As a bit of an example take **Level 0**[2](javascript:;). Reading through it, it never directly addresses the reader and as a result, makes it seem like a secretive military document on a dangerous area meant for the personnel of a secret government agency which it pulls off well. The writing feels mature because of this and gives it the feel of classified government information.

Here are a few examples from the description that could be fixed:

- "you will find yourself at the end of a road, with only a single way to move forwards"
- "you may find yourself suddenly far beyond where the others are"
- "Trying to walk off the path will result in an invisible force holding you back."

2. Unprofessional writing and inadequate details can crack apart a story.[3](javascript:;) This is the same here. While reading this I found myself having to go back and reread some parts of it because some of the text did not make any sense to me. I feel if it went further into detail on some parts then the whole level would be better for it.

Into the text, it mentions, "Trying to walk off the path will result in an invisible force holding you back". Maybe if the text answered how it holds you back since it clearly describes that it isn't an invisible barrier this time it may make the sentence make more sense. Of course, this is just one example. Plenty more could be added to the text and I believe it would make the level more interesting.

3. The idea of a pathway breaking off into three different pathways is an interesting concept. From what I got out of the text it is a gateway between various levels. Perhaps adding some examples of where some of the people who exited this level went would add a bit more flavor and detail to the text.

**Level-666** or **Welcome to Hell**[4](javascript:;) pulls this off quite well adding in recorded manifestations in the text which helps the reader visualize what would happen after leaving the level. It explains what happened to those who entered the level and the outcome without coming off as filler text and more of as simple documentation.[5](javascript:;)

Small possible example:

**Recorded Testimony #X**

---

**Date:** XX/X/XXXX

**Subject:** XXXXX XXXXXXXX

**Entered Pathway:** **The XXXXX Doorway**

**Level Entered Into** **Level-X**

Of course, these are all just my ideas and I may just be bad at grammar and this is all just my opinion. These are all just merely suggestions and besides some of these critiques, it has an interesting concept and a simple premise that make it unique on its own. I do feel that if these small issues were fixed then it may be ready for greenlighting.
