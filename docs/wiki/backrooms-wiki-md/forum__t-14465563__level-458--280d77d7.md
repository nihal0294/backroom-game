**Level 485** is the 486'th Level of the Backrooms.

# **Description**

- The level is an infinite basement with seemingly no end point. It's filled with cabinets and doors. The walls are broken down and there maybe mold. There has been some encounter with some entities such as death moths on this level but mostly it's empty space. You can find liquid pain and almond water in the cabinets. You can be teleport to a different part of the level if you entered a yellow door.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

## The Basementor

- The base has concrete wall surround it and inside there are houses and shops.
- The base is formed by smarts humanoid entities. They have about 32 members.They are safe to interact unless provoked. You can trade stuff with them in the shops.

# **Discovered**

- The level was found on 9/4/2019 by u/UltraSolgaleoZ.The user quickly contacted M.E.G. The user vanished and no trace can be found.

# **Entrances And Exits**

### Entrances

No-clip through a plant in level 5.  
Drink 350ml of almond water in level 9 and then come in to a blue house near the entrance.

### Exits

Walk up a set of stairs and you will be teleport to level 4, 7, 32 or 45.  
Open a blue door and you will be teleport to level 0 or 1
