An important message to you all

02 Jul 2021 22:07

I wrote this level in hopes that it might encourage some people to reflect and recognize the simple, basic things in life, from silverware to shoes, and realize the work and effort that so many unnamed and unrecognized people put into producing these objects for your convenience. During the quarantine, I was bedridden with serious medical issues for six months, and during that time I developed this mindset of gratitude in order to keep a positive outlook on life. It's been transformative and truly helped me in so many ways, and thus I want to share this perspective with you in hopes that it can have a positive impact on you as well. Thank you for reading!
