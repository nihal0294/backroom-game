Level XXX - The Food's Court

30 Jul 2021 15:37

**Page Type:** Backroom Level

**Genre (Optional):** Comedy

**Page Layout (Optional):** Normal layout with a transcript of a security camera on the level in-between "Bases, Outposts, and Communities" and "Entrances and Exits"

**Elevator Pitch:** A food court that is literally a court for humanoids that are giant versions of various foods.

**Central Narrative:** Most of the article will just be describing the Food's Court. In the security camera footage, it will document a wanderer's experience stumbling into this level with a half-finished meal (burrito, burger, slice of pizza. Something like that) and being put on trial for his acts of violence upon food. (with many food puns throughout!)

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** The Level's name will be "The Food's Court" and talk about living foods that hold trials for any wanderers stumbling into the level which I hope will keep readers interested to see how a trial plays out.

**Additional Notes:** I haven't tried to write dialogue much so I'm hoping to get better while writing the security camera transcript.
