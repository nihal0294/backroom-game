now maybe i'm biased but this has to be one of the most impactful short pages out there. its simple yet it packs such a huge punch and man does it hurt.

jean meier is always running from himself. he doesn't allow himself the capacity to feel good or to feel loved because he believes he's undeserving of it. in a twisted fashion, what he is running away from is exactly what he thinks he's chasing. it turns out he can't accept his own kindness towards himself, because to accept it is to accept his past as well. it's to accept change. jean is nothing if not a dreamer, and so when he's faced with the horror of someone who is him, and everything he's not, it's so incredibly destructive (of course, amplified by your concise but raw writing, i could feel the yearning through the screen).

this is the kind of horror in queerness that made me and many others like myself love the movie *I Saw the TV Glow*. it's the terrifying notion of what could've been, that had he opened himself up and searched within, he would find that the truth was there all along.
