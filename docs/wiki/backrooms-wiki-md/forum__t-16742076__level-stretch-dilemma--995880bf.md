Level 'stretch' dilemma.

01 Mar 2024 13:19

i was reading some level pages, enjoying myself. Then I got to open space levels, like Level 11, 100, etc. and it hit me.  
The Backrooms are known to be 'infinite' in length and width. (or 600 million square miles to be exact, which is basically just infinite.) So it stretches infinitely on the X and Z axis. But what about the Y-axis? (or just up and down?)  
i now have many questions racing through my mind. here's a couple to think about.

**can people just keep ascending up in a level non-stop, since it stretches upward infintely?**  
![O4BNfqo.png](https://i.imgur.com/O4BNfqo.png)  
**does space (or an escapeable atmosphere) exist in these levels?**  
*(im talking only about open levels like 11, 100, basically any level that's not an enclosed space)*

**why hasn't any groups wondered about this im serious how THE HELL HAVE THEy NO-**

This is a complicated problem I thought would be good for the fourms to think about. :)
