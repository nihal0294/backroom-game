# Level 395 -  "Do you still remember?"

**Level 395** is the 396th level of the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

**Level 395** is an old-fashioned apartment where most of the rooms have only a worn-out bed.

> This was where my friends used to live. Now they're all gone.

However, one of them, a small room with about 20m2 of floor space, contains some furniture such as a broken bed, a bedstand, an armoire and a desk. Much of the wallpaper in this room is significantly torn.

> I tore it up.

[+ Some notable items in this room.](javascript:;)

[- Hide.](javascript:;)

- A diary, covered in blood.
- A burin, rusty.
- A dancing dress, bobbly.
- A lunch box, filled with an unidentified liquid.
- A pair of socks, dry.

> These are my gifts for you.

- A cigarette butt.
- A pair of men's jeans.
- A broken beer bottle.

> Yours.

  
Only sanguine can be seen outside the French window of **Level 395**.
> You once told me that the color was beautiful.

# **Entities:**

There are a great number of Smilers.

> When you took photos for me before, you tried to make me smile brighter.
>
> Now you see me again. You should be happy too, right?

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities:**

Because this level is extremely difficult to survive, there are no bases, outposts or communities.

> I only let you in.

# **Entrances and Exits:**

### **Entrances**

If you find a very unnatural bed in [Level 54](level-54.md) and sleep in it, you'll be led to this level.

### **Exits**

If you hide under a yellow bed, then close your eyes and hold your breath for 30 seconds, you'll be led to [Level 6](level-6.md).

> That was how you dragged me out of the bed.

> Relax. I'm not gonna do this. You can go whenever you want.

> Why did you take French leave?

> I won't blame you.

> I'll come and find you.
