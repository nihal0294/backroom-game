I would fix the banner JamiMyst already gave it to you and add a picture. You also need to fix the level summary because you are only stating they date it was found and how safe it is when you should be stating something like Level 491 is the 492th level of the backrooms and is often referred to as the Hall of Hatchets. This small level is made up of hallways with axes hanging on the walls with strange substances dripping from them.

after stating the approximate size of the level I wouldn't say "a very small level compared to other levels of the backrooms" it can be seemed as informal. You are also missing spaces after periods, there are also extra spaces in some spots, some capitalization mistakes later in the article and there seems to be some random commas. vicious should also be spelled as viscous.

In the next paragraph you talk about a toe which seems sort of random. I'm not really sure what you are describing but I would assume its talking about the toe of a axe, at first I thought you were talking about a random statue. So i would have a starting sentence saying you were going to describe the axes more closely and say axe toe, axe shoulder etc. I would get rid of the yes part of this "Yes, these axes can be taken down and use for weapons, they are not very reliable, and it is not recommended to use them, cause they break very easily, and very quickly." Doing that makes the article very informal and from context of the beginning the article isn't a pov. I also am not sure what you mean by "The only point of interest is" which make it sound like its adding onto the sentence before when you are talking about something completely different.

This is part strange "the only thing to hear in 492" I would phrase it as On this level you can hear waterfalls because the reader knows the levels number from the summary and title of the page and if waterfalls are the only thing listed that is the only thing you can hear. M.E.G should be in all caps because its an organization and I would describe phantom bugs, make another pg that you link later about it, or just say the feeling of bugs.

Rewrite this part: "Due to the limited amount of exploring in 492,there is not much research about it. it had little to no entities" to something like there is not much known to this level due to lack of exploring or just leave all of that out. If you are going to talk about entities at all have at least a small part about it. You can delete this part: "In summary, there is not much interest of the 'hall of axes' to M.E.G or explorers."  
because the summary should be at the beginning of the page and this isn't really a summary

And this level just being involved in the Fun War would make it a area of interest. If you are also trying to add lore or bases to the Fun War I would contact the original creator for permission but the whole context of the level doesn't really make since for it to be involved.
