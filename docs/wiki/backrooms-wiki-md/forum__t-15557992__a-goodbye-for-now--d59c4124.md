23 Jan 2023 05:43

So, if some of you may have noticed, I've been inactive for months now. I think it was halfway through my marching band season when I fully went inactive!  
First of all, sorry. I didn't mean to wait this long to tell you all. I guess I was too afraid to tell y'all.  
2. I'm leaving permanently. I'm afraid it's where we all split apart, even with the good friends we made along the way.  
Just wanted to say thank you for everyone I bonded with. Truly, y'all are amazing. You made my confidence grow, and now I'm leading a writing group at my school. Exciting, eh? And now I plan on becoming an author. (Let's hope I become as good as Robert)  
Now, this may not be the last time we meet, lads and lassies. If I ever wanna get down into the spooks, I'll gladly join back here to write more.  
To those wanting to salvage my work into something better than young writer me could ever imagine, go ahead. I normally do my sandboxes with a, "YourWeirdPlant1 ," "YourWeirdPlant2," on and on.  
Let it be known that I give every writer permission to use my work as their work if they give me credit for the idea. Thank you.  
Thank you. I love you all, from the bottom of my heart to the edge of the universe. I'll see y'all on another road, friends.  
(Also, if you may kick my discord account from the discord server, I forgot my password to my discord account lol)
