Re: Name dropping Entities

13 Aug 2022 03:14

I would only use entities if they are related to your level in some way. Basically, if they are found there, or play a major role in the lore of the level, they make sense to mention.

Of course, if you do more than just mention an entity, it's always good to ask the author of the entity for permission to use it. I would especially ask for permission to use character or one of a kind entities, like Entity 140 - Blanche. Being a major character, I think it would be best to ask. If it's a common monster like a phobic centipede, skin-stealer, or hound, it's probably not as big of a deal. Most people mention common monsters like that in many articles, and generally it's fine. Though if you are able to ask the author in these cases, I still would.
