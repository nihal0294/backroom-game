I like the Level but I think you could add more Bases, Outposts or Communities, because I think that is Level is really good to live in. But at the end it is up to you how many Bases you add. I also found some errors in the writing.

Just copy this text it is almost similar but the small errors are gone

Level 221 takes the form of a conservatory filled with various plants, out looking a city that looks similar to the one found in Level 11.1 Unlike windows found on most levels, the windows on this level are completely safe to look through. These windows appear to be indestructible to all forces.

The flora found in Level 221 are similar to those found in The Frontrooms but express some unique properties. All plants in Level 221 do not appear to need water to survive and have not shown any signs of growth since the Level's initial discovery on 20/04/2019.

Most rooms on this level are similar in composition. But, some rooms stand out from the others.

But at the end I really like this Level. :)
