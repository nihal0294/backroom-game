Some other minor things:  
- The exit sign and hole are mentioned, separately, twice in the description. **(fixed)**  
- The picture doesn't match with the description too well—feels much more open. I think darkening it instead of saying it was brightened would help.  
- Why is the level called moist? It makes it somewhat awkward to refer to, so I see no reason why the M.E.G. wouldn't name it something easier to fit in an article. You say it's *usually* called "Moist" but then don't actually refer to it with that name again because, case in point, it's awkward to refer to. **(fixed)** *(although the name still sounds a bit silly. ideally, it should be something you're actually willing to use in-article)*  
- Has no one with equipment that could help with reaching the hole ever entered? Say—someone with a backpack big enough to stand on and reach the hole with, a large knife, a rope and hook or really any helpful climbing gear, etc.? The level's entrances seem common enough that this should have happened at least once. If this isn't the case, I think it should be clarified, especially since people have managed to enter with equipment able to precisely measure the air temperature and humidity. **(fixed, but at slight detriment to the concept)**
