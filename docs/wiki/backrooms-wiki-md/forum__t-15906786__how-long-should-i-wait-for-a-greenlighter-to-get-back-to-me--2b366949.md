30 May 2023 06:17

Personally I would ping them a second time after approximately a week or two if they didn't respond the first time. If still no response in another three days I turn to other greenlighters/critics. Or if I ping them and within three days they're very active in other chats but never get to my thread asking them to criticise I'll take that as 'I'm ignoring this request go find another person for crit.'

P.S. by 'never get to my thread' I mean if they never respond to my ping. If they say 'I'll get to this in [certain time] I'll wait patiently. However, I'll ping them to see if they forgot it if they take way too long than they initially estimated.
