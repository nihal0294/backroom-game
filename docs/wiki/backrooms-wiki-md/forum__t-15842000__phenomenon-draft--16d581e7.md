You don’t need a greenlight for phenomecon, or any contest for that matter, but I’ll give some critique.

## Spelling and formatting stuff

> point a and point b while n-clipping

Spelling error

> Additional Information:  
> Add any extra information here, such as a log or tidbit that can’t fit into the other headers. Remove this section if you have nothing else to add.

You forgot to remove this.

## General critique

In the dos you put “Don’t be afraid to no-clip or exit to another level” but in the don’ts you put “Continue to excessively no-clip for no reason.” This doesn’t really make sense as you’re simultaneously saying to no-clip and to not no-clip.  
It also feels strange that it has only occurred twice ever. I’m not sure what the canon date is, but if a guy in 2013 was able to do it in 2 years, more occurrences should happen right? I mean, I’m pretty sure the speednoclippers exist. I think a date change can fix this.  
Last thing, if you can exit, this “midway” it feels similar to the [Blue Channel](blue-channel.md). That might just be me though.

## Final thoughts

Pretty good, but I feel it needs a little work. Best of luck on the contest
