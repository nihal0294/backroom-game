XDX13,

*Some thoughts:*

#1 "…each layout is exactly the same in each house" and "Each location of the room is exactly the same in each house. Some room sizes may vary in different households, however.."

*I might keep the internal layout and **room size** hauntingly identical, as if the structures were mass produced with a 3D printer. Limit the inside differences to furnishings.*

*As well as keeping the outsides of the houses **completely identical** (yards, driveways, trees, bushes.) Think about the confusion found in some housing plans where all the houses look the same. How easy it is to get lost. Think of the angst and frustration that this causes. Maybe add a quote from a wanderer about this.*

#2 "On the second floor, however, one bathroom is reserved for males, and the other for females. There are signs on the door that let you know which is which. There are no consequences for not following these rules, but it still isn't advised to walk into the wrong bathroom, mainly because that's just a no"

*Consider a (slight or humorous) penalty for entering the wrong bathroom. Entering the wrong bathroom no-clips you into the bathroom in the basement. Hope it isn't occupied.*

#3 "Smilers can often be found in these dark areas but very rarely."

*How do we keep the smilers in the basement during the night?*

#4 *Think about an anomalous effect that makes this neighborhood of households stand out. Maybe it is impossible to enter a household after dark? Maybe the buildings can only be seen from the front, like 2D buildings in Hollywood movie sets, but become 3D once you enter? Make all the street signs say the same thing? Liminal Lane? Make the level black-and-white when outdoors, but in color when you enter a household?*

Hope this helps…
