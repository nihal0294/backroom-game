I will start with things you can work on:

Your SPaG could use a little work, so run this through a spellchecker

Try to work on your clinical tone more. In revision one, it is understandable, but you can always replace words with ones that make them more polished and professional. Of course, it seems to be written through the eyes of a scared wanderer in the first few paragraphs, so this could be excused.

I was not able to find any link or reference to this photo. Before it is clear to greenlight (after more people have critiqued it of course), make sure to add image licensing, or if you took it yourself.

As for everything I enjoyed, there was plenty! You opened up with an undetailed, unfurnished, little bit reported by a confused wanderer. I love these kinds of beginnings because they intrigue the reader for more, so great start. The next 2 revisions were not AS favorable as the first, because they didn't have as much backstory, but the increasing detail between the 2 was excellent work. If you could further explain the layout, that would be great, however.
