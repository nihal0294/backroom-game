Beloved,  
No one understood you like I understood you.  
Still, no one but me understands.  
You would've understood why I did it too, a long time ago.

No one loved you like I loved you.  
Still, they all tried.  
You would've known why I can't let grief weigh us down, a long time ago

I'm sorry, my beloved,  
but your [faceless memory](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/just-a-faceless-memory) would've only been a burden.  
I just wish I could say we'd remember you,
