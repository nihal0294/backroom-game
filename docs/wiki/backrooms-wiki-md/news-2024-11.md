Tesseract had a lot of fun with the prompts!

After Six weeks, Promptcon is over! Thanks a lot to everyone for their participation. We got a total of twenty-one pages in the entire event!

We've seen a lot of different directions during this contest. From people using multiple prompts together to others using one in different ways, many themes and formats have been explored, showing how vast and rich this community and this universe are.

Of course, you had to make a choice on your favorite page, and the podium is the following:

- First Place: and with [The Jaws of Emendation](the-jaws-of-emendation.md), a format-screw enigmatic entity set in [Level 78](level-78.md) showcasing what truly lies within the depths of the Backrooms.

A huge thanks to everyone who took their time and creativity to participate, we'll see you in the next one! If you have any contest theme ideas, feel free to let us know on the discord in the suggestion channel!

# Snip snip snip!

---

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/trimmed:entity-2/entity-2.jpg)

Entity 2 - "Windows" is one of the trimmed entries that are open for rewrite. Feel free to apply for it if you have ideas on how to make the page better!

This site has been around for almost five years now. Nowadays, it is an established writing community aiming at expanding the Backrooms universe through quality and compelling writing.

However, things haven't always been this way. Like SCP and Backrooms Fandom before us, the early times of the wiki were characterized by poor writing and lore expansion. Pages published in 2020 barely develop their concept or use overdone ones. Nowadays, they wouldn't leave the critic phase without many required improvements.

Yet, those pages are more often than not the most popular or viewed ones, usually by their low numbers. They are taken as examples to follow by new writers and do not represent the goals of our site anymore.

As such, like SCP and Backrooms Fandom before us, we are officially starting a mass trimming of the wiki pages.

What does this mean? The greenlighters and staff will each week vote on a small amount of old pages, thinking of whether it should stay on the site or not. Criteria used are mainly based on quality, with the thought "Would this page, even if written by a new author, be published as-is today?". If the answer is yes, the page will stay in the main page lists. In the other case, however, the page will be moved into a special trimmed: category. *They are still available to be read on the wiki*, and do still belong in the lore, but they aren't featured on the site directly due to not meeting the minimum quality standards we have nowadays, and setting a bad example for writers.

In addition to this, if a page is deemed important to the wiki (for example heavy link use, or a popular concept), or if the page has the potential to be improved, the page will be open for rewrite, using the tag [to-rewrite](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/system:page-tags/tag/to-rewrite). All of the pages there can be rewritten with the GLMP approbation, by using the [rewrite application form](https://forms.gle/cSLcKC7XwU28qawd6) (you do not need the original author's aproval to apply). So if you like one of the open trimmed pages, and want to see it succeed by developing it, make sure you apply!

You can check the progress on the mass trimming [here](staff__mass-trimming--63583ed5.md), or in the trimming-announcements channel on the discord server.

# Introducing: the Component Hub!

---

![Missing alt text.](http://reyday.wikidot.com/local--files/component-hub/TessCoding)

Tesseract would like to wish you all happy coding!

After nearly 5 years of this site existing, we[1](javascript:;) have finally made a hub for the various components people have made over the years! From the Image Block to the brand new PTS, we have a bunch of tech available for you to use in your page to make it sparkle! There are 4 categories of components:

- Common/Required: These are things like the [Image Block](component__image-block--eb47f2b7.md) and the [License Box](component__license-box--969e0688.md). These components are used heavily throughout the site and may even be required to use in order to post your page! These are designated with the common-component tag.
- Classification: A bit self-explanatory, classification components are general rank systems used for any of the categories! These are things like the [SD Class](component__level-class--cfeebc7b.md), [CECS](component__cecs--a52f6ca5.md), [PTS](component__pts--f4475e75.md), etc. These are designated with the class-component tag.
- Aesthetic: These are components that help add to the visual appeal of a page and allow you to customize it to your liking! These pair well with themes to really create a unique look for your page, for example: [Scrollquote](component__scrollquote-example--7a3e9c01.md) or [Draggable](component__draggable--b5061886.md). These are designated with the aesthetic-component tag.
- GOI: Again, a bit self-explanatory, these are components that are tied to a GOI. For lore reasons or just for fun, these help add worldbuilding to our site in a unique way! A couple of examples of this are the [Backrooms Robotics Box](component__backrooms-robotics-box--a4fa6b17.md) or [IETS](component__iets--a66542f3.md). These are designated with the goi-component tag.

The [Component Hub](components.md) is the first major step in the Tech Reform Project by and , so stay tuned! We have a lot of exciting new things coming up that you wouldn’t want to miss.
