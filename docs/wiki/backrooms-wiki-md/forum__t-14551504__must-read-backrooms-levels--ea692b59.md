Level 129 The White Forest. A pretty cool gliched snow level.

Level 32 Forest of the Skeleton Queen has a cool backstory and horror background.

Level 22 Ruins Left Behind remembers me of movies like Snowpiercer or The Pit because of the distopian society that lived there.

Level 117 Math Class takes me back to the school in silent hill. The strangeness of the lonely teacher and the janitors adds up to the horror too.

Level 648 - "Apocryphal City of Masks" - Its raining men!

Theres one about a dead god too and the Redlands despite the guy in the castle being too much anime in that scenario in my opinion.

Those are some really great levels and I will definitely make sure I check all of them out. Two levels I think you can add to that list is:

Level 790: The fact that such a peaceful, deserted, snow British-style village is a class deadzone fascinates me. The story of the sublevel to level 790, Owenson's Ocean is also a great read.
