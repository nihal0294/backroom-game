> And you didn't think you deserved a better story? I mean, shit, there's a [humanoid alien with world-shifting abilities](nyx.md), [the leader of a powerful and all-knowing faction](fogel.md) and a [furry](herne-the-huntmaster.md).

> **- Herne's not a furry.**
>
> Oh, whatever.
>
> **- Look, at least I don't die like the hundreds of nameless people at the hand of some generic human-killing creature. I get to wait at a vending machine.**
>
> You \*DON'T\* have a name!
>
> **[MUTTERS.] - Something about a pot calling the kettle black.**
>
> What was that?
>
> **- You understood me.**
>
> Yeah, well, to each their role. Yours is to wait at a vending machine for your purchase.
>
> **- And yours is to bitch at me. For what? You'll forget about me anyway. I mean, don't you get it? Your purpose and my purpose are the same: we will follow a story to where it leads us, and then we'll be discarded. The difference between you and me, however, is that you'll be discarded forever. Think of it as a blessing, or a curse. I do not care.**
>
> Who are you?
>
> **- Really? You're resorting to acting like a child?**
>
> No, really. Who \*are\* you? I see you, but I don't. I hear you, but your voice is like the buzzing of neons. I recognize you, but I cannot distinguish your face. Even your smell is one that fails to evoke any particular feelings. You're quite literally \*nothing\*.
>
> **[The PRODUCT does not move.]**
>
> **- I'm** **[CANNED LAUGHTER.]** **What the fuck was that?**

*"Cut!"* The **[DIRECTOR](the-alba-zone.md)**, who was standing nervously behind his camera, advances towards **[CANNED LAUGHTER.]**.

**- Seriously? You got some issues with your ego to insert yourself in that way.**

*"Don't try to deflect your issues on me, big guy. Truth be told, I don't know what to do with you; what's there to write about a person who's defining trait is that they are \*NOTHING\*? I pity you, truly, even your original author gave up the grand plans they had for you."*

> **[The PRODUCT does not move.]**

**- What the hell is wrong with you? Your shitty dialogue derails so you decide to take it out on me, a character you willingly chose despite not knowing what to make of it? Between me and you, I think I know on whom lies the responsibility for this utter failure.**

*Now that is just needlessly mean. I'm really, really trying my best with you, it's not my fault the [others](poi-s.md) failed to inspire me!*

**- I just want to do *something.* It's been years since I have laid dormant, forgotten in the void. You've got so much to write, allow me to be a companion of your stories, just something more interesting than**

*And sometimes the inspiration does not come. Tell me: what is worse, to let you slip back into a state of non-existence, or to force your rebirth out of an insincere process of forced writing?*

**- Aren't you sure this isn't due to a lack of motivation?**

*Maybe? But I want to write about you in a way that expresses feelings that I hold, thoughts that have been brewing in my head, and those come and go like waves crashing on a beach. I can't create a \*you\* if there is no \*you\* in mind.*

**- Sure you can. Those are words, and they hold meanings. It doesn't matter that you are in a crisis about who I am, what I am, I just ask you to write about me, to not forget me, and when I say you, I do not mean just you, I mean [all those who can turn their subjectivity into art.](https://discord.gg/7NQ46hTNWE)**

*I just wish the creative process wasn't so lonely, sometimes. Not just for me, but for everyone. You come into a community, and so much has already been written, but all is awfully… quiet. The pieces are set, the background is here, the scripts are ready to be written, but the theater does not deliver its promised spectacle.*

**- It failed for you. Not for the others, but for you. Is it the fault of the other authors that you failed to resonate with most of the stories told in this [universe](index.md)?**

*No, but…*

**- No, but what? It's not like there is just one theater in which the authors can produce themselves, damn it! Find the others where they are at, show them your art! It may not speak to everyone, but you'll find that more and more will see your creations for what they are, that they'll work towards helping you find your own voice!**

> **[The PRODUCT feels uncomfortable and doesn't know how to react.]**

> Stay put, man, they're talking their issues out.

**- And, by the way, what you have been waiting for to deliver that spectacle?**

*I don't know. I'm just afraid of never finding my place, of never finding my identity.*

**- Everyone tries to find their place. You'll manage. Everyone does in their life, through [one way](lines-please.md) or [another.](phenomenon-21.md) Just don't be so contrarian, use your creativity as an act of love, not of mean-spiritedness. Don't write to spit on others, write because you want your [emotions to echo with strangers.](phenomenon-7.md)**

*So, what do I make of you, how would one best direct you in your adventures?*

**- I am your canvas. I am a kaleidoscope of the thousands of faces, tears and smiles you've seen, the thousands of cries, screams and laughter you've heard. I am the smell of lavender, the vile acidity of vomit and the feeling of not belonging. I am a stranger, a nobody, and yet I am your reflection. All that I ask is to not forget me. All that I ask you is to paint pictures onto me, pretty or not, whatever the shape or form you make out of me.**

*Got it.*

> **[The PRODUCT is giddy at the thought of having its fifteen minutes of fame.]**

**The DIRECTOR** returns to his camera. *Okay…*
