Re: New Level by me, please review !

31 Jan 2021 19:39

> [include :backrooms-wiki:component:level-class  
> |class=4  
> ]

Should have be [[ and ]] insteaf of [ and ]

> It was discovered by reddit user u/dolgozed.

Using reddit username was something that was done in a lot of earlier levels. Nowadays, most people find it kind of annoying. You can keep the reddit username if you want, but I would highly suggest removing it

> The design of Level 262 resembles a College Dormitory

"College Dormitory" doesn't need to be capitalized

> Staying in this section too long may cause you to go insane

Having "insanity" as just a vague thing that happens to people is something a lot of people on the wiki don't like. I would suggest being more specific, maybe saying something like "Staying in this section too long may cause you experience hallucinations and paranoia"

> as the abandoned section changes form a lot, kind of like level 0.

The "kind of like level 0" is unnecessary here. Generally, comparing your level to other levels is bad

> all exits from the level other than No-Clipping locks

"No-Clipping" shouldn't be capitalized

> The entities will also search every **odd numbered** room, and rooms with the lights **on**. so to survive the blackout it is recommended to hide in an **even numbered** room with the lights **off**.

> **Accidentally** trip on a textbook and no-clip into Level -1

The bolding in these is unnecessary and kind of jarring

**Conclusion:** So, as of right now, even if you fix those things, it isn't enough for me to greenlight it. There just isn't enough going on with the level. One idea that you can maybe do to add more meat to it is to include a log of someone who was trapped the level during the black out. That's just an idea though. Let me know when you add something, and I can take another look!
