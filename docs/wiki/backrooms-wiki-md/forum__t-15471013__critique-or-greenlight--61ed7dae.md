Well,  
base concept is interesting.  
I see some problems of continuity and coherence.  
Example :  
**It's said if a new person comes it starts to cycle again, but that hasn't been proven because there hasn't been a person entering level 187 since 1987.**

Has only one person or group entered the level and never left ?  
Due to the nature of the level, it's understandable that new people don't want to enter,  
yet does that means that since 1987, the Level is dark?  
Is the level empty?

**It is also believed that this level has a spirit from the front rooms controlling it, but it's not proven to be true.**  
It's pure speculation and don't have anything to do with clinical tone.  
This should be in a wanderer Log instead.  
Same thing for every time you're saying *people believe*.

**People on this level believe that this level should be a negative level, but it can't due to M.E.G wanting it to be a positive level**  
Well, it can be confusing.  
What are the reasons why people want this level to be classified into negative?

I know now that the level is inhabited with many research, yet due to the light cycle, people don't really exit the level, unless people are born on this level and leave the place forever.  
How do they communicate with other Levels (presence of the M.E.G.) ?

Well, a level that can only be exited could be very interesting due to the population dynamic it can have.

**The flower entities aren't just flowers, they can be the walls, almond water, etc. That means searching this level for supplies will be harder than normal. Bring tons of almond water because you can't get almond water here.**  
Therefore, is any part of the level an entity ?  
You can't bring almond water. Remember the light cycle.

**These people don't get food and water as easily as people on other levels. If you get transported to this level from another level make sure you can survive without food or water for a little.**

Well, why stay on the level ?

**This level is a class Deadzone because this level can kill you by just entering the level without a hazmat suit**  
Well, thanks to the light cycle, we know that no one has entered the level since 1987.  
Still, why people would enter the level anyways if they know they can be insta-killed?

**The Lomn. The Lomn feasts on screams and human flesh. It's also known to stalk its prey and then when the prey is sleeping, it will kill them, then eat their flesh. This entity was found on July 15, 2025.**  
Well, groups.  
How can he feast on screams, if he's attacking a sleeping prey?

**Entrances  
Go to the bottom of a river at Level 186.  
Mess with a flower on any level with flowers.  
Get chased by a flower on level 5.  
Exits  
Find a poppy and eat it to go to level 0.  
Sit in a corner and cry to be sent to level 100.  
Drink almond water to be sent to level 23.**

Well…  
I think you need to change the modalities of entry and exit.

Good Rewrite.
