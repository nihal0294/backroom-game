The Backrooms, by definition, are outside space & time. Well, **our** space & time.

For some reason, many levels are a reflection or emanation of our space & current time, which is why we find elements of contemporary culture — often North American culture — in various levels. Theoretically there should also be reflections/emanations of future times (or future alternative times) in some levels, as well as reflections/emanations of past times (or alternative past times) in various levels.

Thus at some point our intrepid wanderers & explorers ought to encounter a level based on the pop culture from a timeline where a decisive battle went the other way (e.g., Carthage won the Punic wars, or Genghis Khan never existed), or some important discovery was never made. Say a Cyberspace with Steampunk overtones, but everyone speaks pidgin Farsi.

There are some truly strange levels awaiting to be encountered…
