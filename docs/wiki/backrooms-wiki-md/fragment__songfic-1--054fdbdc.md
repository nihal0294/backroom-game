# songfic-1

---

# Three years ago

---

At first, the [damp concrete](level-1.md) was a welcome break from the [sickly yellow](level-0.md).  
Now, it's worse.

How long has it been since you fell? How long have you spent roaming this impossible, uncaring labyrinth, desperately searching for any sign of life?  
It's hard to tell. Your internal clock stopped ticking after the first panic attack. It could be days, weeks, even months that you've been wandering these halls, accompanied only by the humming of the lights, subsisting only off of [discarded water bottles](object-1.md).

You continue to trudge through the concrete hallways, because what else is there to do? Sit in a corner and waste away, entirely forgotten and alone? Granted, that's not too different from what you're doing right now, but—

Suddenly, from the distance, there comes a loud \*THWACK\*.  
You freeze.  
Surely that was your imagination, right?

…

You hear the sound again and immediately start sprinting towards it. brain going a million miles an hour. What is that noise? Could it be another person? You don't want to get your hopes up, but this is the first time you've heard something other than buzzing lights or dripping water, and as you get closer, you swear you can hear a *voice* underneath the rhythmic, percussive strikes.

Right as you're about to reach it, you miss a turn and run face-first into a concrete wall, coming to a painful stop on the floor, cursing your malnourished brain's poor reaction time. After a moment, you gather your wits and glance to your side, finally laying eyes on the source:

[![first_meeting.png](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/fragment:songfic-1/first_meeting.png/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:songfic-1/first_meeting.png)

A woman wearing a flowing blue dress, violently butchering a [pile of limbs](entity-5.md) with a fire axe, while a person in ripped jeans and a leather jacket cheers her on from the sidelines.

After a moment, the latter registers the sound of you impacting the wall, and glances over. "Oh, hey, fresh meat!" they say excitedly, jogging leisurely towards you.

The woman looks up, still hacking away at the creature. "Oh, hello there," she remarks plainly. "I'm Jenna, this is Mae." Mae salutes playfully, then offers you their hand. Hesitantly, you take it, using the support to get back on your feet.

With one last swing, Jenna buries the axe in a leg and turns to look at you. "You're new here, right?" ("*Fresh meat!*" Mae whispers helpfully.) "We were just heading back to [the city](level-11.md). If you need a place to stay, you can join us."

"…O-okay, I guess," you stammer, still reeling from the weirdness of the whole situation. Mae cheers, and starts walking away with Jenna, chanting "Fresh meat! Fresh meat! Fresh meat!"

You trail after them, confused and tired, but no longer alone.

# Present day

---

You tuck your phone in your pocket, frowning. Mae's been acting so reserved this past month; a far cry from their typical enthusiasm. You have no idea what the cause could be, and Jenna still can't get any answers out of them.

You don't want to push, but you're worried about your friend…

You shake your head, pulling your thoughts back to the present. You'll see Mae tonight, so you can make sure they're okay, and it'll be fine.

"Erin, are you ready yet?" you call towards your partner's door. From the other side, you hear something fragile shatter, followed by the honk of a rubber chicken, a tornado warning siren, and a variety of noises you've only ever heard in cartoons. Finally, the door swings open to reveal Erin T. Actor beaming happily at you. "All set!" he proclaims enthusiastically.

---

*Le Papillon* may just be your favorite place in the Backrooms, hands down. Only three years ago, 2941 Wilson Ave was nothing more than a generic, ground-floor office space. Today, it stands as the best (and possibly only) karaoke bar in Level 11; a fun, welcoming refuge from the rest of this inescapable maze. It's Jenna's pride and joy, and you can feel that passion in every brick and board.

And yet, Erin is staring up at it with trepidation, all of his confidence from earlier evaporated. "Are… are you sure they're okay with this?"

You tilt your head, confused. "What do you mean?"

"I'm, er, worried they won't like me." He looks over at you anxiously.

You think about Jenna's messages from this morning, and bite your tongue. "Uhh, what makes you think they won't like you?"

Erin groans quietly. "I don't have a script."  
Before you can respond, he continues, somewhat frantically. "Normally, that would just mean we don't have a scene. But there *is* a scene tonight, and I have no idea if what I'm doing is right or wrong. If they don't like me, that could create tension between the three of you. I could be jeopardizing your friendship just by being here." He takes a couple deep breaths, trying to recenter himself.

You open your mouth, but before you can say anything, the front door of *Le Papillon* slams open, and a panicked Jenna comes rushing out. "Mae is gone. We need to find them," she states, outwardly calm, but with a wild fear hiding in her voice.

Erin flinches. Jenna runs off. You're left standing there in shock.

"Okay, look," you say firmly, turning back to your partner. "Whatever just happened? Not your fault in the slightest. My friends are reasonable people; they won't start hating me just because you like MCR."

"W-wait, what?"

You grab him gently by the shoulders. "The point is, you're not going to mess anything up, and you're not going to ruin my life. I trust you on that. With all that said, it would be really nice to have you on our emergency rescue team, but if you're still worried about—"

"I'll do it," he cuts in. His voice is still shaky, and you can't tell if your little pep talk changed anything, but there's determination in his eyes. It's that kind of look he gets when he knows a fight is coming, and it's a fight he's going to win. You get the idea that even without a script, he knows exactly what comes next.

"Okay, great. Let's catch up to

—FUCK," you splutter, falling to your hands and knees. "Dude, can you PLEASE give me some kind of warning when you do that?"

Kneeling beside you, Erin retches, like a million lions working together to cough up a sun-sized hairball. "…Wasn't me. Scene just did that. Ow."

You shakily help each other up, Erin nearly collapsing again when he notices Jenna standing nearby, watching calmly.

"H-hey there, Jen," you call shakily. "Where we goin'?"

"Level 1," she says grimly, pushing open the door beside her. The jump cut had dropped you on a dark, lonely side street you're fairly sure you've never seen before, although it feels all too familiar. "Where we first met," Jenna continues. "They're scared, and they think they're alone. By the way, hello Erin, I'm sorry I didn't properly greet you earlier."

"…Hi," your partner wheezes.

---

Your ragtag rescue team sweeps through the damp concrete halls. Erin, having mostly recovered, has started a friendly competition with Jenna for who can most efficiently deal with a lunging [hound](entity-8.md). The length of the ieku gives Erin a range advantage, but Jenna's axe cuts through flesh like butter in the blink of an eye. It's hard to say who's winning, but you're perfectly content to be bringing up the rear with the large metal pipe you found on the floor.

Suddenly, you pause. "Wait, stop, I hear something."

The whole hallway goes silent, with the exception of one thing; far off in the distance, a human voice echoes down the corridors. There's a strange sense of *déjà vu* to it all, as if it had already happened to you, just three years prior.

You start dashing towards the source of the noise, Jenna and Erin following close behind. You don't want to get your hopes up, but it sounds so familiar, you don't know what else it could be…

You skid to a stop, narrowly avoiding getting a face full of concrete wall. Pivoting to your left, you finally lay eyes on the source:

A person in ripped jeans and a leather jacket, staring blankly at the floor, emitting the occasional sob.

Jenna rushes over to embrace them instantly. They flinch at the sudden contact, but ultimately lean into the hug, shakily reciprocating. You scoot over towards your friends, wrapping your arms gently around the two of them.

"…I didn't think you'd come here," Mae sniffles. "You didn't have to. You could've had fun without me."

"Bullshit," says Jenna, not unkindly. "We're your friends. We're not leaving you behind."

Mae groans in anguish. "I-I want to believe that, I really do. But I'm scared. I have no one else. We could drift apart, and you'd both have people to go to, but you're my only real friends. I'd be… alone. I don't wanna be stuck here alone."

"We're not gonna leave you behind," you reiterate. "We could never replace you as our friend, even if we tried." Jenna hums in agreement. "We've been a team for three years, and we'll be a team for three hundred more. Okay?"

Wordlessly, Mae hugs you both tighter, like they're afraid you'll disappear.  
You squeeze back. Solid, warm, and not going anywhere.

---

Erin is being very quiet. He was quiet the whole way back too, but that silence doesn't really worry you when it's after an emotional moment. When it's inside of the best damn karaoke bar in the city, though? That's cause for concern.

"Hey." You nudge him gently with your shoulder. "It all turned out okay. You didn't screw anything up."

Your partner stares blankly at you for a moment. "…That's right. It worked out. I didn't need the script."

A devious grin crawls up his face. "*I didn't need the script.*"

Before you can react, Erin jumps up from his seat and confidently strides over onto the stage, clearing his throat into the microphone. "*Ahem.* Excuse me, everyone. May I have a moment of your time?"

The crowd (roughly eight or nine regulars, plus you and your friends) goes silent.

"As expansive as the Backrooms are, with so many unique places to explore, it can feel nigh impossible to forget the one place you can't go: back."

A few murmurs and nods from the audience.

"It's hard not to feel trapped in a place like this, and that's a dangerous thing to feel. You start believing it'll never get better, that you're all alone, and there's nothing you can do."

Mae shifts in their seat uncomfortably.

"And hey, maybe you are trapped in a literal sense. But you're never alone." He glances over at you. "No matter how bad things get, there will always be someone to help you tear down the walls, and be free in every way that truly matters."

"So. If you've ever felt like there's nowhere to go… this one's for you."

The lights begin to dim, and a spotlight appears over the stage.

"If he starts singing *Bulletproof Heart*, I'm gonna kill him," Mae mutters darkly. Jenna nods solemnly. You snort.

In an instant, with a power chord like a thunderclap, Erin is holding his ieku—no, an *electric guitar*—arpeggiating wildly up and down the scale, simultaneously mimicking a soaring synthesized piano and a darkly visceral bassline. Three more lightning strikes, and suddenly the guitar has come into its own, jumping back and forth, from low to high, as somewhere, a drum set is being absolutely thrashed at 200 beats per minute. Erin's face is fixed in deep focus as his instrument wails and oscillates and screams out a desperate, exultant anthem.

##### *On a cold winter morning, in a time before the light*

##### *In flames of death's eternal reign, we ride towards the fight*

You think you hear people cheering. It's hard to tell over the frantic strains of courage bouncing around the room. Your friends are agape; you've never seen Jenna so caught off guard. The song continues, conjuring a bloody warzone of fire and steel, of death and destruction, and yet, of hope.

The melody climbs and climbs, unstoppable, unbreakable.

##### *So now we fly ever free, we're free before the thunderstorm*

##### *On towards the wilderness, our quest carries on*

Erin's hands are a blur, sliding up and down the guitar in the blink of an eye. The melody screeches, discordantly harmonious, haphazardly perfect. The drums continue, never faltering, only growing stronger to match everything else.

##### *Running back through the mid-morning light, there's a burning in my heart*

##### *We're banished from a time in a fallen land to a life beyond the stars*

##### *In your darkest dreams, see to believe our destiny this time*

##### *And endlessly, we'll all be free tonight*

The lyrics speak to something deep inside you, something you've been pushing down inside yourself, something that's been festering for three years straight. It finds that thing, tears it out, and hacks it to bits with a fire axe. You feel like you can do anything.

##### *And on the wings of a dream, so far beyond reality*

##### *All alone in desperation, now the time has gone*

The guitar is chanting, screaming, burning brighter than the sun, piercing your soul with perfect clarity. It shatters, somehow becoming two distinct voices in a furious duet, both desperately scrambling to reach the next note in time, before fusing into a somehow larger whole with one coordinated, defiant cry.

Suddenly, the percussion stops. The bass returns, deep slow power chords outlining the eye of the storm. Erin's voice becomes clearer, like he's speaking directly to each one of you individually.

##### *Now here we stand with their blood on our hands*

##### *We fought so hard, now can we understand?*

##### *I'll break the seal of this curse if I possibly can*

##### *For freedom of every man*

His voice refracts through the microphone and off the walls, becoming a chorus of thousands yearning for freedom, yearning for joy, yearning for love. In one final blast of determination, the melody breaks through, never stopping to breathe, leading the way towards the light.

#### *So far away, we wait for the day*

#### *For the lives all so wasted and gone*

#### *We feel the pain of a lifetime lost in a thousand days*

#### *Through the fire and the flames, we carry on!*

With a triumphant yell from the melody, the bassline merges with the percussion to summon one last blast of music, reaching to the heavens, breaking every barrier in its way, and finally bursts.

The whole bar falls silent once more, but only briefly, before erupting into thunderous applause.

Mae brings you and Jenna into a tight hug, which you gladly reciprocate. Erin trots over awkwardly, and you pull him into the embrace.

Nobody says anything, but they don't need to. You all understand.

Erin is right. You're free.
