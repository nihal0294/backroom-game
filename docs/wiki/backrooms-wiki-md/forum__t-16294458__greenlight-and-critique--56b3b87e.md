Level 249 “Tarnished beach”

SURVIVAL DIFFICULTY:

This level takes the appearance of a Wheat-Field and a beach.The wheat fields are Dotted with Trailer-homes,which should not be entered unless  
As a last resort,as these are inhabited by facelings,which attack on sight.The Wheat fields are also dangerous,as they Have an infestation of entities.It is recommended to enter this level with a firearm.The Beaches Are made from Silver and tungsten powder,Which can be melted and used to make most objects

The beaches are,Effectively,the only areas that should be stayed in for Any Period of time.  
These beaches are,however,geographicly,extremely unstable.If you seek materials,you can enter a castle,though these are filled with ‘Imposter’ items that will harm you.

If you see ships on the sea,It is advised to Swim as fast as you can towards them,as they Are  
usually devoid of entities

Bases and outposts:  
•No human Colonies,bases or outposts have been discovered  
• There is a small shop,with friendly facelings inside,most essentials are inside this shop,each item is,oddly,only 5 drops of almond water to buy

Entrances:

It is possible to enter this level by Suffocating to death in sand on Any beach level.  
Consume part of any entity,this is not recommended.  
Sleep inside a trailer-home or sandbox inside level 9.

Exits:  
A Ship may\* Send you to level 103  
Falling into a sinkhole will send you to level !

Survive Consuming 10-Kilograms of the Sand To be sent to another beach level,Mainly to level 100.

Sleeping in sand or pebbles will send you to level 206
