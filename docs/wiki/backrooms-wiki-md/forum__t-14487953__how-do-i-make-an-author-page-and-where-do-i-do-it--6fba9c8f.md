27 Feb 2022 00:11

To make an author page, you must have 3 articles that are above +1 for 24 hours. Once those requirements are fulfilled, you can create an author page on <http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/b1zarr3vel> and modify the [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/authors-pages](authors-pages.md) page accordingly. If these requirements are not met, then your author page will be deleted and you will be notified.

Hope this helps!
