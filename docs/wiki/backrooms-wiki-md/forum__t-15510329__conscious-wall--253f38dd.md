[You open a bottle, hoping to find Almond Water.](javascript:;)

[Instead, you find a handwritten critique notice.](javascript:;)

- There is very little to go off of here. Quite a bit of description is needed to make this article feel alive. What do walls infected by this bacteria look like? What does the bacteria look like? What does the bacteria do to Wanderers, exactly? Describe the effects on Wanderers it eats, and even psychological effects, if applicable.

> Walk a maximum of three meters from the walls

- I think you mean "minimum". Walk at *least* 3 meters from the walls, so minimum. Also, you need to put a period at the end. Do the same to the sentence after it.
- For its habitat, put down "Any Level with Architecture, e.g. [[[| Level 11](level-11.md)]".

I can't help very much until you add some more meat to this article. Sorry.
