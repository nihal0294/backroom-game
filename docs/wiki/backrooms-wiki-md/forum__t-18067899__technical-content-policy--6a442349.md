Huge congratulations on getting this out finally, this should improve things a ton. But I have some stuff to say anyways :P

---

**Crosslinking**  
I agree with putting all the images on the wiki just for portability and easier management, and to avoid things like imgur region restrictions. But is it really necessary not to allow us to use components from SCP? Why is Snippets acceptable and not SCP, when Snippets are mostly wildly out of date with no updates forthcoming, but SCP components are actively maintained?  
Also "This is the list of permitted sites: GitHub." LOL, why even allow it if it's going to be something not user friendly at all for artists. Why not allow Artstation?

---

**Listpages and Offsets**  
I don't get the comment "(Pages with ten or more fragments may provide ListPages code to list all the fragments instead)" — like, that's already what the offet page is, it's listing the fragments. But just seeing the ListPages won't tell you how many fragments they are. Really, I think that **if you have more than 10 you should just be able to say what number you started at (1 or 0), and what number the fragments go up to.**  
Also, put a wee note about how to contact Tech Team for approval for the hidden tags, and examples of why this may be accepted or rejected.

---

**Link Shorteners**  
Shouldn't be allowed, but the QR codes can be useful in an ARG setting. I'd explain why the QR codes would be accepted / rejected and make clear that staff will check your QR for malicious content.

---

**Hard-coded Themes and Components**  
It would be a good idea to define what counts as a hard coded component. Surely the sharing of basic layouts of CSS is alright, but you know, draw the line where you think is appropriate.

---

**ThemePreviewer Modulet**  
the hell is a modulet  
To be honest it's mostly not a security concern over regular CSS modules. So a better explanation is that staff has added things to BHL, Sigma 9 and even the base reverted Wikidot theme for compatibility with Backrooms components.

---

**URLS**  
What does "truncated to the last full word or to the first four words, whichever is shorter" actually mean. The last full word? So a five word title has to be truncated right, and we choose the first four or last "full" word, based on which is shorter? So like, `the-reinvigorated-narcbacular-cromulation-association` is just truncated to the very vague "`association`"? surely `trnca` would be better truncation.

---

**Rating and Licensebox Modules**  
Can we just change this to fully explain the formatting requirements the pages are supposed to follow? Like, the ones in the [policy that's supposedly being checked](https://backrooms-oversight.wikidot.com/final-page-format-check)?

---

**LOPE Titles**  
I'm just gonna say it, I think you're overly restrictive on the titles in list, totally harmless effects are now banned which created intriguing titles, like Level 599's title. Of course, it can just be changed to like [Level 599](level-599.md) - "Aster" but I think there's no reason to rain on peoples' parade and force them to remove customization like changing what it's referred to as in the list. Another example of why you might wanna do this is for a GOI format, like you might call an object an Asset for the BNTG and doing this in the list is not harmful. It doesn't seem to cause issues with Crom if the metadata is set up correctly, I think our inconsistent page titling caused most of those problems. SCP itself which Crom was made for has creative uses in the SCP mainlist of titles for example using a GIF image to represent a rapidly shifting and changing title and number.

---

**Preview component** (This section is erroneously named "Redirects")  
Why is center aligned text allowed? Surely the leading = will cause issues in lots of cases where the previews are actually being used.

---

**Deletion**  
Explain further what happens to the archived themes. I assume they can be used no further, but are they removed from existing pages? Who can replace them with a new theme assuming one is required e.g. for a GOI? If that's done, will the original theme be replaced?  
To be honest I think there should also be some level of policy dedicated to "rewrites" of themes, which have proved necessary when reviving an old GOI or canon with a very old ugly theme that discourages writing for it. I did this for Morgana Council and was allowed to replace the old etoisle MC theme with me and beach's new one but it was seen as an exception. I'd mention that this kind of exception can happen in certain circumstances just so that people might volunteer to do this kind of work, even if they're likely to be rejected on that basis.

---

**Functionality**  
Our wiki does not function on IE11, it should not be listed.
