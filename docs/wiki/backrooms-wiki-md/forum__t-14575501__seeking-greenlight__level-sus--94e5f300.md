Yea, I read it. However, check CutTheBirch’s view to it.

I've seen a couple people complain about how they don't find this funny, or they think it doesn't have enough content. I disagree to some extent. While I do think that it isn't the funniest joke level here, I don't think that joke levels have to be funny or else. I see joke levels as more of just a place where you can explore silly ideas, and I think it's completely fine to dip into emotions other than humor in them. I think that while this level didn't make me laugh, it is very cute and wholesome. And I think, considering we are currently in the middle of a minimalism contest, making something that conveys those emotions in only 42 words is perfectly fine. +1

  
While level woof is kinda bare-bones, it has a silly idea behind it. Your article just has sus or sussy about 5 times and that’s the article. Perhaps expand on yours more, have more of a idea on what the article is about.
