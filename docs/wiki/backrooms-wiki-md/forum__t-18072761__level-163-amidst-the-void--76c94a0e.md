On the fence between upvote and novote

01 Jul 2026 03:30

I'm going to sort of think out loud here in the response I give, and hopefully be more confident in a decision after.

First things first, I think this level possesses the "vibe" that the original was lacking, especially in the story portion. The narrative was fine, though I feel like the page is missing a little "something" to push it into being "complete"

To me, this feels like 80% of a page, but that 80% is well done.

Oh, and intentional or not, I thought formatting the text to look like a skyscraper was pretty neat. Definitely gave the page more of an atmosphere, which is something it desperately needs for the concept it's trying to pull off.

I felt the ending to be a tad cliche though. While it certainly wouldn't help the aforementioned "80% done" problem, I think the page would do better without it. This kind of first person "cosmic horror" is incredibly difficult to successfully pull off, as you need to leave the reader feeling some sort of lasting impact that eats away at them long after they read the page. But don't let its difficulty dissuade you from trying. I appreciate people trying their hand at it, because that's the kind of shit I like reading on this site.

I'm going to upvote this, because it's REALLY on the fence for me, and I don't want to be cheap and say "haha you were 95% deserving of the upvote under my standards but Im still gonna novote."

So yeah. Purely speaking as a rewrite, this blows the original out of the water. Though as its own page, it still has a few areas needing touch up. I'd like to see more with this, whether that be some tweaks or a "tune up" re-rewrite that doesn't have the time constraints. I'm certainly not trying to speak from a high horse, as my page was a little bit rushed as well due to my own procrastination, but the "finishing this a bit quickly" does show a little bit. Not enough to hurt the page to the point it makes me want to downvote, though.

+1, but not without some reservations. Good page, but pretty rough around the edges.

Oh also when I said "the page would do better without it" I meant like. In its current form. I think having a "blissful cosmic horror" theme would make the page all the much better, but the way it is right now, I think it hurts more than helps

Re: On the fence between upvote and novote

01 Jul 2026 05:45

I was also debating between no-vote and upvote, although your point on “tuning up” the page, as well as this page’s handling of its mysterious mood, led to me upvoting.

However, there are also some things I like more in the original page. While this page describes you entering the level “through a singular elevator located in the center of the building,” how you actually *get* to this point isn’t explained. In the original page, it was more clear on how you were meant to enter the level, which was through the entity Elevators. Additionally, while Elevators (the entity) are partially outdated in their reference to The Keymaster, it feels odd that this page doesn’t bring them up at all, considering the level’s entrance directly involves elevator usage. If the level is entered through the entity, then I feel like the entity should be brought up, and, if not, then I feel like it should be clear that the elevators involved are separate from the entity.

While I do think the narrative used is overall better than in the original article, I also think the narrative could have involved more backstory as to how the person involved came across the level, as well as their initial experiences within it (prior to being called by the void). The corresponding phenomenon in the Frontrooms also isn’t brought up in this article, which it was in the original page (although it may actually be referred to by a different name in real life than the one featured in the original version).

As for some just general thoughts that aren’t related to the original page:

-  
1. I feel it would have been good to imply that there was “more” to the documentation. Right now, the “fade” occurs near the end of a complete sentence, which makes it unclear whether the documentation ended, or if some other kind of shift occurred that hid the rest of it. I feel like having the fade cover the documentation in the middle of a sentence would imply there was more historical, descriptive, or important information within it, while still allowing a focus on the narrative.

2. I feel like it would be nice to have some kind of Content Warning on the page, as the descriptions regarding “jumping” can be viewed as resembling certain methods of suicide.  
-

Although that may have come across as rather negative, I still highly enjoyed the page, and the possible improvements are entirely possible with a few edits, so I’m still upvoting :D

I like a page that has an idea, does it, has a good format with doing it, and then leaves. This page isn't trying to be anything magical, it has the original page and it streamlines it into… not quite what the original was, to my memory, but something that works.

There's a skyscraper, and you get that call of the void to jump. Most don't do it, but one person has. I think the call of the void is something we've all related to. When you're on a cliff or a roof or something, your mind goes "hey, jump" and you're like "wtf dude?" and I kinda feel like that was done here. If anything, I wish the added part wasn't at all the level's fault. If it was just the guy left in a level designed to invoke the call of the void, and then someone jumped in, I feel that's better than "oooh spooky level made me kms". But also, as it's from a first person POV, that could easily just be how the character in question is seeing it. Which, now I've typed that out, I like.

I think my only issue is that I don't see why that extra part is a "new file". It's not volatile, it's not in-universe a new article or anything since it's just prose, so like… why is it here? You can just have a normal collapsible or something, and this would work fine.
