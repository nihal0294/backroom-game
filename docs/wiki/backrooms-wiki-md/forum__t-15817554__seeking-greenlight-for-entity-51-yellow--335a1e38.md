Alrighty first off never number your Entity or Level until you have received critique and are want to see if you want it greenlit.  
"‘Yellow’ is a social creature take likes to stay with other entities. Usually with Entity 682 (T1mothy) but can sometimes be found with Entity 12 (Dunks). Entity 51 can also be found with other instances of Entity 51." I don't recommend including Entities that have not been posted yet.  
Also the image is very large maybe scale it down or remove it.  
"Entity 51 can be tamed, to tame Entity 51. Grab it by its top-hat and pour almond water on it. After tamed, Entity 51 will protect its owner from any entity or other wanderer trying to attack its owner." Kinda reminds me of dollfaces and I like that.  
"Entity 51 can also be tamed by intelligent entities such as Partygoers and Partypoopers. Entity 51 has been found in a burrow on multiple occasions, both at day and night. It is unknown as if Entity 51 is sleeping or not." Partypoopers are dead.  
The variants are a good idea add more detail.  
For the discovery maybe make it more realistic not dull sounding.  
"This event is called a "Party" and is not very known by the Backrooms." Partygoers host parties. =) also how does the backrooms know?  
Side note liquid pain is gone.  
Revise upon this and PM me when you are finished.  
Overall I like the idea though maybe add upon it.
