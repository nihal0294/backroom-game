Entity Idea: Tasters

08 Oct 2022 19:25

Tasters are large worms with human mouths and teeth. They also have short, black, spikes running down from its head to their tail. Their jaws and teeth are very strong, capable of helping them burrow through stone, dirt, and wood. They also have a long human tongue that is capable of grabbing wanders and other entities. Tasters guard 7 unique trees that grow food called the Taste Trees. However, the green tree is considered sacred to them for it has provided food that has kept their species alive for decades. They reside in a level called The Senseless World. Rumors even speculate that they represent humanity's sense of taste.
