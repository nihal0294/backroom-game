# Judgment Day

## Peace to the fallen

December 25, 2024, is a date that will remain engraved in the population's mind.

This day symbolizing the birth of Lord Jesus Christ is also the one where many lost their lives and their peers in an attempt of mass public disruption by unknown terrorists.

This day will hereby be referred as to "Judgement Day".

## M.E.G. Notice

---

Several outposts from various communities and GOIs across a multitude of levels were attacked simultaneously. The assaults all started precisely at midnight when multiple powerful explosions could be heard all across the levels, mostly targeting strategic infrastructures. Right after the explosions, hordes of various hostile entities headed toward the damaged outposts in every direction.

The said entities included [Smilers](entity-3.md), [Skin-Stealers](entity-10.md), [Hounds](entity-8.md), [Dullers](entity-6.md), [Wretches](entity-15.md) and even our own [Facelings](entity-9.md) that turned hostile for unknown reasons. Reports from Base Beta and many other groups and communities on Level 11 and other outdoor levels claim to have sighted an unknown entity; apparently, this would be a variant of [Entity 227](entity-227.md).

We still haven't fully identified the aggressor yet but we do know that Overseer S is involved with the attacks as well as many other M.E.G. members. From now on, they have been designated as defectors and must be terminated ASAP.

Everyone suffered important human losses, the death toll is estimated to be around 75% of the entire Backrooms census population and consequent infrastructure damages were caused by those attacks. To make matters worse, panic and paranoia invaded the minds of most of the survivors. They ended up either siding with the aggressors or simply ran away with our resources. The M.E.G. reputation has taken a huge hit. People don't trust us for their safety as they used to. We can't end like this. We need to find and punish those aggressors, or should I rather say terrorists, if we hope to restore the M.E.G. reputation as humanity's savior.

The M.E.G. should remain the ray of hope for humanity and our future generations.

Overseer Kallaghan, Overseer Helena, Overseer Montreal, you are the only ones I can trust in these critical times. Starting today, I grant you carte blanche, do whatever it takes to stop these terrorists before they strike again.

#### Overseer A
