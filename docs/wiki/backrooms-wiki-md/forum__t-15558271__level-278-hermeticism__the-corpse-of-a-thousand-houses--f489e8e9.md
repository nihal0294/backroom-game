## Man lives eternal by his suffering.

---

To some, this level may be a familiar face. To others, it is a trip into the inconceivable. This page began production in late 2021 following my first attempt at a chuck-e cheese styled page, after I was inspired by the incredible format breaks that utilized insane visuals in order to sway the reader into an unknown place. This was the first of many themes I had constructed, and is perhaps the most convoluted theme I've ever made. Code wise, this page would not be possible (or look as good) without the help of , so I would I like to thank them greatly.

This level comes from a lack of understanding of the wiki as a whole and uses many ideas that could be taken into a very poor route without proper precaution. I've attempted to have death not be a straight forward path, but instead be one that is forked and branches off into many different areas. With that said, I often worried that I did not take this in a unique way when writing, and that this was very cliché. Even now, I cannot deny that, but would like to think that the execution of the page helped set it apart from others trying to do the same thing.

Multiple times when writing this page, I gave up. This was a very difficult thing to work on; from the beginning I knew I wanted it to be short in length, but also give the reader a dip into something extremely complicated. The victim log thing was directly added to try and achieve this effect, though I'd like to attempt to make future revisions to make them more robust, as I feel more interesting situations could've been made for each one. This made is not something I'm happy with; I will always strive for it to be better.

Thank you for taking the time to read the page, it means tons. Not sure if I would be where I am as a writer without the Backrooms as a whole; orrah Amarok.
