At first I didn't buy it. I was like "Water, I see it. Fire, I see it. Earth, I see it. Air tho". That's why this one specifically caught my interest.

Well I was wrong. The sense of liminality was superb, and this page even managed to transmit me a weird "sensation", not as nailed calm as the poolrooms accomplish, rather something else, something *unique* on its own.

One could say that it may be the holes to infinity that inflict respect, the random pathways or the lack of furniture. Whatever it is, The Airrooms are their own experience, and it's definitely a worth read. Therefore, +1.

Also, at the beginning I was like, how can this be class 0. Those holes already count as a danger, but I was wrong, and honestly, good choice. The unique / strange feeling strengthens itself when knowing it's entirely "safe".

Finally, I don't quite get the future thing, it's a bit weird, but eh. Nothing too much tbh. Like it felt a bit off to the whole vive? May just be a me thing tbh don't worry too much about it.
