I am confused on what this is and what is happening, even after reviewing the source code. Is the article an entity or a tale?  
If this is an entity, where is its description? What are its habits? Why is it attacking the person in this log? Who uploaded the log? I have several unanswered questions, that if this was already a created/posted article, would make me downvote.  
Not all people would consider looking at the page source. There doesn't seem to be a way to access that information, such as through an offset. I noticed a few spots where the wording seems a little akward(which I'll edit in here after lunch).  
If this is a tale, the problem of accessing the hidden information still applies. There isn't an established character, and it just seems to be the story of a wanderer being killed by an (undiscovered?) entity, which, in-world, wouldn't be abnormal. And the reader still doesn't know who or what (maybe you were hinting at the entity being the one?) uploaded the file.  
Either way, I suggest you add more to this.  
Akward wording :

> I am tired and he is chasing me,**but why no one receive my distress signal.**

In english, when we use the words when, where, what, why, and how, etc. in a sentence, you normally end it in a question mark.  
I suggest you change the sentence to this: I am tired and it is chasing me. Why is no one receiving my distress signal?

> Don't be afraid, he isn't real

Add a period. Also, not regarding punctuation, if 'he', an entity isn't real, how does it kill a person in the audio log?

> Please check the background management source code information in time

Period at the end. "in time" doesn't seem necessary here.

> help me!!! help me!!! Don't believe it, it……

Capitalize the beginning of sentences in english. The repeated it is unnecessary. Perhaps change it to:  
Help me! Help me! I don't believe it…

> (The sound of the body tearing)

The word the, when refering to a noun, is specefic. I can ask a person for *the* cup, *the* apple, *the* paper, or *the* phone, or ask for *a* cup, *an*(When there is a word starting with a,e, i, o, and u, use an) apple, *a* paper…Well, you get the point. The word a would be better than the word the here.

> The editing content has been forcibly locked and cannot be deleted

Period. The next sentence needs a question mark (?).

> Whether to delete some records of the recording

Did you mean :  
Are you sure you want to delete some of the records of this recording?  
Next sentence made by the computer needs a period.  
There is no need for the elipse(…) between 'The sound of walking' and 'disappears completely'. Add a period at the end there. Also, just a suggestion, but maybe change disappears to fades away.  
The sentences that starts with 'If you choose', 'Wrong selection', and 'Loading succeeded' need periods at the end. I suggest you change succeeded to successful.  
Change record to recording at the end there, or to log.

  
That's all (for now) folks!
