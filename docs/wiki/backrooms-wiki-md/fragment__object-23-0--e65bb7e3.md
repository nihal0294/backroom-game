# **Development**

You're probably wondering about the story behind our signature ammo. It was early 2005. Without groups like **Emstable** to bring about order, many wanderers were fending for themselves. Bandits preyed on the kindness of the [Ariane Circle](cercle-ariane.md), and no one knew who to trust. Our founder, **Miles McCain**, took it upon himself to invent a new ammunition to protect his family. His invention: **Clumpshot**! It was revolutionary! An overnight success on [Level 13](level-13.md)!

*"I never leave home without it."*  
**-Chasoderma James IV of [Venosha](the-venosha.md) Clan Eterlados**

# **Where to find it**

You can purchase your very own pack of **Clumpshot** at any of our **franchises**! Our most popular ones are next to **Malt Mart** on [Level 11](level-11.md) and in **the capital of Boveta** on [Level 650](level-650.md).
