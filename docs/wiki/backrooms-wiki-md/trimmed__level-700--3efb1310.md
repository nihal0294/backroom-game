**Level 700** is the 701st Level of the Backrooms. It appears to be an infinitely expanding fractal-shaped cluster of small islands.

# **Description:**

**Level 700**, as stated above, is presumed to be an infinite fractal-shaped cluster of small islands, similar to that of a Mandelbrot Set. These islands are made up of what seems to be a seamless, brushed metal in a jet-black colour. Around these islands is a sea of saltwater that shifts between various shades of purple and a sky of pure white clouds. These islands have a very hot and dry climate, so constant intake of almond water is recommended.

This level also causes heavy psychological distress, with additional reports of hallucinations, nausea, vomiting, and dizziness. It is advised that those who enter this level do not stay for more than an hour, as the intense heat will lead to lethal heatstroke. Drinking from the sea within this level is also inadvisable due to the high salt content and the temperature of the water.

Each island has steps that lead to the next, repeating the fractal pattern. While you walk down these steps, the size of your body will scale up or down to match the scale of the respective island. Due to this behaviour, from the perspective of the wanderer, each island’s size will be identical to the last. This phenomenon is presumed to extend infinitely, keeping with the geometrical pattern of the fractal. Not much else is known about this level.

Travel to this level is infrequent, as an entrance method has not been replicated.

The existence of this level was confirmed on ▇▇/▇▇/▇▇▇▇. M.E.G. Explorer ▇▇▇▇▇ and his team of three contacted M.E.G. command after they had noclipped into Level 700 via [Totally Symmetrical](level-252.md). They did not make it out alive, but while they were there they collected and transmitted back valuable information on this level. We honour their sacrifice for the archives.

# Bases, Outposts and Communities:

There are no outposts or communities within this level, as extended habitation has been deemed infeasible due to environmental hazards.

# **Entrances And Exits:**

### Entrances

A consistent method of entry has not yet been discovered. It is reported that there is a way to enter via [Level 252](level-252.md), but this is unconfirmed.

### Exits

As of now, this level has no known exits.
