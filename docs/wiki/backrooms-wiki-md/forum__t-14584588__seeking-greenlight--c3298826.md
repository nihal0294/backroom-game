Summary:  
**Level 114** is an endless mountainous landscape in a late state of winter. The ground is covered in snow and the air is cold. Daylight is only as bright as dusk or dawn, with night creating no light at all. The darkness should be avoided at all costs, as packs of hounds and lone smilers track any traveler unlucky enough to have entered this level. If one is to stumble across a cabin, it is advised that they seek shelter inside of it before the little sun that they have fades into desolation.
