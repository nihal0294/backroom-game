Level 207 - "Sprouts of Wonder"

17 Dec 2022 08:49

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre:** Nostalgia

**Core Concept:**

- A long row of hallways that are painted in many different colors and flowers.
- Separated into two areas, one habitable and the other infested with entities.
- Flowers in the dark zone can suck your almond water out of your bottles or body, but avoid to drink regular water.
- Ocularists are floating glass balls that can show other wanderers in close proximity to Level 207, in exchange for a childhood memory by the wanderer that uses it. They are being used by the local wanderer community
- Connected to [Level 165](level-165.md)
