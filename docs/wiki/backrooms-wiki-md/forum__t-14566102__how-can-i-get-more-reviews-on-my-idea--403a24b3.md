Well, you’re in one of the right places, just not the right room.  
To post a draft and get critique on the wiki, post it under the idea/draft critique, depending on how finished the draft is. If it’s 100% finished? Post it under draft. 90% or below? Post it under idea.  
Or you can go to the Discord Server! Your account does have to be 10 days old.
