**Level -3** is a strange but welcome change of pace to the rest of the Backrooms.

# **Description**

**Level -3** is entirely made up of a system of grey, circular, concrete tunnels that seemingly are located above ground. The walls are barren and have nothing on them. The tunnels are very dimly lit by an unknown source, but Periodically you may find a small crack in the wall emitting a bright light. Looking through any of the cracks shows you what looks to be a grassy plane with a forest in the distance. The forest in the distance appears to be level -4 however there is no way to access it without going through [level -3.5](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-minus-3.5).

The tunnel itself is anomalously raining on the inside. There is rain coming from the ceiling constantly, providing a good source of clean water. Another strange property of this Level is that while you are walking through the rain, you begin to feel very calm and rested, as if you just woke up from a good night's rest. this effect does seem to be caused by the rain, however once the water leaves the level, the effect dissipates. This Level provides a safe rest to those who enter the level. however it is not advised to stay within this level for long due to strange unknown effects caused by the rain.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

There seems to be an unknown group of survivors residing in the level, a name has not been given but they appear to be friendly. This group has been dubbed, the Rain Dreamers.

# **Entrances And Exits**

# Entrances

You can enter this level by finding a large rusted metal door with rain coming from behind it in [level 3](level-3.md) or you can find a bunker in [level 10](level-10.md). This level is also accessible via a tunnel located in [level -2](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-minus-2).

# Exits

A door will appear randomly in the maze and will take you to level 0. If you break down any wall and leave the tunnel you shall be taken to [level 3.5](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-minus-3.5). While under the negative effects of the rain, walk for 47 minutes and then noclip to get to [level 404](level-404.md).
