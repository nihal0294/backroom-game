There are a couple different reasons.

1. There's a bit of a grey area on having levels from other websites here since their license may not be compatible with this website's. So we rewrite our own version.  
2. Some authors have requested their stuff be completely removed from the site, but have allowed others to rewrite the page so it's not completely gone.  
3. Some authors want to rewrite their works, giving it a bit more polish than before now that they've grown as writers.  
4. Some pages are… fine, but they're somewhat bare from the earlier times of the wiki, so they're being rewritten  
5. Some pages hit the deletion threshold and so they're being rewritten
