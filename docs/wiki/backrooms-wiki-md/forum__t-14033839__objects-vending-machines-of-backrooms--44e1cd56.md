Objects - Vending Machines of backrooms

23 Mar 2021 15:56

**Page Type:** Object

Sometimes, lost wanderers find vending machines while walking through levels. They are always unexpected and can usually be found in corridor-type structures, thou exceptions may occur.  
Vending machines generally contain useful supplies wanderers can use for survival. It can be anything from chocolate bars to high caliber pistols and ammo.  
The problem is, these machines expect you to pay.  
Some are pretty normal, asking for money, here you are only boned when you don't have money completely. These are convenient for newcomers who got stuck recently and still have scene cash on them. Of course sometimes, It may ask them to pay with indian rupees or japanese yens, or oven more obscure forms or money.  
Some vending machines ask for special tokens that are nowhere to be found, so these are practically useless for anyone.  
And some of them ask for more extreme things. They may ask you to donate some of your blood or maybe even fingers, don't worry, there is a convenient round slot for inserting them into a built-in chopper.

I think such security measures would require even more valuable items, such as Worn Sacks or even parts of a Mobile Vacuum Cleaner. This way, it would be seen as more important vending machines rather than scattered machines that might've just clipped from 6.1. Such valuable items would make wanderers or groups want to have it more in order to gain money, maybe even the Morgana Council would find a way to rig the vending machines for gambling purposes. Basically, there should be a better reason to lose a finger than food or guns that could still be found in certain levels. It would also be interesting to see if all vending machines all linked back to a certain machine that gives them the supply, to offer more lore and intrigue.
