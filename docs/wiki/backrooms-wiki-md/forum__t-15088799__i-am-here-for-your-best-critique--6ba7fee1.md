Re: I am here for your best critique

16 Aug 2022 18:20

First of all, you are still allowed to post drafts in this section, despite what the forum description says. Kind of stupid and confusing, but whatever.

Second of all, your pages have a lot of issues. First, and biggest of all, I have not really seen any mention of the group you are writing for in any other page. To make a group page, they must be mentioned in at least 3 other articles. If you want to make this group, you have to make 3 other pages that somewhat tie into your group in question. Until that happens, keep these pages on the side.

The other issue is that these have a lot of SPaG errors, especially the text box on the first page. To give some examples:

> Mailing service, or M.S., what we do to communicate with large groups, such during as M.E.G You have the ability trade foods as well as services with us depending on top of your needs.

Add a period at the end of M.E.G. Connect the topics of the first sentence better. I would write: "We are the Mailing Service of the Backrooms, or the M.S. We help spread communication between large groups, such as the M.E.G, as well as your fellow Wanderers." This doesn't drastically change the meaning of the sentence while being far more cohesive, and tying into the rest of the text box. Also rewrite the second sentence in a similar matter, I would do: "You have the ability to trade foods with others, as well as services from other groups, depending on your needs."

> We have also created the company M.S.Mail, whose goal happens to be to provide you with an existing mailbox where you has the ability send mail such during as "Overflow of Entities" and etc. .

I don't understand the point of this subcompany existing. Isn't sending mail already the job of the M.S? (Or at least I assume so, it isn't really clear what you mean by "mailing" in the context of the first and second sentences.) I would honestly either just not bother trying to do this and just fuse this group into one single entity, or give M.S.Mail a different purpose different from its parent. Seems pointless to keep it around.

> We also have implanted many devices in various levels, so you can get M.S.Mail.

If you want to keep M.S.Mail around, this is a good starting point. Make M.S.Mail do something like this, implant devices around the levels to somehow get the mail, and research more efficent methods or something. Would make its existence more meaningful than doing the exact same thing as its parent company.

> Our mailing system runs in levels 0-415

Levels 0-415? That is absolutely insane power for any group in the Backrooms, especially one that claims to be a simple middleman for the M.E.G and other wanderers. I feel this is a bit too unrealistic, even for Backrooms standards, so Levels 0-100 or something close to that would be a much better number. They would still have an impact, but not be impossibly spread out.

> M.S.Mail Operatives can send mail, on a analog-computer like device called a "M.S.MAILER", the device has a button, and a black screen with white text, the button is red, and is stretched, the purpose of it is to turn it on. The Operatives send messages of danger on certain levels, and create M.S.MAILER devices.

You spend an unusual amount of time explaining the appearance of the devices that they use. Not even their functions, just what they look like. Cut down this section and make it more concise, and diversify this sections topics.

I don't have any major issues with the second tab, although I do think it could be longer. Overall, nice idea, but make it more professional and realistic.
