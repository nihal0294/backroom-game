Re: Level 104, "The Abyss of Rebirth"

13 Dec 2022 02:33

So, first off, the image isn't showing. I don't know how to help you with that, seeing as I write appealing to the imagination.  
Second, instead of "you", use "Wanderers".  
**This level seems to drain sanity very quickly thanks to the slight humming constantly heard when outside.** Sanity is limited, and insanity is very broad. What kind of insanity? What psychological condition will Wanderers suffer?  
How are Wanderers different after leaving the level (if they entered through death)? An idea of mine is that for these Wanderers, there's something… different about them. Perhaps physiology, or they gained some kind of anomalous ability.  
**When a wanderer enters via death, not the hub door, their body will be stripped away, leaving a walking bunch of bones like a Halloween decoration.** This is very personal, and the simile doesn't quite fit. Consider using "Wanderers who entered this level through death will have all flesh stripped from their bones, leaving nothing but the skeleton." (If that still doesn't sound clinical enough, try rephrasing it. Remember, we are writing from the perspective of a scientist.)  
Create a separate section for the animate skeletons and the entity known as "Reborn". Instead of calling it "he", consider "it" since it *is* an entity.  
**There are only about 500 wanderers that have ever seen it and came out alive, but not for the reason most would think.** Instead of this, perhaps say "There are 500 reports of Wanderers who have entered this level and survived, though not through common means, such as no-clipping in and out of this level."  
Consider creating a separate section for the Reborn's Gate Key, detailing it's appearance, properties, and whether the Wanderer keeps it or not. (I'm assuming they do, since they can use it to enter the level, but it's best to be specific)
