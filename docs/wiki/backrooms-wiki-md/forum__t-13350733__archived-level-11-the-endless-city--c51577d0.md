Unpopulated City?

29 Jun 2020 21:10

There's something about this entry that doesn't sit right with me.  
Why would a Survival Difficulty Rating 1 level which pacifies entities and is literally an endless city not have any settlements?

Sounds like it would be the goal of the M.E.G. to move all operations here and begin thriving.

Maybe its just me but this entry breaks my immersion of the Backrooms having a habitable city with open air and sunlight and such. I think a twist would make this entry work better - some sort of dark secret or lurking danger. There are functioning nuclear power plants here but no one operating them, which is mysterious in itself but not enough to make it \*strange\*. Perhaps the plants stopped functioning long ago, melted down, and made this level a dead-zone of radiation - RAD levels low enough to allow for exploration but too high to dwell for extended periods of time.

Just a thought. Needs a lil bit of spice

Well-written for the most part

23 Feb 2021 06:26

This is a another article I like a lot! It's nicely put together and coherent. It also isn't needlessly edgy or unjustly "epic-feeling". In my opinion, it presents a great and lonely atmosphere! I think the fact that it can replicate real-life cities is a great touch!

I do still have a few criticisms:  
*[…] even clip inside each other which is physically impossible.*

I think it's self-evident that this is physically impossible. A minor criticism for sure but I thought I'd mention it.

*The roads are painted similar to casual roads with yellow lines.*

To be honest, I think this sentence and the phrase "casual roads" are a little off. It's not really a very immersive description since it seems to hand-wave away the appearance of the roads. I know that no one really cares about how the lines look so maybe something simple like "the roads are painted with standard pavement markings" could suffice.

*There is a strange event that can occur where […]*  
I love the event you describe with the rolling cars. However, I think that "there is a strange event that can occur" is an awkward and passive way to begin a sentence. You could instead say "Occasionally, [event] may occur," or something along those lines

Overall I love the idea of this article and most of the writing, but some of it could be more well organized. +1 :)
