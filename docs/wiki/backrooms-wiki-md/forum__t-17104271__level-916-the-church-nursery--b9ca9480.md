-1, but...

05 Aug 2025 11:45

I think that what you have here is a really solid concept, and it's clear that there's something here, but the page sufferers from a couple fatal flaws that make it impossible for me to give it anything other than a -1, much to my dismay.

Firstly is the choice of theme. I don't know if this theme is just like this, or if it was modified for this page, but it makes the page *really* hard to read for me. There's even links in the license box that just become practically invisible. Presentation matters, and this issue nearly threw me off of the page entirely.

Second… there's some poorly written segments.

> The crackers are in a plain, nondescript box and will refill itself once every week.

I believe this is a grammar error- maybe "nondescript box **that refills itself** once every week.

> One artifact, a crayon drawing of a girl saying, "Mommy! Come Back!" above a stick figure in a pink and purple dress with crayon tears is dated newer than all the other artifacts and is named "Molly".

There are commas missing here.

> There is a stairwell leading to a lower landing that, if gone down, will lead to two sets of doors.

Genuine question, is "if gone down" here correct? I would have used "if followed".

> There is one entity in the level, and the first interaction you can encounter with it is a toy phone that stares at the nearest person.

The first… interaction? you can encounter with it? This sentence makes little sense to me. Are you trying to say that this is the first interaction wanderers will have with the entity? Are you saying this is the first form of it you will encounter? Are you trying to do both at once and getting mixed up?

I don't have the time to go over every issue I can find right now, but the message I want you to retain from all this is that there is a good page somewhere in here. It just needs some more time in the oven, and maybe another round of crit. It's a good idea, but the execution prevents me from doing anything other than downvote here, sadly.
