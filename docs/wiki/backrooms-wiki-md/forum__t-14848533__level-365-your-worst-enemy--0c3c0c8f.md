Hi Thomas,

I am so very glad that you enjoyed Level 365. It was my pleasure to create this mystery for the Backrooms.

Here are two samples from my draft of the "Sightless Seers" entity page:

#1.) Description:  
The Sightless Seer resembles a glowing, flying jellyfish that glimmers like an electric oil slick. The disc-shaped body measures 8 centimeters (or 3") in diameter. Its arms and tentacles stretch out to nearly 14 centimeters. For all of its horror-inducing reputation, the Seer can easily hide in an 8-ounce coffee cup. Don't let its size fool you.

#2.) Coffee, Tea or Sightless Seer  
There is a perfect reason why Sightless Seers do not flutter en masse through the mirrors and establish a thriving colony. The Backrooms are toxic to them. Seers continuously lose life energy here until they return to their world. They wouldn't physically enter Level 365 at all except for the fact that "dessert" can only be savored by direct contact with humans.

If good fortune favors the prey, and the wanderer no-clips to safety or takes sanctuary in a well-stocked elevator, the tide turns. The Seer can lose enough energy to make returning through the mirror impossible. It will eventually wither and die. The little corpse becomes cloudy and brittle.

There are human factions that grind dead Seers into powder and add them to boiling Almond Water. The benefits of this beverage are a closely guarded secret.
