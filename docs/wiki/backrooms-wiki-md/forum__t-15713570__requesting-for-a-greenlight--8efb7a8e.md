Ok, so this level is really well written for a first draft w/o crit. Now, let's get on to the ACTUAL crit.

> Street lights are the ones that keep the level from being dark.

the structure of this sentence is a bit strange. You should change it to 'The main source of lights present in this level are the street lights spaced out periodically throughout the level.'

> Level 273 has plants. These plants can't be moved. The plants cannot be eaten either.

The structure of this is also weird. You can just compress it into one sentence by saying 'There is a plant presence in Level 273, though they cannot be uprooted and are inedible, with wanderers who consume it vomiting it out shortly after.'

> The researchers say that they were at a redacted level, then they somehow got here.

Just say 'unspecified level'. it sounds better.

> Level -8 also has walkways. To cross a road.

What's with the extra period? Remove it so that the sentence is more fluid, and rephrase it a little as well.

> The gas stations don't seem to have any words.

What are you trying to say in this sentence? You should rephrase it to make it clearer.

> -People say that sometimes the stores and gas stations will not be to gather.

This sentence is strange as well.

> -Notes with weird messages were recently found in some gas stations.

Provide an example or two of notes found for better lore of the level.

> These signal towers **admit** a light that you can track.

Emit, you mean?  
For the bases part, points would be more presentable, but it's fine if you leave it as it is.  
And… that's all for now! After you're done with the adjustments, PM me if you want me to crit this again. The level has so much potential!
