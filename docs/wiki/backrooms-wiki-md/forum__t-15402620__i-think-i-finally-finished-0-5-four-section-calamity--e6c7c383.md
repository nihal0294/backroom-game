Okay, so as I said before on one of your previous posts, I have decided to not crit this article. But I will give you three things that I want you to know:

Number 1: **I hate the Database New theme.**  
Okay, this one isn't really an issue with your page, but I just want to say I absolutely *hate* the Database New theme. I find it really ugly and noisy, especially with italics, which you use a lot on the page. Once again, this isn't an issue with your page per se, and this is more of a personal thing if anything else, I would just rather use a different theme which would still go well. My top suggestion would be the Danger Zone theme, gives off the same-ish vibe while not being ugly as sin. Speaking of italics, though…

Number 2: **Not everything needs to be italics.**  
You use italics on almost all of the main body of the article, which I find to just look weird, especially with the aforementioned really bad theme. As a general rule of thumb, only use italics on especially important parts on the article. Keep that in mind.

Number 3: **Put content warnings at the beginning of the page.**  
This last one is a bit simpler than the other two, but is still important. For some reason, you put a content warning only at the beginning of section 4. This isn't universal, but you should generally put content warnings at the beginning of an article, so anyone who might be offended by such content can get the heads-up as early as possible.

For now, I would suggest going on to Discord for crit. I might have a way to get the verified Discord account I so crave, but even then I won't be participating in helping this article anymore. Still, you have a good idea, and it would be a shame for it to go to waste.

Hope to see you in future projects, *QuestionableRose22.*
