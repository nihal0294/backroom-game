seeking critique: entity 38 "The Infected"

31 May 2023 19:28

The Infected hostile creatures typically travel in groups. They are highly recognizable with their signature bright purple eyes and shadowy, cartoonish, armless appearance. Numerous sightings of Infected have been confirmed. If faced with a group of Infected, the best course of action is to run as fast as you can before they can call for reinforcements and trap you. These entities are often spotted in dark or dimly lit areas as well as in well-lit areas. Though their behaviors are similar to Partygoers, such as infecting someone to become one of them, they are not intelligent. They are mindless zombies and have similar goals as zombies.
