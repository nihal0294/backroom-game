I Suppose it is About Time I Made This Post

08 Jan 2024 14:15

Well, I've finally made the decision to quite being active on the wiki. Honestly, I had been planning on putting it off until May, but I've really lost interest in critquing other people's work, when I've got finished drafts sitting around collecting dust while I'm helping others get their drafts out into the site. I'm glad I was able to help speed up the process of getting articles out on the site, however, while I did, because I definitely know the frustration of having articles sitting in a sandbox for over a month. For instance, The Necessary Components has been sitting in it's sandbox for 189 days at this moment in time, or in other words, 27 weeks. I've got stuck in being more entertained by creating fictional scenarios with characters on character.ai than I have here. Blame me joining the fandom for that one. I've feel like I hit the highest point I'll ever hit here when I finished the Holiday Exchange. But in any case, I will probably revist this site later, but it'll be a long while. In the meantime, I have a *long* list of works to give out, so I suppose I'll get right on into it:  
So, first of all, the literally only published work I have on-site:  
Phenomena 19: Radio Waves of the Past is not to be rewritten at all if it ever goes into the negatives or is deleted. Ownership of it is still to belong to me.  
Now, my rewrites:  
I am dropping all rewrites I have signed up for. Gat, Level 514, and that other computer level that the rewrite team never bothered to log.  
Now, for my long list of drafts that I either made myself, or took ownership of due to permission:  
Let's start with the finished ones:
