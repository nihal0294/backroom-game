Few nitpicks, but overall, very nice

30 Dec 2024 20:07

I really enjoyed the narrative throughout the level how everyone knew that they were causing damage, and yet due to profits/politics, nothing was done, resulting in a destroyed and hellish landscape.

There are a few nitpicks I want you to change though:

1. Ellipses, the punctuation that indicates a pause only ever has three periods (…). If you have more than three, it makes the writing come across as immature because it doesn't look like you know how to use them. I believe that it was in your second or third offset that you had a bunch? I honestly might go and change it for you.

2. So, yes, while acid rain does have a pH of 4, it's not really going to hurt people. The *most* it does is cause irritation to people's respiratory systems because of the pollutants, but not the rain itself. For comparison, we drink orange juice, which has a pH of 3, and lemon juice, which has a pH of 2. Unless you're allergic to these two substances, your stomachs and skin won't melt if it gets on your skin. Especially if the rain is just water, it's not going to do much to humans. I recommend looking at the acidic hot pools of Yellowstone National Park, as those have *documented* cases of people's flesh melting off when they ignore the signs and go into the pools. Actually those thermal pools kill more people every year than the large animals of the park, so I highly recommend changing the pH and the actual liquid that's falling from the sky from water to a mix found in these pools. Otherwise, it shows you didn't do enough research on the subject.
