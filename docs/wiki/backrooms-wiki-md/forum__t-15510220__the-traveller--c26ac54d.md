The Traveller

05 Jan 2023 16:54

**Page Type:** PoI or Enigmatic Entity  
**Core Concept:** This is someone who wanders the Backrooms and makes his personal notes on the Backrooms, be it Entities, Levels, or anything in between. He releases that information through multiple organizations, such as the MEG. He also releases his notes to people directly if certain conditions are fulfilled. An example would be if someone ended up on Level 39. A flat tree stump would make the Wanderer notice it, and they would find a leather-bound notebook on it. If a Wanderer already has one of these notebooks, then there will be more information added to it. If a Wanderer survives an encounter with an entity, then that informtion will be added to the notebook.  
**Appearance:** This person appears as a male made from diamond. That is because he has undergone the Diamond Cycle (similar to the Wretched Cycle, but you keep your soul. The pain drives most to insanity) and managed to keep his sanity. He is the only documented case of a sane Diamond.  
  
  
I know this isn't the greatest description, so check out my [Sandbox](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/dracodiablo99-sandbox-br) for a little information. Go to the first tab and scroll to "Entities". Let me know if there's anything I need to clarify!

Ah, perhaps I should explain that. When you show up to a place fulfilling specific conditions, a leather-bound tome will appear (if you don't already have one of them). It is yours now. This tome has all the information that the Traveller has observed. It is adressed to the one that fulfilled that condition.

Yes, this is intentional. I included a code that would list the name of whoever was reading this. In my case, it shows me my name. If were to read it, then it would show up with her name instead of either of ours. As for how he knows it, our names are lurking at the edge of everyone's thoughts, just on the forefronts of their minds. I believe people help to keep themselve sane by reminding themselves of who they are. This is why it's so prevalent in the Backrooms. Everyone will eventually lose their mind if they haven't already.  
  
As for your previous critique, there are few reasons he keeps information to himself. One of them is for other people's safety. If he had all the information as transparent as diamond (hehe, funny), it could seriously harm that Wanderer because as noted, some of them have lost their minds. The Diamond Cycle will be further explained, and thank you for bringing that up again. Now, there are also some beneficial things he keeps secret (*very* few). This is because some will abuse the power they have because of those secrets.
