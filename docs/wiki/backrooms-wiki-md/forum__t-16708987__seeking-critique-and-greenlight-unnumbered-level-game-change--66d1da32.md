The thing is, it's not like you're being thrown into a normal game. I'm not writing an isekai anime in this level.

Think of it like this:  
Being thrown into a Legend of Zelda game, but there's no one there. You are not some hero given magic powers to "save the kingdom". You AREN'T the main character, you're you. This isn't like that level that actually allows you to live out a hero fantasy, this is almost an antithesis of that level.  
There are no NPCs, barely any enemies, and you are almost completely alone in a desolate, almost abandoned version of your favorite game.  
And you can only exit by finding the last point of the game.  
Yeah, you might find some weird, cool item. But now, you are isolated and alone in some distorted version of your (probably once favorite game, at that point).
