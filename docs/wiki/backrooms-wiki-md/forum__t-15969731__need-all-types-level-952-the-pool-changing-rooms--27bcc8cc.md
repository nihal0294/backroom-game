Hey, I checked it out and it's interesting for sure. I'm not a critic though so take my words with a grain of salt.

I like the concept of how mysterious entities encourage and eventually force you to leave. A lot can be done to improve this however, for example punctuation (run it through spell & punctuation check like grammarly maybe). Also some of the concepts are confusing and should be elaborated in more detail and in more of an organised manner so the reader can get a better image of the level. I can't say much more since I'm not too good at feedback. I have had my level critiqued on the discord and the way I did it was I politely asked one of the critics in the critique channel, the ones with something like "queue" in their nickname. Ask if you can be added to their queue. I strongly recommend you to try that. Good luck on your level :)
