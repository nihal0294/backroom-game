Dear authors of the Backrooms Wikidot,

an interesting thought of mine came to mind. Imagine a scenario where a huge movie producer company approaches you and asks for the rights to use your level/entity/object etc in their backroom movie. Would you let them? What would your terms be? (e.g crediting you in the credits) Would you demand that they pay since they're such a big and rich company? Would you be excited or more hesitant? I'd love to hear some thoughts!
