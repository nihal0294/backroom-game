Image Licenses

26 May 2022 15:28

I was looking at some people’s sandboxes and some **did not have the licenses**

It is extremely important to link the original page, add the license too and if the photograph asks it, credit them. Now i know, this makes finding images **harder** BUT, if you go on wikimedia or a website similar, the job is done easier. If you want a simpler way to get images just join their discord server, they have a channel dedicated to images with licenses!
