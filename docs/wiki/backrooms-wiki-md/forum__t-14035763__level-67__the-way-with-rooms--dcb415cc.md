Level-993: The Way With Rooms

27 Mar 2021 01:17

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre (Optional):** Horror

**Elevator Pitch:** Level-67 is a hallway with rooms on each side. Only 3 Rooms have been classified. You can sometimes find TV with a VHS Tape. Play it on the TV and you will be teleported to The Blue Channel. There are also some types of doors. Wooden Doors are Regular Doors that send you into the rooms. Silver Doors are traps and you should NOT go in. Glass Door Lead To The End so its not recommended. Many of these rooms include Tables, Chairs, Keyboards, Computers, Monitors, Dust, Rust, and colored walls. Here are The 3 Levels That People have discovered. There are Only 3 Known Rooms In Level-67. More Are To Be Documented Soon.

Room 12748  
The Room shown in the photo. It seems to be the largest out of all rooms. It has colored walls. This is the most dangerous room out of all of the rooms as there are the most monsters here.

Room 89743  
The most claustrophobic room out of all rooms but having a lot of Almond Water. There seems to be a infinite amount of Almond Water. There Is Also a cooler filled with Poisoned Milk. Do NOT Drink it or you will suffer Symptom Headaches, Nausea, Puke, Diarrhea, Dry Lips, Fever, Strokes, Seizures, and Death. Going In This Room Is Highly Recommended As For The Infinite Amount Of Almond Water.

Room 14287  
The Last Discovered Room. Its a Mall smaller than Room 12748. This Is where you can find The Glass Doors. Many of the stores include Target, Computer Stores, Clothing Stores, Video Renting Stores, and Shoe Stores. The Only safe stores are The Video Renting Stores. About 128,794 Entities Were Theorized To Be In Level-67. The Ways To Enter are going through a hallway in Level 0 that has an exit sign at the end. Or Climb through a window In Level 188. The Exits are Going through a Glass Door that leads to the end though this is not recommended. Or Watching The VHS on the TV that would teleport you to The Blue Channel. Some Say that the windows have creatures on the other side of it though We Have No Evidence.

**Central Narrative:** its 2003 and 5 people are in the Backrooms. 1 of them goes into a window on Level-188 and end up in Level-67.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** What I think is special about my Page is that I found nobody talk about a VHS in there backrooms or a computer except for The End and maybe some others I didn't look at.

Re: Level-67: The Way With Rooms

11 Apr 2021 11:00

I see a lot of potential in this article. I do have quite a lot of critique to give here though, I hope you don't mind.

[Show Critique](javascript:;)

[Hide Critique](javascript:;)

> "Level 993 is the 994th Level of the Backrooms. It was discovered by 5 unknown people"

- The word "unknown" isn't needed since it's already stated later. This should probably be taken out.
- A period should be added at the end of "people."

> "5/23/2003.  
> Only About 5 People entered Level 993 and all except 1 went missing. All People were unknown.  
> 3/25/2021.  
> People were sent down to Level 993 and all have gone missing. Further Exploration Is Strongly Not Recommended."

- I recommend putting this in a **Discovery** section and going a bit more in-depth about their disappearance. How did they know that only one was left? Why wouldn't the M.E.G. go and interview that one person before their disappearance? It'd also be nice to see a log in regards to that, but it's not required (to create a block for that log if you do consider it, type this (>), and a space after every line you make. You can add extra spaces with (@ @ @ @ without the spaces)).
- A lot of the capitalization here is not needed. I recommend making all the capitalized letters lowercase except for the beginning words. This is highly recommended for the rest of the page too, since a lot of the same mistakes are made later. In fact, the only things that should be left capitalized besides the aforementioned are any instances of acronyms, any mentions of the word "Room", and links to Entities/Levels.
- The last sentence seems really out of place here. I recommend putting it somewhere else in the article or scrapping it.

> "Level 993 Is A Infinite Hallway With Wooden Doors On Each Side. Many of these rooms include Tables, Chairs, Keyboards, Computers, Monitors, Dust, Rust, and colored walls. Some Claim That There Are Creature On The Other Side Of The Windows But It has not been documented as a true statement."

- The first "a" should be "an," since the next word starts with a vowel.
- The second sentence contains a lot of listing, which can become boring for a reader. It'd be better to "categorize" these items, such as tables and chairs being referred to as furniture, or the keyboards/computers/monitors as electronic devices. Of course, you can still list them, such as adding "such as" after the aforementioned categories, but it's your choice.
- To lengthen your article, it would be efficient to make the dust and rust their own sentence. For example: "A lot of furniture is covered in dust and the monitors, keyboards and computers found are rusted."
- The rooms "including" colored walls don't make much sense here. Instead, this note of colored walls would be better put near the beginning of the paragraph. It'd be nice to specify the colors and textures too to give more imagery to the readers.
- "Creature" should be "creatures."
- It'd be nice to consider featuring the [[Entity 2|Windows]] since they are the closest to wanderers' description.

> "The hallway is made out of concrete and metal. The doors are all wood but some can find silver doors. Don't Go into Silver Doors as they are all traps. Glass doors lead to The End so going through the glass is not recommended. Some claim to see slides but none have been found. The M.E.G. Has planned on going to Level 993."

- How long is this hallway? Considering you mentioned how there are multiple repeats of the Rooms stated below and with the massive number of entities, it would be best to give readers a clear image as to how long the hallway goes. Perhaps it can be infinite?
- "The doors are all wood" kind of clashes with the rest of the sentence. I recommend rewriting it to "Most doors are wood".
- The description on silver doors ends kind of abruptly here? How exactly are the rooms traps? Can the trap be escaped? What does the "trap" within this door looks like?
- The fourth sentence would be better rewritten as "Glass doors lead to [[[The End]] so entering is not recommended."
- With how specific the description is, I think it's safe to assume that [the M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) has already gone there. Otherwise, the only other explanation to this article being created would be that it was created by one of the five people who entered first, which in turn would clash with the discovery.

> "It Is Highly Recommended to keep quiet as there is no telling what the hell is out there. If you find any TVs with VHS tape called "Blue Dreams, I Find The Way" Play it and it would teleport you to The Blue Channel. If you find any Floppy Disc Titled "The RGB" Don't Use it on any computers you find. Playing It would teleport you to The Floppy World."

- The first sentence completely clashes with the **Entity** section.That section clearly states that one will know what there is out there. If you'd still like to keep the "stay quiet" part though, then I recommend simply mentioning the [Howlers](entity-12.md) since that is in there "Do" list.
- "If you find any TVs with VHS tape…" should have "a" before VHS.
- A comma should be placed after "The Way".
- In "it would teleport you," "would" should be replaced with "will."

> "The Floppy World Has Not been documented yet since anyone who goes in It is stuck there forever. Since Level-993 Has Not been that documented. Many Things Still Remain Unknown To The Backrooms."

- If it hasn't been documented yet, why is it called "The Floppy World," and not simply mentioned as "an unknown place?" Furthermore, although they are trapped forever, why can't people with phones or other devices just document it then?
- The second sentence doesn't match up with the rest of the article?
- The third sentence seems redundant - I recommend removing it.

> "There are no known Colonies or Outposts on this level"

- A suggestion: you mentioned a room that seems to be infinitely filled with [Almond Water](object-1.md). I feel as if many wanderers or even the [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md) would take many chances to set up there for that resource alone, despite the entities listed.
- Other than that, if you choose not to add a Colony or Outpost, then a period must be added after "level."

> "There are Only 3 Known Rooms In Level-993 More Are To Be Documented Soon."

- "There are only 3 known rooms in Level 993" should be its own sentence.
- Not sure why there's a dash between Level and 993, but either way it's not needed.

> "The Rooms Shown In The Photo. It seems to be the largest room out of the 3 rooms documented. It is the most dangerous room out of all 3 rooms Because there are the most entities in this room."

- For one, the photo itself doesn't look like a room, but rather a hallway. I recommend using a different one.
- I know that there is a photo attached for this room but it would still be good to describe what it looks like or what is contained inside besides entities.
- How big is this room? In fact, how big are all of the rooms?
- Considering the entities you have listed, it doesn't actually seem that dangerous. For one, most adult [Facelings](entity-9.md) are passive besides the children counterpart, no one has ever even seen a [Howler](entity-12.md) (much less get hurt by them), and based on the lack of description, the room doesn't seem damp enough for [the Disease](entity-19.md) to even spawn in there. The only entity that could really be a threat are [Insanities](entity-15.md), but even then, we're trying to phase out any mentions of sanity or in this case [Insanities](entity-15.md) (because it may be insulting to those with mental illnesses and it's considered too "gamey") so it wouldn't really be in here in the first place. With all this in mind, this actually seems like a safe room.

> "The most claustrophobic room out of all rooms but having a lot of Almond Water. There seems to be a infinite amount of Almond Water. There Is Also a cooler filled with 50 year old Spoiled Milk. Don't Drink it or you will suffer Symptom including Headaches, Nausea, Puke, Diarrhea, Dry Lips, Fever, Strokes, Seizures, and Death. Going In This Room Is Highly Recommended As For The Infinite Amount Of Almond Water."

- Again, what size is the room? What does it look like? What's in it besides the cooler?
- Where is the [Almond Water](object-1.md) stored? How is it infinite?
- "Symptom" should be "symptoms."
- Unfortunately, death isn't a symptom. It'd be efficient to make this mention of death as a separate sentence.
- That last sentence seems redundant.

> "The Last Discovered Room. Its a Mall smaller than Room 12748. This Is where you can find The Glass Doors. Many of the stores include Target, Computer Stores, Clothing Stores, Video Renting Stores, and Shoe Stores. The Only safe stores are The Video Renting Stores."

- I don't understand how this is a room, then?
- The description of the mall is extremely limited here. Why is it safest? Do the other stores have mental effects? Are there more entities? Objects?

- The number stated above is far too specific to be a theory. Not to mention that it wouldn't be possible to accurately number the entities as once again: [Howlers](entity-12.md) cannot be seen.
- The list of entities shouldn't be spaced out.
- With the kind of entities that exist here, the Level just doesn't seem dangerous enough to even be considered a Class 5.
- It takes a bit to recognize what entities they are without memorizing or accessing the pages. I recommend adding its name. To do so, link the page as you do ([[[Blahblah) and add this symbol | and whatever name it needs to be named to. For example: [halbhalb](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/blahblah) (remove the spaces).
- I'm confused? Which Entity 1 proposal are you specifying?

> "Find a hallway in Level 0 that has a Exit Sign or climb through a window in Level 188"

- A period should be added after "188."
- What kind of window specifically? In a few articles, those that have an entry point from [Level 188](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-188) usually describe a motif that sets this window as unique and make it easier to identify the window and where it leads to.

> "Find A Glass Door That Leads To The End. Though it is not recommended to do this. Find a TV with a VHS Tape called "Blue Dreams, I Find The Way", Play it and you will be sent to The Blue Channel"

- It's best to separate these exits into a bulleted list. To do so, add an asterisk (\*) and a space after.
- The second sentence is redundant, it'd be best to remove it.

I'm sorry for how long my critique was. Let me be blunt with you. Again, I see this article has a lot of potential to be a good read. I see your eagerness for this to be Greenlit. I understand how it feels to take so long on an article; my longest took two weeks. I do hope that you take that eagerness to further improve on this article. Once you've made the changes you choose to make, come back to me and I'll take a second look, and even consider Greenlighting it. I anticipate your next redraft. Do let me know if you have any questions.

Details and Professional Language

27 Mar 2021 02:17

Although I haven't had any works published yet I would like to give my opinion and advice.

1. I do believe the image captioned "A hallway leading to level 67" is an image already used on another level. Although I may be wrong, I would double-check if I were you.

2. I know this is only a sandbox but I definitely would say this Level needs to be revised when it comes to the writing. The writing is choppy and hard to read, even in the first sentence. I also believe this level could do with some more professional language. There is a lot of "DO NOT"s in this Level, which is something rarely seen in professional documentation. Try to find other ways to emphasize you not wanting the wanderer to do these certain things without saying "DO NOT." Also basic revision, like the (seemingly) unnecessary capitalization in "…Symptom Headaches, Nausea, Puke, Diarrhea, Dry Lips, Fever, Strokes, Seizures, and Death. Going In This Room Is Highly Recommended As For The Infinite Amount Of Almond Water."

3. I feel like this Level could have a lot of lore behind it, but it just has a lack of information. Like; why is the milk poisoned? Does it spawn that way or is someone poisoning it? What does it seem to be poisoned with?

Of course this is all my personal opinion, I would suggest downloading Grammarly to help detect the tone of your writing and help with writing mistakes. Keep up the good work!
