HOW TO USE THIS FORUM

13 Jan 2022 19:02

Hello everyone! New update to the Idea Critique Forums!

Recently, the staff team has decided to make the ICF template an **optional** addition to your post, as we think that a required template is difficult to fit an idea into, especially with certain ideas. If you still wish to use a template for organization however, a new template has been provided below.

**Page Type:** Entity, Tale, Joke, Level, Other (Specify)

**Genre (Optional):** Action, bittersweet, comedy, horror. More can be found at [http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/tag-guide](tag-guide.md)

**Core Concept:** What is the foundation to the idea? What is the story and how does this build on the premise? What will hook the reader into the idea? How will you keep them hooked?

**Additional Notes:** If you have any concerns or need assistance, lay it out here!
