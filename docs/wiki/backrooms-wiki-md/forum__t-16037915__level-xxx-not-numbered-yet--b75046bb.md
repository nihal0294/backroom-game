Concept Crit

30 Jun 2023 23:19

So, as a newer author, you're going to need some checks before you can get a greenlight. Also, you can just pick a number for now and use it as a placeholder for if it gets greenlit. The list is here [Normal Levels List](normal-levels-i.md)

Consider this a concept crit :)

Simplicity isn't bad, and there are a few things I like:

- I do like the simplicity of the level's environment. The basic description acts like a nice canvas for other things to become more important.
- The water parasite is the thing that gets my attention the most.

However, I think your level is a little too simple. Everything is brushed over too quickly, not giving your reader a chance to feel scared, if that's what you were intended. Speaking of which, what do you want to make your readers feel? Fear at abandoned places? Fear of things lurking in the dark? At the moment, I'm not feeling much of anything. Even with simple pieces, you can still evoke emotions in someone.

Your hidden message at the end suffers from the same problem of being brushed over too quickly. You need to build up to it to really get the full effect of hints at something greater. At the moment, it just feels tacked on at the end.

My recommendation would be to rework the level and incorporate the water parasites a bit more. They're the most interesting thing to me. I suggest making them the source of all the supplies. Rather than just taking over dead bodies, these things are unusually intelligent. They lure wanderers to them by clumping together and mimicking essential supplies. Once ingested or once inside the bloodstream, these parasites take over their hosts, causing them to cough up the dirty water and roam the level. They're the ones leaving the hidden messages and who have ulterior motives.

If you have any questions, let me know! You can ping me in the Discord @BlueSignet or reply to this post :)
