Alright. I'll do the entity first, then the level. Keep in mind that I'm not a GL or GLT, so my crit won't be the best.

By looking at the page, I can instantly tell that it's not ready for a greenlight. However, it does have quite some potential, much more than the average sandbox page.

Before I begin with the main crit, I need to talk abt the theme. Although I *love* it, the top part that says "Neophyxen Technocracy" does not fit the article (Neophyxen is an upcoming enigmatic entity). However, you can change that. For your page, I suggest you use this code:

> [[module css]]  
> :root {  
> —header-title: "ENTITY-73";  
> —header-subtitle: "A light at the end of the tunnel.";  
> }  
> [[/module]]

Alright, time with the main crit.

# SPaG, Clinical Tone and Syntax

> Entity Number: 73

If you're using the code I mentioned above, I suggest you remove this.

## Description

> Entity 73, also known as Humanoids, are humanoid figures that resemble humans very similarly. Only that most of their features are secluded in blackness (Humanoids have pitch black skin) These entities are humanitarian and sentient.

Change to:

> Entity 73, also known as Humanoids, are humanoid figures that heavily resemble humans besides the fact that most of their features are secluded in darkness, as they have pitch black skin. These entities are humanitarian and sentient.

> Humanoids have keen aim and dislike any hostile entities, especially facelings and partygoers.

1. Don't try to mix two different things about these entities in one sentence. You could try saying, *"Humanoids have keen aim and are especially skilled in fighting. They dislike hostile entities, such as Facelings and Partygoers."*
2. Capitalize entities such as 'Facelings'.
3. As of now, Partygoers don't exist; please remove any mentions of them from your page until they return.

> Humanoids speak fluent English and have varying accents (most common accents are western and central american).

Capitalize 'american'.

> (Female humanoids are usually shorter)

Capitalize 'humanoids' and de-capitalize 'Female'.  
  
**Okay. You're done with those errors? Good. Now, the main problem with this part is that all the information is not arranged properly. Try placing sentences with others that have similar topics. I'd rearrange the whole 'Description' header to:**

> Entity 73, also known as Humanoids, are humanoid figures that heavily resemble humans. However, most of their features are secluded in darkness, as they have pitch black skin. These entities will always be either male or female. Other than their size and shape (females are often shorter than males) there is virtually no difference between the two genders.
>
> These entities are humanitarian and sentient. They show no sign of aggression except any for self-defense. Humanoids have keen aim and are especially skilled in fighting. They have a heavy disliking for hostile entities, such as Facelings and Skin-Stealers, and often kill them for their own enjoyment.
>
> Humanoids speak fluent English and have varying accents (most common accents are western and central American). They have white facial features that move as they speak similarily to humans. A Humanoid's facial feature can vary in color, ranging from white to yellow, to even purple.

**I also suggest that you add a paragraph in this header talking about their diet. Maybe they eat entities for breakfast. It's a good way to expand even more on the article.**

## Humanoid Types

> There are many types of humanoids, and they each have their own qualities.

I suggest you remove this, as it has been mentioned in the previous sentence.

> An artist's depiction of a humanoid.

1. Capitalize 'humanoid'. This mistake has been repeated several times.  
2. Since Humanoids definitely have arms, I suggest you add, "Note that it is not finished.  
3. Move this image block to the 'Description' header.

> commonly found on levels 1-5, these variants possess human functions despite it's features being a singular white expression.

Change this to:

> Commonly found on Levels 1 through 5, these variants posess human functions despite their features only consisting of a singular white expression.

> Red-faced humanoids do not communicate with any individual nor entity including other humanoids.

Add a comma after 'entity'.

> They reside in sublevels or often in levels with high entity count.

Replace "high entity count" with "large amounts of entities".

> They have been known to completely ignore the power of a memory worm

Memory worms no longer exist. I suggest you replace these with [Phobic Centipedes](entity-134.md).

> and have surpassed even that thing's mind-control abilities in sublevel 1.5.

Change this to:

> and have even surpassed the mind-controlling abilities of [Mother](entity-30.md) from [Level 1.5](level-1-5.md).

> which increases sanity and prevents any metaphysical alterations.

'Sanity' is no longer a mechanic used on the wiki. Please remove mentions of it from your page. I suggest you change this to:

> which heals wanderers psychologically and saves them from metaphysical alterations.

> Blue-faced humanoids are mysterious and have little info documented on them, as they are extremely rare and it's only known habitat is level 6.

I'd rewrite this as:

> Not much is known about blue-faced Humanoids, as their dwindling population currently resides in the mysterious Level 6.

> Fuchsia-faced humanoids occasionally build outposts and attack any entity that comes nearby except partygoers.

Once again, Partygoers do not exist as of now. Remove "except partygoers" and end the sentebce at 'nearby'.

> It is suggested that you avoid Purple-faced humanoids, as little is known about what occurs after this variant reaches the end of it's patience while most wanderers state that they are simply transported to another level.

Change this to:

> It is highly advised to avoid this variant at all costs, as they have been known to drag offenders to an unknown fate upon being provoked.

> Turquoise-faced humanoids also can fit

Change "Turquoise-faced humanoids also can" to "They can also".

## the rest of the article

> ### Habitat(s): Majority of levels
>
> Humanoids have unknown origin, but are common throughout the backrooms. Mostly residing in safer levels such as 1 and 4. Humanoids can be encountered in nearly any level.

Move this whole thing to right before the 'Description' header.

> safer levels such as 1 and 4.

Add the word 'Level' before '1' and '4'.

> average human and are intelligent.

Add the word 'highly' before 'intelligent'.

> bluish brain-like organ located in their head. (Recovered from autopsy.) Red

Change this to:

> bluish brain-like organ located in their head (recovered from autopsy). Red

> melee weapons on level 4

Capitalize 'Level'.

> ### Humanoid Anomaly
>
> Recently, researchers have discovered an anomaly coming from humanoids originating from level 579. Humanoids originating from this level have reported experiencing strange, metaphysical interactions. These series of interactions has been named "The Stray Mind Effect". As these interactions are only recorded when the host is asleep or under the influence of substances. No other entity besides humanoids have experienced this effect. Descriptions of this interaction vary on a large scale. Most interactions end with a loud buzzing. Similar to the fluorescent lights in level 0.

I feel like this whole header & paragraph is confusing and useless. Please remove it.

> Try to socialize with Humanoids. They are very social and you can befriend them.

Replace 'social' with 'civilized'.

> ; They will

De-capitalize 'They'.

> They have dead aim

Replace 'dead' with 'incredible'.  
  
  
**All of the links are not done correctly. If you wanted to link Entity 9, you would write: [[[entity-9|Facelings]]], which turns into [Facelings](entity-9.md) upon saving the page. Likewise, [[[level-0|Level 0]]] would become [Level 0](level-0.md). Although these links won't work in the sandboxx, they will work once the article is greenlit.**
