Entity Idea: Stinkers

14 Oct 2022 02:59

Stinkers take on the appearance of a skunk, but instead of a white stripe on its back, it is replaced with 2 stripes one green and one is pink. At the end of its tail is what appears to be a small human nose. They have an omnivorous diet and based on what they have eaten causes them to release odors around them as a defensive mechanism. When in danger, the nose will turn either green or pink and it will release a gas similar to that color. The green gas is for rancid odors enough to cause a wanderer to pass out. The pink gas is for pleasant odors that are used as a distraction so it can escape. They can also use both noses at the same time for hunting and tracking.
