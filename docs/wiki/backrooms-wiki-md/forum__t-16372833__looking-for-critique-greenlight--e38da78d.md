Hello! I am here to give you critique. Ill show you the pros and the cons and the good and the bad!  
- Space out the ideas. In writing you need to know how to balance sentences. I saw that in your writing the are a lot of very, VERY long sentences with a lot of "and"s and "like"s, so to say.  
- Don't put "Because it's self-explanatory" in a text made to inform. Will they kill you? Will they bully you? Will they eat your brains? (I am reffering to the jock part)  
- What and who are the students? Are they entities? Are they other survivors?  
- Now, I know that until now I was babbling about not giving enough info, but one of the best stuff in that whole sandbox is the gloom, but you can make it better. Leave the effects of it to the reader's imagination. Make a log about someone going insane, seeing *stuff* and finally, the log ending with either a suicide note or them seeing an "ominous figure" if you know what I mean.  
I hope this'll help! C'ya around.
