The photo is actually a photoshopped version of [this image](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dora_hole_begin.jpg).

My original idea was to create a level with perfectly black walls, and then I had the idea to have walls at different temperatures to serve as a danger and the surfaces to be frictionless to make it impossible to walk.

I discovered there was already a level with perfectly black walls, Level 6.3, so my idea did not seem as original. I still thought the material had potential, so I thought to make it into object article instead.
