(Just fyi, I'm not a greenlighter, just a fellow crit-seeker passing through) I think this article has a lot of potential, first of all!! I like how sociable the entities are, and I especially love the detail about how they not only have counterparts in the Frontrooms, but that they find familiarity in some aspects of their counterparts' lives.

I'd like to see more of that- more things indicating that these entities are partly supersoldiers, but still largely people to some degree- and a bit less of the video-gamey, unstoppable-death-machines stuff. You can keep the military aspects, but maybe add some aspects that give them a little more depth? Things like how their family systems operate, how they socialize with others of their same entity type vs how they socialize with regular humans, or even where they get their uniforms/vehicles from.

I could see this being a fantastic subversion of the "faceless army" trope, if you're willing to expand on the more mundane bits of their lives. You've got the weapons, vehicles, uniforms etc. down already, but how about what they eat? What does fear feel like to them? (I'm sure they were intended to be fearless, but even the *implication* that they can get scared does wonders to flesh them out)

For the more grammatical stuff, I think going over this in any program that can run Grammarly should take care of some of the basic errors. Hope this helps!!
