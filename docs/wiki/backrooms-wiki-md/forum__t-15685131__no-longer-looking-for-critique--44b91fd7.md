Well,  
There is many things to tell  
First, the layout and the format of the level is good.  
Yet, there is some repetitions and awkward sections that break the rythm:  
**Level ### has the appearance of a constantly dark forest and also does not consist of a day or night cycle. Most of the level is filled waist-deep in a dark fog that cannot be seen through. The entire level is constantly pitch black and you need to bring your own light source to see within the level**  
Also, the fact that any contact with the liquid turn you into that liquid be it by skin or by throat.  
=>The section can be condensed.

Second, i think there is something to work out of the concept.  
Inspiration may arise from another level, but it is something you must cautious of.

By the way, i see that you're aiming to do a scary level…  
Well, scaredness come from different sources.  
The first one is terror while the other one is fear.  
The main difference between the two is the unknown. Terror is based on the unknown almost like a form a anguish.  
Fear is more based on known threats like running away from a monster.

If you're seeking inspiration from level 6, you should know that your level is based on fear while level 6 is based on terror. A darkness that can't be vanquished, sound that are extinguished, a level that is dangerous because there is paradoxically nothing.

There is no good choice between fear and terror, it's at most a matter of execution.

About the dark ice, i think it would be interesting to pair it with the liquid and the ash in the trees like some kind of dark cycle of water but applied to the liquid.

About the trees, the way you describe makes me wonder if they're some kind of sentient kamikazes.

**More theories speculate of it coming from an undiscovered level that produces it as some kind of dark energy fuel for other levels as if the unknown level is sentient and is trying to spread its “dark magic”.**  
I'd rather avoid this kind of theories.  
Some are better left unknown or in another level.

About the **unique** parts, i don't think that a dark place filled with entities is that original.  
But it can be okay.  
As i said, it's also a matter of execution.  
And if you're looking for a way to spice up the level, i'd recommend finding the idea by 2 main process:

The first one would be to create something by extracting a characteristic of an object and applying it to another one.  
Example: a forest that behaves like a nocturnal sky

The second one is to ask yourself questions:  
Why does there is a black liquid and black ice with anomalous yet completely different properties in the same level?  
Why does the trees warn the wanderers ?  
And how the wanderers guessed that the trees warn them ?

Good work.

Cordially,  
Mister Nemu.

Hi!

[You open a bottle, hoping to find Almond Water.](javascript:;)

[Instead, you find a handwritten critique notice.](javascript:;)

Alright, I'll begin going line by line. As always, I'm apologizing in advance for my bluntness.

> Level ### has the appearance of a constantly dark forest and also does not consist of a day or night cycle.

"Level ### appears to be a dark forest, accompanied by a noticeable lack of a day/night cycle."  
When I say "appears to be", it keeps the meaning while eliminating the awkwardness.

> Most of the level is filled waist-deep in a dark fog that cannot be seen through.

Not much wrong. I would state roughly (or an estimation of) how much of the Level is covered in fog.

> The entire level is constantly pitch black and you need to bring your own light source to see within the level.

I would move this sentence and take out the mention of the constant darkness, like so: "Wanderers must bring some sort of light source to see clearly in this level."  
Another not. Never use "you". It's not clinical enough. Use "Wanderers", "Travellers", or even "one".

I would change "can be" to "range from". Also, make sure to capitalize "Celsius"

> The level will have random wood cabins scattered around in varying conditions but most are able to be used as temporary shelters for wanderers passing through the level.

This sentence is a bit awkward and feels like a run-on. I would change it to something like "Wooden cabins are scattered throughout the Level. The conditions vary, but most can be used as a temporary shelter for Wanderers."

> Any attempt to take a photo within level ### is impossible due to a type of black ice that will cover the camera and cannot be removed while within the level.

"Attempts to take photos of Level ### have proven impossible due to a substance similar to black ice covering the camera. This substance cannot be removed while on this Level."

> It has also been recorded of enhanced hunting techniques are seen being used by the entities in level ###.

"Entities native to Level ### were observed using enhanced hunting techniques."

> Commonly using the darkness and dark fog similar to tall grass to lurk and ambush wanderers exploring the forest or passing by.

"They commonly use the darkness and fog in a manner siilar to a in tall grass to ambush Wanderers exploring the forest or passing by."

> And According to first-hand encounters with the wooden cabins by wanderers, they say that the cabins are completely empty with no signs of any previous furniture or appliances that could have previously resided in the structure.

Delete the "And" at the beginning. You also don't need "they say that".

> Should any wanderer attempt to scale up a tree nearly instantly the tree will begin to vibrate as a warning for approximately 10 seconds, then when this time is up the tree will violently shake to throw the wanderer off.

After "up a tree", put a comma. After "10 seconds", replace the comma with a period and capitalize appropriately. Delete "then" and put a comma after "is up".

> If the wanderer still persists then the tree's bark will crack and shatter revealing ash inside the trunk.

Put a comma after "persists", delete "then", and put another comma after "shatter".

> Then the trunk and branches will disintegrate into ash as a last attempt to harm the wanderer.

"The tree will then disintegrate into ash as a last attempt to harm the Wanderer." If it's the trunk and branches, that pretty much means the tree. I would also add this sentence to the last paragraph and start a new one afterward.

> After walking approximately 100 meters in any direction you will be able to hear a water current, then after another 10 meters, you will come across a river of an unknown substance that reflects the sounds of flowing water and also flows as if it is water.

"After walking approximately 100 meters in any direction, Wanderers have reported hearing flowing watter. After another 10 meters, they report coming across a stream of an unknown substance that mimics the sound and consistency of water.  
After walking 100 meters from where? I like the bit about looking like it's flowing like water, but in reality, it doesn't flow at all. Very nice. 👌

> A Wood bridge of 1800s appearance crosses the river, also known to be unstable and dangerous to cross.

"A wodden bridge circa the 1800s crosses the river, but it is unstable and dangerous to cross."

> From a wanderers account who made it to the river said "it flowed so well… I wanted to drink it, I was so thirsty… But it looked so weird. I knew I couldn't trust it."

Capitalize the first "it" inside the quote. I would change the intro to the quote to something like "A Wanderer gave their first-hand account after of the river, saying "It flowed…"

> Should anyone fall into the river they will float on the surface of the river but not move nor flow with the river and will be restricted of free movement then will proceed to lose the color of anything on them and themselves and will turn into a dark void color and will then melt and join the river as the strange substance.

"Should anyone fall into the river, they will float on the surface of the river, but they won't move with the apparent flow. Their movements are restricted, and survivors report that after (insert time here), they began to notice they had started to lose color. Eventually, the Wanderer will dissolve and become a part of the river, turning into that substance."

"A Wanderer did consume the substance of the river. They described the taste as bland and dull, but still horrid. After 10 seconds, they described feeling an intense burning. At this point, they could no longer speak, so the M.E.G relied on the field journal of said Wanderer. After another 20 seconds, they describe sharp stomach pain, and 5 minutes after consumption, they said they felt like their body was shattering. A puddle of the substance was found next to the notes."  
I feel like you should come up with a name for the substance. I also feel like you should have a section here or at the end where some random idiot working with the M.E.G. drinks the substance and sits in a room with a camera. Finish off the section by explaining and describing the substance's appearance in greater detail.

> The river seems to also have an endless amount of this unknown substance and does not seem to exhaust its supply of it.

"The river does not seem to exhaust its substance."  
Keep it short and simple; we don't really need to much fluff here.

> The source of the creation of the substance is not known however many speculations and theories have been created.

It's not known how the substance is created, though hypotheses regarding this have been produced.

> Many consist it is being supplied by an unknown source or potentially some kind of entity or entities.

I think the word you are looking for is "insist". "potentially" doesn't fit here as this is already a hypothesis.

> More theories speculate of it coming from an undiscovered level that produces it as some kind of dark energy fuel for other levels as if the unknown level is sentient and is trying to spread its “dark magic”.

I feel like "corruption" fits better here than "dark magic". Put a comma after "other levels".

> However there is no evidence to back up any current speculations and theories, henceforth all are currently invalid until further discoveries are made that connect to them in some way.

I don't think this is needed because theories are just that. If no evidence is there, they remain theories and nothing more.

> Wandering Level 6 until No-clipping into the floor to enter level ###.

Make sure "No-clipping" is lowercased.

Put a period at the end.  
  
  
Alright, so there are some capitalization things that *I* think should be pointed out for more people, as it's a common thing. Here is a list of words I believe should be capitalized:

- Level
- Sub-Level
- Room
- Wanderer (you don't need to capitalize adventurer or explorer)
- Entity
- Enigmatic Entity
- Enigmatic Level
- Object
