# **Core Concept**

It is practically making it similar to the original **Level Fun**, but with a different design. The party rooms are more worn and even destroyed, showing cables and pipes. It looks more like a party room when *the party is over*, the fun is really ruined and the fun balloons are mostly popped. I don't think **Partygoers** live there yet, or even exist again =(

---

# **Additional Notes**

This idea is up to everyone's opinion, if they want the *fun party rooms* to return in the form of decomposition and destruction without any change, or may it be forgotten and forever erased from all eternity. I think it's practically going back to this iconic popular level in the current **Level !** (run for your life), but hey I want your opinion since I could create the level. Anyone who wants to give more ideas about what else can be added to the new **Level Fun** can comment on them but with the condition that they are not so old (that is, don't put anything related to the Partygoers returning or something like that).
