06 Jun 2020 05:38

> Trimmed summary as a courtesy to mobile users

In the original ‘Levels of the Backrooms’ post on Reddit, which is widely considered to be canon, as it introduced Levels 1 and 2, there is a passing mention to places “worse than the Backrooms” | “The darker walls in some of the rooms are entry points into the Backrooms and can be used to exit, although some may lead to places worse than the Backrooms.” What exactly does this line mean? Does it mean Level 1 and Level 2? Does it mean somewhere completely different? Are there other sets of Backrooms that exist? Is it Hell? Who knows?

This thread is to discuss the places that could be “worse than the Backrooms”

My theory is that it’s Level 1 or Level 2. Perhaps Backrooms levels aren’t situated on the same universal plane, but rather you’re descending downwards and downwards, explaining the loss of control you feel. Scary stuff.
