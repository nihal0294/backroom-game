> An Explorer incorrectly handling

"Explorer" shouldn't be capitalized.

> incorrectly handling Entity 451

Punctuation is needed.

> Entity 451 is a book, which title is always

Change "which" to "whose".

> Entity 451 covers and spines

Change "451" to "451's"

> as both Hardback, Softback, and Leatherbound,

Remove "both" and don't capitalize "Hardback" "Softback" or "Leatherbound"

> with the title having no proven relation to the species.

Rewrite this part as:

\*though the title has been proven to have no relation to the species.\*

> Softback Entities have been known to actively seek

Don't capitalize "Entities".

> Softback Entities have weak Psychokinetic abilities

Don't capitalize "Entities". Don't capitalize "Psychokinetic".

> Upon reaching the end of a softback's

"softback's" should be capitalized.

> softback's lifecycle, they're known to open

"lifecycle" should be two words.

> open fully, and burst

Remove the comma here.

> versions of entity 451.

"entity 451" should be capitalized here.

> the established Psychokinetic abilities shown

Don't capitalize "Psychokinetic".

> to the leatherbacks inability to reproduce.

"leathbacks" should be capitalized and have an apostrophe before the s since this use is possessive.

> Leatherbacks are known to hide in Settlements

"Settlements" should be capitalized.

> Upon opening a leatherback open, it will burst

"leatherback" should be capitalized.

> though leatherbacks are recorded to burn

"leatherback" should be capitalized.

> last in 4 Stages.

Unapitalize "Stages." add a linebreak in between each step of creation.

> A paperback begins the starting of a

Capitalize "paperback".

> a paperback is

Capitalize "paperback".

> population of softbacks is

Capitalize "softbacks".

> of softbacks and hardbacks is high, then it will instead become a leatherback.

Capitalize "softbacks" "hardbacks" and "leatherback". Change "is" to "are".

> The entity 451 has reached

Capitalize "entity 451".

> or other entity 451's depending on the species.

Capitalize "entity 451".

> birth new paperbacks from its ashes.

Capitalize "paperbacks".
