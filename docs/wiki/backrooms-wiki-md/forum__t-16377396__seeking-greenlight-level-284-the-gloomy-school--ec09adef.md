Concept Critique

12 Oct 2023 18:16

For a level based on your experience of a school lockdown, I'm a bit disappointed by the execution. You're focusing on what the level looks like, but not the feelings and emotions you felt when you were locked down. I'm MUCH more interested in the emotions than a basic description. I would take a look at [Level 249](level-249.md). It has a similar execution of a place focused on a particular event. I recommend making your page look more like that if you're going to focus on an experience. The level template isn't the best choice.

At the moment, the page is a simple description of "A Place with a Spooky Thing." The wiki needs more than that. Imagine you're a wanderer in the Backrooms. You've come to this new level. What are the first three things that identify this level? What does it look like? What does it sound like? Expand on those descriptions. Use word choice to make me *feel* like I'm really there. Don't just say "Level 284 resembles an outdoor high school campus. The outside is shrouded by this purple gloom." What does the high school look like? Is it pristine? Run-down? Old? New? Give me details. Same with the purple gloom. It's purple, but is it a fog? Is it a haze? The general ambiance of the level? What does it smell like? What are signs you're breathing it in?

Also, I will be honest, our site forums are incredibly slow for feedback. Our Discord is where most critics are at the moment, but we have a small group of people who focus solely on the forums.

Re: Concept Critique

17 Oct 2023 00:39

Okay, much better for sure!

Next thing I'd do is go through and reexamine everything you've written to see if it's necessary for your page. Your page is essentially about a school lockdown because of a chemical hazard, yes? So anything you add should feed into that idea somehow.

Let's look at this paragraph:

> Level 284 is the 285th level of the Backrooms, It was discovered on 09/01/2023. It appears to be an outdoor high school campus with the outside being shrouded with this Purple Gloom

What purpose does this serve? Sure, on the surface level, it tells me when the level was discovered and a brief description, but does your reader truly need to know that? Why is it important that I know that (especially when the next paragraph essentially repeats the same information)? Conservation of detail is important when you're writing short stories, which is essentially most level pages on the wiki. Every character the page introduces, every setting it goes to takes up valuable real estate in the page. So everything you add needs to matter. Conversely, everything that doesn't add to the page's atmosphere or theme should be removed… like the opening paragraph I mentioned.

For example, a lot of your Buildings and Entities aren't doing much. They feel slapped on, like you needed something to fill the space. I recommend either changing them or removing them. Abigail has potential though, especially if she's the lone survivor of the level trying to make sure that other wanderers are safe. Just an idea. You don't have to use it if it doesn't fit your idea :)

The pink text is interesting. It's definitely a step in the right direction.
