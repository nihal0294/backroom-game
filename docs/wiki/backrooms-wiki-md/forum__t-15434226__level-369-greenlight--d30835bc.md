Well, that's a bit dry.  
Still, as promised, here's your review.

Style : OK, i find the theme beautiful. And this is true too for the logo.  
Yet, i'm wondering about the thematic accuracy of theses?  
For example:  
Wouldn't that more coherent if some black cases randomly turn white for a certain period of time as the main particularity of your level is ever-changing nature (more in the nitpicking category)?  
As for the logo, i guess that this is alternating between a regular class 5 and a symbol to represent that monsters can roam freely(in certain area). Wouldn't that broken chains be more coherent with that aspect of the level?

Writing style:  
Mainly OK. But if you're looking for a more classic entry, some part will need to be rewritten in clinical tone (I admit this isn't always easy).

Structure:  
I have nothing to tell about it. The entry is correctly structured.

Core concept:  
If we summarize your level, the main concept is "randomly generated city with patches of forest with malevolent periodic fog".  
As the name of the level is "Concrete Abyss", i was expecting some kind of gradation effect among all the zones.  
Example: Fog, light, danger, number of entities, anomalous effects.  
well, there is a gradation with light, but this is abruptly stopped by the barrier forest.  
I'd recommend it to put the barrier forest in the upper part to keep the idea of gradation.

As for the fog, the idea of fog and negative or malevolent is something that is often associated in fiction leading to harder opportunities to innovate.  
If you're looking for more originality, a vital question is what differentiates my level from other levels.

You're talking about Sanity depletion effect, be more specific with the effects as madness and psychosis exist in many forms. Also, how people noticed your sanity depletion effects ?  
What are the symptoms ?

Zones:  
Many questions arise from this point:  
Is a safe zone coherent with a level danger of 5/unknown ?  
Is the danger zone or the barrier forest the most dangerous zone of the level as the void zone is the 2nd most dangerous zone ?  
Well, for the mansion in the void, i think that bedrooms and refilling stocks of food are nice assets, but if you compare to the entity infestation and the sanity depletion effect, why a wanderer would want to go in this place? A reward? Challenge? A special exit? A call to the void ?

Entities  
I've nothing to say about the cat, go with it.  
A big problem that i can see arise is that some zones are populated with all the entities that can spawn in the backrooms, all of them. The big problem is that it may introduce huge problem in tone consistency as you're potentially mixing everything and in power scaling.

Factions  
Well, the flatlander is living in the void zone with entity infestation and sanity depletion effect.  
Also, what can pushes MEG to mine concrete in the danger zone while others safer alternatives? what are the advantages? Abundance? Special quality of the material? …

Entrances and exit  
No problem with death being a mean of entrance and exit, it's original.  
But others persons may find the process problematic.
