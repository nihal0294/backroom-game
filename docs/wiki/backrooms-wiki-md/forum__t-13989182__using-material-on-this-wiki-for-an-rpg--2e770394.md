Using material on this wiki for an RPG

28 Jan 2021 13:32

I am in the process of writing a table top role playing game based on the Backrooms. Is the material on this wiki considered Creative Commons for that purpose? I want to make sure I will be able to use material legally. I will probably be focusing on the first three levels but would like to be able to use some of the groups and entities on the wiki.

The finished product will be available as a digital download on DrivethruRPG.com and be either free or "pay what you want."

Re: Using material on this wiki for an RPG

28 Jan 2021 15:14

Yep, everything on the wiki is CC BY-SA 3.0 (except for many of the images, but we are working to fix that). So, you're completely fine to use any of it, and even make money off it as long as you credit the wiki somewhere. The only other thing is that to comply with CC BY-SA, you have to release your own work under CC BY-SA 3.0 (this is as easy as just saying it's CC BY-SA 3.0 somewhere).
