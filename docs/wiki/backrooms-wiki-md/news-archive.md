# News Archive

# What is the site newsletter?

The site newsletter is posted on a seasonal basis and aims to help all users understand the events and improvements made to the site within a specific time frame. This helps not only allow better transparency and communication from the staff team to site users, but also helps spread important information and awareness to users across all platforms!

The site newsletter often summarizes the monthly updates in featured pages, Star Critic, alongside progress reports regarding long-term staff projects and other unique news for that period.

Since the site newsletter is often written by multiple staff members, updates may be inconsistent and will not be published on a specific day or time periodically. Because of this, make sure to check for updates regularly!
