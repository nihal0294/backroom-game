# Overview

"…And so, the Gods emerged from the Aether, as they came down to form paradise. Just as a sculptor brings forth their masterpiece from a lump of clay, the divine touch of the Gods brought forth paradise from [The Void](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/the-void). Each God played a pivotal role in creation, blessing us with many gifts…and necessary evils. We give ourselves to the Gods, appeasing them in the hopes that they may continue to bless us with yet more gifts, and usher in eons of prosperity to come. "

In the beginning, The Backrooms were home to many powerful beings, each commanding an aspect of reality. They were as close as family, but that all changed after humans first entered The Backrooms. These companions had reigned as a [pantheon](the-pantheon.md) of gods over the humans, until they were overthrown and their alliance was fractured. Today, many of the "gods" are corrupted, dead or isolated, but they may soon be reunited against a force which threatens their entire home, and all its inhabitants alike.

The Pantheon is one of the more esoteric interpretations of the Backrooms, shedding light on its metaphysical nature and the forces at work behind it. At the center of its narrative are an ancient family whose relationships with each-other transcend time and space. Although this story may be one of Gods, there is an undeniably human element to it. Its themes are of legacy, purpose, betrayal, heartbreak, reconciliation, and more.

## [The Puzzle Maker](entity-64.md)

"Puzzles and enigmas are super fun! Would you like to solve some with me?"

The Puzzle Maker is a harmless entity composed of puzzle pieces; he is friendly and wishes to befriend others and create puzzles for them to solve. He often suffers from loneliness, as he is secluded in the level that he resides in, not having anywhere else to go.
