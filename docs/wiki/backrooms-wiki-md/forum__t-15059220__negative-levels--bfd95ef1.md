Actually, I'm pretty sure a thing about them is that there is rarely any almond water, and instead commonly cashew water. It's sort of the opposite of almond water, in that's it's lethal, and it makes some hostile entities stronger.

They are in a sense just enigmatic levels, with bizarre behaviors that don't normally fit in the main list. The only difference between these and other enigmatic levels, to my knowledge, is how unstable they are. I don't think anyone knows why they are so broken, but some people seem to consider them "going the opposite direction from level 0". That might be why the negative numbers at all, though I'm not sure. If thinking using a videogame canon, it kinda makes sense. They are very similar to infamous glitch areas in videogames, such as the original Mario's Minus World, which is extremely glitchy. That is a negative world, so in the opposite direction you normally go, and it's extremely unstable. Huh…sound familiar?
