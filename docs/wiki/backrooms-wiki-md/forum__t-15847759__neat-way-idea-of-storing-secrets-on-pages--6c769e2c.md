neat way (idea) of storing secrets on pages.

11 May 2023 12:05

So, while going from page to page, and offset to offset, I figured out a way to go to offsets quickly.  
(this ain't revolutionary, but hear me out.)  
backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/whatever-page/offset/1 (i'm using this as a base example)  
backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/whatever-page/offset/|  
backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/whatever-page/offset/2

It would be *"neat"* if someone took advantage of this, and made some secret offsets, or easter eggs. With the only way of accessing these hidden offsets is by changing the URL itself. I think it would fit the same feel as "developer knows your gonna glitch or go out of bounds" type feel. Just a suggestion to put out there.
