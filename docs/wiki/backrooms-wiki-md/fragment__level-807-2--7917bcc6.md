# DESCRIPTION

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-sandbox-2.wdfiles.com/local--files/fragment:collab:pogsitive-2/800px-Burnt_forest_2_GJ.jpg)

Level 807

Level 807 is a destroyed forest spanning an estimated 200 square kilometers[1](javascript:;). The level follows a 24-hour day-night cycle. The level contains a Sun, similar to that of the Frontrooms, but it does not have a Moon.

Level 807's average temperatures range from 31°C to 36°C[2](javascript:;), which are slightly hotter than a typical equatorial climate. Rainfall is incredibly frequent, with at least one storm across the entire level every 9 hours. The rain from these storms are incredibly acidic, with a pH value of 4. These storms can be detected by observing the clouds. If the clouds are pointing towards the Sun, a storm is very likely to arrive soon.

Level 807 also contains several springs. These springs are not only acidic, but also contain a lot of toxic chemicals, such as lead and cyanide. Thus, it is highly advised against using the water from the springs for any purpose.

# RESOURCES

  
Level 807 is one of the most abundant levels in terms of valuable metals. Underground, the U.E.C extracts a wide variety of metals. These include common metals, such as copper and nickel, as well as rare earth metals, like scandium and gold.

# ENTITY PRESENCE

  
Level 807 contains Hounds, Deathmoths and Crawlers. These entities are confirmed to have originated from The Hive, and are not native to Level 807. Their entrance is through Crater Typhon, which also is an exit from this level, although one should never use this as a way to leave.

## Crater Typhon

Crater Typhon is a crater in Level 807 that appeared on November 19th, [data corrupted]. The crater is medium sized. It connects Level 807 and The Hive together, causing entities from there, such as Hounds and Deathmoths, to swarm Level 807. The walls of the crater are made of fleshy inanimate tentacles. It is currently unknown how this crater appeared.

# The 807 Incident:

The 807 Incident occurred on November 19th, [data corrupted] in an area of Level 807. This incident was recorded by now-deceased member Gordon Faring. Gordon Faring is a member of Research Team Circe, which was tasked with testing the spreading properties of the fleshy substance that make up the walls of [The Hive](level-276.md), to see if it can be used to remove [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) presence in Level 807. This experiment was the cause of the creation of Crater Typhon. While visual data from the recording is mostly intact, all sound data has been unrecoverable.

# COLONIES AND OUTPOSTS

  

### Base Echidna

---

  
**Military Leader:** Lieutenant B.Turner of the H.D.F  
**Civilian Leader:** Iyeyasu Takahashi  
**Establishment Date:** ~2021  
**Population:** 83 permanent residents, ~5-10 temporary residents  
**Description:** Base Echidna is currently one of the only bases left on Level 807. It was founded by George Helens in 2021 on a planned operation to settle in the level. It is stationed near Mine Hephaestus, to accommodate the miners and guards that protect the mine from thieves. Furnaces are also placed to smelt the metals extracted from the mine. Most of the miners are [M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md) prisoners, who serve their sentence helping [the U.E.C](the-u-e-c.md). The main office and dorm of the base is air-conditioned to cool the guards from the sweltering heat of the level.

### [B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) Collection Outpost

---

  
**Description:** The Collection Outpost represents the biggest danger towards Base Echidna in Level 807. It was built around the same time as Base Echidna, but is bigger due to including the now abandoned Base Cedar. The questioning of several [M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md) prisoners yielded important information for attacks against the outpost. However, the attacks would often take place after the outpost was just recovering from an attack from the now-departed group, The Ecology Protection Agency. This would allow [U.E.C](the-u-e-c.md) members to be able to seize valuable communications equipment of the [the B.N.T.G.](the-b-n-t-g.md) and [M.E.G](the-m-e-g.md), allowing their communication channels to be spied on.
