## | Information |

---

Mr. Valentin is a mysterious figure who leads the Eternal Repository, an information collective with a large member count. He is a 6' 6" male of Russian descent, age 35, and is almost never seen by most people. His whereabouts are unknown, his standing is neutral, and by all means, Valentin is completely anonymous.

Valentin was brought to the M.E.G.'s attention after a new database was merged with the M.E.G.'s servers, with the directory name being "The Eternal Repository," the name of his organization. Attempts at removing the entries have failed, and with future communications the two groups have reached an agreement to share information.

## | Behavior |

---

The behaviors of Mr. Valentin are hard to determine. With his constant anonymity and secrecy, we can only input what has been rumored.

[ERR401:UNAUTHORIZED]

I'll be taking charge here, if you don't mind.

I am a simple man who wishes to give everyone a chance to be equals. There is too much corruption on Earth. Too much discrimination, too many fallacies, and too many people who wish to twist the truth for their own gain. Ones who rip the facts out of the objective positive and turn it into something that profits them.

My code stretches far and wide, to every corner of the Backrooms that I can reach. Even I, the Master Administrator of the Eternal Repository, am equal among my members. Someone could overtake me right now, but with how our system works the collective likes me. I am in their favor. If someone wants to be an equal, then join us.

No [rumored governmental corruption](the-m-e-g.md), no [crazed masks](the-masked-maidens.md), and no [vigilantie justice](the-eyes-of-argos.md). We provide safety, loyalty, honor, and legitimacy. As black market as we seem, we are very cooperative people.

Unfortunately, you will not receive information on what I look like or my routine. That's a little out of the question, now isn't it?

[Let's get digging, friend.](archived__the-eternal-repository--16af9bf0.md)

# **M.E.G. Database:**

**Event Log: 28237 | Type: Discussion | Date: 02/01/2022**

---

**This log was recorded when Overseer-A, B and C met with The Eternal Repository for the first time. To give good faith, Mr. Valentin appeared to talk to the Overseers directly.**

---

**Begin Log:**

*The meeting room is set for the Overseers, and the three enter as the assistants leave the room.*  
  
**Andrew:** Where is the Repository? Didn't they say they'd be here?  
  
**Kat:** Calm down now, Andrew, I'm sure they'll be here-  
  
*The door opens, and four Sentinel's of Navy enter the room. Mr. Valentin enters soon after, sitting down in the chair that was placed for him*  
  
**Kat:** Well, speak of the devil, Andrew. Here they are.  
  
*Stretch gets up and holds a hand out to greet them*  
  
**Stretch:** Good morning! I'm Overseer-A of the M.E.G., but you can call me Stretch. Nice to meet you!  
  
*Valentin rises, shaking his hand firmly*  
  
**Valentin:** Good to meet you as well, Mr. Stretch. I am Viktor Valentin of the Repository. Glad we made acquaintances.  
  
**Stretch:** This is Overseer-B, Kat, and that's Overseer-C, Andrew.  
  
**Valentin:** Good to meet you both. Say, I expected you three to be a bit older.  
  
**Andrew:** What, saying that we aren't wise enough for this?  
  
**Stretch:** Andrew-  
  
**Valentin:** It's quite alright, Stretch. No, Andrew, I am simply surprised. No need for hostility, my friend. Now, let us talk.  
  
**Kat:** The floor is yours, Valentin.  
  
**Valentin:** I contacted you just so we could greet ourselves and hopefully… get on good terms, persay. I'm sure you've heard about what we do and who we're affiliated with.  
**Stretch:** Yeah, we've gotten talk of your group being with the U.E.C., the Masked Maidens, even the Eyes of Argos.  
  
**Kat:** I'm surprised you got under the Maiden's skin. They're crazy, some of them.  
  
**Valentin:** It definitely wasn't easy, I'll tell you that, but our infiltrators do their best.  
  
**Andrew:** So, you guys make deals of information for supplies, right?  
  
**Valentin:** Correct.  
  
**Andrew:** Is this what you're doin? Bringing up a deal?  
  
**Valentin:** For as hostile as you are, you surely do have a sharp eye.  
  
*Valentin reaches into his cloak and pulls out a file, laying it on the table in front of him.*  
  
**Valentin:** This is information on a recently discovered level. I will let you have ownership of this information, though you will have to hand over something of your own.  
  
**Stretch:** Do you have anything in mind, or can it be whatever?  
  
**Valentin:** Yes, actually. You see, we do not currently have sufficient weaponry. My men behind me resort to close-combat knives and scraps, as most of our materials currently go to our uniforms. We'd like some new, longer weapons like swords or spears. Maybe even a crossbow or two. They would be priceless.  
  
*The Overseers look at each other*  
  
**Valentin:** Of course, the Eyes of Argos would also love this information to spot new… "suspects."  
  
*He begins to pull the file back*  
  
**Kat:** No, wait!  
  
*Valentin stops and turns to look at Kat*  
  
**Valentin:** Yes?  
  
**Kat:** We can spare some spears and a crossbow. Stretch did inventory yesterday.  
  
*Valentin looks at Stretch*  
  
**Stretch:** Yeah, we got extras. How much information is here?  
  
*Valentin opens the folder slightly to reveal 25 pages, all of them filled to the brim from the short glace the Overseers got*  
  
**Andrew:** That's… a lot.  
  
**Stretch:** You can say that again! It's payday!  
  
**Andrew:** Never say that again.  
  
**Kat:** Alright, so for spears and a crossbow we get this information?  
  
**Valentin:** Correct.  
  
**Andrew:** Here's another question I have: why do you want equipment from us? If you've been around since 2016, then you should have great gear right now.  
  
**Valentin:** Your sharp perception continues to value you. Yes, we do have good weaponry. Great weaponry, even.  
  
**Stretch:** Then what's the point of getting our gear?  
  
**Kat:** To study us. He wants to see how prepared we are.  
  
*Valentin gives a few slow claps*  
  
**Valentin:** Wonderful. I love seeing minds work. Yes, I want to study you. I like to know the people I'm shaking hands with.  
  
*The Overseers look at each other again, and after a few seconds look back at the man*  
  
**Stretch:** Alright, we accept your deal.  
  
*Stretch and Valentin rise and shake hands. Andrew sighs and calls for a logistics member to bring the requested weaponry, and a few minutes later they arrive. The Sentinel's of Navy take this weaponry, and Valentin slides the Overseers the file.*  
  
**Valentin:** Pleasure doing business with you, Overseers. I hope we meet again soon.  
  
*Valentin rises and leaves the meeting room, the four Sentinels following immediately after.*
