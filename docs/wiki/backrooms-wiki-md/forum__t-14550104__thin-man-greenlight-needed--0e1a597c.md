So let me get this straight

- It's a thin, slender man with very dark skin. No eyes or ears.  
- Wears a zipped black jacket and black pants with white shoes  
- Was discovered by three people who died, but we somehow know their names.  
- Whistles "Ring Around the Rosie" slowly  
- Few people have survived.

This is very, **very** bare bones. It sounds like some mixture between Jeff and Slender with some Legion thrown in there. It's just another "oh look this entity is scawy! don't approach it! it'll KILL you!" There's almost no story, no background, nothing **interesting.**

It needs to be built upon. Right now this would flop harder than a boulder hitting the sea.
