Mohti unit

15 May 2025 08:22

Concept Title: : “Mohti” unit

Central Narrative: : A unit used to measure the stability of spatial reality

Brief Description: : My idea is that, since the Backrooms has many levels where spatial reality is unstable — for example, Level 789 and Level 404 — then maybe there could be a unit people can use to measure the stability of spatial reality. I named this conceptual unit “Mohti”, with the symbol abbreviation “Mt”. The lowest value is 0, which belongs to baseline reality, and the higher the number, the more unstable the spatial reality is.

Hook: : Honestly, I’m not really sure whether this concept is worth creating. Its feature is that it gives the Backrooms universe a scientific unit to measure reality stability.

First of all, I apologize for the late reply.

Designing a group like this would be that work a lot of care, and the amount of content that could be implemented would be quite limited. Besides, there would be two questions you must satisfactorily answer: "What purpose does measuring the stability of reality?" and "How or according to which criteria do they measure the stability of reality?"

If done well, it could really be great, but if not executed properly, it will likely never go beyond the draft stage. To design such group, you'll truly need strong writing skills and imagination.
