Entity: The Elementals

18 May 2022 11:12

**Page Type:** Entity  
**Page Layout:** Most likely standard, but that might change

**Elevator Pitch:** This concept goes over four distinct entities that each represent elements in nature as well as the backrooms themselves. These beings each embody fire, water, air, and earth respectively. To save time and so you can get the idea, here's a brief rundown of each of the four entities:

1. **Gnomes** *(Earth)*: Short, rotund, solitary, amorphous entities comprised of various matter in their inhabited level, whether inorganic or organic. They can melt into floors and walls when startled, or approached, and blend almost seamlessly into their environment. Docile by nature, they usually pose little threat to travelers. They typically assume a biped or quadrupedal stance though never getting anywhere past 2-3 ft in height. Their common diet consists of mold, discarded inorganic objects, plastics, plaster, and almond water.
2. **Undine** *(Water)*: Transparent, predatory entities that roam the dark, water-based levels of the backrooms. Their natural forms are a ghostly amalgamation of deep-sea fish, dragonfly nymphs, and giant shrimp with bioluminescent patches across their bodies ranging from blue-green to red. However, they have the uncanny ability to assume a human-like appearance when traversing land, described as having sickly pale skin covered in dark veins, four elongated webbed hands, long fin-like ears, and black beady eyes, and gaping maws with sharp pointed teeth. All reported individuals are female and seem to reproduce via parthenogenesis. They are highly social and carnivorous, utilizing their bioluminescent lights and distinctive calls to lull travelers and animals into an entranced state before dragging them to their deaths.
3. **Sylvestis** *(Air)*: Sort of an enigma, I am still trying to find out where exactly where they belong in this.
4. **Salamander** *(Fire)*: Stands out from the others by being a singular entity with its own level. This creature resembles a colossal olm comprised of molten iron, nickel, and rock and roughly eight pairs of legs running along the length of its body. It remains trapped in a cylindrical shaft roughly 300m in diameter and two miles deep, with the rim of the said pit having connected halls and staircases with an undisclosed number of floors surrounding the shaft. Every now and then, the entity will try to climb out of the shaft, causing massive structural damage through its sheer size and ungodly heat. However, upon reaching a certain height, massive streams of almond water pour from large pipes high above which floods the lower half of the shaft and cause the beast to harden and break apart, falling back into the pit. The water boils into hot vapers and escapes from the top of the shaft as the level resets itself, and the cycle repeats.

**Central Themes:** The 4 entities are inspired by the elementals from Paracelsus' works, which the entities and corresponded compositions and names being reminiscent of said creatures. Each entity represents the specific quality of the Backrooms as parts of the natural real world. Their biology and nature are both comprehensible but also break any semblance of normalcy without simply being scary or evil. Basically, they are personifications of various thematic/integral elements of Backrooms while not being in control of it.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** As stated above, the Elementals are a series of strange and peculiar beings that correspond with a specific that follows a sort of mythological concept. The Salamander entity itself is a sort of character in its own right whose true intentions and circumstances of its are left purposefully ambiguous, leaving just enough details to get people interested, but leaving the context up to interpretation.
