Hello, fellow backrooms fans!

04 Aug 2020 17:30

My name's Roger and I joined this site a few months ago because I've been interested in the backrooms and its aesthetic for a while, and also because I got to know some other themes since then that share a lot with the backrooms aesthetic, such as 'liminal spaces' and 'anemoia' (which are featured in YouTube videos with titles like 'pictures that feel strangely familiar but are uncomfortable'.

And let me tell you, there's something strangely familiar, nostalgic and unsettling about them indeed, and they somehow just keep me glued to the screen while watching them, even though they are mostly places in the USA (where I don't live in the first place) and make me nostalgic for things that I never witnessed (this is the phenomenon that's called 'anemoia'). I guess they subconciously remind me of early childhood memories from a time where the way you saw the world was different (if that makes sense), and all of this ties in perfectly with the idea of the backrooms and is a major part of the reason why I love it so much.

Anyways, I hope to come up with some nice enough ideas for backrooms levels, items, entities, etc. (if I'm not constantly lazy of course) and look forward to interacting with the community here too. :)
