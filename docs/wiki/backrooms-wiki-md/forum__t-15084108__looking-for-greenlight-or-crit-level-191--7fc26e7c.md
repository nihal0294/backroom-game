Okay, I'll give it a go.

### SPaG

> Everybody that its in level

Correct to "Everbody that is in the level" or "Everybody that is in Level 191"

> Very Sweet

Sweet should not be capitalized.

> There can sometimes rain suggar.

Suggar -> Sugar

> They were human but they where to much time in the border area.

They were human but they spent too much time in the border area.

> They are a open for wonderers base with over 100 people.

They are a base open for wanderer trading, and house over 100 people.

> This is our base dedicated to sweet taste.

Why does it say "our" when the rest of the article is in 3rd person? Change to "the". The sentence should be rewritten, actually.

> Noclip thru a bucket. There is a chance of nocliping thru fog.

"Thru" is unacceptable and should be changed to "though". You aren't texting. Noclipping is spelled with 3 g's.

Formatting: This looks abysmal. I like that you decided to create a whole theme for this page, but the theme looks trash. The use of tiny dots taking up the entirety of the page really hurts the eyes and makes this *very* difficult to read. You cannot see the header image, title, subtitle, and "my account" very well either. Imo, I wouldn't say I like it. The logo is certainly interesting, but that alone won't save such a distracting theme. I think that [Aesthetic](theme__aesthetic--04aec895.md) would look much better.

Also, why did you create an entire header for taste, and write "Very Sweet?" You added many unnecessary headers which added no helpful statements about your level. As for sentence flow, this was disgusting. Instead of flowing sentences, you put a fact. After fact. After fact. And in a vertical list as well. Please make these sentences flow well, describe to me the level in great detail, and not list facts out.

### Final Comments:

You did not do a great job. You used a theme that made it very difficult to read, had poor sentence flow, had little to no description of the level, and I have no idea what's going on. The only salvageable piece of this I may consider slightly decent was your bare-bones concept. I still do not know, but is the level made of candy? I could ask endless questions, but first, I recommend you restart this sandbox, and work off a Candyland-type idea. PM me if you have questions or Discord at Iamalemon#6339
