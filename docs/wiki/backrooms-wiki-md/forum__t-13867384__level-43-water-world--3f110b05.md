The age does show on this one I will admit, the SPaG is definitely not up to snuff. however the concept and execution are excellent. The way the environment gets more fucked up and weird the further you go eventually culminating to what basically seems like the building turning into a boss fight arena for the evil fish thing and other entities to attack you in. It's a concept that might seem a little bland on the surface but actually reading it is pretty fun and attention grabbing, the details also make it pretty easy for me to picture this level and what it would be like which not every level nowadays can do.  
+1, I definitely want more levels like this one.
