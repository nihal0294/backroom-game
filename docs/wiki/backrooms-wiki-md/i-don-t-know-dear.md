Mum, when are we going to get there?

I don't know, dear.

How long have we been out for?

I don't know, dear.

Ah ok.

---

Mum, when are we going to get there?

I don't know, dear.

Where are we going?

I don't know, dear.

Whyyyyyyyyyyy?

I don't know, dear.

Whyyyyyyyyyyy?

---

Mum, when are we stopping?

I don't know, dear.

But I really need to–  
…ok.

---

Why are we leaving town?

…

…mum?

I don't know, dear.

I don't know either, I liked it there.

I hope we go back. I miss it.

I’m sad.

---

Mum… wait…

…

Mum, why are these hills all the same?

I don't know, dear.

No, stop, you don't see because you're looking on the road – look. The hills are all the same.

I don't know, dear.

Stop saying that, mum! No! No no no!

I don't know, dear.

No! Stop saying that!

Stop it, stop it, stop it!

…

…

Mum?

…

Who are you?
