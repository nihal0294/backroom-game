There are a shocking number of weird ways to make full sentences out of just two words, lol

Will Will Smith smith?  
Smith Will Smith will Smith Will.  
Will Smith will smith.

There are three proper sentences right there. The first one is asking if he will do smithing, the second is saying he is a smith and he will smith someone named Will, and the third is saying he will do some smithing xD

\*Not at all confusing \* xD

That Buffalo one though…just wow. One word repeated 8 times is a sentence?! That's a whole different level of crazy sentence, lol
