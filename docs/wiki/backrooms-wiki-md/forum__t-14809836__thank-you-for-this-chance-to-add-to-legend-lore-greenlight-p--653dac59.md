**Level 365**

> Level 365 is the 366th Level of the Backrooms.

I would personally opt to not use this copypasta phrase due to redundancy. It serves no purpose other than stating the obvious, so a bunch of us are starting to phase it out of our articles.

> "Shutting your eyes can't stop the nauseating smell of drying paint and industrial cleaner. Even that isn't as bad as the creepy feeling of being watched. Two floor-to-ceiling mirrors in this narrow hallway face each other, creating an infinity mirror and a thousand reflections of you. One of your reflections suddenly turns the other way on its own and departs. Welcome to Level 365."  
> - - Anne Dunne, Investigative Scholar of the Paranormal

I would recommend that before the description, you put this instead. Since it's a quote, it would make more sense to have this as a sort of representation people see at the start of the article, and generally, quotes — whilst not totally outlandish in the description — aren't put in it.

> can't stop the nauseating smell

won't stop the nauseating smell

> creating an infinity mirror and a thousand reflections of you

creating an infinite loop of thousands of your reflections.

> Level 365 is a complex labyrinth of mirrored hallways without rooms. Mirrors are mounted on the left wall or the right or both

Level 365 is a complex labyrinth of mirrored hallways that lack rooms. Mirrors can be found mounted on the walls of these rooms, either on the left, right, or both sides.

> Some are like carnival mirrors and distort what you see. Darkened passageways with mirrors covering the walls, floor, and ceiling turn dazzling flashlight beams into colorless kaleidoscopes.

The order of these sentences is mixed. 'Darkened passageways' should be first, 'Some are' should be second.

> There are even black mirrors, dubbed Night Mirrors, known to give wanderers terrifying dreams.

How do they give wanderers terrifying dreams? Elaboration is key because something like this, which isn't spoken about anywhere else in the article, should be explained lest you have potholes in your story.

> Everything begins in immaculate condition.

I'm not quite accustomed to the use of 'begins' in this sentence over here. Could you elaborate on its meaning here?

> and bandages. It is a sinister

and bandages: it is a sinister

> upon darkest fears and thoughts.

Might I suggest another word instead of darkest? Ghastly or ghoulish come to mind for me, though this change is entirely up to you.

> Seers are the reason for the abundance of Wretches on this level.

This line isn't a criticism but holy hell I love how raw this line is. I was puzzled at first and when it clicked I had this awakening in my body. Bravo!

> the Backrooms reality.

The Backrooms  
[sic]. Not that your terminology is wrong here, but it's not all that used and simply The Backrooms fits better here.

> wanderers in need here

wanderers in need.

> Atlantic Ocean and about

Atlantic Ocean, about

> from M.E.G. found me

from the M.E.G. found me  
(note for the future: M.E.G. stands for Major Explorer Group. Saying just M.E.G. wouldn't be correct in this context, so the 'the' is kinda needed. The only exception is in sentences like the one you wrote here, 'M.E.G. team "Looking Glass" ', because here you don't need to say 'the M.E.G. team', as M.E.G. team is grammatically correct in this case.)

> now work this level

now work in this level

---

A bit of my thoughts here.

First off, before I start my nitpicking and criticism, I *love* your writing style. You have such a polished writing that feels so 'alive' despite being somewhat clinical as well. Your expertise in the field clearly shows, and your vocabulary is so sublime. I enjoyed reading this so much!

With that said though, I still have some small critiques to make. Some parts of the article could definitely use some more elaboration, particularly the first paragraph. Of course, you don't have to write a fully analytical/descriptive piece every time you want to write, but you could definitely pull off more than one paragraph on description alone, especially with a level like this. I suggest you split it into two pieces and expand on each more: the level's structure, and the mirrors. That way, you can elaborate as needed on each of the two.

Onto the seers themselves, I suggest that you put them in the standard format for entities in level articles, that is, like so:

> # Entities:
>
> Lorem Ipsum

This would make it fit better into the existing format, and make your article look more polished as well.

On another note, some safety measures would do this good as well. We're told what these things do, but not how we can avoid them. Is not looking at them good enough? Should we run to where there aren't mirrors? Lots of unanswered questions on safety, which an article like this could definitely use.

Lastly, the last two announcements aren't in quoteboxes. For neatness' sake, I suggest you do that, and that you put all those little messages in a section of their own (my preferred choice is 'additional documents', but some prefer 'addenda' or other such things that imply extended documentation). I'd also recommend that you add an author to the second and third message like you did with the first one. Using the same character as the first one would make sense considering she already wrote one message we see, but you could put in some of the higher ups at the M.E.G. as well.

That's all I have to say for now. Once you apply these critiques, feel free to pm me or reply here — I'll see it either way because I'm subscribed to the [notifications site](index.md). I suggest you apply for that whenever you can, it will help you keep up with your threads and discussions. I'll take a look at your draft again once you apply the changes, and I'll look at your second article once we're done with the first one.

Looking forward to hearing from you again Mr.Goerman!

Saria

P.S: If you disagree with me on anything, feel free to point it out and if I'm wrong tell me where I am wrong!

> or 3"

Subjective change, but feet on the site are written mostly as words on here, so the preferable alternative here is simply '3 feet'

> 8 centimeters  
> (or 3")  
> 14 centimeters

This one is also a bit subjective, though I do feel it necessary to say it anyways. When writing measurements, when the number is below one hundred, you should always write it as the word rather than the symbol. In your case, these should be changed respectively to 'eight centimetres', 'three feet', and 'fourteen centimetres'. In cases where the numbers are bigger, you use the symbols and abbreviate the measurement.

More on the measurements, remember that not everything is always the same. You should specify that this is a rough average taken from multiple instances for example, or that this is an observed trend, and even more realistically, that it is just an estimation, since you're not exactly going to get close to these things.

> Description:

Don't let the image do all the justice to the creature's appearance! Your description could definitely be expanded upon to include some of the things we see in the creature's image. Describe perhaps the shape of it, colours it comes in (or whether it's translucent or not), other physical properties, etc. A bigger description would only serve to make your article much better!

> For all of its horror-inducing reputation, the Seer can easily hide in an 8-ounce coffee cup. Don't let its size fool you.

For all the bone-chilling accounts that it is infamous for, the Seer can easily fit inside an 8-ounce coffee cup. But don't let its size fool you.

> We only see the tragic results, after the Seer enters Level 365, after its main meal is finished and it is time for "dessert."

We only see the tragic results of what happens after the Seer enters Level 365; after its main meal is finished and it's time for "dessert".

> goes to work

gets to work

> Behaviours:

Likewise with descriptions, I think there is grounds for a bit more elaboration here. Barring some parts which you didn't repeat from Level 365's article, I would say that this could use a bit more of an oomph to justify the new entity article. What I mean is that in the entity article, you should elaborate more on the things you couldn't elaborate on in the level, and go into a different level of detail. If this is unfeasible in-universe however, you could try to focus more on a story aspect or some other aspect of the article.

> These are powerful creatures of extreme molecular and biological composition. They appear to have no organs for the senses that most physical life as we know it depends upon. Mirrors enable Seers to entangle and manipulate human prey found in the Backrooms.

The inner biology student in me wants to elaborate a bit more here, I'll suggest a little revision:  
"Sightless Seers are powerful creatures whose molecular and biological composition have baffled researchers time and time again. Whilst they are most definitely corporeal, researchers theorize that a quirk in their molecular structure allows them to behave in a way that defies the general laws of physics. Whilst their selective intangibility with mirrors can be accounted for with this theory, researchers are still not sure as to how these beings can traverse to a realm that is not our own, nor do they know what lies there.

It is unknown how Sightless Seers function as a whole, because preliminary research shows that they lack any form of conventional organs which other physical lifeforms depend upon to survive. Research is ongoing, though the current hypothesis suggests that the way in which they function is simply not understandable with the means currently at our disposal."

> moaning in pain and screaming, while this luminous jellyfish thing flickered and touched her face

moaning and screaming in agony, as this luminous jellyfish thing flickered and touched her face

> weapon invites electrocution

This is one of those things I think you should put in the description. Details like this are crucial, but if you don't put them in a description you will seem like you're making up details as you go on.

Hope this finds you well.

Ha! The level AND the entity were originally both number 365. For a great many reasons. I changed it back just now. Just for you. Well, mostly for you. Okay, partly for you.

"It touches my lips and I kiss Death."

Every article should have at least one sentence that the author is proud to have written. As an experienced author, let me let you in on a little secret:

"If you can write something in three words, don't use ten."

I tell this to Cody all the time. It is my mantra.

It is better to error on the side of less exposition, less description, and let the imagination of the reader fill in the blanks. Don't tell the reader what to see. Less will always be more. Every time.

Having stated this, I do not pad the word count.

My boring "diaries" contain clues and foreshadowing.

One last tidbit of advice: You become a better writer by reading and writing.

You, my friend, are well on your way!
