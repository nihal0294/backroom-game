Hello and welcome!

1. So the [Guide-Hub](guide-hub.md) has answers to a lot of your questions. While it doesn't have everything you wanted, the [Templates](templates.md) should give you a good idea of how to start. When you make a sandbox page, found [here](start.md), there should be an option for making objects and entities (if I remember correctly). From an entities template, you could get an idea for what a POI page would look like. Also, take a look at the other POI pages and see how they're formatted and written.

2. Coolkin70 already gave you the link to the syntax page, so I won't link that :)

3. As for this one, you just need to be a little bit patient. The fastest way to get critiques as a new member is to offer a critique for a critique. [Criticism-Policy](criticism-policy.md) has a good place to start for how to do critiques, but this link [here](https://www.writingforward.com/writing-tips/how-to-critique) also has some good advice.
