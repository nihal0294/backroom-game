Re: Exit to Frontrooms?

03 May 2023 21:05

One of the most interesting components of the backrooms is that **you cannot leave** once somebody removes that element, many will be mad, feeling as though you would be taking away from what most know about the backrooms. Levels like The True End, the arcade one on some other sites, are among my least favorite because I personally dislike the idea of exiting the backrooms. It makes the backrooms feel too easy, even if the exit is strenuously difficult.

So, although you by all means **can** make an exit and even enterance to the frontrooms, you would be at risk of having the article deleted because of a possible abundance of dislikes. I would not reccomend it.

As for everything else you said, it isn't against the rules in any way, shape, or form. Also if you put that that what is beyond the exit is unknown, then it technically is not going to the frontrooms at all, making this question a little confusing to me. If you word it like that, no mention of exiting the backrooms or frontrooms, that's perfectly fine, though I feel many will wonder what is beyond the exit which can lead to dislikes on the basis of "plot-holes" per say.

Just my personal opinions and theories.
