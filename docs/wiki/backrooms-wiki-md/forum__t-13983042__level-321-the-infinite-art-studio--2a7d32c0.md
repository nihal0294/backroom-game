Level 321- The Infinite Art Studio

24 Jan 2021 01:14

**Page Type:** Level

**Elevator Pitch:** The 322nd Level is an infinite art studio with near infinite floors of potentially infinite width, and length. Long winding and colorful hallways with bedrooms, art studios filled with all sorts of art supplies for any type of art you can think of and ballet practice rooms. Its theme is art and creation. It seems to be the main focus, in fact.  
But it isn't, is it?

**Central Narrative:** Recently, it has been confirmed that this level exists. This level is themed around art of many sorts and seems to be very peaceful as levels go. But don't count on that being a constant. It has an entity native to it, many of whom have a functioning society. You will rarely run into them, but if you do, and they are not hostile, tread lightly. Be kind with the Drawn Ones.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** The Level is art themed, more specifically on creating art, than just observing it, like in Level 57. It's centering on creation is something I believed would be one of the things that would make it stand out as an intriguing and clever concept for a level.

**Additional Notes:** I'm hoping to make an Entity page for the Drawn Ones soon once this gets a greenlight.
