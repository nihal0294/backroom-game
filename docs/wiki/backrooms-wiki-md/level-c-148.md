**Level C-148** is the 148th level of Cluster C of the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

Level C-148 is a vast shallow water area; irregularly scattered within it are rocks that emerge above the water surface. The boundary of this level (if it exists) has not yet been discovered.

One must absolutely not fall into the water; as the water body of Level C-148 immediately corrodes anything that enters it, leaving no residue whatsoever[1](javascript:;).

# **Bases, Communities, and Outposts**

There are no known bases, outposts, or communities on this level.

# **Entrances and Exits:**

## **Entrances:**

- There is a very low probability of no-clipping from [Level C-147](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-c-147) into this level.
- Additional entrances remain to be explored.

## **Exits:**

- Move carefully on the rocks until a golden rock is found; perform no-clip while standing on it to reach [Level C-67](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-c-67).
- Additional exits remain to be explored.
