**Level Among Us** is the sussiest level in the backrooms.

# Description

Level Among Us is Among Us in real life. Upon entering you will feel like a crewmate or imposter and will slowly start to transform. You can't see other humans here only entities.

# Entities

The entities of this level are the imposters and the crewmates. If you transform into a crewmate you must avoid getting killed by the imposters and if you get imposter you must avoid getting voted out by crewmates.

# Bases, Outposts, and Communities

##### The Sussy Imposters:

The sussy imposters are a group of imposters that survived but decided to stay because they're built different. We must worship them as our new overlords. It is believed they have some sort of connection with The Fridge People on [Joke Level Fridge](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/joke-level-fridge).

# Entrances

You can enter by playing among us in [Level Fun](level-fun.md)

# Exits

You can Exit by winning the game of among us without dying. This will lead you to any random level[1](javascript:;)[2](javascript:;)[3](javascript:;)
