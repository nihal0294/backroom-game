Hey, you won't believe it, but I think I found it.

Yes, yes! This is IT! I'm surprised you were able to find it, Esther.

Ah it was easy, I just called one of my mom's old work friends, Holly. She found it quick.  
  
Man, this page is old, reads like something from when my mom was… well young*er*. Definitely before my time.

Before either of our times, I think.

Definitely! Unfortunately this level seemed to hit the end of its lifespan. I suppose it wasn't [the](https://web.archive.org/web/20210308200757/http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-29) [only](https://web.archive.org/web/20210302015709/http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-30) [one](https://web.archive.org/web/20210226192333/http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-556), but it still sucks. Must be why we couldn't find it in the main database.

Yeah, you're probably right. I was hoping to go to it again, hopefully taking you with me.

Oh well. We only went once, the memories are pretty hazy. It doesn't help that we were young kids when we were there.

Oh, but you gotta admit it was good! The rides, the games, the nice beachside feel it had to it. I'd kill to go back there again.

There's always [345](level-345.md) and [890](level-890.md)? They're both good amusement parks, from what I've heard. Pretty sure 890 had some modifications done to it recently too.

Eh, those aren't the same.

Heh, whatever you say… At least we still have the memories of the place, right?

I suppose so? Maybe I could take you to one of the places you mentioned as a good second option to the old Level 32?

I'm pretty busy, unfortunately. I need to always be available to help out my mom down in the next block over. You could always take your friends to the other amusement park levels there are?.

I don't have many friends, unfortunately. A lot of them moved or got relocated, and I don't have good ways to contact them.

Oh, sorry. I imagine you can't get their codes to message them on the direct line?

No, I've tried. And it's fine, you didn't know.

Hey, yeah. No problem Magnus. Sorry I can't do much, I know it seems like I'm avoiding you, but adulthood decided to hit me hard and, frankly, early. This shit with my mom has swamped me.

It's okay, I totally understand. Is your mom at least getting better?

No, she's… she's not. I'll probably need to head up at some time this week, just gotta arrange it with my sister.

Your dad's still out of it, I assume.

He's still cooped up in [Level 90](level-90.md) like a coward. Whatever, it doesn't matter too much. If he doesn't want to be there, my sister and I will step up and take the role he refuses to.

Well, good luck. I'll speak to you later, Esther.

Aye, cya.
