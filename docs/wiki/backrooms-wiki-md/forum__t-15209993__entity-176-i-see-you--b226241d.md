Entity 176 -- "i see you"

22 Sep 2022 12:00

[[module Rate]]

**Entity Number:** 176

### Habitat(s): Everywhere

Entity 176 can appear in any level.

### Description:

Entity 176 is a humanoid being that kills wanderers through psychological and physical means.

### Behaviors:

The entity will randomly appear into wanderer's minds, being able to see their thoughts. It digs out the deepest and darkest thoughts in their minds, making them remember it, which drives the wanderers to want death. 176 will then persuade the wanderer to go to [Level 64](level-64.md), and will then snap the wanderer's neck, tear their eyes out, and hang their body from a ceiling fan, usually writing "i see you" in black paint on a nearby wall.

### Biology:

A tall shadow of a humanoid, with small gray slits where eyes would be. Wanderers attacked by "i see you" will repeatedly mention the entity smiling at them.

### Discovery:

A member of Team Reed was attacked by the entity while in [Level 1](level-1.md). The entity forced the member to think about their past in [reality](the-frontrooms.md), in which the member had killed his family and sold drugs. The member eventually went to Level 64. Another member followed him to it. Both were killed, hung, and had their eyes torn out. A nearby wall in Level 64 had "i see you" and "peekaboo" written on it.

### **Do's and Don'ts:**

### **Do:**

- Force happy memories into your mind to block Entity 176. This will drive the entity to either appear and take your eyes, or leave and search for a different wanderer.
- Do not have memories of doing bad things. The entity can use this against you

### **Don't:**

Fall to the entity
