Here's my POI. Please give critiques and maybe a greenlight.
Deanos Eugeneous, more commonly known as The Silver Anchor, is a mysterious and illusive person who is the head, known simply as the Grossadmiral, of the O.R.A.. He is a 6,2" male that looks like he's in his 30's1 and has a Celtic accent. Any form of tracking or recording does not work on him as all tracking and recording devices seem to short circuit when near him2.
He got the attention of M.E.G. when he saved some Team Quick Match members traveling through Level 138 to go and find someone on Level 34 (The details of this rescue can be read in the log below). After saving them he followed them back to Base Alpha. After which he talked with the Overseers to properly introduce himself. After a few days of talks they made an alliance on the grounds that they would not go against each other if either one got into a conflict.
