So, first thing I'm gonna tell you: I'm not a greenlighter. I just saw this post, and I thought it looked interesting, so I clicked on it.

So, when I looked at the post, it just seems… Incomplete.  
Here's what I can offer for critique:

[+ show block](javascript:;)

[– hide block](javascript:;)

##### Let's take a look at the sentence structure:

> Level ### is a long hallway where the wanderer will walk there are no doors for the first 15 minutes of walking when they walk at 15 minutes there will be iron bars with a note on them saying "turn to the right and you will find a door" the wanderer must go into the door and then they will enter a desert in front of them will be a backpack full of supplies they will keep walking and eventually they will meet a very dehydrated warrior the warrior will be sitting under the slim shade of a cactus the wanderer can choose to give the backpack to this warrior and leave or not give the backpack to the warrior if the backpack is given to the warrior the wanderer will have their choice of 5 weapons a katana, greatsword, longsword, great axe, dagger. The warrior will then lead the wanderer out of the desert and to the door which they came in however if the wanderer does not give the backpack they will not get a weapon and will have to find their way back to the main hallways of Level ###……  
> And so on.

This needs some periods and commas; everything feels like a run-on sentence. How about separating the sentences like this:

> Level ### appears as a long hallway. When entered, there are no doors for the first 15 minutes of walking, but after walking for 15 minutes, there will be some iron bars with a note saying "Turn to the right and you will find a door"…  
> (The rest of the stuff, fixed like this.)

If you separate sentences like this, it is easier to read, and people can understand it better.

The rest of the description is explaining how the wanderer can exit this Level, and it's really interesting and creative, but I feel like it has some problems with it.

I like how it makes the wanderer feel like they must survive, but it just seems like "If they do (1) then (2), and if they do (a) then (b)". I feel like the writing would feel better if it focused more on the 'wanderer's choices'. In the Exits, it says that the endings are different whether you are good or bad, and I really liked that idea. I thought maybe you could apply that to the 'choices', so if the wanderer acts a certain way, then something will happen, otherwise not.

##### Something about the documents section:

The documentations section says:

> There is only one peice of documentation for this level.  
> The first peice of documentation is a very old document that states only this  
> "This level has a dark side its allure to save yourself is overwhelming the level seems to make you selfish and not want to help others DO NOT ENTER"

First, you spelled piece wrong, not a huge thing, but something that I noticed.  
Second, I feel like if there is only one piece of documentation, there would be a problem with how they know about the whole thing.

I like how the Level tries to test the wanderer on their morality though! That's a really interesting idea for your Level.

##### Bases, Outposts, and Communities help:

You wrote:

> There is only one town on this level. This town is very populated with free food, why the food is free, no one knows the only thing that is 100% known is that there is only one exit.

This sentence just seems a bit… Awkward.

I feel like you could fix it into something like this:

> There is only one town on this Level. After seeing a large truck, the town can be visited. The town has a considerable population, and it has free food. However, it is unknown why the food is free… (Some more information on the town)

##### Something about the discovery:

You wrote the discovery part like this:

> The Level was discovered by an unknown person they wore a mask and never showed their identity  
> this person only said that no one should go into this level.

A Level being discovered by an unknown person is cool and interesting! However, I think that having one mysterious person say something about a Level and disappearing isn't the 'true' discovery of a Level; it is just what inspired other wanderers to search for a Level.

How about something like this:

> This Level was first discovered after searching for a presumed Level which an unknown masked person mentioned. They said that no-one should enter 'This Level'.

##### Help with the Entrances and Exits:

You wrote this:

> Entrances  
> While Wandering around Level 0 One might find a hallway that is very long and does not change. After 15 minutes of walking through this hallway they will enter Level ###
>
> Exits  
> There are two exits to this level the first is to be nice and help the people who are in trouble during the level after this is done at the end they will be rested and then let out of the level with a choice of what level to got to from 1 to 100  
> The second is to not be nice and then kill the 10 entities who will be hunting you which will reveal a door to Level 40

As I said it before, I think that the Level having two 'endings' is a really cool idea. I'm surprised that nobody has thought of it before! (Not that I know of at least lol;;) It just seems a bit uncomfortable, and I think you could split this into several sentences.  
Maybe you could smoothen it out like this:

> While wandering around Level 0, one might find a hallway that is very long and does not change. After 15 minutes of walking around in this hallway, they will find themselves in Level ###.
>
> There are two exits to this Level: The 'kind' ending and the 'mean' ending.  
> If you are kind to everyone and help people, you will be rewarded with transportation to a Level of your choice between 1 and 100.  
> However, if you are unkind to everyone and kill 10 entities, a door to Level 40 will appear.

Wording it like this makes it seem more mysterious, and makes it a bit easier to read.

---

That's it!

I think if you apply these things to your Level, it will become a much better piece. Your idea is **really** cool!

As I said before, I'm not a greenlighter, so after you fix these things up, why don't you PM a greenlighter to get more critique and possibly a greenlight?
