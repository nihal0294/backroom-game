Enchanted Weapons

31 Mar 2022 22:11

**OBJECT NUMBER**: 12  
**DESCRIPTION**: enchanted weapons are seemingly normal weapons from the medieval era and typically are extremely rare, having no known pattern in which they spawn and have unpredictable properties. These properties always have at least a minor downside, but are not guaranteed to have an ability of some kind that is generally considered to be "usefull". A list of object 12 instances is present below.

**SPAWNING**: enchanted weapons have no known pattern of spawn, and are extremely rare. However known locations that such items have spawned are levels 5, 8, 12, 24, and 31. The correlation between spawn location, exact item, and/or properties is entirely speculative.

**LIST OF ENCHANTED WEAPONS**:

#12-1 a bow capable of firing any items nearby at a target with perfect accuracy without touching the fired item. This is observed as the item simply disappearing from its original location and reappearingloaded into the bow. The effective range appearsto be about 15 meters. Known to cause severe bruising to the user's arms and fingers to suddenly appear at random.

#12-2 a dagger capable of "telepathically" cutting anything withing direct line of sight of the user. Simply slashing in an area in front of the user and within line of sight will cause the slashing of the blade to seemingly move forward without the blade itself actuallygoing anywhere. Known to cause random pains during usage and occasionally open cuts.

#12-3a crossbow capable of creating a duplicate of any item being fired and firing this duplicated item instead. the downside is not known, but suspected to be spontaneous combustion of random object around the user.

#12-4 a shield capable if creating invisible barriers. There is no known limit to this effect and elaborate invisible mazes have been achieved. Know to create invisible walls in the user's path on its own on rare occasions.

#12-5 this item cannot be spoken about, as any verbal or written description of the object causes the reader/listener to undergo gruesome, painfull, and rapid mutations, often resulting in the death of the subject. Even viewing the object will cause this effect. However the reason it cannot be described is not affected, and proven safe to be placed in written text.

#12-6 a sword capable of "cutting into" other desired areas, including what appears to be a new floor only accessible by means of the sword. This takes the form of an area In which the sword slashed to appear as if it were "ripped open". The resulting portal can be expanded by pulling on the edges. the portal generally leads to the location the user was thinking about at the time, and slashed across the portal horizontally causes the portal to close. The only known downside that exists seems to be that the portal does not always go where the user intends, and instead leads into a somewhat risky area, but never in immediate or direct harm.

#12-7 a katana capable of causing a "withering" effect on anyone or anything it makes contact with. As long as the victim has some form of biological organ, the organs it possesses will shrink and turn black. The pupils will dilate and twitch constantly and the victim will experience extreme pain. The only known cure is activated charcoal. The downside being that for every 4 individuals killed in this manner, the next attack will affect both the user and the target. This resets after both individuals die. If one survives due to being cured with activated charcoal, the other dies instantly if not already dead. Object 12-7 is also compulsive, causing anyone seeing it in any way, via direct eye contact, recordings, or photos, to instinctively kill anyone they can in order to get object 12-7, and then using it kill as many people as they can. These individuals are called "dark katana minions" by Dr. James Quin. Object 12-7 is locked in a bullet proof, 8 digit code locked metal box, the code being random. A reality freshener is placed in direct contact with object 12-7 inside the box to prevent anyone using a deuclidator to retrieved it.

**DISCOVERY**: the enchanted weapons were discovered when a M.E.G. researcher named James Quin came across object 12-1 in level 5. Since then, more enchanted weapons were discovered. After the 5th instance of object 12 was discovered, the objects were collectively designated object 12.  
James is currently leading team "Enchanted Knights" to find and recover all instances of object 12 and keep the especially dangerous object 12 instances from getting into the wrong hands.
