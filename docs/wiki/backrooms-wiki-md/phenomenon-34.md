Christmas, also known as Yule, Crimbo, Noel, and Xmas, is a hybrid secular/religious winter festival celebrating the birth of the religious figure Jesus of Nazareth. It incorporates many different customs from many traditions, effectively combining most winter celebrations into one contiguous holiday. Due to the major upheavals in routine and major events that happen during this holiday, it presents both opportunities and challenges for espionage.

## Gatherings

During the Christmas season, people enjoy coming together for various reasons, from culinary delights to family traditions.  
(Add more of this later)

## Christmas Tree

A Christmas Tree is a (usually evergreen) tree or artificial substitute that has been decorated with festive decorations such as Christmas baubles, tinsel, or string lights. Due to the variety and number of such decorations, it is easy to bug such trees with listening devices (See file bugs.odf for more information). It is also possible to trap Christmas trees with explosives, though the often central location of these trees may cause unnecessary collateral deaths if not used with caution.  
IMPORTANT: Ensure you check a tree is actually a tree and not an instance of [Entity 56](entity-56.md) before adding any devices.

## Christmas Food and Drink

Several types of food and drink are almost exclusive to the Christmas season, such as mince pies, Christmas cake, and Christmas pudding. Several of these special foods can cause difficulties for an agent, but can also be used to their advantage. Mulled Wine (a cooked wine or grape juice with added spices) can be served both hot and cold, so if an agent is planning to poison it, they must bring either poisons that work in both hot and cold drinks, or bring two different types. Brandy is used to set alight a Christmas pudding when it is served, bringing about a good opportunity to replace it with something more flammable for a good accidental explosion.
