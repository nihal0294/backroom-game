Level 826 - The Vents

12 Aug 2020 22:22

**Page Type:** Level

**Elevator Pitch:** A ventilation system that sometimes has powerful fans or leads to various levels/rooms. The layout of the vents is very erratic and sometimes doesn't make sense. The material of the vents will differ between different places within the level, even changing to things that aren't usually found in vents.

Materials found in vents:

- Iron
- Lead (Despite being lead, it will not poison the air.)
- Plastic
- Solid Mercury (The mercury is at room temperature but is solid anyway.)
- Solid Air
- Holes (The holes will either lead to other vents or other levels.)
- Rubber

You can enter this level by finding a vent in another level, unless the vent leads to somewhere else as specified in the level's article.

You can exit this level by finding any level, room, or door.

**Central Narrative:** none

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** um?

**Additional Notes:** This level is based on a little bit of text from [Level 807](level-807.md). I want to add more to this level but I can't think of anything right now.
