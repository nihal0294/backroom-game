I… I'm flattered. If someone were to tell me, 14 months ago, that I'd have someone who writes published books and articles for various magazines praise my works, I'd probably dismiss it as impossible… yet here we are.

I have to confess that I have snooped around your sandbox a bit, Mr. Goerman, even though that's a tad rude, or so I think, at least, but regardless of that, I was not disappointed.

Clinical tone is the standard around these parts, something we inherited from the SCP Wiki's influence, and something I find… boring. I'm a college student. I'm in a biomedical sciences course. My studies involve a fair amount of clinical tone reading and writing, and perhaps due to that, I don't find it fun to try my hand at it.

I was very pleasantly surprised to see something that read like the back cover of a book, or flavour text in a boardgame manual, and I'm eager to see where that draft ends up!

I don't know what are your expectations or plans for your presence on this site and community, but if given the chance, it would be my pleasure to work with you, and I'm sure I'm not the only one of this opinion.

If you have any questions regarding my works, this site's lore and function, or anything else that might come up, don't hesitate to reach out. In fact, the simple prospect of a chat with a more experienced writer is quite enticing by itself to me!
