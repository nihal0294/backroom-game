Gaseous Assassin

02 Feb 2024 02:32

**Page Type**: Entity

**Genre:**  
Mystery, perhaps? (I'm still unsure to what extent I want to explain why the entity does what it does, or leave readers guessing).

**Core Concept:**  
A humanoid entity made completely out of black gas, with glowing purple eyes. It has glowing purple daggers that it can use to attack, and can make these appear or disappear at will. It attacks humans, as well as other entities with obvious abilities that defy the laws of reality (eg. Dullers, Partygoers) in order to drain some sort of reality-bending "energy" from them. The Gaseous Assassin must consume this energy to maintain its physical form and avoid fading out of existence, but feels sorry for its victims. When not attacking, it lurks in dark areas like a Smiler, but the two entities usually can't be found in the same hiding spot.

**Detailed explanation of the reality-bending "energy":**  
The Backrooms is filled with a mysterious energy that allows for things that defy the laws of reality. This energy "builds up" on humans and other entities over time, allowing for reality-breaking behaviour such as noclipping. Most entities get more of this energy over time, but the Gaseous Assassin instead loses it, since it consumes so much holding its gaseous body together in a humanoid form. Hence its need to drain the energy from other entities.

Great, I will make the sandbox for this entity as soon as I have the time!

To answer your questions:

- If the entity steals energy from a wanderer, they will be unable to noclip, leave the level they are currently on, or otherwise break the laws of reality for a period of time. They will also appear "more real" to those around them and witnesses will have photographic memories of them.
- The entity will not attack someone whose energy has already been drained. (I think this is what you meant with your second question?) Additionally, it will prefer getting its energy from other "less sentinent" entities rather than humans, if possible.
- The energy basically gives each entity their unique reality-breaking powers, such as the Dullers' ability to noclip, the Partygoers' ability to turn humans into Partygoers on touch, or Facelings' ability to… stay alive despite their missing organs I guess? These entities will also lose their powers in the event of a Gaseous Assassin attack.
- The majority of human (or entity) victims of the Gaseous Assassin are killed during the attack, but some survive. Survivors will be able to recover the energy, but it takes a few days.
