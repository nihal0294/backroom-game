A Level Idea

09 Sep 2022 13:08

Page Type: Level

Genre (Optional): Horror

Core Concept: The level is primarily an industrial setting in a state of disrepair, with damaged walls and failing lights. The level can be accessed seemingly randomly whenever a key is used to open a door. Once there, the wanderer must find keys and put them into the doors that they find. The level has no hostile entities. Several documented key types have been found and there seem to be rules about the keys. For example, the seventh key you use will never kill you and using the same key too many times will cause you to lose a limb. You need to use the keys to move around, because otherwise you’d starve. Places the keys have taken you: other levels, but this is very rare, a very large space which has been turned into a base, however it suffers from power outages (this base has a stockpile of proven "safe" keys, something horrific that has caused all survivors to attempt to cut off their ears and stab out their eyes, a setting of the same level populated with people working in a factory and making keys ( implied to be the past of the level) ,and an unknown space that causes cuts you up and causes pieces of you to appear randomly on the level ( or other levels) along with keys next to you, implying that the keys are made of the bones of Wanderers. Additionally, people when asleep there and even when they leave, continuously dream of keys and using them. Keys are interspersed randomly along the level btw along with doors.

Additional Notes: This is my first idea, so tell me what you think! I’m also not sure what danger level it is.

so…the issues I see with this is that it sort of lacks many significant horror elements and the ones it does have honestly feel like an afterthought.

mainly, just having a level be dangerous isn't scary. Using the same key a bunch and losing a limb doesn't really inspire much fear and probably will not do much within the article.

The elements I see as possibly being more scary are the parts of wanderers cutting off ears and eyes and such. The issue is it feels like horror for the sake of horror. It lacks an explanation given in this idea summary so it just feels random and more like an afterthought. Doing horror relies heavily on how you execute it and it needs proper build up and payoff. Just mentioning these things in the description or something will not be scary. Hell, even the idea of keys being made of wanderers also lacks explanation and context that would make it feel scary. It just feels more random then actually fear inspiring. Like, wouldn't it be scary if it was something like

# **CW CW CW GOREY**

Through some (reasonably written, not just sanity) effect in the level, wanderers who spend enough time in it will become obsessed with keys to the point where they

# One last CW

Attempt to cut out their own skin and bone to create more keys for this level.

That could, if written correctly, be a revelation which could either be used to close out or lead readers further into the article which may work towards some body horror stuff.
