That may not be that interesting, unless there's a plot/lore reason for it that fits. I think new and original ideas are a safer bet for something people will like. Not that you can't build off an older idea or transform something old into something new. It's just harder to make something good when you limit yourself to only mixing old ideas together. Of course, good writing could sell concepts that aren't incredible on their own, so you could probably make something good out of it. You just need to know what you're doing.

Those are my thoughts on that.
