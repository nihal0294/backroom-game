So, some words about this page.

I dislike the usage of the clinical tone in this fictional universe for the most part. Few authors have been able to pull it off successfully, and it is often associated with our legacy as a "SCP rip-off". Almost every time I have seen it used, it seemed to me that the authors had no clue of what they were doing, and were simply mindlessly copying what the others had done.

So, with that in mind, I wrote a page using the clinical tone. Wanted to see if *I* could do it. Hope you people enjoyed this page.

I won't go too deep in the themes of the page, I think you guys can figure it out by yourselves :)
