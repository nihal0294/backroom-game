Level 79: Deja Vu

15 Jul 2020 05:52

Page Type: Level

Elevator Pitch: Level 79 is a massive, continuously expanding (yet finite) expanse of lobbies, hallways, waiting rooms and other places, constantly shifting and rearranging itself. Every visitor contributes to the level's size, as their own memories are thrown in to the mix. The memories chosen are generally very vague- dreams that people have gradually come to believe, memories from childhood that have been almost completely forgotten, etc. This contributes to an extreme sense of deja vu, as you not only *think* you've been there, there's a good chance you have.  
![50d7efd9e634da148290c9ef76f5632c99ddac0a.jpg](https://66.media.tumblr.com/16ce835623ce9ef9059cc2b1a85e2c9d/0ba400b24c55ab90-01/s640x960/50d7efd9e634da148290c9ef76f5632c99ddac0a.jpg)  
A photo of Level 79

Hook: The constantly shifting and expanding nature makes Level 79 extremely difficult to map out or explore. As a result, little is truly known about the level. While no entity sightings have been confirmed, nor has anyone ever been seen/heard dying, several deaths have been documented- most often violently. Parties setting out to explore the level often split apart gradually, with members getting lost. When the rest of the party finds them, they are often mangled or violently ripped apart, always in a similar manner. For this reason, survivors often say that there is an entity presence- while this entity (or several) has varying names, most refer to it as "Bing Bong", in a dark reference to the forgotten monster from Pixar's *Inside Out*. No documented person has ever seen the entity and survived.

Do's/Don'ts:  
Do:  
Travel with a partner or group.  
Stay away from dark areas.  
Don't:  
Split the party.  
Travel alone.  
Lose your light source, or travel in dark areas.

Entrances and Exits:  
Entrances:  
While there have been several reports of doors from Level 18 leading to Level 79 and then vanishing, as well as less common reports of entering from other Levels, most people find themselves here after cases of Deja Vu and losing focus, especially when turning in halls or going through doors.  
Exits:  
To exit, simply open doors or turn around in halls, or stop focusing and daydream of other levels. While rare, these methods will somewhat unreliably lead you to other levels as suddenly as you arrived, often placing you in Level 0, Level 18 or other levels semi-randomly. If attempting to daydream out of the level, you'll usually end up in the level you're daydreaming about.
