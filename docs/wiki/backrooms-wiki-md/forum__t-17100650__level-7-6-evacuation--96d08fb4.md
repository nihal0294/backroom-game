Quick explanation: (please do not open this if you want to think about it for a bit longer)

[+ show block](javascript:;)

[– hide block](javascript:;)

Firstly, there is no ceiling, just a vast opening. Some books are just scattered all over the place and they represent the struggle of the writer: their works are never enough, and the worst is to be thrown away, forgotten if necessary. The metaphor behind the poem is the magnum opus of the writer, their final work, the proof of their courage (symbolised by the red dye, but that's not really important) to press forward and give their all. It is the work they will be known for in the future, for the ship, at the end, may as well have sunk to reflect this, but we'll never know.

If you still think this is very confusing, that's a-okay. I haven't written in clinical tone in like, forever, so this page is definitely not the best thing I have created. Then again, the interpretation was the point of the experiment, and it didn't have to succeed, strictly speaking. Thanks for the comment! Let me know if you change your mind, or not.
