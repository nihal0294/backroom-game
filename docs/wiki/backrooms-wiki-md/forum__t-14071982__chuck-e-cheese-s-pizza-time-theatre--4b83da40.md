Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza Time Theatre

23 Apr 2021 20:03

**Page Type:** Level

**Elevator Pitch:** The first Chuck E. Cheese location has noclipped in the backrooms after the third location was opened.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** Nothing at all ¯\\_(ツ)\_/¯

Class: 0

Description: Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza Time or for short "PTT" is the n+1th level of the backrooms. if wanna explore this level you will need a flashlight because the generator has died a long time ago. The level consists of one dining area, kitchen, and two bathrooms. The animatronics here are inside portraits as seen in some early footages.

Discovery: PTT was discovered when one M.E.G agent found a 70s poster of PTT Which teleported him here.

Entrances and exits:

Entrance: Find a 70s poster of PTT and if you look at it for 10 secojds you'll enter here.

Exit: To exit just simply open and enter the exit door.
