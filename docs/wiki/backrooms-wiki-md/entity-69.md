# Entity 69 - "Forbidden Signs"

# **M.E.G. Archive**

# Description:

---

![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/local--files/entity-69/1)

Photo attached to a report of signs in unkown location.

Signs are prominent and mysterious entities reported by various accounts with consistent details. At first glance, they appear to be standard traffic signs found on all Frontrooms streets. However, when you take a closer look, you will notice that they have unusual, quite anomalous illustrations, and it is ill-advised to seek meaning in them. We could describe them as aggressive, but not in the way we understand that term.

Any prolonged contemplation of them or the images they depict can drive even the mentally strong crazy. One way to describe it is as a sort of mind trap, also referred to as cognitive biases or negative thoughts.

Mind traps are automatic, faulty thinking patterns that influence our emotions, behaviors , and decisions. They include tendencies to jump to conclusions, make extreme judgments , and respond to situations inappropriately.

# Behaviour:

  
Using potentially forced no-clipping, instances of entity 69 can appear practically anywhere. No ground or anomaly can prevent them from arising under any circumstances, their unusual way of moving working to their advantage. They usually appear in places close to people, attempting to attract the attention of unwary wanderers. Every straight road, empty room, or base can be used as their hunting grounds. After attracting the wanderer's attention, they begin to haunt them throughout their journey.

Entity 69 instances typically stick together in large groups. They often block entire passages on levels with no air, although many known cases exist where they reveal themselves completely alone. It is difficult to determine whether they are genuinely social creatures or possess cooperative thinking.

### NOTE

The intriguing thing about the signs is that they require water to survive, much like humans do. It is readily absorbed by their bodies. They have frequently been observed absorbing moisture from soils, sometimes producing the gurgling sound a straw makes when a glass is almost empty.

---

As previously mentioned, signs always contain pictures and inscriptions that make no sense. They often display questions that directly challenge wanderers' existence or confuse them in unpleasant ways. The recipient's brain is subjected to a never-ending barrage of questions and problems to solve, which often results in them being confused. # Biology:

---

Entity 69 is not a living creature by our understanding of biology. It is self-aware despite lacking any biological processes or functions that would typically be regarded as living. Whether or not they feel pain and emotion is a matter of debate.

## Survival Guide

---

If you notice unnatural-looking signs in the backrooms, you should try not to pay attention to them under any circumstances. Run away from them as soon as you can if you see that they are beginning to pursue you. If fleeing is not an option, several methods can be used to dispatch Entity 69, as it possesses little to no ability to defend itself.

If you already fell under its influence, to escape Entity 69's mind traps, focus on bringing yourself back to the present moment and challenging the overwhelming thought patterns you are experiencing. This can be accomplished through mindfulness practices like meditation or focused breathing, along with acknowledging and reframing thoughts.

Any qualities or traits discovered that are not currently listed should be reported immediately and summarized.
