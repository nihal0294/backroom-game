Through a pit of cold, lifeless earth  
With an empty sky devoid of light,  
A purpose is demanded from an  
Uncaring and selfish nihility.  
Carved with an agony unbefitting  
Of a nonexistantant,  
Of breath not given, but forced  
By the onde of earth untempered,  
A twisted visage crawls anoxic.  
Misshapen hands claw at a barren face;  
A sculptor blind to its creations.  
Within a pit and below that empty sky,  
It becomes the wrath of a child of no god.
