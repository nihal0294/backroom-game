**Entity Number:** 509

### Habitat(s): [Level 71](level-71.md)

VØıÐ does not have a specific location, rather he is in Level 71's entirety.

### Description:

VØıÐ is an inhuman organism composed of what bases believe to be a previously unfound substance called "Void", which is found in deeper parts of Level 71. Bases have seen this creature numerous times but had not interacted closely with it or found any signs of more creatures of this species until —, when a member of one of the bases found a VØıÐ corpse in a hallway.

### Behaviors:

VØıÐ wanders around the area of Level 71, and can apparently sense humans within 10 feet. Upon detecting a nearby human, it will scream and pursue the human until said human is dead or out of range. Survivors of encounters with VØıÐ have been reported to act as though their soul was removed from the body.

### Biology:

VØıÐ takes the shape of a deformed cross of a dog and a human. It is entirely black with lidless, big white eyes. It has 4 horns on its head.

### Discovery:

On ——, a base member named Tom found a diary in Level 71. The diary was identified by its pages as property of a previously missing Level 4 TBD member named Jack Darrison. Tom found that numerous pages were missing from the diary. Eventually, other base members claimed that Tom was slowly going insane as he read the diary, eventually ripping out numerous pages and losing all sanity. He murdered 68 other base members with a knife before stabbing himself. The ripped pages were recovered and hung up. One of the pages described the first encounter with the VØıÐ.
