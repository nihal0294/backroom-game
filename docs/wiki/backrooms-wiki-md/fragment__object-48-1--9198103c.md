## Description

**Liquid Pain** is an acidic substance with a translucent, red hue. It is extremely dangerous to wanderers due to its fatal effects, and it is advised to avoid this substance as much as possible. One should only handle Liquid Pain while under the supervision of an M.E.G. operative and while utilizing the proper equipment.

**Liquid Pain** is a variation of [Almond Water](object-1.md). It is the product of a chemical reaction, resulting in the hydrogen cyanide reacting with the potassium chloride and creating hydrochloric acid and potassium cyanide. Both of these compounds are fatal if consumed. This reaction occurs completely at random and has been attributed to [Liminal Echo](phenomenon-7.md).

Due to **Liquid Pain** being a variation of Almond Water, it has a very similar smell[1](javascript:;) and density to that of its parent, with the main notable differences being its appearance and effects. Unlike Almond Water, however, Liquid Pain has a slight red hue. This is currently unexplained, but the leading theory is that this is the result of Liminal Echo’s counter-phenomenon, [Autocorrect](phenomenon-33.md). Liquid Pain is extremely rare in comparison to other objects, but can be found on any level at any time, in no particular pattern.

It is worth noting that **Liquid Pain** bears a lot of similarities to that of [Cashew Water](object-44.md), however, the two objects are largely unrelated. While Liquid Pain is a variation of Almond Water, Cashew Water is a completely separate substance that happens to share most sensory observations with Almond Water.

## Properties

While the specific effects of ingesting Liquid Pain remain unknown, the information we've been able to collect suggests that Liquid Pain kills approximately 3 hours after consumption. During this time, one may experience the following symptoms:

- Heartburn
- Difficulty breathing
- Soreness/dryness of the oral cavity
- Bloody coughing and/or vomiting
- Severe chest, stomach, and abdominal pain
- Fatigue

These symptoms are caused by the increase in concentration of hydrochloric acid in the stomach, and will eventually lead to the corrosion of tissue in the digestive track, introducing the potassium cyanide into one's bloodstream. Once this occurs, the wanderer in question will expire within minutes. Wounds may also open in the abdominal area from the hydrochloric acid during this. Because of these effects, it is imperative for one to expel Liquid Pain from one's stomach as soon as possible.

## Do’s and Don’ts

### Do:

- Treat any wounds from **Liquid Pain** as soon as possible
- Immediately report where the substance was found to the [M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md)
- Label all found bottles of **Liquid Pain**

### Don’t:

- Make physical contact with **Liquid Pain**
- Drink **Liquid Pain** or any [Almond Water](object-1.md) with a red tint
- Handle **Liquid Pain** without supervision
