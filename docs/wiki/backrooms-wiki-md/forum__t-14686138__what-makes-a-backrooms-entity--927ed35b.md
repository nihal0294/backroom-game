06 May 2022 13:36

I've been trying to brainstorm good entity ideas for the Backrooms, but I've been having trouble coming up with concepts that feel at home in the setting. So I guess my question is, in your opinion, what exactly makes a backrooms entity, and what separates it from other things like SCP? Are there certain parameters to be abided by, do they have to fall into certain genres, or does it really matter?

If you are making a backrooms entity as long as it is along the lines of… anything. There are entities that are just coconuts, to entities that are the size of houses and unlike anything ever seen. Check around a few entity pages, and I'm sure you will find some inspiration. The entity I am working on rn is a whole freaking level. As long as you don't blatantly steal someone else's idea, anything will work. Though make it feel at home in a certain level.
