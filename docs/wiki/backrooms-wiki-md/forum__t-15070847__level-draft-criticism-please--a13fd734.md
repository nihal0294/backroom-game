Re: Level draft needs criticism

05 Aug 2022 03:31

> The objects usually consist of:
>
> - Recliners
> - Chairs
> - Tables
> - Beds
> - Couches

There is no need to put those items in bullet points.

> …in this level.  
> It is possible to find…

I don't think you have to make a new line for that.

> This entity is Entity-333, Also known as "The Fire Salter"

Please go into detail about this entity (and make a page about it). Also, you forgot a full stop at the end.

> Noclip into a lava river in Level 42

Level 42 is meant to be really difficult to escape, so the fact that no-clipping is an easy exit from it ruins the fun of exiting the hard way.

Additional suggestions:

- The page is pretty short, so I think you should add something like a phenomena that happens on the level (make sure to go into detail) and a 'Bases, Outposts, and Communities' tab. However, if your level doesn't have any, just write that there aren't any.
- If you want to change your survival difficulty to say 'Minimal Entity Count' or something like that, replace the code of the SD with this:

> [[include :backrooms-wiki:component:level-class  
> |class=3  
> |three=Minimal Entity Count  
> ]]

- Add an author/image licensing collapsible. You can find how to do this at [this link](templates.md).
- Be careful what image you use, it may not be allowed. I used Unsplash for my images on my sandbox pages and to add proper crediting in the author/image licensing collapsible, I wrote this:

> *[link] Image by (creator of image) is licensed under the Unsplash license.*

Overall, the concept is pretty good, but once again, you should really expand on the level. There's a ton of potential for any kind of level.
