14 Jun 2025 08:16

Your writing style is solid, but I think the idea still needs more development. From the sentence “The primary objective of Level 89 is to traverse the entire walkable path…”, what I gather is that the level might possess some form of sentient consciousness and wants those who enter to complete it—but at the same time, it actively prevents them from doing so. Or perhaps something else entirely. Still, this seems to be just a speculative theory and probably doesn’t hold much weight.

There doesn’t seem to be a core message or theme that the level is trying to convey, so there’s not much I can critique in that regard—but content-wise, I think it feels underdeveloped. You mention Entity NA051 and Entity NA010, but you don’t expand on them at all. The level feels strong in concept but under-thought in execution. The core idea of “a path that can’t be completed” is solid, and the fact that the M.E.G. doesn’t understand why people end up here also makes sense—but that’s pretty much it. In the end, there’s nothing tangible to hold onto.

In a level like this, if there isn't something inherently interesting about the level itself, it's reasonable to compensate for that with a strong story. Add a story so that the reader becomes more interest in the narrative behind the level than in the level itself. Level should be read to understand the story, not the story should be reat to understand level.
