Interesting suggestion about level difficulty classes

13 May 2022 07:20

I find the difficulty classes very fascinating, especially the unique and special ones. I would love a way to see every level with that difficulty class. I suggest a feature where you can click on the difficulty class of any level and it would bring you to a page that lists every level with that difficulty class. For example, I am on the page of level 9, and I click on the difficulty class logo (Class 5) and it brings me to a page that lists every level that is class 5 difficulty. With this, you can maybe also access the lists of levels with each difficulty class from the home page.

I think this would be really cool, what do you guys think?

Assuming a way can be found to do this, it would be a nice quality of life addition. It could be used to find some of the more mysterious levels easily (ones with undetermined difficulty class), dangerous ones, and so on.

As for the actual execution of this… I'm not sure how that would work. You could probably make a wiki page with a list of all levels given a certain classification, but you'd have to add text with a link to there on every single level page. Is it possible? Absolutely. Is it practical? Probably not.

If you can make the page and convince every level author to add such text, then that's nice, but that would be an incredibly difficult and tedious undertaking. Not to mention, there's no guarantee you can get in contact with every author or get permission.
