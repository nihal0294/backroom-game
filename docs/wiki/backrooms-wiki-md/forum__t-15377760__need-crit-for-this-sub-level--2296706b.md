ok, going line by line

clarity: "The space is oftentimes largely unfurnished and mostly empty, with only acute amounts of tables, computers, and occasional litter being scattered around." saying "acute" can confuse the average reader. Speaking in clinical tone does not mean fill the level with unneeded words.

shouldnt there be comma\/  
small one: "Any electrical device found within this sub-level is often inactive or in a state of disrepair."

another unneeded word: "Due to a lack of power or active light sources, the level is vastly deprived of any form of illumination." Vastly Deprived. I dont know why but this just really urked me more than other mistakes.

i cant really help because this confuses me: "The odd phenomenon present within Level 4.5 causes any light source equipped produce an exceedingly absurd amount of light, to the point of boiling wanderers or entirely blinding them, if exposed to any light extruding from the windows." This grammer is making my head spin. here is a revised version: "A phenomenon present within Level 4.5 causes any source of light to produce an abnormal amount of light, to the point where it will boil wanderers, if exposed to light from windows.

thats about it that i have seen so far, so besides that it is just a bit short
