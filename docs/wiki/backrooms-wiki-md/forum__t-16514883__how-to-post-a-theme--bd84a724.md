Slight correction, I said that I can only greenlight written drafts as far as I know. but anyways…

You can find members of the technical team by going to the "Meet the Staff" page, pressing "ctrl" and "f" on your keyboard, and then typing "technical team". Go through until you find a staff member you feel you want to reach out through/can reach out through via pm or Discord, and contact them about your theme.
