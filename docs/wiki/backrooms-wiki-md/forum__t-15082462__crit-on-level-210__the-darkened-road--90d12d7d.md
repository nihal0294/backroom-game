Re: Crit on Level 210: The Darkened Road

14 Aug 2022 22:04

Ask and you shall receive, UserUnhacked. Keep in mind I am a GLT, so my crit will not be perfect. You have creative liberty over this page.

# SPaG:

> one can see certain features1 on some grass,

The footnote is useless, just describe the features in the sentence.

> Most features other than the road are completely non-collidable and attempting to walk on the grass will make you fall into a massive pit.

It will make you fall into a massive pit, you say? I would like it if you could describe the pit. Is it infinite or finite? Is it just a black void? Or is it more like a pitfall trap?

> with most having an IETS rating of 4C or more.

Eh… having "more" of an IETS rating sounds odd. This could be reworded to: with the majority of entities found in the pit having an IETS rating of 4C or higher. Also note that not all entities have IETS scores on them, so while you are welcome to use this idea, keep in mind that not everyone uses IETS on their entities.

> A photograph of Level 210. Notice the streetlights and the grass, they are actually fake.

I get the feeling that you are talking to me, and you also kind of slipped up with your clinical tone. Try "A photograph of Level 210 and its non-collidable features."

> As far as the Pit goes, entities like The Conciousness Disease, Porters, Phobic Centipedes, Party Hosts, Scream Eaters, Athenian Ducks, Bone Thieves, and even copies of Entity 987 and Entity 297 have been discovered.

"As far as the Pit goes" sounds like I'm talking with a buddy. You broke clinical tone again. You can replace it with "Inside of the Pit"

> Living cars also have psychokinetic abilities, which means they can move things with their 'minds', forcibly kill entities, and even fly. However, they only usually use these when they really need to.

"which means" -> "meaning that". "Really" is a word you *never* want to use in writing with a clinical tone. Change to "when they are required to".

> varying in gender, race, languages, and much more.

"languages, and much more." -> "native language, and other characteristics."

> has given them a really good reputation

Again, get rid of "really".

> injuries are not that dangerous.

Normally, injuries aren't described as dangerous, as something dangerous is something that can harm you, not the result of the harm. Try "serious" instead of "dangerous".

> These entities have adapted to living cars

Capitalize "living cars", as you did so earlier.

> Wanderers that pass by the Star without looking at it will not fall victim to its attacks.

Why is this sentence floating? You can add it to the end of the last paragraph.

> by using neurological abilities4

As of right now, Partygoers do not exist, so remove the footnote and the Partygoer link.

> Graveyards also have an aura of sadness to them, which affects all wanderers and entities entering them.

One: The floating sentence looks ugly, so put it at the end of the last paragraph, and two: what the heck is an aura of sadness? I don't know what that means, but it could be a me-thing. You may want to describe it in case readers are confused, as I am. Just my opinion, and I could be wrong.

> the path you're standing on

C'mon, you broke clinical tone again! Never use "you" on a clinical tone-based page.

> fall into the ditch

Is this ditch the same thing as the Pit? If so, just call it the Pit, as a ditch is not the same thing entirely.

> Although the lasers are deadly, it is pretty easy to run away from the location of the anomaly.

Remove "pretty", as it breaks the clinical tone.

> When triggered, Level 210's bottom will start rising rapidly

You need to mention that the Pit *does* have a bottom earlier in article when you introduced the Pit.

> If you hear shaking and vibration not coming from the road, multiple hostile entities will attack you; please be wary.

To be honest, I do not know what you are telling me. Please rewrite this sentence.

> The 'Escape' Phenomenon

Uncapitalize "Phenomenon".

> "WARNING: ESCAPE THE LEVEL NOW"

Uncapitalize this sentence. It is kind of cringe, and capitalizing this will not emphasize the volume. If you want to emphasize volume, describe it or add an exclamation point.

> The Travelers are the only known group in Level 210. They have 36 members in the level who:  
> Explore the level  
> Document new phenomena and entities  
> Look for levels connected to the level  
> They also have 11 other members who provide food and water to the main members of the group.

This section was formatted very strangely. I can't really put my finger on how to fix it, but I think removing the bullet points would be a good idea. Having the 11 members who collect food bit could also be brought up into one paragraph with everything else.

> Level 210 can be accessed by:  
> No-clipping into the road of Level 9 or Level 166  
> Touching a streetlight in Level 45  
> Traveling 1 mile on the asphalt road of Level 150

You didn't add any periods to your entrances.

> All of your links on the page are linked incorrectly. As Natedagreat told me when I made the same mistake, the 2 bracket format cannot be used to link pages. If you wanted to link Level 0, you would put: [[[level-0|Level 0]]]. The text after the bar is the text that would be showing. When implemented, the links will not send you to the correct pages, but the links will work on the main site, once the page is out of sandbox.

# Conceptual Crit:

This was actually better than I had initially expected. The main description issue is that its just boring. It a dark road. Cool. Oh and there's a pit. Nice. Maybe add some more special areas to spice it up, or change the landscape. The main question is *what is the pit?* You really didn't go into much description here. The info I have gathered is that it has a floor, there are dangerous entities, and that its hot. Done. It also seems you added it because it was convenient, and not for any real storyline purpose. Why is the pit there? Is it natural or manmade? What is it's purpose?

> Haven't they connected the dots already? They say you can enter Level 601 from this level, and they also say there's these arms that-

> SHUT THE HELL UP NUMBSKULL

This is the part I'm not so sure of. Yet again, someone altered the original text. Intruiging. I do not see any connection or reason why this was done, and personally, I think it should be removed entirely. It doesn't add anything except a filling for the page to be longer. Now I am aware that you are making 2 levels I belive that tie into this level's storyline. Cool, but if you are going to make it seem someone or 2 someones edited the page, I need to see a purpose for it on the first released page.

Finally, the Living Cars. These thing are legitimately so freaking confusing. So if you get in, it drives you somewhere really fast, but if you are in the driver's seat, you get transported to another level, as well as if you pull the chain. All you did was add a convenient exit. I see no purpose of traveling at insane speeds to who knows where on an already dangerous level rather than just leave it. Ther base concept is somewhat flawed, as well. Also, I know that you did mention they come in a variety of different looks, but maybe a base understanding would help. Are they all modern, or can some be older? Do the areodynamics of the vehicle affect it's speed?

I wouldn't send this into a GL quite yet. Give it a week more of work, maybe sooner, and I think it could be considered, after all of the GL's crit, of course. Implement my recommendations, specifically SPaG, and this could go somewhere. Happy writing!
