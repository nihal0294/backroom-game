Hi there MochiCat! I would be glad to review your entity!

So, there were a few obvious things I could see in the draft. First of all, your linked level needs correcting because the link just sends you to an empty sandbox. Second, your clinical tone could use a bit of work, because this level seemed to present to me as if I was your best friend you were talking to, so try to make it a bit more "serious", but it is good for the most part.

Next, the actual content of the article: I think you did a good job of basing these off actual teru teru bozus, but I am not a fan of the fact that they are identical to the real thing, and that you could have changed the name around, other than just calling them, yknow, what they are. I push you to get creative with that, and hope to see some "intriguing" name next time you update it. Also, it would be nice if you added a photo, because I had to look them up. As for the anomalous traits about the entity, they seemed pretty cool. Basing them off of the real thing benefitted greatly in this case, with rain and storm affects being the main attack. I don't quite understand what you meant with "drowning with rain", so you could explain that in greater detail. I like the idea of a seemingly cute and harmless being but in reality, its gonna "put you out" pun intended.
