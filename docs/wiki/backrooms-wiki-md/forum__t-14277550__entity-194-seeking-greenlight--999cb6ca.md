Entity 194 - Seeking Greenlight

11 Nov 2021 20:58

**Page Type:** Entity

**Page Layout:** Description, Behaviour, Biology, Known Habitats, Recommended Actions

**Elevator Pitch:** Entity 194 is a species of entity that appears in 28 separate habitats and is split into 4 similar subspecies. All Entity 194 species have the appearance of electrical power cables seen in suburbs or countryside.

**Central Narrative:** Entity 194 was first discovered in Level 11 on 18 July 1972 by two wanderers exploring the level. Entity 194 abducts both wanderers and integrate them into a mass of wires at the tip of one pole. Due to the quiet nature of Entity 194, it is forgotten by all wanderers

Entity 194 was rediscovered 20 years later on 27 Feb 1992 by three wanderers finding a building to settle in. One wanderer had been wielding a camera for unknown reasons, two of the wanderers are killed by the instance. The camera recorded about 2 hours worth of footage, 4 minutes of which were focused upon Entity 194; this footage is sent by the wanderer to the M.E.G. two weeks later.

Since 1996, nearly 400 instances were identified by the M.E.G. and recieved a Hazard Classification of 3.5 / 6.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** Very few entities on this wiki appear as harmless, however are actually extremely dangerous.
