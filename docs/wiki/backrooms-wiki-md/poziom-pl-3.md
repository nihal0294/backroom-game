## Description:

**Level PL-3** comprises a spruce forest area containing a basketball court enclosed by metal fencing. Access to the court is provided via two opposing metal gates on opposing sides of the playing area. An electric lamp, continuously illuminated despite lacking a discernible power source, is situated near one gate.

**Level PL-3** is non-euclidean in nature. It prevents any wanderer from traveling more than two (2) five (5) kilometers from the pitch. Should they attempt to do so, **Level PL-3** will somehow reroute the wanderer to the vicinity of the pitch, irrespective of their direction of travel.

**Level PL-3** maintains constant darkness, accompanied by temperatures never exceeding the threshold of 15°C. Overhead Level PL-3 appears a sky nearly identical to that of Earth, the primary observable differences being a still moon and minimal cloud cover.

In accordance with the level’s low entity count, the only entities susceptible to potential encounters with wanderers are [Vanishers](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/byt-pl-3) and packs of [Deadcrows](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/byt-pl-2). Taking into account both species' aversion to light, their presence from within the pitch is rare.

## Bases, Outposts and Communities

### 23rd Outpost of the Polish Committee of Explorers

• The outpost for the group is located 200 meters from the north entrance to the pitch.  
• Between four to six personnel hailing from the group are stationed at this outpost.  
• Personnel stationed at this outpost are primarily tasked with documenting and observing entities encountered within this level. Furthermore, personnel are to consistently be on the lookout for potential exits.  
• The Outpost provides continuous shelter for wanderers seeking refuge within **Level PL-3**.  
• Supply shipments from the 17th outpost are provided to this outpost on a monthly basis.

---

## Entrances and Exits:

### Entrances

• **Level PL-3** can be accessed via a variety of wooded areas located within [Level PL-2](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/poziom-pl-2).  
• Evacuating the courtyard of [Level PL-9](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/poziom-pl-9) will grant a wanderer entry to **Level PL-3**.

### Exits

• If, by chance, a wanderer falls asleep within **Level PL-3**, it is possible for them to awaken within [Level PL-7](poziom-pl-7.md).  
• Wandering with sporadic shifts in direction can lead wanderers to [Level PL-18](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/poziom-pl-18).

## Incident Report

> On July 22, 2018, personnel from the 23rd Outpost of the Polish Committee of Explorers, during routine patrol, witnessed the materializing of a basketball within the confines of the level’s basketball court. Shortly following this, the ball subsequently bounced repeatedly without any apparent external influence before passing through the hoop. Following this event, the ball was immediately secured and confiscated by P.C.E. personnel.  
> -Senior Explorer Marcin Roja
