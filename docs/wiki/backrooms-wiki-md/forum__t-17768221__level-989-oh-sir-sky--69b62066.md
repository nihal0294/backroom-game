Copy/pasting my thoughts from Discord to Wikidot for public viewing/ease of access.

Ok before I start apologies if this isn't super coherent because I am not that awake rn but anyway

I can kinda see what you're going for with the I guess whimsical story you've chosen to do here, but I feel as if the actual writing does not. The writing style itself is relatively bog-standard, much of it being clunky or awkward to read, with quick and plain exposition about things that make the set-up feel "grounded"; I think this works against the story's nature in a lot of ways. Its prose and plot frames it in the same way a much more generally "serious"-vibed story would despite the more fairy-tale kind of fantastical nature of its actual details and antagonists. I feel it does not lean far enough into what it appears to be trying to be with the whimsy, and at least to me that makes the story come off as corny, because it feels like the page is trying to take these antagonists seriously in a way that I simply cannot.

Apart from but adjacent to this, I also felt a decent chunk of the general prose was not super engaging and at times made me want to skim. Might be a personal taste thing idk.
