Level 148 idea

29 Apr 2022 16:52

Level 148 is class 4 it has wreches, smilers, death moths , skin stealers, and entity 1694. It is advised to leave before night because entity 1694 will hunt you if it even smells you. No image of entity 1694 is available, everyone who has tried has died instantly. The level itself is an old middle school with no windows. The floor is a brownish tan color with white brick walls and red brick in some parts. There are also facelings and hounds in some of the class rooms. It is advised to not drink the water in the water fountains as it is carpet fluid. The food in the cafeteria is fresh and is changed daily and there’s almond water in the fridge for you to drink. But there is a lot of entities in the cafeteria including duller ,stranglers ,and partygoers. You can enter though two red doors in level 4 or no-clipping through a white brick wall in level 22. The only way to exit is by entering the staff only room that is guarded by smilers and skin stealers. You can tell if it is day by a clock on the walls with the word day and night the hour hand on the clock will point to either day or night. The second it points to night you will have to run. But if you don’t get out within 5 minutes of being in level 148 you will be trapped forever!
