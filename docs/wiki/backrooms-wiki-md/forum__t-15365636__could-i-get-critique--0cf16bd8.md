Ok, so I will critique, in bullet points, to find the sentence press ctrl+f. Most of this is in my opinion, do what you want to do.

- I believe "cinema style levels, such as Level 68 and other levels of the such." should be "cinema levels, e.g. Level 68"
- ", however," should be "however"
- This one is really up to you, instead of "[[REDACTED]]" you can hide it in codes, or get some changing text from Level 404's history and see it, but you can also make it very cryptic.
- Maybe "cinema or video level." can be "cinema levels".
- " Also, when arriving into Level 387" you don't need the Also, just make a sentence as so " When arriving into Level 387".
