Cars in the Backrooms? Not much use in interior levels. Okay in exterior areas with roads or traversable terrain, if you could find a way to get them there.

"…Level mainly consists of several cars unable to work…"

Malfunctioning cars makes this a moot point.

Let's try something else.

Maybe some vehicles might serve another purpose. Sitting in an ambulance, with the engine running, might have healing properties. Some trucks might have refillable inventories of items. Food trucks might carry food. Some "cars" or (collapsibles on the page) might have signs or decals that offer clues or secret information. Perhaps make one collapsible hold a link that sends the wanderer to a different level (with that author's permission).

Make it interesting. Boxes holding 99 keys might be found there. Only ONE set of keys can be removed at one time. Every set of keys is labelled with the make, model, location of each vehicle… but no box is on the same floor as the vehicle.

The license plate gives the location (level) that the car (once unlocked and started) will send the wanderer.

All cars are kept locked. You must use the proper key.

Hounds are everywhere! During the daytime, they are docile. At night, they turn into "junkyard dogs." The only safe place is inside a vehicle until daylight. Make the day/night transition a race against time.

Readers only need to know that these hazards exist. Spell out that they need to exit the level ASAP.
