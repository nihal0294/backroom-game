The reason I am considering this is because there is a level that exists in the main wiki that would be infinite, and meet all the characteristics of a main level, but have a cool narrative behind it that gives it a real "secret" feel. Plus, the MEG would never be able to find it, due to its property of having no evidence of its existence and being hard to exit.

This level is:  
**LEVEL 12** "The Matrix"  
This level would probably be called "Level 11" or "The Matrix" in desolation lore, but it would basically censor itself so that the MEG doesn't believe it exists, but it would be a main level all the same. There would be a decent amount of people who believe that it exists though. It could be accessible through windows or no-clipped from level 10 and be an infinite white void with random furniture scattered around, as well as doors that lead to random locations within the level. Much like Level 12 in the main canon, "Level 11" would be hard to exit, needing a specific pattern to do so.

Is it OK to include a 12th main level that is unknown? Meaning that it is not considered a main level *yet* because the MEG is unaware of its existence?
