Room: The Bathrooms

20 Dec 2020 20:28

The Bathrooms is a room present in grimy levels. It appears to be an infinite public bathroom. There is a repeating pattern of 5 stalls and 7 urinals across the room's right wall, with an endless row of sinks, mirrors and hand dryers on the left wall.

Traveling further into the level has it become more and more disgusting and dilapidated, with clogged toilets, graffiti, broken urinals and dangerous entities appearing. Nobody has managed to travel more than 10 miles in this room.

Noclipping into the toilets will send you to Level 8.
