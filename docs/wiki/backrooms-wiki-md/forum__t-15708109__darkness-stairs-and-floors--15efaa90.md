Darkness, Stairs, and Floors

02 Mar 2023 15:39

Core Idea:

Okay so this idea sounds sorta basic, but hear me out

This Level contains exactly as the tittle says, “Darkness, Stairs, and Floors”. Most floors would be exactly the same appearance (same floor, wallpaper, etc.) but some special floors (I’ve nicknamed “Door Floors” o3o you get to know why that if you ask for the draft, hehe.) which vary wildly from the rest of the floors, perhaps leaving the stairwell just shrinks you to 10% your size, in a room that is 1/10th the size it normally was, okay, but hear me out, what if you could play checkers in that floor. What if, one of the floors opened into a giant cave that have rock structures that grew super fast, and goat milk dripped from the ceiling?

I know this sounds pretty dumb, but if you find yourself interested, hmu and I’ll send you a draft!
