# Cosmology and Properties

**The Hive**'s nature is unique among levels, existing in the [Blue Channel](blue-channel.md), yet also connected to the [astral world](the-grave.md).

As such, the Hive works as a threshold between the two, capable of manifesting not only the thoughts of the living, in typical [Liminal Echo](phenomenon-7.md) fashion, but also the bodies of the dead. The ideas, emotions, thoughts, and sometimes even souls can linger away from the immaterial, getting caught in the Hive's very flesh to be given physical form through eggs.

However, the decayed state of the Hive got the better of these immaterial concepts. Only able to shape with its own rot, every creature born from the Hive is but a monstrous shadow of what they were supposed to represent. Companion turned [Predator](entity-8.md), squirmish turned [gigantic](entity-4.md), life turned undeath. Whether they came out of hope or despair, the thoughts given form are twisted I know you didn't mean to but I don't think she'd like to be referred to as twisted. - Somalia by the Hive, not only strengthening the danger of the level in itself but also the entire Backrooms due to the many no-clipping points present within it.

Despite this apparent wickedness, the Hive holds the faint yet real possibility to find one's way back to their deceased loved ones. Indeed, the threshold between the Backrooms and the Grave still holds, meaning the process of revival spoken about by the Lost still holds true. While the journey is dangerous, the possibility to discuss with or even revive dead wanderers is a real possibility, currently being researched by the M.E.G. # History and Cultural Impact

The Hive hasn't always been the biological hell it is today. Centuries ago, during the Lost Era, the Hive was known as *Paradeos*, a lush, idyllic threshold between the body and mind, bearing the same properties without an ounce of corruption.

Yet, some time after the end of the Lost era, when [their Capital](level-51.md) fell, a malevolent, restless human soul found its way to the level from the Grave. There, its hatred kept it active, fusing with the hive mind of *Paradeos*, slowly corrupting it Wasn't Leo supposed to not know that in detail? How much more is he keeping secret from us? - Somalia.

Over the centuries, the name of Zephyr, the Hive Queen of a malevolent living hell releasing its spawn to torment the living, became engraved in backborn culture. *Huvvat*, its new name, was a lurking nightmare for every culture at the time, from the tribes of [the Venosha](the-venosha.md) to the town of [Godspire](level-116.md).

The continuous flow of entities fueled many stories from the unfortunate explorers who fell into it, whether using the [Level 8](level-8.md) entrance Your demand to unseal it is rejected, by the way. # Entrances and Exits

The only reliable way to enter the Hive is through a tight, long cave corridor on [Level 8](level-8.md) near M.E.G. Outpost Hollow Nest, although it is currently closed and guarded by operatives from the base.

No-clippable surfaces, while unreliable, can also serve as both entrances and exits to and from the Hive. Entrances are usually present in levels with high entity counts, while exits can be found more rarely in the Hive, often near entity swarms using it to leave the level.
