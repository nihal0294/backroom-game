Artificial Entrances to Backrooms Levels

07 Jul 2025 22:17

Concept Title: **The Warp Project**

Central Narrative: An artificial gateway that allows you to go to other levels.

Brief Description: The idea is, there will be many "portals" that will be created by the humans(wanderers?, Goi-s?) that act like easier escapes or easy access to other levels. These "portals" will be placed in bases and outposts. Some of the Gates can lead to but are not limited to, Level 0, Level 1, and Level 11. These "portals" might be operated by a new GOI.

Hook: I feel like this idea could make travel in the backrooms more easier. But, I do not know if this idea is already taken or if it is already a thing in the backrooms lore.
