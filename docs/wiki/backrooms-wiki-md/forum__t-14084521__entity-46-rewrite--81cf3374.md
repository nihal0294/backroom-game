# Entity 46 - Faultcrawlers

---

**Entity Number:** 46

**Habitat** - Majority (absent from levels 5, 7, and 8)

### Description

Faultcrawlers are centipede-like entities varying in size. While they do not pose any immediate threat to the wanderer, large numbers of Faultcrawlers may be a sign of more dangerous entities nearby.

### Behaviors

Faultcrawlers are often seen in small groups, gathered around patches of mold. They make their habitat within cracks inside walls, with an estimated population of hundreds. Whether they create these cracks, or simply inhabit them is currently unknown.

These entities will be completely docile towards the wanderer, but will attack if provoked. It will also attack any assailant they deem a threat towards their nest. Larger Faultcrawlers are often seen near or inside the nest, presumably as guards, while the smaller ones are seen bringing food back to the nest. Ongoing research suggests a form of hierarchy, or queen.

### Biology

These entities can vary in size wildly, anywhere from 2-36 inches. They sport large elongated mandibles that resemble a stag beetle's. It is ill-advised to consume these entities, due to the large amount of poison in their bodies. They feed on moss and scraps of meat leftover from violent entity attacks.

### Discovery

This entity was first discovered by Mrgrimm150 on May 20, 2020.

---

# **Do's and Dont's:**

### **Do:**

- Leave the entity alone. It poses no direct threat to the wanderer.
- Stay away from food patrols carrying meat. As they do not wander far from their nests, this indicates the high probability of a nearby hostile.

### **Don't:**

- Attempt to eat the entity, because of its high toxicity.
