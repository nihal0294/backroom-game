### **Entity #:** 138

### **Habitat(s):** Majority, excluding The Atelier.

# Description

**Broken Record**, is a tall, slender entity that is similar to Entity 137 in almost every way, but with minor differences. The only outfit it has been seen wearing to date is a normal black suit with white gloves and a mask. The kind of mask, however, varies. It is theorized that it has some sort of familial ties or connection to [Entity 137](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-137)[1](javascript:;), but whether they are actually related or just have a familial bond is currently unknown. It seems to be either unable or unwilling to enter The Atelier, but it’s not known why exactly.

# Behaviors

**Entity 138** has been found to “mimic” [Entity 137](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-137) as it wanders around the Backrooms in search of wanderers to interact with[2](javascript:;). It has a much more “bubbly” and upbeat personality than Entity 137. It uses much less formal language and overall a more casual personality.

**Broken Record** has been found to be very unpredictable. It may choose to simply talk to wanderers and give them assistance, or it may attempt to lure them into a false sense of security in order to kill them. It’s advised that people avoid it at all times because of this.

It shows many sadistic qualities, as it appears to gain immense pleasure and enjoyment in causing pain to others. It also doesn’t seem to show any remorse for its actions, referring to it as a “hobby” or leisurely activity[3](javascript:;).

# Biology

**Entity 138**’s biology is theorized to be almost the exact same as [Entity 137](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-137)’s, but with a few key differences. These differences are as follows:

- Black hair instead of dark brown hair
- Extremely pale, almost white skin instead of just a mild pale color.
- Blood that is a darker red in color instead of dark crimson

Due to the fact that it always dons some sort of mask, its facial features are mostly unknown with the exception of its eye color (black). It is assumed, however, that its facial features are also identical to [Entity 137](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-137)'s.

It is also noted that there are minor differences in its music, such as off-key notes, “glitched” or repeated sections, and differences in quality. The songs themselves are the same otherwise.

# Discovery

The first hint of its existence was on May 15th, 2004, when rumours about [Entity 137](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-137) being dangerous and untrustworthy began to spread. However, many theorize **Broken Record** has been around for the same amount of time as Entity 137.

# **Do's and Don'ts**

### **Do:**

- Listen to the music carefully before approaching if it’s not visible
- Check for grey skin or any other defining features before approaching if it is visible
- If you’re spotted or approached by it, stay calm and act as normal as possible. Be polite and try to get away as soon as you can without angering him.

### **Don't:**

- Stay with it for long periods of time unless necessary
- Approach without listening to the music first
- Run or freak out if you’re approached or seen by it
- Attack it, unless you know for certain you can stun it long enough to get away
- Insult it or provoke it
