A review, because I'm doing those now:

16 Nov 2024 12:37

I will say that due to the repetitiveness of some of the other field levels, this was better than I expected, but not perfect.

The clinical tone segments convey what they intend to effectively, although it does repeat things that are close together sometimes, and doesn't mention the most prominent danger in the level until halfway through the page. The "unknown safety" rating also doesn't make much sense, since it's clearly described later that there are no physical threats, only the psychological one. This is going to deduct a point from the rating.

As for the interjection sections from whatever entity controls the level, I don't see any issues with it that the clinical sections did, and it makes proper use of more complex language without feeling too bloated. However, it does seem to make a shift in tone halfway through the page. At the start, it felt like the entity was warning you about the hazards of memory loss over time. but as it gets to the Phenomena paragraph, it feels like they start mocking people for being able to remember things. I'm also not sure why they say "Why can't you remember?" in the image caption. The good and bad here balance each other out, so I'm not going to change the rating because of it.

I may be wrong here, but it does also feel like you only worked on the page by yourself, and didn't check for ways to fix some issues with the page. At the time of writing, there are a few SPaG errors scattered throughout the page, the image license is incompatible, and the link to the M.E.G isn't functional. As always, I would suggest using the forum or Discord and asking for critique, which would prevent these problems. I'd take half a point away for this, as it's not detrimental to the whole page, but it also makes it look like it was done with less effort.

For other points, the page did keep me interested enough to read it all, but it didn't invoke much emotion. The page also does fit the memory horror genre, and it's clear that it's part of that.

Overall, 3.5/5. Enough for me to upvote it, but there are still some improvements to be done.
