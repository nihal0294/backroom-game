The Real End

22 Nov 2025 06:18

Sorry if I seem a little hateful when I say this…

But I feel like we've lost who we are.  
I think the original thing that made The backrooms appealing was the unknown.  
I like all the levels, all the factions, humanity forced to near the bottom of the food chain, yet they still push on.  
But I think we need to push back to that unknown.  
So, I would like to request something of y'all, of all of you.  
One big tale, collection of multiple authors, each helping each other, every one of them pushing.  
One final push for the people that we write, the characters we bring together, that we give life, to reach the end. The true end.  
One last big tale.  
One last push.  
I don't want to see our site die.  
Because, we are like humanity, we push through everything to seek one more step, to live.  
And with that final step, we will give a new beginning.  
We  
Will  
Push
