**Level 101** is the 102nd Level of the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

**Level 101** is a large dark maze inside an asylum that stretches out for 25km. Similar to normal asylums there are graffiti and blood on some of the walls. **Level 101** consists of a large number of hallways found in normal asylums and occasionally asylum rooms. Broken furniture and doors leading to different places in **Level 101**. Lights inside **Level 101** will randomly go out or break until they power back on or reform. Almond water can be found randomly in **Level 101**. Only five people can enter **Level 101** at a time.

Occasionally a faint cry has been heard from somewhere in **Level 101**. The cry has been estimated to be that of a eight year old child. All attempts to find the child have failed as the cry can only be heard for a few seconds. It is unknown how the child got their or if it even is a child. Thermal heat detectors and following the cry of the child has proven ineffective.

Side effects of staying in **Level 101** for too long include:

- Nausea.
- Vomiting.
- Stomach pain.
- Liver pain.
- Decrease in mental health and sanity.
- Violent outbreaks.
- Intense stress.
- Heavy depression.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

Due to entities and no edible food sources there are no bases, outposts, or communities in **Level 101**.

# **Entities**

Smilers, Deathmoths, Skin Stealers, and Hounds

# **Entrances And Exits:**

### Entrances

Enter a cracked wooden door on **Level 0**.

Touch a massive blood stain on **Level 444**.

### Exits

Entering doors labeled 5-94 will take you to **Levels 5-94**.

Finding a door labeled exit on it and entering it will take you to **Level 0**.

Starting things off, *please* put this into a sandbox before posting a draft. You can easily find the link to it by looking at the front page or hovering over the "Wiki" button up top.

- The Class Difficulty code does require another set of brackets to work properly, so I recommend adding that.

- Into the context of things, I see that the article requires a SPaG check. For example, there are quite a few places where commas would be best placed, like the first sentence of the description. There is also a *lot* of repetition with "Level 101", which can easily be balanced with other phrases such as "This Level" or if wanderers have given the Level a nickname/the Level has a sign of some sort that reveals its name, use that.

- There is one particular, peculiar sentence here as well. "Similar to normal asylums there are graffiti and blood on some of the walls." This wouldn't quite make sense as it's. Well. You wouldn't imagine actual asylums having that much neglect for their buildings, unlike this Level which is, well, abandoned. Perhaps you could rephrase that?

- The actual description of the Level, besides the reports of a crying child, is rather bare-bones. Perhaps describe the other places within the Level, and add some entities inside, i.e. Deathmoths in the cafeteria, facelings in a recreation room, etc. Since the class difficulty is set to 4, it would be important for wanderers to know exactly what kind of entities are inside (just make sure the entities you use aren't exclusive to certain Levels). This would help give your Level more depth, instead of simply depending on the second paragraph for more intrigue.

- Speaking of the crying child, a lot of the sentences in that paragraph could definitely be meshed together and formed more concisely. It would be best to try and do that.

- I think it would be best to describe the side effects in stages, as it would be more immersive and unnerving than if it were to simply be listed in bullet points.

- That being said, with the way you wrote the entrances and exits, those also require bullet points. Bullet points can be created by adding an asterick (\*), a space, then the sentence you plan to use. More information on formatting may be found here: [Wikidot Syntax Page](wikidot-syntax.md).

There is a lot of potential in this article, so I recommend you keep trying to improve it! For some articles, it does take a while to get them exactly how you want it - but if you are willing to keep trying, I know you'll succeed! **Level 101** is the 102nd Level of the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

**Level 101** is a large dark maze inside an asylum that stretches out for 25km. Unlike normal asylums there are some blood stains and claw marks on some of the walls. **Level 101** consists of a large number of hallways found in normal asylums and occasionally asylum rooms. Broken furniture and doors leading to different places in the level. Lights inside **Level 101** will randomly go out or break until they power back on or reform. Almond water can be found randomly in **Level 101** in various places including the cafeteria, the hallways, the asylum rooms, and next to blood marks on the wall. Only five people can enter **Level 101** at a time.

**Level 101** follows a 24 hour day and night cycle. Lights will go on and off representing a normal day and night cycle. Entities are known to be more active during the night because it makes hunting prey easier. **Level 101** contains mannequins that are dressed in lab coats, security uniforms, mental patients uniforms, and casual civilian clothing. These mannequins are not sentient or alive. Some security mannequins have weapons on them like tasers, stun batons, ammo bullets, and semi-automatic pistols, however these weapons aren't effective on entities as they only stun them for a short amount of time.

Occasionally a faint cry has been heard from somewhere in **Level 101**. The cry has been estimated to be that of an eight year old child. All attempts to find the child have failed as the cry can only be heard for a few seconds. It is unknown how the child got there or if it even is a child. Thermal heat detectors and following the cry of the child has proven ineffective.

Anyone who stays for too long while have nausea after an hour trapped in **Level 101**. Then headaches will set in after an hour and a half. This is then followed by intense stomach or liver pain leading to immense pain in the stomach area. Another hour after this heavy depression will follow. Then eventually violent outbreaks. This will usually drive them into insanity and then possibly self harm.

The entities have used **Level 101's** layout to there advantage. This makes escaping highly dangerous due to entities and their strategies for catching prey. Death rats are known to hide in cafeteria food until someone comes along and eats the food. The death rats will leap into the person's throat and start to choke them or eat them from the inside out. Smilers will hide in the shadows almost perfectly and if someone steps within 1-5m of a hidden smiler it will leap out and drag them into the shadows where they will start to eat them alive. Hounds will hunt in packs of 10-40 members. They will attempt to corner a target and leap at them eating them alive as other members block possible escape routes making hounds very dangerous entities. Deathmoths will hide on ceiling and leap down at their prey and inject a powerful acid in them instantly killing them. Skin stealers will wear the skin of dead humans and attempt to give someone a false sense of security and lure them to another entity or skin stealer killing them.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities**

Due to entities and non edible food sources there are no bases, outposts, or communities in **Level 101**.

# **Entities**

Smilers, Deathmoths, Skin Stealers, Death Rats and Hounds

# **Entrances And Exits:**

### Entrances

- Enter a cracked wooden door on **Level 0**.

- Touch a massive blood stain on **Level 444**.

### Exits

- Entering doors labeled 5-94 will take you to **Levels 5-94**.

- Finding a door labeled exit on it and entering it will take you to **Level 0**.
