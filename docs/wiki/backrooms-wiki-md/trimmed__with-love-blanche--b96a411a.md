It's nice, you know? Walking through the endless archives, handpicking what to give to guests.

Home was exactly where he and I need to be, and where we need to stay.

Looking through the thousands of books I collected throughout my life in search of the perfect gift, I hadn't expected to receive a letter from an old friend.

"[I](entity-180.md) will miss you" He said. Some things must be hidden. The Beyond must be hidden.

Through the thousands of books searched and archived, and everybody I remembered and met, the people I kept safe. Five words struck in my head:

###### ***I need to find him.***
