Blorb Shitto now being canon aside… :P

I think this is a wonderful article. I very much enjoy how it touches on how we as authors and creators of a hobby website often create many drafts and that we… lose interest in them. Maybe the idea was never viable, maybe we became obsessed with a new shiny idea, maybe we outgrew the idea, but whatever the reason, they come here.

If I remember right, this is Akimoto's 100th article, and from talking with him and learning about his history on the SCP wiki, I think this is a nice retrospective with some of his characters from there. It's nice, gives me insight into his brain, and helps me understand him as an author more.

Atlas is more secretive, I can tell where they've touched the page, but they're being sneaky and subtle.

Also I do have to applaud the images because they really add so much to the article itself.
