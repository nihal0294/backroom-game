# Description:

Level 985 is a safe level that consists of a dark void. This void appears to go on for about 50 feet in any direction before a solid wall prevents further movement. Despite a lack of visible light sources, the level is very well lit. The only notable landmark is a locked door. If a Wanderer is in a group when they arrive at this level, the door will appear to be a simple, immovable plate of iron, with no handles or knobs. However, if a Wanderer is alone in this level, the door will take the appearance of the main entrance to the home that the Wanderer is most familiar with, be it a childhood home, a hotel room, or a log cabin, for example. The door will be locked if it is capable of locking, but the Wanderer will find a method of bypassing the lock either on their person or somewhere near the door.

Once the door is open, a Wanderer will feel a strong compulsion to pass through it. Doing so will leave the Wanderer in a white void with the same dimensions as the dark void. Turning back to the door will reveal a note stuck to its surface, with a message written on it.

If this is the first time the Wanderer has been here, going through the door again will bring the Wanderer to a place that appears to be [Earth](the-frontrooms.md). The Wanderer will enter the new area outside the front door of said home. While in this area, the Wanderer can freely interact with reality in any way they wish, such as talking, moving, and affecting objects normally. After a period of 24 hours, the Wanderer will pass out and wake up feeling well rested back in [Level 0](level-0.md).

If this is not the first time the Wanderer has been here, the note will have a different message, and returning through the door will bring the Wanderer back to the dark void.

# Entities:

No entities have ever been detected within this level.

# Entrances and Exits:

## Entrances:

If the 'cute' item handed to Kitty in order to exit [Level 974](level-974.md) held great emotional significance to the Wanderer, they have a 100% chance of ending up in this Level.

If a Level has a significant resemblance to the Wanderer's home, they have a 25% chance when exiting the Level to end up in this Level instead.

There is a small chance to no-clip here from any Level. A Wanderer with an especially strong desire to return home will have a higher chance.

## Exits:

Wanderers will end up in [Level 0](level-0.md) after they make their peace.

If you cannot make your peace, no-clipping into the boundary wall will send you to a safe Level.
