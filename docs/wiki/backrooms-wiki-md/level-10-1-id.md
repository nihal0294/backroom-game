## **Description:**

  

![Missing alt text.](http://marlin-paskalis.wikidot.com/local--files/gambar/Restoran)

Picture taken at **Level 10.1-ID**.

**Level 10.1-ID** or Kalasan Belakang Restaurant is a fast food restaurant. It consists of 15 tables that can accommodate 30 customers, 2 air conditioners can be found as well. There is a cashier and a number system for taking food. The kitchen section consists of simple kitchen utensils in general such as cauldrons, spatulas, fryers, grills, and pots. Every food item is known to come from the kitchen door which is prohibited to be entered except the restaurant owner. This level is free of harmful entities.

A clock is displayed on the kitchen wall. The clock has a different time from the current time with a time difference of about 4 hours and 3 minutes. In other words, if our time is 05:00 then their time is 09:03. There are 5 measuring cups arranged on the cashier's desk that are used to measure [Almond Water](object-1.md).

The restaurant sells a variety of food from meat to vegetables. All meat dishes will be cooked with the same seasoning as Kalasan Chicken. Vegetable-based foods that can be found such as sayur asem, and fresh vegetables.

It is theorized that this level has a larger space in the currently known part of the level. After an wanderer managed to enter the kitchen door, he found that [DATA DAMAGED].

Skip the boring words above and just pay attention to my tantalizing words. Do you know Kalasan Chicken[1](javascript:;)? If you do, congratulations because we just opened a Kalasan Chicken restaurant in Backrooms. But if you don't know, Kalasan Chicken is chicken that has been seasoned and the salty and sweet flavors combine to make an amazingly delicious taste.

Of course, we don't just sell chicken, we also sell empal[2](javascript:;), milkfish, squid, and pigeon with Kalasan seasoning as well as fresh vegetables. Our specialty is a portion of warm white rice, of course this one does not come from a level of poisonous plants. All of that only costs about 5-10 glasses of Almond Water with a glass measure that we provide at the cashier's desk. Please see the price menu directly at the place for more details.

Don't forget to bring your own drinks, we don't sell any. Unless you want to just drink Sayur Asam soup[3](javascript:;).

Bring your friends or family here, it's spacious enough to accommodate everyone. There is a 25% discount for those of you who bring or dine with 9 friends, colleagues or family.

We are open 16/24 hours. If you see no one at the cash register or kitchen, then we are closed. On the other hand, if there is someone at the counter or kitchen, we are open.

What are you waiting for? Come here and eat as much as you want for cheap. Wait, do you still not believe in the origin of the ingredients? Well, all we can say is that we're sorry that the origin of the ingredients is a company secret but we can assure you that it's not human meat.

  

## **Discovery**

  

It was first discovered by a wanderer who got lost on [Level 10-ID](level-10-id.md) who entered a random shop. It is known that the wanderer was experiencing the effects of the level and when entering **Level 10.1-ID**, the effects are known to disappear.

  

## **Bases, Outposts, and Communities**

  

## **Sellers**

  

Sellers are the first ones to settle at the 10.1-ID Level. They are friendly and easy to trade with. The members are known to be about 4 people with 2 as cashiers and 2 as cooks.

  

## **Entrances and Exits**

  

### **Entrances**

The main entrance to **Level 10.1-ID** is through [Level 10-ID](level-10-id.md) by following the route that has been recorded. The route starts at the front door of the Abiprasada Cemani base, after that turn left until passing through 3 alleys. At the next alley, turn right. There you will meet 2 intersections later. The first intersection turns left and the next intersection turns right, and five houses after the last intersection you will find a shophouse which is the entrance.

### **Exits**

- The back door will lead directly to [Level 3-ID](level-3-id.md).
- Passing back from the entrance can take you to [Level 22-ID](level-22-id.md).
