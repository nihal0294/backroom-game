in Discord server, there's a section called "sandbox forum", where it works similar to our forum here in wikidot, where you post your thread with your sandbox link, the only difference is that people can give the so called "reaction" to your thread, there's 3 kinds of "reaction" which i'll be calling them checkmarks, the 3 types are:

- Concept.
- Format.
- SPaG

you need to gather 3 on each of the category which in total is 9 checkmarks, then, call a Greenlighter once you gathered them all, despite greenlighter have the right to **reject** the clear, for most of the time they'll just simply let you pass (a.k.a. cleared) when you have all the checkmarks.
