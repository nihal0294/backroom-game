SEEKING GREENLIGHT : Level 763 - The Entity Zoo

27 Feb 2022 09:03

Level 763 is a Zoo for entities in simplest terms.

The level resembles level 0 in layout and design, with the exception of the wallpaper being changed to a more earthy shade of green/brown as well as the "Exhibits" scattered about the level.

Each exhibit is a self contained area, ranging in size from around 5'x 5' to upwards of 100' x 100'.

The exhibits contain a multitude of common backrooms entities such as deathmoths, skin stealers, party-goers and such.

While most exhibits contain entities, others do not. Some have had their enclosures broken into, or rather, broken out of.

Along with the entities contained, there are also other human like entities called "Staff".

The staff regularly make rounds and do basic upkeep tasks such as cleaning, the staff themselves are peaceful.

In the event that an exhibit is breached, the staff will scramble to contain the escaped entity, sometimes resulting in a mix up causing travellers to be mistaken for entities and being thrown into the exhibit instead.
