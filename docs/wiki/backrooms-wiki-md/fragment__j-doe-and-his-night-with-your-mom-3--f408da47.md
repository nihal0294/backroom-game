# fragment:j-doe-and-his-night-with-your-mom-3

[J. Doe](j-doe.md) is a wanderer who has often been sighted walking through the halls of Level 1. Their most notable feature is the fact that anyone who sees them has had difficulty describing exactly what they look like. Due to this fact, a full description isn't possible at this time.

  
  
  
“Hey!”  
  
  
There is a guy over there.

They’re doing.. well, something for sure. Something.

You don’t know them. Do you?

Maybe they know you. Maybe they don't. Can anyone know anyone?

You don’t know them.

There have been a number of speculations on the nature of J. Doe, though they are as confirmed as much anything has ever been confirmed, and will be confirmed as much as anything has ever been confirmed. Confirmed.

Please consult your nearest for knowing J. Doe.

  
  
  
They look weird. Or, more like not-weird. So much that it comes back around to being weird.

It’s almost as if you’d forget them if you turned around.  
  
  
“Oh. Okay bye.”

“Maybe they’ll come around again.”  
  
  
Oh right, the guy. The guy?

Hey Jan, I need to ask you something. Can you take a look at this? There’s this guy, I think. Do you know them? Very generic. Ever tried talking to a nobody?

I think so, kind of. I think I saw them in somewhere when something. Pretty sure they had eyes. Doesn’t everyone seem like a nobody?

  
  
  
A few eyes blink. A few times. A few seconds. Maybe more? The visage of something that might be/have been/will be, shattered into a million pieces in the face of it all and yet strung together anyway. The ground, some glass, a bird, a plane, what does it all mean? That doesn’t matter.  
  
  
Who are you?  
  
  
Hmmmm  
  

The following is a list of all the data we have been able to gather on J. Doe. Please inform us for any new information you may have about them.

Name: J. Doe  
Status: Alive?  
Age: ??  
Gender/Sex: J. Doe  
Appearance: Ambiguous, generic, J. Doe  
Personality: Outgoing, strange, J. Doe, You  
Last sighted at: Where  
Last sighted on: Yes  
Affiliations: Me, You, You

  
  
  
  
  
A [self] walks along the concrete floor, in the only hypothetical space that allows [ego] to permeate. The one (or two? three, four) that understands why it is and what it is here, under a sky of [id] that questions not what is, and conceives of what may be. What becomes of me and you and we and you and how we walk through the night and see each other and ourselves. Where does existence say about Doe? And us and us and us across a [superego] of understanding and non-understanding as they are what you are but a thing for what they are.  
  
  
*Due to the nature of such ponderings, the Doe finds itself unable to be meaningfully confirmed, returning to an eternally stagnant and changing aspect of reality.*  
  
  
…  
  
  
…  
  

Hey, you asked me to interview J. Doe. Well I didn’t really do that and stuff but I got this thing, pretty cool right? Uh, whatever it is.

---

**Begin**

Someone:

J. Doe: Hey there! I saw you pretty good over there and it would be rude not to say hi.

Some: Hm ok. I see you too.

J. Doe: Wow I like you already. What’s your name?

One: Me?

J. Doe: Wow, Me too. But what is Me, am I right? Something probably.

Someone: Mmm something, it is something. Is it defined by existence? What is Me hmm..

J. Doe: Hmm.. but hey, you ever wonder why you are? It’s pretty interesting if you think about it. And have you ever seen yourself? Do you think anyone sees you? You you you me me me. Does the self understand itself? Hmmm. Anyway, who are you, again?

: You, maybe. Or nobody, somebody, does it matter? What are any of us? Describe describe is it ever right? Nothing’s right.

J. Doe: Funny how that works. Funny how Me works. I think…. I’m not sure how to do that, actually, think.

J. Doe: Oh shoot! Am I late?

**End**

  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
  
*You have one(1) new message*

…  
  
  
Look in the mirror. What do [you](j-doe.md) see?

What are you? Does that question even make sense?  
  
  
*This is a funny place isn’t it?* You know what you are. Just as everyone else does. You are **know**, and you are **are**. You are here because of them and their selves exist through you.

Maybe they don’t know you because they don’t know themselves. Or others. Or maybe you’re everyone, an abstract thing, or nothing at all. They don’t know any of that either.

But perhaps you might be the answer to that. But can you even know you? Do reflections really show us? Does mind perceive us for who we are? What defines self here? You and me and they and he. I don’t think they see you, but maybe you see them. So much more/less than meets the **ego**.  
  
  
Oh well.  
  
  
But hey! Coffee’s ready. Isn’t it cool that they wrote an article about your eyes?
