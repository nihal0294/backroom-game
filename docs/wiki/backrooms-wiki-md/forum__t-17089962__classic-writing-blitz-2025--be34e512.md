This is disappointing

10 Apr 2025 01:00

Okay so been a bit and I've been thinking and talking about this.

Like I joked about earlier, we've had this before. Rey pointed this out in the server, this combines two of the most complained about cons we've had on this site: mincon and classicon. Both had lots of issues. Classicon felt very underwhelming because every page had to confine to the structures that were dominant in times we are very much moving on from. They had potential but could not fulfill that potential to an extent the writers were capable and ultimately became pages with issues similar to the classic pages that are just kinda on site now.

Don't get me wrong, I understand the thoughts behind this kind of event. It has charm and there are things to enjoy with the classic pages. It's just that we're moving away from them for a reason. This event really does just feel like classicon and mincon mushed together but with even more restrictions. It also seems to really dig into the issues that come with the old pages. They were copies of pages that were copies of SCP (hence the strange "clinical tone"). They were bland, not just in wording but in visuals. People felt safer following the lead of others rather than figuring out what worked for them. I think there's ways this event could have been a celebration of that rather than a return.

Sorry but this feels underwhelming.
