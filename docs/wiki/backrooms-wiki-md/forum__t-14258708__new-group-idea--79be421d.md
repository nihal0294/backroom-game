Re: New group idea

03 Nov 2021 15:00

HI! Took a look at your idea, with a couple of pointers (I hope you don't take offense to them):

It's a little difficult to get an idea of the group. There isn't anything that mentions the number of folks who are part of the group, what the purpose of their group is, or even *why* they use Almond Water for trade.

I can also tell that this group appears to be dependent on the existence of the M.E.G., clinging to the coattails of "Oh, no, what? We're *not* the M.E.G. That's ridiculous!" if any mentions of the M.E.G. were removed, I'd actually think they were just some small-time traveler group. In some other cases, like with the B.N.T.G. and the U.E.C. formerly being a part of the M.E.G., only mentioning them would be fine-but the article appears to go back to mentioning them, which takes away their potential independence from the M.E.G.

Going back to the Almond Water thing, it's… less helpful that their only currency is Almond Water. For the group themselves especially, because then they need to spend more time scavenging for useful resources (they're also… giving more Almond Water for Almond Water? It sounds like a cheat on their part for me.). If anything, if people needed things, they'd actually just go to the B.N.T.G. They have the Traders' Vault, which doesn't even appear to require *any* currency to acquire their resources.

I also don't understand their hostility towards the M.E.G. Groups like the Masked Maidens, the Followers of Jerry, and the U.E.C. all have relatively good reasons for their being aggressive. This isn't necessarily a problem ***if*** there's going to be some kind of revelation of their motives later on: But the rest of the article simply doesn't have enough content for that kind of lack to be acceptable. Them randomly taking out wanderers for no apparent reason honestly makes them less trustworthy than the M.E.G., too.

The idea, while in need of sprucing up, does indeed have potential. The first thing I thought of when the name of their group came up was some kind of store or manufacturing group.

Do pm me on Wikidot pms if you'd like some more advice! I'd love to help out.

Yours truly,
