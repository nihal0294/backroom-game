**Level 107** is the 108th Level of the Backrooms.

# **Description:**

**Level 107** is an expansive collection of heavily deteriorated rooms that appear to have been uninhabited for an extended period of time. The level is characterized by tan, tiled walls that remain consistent throughout the environment, while many ceilings have had their tiles forcibly removed, exposing tangled wiring and decayed wooden supports of unknown origin. Doors connecting the rooms are frequently damaged or completely detached from their hinges, limiting movement between areas. A notable and consistent feature of **Level 107** is the presence of a [power box](object-5.md) mounted on the wall of every known room. Additionally, some rooms are reinforced by steel support bars extending from floor to ceiling, though their purpose remains unclear.

The floors of most rooms are covered in a layer of chipped wood and debris, making strong footwear highly recommended as to avoid injury. Thick, opaque windows in rooms appear to [emit strong, bright light](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/trimmed:entity-103), but they are the only source of natural light on **Level 107**, so a light-emitting device is strongly recommended. **Level 107** constantly emits an ambient noise described to resemble a screeching audio recorder emitting static. This ambiance can easily cause people to go insane if heard for long enough, usually taking its toll after 72 hours of repeated listening.

# **Entities:**

**107** is plagued with a variety of entities. [Wretches](entity-15.md) can be found in many rooms, often with supplies like [Almond Water](object-1.md) and rations nearby. Additionally, [Mockers](entity-27.md), [Wormlings](entity-43.md) infected with [The Disease](entity-19.md), and [Tiletraps](entity-57.md) can often be found lurking within **107**, as well as other common entities.

Reports of other unidentified entities have also been confirmed, and many people that have gone to **107** have reported [loud screeching noises](entity-199.md) coming from the ceiling, but this is not backed up with any evidence.

# **Bases, Outposts and Communities:**

There are no known bases or outposts on this level, due to the pre-existing audio conditions of **Level 107.**

# **Entrances And Exits:**

### Entrances

Recent reports have shown that specific doors on [Level 106](level-106.md) will take you to **Level 107**. Additionally, it has been proven that finding [a peculiar well](trimmed__level-288--20a39680.md) on [Level 135](trimmed__level-135--e2f606c5.md) has a very small chance of sending individuals to **107**.

### Exits

**107** features certain holes in the wired roof every 35 rooms, according to a new study by researchers. Climbing up these holes in the roof will transport you to another level, usually leading to [Level 68](level-68.md). No-clipping through the floor brings you to [Level 108](level-108.md).

# **Description:**

**Level 107** is an expanse of heavily dirtied and destroyed rooms that do not, at first glance, seem to have been inhabited by any living beings for quite a while. The tiled tan walls of **107** are signature of its appearance, and the roofs above rooms seem to have their tiles forcibly torn out, leaving a writhing mass of wires and unidentified rotten wood an unknown substance supporting the tops of rooms. Many doors between rooms are heavily damaged, and most have fallen off their hinges. One extremely notable thing about **107** is that every single room seems to have a [power box](object-5.md) attached to one of the room's walls. These power boxes are notable for having dangerous anomalous effects when used. Steel bars can be seen supporting certain rooms that stretch from the floor to ceiling.

The floors of most rooms are covered in a layer of chipped wood and debris, making strong footwear highly recommended as to avoid injury. Thick opaque windows in rooms appear to [emit strong, bright light](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/trimmed:entity-103), but they are the only source of natural light on **Level 107**, so a light-emitting device is strongly recommended. **Level 107** constantly emits an ambient noise described to resemble a screeching audio recorder emitting static. This ambiance can easily cause people to go insane if heard for long enough, usually taking its toll after 72 hours of repeated listening. It is heavily noted that this ambient noise seems to have unique effects on victim's sanity.

# **Entities:**

**107** is plagued with a variety of entities. [Wretches](entity-15.md) can be found in many rooms, often with supplies like [Almond Water](object-1.md) and rations nearby. Additionally, [Mockers](entity-27.md), [Wormlings](entity-43.md) infected with [The Disease](entity-19.md), and [Tiletraps](entity-57.md) can often be found lurking within **107**, as well as other common entities.

Reports of other unidentified entities have also been confirmed, and many people that have gone to **107** have reported [loud screeching noises](entity-199.md) coming from the ceiling, but this is not backed up with any evidence.

At the time of writing, all above listings of entities are considered defunct due to false spread of information. Further research is required to determine the real inhabitants of **Level 107.**

# **Description:**

**Level 107** is a dangerous anomalous level that affects those who travel to the stated level.

The true appearance of Level 107 is unknown, due to it's anomalous effects; when a living being travels to **Level 107**, they are subject to an intense mental decrease. The being is then transported to an area that is supposed by many to be **Level 107.** A description of this area can be found in the Original Documentation. What makes this anomaly so dangerous is that anyone who is asked exact questions on the appearance, inhabitants, or description of **Level 107** (F.E. What is the purpose of Level 107?) will be subject to an immediate, violent death. Researchers theorize that this is **Level 107's** way of purposefully enclosing information.

# **Entities:**

The only entity inhabiting the real **Level 107** is, in all regards, **Level 107** itself. It is theorized to be a massive entity that uses psychic-based warp manipulation (P.B.W.M.) to send individuals traveling to **107** to the false pocket dimension, this process taking about 2.3 seconds per individual sent. Further research is being conducted on how to explore the true **Level 107.**
