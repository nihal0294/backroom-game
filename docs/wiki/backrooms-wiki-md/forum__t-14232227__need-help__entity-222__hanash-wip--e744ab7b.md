Entity 222: Hanash (WIP)

19 Oct 2021 16:59

This is a very, very rough draft, and I would like to know what part would be good to remove and what part would be good to focus on.

Hanash changes between either humanoid or a large snake depending on what their task is. They are both useful for groups to know but are suspicious.

They claim to want to help the dead rest and be protected from the deprivations of other entities, but the way they go about it unnerving.

They don't find everyone, but when they find a dead adventurer or the most decayed of wretches or former human entities, if they meat certain criteria, they will "revive" the dead as a snake, alive and fully with the person's personality and sometimes able to speak if they have spent a long time in the back rooms.

He will then carry them to his level, were they will be cared for. Many do try to leave or protest. But many also either give in or protest less due how it is a safe level, with a consistent supply of food water, and their request are granted as long as it is reasonable, from entertainment, to messages being sent to or from the living.

The fact that information from agents or wanders can reach their leaders, friends, or families and reveal vital information leads to at least a warily neutral relation with the entity among some organizations, or a more outright friendly reception among others.

I need help with learning how to put in pictures or to set up interview logs though.
