Simple process! Remove everything after .com/ on the url, then write in the title of your article (unless it’s lengthy, then you would put in abbreviations and such if you wanted). Just make sure that, in place of spaces, you use hypens (-). It should bring you to a page that says something about it not existing yet, and give you the option to submit the writing. Copy and paste your stuff on the sandbox to that page, click save, and voila! Should work.

I’m not able to go in depth right now, but just make sure you throw your stuff into a grammar checker before posting. I noticed a line without a period, plus some general SPaG issues. Might wanna put a colon (:) after “The Brain”, too.
