Looking for help in Church of the Veiled collab

27 Sep 2021 01:12

Hello! We are requesting additional hands to collab with us in the COTV rewrite.

Earlier this year, created the Church of the Veiled (COTV) GoI page. While the concept of a Pure Evil GoI was interesting, the execution was left wanting. I left suggestions in the discuss page, Snowy reached out to me, and we decided to collab.

However, I was already involved in another collaboration which is taking up my time and energy. That collab is nearing completion. With this article, on the other hand, I seem to have writer’s block. Snowy is also not able, in terms of writing skill, to flesh out his ideas for the COTV to the level that he desires and which matches the quality of the wiki. As a result, **we are offering interested writers the opportunity to work with us on this article.** Critique is also valued, though full contribution would be preferred.

The article before rewrite may be found [here](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/the-church-of-the-veiled).

The incomplete rewrite may be found [here](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/viewpagecotv).

If you would like to contribute, we request that you follow the steps below.

1. Read and get an understanding of the concept and ideas.

2. Produce in the sandbox a short write-up of content under any of the vacant headers in the rewrite (customs&liturgies, priesthood, laity).

2. Contact me, , through either Wikidot PMs, this thread, or discord (Kai4Christ#2063). You may request further information if you so require it before finishing and showing us your write-up.

3. If Snowy and I find your grasp of the concepts solid, your ideas interesting, and your writing proficient, you may come on board as our third writer.

Thank you for your time.

Regards,

Kai

P.S. - to the wiki staff, I know this is a bit strange and I apologize if it somehow violates wiki or forum rules. If this is posted wrongly or something else is not allowed, just let me know.
