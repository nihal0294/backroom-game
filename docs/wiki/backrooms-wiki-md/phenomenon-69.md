### Description

Phenomenon 69 refers to the peculiar mechanism by which human excrement and waste is discarded from toilets in [Level 11](level-11.md).

It has long been known that there is no one hypothesis on the waterworks within the Backrooms that provides a satisfying, unified explanation as to what happens to faeces, urine, vomit, tissues, and other undesirables that are discarded within lavatories throughout the different levels. Some levels appear to have their own intricate plumbing systems that remove and relocate waste[1](javascript:;), whilst others simply do not. Obviously, some levels do not even have toilets, leading to many wanderers having to resort to open defecation, though where the utilities are present, it is generally the case that structurally intact bathrooms within the Backrooms make do one way or the other[2](javascript:;). Thankfully, Level 11 possesses the relevant amenities so as to allow hygiene to proceed with some degree of normalcy left intact, though it operates uniquely from other lavatorial technology.

Defecation and urination occur without interruption; the anomaly presents specifically upon flushing. Flushing typical toilets releases water to flush out the contents, they go through the trapway, and the bowl is refilled – in Level 11, sanitary waste abruptly disappears, and clean water is spontaneously generated to replace it. The effect is conducive only to toilets or structures that serve the function of toilets (viz. porta-potties, outhouses, etc), and does not seem to apply to sinks, showers, bidets[3](javascript:;), or other such fixtures. Likewise, only things that are generally understood to be waste seem to be affected by the mechanism itself, though the exact definition of what counts as ‘disposable waste’ is a vague black box for all intents and purposes. Should one attempt to flush waste that is not eligible, toilets in Level 11 simply do not work – if unflushable waste is added onto waste that is otherwise flushable, the offending material is left in the bowl intact.

Level 11 does not have any sanitation plants, and no functional septic tank has ever been found. Where the excrement is transported remains unknown. We do not know why this is the case specifically in Level 11 only.

In essence, this is all there is to know about Phenomenon 69.
