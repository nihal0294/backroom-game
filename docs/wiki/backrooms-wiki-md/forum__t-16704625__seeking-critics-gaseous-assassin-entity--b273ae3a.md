# Habitat(s):

> Gaseous Assassins are rare Entities, but can be found on any Level with dark areas to hide in.

Lowercase the first E in Entities and the first L in Level.

---

> The less well-lit a Level is, the more likely you are to find Gaseous Assassins there.

Lowercase the first L in Level and delete the comma after "is."

---

# Description:

> Gaseous Assassins are humanoid Entities with glowing purple eyes and bodies made entirely out of black gas.

Lowercase the first E in Entities.

---

> They are hostile to humans, as well as some other Entities found in The Backrooms.

Lowercase the first E in Entities, the first T in The and capitalize the first H in Humans.

---

> Due to their speed, agility, and reality-breaking abilities, they pose a great threat to whoever they decide to attack.

I recommend deleting the comma before the "And."

---

> However, they generally prefer hunting other Entities rather than humans, which you can often use to your advantage to avoid falling prey to them.

Lowercase the first E in Entities and capitalize the first H in Humans.

---

> The reason behind Gaseous Assassins' hostility is unknown, but one theory has recently gained popularity.

Add a S after the '.

---

# Behaviors:

#### Hiding

> They can usually be found hiding in unlit areas of Levels, recognisable only by their glowing purple eyes, with their black bodies hidden in the shadows.

Lowercase the first L in Levels.

---

> Gaseous Assassins never share the same hiding spot, and avoid hiding close to Smilers (despite the two Entities having very similar habitats).

Lowercase the first E in Entities.

---

> The most common Entities to fall prey to Gaseous Assassin attacks are humans, Wretches, Dullers, Partygoers, and Facelings.

Lowercase the first E in Entities, then capitalize the first H in Humans and enclose it in square brackets to create a link (humans are entities too.)

---

#### Hunting

> It is very hard to escape from a Gaseous Assassin once it decides to attack you.

I added a comma after Gaseous Assassin.

---

> They can run faster than most adult humans, and can quickly dodge, roll, slide, and sidestep to avoid obstacles and continue a pursuit.

I removed the comma before "And" in the "roll, slide, and sidestep" part and capitalize the first H in Humans.

---

> They are also capable of teleporting "back in time" to the position they were in a few seconds ago, and some have even been observed no-clipping through doors and walls.

I recommend that you remove the link to the word "no-clipping."

---

> The victim is observed to glow purple for a few seconds, and the Gaseous Assassin's eyes slowly get brighter.

You forgot to bold the name "Gaseous Assassin's."

---

> The Entity only attacks one victim at a time, and will usually leave everyone else alone unless provoked.

Lowercase the first E in Entities.

---

#### Other Behaviour

> This leads researchers to believe they are fairly intelligent, although not as intelligent as humans.

Capitalize the first H in Humans.

---

> They are most commonly reported saying "I am sorry" or "sorry, but I must" to witnesses after killing a human.

Capitalize the first H in Human.

---

> The best way to defend yourself (or another Wanderer) from a Gaseous Assassin which has already started attacking is to create significant airflow, and attempt to disperse the gaseous body of the Entity.

Lowercase the first E in Entity.

---

# Biology:

> While gas would normally spread out and disperse as much as possible, the gas that this Entity's body is comprised of instead stays closely packed together, creating the humanoid shape of the Entity.

Lowercase the first E in Entity's and Entity.

---

> Glowing purple daggers appear in Gaseous Assassins' hands when they attack.

I added a comma after Gaseous Assassins.

---

> The black gas that this Entity is made of is safe to inhale, with no adverse effects.

Lowercase the first E in Entity. What were you trying to put after "is made of."

---

> However, doing this will make the Entity extremely angry and send it into a frenzy, attacking everyone in its sight.

Lowercase the first E in Entity.

---

# Discovery:

> The Entity quickly catches up, the camera falls out of the Wanderer's hands, and he is heard screaming before the video cuts out.

Lowercase the first E in Entity

---

# The "reality-bending energy" theory

> The "reality-bending energy" theory

You missed putting a double point after theory. Additionally, you also have to capitalize the R in Reality, the B in Bending, the E in Energy, and the T in Theory.

---

> Although nobody knows for sure why Gaseous Assassins attack humans and other Entities, it is clear that they do something to their victims after attacking them (or perhaps take something from them).

Capitalize the first H in Humans and lowercase the first E in Entities.

---

> According to one theory, which has recently gained popularity among M.E.G researchers, The Backrooms are filled with a mysterious energy that allows places, Entities, and objects to defy the laws of reality.

Lowercase the first T in The and E in Entities. Also, you should change the word "places" to "levels."

---

> This energy builds up on most Entities over time, including humans, and allows them to do things that break the laws of reality.

Lowercase the first E in Entities and capitalize the first H in Humans.

---

> For humans, this manifests as the ability to no-clip.

Capitalize the first H in Humans.

---

> Many Entities also gain other reality-breaking abilities.

Lowercase the first E in Entities.

---

> It is theorised that unlike other Entities, Gaseous Assassins lose this energy over time rather than gaining it.

Lowercase the first E in Entities.

---

> When Gaseous Assassins attack other Entities, they drain this energy from their victims, using it to maintain their physical forms and stay alive.

Lowercase the first E in Entities.

---

# Dos and Don'ts:

> Lure other Entities towards the Gaseous Assassin if it is possible to do so safely. It will likely attack them instead of you, giving you some time to pass by safely.

Lowercase the first E in Entities.

---

> Inhale part of the Entity's gaseous body.

Lowercase the first E in Entity's.
