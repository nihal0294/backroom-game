Level "The Goddess Awaits" (Seeking critique)

05 May 2022 10:34

**The Goddess Awaits** is an enigmatic level of the Backrooms.  
This is the basic idea:

- The draft is divided into two tabs.
- The first tab shows what a wanderer has written after accessing someone else's account.
- The second tab contains the description of the level.
- The level appears different to everyone and only one person can stay at the time.
- It has sentient geometry.
- At the centre of the park, there is a statue representing Athena.

Link to my abomination [here](http://backrooms-sandbox-2.wikidot.com/forestiswatching-2nd-attempt).
