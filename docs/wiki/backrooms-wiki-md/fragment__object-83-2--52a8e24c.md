# Official Statement: Robovac Sightings In and Around U.E.C. Outposts

With their continued attempts at surveilling U.E.C. outposts using their Robovacs, Backrooms Robotics imperils the close relationship that our two groups have enjoyed since the foundation of the Coalition. In the past month alone, over twenty Robovac sightings within and around U.E.C. colonies have been reported to the Homeland Defense Force. They have been sighted at both civilian settlements and secure faculties.

While Backrooms Robotics and the U.E.C. have enjoyed a close relationship for many years, the Coalition will not tolerate hostile actions such as espionage from supposed allies.

"Backrooms Robotics might think that just because they are our main trading partner, we will extend a degree of leeway to them that we do not give to other groups. They are mistaken," says Brigadier A. Smith, Homeland Defense Force head of counterespionage. "Unless they discontinue their espionage against us, apologise, and provide a full explanation for their actions, we will be forced to re-evaluate our relationship with them."

The Homeland Defense Force has announced that due to the attempts at spying using Robovacs, they will be launching a broader investigation into other products purchased by the Coalition from Backrooms Robotics. Citizens of the Coalition are recommended to deactivate any Backrooms Robotics devices they own until the Homeland Defense Force investigation is complete.

The Homeland Defense Force advises all loyal citizens of the Coalition to be vigilant and to report any Robovac sightings to a H.D.F. officer immediately.
