12 Jan 2025 10:46

## Introduction

Welcome to the Idea Forum, a place where you can get your concept written down and attain the proper confidence/direction to continue with your drafting process. It's important to make sure you make the proper preparations before making a post here; coming into this environment without looking into anything will lead to time being wasted and frustration building.

## Required Reading

- [Site Rules](site-rules.md) - Needed to both join the site and understand how to conduct yourself.
- [Criticism Policy](criticism-policy.md) - Outlines rules to follow when giving/getting feedback.
- [Greenlight Policy](greenlight-policy.md) - Clarifies the page posting process.

## Guidelines

### How to receive feedback:

- Each one of your ideas may only have one forum post dedicated to it and vice versa.
- The format is required.
- Once you no longer need feedback, reply or edit to the post so others know.
- Do not link sandboxes here. This forum is only for concepts.
- Your post title should just be the name of your idea. Do not specifically ask for a greenlight/critique in it, or it will be changed.
- Do not ask for critique from more than one person at a time.
- **Be patient and do not harass anyone who is a part of the critique process.**
- It is your responsibility to reach out to critics.
  - - [Greenlighter List](greenlight-policy.md) - Trusted reviewers.
    - [Critic List](forum__t-16432420__critiquer-profiles__meet-the-critics--e087d110.md) - Volunteers who are often more active.

### How to give critique:

- Provide constructive feedback with substance—avoid empty comments.
- Stay civil and avoid dogpiling on ongoing discussions.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the author.
- Be honest without sugarcoating; this helps the author recognize areas for improvement.
- Avoid being overly harsh to prevent discouraging the author from seeking more feedback.

## Template

```
**Concept Title:** [The name of your idea; this should include the page type.]

**Sensitive Content:** [Specify mature themes. Optional.]

**Central Narrative:** [A 1/2 sentence summarization. Describe the major points without focusing on little details. What is the story you're trying to tell?]

**Brief Description:** [A brief description of this work, or an excerpt. Include some of the minor details here without going too in-depth with them. This space should mostly focus on the actual physical aspects without touching on subtext too much.]

**Hook:** [Describe what makes your concept different. Why should the reader care about this idea as opposed to any of the other work here? What will make this memorable?]

**Purpose:** [Describe what you want your concept to do. What emotions do you want your reader to feel? How does the work build onto the setting of the Backrooms? What other pages will they view differently after reading this? Optional.]

**Additional Notes/Remarks:** [A place to bring up miscellaneous things. Questions for the reviewer, sources of inspiration, and so on. Optional.]
```
