There is literally a line at the bottom of the authors hub page that kind of stated Artwork pages are a thing, yet we have no mainpage for them. There are a bunch of amazing artists here and well, I mean, make it easier for the people not on Discord, maybe.

I just looked at the other thread. Maybe there aren't that many people who draw here, but there *are* a few users who have created a considerable amount of works. I'm not brave enough to call out names though.

Also, we already have a *Featured* Art Archive. I think Featured is supposedly something that comes *after* the actual archive. It's only a matter of time before it expands into at least the same scale as the Objects page. I can only assume the same can be applied to an Artwork hub.
