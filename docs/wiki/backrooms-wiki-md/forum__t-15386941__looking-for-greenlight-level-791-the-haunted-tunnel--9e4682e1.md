I do have a critique in store for you, but one thing you need to know before we continue is that you are not using the proper image block code for your images. You will need to upload your images into the "Files" tab that you can find under every page, give them a name, then use this code:

[[include component:image-block  
|name=filename or url  
|caption=Description  
|align=right  
]]

Anyways, I have some quick ways you can improve your level for you aside from the images. Here we go:

> Level 791 is the 792nd level in the backrooms.

Capitalize Backrooms, you will need to do this in every context but here is especially important.

> The lights on top of the level are the same fluorescent lights as Level 0 and the buzzing will drain the sanity from people who listen to it for too long.

Sanity isn't really a term used in this wiki much, as far as I can tell. It's much more associated with the Fandom version of the Backrooms. I won't stop you from using it, but just keep that in mind.

> Sometimes, shadowy figures can be seen outside of the window. There is also a chance for the window to be a Window entity.

A shadowy figure is the key defining feature of a Windows entity, correct, but it's a bit odd how you worded it. I would write "Sometimes, shadowy figures can be seen outside of the window. This is usually a signal that it is a Window entity."

> The edge

Capitalize "edge". Simple.

> There are no known bases, outposts or communities on this level

Add a period at the end. Also simple.

> Getting kicked out by the funky dealer in Level 714 has a chance of bringing you here.

> No-Clipping through a red wall on Level 790 can bring you to this level though only one person ever did it.

These should both be on a list for easier reading.

That's all I noticed. Take these to heart, and be sure to fix the image code. There are still a few more issues on this page, so I would suggest asking another critter or going to the Discord.

*-QuestionableRose22*
