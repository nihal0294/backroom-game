Hi. I don't usually make these, but this time I felt like it'd be weird if I didn't. Some may recognize this as my alt draft from Duelcon2025.

This is a page about a lot of things. I wrote it with a lot of things running through my mind, in a very dark place. It is the rawest thing I have ever written. I have probably called past drafts/pages experimental before, but this one takes the cake. There are a plethora of ways to interpret the meaning here, and all of them are as right as you want them to be.

There are a lot of reasons not to like this page, and I figure it might not survive. I'm not sure I really care. I still wrote it regardless.
