Level 123 - Mirror Maze

10 Jan 2021 05:42

**Page Type:** Level

**Elevator Pitch**: A red infinite maze filled with mirrors and mixed color lights that are so bright, staring into it results in blindness. Related to Level 6, except nothing will make you perfectly safe except for [[ACCESS DENIED](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/super-almond-water)].

**Central Narrative:** Entering Level 123 is a led red room that's filled with many mirrors. The further you go the brighter the lights become, when going through level 123, not knowing where your going and a chance of running into a highly hostile entity without know if it is an entity or not is a big danger. The maze is very rare for someone to exit due to its bright lights and infested entitys. Pretty much Level 123 is a death trap, period.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** Level 123 exit is impossible to be found, and is not recommended for exploration.

**Additional Notes:** I have more information on Level 123, I just don't want to add it because this isn't a draft critique.
