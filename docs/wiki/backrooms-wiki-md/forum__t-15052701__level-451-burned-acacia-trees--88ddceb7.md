Hi, .

I think it's better if I break this down, first.

[[+] Critique](javascript:;)

[[–] Critique](javascript:;)

This level is made up of a completely burned acacia forest, and some straight dirt path, although this is extremely rare. This level is immersed in an eternal day, and the sun hits it very hard, so that the ambient temperature reaches 50°C (122°F).

  
First of all, ther shouldn't be a comma after "forest".  
Second of all, "path" should be plural.  
To specify that it's always daytime, it's better to say "In this level, it's always daytime, as there is no day-night cycle". Saying that "the sun hits very hard" should be omitted.

---

About once every 12 hours, the trees begin to turn green again. One way to tell when they're going to turn green is when the ground starts to shake. If you feel this, leave the area immediately and look for a rainy area, a dirt road, or any safe zone in this Level, because when a tree turns green again, a fire starts and spread throughout the whole area. forest.

  
You should talk about the fire *before* saying that the trees will turn green again. It's also better to list the safe and dangerous zones of the level beforehand.  
"If you feel this" should be replaced with something like "If this is to occur".  
"forest."?

---

Additional: the SD seems accurate to me.

In general, the tone is closer to personal than it is to clinical, since the article is poorly organized. There are also a few minor SPaG errors.

I'm sorry if I sounded rude or mean, but the article isn't ready for a full crit.

Take care.

PS: Don't say that you are stupid. It's just not true.
