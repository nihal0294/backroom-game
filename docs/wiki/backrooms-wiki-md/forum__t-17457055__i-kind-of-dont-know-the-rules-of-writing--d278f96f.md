(My explanation may not be 100% super duper accurate, so please do ask staff questions if necessary.)  
From the beginning:  
1. Create/write your page on the [sandbox site](index.md) (if you haven't done so already)  
2. Once you believe it is ready to receive critique, post your draft to the critique subforum here on the wikidot or the Discord. This process might take a while if there are major errors in your writing. Make sure to save/screenshot any positive critiques as you need a few.  
3. Once you have received **at least** 3 overall positive crits that meet the critique reqs, you may contact a GL/GFT to request a go-ahead.  
4. IF  
~ They reject it, go back and repeat steps 2 and 3.  
~ They give a go-ahead, they'll give you a password, and you'll be able to post

If you have any further questions, ask one of the staff members. They'll probably give a more detailed response about certain aspects of the GL policy.
