Enigmatic Entity idea: The Formless One

21 Oct 2022 13:32

Alright.

After the death of E. Isle and Eden G., this guy called Trevor Moore was promoted to senior archivist of the M.E.G. He then sent a bunch of teams to [Level 51](level-51.md), as he was interested in it and wanted to look for more. And oh boy, did they find stuff. First, they found the inscription containing info abt [The Pantheon](the-pantheon.md). A few weeks later, they find *another* inscription containing a few storys abt the entity Im gonna talk abt.

## first tale:

This tale talks abt The Formless One's desc and behaviours. The entity (as said in the summary), is capable of changing *anything*, not excluding himself. He's passive and uses his abilities to create harmony across the Backrooms by chemically altering the minds of hostile entities and wanderers to become more passive. Oh, and for some reason, anyone affected by this entity never needed to eat again. Strange.

Although the formless one was never part of The Pantheon, he had close ties with members of it like Augustus. The latter actually gifted him a level (level 360) to play around with ideas, kinda like a sandbox. One of these ideas is a former, safer version of my upcoming level ([here](level-210.md)). Augustus liked it so much that he actually gave it its own plane of existence in The Backrooms and moved it out of the sandbox. Fun!

## second tale

This is the part where things go downhill. After eternities of creating harmony across the backrooms, The Formless One began to notice something. Everytime he transformed a living thing, a black, sticky mold would eventually start forming in the location of the transformation. These would soon turn into Shadowed Beings (check my upcoming level). They hated the peaceful ways of their creator and, eventually, started a war. Every single shadowed being knew that he was incredibly power. So, instead of using their normal abilities (they realized that The Formless One was immune to their neurological attacks), they would have to outsmart him. Firstly, they tried getting [Bone Thieves](entity-121.md) to get them to help. But you can guess how that turned out. Next, they tried to get him so angry that he would tear a hole in reality in rage. Although that sounded incredibly dumb, that actually worked. It caused Level 210 to become corrupt and deadly, and caused several hostile entities to target The Formless One. But behind the scenes, a shadowed being was working on a document about ascension. Along with its friend, they knew that they were going to get rid of him once and for all. Upon finishing the document, the effects of ascension took hold on the writer, and they eventually died. However, the writer's friend, who hadnt looked at the document, grabbed it and ran to the scene of the battle. With amazing accuracy, he flinged the document into the arms of The Formless One. And upon reading a single word, his ascension happened and he was dead. Level 210 was now overrun with Shadowed Beings, and it's now a literal deadzone.

**HOWEVER,** this isn't the last of him! Although [Level 555](level-555.md) is meant to be an idling place for those who die to ascension, The Formless One isn't normal, and thus, can still do several different things. Such as alter documents to tell people to avenge him. More will come soon!
