Re: Seeking Greenlight for Level 415

05 Aug 2022 04:18

> Level 415 is the 416th Level of the Backrooms.

Don't capitalize the second use of "Level" in this instance.

> It was discovered by an expedition team on April 15, 2009.

Is this information actually important for the reader to know? This seems like arbitrary details to be honest; it'd be better left replaced by a more throughout introduction to the level's environment.

> Level 415

Add more information to this caption; you can say this is "An image of Level 415's main space." or something of the sort, but just stating the level's number wont work here.

> as dizziness, and nausea

Remove the comma.

> as dizziness, and nausea1.

Put the footnote after the period.

> Most personnel at Level 415 reported

Replace "at" with "in".

> reported a distinct smell of Mint in the

Don't capitalize "Mint" here; it isn't a proper noun.

> Level 415 from another angle

Add punctuation to this caption.

> The number of books on the shelves and the quantity of hostile entities gradually increase with each floor, as higher floors tend to have more books and entities than lower floors do.

The information after the comma is totally useless to the reader; you've just stated what was already said. Remove that bit.

> Wanderers trapped in Level 415 often use these items as props.

"props" is a weird word to use here; elaborate on what you mean by that.

> to have consumed a portion of almond water,

"almond water" no longer exists; don't mention it in your page.

> throughout the day, there seems to be an

This comma should be a semicolon.

> At present, M.E.G. has not studied the composition of this gas.

If they haven't, then I'd prefer if this was just left out of the page.
