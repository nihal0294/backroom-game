(Looking for greenlight) The psychedelic experience: hallucinogenic drugs induce a state between the front and backrooms

03 Dec 2023 06:09

Looking for critique and/or greenlight.

Page Type: Level, entity

Core Concept: Psychedelic drugs allow people to temporarily (and unknowingly) enter parts of the backrooms.

In real life, high doses of psychedelic drugs, people will often fall into an all encompassing visual and sensory experience. This experience feels like a dimension made of complex geometric patterns and fractals, and at high doses this geometry can form a visual representations of thoughts, memories, concepts, emotions, and brain function that is innately understood by the user. Users may also experience ego death, which is a hallucinatory state in which an individual can no longer recognize their sense of self and existence.

At the highest levels, entities are seen. Psychedelic entities can range from simple geometric creatures running around within a psychedelic space up to fully autonomous and sentient beings than can interact with or speak to you. Often times these entities have an aura of spiritually and/or some sort of transcendence between the psychedelic realm and reality,

Psychedelic trips can also be an incredibly emotional, profound, or even life-changing in some cases. This is one of the reasons they are associated with shamans and mystics.

The idea behind this as a backrooms level would a level where wanders could spontaneously find themselves in this psychedelic realm. From the backrooms perspective, the wanderer sees those who use psychedelic drugs as they trip within this realm, but can never interact with them. They can interact with the entities though, and said entities explain that the geometry is a representation of the inner workings of the quantum and karmic forces that have interfaced with the backrooms. The entities also claim that they are guides of this space, shepherding people who visit from reality as they explore this transitional space between the front and back rooms while tripping. They also prevent these people from wandering into the backrooms via a conceptual wall, represented as an endless geometric sheet that divides the space. This wall is also what prevents entities from leaving the backrooms through this level, and for that reason the wall cannot be removed or traversed. The entities claim that what has been in the backrooms can no longer return to reality, as existing within the backrooms permanently alters a person's ability to exist within the front rooms; the mind has adapted to the reality of the backrooms, but in doing so gave up the ability to function in the frontrooms.

In this level there are only two types of entities, the sentient entities that can communicate with people, as well as "machine elves" which are more like animals and have intelligence comparable to that of a dolphin or monkey. Neither of these entities are dangerous, and are mostly aloof and at times sympathetic towards wanderers. Its implied that they may know how the backrooms work, but they've never shared that info with any wanderers.

This level can also induce states of extreme euphoria, or extreme dysphoria in wanderers depending on their state of mind when they enter the level, much in the way one's state of mind going into a psychedelic trip will influence whether or not someone has a good or bad trip.

I might be able to use that image. Maybe as a precursor room to the actual level as I want the main part of the level to have a far more intangible feel to it that doesn't feel like it can be comprehended.

I'm also kind of floating the idea that as one enters the level, it becomes more and more intense, much like the psychedelic experience intensifies with higher and higher doses. Basically, the deeper someone goes, the harder it is to keep going as they find it harder and harder to recognize themselves within the space, making it harder to go deeper and harder to retreat. With enough mental fortitude, they can break through and see the entities that inhabit the space, initially just the machine elves, but if one goes far enough, the autonomous sentient entities. If they find themselves able to comprehend and understand what the psychedelic geometry represents, and in turn understand how reality interfaces with the backrooms, they become one of those transcendent entities, able to peacefully reside within this level that sits somewhere between the backrooms and front rooms, able to interact and guide those who visit from the front rooms via psychedelic drugs.

There's also the idea that the machine elves are humans and backrooms entities that have wandered into this level, and unable (or perhaps in the case of some humans, unwilling) to leave, and as a result have taken on an alternate peaceful form of existence within the level as machine elves, no longer able to re-establish their existence within either the backrooms or front rooms, forever altered by this space between spaces.

I don't want this level to be malevolent, but not completely safe either. Rather I want it to feel like a level where some (but not all) wanderers can come to terms with their situation, an impartial environment that provides peace for those who accept it and a sense of purpose or contentment. Essentially it does what psychedelic drugs can do to people in real life. It can be a bad experience that triggers insanity, but with the right mindset it can provide affirmation and contentment to those who are ready for it.
