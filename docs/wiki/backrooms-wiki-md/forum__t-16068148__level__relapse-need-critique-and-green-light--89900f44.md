Level: Relapse (need critique and green light)

29 Jun 2023 12:30

The level itself is a small suburban 2 story home. In the living room of this home, there is a tumorous growth with no discerning physical features other than a human arm. The tumorous growth is theorized to be former victims of the level. in its hand, there appears to be a golden cigarette pack as well as a golden lighter. the tumorous creature will tell the victim not to "make the same mistake it did" and will shortly pass away after making this statement. in increments of 1 hour, the house will begin to degrade with the first hour having noticeable mold like growths on the walls and ceilings with the wall paper beginning to decay. after a long enough time of not smoking the cigarettes, the house will expand and continue to decay with the rooms and corridors becoming noticeably narrower and more claustrophobic. The formerly pleasant windows become boarded up and the house is completely decayed with maggots and fly like creatures found in the walls of every room. at this point, entities begin to roam the halls of the house. Though, it is notable that the entities will simply try to scare the explorers into using the cigarettes. The level will also begin to tempt explorers with fake exits that lead back to the cigarettes as well as entities seeming to block off "pleasant" parts of the house and it appears to explorers that their loved ones are in the "pleasant" parts of the house. if explorers choose to smoke the cigarettes then the "pleasantness" of the house will be maintained. However, the speed at which the house degrades will increase, this forces explorers to smoke more. Eventually, if the explorer smokes enough then they will have tumorous growths form around their body which will overtake any formerly human features in the body except for their arm.

(ask me any questions or if you feel the idea needs critique please feel welcome to.)
