Novote, good information and story that could have been presented better

09 Aug 2026 21:19

Nothing groundbreaking, but it's nice to read about the discovery of the Manila room and how it feels to see someone there (and not see someone outside of it).

I respect the balls to put numbers on how many people fall into L0, die there, and the probability of finding the room itself. It's nice to have something to base ourselves on now instead of speculation.

It's also fun to see pre-meg (and even pre-leaders!) lore, and see an evolution of it as the Backrooms' population got bigger and more modern. The broader Level 0 timeline is rather wacky, but it doesn't have to be a bad thing. The lore is well-used and it doesn't feel overwhelming.

As for the negatives, I wish Mary was less… Direct, sometimes. Feels like she talks to me, the reader, instead of to herself in her journal/testimony.

My main gripe is that the page seems to hesitate between a Wikipedia-style IMBH article and a more personal transcription of Mary's journey (and these parts feel more like fragments of a POI page, notably the journal). Sometimes, the Manila Room feels like a collective effort, and sometimes it feels like a one-woman effort that succeeded because she's so cool and saves so many people.

I think a lot of parts (for example, what happens after her death) would benefit more from having their writing POV from the Manila Room instead of the people who do stuff about it (for example, "With the death of Mary, volunteers were worried that they would be killed as well"). into "After the death of Mary, the Manila Room's visits became scarcer, as other volunteers grew worried about their potential fate there.").

The page is human-centric, obviously, and has both a story and a history, but having people in it doesn't mean the sentences have to be focusing on what they do (but rather on what happens to the Manila Room and then the why).

I'll novote because I think the page about the Manila room could have been written in a way that's more about the Manila Room. It's less about the information and more about how sentences are constructed. It's still an article that shows the room, what it is and what it went through which saves it from being a downvote from me.

Also this is a small note but love to see Dapper's ost used there!
