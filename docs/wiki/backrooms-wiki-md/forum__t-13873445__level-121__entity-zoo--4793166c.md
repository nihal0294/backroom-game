Level 121: Entity Zoo

24 Nov 2020 14:45

Level 121 is a sort of "Zoo" level. The appearance and layout is similar to the indoor sections of real zoos, where they keep insects in such, with entities in rooms with glass walls so you can see inside them. For unknown reasons the entities are unable to escape and the glass cannot be broken to let anything out or to get in. The rooms contain various common entities (Facelings, Hounds, Deathrats, Deathmoths, Skin Stealers, etc). The level also has "Gift Shops" with various Backroom themed items, such as Entity plush toys, T-Shirts, Children's play-sets resembling various levels one of which had packaging that read (LEVEL 0 FUN PACK. INCLUDES 2 ROOMS, TRAVELER ACTION FIGURE, AND 2 ENTITY ACTION FIGURES), Various items with the phrase "I Survived Level 121", assorted novelty items and candies, etc. These items can be taken between levels without them disappearing. There aren't any hostile entities that are able to harm travelers, however there is a new type of faceling exclusive to 121. They resemble the staff and tend to the zoo (Clean enclosures, work in the check out of the gift shop and restock).

I know this is long winded but it's an idea i've been incubating for a while. Thanks for your feedback!
