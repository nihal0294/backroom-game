## Description

---

Entity 415 are large beasts nicknamed Howlers. These entities are usually 11 feet tall, and are very lanky in structure. A Howler is easily recognizable by its glowing green eyes, black rock merged into its body, and constant low growls that the entity passively emits. These growls can be heard from a 10 foot distance, and if one hears a growl it is highly advised to move away or in the opposite direction of a Howler.

## Behavior

---

Howlers are not hostile towards wanderers, and will even run away from humans approaching them. These entities only fight back when absolutely necessary, using their hands to slash at approaching wanderers. Despite this, Howlers show incredible speed and endurance when running during a chase, even climbing on objects to gain height.

When a Howler is in an open area alone and is in danger, it will begin to convulse and hunch over, wrapping its arms around its chest. The dark rocks that cover its body will begin to grow, and after around 30 seconds it will shoot up and lean backwards to emit a ear-deafening shriek. Any animalistic, feral, or normally hostile entities within a 2 mile radius will be able to hear a scream from a Howler, and it is theorized that the Howler has a cognitive effect which "calls" other entities in the specified radius.

These entities will quickly begin to rush towards the sound of the howl, and at that point wanderers must begin to sprint **as fast as they can.** Said entities will enter an enraged state, similar to entities from Zone 3 of [Level 197](level-197.md), and will not stop chasing their target until caught. When one escapes the level they are in, the Howler will resume its drone-like state until finding another wanderer, to which the cycle repeats again.

## Biology

---

Howlers, when dissected, are very similar to humans, however there are a few changes.

[![Missing alt text.](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--resized-images/trimmed:entity-415/Howler/medium.jpg)](https://backrooms-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/trimmed:entity-415/Howler)

A sketch of a Howler, drawn by the Data Digger who first encountered one.

- Howlers' hearts will be polygonal black rocks, which beat just like a human's heart. Grinding can be heard as it pumps black blood through the being's body, assumedly the rocks of the heart rubbing together.
- The lungs of a Howler are much larger than humans, estimated to be three times bigger than that of a middle-aged human. The organs wrap around the rib cage, spine, and extend down to the pelvis and even the tailbone. The lungs are supported by more black rock, resembling webs with how thin the material was.
- Their vocal cords are much bigger and more complex than that of a human, and the neck bulges when it growls or shrieks. Cords are much thicker and more durable, allowing the high-pitched scream to last for long durations.
- The skin of Howlers is very tough, as most of the body is covered in the hard black rock mentioned prior. To cut into the skin, it took four members prying open the stomach and two cutting into the rock. The only weak points on a Howler are the head, stomach, and spine. Every other part of the body is in some degree covered by rocks.
- Howlers' eyes are oddly similar to humans, except for the fact that the whites of the eyes are black and the iris is a dull green. Inspecting the retina shows no differences, and it is not known how these entities make their eyes glow green.

Every other aspect of a Howler mirrors that of a human. The leading theory from the Repository is that a type of infectious pathogen enters the bloodstream of a bleeding / wounded human and somehow morphs the body into a Howler. More research is needed to approach a conclusive result.

## Discovery

---

While exploring the forest of [Level 220](level-220.md), a Data Digger came across a Howler alone in the clearing. When approaching it to see if the entity was peaceful, the Howler ran and climbed to the top of a hill. A log of further events is documented below.

[Log ERAF:103/E](javascript:;)

[Collapse information](javascript:;)

This log was recorded by a Data Digger who was exploring Level 220. She had turned on her recorder as the entity was running away.

**Digger:** Damn it, come back here you little-!

*She lets out an aggravated breath as she runs after the entity, and it stops atop the hill.*

**Digger:** Alright, can we talk now?

*She lets out heavy breaths from the chase, and the entity hunches over. Rocks begin to emerge from its back, and the recorder picks up this grinding noise.*

**Digger:** What the hell is going on with that thing-

*The entity snaps up and arches backward, releasing a loud shriek. The scream is loud enough to partially damage the recording device, and audio quality significantly decreases after this event. Any words partially or fully surrounded by parenthesis are assumed to have been said by any being in the tape.*

**Digger:** Holy sh(it!) [Unintelligible]

*The digger quickly runs away, and her footsteps are barely heard in the leaves. The roars and sounds of other entities are quickly heard in the tape.*

**Digger:** Oh god! What (the hell?! Where did) they come from?! I have to fucking g(et out of here!)

*Heavy breaths and loud sounds from entities are heard for the next 4 minutes and 28 seconds, with static intensely distorting sounds in the tape.*

**Digger:** Fuck, what ha(ppened to my ear? Jesus it) hurts so bad!

*At this point an entity is suspected to have injured the digger, as a distorted scream of pain comes from her.*

**Digger:** AAgh! FUCK! Christ sake my arm!

*Curses and expletives are said by the digger as she runs, entering a building.*

**Digger:** Come on, red wall, red wall… THERE!

*The digger finds a red wall and jumps through, appearing in [Level 1](level-1.md). M.E.G. operatives recognize her uniform to the Repository and notify us, and an hour later she is taken into our medical care. Currently, she is going through therapy to help heal her arm and hearing.*
