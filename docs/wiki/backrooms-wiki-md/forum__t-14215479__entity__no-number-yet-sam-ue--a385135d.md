**Page Type:** Entity

**Page Layout:** I wish to layout the page as closely to the typical backrooms format for entities.

**Elevator Pitch:** This entity is named Sam, and has a group surrounding him called the chillgoers, who almost worship him like a God. He has powerful abilities, and is usually morally good.

**Central Narrative:** A wanderer meets an entity named Sam, who takes him to his place and tells the wanderer about himself. The same wanderer later escapes to the frontrooms with the help of Sam. On the topic of Sam himself, he is very unsure of his motivations, as he is quite young, however he generally does the right thing. His cult however, is much more sure of their motivations, which is to get everyone to chill and relax. They once made a sub par effort to stop the fun war. Also, Sam has to avoid the M.E.G. from capturing him because he can unlock levels.

**Hook/Attention-Grabber:** A cult of chillgoers worship a somewhat powerful and well meaning entity named Sam.

**Additional Notes:** I also plan to make 3 more levels in my overarching narrative, and I'm currently introducing this story on the official backrooms subreddit.

"Entities that cause people to worship them" has been [done](entity-7.md) [before](entity-180.md) on this site, so your story needs to stand out, and I trust you will do that. However, there is a few issues you need to address:

> This entity is named Sam, and has a group surrounding him called the chillgoers, who almost worship him like a God.

I think you may need a more creative name, friend. Also, why do they worship Sam as a god?

> A wanderer meets an entity named Sam, who takes him to his place and tells the wanderer about himself. The same wanderer later escapes to the frontrooms with the help of Sam.

There are two things wrong with this statement. One, you never specify what "his place" is. Is it a level? A sub-level? An entirely different plane of existence? Specification is really needed here. The second problem is the massive general canon break. Nobody has found a way out of the Backrooms, and nobody will for a long, long time, especially some random entity worshiped by a cult.

> On the topic of Sam himself, he is very unsure of his motivations, as he is quite young, however he generally does the right thing.

Who exactly is this talking about? Who is "unsure of" the other's motives? Who is "quite young"? Who generally "does the right thing?" I know I'm bouncing on vague details, but I am a person for information.

> His cult however, is much more sure of their motivations, which is to get everyone to chill and relax. They once made a sub par effort to stop the fun war.

These are basically clones of the Partypoopers. Friendly to everyone, goal to calm down everyone in the Backrooms, and fought in the Fun War, of all places? You may need to change their goals and their personalities, just to avoid lifting of of them.

> Also, Sam has to avoid the M.E.G. from capturing him because he can unlock levels.

The M.E.G does not capture people. They are researchers, and if anything they would stay away from Sam. Also, what do you mean by "he can unlock levels"?

> A cult of chillgoers worship a somewhat powerful and well meaning entity named Sam.

That is just a summary of the cult, and not a very good attention grabber.

Overall, while I do see a well thought-out framework, vague words, general canon breaks and copying of previous entities just makes this fall apart. I suggest focusing more on Sam himself, his motivations, and his abilites.

**P.S:** I would also like to see the reddit post if that is okay.
