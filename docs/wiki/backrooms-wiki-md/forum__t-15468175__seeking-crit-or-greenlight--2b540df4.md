Hello.  
well,  
here some points of improvements.

**Good Boys are dog like creatures that have varied mental and psychological effects depending on the "breed" of the entity.**  
=> really interesting concept:  
to my opinion, you can even go a bit further.

**Good Boys seem to be a subspecies of a common pet in the Frontrooms commonly referred to as "dog", and seems to have a grey, void-like inside.**

Are dogs like good boys or good boys like dogs?  
You can even make people completely doubt the true origin of dogs.  
maybe even make dogs a subspecies of good boys or make it unclear.

**Good Boys have 6 Variations, which all have drastic mental effects depending on the breed, ranging from completely harmless, to mentally defining.**

well, the husky attack hounds and some of the good boys are clearly beneficial with the exception of chihuahuas.  
Yet,  
if you're going in that direction, you can tell that for every good characteristic that a good boy can bring a bad one can compensate this.

Also, are the characters traits brought by the good boys temporary or permanent?

What happens to someone who have many good boys?

**They seem to trust any human they encounter and, are immune to Crawler Fungus. It also seems that killing one will replace your blood with unboiled Memory Juice, almost instantly killing the victim.**

The defense mechanism is interesting yet has many limitations.  
-First, not every entities are sentient enough to realize that by killing a good boy, they're killing themselves.  
-Secondly, thanks to human greed and the rarity of memory juice, i can see how this can be horrifyingly exploited.

Here are my points for now.

I'm a dog person, so I'm positively biased towards this.

- **Good Boys that appear like Golden Retrievers, are incredibly lazy, and will only work with a reward.** Simple fix. The comma after "Golden Retriever" isn't needed.
- **Good Boys that have the appearance of Chihuahuas are very dangerous, due to the fact that they quickly drain ones sanity until becoming a Wretch.** Again, delete the comma. Also, you should specify that the Wanderer becomes a Wretch. Also, how do they lose their minds? There are a lot of different types of insanities, so take a look at it.
- **Poodles are seemingly normal compared to the other variants with only a confidence boost.** Add a comma after "variants".
- **Siberian Huskies are a breed that is a finely tuned weapon that can be used against Hounds.** It's not quite grammatically correct. Perhaps rewrite it to "Siberian Huskies are a breed of fine tuned weapons that can be used against Hounds."
- **Good Boys seem to be a subspecies of a common pet in the Frontrooms commonly referred to as "dog", and seems to have a grey, void-like inside.** "Void-like inside" doesn't match the analytical tone used here. Perhaps use "and they do not seem to have internal organs. All that Wanderers have reported seeing is a sort of "fog" in their place." Add a little mystery to it.
- **Thus no one knows how they can live without any internal organs.** Again, simple fix. Add a comma after "Thus".
- **They seem to trust any human they encounter and, are immune to Crawler Fungus.** Delete the comma.
- **Good Boys were discovered in Level 10 in the form of a Golden Retriever variant, spotted fighting one of the many Archers, by the MEG.** Put a period after "variant" and start a new sentence. "It was spotted by the M.E.G. fighting one of the many Archers."
- **It was killed on sight as it was confused for a Hound, until it discovered that it was a different entity, leading to the death of MEG Operative Mike.** Rewrite: "Being mistaken for a Hound, it was killed on sight by a M.E.G. operator. Only afterward was it discovered to be a different entity, however, the operator that killed the entity had already died."

I love this a lot, and it's only slightly biased. I love dogs too, so I say go for it!
