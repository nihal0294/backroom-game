Entity Number: 251  
Habitat - Commonly found at Level 251 but patrols can be found on various levels

Entity 251 resembles soldiers, they wear airtight uniforms, gas masks, and full head covering helmets leaving no skin exposed; their camo changes depending on the environment you find them in from city camo to snow camo.  
They have the B.D.C. (Backrooms Defense Coalition) logo on their left shoulder, which appears to be two rifles positioned to look like an "X" and a shield behind the rifles. They wield their country’s standard issue rifle, this allows high variety among them. They posses an advanced knowledge of no-clipping and use it on a regular basis. Humans can't use their weapons either; they seem to jam whenever anything except an Entity 251 attempts to use them. Only around 45% of Entity 251 are armed with any sort of gun as the rest use melee weapons, this appears to be due to the loss of a factory level that produced their guns. Navy versions of these entities are sometimes found on Level 17, and medic variants are sometimes found on the Level 14 level, but both are rare.

Behaviors  
Entity 251 instances are incredibly friendly to wanderers and other non -hostile entities, especially Blanche. They have been known to offer supplies even if it is their last batch of supplies; they are however, incredibly hostile towards hostile entities and will not think twice about killing the entity down; Entity 251 will attack hostile wanderers as well but seem to forget about this after about a day. Entity 251 are found in only small groups of around 6-15 soldiers depending on how dangerous a level is. They are always hostile towards Entity 187 no matter what, this has caused bloody battle to take place near high population centers when the two sides encounter each other.

Biology  
Entity 251 shares an incredibly similar biology to humans, there is no difference between us and them in way, shape, or form. They bleed like humans, they have the same internal organs, their skin and bones appear to be the only difference as it seems to be tougher enabling entity 251 to withstand stronger attacks making them less fragile than a regular human. They eat and need to urinate and defecate. Entity 251 is unable to experience Wretched Cycle.

Variants  
They aren’t completely different but specialize in different things.

**Soldier** The most versatile and common of all variants. They use long and short swords frequently to preserve ammo for more desperate situations

**Medic** Their job is to keep Entity 251's friends healed, they wield pistols, SMG’s and short swords, identifiable by their red cross on their helmet.

**Heavy Assault** A semi-common variant found when in more dangerous levels (Class 3 levels and above usually). They aren’t found on many safe levels and are incredibly aggressive and it is best to stay out of their way. Identifiable by the extra padding they wear.

**Marine Marines** are experts at aquatic combat but are just as dangerous above ground. Marines are used as special operations soldiers frequently and are used against the Masked Maidens. They are identifiable by their all black gear. They are one of two variants that can lack gas masks.

**Breacher** Breachers are Close Quarters Combat (CQC) experts and usually wield axes or shotguns and carry explosives, Identifiable by full front body padding.

**Sniper** Less common since most of the Backrooms are CQ and narrow hallways. Identifiable because they wear little to no armor.

**Desert** Found in desert levels or levels were the heat is extreme. They wear combat suits that keep them cool and hydrated in bleak conditions. Identifiable by their bulky combat suits.

**Snow** Found in snow levels or levels where the temperature drops into the negatives. They wear combat suits that keep them warm. Identifiable by their pitch white bulky combat armor.

**Pyro** Pyro's wield flame throwers and Molotov cocktails. They are crowd control and used to clear out entity infestations. They are identifiable by their smoke covered uniform and gel tank on their back.

**Officer** A rare variant, officers seem to boost morale of any other variant around them. They are commonly the last line of defense against masked maiden breaches as they guard the files. They know a lot about the masked maidens and are therefore frequently targeted by them. identifiable by a star on their helmet and their weapon, they are the only ones who use revolvers and Winchester rifles. They are one of two variants that can lack gas masks.

**Pilots** They fly helicopters, planes, and are armed with pistols, SMG's, and combat knife for CQC, they are identifiable by their pilot outfits.

**Mechanics** Mechanics keep everything running with their technical skills. Mechanics wear different colored jumpsuits since they specialize in a variety of tasks from bomb defusing to fixing tanks. They are armed with a SMG for self defense purpose and a combat knife. They are identifiable by their colored jumpsuits.

**Scout/Pathfinder** Scouts are the vanguard of entity 251. Scouts explore new areas ahead of main groups. Armed with scout rifles and pistols. They are identifiable by their light combat armor.

**Zero G** These guys are found in space levels on occasion and are probably the rarest as they only operate in zero G environments but are sometimes found in areas where the gravity is a fair bit lighter than we are use, they are identifiable by their combat space armor.

**Major General or MG** The leader of Entity 251, they are elected by a tribunal of all the highest officers, the current one is 'Georgia' and has been for the past 15 years, the MG can step down at any time. The MG has the full might of Entity 251 at their disposal. They are identified by their unique heavy armor which sports some brighter colors making them easier to find in a crowd.

**Mech pilot** Trained to pilot entity 251's 13 foot tall mechs, all 3 mechs have been rendered inoperable after a massive battle. The pilots can seen lounging around installation 12 with little to do.

**Admiral** Little is known about this entity variant but is believed to be a navy counterpart to the MG, since entity 251 does not currently own any water levels he has not been seen.

Discovery  
Entity 251 were discovered on Level 251 which also happened to be the level they appear to originate from. M.E.G. Operative Lock made first contact while attempting to return to Level 1 and no clipped in the wrong area by accident and landed him in Level 251, specifically in the middle of a group of Entity 251. Reports from the formation of M.E.G. have shown evidence that these soldiers have a large presence on Level 1, whether they still do is up for debate.

Relations with G.O.I.  
This was part of the article was added after the original article was written since they're relation's with G.O.I.'s can get confusing.

**M.E.G.** The M.E.G. have a good relation with Entity 251 and work together on a regular basis allowing Entity 251 to have extensive info on discovered levels and officers are commonly found at large M.E.G. bases looking through new files.

**Masked Maidens** The relation here is questionable. The Masked Maidens and Entity 251 have gone at it before in full blown fighting but are not openly hostile to each other as entity 251 are not the Masked Maidens targets, they are just protecting their targets. They try to avoid killing Entity 251 if possible.

**U.E.C.** Hostile relations, entity 251 have launched raids against U.E.C. Compounds. The purpose of these raids is unknown.

**The Lost** Entity 251 tends to trade with the lost on a regular basis for rare resources and sometimes objects we have yet to identify.

**The B.N.T.G.** They sport a good relationship with each other for trading purposes. What Entity 251 trades for is a secret between the B.N.T.G. and Entity 251.

**Eyes of Argo** An increasingly hostile relationship and is now risking all out war against the eyes of Argo despite attempts to defuse the situation as entity 251 mobilizes.

**Followers of Jerry** They will kill followers on sight, Entity 251 sees them as an infection that must be cut away to continue progress.

Do’s and Don’ts

Do:  
Be open and friendly to them and offer them knowledge of the area and they will in return offer directions to nearest exit or outpost with lists of exits for the area.  
Ask for supplies even if their running low, they will always offer some if you ask.  
Stay with them if you want to go to Level 251 as they can always find their way home, no matter where the are.  
Address them by name, they each have a unique name and if you know it, use it.  
Don’t:  
Attack them, they will not hesitate to kill attackers.  
Steal, they won’t kill you but will not help you unless your life is in danger.  
Be rude, they may speak in military lingo most of the time but they know manners and don’t like dealing with people who won’t respect them.
