Level 165 - The Garden of Eden

11 Oct 2020 21:23

**Page Type:** Level

**Genre:** Undefined

**Page Layout:** Same ol’ Layout

**Elevator Pitch:** Level 165 is a seemingly infinite garden outlined with a white picket fence. Level 165 houses all species within the Kingdom of Plantae including trees, household plants, cacti, fungi etc.

**Central Narrative:** Level 165 was discovered on ██/██/████ by Jeremy ██████ and Thomas ███████ while in Level 9. Jeremy and Thomas ran into a house to escape from an instance of the Mangled. To pass the time, Jeremy explored the house and stumbled upon the back-door and accessed Level 165.

**Attention-Grabber:** This is the first garden-like level.
