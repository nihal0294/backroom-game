#### Description & Properties

Dr. Lambert and I are engineering a new high-velocity rifle platform. Since we need a weapon capable of launching a projectile at very high speed; a built-in particle acceleration technology will be implemented, to maximize the projectile velocity while cancelling the considerable amount of excess energy generated from the shot thanks to an energy generation & absorption system based on [Object-51](object-51.md): "Pockets" and Object-770-C: "The Crystal of Chaos".

As seen in the weapon blueprint, the two main capsules contain an instance of Object-770-C for power generation, with some mystical matter from Paradise 770 to cool the generated heat, and a modified instance of Object-51, for energy excess absorption. Once the trigger is pulled and the projectile propelled, the smaller capsules containing a miniaturized particle acceleration technology will amplify the projectile's velocity exponentially.

According to the data from simulations, the effective range is estimated to exceed over 2 million kilometers, with an initial projectile velocity of 299,774 kilometers per second[1](javascript:;). No one, not even a god, could evade a shot from this weapon.

---

#### The Scourge of Olympus

Doctor Blackwell, on his part, is tasked to engineer a special projectile that can withstand the speed of light. The projectile will also host a sample of a special ADN-based virus he himself conceived that he refers to as "The Scourge of Olympus". If at least 250mg of the virus enters in contact with the skin of a godlike being, the scourge will start to penetrate the skin and expand inside the body. The infected tissues will slowly but surely deteriorate, and after 3 minutes, all that will be left is a puddle of blood and fluids.
