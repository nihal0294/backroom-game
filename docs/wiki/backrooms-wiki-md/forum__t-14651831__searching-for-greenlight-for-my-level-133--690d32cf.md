This article seems a bit bland and vague. There could be more.

Now, I'm no greenlighter nor have I posted an article, but I felt like I've read enough articles to be able to crit *decently* well. I'm bad at catching SPaG errors, so don't depend on me on that. If you wish, do take this with a grain of salt though.

As said above, this level could really use a unique twist, as this article currently just seems like another one of those generic town levels that feels metaphorically empty. But I do feel that that should be left to your own creativity.

Ah yes, flow issue. Excess of periods, not enough comma, single paragraph.

> Level 133 is an infinite town with the buildings mostly being places of worship. There are a variety of different places of worship. Many places of worship that can be found in the Level are resembling places of worship from the Frontrooms such as churches, mosques, synagogues, mandirs, viharas , etc.

This could be changed to:

> Level 133 is an infinite town where most of the buildings are places of worship with a great diversity of religious buildings that includes, but not limited to: churches, mosques, synagogues, mandirs, and viharas.

This change makes it less cumbersome to read. With the previous one being so 'detached', it can be time-consuming to try recalling precise details from many sentences before. Now, each related details flow *somewhat smoothly* to the next.

---

> The climate in Level 133 sits at 15°C. There is a normal day and night cycle like on earth.

This could be changed to:

> There is a day and night cycle consistent to the one on Earth, but despite the fact that temperature typically drops down in the absence of sunlight, the climate in Level 133 stays consistent at 15°C regardless of time.

This change also resolves vague points.

---

> Most places of worship on the level are in a good condition but some of them are damaged or even completely broken down. Almond Water can be found in fountains and in some rivers.

The latter sentences should be in a new paragraph as the topic shifts from the religious buildings to the general infrastructure of the level.

---

> In the places of worship, there will also be facelings who are not hostile either. They will pray and have ceremonies.

This could be changed to:

> Friendly facelings reside in the places of worship, who spend their time praying and having ceremonies.

A more active voice puts emphasis on the facelings.

---

There is much more that I could talk about this, but that would be a lot for one post (and I'm lazy lol), so try to consider the flow- how you want your reader to explore the article like they are exploring the level. Where should they start? What catches their eye the most the first time? What would they notice upon further exploration? etc etc.
