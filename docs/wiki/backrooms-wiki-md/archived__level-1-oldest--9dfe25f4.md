**Level 1** is the 2nd Level of the Backrooms.

It is roughly the same as [Level 0](level-0.md), with only a few major differences.

# **Description:**

Exact descriptions of **Level 1** vary somewhat, though most major details remain the same. The floor of this level has been described as being many things, like grass, sand, pavement, etc. But the grimy concrete walls and low-hanging fog are almost always the same. In some places, the walls have been poorly painted with scenery to match the floor, but no one knows why.

However, the most common "theme" in **Level 1** is similar to the mono-yellow design of [Level 0](level-0.md). The key differences are concrete walls instead of wallpaper, tile floors instead of carpet, and dimmer fluorescent lights. Beings typically lurk at or above **Level 1**. Known beings on **Level 1** include [Dullers](entity-6.md), [Adult Facelings](entity-9.md), [Hounds](entity-8.md), and [Skin-Stealers](entity-10.md).

There are multiple ways of reaching **Level 1**. Like other levels, it can be reached randomly with stairs, elevators, and hallways, and one also has a small chance of landing here when No-Clipping into the Backrooms. In addition, at any point, there's a chance the lights will begin to flicker on and off. Eventually, the lights will turn off for minutes or hours at a time. While the lights are off, this Level becomes very dangerous, so bring a light source if you're exploring.

Level 1 is home to [The Hub](http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/secret-the-hub).

# **Colonies And Outposts**

## [the-b-n-t-g](the-b-n-t-g.md) 'Traders Keep':

- Traders Keep is a huge city complex.
- Has around 412 Inhabitants.
- Has working city departments, like police, and fire departments.
- Traders Vault is the main place for all of the supplies for The Backrooms's Wanderers.
- Traders Vault is set up in The [Storage Hall](traders-vault.md) in **Level 1**.
- Keeps communications with [The M.E.G.](the-m-e-g.md)

# **Entrances And Exits:**

###### Entrances

Get here from [Level 0](level-0.md). In [Level 11](level-11.md), if you walk into a building, you may end up here. If you climb one of the trees in [Level 11.1](level-11-1.md) you will have a chance of entering **Level 1**. Open one of the doors in [Level 4.1](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-4-1) to enter this Level. Enter one of the unlocked doors in [Level 54](level-54.md) to get to **Level 1**. Walking through one of the doors in [Level 82](level-82.md) can lead you to **Level 1**. The end of the fifth event in [level-92](trimmed__level-92--cb3388d4.md) can lead you here. You can get here after completing The King's trial in [Level 94](level-94.md). Noclip through any ceiling in [Level 283](level-283.md)'s Lounges to get to **Level 1**

###### Exits

You can leave **Level 1** by simply continuing to explore the Backrooms. **Level 1** usually leads to [Level 2](level-2.md) or back to [Level 0](level-0.md). In **Level 1**, it is possible to find "Holes" in the walls that will lead to [Level 19](level-19.md) which are very rare. Entering a dark wall can rarely lead to [Level 28](level-28.md). Rarely, you may be transported to [Level 38](level-38.md) randomly. Noclip through the ceiling and you will be at a party! =)
