# **Level 5.5 (Level 5 - Sector B)**

My criticism, and the first thing I say is that I recommend that you do it in a sandbox separate from yours, in which you save your created levels.

[Your biggest fear at Level 5.5 is...](javascript:;)

[Criticism of the draft...](javascript:;)

> Level 5 - Sector B is the 5.5 level of The Backrooms.

Try changing this opening sentence to "Level 5.5 is the Level 5.6 of the Backrooms." I assure you it sounds better (optional).

> This level is, well, no one knows what it is. The level changes to whatever your deepest fear is. The level when an M.E.G. Employee photographed it was just pitch black. We have no clue what entities are really on the level. The M.E.G will continue to monitor this level for any changes in the level itself. Below is an interview with a wanderer and an M.E.G. Employee about this level.

Let's see, take out the period after the "G" in the parts that say "MEG". Put this [[[]]] on every part that says "MEG".

> James McGarlion: I accidentally noclipped through the wall of level 5

Tip, each name of the person in a recording must have **double asterisk on the sides**, so as not to cause confusion. And put "Level 5" with [[[]]].

> James McGarlion: No, I usually see levels full of people, it was weird as when I asked someone else that came onto the level they said that it was basically like level 7, well except the fact that the level was much bigger.

Put "Level 7" with [[[]]].

> M.E.G Employee: I may as well ask, was there any almond water on the level?

James McGarlion: No, oddly there was no almond water on the level.  
M.E.G Employee: Do you know why there was no almond water?  
Put "Almond Water" and [[[]]]

I ask you a question about bases, outposts and communities… Are you sure of your decision? Are you sure that any group can be placed within a level that has no specific form?

> You may noclip through a wall to get to this level anywhere on level 5

On level 4, you can leave the building to get to this level.  
Put "Level 5" and "Level 4" with [[[]]].

> You can find a white door and head through it to go back to level 5 or 4 (it changes via how you entered).

Put "Level 5" with [[[]]] (also at number 4).
