# **Description**

> <Text Editor/Automatically Saved File Recorded by a lost anonymous M.E.G. member.>

…………

> What is this place？

### This is a twisting and endless clean white hallway，the clean white lights hang upon the ceiling that is reachable by hand, waiting for your arrival.

> Why is it clean white here?

### Because it has no color.

> Why does it have no color here?

### Actually，this place is colorful. You should realize this fact: white light is the summation of all colors.

> This place is not the clean white in my imagination.

### Please ignore the black stains on the floors and walls; they will become colorful and vibrant.

> Why did I arrive here?

### Because your arrival became richly colorful, your arrival brightened and colorized it.

> Should I leave?

### Your departure will make this place lose color again, you should not leave this place.

> It's as if the surroundings gained color.

### This is to welcome you. This is all thanks your arrival.

![IMG_20220202_192350.jpg](https://backrooms-wiki-cn.wikidot.com/local--files/level-c-5/IMG_20220202_192350.jpg)
> Should I stay?

### Of course. You will become as colorful as this place.

> The color is spreading…

### It is very wonderful. Wonderful violets bloom beneath your feet. Ultramarine seeps into the sky, jade green embellishes and interweaves within, and pale yellow gives this place a piece of life, with a touch of scarlet reflecting your vibrant image.

![notepad1651073723351.jpg](https://backrooms-wiki-cn.wikidot.com/local--files/level-c-5/notepad1651073723351.jpg)
> The color is becoming more vibrant…

### It is very great. All color in the world is made by it; the world also becomes more colorful because of it. The world becomes more colorful because of you.

> My color is fading.

### Everything is fading; only color is growing.

> I am covered by endless color.

### That place is your warm bed. When you rebloom that day, you will become filled with color again.

![IMG_20220427_233533.jpg](https://backrooms-wiki-cn.wikidot.com/local--files/level-c-5/IMG_20220427_233533.jpg)
> Will I feel lonely?

### No, this place also has them, to be with you; you will not be lonely.

> I will be integrated into it…

### Become one with the colors, this place is your forever-staying affection…

> \*Log Interrupted\*
