1. The level's idea is very, very bland. Having a 'void' level has already been done multiple times (such as [Level 600](level-600.md) and the now-deleted [Level 907](https://web.archive.org/web/20210226191934/http://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-907).  
2. The writing style that you've used has already been heavily milked. Some examples of that writing style are [Level 99](level-99.md) and [Level 136](level-136.md).  
3. The document has multiple SPaG errors, mostly capitalization. I suggest you use a grammar checker to get those cleared.  
4. 'Sanity' is no longer a mechanic used in the wiki. Please remove any mentions of it it from your page.
