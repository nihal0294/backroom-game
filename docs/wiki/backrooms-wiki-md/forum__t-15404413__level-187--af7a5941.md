# Level 187 - "The Flower Shop"

---

Class 4

Level 187 is the 188th level in the backrooms.

# Description:

"Here in this level you can't die. Here in this level you will stay. Look into its eyes, stare, let it transform you. You can't stop it, enjoy it, you will become one of us now." Before you look into its eyes you look at the flowers. They aren't flowers anymore this Thing is deforming them. "Now let go, enjoy it, enjoy the last moments of your life."

The Level is an old flower shop. The walls aren't the best and it smells like moist carpet even though [Level 0](level-0.md) is the only level that has moist carpets as we know of.

# Entities:

The only entity known to live here is the Flower. The Flower is a beast that if you look into its eyes turn you into a follower of The Leaf Cult.

# Outposts and/or Communites:

The Leaf Cult

# Entrances

Go to the bottom of a river in [Level 186](level-186.md)

# Exits

There are no found exits to Level 187.
