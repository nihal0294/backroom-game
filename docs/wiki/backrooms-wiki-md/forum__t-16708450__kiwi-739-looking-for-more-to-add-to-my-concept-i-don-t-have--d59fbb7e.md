###### Kiwi-739 is the 740th level in the backrooms.

# Description:

##### Kiwi-739 is one of the less popular levels of the backrooms, but there is a chance you might stumble upon another wanderer. Stay with them. Do not leave them. You will need them. They can help you find resources for levels later on. If you exit the level, they will come with you.

##### Kiwi-739 (better known as K-739) is a heavily forested level in the backrooms. It has 3 main sections that vary in safety. Most of this level is relatively safe with most of it being quiet and calming. There are several waterfalls around the level.

#### Section 1: Green Zone

##### This is the safest and calmest part of this level. There are no entities in this section. This is where you will feel calmer and be at your best. you can find almond water, cashew water, and other helpful resources.

#### Section 2: Blue Zone

##### This section is slightly less safe, but still relatively secure. The only entities are present are facelings. Besides the facelings and being less safe, not much is known about this level. This is the rarest section.

#### Section 3: Purple Zone

##### This is the least safe part of K-739. Entities vary from Facelings to Clumps to Smilers. Section 1 makes you feel calmer and safer, well this section does the opposite. You will feel more stressed and on edge.

# Bases, Outposts and Communities:

#### There are no known bases, outposts or communities on this level.

# Entrances And Exits

#### Entrances: The only way to enter K-739 is to climb the other waterfall in level 27.

# Exits:

#### Going behind a waterfall in this level will take you to level 27.
