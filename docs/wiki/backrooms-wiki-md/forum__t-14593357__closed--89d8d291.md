### SPaG (Spelling, Punctuation, and Grammar)

Overall there are a lot of grammar errors, I suggest the [Hemingway Editor](index.md) to check your page for errors.

> Level 682 is the 683’d Level of the Backrooms.

Level 682 should be **bolded**.[1](javascript:;)  
"683'd" should be 683rd.

> Level 682 is a small area just about 5 square miles of storage sheds, gates and hallways.

***Level 682** is a relatively small area of approximately five (5) square miles. This level is a conglomerate of storage sheds, gates, and hallways.*

Skipping two paragraphs, as they are discussed in Concept, Tone & Clarity.

**Bases, Outposts and Communities**

> There are no bases due to the unsafe nature of this level

Add a period, and reword.  
*Due to the unsafe nature of this level, there are no bases, outposts, or communities.*

**Entrances And Exits**

> Walk backward through Level 428 for 12 miles. You will find the end of the hallway and another door. This door will transport you to Level 682

*Walk backwards in [Level 428](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-428) for twelve (12) miles; after this, there will be a door that leads to **Level 682**.*

### Concept, Tone & Clarity

Overall, the page greatly lacks a clinical tone,[2](javascript:;) and should therefore be reformatted to suffice.

> It is also one of the few levels that the only escape is more pain.

Clarify what you mean by "the only escape is more pain," I notice a =) later in the Exits section. I assume this means that the only exit is to [Level Fun](level-fun.md), but it would help for that to be clarified.

> Behind each corner could be anything really, Almond Water, food, A face in a piece of wood that looks humanoid that sucks you into the wood slowly killing you with splinters before it consumes you? You never know.

Lacks clinical tone.  
*Corners can contain anything varying from [Almond Water](object-1.md), to hostile entities, such as [Woodlins](entity-45.md).*

> Now you may ask, “What about the Kites?”, to that I answer, there are none. Even though this level is completely outside, the kits don’t even want to exist here as it’s pitch black in the sky as well as a weird fog which makes it impossible to traverse this level while knowing what is in front of you.

Lacks Clinical tone.  
*Despite this level being outdoors, there have been no sightings of [Warning Kites](entity-22.md) within **Level 682**. There is also extremely dense fog, which makes it difficult to see here.*

> Another question you may ask if you were paying attention would be “Well what’s in the storage shed?”. The answer to that would be human bodies, Smilers, Skin Stealers or Clumps.

*All storage sheds within **Level 682** have been reported to contain either hostile entities, or human corpses.*

> So it is highly recommended to not go in the storage containers as the best thing you could find would be rotting human bodies. Also if you think “Well that would be good as you could go in and take supplies off of them.”, Then you're wrong. These bodies are rotting piles of flesh with nothing sustainable or helpful in them. The most you’ll get from them is a sickness to keep you company.

*It is highly recommended to not enter any storage sheds found, due to nothing of value inside. All corpses have been discovered extremely late into the decaying process, and have no supplies nearby to them.*
