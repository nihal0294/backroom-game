SEEKING GREENLIGHT: Tape Recorder

28 Jan 2022 12:06

Page Type: Object

Genre: N/A

Core Concept:  
The tape recorder is an object that can occasionally be found in Room XXX (The Musician's Room). It can also be given out by The Musician themself if they are particularly fond of someone or if they recognize that someone is struggling to get through the Backrooms for whatever reason.

[+ show block](javascript:;)

[– hide block](javascript:;)

This object appears to be like a normal Onn. Cassette Recorder in appearance and works almost the same as one that would be found in the Frontrooms. The only exception is that the "Record" and "Eject" buttons do not work.

When played, it plays music that sounds nearly identical to the ones that play when The Musician is present. They also seem to have a very similar quality: The majority of entities will either become docile or passive in the presence of the music or will avoid it entirely. However, its effects don't work on every entity in the Backrooms, so it's not a 100% foolproof solution.

It has been found to be susceptible to the majority of things that a regular tape recorder would be susceptible to, but is still slightly more resistant to damage.
