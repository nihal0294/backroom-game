Really like the state of the page right now! The change made this page a lot more enjoyable, a lot better than it was. My critique is below.

[+ Critique](javascript:;)

[- Critique](javascript:;)

The change you added to this page was pretty well done, I enjoy it a lot. The section about the Toothy Ones was very intriguing, was a pretty good hook for their eventual entity page, I really wanted to learn more about them. It added a layer of depth to the level and pretty much fixed my problem with the page.

The second paragraph seems pretty odd when compared to the rest of the page. I can't tell if you're trying to insinuate that the level is alive, and if it is it adds a lot to the page which should be expanded beyond that single paragraph.

My 6/10 is now a 8/10, definitely deserving an upvote.

The rest of my issues with the page are pretty much grammar nit picks, some of which I've listed down below:

"-with white paint that has itself decayed to nearly faint white streaks," This chunk is clunky to read, I've rewritten it to fix that issue: "-with white paint that has decayed and now only faint white streaks,"

"The ceiling is always slanted, as to cater to how real roofs of households leave attics to have a slanted ceiling." Saying that it caters to real roofs is odd wording and doesn't align with its actual meaning, I've rewritten it to fix that issue: "The ceiling is slanted in all parts of this level, similar to that of a suburban house."

You capitalize the words "Wanderer" and "Communities" a lot, which isn't grammatically correct.
