[I lied, you've just got critique.](javascript:;)

After reading your article, I noted several problems with it. There are several grammar, spelling, and repetition mistakes in your level. Also, in several parts of it, the wording is very awkward. I'll break it down paragraph by paragraph. I'm guessing the level is essentially a ghost town in a winter setting based on the description. I won't say you need to fix something multiple times; I will only say it once.

> "Level 301" or better known as "Dark Path" is a supernatural place that has a dark view of a wide highway old houses on the right and left. It is estimated that the temperature in this place reaches 3°C to -1°C with snowy weather and ligthning everywhere"

First of all, you need to include a comma between "Level 301" and "or", and "Dark Path" and "is". "dark view of a wide highway old houses" definitely seems weird saying it aloud. I think you meant to include the word "with" between "highway" and "old houses".  
In your next sentence, it is not necessary to include "It is estimated that". You said later that MEG scientists had visited the level. One of the things they would likely do is take the temperature of the place. You should try wording it better, as you know almost everything about the level since you are writing the document(or more than most people who have not been to it). You also need a period at the end of every sentence that is not a question or exclamation.

> "Level 301" has the characteristics of a place that is quite scary, starting from cracked roads, broken and disordered sidewalks, flashing and destroyed street lights, even the houses on either side of the road have also been destroyed even though according to Prof. [CENSORED] Previously, this place was in "The Frontrooms" or the world of Reality that we live in, but due to a distortion of place and time, this place finally moved accidentally to this supernatural place"

Definite problems with this one. It is one run-on sentence. Additionally, there is no need to tell your reader that the level seems quite scary. That would be breaking formality and the level instantly seems not scary. I recommend changing the comma between "lights" and "houses" to a period and changing your wording in the next sentence. Additionally, just because you can't come up with the name of someone doesn't mean you should censor it. This isn't the foundation. In universe, this information is being used to save someone's life, and the reader would find all the "censorship" irritating. I suggest you use a name generator if you can't come up with a name. I have done so before.  
Get rid of "or the world of Reality that we live in". Most of us already know what the frontrooms are. I think you meant to say "space and time" instead of "place and time". You mistyped lightning. Replace "this place finally moved accidentally to this supernatural place"(you seem to repeat place a lot, replace it with a synonym or the level name) with something like "the town clipped into to the backrooms, leaving [something of your choice here]." I say you also add the name of the town, as it is bound to be somewhere in the level.

> "Level 301" has its own unique place, namely it only has 1 Entity and only has 2 Days and 2 Weather, namely Nights with Snowy Weather and Evenings with Rainy Weather. For Entities, the name of the Entity is "Dark Smile Guy" [FULL EXPLANATION IN THE ENTITY SECTION]

I have no idea what you mean by "Level 301" has its own unique place". You should elaborate more. Refrain from using numbers unless referring to temperature or the name of the level. Say "two" instead of "2", and "one" instead of "1".  
"Nights with Snowy Weather and Evenings with Rainy Weather." Don't capitalize letters in the middle of a sentence unless it is a title.

> For Entities, the name of the Entity is "Dark Smile Guy"

Please change the name of your entity. "Dark Smile Guy" is way too simple. Also, based on the later description, it is basically a murder machine that kills you for no apparent reason, which makes the whole article less interesting. The reader would want to know its motives, or if it even has a motive. You said it was a "Guy", which makes me wonder if it is intelligent, or has some kind of reason for doing what it does. I suggest diving into the entity more.

> "Level 301" also has a secret place called "The Ground Room". In this place you can also ride a Steam Train which you can move with the help of the Instructions on the train. The only way to enter "The Ground Room" is by dropping to Asphalt Road at Level 301 and get out of "The Ground Room" by taking the Train

Yeet "also" out of your first sentence, as you repeat it too much, ten times to be exact, in your level.  
"In this place you can also ride a Steam Train which you can move with the help of the Instructions on the train." I don't see why someone would leave instructions on a train; the person driving the train wouldn't need a reminder on how to do it, they should be experienced enough.  
"The only way to enter "The Ground Room" is by dropping to Asphalt Road at Level 301 and get out of "The Ground Room" by taking the Train"  
I'm not sure what you're trying to say here. Elaborate more.

> "Beginning in 1888, an employee of the Old Station named [CENSORED] requested a repair letter to be given to the city government [CENSORED] to immediately get a new renovation, but 3 days later the Station experienced a strange shape where the roof of the Station Changed into shaped like a Time Circle and at 1889/07/9 the Station Disappeared along with the Tunnel and the City [CENSORED] No one knows why the Station and the City were distorted however, the strong assumption is that the government used the wrong way to repair the Station"

How do you know about this employee, especially with the year being so long ago?  
"the Station experienced a strange shape"  
Once again, I have no idea what you mean here.

> "Level 301" also has an Entity, he is "Dark Smile Guy". Having a tall, thin body, sharp claws and a big smile, he can move quickly and tear his prey apart. According to the theory of various MEG scientists, the entity came from someone who requested a station repair letter from the government, but in the end it ended in distortion. This entity will only appear in "The Ground Room", it may appear on the Surface Level 301, but it is very rare for it to do so"

Why is it so rare for him to appear? What do you mean it came from a person who requested the station be repaired?

> "You can survive by staying on the surface Level 301 so your life level is much more than in "The Ground Room", and in the ruins of the house there must be around 4-6 delicious 1900's Snacks. On the surface Level 301 is also rare once an entity appears, making it impossible to die on the surface of level 301"

Very awkward wording here. Additionally, if an entity *can* appear on the surface, it is not impossible to die. You should also list the entities that can appear on the surface.

> "At the Eagle Post there are about 30 explorers who want to get out of Level 301, they are focused on surviving, looking for weapons and looking for food or drink. They are also focused on building their base which is quite large, every explorer who joins this "Eagle Community" will be given the Eagle Symbol on every Explorer's Armor. They are all friendly and peaceful Members and there is definitely no conflict between members of the Eagle Community"

No need to repeat "looking for" here. You also don't need to say it is quite large. Let the reader judge that for themselves. Change "Explorer" to wanderer, as that is what people who wander the backrooms are called on site. Also, a wanderer is a normal person. They don't come willingly with battle armor on.  
"there is definitely no conflict between members of the Eagle Community"  
Because you used the word definitely, you make this whole sentence seem like you are being sarcastic.

> "The Berserker Community is the 2nd largest Community after the Eagle Community which has 25 Members. However, unlike the Eagle Community, this Community focuses on looking for equipment, crafting weapons, crafting and finding materials in Level 301 houses to make a Bait or Trap for Entities approaching their Headquarters. This Community has the Purpose of killing the Entity "Dark Smile Guy" in "The Ground Room" together. However, this Community's relationship with the Eagle Community is very good, in fact they love to trade essential materials"  
> Sabre Communities

"This community has a total of 20 members and is the last community at level 301. This community has a goal to kill the entity in "The Ground Room". They have sufficient weapons and strong defense armor, they also skilled at setting traps and strategies. They are Brutal towards Entities, but this Community has very good relations also with the Berserker and Eagle Community. This Community also cooperates with the Berserker Community to defeat the Entity in "The Ground Room"  
These two communities have the same purpose, so why aren't they one and the same?

> "In "The Ground Room" there is an Anomaly shaped like a Steam Train that can still be used. Inside the Train there are lots of piles of Coal to be put into a Coal Burner to be converted into Energy and Drive Systems and Machines That train. The train is still functioning properly anind if anyone wants to run this Train, there is a note about "How to Run This Train". As you know the name of this Old Train is "Steam-Boy", this Train was made in the past to carry the Coal Carriage, but he was able to transfer to this Level 301 due to a Severe Accident Outside the Level 301 Tunnel"

Just because it can convert coal into energy(what type of energy?), doesn't make it an anomaly. It's just an ordinary steam train. Also, don't give the train a gender, as it is an object. Explain what the "Severe Accident Outside the Level 301 Tunnel" is. What happened in this "Severe Accident"? You also need to come up with a better name for it.  
I'm skipping the uniform and engine descriptions(good engine name).

> "The Old Station here is the Place or Habitat of the Entity "Dark Smile Guy" which is more precisely located in the Railroad Control Corridor at the Old Station. This station is still suitable for use even all the furniture and equipment at this station are still well maintained and maintained . But if you enter the Corridor of this Station then you will see a Corridor that is Very Dirty, Smelly, Full of Fungi and Parasites and filled with black liquid"

You repeated maintained twice. After you use Grammarly (or some other spell check), reread your writing to make sure it makes sense. Again, you don't have to tell the reader it is "Very Dirty, Smelly". Give it a more vivid description. Also, why should we be worried about these Fungi? What are the Parasites? The black liquid?

> "You can get out of Level 301 in 2 ways. The first is "Search for a house with a shape that is still intact and in order, then enter, if you are lucky then on the 2nd floor you will see a white light in Room 3, if you find it enter and you will be transferred to Level 303. The second way is "By crashing the Entity "Dark Smile Guy" using the Steam Train and if you are lucky, then your Steam Train and "Dark Smile Guy" will fall and roll off the Rails, and you will automatically move to Level Level 302"

Stop repeating "if you are lucky". Also, the sentence starting with "Search" and ending with "303" is a run-on and needs to be broken up into two separate sentences or rephrase it. You need to rephrase the next sentence. You're trying to say the wanderer needs to crash the train into the entity. Your current wording is very confusing.  
\*Gasp\* I'm finally at the last paragraph. Taking a breather here to tell you some good things about what I've read so far. The concept is interesting. I(emphasis on I) haven't come across any articles saying that the level was originally part of the frontrooms. Therefore I say your idea is unique in that respect. However, you need to develop it more. You also need to change or get rid of some details, such as the entity, the train engine, and the machinist uniform. You should also run it through a spell check, as you don't have to worry about bad spelling. You repeated "Level" twice.

> "The only way to enter Level 301 is by "Entering Level Level 300 then look for a bridge and if you are lucky then you will immediately be transferred to Level 301, but if you are unlucky then you will not be transferred to Level 301"

You repeated "Level" twice again. I suggest finding a synonym for entering so you don't repeat it over and over and over and over since it creates an irritating effect and detracts the reader from what you're trying to say. Yes, I know it can be challenging to find synonyms so you can say the same thing in different ways, but there are tools to help you with that, such as thesaurus.com. I'm sure you noticed me repeating the same words, but sometimes there aren't a lot of synonyms for what you are looking for.  
Back to the paragraph.  
Change "unlucky" to something else. Luck doesn't have to do with anything when regarding entering or exiting a level. Say there is a chance that you can go to 301, and there is a chance you won't and will go elsewhere.

  
Good luck with editing your level :D.  
That's all (for now) folks!
