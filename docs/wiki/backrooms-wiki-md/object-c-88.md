**Object Number:** C-88

**"Short Dagger"** will only appear under the following special conditions:

- Two wanderers are engaged in a physical altercation;
- Neither party possesses any lethal weapons;
- The survival difficulty of the level is below 3[1](javascript:;);
- The level must contain only these two individuals present when the above conditions are met.

![A small dagger with a black plastic and a short, silver blabe, resting on an orange surface.](http://rzx.wdfiles.com/local--files/backrooms/db)

A "Short Dagger" recovered by CBS, with the affected wanderer now effectively contained.

# Description

**"Short Dagger"** appears as a short-bladed dagger with a handle made of black plastic, approximately 12cm in length, with bloodstains on the blade, and the DNA in the blood currently cannot be analyzed.

# Properties

The anomalous property of **"Short Dagger"** lies in the fact that, after the special conditions are met, it will appear in the possession of the "weaker" one among the two wanderers in conflict, regardless of whether that party is in the right. The wanderer who obtains the **"Short Dagger"** will be mentally affected, causing them to experience extreme rage, and at that point, the wanderer will use the **"Short Dagger"** to attack the other one at all costs until the target loses vital signs, after which[2](javascript:;), the **"Short Dagger"** will vanish.

# Discovery Location

CBS discovered a wanderer in the male restroom of [Level C-32](https://backrooms-wiki-cn.wikidot.com/level-c-32) who was frantically stabbing a CBS agent with the **"Short Dagger."** The agent had sustained multiple injuries and, after emergency treatment, is now out of danger. The wanderer has been effectively contained, and the sole remaining specimen of Object C-88 has been recovered.
